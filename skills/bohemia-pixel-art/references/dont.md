# WHAT NOT TO DO (each one has cost us a round)

- Drawing a tile rectangle by rectangle in code, then comparing it to his pack (ROUND ELEVEN: "we tried matching the
  assets already and it made the mess").
- Making it small: anything under its family's floor (rule 105: "you use the tiniest amount of pixels possible").
- A black outline round anything. Pillow shading (dark all round the edge, no light direction).
- A checker dither or a noise layer to fake texture or grime. Filters for horror; horror is one calm wrong thing.
- A grid of dark lines where floor tiles meet (rule 104b). A roof drawn from above (rule 104e).
- A mirrored half sold as a facing (rule 107). Scaling by 1.5. Any blur or resample.
- Saying it looks good. Sending it to his tab as FINAL. Editing a placeholder (rule 101c).
- Citing a reference game he has not named, or one outside its department.
