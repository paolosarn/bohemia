Read it this way: size is the floor; colours and the top-eight share say SURFACE, not ramp; value span and mean say a
wide range on a dark middle; saturation says the colour lives on things, not floors; light says from above; the edge
says no line on the ground, a soft ring on things, a dark line on buildings, a hard frame on doors; grain says fine and
clustered.

From the laws, not the bank: the person's 112 px box (about 100 painted, one size on every surface, the ground may
zoom, the person may not); twelve keys a bar at 120 BPM and no joint past 120 degrees between keys; a square grid; one
pixel size, whole-number scaling only; eight drawn facings for anything that turns (rule 107).
