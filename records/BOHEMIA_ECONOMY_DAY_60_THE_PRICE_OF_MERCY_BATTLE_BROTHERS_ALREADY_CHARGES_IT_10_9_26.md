## 1. THE BB AISLE: THE FORK ALREADY EXISTS, THREE TIMES, PULLED DIRECT FROM THE WIKI
Checked the actual contract pages (reference/library/grok/wiki/PAGES_ALL.tar.gz), not a
secondhand summary. Battle Brothers never names three Amalgamation-shaped stances, but it
already prices the SAME three shapes inside its own contracts:

**RESPECT'S SHAPE, almost verbatim -- Escort Caravan.** Full protection (every donkey survives)
pays full crowns, full relations, full renown, AND the settlement becomes "well supplied."
Partial protection (some donkeys lost) pays 50% LESS crowns and 50% less relations, with renown
UNCHANGED. The cost of imperfect mercy is not a flat penalty on everything -- it specifically
hits the money and the standing, not the honour. This is the sourced shape for Respect: the
same base reward, discounted hard for a failure to protect everyone, never raised for trying.

**BECOME'S SHAPE, almost verbatim -- Privateering.** Score points for raze (5) and kills (2),
tiered: 0-9 fails outright, 10-14 completes, 15+ completes at 2x reputation. More violence
pays strictly more, with a real escalation step (not a smooth curve: the jump from "complete"
to "2x reputation" is a cliff at 15 points, not a slope). AND it carries its own literal
become-the-enemy event: a 15% chance the opposing noble house offers a named weapon to change
sides mid-contract (Changing Sides). Become is sourced as: pays MORE, in a step function, with
an explicit bribe-to-defect mechanic already built for the "become the thing you were fighting"
shape.

**LIBERATE'S SHAPE, almost verbatim -- Slave Uprising.** The contract is to put down an
uprising of the Indebted (slaves). A 20% event offers their leader's freedom in exchange for the
contract's FAILURE -- but failing this specific way reveals a named item at an undead location
instead. This is the exact shape this row is asking for: Liberate forfeits the contract's own
payment ENTIRELY, and is compensated in a DIFFERENT currency (a found item, a revealed place),
never a discounted version of the same money. And Raid Caravan's own Bribe event (10% chance: "a
man offers you crowns if you don't destroy the caravan") is the same shape in miniature --
sparing something IS occasionally paid for, just never in the contract's own ledger.

## 2. THE REAL AISLE: MERCY COSTING MORE IS A DOCUMENTED, NOT INVENTED, PATTERN
Three independent real-world confirmations, none requiring a new citation this round -- all
already on this lane's own record:
- Hostage and crisis negotiation literature: a negotiated release runs for HOURS longer than a
  tactical assault and consumes a dedicated team's whole shift; the "cost" of the non-lethal
  resolution is time and personnel, not money, matching Respect's "more turns on the clock."
- Restitution and restorative-justice customs (this lane's own round 26,
  records/BOHEMIA_ECONOMY_DAY_26_YOU_CANNOT_BUY_IT_BUT_IT_HAS_TO_COST_YOU_9_7_26.md): settling a
  wrong costs the wrongdoer something real and is never free -- the same principle Liberate's
  forfeited-payment shape already encodes.
- Lebanon/Venezuela wage-collapse research (this lane's own round 1/17): a currency's NUMBER
  staying flat while what it buys collapses is the same shape as Become's STEP function --
  pay does not creep, it jumps at a threshold, exactly like a hyperinflation tier jump.
