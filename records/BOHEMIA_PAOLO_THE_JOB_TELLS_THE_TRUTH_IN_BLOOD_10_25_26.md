## THE RULING (rule 208)
(a) THE STARS ARE THE TRUTH ABOUT THIS JOB FOR THIS CREW, TODAY: every job's danger is worked out from (1) the fight it is sure
    to bring (its enemy as rolled: kind, count, gear, level) and (2) the chance of every other fight on the way (the trip's length
    and the roads' parties, rule 186(c)), each played out with the same calculator the AUTO button uses (rule 144(e)) against his
    crew as it stands (who is able, laid up, what they wear). The danger is the chance he comes home with someone dead or down,
    weighted by how bad.
(b) THE STARS ARE RED: one to three red stars (Battle Brothers' skulls in our colour), from that number. Draft cut-offs
    [COORDINATOR'S DEFAULT, rule 205, his to kill; TUNING sets them]: one star when he comes home clean nine times in ten or
    better, three stars when it is worse than six in ten, two between. A job that kills him outright most times (the wolves on a
    lone man) carries three and the bloodiest button.
(c) THE BUTTON BLEEDS: the job's button on the town's board, the offer and the phone's card wears blood in the crashbox look
    (rule 130): clean for an easy job, more and darker blood as the danger rises, one continuous scale (the way the AUTO button's
    colour runs, 144(e)), never only three looks.
(d) IT CHANGES WHEN HIS CREW CHANGES: hire, lose a man, a man laid up, new gear: the stars and the blood are worked out again
    the next time he looks.
(e) THE MEET CARD COUNTS TRUE: what the meet card says (how many, what kind) matches what stands on the board; never '4 MEN' for
    two beasts.
(f) THE GATE: for 200 rolled jobs on the phone profile, each job's stars against 100 AUTO plays of that job with his crew as it
    stood: a one-star job comes home clean at least 85 in 100, a three-star job under 65 (TUNING's numbers); the button's blood
    rises with the measured danger (rank order); the meet card's count equals the board's.
(g) WORLD [the job tells the truth, in blood] FIRST (the number and the board's look, its own marked blocks); UI NOTE (the blood
    in the crashbox kit); TUNING NOTE (the cut-offs); line 129 on his list; EYES takes ten jobs and checks the stars against what
    happened.
