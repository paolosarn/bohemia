('stamina' is the man's action points and fatigue for the turn.)

## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (his words, rule 33)
Tap a tile farther than the man can reach this turn: the path is shown; tap it again and he walks that path as far as his action
points and fatigue let him, and stops there.

## WHAT OURS DOES NOW
A tile past his reach this turn is refused (the fight's tap path, slices/BOHEMIA_FIGHT.html; the lit tiles show only what he can
reach), so he must pick a nearer tile himself.

## THE RULING (rule 206)
(a) FIRST TAP on any open tile, near or far: the whole path is drawn; the part he can walk this turn is lit, the rest is dim,
    and the tile where he will stop is marked.
(b) SECOND TAP on the same tile: he walks the path as far as his action points and fatigue allow this turn and stops on the
    marked tile; the rest of his turn is his (he can still shoot if he has the points).
(c) The path is the real one: around blockers, through his own men (rule 199), never ending on a friend, and it counts the free
    swings he would take leaving a zone (rule 198(c)): a path that walks out of a zone says so on the first tap.
(d) Computer: the same with a click and a second click (Battle Brothers' double click). AUTO already plans far moves; nothing
    changes for it.
(e) THE GATE: in rolled fights on the phone and computer profiles, a tap and a second tap on a tile past his reach moves him
    along the drawn path to the marked tile, never refused; the AP and fatigue spent match the steps; never onto a friend.
(f) COMBAT [a far tile walks as far as he can] right after [the pistol is never a club], before [walk through your own men];
    line 126; EYES checks.
