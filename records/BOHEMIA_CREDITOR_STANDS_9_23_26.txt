BOHEMIA -- CREDITOR STANDS (9/23/26, PEOPLE lane)
VAMILY job [creditor stands], row YOU-DO-NOT-INHERIT-A-BILL-YOU-INHERIT-THE-PERSON.

THE ROW, VERBATIM: "DYNASTY's carry research sharpened the coordinator's own 9/5
ruling and it is adopted: in the real world a child is NOT liable for a parent's
debts, and the only reason is that a court stops it. Bohemia has no courts, so the
debt carrying is not a fantasy mechanic, it is what the law exists to prevent,
running in a valley where the law is gone. But the form was wrong: an heir does
not inherit a NUMBER, they inherit THE PEOPLE HE OWED, still standing there, still
remembering (which is also ECONOMY's finding: a debt is a person who remembers).
Build the creditor who is still there after the fold, not a balance on a card."

WHY THE ORGAN SAID OTHERWISE, AND IT IS NOT A SLOPPY LINE. inherit() drops an

The DYNASTY half of that is right and this round does not touch it: a thing one
person saw and never mentioned must never become the thing your child is judged
for. What is not right is deciding, for everybody, that they are gone.

*** AND THE REPO ITSELF HAD ALREADY REFUSED THAT CALL. *** bohemia_family.js, on
its parked bury() writer, in its own words:

    "What had no caller was a writer for a death that happens LATER -- old age,
     mostly -- and WHEN a person dies of age is a magnitude, so it waits on Paolo."

Nothing in this game ages anybody out. So the organ decided the ONE thing the
game had explicitly refused to decide, for the whole valley, as a default, and
then the surface went on drawing them. A game may not tell you somebody is dead
and then draw him at his door.

AND THE REAL WORLD AGREES, which is the other angle this lane owes. A generation
step is about thirty years. A lender who was an adult when he handed it over is
very likely alive at the end of it: in modern US life tables a thirty-year-old man
reaches sixty something like nine times in ten. You do not get to bury a valley to
close a ledger.

================================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================

A. THE ORGAN ASKS INSTEAD OF ASSUMING. inherit() takes an OPTIONAL `alive`
   predicate on the witness's owner id, the same shape witness() already uses for
   `where`. WITH NOTHING PASSED THE ANSWER IS BYTE FOR BYTE WHAT IT ALWAYS WAS
   (carried 0, died 1, the deed gone), which is why both gates above stay green: a
   caller that cannot say who is alive has not earned a different answer.

B. WHAT A LIVING WITNESS KEEPS IS A MEMORY, NEVER A CHARGE. The deed stays his,
   still pointing at the PARENT, and is stamped `ended`. forceOf returns 0 for an
   ended deed, so:
       what the father was worth to him     -4.00
       what the child is charged              0
       what the father is still charged       0
   The dynasty rule is untouched and no number that was on the board before this
   existed has moved. The man remembers your father. He is not billing you.

C. THE CITY ANSWERS, AND SAYS WHAT ITS ANSWER IS WORTH. ctFold hands the organ
   ctStillHere(), built from the world's own census, and the file says out loud
   that it answers STILL HERE and not STILL ALIVE, because when a person dies of
   age is Paolo's and is still waiting. It is conservative where it cannot see:
   the census is the block he is standing on, so anybody further out reads as gone
   and keeps the old behaviour exactly. It never claims somebody is alive that it
   cannot find.

D. AND THE ROW'S OWN QUESTION IS ASKABLE IN ONE CALL. whoRemembers() returns
   PEOPLE -- who they are, what they saw, whether they watched it themselves --
   and there is no balance, no amount and no total anywhere in the answer. That is
   the row's sentence made into a function.

E. HE SAYS IT, ONCE, WITH A NAME AND A FACE (rule 19c). The bark sits ABOVE
   everything else on the street, because meeting somebody who knew your father is
   the loudest thing that can happen there, and fires once per person per story.
   IT CUTS BOTH WAYS ON PURPOSE: the man your father did a turn for is standing
   there too and says that. An heir who only ever meets the debts is a punishment,
   not an inheritance.
