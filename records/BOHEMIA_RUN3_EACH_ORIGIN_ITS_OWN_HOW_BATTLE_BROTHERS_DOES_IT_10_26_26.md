## THE SIX LEVERS BATTLE BROTHERS USES (the patterns; ours use only the first and a little of the third today)
1. THE ROSTER AND THE LOSS: a cap (12, 18), a bigger company (25 and 16), a hiring whitelist (lowborn only), a ratio of two kinds of
   men (Manhunters), a death that ends the game (Lone Wolf, Gladiators).
2. FLAT ECONOMY: prices 10% either way, renown at 66%, wages 50 for 35, bag slots 81 to 108, starting money 450 to 3200.
3. STANDING AND PLACE: renown -100 to 200, reputation, a hostile or a friendly house, the start on the map (south, north, a raided
   village, an arena city).
4. ONE FORCED SYSTEM that changes what you play for: oaths, sacrifices, potions and their sickness, the whip and the expendable men.
5. SMALL PASSIVE EDGES: speed, a scouting report, longer tracks, first to act, more loot, extra trophies, never confident, no mood
   loss on retreat, starting hurt or unhappy.
6. CONTENT ONLY IT GETS: events (Anatomists 23, Oathtakers 12, Cultists 7), items (the skull, the ledgers, a potion), recruits only
   it meets (cultists in any town, squires, indebted).
The three easy Companion origins differ only by money, renown, the tutorial and the start: they are the baseline the other twelve
change.

## WHAT OURS HAVE NOW (measured in origins.json and the door, BUILD 10/26zj)
Each has its men (rolled once a game by engine/bohemia_origin_start.js at real levels, three talents, his own kit), its batteries,
its roster and field caps, a corner on the map (the start kinds, rule 236(c)) and a line (draft). Its 'rules' field is Battle
Brothers' own words, shown on the door, and none of them RUNS: no price multiplier, no first round, no loot edge, no whitelist, no
loss rule, no forced system. Its men wear the people the rolled company wears (person_town_*), the same clothes in every origin.
Its start town sells what every town sells.

## WHAT STEP 2 BUILDS (the row, in order, the data a modder reads in origins.json, each with its gate leg)
(a) ITS LOOK: a 'look' per origin (the crew's colours and clothes inside runway and analog horror: the Faithful in ash and bone,
    the water truck in hi-vis and rubber, the deserters in their old faction's colours with the patches torn off, the lab rats in
    lab whites gone brown...) handed to COOK THREE as a NOTE to render; until the renders land, the origin's men are dealt from
    the whole people whose clothes are nearest its palette (the same rule as the chair: never a part painted on a body).
(b) ITS SHELF: two or three things only its start town sells (the water truck: water by the tank; the faithful: a relic; the lab
    rats: a sample case; the hunters: a trophy rack...), rows in origins.json the settlement's stall reads when the town is the
    start town, priced by ECONOMY's own formula.
(c) ITS RULE: one rule of its own in Battle Brothers' way, from the six levers, the ones the game can already run first: the
    water truck's 10% (the stall's price), the deserters' first round (COMBAT's initiative, a NOTE), the raiders' loot (RUN FOUR's
    loot roll, a NOTE), the Block Watch's whitelist (the post), the Lone Wolf's and the Fight Pit's loss (the struck-down rule,
    CAMP), the hunters' extra trophy (the loot); the forced systems (oaths, sacrifices, potions) are each a row of their own.
(d) THE GATE: every origin starts with its look on his men, its start town's shelf carries its things, its rule fires once in a
    measured game.
