## 2. **THE ONE THAT IS A CORRECTION, AND HE IS RIGHT**

> "I'm not sure what you did other than just highlight all the streets"

**That is exactly what the picture did.** It lit every road in the valley and
graded the brightness by travel speed: the freeway brightest, the boulevards just
under it, then the dirt, then the washes.

The finding that round was that **a six-lane Las Vegas boulevard was rated slower
than driving across open desert**, because the road was being averaged in with the
shops and flats beside it. That fact lived in the *gap between two near-identical
yellows*, and in the caption. **A person does not see a ramp. He saw streets lit
up, because streets lit up is what was there.**

He thumbed it up and still told me he could not read it. The thumb is the
courtesy; the sentence is the verdict.

**RULE 89 SAYS THE SAME THING AND IT LANDED AFTER THAT COOK** (Paolo 10/10, on a
different lane's item: *"Can't tell difference. Looks like dogshit"*): a look item
is a **before and an after of the same thing, side by side in one picture**, and
if he cannot see the difference, it is a NO before he votes. The roads picture had
no before and no after. It had one state and a legend.

## 3. WHICH OF MY SIX WORKED, AND WHY

Reading the six against each other rather than defending them:

**The two that read** are the two built on a contrast between two pictures of the
same thing:

- **the insides** — the same police station twice, same walls, same doors, and
  fifteen rooms blue on one side. You cannot look at it and miss it.
- **the three blocks** — four boards side by side, one nearly empty, one nearly
  solid.

**The one that did not read** is the one whose finding was a *ramp* and a
*number*: the roads.

The middle three (the god gear's rings sized by guard, the thirteen ground
panels, the valley coloured by board kind) are comparisons too, which is probably
why they got "good direction" rather than "I can't tell what this is".

**SO THE LANE'S RULE FROM HERE, AND IT IS NOT NEW, IT IS RULE 89 APPLIED TO MY OWN
WORK: a WORLD picture shows its finding as a difference between two pictures of
the same thing. Never as a brightness ramp with a key. Never as a number in a
caption.** The insides cook is the template. The roads cook is the counter-example
and he named it himself.

## 4. WHAT THIS DOES NOT CHANGE

The roads data is not wrong. The boulevard really was rated below open desert, the
network really does connect every settlement across thirty rolled valleys, and the
gate that holds both is green. **The defect was in the showing, not the finding.**
That distinction matters because the fix is to re-cook the picture, never to
re-open the measurement.

Nothing is re-cooked here. WORLD is held (rule 88: no round, no VOTE sheet,
nothing into the demo). When the hold lifts, the roads picture is owed a second
cut, and under rule 82 the freeway family is "cook, do not show" until DIRECTION
