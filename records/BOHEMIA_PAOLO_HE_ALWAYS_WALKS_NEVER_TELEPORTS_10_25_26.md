# PAOLO 10/25: HE ALWAYS WALKS, NEVER TELEPORTS (LOCKED, rule 204; strikes 169(c)'s last clause)

## HIS WORDS (verbatim, voice to text)
"Bro, am I tweaking or am I seeing my guy instantly teleported to the different settlement stores instead of walking what the
fuck is going on?"

## WHY, HONESTLY
He is not tweaking. Rule 169(c) (the coordinator's words) ended 'a walk that would confuse is not walked'. RUN TWO built it as
written (172cd52e, slices/BOHEMIA_SETTLEMENT_SCREEN.html): 'a way over sixteen beats is not walked, he is already there', and
'a picture with no ground traced is never walked'; when the path finder finds no way (people standing in it, a store's door
inside a traced block) he goes to 'the nearest ground'. Each of those puts him at the store in one frame: a teleport.

## THE RULING (rule 204)
(a) HE ALWAYS WALKS: every tap in a town moves him there on the ground, every time, whatever the distance. No frame ever moves
    him more than a step. 169(c)'s 'a walk that would confuse is not walked' is STRUCK.
(b) A LONG WAY IS RUN, NOT SKIPPED: past a short walk he breaks into his run (the rig's own run, on the 120 beat), so no walk-up
    takes longer than about four seconds (eight beats); a tap during the walk turns him to the new place from where he stands.
(c) THERE IS ALWAYS A WAY: the store's door, the stall's front and every person's side are reachable on the traced ground (the
    trace is fixed where it is not); people in the way step aside or he goes round them; no way found is a bug the gate
    catches, never a reason to jump.
(d) The sheet can open while he walks (cd2582ba), but he is seen walking under it.
(e) THE GATE: on every town picture, phone and computer, every store, stall, door and person tapped from every other: his
    position moves at most one step a frame (zero jumps), he arrives beside the thing, no walk-up over about four seconds, his
    feet on the ground every frame (169(d) stands).
(f) RUN TWO [he always walks, never teleports] FIRST, before its other open rows (it is the bug he sees); line 125; EYES checks.
