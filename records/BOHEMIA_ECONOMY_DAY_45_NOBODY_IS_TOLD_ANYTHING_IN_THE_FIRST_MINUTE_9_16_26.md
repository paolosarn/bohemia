## 0. THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG

The row says **"handed"**, and that is the assumption to break.

**In all three cities, the first sixty seconds contain no information from an institution
at all.** No announcement, no notice, no official word, no sign. Argentina's government
*did* make the announcement — and what actually reached people was **the sound of their
neighbours**:

> *"Buenos Aires residents began appearing at windows **within hours**, banging pots and
> pans with spoons… the cacerolazo was middle-class, decentralized, and furious… the news
> spread **organically through neighbourhoods rather than through formal institutional
> channels** — neighbours telling neighbours."*

Caracas is the same fact wearing a family:

> *"Rubén Darío Peña was **woken up at 7:00 a.m. by his mother** to go wait in line to get
> into stores."* Queues formed **at 4 a.m.** outside supermarkets all over the city; lines
> ran **beyond six blocks**.

And Beirut:

> *"People **screamed at the granite faces of ATMs** that suddenly had manners, limits, and
> no cash."* And what they actually did: *"parents **patting empty wallets** for transport
> money to get to work, **bargaining with cab drivers who were also out of cash**."*

**Nobody in any of these accounts was told. Somebody woke them, or they reached for
something and it did not work.** The first minute of a crash is not a briefing. **It is
being late to something other people already know.**

So the deliverable is not three facts to hand a player. **It is three things to let him
overhear, in this order**, and the order is what the record gives us for free.

---

## 1. THE THREE, IN ORDER

### ONE — **SOMEBODY WAKES YOU, AND IT IS SOMEBODY WHO KNOWS YOU.**
*A mother at seven in the morning. A neighbour at a window. A cab driver who is also
short.* Every account starts with **a person**, never a number and never a sign. The very
first thing a stranger meets should be a **mouth**, and it should be somebody with a reason
to bother — the news arrives inside a relationship, which is the thing fourteen rounds of
this study already say Bohemia's economy is made of.

### TWO — **THE THING IN YOUR POCKET DOES NOT WORK.**
Beirut is the cleanest: **you pat your wallet for the fare and it is not enough**, and the
driver is short too. Not *"prices went up"* — round 36 already found the first sight is
never a price. **What you are carrying stopped working**, and the person you would ask for
help is in the same position. **This is the fact that has to be felt rather than read**,
and it costs nothing: it is a refusal, and this game refuses well already.

### THREE — **THERE IS A PLACE TO GO, AND OTHERS ARE ALREADY THERE.**
A queue at four in the morning, six blocks long, before the doors open. You do not learn
*what to do* in the first minute. **You learn where everybody already is**, and that you
are behind them. That is round 36's third sight arriving a full nine minutes earlier, and
it is the one that turns a minute of confusion into a direction to walk.

**The order is the finding.** A person, then a failure, then a place. Reverse any two and
it becomes a tutorial: a place with no reason, a failure with nobody to feel it with, or a
person with nothing to say yet.

> **DO NOT HAND HIM THREE FACTS. LET HIM OVERHEAR THEM, IN THIS ORDER.**
>
> **ONE, A PERSON WHO KNOWS HIM WAKES HIM.** Not a card, not a line of narration, not a

## 7. THE TOOL THAT LIED, AND THE FIX

**My own claim push reported success on a push that did not land.** The inline loop read
`if git push -q origin HEAD:main; then echo pushed`, which swallowed the failure output and
took the exit code at face value: it printed **"pushed"** while `VAMILY.md` on main still
said OPEN. Caught by checking the board after, not by the tool.

**Fixed:** the helper now pushes, re-fetches, and **verifies with
`git merge-base --is-ancestor HEAD origin/main` before it will say the word landed.** An
exit code is a claim; containment is a measurement. That is the same rule this lane already
applies to every probe, applied at last to its own plumbing.
