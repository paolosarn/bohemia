## The one change to the measured roof, written down
The eave (13.7 percent) and lean (5.7 percent) were measured as fractions of the ranch's front. On
a 26 m building that is a 3 m overhang and a 1.3 m lean, which no carpenter builds. An eave is a
rafter's length. This sheet takes the ranch's eave and lean IN PIXELS from the plain ranch's 10 m
front (eave 78 px = 1.37 m, lean 41 px) and lays them on every roof here. The roof's height keeps the
measured 1.49 times one storey (254 px): a motel room is as deep as a ranch. The tool asserts the
eave stays under 1.6 m.
