WHAT THAT MEANS, said plainly:
  - THE HAIR IS STILL THE MOST BANDED ART IN THE REPO at native scale. Every other
    bank in the building reads 0.0 and the hair does not. The bounce-back stands.
  - THE CMU COMPARISON IS GONE. 31.8% as baked, 0.0% native -- that number was its
    4x zoom, nothing else, so the hair was never "twice as bad as the block".
  - The honest gap is against ZERO, not against 31.8, and the honest magnitude is
    a fraction of 62.

  the haircut sheets alone   77.0% as baked, 15.4% native  (11 sheets)

A WRONG GUESS, KEPT BECAUSE IT COST A BUILD: the obvious suspect was the flat cream
SHIRT under every head on the sheet. Splitting a sheet in two says otherwise --
head 71.9%, body 78.0% as baked -- so both band and the shirt is not the cause. A
fix aimed there would have missed.

WHAT WAS FIXED THIS ROUND, and the number it moved (native scale, the honest one):
  the partings ran DEAD STRAIGHT down the whole mass. The 8/28 drift that was
  supposed to stop that re-rolled a fresh -1/0/+1 EVERY ROW, which is noise around
  a fixed column, amplitude one pixel -- a ruled line with a burr on it. Replaced
  with a real walk that remembers where the last row got to, leashed to two cells
  so it never leaves the skull.
     first attempt, one walk for the whole head    25.3% -> 23.4%   (a rounding error)
  AND THAT FAILURE IS THE LESSON: every parting asked the same row and got the same
  answer, so the whole comb shifted TOGETHER and the distance between neighbouring
  partings never changed. BANDING IS ABOUT THE GAP, NOT ABOUT WHERE THE LINES SIT.
  Slide a picket fence sideways and it is still a picket fence.
     each parting walking its own way             25.3% -> 15.4%

STILL NOT DONE, said plainly: the other half of their sentence. "A flat cream mass"
is about the FORM of the back of the head -- there is no light on the crown and no
falloff at the nape, so the mass reads as a cut-out. That is a separate fix and it
is not in this round.

READ THIS BEFORE DRAWING A CONCLUSION. A NUMBER IS NOT A FINDING UNTIL YOU KNOW
WHAT IT IS COUNTING. This instrument reads a PICTURE, and a picture of a head
contains a head; a skull is nearly the same width for many rows all by itself.
That is why the comparison that matters is hair against the other banks AT THE
SAME SCALE, which is the last column and nothing else.
