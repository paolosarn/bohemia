# RESEARCH RECORD — 3-CURRENCY ECONOMY + CENTURY RULE (coordinator, 7/26/26)
Commissioned by Paolo ("you can do big brain research on that"). Full report
delivered in the coordinator session; distilled here for the lanes.

VALIDATION: the locked 3-currency design (RESOURCES/ELECTRICITY/CLOUT) is the
pattern best-in-class small-economy games converged on (Fallout Shelter's 3
resources, Frostpunk's 2-loop core, Kingdom Two Crowns' single coin). Civ-5
style parallel tracks confirmed as the anti-pattern. THE ONE-SENTENCE TEST:
if a number can't be an object on screen, it doesn't get to exist.

RECOMMENDED SHAPE (pending Paolo's knob rulings):
- RESOURCES = the BUILD-AND-FEED wallet (construct + feed). Icons pop out of
  the buildings that make them (apple/duct tape travel visibly, Kingdom-style).
- ELECTRICITY = the RUN wallet: buildings are BUILT with resources but only
  WAKE UP powered; housing needs power only to be safe at night. Power is the
  currency of safety and reach (light=territory law becomes the economy).
  Deliberately never enough.
- CLOUT = the PEOPLE-AND-PERMISSION wallet: recruits, access, prices, big
  named declarations. NEVER exchanges into the other two (CK3/FNV rule: fame
  buys treatment, not goods). Shown as fame TIER + pool. The only currency
  that crosses act breaks at full value = the dynasty currency.
- CHAINS: max 2 steps (3 for one endgame showpiece). One chain per currency.
- CENTURY RULE mechanism (no 100-year simulation): at act end store per-cell
  INVESTMENT TIER 0-3 + landmark booleans + dynasty clout tier. Next act
  renders each cell from its tier (ruins -> showpiece via tier-swapped tiles;
  tilespec per-act materials already support this), inherits working systems
  from high tiers, and opens with ONE narrated fly-over of what the last
  generation built or let rot. Per-cell tiers keep cause LOCAL (the block you
  powered is the block alive in 2120).
