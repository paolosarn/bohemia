# RUN TWO: THE RARE MAN AT THE POST (10/25, rule 183(a), BUILT)

Numbers: TUNING's recruit.rare (records/BOHEMIA_TUNING_THE_FIRST_FIFTY_DAYS_AND_THE_LONE_WOLF_10_25_26.md s1e and s3; the NOTE
[lone wolf numbers] on RUN TWO's board, item 3). The mechanism is this lane's.

HIS WORDS (10/25): "there definitely isn't incentive for the first 50 days to grow your crew unless it's god-tier recruits... that's
why in Battle Brothers I always loved playing Lone Wolf."

## WHAT WAS TRUE
The post dealt six lowborn backgrounds at level 1 (engine/bohemia_goodbros.js: farmhand, messenger, butcher, brawler, thief,
militia). The god-tier man Battle Brothers deals now and then in a big town never existed. TUNING measured the trade he describes:
an ordinary early hire makes the good wolf worse (never-lost runs 34 -> 14 in 100); a god-tier hire, if he can be paid, makes him
better (34 -> 48 hired the day the purse covers him, 34 -> 74 on day one).

## WHAT IS BUILT
1. **records/target/bb/rare_recruits.json** (a modder's file): three men, Battle Brothers' own rare backgrounds in our names: a road
   knight (hedge knight), a gun for hire (sellsword), a blade master (swordmaster); each with his three talents, his plan (which
   four stats rise a level), his own mid kit (the hedge knight's is TUNING's measured: a winged mace, a kite shield, mail and a nasal
   helm with mail; the gun for hire a crossbow, a mail shirt and a nasal helm; the blade master an arming sword and padded leather),
   his base (150, the blade master 400: the Game Guide's), and one line in his own mouth (draft:true).
2. **The post deals him** (slices/BOHEMIA_SETTLEMENT_SCREEN.html, __THE_RARE_MAN_AT_THE_POST__): only at a town or a fortress, a slot is
   his 3 times in 100 (the wiki's 2.2 to 8.3%); the same man all week, like the rest of the post. Measured over 400 weeks of six
   slots: 2.4 in 100 at a town, 3.6 at a fortress, never at a camp.
3. **He is made rare**: level 2 or 3, every stat rolled in the top quarter of his background's range, his levels taken on his plan
   (four picks a level, rules.json), three talents. 144 rare men over the run, every one in range.
4. **He is priced like it**: the hall's own formula with his base, his level and his kit at 1.25, four fifths: a level-2 road knight
   in his mid kit is exactly 500 batteries (TUNING's 497 plus the pistol he holds at the post); a gun for hire at level 2, 227. His
   night is the bill and his background's wage (a road knight 4 a night at level 2).
5. **He reads as rare the moment he is met**: a gold frame around his card, a RARE badge, his level, what he wears in our words
   (PIPE, RIOT SHIELD, PLATE CARRIER, FULL RIOT HELM), his line in quotes, his three stars lit.
6. **Hired, he walks in wearing his kit**: weapon, shield, body and helm on him (the crew screen's hired path and the map's hand-over
   to the fight now keep a shield and a helm; an ordinary hire carries none, as before), the pistol in the bag, his level, his
   numbers, his stars and his line.
7. **FOUND AND FIXED ON THE WAY: every hall card ran off the phone.** On the 390 px phone every man's stats ran 23 to 46 px past his
   card's edge, the sheet scrolled sideways and the fourth column and the night's price were cut. The stats now take as many columns
   as fit (two on a phone, four on a computer); nothing on any card is past its edge.
