THE SHIRT THAT IS NOT CLOTH  --  CHARACTER lane, 9/23/26, [runway redo]

HIS INSTRUCTION WAS "ANALOG HORROR DIRECTION MEETS Rick Owens meets Balenciaga meets
Bottega Veneta." Two rounds of runway work answered the middle two names, and BOTH
RECORDS SAID PLAINLY THAT NEITHER ANSWERED "ANALOG HORROR MEETS", because a better-cut
trouser is not a wrong thing. THIS IS THAT HALF, and it is the one house code that
answers rule 20 directly instead of being decorated with it afterwards.

RNWY-16: the FW22 show opened on a white tank and straight jeans made ENTIRELY of
leather, tooled to look exactly like cotton; the SS23 check was printed twelve times
to reach flannel's depth. THE ANALOG HORROR BIBLE'S RULE 1 IS "the ordinary frame, one
wrong thing... name the wrong thing in one sentence or the frame fails." The sentence
here is six words: THAT SHIRT IS NOT CLOTH.

MEASURED, NOT ASSERTED, because "you can just about tell" is a taste sentence:
  THE MID STEP IS IDENTICAL, which is what makes it read as the same garment:
    DUST PLAID  cloth mid [128,110,80]   hide mid [128,110,80]   SAME
    BLUE JEANS  cloth mid [64,80,110]   hide mid [64,80,110]   SAME

  AND THE VALUE SPREAD IS THE WHOLE MECHANISM -- dark floor down, specular up:
    DUST PLAID  cloth spread 78   hide spread 148   1.90x
    BLUE JEANS  cloth spread 88   hide spread 166   1.89x

  HOW MUCH OF THE BODY ACTUALLY CHANGED. A near-zero answer would mean this cook is a
  lie dressed as a finding, so it is printed either way:
    DUST PLAID SHIRT  -> DUST PLAID HIDE   355 of 2893 painted pixels moved  (12.3%), worst pixel 122/765
    BLUE JEANS        -> BLUE HIDE JEANS   332 of 2893 painted pixels moved  (11.5%), worst pixel 150/765

WHY THIS IS A RAMP AND NOT A SHEEN RENDERER. My own reference line for RNWY-16 says
the sprite-scale tell is a hard specular band, and a band needs new drawing code in
the garment generator -- which is COOK's art, not this lane's (ONE SYSTEM, ONE
SESSION). So it reaches the same read through the one channel that is DATA: the ramp.
GARM-01 is the licence -- "at sprite scale fabric is VALUE BANDS, not drawn fold
lines" -- and GARM-03 is kept too, because the hue is spent on nothing at all: it is
copied from the cloth, and only value moves. If COOK later wants the real band, this
is the data version of the same idea and it blocks nothing.

NOT WIRED TO ANY FACTION. The two garments are on the rail and nobody wears them. Rule
18 holds the play surface; rule 30 says a re-cook goes through the bible before it
comes back, and this went through rule 1 by construction. The picture is in VOTE.

HONEST LIMIT: this is ONE wrong thing done ONE way. It does not make the valley analog
horror and nobody should read it as that. It is the first garment in this wardrobe
whose whole reason to exist is that something about it is wrong.

AND ONE THING THIS ROUND DID THAT NEEDS SAYING OUT LOUD.

Adding two garments made SHAPE FROZEN red, because the baseline is a frozen list and two
new names are not on it. The fix is to re-bake the baseline, which my own tool refuses to
do without a written reason and a record on disk. Correct.

*** BUT THE RE-BAKE ALSO SWALLOWED SOMEBODY ELSE'S DRIFT, AND THE FIRST REASON I WROTE DID
NOT SAY SO. *** All 330 existing silhouettes had already moved by about +15 opaque pixels at
PORTRAIT's eye commit 79ae65d -- this lane pinned that last round by running the gate at
that commit and at its parent, and it was still red at the start of this one. Re-baking
makes that drift THE NEW ACCEPTED TRUTH. The gate will never report it again.

I wrote a reason naming only my two garments, looked at it, and re-baked with the whole
thing written down instead. A baseline re-bake is the one operation in this lane that can
make a live defect invisible, and a reason that mentions only the half you are proud of is
how a defect gets lost forever with a green tick over it.

SO, PLAINLY, FOR WHOEVER READS THIS NEXT: +15 PIXELS ON EVERY BODY IN THE GAME IS NOT FIXED
AND NOT EXPLAINED. It is now invisible to the gate. It lives here and in the baseline's own
reason field, and nowhere else.
