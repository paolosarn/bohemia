# POCKET CITY 2'S BUILDINGS AGAINST OURS (Paolo 11/5, the coordinator's research)

## HIS WORDS
"I want you to research every building type in Pocket City 2 and like the eight different genres that they have buildings for and
see if you're missing any of those buildings"

## WHERE THIS SITS IN THE REFERENCE LAW
Pocket City 2 was ours for the drop-in transition (7/1) and the zoom range (9/30) only. From 11/5 it is also A CHECKLIST OF BUILDING
KINDS, by his ask: a list our map is checked against, never its look, its art, its city-builder rules or its fantasy pieces. Every
building we add is a REAL Las Vegas place in the collapse (realism first), drawn our way.

## WHAT WE ARE MISSING (each a real Las Vegas place, in the order the coordinator would build them)
1. THE LAB THE BEASTS BROKE OUT OF (their eco research lab). Our biggest hole. The whole beast lore (rule 9/29: every beast is a
   de-extinct animal that broke out of a lab when the dollar died) has no building: Tule Springs' lab is named in the map's text and
   never drawn. A place to find, fight in and learn from.
2. A BANK WITH ITS VAULT (their bank and investment bank). The most realistic economic crash simulator has no bank on the map. The
   dollar died: a bank is a building full of paper nobody wants and the strongest room in town.
3. THE UNEMPLOYMENT OFFICE (their employment center). The line around the block is the crash in one picture, and it is where a
   company finds men who need work.
4. THE HARDWARE STORE (their home improvement center). The first place looted in every real collapse: tools, wire, sheet metal,
   seeds, propane. A scavenging site and a home base's supply.
5. THE GAS POWER PLANT (their power plant). The valley's real power, beside the dam and the solar: if batteries are the money, the
   plant that charges them is the mint, and whoever holds it holds the money supply.
6. THE AQUARIUM (Shark Reef, under a Strip casino, real). Sharks in a tank nobody feeds, in the dark of a dead casino: analog horror
   and a beast fight in a place nobody else has.
7. THE ANIMAL SHELTER (their animal shelter). The valley's dogs (already in the game) came from somewhere; a place for them, and for
   a dog in the company.
8. THE RANCH AND THE GREENHOUSE (their animal pasture and greenhouse). Food in a desert collapse is what people fight over: the north
   valley's and Pahrump's real dairies and ranches, the greenhouse that keeps a settlement fed.
9. THE BUS YARD (their bus depot). A fleet of city buses is a caravan waiting to happen: a faction's wagons.
10. THE CAMPGROUND AND RV PARK (their campground). Lake Mead's and the valley's real ones: where the roaming parties live.
11. THE TWO MUSEUMS WE DO NOT HAVE (their museum): the Mob Museum downtown (the Mob faction's own temple) and the natural history
    museum with its dinosaur (the fossils the labs worked from).
12. THE TV STATION (their news station): beside the radio station, the feed's other voice.
13. SMALLER ONES: the public works yard (city trucks and tools), the rec center (the real cooling centers, a shelter), the scrap mill
    (their steel mill; our junkyard has no mill), a fishing dock at the marina, the indoor amusement park, billboards (a prop the
    feed can use).

## WHAT WE LEAVE OUT, AND WHY
- Snowplow depot (no snow in the valley), nuclear, fusion, wind (the valley has none), lumber mill (no forest; Mt Charleston's
  lodge stands for it), space center, space elevator, pyramid city, igloo: not in a real Las Vegas.
- The obelisk and the alien monolith: fantasy, not our world. (Paolo 11/5 straight after: the valley has REAL magic stones, Seven Magic Mountains by Jean; rule 410 put it on the map.)
- A zoo or petting zoo: the beasts are lab-made by law, never zoo animals that got out; a zoo would muddy that. Left out unless he
  wants it.
- Their castle, colosseum, pirate ship, steel tower and sphinx are the real Strip's casino themes; they belong on our casinos as
  their shapes (named our own way, his names), a look job for COOK FOUR at final (rule 404), not new kinds.
