PART ONE -- THE SCHOOL: HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES THE COMPANY
================================================================================
The company is the thing every Battle Brothers player remembers, and players
remember individual brothers BY NAME years later. Nobody wrote those stories.
They come out of five pieces:

1. A BROTHER IS A BACKGROUND FIRST. Every recruit arrives as one civilian word:
   butcher, farmhand, sellsword, hedge knight, wildman, cripple, beggar,
   daytaler, poacher, squire, witchhunter. That word sets his starting gear, his
   starting stats, the ceiling he can grow to, what he costs to hire and WHAT HE
   COSTS EVERY DAY. It is the densest characterisation in the game and it is one
   word.

2. THE WAGE IS THE SPINE, AND IT IS WHY IT IS A COMPANY AND NOT A PARTY. Every
   brother costs crowns every single day, paid at the day's end, forever. A hedge
   knight is superb and ruinous; a beggar is useless and nearly free. You cannot
   field a roster of the best men because the wage bill outgrows the contracts.
   THE MONEY IS THE ROSTER.

3. HIDDEN TRAITS, FOUND OUT BY PLAYING. Craven, bloodthirsty, iron lungs, fat,
   clumsy, dumb, hesitant, bleeder, loyal. Mostly hidden at hire. You learn them
   by watching the man in a fight. THE GAP BETWEEN THE MAN YOU THOUGHT YOU HIRED
   AND THE MAN YOU GOT IS WHERE THE STORIES COME FROM.

4. MOOD AND WHO LIKED WHO. Brothers form opinions of each other. Mood runs from
   angry to eager and moves on pay, food, victories, defeats and casualties, and
   a man takes it harder when the one who fell was one he liked. Mood feeds
   straight into resolve, so the social state is not decoration: it breaks the
   line in a real fight.

5. PERMADEATH, SO THE LOSS LANDS. A brother who dies is gone. That is the whole
   reason anybody remembers a name.

WHAT PLAYERS COMPLAIN ABOUT, AND IT IS ALL ONE COMPLAINT: THE DELIVERY.
   - The roster is a spreadsheet. You run the company through a grid of small
     portraits and tooltips.
   - A background is a word you hover over.
   - A brother never says anything. Ever.
   - Events are illustrated cards with text, which is Paolo's own sentence.

THE REAL WORLD, because everything in Bohemia is grounded in the real:
   - The free companies (the White Company, the Catalan Company) were held
     together by REGULAR PAY and the share-out. The recorded cause of a company
     turning on its employer is almost always ARREARS OF PAY: when the money
     stopped they plundered whoever was nearest. This repo already has that half
     built and it is called [broke raiders] BB-UNPAID-TURNS-PREDATORY.
   - What actually bound a band was the PAIR. Men marched, slept and fought
     beside one specific other man. The combat-motivation literature is argued
     over in its details and keeps landing on the same place: people do not hold
     a line for a cause, they hold it for the man next to them.
   - And in a collapse people move in twos. Every recorded collapse has this.

================================================================================
A. A PERSON NEAR YOU TELLS YOU WHAT THEY USED TO BE. Once per person, ever, past
   the quiet minute, inside the speaking-to-you distance, and you can walk past.
   Fifteen written lines get a mouth. Somebody telling you their life tells you
   their name, which is this lane's own rule from [a name].

   Nothing to press. WALKING ON IS THE REFUSAL and it costs nothing, which is
   rule 32a built instead of written down. And Battle Brothers agrees with him:
   you do not get a brother by walking past one, you stand in front of him, he
   tells you what he was, and then you take him on.

   found out by playing rather than read off a tooltip, because the man tells you
   himself. A trade that DIED keeps nothing and says nothing here: the dealer,
   the valet, the pit boss and the floor man carry keeps:null on purpose, and the
   silence is the joke landing.

D. ONE AT A TIME, AND NO ROSTER. Who walks with you is one id recomputed from
   what was said and how far away they are, and the follow pass drops them at the
   same line it drops everybody else. A save cannot carry a ghost.
