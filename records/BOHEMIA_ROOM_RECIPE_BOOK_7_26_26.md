## THE COMPOSITION LAWS (apply to every room)
1. WALL BIAS 80/20: ~80% of furniture touches a wall. Mid-floor only: dining
   table (on rug), coffee table (inside couch group), kitchen island, garage car.
2. ONE ANCHOR PER ROOM: bed / couch+TV / table / workbench / car. Place it
   first; everything else orbits it.
3. PAIR LOGIC: nightstands flank bed, lamp on nightstand, TV faces couch,
   chairs face table, washer beside dryer. Relationships create realism.
4. RUG-GROUPING: one rug per zone, under/in front of the anchor; the rug
   binds the furniture group.
5. TRAFFIC LANES: unbroken 1-tile-min (prefer 2) path door-to-door and
   door-to-anchor; doorway swing tiles always clear.
6. NORTH-WALL TALLNESS (45-view): tall pieces (fridge/bookcase/wardrobe) on
   the top wall so fronts show and nothing occludes the player; south wall low.
7. WALL DECOR RHYTHM: one wall item per 3-4 wall tiles; window every 3-5;
   never two tall wall items adjacent; every window gets curtains/blinds.
8. CLUTTER OBEYS SURFACES: small props ON furniture in clusters of 2-3;
   floor clutter only corners/under tables; max 1 floor cluster per lived room.
9. DENSITY TARGETS: living spaces 30-45% of floor tiles occupied; bath/
   laundry 50-60%; garage 20-30% (+car void); shops/diners 40-55%. Under 20%
   reads unfinished; over 65% unwalkable.
10. SMALL ROOMS (<4x4): 3-5 pieces, all on walls, one rug, one large anchor.

## THE DEAD-WORLD TRANSLATION (act 1, ~15-20 years dead, VEGAS DRY)
MASTER RATIO 70/20/10 per room: 70% intact in place, 20% degraded in place
(dust, sun-bleach, sag), 10% disturbed (toppled/looted/added). Majority-
wrecked reads video-game-trashed; majority-intact-but-wrong reads REAL.
FIVE AGING PASSES in order: (1) dust/fade + hard sun-bleach gradient near
unboarded windows — desert decay: pale and dry, no mold/moss except at pool
water lines; (2) LOOT pass, 1-3 portable valuables gone, ALWAYS leaving
evidence (TV = dust rectangle + cable; tools = pegboard shadow outlines;
meds cabinet swept empty — meds are always first-loot); never loot couches/
beds/counters (nobody hauls those); (3) TOPPLE pass, 0-2 pieces max, rotated
90 + shifted 1 tile, spill points toward the exit; (4) CLOSURE pass, 1-2
boarded/blanketed street windows (never all), one bad door, half-fallen
curtains; (5) STORY pass, MAX ONE beat per house (half-set dinner table,
spilled suitcase, height marks on a doorframe).
NEVER VIOLATE: pairs stay explainable; traffic lanes survive the wreck; the
anchor never disappears — loot the TV, never the couch: the room must still
declare what it IS at a glance.

## R01 LIVING ROOM (~6x7)
MANIFEST: couch, armchair, coffee table, TV stand, TV, side table, table
lamp, bookshelf, large rug, wall art x2, curtained window x2, floor plant.
PLACEMENT: couch back to south/west wall; TV stand on wall FACING couch 2-4
tiles away; coffee table between on the rug; armchair perpendicular, also
facing TV; side table+lamp at couch arm; bookshelf north wall; plant corner;
art over couch. DENSITY 35-45%.
DEAD: TV looted (dust rectangle + cable), one cushion gone, one story prop
on the coffee table, armchair toppled or not (coin flip), rug bleach
gradient at window, one window boarded, bookshelf standing but 70% empty.

## R02 KITCHEN (~5x6)
MANIFEST: base counter x4-6 (L-run), sink counter (under window), stove (in
run), fridge (end of run), overhead cabinets x3-4, small table, chairs x2-4,
trash bin, counter clutter x2. PLACEMENT: counter L on north+side wall;
table mid-floor on rug/zone tile; nothing else mid-floor. DENSITY 40-50%.
DEAD: fridge ajar (dark inside), microwave-size gap on counter, cabinets
open and bare, one chair toppled, ONE story prop max, dry stain near sink,
drawers pulled.

## R03 MAIN BEDROOM (~5x6)
MANIFEST: double bed, nightstand x2, lamp x1-2, dresser, wardrobe, mirror,
rug, curtained window x1-2, wall art, hamper. PLACEMENT: bed headboard
center north wall; nightstands flank BOTH sides; dresser opposite with
mirror; wardrobe corner; rug door-side of bed. DENSITY 40-50%.
DEAD: mattress stripped/half off (looters check under), both nightstand
drawers pulled, wardrobe open + 1-2 fallen garments, mirror cracked OR
intact (never every mirror in a house broken), heavy dust, window bleach.

## R04 KID'S BEDROOM (~4x5)
MANIFEST: single bed, nightstand, small dresser, toybox, desk, small chair,
posters x2-3, bright rug, toy cluster. PLACEMENT: bed corner; desk+chair
side wall; toybox foot of bed; posters dense (kid rooms break decor rhythm
on purpose); toys ON the rug. DENSITY 40-50%.
DEAD — THE RESTRAINT ROOM: leave 85-90% intact, just dust + bleach, one toy
mid-floor. An untouched kid's room in a looted house is the loudest story
beat in the genre; do NOT also topple things here. Alternate: emptied-fast
(open drawers, no toys, missing-suitcase gap) for "the family made it out."

## R05 BATHROOM (2x3 to 3x4)
MANIFEST: toilet, sink + medicine cabinet, bathtub/shower, towel rail,
bathmat, small bin, small window x0-1. PLACEMENT: tub along the long wall;
toilet+sink share the other wall, sink nearest door; mat centered.
DENSITY 50-60% (tight is right).
DEAD: medicine cabinet open and SWEPT EMPTY (always), dry brown rings in
tub/toilet, towel gone (bare rail), squatter variant: tub as water storage.

## R06 GARAGE (~6x7)
MANIFEST: car x0-1 (2x4 void reserved regardless), metal shelving x2-3,
workbench, tool pegboard, storage boxes x2-3, water heater, oil-stain decal,
garage door. PLACEMENT: car void aligned to the door; everything else lines
the walls AROUND the void; oil stain inside it. DENSITY 20-30% + void.
DEAD: car present = they died here / absent = they ran (pick per lot; the
void + oil stain tells it); tools gone leaving pegboard shadow outlines; ONE
shelf toppled with spill; boxes torn open; some doors bent half-open.

## R07 LAUNDRY/UTILITY (~3x4)
MANIFEST: washer + dryer (side by side, always touching), wall shelf above,
basket, ironing board, water heater/furnace, detergent clutter.
DENSITY 50-60%. DEAD: machine doors hang open, petrified laundry in basket,
detergent looted (soap is currency), some drums stripped for parts.

## R08 BACKYARD/PATIO (~6x8)
MANIFEST: patio table, patio chairs x2-4, grill (against house wall, never
mid-lawn), planters/garden beds x2, clothesline, one kid item (swing/
sandbox/bike), fence, concrete patio zone. DENSITY 15-25%.
DEAD — VEGAS RULE: lawn is DEAD dirt/dry weeds, xeriscape gravel survives
perfectly; pools are EMPTY with debris (prime Vegas iconography); grill
tipped or looted for its tank; clothesline snapped, one end dangling.

## R09 CORNER STORE (~8x10)
MANIFEST: checkout counter + register, gondola shelves x4-6 in PARALLEL rows
(2-tile aisles), wall shelf runs x2-3, glass cooler bank (back wall),
endcap, magazine rack, door mat, storeroom door. DENSITY 45-55%, aisles sacred.
DEAD — the most looted room in the genre: shelves 90% bare but STANDING,
scatter of worthless product at shelf bases, ONE gondola toppled diagonally
across an aisle, register drawer open and empty, one cooler door off,
boards/grate on the storefront glass. Squats nest BEHIND the counter.

## R10 DINER (~9x12 + kitchen)
MANIFEST: window-wall booths x4-6 (one window per booth), lunch counter with
fixed stools x5-8, back-counter (coffee machine, pie case), pass-through,
register at door end, jukebox, menu board, checkerboard floor zone; kitchen:
flat-top, fryer, prep counters x2, big fridge, shelving x2, sink (R02 logic,
all wall-run). DENSITY 45-55% front of house.
DEAD — act-1 Vegas gold: window booths sun-bleached HARD, one bench slashed
spilling stuffing, 2-3 stools toppled/seatless, pie case empty and cracked,
menu board still readable (frozen prices = free lore), kitchen stripped of
pans but flat-top and fryer remain (too heavy). Prime squat: nest in a back
booth, fire on the flat-top.

## R11 MOTEL ROOM (~5x7 + en suite)
MANIFEST: double bed x1-2, nightstand x1-2, lamp, dresser with BOLTED TV,
luggage rack, round table + chairs x2 at window, AC under window, bolted
art, blackout curtains, bathroom per R05. DENSITY 45-55%.
DEAD — vary the DECAY not the manifest (repetition IS motel realism): per
room roll one of: bolted TV smashed in place, mattress gone walkabout,
struggle scene (overturned table, burst suitcase), pristine-but-dusty
"never checked out", squatter nest, chained shut. ONE story-beat room per
motel; keys/ledger at the office.

## R12 SQUATTER OVERLAY (apply ON any dead room, usually R01)
ADDITIONS: dragged mattress + blanket nest in the corner with two walls and
sight of the main door (squatters sleep near the exit); fire ring/barrel on
bare floor with the rug pulled back (visible fold) 2-3 tiles from nest,
under a window/flue; water jugs + bag pile within 1 tile of nest; candle
clusters at nest and watched door; original couch SHOVED as a barricade
(its old rug rectangle left clean); food-trash ring radius 1 around nest
ONLY; one personal-touch prop (photo wall, bottle collection, light string).
READING RULE: an occupied squat is tidy-in-its-logic (fuel stacked, jugs
lined); an abandoned squat gets the dust pass ON TOP — two eras of
abandonment in one room, the strongest environmental one-liner available.
