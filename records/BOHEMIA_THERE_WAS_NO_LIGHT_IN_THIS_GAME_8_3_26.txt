Every texture decision of the last seven days was made inside a 110-value band

That is exactly why none of it reads. You cannot see the art because there is
nothing lighting it. Denser texture was never going to fix that, and I kept
making denser texture.

WHAT SHIPPED

1. THE LOOK -- one grade over every world tile.
   A tone curve (contrast around the world's own median, black point down, white
   point up) so the frame uses the screen, then a SPLIT TONE: the lit end goes
   toward the sun's colour and the dark end goes toward the SKY's, which is blue.
   That is not a style, it is what a desert does. A shadow is not the ground
   turned down; it is the ground lit by the sky.

   THE FIRST CUT OF THIS WAS A NO-OP AND MEASURED AS ONE. It ran a per-CHANNEL
   lookup: each channel split-toned by its own value. The source art is red-over-
   blue everywhere, so red took the highlight boost and blue took the shadow
   boost IN THE SAME PIXEL, and the hue never moved. Cool pixels: 0.0% before,
   0.0% after. It looked like a working feature. The split has to key on the
   PIXEL'S LUMINANCE and it has to be a blend TOWARD a colour -- a multiply
   cannot put blue on top of red, ever. Same lesson as the 8/2 perimeter cap,
   where a multiply blew to pure white and only a blend-toward-target worked.

2. THE SUN -- every solid mass now throws a cast shadow onto the ground,
   down and to the right, matching the upper-left key light every cooked tile in
   this repo already has. Two cells long, full then half; that step is the
   penumbra at this scale. It is a MULTIPLY TOWARD A BLUE, not a black wash, so
   the bought texture underneath survives it -- a black rectangle at 55% would
   erase the tiles he paid for, which is the entire point of having them.

3. A CACHE BUG KILLED ON THE WAY PAST. look() memoises a graded canvas per
   image and the first key was src.length. Two tiles of the same size encoding
   to the same byte count collide, and with hundreds of 44x44 PNGs that is not
   hypothetical -- it swaps one wall for another and reads as a world bug rather
   than a cache bug. Keyed on a stamped per-image id now.
