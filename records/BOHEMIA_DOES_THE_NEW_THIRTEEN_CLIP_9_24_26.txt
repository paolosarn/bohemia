*** AND THE FLOATING-CLOTH COLUMN IS A BAD INSTRUMENT. I AM NOT GOING TO DRESS IT UP
AS A FINDING. *** It counts a painted pixel with no rig bone under it or in any of its
eight neighbours. It reported 13 OF 13 -- and 10 of 13 on the set he already lives
with and likes. Two tries at fixing it and the numbers barely moved:
  try 1: hair has no bone at all, so every hairstyle read as floating. Started the
         test below the head. 28 to 278 pixels a body, barely changed.
  try 2: there is nothing left to exclude, BECAUSE THE PREMISE IS WRONG. A coat is
         SUPPOSED to hang past the leg. A duster hem, a mantle drape and a wide
         trouser all sit cells away from any bone, correctly. "No bone nearby" is a
         description of clothing, not of clipping.
WHAT HE MEANS BY CLIPPING is one thing poking through another that should cover it --
layer order, not floating -- and I do not have a general instrument for that. So the
column above is REPORTED AND DISCARDED, the thirteen were rendered and LOOKED AT by
eye, and what that found is in the handoff. A number that fires on the control is not
a measurement, and shipping it as "13 of 13 clip" would have been this lane inventing
a threshold for the third time.

population is the ruler. Measured over CLOTH ONLY, never skin or hair.

*** AND THE HONEST READING OF MY OWN NUMBERS. *** The approved set was rendered in the
NEUTRALS the search chose them in -- charcoal, slate, ash, bone -- so its vibrance
number is near zero BY CONSTRUCTION and says nothing about how bright it will be once
it is dressed in faction colours. THE VIBRANCE RULING IS NOT ABOUT THIS SET AT ALL: it
is about the colourways, and it bites the moment these shapes are dressed. So the
number that matters is the one for THE THIRTEEN HE LIVES WITH, above, and the fix
belongs on the ramps, not on the shapes.
Reporting it the other way round would have been a green tick on a question I never
asked.
