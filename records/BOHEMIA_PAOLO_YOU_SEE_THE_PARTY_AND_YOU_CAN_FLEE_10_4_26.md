# PAOLO 10/4/26: IN BATTLE BROTHERS A FIGHT IS NOT A RANDOM EVENT; YOU SEE THE ROAMING PARTY AND 95% OF THE TIME YOU CAN FLEE
His words: "In battle brothers it's not like some random event when combat starts you see the roaming party 95% of the time
you're actually able to fleet from them and go on a different direction, bro / What do you need grok to do more of"

## MY READING (rule 68, LOCKED)
1. A FIGHT COMES FROM A PARTY YOU CAN SEE. On the map every party is drawn and moving; you see it coming; you can turn and
   go another way, and most of the time you get away (speed against speed, roads against dirt, your choice); a fight
   happens when a party catches you, when you walk into one, or when you tap one to engage. A fight that starts as a
   popup, a timer or a 'stop the march' event is WRONG and is removed from the loop (the 'roaming crews that stop the march
   after the first minute' in RUN TWO's loop and the demo's fold is exactly that).
2. ROAD EVENTS STAY FACES, NOT FIGHTS (rule 33 s2): a person, a find, a choice; never an ambush out of nowhere.
3. THE NUMBERS ARE BATTLE BROTHERS': party speeds by type, vision range, how pursuit works, how far a party chases, when it
   gives up, when it is faster than you (Grok ask 20); TUNING's table.
4. WHAT GROK DOES MORE OF (his question): exactly this kind of thing, the hidden rules of the world map that only players
   and the data know: party movement and pursuit; and measuring (UI sizes, map pixels, hex counts) from screenshots and the
   game's data files rather than prose.
