## THE SCHOOL HALF (recall, vol 10)
Battle Brothers draws a town as ONE painted view with its buildings as clickable icons. Inside a
building is a panel: the market (a grid of items with prices), the recruits (a row of faces with
background and cost), the tavern (rumours, one line each), the temple (heal), the hall (contracts).
The resource bar stays on top; you never walk. The mouse is why it works: a cursor can hit a small
icon anywhere on a big screen, and hovering says what a thing is before you commit.
THE PHONE HAS NEITHER: no hover, and a thumb that reaches the bottom of the glass easily and the top
badly. So the question BB never had to answer is where the buildings are for a THUMB.
WHAT MOVES THAT BB'S PICTURE DOES NOT (33g): the place under the plates is our live street, with
people walking and light changing; BB's town is a painting.

## THE SHAPE, AS THREE OPTIONS (rule 25: asked once, three options)
A TAP THE BUILDING: BB straight, a name plate on each roof, 44 tall. Cheapest, most like BB; the
  narrow house is a sliver and every building is in the hard-to-reach half.
B THE PLACE AND A SHELF: buildings stay tappable with small name tags, AND a row of five 84 px plates
  sits on the empty ground at the bottom (the hall, market, barber, home, scavenge), each with one
  line of what is there. Nothing covers a building, because the shelf sits on dirt. MY DEFAULT if he
  says nothing: it keeps BB's picture and gives the thumb its reach.
C THE PLACE AS A HEADER: the place cropped to the top 40%, the buildings a scrolling list of 74 px
  rows. Most phone-native, least BB.
