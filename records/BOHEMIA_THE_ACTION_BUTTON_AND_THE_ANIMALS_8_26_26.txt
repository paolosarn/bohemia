WHAT IT DOES NOW
================

It asks what is in front of him and says so on its own face:

    somebody next to you   their address, from the person system's own label
    a door you are facing  ENTER
    a market you stand in  TRADE
    inside a building      LEAVE
    nothing                it goes quiet and says nothing

THE PATTERN IS NOT NEW AND THAT IS THE POINT. #cttalk already worked exactly this
way, and its own comment says why: "THE BUTTON ONLY EXISTS WHEN SOMEBODY IS THERE.
That is what makes it the ONE CONTEXTUAL VERB and not a menu." That rule was
right; it was just stuck on a small pill above the pad while a bigger, rounder,
better-placed button sat in the middle doing camera work. The rule moved onto the
button his thumb is already on.

EVERY VERB CALLS THE OWNER THAT ALREADY EXISTED -- inEnter for doors, ctOpen for
people, showMarket for the market, stepOnce for walking out. Not one line of their
insides was copied. That is the whole lesson of this week (a door predicate copied
into homeFind, an objective hint copied into an inlined module, a toolbar offset
copied into popwrap), and the gate fails if it stops being true.

WHERE THE CAMERA WENT: a small DROP IN / CITY chip beside WHOLE MAP. NOT DELETED.
Zoom is the way and it is measured working, but NO DISTRICT IS A PRISON (Paolo
8/1) and a player whose fingers cannot pinch still needs one. It survives at the
size of a preference instead of the size of the primary verb.

MEASURED, ON HIS OWN FRONT DOOR:
    standing in the open   ""       quiet
    facing the door        ENTER
    pressed                inside
    inside                 LEAVE
    pressed                walks onto the door
    pressed                outside, and the button goes quiet again

Two presses to leave, both REAL STEPS through the real door, no teleport --
because __STEP_INSIDE__ (his own request: "WHY WHEN I ENTER A HOUSE I CANT GO LEFT
AND RIGHT") deliberately puts him one cell THROUGH the threshold, so the door is
one step back and walking to it is the honest way out.

AND TWO THINGS THE GATE HAD TO LEARN ABOUT ITSELF
=================================================

1. A FIGHT LEGITIMATELY TAKES THE SCREEN. Walking into a building rolls for one
   (deterministic per footprint), and when it fires the shell shows the combat
   frame -- so the city measures 0x0 and every button in it becomes unclickable.
   THAT IS CORRECT BEHAVIOUR. My probe read it as "walking into a house destroys
   the entire HUD, the pad and the action button included" and I was one sentence
   from writing that up as a major bug. I checked the shell instead. SECOND TIME
   TODAY a probe was confidently wrong about a working system, and both times the
   thing that saved it was measuring one more layer instead of concluding.
2. SO THE DOOR THE GATE USES IS HIS OWN FRONT DOOR, because
   __NOT_YOUR_OWN_HOUSE__ makes "no ambush here" a LAW rather than luck, and
   cityFightRoll() reads INSIDE and so cannot be asked before you are already in.

MUTATION TESTED: both patches reverted turns 10 claims red, and the log reproduces
the complaint verbatim -- facing a door, the button reads "CITY". One claim went
green under that mutation because he was never inside, so "he is outside" was
trivially true; it proves the round trip now.

THE VALLEY HAS ANIMALS IN IT
=============================================================================

Same message:

    "maybe I wanna fuck around and start putting, you know, dogs and and swarms
     of flies as, like, low tier, you know, biome level one enemies or something"

THE RESEARCH DELIVERED THE DAY HE FIRST ASKED ALREADY ANSWERED THIS, and nobody
built it (records/BOHEMIA_RESEARCH_WHAT_LIVES_IN_A_CITY_OF_CORPSES_8_25_26.md):

    the reason the city feels dead is not that we lack enemies. It is that we
    lack ANIMALS. Ravens on a roofline, rats at a bin, a coyote crossing the wash
    three blocks away and not caring about you -- none of those are combat and
    all of them are life. Tier 1 is mostly not an enemy system at all. It is set
    dressing that moves, and it is the cheapest fix on this list for the loudest
    complaint on his list.

SHIPPED: FLIES, RATS, RAVENS. Every one is a small dark moving mark -- a swarm is
specks orbiting a point, a rat is a dash at the foot of a wall, a raven is a
silhouette on a roofline. NONE NEEDS A CHARACTER SPRITE, which is exactly why this
lane can ship them without inventing creature art.

A FLY SWARM IS A READ: it means SOMETHING DIED THERE. That is information the
player can use before we own a corpse system at all.

DETERMINISTIC, like the sky already is: life is hashed from (seed, cell), so a
block holds the same animals every time it is walked and on every device. A valley
that reshuffles its rats when you turn around reads as noise.

DENSITY IS BY PLACE, which is the Valheim half of his ruling -- difficulty and
life live in THE GROUND, never in a level number on the player. Eight districts,
real numbers as a first attempt, all tagged as dials he has not ruled. Nothing
here decides which FACTION holds anything; that is reserved and was not touched.

THEY MOVE ON THE BEAT. 120 BPM LAW: drift, hop and wing-flick are phases of the
same clock his footsteps are on.

IT IS NOT AN ENEMY SYSTEM, and the gate holds that: no damage, no health, nothing
that can hurt him. NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL is untouched. He said "enemies"; the
ecology's own answer is that Tier 1 barely fights.

*** THE DOG IS DELIBERATELY NOT BUILT. *** He named it first and the research
calls it the headline ("somebody's pets, in a pack, ten years on ... the most
Bohemia enemy imaginable"). But a dog is a BODY, and a body is character art. The
45 DEGREE ART LAW and REUSE-FIRST both say a lane does not invent creature pixels
because it is in a hurry. Its row sits at 0 in the density table, the gate fails
if that row disappears, and an art request is filed. When the art lands it is one
line.

PERFORMANCE, because his item 7 is performance and answering "the city is dead"
with a stutter answers nothing: window-culled, hard-capped per frame, and the
whole pass is skipped at city zoom where a 2px mark is a dirty screen rather than
