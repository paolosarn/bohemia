# THE COORDINATOR'S SCHOOL, ROUND FOURTEEN: THE QUIET SHIFT (10/16, sweep AL; rule 90 continues one round per sweep)

## The world outside game development

The firehouse between calls. A shift is not idle when the bell is quiet: the incoming crew checks every piece of protective gear first, then the trucks, then drills; pre-shift checks run up to two hours; apparatus checks are done at the start of each shift or at a weekly drill. The farm in winter: the season with no crop is when every machine is inspected, worn belts and blades replaced, handles rebuilt, because a breakdown found in the cold shed costs nothing and the same breakdown in the harvest costs the year. In both, the quiet is the maintenance window, and the maintenance is scheduled by the quiet, not by a failure.

## What the coordinator does not do

It treats a quiet sweep as a short sweep. When the lanes have not run (this sweep: eleven commits on 10/13 in forty minutes, then three days of nothing; sixteen of twenty-six lanes silent since the sweep before last), the coordinator reads what little landed and ends. Meanwhile the maintenance the laws call for sits undone: the canon index was last regenerated before a dozen laws and forty records landed; the status lines on the front page have grown a sweep's note every sweep against rule 96's 'newest sweep only'; TURNs are named in notes and not applied; the archive registry is not read. The CLAUDE.md standing job ('periodically fold addenda into the masters and archive the folded') has no trigger, so it never fires.

## The one change, applied this sweep

A QUIET SWEEP is a sweep where more than half the running lanes have no commit since the sweep before last. It spends the notes it does not need on the maintenance nobody schedules, in this order: regenerate the canon index; rebuild the Grok master; evaporate the status lines to the newest sweep (rule 96), the removed text into the sweep record; apply the TURNs already named; read the archive registry for a law superseded and not moved. Done this sweep: the canon index regenerated (407 files, 2 supersession chains); the Grok master rebuilt; THE CUT LINE and THE RELEASE LINE evaporated to the newest sweep (the removed text is in records/BOHEMIA_COORDINATOR_ROUND_10_16_26_AL.md); the TURNs on COOK TWO, UI and SOUNDS re-named on their rows. Rule 90 carries the round.
