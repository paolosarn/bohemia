   Play begins at the BEGIN tap, which is the only honest anchor for "the first
   minutes are his". The shell owns that tap and lives in a different document

   happened and the city stamps its own clock. This lane has already paid once for
   reading a game-minute against performance.now() ([a human being], two clocks in
   one file) and is not paying again.
   AND THE FRAME IS USUALLY NOT BUILT WHEN HE TAPS BEGIN, so the message can land
   before anybody is listening. The shell also leaves a flag on itself and the
   city reads it once the frame exists. A surface with no door in front of it --
   the workshop, a gate driving the city on its own -- stamps itself, so nothing
   is mute for ever waiting on a tap that is not coming.

   ctMaySpeak() is asked first thing in barkTick, so a mouth added later is quiet
   by default instead of quiet only if somebody remembered. A bubble already up is
   left alone, so this can never cut somebody off mid-sentence.
   AND NOTHING FIRES IN THE DEMO AT ALL, which he said in the same breath.

                      exactly as it was so the street he already has does not
                      change under him
   They are not all one number ON PURPOSE, and his own sentences are why. What is
