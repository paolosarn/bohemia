# HARVEST THREE: NOTHING IN HIS MOUTH IS A TELL (coordinator, this round)

## SCHOOL MODE PAID OFF THE ROUND AFTER HE ORDERED IT
He made WORDS study before writing on 9/6. Its very first schooled question came
back and **inverted its own earlier answer.** From the round: of 158 measured
deception cues, 118 show no association with lying at all, and PAUSES ARE ON THAT
LIST BY NAME. People score 54% at spotting lies -- catching 47% of them and
clearing 61% of truths -- which means, in practice, THE LIAR IS BELIEVED.
So the bank now has the liar as the most fluent person in his scene and the
FRIGHTENED man as the one who stumbles. The unschooled 9/4 round had it exactly
backwards. That is the whole argument for school, delivered one round after he
made the rule.

## THE SENTENCE THAT IS A GAME MECHANIC, NOT A WRITING NOTE
> "He is broken by one question he did not prepare for, and what exposes him is a
> fence the player can walk to. Nothing in his mouth is a tell."

That is a design rule for the entire game and it arrived inside a writing round:
**YOU DO NOT CATCH A LIAR BY READING HIM. YOU CATCH HIM BY WALKING TO THE THING.**
No tell in the face, no wobble in the voice, no highlighted dialogue option, no
skill check that rolls it for you. The lie is checkable because the world holds
the fence, the empty shelf, the unlit window, the tracks in the dirt.

## THE RESEARCH BEHIND MAKING THAT PLAYABLE
- The strongest investigation design hands the player the clues and the systems
  and lets them piece it together, rewarding observation, instead of a special
  ability that detects truth for them.
- What players need is not a detector but a PLACE TO PUT WHAT THEY LEARNED: a
  board, a map, a list that cross-references findings so leads connect.
- Deduction works when premises come from what the player OBSERVED, and the game
  lets them match those premises against a claim.
Sources: [Unlocking investigative RPGs: the mechanics and structure of investigation](https://norerolls.co.uk/2023/06/24/unlocking-investigative-rpgs-and-mystery-solving-part-1-the-mechanics-and-structure-of-investigation/),
[Reasonable doubt as a game mechanic](https://www.debigare.com/reasonable-doubt-as-a-game-mechanic/),
[Deduction as a central mechanic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deduction_board_game).

## WHY OUR GAME IS ALREADY BUILT FOR THIS AND NOBODY NOTICED
We have a world that keeps its own records: gossip with hop counts and hearsay
decay, deeds that travel and fade, tracks on the travel map that differ by
faction, lights that say whose they are, blocks with owners, shelves that empty on
a schedule, a debt with a lender's name on it. Every one of those is a fence a
player can walk to. What is missing is only the ASKING and the CHECKING.

## ROUTED
- QUESTS [check the claim]: an ask can be a claim, and the claim is checkable by
  going somewhere the world already tracks.
- PEOPLE [fluent liar]: the liar is the fluent one, the frightened one stumbles,
  and no tell ever lives in the face or the voice.
- UI [what you know]: somewhere to put what you learned, that cross-references
  what the world told you. It is options-on-a-sheet work, which is this lane's
  new shape anyway.
