PAOLO 10/10: "the real way we get to a final graphic asset is doing a HIGH-QUALITY ONE
AT A TIME, a sheet, and asking for my feedback every time."

ONE ASSET: a 96 x 96 map road tile, the master the map samples at every stop. A
CANDIDATE. It is in nothing and goes into nothing until he votes FINAL on its sheet.

THE STUDY CAME FIRST (rule 100c) and three of its seven numbers decided this asset
before a pixel was drawn:
  COLOURS   4,821 in one of his tiles, top eight carry 8%. His ground is a SURFACE, not
            a ramp, and ours carry seven colours. So the carriageway IS his tile.
  THE EDGE  only 22% of his road family tiles cleanly; they are slabs laid in a grid. A
            map road must repeat, so the base is the one of his that MEETS, measured.
  OUTLINE   no dark ring anywhere: his edge runs +15 LIGHTER than his inside.

*** AND THEN THE PICTURE CORRECTED THE NUMBERS. THIS IS THE PART WORTH KEEPING.

The first cut of this tile read his MARKING family, found warning signs, blood and
bones, and wrote the headline THE PACKS HOLD NO ROAD PAINT. On that headline it cooked
a lane line: a white lifted off his road grit and mixed down the middle. Eight
measurements went green. Then it was looked at, at one art pixel to one phone pixel,
and the line read as a SCUFF. Not as paint. A player would see a smear.

So his own road family went on a contact sheet, and there it was:
  1. Cracked street tiles#23   HIS dark street, with WORN ORANGE ROAD PAINT on it.
                               11% of the tile. 90 of its 96 rows carry paint.
  and five more of his street tiles carry it: #18, #19, #20, #21, #22.
THE PACKS HOLD ROAD PAINT. Nobody had looked. The measurement was asking the marking
family, and his road paint lives in the ROAD family, on the road.

SO NOTHING ON THIS TILE IS COOKED, which is the strongest version of it there is:
  THE CARRIAGEWAY  his #0, whole and untouched, the street tile of his that MEETS.
  THE LINE         his #23 own paint pixels. For every row, the longest unbroken run
                   of his paint in that row is moved SIDEWAYS to the middle. Nothing
                   is rotated, scaled, blended or recoloured.
  THE COLOUR       his worn orange, because it is literally his pixels.
  THE WEAR         his. Where his paint wore through, the line thins or stops. 6 rows
                   are bare because 6 of his are.

AND THE RULER THE FIRST CUT NEVER HAD: the tile is box averaged down to a 16 px land
cell, the size the map actually draws it, and the painted column is measured against
the carriageway beside it. It reads 16.01 times as strong. A road you cannot follow at a
glance is not a map road, and no amount of grain at 96 px fixes that.

THE ONE QUESTION, AND IT IS ABOUT THE WORLD AND NOT THE ART: nobody has repainted a line
in this valley since the dollar died, so how much paint is left on a road is a reading
of how long ago that was. A WORN, as drawn. B GONE, bare carriageway. C FRESH, which
would say somebody is still in charge.

THE LESSON, AND IT IS THIS LANE'S OLDEST ONE: MEASURE AND LOOK. Green guards over a
worse picture is the most dangerous state there is, because it reads as finished. The
fault was not in any of the eight numbers. It was in the question they were asked.
