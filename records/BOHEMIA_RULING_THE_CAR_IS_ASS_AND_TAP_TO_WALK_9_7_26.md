## 2. TAP TO WALK, WITH FOOTPRINTS, ON THE BEAT
The pad stays ("as well"). Added:
- **Tap a tile and you walk there.** No menu, no confirm.
- **Footprints show first.** The moment you tap, ghost footprints lay out the path
  from you to the tile, so you can see you are about to walk there before you do.
- **The walk happens on the beat.** One step per beat at 120 BPM, the same beat the
  fight keeps, the same beat the front door plays. "Very important." Tap-to-walk
  does not become a teleport and does not become a free-speed run; it is the pad's
  walk with the tapping done for you.
- A tap on an unreachable tile shows the footprints stopping where the path stops.
