## 1. THE VOICE: SCARY FIRST (LOCKED, rule 455)
'It is hell. People actually feel like they're in hell. They're in hell first, before they're in a barely surviving, one percent
civilization experience. Whatever you do in this game, when you evoke a feeling out of the gamer, it is people reacting to how
scary and real hell is. The emotions: it will be scary and then it can be scary happy, scary funny, scary serious. There still
could be humor, but it has to be scary and then funny. I want everyone's voice to be: they can be scary confident, scary trying
to be a leader, scary political. There's two words, but the first word is always scary, and whatever emotion you're trying to get
out of people, the second one follows that.'
'I really do believe people will talk to each other more desperately. It'll be this kind of thing: damn bro, you're really not
gonna help starving people out because of the kindness of your heart, another piece of shit, he's gonna do it for batteries, so I
got some batteries for you.'
'The vibe I'm creating, from social media to people actually talking to you.'

## 2. NOTHING GOES TO WASTE: THE GRINDER (LOCKED as the world's truth, rule 456; how the player touches it is asked once)
'Serious camping and a 99% collapsed civilization... a grinding meat grinder or something, because every interaction, I really
view this as a really big deal: nothing can go to waste. People don't want a little bit of meat that they can't even eat because
they have to worry about bones. How does that work with bones and animals?'
'I do have this huge belief that people do not view the killing of other people and just, their bodies are gonna rot away, are you
serious? We have to make mystery sausage out of this, because if not, we don't know what our next meal is. This is one of the
horror aspects of my universe. Only the sick people will joke about it. The little kids, they have to eat the mystery meat. If I
personally was a father, I'm not gonna let a body rot. We're going to preserve it.'
'Maybe we can implement that in the game in a fun way. If you wanna build this building it could be like a mystery meat, or maybe
not even a building: your things are more like food trucks, which I kind of fuck with that vibe too.'
This carries rule 388 (THE MYSTERY MEAT, DON'T ASK DON'T TELL, 11/4) further: it is not hidden shame, it is duty; a father does it.

## 5. THE CAR CAMPS (his thinking for when things get better, NOT locked)
'If society rebuilt, I do think most of the thinking would be the same. Maybe more people just live in their cars instead of
paying rent. Maybe the technology and the production of it got so good even before the economic apocalypse: the first one who
invents a car that is solar panel and makes it very cheap. It could be dog shit too. More like a gypsy thing going on, migration,
but more centralized at locations: they all just park next to each other in the ruins, and that's how it starts off, and then when
things get better there's still that sort of vibe and swag around.'

## WHAT THE COORDINATOR SET AGAINST THE REAL WORLD (the holes, said to him the same reply; none of it is canon until he says so)
- THE BONES: in a world that wastes nothing a carcass has no waste. Meat is ground because ground meat hides what it was and
  stretches with filler; bones are boiled for broth and the marrow, then ground to meal for the soil; fat is rendered for cooking,
  candles and soap. The grinder is one machine in a line of three (the grinder, the pot, the press).
- THE SHAKES are real: kuru, among the Fore of New Guinea, came from eating the dead, the brain most of all; it kills slowly,
  years later. Rule 388 already carries the shakes. A town that knows leaves the head.
- THE FEED: the world's internet needs data halls and cables nobody is left to run. What a lit block CAN run for almost nothing is
  its own local net (Havana's street network SNET ran on rooftop radios; the weekly 'paquete' carried the rest by hand). So the
  real version is one feed per lit place, and news crossing the dark between them at the speed of whoever walks it.
- THE SOLAR CAR: a car's roof holds about a kilowatt of panel; in this valley's sun that is a few kilowatt-hours a day, 20 to 30
  kilometres of driving. A solar car cannot commute. It parks for days to fill, then moves: which is exactly a camp that gathers
  and then migrates. And in a world where BATTERIES ARE THE MONEY, a car is a bank vault on wheels.
