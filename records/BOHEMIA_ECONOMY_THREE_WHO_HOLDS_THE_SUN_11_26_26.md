# WHO HOLDS THE WORKING SUN (ECONOMY THREE, 11/26/26; VAMILY rules 459(e), 480(b); the coordinator's word: 'yours to decide from the research; pick, write it down, build it')

THE QUESTION (rule 459(e), the coordinator's reading 11/17): a working Las Vegas roof fills about 14,600 AA's a day
(records/BOHEMIA_RESEARCH_HOW_MANY_BATTERIES_ARE_IN_THE_UNITED_STATES_11_17_26.md), so if the sun worked for everybody a charged AA
could never buy a bag of rice. Who holds the working sun?

## THE DECISION (this lane's, his to correct)

1. MOST ROOFS ARE DEAD. In the United States nearly all rooftop solar is GRID-TIED with no battery, and a grid-tied inverter is
   built to switch itself off the moment the grid is gone (the anti-islanding safety rule, so a panel never back-feeds a line a
   lineman is working on; IEEE 1547, UL 1741). With the grid dead, those six million roofs make nothing, even at noon in July.
   (General engineering knowledge, not from this round's lookups; the research record counts the roofs: SEIA 2026, 6 million+.)
2. WORKING SOLAR IS RARE: a roof that still works is one that had a battery and an inverter that can run without the grid, or one
   somebody rewired (panels straight to a charge controller and a bank), which takes parts and know-how. Inverters wear out in
   about ten to fifteen years; panels last decades. So the working arrays are few, and they are HELD: by whoever holds a POWER
   PLACE on the map. Today that is the factions; the game already pays it (a live power place puts one battery a day in its
   holder's pocket, slices/BOHEMIA_CITY_WORLD.html 'a day on a live wire'). Take one, and it is yours.
3. THE SUN IS FREE, THE CELLS ARE NOT. A lithium cell left flat too long never takes a charge again, and a lead-acid battery left
   flat is ruined within about a year (the research's own note); years in, most of the 2.3 billion are dead. A panel does not
   print money: it can only charge a cell that still takes a charge. That is why his own worked panel (home_base.json
   builds.solar: four rolls of tape to set up) puts by ONE AA a morning, and a hand on it one more: the panel is not the limit,
   the cells worth charging are. The mint is the charged cell.
