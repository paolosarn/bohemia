Q3. AND WHAT DOES IT LOOK LIKE FROM ACROSS THE STREET?

  Two outfits that differ only in a hat nobody can make out at 56 px are ONE
  outfit to a player. So the body is rendered and hashed two ways: by SHAPE alone
  (colour thrown away) and by shape AND colour.

  bodies rendered                 300
  different SILHOUETTES            223   (74.3%)
  the commonest silhouette worn by 9 of them
  different once colour counts     300   (100.0%)
  the commonest whole look worn by 1 of them

  A WARDROBE CAN BE RICH ON PAPER AND A UNIFORM ON THE STREET. If the outfit
  count is high and the silhouette count is low, the rail is not short -- the
  SHAPES are, and that is what "behind the structure law" means: progress is new
  SHAPES, never recolours (STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR, 7/19).

READ THIS BEFORE DRAWING A CONCLUSION. A NUMBER IS NOT A FINDING UNTIL YOU KNOW
WHAT IT IS COUNTING. These are counts of what the PICKER produces, which is the
honest question for this lane; whether any single garment is good art is DIRECTION
