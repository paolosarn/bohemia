# BATTLE BROTHERS SCHOOL: HOW LONG IT TAKES TO CROSS THE MAP AT THE FASTEST VANILLA SPEED (coordinator 10/1/26, his ask)
Paolo 10/1: "find out how long it takes on the fastest vanilla speed to travel across the Battle Brothers map north to
south or east to west right now". Sources are player reports on Steam and the wiki as search returns them (our sandboxes
are blocked from both sites; Grok can confirm, ask 6/7).

## THE NUMBERS
- Vanilla map speeds: pause, 1x (key 1), 2x (key 2). 2x is the fastest vanilla speed. Mods add 4x and 8x (Nexus 'Faster').
- One in-game day at 1x is about 1 minute 45 seconds of real time (Steam 'hours?' thread). At 2x: about 52 seconds.
- Crossing the whole map: players report 2 to 4 in-game days (post-DLC map; 'three or four days to cross the continent';
  'less than 3, sometimes less than 2'; one seed: a full road loop of several towns about 10 days).
- SO, AT 2x, NORTH TO SOUTH OR EAST TO WEST: about 1 minute 45 seconds to 3 minutes 30 seconds of real time. At 1x: 3.5
  to 7 minutes. On roads the low end; through forest, hills or swamp the high end (terrain slows the party).

## WHAT IT MEANS FOR US
- Our valley at our fastest pad speed (5x) should cross in about the same real time: 2 to 3 minutes corner to corner, and
  a block-to-block hop in seconds. TUNING's numbers table gets a row: CROSS THE MAP AT TOP SPEED = 2 to 3 min real.
- Battle Brothers' day is short on purpose (under two minutes); events, contracts and wages are paced per day, so a day
  must feel like a unit the thumb can spend. Our clock on the map should run at the same order: a day in about two
  minutes at 1x.

## WHAT IS CERTAIN / NOT
Certain: three vanilla speeds; 2x is the top. Reported, not measured by us: 1:45 per day; 2-4 days to cross. Not known:
the exact post-Blazing-Deserts map width in tiles. ASK GROK (ask 7) to measure a crossing with a stopwatch at 2x.
