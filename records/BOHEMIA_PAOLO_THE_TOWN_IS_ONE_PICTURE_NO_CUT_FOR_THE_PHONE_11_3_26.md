## RULE 383. THE TOWN IS ONE PICTURE
1. Every settlement screen (all 14 towns, his camp, his own settlement; tall and wide; day and night) is drawn whole, as one place,
   and the phone floats over it. Nothing is cut to make room for the phone: no straight line, no rectangle of other ground, no
   mirrored strip, no fade.
2. Making room is done in the LAYOUT, before the picture is drawn: what he taps (the buildings) stands where the phone is not; what
   sits under the phone is the same ground and the same kind of things the rest of the town has (a wall, a car, a tank, the dead,
   people), whole, never sliced, and it is fine for the phone to hide them.
3. The ground is continuous across where the phone sits: the same tiles, the same dirt, the same light.
4. GATE: in every town picture, tall and wide, day and night: no long straight edge in the ground along the phone's rectangle (an
   edge measure across its sides against the picture's own), no tile band that mirrors, and no prop or building cut by the phone's
   line (every object's outline whole).
5. LANE: RUN (the towns' pictures; RUN TWO's tool, the four layouts), QUEUED (QUIET DAYS, rule 382). [bb] Battle Brothers' town
   screens are one painted scene each with the menus laid over it; nothing in the scene is cut for the menu.
