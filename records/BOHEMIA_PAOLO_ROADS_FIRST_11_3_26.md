## THE LAW (rule 356)
1. ROADS FIRST: every map (the fight boards, the overworld, the towns) is built in this order: THE ROAD NETWORK (the streets, how they
   join, their widths, the sidewalks and kerbs along them), then the lots between the roads, then the buildings on the lots, then the
   things on the ground. Never a picture first and roads painted onto it.
2. A STREET NEVER STARTS FROM THIN AIR: every road ends at another road (a junction), at the map's own edge (it carries on off the map),
   or at a real dead end that reads as one (a cul-de-sac's turning circle, a gate, a closed barrier, rubble across it). Every sidewalk
   follows its street round its corners.
3. NOTHING IS CUT AT A CELL'S EDGE: an asset is whole; if it is bigger than a cell it spans its cells as one piece; the cell grid is for
   play and never shows as a seam in the picture (rule 346).
4. FREEDOM INSIDE IT: 'you can have fun and do what you want': the layout, the landmarks, the twists are the lanes' to invent, so long as
   1 to 3 hold.
5. DOWNTOWN'S ROOFTOP-TO-ROOFTOP is kept and spreads where it fits: men cross from roof to roof, height gives accuracy, low gives less
   (rules 321, 328).
6. THE GATE: the road graph of every map is connected (no road with a loose end that is not the edge or a real dead end), and no asset is
   clipped at a cell border.
7. THE TOWNS: no duct tape round the phone; the phone's ground is the settlement's own ground, and no fade or blur at its edge.
