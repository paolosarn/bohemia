## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
Every item's card lists what it does in its own numbers: a shield its melee defence and ranged defence, its durability and its
fatigue; armour its durability and fatigue (and the initiative it costs); a weapon its damage, armour damage, armour pierce,
head-hit chance, hit bonus and fatigue per swing. Put it on a man and his numbers on the character screen change at once (his
defence rises with the shield on his arm; his fatigue and initiative fall with heavy armour). That is what he means by 'it
reflects in your stats'.
