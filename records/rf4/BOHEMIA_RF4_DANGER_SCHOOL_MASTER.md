## BATCH 4 (20 new screens, upload order preserved)

### 41 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183202)
WHEN YOU START FLYING, A COUNTDOWN EQUAL TO YOUR LEVITATION STAT BEGINS, DISPLAYED AT THE TOP LEFT. WHEN IT RUNS OUT YOU WILL STOP FLYING. A LEVITATION POT GIVES 10 LEVITATION FOR 200 TURNS, DON  CONFUSE THIS WITH YOUR FLIGHT TIME.

### 42 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183246)
CURSED FLOOR HEALS UNDEAD ENEMIES. DO NOT LET ENEMIES FIGHT ON TERRAIN ADVANTAGEOUS FOR THEMSELVES.

### 43 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183312)
LIQUIDS PREVENT SPRINTING, FAST MOVEMENT AND MOVEMENT ABILITIES. USE YOUR SP ON LAND AND HIDE BEHIND THE WATER TO SLOW THE APPROACHING ENEMIES.

### 44 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183341)
SLEEP BOMB CAN BE USED TO BLOCK LINE OF SIGHT, PREVENTING ENEMY ATTACKS.

### 45 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183430)
SLEEPING AND UNSTABLE GIVE CRITICAL HITS WITH PHYSICAL DAMAGE, AND CRITS FROM DIFFERENT SOURCES CAN STACK. BY STACKING TWO CRITS, YOU CAN KILL THESE ENEMIES IN ONE HIT.

### 46 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183458)
WAIT FOR YOUR COOLDOWNS TO RESET BEFORE ATTACKING. USE KNOCKBACK TO PREVENT ENEMY ACTIONS WHILE YOU DEAL DAMAGE. IMMEDIATELY SLEEP THE ENEMY AGAIN AND REPEAT.

### 47 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183604)
SLEEP BOMB BLOCKS CLOUD ATTACKS. PLACE YOUR SLEEP BOMB BEFORE THE ENEMY ATTACKS, TO PROTECT YOURSELF FROM THE CLOUD.

### 48 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_202427)
SLEEPING ENEMIES WILL RELEASE CONSTRICTING EFFECTS.

### 49 (Screenshot_2026-08-16_232908)
WHEN AN ENEMY FOLLOWS YOU AROUND A CORNER, IT CREATES ROOM TO KNOCK THEM BACK. USE THIS SPACE TO CYCLONE STRIKE WITHOUT TAKING DAMAGE.

### 50 (Screenshot_2026-08-16_232959)
USING THE EDGE OF STORM SHOT YOU CAN TARGET AROUND CORNERS OR PILLARS, IN A DIAGONAL OR STRAIGHT LINE.

### 51 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182525)
THE RINGS INDICATE ENEMY DETECTION RANGE. IT STARTS AT 6 TILES, AND IS REDUCED BY 1 FOR EVERY STEALTH. USE SP TO SNEAK IN AND GRAB THE STEALTH ITEMS BEFORE BEING DETECTED.

### 52 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182557)
EVEN 1 STEALTH PREVENTS ENEMIES FROM WAKING UP. REMOVE YOUR STEALTH ITEM AND STAND ADJACENT TO THE ENEMY TO WAKE IT UP, TO PREVENT TAKING A HIT. KITE IT INTO TERRAIN TO KILL.

### 53 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182722)
SPEED POINTS REGENERATE EVERY 5TH IN GAME TURN, NOT 5 TURNS AFTER USED. IF YOU USE A SP ON TURN 4, IT IMMEDIATELY REGENERATES.

### 54 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182748)
ENEMIES WILL ALWAYS ATTACK LEFT TO RIGHT, TOP TO BOTTOM, CLOSEST FIRST. THIS RESULTS IN A HIT ORDER OF: W, N, S, E, NW, SW, NE, SE.

OBSERVE THE ORDER THE GLADIATORS ATTACK IN, SEE HOW IT IS THE SAME? THE BLOOD AROUND THE PRACTICE DUMMY ALSO SHOWS THE PATTERN.

### 55 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182811)
LEVELING UP RESTORES ALL COOLDOWNS. INVESTING A TALENT POINT RESTORES THE COOLDOWN. AN AMNESIA POTION CAN BE USED TO FORGET A TALENT AND REFRESH THE COOLDOWN TWICE.

### 56 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182830)
AN ALERT PRESENCE ATTRACTS GREATER FOCUS.

### 57 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182929)
EXPERIENCE ISN'T FOUND IN ENDLESS BATTLES, BUT IN WHAT FUELS THEM.

### 58 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_182955)
SPAWNED ENEMIES GIVE NO XP. WHEN A SPAWNER DIES BY RUNNING OUT OF SPAWNS OR HP, IT GIVES EQUAL EXPERIENCE. USE THE LEVEL UP FROM BOTH SPAWNERS DYING TO KILL REMAINING ENEMIES.

### 59 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183023)
MOST RANGED ENEMIES HAVE A 25% CHANCE TO RETREAT IF THE PLAYER IS WITHIN THREE TILES.

### 60 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_183116)
WEIGHTLESS STEPS HAVE THEIR LIMITS.
DONT CLICK THE PIT TILES, USE NUMPAD TO MOVE. GRAPHICS SLIGHTY BROKEN.

### 76 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_203045)
CHANNELED ABILITIES LEAVE ENEMIES VULNERABLE FOR A TURN, USE THIS OPENING TO ATTACK.

### 78 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_203126)

TURRETS CAN ONLY FIRE IN ONE DIRECTION AT A TIME. APPROACH BY ZIGZAGGING BETWEEN SIDES (COLORS), FORCING THE TURRET TO TURN. WHEN CLOSE, USE SP TO CHANGE SIDES AND GET A FREE HIT.

### 80 (Screenshot_2026-08-17_203213)
