================================================================================
THE CAUSE, AND IT IS THE SAME SHAPE AS LAST ROUND
================================================================================
roadChoose's ride only ever moved city.x,y. That is the correct position in CITY
mode and the WRONG ONE ON FOOT -- and the road card has fired on foot since
__ROAD_INTERRUPTS_ON_FOOT__, which is the entire demo. So the lift was a no-op on
the surface where it mostly happens.

The machinery was built, tested and right. It was pointed at the wrong surface.
Last round: the accept worked and had no door. This round: the ride worked and
moved a position the player was not standing in.

AND IT NARRATED THE DRIVE REGARDLESS. The `say` line printed whether or not
anything moved, so even after the movement was fixed a cab that could not start
would still have told him it drove off. That is the same bug in words, and fixing
only the first half would have left it.

================================================================================
NO NEW NUMBER WAS INVENTED
================================================================================
The table already says how far: ride 2, which is two of the map's own tiles. On
foot a map tile is FN walked cells, FN being the game's own constant (OM.TILE_FINE).
So the same ride is the same distance on either surface and nothing was typed.

The cost is the design's and is untouched: the heading is the CAB'S, derived from
the encounter's seq, so it is two tiles in a direction he did not choose, paid
back in the walk. The roster's own verb is "empty cabs still crawling PICKUP
LOOPS" -- the cab drives its loop, not his.

AND IT STILL OBEYS THE MAP. The cab stops the moment the road does, which is why
the measured rides are shorter than the full two tiles and differ by heading.
