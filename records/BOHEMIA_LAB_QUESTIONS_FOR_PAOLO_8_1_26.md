## TIER 1 — THESE CHANGE WHAT GETS BUILT, NOT JUST HOW

### Q1. CAN YOU BE ATTACKED WHILE CAMPED?
*Checked: no mention of camp safety anywhere in laws/ or records/.*
The entire mobile camp rests on this and it has never been said. If a camp is SAFE,
it is a save room and setting one up is pure upside. If it can be **raided**, then
every camp is a gamble and "should I stop here" becomes the best decision in the
survival system. Valheim's whole base loop is that the camp can be attacked.
**→ safe / raidable / raidable only in some places**

### Q2. IS THE CROSSING INTERRUPTIBLE?
*Checked: nothing about interrupting a walk.*
It is 12,288 steps across the valley, ~12 h of game time. Right now nothing says
whether anything can HAPPEN during them. If the walk is uninterruptible, it is
travel and the map is a menu. If things can hit you mid-crossing, the walk is
gameplay and the camp exists because of it.
**→ nothing happens / random encounters / only scripted spots**

### Q3. WHEN A GENERATION CHANGES, WHAT DOES THE HEIR ACTUALLY KEEP?
*Checked: canon says heirs inherit the choice log and dynasty saves span three
generations — but never what a PLAYER keeps in their hands.*
The wardrobe is the progression (195 canon items) and standing is now the currency.
Does the heir inherit the clothes? The standing with each faction? The companions?
The camp gear? **This is the single biggest unknown in the whole dynasty spine** and
it decides whether act 2 starts rich or naked.
**→ list what carries: clothes / standing / companions / gear / nothing**

### Q4. DOES THE CITY-BUILDER RUN ON THE SAME CLOCK AS THE RUN?
*Checked: "same clock" appears re lighting and the fight pulse, never re the two
scales.*
You spend time by taking actions on the run. Does the CITY also advance while you
walk — so being away costs you — or is the builder outside time, a planning screen
you drop into? Pocket City 2's city runs constantly; a roguelite run does not.
**→ one clock / two clocks**

### Q6. ROGUE LEGACY — WHY IS IT NOT A REFERENCE?
*Checked: appears only inside the questbook index, never as a design reference.*
Bohemia is a **dynasty roguelite across generations**. Rogue Legacy is the game that
invented that: heirs with inherited traits, your gold and your castle carry, each
child is a fresh run with the family's accumulated advantages. It is the closest
existing thing to Bohemia's spine and it is absent from canon. Either you have
already ruled it out for a reason I should record, or nobody noticed.
**→ measure it / deliberately not / never heard of it as a fit**

Loot being simple does not settle whether you can carry everything. Zomboid weight
is the anti-reference. Valheim has a hard cap that slows you. Or no limit at all.
**→ hard cap / soft slowdown / none**

## TIER 3 — THE NITTY-GRITTY YOU ASKED FOR

### Q9. CAN YOU REFUSE A QUEST, AND DOES REFUSING COST STANDING?
*Checked: not ruled.* If refusing is free, quests are a menu. If it costs standing
with whoever asked, the questbook becomes a politics system for one line of code.
**→ free / costs standing / cannot refuse**

### Q10. DO COMPANIONS HAVE THEIR OWN STANDING, OR DO THEY SHARE YOURS?
*Checked: not ruled. Companions are canon (they pull bullets out) and standing is
new.* If a companion has their own opinion of a faction, they can object to a job —
which is the cheapest character writing there is.
**→ own / shared / own but only some of them**

### Q11. DOES THE PLAYER HAVE TO SLEEP, OR ONLY CHILL?
*Checked: the camp law has chill vs sleep as options but never says sleep is
required.* Crisis Response and Zomboid make sleep debt a real pressure. Yours could
be optional comfort or a hard need.
**→ optional / required / required only late**

### Q12. WHEN SOMEONE BEGS, CAN YOU ACTUALLY SPARE THEM — AND DOES IT DO ANYTHING?
*Checked: not ruled anywhere; yesterday's traumatic-not-gory law names begging as a
tool but not an interaction.*
This is the one I most want an answer to after watching your Crisis Response frames.
If begging is only audio, it is atmosphere. If sparing someone is a real choice with
a real consequence — standing, a witness, someone who remembers — then the violence
costs you in the exact way you said you wanted.
**→ audio only / real choice with consequences / real choice, no consequences**
