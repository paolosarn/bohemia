## 1. DINOSAURS: THE HONEST WALL, AND THE BACK DOOR THAT IS REAL
- THE WALL: no dinosaur DNA exists or can. The youngest non-bird dinosaur is 66 million years old; DNA is gone
  after about a million in the best cold, and amber does not keep it (tested; the film lied). Nothing from
  deep time comes back by cloning: no T. rex, no raptor, no Titanoboa, no Megalania, no giant dragonfly.
- THE BACK DOOR: BIRDS ARE DINOSAURS, and the old genes are still in them, switched off. Real labs have already
  switched some back on: chicken embryos grown with a dinosaur-like snout instead of a beak (Harvard, 2015),
  embryos with the leg bones of their ancestors, the 'chickenosaurus' program (Jack Horner) that aims at teeth,
  a long tail and clawed hands. That is not cloning; it is waking the ancestor inside a living bird. A lab in
  the 2040s that does this to an OSTRICH, an EMU or a CASSOWARY (a cassowary already kills people with a 12 cm
  claw) gets a two-metre flightless runner with teeth and hands. REALISTIC, and it walks a desert well. This is
  Bohemia's 'raptor', and every character who calls it a dinosaur is half right.
- THE SAME DOOR FOR OTHER ANIMALS: elephants carry mammoth genes (that is how the mammoth program works), horses
  carry zebra stripes, cattle carry the aurochs. Waking old genes in living animals is the cheap half of the lab.

## 2. INSECTS: NO GIANTS, BUT THE REALEST LAB ESCAPE IN HISTORY
- NO GIANT BUGS: the Carboniferous dragonfly with a 70 cm wingspan lived in air with 35% oxygen; today's 21%
  cannot feed a body that size. Physics, not DNA, keeps them dead.
- THE PRECEDENT THAT IS EXACTLY OUR STORY: the AFRICANIZED 'KILLER' BEE. In 1957 a lab in Brazil breeding a
  hardier bee let 26 swarms escape; they crossed South and Central America and reached Nevada in the 1990s. A
  lab animal that broke out and took a continent, already in Las Vegas. It is not fiction; it is the model.
- THE ENGINEERED ONES THAT ARE REAL NOW: gene-drive mosquitoes (released in Florida and Brazil to crash wild
  mosquito populations; a drive that escapes its target is the nightmare the papers debate), sterile-insect
  fly programs, RED IMPORTED FIRE ANTS (a ship escape, now the US South's plague), the ASIAN GIANT HORNET
  (Washington state, 2019 to 2021, eradicated with effort that a collapsed state cannot spend).
- FOR THE GAME: the swarm is an enemy KIND Battle Brothers does not have and we get for free: a beast you cannot
  fight with a gun, only with fire, water, smoke and walls; a hive on a home base is a raid by nature; a
  gene-drive mosquito is a disease clock on a district (WORLD's water and LIFE+CITY's lidded tank again).

## 3. VERY EARLY EARTH: NOTHING WALKS, BUT SOMETHING WAKES
- No animal from deep time can be made. What can, and does, come out of old ice is MICROBES: a 30,000-year-old
  giant virus revived from Siberian permafrost (2014), 48,500-year-old 'zombie viruses' revived alive (2023),
  anthrax from a thawed reindeer carcass that killed a child and 2,000 reindeer (Siberia, 2016). Bacteria
  spores from salt crystals have been claimed at 250 million years (disputed).
- FOR THE GAME: a plague is a beast without a body; the lab's cold rooms and the thawing north are where it
  comes from; the rumour arrives before the sickness; the long-injury rule (36b) already has the shape (out
  for weeks, a mark that stays). PEOPLE and TUNING, one row, later; not a road beast.

## 4. PLANTS: THE BIGGEST INVADERS ON EARTH, AND THE ONES ALREADY IN THE VALLEY
- ALREADY HERE, REAL: TAMARISK (salt cedar) chokes the Colorado and Lake Mead's shore, drinks the river, salts
  the soil; SAHARA MUSTARD and RED BROME carpet the Mojave and carry fire; CHEATGRASS turns the West into a
  fuse (the Great Basin's fire cycle went from every 60 years to every 5); BUFFELGRASS in Arizona; RUSSIAN
  THISTLE is the tumbleweed itself, a Russian import. The dead city burns because of plants nobody planted.
- REVIVED FROM DEEP COLD, REAL: a 32,000-year-old flowering plant grown from permafrost seeds (2012); a
  2,000-year-old Judean date palm grown from a seed found at Masada ('Methuselah', 2005), now fruiting. Old
  plants come back easier than old animals.
- ENGINEERED, REAL: drought crops, fast poplars, the lab's own 'reclaimed ground' plantings (the 32b future gets
  techier: reclaimed ground is planted ground). A crop that escapes is a weed with a patent.
- FOR THE GAME: plants are ECONOMY and WORLD, not COMBAT: what grows on a held lot, what burns, what the camels
  eat, what a garden yields (the hog's target), the date palm as the reclaimed future's tree. The fight's one
  terrain effect stays the mound (rule 33); a burning brome field is a road event, not a tile stat.

## 5. BIRDS: THE ONES WE CAN REALLY MAKE, AND THE ONE THAT HUNTS MEN
- HAAST'S EAGLE (New Zealand, gone ~1400): 15 kg, a 3 m wingspan, hunted 200 kg moa, and by Maori account
  hunted people; DNA is good; a living eagle is the surrogate. THE ONE BIRD THAT IS A BOSS. Deserts suit eagles.
- THE PASSENGER PIGEON (billions; the sky event); the CAROLINA PARAKEET (a green flock in the palms; Los Angeles
  already has feral parrots by the thousand, real); the GREAT AUK (coast only); the DODO (a joke and food); the
  MOA and ELEPHANT BIRD (3 m flightless meat; slow to breed; a hunt).
- ALREADY INVASIVE, REAL: the STARLING (200 million in the US from 100 released in 1890), feral pigeons, house
  sparrows, feral chickens (Hawaii, Florida), feral peacocks (Los Angeles), Egyptian geese. The dead city's
  sky is loud.
- TOO OLD: terror birds, teratorns (La Brea tar), Argentavis. Say it in a mouth.
