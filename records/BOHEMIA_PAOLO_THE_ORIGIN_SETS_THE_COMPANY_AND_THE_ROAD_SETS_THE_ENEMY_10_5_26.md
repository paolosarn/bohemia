## WHAT IT MEANS (rule 75, five parts)
a. THE ORIGIN SETS THE COMPANY. The origin you pick at the door decides who you start with, how many, with what gear and batteries, and the FIELD CAP: Lone Wolf starts one veteran alone; Peasant Militia fields 16 (25 on the roster) instead of 12; the standard start is three men and a cap of 12. Every origin's numbers come from Battle Brothers' origins as data (records/target/bb/origins.json, built from the wiki dump; Grok ask 10 is now first). RUN's door hands the origin; the company is built from it; COMBAT reads the field cap from it. Today the door shows fifteen origins and none of them changes the company. That is the defect.
b. THE ROAD SETS THE ENEMY, NEVER TWELVE AGAINST TWELVE. A party's size and make-up come from Battle Brothers' math: your roster strength (the 12 strongest, 10 plus 2 a level past 1), the days passed, the difficulty, the job's skulls, the part of the map (the pool); gear tier rises with days and difficulty (more leaders, fewer thugs); day one is not day one hundred. On the first roads near the start a party is 'a few' (2 to 3 thugs). His 'for demo purposes just one enemy' is read as: small first fights by the math, so he can test the rest of the game, never a hand-placed one. The fight takes the party the map hands it; it never deals its own twelve.
c. TIME STOPS IN SIGHT (his modded Battle Brothers). When a hostile party enters your sight radius on the map, the clock pauses and the party is drawn with its banner and its count word (a few, some, many); a tap on your route continues the same move, a tap elsewhere makes a new one; you can walk away (rule 68). Amends rule 68 as 68a. Sight radius from Battle Brothers' map rules (1 on most ground, 1.25 on hills, 2 on mountains; night shorter).
d. A MARKET. A settlement has a place to buy and sell weapons and armour (Battle Brothers' weaponsmith, armoursmith and marketplace, as one stall or three by the size tier), from weapons.json and armor.json with their numbers, at 10 wiki crowns to 1 battery; and the board gives contracts (it exists; it must read as the place you get work).
e. EVERY WEAPON SAYS WHAT IT DOES. A weapon's card shows its one-line role in plain words (the pistol is the dagger: one tile, cheap, spends bullets; the pipe is the mace: stuns; the car is the handgonne: a cone, range 2, spends gas, the whole turn to reload) with its numbers from weapons.json; WORDS writes the lines, COMBAT shows them, the market shows the same card.
