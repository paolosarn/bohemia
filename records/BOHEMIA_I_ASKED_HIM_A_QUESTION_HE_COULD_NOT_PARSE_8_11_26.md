# "5. IDK WHAT U MEAN" — I ASKED A QUESTION MADE OF MY OWN JARGON
## 8/11/26, CITY lane

I put this on a numbered list of things only Paolo can decide:

> **5. The run slice: show, merge, or retire?**

His answer: **"5. IDK WHAT U MEAN"**, and he was right to say so.

## WHAT WAS WRONG WITH IT

"The run slice" is a FILENAME. `slices/BOHEMIA_RUN_CURRENT.html` is a build
artifact from an era when the walked game lived in its own page; the alpha's RUN
tab now opens `BOHEMIA_CITY_WORLD.html` instead. "Show / merge / retire" is
three pieces of repo plumbing wearing the costume of a design question.

**Nothing in that sentence is about the game.** He judges what he can see. There
is no screen anywhere in Bohemia called "the run slice", so the question asked him
to hold a mental model of our file layout in order to answer — which is exactly
the labour the whole LOOK-tab ruling exists to stop.

The QUESTION FORMAT law says every question arrives with a researched explanation
and two or three conclusions he picks with one letter. I gave him a bare noun
phrase and three verbs. That is not the format, and the format exists because a
question he cannot parse costs him a turn and gets him nothing.

## THE RULE I BROKE, STATED PLAINLY

**A DECISION HE CANNOT SEE IS NOT HIS DECISION.** If answering requires knowing
which of our HTML files is canonical, it is a plumbing choice and it is MINE. The
things that are genuinely his are the ones he can look at or feel: how thick the
dead lie, whether the Strip reads as buildings, whether the overview is smooth.

## SO I DECIDED IT

**RETIRE IT AS A SURFACE, KEEP IT AS A SOURCE.** Nothing he taps opens it, the
ONE WORLD TAB law already says RUN shows the walked world, and a second walkable
page is exactly the "one system, two bodies" drift ENGINE SYNC bans. It stays in
the repo because tools still build from it and other lanes still read it; it does
not get a tab, a link, or a place on any list of things to judge.

That was always a call I should have made. It goes back on the shelf as mine.

## THE LIFE LESSON UNDERNEATH (never preached in game)
If somebody has to learn your filing system to answer your question, you asked
about your filing system.
