## THE SHORT VERSION
**Film says they crumble. The real record says they REVISE, instantly and calmly,
absorbing whatever you just showed them into a new version of the story. There is
an entire interview technique built for no other purpose than to stop them doing
it. And they revise using one of eight moves that have been catalogued since 1968,
because the thing they are protecting is not the story, it is who they get to keep
being.**

## 1. THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG, AND IT IS IN THE TECHNIQUE'S OWN RATIONALE
The Strategic Use of Evidence approach holds evidence back and takes the suspect's
full account FIRST. The stated reason:

    **late disclosure is superior to early disclosure because it PREVENTS SUSPECTS
    FROM CHANGING THEIR ACCOUNTS to explain already revealed evidence**

**The entire method exists because people revise.** Not because they break. If
confrontation produced collapse, you would disclose early and watch it happen.

**AND THE NUMBERS CLOSE A LOOP THIS LANE OPENED IN Q2.** Q2 measured that
behavioural cues to deception average **d = 0.25**, and that people score 54%
reading a person. Here:

    statement-evidence inconsistency, one of the strongest cues in the
    whole deception literature                            **d = 1.83**
    trained interviewers using it                         65 to 85%
    untrained                                             43 to 56%

**CHECKING THE CLAIM IS ROUGHLY SEVEN TIMES THE SIGNAL OF READING THE PERSON.** Q2
reached "catch a lie by checking, never by reading" from the null side. This round
reaches it from the positive side, in a different literature, with a number. **Two

## 2. WHAT THEY ACTUALLY SAY, AND IT HAS BEEN CATALOGUED SINCE 1968
The sociology of this exact moment is called ACCOUNTS: the thing a person produces
when their conduct is put under valuative inquiry. There are two families and eight
standard moves.

**EXCUSES admit the act was wrong and deny responsibility.**

    appeal to accident          it happened, it was not steered
    appeal to defeasibility     I did not know, I was not told, I was not fully
                                in possession of the facts
    appeal to biological drives I am built like this
    scapegoating                somebody else's doing

**JUSTIFICATIONS accept responsibility and deny the act was wrong.**

    denial of injury            nobody actually got hurt
    denial of the victim        they had it coming
    condemnation of condemners  look at who is asking
    appeal to loyalties         I did it for my people

**AND THE PURPOSE LINE IS THE WHOLE WRITING RULE: an account is offered so the
person can keep the social identity they had a minute ago.** They are not primarily
dodging a consequence. They are trying to still be the same man when the
conversation ends.

**SO WHICH OF THE EIGHT SOMEBODY REACHES FOR TELLS YOU WHO THEY THINK THEY ARE**,
and it is free characterisation at the exact moment the scene is hottest. A man who
says "nobody got hurt" and a man who says "I did it for my people" have been caught
doing the identical thing and are two completely different people.

## 3. THE APOLOGY, RANKED, AND THE TWO PARTS THAT WORK ARE NOT THE ONES WE WRITE
If an account fails, what follows is an apology, and the components have been
tested and ranked:

    1. **acknowledgement of responsibility**      strongest
    2. **offer of repair**                        second
    3. expression of regret          }
       explanation of what went wrong }            tied third
       declaration of repentance     }
    6. request for forgiveness                    weakest

**THE TWO THAT WORK ARE THE TWO THAT COST SOMETHING: admitting fault, and offering
to fix it.** "I am sorry" sits in the middle tier and "please forgive me" is the
weakest thing on the list.

**AND THAT IS EXACTLY THE APOLOGY FILM CONVENTION WRITES**: regret plus a plea for
forgiveness, which is the two weakest components and nothing else. More components
rated better overall, but the ranking is the usable part.

## 4. THE GAMES HALF, AND ITS COMMON FAILURE IS THE OPPOSITE OF REAL BEHAVIOUR
The design writing on confrontation lands on one good sentence: **a contradiction is
valuable when two statements cannot comfortably exist together.** And the pleasure
is in the MATCH, not the search: finding the claim that collides with the evidence.

There is also a pacing rule worth keeping: **hold the strongest contradiction until
the story is established**, because confronting early wastes the clearest chance to
challenge the timeline. That is the same shape as the evidence technique in section
1, arrived at by feel rather than by study.

**AND THE FAILURE MODE PLAYERS ACTUALLY REPORT IS THE INVERSE OF THE REAL
BEHAVIOUR: characters who do not adapt their stance when confronted**, who go on
repeating a line that has just been disproved. **Real people revise too fluidly;
game characters revise not at all.** Both are wrong and they are wrong in opposite
directions, which means the correct target sits between them and nobody is
occupying it.

## 5. OUR OWN BUILD, MEASURED, EVERY HIT READ BY HAND
Ten patterns across 1,669 NPC lines, one for each of the eight account moves and
two for the strong apology components.

    excuses of any kind          0
    justifications of any kind   0
    apologies for a transgression 0
    real admissions of being wrong 1

The raw pattern hits were 2, 4, 1 and 2, and reading every one of them, almost all
were false positives: "nothing runs that clean by accident" is an inference, "I am
just going to look at you" is a threat my regex mistook for condemning the
condemner, and "I will put it down and you read it somewhere else" is a quirk about
not saying something aloud.

**THE ONE REAL HIT IS GOOD AND IT IS ALONE:** a watcher saying "both times I was
wrong I was early", owning two bad weather calls. **That is an acknowledgement of
responsibility, the strongest apology component, and it is the only one in the
game.**

**AND THE SORRY COUNT IS THE BEST THING IN THIS ROUND.** My first pattern said zero,
which was my pattern being too narrow, so I widened it and read all eight hits:

    "Sorry, I'm counting."
    "Sorry. I do that. I've been alone a lot."
    "Sorry. I know. I keep doing it. My mother did it, I hated it, and here we
     absolutely are."

**EIGHT SORRIES IN THIS GAME AND EVERY SINGLE ONE IS FOR A MANNERISM. NOBODY
APOLOGISES FOR ANYTHING THEY DID. THEY APOLOGISE FOR BEING THEMSELVES.**

**AND THE CHAIN IS NOW THREE ROUNDS LONG AND CONSISTENT.** Q2 measured that nobody
in this game lies. Q17 measured that no rumour is ever wrong. Q19 measures that
nobody is ever caught, and no account for anything is offered by anyone. **It is one hole with
three names, and it is the hole where the moral weight of a city goes.**

## 6. WHAT SCHOOL LEAVES ME HOLDING FOR ROUND TWO
1. **NOBODY COLLAPSES. THE FIRST LINE AFTER THE CATCH IS A REVISION**, delivered
   calmly, that absorbs the new fact.
2. **THE REVISION USES ONE OF EIGHT MOVES, AND THE CHOICE IS THE CHARACTER.**
3. **WHAT THEY ARE PROTECTING IS THE IDENTITY, NOT THE STORY.** The tell is that
   they will surrender facts to keep being the same man.
4. **AN APOLOGY THAT IS ONLY REGRET AND A PLEA IS THE TWO WEAKEST PARTS.** The
   strong ones are "it was me" and "here is how I fix it".
5. **THE CATCH ITSELF IS A COLLISION BETWEEN TWO STATEMENTS**, not a read of a
   face, and it is worth seven times the signal.
6. **AND OUR CAST APOLOGISES ONLY FOR EXISTING**, which is a voice we already have
   and can play against.
