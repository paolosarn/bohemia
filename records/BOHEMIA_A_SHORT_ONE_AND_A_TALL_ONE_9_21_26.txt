AND 18 IS NOT THE 24 THE NUMBERS 0.88 AND 1.12 LOOK LIKE, WHICH MATTERS TO
WHOEVER WIRES THIS. The stage scales the SKELETON, and the head is a rigid stamp the
rig refuses to shrink, so a 24-point span on the bones buys 18 points on the
whole standing body. If the intent was that a tall man really is a quarter taller
than a short one, the dial has to run wider than 0.88 to 1.12, or the head has to
come along. THE DIAL NUMBER IS NOT THE HEIGHT NUMBER.

HEADS TALL IS THE NUMBER THAT SAYS IT READS. Measured off the RIG PART GRID, never
off the painted picture, because a haircut adds rows no bone made -- that error cost
this lane six points on a child last round. Between adults the head stays near its
own size, so heads-tall RISES with height, and that rise is what a person sees as
"tall" rather than as "zoomed in".

THE SPAN IS WIDER THAN A REAL STREET AND I AM SAYING SO RATHER THAN ADOPTING IT
QUIETLY. Adult male stature has a standard deviation near 7 cm on a mean of 175, so
the middle 91 of every 100 men run 163 to 187 cm, which against the median is 0.93
to 1.07. The default's 0.88 to 1.12 reaches 154 cm and 196 cm: the ends of a whole
population, not the middle of one. That is a fine choice for a game and a bad one
for a crowd, and it is exactly the kind of thing he can rule on in one look.

against the ceiling of the sprite box and anything above it is gone. The rig's own

frame" -- and every stage that has ever shipped is 1.00 or below. WHOEVER WIRES
THIS ROW MUST GROW THE FRAME FIRST, or tall people in this game will be scalped.
I wrote that risk into this tool BEFORE running it, so it is a prediction that
came true and not a story told afterwards.
