THE PRICE IS NOT A NEW NUMBER, WHICH IS THE PART WORTH KEEPING
================================================================================
priceOf() is the GRUDGE ITSELF: the same negative sum wouldSquare already adds
up, which is the same sum forceOf already weighs, which is his STANDING dial and
nothing else. A heavier wrong costs more because it IS more. Rounded UP, because
EVERYTHING COSTS ONE means a wrong you can feel cannot cost zero and half a
battery is not a thing anybody can hand over.
AND WHILE HIS DEED TABLE IS EMPTY IT COSTS NOTHING. That is the truth, not a
hidden default: nobody has ruled what a wrong weighs, so nobody is owed anything
for it. The gate holds that case on purpose so a future round cannot quietly slip
a number in underneath it.

*** AND THE WORD 'paid' IS NOW REFUSED WHEN NOTHING WAS PAID. *** That is the
whole row. makeRight takes opts.paid; ask for 'paid' with nothing handed over and
the record says 'forgiven' -- THEY LET IT GO -- because that is what happened. The
wrong is still settled either way: this refuses the WORD, never the forgiveness,
exactly as the ruling's second half requires. A caller cannot lie to the ledger
even by accident, which is the only real fix for a bug whose cause was a caller
lying to the ledger by accident.

IT IS A TRANSFER, NOT A DRAIN. The purse has both and they mean different things:
a drain is consumed, a transfer goes TO somebody. Restitution goes to the person
wronged, so transferOut is the right door and the ledger row says so.
