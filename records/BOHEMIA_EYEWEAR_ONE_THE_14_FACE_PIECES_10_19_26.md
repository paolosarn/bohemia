## 2. THE METHOD

tools/bohemia_character_eyewear_one.py, called by the barber tool, on PORTRAIT's starred head on the barber's mannequin
(the face under each piece is PORTRAIT's, never touched), each kind modelled once, each piece in its own ramp's mid.
CLOTH BRIDGES THE HOLLOWS: a mask is laid on the face's own radius (rays from the head's axis), max-filtered across its
neighbours (3 columns, 2 rows) and smoothed, so it stands off the nose and does not sink into the mouth, then pleated
(the dust mask's three) or folded (the bandana's); below the chin the bandana falls straight and is cut to a point.
The flat game's law holds: the mask, the bandana and the face wrap sit below the eyes, never over them.

WHAT LOOKING CHANGED: the gas mask's lenses were set off the eye's own radius and sat INSIDE the rubber face, which
bridges the brow and the nose (cycle 1: a black band with a filter); they are set off the bridged radius now and stand
proud in their rings. The other five kinds read as what they are on the first pass.
