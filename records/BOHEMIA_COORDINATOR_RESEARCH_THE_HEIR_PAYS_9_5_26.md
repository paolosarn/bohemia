# THE HEIR PAYS: WHAT AN HEIR REALLY INHERITS WHEN THERE IS NO LAW (coordinator research round, 9/5/26)

## THE QUESTION
Last round I decided, on my own, that an heir inherits every debt in full. Was that realism or cruelty for its own sake? Both aisles.

## AISLE ONE: THE REAL WORLD
- **Where there is a state, debt dies with you.** The estate pays what it can; what it cannot pay goes unpaid; a child is never personally liable unless they co-signed. Sources: [InCharge: can you inherit debt](https://www.incharge.org/understanding-debt/family/can-you-inherit-debt/), [Sims & Campbell: when you can inherit debt](https://www.simscampbell.law/inheritance-with-strings-attached-when-you-can-inherit-debt/).
- **Where there is no state, the family pays.** In informal lending the lender collects from whoever is left. Debt bondage is passed parent to child: when the parent can no longer work or dies, the child works the debt, from ages five to ten in brick kilns and fields, and accrues more while doing it. A loan shark's terms are never clearly stated and the sum is designed to be unpayable. Sources: [End Slavery Now: bonded labor](http://www.endslaverynow.org/learn/slavery-today/bonded-labor), [Regent Center for Global Justice: what is debt bondage](https://globaljustice.regent.edu/2021/01/what-is-debt-bondage/), [Genicot, Georgetown: child bonded labor](https://faculty.georgetown.edu/gg58/CBL.pdf), [Wikipedia: loan shark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loan_shark).
- A collapsed Vegas run by factions is the second world, not the first. My default stands, and now it has its reason: the faction that lent to your parent comes to YOU.

## AISLE TWO: THE BEST GAMES
Games with heirs split into two kinds. In one, the heir starts clean and only the good things carry (money, upgrades, the house), so dying is a mild reset and the generations blur together. In the other, the heir carries the parent's consequences (enemies, debts, reputation, unfinished business), and players remember those games for the generation where it went wrong. The mechanic in plain words: what carries must include the BAD, or the fold is a respawn with a costume change. (No game named; the 8/28 law.)

## THE FINDING THAT CHALLENGES WHAT WE BELIEVE
We have been treating the fold as a save-game feature (what fields persist; DYNASTY Q3 found the persistence is backwards field for field). It is not a save feature. It is the one moment where a life lesson lands without preaching: everything you did not pay while alive, your kid pays. The parent's whole game is, in secret, deciding what to leave the child. That is the theme ("nobody is anything without family") as a MECHANIC, and it costs nothing to build because the debt ledger and the deed weights already exist.
