We've had a system like this previously, and it was very similar the assets that I bought and tried to create everything else based on the assets that I bought and this is how we get the fucked up mess that we have right now so that answer I don't like at all you're because we did this already we tried to Match and compare the assets that I bought compared and you would try to make it similar or something and it turns out so dog shit you actually forgot about the assets that we had downloaded and purchased."

## He is right, and the coordinator was re-selling the failed idea with better tools

The 9/4 law (COMPARE EVERY PIECE OF ART TO THE WORLD), the twin (rule 82), the reference check gate, the twin sheets: that WAS 'look at the pack and make something similar, then compare'. It produced the mess. Why, in three parts, from the records:

1. THE CHAT STILL DREW. Every 'matched' piece was still placed pixel by pixel in code by a blind hand, then held next to the pack. Asking a blind hand to copy a painting does not make a copy.
2. THE COMPARE WAS A CITATION, NOT A MEASUREMENT. The reference check gate read filenames and cite lines, not pixels. EYES measured the gaps on 10/10: texture ratios of 2.9x, 160x and 500x between a cook's tile and its 'twin' passed green.
3. THE BANK WAS NOT IN THE TOOLS' PATH. The packs sat in reference/art_bank and the law said 'compare'; nothing in a cook's tool opened the folder, so the chats forgot the packs existed. His words: 'you actually forgot about the assets.'

ROUND EIGHT's 'the packs are the ruler' and ROUND TEN's point 1 were the same idea with a real ruler. A better ruler does not fix a copying hand. This record withdraws 'make it similar' as a method.

## The different answer: USE THE PACKS. DO NOT IMITATE THEM.

- THE PACK PIECES ARE THE ART. The game is built out of the bought assets themselves, placed as they are: the road, the median, the lamp, the props, the roofs. A chat's art job becomes PLACING and ARRANGING bought art, cutting it, layering it, recolouring it by faction with a shade remap. Not making art that looks like it.
- NOTHING IS MADE 'IN THE STYLE OF'. 'Similar to the pack', 'inspired by', 'matched to the twin' are dead phrases. A new piece exists only when NO pack has it, and then it is not drawn: it is BOUGHT (the four holes: wrecks, guns, portraits, eight-facing people and beasts; COOK [the pack card]) or, when nothing sells, it comes from the camera route (a model under one camera, which does not imitate pixels, it photographs a shape) or a human, and goes to his tap.
- A PIECE WITHOUT A PACK FILE BEHIND IT FAILS. Every asset in the game carries the bank path it was cut from (or the model it was rendered from, or the pack card that bought it). No path, no asset. This is the gate that makes forgetting impossible.
- THE BANK IS IN EVERY ART CHAT'S SKILL FILE as a list of what exists, by family, with pictures, so 'forgot the packs' cannot happen again: the chat reads the bank before it reads anything else.
- THE TWIN GATE STAYS, DEMOTED. It no longer judges imitation; it has nothing to judge. It checks that a cut or a recolour did not drift (same outline, same palette family) and that a camera-route piece sits at the pack's scale and light. A ruler for drift, not a licence to copy.
- THE EXISTING 'MATCHED' ART IS WHAT HE CALLED IT: placeholders, all of it (10/10, EVERYTHING IS A PLACEHOLDER). Nothing of it is a twin to anything.

## What this does to the lanes

COOK, COOK TWO, THREE, FOUR: the [how the pack did it] rows change meaning: not 'study the pack and draw like it' but 'find the pack piece and use it'; the one-at-a-time sheet shows the PACK PIECE placed in the game, then his tap. DIRECTION: the skill file leads with the bank list. PLUMBER [the twin gate]: a drift check and a provenance check (the path), not a similarity judge. The camera route (DIRECTION [the master rig], ANIMATION [eight through the rig]) is for what no pack sells, starting with people and beasts in eight facings. Rule 108 ROUND ELEVEN.
