  everything -- so the amplitude numbers in the recipe did not mean what they
  looked like. Real speech runs consonants about -7.4 dB against vowels
  (fricative contrast 7-14 dB, sibilants strongest at -2.4, dentals weakest at
  -21). Mine ran +8. Sixteen dB the wrong side.
