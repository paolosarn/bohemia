## WHAT WAS WRONG
Every round since 9/4 took his last sentence and built a wing on it. Batteries became
a law, then a research swing, then four jobs, all in one direction, while things I
actually believe about the game sat on the board marked "needs Paolo" for weeks.
That is a secretary, not a manager. He is right.

## THE RULE (LOCKED, amends the central-chat law)
1. I hold my own view of the whole game and I say it, whether or not he asked.
2. Every round carries at least one thing that is MINE: not from his last message,
   from my read of the whole, with my reason. It goes on the board as a job the same
   round. He corrects what he hates.
3. A line marked "needs Paolo" that I could decide with a defensible default is a
   line I failed to decide. Identity and names stay his. Everything else I pick.
4. When I disagree with him I say so once, plainly, then do what he ruled.

## WHAT I ACTUALLY THINK THE GAME NEEDS, IN ORDER (my list, nobody asked)
1. **The game opens as the animal.** Gen 1 is an Animal by his own pillar, and the
   demo opens as a human family at a table. The first minute of the game hides the
   one idea nobody else has. Dynasty found the four-legged renderer already exists.
   The coyote cold open is the most Bohemia thing we could ship and nobody is on it.
2. **Death is a handoff, not a game over.** THERE ARE NO RUNS. So the most important
   unbuilt scene is the one where you die and the game hands you your heir. Today
   death is killed (7/2) and marked "needs Paolo". Without it the stakes are zero
   and the three-generation idea is a sentence in a document.
3. **The beat must be heard before it is explained.** 120 BPM is the law and the city
   makes no sound (day 22). A heartbeat on the walked street from second one, before
   any song, would make the whole game feel like itself.
4. **Crossing a colour must cost something the moment you do it.** Colour is territory
   and nothing happens when you walk into the wrong colour. A look, a tail, a stop:
   territory that does not react is paint.
5. **The bag of rice is the tutorial.** Everything costs one and a day of work pays
   one. The one thing you MUST buy every day is the whole economy in miniature.
   Teach it by making you hungry, not by a text box.
