# THE TWO HOLES, CHECKED IN PIXELS AND NOT IN NAMES

COOK [the two holes], 10/16/26. The row's own constraint: **"a hole is declared only
after the count says zero."** Both holes had been declared off PACK NAMES. This lane has
been burned by names three times -- "12. Weapons and armory" is viking axes, "Statues and
figures" is stone, and "9. Abandoned cards" was 35 wrecked CARS he already owned -- so
this round swept the pixels of all **8,674 tiles** instead of the 294 names.

## HOLE 1: PEOPLE IN EIGHT DIRECTIONS -- REAL

Ranked every tile by the silhouette a standing person has and a prop does not: a narrow
top (the head), a sudden widening (the shoulders), a waist, then legs.

- **1,251 of 8,674 tiles score at all.**
- The top 160 were LOOKED AT, one by one, at 1:1.
- They are crosses, windsocks, signposts, torches, lanterns, railings, fences, mailboxes,
  birdhouses, flowers, a toilet and a telescope. **Not one is a person.**
- The nearest human shapes he owns: `12. Weapons and armory` #30-#33, armour on a dress
  stand, and `Statues and figures`, robed stone figures.

## HOLE 2: HEAD-ONLY PORTRAITS -- REAL

Ranked by what a portrait is: about as tall as wide, filling its box, near-symmetric,
two dark marks up top and one below.

- **5,295 of 8,674 tiles score**, and that number is the ruler failing honestly: a full-bleed
  wall or floor tile is square and symmetric by nature, and the top of the ranking is
  exactly that. The LOOK is what decided, not the score.

### *** AND THE FIRST COUNT OF THIS HOLE WAS WRONG. ONLY LOOKING FOUND IT. ***

The first cut of this page said **EXACTLY ONE drawn human face exists in 8,674 tiles**,
because the look-sheet labels are truncated and "Miscellaneo#44" was read as
`19. Miscellaneous (1)#44`. Pulling the three packs whose names start that way and putting
them side by side showed the real tile is `Miscellaneous#44`, that `#43` beside it is a
SECOND face, and that `Banners and posters` holds about seven more (#1, #8, #9, #31, #34,
#35, #38).

**THE CORRECTED CLAIM:** he owns roughly a dozen drawn human faces, and every single one
is a face PRINTED ON A POSTER, A BANNER OR A SIGN -- wanted bills, neon adverts, a
hologram -- in a neon cyberpunk style. **Not one is a character portrait, not one is
head-only, and none is in this world's look.** The hole stands. The number did not, and
the number is corrected here rather than quietly dropped.

## HOW BIG EACH HOLE IS

The laws already name the roster: 13 gang looks, 6 enemy tiers, about 75 enemy types, 17
beasts, 3 bosses, at least 12 factions. Rule 107 wants eight facings each with an idle and
a step, so **one BODY is 16 drawings before it wears anything**, and one FACE is one
drawing at or over 96 by 96 (rule 105). That is what makes the three routes below
comparable instead of a feeling.
