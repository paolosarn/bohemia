## THE COORDINATOR'S DRAFT OF OURS (draft:true, his to change; Battle Brothers first, our swag second)
- WHERE: the Mob's arena is a building on its Strip settlement screen (tap it, Battle Brothers' way): a dead casino's showroom or a hotel
  pool drained to a pit, the crowd in the old seats with flashlights and lighters; analog horror at the source (a dead casino's
  scoreboard still running, the MC on a cracked PA).
- THE CARD: tap the arena and see the next match plainly (the opponents' faces or beasts, how many, the purse in batteries); tap your
  three (or five) from the crew on one screen with big faces; FIGHT. No collar; the accessory slot shows crossed out on each.
- THE FIGHT: the fight we have, on a small arena board (the pit); no retreat button; no loot; the crowd's sounds react to the fight;
  the clones come from the Mob's own farm (the bull, the camel, the bear, the lion, the hyena, the sloth at the top of the card).
- THE PAY: batteries, only on a win; the purse grows with the danger. Every fifth match the Mob's own champion or a prize clone, and a
  piece of arena gear nowhere else in the game.
- THE RANKS: a man who wins arena fights climbs (Pit Fighter, Arena Fighter, Arena Veteran) with a small trait each, Battle Brothers'
  numbers as the floor (TUNING's table).
- THE TOURNAMENT: a feed post tells you the Strip is hosting one for a few days; five men, a run of matches, a big prize.
- THE MOB'S STORY: the arena is part of how the Mob keeps people coming and fed; it is fun in a dead world, and it is also where the
  Mob shows who owns the meat.
