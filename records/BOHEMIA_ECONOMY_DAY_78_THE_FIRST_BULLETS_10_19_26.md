## 1. HOW BATTLE BROTHERS PRICES A SHOT
Sources (reference/library/grok/wiki/PAGES_ALL.tar.gz): bb_all/0055_Ammunition.txt, 1616_Quiver of Arrows.txt,
1617_Quiver of Bolts.txt, 1140_Large Powder Bag.txt, 0935_Handgonne.txt, 0766_Fire Handgonne (5 fatigue).txt,
1678_Reload Handgonne (20 fatigue).txt, 0460_Crossbow and Firearms Mastery.txt, 1166_Light Crossbow.txt,
1782_Shoot Bolt (5 fatigue).txt, 0002_A New Company.txt, 0827_Game Guide.txt (Economy), 1244_Market prices for
various items.txt; records/target/bb/stash_rates.json ammo (GROK_134, PASSED).
- ONE PILE, POINTS NOT BULLETS: a bundle is 50 points, worth 100 crowns (2 crowns a point).
- WHAT A SHOT TAKES OUT OF THE PILE: an arrow or a bolt 1 point, a handgonne shot 2, a throwing weapon or a fire
  lance charge 3.
- THE QUIVER IS THE MAGAZINE AND THE PILE IS THE WALLET: quivers of 10 (large 14), powder bags of 5 (large 7);
  every quiver, bag and bundle REFILLS AFTER THE BATTLE from the pile, never in the fight; an empty pile leaves
  them empty.
- THE GUN COSTS DOUBLE A SHOT AND FIRES HALF AS OFTEN: Fire Handgonne 3 AP, Reload 9 AP, so a handgonne fires every
  other turn until Crossbow and Firearms Mastery (reload 6 AP, every turn). Per turn the handgonne and the crossbow
  both spend about one point; a bow on Quick Shot spends two.
- THE GUIDE'S OWN WARNING: 'Ammunition expenses can rise up fast if several recruits are using Bows with Quick Shot.
  It is advised to mostly stick to Crossbows in the early game... use only 1 Ammunition per attack.'
- THE START: A New Company carries 40 points at Medium (20 Low, 80 High) beside 2,400 crowns; ONE of its three
  Companions is the ranged one, with a light crossbow ('almost a full turn to reload').
- THE COUNTER: supplies sell at the town's buying modifier (1.04 to 1.22 in the guide's examples, sometimes 1.5),
  so a bundle is 104 to 150 crowns; an Arrow-Maker's Shed makes it 'unusually low'.
