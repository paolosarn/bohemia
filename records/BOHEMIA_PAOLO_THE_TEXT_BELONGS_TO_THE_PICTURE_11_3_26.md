## WHAT HE LOVES (for the taste canon)
The camera-feed loading screens: 'literally changing my life and the belief in what I can put out in the universe... a way to get you
wrapped up into the world.' One of his top three or four moments of the whole project, beside the combat overhaul and the first time he
saw what the characters would look like (the 3D rig). Made from our own creativity. The bar for everything that follows.

## THE LAW (rule 357)
1. THE NOTES BUTTON IS GONE, from everywhere (the demo and the alpha, every screen). Its gate legs go with it. (The 9/20 'only his tiny
   NOTES button' clause is dead; the alpha's VOTE tab stays.)
2. NO BACKING BEHIND THE START SCREEN'S TEXT: BOHEMIA, POST ECONOMIC APOCALYPSE, NEW GAME and CONTINUE have no shadow, no box, no contrast
   panel, no glow, no blur behind them. The picture is composed so the text reads on its own: every feed, tall and wide, is framed with a
   quiet place made for the words (a dark sky, a blank wall, the road's asphalt, a shadow across the lot), the words' colour and weight
   chosen per feed, and nothing moves under the words.
3. FOR ALL TEXT FROM NOW ON: wherever text sits, it looks like it was meant to be there by what is behind it; the minimalist's way, tamed
   and beautiful. Text is placed by composition, not rescued by a box.
4. THE GATE: no element behind the start screen's four words (no background, shadow, outline or blur on them or their parents), the words'
   contrast against the real pixels behind them measured on every feed tall and wide (it must read), and no NOTES button in any build.
