1. WHO: his FACE, a photograph in a paper frame pinned on the house's dark board (the sheet's and the opening card's), and an edge

2. HOW HE IS: three lit bars the whole width of the card (blood, armour, wind: the fight's own colours).
3. WHAT HE HAS LEFT: his AP as pips, one a point, lit while he has it.

outclasses you (the board's own mark over his head), the sidestep when he dodges, a plate when his armour holds. HIS SHEET READS EACH
MARK BESIDE ITS WORD (OUTCLASSES YOU, DODGES, ARMOUR: the plate's own words, none added, rule 454(a)), and says the level his plate
says (it said LEVEL 1 for a man whose plate said LV 5).
