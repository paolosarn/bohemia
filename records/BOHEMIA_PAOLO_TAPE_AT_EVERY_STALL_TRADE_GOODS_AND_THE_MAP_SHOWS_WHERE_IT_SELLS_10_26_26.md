## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (the wiki copy, ECONOMY day 68 s1b; recall where marked)
Every marketplace sells Tools and buys what you carry. Trade goods are made where a town has the attached location (a salt mine, a
dye maker): bought there at the plain price and sold back there at only 15 percent; anywhere that does not make them it buys at 110
percent, so the money is in carrying the good from where it is dug to where it is not. Nothing on the world map tells you which town
pays most; you learn it by visiting (his frustration, and ours to beat).
