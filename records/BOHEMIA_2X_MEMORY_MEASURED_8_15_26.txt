===============================================================================
MEASURED, on the real alpha, with the caches WARMED
===============================================================================
Warmed by cycling 12 clips x 8 facings x 4 phases through drawChar on the real
character canvas -- a cold cache reports a comfortable number that means nothing.

    HD_CACHE      524 / 768 frames warm  =  25.1 MB
                  at FULL CAP (768)      =  36.8 MB
    FRAME_CACHE   578 / 768              =  13.8 MB
    iOS ceiling   224 MB
    HEADROOM at a completely full character cache   =  187.3 MB

===============================================================================
*** AND HERE IS THE PART THAT DECIDES IT: THE CACHE DOES NOT GROW AT 2X. ***
===============================================================================
`G.hd` DEFAULTS TRUE, and drawChar already upscales every frame 56 -> 112 through
Scale2x before storing it. SO THE FINISHED-FRAME CACHE IS ALREADY PAYING THE 112
PRICE, TODAY, ON EVERY FRAME.

    HD_CACHE entry size, before the migration:  112 x 112 x 4
    HD_CACHE entry size, after the migration:   112 x 112 x 4      <- identical

36.8 MB at full cap before, 36.8 MB at full cap after. The migration changes where
those 112 pixels COME FROM -- native authored art instead of a machine upscale --
not how many bytes are retained.

WHAT ACTUALLY GROWS 4x is the per-frame BAKE WORKING SET: buildFrame composing on a
112x112 part grid instead of 56x56.

    per frame now   0.04 MB   ->   at 2x   0.16 MB

That is TRANSIENT, not retained -- it is the scratch for one frame, freed before the
next. It never accumulates into the ceiling.

===============================================================================
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE MIGRATION
===============================================================================
1. NO CACHE BUDGET CHANGE IS NEEDED. The item's fallback ("if it comes back tight
   the answer is cache budget") does not have to be exercised. HD_CACHE.max stays
   768.
2. THE ITEM'S OWN CPU CLAIM IS CONFIRMED FROM THE SAME FACT. Step (3) of the
   migration is "turn Scale2x OFF ... and it hands back per-frame CPU". It does,
   and this is why: Scale2x is running on EVERY cold frame right now purely to
   manufacture the 112 the cache already stores. Authoring at 112 deletes that work
   rather than adding any.
3. THE HONEST LIMIT OF THIS MEASUREMENT: taken in desktop Chromium at DPR 2, not on
   his phone. It is an ARITHMETIC result, not a device result -- the entry size is
   w*h*4 and the cap is a constant, so the before/after equality holds on any
   device. The 224 MB figure remains the pinned iOS floor from the 7/27 mobile
   render contract and carries that contract's own honesty clause. Nothing here
   claims a live iOS reading.
