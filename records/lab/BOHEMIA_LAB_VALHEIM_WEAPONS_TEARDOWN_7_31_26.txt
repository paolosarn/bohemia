=============================================================================
THE FINDING
=============================================================================
VALHEIM'S WEAPON SYSTEM IS A DAMAGE-MULTIPLIER SYSTEM WEARING A WEAPON LIST AS
A COSTUME. Almost nothing good about it is the damage printed on the weapon. It
is FOUR MULTIPLIERS YOU EARN — know what you are fighting, stand behind it, time
a block, have used the thing before — and the weapon's real job is deciding which
of those four you can reach.

WHY IT LANDS ON US EXACTLY. His north star
(laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_WHAT_COMBAT_IS_FOR_7_27_26.md, LOCKED) is "deal the most
damage and take the least amount of damage by positioning and abilities and
deeper understanding of mechanics." His own audit
(records/BOHEMIA_COMBAT_AUDIT_AGAINST_THE_NORTH_STAR_7_27_26.md) then found, in
capitals: "DEAL THE MOST DAMAGE BY POSITIONING — NOT IMPLEMENTED AT ALL" and of
the seven ability verbs, "None of them increases your damage." Half the sentence
has no code behind it. Valheim's four multipliers map onto that missing half one
for one:
    damage type vs resistance  ->  DEEPER UNDERSTANDING OF MECHANICS
    backstab                   ->  DEAL MORE DAMAGE BY POSITIONING
    parry into stagger         ->  DEAL MORE DAMAGE BY WHAT YOU SPEND
    weapon skill               ->  the switching cost that makes the choice real
That is the whole reason this is a lab row and not a paragraph.

--- SOURCED: THE WEAPON CLASS SYSTEM, from real open-source C# ---
SKILL_TYPES_BEFORE_JUMP 14 — [SOURCED ValheimPlus/ValheimPlus/GameClasses/Skills.cs:103-116]
  The enum opens at Skills.cs:101 `public enum SkillType` and its first fourteen
  members are, verbatim and in order: None, Swords, Knives, Clubs, Polearms,
  Spears, Blocking, Axes, Bows, FireMagic, FrostMagic, Unarmed, Pickaxes,
  WoodCutting. THAT IS THE WEAPON TYPE LIST, and it is a list of SKILLS, not a
  list of items — which is the first structural finding: in Valheim a weapon
  type IS a skill you level, so the type is a long-term investment and not a
  stat block.
SKILLTYPE_JUMP 100 — [SOURCED Skills.cs:117 `Jump = 100,`]
  The numbering JUMPS to 100 for the non-combat skills (Jump, Sneak, Run, Swim).
  Combat skills are 1-13, movement skills are 100-103. A deliberate gap so new
  weapon classes can be added without renumbering anything.
SKILLTYPE_ALL 999 — [SOURCED Skills.cs:121 `All = 999`]
  A sentinel meaning "every skill", used by their raise/lower-all paths.
  ALSO SOURCED, and it is the load-bearing line for the whole model:
  [SOURCED ValheimPlus/GameClasses/Player.cs:376]
    `if (item?.m_shared.m_skillType == Skills.SkillType.Bows)`
  A weapon ITEM carries `m_shared.m_skillType`. That single field is the entire
  weapon-type system: the item declares its class, the class is a skill, the
  skill scales the damage. Everything else on this page hangs off it.
  And the per-skill switch at Skills.cs:20-72 enumerates them one by one
  (Swords:22, Knives:25, Clubs:28, Polearms:31, Spears:34, Blocking:37, Axes:40,
  Bows:43), which is independent confirmation that these are the real names.

--- DOC: THE TYPE SYSTEM ---
DMG_TYPES 10 — [DOC the documented damage-type list: slash, blunt, pierce, chop,
  pickaxe (physical) + fire, frost, lightning, poison, spirit (elemental)]
PHYSICAL_TYPES 3 — [DOC slash / blunt / pierce are the three that weapons use in
  combat; chop and pickaxe are for trees and rock]
SLASH_WEAKNESS_COUNT 0 — [DOC no enemy in Valheim is weak to slash]
  THIS IS A DESIGN STATEMENT STORED AS A NUMBER, and it is the most quietly
  clever thing in the system. Slash is what swords do. The sword is the default
  weapon. So the default weapon has no matchup to exploit — it is never the best
  answer and never the wrong one. Nobody ever feels stupid for carrying a sword,
  and nobody ever feels clever for it either. A generalist that is deliberately
  flat is how you make specialists matter without punishing the casual player.

--- DOC: THE RESISTANCE LADDER, as multipliers ---
MOD_VERY_WEAK 2 — [DOC "Very weak: 200% damage taken"]
MOD_WEAK 1.5 — [DOC "Weak: 150%"]
MOD_WEAK_SLIGHT 1.25 — [DOC "Slightly weak: 125%"]
MOD_NORMAL 1 — [DOC "Neutral: 100%"]
MOD_RESIST_SLIGHT 0.75 — [DOC "Slightly resistant: 75%"]
MOD_RESISTANT 0.5 — [DOC "Resistant: 50%"]
MOD_VERY_RESISTANT 0.25 — [DOC VeryResistant, the rung below Resistant]
MOD_IMMUNE 0 — [DOC Immune; their enum also carries Ignore]
  THE ORDER OF OPERATIONS IS THE MECHANISM, not the ladder. Resistance is applied
  PER DAMAGE TYPE, separately, and only THEN is armour applied to the total.
  [DOC "the resistances are applied separately to each damage type and then the
  armor is applied to the total damage"] That is why a split-damage weapon (the
  knife is pierce + slash) gets PARTIAL credit against a resistance instead of
  being shut out — and it is the single most portable idea in this document,
  because it turns "wrong weapon" from a wall into a tax.

--- DOC: ARMOUR IS A CURVE, NOT A SUBTRACTION ---
ARMOR_PIVOT_DIV 2 — [DOC if armour < damage/2, damage taken = damage - armour]
ARMOR_SQUARE_DIV 4 — [DOC otherwise damage taken = damage^2 / (4 x armour)]
  So armour subtracts while it is small relative to the hit, and goes quadratic
  once it is large — which means it ASYMPTOTES and never reaches zero. You can
  always be hurt. That is a deliberate anti-invulnerability shape.

--- DOC: BACKSTAB, the positioning multiplier ---
BACKSTAB_DEFAULT 3 — [DOC "most weapons have a 3x multiplier"]
BACKSTAB_TWOHAND_CLUB 2 — [DOC "two-handed clubs only get a 2x multiplier"]
BACKSTAB_KNIFE 10 — [DOC "Knives, however, sport a 10x multiplier"]
BACKSTAB_MIN 2 — [DOC "ranges from 2x to 6x and is shown on the weapon tooltip"]
BACKSTAB_MAX 6 — [DOC the top of that same stated range; the knife at 10 is the
  documented outlier ABOVE it, which is itself a statement about the knife]
  THE KNIFE IS THE WHOLE ARGUMENT. Ten times. A knife is a bad weapon from the
  front and the best weapon in the game from behind, and that is expressed in ONE
  NUMBER with no special-case system attached. That is what a well-chosen
  multiplier buys you: a playstyle for free.
  Note also that the multiplier is ON THE TOOLTIP. The game tells you. Their
  "deeper understanding of mechanics" is not hidden knowledge, it is printed —
  which is worth remembering against our own legibility debt.

--- DOC: PARRY AND STAGGER, the spend multiplier ---
PARRY_SHIELD 1.5 — [DOC "Most shields in Valheim give a 1.5x Parry Bonus"]
PARRY_BUCKLER 2 — [DOC "except the Bronze Buckler, which offers a 2x bonus"]
PARRY_BOW 1.5 — [DOC "All bows also give a 1.5x bonus"]
PARRY_SWORD 2 — [DOC "melee weapons range from a 2x bonus for swords"]
PARRY_KNIFE 4 — [DOC "to 4x bonus for most knives"]
STAGGER_DMG_MULT 2 — [DOC "while staggered, the enemy is unable to act and takes
  double damage"; and "damage dealt to the enemy in a period of time after a
  successful parry ... is always 2x"]
STAGGER_LIMIT_PCT 40 — [DOC "a stagger rate of 40% of their max health"]
STAGGER_DECAY_PCT 20 — [DOC "Stagger generated decays at a rate of 20% of your
  stagger limit per second"]
  TWO SEPARATE ROADS TO THE SAME 2x, and that is the good part. You can grind an
  enemy into stagger by accumulating damage against the 40% limit, OR you can
  parry once and get it instantly. [DOC "Parrying an attack causes an instant
  stagger without contributing towards the stagger limit"] Patience or timing,
  same reward. And your DEFENCE choice (what you block with) sets your OFFENCE
  ceiling, which collapses two decisions into one item.

--- DOC: SKILL RAISES YOUR FLOOR ---
The documented player skill factor is min([0.25 ... 0.55] + 0.006 x skill level,
1.0) — a random roll between a floor and a ceiling, both of which climb. Minimum
damage goes from 25% of the weapon at skill 0 to 85% at 100; maximum goes from
55% at 0 to 100% at 75 and above. Each term of that formula, one per line:
SKILL_FLOOR_AT_0 0.25 — [DOC the floor term at skill 0: 25% of weapon damage]
SKILL_CEIL_AT_0 0.55 — [DOC the ceiling term at skill 0: 55% of weapon damage]
SKILL_PER_LEVEL 0.006 — [DOC the per-level coefficient on BOTH ends]
SKILL_CAP 1 — [DOC the min(..., 1.0) clamp; you never exceed the printed damage]
SKILL_MAX_LEVEL 100 — [DOC the skill cap, where the floor reaches 85%]
SKILL_CEIL_MAXED_AT 75 — [DOC "maximum ... 100% of weapon damage at skill level
  75 and above" — the level past which extra skill buys no power at all]
  THIS IS THE BEST IDEA ON THE PAGE and it is almost invisible. Your damage is a
  RANDOM ROLL between a floor and a ceiling, and levelling raises BOTH — but the
  ceiling is finished at level 75. So the last quarter of mastery buys you no
  extra power at all, only CONSISTENCY. Mastery means you stop getting robbed.
  That is a completely different feel from "+1 damage per level", it is much
  closer to how getting good at something actually feels, and it costs one
  formula.

=============================================================================
CONTENT — theirs, and honestly labelled
=============================================================================
The six weapons on the page (sword, club, knife, spear, atgeir, bow) use
Valheim's own CLASS names, which are SOURCED from the enum. Their per-weapon
DAMAGE SPLITS are REPRESENTATIVE early-game profiles, not exact copies of a
specific tier's stat block — the exact per-item values live in the game's asset
bundles behind the 403s listed above. The page's job is the SHAPE of a profile
(this weapon is blunt, this one splits pierce and slash), and the teardown says
so instead of implying each number is a measurement.

Their secondary attacks, as documented, because the pattern matters even though
the page does not implement all of them: [DOC] atgeir spins in a circle hitting
everything around you and knocking it down; swords stab with a long windup for
more damage; spears THROW and are retrievable; knives lunge forward. EVERY class
has one, on its own input, with its own stamina cost. So a class changes HOW you
fight, not just how much you hit for.

THE FOUR CREATURES are Valheim's own, chosen because their documented matchups
cover the three physical types cleanly. Their hp and armour are representative.

=============================================================================
WHAT IS NOT IMPLEMENTED (so nobody reads this page as complete)
=============================================================================
- The seven elemental types. The page models the three PHYSICAL types, because
  those are the ones that make weapon CHOICE a decision. Fire/frost/lightning/
  poison/spirit are named and tagged and not modelled.
- Stamina per swing. Real Valheim gates aggression with stamina; the page is
  turn-based, so a turn IS the cost. Named as a divergence, not an omission.
- Secondary attacks. Documented above, not implemented. The atgeir sweep in
  particular is a real AoE mechanic and modelling it would be a second page.
- Stagger by ACCUMULATION. The page implements the parry road to stagger; the
  40%-of-max-health grind and its 20%/sec decay are recorded as numbers and not
  simulated, because the decay is per-SECOND and the page has no seconds.
- Blocking (as opposed to parrying), shield-specific block values, and the
  Blocking skill.
- Weapon durability, two-handed vs one-handed shield slots, and the real
  movesets.
- Skill LOSS on death, which their Skills.OnDeath / LowerAllSkills path handles
  (visible at Skills.cs:124-128). Relevant to a roguelite and deliberately left
  for Paolo.
- BOHEMIA'S WEAPON TYPES, RESISTANCE TABLE AND POSITIONAL DAMAGE. All three are
  [PENDING Paolo], all three belong to the COMBAT lane, and this page invents
  none of them. NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL.

THE ARITHMETIC, CHECKED
=============================================================================
club (28 blunt) into a skeleton (2x blunt, 0 armour), skill 0, roll pinned max:
  28 x 2 = 56 typed;  x 0.55 = 30.8;  no backstab;  0 armour -> 30.8
spear (26 pierce) into the same skeleton (0.5x pierce):
  26 x 0.5 = 13 typed;  x 0.55 = 7.15 -> 7.15
  SO THE RIGHT WEAPON IS A 4.3x SWING ON THE SAME ENEMY, before you have moved
  a single step. That is the knowledge lever, measured.
knife (12 pierce + 6 slash) into a seeker (2x pierce, 1x slash, 20 armour),
skill 0, roll max, FROM BEHIND:
  12 x 2 = 24, 6 x 1 = 6, typed 30;  x 0.55 = 16.5;  x10 backstab = 165;
  armour 20 < 165/2 so subtract -> 145
  From the FRONT: 16.5, and 20 armour >= 16.5/2 so 16.5^2/(4x20) = 3.4.
  145 vs 3.4 — A 42x SWING FROM WALKING AROUND SOMETHING. That is the
  positioning lever, and it is the half of his sentence Bohemia does not have.
skill floor/ceiling: level 0 -> 25%-55%; level 50 -> 55%-85%; level 75 ->
  70%-100% (ceiling done); level 100 -> 85%-100% (floor only). Confirmed against
  min(0.25 + 0.006L, 1) and min(0.55 + 0.006L, 1).
armour curve: a 10-damage hit into 3 armour -> 3 < 5, so 10-3 = 7. The same hit
  into 20 armour -> 20 >= 5, so 100/80 = 1.25. Never zero.
