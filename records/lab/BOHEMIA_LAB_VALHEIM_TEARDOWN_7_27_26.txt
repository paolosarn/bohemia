THIS IS A MODEL, NOT A MEASUREMENT
----------------------------------
Say it before anything else, because it is the difference between this row and
every other row in the lab.

NO SOURCE. Valheim's logic ships as a compiled Unity assembly
(valheim_Data/Managed/assembly_valheim.dll). It is not decompilable here: the
binary is behind Steam, this environment's network policy reaches
raw.githubusercontent.com and almost nothing else, and a DLL is not a text file
you can read directly the way Stardew's decompiled C#, Zomboid's shipped Lua and
A Dark Room's JavaScript all were. Every probe is recorded below so nobody
repeats them.

THE TWO CONSTANTS THAT ARE REAL SOURCE
--------------------------------------
ValheimPlus is a Harmony mod whose whole job is to overwrite vanilla values, so
its patches NAME them. Both files were fetched and read:
  raw.githubusercontent.com/Grantapher/ValheimPlus/master/ValheimPlus/...
  GameClasses/SE_Rested.cs        patches SE_Rested.UpdateTTL and
                                  SE_Rested.GetNearbyComfortPieces
  Configurations/Sections/PlayerConfiguration.cs:11
                                  restSecondsPerComfortLevel = 60
  Configurations/Sections/BuildingConfiguration.cs:9
                                  pieceComfortRadius = 10f
The radius is doubly confirmed: the transpiler in SE_Rested.cs skips its own work
when `config.pieceComfortRadius == 10f`, with the comment "expecting exact
constant" — i.e. 10f IS what vanilla compiled in, and the patch rewrites the
first float literal in GetNearbyComfortPieces to replace it.
It also names the real vanilla API surface, which is worth as much as the numbers:
  SE_Rested.m_TTLPerComfortLevel        the per-comfort-level seconds field
  SE_Rested.GetNearbyComfortPieces()    the 10 m sweep
  Player.EatFood()                      where food enters the stomach
  ItemDrop.ItemData.SharedData.m_food / m_foodStamina / m_foodEitr / m_foodBurnTime
                                        health, stamina, eitr, duration per food
(Player.cs:254, 281, 330-332 in the same repo.)

THE THREE MECHANICS
-------------------
1 FOOD    — three slots; each adds max health and max stamina and decays as it
            burns; an empty stomach leaves you weak, not dead.
2 RESTED  — 20 s of Resting by a fire under a roof, then +50% health regen and
            +100% stamina regen for a stretch.
3 COMFORT — one item per CATEGORY within 10 m sets the comfort level, and the
            comfort level IS how many minutes Rested lasts.

CONTENT vs MECHANISM
--------------------
MECHANISM (portable, and the reason this page exists): the three-slot stomach,
buff value decaying with the food's remaining time, the top-up-below-half rule,
a timed idle ritual that grants a buff, and above all COMFORT — a
highest-per-category sum inside a radius that converts furniture into buff
duration.
CONTENT (theirs, documented, NOT Bohemia canon): every food name and number,
every comfort item and its category, the 8-minute base, the 20-second ritual and
the 17 cap. Bohemia's survival numbers are Paolo's to rule after he plays this.

THE COMFORT TABLE (all [DOC], and the CATEGORY rule is the mechanism)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Only the highest-value item in a category counts. Duplicates do nothing. That is
what makes decorating a real decision instead of a stacking exploit.
  fire     campfire 1, hearth 2
  shelter  a roof over the spot, 1
  bed      bed 1, dragon bed 2
  chair    stool / bench / sitting log, 1
  table    table 1, round table 2 (long heavy table 2)
  banner   any banner, 1
  rug      any carpet or rug, 1
  maypole  1 (seasonal, and the reason 18 exists)

WHAT THE MEASURED PAGE PROVES (gates/lab_gate.js, live, in a real browser)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Comfort 1 gives exactly 8:00 of Rested; every category added gives exactly one
  more minute; a SECOND rug adds nothing; a hearth replacing a campfire adds one.
- Rested arrives at exactly 20 s of standing still at the fire under a roof, and
  not at 19.
- Three foods stack their health and stamina onto 25 / 50; the fourth is refused;
  a food at 60% remaining cannot be topped up and the same food at 40% can.
- A food's contribution SHRINKS as it burns: max health falls as the bar drains.
- An empty stomach never kills you: ten minutes in the meadow with nothing eaten
  leaves you alive at 25 max health.
- The mountain is survivable fed and rested, and not survivable empty.

WHAT IS NOT IMPLEMENTED
-----------------------
- EITR, the whole magic axis. m_foodEitr is real and named; nothing here uses it.
- The +50% XP from Rested, and skill levels entirely.
- COMBAT. NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL. The mountain hurts you with cold; there is
  nothing up there to fight, and the failure state is a blackout at the fire, not
  a corpse.
- Wet, Cold (as distinct from Freezing), Burning, Smoked, and every other status
  effect; fire fuel burning out; buildings, stability and crafting stations;
  hunger as a separate axis (Valheim has none — food is the axis).
- The real vanilla food-decay CURVE. The page models the contribution as linear
  in remaining time. Vanilla's exact curve is inside the DLL.
- The real base regen rates, for the same reason (see the three declared keys).
- Sitting. In Valheim sitting speeds up Resting; here standing still at the fire
  under a roof is the whole ritual.
