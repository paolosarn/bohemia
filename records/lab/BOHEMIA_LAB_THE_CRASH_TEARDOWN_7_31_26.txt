THE FINDING
=============================================================================
BOTH GAMES MODEL A UTILITY *VANISHING* ON A TIMER. REALITY MODELS IT GETTING AN
*OWNER*.

Zomboid switches the power off on day 14 and that is the end of the story: the
grid is a boolean that flips once and never flips back. What actually happened in
Lebanon is that the state grid fell to about FOUR HOURS A DAY and private
generator cartels sold you the other SIX AND A HALF, by the ampere, for OVER A
HUNDRED DOLLARS A MONTH -- priced in hard currency while wages were in the money
that was busy dying. The utility was not deleted. It was privatised at gunpoint.

That is strictly the better mechanic, because a boolean is not a decision and an
owner is. And BOHEMIA ALREADY HALF-HAS IT: CLUSTERED POWER (12% lit, OWNED, the
NETWORK eerily perfect) and LIGHT=TERRITORY and "nobody patrols the dark". Those
laws were written as atmosphere. Lebanon is the evidence that they are ECONOMICS.
THE CLAIM THIS ROW MAKES: Bohemia's power law and Bohemia's economic collapse are
ONE LAW, and we have been treating them as two.

SECOND FINDING: EVERY CURVE IN A COLLAPSE FALLS ON A CLOCK YOU DO NOT CONTROL.
THE ONLY ONE THAT RISES IS THE ONE YOU BUILT WITH YOUR HANDS. The money, the
withdrawal cap, the state's hours of power, the price of an ampere -- all of them
move without asking you. Comfort is the single number on the page that only moves
when the player moves it. That gap IS the game, and it is why the fusion he named
actually fuses: the crash supplies the falling curves and Valheim supplies the
only rising one.

THIRD FINDING, and it is the cruellest thing on the list: THE FREEZE. Games model
poverty as an empty wallet. Reality's version is worse -- the money is RIGHT
THERE, it is yours, it is on a screen with your name on it, and you may have
sixty euros of it today. Nothing in either game models "you are rich and you
cannot reach it", and three separate real countries made it law.

CONTENT — whose is whose, stated plainly
=============================================================================
THE DEVALUATION CURVE is FITTED, not quoted, and this is the one derived number
on the page. Lebanon's real path was jagged (a black-market rate that ran far
ahead of every official one, then a step change in Feb 2024). The page fits the
constant monthly rate that takes 1507.5 to 89,500 over five years, which works
out to about 7% a month. That is honest as a SHAPE and dishonest as a HISTORY,
and the page's own code comment says so. Nobody should quote 7% as a Lebanese
figure.

THE STARTING BALANCE (20,000 local) and THE COMFORT COST LADDER (200 x the next
level) ARE MINE, and they exist only so the page can be pressed. They are not
proposals. Bohemia's prices belong to Paolo and the economy belongs to WORLD.

THE THREE CAPS are real laws from real countries and are quoted as such. The page
lets you switch between them because feeling 60/day next to 400/month is the
fastest way to understand that the CAP SHAPE matters more than the number.

THE ARITHMETIC, CHECKED
=============================================================================
Fitted monthly decay: (89500 / 1507.5)^(1/60) - 1 = 0.0700, so about 7.0% a month.
  Day 0:    1,507.5 to the dollar, money worth 100.0%
  Day 365:  3,400 or so, worth about 44%
  Day 1825: 89,500, worth 1.68% -- i.e. 98.3% gone, which matches the
            documented "more than 98 percent" to a decimal.
The freeze, under Lebanon's cap ($400/month) against a 20,000 balance: 50 months
  to get your own money out, during which the money loses ~97% of its value. YOU
  CANNOT WIN THE RACE. That is not a balance bug, that is the actual finding, and
  it is what "essentially frozen" means in a sentence a player can feel.
The cartel, per ampere: $100/mo / 5A = $20 per ampere-month. In local money on
  day 0 that is 20; on day 365 about 45; on day 1825 about 1,188. The SAME five
  amperes, sixty times the wage cost, for the same six and a half hours of light.
Comfort: 480s at 1, 720s at 5, 1,020s at 10, 1,440s at 17. Rises only when the
  player spends. Falls never.
Zomboid's day 14 against Lebanon's five years: THE TWO CLOCKS ARE THREE ORDERS OF
  MAGNITUDE APART, and that gap is the most useful thing on this page. A game
  needs the collapse legible inside one run; history takes half a decade. Whatever
  Bohemia picks, it is picking a compression ratio, and it should pick it on
  purpose rather than by accident.
