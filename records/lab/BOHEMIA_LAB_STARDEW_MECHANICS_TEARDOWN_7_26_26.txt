MECHANIC 1 — FISHING            (Menus/BobberBar.cs)
==============================================================================
THE WHOLE MINIGAME IN ONE SENTENCE: one number grows while the fish is inside a
bar you are juggling against gravity, and shrinks while it is not.

KEY               VALUE   SOURCE               WHAT IT MEANS
BAR_TRACK         568     BobberBar.cs:145     the whole water column, px
BAR_BASE_H        96      BobberBar.cs:90      your bar at fishing level 0
BAR_H_PER_LEVEL   8       BobberBar.cs:90      + 8px per fishing level
BOBBER_START      508     BobberBar.cs:146     the fish starts near the bottom
BOBBER_BOTTOM     532     BobberBar.cs:282     and is clamped there
BOBBER_TOP        0       BobberBar.cs:284
CATCH_START       0.3     BobberBar.cs:74      distanceFromCatching at the cast
CATCH_GAIN        0.002   BobberBar.cs:367     +1/500 per tick, fish in the bar
CATCH_LOSS        0.003   BobberBar.cs:398     -3/1000 per tick, fish outside
BAR_GRAVITY       0.25    BobberBar.cs:292     bar accelerates DOWN, always
BAR_LIFT          0.25    BobberBar.cs:292     and UP while you hold the button
BAR_IN_DAMP       0.6     BobberBar.cs:297     BOTH are x0.6 with the fish inside
BAR_BOUNCE        0.6667  BobberBar.cs:312     -2/3 of speed at either end
FISH_SMOOTH       5       BobberBar.cs:273     speed chases accel over 5 ticks
FLOAT_ACCEL       0.01    BobberBar.cs:267     floaters/sinkers drift, forever
FLOAT_MAX         1.5     BobberBar.cs:267     clamped
SIZE_DROP_MS      800     BobberBar.cs:129     1 inch lost per 800ms of struggle
FISH_ABOVE        16      BobberBar.cs:285     the fish's extent above its pos
FISH_BELOW        12      BobberBar.cs:285     and below: a 28-unit body

THE FOUR DESIGN FACTS UNDER IT:
1. THE ENTIRE FISHING SKILL TREE IS BAR HEIGHT. Level 0 gives you a 96px bar in
   a 568px column (17%). Level 10 gives you 176px (31%). There is no other
   mechanical reward for levelling fishing. The skill curve IS one number.
2. GAIN AND LOSS ARE NEARLY EQUAL (0.002 vs 0.003). Losing is only 1.5x faster
   than winning, so a fight is long, recoverable, and always feels close. Make
   loss 3x and the same minigame becomes rage.
3. THE BAR IS DAMPED WHILE YOU ARE WINNING (x0.6 both directions,
   BobberBar.cs:297). Being on target makes the controls calmer. That is the
   feel of "gripping" the fish, and it is one multiplier.
4. DIFFICULTY IS A SINGLE 0-110 SCALAR that feeds THREE separate things: how
   often the fish picks a new target (:259), how far it picks (:264), and how
   fast it eases there (:272). One authored number per fish, three behaviours.
   Plus five motion TYPES: mixed / dart / smooth / sinker / floater (:100-122).

CONTENT (ours): the fish table (name, difficulty, motion, size range). The real
values live in Content/Data/Fish. Our stand-ins keep the real ranges: Carp 15,
Sardine 30, Pike 60, Catfish 75, Octopus 95 (sinker), Legendary 110.

THE TOLERANCE, which is the number that actually decides whether fishing is fun:
the fish's own body is 28 units tall (bobPos-16 .. bobPos+12, :285) and it must
fit ENTIRELY inside the bar. At level 0 that is 96-28 = 68 units of slack in a
568-unit column, 12%. At level 10 it is 176-28 = 148, 26%. THAT is the skill
curve, stated as the thing a player feels.

DECLARED DIVERGENCE: the master's inBar test carries a -32 sprite-origin offset
between the bar's coordinate space and the fish's. We have no sprites, so the
offset is dropped and the fish is DRAWN at exactly the extent that is TESTED.
The mechanism (a 28-unit fish inside a 96-unit bar) is unchanged.

TWO PORTING BUGS THIS FOUND, BOTH ONLY VISIBLE BY MEASURING, both now gated:
  (1) The `else` that re-rolls the fish's target must ALSO run when the target
      is -1 (its resting state, :276). With an extra `target !== -1` guard on
      that else, a resting fish keeps its last velocity forever, drifts into the
      floor and parks there — and every fish above difficulty 30 becomes
      uncatchable. Measured: pike 0/12 before, 12/12 after.
  (2) Copying the -32 offset into a page that has no sprite offset made the
      drawn bar and the tested bar 32 units apart, so the picture lied about
      whether you were winning. Fixed by dropping the offset, not by nudging the
      art.
MEASURED CATCH RATES with one fixed controller and a SEEDED rng, at level 0.
THE SEED IS NOT A CONVENIENCE, IT IS THE GATE'S CORRECTNESS: the lab gate went
red once inside the full suite and green standalone immediately after, which is
the signature of a coin flip, not a bug. The catch checks drive the REAL
minigame, and against Math.random even a carp escapes occasionally. Both live
rows are now seeded end to end and unseeded when they finish, so a red means a
regression and never bad luck:
  carp 15 -> 12/12 · sardine 30 -> 12/12 · pike 60 -> 12/12 · catfish 75 -> 10/12
  octopus 95 -> 0/12 · at level 10 the same octopus -> 4/12
That last pair is the whole fishing skill tree, measured: nothing changed except
the bar got 80 units taller.

NOT IMPLEMENTED, ON PURPOSE: treasure chests, bait, tackle, the quality roll,
the trap/cork/lead bobbers (their branches are read and cited in the code
comments but each is one modifier on the rules above).

MECHANIC 2 — FARMING            (Crop.cs + TerrainFeatures/HoeDirt.cs)
==============================================================================
THE WHOLE SYSTEM IN ONE SENTENCE: a crop advances one day ONLY if its soil was
watered at the day rollover; being out of season outdoors kills it.

KEY               VALUE   SOURCE               WHAT IT MEANS
WATERED           1       Crop.cs:522          the ONLY state that advances a crop
DRY               0       HoeDirt.cs:607       soil dries every morning
SPEEDGRO          0.10    HoeDirt.cs:387       Speed-Gro removes 10% of total days
SPEEDGRO_DELUXE   0.25    HoeDirt.cs:389
SPEEDGRO_HYPER    0.33    HoeDirt.cs:391
RETAIN_BASIC      0.33    HoeDirt.cs:606       chance the soil stays wet overnight
RETAIN_QUALITY    0.66    HoeDirt.cs:606
RETAIN_DELUXE     1.00    HoeDirt.cs:606       always

Crop.newDay (Crop.cs:509-535) verbatim in shape:
  if (outdoors && (dead || !seasonsToGrowIn.Contains(season)))  Kill();
  else if (state == watered) {
     dayOfCurrentPhase = min(dayOfCurrentPhase + 1, phaseDays[currentPhase]);
     if (dayOfCurrentPhase >= phaseDays[currentPhase] && currentPhase < last)
        { currentPhase++; dayOfCurrentPhase = 0; }
  }
Growth is a LIST OF PHASE LENGTHS, not a total. That is why fertilizer can shave
days off the early phases and why a crop's art changes at uneven intervals.
After harvest, a regrowing crop sets fullyGrown and counts regrowAfterHarvest
DOWN (Crop.cs:246-248) — the same field, run backwards.

THE FOUR DESIGN FACTS UNDER IT:
1. FORGETTING TO WATER IS NOT PUNISHED, IT IS WASTED. No damage, no death: the
   day simply does not count. The cost is TIME, and time is the currency.
2. THE SEASON IS THE ONLY HARD KILL. Content, not mechanism: a list of seasons
   per crop. Miss the window and the whole plot dies at the rollover, at once.
3. FERTILIZER BUYS TIME TWO DIFFERENT WAYS and they are not interchangeable:
   Speed-Gro removes a PERCENTAGE OF TOTAL GROWTH DAYS at planting time
   (HoeDirt.cs:382-400 computes it once), while Retaining Soil buys you a
   CHANCE TO SKIP A CHORE. One is power, the other is convenience.
4. EVERYTHING RESOLVES AT SLEEP. Nothing grows while you watch. The whole
   farming loop is: spend the day, then find out. That is the same shape as
   TIME IS SPENT BY ACTIONS.

CONTENT (ours): the crop table. Real shape (phase lists + regrow + season), our
numbers.

MECHANIC 3 — MARRIAGE           (Farmer.cs + NPC.cs + Friendship.cs)
==============================================================================
THE WHOLE COURTSHIP IN ONE SENTENCE: one integer, fed by rationed gifts and a
free daily hello, drained every day you ignore them, against a ceiling that only
moves when you commit.

WEDDING_DELAY      3       NPC.cs:1095         worldDate.TotalDays += 3

Farmer.resetFriendshipsForNewDay (Farmer.cs:2540-2563) verbatim in shape, and
note the two penalties STACK — this is not an if/else chain all the way down:
  spouse and you did not talk  -> -20
  dating, under 2500, no talk  -> -8
  talked                       -> clear the flag, no further decay
  else, if (not-undated and < 2500) or (undated and < 2000)   -> -2 ON TOP
  so an ignored partner below 2500 actually loses 10 a day, and an ignored
  spouse below 2500 loses 22.

THE FIVE DESIGN FACTS UNDER IT, AND THIS IS THE GOOD PART:
1. THE CEILING IS THE STORY, AND IT IS A HARD WALL. Utility.cs:2901
   GetMaximumHeartsForCharacter returns 10 for a villager, but EIGHT for a
   DATABLE one you are not dating, and changeFriendship clamps at
   (maxHearts + 1) * 250 - 1. So an undated love interest hard-caps at 2249
   points and NO amount of gifting moves it. You cannot grind past a
   relationship, you have to ACT — accept the bouquet, and the ceiling becomes
   10 hearts (2749); marry, and it becomes 14 (3749). Progress gates are STATE
   CHANGES, not point totals. A second, softer wall sits under it: the -2/day
   neglect decay stops applying to an undated villager once they pass 2000
   (Farmer.cs:2557), so the game stops punishing you for parking there.
2. GIFTS ARE RATIONED, NOT PRICED. One a day, two a week. The limit is what
   makes a gift mean something; without it, the whole system is a money check.
   Birthdays bypass the ration AND pay x8, which is why players write the
   calendar down. One number turned a date into an event.
3. IGNORING SOMEONE COSTS MORE THE CLOSER YOU ARE. -2 stranger, -8 dating, -20
   spouse. The mechanic gets HARDER after the wedding, which is the exact
   opposite of how a reward system is usually built, and it is why the marriage
   keeps being a thing you do instead of a trophy you won.
4. TALKING IS FREE, SMALL, AND DAILY (+20, once). It exactly cancels ten days of
   stranger decay. The floor of the system is "show up".
5. A HATED GIFT IS -40, HALF a loved gift's +80. Getting it wrong is cheap
   enough to risk and expensive enough to learn from.

CONTENT (ours): the gift taste lists, birthdays, and who is datable. Real shape,
our stand-ins.

NOT IMPLEMENTED, ON PURPOSE: heart events, dialogue, jealousy (NPC.cs:1545 has a
real 0.3 - luck chance of a dating partner reacting badly to you gifting a
rival), children, divorce.
