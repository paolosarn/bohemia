=============================================================================
THE FINDING
=============================================================================
TEN YEARS COLD, MONEY IS NOT THE CURRENCY. STANDING IS.

Once money is banned as a system (clause 2) and the utilities are dead everywhere
(clause 3), there is nothing left to trade WITH. What is left is WHO OWNS THE
WORKING THING AND WHETHER THEY WILL DEAL WITH YOU. That is not a workaround for
the ban -- it is a better game, and New Vegas is the proof it works.

AND THE REASON IT WORKS IS ONE STRUCTURAL DECISION:
FAME AND INFAMY ARE TWO SEPARATE COUNTERS THAT NEVER CANCEL.
Not a slider from hated to loved. Two numbers, tracked apart, and your TITLE is
read off BOTH. That is why New Vegas has words no other game has -- WILD CHILD,
DARK HERO, SOFT-HEARTED DEVIL -- titles that only exist because the game refused
to average you out. A single slider can express "how good are you". Two counters
express "what kind of person are you", which is a different and much better
question. ONE SLIDER IS A STAT. TWO COUNTERS IS A PERSON.

SECOND FINDING, and it is the city-builder half he asked for:
YOU DO NOT BUILD TO GET RICH. YOU BUILD TO BECOME SOMEBODY THEY HAVE TO DEAL
WITH. With money banned, a building cannot pay you -- so what it does is make you
WORTH DEALING WITH, and under the CENTURY RULE that compounds across three acts.
That is the join he was pointing at with "THAT ALSO DOUBLES AS A CITY BUILDER":
the builder is not a separate mode bolted to an RPG, it is HOW YOU EARN STANDING.
A spreadsheet city-builder asks "what does this produce". This asks "who does this
make me".

THIRD FINDING: THE SAME DEED IS WORTH WILDLY DIFFERENT AMOUNTS.
Their per-faction thresholds are not close to each other -- the Brotherhood
accepts you at 3, the Legion idolises you at 100, a spread of 33x. A small,
insular, desperate outfit is CHEAP to impress; a huge bureaucratic one barely
notices. That is how you make a small faction matter without buffing it, and it
costs one number per faction.

THE ARITHMETIC, CHECKED
=============================================================================
The spread: Legion idolized 100 / Brotherhood idolized 20 = 5x on idolized, and
  Legion accepted 15 / Brotherhood accepted 3 = 5x on accepted. Across the whole
  set, dearest-over-cheapest idolized is 100/20 = 5.0x. The 33x figure people
  quote is Legion-idolized-over-Brotherhood-accepted (100/3), which is the more
  honest way to feel it: the cheapest door in the game costs 3 and the dearest
  costs 100.
The mixed axis, reachable in play: "work both sides" gives +6 fame and +6 infamy
  to both NCR and Legion. Four presses = 24 fame, 24 infamy on each. On NCR that
  is fame tier LIKED (>= 40? no -- 24 is ACCEPTED) with infamy over SHUNNED (15),
  so the title reads SOFT-HEARTED DEVIL. Eight presses = 48/48: fame LIKED (>=40)
  and infamy still under VILIFIED (50), so DARK HERO. Nine presses tips infamy
  past 50 and it becomes WILD CHILD. THE TITLE CHANGES THREE TIMES WITHOUT EITHER
  NUMBER EVER GOING DOWN, which is exactly what a single slider cannot do.
The idolized gate: fame 95 with infamy 5 is NOT truly idolized (infamy must be
  under 4). Fame 89 with infamy 0 is not either. You need both, and that is the
  point.
Comfort, second job: built 0 -> worth x1.0; built 4 -> comfort 5, rested 720s,
  worth x3.0. Every deed counts triple once you are somebody.
The century: 4 things built = 4 at act 1, 8 at act 2, 16 at act 3. Crude, mine,
  and flagged as such -- the real mechanism is his.
