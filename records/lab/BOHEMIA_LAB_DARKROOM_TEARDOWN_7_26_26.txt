WHY THIS GAME
-------------
Paolo killed the Project Zomboid loot emulation ("that was really bad and not
fun"), ruled that a found thing is a RESOURCE WITH A COUNT, that looting must be
FAST, and that the game should be MINIMALISTIC in that regard. Then: "we can try
it again except it could be faster. You could try it something else."

A Dark Room is the far end of that axis and it is not a guess: ten resources,
one scene per place, one tap to take a whole container. It is also the only
strong candidate whose ENTIRE SOURCE is legally readable, in JavaScript, the
language our engine is already written in.

THE FOUR MECHANICS
------------------
1 SCAVENGING — walk onto a place, one weighted branch, one loot roll, one tap.
2 HAULING    — everything weighs 1 unless the table says otherwise.
3 SUPPLIES   — a step is a spent action: it drinks, and every second step eats.
4 JOBS       — the whole village economy is one table, ticking all-or-nothing.

CONTENT vs MECHANISM
--------------------
MECHANISM (portable, and what we are here for): the two-tap scavenge, the
weighted branch, the {min,max,chance} roll, one-weight-by-default, the bag
ladder, the take-all clamp, a step costing a supply, and the all-or-nothing
worker tick.
CONTENT (theirs, not ours, and NOT canon for Bohemia): the ten resource names,
the ten job names, the prose of each scene, and every probability below. Our
resource kinds and yields are [PENDING Paolo] per clause (a) and (b) of the loot
law and NOBODY invents them here.

THE THREE FUNCTIONS THAT ARE THE WHOLE LOOT SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------
events.js:921-931, drawLoot — nine lines and looting is done:
    for(var k in lootList) {
      var loot = lootList[k];
      if(Math.random() < loot.chance) {
        var num = Math.floor(Math.random() * (loot.max - loot.min)) + loot.min;
        ...
That is it. A chance per resource, then a count. No containers-within-containers,
no per-item weights to check, no queue.

events.js:1281-1290, the weighted branch — the lowest key strictly above the
roll wins, so {0.25:'medicine', 0.5:'supplies', 1:'occupied'} is a 25/25/50
split written as cumulative bounds.

events.js:963-970 + Events.takeEverything — take-all clamps per row to
    Math.min(Math.floor(Path.getFreeSpace() / Path.getWeight(name)), numLeft)
so a full bag never BLOCKS the tap. The button just renames itself from "take
everything" to "take all you can" and gives you what fits. Nothing is refused.

WHAT THE MEASURED PAGE PROVES (gates/lab_gate.js, live, in a real browser)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A house is searched in exactly TWO taps: "go inside", then "take everything".
- The yield is always {resource: count} and never a named object.
- The scene prose that explains the amount is READ, never inventoried.
- With ten free units and a 20-cloth shop haul, the take-all takes 10 and the
  button says TAKE ALL YOU CAN — no refusal, no failure state.
- Walking 10 tiles drinks exactly 10 water and eats exactly 5 food.
- One gatherer produces 1 wood every 10 income ticks, and a charcutier with no
  meat produces NOTHING rather than going negative.

WHAT IS NOT IMPLEMENTED
-----------------------
- COMBAT. FIGHT_CHANCE and FIGHT_DELAY are recorded and unused; the 'occupied'
  house branch keeps its real loot table and its real prose and the squatter
  runs. Bohemia has NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL and a fight minigame is not what
  this page is testing.
- CRAFTING AND BUILDING (Room.Craftables), the trap drop table, the whole
  spaceship endgame, thieves, and the perk tree.
- The real 61x61 procedural map with STICKINESS-based terrain: this page uses a
  small authored 20x26 grid so the walk between places is phone-sized.
- Their supply-damage cadence: here, a step with no water costs 1 health. Theirs
  is more forgiving in the early game.
- THE VILLAGE BUILD TREE. Huts here are a fixed count; in theirs they are built.

HONEST NOTE ON THE RNG
----------------------
The gate seeds the page's RNG (window.LAB.seedRNG) before it measures anything
with a chance in it, and unseeds at the end. LAB-03 went red once inside the
full suite and green standalone because a 0.8-chance row is a coin flip; that is
a flaky gate, not a gate.
