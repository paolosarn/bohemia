## THE QUESTION
ECONOMY Q34 (ae4190b2) measured the valley: 16 hubs, 11 goods, ONE distinct price. Every
good costs one battery everywhere, and price() does not even take a hub. The row also
found that the biggest money in a real crash is not hoarding and not violence but being
the one who buys at one price and sells at another. That is arithmetically impossible
here. So the swing asks: in a real crash, HOW different are prices for the same thing,
how close together, and what does that mean for what WORLD builds first.

## WHAT THE RECORD SAYS
1. ARGENTINA 1988-1997 (Alvarez, Beraja, Gonzalez-Rozada, Neumeyer, Quarterly Journal
   of Economics 2019, on the product-level data under the consumer price index). When
   inflation goes up, the SPREAD of prices for the same good across stores goes up with
   it, elasticity about one third. At 500 percent a year the waste from price spread
   alone is about 8.5 percent of everything the country makes. Not the high prices: the
   spread between shops. That spread is a real, measured, first-order fact of a crash.
2. LEBANON 2020-2021. At least FOUR exchange rates lived at once: 1,507 official, 3,900
   on the central bank's platform, up to 15,000 on the street. Importers who qualified
   bought dollars at an 88 percent discount to the street and sold the goods at about
   eight times what they paid. Same tin of milk, one shop bought it cheap through the
   door, the next shop over paid street price. Who had the DOOR was the whole game.
3. VENEZUELA (already in Q34): about three hundred billion dollars moved through the gap
   between the subsidised rate and the street rate. The Tsar of Mercal got rich selling
   corn flour INTO the subsidised market, not holding it.

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR BOHEMIA, DECIDED
- THE SPREAD IS THE GAME, AND WE HAVE ZERO SPREAD. A price that is the same in every
  hub cannot be arbitraged, cannot make a smuggler, cannot make a route worth walking,
  and cannot make Paolo's "most realistic economic crash simulator" real. Two prices two
  blocks apart is the smallest thing that makes the valley an economy.
- ORDER: WORLD [every pocket] stays top (nobody can profit if everybody spends from one
  purse), and [two prices] goes directly under it. They are independent: a price can
  differ by hub before the purses split. If the lane can land [two prices] first, it
  should, because it is the smaller change (price() takes a hub; buy() already takes one
  and never reads it).
- THE RULE FOR THE FIRST VERSION, realism first: the spread follows WHO HOLDS THE HUB
  and how far the good travelled, not a random roll. A hub the holder controls buys
  through the door (cheap); a hub two factions away pays the street. Distance is
  already measured (nine metres a minute, 384 m a block). The 88 percent Lebanon number
  and the one-third Argentina slope are the premises WORLD tests; neither is a constant
  to type in.
