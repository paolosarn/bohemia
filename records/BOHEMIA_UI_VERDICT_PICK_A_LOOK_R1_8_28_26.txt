  THREE RULINGS INSIDE THAT, AND THEY ARE NOT TASTE, THEY ARE DIRECTION:

  1. THE SHADOW IS TOO STRONG. On RAISED, the drop under each letter was almost
     as dark and as wide as the letter itself, so the type read DOUBLED, not
     lit. A raised surface is read by a HINT of shadow. The shadow is evidence
     of the light, it is not a second copy of the letter.

  2. THE HOLOGRAM EFFECT IS TOO MUCH. Every round-three and round-four hologram
     put the scanlines, the flicker and the tear ON TOP OF THE WORDS. That
     degrades the thing you have to read, which is the one thing a UI may never
     do. LESS.

  3. AND HERE IS THE ONE THAT CHANGES THE BUILD: "MAYBE IT SHOULD BE MOVING IN
     THE BACKGROUND." The motion belongs BEHIND the letters, not through them.
     That is the whole answer to (2) as well: put the field on its own layer
     under the text and the letters stay perfectly crisp while the panel is
     still alive. Both notes are one instruction.

  WHY BOTH ANCHORS DYING IS NOT A FAILED ROUND: he killed the two he had
  already approved and kept two VARIANTS of them. That is not a reversal, it is
  a refinement -- the same taste from the three-round pattern (LIGHT and RAW
  MATERIAL, never ink or machinery), now with a tolerance on it. He is telling
  me how MUCH of the effect, not whether.
