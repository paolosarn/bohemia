## 1. THE HARD LIMITS (what the real science allows, 2026)
- DNA HAS A SHELF LIFE. The oldest DNA ever read is about 1 to 1.2 million years (Siberian mammoth teeth, 2021) and
  the oldest environmental DNA about 2 million (Greenland permafrost, 2022); models put the outer wall near 2.6
  million years, and only in permafrost. WARM ground kills it in tens of thousands. So: NOTHING OLDER THAN THE ICE
  AGE COMES BACK. No dinosaurs, no Titanoboa, no Megalania, no giant dragonflies. Anyone who says otherwise in the
  game is lying, and that is a good line for a mouth.
- WHERE DNA SURVIVES: permafrost (Siberia, Yukon), BONE-DRY CAVES (Nevada, Arizona, Patagonia: dung, hair, skin),
  cold deep lakes, museum skins and eggs (last 400 years), frozen tissue (the San Diego Frozen Zoo). TAR PITS
  RUIN IT (La Brea's sabre-tooths have almost no usable DNA); so does the tropics.
- YOU NEED A MOTHER. De-extinction is editing a living relative's genome and growing the animal in a surrogate
  of the right size. Mammoth -> elephant. Dire wolf -> grey wolf (done, April 2025: three pups from 13,000-year-old
  DNA). Thylacine -> a dunnart, with an artificial womb. Moa and dodo -> chicken germ cells. NO LIVING RELATIVE
  OF THE RIGHT SIZE, NO ANIMAL: ground sloths (the tree sloth is 5 kg), glyptodons, giant beavers, elephant birds
  are the hard cases; big cats and bears and horses and camels are easy.
- WHAT IS REAL TODAY: the dire wolf (2025), the woolly mammoth on track for 2028, thylacine, dodo, moa, bluebuck,
  quagga (back-bred), aurochs (back-bred as Tauros cattle), Pyrenean ibex (cloned 2003, lived minutes). By the
  crash years (2040 to 2045) a lab in the desert holding a dozen species is not fantasy; it is a decade of the
  current pace.

## 2. THE VALLEY'S OWN DEAD (the free story)
Tule Springs Fossil Beds, 20 miles north of the Strip, is one of the largest Ice Age finds on Earth (100,000 to
12,500 years ago): COLUMBIAN MAMMOTH, CAMELOPS (the American camel), DIRE WOLF, AMERICAN LION, SABRE-TOOTH CAT,
GIANT GROUND SLOTH, BISON, HORSE. Gypsum Cave, inside the valley, kept Shasta ground sloth dung and hair dry enough
that its DNA was read in 1998. The lab that made the beasts was in Las Vegas because the bones were in Las Vegas;
what broke out is what used to live here. Nobody has to invent a reason.

## 3. THE TEST FOR 'DOES WELL IN A HURT HUMAN CIVILIZATION' (the Mojave, no power, thin people)
Score 0-3 on each: HEAT AND WATER (Vegas summers, no pumps); DIET (generalist and scavenger beats specialist; garbage,
dogs, livestock, people, golf-course grass); BREEDING SPEED; NEAR-HUMAN LIVING (thrives at the edge of a city);
DANGER to a man on foot; DNA + MOTHER (can it really be made). The winners are the ones that would win in real life.

## 4. THE TABLE (first attempt, draft; WORLD and COMBAT refine)
STRONG (thrive; the road's beasts)
- DIRE WOLF (13 kyr; wolf surrogate; MADE 2025). Pack hunter and scavenger, breeds yearly, follows men, heat is fine
  at night. Score 15/18. THE Battle Brothers direwolf, and the most real beast in the game.
- CAVE HYENA / SPOTTED HYENA LINE (30 kyr Eurasian DNA; spotted hyena surrogate). The single best-adapted large
  animal to living beside humans that has ever existed: eats bone, garbage, corpses; matriarch packs; laughs at
  night. 16/18. The Battle Brothers hyena, better than theirs.
- CAMELOPS (12 kyr Nevada bones; dromedary surrogate). A desert camel, water-thrifty, eats creosote; useless as a
  monster, priceless as a BEAST OF BURDEN and a war mount; the caravans' animal. 14/18 (danger 1).
- PLEISTOCENE HORSE (700 kyr DNA read; horse surrogate). Realism check: feral horses ALREADY thrive in Nevada by
  the tens of thousands. Not a lab beast, a wild one, and the truest mount. 15/18.
- AUROCHS / FERAL CATTLE (back-bred; cattle). Vegas had ranches; a 900 kg bull in a dead suburb is a real killer of
  men and the valley's meat. 13/18.
MID (survive near water and ruin; the boss and the hunt)
- AMERICAN LION (12 kyr Nevada; lion surrogate). 350 kg, hunts alone or in pairs, needs big prey; with the
  mammoths and camels gone it hunts cattle, horses and people. Slow breeder. 12/18. A boss, not a road beast.
- SMILODON, the sabre-tooth (28 kyr; DNA poor from tar, better from cold sites; lion surrogate). Ambush, short
  legs, cannot chase; lives at the edge of the reservoir and the golf courses. 10/18. Rare boss.
- SHORT-FACED BEAR (12 kyr; brown bear surrogate). 900 kg scavenger with a nose that smells a carcass for miles;
  the Battle Brothers unhold in fur. Needs meat volume; hangs on where crews leave bodies. 11/18.
- SHASTA GROUND SLOTH (Gypsum Cave DNA; NO mother of the right size: the hard one, a lab stretch a decade past
  the others). Slow, plant-eater, a walking meat locker; a HUNT, not a threat. 8/18, but it is Vegas's own.
- COLUMBIAN MAMMOTH (Nevada; elephant surrogate; the real program). Needs 150 kg of forage and 100 litres of water
  a day: survives only at Lake Mead's shore and the dead golf courses. One herd, a landmark, the Ijirok. 9/18.
- HARRINGTON'S MOUNTAIN GOAT (Grand Canyon caves, dung DNA; goat surrogate). Cliffs and the Spring Mountains; food
  for crews, not a fight. 11/18.
- BISON ANTIQUUS (bison surrogate; trivial). Herd, water-bound, dangerous when pushed. 12/18.
WEAK HERE (real to make, wrong for the Mojave; possible imports from other labs)
- WOOLLY RHINO, CAVE BEAR, CAVE LION, IRISH ELK, STEPPE BISON, MUSK OX: cold animals, die in a Vegas July. Scores 5-7.
- THYLACINE (Australia; small, shy, eats small game): survives in the hills, a rumour more than a fight. 7/18.
- MOA, ELEPHANT BIRD, DODO: flightless, slow breeders, walking meat; the dodo is a joke and Bohemia can use a
  joke; the moa (3 m tall) is a real kick. 6-8/18.
- PASSENGER PIGEON, CAROLINA PARAKEET, GREAT AUK, STELLER'S SEA COW: no threat, some are food; the pigeon in the
  billions is a SKY EVENT (the feed would post it). 4-6/18.
- QUAGGA, TARPAN, BLUEBUCK: grazers, food and mounts. 9/18.
IMPOSSIBLE (say so in a mouth; the lie is a good line)
- Dinosaurs, Megalania, Titanoboa, Meiolania, terror birds, Deinotherium: too old, no DNA, ever.
NOT LAB ANIMALS BUT ON THE ROAD ANYWAY (the manager adds, rule 41)
- Vegas's EXOTIC ESCAPEES: the Strip's show tigers and lions, the private-zoo big cats, monitor lizards and pythons
