So there was NO POINT cooking a crowd: one exists, by the lane that owns rigs, at this
exact scale, and cooking a second one would have been this lane drawing something that
was already in the cupboard -- the mistake it made twice last round.

WHAT NOBODY HAD TOUCHED IS THE ROW'S FIRST FOUR WORDS: ONE REAL PLACE FIRST. The three
places are SIZES, not places. Camp, town and fortress are how big a settlement is; none
of them is ANYWHERE. His block is somewhere. It is the one he wakes on, it is the home
base of the third votes, and it is the only address in this game that is his.

SO THIS ROUND MAKES THE FOURTH PLACE, AND IT IS A PLACE AND NOT A SIZE:
  board  THE KERB: the board on its pole where the block's paper goes
  hall   YOUR HOUSE: the door you wake behind, your step under it,
  lot    THE DRIVEWAY: his own 7/28 wreck, still on it. SCAVENGE.
  stall  NEXT DOOR: his gate shut, his table out at the kerb
                 and the couch out front nobody moved
Four taps, and they are THE CAMP TIER'S OWN LIST on purpose, so RUN TWO wires a new
place by naming it and nothing else.

BUILT FROM THEIR OWN MACHINERY, NOT A SECOND SET OF PARTS. The ground, the houses at 45,
the stall, the board on its pole, the night and its measure are COMBAT TWO's own
functions, imported and called. The finish (rain down the walls, the ground worn where
people walk, what they dropped, the light out of a door) is this lane's own 10/10 pass,
imported. The wreck and both lamps are HIS 7/28 sprites, placed by their own placer.
FOUR THINGS ARE NEW: your step, your driveway, the gate next door, the couch.

*** AND THE LABELS COME OFF, WHICH IS THIS LANE'S OWN DEFECT FROM LAST ROUND, NAMED
*** HERE WITH THE EVIDENCE AND FIRST IN THE QUEUE FOR THE NEXT ROUND.
COMBAT TWO's manifest says, in their own words: "no text on the picture; name it only
when touched." Their tool's own law says each usable building must say what it is with
one object you can see from across the screen -- "NEVER A LABEL." On 10/10 this lane
painted CUTS, CLINIC, ROOMS, TRADE and NOTICES onto their six day sheets and six night
sheets as floating word boxes. That is three things wrong at once: it is text on the
picture, it is a SECOND name on top of the one RUN TWO's screen already gives on touch,
and rule 19 bans player-facing prose with no mouth and no portrait behind it. THIS place
carries no text at all and loads no font. The twelve sheets that carry it are the next
round's first job, done as its own thing so a correction to three other places is not
smuggled in under a fourth.
