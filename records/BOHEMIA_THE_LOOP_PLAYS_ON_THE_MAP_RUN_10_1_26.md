# THE LOOP PLAYS ON THE MAP (RUN, 10/1/26, [fold the loop], rules 52 and 58)

> **PAOLO 10/1:** *"I want the demo."*

RUN TWO built the whole Battle Brothers loop in one file (slices/BOHEMIA_LOOP.html: a placeholder
map, a placeholder 6x8 fight) and the settlement screen as a file this map plugs in. **The fold keeps
the real map** (his body, the cast, the towns, the phone's pixels, the icons he just ruled on) **and
the real fight** (which ends and brings him home in under a second), and brings the loop onto them.
Swapping the real map for the placeholder would have undone the two things he asked for this round.

## THE LOOP, AS THE DEMO PLAYS IT NOW

1. **Tap a town** (a faction's home base) and travel there on the speed pad.
2. **Arriving opens RUN TWO's settlement screen** over the map, named for the town, with his batteries:
   the hall, the board, the stall, the barber, the bar, scavenge.
3. **The BOARD gives a contract** (two at most, rule 51). LEAVE, and **a JOB is on the map**, six to
   ten blocks out: three of the cast under a red flag.
4. **Reach the JOB: the real fight**, through the one door every fight uses.
5. **Clear it: paid** (one battery, the purse, the bar). **Walk out: the job stays, unpaid**: the fight
   calls leaving a win, but the crew is still on the corner. **Lose: the job is lost.** Home either way.
6. Touching the job or town you are already standing at IS arriving (a walked-out job could not be
   retried from where he stood: the touch said "You are standing there").

Batteries spent in the settlement screen (stall, barber, hall) are settled against the same purse,
so there is one number.
