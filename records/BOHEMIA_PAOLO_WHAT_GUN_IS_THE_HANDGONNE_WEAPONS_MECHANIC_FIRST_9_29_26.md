## THE RULING (LOCKED; the same rule as the beasts, 42i): WEAPONS ARE MECHANIC FIRST, SKIN SECOND
Every Battle Brothers weapon CLASS is a rule (what it does to armour, shields, reach, fatigue, morale, how many
tiles it hits) and each rule gets the Bohemia weapon that FITS it: 'what type of gun fits their type of melee
weapon.' His example: Battle Brothers' handgonne (the Blazing Deserts gun: one shot, a cone that hits everything
in front, slow to reload) is our SHOTGUN or our BAZOOKA. The manager's answer: the handgonne is the SHOTGUN (a
cone, one shell, everybody in the spread; reach 1); the BAZOOKA is Battle Brothers' MORTAR (the city-states'
artillery: one shot, a long arc, a splash that hits a tile and its neighbours, useless close). Both in the table.

## WHAT THE MANAGER ADDS (rule 41): AMMUNITION IS WHY ACT ONE FEELS MEDIEVAL
In a real collapse guns are everywhere and BULLETS are not: factories stop, stocks run dry, reloading is a
craft. So act 1 is melee and bows and a few precious rounds; a bullet costs one (EVERYTHING COSTS ONE) and a man
with a working rifle and ten rounds is a rich man; by act 3 the reclaimed future makes bullets again. That is
the Battle Brothers 'medieval slight fantasy' feel he named (42, s5) explained by the economy, not by a rule.
TUNING [ammo] researches it; ECONOMY prices it; it is why bows, crossbows, machetes, fire axes, bats and spears
are real weapons on the board and not flavour.
