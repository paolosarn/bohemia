redo quotes the kill, notes are rulings, stale is dead, decide-then-correct, aesthetic and

spots, and two appendices (a minimal queue page, the bot's read-back). No project name, no
person's name anywhere in the export. Published as a page for his boss to open.

## ANSWERS TO HIS THREE QUESTIONS
- Ask me, not the UI chat: the UI chat knows one lane; the voting process and the laws are the
  manager's, and the UI chat is mid-round on his own asks.
- Blind spots are section 10 of the playbook (eleven).
- What he did not know he did not know: a web page cannot write a file, so verdicts leave by the
  clipboard and the bot commits them; the queue belongs where the decider judges, not in the
  public build; names stay the human's; a sound needs a tap.
