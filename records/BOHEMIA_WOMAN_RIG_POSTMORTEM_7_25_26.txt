WHAT ACTUALLY WENT WRONG (root causes, not excuses)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. I VERIFIED THE IDLE POSE. HE WATCHED THE ANIMATIONS.
   His words: "I go through the animations, it's all fucked up." Every
   verification pass I ran screenshotted a STANDING body. I attempted an
   animated capture once, the phase-forcing did not take, all frames came back
   identical -- and instead of fixing the harness I switched to analysing the
   rest data numerically and moved on. That is the VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE
   law satisfied in letter and broken in spirit. Everything he complained about
   in v3 lived in motion, which is exactly the surface I never actually looked
   at. THE SINGLE BIGGEST FAILURE HERE. If the harness for the surface that
   matters does not work, fixing the harness IS the task; it is not a detour
   from the task.

2. GREEN GATES NEVER PREDICTED HIS VERDICT, NOT ONCE.
   v1 shipped 14/14 green and was dogshit. v2 15/15 green, BBL. v3 15/15
   green, bug list. v4 19/19 green, deleted. The gates were real and they did
   catch real defects (a torso row silently lost, a coverage bug, strays) --
   but every invariant in them was one I chose, so they could only ever prove
   the thing matched my own model. A gate cannot rescue a wrong approach; it
   only makes a wrong approach regress-proof. When four consecutive green
   ships get rejected, THE GATES ARE MEASURING THE WRONG THING and adding a
   fifth check is the wrong reflex.

3. I HAD THE ANSWER IN HAND AND KEPT GOING ANYWAY.
   The research Paolo asked for came back with, plainly: NO STUDIO DERIVES A
   FEMALE BODY BY TRANSFORMING A MALE ONE. They author a separate body on a
   shared skeleton, or cross-fade hand-sculpted morphs. Pure squash/stretch is
   explicitly rejected because affine scale PRESERVES ratios and dimorphism IS
   ratios. I reported that finding accurately, called it a "ceiling", flagged
   it as pending his call -- and then spent the same turn tuning the transform
   to the research's own numbers, as if better parameters could clear a
   structural ceiling. I should have stopped at the finding and put the
   decision to him before touching another control point.

4. EVERY TUNE MANUFACTURED FRESH BUGS.
   A hip seam that read as the leg detaching. Strays by the butt. Misplaced
   nipples. A silently wrong garment contact law on every garment she wore. A
   cull that ate the neck. A cull that ate the torso apex. Each one was real,
   each one got root-caused and fixed -- but a pipeline that generates new
   defects on every parameter change is telling you the pipeline is wrong. I
   read each bug as a bug instead of as evidence.

5. THE SCOPE MULTIPLIED, WHICH IS THE REAL ARGUMENT FOR SLIDERS.
   A second authored body does not add one thing, it forks every downstream
   contract: garments, the shading/border laws, skeleton-anchored details,
   clip poses, the de-speckle pass. Three of the bugs above were literally
   "this code assumed there is only one body." One rig with parameters forks
   nothing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WORTH KEEPING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Real findings, paid for. All of these carry into the slider work:

- rigSkel(d): skeleton-anchored render details (nipples, shoulder blend, and
  the GARMENT CONTACT LAW) were reading hardcoded BAKED landmarks. That was a
  latent bug on ANY modified body. KEEP THIS FIX even after the strip.
- The ANATOMY border rule outlines any pixel with empty space above it, so any
  width change must keep part joins continuous or it draws a cut-line across
  the limb. This will bite the belly dial immediately.
- Parts genuinely OVERLAP in the source; per-row remaps must track membership
  per part or the torso silently loses rows and garments corrupt.
- A de-speckle cull must never touch thin art (the neck) and must never empty
  a row (the profile torso apex).
- seg()'s WIDTH LAW makes height dials and width dials fundamentally different
  machinery: bone-length changes ride the skeleton natively, width changes
  cannot ride it at all.
- Anthropometry, if the height dial wants grounding: body height varies ~0.92x
  where head size varies only ~0.96x -- scale the body, barely scale the head,
  or a shorter adult reads as a child.
