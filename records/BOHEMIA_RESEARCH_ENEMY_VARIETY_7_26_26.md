ANTI-BOREDOM LAWS (from the masters, recommended to ship with any roster):
- 70/20/10: ~70% ambient/vignette, ~20% interactive, only ~10% forced combat.
- STORYTELLER BUDGET, not dice: tension meter spends big when player is
  healthy + it's been quiet; small after hard fights; ~90s min gap.
- DISTRICT + DAY/NIGHT key the spawn table; no global spawns ever.
- RARE IS SACRED: spice events hard-capped, never twice a session.
- Same-opener-two-outcomes: some cries for help are real, some are traps.
- VARIETY = A DIFFERENT VERB, never a bigger HP bar. Enemy telegraphs ride
  the 120 BPM beat grid (rattle = 2 beats, drone LED on quarter notes).
- Defeat ≠ always death (Kenshi): robbery/humiliation as outcomes make an
  attack-anyone world sustainable.

ACT-1 CANDIDATE ROSTER (12, thumbable):
1 FERAL DOG PACK — swarm, circles, darts on off-beats; rout the alpha and
  the pack flees. Fights end by breaking morale, not HP bars.
2 COYOTE SHADOW — follows you a block, commits only if you're hurt/loaded,
  bolts when hit. A living low-HP warning, mostly never a fight.
3 RATTLESNAKE — static tile trap, 2-beat rattle telegraph, venom timer.
4 DESPERATE SCAVENGER SHAKEDOWN — pipe-armed, can be paid/scared/dropped.
  The moral mirror of attack-anyone.
5 TOLL CREW — raiders at a legible chokepoint want a cut not a corpse:
  pay / fight / detour.
6 THE SNATCHER — grabs an item and RUNS; beat-timed chase. Loss without death.
7 CRAZED WANDERER — attacks off-grid/syncopated, immune to intimidation,
  might just scream past. Incentives don't work on him.
8 BOUNTY SQUAD — spawns ONLY from your murders of peaceful people;
  competent, escalating. The systemic price of attack-anyone.
9 DEAD CASINO SECURITY BOT — antique still enforcing 2020s trespass rules on
  its old property; slow, lethal, perfectly learnable loop; alarm summons
  the district's current owners. (Real: K5 bots patrol Vegas casinos today.)
10 FACTION SPOTTER DRONE — patrols owned light, pings your position unless
  downed first (2-beat LED telegraph). Cut the light, ground the drones.
11 GHOST ROBOTAXI — empty cabs still crawling pickup loops; rare corrupted
  one bull-rushes on the beat; intact one = a spoofable ride.
12 PATROLS COLLIDE — two squads (or squad vs dog pack) fight EACH OTHER at a
  territory seam; join, third-party, loot after, or walk on.
