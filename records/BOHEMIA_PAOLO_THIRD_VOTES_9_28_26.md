4. THREE GENERATIONS, ALWAYS. DYNASTY's "three crises, not three generations" DOWN: "It will always be three
   generations." The acts are the generations; a crisis is inside an act, never instead of one.
5. TERRITORY IS NOT A MECHANIC; HOME BASES AND ROAMING PARTIES ARE. "Territory is convoluted... not a real gameplay
