THE PART THAT MATTERS MOST: A NUMBER SAID HE WAS WRONG, AND THE NUMBER WAS THE
THING THAT WAS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
First measurement taken was EDGE PARITY -- what share of the hair's left and
right silhouette edges sit on an odd pixel, i.e. on a position the old 56 grid
could not reach. Result: 50.9% across all 15 styles and all 8 facings.

50.9% is the score of a shape that fully uses the fine grid. Read literally it
says: the hair is already native, he is mistaken.

IT WAS TRUE AND IT WAS IRRELEVANT. The 8/21 sub-cell wobble pass gives the
OUTLINE a one-pixel step. The outline is not the haircut. THE SHAPE IS THE
INSIDE. The metric was measuring the one part of the hair that had already been
converted and reporting it as the whole.

Second measurement, of the INSIDE: runs of a single colour bounded on both sides
by a different hair colour -- a strand, a parting, a fade step, never the
silhouette edge.

WHAT WAS BUILT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
strandPass in genHair. A deterministic one-pixel parting running along the same
flow axis texSkip uses, so it rotates with the view exactly like his cornrows do
(his 8/2 ruling). Seeded off the style name, so an NPC never shimmers and the
same haircut is the same haircut every time it is drawn.

  - IT IS A SHADE, NOT A SKIP. Never removes a hair pixel, only darkens one to
    the style's own shadow tone. His first fade attempt was killed for showing
    skin through the hair; this cannot do that.
  - SPARSE ON PURPOSE. One line every four cells. A buzz cut is allowed to be
    nearly solid. The target is "can express a one-pixel mark", not "covered in
    stripes".
  - SOLID STYLES ONLY. Locs and braids already carry their own marks at his
    approved 2:1 ratio and are not touched.
  - S>1 ONLY. The 56 wardrobe is byte-identical; clothes_4x_gate's 1,744 pinned
    hashes still hold.

AND THE PART I ONLY FOUND BY LOOKING AT IT. The first cut drew the parting at a
FIXED phase, which is a ruled vertical bar down the crown. Shot it, looked at it,
and it read as a barcode -- straight into HOW HAIR AND SHAPE WORK (8/1, LOCKED):
"no straight lines, hair is little off shapes". The parting now DRIFTS by one
pixel, deterministically, in segments two cells long down the cross axis. One
pixel is exactly the point of his ruling: it is a move the old grid could not
make, and it is what turns a rule into a strand. VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE
caught this; no amount of reading the pass would have.
