## FINDING ONE, AND IT IS THE HARDEST NUMBER WE HAVE SEEN
DYNASTY researched what a hundred-hour game asks of a player and came back with
this, measured across mobile in 2024-25:
```
median session          5 to 6 minutes      (top quarter: 8 to 9)
sessions per day        about 4
day 1 retention         27% average
day 7 retention         3.4% to 3.9% median (top quarter 7 to 8%)
day 30 retention        1% to 7%
```
**We are building a hundred-hour game for a player who plays five minutes at a
time, four times a day, and who is 96% likely to be gone by the end of the week.**
A hundred hours at that rate is more than a year of real time. That is not an
argument against the hundred hours -- it is his game and the three generations are
the point -- but it settles HOW the hundred hours must be shaped, and nothing on
the board was shaped that way.

## WHAT THE CRAFT SAYS ABOUT SHORT SESSIONS IN A DEEP GAME
- **Bite-sized chunks with real stopping points.** The gameplay is broken into
  small actions with natural breaks, so a player can leave at a good moment rather
  than abandoning something half-done.
- **Ease them back in.** When a player returns, the action pauses and the game
  reminds them what they were doing. It does not assume they remember.
- **Never require the player to carry details in their head between sessions.**
  Long sequences that depend on remembering things are the thing that breaks.
Sources: [Mobile session design: easy in, easy out](https://mobilefreetoplay.com/mobile-session-design/),
[persistent sessions and saving ongoing progress (itch.io devlog)](https://itch.io/devlog/729629/introducing-persistent-sessions.amp),
[how browser games save progress](https://unihfygames.com/blog/how-browser-games-save-progress).

**WHAT WE ALREADY HAVE:** the save is hard (it survives seven hostile-browser
failure modes and carries the people), the home screen install IS the save, and
the day has a bed. **WHAT WE DO NOT HAVE:** a five-minute unit of play that is
worth something on its own, and anything that tells a returning player what they
were in the middle of.

## FINDING TWO: NOTHING IN THIS GAME CAN BE FORGIVEN
DYNASTY searched the standing web, the deeds module and the dynasty engine for
forgive, forgiven, settle, settled, absolve, pardon, spare, redeem. **Zero. Not
one.** Every function is about getting a deed INTO the world -- publish, sayWhy,
reach, hops, weight -- and there is no function anywhere that RESOLVES one. A deed
is written, it travels, it fades, and it is never settled.
That is a hole in the middle of the game's theme. Generation three is the ANGEL.
An Angel that cannot forgive, settle, spare or make anything right is a name on a
document. And it is not only a generation-three problem: a player who wronged
somebody in hour two has no way, ever, to make it right, which makes the standing
system a one-way ratchet toward being hated.
