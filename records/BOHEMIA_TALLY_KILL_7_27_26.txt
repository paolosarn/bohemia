BOHEMIA — THE TALLY IS DEAD (7/27/26)
POST-MORTEM. Killed by Paolo, unbuilt, at the pitch.

VERDICT, verbatim:
  "so just more 'data' for players to be proud of?"
  "this was terrible i hated this this was not a gameplay mechanic this is more
   data to be proud of no one gives a fuck"

WHAT IT WAS
The end-of-fight receipt, re-staged: instead of a silent sheet of numbers, the
score would assemble itself on the beat — kills clicking in on sixteenths, the
accuracy bonus on the next beat, the wager paying on the downbeat, each element a
semitone up the faction scale, accelerating into the grade. Pitched as the #1 item
in records/BOHEMIA_COMBAT_RESEARCH_THE_ADDICTIVE_SAUCE_7_27_26.md.

NOTHING WAS BUILT. It died at the pitch, which is the cheapest possible place for
it to die and the only good news in this file.

WHY IT WAS WRONG — HIS WORDS ARE THE DIAGNOSIS
"THIS WAS NOT A GAMEPLAY MECHANIC."

That is exactly right and it is the whole failure. A tally does not change a single
decision the player makes. You cannot play toward it, you cannot play against it,
you cannot get better at it. It happens AFTER the fight is over and the outcome is
already fixed. It is a presentation layer wearing a mechanic's clothes.

He asked for "interesting awesome addictive juicy sauce" for COMBAT. I gave him a
scoreboard animation.

THE REAL ROOT CAUSE — I RANKED BY RESEARCH, NOT BY PLAY
The pick-list had six items. Five of them were mechanics: THE BANK (a real
decision, every kill, with something to lose), ENEMY INTENT (changes how you read
the board before you commit), THE BEAT COUNTER (changes whether you can learn the
timing), THE DISTRICT REMEMBERS (changes what the world is), THE KILL CAM EARNS
ITS LENGTH (changes what the game rewards).

ONE of them was pure presentation. I put it at #1.

I ranked it first because the Balatro research was the most impressive thing I
read that day, not because it changed what anyone DOES. That is the same failure
as the 7/20 queued-actions grammar kill, whose graveyard line reads: "three
research rounds said queue; Paolo's hands said no -- research ranks candidates,
only PLAY decides."

Twice now, research has been allowed to outrank play in a ranking. That is the
pattern this file exists to break.

AND I HALF-KNEW. My own reply, one turn before the kill, said the tally "only
works if the number costs something" and that the receipt's payout is decorative
("real XP numbers PENDING Paolo" is in the source). I spotted the hole and then
kept the item at the top of the list anyway, hedged instead of pulled. A pitch
with a hole in it is a dead pitch; say so and drop it, do not ship it with a
caveat attached.

THE TEST THAT WOULD HAVE CAUGHT IT — now the lane's rule:

  *** IF IT DOES NOT CHANGE A DECISION THE PLAYER MAKES, IT IS NOT A MECHANIC. ***

Ask it BEFORE the item goes on a list, not after he reads it. Anything that
happens after the outcome is locked — a tally, a grade, a summary, a stat, a
badge, a receipt — is FEEDBACK. Feedback can be good, and Bohemia already has more
of it than it uses. But it is never the answer to "make combat more fun", and it
never leads a pick-list again.

WHAT IS DEAD AND WHAT IS NOT
DEAD, FINAL: the animated end-of-fight tally / score cascade, in any form. No v2,
no "what if it were shorter", nobody re-pitches the receipt as entertainment.

NOT DEAD: the receipt itself, which already exists and is fine as what it is — a
piece of paper at the end of the night. Untouched, unchanged, not the problem.

NOT DEAD: the other five items on the research doc. They were never judged. The
doc stays; item 1 is struck through and this file is the reason.

STANDING: laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_STOP_PRODUCING_7_26_26.md. This is the second
rejection of a scoring-presentation idea in this lane, so scoring presentation is
ENDED as a subject for the session. Nothing was built and nothing gets built.
