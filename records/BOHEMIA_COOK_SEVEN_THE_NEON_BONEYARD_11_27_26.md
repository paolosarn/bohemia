## WHAT THIS IS
COMBAT FOUR's neon boneyard: its sign shop or its dark gallery. Every one of its 15 pieces was a stand-in (the street board's sign, container,
heaps, wreck and dozer, the casino's screens, seats and bar, the tunnels' rack and camp, the garage's booth and core) on the mall's floor, and its
roof was drawn by the page. They are in the folder now under its names, and the boneyard lays them with no code changed:
- THE FLOOR (boneyard_floor_0.webp, floor.floor and floor.ring, both rooms): the hall's sealed slab with PAINT SPILLED ON IT IN EVERY COLOR A SIGN
  EVER WAS: pools where a can went over and their splashes, drip trails, a roller's track, footprints walked out of a pool, in rust, mustard,
  white and a teal of its own (nb_teal, the garage paints' make of ground); tube glass ground into it; every mark inside its tile.
- THE PIECES (boneyard_art.json's kit, COMBAT FOUR's 15 names): A GIANT LETTER (nb_letters: three channel letters stood in a row, C A S, and two
  taller behind, N O; THE I IS MISSING; their faces faded rust, mustard, teal and bone, every socket empty; so a run of L spells a dead
  casino's name across the floor). A DEAD SIGN ON END (nb_signs: a tall blade, a round-topped panel, a shield, a pole sign's pole, a star and a
  long black panel with a word in dead tube, stood in rows like headstones, their dead tubes still on their faces). RACKS OF SIGN FACES
  (nb_faces: two A-frame racks a tile long, faces leaning on both sides near to the roof). A SIGN FACE ON SAWHORSES (nb_sawhorses: as long as a
  bus and waist high, a tile long so SSSSSS is one face as long as six houses; its letters masked out and half painted, a can and a roller left
  on it). A RACK OF TUBE GLASS (nb_tubes: two racks of new sticks, a carton, sticks dropped and broken). A BENDER'S BENCH (nb_bench: two benches,
  the ribbon burners cold, A HALF BENT TUBE STILL IN THE CLAMP over its pattern paper, gas cylinders, the bombarder and its gauges). A SIGN CAN
  (nb_cans: one on its side with its letter mounts, one on its face, one on end). TRANSFORMERS (nb_transformers: three heaps, the loose ones,
  cable in coils). A FALLEN ARROW (nb_arrow: nine metres of it lying on the floor, still pointing, its sockets empty; a smaller one broken off).
  A BULB RAIL (nb_bulbs: a marquee's rails crossed in a pile, a corner of its frame, a carton split and sixty bulbs rolled out). A SCISSOR LIFT
  (nb_lift: folded down, its charger, a pallet of sign parts). CRATES OF BULBS (nb_crates: every one still wrapped, two opened). A PROJECTOR
  (nb_projector: three on tall stands aimed up at the dead signs, one on a road case, the generator and its cables, a reel case open, cable on
  its spool). THE MUSEUM'S WALL (nb_wall_ew, _ns: block painted black for the dark, a white line, two dead tubes run along it).
- THE CEILING (boneyard_ceiling.webp, rule 451(a), 'joist'): the truck stop's steel roof, and in every bay a dead neon loop hanging from it on its
  wires, rust, teal, mustard or white. boneyard.json's ceiling entry: "have": true, "tile": [10, 10] (the one agreed edit in COMBAT FOUR's file).
