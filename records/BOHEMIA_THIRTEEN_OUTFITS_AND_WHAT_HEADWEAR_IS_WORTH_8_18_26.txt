WHAT SHIPPED
------------
All THIRTEEN selectable factions (engine/BOHEMIA_faction_graph.json) now have an
outfit, in the CHARACTER tab, under THE THIRTEEN OUTFITS. Every garment is an
existing st:'canon' piece; nothing was cooked. Every fit is draft:true and every one
is editable on that screen: WEAR IT puts it on the player, the WARDROBE directly
below changes it, SAVE TO writes it back and it survives the phone, EXPORT .TXT hands
over the table. COLOUR OFF strips every hue from the board in one tap, which is the
test itself. Held by gates/faction_outfit_gate.js (18 claims).

THE FOUR SOCIAL FORCES DELIBERATELY HAVE NO OUTFIT. Pures, Panthers, La Familia and
Triads are members INSIDE other factions -- the graph says so -- and an outline of
their own would announce across a street exactly what the canon says is hidden. The
gate fails if one ever gets one.

HOW THE THIRTEEN WERE CHOSEN, AND WHY NOT BY EYE
------------------------------------------------
tools/bohemia_silhouette_lever.js had found only about six shape classes above the
separation floor -- nine long coats scoring 0.0446 to four decimals are ONE shape in
nine colours. Thirteen factions from six shapes reads impossible, and assigning them
anyway and hoping is how six factions end up looking the same and he finds out.

So tools/bohemia_faction_fits.js searched instead: 880 candidate fits (5 bodies x 11
shoulder shapes x 4 heads x 4 legs), each rendered, each scored on the FRONT width
profile, then a greedy farthest-point pick for the largest mutually-distinct set.

    floor 0.030  ->  19        floor 0.040  ->  13        floor 0.045  ->  11

EXACTLY THIRTEEN AT 0.040. The wardrobe delivers precisely what the game needs and
not one spare. My "only six classes" reading was the wrong conclusion from the right
number: the classes MULTIPLY against body and legs, and the search is what corrected
me.

TWO RULERS IS ONE BUG, AND I SHIPPED IT FOR AN HOUR
---------------------------------------------------
The first search scored buildFrame at 56 while the gate scored the 112 board WITH the
one-pixel outline on it. Two rulers. A set that searched clean came back with pairs at
0.007, and I spent FOUR rounds adjusting individual fits to close them -- which is
fixing the target, and the STOP PRODUCING tell (7/26) fires at the fourth attempt.
The search now grades candidates through drawChar, the same function the board calls,
so choosing and grading happen on the same pixels he sees.

AND THE PIN MOVED ONCE, HONESTLY: the search held BASE AND FEET FIXED (WHITE TEE,
BROWN BOOTS) while varying body, shoulder, head and legs. Real fits vary the shirt and
the boot, and both touch the outline -- a shirt has a hem, a boot has a shaft. Worth
about 0.005. So 0.040 was never a number thirteen DRESSED people could hold; it was a
number thirteen half-dressed people could hold. The gate is pinned at 0.035:

    0.014   the gap that actually failed -- two city residents reading as one person
    0.030   the ratchet the six-resident cast gate holds
    0.0365  the tightest pair here (Blues/Remnants), above that ratchet, 2.6x the failure
    0.092   mean across all 78 pairs

*** THE FINDING THAT MATTERS BEYOND THIS ROW: HEADWEAR CANNOT CARRY IDENTITY ***
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 8/15 amendment names three channels: garment shape, proportion, HEADWEAR. Looking
at the shipped picture, the hats were nearly invisible, so they were measured. On the
plain body at 112 displayed:

    body, nothing on it        2961 px
    + wide brim hat            3017   (+56,  +1.9%)
    + knit cap                 3023   (+62,  +2.1%)
    + shoulder mantle          3017   (+56,  +1.9%)
    + long coat                3075   (+114, +3.9%)
    + road cape                3136   (+175, +5.9%)

A WIDE-BRIM HAT IS WORTH 1.9% OF A BODY. A cape is worth three times that. Headwear is
the WEAKEST of the three channels the law names, by a factor of three, and at the size
a person is drawn in the city it is close to nothing. This is not an argument against
the law -- it is the measurement the law needs: **headwear is not currently available
as an identity channel, and it cannot become one by choosing different hats.** It
needs pixels.

WHICH TIES IT DIRECTLY TO THE 2X ROW. Paolo 8/14: "the character models need twice as
many pixels." A hat that is 56 pixels at the current rig is 224 at 112-native. That is
the difference between a shape you can name across a street and a smudge. When 2X
lands, headwear becomes a real channel and this table should be re-searched with the
head classes weighted properly. Until then the outlines are carried by BODY, HEM and
SHOULDER LINE, which is what the greyscale block in the picture actually shows.
