# HOW THE PACK DID IT: A CRACK (UI [the phone's crack], 10/16/26; rule 100c, opens the round)

His words, twice, with no row until this round: 'these cracks look like a seventh grader coded it' (eighth votes) and 'the
cracks look like shit, they're literally just straight lines' (eleventh votes). The phone's crack today is a few SVG polylines
in the city file, stretched to the phone (preserveAspectRatio none), 30% cream.

## WHERE HIS PACKS DRAW BROKEN THINGS
1. PACK 5 'Windows and broken glass' (23 tiles, approved). Each pane is 66 to 80 by 96. The glass is sky-blue and white
   (window glass seen from outside), in shards round a black hole, with an impact star in some panes.
2. PACK 15 'Cracked floor details' (16 tiles, NEVER JUDGED, read lossless from banks/BOHEMIA_HD_TILE_REPO_part*.txt). Tiles
   2 to 9 are crack networks with no floor under them, 96 wide, 42 to 74 tall.
   - SHAPE: a centre where it was hit, four to six branches forking twice; the lines run 2 to 3 pixels wide (median run), with
     a dark rim either side.
   - COLOUR: 1,580 to 2,625 colours a tile, a white-hot core through yellow and orange to a dark red rim; 42 to 51% of the
     tile is painted.
   - That is how a glass crack reads too: a light edge where the break catches the light, a dark edge beside it, branches
     that fork and wander, never a straight line.

## WHAT THE ROUND FOUND (both tried at 1:1 on his phone through the one driver)
- PACK 5 FAILED, and was not sent. A pane's star is 25 by 29 pixels, which is 8 points on a 156-point phone. The window glass
  is blue and reads as frost or snowflakes on black. A running crack needs lines drawn between the stars, which rule 108 bans.
  The record: records/target/ui_the_phones_crack/UI_THE_PHONES_CRACK_FINDING.png (candidates A to D).
- PACK 15 WORKS. The pixels are recoloured to glass, a ramp from (12,10,8) to (246,240,224) by each pixel's own brightness.
  Every place and every edge is kept, and two runs are given a quarter turn, which is lossless.
  - F: the star at the dropped corner and one run down the right edge.
  - G: the star and two runs down the right edge.
  - Both sit in the corner and the margin. They cover at most 2% of one line of words, where the hairline crack runs
    through the words.
