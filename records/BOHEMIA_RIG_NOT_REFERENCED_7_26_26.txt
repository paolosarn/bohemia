=== BOHEMIA — THE GAME IS NOT DRAWING THE BODY IN THE RIG TOOL (7/26/26) ===
Lane: CHARACTER. Found by asking his question honestly instead of defending.

HIS QUESTION
  "Are you tweaking? Are you not referring to the Rig at all?"

THE ANSWER: effectively, no. The alpha carries TWO copies of his painted body
and they have drifted apart.
  - BAKED                  -> what the CHARACTER and ANIMATION tabs draw
  - BAKED inside RIG_B64   -> what the RIG tab (his authoring tool) draws
Same file, two different bodies. Nobody had ever checked.

THE DIFF (tools/bohemia_rig_sync_audit.js, reproducible in one command)
  skeleton  : identical
  layers    : 20 PAINTED PARTS DIFFER, 65 pixels apart in total
  pose      : DIFFERENT

  the NECK is smaller in the game in EVERY direction:
      S  rig  8px -> game  4px        SE rig 10px -> game  6px
      E  rig  9px -> game  6px        NE rig  6px -> game  7px
      N  rig 12px -> game  8px        NW rig  6px -> game  8px
      W  rig  9px -> game  6px        SW rig 10px -> game  6px
  the HEAD is smaller in the game:  S rig 24 -> 20,  N rig 92 -> 80
  the TORSO differs in all eight directions
  the FACE differs on E and W

Side by side: records/anim/BOHEMIA_RIG_VS_GAME_BODY_7_26_26.png (idle, 7x, all
eight facings, bare). The rig-tool body has a real neck; the game body's head
sits down on the shoulders.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN ANYTHING ELSE IN THIS LANE
He has been opening the RIG tab, seeing the body he painted, then opening the
ANIMATION tab and seeing a different body move -- and being told the renderer
was the problem. Some of what he has been calling tweaking is not the animation
at all; it is a body he did not paint. Every judgement he has been asked for on
character motion was taken against the wrong art.

It also explains "it didn't used to be like this" better than anything else
found so far: a drift, not a design limit.

WHICH ONE IS CANON IS HIS CALL, AND NOTHING WAS CHANGED
The rig tool is the authoring surface, so its copy is almost certainly the real
one -- but "almost certainly" is not good enough to overwrite his painted
character with, and RIG LAW is explicit that his painted regions are mine to
read and never to rewrite. Git cannot settle it either: the repo history was
rewritten in the 7/26 slim genesis, so both copies date to the same commit.
NO BODY DATA WAS TOUCHED. He picks, then it gets synced and locked.

THE GATE THIS OWES (the same turn he picks)
One canonical painted body, exactly like the ENGINE SYNC LAW does for modules:
the alpha's BAKED must equal the rig tool's BAKED, byte for byte, or the build
fails. BAKED was never covered by the sync gate because it is not a BOH_*
module -- that hole is how two bodies lived in one file without anyone noticing.

PENDING PAOLO, ONE QUESTION
  Which body is yours: the one in the RIG tab, or the one the game draws?
  (Look at records/anim/BOHEMIA_RIG_VS_GAME_BODY_7_26_26.png -- the neck is the
  fastest tell.) Say which and it gets synced everywhere and gated shut.
