THREE RULERS AND TWO OF THEM COULD NEVER FAIL
8/27/26 - RUN lane
================================================================================

WHAT WENT RED, AND IT WAS NOT THE GAME.

gates/see_through_while_moving_gate.js held eleven claims. One of them went red
on main:

    "and the fade is aimed at him rather than being a filter over the whole
     world (1044 of 3624 elsewhere solid)"

It wanted more than half of every non-covering facade draw to be fully solid.
It read 29%. When the gate shipped on 8/25 it read 3145 of 3771, which is 83%.

THE LAW WAS NEVER BROKEN. THE RULER WENT OUT OF DATE.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THE NUMBER MOVED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

On 8/26 a lane built the thing Paolo asked for in the playtest dispatch, item
one: "the wall changed under him AND there is no wall-opacity system at all,
though he thinks there is -- build the fade."

So it got built. XRAY_R is 5. Every wall within five cells of him now ramps open
whether or not it is covering him, and xrayEase carries the value across frames
instead of snapping it. That is a deliberate five-cell aperture that follows him
around, and there are a LOT of facade draws inside five cells of a person.

The old claim averaged over the whole screen. A correct new feature dragged a
whole-screen average under a whole-screen threshold. Measured by distance, the
build was doing exactly what the law says:

    within XRAY_R (5)    20% solid     the aperture, on purpose
    6 to 12              82% solid     the ease, trailing off
    13 to 30            100% solid
    beyond 30           100% solid

THE FADE IS LOCAL TO HIM. That is the law. The gate just stopped being able to
see it.

FIX THE RULER, NEVER THE TARGET (8/1). This is that, and it is worth saying that
the temptation ran the other way: the cheap move is to "fix" the game until the
old number comes back, which would have deleted a feature he asked for by name.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AND THEN I WROTE TWO RULERS THAT COULD NOT FAIL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the part worth keeping.

A gate that cannot go red is worse than no gate, because it reports safety. I
mutation-test every claim now, which means: break the thing on purpose, and if
the claim still passes, the claim is decoration. The mutation here is one line,
forcing every wall in the world to draw faded:

    a = WALL_SEE;   right before g.globalAlpha = a

RULER 2. "A wall beyond XRAY_R plus six is solid," measured as Chebyshev
distance from where the draw landed.
    real build      322 of 322 solid
    every wall faded 322 of 322 solid
    IT NEVER MOVED.

RULER 3. Same idea, measured in COLUMNS only, "beyond eleven columns."
    real build      160 of 160 solid
    every wall faded 160 of 160 solid
    IT NEVER MOVED EITHER.

BOTH FAILURES HAVE THE SAME ROOT AND IT IS GEOMETRY, NOT LOGIC.

A facade draws its courses UPWARD from its own cell. A wall three cells north of
him does not land three cells away on screen; it lands three cells PLUS its own
height. So "far in screen rows" was catching walls that are near him in the
world, and the far bucket filled up with things that were never going to fade.

And the column version failed for a different reason that looks the same from
outside: at HC 44 on a 390 pixel canvas the viewport is about NINE columns wide.
A wall twelve columns away IS NEVER DRAWN AT ALL. That bucket held 160 draws of
something that was not a facade, always solid, forever.

TWO DIFFERENT WRONG ANSWERS, both of which printed a confident 100% and both of
which would have sat green through any regression this gate exists to catch.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT ACTUALLY DISCRIMINATES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The ring just OUTSIDE the game's own aperture and still on the screen. Measured
both ways:

                       within 5     the ring     beyond
    real build          20% solid    82% solid    100%
    every wall faded    20% solid     0% solid    100%

That is a real gap, so the threshold sits between the two worlds at 35% rather
than on the edge of either. Today it reads 342 of 582, which is 59%. Under
mutation it reads 26 of 586, which is 4%. The claim dies on the thing it exists
to catch and does not flicker on the thing it does not.

TWO THINGS THE FINAL RULER DOES ON PURPOSE:

1. THE INNER EDGE IS READ OFF XRAY_R, NOT HARDCODED. A lane that widens the
   approach-fade widens this exemption with it, instead of turning a true claim
   red six weeks from now. That is the whole failure above, prevented.

2. THE COMMENT SAYS "SCREEN DISTANCE" BECAUSE IT IS SCREEN DISTANCE. It is not
   world distance and a facade's drawn row includes its height. Writing "cells
   from him" would have been a small lie that the next person debugs for an
   hour. The first draft of this gate DID say that, and it had to be corrected
   in the same turn it was written.

A second claim was added underneath, because the first one alone would pass on a
world with NO fade at all: something near him really is opening up.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE RULE THIS LEAVES BEHIND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*** A WHOLE-SCREEN AVERAGE IS NOT A MEASUREMENT OF A LOCAL EFFECT. ***

If the law says a thing is aimed at him, the claim has to ask about distance. An
average over everything on screen answers a different question, and it will go
red the first time somebody correctly makes the local effect bigger.

And the older rule, which this is the third or fourth entry for: A CLAIM THAT
PASSES WITH THE FIX REMOVED IS HOLDING NOTHING. Two of the three rulers here
were written by somebody who believed they were being careful.
