   grouping, no placeholder, so the browser picks the first one and BUILD places
   it. The single worst first-hour moment in the game, found by playing it.
   -> LIFE + CITY [build default].
2. **We built the courthouse and never issued a loan.** Every mechanism courtless
   credit needs is live on the walked surface -- news walks a real acquaintance
   graph three hops, memory decays, belonging and deeds are recorded, the purse
   refuses debt -- and nothing in the game ever LENDS anybody anything. The
   enforcement machine has never had a debt to enforce. -> WORLD [someone lends].
3. **There is no back of house anywhere.** Our own economy module has said since
   7/19 that downtown matters because of "deep casino/resort dry stores". There is
   no dry store, no laundry, no boiler, no loading dock, in any building in the
   game. Every job the city claims to offer happens in a room that does not exist.
   -> WORLD [back of house].
4. **The city is a thousand feet above its own water.** The valley floor is about
   2,028 feet; Lake Mead hit 1,040.5. Everything Vegas drinks is lifted that gap by
   22 vertical pumps and two booster stations. Water is not scarce, PUMPING is. That
   is the cleanest tie between power, territory and thirst we will ever get, and it
   is real. -> WORLD [water lifted].
5. **Our city gossips about nobody but the player, and has never once been wrong.**
   The rumour machine has hop counts and hearsay decay, and in 1,669 lines it has
   passed on news about someone other than the player exactly ONCE. We even wrote
   the line where a stranger says "probably the wrong version," and there has never
   been a wrong version. -> PEOPLE [rumours travel].
