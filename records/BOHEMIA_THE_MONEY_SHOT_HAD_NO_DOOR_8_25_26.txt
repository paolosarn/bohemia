8/25/26 -- RUN lane. Found by PLAYING the back half of the demo, which nobody had.


WHAT HAPPENED
=============

I played the demo past the point anybody had played it: resolve the job, the
reckoning, sleep, day 2. It plays. Day 2 opened with a new job and then, a few
seconds after GET UP, THE VISTA fired by itself -- demo critical-path row 11, the
overlook where the whole valley opens up. The shot looked good.

Then I tried to leave, and could not. Measured on the real surface, six gestures,
each from a clean open:

    tap the world              -> still open
    Escape                     -> still open
    the MODE / DROP IN button  -> still open
    tap the vista card         -> still open
    WHOLE MAP                  -> still open
    walk the pad               -> still open

vistaClose() existed. It was correct. It restored his cell, his mode, the overmap
marker and his house. THE ONLY CALLER IN THE ENTIRE REPO WAS A GATE:

    gates/vista_beat_gate.js:127   window.__VISTA.close()

Nothing a player could touch called it. He reaches the best moment in the demo,
which the game plays FOR him, unprompted, and then the game keeps him there.

That is his own report from 8/24 -- "When I press standing, and I press close, it
doesn't close ... pretty please just make sure all the buttons work" -- on the one
screen the demo exists to show off.

AND THE TITLE WAS PRINTED ON THE TOOLBAR. `top:64px`, hardcoded, while the toolbar
occupies 49..80 and the objective line sits under it. So "THE VALLEY / the
neighbourhoods, the boulevards, the freeway - 4.4 km of it" was drawn across the
music button, the save button and the day's objective. Measured after: all four
corners of the card's ink had another element on top of them.


WHY EVERY PANEL CLOSES DID NOT CATCH IT
=======================================

I built every_panel_closes_gate.js on 8/24 for exactly this class of bug, and it
was green through all of this.

It walks the chips in the toolbar, the builder drawer and the bottom-left column,
opens whatever each one opens, and proves it can be closed. THE VISTA IS NOT
OPENED BY A CHIP. The day loop opens it.

An enumerating gate is only ever as complete as its enumeration. That is a
permanent property of that gate, not a defect I can patch out of it -- widening
the sweep to "everything" would just move the boundary. So the fix is not to
change EVERY PANEL. It is that a thing no chip reaches gets its own gate.

THE GENERAL FORM, and this is the fourth member of the family this week:
A FINISHED THING WITH A PUBLISHED SEAM AND NO CALLER.
  - __VISTA.open() was published and the run never called it (fixed 8/23,
    __THE_VALLEY_IS_A_PLACE__)
  - come_up was approved and wired only in the slice nobody opens (SOUND, 8/22)
  - homeFind's door test knew one door kind of four (fixed 8/24)
  - and now vistaClose(), whose only caller was the gate that proved it worked.
A GATE CALLING A FUNCTION IS NOT THE SAME AS THE GAME CALLING IT, and a gate that
reaches for the function instead of the gesture will be green either way.


THE FIX
=======

tools/bohemia_the_vista_lets_you_leave_patch.py
  - a TAP on the world closes it, reusing the city's OWN tap-versus-drag test
    (CB._tapMoved) rather than inventing a second one, so dragging to look around
    the valley still looks around the valley
  - a tap on the CARD closes it (its pointer-events were `none`, so the card
    could not even be tapped, and there was nothing else to tap either)
  - ESCAPE closes it
  - the card SAYS SO: "tap anywhere to go back". A way out he cannot see is one he
    does not have -- his words about the STANDING card, two days before this.
  - the card MEASURES what is above it instead of guessing 64px

tools/bohemia_every_control_leaves_the_vista_patch.py
  The other three were not three more bugs, they were ONE: the vista is a
  full-screen camera state and nothing else in the file knew it existed. Every
  control just did its own job on top of it -- DROP IN even flipped MODE
  UNDERNEATH the card, leaving the title of the valley printed over his own
  street, which is worse than stuck.

  AND THE FILE HAD ALREADY SOLVED THIS ONCE. SKY is the other full-screen camera
  state, and WHOLE MAP has opened with `if(SKY){ skyExit(); return; }` since
  __ONE_ZOOM_TO_THE_MOON__. The moon got the treatment; the overlook never did.
  vistaBail() is that shape, and the three controls ask it first.

  DROP IN and WHOLE MAP    COME BACK, and stop. They are camera buttons and the
                           vista is a camera; doing their own job as well would
                           leave him somewhere he did not ask to be.
  THE PAD                  COMES BACK AND THEN WALKS. A direction is not "get me
                           out of here", it is "go that way". Swallowing the press
                           would make the first step out of every vista dead --
                           the same not-working-button he reported on STANDING.
                           MEASURED: 4 cells walked on the way out.


AND ONE OWNER FOR "WHAT IS ABOVE ME"
====================================

The population card worked out the top-chrome measurement on 8/24. The vista card
would have been the SECOND hand-written copy, and a third was coming. Copies drift
-- that is the whole story of this week: a door predicate copied into homeFind, an
objective hint copied into an inlined module, a toolbar offset copied into popwrap.

topChromeBottom(el) is defined once and both callers ask it. It answers in the
positioned element's OWN offsetParent coordinates, because asking the element
being measured is how the popcard fix got it wrong on its first try.

The gate holds this in SOURCE as well as pixels: one definition, at least two
callers, and exactly one place in the file running a max of chrome bottoms.

AND A STANDING RED THAT WAS NEVER A BUG
=======================================

VISTA BEAT has been red on one claim: "and it PLAYS after GET UP, so it is never
under the wake card". The behaviour was never wrong. The claim demanded the EXACT
source text `cardShow(h,function(){ cardHide(); try{ vistaBeatMaybe` -- the call
as the literal next statement after cardHide(). On 8/22 SOUND added the come_up
sting to that same handler, in between, and the claim went red while the
measured claim eleven lines below it -- "AND THE WAKE CARD IS GONE" -- stayed
green the entire time.

A CHECKER THAT MATCHES FORMATTING INSTEAD OF BEHAVIOUR goes red every time
another lane legitimately touches the line, and a standing red hides real reds.
FIX THE RULER, NEVER THE TARGET (8/1). It now cuts out the GET UP handler's body
and asks whether the beat is fired from inside it, with anything allowed in
between, plus the other half the claim always meant: that the WAKE only ARMS it.

THE REPLACEMENT FELL INTO THE ADJACENT TRAP ON ITS FIRST RUN, which is worth
keeping written down. Scanning 900 characters after DAY.on('wake') for
"vistaBeatMaybe()" ran past the end of the handler and matched
`function vistaBeatMaybe(){` -- THE DEFINITION, twenty lines below. A checker that
cannot tell a mention from a use, inside the fix for a checker that could not tell
formatting from behaviour. The handler's body is cut out first now.
