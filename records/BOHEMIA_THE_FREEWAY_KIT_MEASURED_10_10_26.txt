RULE 77, AND THE PART THAT IS NOT A LABEL. Paolo 10/5: "the tiles aren't
speaking to each other... these things should conjoin easily like Legos." Every
piece declares what runs out of its four sides. THEN THE GATE LAYS TWO OF EACH
RUN END TO END AND MEASURES THE JOINT against the inside of the piece, because a
declared edge that does not actually meet is a label and not a joint. A barrier
run that cannot be laid end to end is six barriers, not a run.

COVER OR BLOCKER IS MEASURED, NOT FILED BY EYE. A man's chest is 1.30 m: over it
stops a shot, under it does not. This lane's own 9/30 round filed two pieces
wrong by eye and the tape corrected it, so the tape runs here too. The barrier
run is 0.81 and is COVER. The fence is 1.83 and is a BLOCKER YOU CAN SEE THROUGH,
which is the only reason to put one on a board: you know what is coming and you
still cannot get to it. The truck is 2.80 and stops everything.

AND ONE THING THIS KIT DOES THAT THE 9/30 KIT GOT WRONG. On 10/1 this lane filed
its own correction: the block war kit was drawn flat, straight on, while COMBAT
TWO's board is cut for a camera pitched forty-five degrees, so those pieces do
not sit on it. Every piece here carries its FOOTPRINT (w x l, what it covers on
the ground) separately from its FACE (the picture), so COMBAT TWO can place it on
a pitched board without squashing a finished picture -- which would resample
every one of his pixels, the exact fault the far-end round was about.
