=== 2. WHAT HE ACTUALLY SEES ==============================================
STROBE = a cell that changes and changes straight back across three consecutive
frames (there/gone/there, or tone A/B/A). A rigid limb swinging past you never
does that; the renderer changing its mind does. Measured on the COMPOSITED
frame -- the surface he watches -- on E and W.

  both passes on (what he judged) : 4.65 strobing pixels per frame  (1674 on/off, 5015 tone)
  both passes retired             : 4.74 strobing pixels per frame  (1630 on/off, 5200 tone)

READ THIS HONESTLY: section 1 is a big win and section 2 barely moves. Removing
the two invented passes stops the renderer FABRICATING, but it is not what he is
looking at. The strobe is somewhere else, and section 3 finds it.

   3143  191,175,166 <-> 153,137,129

    149  191,175,166 <-> 172,158,149
    144  224,211,203 <-> 191,175,166
    110  191,175,166 <-> 224,211,203

Every one of those is a pair of BODY SKIN RAMP tones. Nothing else is in the
list. The body is not drawn from painted pixels at all -- buildFrame recomputes
its tone every frame off the DEFORMED grid: a dark ANATOMY LINE wherever an
orthogonal neighbour is empty (outer silhouette) or belongs to a different limb
group (arm against torso), plus a light SKY TOP-LIGHT where the two cells above
are empty. In profile the arm sits inside an 8px torso, so a one-pixel swing
reclassifies whole runs of pixels between skin tone and line tone -- and back
the next frame. That is the morphing underneath the arms, and it is his own
7/26 ruling being broken: the lighting layer is being re-derived under the
animation instead of behaving like light.

=== 4. THE CANDIDATE FIX, MEASURED BEFORE ANYONE BELIEVES IT ==============
Bind the anatomy line to the REST pixel (where he painted it) and carry it
through the SAME inverse sample the art rides, so the line travels WITH the
limb instead of being re-derived under it.

  today (re-derived per frame) : 4.36 line flips per frame
  rest-bound line             : 2.02 line flips per frame   (54% removed)

NOT A CURE, and it is not shipped. It halves the dominant defect; the residual
is the inverse sample landing a cell on a different source pixel between frames.
Shipping it is Paolo's call, not a green number's.
