## 2. THE RULER COUNTED THE CURE AS THE DISEASE, AND THAT WAS FIXED BEFORE ANY NUMBER WAS QUOTED
The first cut of the glow row matched any `box-shadow` carrying an `rgba`, which is most of
them. But DIRECTION's 9/11 object language **rules** that every panel must show its body
with "a shadowed lower edge or a visible side, one or the other, always". A ruler that
counts the ordered fix as the tell pushes the work back toward the flat hairline it is
trying to leave, and the number falls by doing the wrong thing.

The tell is *dark plus GLOW equals premium*: a halo with no offset, or a wide coloured
blur. A shadow with a vertical offset and a tight blur is an object having a bottom edge.
They are separate rows now because they are separate things. This was changed **before**
the before/after was taken, so both columns above are the same ruler.

## 3. WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGED ON HIS SCREEN
**EMOJI ARE OUT OF THE CONTROLS.** 24 to 2, and the two left are the thumbs in a judge
tool, not the game. DIRECTION's ruling said what replaces them rather than leaving a hole:
act one's casing register is **DIN-stencil caps**, and a stencilled label on a crate or a
breaker box is *letters*. Two chips were emoji ONLY -- a floppy disk and a spanner -- and
they say **SAVE** and **TOOLS** now, which is what a stencil would have said.

**THE HAIRLINE AND THE CARD RADIUS ARE GONE FROM EVERY CONTROL THIS LANE OWNS,** and the
**skin** draws them instead. Nineteen chips each carried their own `border:1px solid` and a
5px card radius in their own rule -- which is both the tell and the reason it could never be
changed in one place. One rule, reading the skin:

    an edge      a lit rim above, a dark base below, a real value step
    a body       the line under the chip is the side of the object
    a corner     2px, an object's corner, not a card's
    tracking     a stencil's, tight and even

Act two is now seven values in one place, not a rewrite.

**AND THE DEAD SOURCE WENT WITH IT.** The first pass left the old `border:1px solid` and
`border-radius:5px` in the file and overrode them at runtime. The screen improved and **the
count went UP by one**, which is the ruler doing its job: slop the skin hides is slop that
grows back. The declarations are deleted now, so the source and the screen say the same
thing.

## 4. A COIN WAS FOUND IN OUR OWN GATE AND REPLACED RATHER THAN RE-RUN
`thumb_gate`'s "no walk button draws a second arrow" leg came back **14/1, 15/0, 15/0,
15/0**, the red naming a single diagonal. That is a coin, not a finding.

**Why it became one:** the test shoots a *rectangle of the page* at each control and
diffs it against the same rectangle with the text hidden. The pad stopped being an opaque
circle on 9/7 when it became one ring cut into eight wedges, so the gaps between wedges and
the corners of every wedge's bounding box are now **the live, animating city**. Two shots a
moment apart differ because a car moved, and the gate blamed the button. The instrument was
right for the control it was written against and wrong for the one that is there now.

What it is actually asking is whether a stray **text glyph** is drawn on top of the shape.
For an SVG group that has an exact answer with no pixels in it. The pixel diff is kept for
any control still drawn in HTML, which is the case it was written for. **Three runs, 15/0
each. Mutation-proved:** put a glyph in one wedge and it names it -- `↗(glyph "↗")`.
