## FINDINGS
- A SIEGE IS A CHAIN OF EXISTING PIECES, NOT A MODE: warning on the map -> offers in two halls -> preparation
  days -> ONE fight on the base's own close grid -> HELD / TAKEN / RUINED written to the base, the deed and the
  siege's own record -> aftermath. Kenshi's outposts (Q151.W6, W7, W10) give the rules; its flaws (Q151.X1,
  X2) give: at most one siege per base per act, and every siege contract has a talk option. TAKEN and RUINED
  pay the same. A fall is learned within two map stops through the map, the feed, prices, a refugee event and
  a new offer, never a popup, and always opens witness / rescue / retake. The main character is carried to a
  clinic, never killed.
- ACT 1 HAD NO BOOM CONTRACTS until this round; six boom situations (glut, surplus, caravan, new road, festival,
  tournament) now write act 1 boards, and wealth draws raids (Q107.X6, Q044.W4).
- PAY FOR THE RESULT, NOT THE METHOD: then kind and cruel roads pay the same with no special rule (Q148.W9).
  Where the kind road cost the family batteries, the client pays it (the base owns the problem).
- SITUATIONS CHAIN BASE TO BASE, one link, within two map stops, carried by a face or a board.
