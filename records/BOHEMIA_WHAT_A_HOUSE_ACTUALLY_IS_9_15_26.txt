=== WHAT A HOUSE IS, MEASURED ===
Generated 12 suburbs from the real district generator, walked every building footprint.

  a fine cell            0.75 m         the overmap's own CELL_M, read not retyped
  a HOUSE footprint      13 x 12 cells  9.8 x 9.0 m   median of 122 houses
  the same, mean         14.6 x 14.0    range 12 to 21 cells
  a LOT, kerb to kerb    25 cells       18.8 m        median pitch between neighbours
  an overmap tile        128 cells      96 m
  a step today            1 cell        0.75 m
  the body today        104 px painted at HC 44, which is 2.4 cells, 1.77 m

18.8 m is a real American tract lot (about 62 feet) and 9.8 x 9.0 m is a real small house
on it. The world we built is already at honest human scale. That is worth saying because
it means nothing has to be redrawn.

=== TWO NUMBERS THAT WERE HANDED DOWN AND ARE WRONG (rule 12: a premise, not a gate) ===
  the law's own parenthetical   "a house is the city's lot cell (the overmap tile)"
  LIFE+CITY's row [lot lattice] "a lot is FN=32 fine cells today"
An overmap tile is 128 cells, 96 m. That is a CITY BLOCK, not a house: about five lots on
a side. And FN is 128, not 32; 32 is a number from a different module's grid.
HIS RULING IS NOT IN QUESTION. What a house IS in the world we built is a measurement, and
a step of 128 cells would be a step across a whole block. THE ANSWER IS 25.

=== THE RESULT THAT IS NOT OBVIOUS, AND IT IS THE POINT OF THE ROUND ===
He asked for people to be LARGER. The body does not need one new pixel.
"About half a lot tall" at today's 104 px body means A LOT MUST DRAW ABOUT 208 px. Today a
lot draws 1,100 px, which is why you cannot see one: the camera shows 8.6 cells, 6.4 metres
of bare ground, and nothing on that screen reads as a house.
At 208 px a phone shows about 1.8 lots across. THE PERSON BECOMES 5.3 TIMES LARGER AGAINST
THE WORLD WITH THE SAME SPRITE. What has to move is the ground under him, and that is RUN's
zoom, not this lane's art.
