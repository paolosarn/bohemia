THE MISS RATE

  NOT ONE BODY ON THE STREET BELONGS TO A FACTION. 171 of 171 answer none.
  AND NO RESIDENT IS STANDING ON GROUND ANYBODY HOLDS (0 of 2199).
  AND THE WALKED CITY NEVER LOADS THE FACTION COLOUR MODULE AT ALL.
  AND THE FACTION CAST NEVER BAKES, so there is nothing to dress them in.

  A miss rate needs two things to compare. The colours exist, the law exists,
  the ground can answer who holds it -- but on the surface Paolo walks there is
  no affiliated person and no claimed block under them, so "does this body wear
  this block's colour" has no subject. Fixing the picker would be fixing the
  half of the sentence that is not broken.

*** AND THE ROW'S OWN WORDING HIDES A TRAP, NAMED HERE RATHER THAN WALKED INTO.
"EVERY dressed person's colour matches the faction that owns the block" would,
read literally, put the whole street in gang colours -- every shopkeeper, every
kid, in Cartel brown for standing on Cartel ground. That is the opposite of the
law it cites. The law calls colour "a statement of who would defend you" and says
wearing your colours is A CHOICE WITH A COST; the style card allows ONE saturated
piece per body and lists dust, ash, bone and lead as legal cloth. A valley where
everybody is in uniform has no signal in it, because a colour only means
something against another colour -- the law's own line about the Bloods taking
red to contrast the Crips' blue. So the miss rate above is for AFFILIATED bodies,
and civilians are reported, never counted as misses.

READ THIS BEFORE DRAWING A CONCLUSION. A NUMBER IS NOT A FINDING UNTIL YOU KNOW
WHAT IT IS COUNTING. Hue is read off the PIXELS THE GAME BLITTED, in twelve bins,
ignoring grey -- so "wrong colour" here means the body is visibly a different hue,
not that a hex differs by a shade.
