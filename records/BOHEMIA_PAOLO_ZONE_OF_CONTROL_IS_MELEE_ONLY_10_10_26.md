## WHAT I HEARD (rule 91c; correct me with one word)

1. A fighter has a zone of control only while a MELEE weapon is the one equipped, and it is the one tile ring around him.
2. A fighter holding a RANGED weapon (pistol, rifle, scope) has no zone of control: anyone can walk left, right, up, down and across the tiles next to him, and past him, with no stop and no free hit.
3. It follows the weapon in hand, so it changes the moment a man swaps.

## School line
Battle Brothers gives every character a zone of control on adjacent tiles whatever he holds, with the free attack when you leave it. This is our twist on it (MODDED VANILLA++, Paolo 9/28), and it makes the ranged line a thing you can run through, which is what he wants the fight to feel like.
