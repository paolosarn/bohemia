## PART ONE: THE RESEARCH (RESEARCH FIRST, rule 489)

### A. What guns a 2050 American collapse would have (the real world)
1. THE COUNT. The Small Arms Survey (2018) put American civilians at about 393 million firearms, 120 for every 100 people and
   about 46 percent of the world's civilian guns (https://www.weforum.org/stories/2018/06/there-are-more-guns-than-people-in-the-us ;
   https://www.statista.com/chart/14468/us-civilians-own-393-million-firearms ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_Arms_Survey).
   Rule 489(a) already reads this: the valley is not short of guns, it is short of ammunition.
2. THE AUTOMATIC-LOOKING RIFLE IS ALREADY EVERYWHERE, BUT SEMI-AUTOMATIC. NSSF's count of "modern sporting rifles" (AR-15 and
   AK-pattern, magazine-fed, semi-automatic) went 19.8 million (2020) -> 24.4 million (2022) -> 28.1 million (2024) -> 32.1
   million (January 2026) (https://www.americanrifleman.org/content/data-reveals-more-than-28-1-million-msrs-in-circulation ;
   https://www.thefirearmblog.com/blog/2020/11/19/20-million-modern-sporting-rifles/ ;
   https://www.nssf.org/articles/nssf-releases-most-recent-firearm-production-figures-2024/). So the common rifle in a dead Las
   Vegas is a 30-round semi-automatic: it fires every time you pull, it does not spray.
3. REAL AUTOMATIC FIRE COMES FROM THREE PLACES. (a) Police and National Guard armories: the select-fire carbine (the M4, whose
   fire selector has a three-round burst; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M4_carbine). (b) The belt-fed squad gun: the M249, 725 to
   850 rounds a minute cyclic, 85 a minute sustained, fed from a 200-round box that weighs 3.1 kg filled; the gun itself about
   6.9 to 7.5 kg, about 10 kg loaded (https://man.fas.org/dod-101/sys/land/m249.htm ; https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/M249 ;
   https://365.military.com/equipment/m249-squad-automatic-weapon). (c) THE CONVERSION: a quarter-sized "switch" (auto sear) that
   turns a pistol automatic. ATF: 814 recovered 2012-2016, 5,454 in 2017-2021, a 570 percent jump; more than 5,800 seized in 2023
   alone; they are 3D-printed for cents (https://www.npr.org/2023/02/10/1153977949/major-takeaways-from-the-atf-gun-violence-report ;
   https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/swift-links-machine-gun-conversion-atf-north-texas ;
   https://www.foxnews.com/us/3d-printers-turns-handguns-machine-guns-just-40-cents). This is the most real "automatic gun" of a
   2050 collapse: a printed piece of plastic on a pistol everybody already owns.
4. HOMEMADE AND PRINTED GUNS ARE REAL AND ALREADY AT WAR. The FGC-9: a 3D-printed semi-automatic 9 mm carbine (2020), printed
   receiver, grip and stock, a Glock-pattern magazine, under 500 to 600 dollars with the printer; used by Myanmar's People's
   Defence Force; seized in Australia and Europe (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FGC-9 ;
   https://www.ammoland.com/2024/12/fgc-9-and-3d-printed-firearms-are-in-use-in-myanmar-conflict-video/). Philip Luty's 9 mm
   submachine gun, built from hardware-store steel with hand tools (Expedient Homemade Firearms, 1998), documented in nine
   countries including the United States; its weak point is the magazine (https://armamentresearch.com/pa-luty-9mm-submachine-guns/ ;
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Luty). The slam-fire pipe shotgun (a 12-gauge shell in a plumbing pipe, a nail for a pin) is
   the oldest improvised gun there is (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Improvised_firearm).
5. THE ROCKET. The RPG-7 is the world's common reloadable rocket: effective about 200 to 330 m against a vehicle-sized target, a
   backblast danger zone of about 20 m behind the tube; in wind a gunner cannot count on half his rounds past 180 m
   (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RPG-7 ; https://military.com/daily-news/2019/06/07/how-rpg-works.html ;
   https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA393159.pdf). The American one is the M72 LAW: one rocket in a tube you throw away after
   (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M72_LAW). On our house tiles (a tile is a house, about 12 m) 200 m is the whole board.

### B. The drones of today's wars (the real world)
1. THE NUMBERS. Ukraine made about 1.7 million drones in 2024; its defence minister said over 1.5 million of them were FPV
   (first-person-view) drones; the ministry's 2024 buy alone held 40,000 multi-rotor scout drones, over 12,000 of them
   night-capable (https://en.interfax.com.ua/news/general/1092556.html ;
   https://euromaidanpress.com/2024/12/11/ukrainian-defense-ministry-delivers-over-1-2-million-drones-to-defenders-in-2024/ ;
   https://kyivindependent.com/ukraine-produced-over-30-000-bomber-drones-in-2024-minister-says/amp).
2. THE SCOUT DRONE. A hobby quadcopter (the Mavic) is the war's eyes: it scouts, passes exact coordinates and walks artillery
   onto the target, so cheap shells hit like guided ones; some drop a grenade on a wire (https://www.npr.org/2023/03/21/1164977056/a-chinese-drone-for-hobbyists-plays-a-crucial-role-in-the-russia-ukraine-war ;
   https://www.rusi.org/news-and-comment/in-the-news/ukrainian-drones-drop-grenades-russian-soldiers ;
   https://dronexl.co/2023/06/01/dji-drones-redefine-warfare-ukraine).
3. THE CHARGE DRONE (the FPV). A racing quad with a 25 cm (10 inch) frame carrying an RPG warhead or a fragmentation charge;
   frame 500 to 1,000 dollars, a soldier's own build about 330 dollars; fragmentation for men on foot, RPG heads for vehicles;
   one 2025 paper puts FPV strikes at about 65 percent of Russian tank losses (https://armyrecognition.com/ukraine_-_russia_conflict_war_2022/how_ukraine_s_cheap_fpv_drones_are_used_on_the_ukrainian_battlefield.html ;
   https://reference-global.com/download/article/10.2478/kbo-2025-0005.pdf ; https://www.kyivpost.com/post/38865). A pilot flies
   it in goggles from cover: he is a man on the ground you can find (rule 490(c)).
4. HOW YOU LIVE THROUGH ONE. You hear it, you move, or you shoot it. Units train with 12-gauge pump and semi-automatic shotguns
   against FPVs; a shotgun is the most reliable small arm against them (jamming cannot stop a fibre-optic one), though even a hit
   does not always drop it; one tested round downed over 80 percent within 50 m
   (https://www.kyivpost.com/post/44046 ; https://www.defensenews.com/global/europe/2024/12/11/in-ukraine-long-guns-become-desperate-defenses-against-small-drones/ ;
   https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/why-shotguns-are-fast-becoming-ukrainian-soldiers-last-line-of-defense-against-russian-drones-6035).
   That is rule 490(a) and (d) already: it takes a turn to arrive, you can step away, a gun can drop it, a sword cannot.

### C. Night and heat sights (the real world)
- A NIGHT SIGHT (an image intensifier, the PVS-14) multiplies the light that is there; smoke and fog defeat it.
- A HEAT SIGHT (thermal) reads heat, not light: it sees a man in the dark and through smoke, fog and dust, and it is stopped by
  window glass (https://www.americanrifleman.org/articles/2018/8/27/thermal-and-night-vision-revealed ;
  https://www.thefirearmblog.com/blog/2022/07/29/friday-night-lights-101-night-vision-thermal-one-right ;
  https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA453392.pdf, the Army's fused-goggle study: smoke did not hide targets under the thermal overlay).

### E. THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG (measured against our repo, the round's one)
OUR GUNS WERE ALL ONE MECHANISM. Measured in weapons.json before this round: 6 guns of the valley's own and 11 Battle Brothers
rows they ride, every one of them a single shot at a single man; the rifles reload after EVERY shot (the crossbow's Reload, 4 AP),
so our "rifle" fires like a bolt-action, when the rifle most of the valley owns is a 30-round semi-automatic (32 million of them).
Not one gun hit more than one man except the shotgun's cone. Not one drone. The night took 30 percent off every shot and no sight
answered it. That is what Paolo felt as "very simplified": not too few guns, too few WAYS to shoot.

### F. WHAT THE RESEARCH DECIDES (the design, every number traced)
FIRE MODES, through the weapon system already in the game (a row's keys, ours.json numbers with their source):
1. SEMI-AUTOMATIC (a magazine): no reload between shots, the magazine reloaded whole when empty (the pistol's way already,
   ours.pistol_shot.reload). The shot costs Battle Brothers' Quick Shot, 4 AP: two a turn. The magazine is the real one (a
   Glock-pattern 17, an AR's 30); what he carries into a fight is that one magazine (rule 478(a): every gun carries its rounds).
2. BURST (automatic): one press fires N rounds at the man aimed at, each its own roll, each round after the first harder to land
   (recoil, -10 a round: one Battle Brothers step, the same step ours.range_bands_card uses; draft, TUNING's). N is the real
   gun's: the M4's three-round burst; a switched pistol empties fast, three; the box SMG four. A burst costs Battle Brothers'
   Aimed Shot, 7 AP: one a turn and a step. EACH ROUND IS ONE OF WHAT HE CARRIES: automatic fire burns the company's rounds three
   times as fast, which is the real lesson (and rule 478's scarcity doing its work: the stock pays for spraying).
3. THE BELT (the machine gun): a burst of six that TRAVERSES: two rounds at the man aimed at and two at each tile beside him across
   the line of fire (shape 'Traverse', kind 'across', count 3: his 'a machine gun is comparable to a greatsword'). A belt change is
   the handgonne's Reload, 9 AP: a whole turn. A 100-round belt (half the M249's 200 box; the soft 100-round pouch is real). Heavy:
   his weight -18 (the M249 at about 10 kg loaded against the handgonne's -14, Battle Brothers' heaviest ranged row).
4. THE ROCKET: one round, the BLAST (shape 'Blast', kind 'blast': the tile hit and its eight, Battle Brothers' Fire Pot's seven on
   squares), every man of the other side in it his own roll (rule 117: a sweep strikes only the other side; Battle Brothers' pot
   burns friend and foe alike, ours keeps Paolo's 117), the handgonne's reload (9 AP: fire, reload and move never in one turn). The
   reloadable launcher refills from the stock at a dear price a rocket; the throwaway tube is the FIRELANCE: one use a fight,
   refilled after the fight from the stock.
5. THE CHARGE DRONE (rule 490): LAUNCH (Battle Brothers' Throw Fire Pot, 5 AP) sends a drone at a man up to 8 tiles off: it flies
   in that turn and hovers, its tiles lit (his tile and his eight, the Fire Pot's), and it blows at the start of the operator's
   NEXT turn on whoever is still on them (rule 490(a)). A man who steps off escapes; a gun can shoot it out of the air first (a
   shotgun best, the Ukrainian finding); a melee man cannot touch it (rule 490(d)). If it lands it is close to a one-hit kill on
   most men (rule 490(b): armour and cover cut it; heavy plate may live). DROP THE OPERATOR AND HIS DRONES FALL (rule 490(c)). The
   drones are Battle Brothers' THROWABLE ITEMS: two in a kit, never refilled from rounds; a kit is bought again. The charge's
   number is draft and TUNING's research (rule 490: "TUNING's research for (b)'s number").
6. THE SCOUT DRONE: SPOT (4 AP, the Quick Shot's) hangs a drone over the board: for two of his turns his whole side sees every man
   of theirs (no dark tile hides them) and his side's shots at them take +10 (the drone corrects the fire, NPR). Its own row, no
   charge.
7. SIGHTS: a NIGHT SIGHT takes the night's blindness off his shot (the dark tile's -30 percent, Battle Brothers' Nighttime); a HEAT
   SIGHT does that and sees through smoke (the cell vest's smoke, ours.cell_vest). A row key, 'sight'.
THE NUMBERS OF EACH PIECE: as ARMORY 1's rule, each piece sits between two Battle Brothers rows of its family (the mean), so every
number traces to two wiki pages, and the tool refuses a copy. Automatic pieces sit on LOWER rows than the hunting rifles (a 5.56
or a 9 mm round is lighter than a .30-30 or a deer round): what makes them strong is the rounds a press, not the round.
DAMAGE ON THE OLD GUNS: UNTOUCHED (rule 489(d)). The research Paolo asked written for his decision on gun damage is BESTIARY 2's
and TUNING's (rule 489(d) names it); this lane adds nothing to the old rows.
