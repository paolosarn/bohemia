## THE RULING (rule 238)
(a) ALL THE BUTTONS LIVE ON THE PHONE: every button he presses on the map, in the town and in the fight sits on the phone, in his
    corner (rule 213), and nowhere else on the glass, except the town's own buildings he taps and the board's tiles.
(b) THE PHONE NEVER GROWS: one size, always (rule 213). No fix for any UI problem is a longer or bigger phone. When more must fit,
    the pieces get SMALLER (tiny is fine, his words: 'don't be scared'), or they page or scroll inside the phone, never past its
    edge. Small but tappable: icons can be small on the glass; the place a finger can hit may reach a little past the icon, never
    over another button.
(c) A BUTTON IS AN ICON, NOT A COLOUR: every button reads by its shape (a picture of what it does, rule 224), so it reads in black
    and white; colour only adds (the blood, the danger), never tells two buttons apart by itself.
(d) WHERE HIS JOBS ARE, AT A GLANCE: each held job's card (rule 232) shows which way and how far, and its place is marked on the
    map; never the good jobs nearby, never the valley's best.
(e) THE GATE: the phone's rectangle is the same size in every screen, with zero, one and two jobs, phone and computer; no button
    outside it on the map, the town or the fight (taps on buildings and tiles aside); every button's icon tells it apart from the
    others in greyscale; each job card points to its job.
