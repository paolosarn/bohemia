================================================================
POST-MORTEM — CASINO AND RESORT, AND WHY IT IS NOT AN ART FAILURE
================================================================
The two kills are the two most Vegas things on the board, judged by the
man who set the game in Vegas. That is not a coincidence and it is not
about the pixels.
THE WORLD LANE ALREADY FOUND THE CAUSE, FIVE DAYS EARLIER, IN ITS OWN
COMMIT TITLE (17b1d49c, 8/15):
    "THE STRIP AND THE RESORTS DO NOT EXIST AS PLACES —
     THAT IS WHY THEY HAVE NO ART"
An icon is a portrait of a place. Every other icon in this batch depicts
something the world has actually built: a fort, a dam, a prison, a
convention hall, a go-kart track. CASINO and RESORT depict places that
have never been designed — no footprint, no interior, no what-it-is-for.
So the artist was drawing a logo for a building nobody has built, and the
only available reference was the real Las Vegas, which is the one thing
this game is not (ours is ten years after the money died).
THE LESSON, AND IT IS THE ONE TO CARRY: **DO NOT COOK AN ICON FOR A PLACE
THAT DOES NOT EXIST YET.** The icon is downstream of the design. Cooking
it first produces a picture with nothing behind it, and he can feel that
even when he cannot name it — which is exactly what "NO" means here.
WHAT A FRESH COOK MUST NOT REPEAT: it must not be attempted at all until
the Strip and the resorts EXIST AS PLACES in the world — footprint,
interior, purpose, what a player does there ten years after the crash.
When they exist, the icon will have something to be a portrait of.
NOTE ON THE STRIP ICONS THAT SURVIVED: strip and strip_x came back COULD
BE BETTER, not NO. So the Strip as a SHAPE reads; the CASINO as a
building does not. That is a useful split and it points the same way —
the silhouette of the place is fine, the institution inside it is empty.
