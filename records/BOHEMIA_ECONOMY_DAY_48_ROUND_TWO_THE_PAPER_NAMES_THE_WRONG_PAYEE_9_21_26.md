### THEN THE CADENCE, AND IT IS THE WHOLE ANSWER

    a 30-day month of walking                 120 real minutes = 2.0 REAL HOURS

    monthly notices before day 67, where the
      world runs out of things to say (round 19)         2

**A monthly notice never once appears inside the only five minutes anybody measures, and it
appears twice in the entire lifespan of the content this game currently has.** That is not a
mechanic, it is a rumour.

### AND THE OTHER CLOCK IS WORSE IN THE OTHER DIRECTION

A paper on that clock is a ticker, and the feed module's own header already says why a ticker
is the bug: a source that re-states the world every beat is a status bar, not a feed.

    on a monthly clock       0 in five minutes, 2 in everything we have
    on the feed's clock      75 in five minutes

## 3. *** THE FINDING: THE PAPER NAMES THE WRONG PAYEE. ***

The form WORDS shipped says **"Reconnection fee: one battery."** Correct format, correct law,
correct amount. **And the office cannot take it, because two shipped rounds already decided
who can.**

- **Round 47, measured over ten seeds:** a water plant runs only if the street it fronts is
  live, streets are held ground, and **in every valley that has running water at all, exactly
  one faction holds all of it.** Four valleys in ten boot with nobody having any.
- **Round 48 round one, measured:** the only charge in this game with a sender is `rentOn`,
  and the sender is a faction standing on its own ground.

**So the live claim on the wire belongs to whoever holds the street, and the paper names an
office.** Not a lie, not a trick, not written by anybody to deceive: the form was printed when
the office was the payee and the form has not been updated because there is nobody to update
it. **The bill is correct about a world that ended.**

That is the whole effect and it costs one line of nothing: **the frame is ordinary and one
thing in it is wrong** (the analog horror law's own first rule), and the wrong thing is a
payee line on a form, said plainly and never explained. Rule 20(g) caps it at that: a vibe for
fun, not a thesis. Nobody in the game remarks on it.

**And it is the cheapest possible teaching device for the thing this game is about.** A player
who pays the paper has learned nothing. A player who works out that the battery goes to the
faction on the street has learned how the valley actually works, from a piece of paper that
never said a word to him.
