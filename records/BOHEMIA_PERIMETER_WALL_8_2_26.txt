================================================================================
THREE BUGS THAT WERE LIVE, AND NONE OF THEM WERE VISIBLE IN A CONTACT SHEET
================================================================================
1. WB4 WAS BEING SMEARED, AND IT IS THE ONE HE KEPT OUT OF FORTY-EIGHT.
   Batch 2 was 48 candidates; he passed exactly one. WB4 is stored in the bank as
   a 792x264 TILING PREVIEW — the true 44x44 art upscaled 3x and repeated 6x2, so
   it could be judged as a run of wall instead of as a chip. drawPerim() does
   drawImage(im, X, Y, 44, 44), so that entire sheet was crushed into ONE cell.
   One community in thirteen has been wearing a grey smear instead of his wall.
   RECOVERY IS EXACT: the sheet is a pure integer nearest-neighbour upscale, so
   taking the first module and sampling one pixel per block returns the original
   bytes. tools/bohemia_perim_rescue.py asserts every block is solid first and
   REFUSES rather than guess — which it did, correctly, when the caller's first
   attempt guessed the scale from the dimensions and picked 6 instead of 3.

2. A TWO-CELL-THICK WALL DREW TWO WALLS.
   The perimeter is two cells thick where it runs east-west, and every cell was
   drawing its own coping, so the run read as two walls stacked. Fixed with a
   third form: if the cell to the NORTH is also wall, this one is the FACE below
   the coping, not another top. Caught on the real surface, never in the sheet —
   a single tile looks correct either way.

3. GATED COMMUNITIES HAD NO BLOCK ART AT ALL, AND THE GATE COULD NEVER DRAW.
   isSuburbCell() was CELLNAME==='suburb'. But engine/bohemia_suburb.js says in
   its own words: "Three district types share this generator - suburb, gated,
   estate." They emit the same grid codes from the same code. So every gated and
   estate community in the valley fell through to genericTile() and rendered with
   NONE of it: no bought ground, no texture-matched house skins, no window
   openings, no perimeter wall. And code 5 — the gate assembly — exists ONLY on
   the exact districts that predicate returned false for, so the gate mouth could
   never draw anywhere in the game.
   Found by walking to one. The gate screenshot kept coming back empty, the valley
   scan said the nearest estate was 42 cells out, and standing in it showed a
   generic block.

================================================================================
WHAT MAKES A PERIMETER WALL READ AS A WALL AND NOT A STRIP OF TEXTURE
================================================================================
The anatomy is taken from WB4 — the wall he kept when he killed 47 others. It is a
capped wall with a pillar and a shadowed panel, and that is what every tile here
is built on. No pixel of it is copied.

  CAP       a wall is CAPPED. The coping is a horizontal surface, so it faces the
            sky and is the brightest thing on the tile (45 DEGREE ART LAW), and it
            is COURSED — individual cap stones with joints, on an 11px module that
            divides 44 so nothing cuts at the border he circled on 8/1.
  OVERSAIL  the cap is proud of the face, so it CASTS. That shadow is what
            separates a capped wall from a painted stripe.
  PILLAR    panels run BETWEEN pilasters. The pillar is proud, has ITS OWN
            coursing deliberately out of phase with the panel behind it, wears a
            cap that is taller and wider than the wall's, and casts onto both the
            panel and the wall cap beside it.
  GRADE     thirty years of blown dirt splashed up the bottom courses, and the
            weeds that live at the foot of a wall because that is where the runoff
            goes — drawn as BLADES, seen edge-on, not the ground cook's rosette.
  GATE      you LOOK THROUGH a gate. The open mouth shows the ground on the other
            side, in the wall's thickness-shadow at the head and opening out to
            sky-lit dirt beyond. The first version was a near-black rectangle,
            which is the taste canon's named failure and wrong besides. The steel
            variant is a barred leaf still hanging, pickets on 4px centres, rust
            running down off the rails, and the ground behind it dimmed — 12%
            CLUSTERED POWER means nothing over there is lit.

THE GATE IS AN ALPHA OVERLAY IN FOUR PIECES PER KIND. Transparent where the
community's own wall belongs, opaque where the hole is, so one gate matches all
eighteen wall designs instead of one — same argument as the window openings. And
it comes in l / m / r / lr, because the entrance aperture is SEVEN TILES WIDE and
it is ONE gate: repeating a jambed tile across it drew four separate barred gates
side by side (seen at estate cell 8,35).

VERTICALLY THESE TILES DO NOT WRAP, AND THAT IS CORRECT. Every other tile in the
texture-match set is seamless on both axes because it is a FIELD. A wall has a top
and a bottom in the world. So the seam test is HORIZONTAL ONLY — and against the
tile that ACTUALLY follows it, since a pillar's right edge always meets a FACE and
never another pillar. Testing a pillar against itself measured a join that never
happens in the game.

================================================================================
THE MISTAKE WORTH KEEPING
================================================================================
MEASURE THE FINISHED TILE, NOT THE FIELD IT STARTED FROM.
cook_to_target draws a texture field and redraws it until the FIELD lands inside
his measured tolerance. That is the right loop for a texture and the wrong one for
a wall, because the architecture added afterwards changes the number: the cap and
its joints and its cast shadow ADD local contrast, and the capless base form,
which has none of them, LOSES it. Four base tiles shipped at edge below his 14.27
floor while their fields had passed — smooth art getting through on a measurement
of something else. The gate caught it; the cook now redraws from a fresh seed
until the WALL measures right.

Same shape as the light bug: the cap was brightened with `v * 1.30`, then the
pillar brightened the same pixels again with `v * 1.34`, and the coping came out
as pure 255 white — a blown highlight with no material in it, on the most-read
surface of the tile. A multiply has no ceiling. Light is now a BLEND TOWARD THE
SKY, which cannot clip however many times it is applied and keeps the grain
inside the highlight.

================================================================================
GATED
================================================================================
gates/perimeter_gate.py — 55 checks, registered in gates/bohemia_gates.py as
PERIMETER. The ruler is HIS OWN BOUGHT ART, re-derived from the style target every
run so it cannot drift. It holds all three bugs above as regressions, plus: the
cap is the lightest band, it oversails and casts, the base form carries NO second
coping, the pillar is proud AND casting (brightness alone drew a stripe), every
module divides 44, the gate overlays are transparent where the wall belongs and
carry real dark, the wall is drawn BEFORE the hole is punched, his 13 walls are
still in the repo and still loaded, WB4 is rescued and not smeared, and the
shipped bytes are the cooked bytes.
