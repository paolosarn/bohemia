================================================================================
THE PICK IS THE ASK ITSELF, NOT A SECOND GESTURE
================================================================================
The row says "the player picks from a mouth... never a card." Rule 19/20
already killed the only surface a multi-option picker could live on (a
card), and building a brand new button for this would be UI's call, not
this lane's, mid-round, on a surface (the settlement screen) that does not
exist yet. So the pick IS the ask: the person already walking beside you
(rule 32a, [bb company]'s own proven "stay near me and this happens"
grammar) proposes the company's next goal, deterministically, and it is the
company's goal from that beat on. Ignoring it costs nothing -- the
companion simply has nothing new to say until the real facts move, which is
an honest "no" with no penalty, matching rule 35c's free no.

================================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================
engine/bohemia_ambitions.js: a pure module, three GOALS (id, the ask,
the done line, a threshold, two pure check functions reading only the facts
object handed in), one REWARD (currency + amount, both tuned:false), and
four functions (progressOf, offerFor, askLine, doneLine). No roster, no new
save field for progress -- every check reads a fact another module already
owns. offerFor is deterministic: the same facts always propose the same
goal, and a done goal is never re-offered.

The walked city (ctAmbitionBark, wired into the SAME bark ladder every mouth
in this game already goes through, right beside [bb company]'s own barks):
gated on a real companion actually walking with you (CT_WALKS_WITH); when no
goal is active, the next undone one is asked, once; when the active goal's
real condition becomes true, the companion says so and the reward is paid
through the real purse credit() function, once, guarded against a second
payment; then the company has no goal again until the next ask.
