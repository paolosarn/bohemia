## 0. THE ONE SENTENCE
The world does not change when the size does: ONE register (hue 18–47
warm), ONE light law, ONE wrong thing per frame. What changes with size
is WHICH bible rules carry the tone, because a 28 px person cannot act
with his face and a map cannot hold a pose.

## 2. THE CLOSE GRID (the 28 px person's bible)
At one cell the body has no face and four to six colours. So:
- THE TONE MOVES OFF THE BODY AND ONTO THE ROOM. Close-scale horror is
  R4 + R3 + R7: the light that was in the room (a lit doorway on a
  dark street, cell-sized fixtures), the long hold (a still street
  where one thing moves), occupancy wrongness (a lit window the census
  says is empty). The BODY carries silhouette and one accent only.
- THE SPRITE'S FLOOR SPEC (what CHARACTER [small body] cooks to): at
  28 px a person is SILHOUETTE + ONE VALUE SPLIT + ONE ACCENT. The
  runway survives as the two poles read at arm's length (a shoulder
  line or a drape line, one or the other); the faction accent obeys
  the contrast rule's two-channel minimum scaled down (>= 0.15 value
  from the ground OR the 4 px silhouette break, plus the accent).
  Hair is a shape, not strands (the hair laws bind the ASSET, the
  portrait; the sprite gets the haircut's SILHOUETTE only).
- THE FACE LIVES IN THE PORTRAIT. R6 (the still face) binds the talk
  panel and the bust, which the 112 assets now are (rule 34c). A tiny
  walker never fakes a face - two pixels of shadow under a hood beat
  four pixels of wrong eyes.
- THE HONEST GRID IS A BIBLE RULE WEARING ENGINEERING CLOTHES: "the
  drawing agrees cell for cell" is R1's precondition - one wrong thing
  only reads in a frame whose everything else is TRUE. A pasted
  picture over disagreeing cells is the lie that kills the tone.
- WEAR AT CELL SIZE: the hand-made mark (Newgrounds cut) is per-cell
  damage with authorship - a cracked cell next to a whole one, never
  a repeated stamp (the edge-that-drums lesson at floor scale).

## 3. THE MAP (the marker's bible)
The [bb look] card (9/24) stands whole and is this section: the map is

## 4. THE SEAM (the squeeze, both directions)
No new lens effect: the squeeze is the camera moving, between frames,
diegetic or dead (the eras card's flip discipline applies to the zoom
too). The person is one cell at every close zoom and a marker on the
map - he never grows on the way out (rule 34a). The phone appears at
the map and leaves at the street (32c); its arrival IS the seam's
punctuation, and it needs no flash to announce itself.
