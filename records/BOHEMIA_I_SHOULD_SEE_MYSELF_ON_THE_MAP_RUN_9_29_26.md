## THE BUG THAT WAS UNDER IT: THE PARTIES NEVER MOVED IN DAYLIGHT

day is 89.3 blocks over 960 waking minutes, so **one block of travel was 0.93 of a step, floored to
zero, every call**. A street step was 0.008. **Measured: five game hours, 28 parties, 0 moved.**
They only moved across a night's sleep. The remainder is now carried between calls: same distance
per day as the module's own number, no longer thrown away. **Now 28 of 28 move and 18 stand on the
road inside five game hours.**
