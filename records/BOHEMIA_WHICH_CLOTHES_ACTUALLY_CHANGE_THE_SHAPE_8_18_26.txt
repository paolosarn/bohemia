BOHEMIA -- WHICH CLOTHES ACTUALLY CHANGE THE SHAPE
CHARACTER lane, 8/18/26. Measurement, not a proposal. Nothing here is pending Paolo.

WHY THIS WAS MEASURED
---------------------
The six city residents became six silhouettes on 8/17 (STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR, amended
8/15 to govern identity). Five of them separated cleanly. TWO DID NOT: the wide brim
and the poncho sat 0.014 apart on the width profile -- close enough to read as the
same person with the colour stripped out, which is the whole test.

I tried four things to fix it: bigger height spread, belly and shoulder dials pushed
harder, a leg-garment swap, a hat swap. Every one of them moved the number by about
0.01 and none of them closed it. Under STOP PRODUCING (7/26) a fourth version means
the approach is already wrong, so I stopped fixing the attempt.

WHAT I HAD NOT DONE, AND SHOULD HAVE DONE FIRST
-----------------------------------------------
REUSE-FIRST (7/22) says prove nothing in the bank does the job before cooking. I was
one step from cooking a new garment, and I had NOT proved that. The wardrobe holds
202 canon pieces across 12 layers. I had tried maybe fifteen of them.

So tools/bohemia_silhouette_lever.js ranks the ENTIRE wardrobe by how much each
single garment changes the outline: one body, one pose, one garment at a time,
against the same body wearing nothing on that layer, scored with the exact metric
the cast gate uses -- so every number below is directly comparable to the 0.014 gap.

THE RANKING (front / back, top of 202)
--------------------------------------
   front    back  layer    garment
  0.0528  0.0000  back     SHOULDER MANTLE / OLIVE SHOULDER MANTLE
  0.0446  0.0568  outer    WASTELAND DUSTER  (and 8 other long coats)
  0.0303  0.0199  back     ROAD CAPE
  0.0238  0.0227  head     CHINESE RICE FARMER HAT  (and the other wide caps)

  168 of 202 canon garments change the FRONT outline by LESS than 0.014.

THREE THINGS THAT CAME OUT OF IT
--------------------------------
1. THE ANSWER WAS ALREADY IN THE WARDROBE. The SHOULDER MANTLE moves the front
   outline 0.0528 -- nearly FOUR TIMES the gap I could not close with dials -- and I
   had never put it on anybody. A new cook would have been a new garment answering a
   question an existing garment already answers.

2. I HAD BEEN MEASURING THE WRONG DIRECTION. One resident wore a RUCK PACK
   specifically to break his outline. The pack scores 0.0000 on the FRONT. It is a
   back item; it is invisible on somebody walking toward you, which is the direction
   you meet a stranger in. It does not appear anywhere in the top 22. Every score in
   this file is the front for that reason, and the tool prints the back too so the
   difference is visible instead of assumed.

3. THE WARDROBE IS MOSTLY RECOLOURS. 168 of 202 pieces do not change the front
   outline meaningfully. That is not a complaint about the wardrobe -- colourways are
   legal -- it is the measured shape of STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR (7/19): if progress is
   new SHAPES, then 34 of 202 garments are carrying the structural range of the game,
   and a new garment cook should aim at a silhouette nobody occupies rather than at a
   colour nobody has.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE GAME
------------------------
  pack     RUCK PACK        -> OLIVE SHOULDER MANTLE   (front-invisible -> strongest lever)
  poncho   DUST PONCHO      -> ROAD CAPE, id renamed to 'cape'

  closest pair    0.014 -> 0.04     (three times further apart)
  mean spread     0.070 -> 0.093
  gate pins ratcheted: MIN_PAIR_PROFILE 0.010 -> 0.030, MIN_MEAN_PROFILE 0.070 -> 0.085
  gates/city_cast_silhouette_gate.js: 6 passed, 0 failed

The pins are ratcheted because a pin left at the old value would let this regress
silently, and a gate that cannot fail the thing it just caught is not a gate.
