*** THEN THE CONTROL LANDED, AND THE CONTROL IS THE WHOLE FINDING ***
================================================================================
Before believing "this game has no names", I asked whether a name could exist at
all. ONE CALL TO CT_MET.ask, on the person standing at his door:

  before      ctPersonName -> "WATCH"        tier stranger    namesKnown 0
  after       ctPersonName -> "Marisela Escobar"   tier asked    namesKnown 1

And three more keys, cold: Amos Ellison, Perla Nguyen, Renata Duong.

*** THE NAMES WERE ALWAYS THERE. *** 64 given names and 48 surnames have been in
bohemia_people since 7/31, deliberately mixed because Clark County is, and the
law over them is RIGHT:

  "A NAME IS EARNED, NEVER GIVEN."

AND THE EARNING HAD EXACTLY ONE DOOR IN THE ENTIRE GAME: a button called #ctask
on #ctcard. That is the card surface rule 19(a) killed on 9/20. So the law was
not being kept. IT WAS STARVING: nobody could ask, so nobody had a name, and a
law nothing can satisfy looks exactly like a law being obeyed.

AND A THIRD THING WAS BUILT AND NEVER REACHED. ctNames draws a gold name tag
over a person's head with a dark ring so it reads over pale ground and dark
alike. It has drawn NOTHING for the life of the game. Same shape as the face
last round: built, correct, and unreachable.

================================================================================
THE DOOR OFF THE CARD, AND IT IS THE REAL-WORLD ONE
================================================================================
A stranger who walks up to YOU wanting something opens with who they are,
because they want something. Somebody you walk past owes you nothing.

So: an INTERACTIVE moment earns the name, an ambient bark never does. The law is
untouched, the crowd stays strangers, and the law finally has a way to be
satisfied that is not a button on a dead surface.

ONE WRITER: CT_MET.ask, the ledger's own call, the same one the card's button
made, with the same arguments. Nothing here invents a name, stores a name, or
keeps a second list of who is known.
ONE READER: ctPersonName, which the down card, the crew list and the lock list
already read, so a person cannot be called two things on two surfaces. That is
this lane's own recurring defect and it is designed out rather than checked for.

AND THREE THINGS LIT UP AT ONCE off that one bit:
  the gold tag over their head       ctNames, drawing for the first time
  the heading in their own bubble    above their words, beside their face
  every list that names people       same answer, because it is one answer

A NAME LOOKS LIKE A NAME AND A TRADE LOOKS LIKE A LABEL, which is why neither is
transformed: the name arrives mixed-case from the bank and the trade arrives
shouting from ROLE_WORDS. They are different colours too, because one is a fact
about them and the other is a fact about what you do not know.

================================================================================
*** AND FIXING MY OWN INSTRUMENT FOUND A CLAIM THAT COULD NOT FAIL, SHIPPED BY
*** THIS LANE ONE ROUND AGO
================================================================================
ctDeedBark(now) sets BARK.until from whatever its caller hands it, and its ONE
real caller is barkTick, which runs on performance.now(). My probe handed it
ctMinuteNow() -- a game minute, about 360, against a wall clock past 15,000. So
the bubble was ALREADY EXPIRED when the first render ticked it.

THAT IS THE SAME TWO-CLOCKS BUG I FIXED IN THE GAME LAST ROUND, sitting in the
instrument I fixed it with. And it had a cost: FACE AT THE DOOR's control claim,
"before the head arrives the bubble draws with no face", was passing because
NOTHING DREW AT ALL. A claim that cannot fail is worse than no claim.

FIXED, AND THE FIX IMMEDIATELY CAUGHT A SECOND WRONG THING. With the right
clock the control read THREE face draws, not zero: by the time any probe runs,
the boot walk has already barked and the face cache is holding heads. TIME WAS
NEVER THE VARIABLE. The control now takes that speaker's head away and proves
the bubble still draws its words with no face, which is NO FACE, NO SPACE stated
as something that can fail:

  the old control, right clock       3 face draws, RED
  the new control                    0 face draws, bubble drawn: true, GREEN

FACE AT THE DOOR is 31/0 again, and one of those 31 now means what it says.
