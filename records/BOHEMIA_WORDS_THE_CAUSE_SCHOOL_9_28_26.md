## FINDING 1: REAL PEOPLE ARE SHARP ABOUT THE TECH AND FLAT ABOUT THE MECHANISM
Searched first-person accounts of the dot-com bust and the telecom crash (Lucent/Nortel-era
vendor financing, the exact real-world shape of LOCKED #2). Two things repeat:
- THE LOSS IS TOLD IN A NUMBER, FLAT. One worker's account: making $120k at a dot-com, "felt on
  top of the world," then after the shutdown "the highest paying job was $60k" -- "quite
  depressing." No mechanism named. The number carries the whole sentence.
- THE WORLD-ENDING FEELING IS TOLD IN ONE LINE, NOT AN EXPLANATION. On 2008: "2008 was horrific.
  I thought the world was ending." On the dot-com bust: laid-off tech workers gathering daily in
  a San Francisco park because there was nowhere else to be. Nobody in these accounts explains
  circular financing, collateralized debt, or vendor loans -- the people who lived inside the
  mechanism could not see it while it was running, which is the real texture LOCKED #2 already
  banks on ("real capability was built on circular funding... the loop reversed").
SO: a person who lived through the crash does not explain the crash. They report a number, a
feeling, a place they ended up. The mechanism is for the machine's own mouth (the institution,
Q26, THE FIRST NOTICE), never for a person's.

## FINDING 2: THE HEDGE IS ALREADY THIS LANE'S OWN PROVEN RULER, AND IT PREDICTS THE SHAPE
"THE SURER THE WRONGER" (9/24, records/BOHEMIA_WORDS_THE_SURER_THE_WRONGER_9_24_26.md): A TELLER
ONLY HEDGES ABOUT THE PART THEY KNOW THEY LOST. Applied here, not re-derived: people are FLAT and
CONFIDENT about the technology (they used it, they remember exactly what it did, they can
describe the demo, the ad, the day the office got the good coffee machine) and HEDGY or SILENT
about the financial mechanism (nobody says "vendor financing" in a testimony; they say "it just
fell apart" or "then everything stopped"). This is the same asymmetry the rumour-frame school
already measured in a different system, now confirmed in the real record: certainty tracks what
a person actually witnessed, and the mechanism was never witnessed by anyone at street level. IT
MAKES HIS "MORE FUN" NOTE A CRAFT INSTRUCTION, NOT JUST A MOOD: the confident, detailed, almost
proud half of every line is the tech; the flat half is the money, because that is also the
truthful half.

## FINDING 3: HINDSIGHT BIAS IS THE WRONG DETAIL TO PUT IN A MOUTH, AND A GIFT WHEN INVERTED
Real research on the 2008 and dot-com crashes: after the fact, "everyone saw it coming" becomes
the dominant story, and it is FALSE -- surveys from 2004-2007 show most economists did not
predict 2008; the people now claiming certainty are remembering wrong, not lying. Two uses:
- A LINE THAT CLAIMS FORESIGHT IS A LIE THE SPEAKER BELIEVES, not a fact the world confirms --
  usable as rule 20h's "one wrong detail, never explained": someone says "I always said it was
  too good to be true," and nothing in the scene backs that up or calls it out. The horror is
  that he is wrong about his own memory and will never find out.
- INVERTED, IT IS A GIFT FOR THE DEAD-INSTITUTION REGISTER (rule 20h's "a voice that is too
  even"): the machine's own record (a notice, a report, a sign) can carry the actual
  contemporaneous confidence -- projections, a launch date, a rate of return -- stated in the
  same calm register as everything else that institution says, so the wrongness is never
  flagged, only sat beside what the world became.

## FINDING 4: THE CRAFT PRINCIPLE THIS LANE ALREADY OWNS, RESTATED FOR THIS ROW
General environmental-storytelling craft (no new reference game named; this is a genre
principle, not a title): THE ENVIRONMENT SHOWS WHAT HAPPENED, A MOUTH SAYS WHY IT MATTERED TO
THEM. That is rule 19 and rule 29 already: no lore card explains circular vendor financing to
the player; a person's one line says what it cost them, and the mechanism stays implied by the
world around it (a dark datacenter, a warrant-relic, the phone's own dead ads). This is the same
shape [horror ask] already shipped and proved: "Somebody still chalks the meter box every month.
Never seen who." -- ordinary, unexplained, no card. [the cause] asks for the same shape aimed at
a different wound: not the utility still running, but the wonder that was real once.
