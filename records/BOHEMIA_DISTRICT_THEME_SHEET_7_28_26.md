# THE DISTRICT THEME SHEET — 36 HOOKS, ONE PER TYPE (7/28/26, WORLD lane)

Ordered by Paolo 7/28: *"each grid each district should feel like its own landmark…
unique district themes for the types that you need diverse and fun."*
Law: `laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_EVERY_DISTRICT_IS_A_LANDMARK_7_28_26.md`

**THE TEST EVERY ROW HAS TO PASS:** could a player describe this cell to another player
in five words and be understood? *"The one with the drained pools."* If two rows could be
swapped and nobody notices, both rows failed.

**ACT 1 ONLY.** Every hook below is the dead world as it stands. No act-2 anything.

**These are PROPOSALS, not canon.** He asked me to make them, so I made them — but
CONTENTS-PAOLO'S: he approves, edits or kills. Nothing here is being built until he
picks. Marked ★ = the ones I would build first.

---

## THE RULE I USED

Every hook is built from **one silhouette + one frozen event + one thing you can only do
here.** Generic districts fail because they have a programme (a school has classrooms)
but no hook (a school where the buses never came). The silhouette makes it readable at
map zoom; the frozen event makes it a story; the third makes it worth walking into.

## RESIDENTIAL

| type | THE HOOK (silhouette · frozen event · only-here) | notes |
|---|---|---|
| **suburb** ★ | **THE WALL AND THE ONE GATE.** A walled subdivision seen from outside is a blank stucco ribbon with one broken gate. · The gate arm is down and the guard booth is empty. · You are *inside* somebody's whole life, house after house. | already approved; needs the wall to read from outside |
| **apartment** | **THE COURTYARD POOL.** Three storeys wrapped around one drained kidney pool. · Balcony furniture blown into the deep end. · The only vertical stack of homes — you go UP. | approved; the pool is the hook it does not have yet |
| **trailer** ★ | **THE EMPTY PADS.** Half the lot is concrete pads, hookups and skirting where homes were **towed away**. · The ones that left, left; the ones that stayed, stayed forever. · Reading which is which. | rejected. This is the foreclosure story and it is free |
| **town** | **THE WATER TOWER OVER A ONE-STREET WALL.** · The sign came down across the street. · The only place with a *main street* to walk down. | approved. He asked why a water tower — it is the tallest thing in every real desert town and the reason the town exists |

## THE FIVE I WOULD BUILD FIRST, AND WHY

1. **waterpark** — went from *"so fucking terrible"* to potentially the best-reading
   district in the game with no new tech. Slides are an unmistakable silhouette.
2. **farm** — his own correction, factually right, and the centre pivot is the single
   strongest shape available.
3. **golf** — the dead-green-against-desert boundary is a free, gorgeous silhouette.
4. **trailer** — the towed-away pads are Vegas's real story and cost almost nothing.
5. **firestation** — open doors, empty bays. One image, whole story.

Every one of the five is a **shape change, not an art change**, so all five can be built
and gated before a single tile exists.
