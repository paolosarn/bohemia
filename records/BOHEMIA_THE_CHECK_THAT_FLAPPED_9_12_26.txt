================================================================================
*** THE REAL DEFECT IS WHY IT READ RED FOR ONE LANE AND GREEN FOR ANOTHER ***
================================================================================
The browser pass walks the city from (8,8) outward and asks the FIRST spanglish
speaker it reaches, then judges the whole law on that one answer. That is a real
check on the real surface and it stays.

BUT ONE SAMPLE OFF A SPATIAL WALK IS A COIN FLIP WEARING A CLAIM'S CLOTHES.
Which person you reach first depends on what else is in the tree, so ECONOMY read
this RED two rounds running and this lane reads it GREEN, on the same law, with
nobody's content at fault. A check that answers differently depending on who is
looking teaches nothing and gets ignored -- which is exactly what happened for
two rounds, and is why a row got written asking a lane to fix a thing that was
not broken.

THE FIX: the gate now ALSO sweeps the quirk organ directly. 4000 people, both
light states, and every spanglish line they can say must carry a Spanish word.
Fast, no browser, and it cannot flap.

REPOINTED, NOT LOOSENED, and strictly stronger. A green used to mean "the one I
bumped into was fine". It now means "no spanglish speaker in the valley can
answer without Spanish".

================================================================================
THE LESSON, FOR WHOEVER WRITES THE NEXT CHECK
================================================================================
A CHECK THAT SAMPLES ONE THING OFF A WALK IS NOT A CHECK, IT IS A WEATHER REPORT.
It cost two lanes two rounds each and produced a board row aimed at the wrong
target. Anywhere a claim picks "the first X it finds" on a surface whose contents
vary, the claim should sweep the organ underneath as well. The browser pass
proves the wire is live; the sweep proves the rule holds.
