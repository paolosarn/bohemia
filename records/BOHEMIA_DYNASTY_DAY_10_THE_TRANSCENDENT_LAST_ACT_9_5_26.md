## 2. THE REAL AISLE (a) -- A NEW RULE SET IN THE LAST ACT DESTROYS THE PLAYER'S EXPERTISE
The learning literature is blunt about this. **Near transfer** -- between domains
that share surface features and structure -- works reasonably often. **Far
transfer**, between loosely related domains, *"has been difficult to document in
experiments, industrial psychology, education, and research on analogy,
intelligence, and expertise."* Theories of expertise emphasise that learning is
**domain-specific**.
And the sting is the part nobody expects:
> *"The longer you train a specific skill, the more you get proficient in things
> that are highly specific for a particular skill, and being an expert in
> something might limit the potential of transfer."*
**Mastery makes transfer HARDER, not easier.** So after ninety-odd hours of
street-level fighting on the beat, a final act with genuinely new rules would
hand the player a domain their expertise cannot reach.
> *** A NEW KIND OF POWER IN THE LAST ACT MAKES THE PLAYER A BEGINNER AGAIN AT
> THE EXACT MOMENT THE STORY SAYS THEY HAVE BECOME A GOD. ***

## 3. THE REAL AISLE (b) -- AND POWER HAS A MEASURED COST, WHICH IS PERSPECTIVE
A well-known set of experiments on power and perspective-taking:
- Asked to draw an **E on their own forehead**, people primed with high power
  drew it **facing themselves** -- legible to them, backwards to anyone looking
  at them. Low-power people drew it for the reader.
- High-power participants **failed to account for other people not knowing what
  they knew**, anchoring on their own vantage point.
- High-power participants were **less accurate at reading other people's
  emotions.**
> **POWER DOES NOT MAKE YOU CRUEL. IT MAKES YOU UNABLE TO SEE FROM WHERE ANYBODY
> ELSE IS STANDING.**

## 6. *** THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG ***
### THE INSTINCT FOR A TRANSCENDENT ACT IS NEW POWERS, AND NEW POWERS ARE THE ONE THING THAT WOULD BREAK IT
A last act that hands the player a new body with new verbs is the obvious read of
"transcendent", and section 2 says it is the read that throws away everything
they spent the game learning. The player would arrive at the most important hour
of a hundred-hour game as a novice.
**AND WE ALREADY HOLD THE CORRECT ANSWER, IN HIS OWN ZOOM LAW, WRITTEN FOR A
DIFFERENT REASON.** One camera, one scalar, the same diamond at every band, no
dead ends.
> **THE RULES MUST NOT CHANGE. THE SCALE CHANGES. THAT IS NEAR TRANSFER, WHICH
> WORKS, INSTEAD OF FAR TRANSFER, WHICH DOES NOT.**
Same verbs, further out. The player who mastered a street keeps every hour of it,
and what is new is what those same actions now touch.
### AND THE COST IS ALREADY DRAWN ON THE SCREEN
At planetary zoom a person is not visible. That is not a limitation to design
around, **it is the measured cost of power rendered as a camera.** Rounds 6, 8
and 9 all found that this game's real subject is people who remember you: the
witness web, what got retold, who still says your name. **Act 3 is the act where
you can finally act on everything and can no longer see a single one of them.**
That is the transcendence and the tragedy in one scalar, and it needs no stat, no
new verb, and no damage number.

## 7. WHAT WE TAKE AND WHAT WE REFUSE
**TAKE**
- **No new rule set.** Act 3 keeps the verbs. Whatever is new is what they reach.
- **The scalar is the mechanic.** One gesture, already built, already on his
  phone, already reversible until the moment canon says it is not.
- **Losing sight of people as the price of altitude.** Measured, thematic, and
  free: it is what the camera already does.
- **The one-way step as the only irreversible thing in a game with no runs.** His
  law already says no band may be a dead end, and then says the moonshot is
  one-way. Those are not in conflict: **the whole game is reversible right up
  until the single moment that is not.** That is where an ending gets its weight.
**REFUSE**
- **A new moveset for the Angel.** Section 2 is the whole argument.
- **Bigger numbers.** Day 9 and round 4 already ruled growth horizontal, and a
  final act that scales damage is the power-creep failure round 4 measured.
- **A different camera or a second projection.** His 7/25 law and the zoom law
  both forbid it, and the existing code honours both.
- **Deciding what the Angel is, what happens on the moon, or what the
  Amalgamation is.** All canon, all locked or reserved, and this round reads it
  without adding a word.
- **Treating "transcendent" as a permission to change genre.** The impossibility
  is the point of act 3; the impossibility is not an excuse for a new game.
