FOR DIRECTION AND COOK, BEFORE 280 PIECES ARE COOKED AGAINST THE CARD: a belt is
56 pixels and a pair of shades is 76. Four to six hue-shifted steps on 56 pixels
is nine pixels a tone. The step count may need a floor by garment SIZE, or the
small pieces will fail a rule they cannot physically pass. Reported, not decided
-- the card is DIRECTION's and the cooking is COOK's.

READ THIS BEFORE DRAWING A CONCLUSION. The wardrobe PREDATES the card, so a
garment outside the band is not a defect -- it has simply never been cooked to
that number. WHAT THIS MEASURES IS THE PIPELINE: if garments with rich ramps in
their gen functions arrive on the body with the same richness, the wire carries
what COOK sends and the remake is safe to run. A NUMBER IS NOT A FINDING UNTIL
YOU KNOW WHAT IT IS COUNTING (9/5, the round the brown boots were called an
accent).
