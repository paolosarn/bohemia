## THE VERDICT: FAIL, AND THE LIARS NAMED IN COVERAGE ORDER

### THE SPAWN STREET FRAME
1. THE FLAT TAN VOID (about 40% of the frame). One unbroken value with a
   few dots. It is supposed to be a desert lot; it reads as beige paper.
   It sits BELOW the density floor the card already rules (5A: >= 4.5
   colours/kpx) - this is the same too-clean disease EYES measured at
   0.03x on sidewalks, on the demo's own opening screen. -> COOK: hardpan
   family to the density floor.
2. THE ROADBED THAT IS NOT A ROAD (about 30%). The dark surface is the
   crack/rubble texture at wall density with NO ROAD GEOMETRY: no kerb
   line, no travel lane, no double yellow, no parking lane. A street reads
   as a street because of its LINES, not its material (TG-04 names all
   four). -> COOK: an asphalt street family carrying kerb line + double
   yellow + lane width as drawn geometry.
3. THE SIDEWALK THAT READS AS A GARDEN PATH (about 10%). Grey pavers with
   a neat row of weeds, no kerb edge dividing it from the roadbed. A
   sidewalk is a BAND between kerb and wall; without the kerb it is a
   path through a yard. -> COOK: 1px kerb edge, weeds to the seams not
   the centreline.
4. THE WALL WITH NO FEET (the brick band mid-frame). It cuts across with
   no base shadow, so it reads as a texture change instead of a standing
   thing. -> LIFE+CITY layering: standing structures carry a 1-2px ground
   shadow at their base line, always.

### THE FREEWAY FRAME - WHY HE SAW AN OVERPASS
5. THE DECK (about 35%). Pale blue-grey concrete slabs with TRANSVERSE
   joint lines every few tiles, BRIGHTER than the ground beside it, with
   a pier-like tan colonnade pattern at its foot. That is the anatomy of
   a BRIDGE DECK: pale slabs, expansion joints across the direction of
   travel, piers below. A real at-grade Vegas freeway is the opposite:
   DARKER than everything around it, white dashed lane lines RUNNING WITH
   the road, shoulder stripes, a median. The same polarity inversion the
   aerial verdict measured (9/13) - our big surfaces keep landing bright
   where the real thing is dark. -> COOK: freeway surface = dark asphalt
   (the aerial card's road band, 0.22-0.43, sat <= 0.31), white dashes
   longitudinal, joints killed or made 1px and longitudinal; the pier
   pattern is a real overpass-only element and never appears at grade.
   -> LIFE+CITY: at-grade freeway cells must not carry the elevated
   deck's edge treatment.

## THE ONE SENTENCE FOR EVERY LANE THAT DRAWS GROUND
A road is recognised by its LINES and its DARKNESS, not by its texture:
kerb, lane, dash, median - dark ribbon through bright ground. Every
ground family that violates either is on the fix list above, ordered by
how much of his screen it covers.
