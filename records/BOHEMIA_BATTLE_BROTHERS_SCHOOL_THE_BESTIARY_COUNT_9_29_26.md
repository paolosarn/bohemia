## 2. WHAT EACH ARCHETYPE IS IN BOHEMIA (first attempt, draft; his example leads)
Battle Brothers' roster works because every enemy TEACHES one thing (wolves: they flank; goblins: they kite;
orcs: they shove; undead: they do not stop; necromancer: kill him first). Ours keeps the lesson and swaps the skin.
- NECROMANCER -> THE MECHANIC (his words): a man who keeps reviving his robots; kill him and the machines drop.
- WIEDERGÄNGER (the slow dead that get up again) -> SALVAGE DRONES / DEAD MACHINES that reboot unless put down
  properly; the Amalgamation's cheapest bodies.
- ANCIENT DEAD (the legion that holds a line) -> THE OLD ORDER'S MACHINES: security drones and exo-suited
  remains still running the last order they were given; the horror bible's 'nobody left who should be running it'.
- NECROSAVANT (the vampire that jumps) -> the fast machine that relocates; the dial shot is the only answer.
- GEIST (the scream that breaks morale) -> a SIREN / BROADCAST unit; the too-even voice at volume.
- BRIGANDS -> ROAMING CREWS (37e): scavengers, poachers, marksmen, a leader with a name.
- NOBLE HOUSES -> THE FACTIONS' HOME-BASE GUARDS (the Mob, the Trades, the Network, the Reds...), armoured, in colour.
- MERCENARIES / HEDGE KNIGHTS -> OTHER COMPANIES like yours, the rival crew with a runway look.
- BARBARIANS (with beastmasters and unholds) -> THE LAB-BREAKOUT PEOPLE: cult-like keepers who walk with beasts.
- NOMADS / CITY-STATES (guns, mortars, assassins) -> THE STRIP'S CASINO SECURITY and the desert road crews:
  the only faction with working heavy guns and a mortar.
- CULTISTS -> THE AMALGAMATION'S FAITHFUL (act 2 and 3 grow them).
- GOBLINS (kiting, nets, poison, wolf riders) -> KIDS AND RUNNERS on bikes and dirt quads with slings and nets.
- ORCS (mass, shove) -> THE HEAVY: exo-suit men, crash-bar shields, they push you off the tile.
- BEASTS -> DE-EXTINCT ANIMALS, one for one where the fit is exact: Direwolf = the dire wolf (Colossal's real 2025
  pups make this the most real beast in the game); Hyena = the cave hyena; Unhold = the short-faced bear or the
  ground sloth (armoured = with lab plating); Lindwurm = the big monitor lizards Vegas exotic-pet trade keeps; Alp
  = a night thing (the horror line, not an animal); Schrat = the mesquite/Joshua-tree walker (fantasy; a maybe);
  Webknecht = nothing (no giant spiders; a swarm of lab things instead); Kraken = the reservoir's thing (Lake Mead);
  Ijirok = the mammoth. The beasts list proper: records/BOHEMIA_THE_BEASTS_OF_BOHEMIA_DE_EXTINCTION_ROUND_ZERO_9_29_26.md.
- CHAMPIONS -> NAMED MEN with a unique piece of runway gear, on the tracks FACTIONS already draws.
Every row is WORLD's and COMBAT's to knock down; the COUNT is his and stands.
