## THE LAW (rule 349)
1. NOTHING IS BUILT FOR THE SETTLEMENT UNTIL THE RESEARCH ROUNDS ARE DONE AND HE HAS PICKED A DIRECTION: no models, no screen, no data.
   HOME's first build round was stopped before it pushed anything.
2. THE PHILOSOPHY: ONE SCREEN. The player's settlement is one screen he customizes extremely (tall and wide, rule 348). Maybe a second,
   indoor screen comes later as an upgrade, maybe when act two starts: an open question for the research, not a decision.
3. THE BAR: AAA and premium.
4. THE RESEARCH: rounds on the most successful settlement and base games, the one-screen ones above all: what they put on the one screen,
   how a tap builds or changes something, how deep the customizing goes and how easy it stays, what it costs to keep, how it grows and
   shows progress, what makes people come back to it (the itch), and what sales and reviews say worked. Real sources (talks, postmortems,
   interviews, numbers), never memory alone.
5. THE ANSWER TO HIM: two or three directions (A/B/C), each SHOWN as pictures of our one screen, not text (the 9/21 votes), in the VOTE
   tab, with the realistic one first (rule of 8/4). A game named in the research is research, not a reference: only he makes a game a
   reference (CLAUDE.md), so each direction is told in our own words and the game it learned from is named only as its source.
