A REAL SLOT FINDING, NOT GLOSSED OVER: PADDING (outer) and a faction's own coat
(also outer) compete for the same layer. On a faction that already wears a coat,
adding padding REPLACES it rather than layering under it -- true to the wardrobe's
own engine, worth knowing before TUNING prices padding as an addable option. The
cloak (back) and the spike (gear) do not have this problem; both layer cleanly.

A SECOND FINDING, FROM A PICTURE THROWN AWAY: the first try put the cloak on Reds,
whose own duster already runs floor-length -- the cape only showed at the collar and
the open chest, barely readable, even though the silhouette check still cleared the
floor. Caught by looking at the picture, not assumed. Moved to Remnants, whose own
coat ends at the thigh, so the cape shows its full length below the hem. THE CLOAK
READS STRONGEST OVER A SHORT OR NO OUTER COAT -- worth knowing before anybody prices
it as a universal add.

CARTEL, CLOAK + SPIKE: already wears the cloak (steel road cape); the spike layers on cleanly, gear collides with nothing.
MOB, PADDING: wore no coat; the padding is a clean add here -- on a faction that already wears one, it would REPLACE the coat, not layer under it (the finding, stated in the record).
REMNANTS, CLOAK ALONE: wore no back-slot garment and their own coat ends at the thigh; the cloak alone, isolated from the spike, so its own trade (conceals, but flaps and catches) is the one thing changing.

SILHOUETTE CHECK: all three worn-with-attachment bodies compared pairwise, same
16-sample profile the runway work uses; the CLOSEST pair sits 0.0575 apart -- CLEARS
the collapse floor (0.02). Three different people, not one costume shown three ways.

THE TRADE, IN WORDS, TUNING'S TO NUMBER: a cloak conceals but flaps and catches; a
padding adds protection at a cost in fatigue; a spike threatens (a called-shot
deterrent or a morale read) without adding armour of its own; an aketon is comfort,
not a stat, which is why it is invisible. No number is hard-coded on any of these
garments -- rule 36d's own line, no felt number outside TUNING's one table.
