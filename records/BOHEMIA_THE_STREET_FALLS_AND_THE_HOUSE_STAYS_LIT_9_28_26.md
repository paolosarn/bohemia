## 6. THE SPINE IS A FACT, NOT A STYLE

The derive's `reclaimed.lit` is always 0 (*"what relights a circuit is a price and prices are his"*),
so the old circuit's light never changes in any past. The third house, lit with nobody in it, is lit
after a building past, after a smaller one, and after a raid. **The street rises or falls around it and
it never changes.** In the raided panel it is the only warm window left on its side of the road, which
is louder than in any other panel. The factory **refuses** if the derive ever starts moving the light,
because then this sentence stops being true.
