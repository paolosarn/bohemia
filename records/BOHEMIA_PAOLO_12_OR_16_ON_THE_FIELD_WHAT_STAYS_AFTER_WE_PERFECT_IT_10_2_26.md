## THE ENEMY AI IS PART OF THE REBUILD (rule 63b)
Battle Brothers' fight is its AI as much as its rules: brigands hold a line and flank with archers behind, goblins kite
and net, orcs charge and shove, wolves run round the back for the weakest, the dead walk straight and do not stop, the
necromancer stays behind two shielded bodies, barbarians rush, nobles hold formation. Each is a behaviour the library
already names per archetype; the rebuild gives every enemy kind its Battle Brothers behaviour before any skin.
