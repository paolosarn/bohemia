================================================================================
THE CAUSE IS ARITHMETIC, NOT OPINION
================================================================================
The bark bubble lifted itself 0.9 of a CELL above the speaker's cell. But a body
is not drawn in cells. It is drawn on the ZOOM LADDER, and at the zoom the demo
opens on that is 112 pixels against a 44 pixel cell.

  the bubble clears a head only while       lad <= 1.9 x C
  at the door                               C = 44, so the limit is 83.6 px
  a body is                                 112 px
  so the panel covered                      28.4 px of the body, which is the face

AND IT WAS NOT ONE UNLUCKY ZOOM. Checked at every rung the ladder can put him on,
the old lift covered the face at SEVEN OF TEN, worst 102 pixels at C = 64:

  C= 12  body  28px  overlapped by   5.2     C= 44  body 112px  overlapped by  28.4
  C= 17  body  56px  overlapped by  23.7     C= 64  body 224px  overlapped by 102.4
  C= 24  body  56px  overlapped by  10.4     C= 96  body 224px  overlapped by  41.6
  C= 32  body 112px  overlapped by  51.2     C= 16, 31, 63  cleared, by luck

A near-black panel at 0.92 alpha, over the face, every time anybody spoke.

================================================================================
AND THE NEW LIFT IS NOT A NUMBER EITHER
================================================================================
It is the body's own top, which the people pass already computes as
sy + C - lad, minus the bubble's height and the padding it already had. Every
value was already in the function. Nothing was picked.

*** THE LADDER WAS WRITTEN OUT TWICE IN THIS FILE AND THE BUBBLE COULD SEE
NEITHER COPY, SO IT GUESSED. *** That is the real shape of this bug. Two copies of
a rule are how a third thing ends up disagreeing with both. There is ONE now, and
three readers.
