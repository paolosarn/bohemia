## THE RULING
THE FOLD WINS. Newest date wins, the fold has the gate, and its version is the better
mechanic: the heir does not inherit a number, the heir inherits LESS (the ledger already
carries that) and inherits the PEOPLE the parent owed, by name, still standing at the
door. The collector who visits the heir ([collector heir], shipped) is one of those
people. He is not collecting a balance; he is collecting on a memory. The debt as a
count of nights and favours is cleared through the favour module's own writer; the
creditors are kept by name on their own save key. That is what WORLD built and it stands.

enforces a dead man's paper, so the paper dies. What does not die is the lender, who
knows the family and comes back. That is the Lebanese generator collector and the
Russian krysha in ECONOMY's own record: the debt is a relationship, not a balance.
