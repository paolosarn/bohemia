## SLIDE (rule 26)

measurement, built over three rounds. It is deleted, not left as a dead option: two
feels, GRID and SLIDE, default SLIDE, and the stations, the worth-it test and the
drop-a-frame in the body clip are gone with it.

**WHAT DOES NOT GO IS WHY SLIDE WAS NOT ALREADY WHAT HE HAD.** The glide carried a
handwritten four-cell ceiling written 8/23, when a step was ONE cell; rule 16 made a
step twenty-five, so every full step died on its first drawn frame. The chip said
SLIDE, the source said SLIDE, and what he walked was GRID.

Measured on the pixels the game draws (performance.now() stubbed, render() called at
each of the sixty moments a sixty-frame beat lands on, because this box draws at
about twenty): **SLIDE moves the world on 31 of 60 frames. GRID on 0.**

means on screen is one thing: the drawing never skips a compass point. That needs no
notion of a turn at all and nothing inside can game it.
