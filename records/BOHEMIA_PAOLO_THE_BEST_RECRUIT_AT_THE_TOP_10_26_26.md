## BATTLE BROTHERS, AND WHERE WE BEAT IT (rule 33)
Battle Brothers prices a recruit by his background (a Hedge Knight costs far more than a farmhand) and hides his talents (the
stars) until you pay to try him out; the hall has no sort or filter, so finding the best man is reading every card by hand. He
calls that a failure that 'doesn't respect your time'. The level-up is the source of a man's worth: our rules.json experience
block (Battle Brothers' level table to 11, a perk point a level, attributes raised a level, ours FOUR not three; veterans after 11),
and the stars widen each raise's roll.

## THE RULING (rule 216)
(a) A CALCULATOR FOR A RECRUIT'S WORTH: each man at the post gets ONE number, his CEILING: the stats he is expected to reach at
    level 11, worked out from his base stats and his stars through the game's own level-up rule (the same function the crew
    screen's level-up uses, rules.json's experience block, four attributes a level, the stars' wider rolls), the player assumed
    to raise the stats that matter for his kind of fighter (melee or gun). Built from the game's own numbers, never a guess;
    TUNING checks the formula.
(b) THE BEST AT THE ABSOLUTE TOP: the hall lists recruits by that ceiling, highest first, every visit.
(c) THE PRICE BESIDE IT: each card shows his price (his background's wage and hire cost) next to the ceiling, and a cheap man
    with a high ceiling is marked as a BARGAIN (his worth over his price beats the hall's usual); a Hedge Knight type costs more
    and says why.
(d) [COORDINATOR'S DEFAULT, rule 205, his to kill] one tap on the hall's header switches the order between BEST and BEST FOR
    THE PRICE.
(e) The stars and the trait stay shown before he hires (rule 146(e), the trait before you hire); nothing is hidden behind a
    try-out fee.
(f) THE GATE: on every hall, phone and computer: the order is the ceiling's, highest first; the ceiling equals the level-up
    function's expected value within a point for 100 rolled men; BARGAIN marks only men whose ceiling per battery beats the hall's
