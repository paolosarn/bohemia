## ★★★ R-SUMMON — THE ONE HE TOLD ME TO MARK DOWN. NEW MECHANIC, HIS INVENTION.

> "THE BOOK / who owes whom / call in a debt anywhere — what does this mean? can this be
> like a final fantasy summon. **actually thats a fire idea. mark that down some where.
> maybe clout is the mana?** idk"
> — Paolo, 8/7/26

**CALLING IN A DEBT IS A SUMMON.** Not a menu, not a reputation check: you spend something
and a *person* arrives and does something no one else can. Final Fantasy is the named
reference for the shape.

**AND CLOUT IS THE MANA.** He floated it with "idk", so the COST is his to confirm, but the
shape is his and it is recorded: clout is already a locked currency
(`laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_THREE_CURRENCIES_CENTURY_7_26_26.md`), it is already "producible by
buildings, spendable in many ways", and nothing in the game yet spends it on anything
dramatic. A summon is the first real sink for it.

**WHY IT IS GOOD, IN OUR OWN TERMS:** it makes a debt a *thing you hold and spend* rather
than a number that modifies a conversation. That is the same move the stories-spread research
already landed on for reputation (a story is an OBJECT, not a score) and it is why it fits.

**[PENDING Paolo]** whether clout is the cost; what a summon actually does when it arrives;
how many exist; whether they are finite (a debt spent is gone) or recharging. **NOT MINE.**
Nothing about the summon is designed here beyond writing down what he said.
