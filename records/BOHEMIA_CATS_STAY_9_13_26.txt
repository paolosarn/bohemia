================================================================================
THE FREEZE, AND WHY IT IS A GATE AND NOT A SENTENCE IN A RECORD
================================================================================
A LAW WITHOUT A MACHINE GATE IS NOT ENFORCED. "Frozen as canon" written in a file
is not frozen, it is merely unvisited: the next lane to tune a density never reads
this record and nothing says a word. gates/cats_stay_gate.js holds:

  the three kinds, and NO FOURTH        a new animal is a thing he has not seen
  every density number, all 8 districts he approved what he met AT these numbers
  no district gained a district         an addition underneath the freeze
  THE DOG STAYS AT ZERO                 see below
  the per-frame cap, 90                 his item 7 is performance on a phone
  determinism                           see below
  and they still DRAW on the real demo  a frozen table over a dead draw

THE DOG IS ZERO AND THAT IS A DECISION, NOT AN OVERSIGHT. He named the dog FIRST
back on 8/26 and the lane that built the animals still left it out, in writing: a
dog is a BODY, a body is character art, and a lane does not invent creature pixels
because it is in a hurry (45 DEGREE ART LAW, REUSE-FIRST). The gate keeps it at
zero so a hurried lane cannot quietly grant it. A new ruling opens it.

DETERMINISM IS WHAT MAKES A FREEZE MEAN ANYTHING. Which animal lives where is
hashed from the seed and the cell, so the same block holds the same animals every
time it is walked and on every device. If the valley reshuffled between visits,
"the ones he saw" would not be a set anybody could freeze and his approval would
attach to nothing. Nothing in the whole animal pass reaches for a random number.
