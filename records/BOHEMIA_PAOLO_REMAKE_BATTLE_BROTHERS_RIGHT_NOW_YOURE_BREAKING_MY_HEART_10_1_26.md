# PAOLO 10/1/26: WHAT WOULD HAPPEN IF I TOLD YOU TO REMAKE BATTLE BROTHERS RIGHT NOW? YOU'RE BREAKING MY HEART
His words, with VAMILY: "Bro, like what would happen if I told you to remake battle Brothers right now and put it in our
fucking game like why is it so fucking difficult I've seen so many YouTube videos where off of one sentence you can create a
whole game like you're breaking my heart right now"

## THE HONEST ANSWER
The one-sentence games on YouTube are one agent, one file, one sitting, one loop, ugly. They work because nothing stands
between the sentence and the file. We built the opposite: twenty chats, three hundred checkers, a 500 MB repo, and every
chat spends most of its round reading laws, claiming rows, writing records and registering items. That paper is what he
pays for and what he feels as slowness. The fix is to do, for the loop, exactly what the videos do.
