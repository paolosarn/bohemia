WHAT MOVED AND WHAT DID NOT
----------------------------
He said "just be concerned with the coloring", so ONLY the colour moved:
  KEPT   the sign-painter letterforms (F_BRUSH: swelling stems, 8x10, its own counters)
  KEPT   the board, its double frame, and the signwriter's down-right drop shadow
  KEPT   the layout and the tracking
  TAKEN FROM 3   the dark grainy wall, the off-white spray ink, the uneven coverage,
                 the overspray halo and the runs
  GONE   the gold, the brown board, the warm gradient
Widening this into a redesign would have been answering a brief he did not give. The
temptation was real: the gold is the prettiest thing in the ten and it is not what he
asked for.

MY OWN PICK IS SUPERSEDED, ON THE RECORD
-----------------------------------------
I had picked 1, DEAD MARQUEE, and said why. He picked differently. His call ends it, and
the reasoning stays on file only so the next session does not re-litigate it.

WHAT IT TOOK TO PUT IT ON THE FRONT SCREEN
-------------------------------------------
The splash was drawing an UNJUDGED wordmark - renderWordmark(), a GLYPH table baked into
the alpha, italic and sheared with a fault-slip. Nobody ever thumbed it. His logo wins on
that alone; the old renderer stays in the file because other surfaces call it.

TWO THINGS CAUGHT BY LOOKING AT THE REAL PHONE SCREEN, NOT BY A NUMBER:
  1. The first wiring letterboxed a 400x130 logo inside the old 640x170 canvas at
     integer scale 1. It came out SMALL and adrift in a field of dead canvas. The canvas
     takes the artwork's own size now, so CSS lays out the logo itself and it fills its
     box; image-rendering:pixelated keeps it crisp on the phone.
  2. The patch REFUSED to run on its first attempt because '<div id="front">' matches
     twice in the alpha - once as markup and once inside a comment. That refusal was
     correct. A two-match anchor in a 34 MB single-file build is exactly how the wrong
     thing gets rewritten, and this file has been truncated to zero bytes once before.

GATE
----
gates/logo_gate.py, 24 checks, registered as LOGO. It holds the brief (the LETTERFORMS
must differ, checked on the raw glyph bitmaps so a filter-farm cannot pass), the clipping
bug (no logo may run off its own frame), and now the front screen: the bytes on the
splash must BE the judged bytes, blitted without smoothing.


================================================================================
CORRECTION — 8/2. I BUILT IT BACKWARDS AND HE CAUGHT IT.
================================================================================

PAOLO, sending a zoomed screenshot of the GOLD letter: "I told you use this color
and you didn't what the fuck"

HE IS RIGHT. His instruction was:
  "If you can put the coloring of the [Sign] painter exactly as the Punk stencil
   is just be concerned with the coloring"

I parsed it as SIGN PAINTER'S SHAPE + STENCIL'S COLOUR and shipped a white stencil
wordmark on the front screen. The sentence says the opposite, and it says it
clearly once read properly: the thing that stays "exactly as it is" is THE PUNK
STENCIL. The thing being APPLIED is THE SIGN PAINTER'S COLOURING. Gold onto the
stencil, not white onto the sign painter.

Both readings are grammatically available, which is exactly why it should have
been ASKED rather than assumed - a one-word question would have cost nothing and
it cost him a wrong front screen instead.

THE FIX: logo 3 unchanged in every respect - the dark wall, the F_STENCIL cut-plate
letterforms with their bridges, the uneven coverage, the overspray halo, the runs -
wearing logo 5's gold.

AND THE GOLD IS SAMPLED OFF HIS OWN SCREENSHOT, not re-derived. Guessing a second
time was not an option:
    his letter body        rgb(202, 173, 101)   #CAAD65
    his lit top of stroke  rgb(224, 196, 119)   #E0C477
    my shipped mean ink    rgb(205, 174, 101)
Three points off on one channel, which is the JPEG of a photographed phone screen.

ALSO FIXED: the front-screen patch refused this legitimate re-patch because the new
PNG compressed 4 KB smaller than the one it replaced. The instinct was right and the
calibration was wrong - that guard exists to catch the alpha being TRUNCATED, which
has really happened to this file, not to catch a payload swap. It is 2% now.
