## THE RULING
The Vegas lab is ONE source, never the only one. Labs a state or two over (California, Arizona, Utah, Texas, the
Colorado plateau) let their animals out the same week the dollar died, and nature did the rest: animals walk.
The manager's 'the lab was in Vegas because the bones were in Vegas' stands as a story hook, not as the law of
where every beast came from.

## WHAT THE MANAGER ADDS (rule 41): NATURE'S CLOCK IS THE THREE ACTS' CLOCK
- HOW FAR THEY WALK: a wolf pack or a hyena clan covers 20 to 40 km a night and spreads hundreds of km in a
  decade; big cats disperse as young males over 100+ km; camels and horses follow water and grass; a mammoth
  herd moves a few km a day along a river. Between act 1 and act 2 there are 35 years: everything that can
  reach the Mojave has reached it.
- THE CORRIDORS INTO THE VALLEY are real roads and rivers: the I-15 from California (the Cajon corridor), the
  Colorado River from Arizona and Utah (Lake Mead is the water everything ends at), the US-95 from the north.
  A beast's arrival is a road event (RUN [road events]) and a rumour in the feed before it is a fight.
- SO THE ACTS ARE WAVES: ACT 1 has the local escapees and the first arrivals (dogs, cattle, dire wolves, a few
  cats); ACT 2 has established packs, hyena clans, camels and horses in numbers, the lions and bears at the water;
  ACT 3 has whatever the player's line let breed or hunted out, derived from the ledgers like everything else
  (rule 31). Which animals arrive when is WORLD's data file; the derive moves them.
- PROVENANCE IS FLAVOUR: a hyena clan out of an Arizona lab, a mammoth herd walked down the Colorado from a Utah
  reserve, tigers from a California roadside zoo. A mouth can say where a beast came from and be wrong about it.
