## THE RULING (LOCKED)
THE ZOOM RANGE IS A PILLAR OF THE FEEL. Bohemia's camera runs the Pocket City 2 range: from ONE PERSON up close
(a face on a bench, full 112 px art, the barber, the talker) through THE SETTLEMENT SCREEN (a place with its
buildings you tap) through THE BLOCK to THE WHOLE VALLEY on one screen, as one continuous pinch with the same HUD
at every level (rule 24) and no mode. The near end is LOOKING, not walking (38b stands: you do not move him tile
to tile; the camera moves). Where our rules differ from Pocket City 2 and stand: the person is one pixel size on
the surfaces he is drawn on (21, 34: he does not grow with the zoom; at the far end he is the party marker, not a
scaled sprite); the map is where travel happens (33).
POCKET CITY 2's department widens from 'the drop-in transition only' (7/1) to THE DROP-IN TRANSITION AND THE ZOOM
RANGE: how far in, how far out, and that it is one motion. Nothing else of it (its look, its building, its
economy) is a reference; 43 already refused its edit-everything route.
