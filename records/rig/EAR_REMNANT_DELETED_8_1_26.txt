THIS IS THE ONLY EDIT EVER MADE TO PAOLO'S PAINTED RIG BY CLAUDE, and it happened
only because he was asked a direct question and gave a direct answer.

RIG LAW: "Paolo's painted regions are SACROSANCT: never reshape, mesh, mirror, or

WHAT WAS WRONG — HIS DATA, NOT THE RENDERER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7/31, his words: "biggest changes are for ne and nw where i tried to simulate ears
but ur redarded so i just removed it."

The removal was incomplete. Four pixels survived on each side, and where he
cleared one it left a real gap in the skull:

    NE, column 32              NW, column 23
      row  8   FACE              row  8   FACE
      row  9   FACE              row  9   FACE
      row 10   (HOLE)            row 10   (HOLE)
      row 11   head              row 11   head
      row 12   FACE              row 12   FACE
      row 13   FACE              row 13   FACE

8/1, his report: the head "looks like the head is cut off ... and it's very strange
at some cardinal directions." That vertical dark slot on the back-three-quarter
views is this column. FACE pixels on a view where no face is visible take face
shading; the hole takes nothing at all.

His 7/31 face export was otherwise applied byte-for-byte in all 8 facings —
verified part by part against records/rig/RIG_FACE_7_31_26.json before touching
anything. The defect was in the source, not in the transcription.

WHAT "DELETE THEM" WAS TAKEN TO MEAN, AND WHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Deleting the pixels OUTRIGHT would have been a second unauthorised edit. They sit
one column IN from the skull's edge (col 33 on NE, col 22 on NW are the true
edges), so removing them would punch a channel through the back of the head.

What he is deleting is the EAR, not the skull. So:

    the four FACE pixels  ->  HEAD   (part 2 -> part 1)
    the one HOLE          ->  HEAD   (nothing -> part 1)

Ten pixels changed across both facings. Nothing added to or removed from the
outline. The result is a solid, continuous skull with no ear and no gap.
