# COOK FIVE: THE COOLING CENTER AND THE DOME WEAR THEIR OWN PIECE (11/17/26)

The coordinator's routine 'COOK FIVE full sweep' (VAMILY rules 453, 441, 408, 409(c)): '... the bus yard and the greenhouses,
WORLD 14's two newest places'. BUILD 11/17n - THE COOLING CENTER AND THE DOME. The sweep's third build, on the second's draw (BUILD 11/17k).

## WHAT THIS IS
WORLD FOURTEEN's two newest real places (BUILD 11/14q) wore the kit art of the kind each is like: THE COOLING CENTER a school
like any school, THE DOME a casino like any casino. Each has its own piece now, through the map's rig, in the same frame as
the bus yard and the greenhouses, drawn on its block by slices/bohemia_place_pieces.js (no code changed in this build: two
entries in slices/place_pieces/place_pieces.json and four pictures).

THE COOLING CENTER (the county's Cambridge Recreation Center): the gym's tall block and the rooms beside it, every window
lit cool (the only cool light in the valley), the county's blue banner with its white bar, the chillers on the roof, the
generator on its trailer breathing smoke with its cable across the lot to the open doors, two shade canopies with cots
under them, the water barrels, and the line of five to the door. On the beat: the generator's smoke, the cold light out of
the doors.

THE DOME (the Adventuredome): ten courses of pink glass on a drum, ribbed at its foot and waist, a lantern on its crown, the
coaster's red track out round the glass on its posts and its loop stood at the front, the way in under a dead marquee with
three letters gone. On the beat: the train's lamps, one side of the glass then the other: WORLD FOURTEEN's own line, 'every
night at eleven the coaster under the pink glass runs once, empty, and finishes the run'. Pink is rose here, never purple
(PURPLE RESERVATION: the gate counts 0 purple pixels).

The folder is 99,016 bytes now (eight lossless WebP pictures and the list). Four of the eighteen real places have their own piece.

## WHAT WAS TRIED AND THROWN OUT
THE DOME IN FIVE COURSES read as a stepped cake, not a dome. Ten courses on a round, the ribs kept to its foot and waist.
