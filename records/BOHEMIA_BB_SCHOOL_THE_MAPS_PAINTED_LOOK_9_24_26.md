## 1. SCHOOL: WHY BB'S HAND-PAINTED MAP READS AT A GLANCE
1. VALUE DOES THE WORK. Forest is the darkest paint on the map, plains
   mid, roads a thin lighter worn line over everything they cross, water
   dark and flat. You can grey the frame and still plan a march - the
   palette is muted earth so the few saturated things (banners, the
   marker) win automatically.
2. PLACES CHEAT THEIR SIZE. A village is painted bigger than the terrain
   scale so it reads as a destination at arm's length. Landmark first,
   cartography second.
3. ONE SATURATED MARKER. Your banner is the most saturated object on
   the map; roaming parties are small figure-clusters with banner tints;
   TRACKS are painted footprint chains, tiny but high-contrast, a
   different print per kind - you read danger from the ground.
4. THE FOG IS THREE VALUES: unexplored parchment, explored-but-unseen
   dimmed, the sight radius full. Night multiplies value down and
   visibly shrinks the radius - ambush tension is drawn, not told.
5. WHAT PLAYERS REMEMBER of the look: the night march with something's
   tracks crossing yours. WHAT THEY COMPLAIN about: the map reads the
   same everywhere after fifty hours - painted variety runs out.

## 2. THE CARD: OUR VALLEY FROM ABOVE, UNDER THE BIBLE
THE ONE DIFFERENCE FIRST: BB's map is an ILLUSTRATION; ours is THE
WORLD SEEN FROM ABOVE - the aerial card's resampled truth (derived,
never redrawn), the rim (2B), the eras card on top. The map is the
city view we have; nothing is repainted to look like a map.
1. VALUE SEPARATION IS ALREADY OURS: ground brightest, roads darkest
   ribbons, roofs warm between (the aerial card's own bands). BB's
   lesson 1 needs nothing new - the greyscale test in the aerial card
   IS that lesson.
2. NO SCALE LIE. One house is one house at both sides of the switch
   (the 9/7 stop). A destination reads not by cheating size but by
   BEING ALIVE: occupancy light at night, smoke and steam by day,
   its sign lit if it has power - diegetic landmarks (R4: every lumen
   names its fixture; R7: drawn from world data). A dead block reads
   dead; that too is information.
3. THE MARKER LIVES ON THE GLASS, NOT THE WORLD. The cracked iPhone is
   the map's chrome (32c); the party marker, the tapped destination
   and the route line are drawn by the PHONE on its screen. The world
   itself keeps the crowd rule: a party on the ground is 2-3 px dots
   moving. Nothing saturated is ever painted onto the valley.
4. TRACKS ARE RULED ALREADY and stay: fade along the length, brightest
   end is the head, the holder's own ink, thin marks never fills
   ([judge tracks] PASS, 9/13). A print per kind when PEOPLE's rows
   need it - the footprint bank COOK just voted through is the pixel
   source.
5. THE FOG IS THE DEAD INSTITUTION'S STALE DATA. Not parchment, not a
   lens veil (R8: nothing sits on the lens). The phone shows what the
   Power Authority's sensors still report: blocks nobody has walked
   render on the GLASS from last-known data, dimmer, in the console's
   own phosphor - the map that is wrong because nobody is left to
   update it is the horror form of fog, and it lives in the phone's
   drawing, never on the world's pixels.
6. NIGHT is the cloud rule's arithmetic: value multiplied, nothing
   else; the reported radius shrinks on the glass.
7. FACTION COLOUR ON THE GROUND stays the territory card at every
   zoom: one-pixel rims on the holder's side, marks under 2%, fills
   never, purple the Amalgamation's alone.
8. THEIR COMPLAINT, OUR ARMOR: BB's late-game sameness is painted
   variety running out. Ours cannot run out the same way - the valley
   is one real city whose districts differ by real land use (the
   dossiers), and the eras card changes the same ground over time.
   Truth, not a variety pass.
