## THE ANSWERS GIVEN (and now the rules)
1. YES ON THE IPHONE: one painted pixel per screen pixel is drawing at the phone's real resolution instead of a
   third of it; the map is painted once into a cached picture per zoom level and only what moves is redrawn per
   beat. PLUMBER [native map]: make the map canvas native and prove 60 on a phone with the cache; the whole map is
   never repainted per frame.
2. FLASH FRIENDLY = plays off one link in a browser, no install, small and fast. That is what the game is. THE
   BUDGET (PLUMBER [flash light], measured every round): the demo loads under 10 MB and shows its first frame
   under 5 s on a phone on cell data (a Newgrounds game was 2 to 10 MB).
3. ROBLOX FRIENDLY = kids on cheap phones jump in with no setup: same answer, yes. Literally inside Roblox: not
   now; Roblox is its own engine and language, a port is a rebuild; nothing we do prevents one later (data files,
   small 2D art). Roblox is NOT a reference game; he asked a question, he did not name one.
4. PROCEDURAL, LIKE BATTLE BROTHERS. Two readings, both true and both on the board:
   (a) THE WORLD IS GENERATED PER NEW GAME, on Vegas's real bones. The valley's shape, its roads and its named
       places are Las Vegas (canon); everything PLACED on it is rolled at a new game: which of the fourteen parts
       are home bases and whose (37e already says 'generated'), the camps and ruins, the roaming crews, the
       contracts, the prices, the names, the three prepared descendants' bank. Two games are two valleys. WORLD
       [bb places] and FACTIONS [home bases] carry it; RUN's demo rolls one seed and pins it.
   (b) THE MAP IS COMPOSED, NOT PAINTED AS ONE IMAGE. Battle Brothers builds its map from small hand-painted
       tiles and decals at native resolution, so the pixel count is a screen fact, not an asset-size problem.
       Ours is the same: tiles and markers composed at native resolution, cached per zoom. That is why the
       pixel floor costs nothing on a phone and nothing on the 10 MB budget.
