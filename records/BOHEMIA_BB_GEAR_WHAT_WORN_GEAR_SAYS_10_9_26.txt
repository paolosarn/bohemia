SCHOOL (reference/library/battle_brothers/04_ARMOUR_SHIELDS.md): Battle Brothers reads a
man's kit at a glance by TIER AND SILHOUETTE, not colour. Rags and padded cloth read
soft and thin, mail and lamellar read bulkier, a great helm covers the whole head, a
shield widens the arm. The hex board is small and the camera is often far; the game
stays readable because the SHAPE survives being small, every time. BB never counts on a
player being close enough to read a colour.

THE SHAPE FOR US, MEASURED: the runway thirteen is our version of that kit-reading, a
faction's look is how a stranger knows who they are before they are close. Shot all
thirteen at this lane's real 112 render, then downsampled with the engine's own
nearest-neighbour rule to 28 px, this repo's own precedent for a small figure (rule 34).
Not a claim about the live map camera's exact zoom; an honest proxy for how small before
the shape has to carry the identity alone.
