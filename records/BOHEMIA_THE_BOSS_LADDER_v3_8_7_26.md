## WHAT HE CHANGED, AND IT IS BIGGER THAN A LONGER LIST

**1. ★★ BESTING IS NOT KILLING. EVERY BOSS CAN BE DEFEATED PEACEFULLY.** He said "killing or
defeating peacefully" and he used the word "besting", not killing. So the route is a real
choice on every single one.

**2. ★★ AND THE TWO ROUTES GRANT DIFFERENT THINGS. THAT is the "whatever direction".**

| | |
|---|---|
| **KILL → YOU TAKE THE THING.** | The forge, the valve, the keys, the vehicle. Immediate, certain, yours. You own a capability outright and nobody can take it back. |
| **SPARE → YOU GAIN THE PERSON.** | They make things you never could, teach what dies with them, run it while you are elsewhere, or become a debt you can spend later. Ongoing, and better — but they have to survive, and they can be lost. |

One boss, two futures, and the player picks. **That is a way to build the game in whatever
direction, and it doubles the ladder without doubling the bosses.**

**3. MY LOCK/KEY RULE WAS TOO NARROW.** v2's grants were all world-verbs. He named the missing
ones himself: *"customize or better equipment"*. So every boss now declares a **KIND**:

- **WORLD** — a new thing you can do to the valley
- **GEAR** — a new category of equipment that can exist at all
- **LOOK** — customization: how you, your camp, or your city can be shaped
- **PEOPLE** — crews, specialists, and summons

**4. WHAT SURVIVES FROM v2, because he said "ur kinda right":** one boss = one lock = one thing
that was impossible and now is not, and no two bosses may open the same lock. That is still the
rule that stopped strip/wreck/toll being three bosses, and `gates/boss_ladder_gate.js` still
holds it across all 34.

## CARRIED FORWARD FROM v2, BECAUSE THE LIVE DOC HAS TO STAND ALONE

**VALHEIM'S FORSAKEN POWERS ARE PASSIVE BUFFS, NOT KEYS.** Eikthyr is −60% stamina use,
Bonemass is −25% physical damage taken, Moder is a permanent sailing tailwind. Not one makes a
previously impossible thing possible. **What actually gates Valheim is the biome's ore** — the
"material vibes" framing he killed on 8/3. **SO THIS LADDER IS NOT VALHEIM'S MECHANISM, IT IS
METROID'S** — the genre this ladder is actually copying is lock-and-key ability gating, where each ability answers a different class of
obstacle. That is where the one-lock-per-boss rule comes from.

**AND HABITABLE IS DEFINED, because he asked what it meant** (*"define habitable so if people
have to shit in a hole in the ground its not habitable"*): every district carries a **FILTH
level**, and while it is high **its population cap is ZERO** — you can build whatever you like
there and nobody stays, because sanitation is the mechanic and not a word. **Habitable means a
settler will accept a bed there.** THE DRAIN is the only thing that lowers it.

## THE RESEARCH ON THE KILL/SPARE SPLIT

- **SIFU** is the closest shipped model: sparing a boss unlocks a **Talisman**, one per boss, a
  whole parallel progression track you only get by not killing — and sparing the final boss
  gives an outfit. Mercy is not a moral pat on the head, it is a **second reward tree**.
- **The general shipped pattern**: spared bosses "pay tribute in the form of items that are
  usually superior to what you would get by killing them." So **the spare route pays better and
  costs more skill.** That is the balance lever, and it is his to set.
- **BALDUR'S GATE 3**: staying a blade earns you a **party member**. Sparing gains a *person*,
  which is exactly the split above.
- **SHADOW OF MORDOR's Nemesis system**: dominating rather than killing turns an enemy captain
  into **your** captain. Already the reference in our own 8/3 stories-spread research.

**WHAT WE ARE NOT COPYING: a morality meter.** No karma bar, no good/evil axis. The choice is
economic and practical — a thing now, or a person over time.

| | ACT 1 | ACT 2 | ACT 3 | TOTAL |

| **WORLD** — a new thing you can do | 5 | 7 | 4 | 16 |
| **GEAR** — equipment that can now exist | 4 | 2 | 0 | 6 |

| | 11 | 14 | 9 | **34** |

GEAR front-loads into act 1 because equipment is a body-scale problem. PEOPLE back-loads into
act 3 because a city that runs itself is the act-3 subject. That is the escalation, and it is
the reason the acts are three different verbs: **you do it / your crews do it / the city does
it around you.**

## WHAT IS STILL HIS

- **THE VOICE**: he explicitly did NOT approve it. He said *"im not even saying i like this flesh it out
  more"*, and one more pass does not turn that into a yes.
- **Whether clout is the summon's mana.** He said "idk". Not a ruling.
- **How you spare somebody.** Beating them without killing, buying them, out-arguing them,
  proving something to them: that is the COMBAT and PEOPLE lanes' mechanism and his call on
  feel. This record only says the route exists on every boss.
- **Whether the spare route pays better, and by how much.** The shipped precedent says yes.
  Every number is his. NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL.
- **Who these people are.** The names are handles for a mechanism. Faction identity is
  CONTENTS-PAOLO'S, and not one faction is named here.
- **Not because 34 is a target.** He asked for 10 to 20 more than 17; this is 17 more. Cut freely.

## WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

Designs no combat, sets no number, writes no faction canon, builds nothing playable, and puts
nothing in a tab. It is a shape, a choice on every node, and a machine holding the rule that
no two bosses may be the same boss.
