===============================================================================
WHAT DIED
===============================================================================

Plus tools/bohemia_sprite_face_strip.js and gates/sprite_face_scale_gate.js.

===============================================================================

===============================================================================
"maybe all faces maybe all eyes eyebrows and mouths should be twice the size idk"
— and the measurement backed him up completely. His whole overworld face is:

    S    eyes 6px    nose 3px    lips 2px
    SE   eyes 6px    nose 2px    lips 2px
    E    eyes 3px    nose 1px    lips 2px

A TWO PIXEL MOUTH. At the size a person stands in the world there is nothing
there to read. The problem he named was real.

===============================================================================
WHY IT DIED ANYWAY — THE GEOMETRY SAID NO AND I SHOWED HIM THE NO
===============================================================================
Each eye is 2px with EXACTLY ONE PIXEL between them, on a head about 10px wide.
So:
    x1.5   changes ZERO rendered pixels (a 1.5 scale of a 2px box rounds back to
           the same 2px box). Caught only because the gate counted changed pixels.
    x2     the mouth finally reads AND THE TWO EYES FUSE INTO ONE BAR
    x3     a full visor

I shipped him a four-column strip (x1 / MOUTH x2 / ALL x2 / ALL x3) and asked him
to pick. He picked none of them and said the whole thing turned him off.

*** THAT IS A FAIR VERDICT ON A BAD DELIVERABLE, and the failure is mine, not the
    dial's. *** Three of the four columns I put in front of him were visibly worse
    than shipping. THE HONEST ANSWER TO HIS QUESTION WAS "the geometry cannot do
    this, it needs a repaint" — one sentence — and instead I built the dial, built
    the gate, built the contact sheet, and handed him a menu of options I already
    knew were bad. He judged the menu. He was right to.

STOP PRODUCING, 7/26: surface NOTHING unasked while he is unhappy with the
baseline, and a rejection ENDS THE FEATURE. It is ended.

===============================================================================
WHAT THE SLOT IS ANSWERED BY — AND IT IS NOT A DIAL
===============================================================================
A 2px eye cannot be twice as big and still be two eyes on a 10px head. More room
has to come from somewhere, and the only place it can come from is A REPAINT of
the facial layer with the features authored larger from the start. THAT IS HIS
ART AND HIS CALL. Nobody re-pitches a scale, a multiplier, a "smarter" resampler,
or a per-feature table for this. The measurement above is the reusable part: it is
why the answer is a repaint, and it does not need taking again.

The CHIN work from the same afternoon is NOT part of this verdict and stays — he
approved it, twice, and it is law now
(laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_THE_CHIN_LAW_8_11_26.md). Different surface, different
problem, approved outcome.

The PORTRAIT feature dial (window.BOH_FACE_FEAT) also stays. It is inert at
default 1, it is gated, and it was never the thing he rejected — it was the thing
he told me was the WRONG SURFACE, which is a different sentence. It is not to be
surfaced for judgment again either.

===============================================================================
THE LESSON, IN ONE LINE
===============================================================================
WHEN THE MEASUREMENT ALREADY SAYS NO, SAY NO. Do not build the thing and let him
discover the no by looking at three bad options.
