BOHEMIA COMBAT — ANIMATION REQUESTS ROUND 3 (8/1/26, for the Animation chat)
==============================================================================
Round 1 (cover-rise, cover-drop, gun-walk, cover-fire, get-shoved, floor-rise,
shiv-jab, bat-arc, spear-drive) and Round 2 (crouch-aim, flee/scramble) are
COOKED AND WIRED. This is one request, and it comes with a MEASUREMENT rather
than an opinion.

THE RULING THAT DEMANDS IT
--------------------------
Paolo 7/31: "when they die they're either bleeding out wiggling around which
you might have to make a new animation for and ALL OF IT HAS TO BE TRANSLATED
FROM THE TYPE OF HEADSHOT THEY GOT... if I'm using a shotgun everyone fucking
dies".

COMBAT v109 shipped the MECHANISM for that on 8/1: the fall is no longer rolled
on dice, it is inherited from the weapon and the range (deathFall(e,src,dist)),
and every one of the six kill sites asks the same function. The mapping is live
and gated.

THE PROBLEM, MEASURED
---------------------
Before wiring it I rendered all three baked death variants on the real surface
and measured every frame (alpha centroid, bounding box, frame 0 vs frame 11):

    death[0]   driftX -10.5   flatten 0.73   widen 2.33
    death[1]   driftX  -0.3   flatten 0.25   widen 3.64
    death[2]   driftX  -0.3   flatten 0.25   widen 3.64

  * death[0] is the one that TRAVELS: the body ends ~10px from where it
    started and stays comparatively tall. Knocked off its feet, sideways.
  * death[1] collapses straight down in place, 100px tall down to 24px.
  * death[2] ENDS PIXEL-IDENTICAL TO death[1]. Same centroid, same box.

SO THE BANK HOLDS TWO DISTINCT FALLS, NOT THREE. v109 therefore ships an honest
two-way read (KNOCKBACK vs COLLAPSE) rather than a three-way fiction built on
art that cannot express it.

THE REQUEST
-----------
1. death-fold      ~2 beats   A THIRD, GENUINELY DISTINCT FALL: the body folds
                              FORWARD over the wound and goes down face-first,
                              rather than travelling sideways (death[0]) or
                              collapsing flat under itself (death[1]/[2]).
                              ACCEPTANCE, machine-checkable, same method as the
                              measurement above: |driftX| < 4 AND flatten > 0.5
                              at the last frame, i.e. it must be measurably
                              NEITHER of the two we have. If it comes back
                              matching death[1]'s numbers it has not been
                              delivered.
                              WIRING IS ALREADY THERE: add its index to
                              FALL_DROP (or give it its own constant) and
                              deathFall routes to it. No engine work needed.

WHAT THIS UNLOCKS
-----------------
A rifle death that is not a pistol death. Today "translated from the type of
headshot they got" can only say throw-him or drop-him. A third fall makes it a
real read, and it is the only thing standing between the mechanic and what he
asked for.

NOT REQUESTED, DELIBERATELY
---------------------------
The bleed-out wiggle is DONE and cost no art. v109 makes a downed man alternate
prone112 and crawl112[0] on the 120 grid (measured centroids 71.5 vs 72.4 -- a
small shift of a body that cannot get up, not a crawl and not a corpse). Both
clips were already baked and already approved. This lane cooked no pixels.

THE SEAMLESS-TRANSITION FINDING (Paolo asked for the research)
--------------------------------------------------------------
A cut between two clips reads as a POP when the outgoing clip's current frame
and the incoming clip's frame 0 disagree about where the body is. Sprite work
has exactly two fixes: share a pose at the seam, or hold the outgoing frame
until the incoming clip's natural start. This engine has a third advantage
nobody was using -- EVERYTHING IS ON THE 120 GRID, so a transition that lands
on a beat boundary is one the player's ear has already been told to expect.
That is why the cover bob, the new writhe and the fire cycle all run off the
one shared _bpmClock rather than off performance.now(). Any new clip should
declare its length in BEATS, not milliseconds, and should start and end on a
pose another clip can hand off to.
