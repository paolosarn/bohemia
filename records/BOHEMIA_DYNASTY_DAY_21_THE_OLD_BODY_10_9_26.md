## 0. THE FINDING THAT PROVES THE ROW'S PREMISE WRONG
The row asks what our second generation feels like at forty and sixty. **Nobody is ever forty or sixty in play.** Measured on the repo this round: his 10/2 ruling (rule 67) says nobody dies of old age inside an act; an act is about 130 lived days (Q22's own figure); the strip puts acts 35 years apart; heirs arrive 15 to 35 (engine/bohemia_heirs.js ROWS). The only age the player ever sees is the age he ARRIVES at, and the only lever on it is the OLD or YNG button shipped this round (acts.age, saved, changing no face or body yet). So ageing is not a slope over a life. It is one choice per act. The design question is what OLD and YNG should change in the hands.

## 1. WHAT REALLY CHANGES BETWEEN TWENTY-FIVE AND SIXTY (recall)
- Grip and muscle: peak about thirty, then slow loss, roughly 3 to 8 percent of muscle a decade after thirty, faster after sixty (sarcopenia). Hard manual work slows it.
- Eyes: near focus goes in the mid-forties (presbyopia); small print and fine work are the first thing people say they notice.
- Recovery: a bruise, a sprain, a sleepless night take longer; healing a broken bone slows with age.
- Hands: joints stiffen, cold hurts, the tremor is rare before sixty.
- What does NOT fall: judgement, pattern reading, and the skills of a trade (experts in their field stay good a long time). People mostly say they notice time and recovery first, not strength.

## 2. THE BEST GAMES' LESSON (kept from Q5): AGEING IS A TRADE, NOT A SLOPE
So OLD is not YNG minus numbers. Proposed, all draft:true and TUNING's:
- YNG (arrive in your twenties): recovers fast, learns fast (more experience per fight), fewer perks to start, less of the family's gear knowledge.
- OLD (arrive in your forties to fifties): injuries last longer, he tires sooner, the small print on his phone needs a glasses item; BUT he starts with more of the inherited perks and the first read of a fight, and his shots do not drift. He is the veteran Battle Brothers hides behind a level.
This uses engines that exist: bohemia_down.js already has injury days (a week, a season, a year), and OLD can simply multiply them (say 1.5x) while YNG divides (say 0.75x): a felt difference with no damage number, honouring NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL.

## 3. WHAT FORTY AND SIXTY FEEL LIKE, IN THE FEW PLACES THE PLAYER SEES THEM
- Forty: the first thing is the hands at dawn (stiff, a slower first minute of a day) and the phone text (a glasses slot). The body is a trade, not a loss.
- Sixty: the leg injury that took a season at twenty-five takes most of a year; he carries what he has learned, and stops being the one at the front of the line. This is the natural time for the HEIR to step in, and the game never kills him of age.
