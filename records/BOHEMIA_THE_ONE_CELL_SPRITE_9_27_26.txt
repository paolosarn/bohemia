about 28 px on a 32 px cell. The 112 body is the HD source now, never the walked
sprite. Block-majority downscale of the game's own render, THEN a palette collapse to
the card's own floor spec (silhouette + one value split + one accent), plus a
mechanical rim shade replacing the outline the downscale erases. No hand pixel art.

THE PALETTE COLLAPSE, MEASURED: every body above went from whatever the block majority
happened to keep (23 distinct non-skin colours on average) to at most 3 (light base, dark base, one accent),
by construction, refused if that ever slips (guard 4).

AGAINST REAL GROUND, MEASURED (guard 5): every body stands on the walked city's own
sidewalk, #8a8478, not an invented placeholder. The card's rule is an OR --
body/rim in the table above is BODY colour value distance from ground, then the RIM
shade's own edge pixels, separately, because they are different pixels answering the
same either/or. ALL SEVEN sit within 0.15 value of the sidewalk on their BASE colour
alone -- the collapse to one value split flattened away whatever incidental higher-
contrast pixel (a white sneaker, a black boot) the noisy original happened to keep --
so every one of them is actually relying on the rim. 6 of 7 clear 0.15 on the rim edge alone; 1 of them (Church) sits BETWEEN 0.05 and 0.15 on both
measures -- above the hard floor (guard 5) but below the card's stated number, so the
honest read is MARGINAL, not clean, and worth DIRECTION's eye rather than a green tick.
