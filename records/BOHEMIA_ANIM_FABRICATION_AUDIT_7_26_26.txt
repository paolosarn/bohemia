=== BOHEMIA — ANIMATION FABRICATION AUDIT (7/26/26) ===

Paolo 7/26: "the arm moves and then it's just like morphing and glitching
and providing extra pixels and it's looking like dog shit."

THE METRIC: a limb is a fixed number of PAINTED pixels. Moving a rigid
thing can never make it bigger -- occlusion only ever takes pixels away.
So for every part in every frame:  invented = on-screen minus painted.
Anything above zero is the renderer drawing pixels he never painted.
Measured through the shipped alpha in a real browser: posedSkel() ->
Skinner.skin(), the same two calls the game itself makes.

SCOPE: 102 clips x 8 facings x 8 phases = 6528 frames.

--- THE A/B: WHAT THIS TURN REMOVED -------------------------------------
  Same frames, same code path, one switch. The JOINT WELD stamped limb
  pixels a SECOND time under the parent bone near the shoulder/elbow --
  duplicates, sprayed exactly at the torso/arm junction Paolo named.

    renderer he called dog shit : 34636 invented pixels, 4753 dirty frames
    with the weld retired       : 13444 invented pixels, 3377 dirty frames
    REMOVED                     : 21192 pixels  (61.2%)

  NOT A FULL FIX, and this report is where that is said out loud. The
  remaining invention is inherent to resampling pixel art through a
  continuous bone transform, and it only reaches zero when limbs stop
  being resampled. Two ways to force zero were built and MEASURED at
  exactly 0 invented pixels this turn, and BOTH were rejected on the
  render because they shredded the silhouette:
    - ONE SOURCE, ONE PIXEL (veto a painted pixel landing twice)
    - PIXEL CONSERVATION (forward-splat each painted pixel to one cell)
  The real answer is a quantised angle atlas or painted frames. See the
  addendum's order of work.

--- WHERE THE INVENTED PIXELS COME FROM (named in the source) -----------
  JOINT WELD        stamps limb pixels a SECOND time under the parent
                    frame near the shoulder/elbow. Duplicates, exactly at
                    the torso/arm junction Paolo named.
  refineSkin        invents pixels with no rest source at all (src=-1) to
                    close pinholes the resample opened.
  EVERY PIXEL LANDS forward-splats painted sources the inverse sample
                    missed -- necessary only BECAUSE the resample misses.
  MIN HAND SLIVER   stamps a 2x4 rectangle of hand pixels when a head-on
                    pose buries a hand. Draws a hand the pose does not put
                    there. This is the hand "feature" he says clips wrong.

  All four exist to paper over damage from ONE root cause: limb pixels are
  RESAMPLED through a continuous bone transform. At any angle that is not a
  multiple of 90 degrees that resample cannot be lossless. On pixel art,
  lossy resampling IS morphing. The HEAD is exempt by law (HEAD RIGID STAMP,
  Paolo 7/2/26) and the head is the part nobody complains about.
