## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
A new campaign opens on a short intro for the chosen origin: a painted scene and a few lines about who these men are. Then the
company stands on the world map beside a starting town, and the first thing a player does is walk into it to hire, buy and take
a contract. Each origin sets its start: the men, the gear, the money, sometimes the place and the rules (the Lone Wolf, the
Militia, the Cultists), and some have their own unique things.
