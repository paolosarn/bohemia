WHY IT LANDED WHEN THREE BATCHES BEFORE IT DID NOT
---------------------------------------------------
Not the shapes. Every previous post-mortem blamed the shapes. Measured:

                        colours/tile   edge   grain    sat
    HIS BOUGHT concrete        1443    20.9   64.7%   0.274
    my recooked tileset         417     8.7   24.4%   0.323
    my house skins               81     9.4   26.2%   0.383
    my CMU wall                   4     7.1   14.4%   0.082
    my perimeter wall          1222     7.0   23.2%   0.466

HIS ART IS ROUGH AND GREY. PAINTED ART IN THIS REPO WAS SMOOTH AND TOO COLOURFUL. ~2.5x
his local contrast, ~2.7x his grain, at ~60% of his saturation. A different ORDER OF
DETAIL, which no choice of colours can fix.
  edge  = mean |luminance delta| between horizontally adjacent pixels
  grain = % of adjacent pairs differing by more than 8 luminance

STANDING RULES THIS VERDICT CREATES
------------------------------------
1. THE RULER IS HIS LIBRARY, NEVER A TASTE. The target is re-derived from the purchased
   bank on every gate run, so it cannot drift.
2. NO SMOOTH ART SHIPS AGAIN. A tile under ~600 colours, or under edge 14, or under 50%
   grain, does not go beside his art. The gate refuses it.
3. TOO GREY IS ALSO A DEFECT. Saturation has a FLOOR (0.15) as well as a cap (0.34);
   dead-neutral CMU at 0.067 was as wrong as a 0.47 painted wall.
4. PINK IS NOT A DESERT COLOUR. Desaturating a dark red at constant value makes salmon.
   Clay goes BROWN. Permanent negative test.
5. STRUCTURE MUST SURVIVE THE GRAIN. Noise that hits every number and tells you nothing
   about the material is mush. Permanent negative test.

KNOWN WEAKNESS, STATED AT THE MOMENT OF APPROVAL
-------------------------------------------------
His tiles carry BIG FEATURES that break the field: a manhole, a weed clump, a long
crack. The approved batch is consistent texture at the right density. That is the
distance between "same material family" and "same pack", and it is the next cook.
