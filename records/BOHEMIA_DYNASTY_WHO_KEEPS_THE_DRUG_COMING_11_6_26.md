## 1. THE REAL FLOOR (what governments and companies really did, and how it looked from the street)
- COUPS. Iran 1953: the CIA's own internal history, released in 2013, says the overthrow of Prime Minister Mosaddegh was carried out
  under CIA direction (operation TPAJAX). Guatemala 1954 (operation PBSUCCESS). Chile: the US Senate's 1975 staff report, Covert Action
  in Chile 1963-1973, called the covert involvement "extensive and continuous", with more than 8 million dollars approved for it from
  1970 to the coup in September 1973.
- MKULTRA, 1953 TO 1973. The CIA dosed people with LSD and other drugs, many of whom never knew, across about 149 subprojects at more
  than 80 universities, hospitals and safe houses. In operation Midnight Climax, men were lured to apartments in San Francisco and New
  York, drugged, and watched through two-way mirrors. In January 1973 the director ordered the files destroyed; about 20,000 pages
  survived in a financial records building nobody had swept, and came out in 1977. THE STREET SAW NOTHING. A man had a bad night.
- THE CONTRA YEARS AND THE 1998 REVIEW. The CIA's inspector general (the Hitz report, two volumes, 1998) found the agency kept working
  with people it knew, or had been told, were moving cocaine, and a 1982 agreement with the Justice Department left drug crimes off the
  list of crimes officers had to report about helpers who were not employees. The same review found no proof the CIA conspired to sell
  drugs. THE TRUTH WAS SMALLER THAN THE RUMOUR AND BIGGER THAN THE DENIAL.
- IT DOES NOT TAKE A CONSPIRACY. Purdue Pharma pleaded guilty in 2007 and again in 2020 over how it sold OxyContin: a legal company
  kept a drug coming for money. And the cartels' switch to "P2P" meth, cooked from common legal chemicals, made it cheaper and purer
  than ever (Sam Quinones, The Least of Us, 2021). Quinones links it to paranoia and psychosis and the tent camps; that is a
  journalist's argument and scientists dispute it, so the game never states it as fact.
- WHAT THIS FLOOR TEACHES THE WRITERS: from the block, a long game and a business look the same. The drug got cheaper and cleaner,
  certain streets got it first, and the people who could explain it were not talking.
