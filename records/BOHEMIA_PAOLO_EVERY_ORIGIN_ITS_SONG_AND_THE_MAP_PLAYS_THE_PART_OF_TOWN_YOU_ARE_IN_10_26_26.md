(a) THE FACTION SONGS GO IN NOW, AS THEY ARE, AS PLACEHOLDERS: rule 122's fence opens for them (newest date wins: he asked).
    The other old songs (the menu's, the day's, dusk and dawn, the rooms') stay silent until he says.
(b) EVERY ORIGIN ITS SONG: each of the 15 origins plays a faction song as its own (its group's nearest: THE FAITHFUL the CHURCH's,
    THE WATER TRUCK the CARAVANS', the rest SOUNDS' table, written in its record, his to change); it plays in the intro (rule 236)
    and when his company is at home.
(c) THE MAP PLAYS THE PART OF TOWN YOU ARE IN: on the overworld map the music follows where the party is: near a faction's part of
    the city, that faction's song; on the open road and the empty land between, the overworld songs (the thirteen he voted up).
    The change crosses on the beat at a phrase line (never a cut, rule 229), so walking from one district to the next the sound
    changes under him.
(d) THE INTENSITIES: the faction songs come in levels on his own machine; the map uses a low level, a threat near (a roaming party
    closing) raises it; the fight keeps rule 207's three levels.
(e) ON THE JOURNAL: every faction song is REMADE later the way the thirteen were (the Crashbox analog horror machine-party, rule
    119), each keeping its three levels; until then the placeholders play. A row on SOUNDS' board and a line on his list.

    beat; the open road plays an overworld song; a new game for three origins plays each origin's song in the intro; no menu, day
    or room song plays anywhere.
