## RULINGS (NOTES ARE RULINGS)
1. A MAKING CHAT COOKS EVERY ROUND. Sounds makes sounds. UI makes panels. Cook makes tiles.
   Character makes bodies and clothes. Animation makes clips. Portrait makes faces. Words
   makes lines. People makes people. Every round, a real thing he can hear or see, never a
   round that only checks.
2. THE HOLD IS ON THE PLAY SURFACE, NOT ON THE MAKING. Rule 18 still keeps the code off the
   demo and the alpha's play tabs until the cut holds. The cooked thing goes to the VOTE
   tab in the alpha, which is exactly what the tab is for. "Measure your part" is dead as
   a round's whole output; measuring rides beside the cooking, never instead of it.
3. "CODING AND CHECKING WHETHER THE SOUNDS ARE BROKEN" is not a sound chat's round. Gates
   and instruments are the plumber's trade and a lane's proof line; they are not the
   product. A making lane's report leads with the thing it made and where he can hear or
   see it.
4. "I UNDERSTAND YOU HAVE TO IMPLEMENT THE CODE IN THE PLUMBING": recorded. He is not
   asking the lanes to skip the wiring. He is asking that the wiring never be the only
   thing a round produces.
5. "EVERY TIME, NOT NEVER": the measure is per round, per making lane, one cooked thing
   minimum, registered in the VOTE tab. A machine gate is owed (PLUMBER [cook gate]).
6. THE VOTE TAB PLAYS SOUND. A sound he cannot hear in the tab is not cooked. UI owns it.
