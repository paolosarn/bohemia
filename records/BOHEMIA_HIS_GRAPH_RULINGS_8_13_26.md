## R-GATE — A PREREQUISITE CAN BE A FIGHT *OR* A JOB

*"then you do have to take out certain bosses or do certain quests and shit"*

Two kinds of key open a node: **besting a boss**, or **completing a quest**. They are
interchangeable as gates. So the graph's edges are not "boss unlocks boss" — they are
"acquisition unlocks acquisition".

## R-ACQUIRED — ★★★ NOT EVERYTHING IS A BOSS, AND THIS IS THE BIGGEST ONE

*"I don't know if it's all gonna be just bosses like maybe a lot of them could be but the idea is
that like it's something that's ACQUIRED so it doesn't have to maybe not killing or persuading a
particular person like it's just a quest and that's that would be fun."*

**The unit of the ladder is the ACQUISITION, not the fight.** For a month this has been called THE
BOSS LADDER and every one of the 53 rows was written as "somebody holds a thing and you take it from
them." He is saying the holder is optional. Some of these are a person you beat or spare. Some are
**just a job you do** — no antagonist, nobody to persuade, no fight to win, and *"that would be
fun."*

That means each node needs a **ROUTE**: is it a boss, a quest, or something you simply come across?
And it means the artifact is misnamed. **[PENDING Paolo] on the rename** — that is his to call, not
a thing to quietly do to his file.

## R-NARRATIVE — VALHEIM'S SIN IS THAT IT DOES NOT TRY AT ALL

*"I mean imagine if Val Heim just give a little fuck to have some sort of narrative direction like
just a little bit you know and I think that's really important"*

Valheim's five bosses have essentially no story attached: a stone with a name, a summon, a fight, a
power. He is not asking for a story-heavy game. He is asking for **a little**, where Valheim gives
none, and he calls it important. **A LITTLE NARRATIVE DIRECTION PER NODE.** Not a plot.

## R-FLUID — NARRATIVE MAY NEVER STOP PLAY

*"we want this game to be fluid like we don't want to be bogged down by being stuck in a cut scene"*

So the narrative direction cannot be delivered as a cutscene the player waits through. Whatever
carries the story has to happen **while the game is still moving.** This is a hard constraint on
HOW, not a request for less story.

## R-NOT-MMO — AND IT MAY NOT BE CHEAP

*"I don't want this to be cheap either like I don't want this to feel like an MMORPG, which is
bullshit"*

The obvious cheap way to add narrative without cutscenes is an MMO quest log: a marker, a name, "go
kill 6 rats", a paragraph nobody reads. **That is explicitly banned.** So the three constraints
together are a real vice:

| | |
|---|---|
| **more than Valheim** | Valheim's zero-narrative bosses are the failure he named |
| **but never a cutscene** | it cannot stop the game to tell you something |
| **and never an MMO quest log** | markers, fetch counts and unread text are "bullshit" |

**That triangle is the actual design problem this ruling set hands us**, and it is not solved here.
Recorded so it cannot be forgotten, and flagged.

## WHAT THESE RULINGS COST, HONESTLY

**R-FAN and physical reality disagree, and he needs to know that.** If the graph's edges are only the
ones that are *physically necessary* — you cannot catch rain off a roof you cannot reach, you cannot
open a quarry without explosives, you cannot save seed before you have a crop — then **TWENTY
nodes have no prerequisite at all** and the game opens with twenty doors, not four to six.
(That number is MEASURED by `node tools/bohemia_ladder_graph.js`, not counted by hand. I typed
"twenty-one" here first and it was wrong — the third hand-typed number I have gotten wrong in two
days, so the gate now checks this sentence against the measurement instead of trusting it.)

Physical necessity cannot get us to 4-6. Something non-physical has to gate the rest, and R-GATE
already names it: **quests.** A quest gate is the tool that closes a door physics leaves open.

**Which nodes sit behind a quest is content, and content is his.** The mechanism is built, the
physically-necessary edges are derived and each one carries its reason, and the quest slots are
EMPTY and waiting. Per MECHANISM-MINE / CONTENTS-PAOLO'S, filling them in is not mine to do.
