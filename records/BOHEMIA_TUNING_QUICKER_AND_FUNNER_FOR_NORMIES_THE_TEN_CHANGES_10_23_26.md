# TUNING [quicker and funner for normies]: THE TEN BIGGEST CHANGES (10/23)

RESEARCH ONLY (rule 133(e), rule 38g). Nothing was built, no card. This page is the report. The walk it
measures is banks/BOHEMIA_TUNING_QUICKER_AND_FUNNER_THE_WALK_10_23_26.json (draft:true; nothing in the game
reads it).

HIS WORDS (rule 133, 10/23): "the whole point of this project was to be like Battle Brothers but quicker
and funner for normies... also kind of hardcore vibes if you're fucking around too much... all the way from
the traversal to the combat to the way you interact with the world... like someone who plays Stardew
Valley would play it." He puts the game at about 45 to 50 percent of the full Battle Brothers experience.

THE FRAME: Battle Brothers is how a system works (rule 41) and never how hard it is (rule 117). Stardew
Valley is his named bar for the FEEL only: pick it up and keep going. Every other game on this page is a
RULER (a number someone measured), never a reference (the 8/28 ban). His standing rulings this page obeys:
the phone only shows (rule 51); nothing speaks in the first 60 s and nothing stops the march (the second
votes); no authored first fight (the fifth votes: "who are you to decide what the first fight is? That's the
great thing about Battle Brothers"); a quest is people, nothing pops up (rule 19); a man struck down dies 20%
of the time, else a long injury, and the main character never dies (rules 36, 37).

## THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG

**THE FIGHT IS NOT THE SLOW PART. THE MAP AND THE TOWN ARE.** On AUTO the fight won both of a stranger's
first fights by itself in 28.6 s (6 rounds) and 23.3 s (4 rounds). What cost the stranger was everything
around the board: a phone listing two jobs that do nothing when touched; the word CHURCH on the map sitting
on a moving patrol, so the tap meant for the town walked the crew into a fight 3.9 s later with no word; the
map coming back on PAUSE after every fight, so the next tap on a place moved nothing for 15 s; a town picture
with no words where 12 aimed taps never found the hiring post; and a crew screen showing twelve men to a crew
of three. Four wrong taps and one unplanned fight before the first town. AND THE HARDCORE HALF OF HIS SENTENCE
IS AT ZERO: from 06:00 to 11:10 nothing was charged (240 batteries in, 240 out, moved only by the pay), so
"fucking around too much" costs nothing.

## THE TEN, RANKED (by how many strangers it loses, times how early)

### 1. EVERY FIGHT IS SIZED TO THE CREW IN FRONT OF IT  [RUN + COMBAT]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: jobs are sized with a floor of 0.75, "about 8 level 1 characters", so a 3-man crew gets
  jobs built for 8 (wiki Game Guide, sourced). The developer: new players meet "really strong enemies at the
  very beginning, then gets wrecked... especially bad for new players". A Switch review needed 6 restarts to
  pass day 9.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: the first threat is survivable by accident (library FH-01); a tiny first board
  (Into the Breach: 3 mechs, 8 x 8, survive 5 turns); one fight with two heroes in one corridor (Darkest
  Dungeon's Old Road).
- OUR DEMO: the map's own fights were sized right in this walk (3 v 3 on the road, 3 v 2 on the job; both
  won). But the street's crew hand-over still sends a roster with no party (streetFightOnStep,
  slices/BOHEMIA_CITY_WORLD.html 71427), the fight's options still read only the party (nfOpts,
  slices/BOHEMIA_DEMO.html 12422-12430), and the fallback is still nine brigands (slices/BOHEMIA_FIGHT.html
  599). A NEW CREW wins 0 in 100 of those (TUNING 10/19, 5ff7e86d). The one-line fix has not landed.
- THE CHANGE: (a) nfOpts turns any roster into a party of its length; (b) the fallback band is sized by the
  company (the 70-in-100 line at early gear: 1 man 1, 3 men 3, 6 men 7, 12 men 13); (c) on the first game
  day no fight puts more than the company plus one on the board. NUMBERS: the street's fights 0 in 100 to
  about 90; the map's first job stays 90 to 97.
- HARDCORE KEPT: two- and three-skull jobs, the roaming bands and the dire wolves stay exactly as hard; he
  chooses to meet them.

### 2. THE FIRST TAP GOES SOMEWHERE  [RUN (the map) + UI (the phone)]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: about 17 towns on a fresh map; a job's skulls say nothing about distance (study day 19);
  the clock runs while you read, and three mods exist only to pause it.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: one visible person to walk toward (FH-02); one errand, not a menu (FH-04,
  Stardew's mailbox); time to fun under 60 s on phones (Jon Lai), the basics inside 60 s (Rohrl, PopCap).
- OUR DEMO: tapping a job on the phone lights the card and nothing else (0 of 2 taps do anything). The word
  CHURCH sat on a moving patrol beside the town; tapping it walked the crew 2 blocks into a fight in 3.9 s.
  After the fight the map came back on PAUSE; the next tap on a place set a 2-block route that moved nothing
  for 15 s until 1x was tapped. 4 wrong taps and 1 unplanned fight before the first town.
- THE CHANGE: (a) a job tapped on the phone draws its road to the town that posted it and lights that town
  (the phone still only SHOWS, rule 51; the next tap, on the lit town, goes); (b) a tap on a place always
  goes: on PAUSE it starts at the last speed used; (c) after a fight the map resumes at the speed it had;
  (d) a town's name and a party's name never look alike (a town's plate sits on the ground, a party's rides
  its flag and moves with it); (e) a finished job leaves the phone. NUMBERS: dead taps on the phone 1 to 0;
  wrong taps before the first town 4 to 0; the 1x tap after every fight 1 to 0; play time from BEGIN to the
  first job's fight under 60 s.
- HARDCORE KEPT: every block still pays its clock (124 game minutes for 22 blocks); patrols still roam.

### 3. LOOK BEFORE YOU FIGHT  [RUN (the map) + UI (the plate) + TUNING (the skull line)]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: a party is only a word ("a few" 2-3, "some" 4-6, "many" 7-10, sourced); the Numbers mod
  exists to put the count back.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: every enemy's next attack is drawn before you move (Into the Breach); the
  combat forecast and the one-button danger zone (Fire Emblem); "a cold hand should never lose to something
  the screen did not announce" (FH-05).
- OUR DEMO: about ten groups stand on the first map screen with no mark for who is hostile. One tap on a
  named party is a fight: no count, no skulls, no flee. The board opens on FIGHT, AUTO, WAIT and no way out.
- THE CHANGE: the first tap on a party shows how many, their skulls against HIS crew (one skull = he wins
  about 9 in 10 on the TUNING line) and where they are going; the second tap attacks. A party coming for him
  shows the blocks to contact and lights the road away (rule 68: most of the time you can flee). NUMBERS:
  1 tap and 0 numbers today; 2 taps and 2 numbers (how many, skulls against you).
- HARDCORE KEPT: two taps and you fight; caught on dirt by a faster party, you fight anyway.

### 4. THE EASY END IS THE DEFAULT  [RUN (the door) + WORDS (the card lines)]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: four knobs before the first second of play, jargon names, effects felt hours later; the
  title screen itself recommends Beginner.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: Fire Emblem's Casual mode arrived when the series would have ended under
  250,000 sales, and the director who fought it now plays it; XCOM 2's Commander-or-harder Ironman win is
  held by 3.85% of its Steam players.
- OUR DEMO: the crew pick opens on SEEN IT + THIN SHELVES (slices/BOHEMIA_DEMO.html 13506), which is Battle
  Brothers' Veteran with medium funds (TUNING 10/18). A stranger who taps BEGIN plays that. Measured on the
  bench: Battle Brothers' Veteran wins 46 in 100 of the four first fights, our rule 117 start 78 (d4bc2e2c).
  And "start rich, pay x1.5" does not say whether he pays or is paid.
- THE CHANGE: default NEW HERE + FULL SHELVES; each card says what it does in plain words ("jobs pay half
  again"). NUMBERS: one line of code; 46 to 78 in 100 for every stranger who taps BEGIN.
- HARDCORE KEPT: OUTLIVED IT + BARE SHELVES stay one tap away (rule 117(a): the sliders go up from easy).

### 5. ONE BILL, IN DAYS: THE HARDCORE UNDER IT  [ECONOMY (the number) + RUN (the charge) + UI (the bar)]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: wages at noon, 2 food a man a day, tools, medicine, ammo: four stockpiles drain at once;
  the bar shows totals and never when they run out; an angry man deserts.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: one supplies number for the whole caravan (Banner Saga); food bought per trip,
  8, 16 or 24, and hunger costs health (Darkest Dungeon); wages and food paid at camp (Wartales).
- OUR DEMO: nothing was charged from 06:00 to 11:10; no origin carries food and nothing is charged in the
  first hour (TUNING 10/19). A crew can stand still forever. The bar carries five money numbers, three "-".
- THE CHANGE: one nightly cost in batteries for the crew (wage and food as one: "the loan of one a night is
  the wage", ECONOMY [bb money]), paid at nightfall and shown as DAYS LEFT beside the battery count; at zero,
  one man walks each night and his face says why. The stall's food, meds and rounds stay for whoever wants
  them; the bar shows two numbers, not five. NUMBERS: the runway at our start 27 to 48 days against Battle
  Brothers' 46 (ECONOMY day 74); the bar 5 numbers to 2.
- HARDCORE KEPT: this IS the hardcore. Dawdle and the crew leaves, one face a night. Never a game over for
  the main character.

### 6. A WIN IS A MOMENT  [UI (the bar, the tally) + COMBAT (the drop) + SOUNDS (the count-up)]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: a loot screen after every fight; the pay from the client's face.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: Stardew pays what you shipped while you sleep, and the end of the day is the
  moment (study day 13: the end of the day is half the memory); medium "juice" beats none and beats too much
  on enjoyment and play time (Kao 2020).
- OUR DEMO: the first fight, won: straight back to the map on PAUSE, 0 batteries, no loot, no line. The
  job, won: one line on the map, the number jumps 240 to 270 with no count-up, the finished job stays on
  the phone.
- THE CHANGE: after every won fight, 3 s on the beat: the battery number counts up where it lives with a
  sound, what dropped flies into the bag icon, the hurt man's face shows his days out. A paid job is paid by
  the client's face, one line. Nightfall (the day loop's reckoning at 22:00) becomes the day's tally:
  fights won, batteries in and out, days left; one tap. Nothing pops up: numbers move where they live, a
  person says the line (rule 117(c)). NUMBERS: 0 s to 3 s or less per win; 1 tap at most to go on.
- HARDCORE KEPT: a lost fight gets the same 3 s: who is hurt, what it cost.

### 7. THE TOWN SHOWS ITS DOORS, AND THE MEN WAITING ARE FOR HIRE  [RUN TWO + COOK FOUR + WORDS]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: one painted view, the buildings as icons that name themselves on hover; on a phone there
  is no hover (BB school, interface round five).
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: one screen of named buildings that brighten as they grow (Darkest Dungeon's
  hamlet); the room that matters in plain sight (FH-06).
- OUR DEMO: the picture carries no words by design ("name it only when touched",
  slices/settlement_ground_packs/settlement_ground.json). 12 aimed taps found the barber, the doc, the stall,
  the smith, the Elder at a desk (the jobs) and the scavenge lot; never the hiring post or the bar. The 11
  people standing in a line in front of the stall are "somebody on the street".
- THE CHANGE: the three doors that matter (work, hands, food) are lit on arrival (a bulb over the door, a
  flickering sign that is a picture: a gun, a cross, a bottle; still no words on the picture); the Elder
  says where the three are, once, the first time; the people standing in the line ARE the hiring post.
  NUMBERS: places found 6 of 8 in 12 taps to 8 of 8 in 8; the jobs and the hiring 1 tap each.
- HARDCORE KEPT: prices, stock and the night stay as they are.

### 8. ONE VOICE TEACHES THE FIRST HOUR  [WORDS + UI (the face) + COMBAT (the fight's three lines)]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: no teaching in the game; its one teaching event fires after 2 contracts; the tutorial
  origin's scripted fight kills most of your men; a reviewer: "the first half an hour with this game is
  confusing because nothing is explained."
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: rules come in one at a time (Marvel Snap's locations; Darkest Dungeon's Old
  Road); lightening a heavy tutorial raised day-1 retention 10% (devtodev).
- OUR DEMO: the fight writes its instructions on a paper strip from nobody ("Drag a man inside the chalk",
  "Tap inside the chalk to walk. Tap a marked man to hit."); the map writes one-liners from nobody.
- THE CHANGE: his second (a face from his own crew) says one line the first time each of eight things
  happens: a job seen on the phone, a party in sight, the first town, the first fight (aim, cover, reload:
  COMBAT's [the first fight teaches]), the first pay, the first man hurt, the first hire, the first
  nightfall. Each once, never in the first 60 s, never stopping the march, a tap skips it. No fight is
  scripted; the world still picks the first fight. NUMBERS: 8 lines of 12 words or fewer, under 30 s of
  reading in the first hour; Battle Brothers says nothing before 2 contracts.
- HARDCORE KEPT: the voice tells him what a thing is, never what to do.

### 9. HIRE WHAT YOU SEE, AND THE CREW SCREEN SHOWS YOUR CREW  [RUN TWO + PEOPLE + ECONOMY]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: a hire costs his background, his gear and his level, plus a daily wage; traits hidden
  behind a try-out fee, stars hidden until hired; players hire, look and reload; a mod exists to show stars.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: recruits arrive free every week with their quirks showing (Darkest
  Dungeon's stagecoach).
- OUR DEMO: a recruit shows 1 starred stat; the first hire costs up to 225 against the wiki formula's 49
  (TUNING 10/19); the hiring post was not found in 12 taps; CREW opens YOUR COMPANY 12 / 20 with twelve named
  men while the crew is 3 (the picks: company 3, cap 12; the bar 3/12; both fights 3).
- THE CHANGE: a recruit's card shows his face, his 3 best things as stars, his one trait in plain words, his
  price and his nightly cost; the price at or under Battle Brothers' formula; the crew screen reads the
  company the origin built. NUMBERS: stars 1 to 3; first hire 225 to 49 or under; the crew screen 12 to 3.
- HARDCORE KEPT: the wage is real every night (change 5); a bad trait is still a bad trait.

### 10. THE FIGHT BY HAND, QUICKER  [COMBAT]
- BATTLE BROTHERS: tough fights 30 to 40 min with no speed control in the base game; players install
  Swifter and Autopilot; no save inside a fight.
- HOW FRIENDLIER GAMES DO IT: XCOM 2's Zip Mode cut a 3 s pause to 0.6 s; an auto-battle button pitched at
  newcomers (Fire Emblem Heroes); take back any move, reset a turn once (Into the Breach); a match of about 3
  minutes (Marvel Snap); the median phone session is 4.45 to 6 minutes (GameAnalytics).
- OUR DEMO: AUTO won both routine fights in 28.6 s and 23.3 s, so the engine is quick. By hand it is 12 to
  18 of his turns (3 men, 4 to 6 rounds): NOT MEASURED this round. The fight saves only the formation, so a
  phone call that kills the tab mid-fight loses the fight.
- THE CHANGE: (a) when the fight reads 80 in 100 or better for him, AUTO glows and one tap finishes it; (b)
  their turns play at double speed and a tap skips a walk; (c) take back the last step once a turn, never a
  blow (the dice stay honest); (d) the turn ends itself (rule 125, already on COMBAT's board); (e) the fight
  saves every turn. NUMBERS: a routine fight by hand 2 min or less, a tough one 15 or less (rule 40); AUTO's
  23 to 29 s stays.
- HARDCORE KEPT: AUTO plays his men as they are; an injury taken on AUTO is still 30 to 40 days; no dice are
  ever rolled again.

## ALREADY QUICKER THAN BATTLE BROTHERS (KEEP; nobody touches these)
- The clock moves only when he moves; Battle Brothers' runs while he reads.
- Travel: 2 blocks a second at 1x; the farthest town (38 blocks as the crow flies) in about 20 s at 1x, 4 s
  at 5x; Battle Brothers' map crossing is 3.5 to 7 minutes at 1x.
- No haggling: Take it or Not now; two jobs at once; paid on the spot when the job's fight is won.
- A man struck down dies 20% of the time (Battle Brothers 67%); the main character never dies.
- The crew pick is the loading screen: BEGIN to a map that takes a finger in 2.5 s at 4x.
- AUTO exists and wins routine fights in under 30 s.

## WHAT THIS ROUND DID NOT MEASURE
- A fight played by hand (both were on AUTO): COMBAT owes that number before change 10 is sized.
- Nightfall's reckoning card at 22:00 (the walk ended at 11:10).
- How often a stranger meets the street's crew hand-over (the nine-brigand fight) in a real first hour.
- A real phone. Every number is this container's Chromium on the phone profile.
