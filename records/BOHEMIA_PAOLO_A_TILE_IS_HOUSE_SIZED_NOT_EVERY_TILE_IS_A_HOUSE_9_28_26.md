# PAOLO 9/28/26: A TILE IS THE SIZE OF A HOUSE. THAT DOES NOT MEAN EVERY TILE IS A HOUSE. (voice-to-text, verbatim, to the coordinator)

"And keep in mind like my one tile is the size of a house doesn't mean every tile is a fucking house. It still
has to look like a fucking city. We have neighbors. We have so many asses we have streets bro like come on."

## THE READING (garble: "asses" = assets)
"A COMBAT TILE IS A HOUSE" IS A SIZE, NOT A CONTENT RULE. The tile is HOUSE-SIZED. What is ON a tile is whatever
that piece of the city is: a house, a yard, a street, a sidewalk and kerb, a parking lot, a slab where a house
was, a car, a shed, a pump, a wall, a roof (the mound). The fight board is a real piece of the city with
neighbours and streets, drawn from the assets we already have (the walk's banks dress the tiles, s13a). A board
of nothing but houses is WRONG and reads as a grid of boxes, not a city.
