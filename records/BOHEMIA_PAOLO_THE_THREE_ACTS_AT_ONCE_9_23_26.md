1. THE THREE ACTS RUN AT THE SAME TIME AND HE FLIPS BETWEEN THEM. Not one after the other.
   Gen 1 Animal, Gen 2 Human, Gen 3 Angel are all open from the start of the game and one
   control flips him from one to the next, the way Ocarina of Time flips child Link and adult
   Link.
2. THE FUTURE SHOWS THE PAST'S SCORE. Flip forward early and the city is built like shit,
   because he has not done enough in the earlier act. "If you want to see more awesome
   futuristic technology life you gotta do more in the past." The future is the report card
   of the past, drawn as a city.

   a simple option." A place in the world, or a button. Not decided by him; decided by
   default below, and it goes to VOTE.

   in another.
5. THE THREE DESCENDANTS ARE NAMED, MALE OR FEMALE, "at the start of the game probably... or
   maybe slides into that early on into each act." Both readings kept; default below.

its music. Every other game in the school record is research, not a reference; none of them
is named by him and none enters the design by name.
