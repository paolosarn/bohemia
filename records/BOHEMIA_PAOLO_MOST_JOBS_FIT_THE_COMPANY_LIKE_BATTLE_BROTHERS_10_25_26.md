## WHAT BATTLE BROTHERS DOES (Grok's 10/24 pages on main: GROK_ASK_105_KEYBOARD_CONTRACTS_SCALING, GROK_CONTRACT_AMBITION_LIST)
A contract's enemy strength comes from the company's roster strength (the twelve strongest men, 10 plus 2 a level each), the
contract's base value, its skull rating and the difficulty setting; the skulls tell the player the danger before he takes
it, and early boards are mostly one- and two-skull work. Overpowered fights are the three-skull exception, chosen.

## THE RULING (rule 192)
(a) MOST JOBS FIT THE COMPANY: on any board, most jobs offered are winnable by the company as it stands; a job far above it is
    the exception, rare early, and only ever on a job whose stars say so (rule 146's stars are the skulls).
(b) THE STARS TELL THE TRUTH: a one-star job is won by his company most of the time, a three-star job is a real risk; the
    win rate per star is measured and holds.
(c) NO TEST SETTING LEAKS INTO HIS GAME: anything built to force tough enemies for testing (a superior enemy forced on, a
    scaling floor, a late mix early) is off in the game he plays; the gates may switch it on for themselves only.
(d) MEASURED: over many rolled boards and fights at each stage of the game (day 1, day 20, day 50, day 90), the share of
    offered jobs whose enemy outclasses the company, and the win rate per star, against Battle Brothers' shape; written in the
    one table (TUNING) and fixed in the job and spawn math (WORLD, with COMBAT for the fight's dealing).
(e) WORLD [most jobs fit the company] first; TUNING measures in its next round; EYES checks the board's jobs by eye.
