## 1. NEWGROUNDS IS NAMED (the coordinator misread it as "new ground" one message earlier; corrected)
His 9/24 sentence: "we can put more life into it with this analog horror, pixel, Newgrounds bullshit
we're doing." NEWGROUNDS is the early-2000s flash site: hand-made web animation and games, small teams,
loud, crude on purpose, sprite-and-tween motion, a home-made energy no studio product has. It is a
website and an era, not a game, so the reference rule reads it the way it reads analog horror: A LOOK
AND A VIBE, owned by ONE department, DIRECTION's bible. What of the Newgrounds era fits INSIDE analog
horror is what survives (the way FFX's sound was amended 9/20). Nobody studies a Newgrounds game as a
game; DIRECTION writes the page on what the era looked and sounded like and what it teaches our pixels
and our motion ("put more life into it").

## 2. WEAPONS HAVE SHAPES ON THE TILES, LIKE BATTLE BROTHERS
Battle Brothers has weapons that hit several tiles at once (a greatsword's swing, a battle axe's arc,
three or six tiles). Our version: A MACHINE GUN IS THE GREATSWORD. Alongside the reach rule already
ruled 9/22 (pistol 1 tile, rifle 2, scope 3), a weapon has a SHAPE: a machine gun sweeps a line of
tiles in one beat instead of one tile. This is Battle Brothers flavour in the fight, which rule 33d
allows as the second influence under Rogue Fable 4's speed. COMBAT [weapon shapes]: school on BB's
multi-tile weapons (which shapes, what they cost, why they read), then the shape table for ours in
plain words (pistol, rifle, scope, machine gun, shotgun as the spread), everything still costing one,
the dial for the important shot.
