## 1. THE DEMO'S LAST THIRTY SECONDS  [ASKED — open since 8/25]
Why it is a real question and not mine to just pick: the research says
memory of an experience is almost entirely two data points, THE PEAK AND
THE END (Kahneman/Fredrickson, and duration is neglected). Our ruled cut
puts both peaks in the first five minutes and ends on GOING TO BED. The
ending is half of what a stranger keeps and nobody has designed it. All
three options below are cheap, built on existing parts, and change no
beat of his cut. WHICH ONE IS TASTE, AND TASTE IS HIS.
  **A — THE OVERLOOK AGAIN, CHANGED.** Already locked canon that the
  vista upgrades per act. End on the valley a second time, different
  because of the day he just spent in it. A peak and an end in one shot,
  and it says what the game is about with no words.
  **B — THE LIGHTS.** He stands at his own door at night and sees the
  ~12% of the valley that still has power. LIGHT = TERRITORY is canon and
  that network is "eerily perfect" and OWNED. It ends the demo by showing
  the enemy without naming him.
  **C — THE PHONE HE CANNOT ANSWER.** The day ends, he sleeps, something
  lands on the phone as the screen goes. Cheapest of the three; the
  phone already carries the day's work.
COORDINATOR'S READ: A is the safest and B is the one that would make a
stranger say "what IS that." Both beat C. But this is exactly the fork
where his taste is the whole answer.
