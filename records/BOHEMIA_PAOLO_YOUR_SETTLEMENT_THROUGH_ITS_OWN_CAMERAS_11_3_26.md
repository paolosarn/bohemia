## LOCKED
1. HIS PICK: A, THE BLOCK YOU TOOK ('it was always going to be A'): a real Las Vegas place you plant on, about twelve fixed lots, grows in
   place (records/BOHEMIA_HOME_RESEARCH_ROUND_3_OUR_ONE_SCREEN_11_3_26.md).
2. YOUR PLACE IS SEEN THROUGH ITS OWN SECURITY CAMERAS: the rest of the game stays at its 45-degree bird's-eye; your settlement, and only
   yours, is seen through fixed security cameras, PS1-era footage, the same look and pipeline as the loading screen he starred (rule 342).
   More intimate because it is yours. It works exactly the same way: you tap a lot, it shows what stands there, its level, its look.
3. THE CAMERAS UPGRADE THROUGH THE ACTS: the footage itself shows the progress (and a fall shows as the cameras failing).
4. STILL ONE SCREEN in spirit: the cameras are views of the one place, switched like a monitor.
5. THE ASSETS ARE MADE FOR THE CAMERAS: every piece that stands on a lot is modelled once through the master rig and rendered from each
   camera, so what you build appears in every view that sees its lot.

## THE COORDINATOR'S CALLS (draft; his to change)
- HOW MANY CAMERAS: three outside in act one, placed where real security cameras go on a walled Vegas block: CAM 1 THE GATE (high on the
  gate post, looking down the drive and the street: the main, the purifier, who comes in), CAM 2 THE YARDS (a corner of a house eave,
  wide lens across the backyards and garages: the kitchen, the beds, the pool), CAM 3 THE ROOFS (a pole or a tower: the whole block, the
  panels and the tank, the wall). Each shows four to six of the twelve lots, so every lot is a thumb wide on a phone. Act two adds CAM 4
  INSIDE (his indoor upgrade: the clubhouse or the storm drain under the block).
- THE CAMERAS BY ACT: act one, a dying system (grainy, nearly black and white, low frame rate, a time stamp that is wrong, one camera
  flickers); act two, rebuilt (colour, steady, the inside camera); act three, modern (clean, sharp, more cameras), or failing if you let
  it fall.
- SWITCHING: tap the camera's label or swipe; the cut between cameras is a monitor's (a roll, a frame of static).
- TALL AND WIDE: every camera shot both ways (rule 348).
- PERFORMANCE: pre-rendered like the towns (never real-time 3D): one plate per camera, each lot's piece and state as its own layer from
  the same camera and sun, so no combination is re-rendered; 60 frames a second on a phone.
