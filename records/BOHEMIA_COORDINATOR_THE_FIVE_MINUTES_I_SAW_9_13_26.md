## WHAT A STRANGER SEES, IN ORDER
1. TITLE. Fine. "TAP TO ENTER", the stamp "DEMO - BUILD 9/13v - WHO WOULD FOLLOW YOU".
2. THE FIRST CARD, before you have taken one step: "THE METER READER. Nobody has picked it
   up yet. About 4 and a half hours on foot, about 8 and a half hours there and back. Pays
   one battery." with three haggle buttons and GET UP. A stranger's first offer in this
   game is an eight-and-a-half-hour round trip for one battery, and three choices about a
   currency he has not met. -> QUESTS [first ask] premise: the first ask must be minutes
   away and the haggle should not be on the first card.
3. THE STREET (frame F, G). The top half of the phone is a WALL OF BRICKS drawn head-on,
   a huge flat facade; under it a beige band, a thin kerb strip with tufts, then asphalt
   with cracks filling the bottom. The player stands on the beige band. It reads as
   HORIZONTAL STRIPES OF TEXTURE, an elevation, not a street seen at 45 degrees. THAT is
   "the streets don't look like streets": there is no ground plane; a wall, a strip, a
   strip, a strip. The kerb strip reads as a sidewalk turned sideways. A flat freeway
   drawn as one more band would read as a bridge for the same reason. -> DIRECTION
   [streets read], COOK [streets fixed], LIFE+CITY [freeway reads]: the premise is the
   missing ground plane at street zoom, not the tile art.
4. THE ZOOMED-OUT VIEW (frame I) READS BETTER THAN THE STREET: kerbs, a road with edges,
   houses in a row, a block with an orange box. That is where the 45-degree world lives.
   The walked view is the one that fails.
5. THE UI. Left rail: STANDING, BUILD HERE, SCAVENGE 8H, MARKET, CITY, WHOLE MAP, BIKE,
   SLEEP. Top: MUSIC, SAVE, PHONE, OUTFIT. A gear. The pad with "DROP IN" written across
   the face. That is thirteen controls on a phone before the player has walked a block.
   CITY, WHOLE MAP and DROP IN are exactly the buttons he named. The half-size ruling is
   not visible in the demo. -> UI [no tabs] first, and the demo must carry the half-size.
6. TEXT COLLISIONS. The job line at the top ("Find why the block browns out · get
   inside...") is huge and runs under the gear button. The "that one is not moving.
   there is something behind it. BACK OFF" card sits on top of BUILD HERE and SCAVENGE.
   A floating speech bubble ("They do not know that yet. Neither did I for a while.")
   with no visible speaker when zoomed out. -> UI [every card] and RUN [dead cards]: what
   does BACK OFF do, and who is talking.
7. AN ORANGE VERTICAL BAR down the right edge of the street (frame G), a block border
   drawn as a line through the walked view. -> LIFE+CITY [freeway reads] premise: the
   same class, an edge line that reads as an object.
8. THE CATS. Four of them, moving, around the player. He is right, they are cool.
9. NO FIGHT, NO CAR, NO ENEMY in the whole drive. A yellow-coated person stood to the
   right and said nothing. -> COMBAT [first fight].

## THE ONE SENTENCE
The demo fails at street zoom because there is no ground: it is a stack of texture bands
under thirteen buttons, and everything he named follows from that. The zoomed-out view
already reads as a city.
