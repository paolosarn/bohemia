# WORDS Q5 -- REFUSAL: NOT ANSWERING, CHANGING THE SUBJECT, LYING BY OMISSION
# VAMILY research round, 9/5/26, lane WORDS (words-8dqrnq). MODE: RESEARCH.
# The question, verbatim: "Refusal. How the best games let a character NOT answer,
# change the subject, or lie by omission, and how the player still learns
# something. Against our asking module's eighteen blocks."

## THE ANSWER IN ONE LINE
**A refusal teaches the player only when it BREAKS a rule of conversation, and all
four refusals in our asking module are the one kind that breaks none: "not my
department". Four blocks, one idea, and the player learns nothing but "wrong
person".**

## 1. THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG
The eighteen blocks are 14 answers plus 4 reusable refusals. Here are all four:

    worker   I work. I do not keep track of who is doing what to who.
    scav     Ask somebody who stands still for a living. I am never in one place long enough.
    keeper   I hear things across this counter all day. That is not one of them.
    watch    I watch this street. That is not this street.

**Every one is a competence disclaimer.** Different jobs, identical move: I am the
wrong person, try elsewhere. Under Grice that is a maximally COOPERATIVE reply. It
withholds nothing, hides nothing, and therefore implies nothing. It is a routing
message wearing a character's clothes.

Four reusable refusals is a good economy. Four reusable refusals that are all the
same MOVE is one refusal with four skins.

## 2. WHY A REFUSAL CAN TEACH ANYTHING AT ALL
Grice: a speaker who openly FLOUTS a rule of cooperation forces the listener to
work out why, and that inference is the implicature. Conversation analysis adds
the shape: a refusal is a DISPREFERRED response and is marked by delay, a preface,
an account and mitigation, which is exactly how a listener knows it is a refusal
before the content arrives.

So the payload is set by WHICH rule gets broken. Four rules, four different things
the player learns:

    RULE BROKEN        THE REFUSAL LOOKS LIKE            WHAT THE PLAYER LEARNS
    quantity           says less than the question       there is more, and it is
    (say enough)       needs, and stops                  being kept from me
    relation           answers a different question      the real subject is
    (be relevant)      entirely                          dangerous to touch
    manner             vague, hedged, ambiguous          they are covering, and
    (be clear)         where they were clear before      they are not good at it
    quality            says something the player can     THEY LIED, and the player
    (be truthful)      later discover is false           finds out later, not now

**"Not my department" breaks none of them, so it carries no payload.** That is the
whole diagnosis in one sentence.

## 3. WHAT THE BEST GAMES DO
**DISCO ELYSIUM.** A failed check is not a closed door, it is a DIFFERENT ROUTE:
failure returns a new lead, a scene that reveals the character, or something
funny. **The rule is that a refusal must return a different payload, never a
null.** Ours return a null with an apology attached.

**OXENFREE.** Silence is a first-class option and the world does not wait for you.
Characters keep talking whether or not you answer, so declining to speak is itself
a move that costs and reveals. Our silent options already exist and reach the same
node as the spoken ones, which is right; what is missing is the world continuing
without us.

**PENTIMENT.** Its subject is that accounts of the same event disagree and the
player decides what to believe. A refusal there is not an obstacle, it is
evidence.

## 4. MEASURED ACROSS OUR WHOLE CORPUS
1,669 spoken lines, swept for every refusal move:

    move                              lines   share
    flat no / cannot say                 32    1.9%
    silence as the answer                19    1.1%
    do not know                          12    0.7%
    changes the subject                  12    0.7%
    NAMES A COST OF TELLING              11    0.7%
    not my domain (competence)            6    0.4%
    answers a question that was not asked  1    0.1%
    ANY refusal move at all              91    5.5%

**And the good one already exists.** The eleven lines that NAME A COST are the only
ones that teach: "I've got a kid", "it's your face they'll remember", "you'd be
asking in front of people". Those withhold AND explain why, which tells the player
the subject is dangerous without answering it. That is a quantity flout done
properly, it is in the hand-written quest scenes, and **none of it reached the
system that handles refusal at scale.**

Our best refusal writing and our refusal MECHANISM have never met.

## 5. WHAT THIS SAYS TO DO, WHEN THE LANE RETURNS TO BUILD
1. **FOUR REFUSALS, FOUR DIFFERENT MOVES, NOT FOUR JOBS.** Replace the trade-shaped
   set with a rule-shaped one: one that says too little, one that answers a
   different question, one that goes vague, one that names the cost. Any trade can
   speak any of them, so the economy improves rather than costing more.
2. **A REFUSAL RETURNS A PAYLOAD.** Disco Elysium's rule. Even a dead end hands
   over one fact: who to ask instead, what it would cost, or that the question
   itself was noticed.
3. **MARK IT AS DISPREFERRED.** A real refusal is delayed and prefaced. Ours arrive
   instantly and fully formed, which reads as a menu entry, not a person deciding
   not to tell you. This is the same beat the earlier rounds asked for: a held beat
   before the line does the marking that the page cannot.
4. **LYING BY OMISSION NEEDS THE WORLD TO CATCH IT.** A quality flout is only worth
   writing if the player can later discover it was false. That is a memory-organ
   question before it is a words question, and it is not mine to decide.
