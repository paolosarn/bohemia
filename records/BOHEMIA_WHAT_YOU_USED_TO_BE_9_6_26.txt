WHY IT IS A WORD AND NOT A STAT, WHICH IS THE ROW'S OWN BEST FINDING
"A BACKGROUND IS NOT WHAT SOMEBODY CAN DO. IT IS WHAT THEY STILL THINK THEY
ARE." 55% of American workers derive their self-identity from their job; losing
the role is a status passage; and people who lose one build an ALTERNATIVE WORK
IDENTITY rather than call themselves unemployed. The occupational identity
outlives the occupation. So ten years on, the valley is full of people who still
introduce themselves by a job that has not existed for a decade. NO DAMAGE
BEFORE THE DIAL is not a constraint this worked around; it is the better read.

THE MISTAKE OF MINE, AND THE GATE FOUND IT
================================================================================
*** ONE HUMAN HAD TWO PASTS. *** wasOf fell back to `person.id` when there was
no key. That looks generous and it is a SECOND IDENTITY: the walked city's
roster object carries id '12:12:900' while the card's person carries key
'P:city:12:12:900', so the same human came back with a different former trade
depending on which object asked. The gate caught it by finding a water-plant
worker in the roster and opening a card that said something else entirely.
Fixed by refusing without a key, exactly as nameOf refuses without one, and the
refusal is now a claim so it cannot come back.

A second gate mistake, twice over, both mine and both about the CHECK: the card
claims first hand-placed the player at a roster person's coordinates (ctAdjacent
answers about DRAWN bodies, so all three went red while the feature worked), and
then swept the clock at the spawn where the screen holds about ONE body and it
was always the same off-Strip cab driver. It goes to where a body of that house
actually lives now, and everything after that -- the clock, the draw, standing
beside them, opening the card -- is the real thing a player does.
