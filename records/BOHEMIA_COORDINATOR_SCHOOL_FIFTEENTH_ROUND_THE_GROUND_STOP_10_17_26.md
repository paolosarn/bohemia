# THE COORDINATOR'S SCHOOL, ROUND FIFTEEN: THE GROUND STOP (10/17, sweep AM; rule 90 continues one round per sweep)

## The world outside game development

Air traffic control. When the sky over an airport is full, or weather cuts the arrival rate, the controllers do not let planes take off and circle; they issue a GROUND STOP or a ground delay program, and the planes wait at their origin gates with engines off. The order they are released in is not who shouted loudest; it is the schedule and the arrival rate the airport can actually take. A flight held on the ground costs fuel for nobody; a flight held in the air costs fuel, attention and risk for everyone. The FAA's own glossary: a ground stop is a procedure requiring aircraft that meet specific criteria to remain on the ground; a ground delay program assigns departure times so arrivals match the rate the airport can accept.

Sources (search text): https://www.fly.faa.gov/Products/Glossary_of_Terms/glossary_of_terms.html ; https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/foa_html/chap18_section_6.html ; https://www.fly.faa.gov/ois/

## What the coordinator does not do

It opens chats because the rule says open chats. Rule 112(b) says: every sweep, open a fresh session for every running lane with an OPEN row and no running session. This sweep the fleet had just finished 21 swings in one hour and he had just said he hit the five-hour usage cap. Opening 21 sessions into a closed window would have put 21 planes in the air with no runway: each would start, read the board, and stall on the first call, and the next sweep would find 21 half-started sessions and no commits. The rule had no clause for the sky being full.

## The one change, applied this sweep

A GROUND STOP: when the usage window is reported closed (his word, or a lane's summary reporting a limit), the sweep still runs its reading, its triage, its record and its handoff, but it OPENS NO SESSIONS. The finished sessions are archived (free), the board is written with the next round for every lane, and the lanes wait at the gate. When the window reopens, the next sweep releases them in rule 113(b)'s order: the build lanes first while the STOP block stands (RUN, RUN TWO, PLUMBER), then art, then design, then QA, then UI, words and sound, then research, and the count of sessions open at once follows the hours split. The routine keeps firing hourly; a sweep under a ground stop is a short sweep by design, and it says so in the five lines. Rule 112(b) is amended in place with this clause.

## Why it is a round and not a confirmation

Rounds thirteen and fourteen taught the sweep to measure silence and to use quiet time. This is the first that tells the sweep what to do when the fleet is ready and the sky is not: hold on the ground, release by schedule, never circle.
