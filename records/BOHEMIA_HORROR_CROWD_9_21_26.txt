================================================================================
The crowd he sees is __THE_STREET_IS_NEVER_EMPTY__, the 9/7 borrow, and it was
the right call at the time. Measured here with the borrow switched off in a probe
(nothing committed):

  at ten in the morning   41 people outside in the world

                          the borrow makes it 23
  at eight at night        4 people outside in the world
                          the world itself puts 3 on his screen
                          the borrow still makes it 24

At the busiest hour of the day the honest street is two people. That is why the
borrow exists and the 9/7 record already did this arithmetic: a sight radius of 9
cells covers 361 cells against a window of 12.8 million, so the chance of seeing
anybody is about 2%.

THE DEFECT IS NOT THE BORROWING. IT IS THAT THE BORROW CARRIES A FIXED NUMBER
INSTEAD OF THE NUMBER THE WORLD EARNED. Fix that and R7 is satisfied for free, in
both directions: busy at ten, and at eight at night you walk the same street you
walked this morning and nobody is on it.
