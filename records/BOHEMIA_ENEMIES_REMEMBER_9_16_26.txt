================================================================================
roadChoose is where EVERY encounter choice in this game lands. Measured with a
brace-balanced read of the function itself, and a control to prove the reader
works (3 hits of a string I knew was in there):

  roadChoose            5,230 characters
  deeds published       ZERO
  witnesses             ZERO
  looks at the crowd    ZERO

So you could hand a starving man your water, or face him down and watch him walk
off down the wash, and NOT ONE PERSON IN THE VALLEY KNEW IT HAPPENED. Every
encounter was a one-shot game inside an architecture whose only real advantage is
that nothing is one.

AND THE OTHER HALF. ctSeenMeCount, which answers "what did this person personally
watch you do", was read in exactly ONE place: a crew list on a card. Nobody on
the street had ever shown that they know you.

NOTHING IS STORED AND NOBODY IS INVENTED. There is no book of who you spared,
because the man is already carrying it. The man himself is a body the game had
already drawn: a street with sixty-one people on it does not need a sixty-second
one for a guy to step out of the wash.

AND NO WEIGHT IS WRITTEN. DEED_WEIGHT still ships empty. Naming an act is
mechanism; what it is worth is a ruling, so these weigh ZERO until he rules the
table, exactly like the five kinds this surface already published.

THE THREE ARMS OF HIS OWN APPROVED ENCOUNTER, which is scavenger_shakedown,
"pay / scare / drop -- the moral mirror of attack-anyone":
  GIVE HIM SOMETHING   favour
  FACE HIM DOWN        spared
  put him down         downed
Three different acts on purpose. Tit-for-tat copies your LAST move, and a memory
that cannot tell a handout from a beating cannot copy anything.

================================================================================
*** A KIND NEEDS A ROW IN TWO TABLES OR IT IS REMEMBERED AND MUTE ***
================================================================================
This is the round's own lesson and it cost two measurements to find.

FIRST, publishing `spared` reached one person and LANDED NOWHERE: the count came
back 1 and the man's memory was still empty. ctKnownDeeds drops any kind with no
row in CT_DEED_WORDS, which is correct design -- a deed nobody can talk about is
not a deed the city can show -- and invisible from the writer's side.

THEN it landed and he still said nothing, because the street bark reads a SECOND
table, CT_REACT. Two tables, two different questions: what a deed IS, and what
somebody SAYS when they see you again. A kind needs a row in both.

Only `saw` and `heard` are written, not `theirs` and `rival`: these deeds carry
no outfit, so those two buckets are unreachable and ctReactLine falls back to
`saw` anyway. Writing content nothing can reach would be dead weight on a table
whose whole point is that somebody says it out loud.

================================================================================
AND THE HALF I BUILT AND DELETED BEFORE IT EVER RAN
================================================================================
The first version of this shipped a second line writer, a set of its own words,
and a book of who had already been told, to make the man turn up and say so on
the street's line. Then CT_DEED_WORDS turned out to feed ctDeedBark, whose own
header says it exists so people speak "WITHOUT YOU OPENING ANYTHING".

TWO WRITERS FOR ONE FACT is the exact defect this lane has found in its own work
three rounds running: two copies of the body ladder that put a speech bubble on
somebody's face, two ids for one man in the lock, two windows with one name in
the instrument. So the second writer was deleted before it ever ran, and the
feature is now a table and one call. It is also better: he hears it from the
man's own mouth instead of from a caption.

AND IT ONLY READS BECAUSE OF LAST ROUND. The bark is a speech bubble over a head,
and until [a human being] shipped, that bubble was painted ON the speaker's face.
