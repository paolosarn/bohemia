## WHAT THE SURVEY SAYS, IN SIX LINES
1. The games most like ours (a company that travels and fights) keep the home SMALL and on ONE screen: the hamlet, the ant farm, the
   ship, the castle, the cult. None makes you build tile by tile.
2. Battle Brothers has no home, and its players built one anyway (the Stronghold mod): your own fortress on the map, built from the
   retinue screen, attacked by its neighbours, fed by a road. That is rule 347 nearly word for word, made by the people who play it.
3. The people make it: the castle grows from recruits, Evermore's buildings stall without a citizen, the cult is 'the followers'.
4. Growth is in place (levels, rooms, people), never past the frame.
5. The biggest numbers belong to the simplest taps (Clash, Fallout Shelter, Townscaper); the worst reviews to free placement (Fallout 4).
6. Inside a game of journeys the home opens LATE: Evermore at chapter 4, Battle Brothers' Stronghold only after the cart is fully upgraded.
   That matches 'never in the first minute' and his 'are you sure? This is permanent'.
