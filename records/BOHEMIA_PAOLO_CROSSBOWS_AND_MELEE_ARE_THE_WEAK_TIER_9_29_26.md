## THE READING (garble: 'Malay' = melee)
A direction, not a hard ruling: crossbows and real melee weapons (bats, machetes, axes, pipes, knives, spears) are
in the arsenal as THE WEAK TIER, comparable to Battle Brothers' weaker weapons, and they matter because AMMO RUNS
OUT: 'if you have no ammo you gotta do what you gotta do.' This is the ammunition rule (46d, TUNING [ammo]) seen
from the player's side: the gun is the good weapon, the round is the scarce thing, the blade is what you hold when
the round is gone. It goes into COMBAT [weapon shapes]' research and the VOTE options (47b), not as canon yet.

## THE MANAGER'S NOTE FOR THE RESEARCH (rule 41; a hole to pull, gently)
'Weak' in Battle Brothers is not 'useless': the dagger is the weakest weapon and the one that kills the armoured
man through his plates; the cheap spear holds a line no sword can. Real crossbows kill at 40 metres and reload in
ten seconds; a machete kills in one swing; a fire axe opens a door and a skull. So the weak tier's job in the
research is Battle Brothers' job for its weak weapons: cheap, everywhere, no ammo, and each with ONE thing it does
that the gun cannot (silence, no reload, breaking cover, the puncture). The options he picks in VOTE say which.
