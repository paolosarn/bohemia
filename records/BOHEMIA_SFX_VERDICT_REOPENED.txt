BOHEMIA — SOUNDS HE JUDGED THAT HAD TO BE REOPENED
==================================================
Every line here is a thumb Paolo already spent that no longer points at the
sound he heard. verdict_frozen_gate.py REFUSES to re-bless a judged candidate
unless its id appears below with a reason, so this file is the only door, it is
committed, and it is permanent: nothing ever gets removed from it. The gate
prints every line of it on every run, so a reopening can never go quiet.

The bar for a line here is deliberately brutal. "I found a better voice" is not
a reason. "It was illegal, or it was broken" is. One line per id, four fields,
and the reason stays on the one line so the gate can read it back.

FORMAT:  id | date | what he heard -> what he gets now | why it could not stand

HOW IT HAPPENED. Nothing in the machine checked a voice NAME against
gates/bohemia_graveyard.txt, so SFX-07 and SFX-08 borrowed two voices Paolo had
killed on 7/19 and shipped them into moments he then judged. The measurement
tool was also reading each voice on a clean wire instead of through the gain and
panner the engine actually plays it through, so a voice that dies in that path
measured perfectly healthy. Both holes are closed: instrument_gate.py now sweeps
every borrowed name against the registry, and
tools/bohemia_sfx_instrument_measure.py measures on the engine's own path.

approvals of a sound with no instrument in it. They now play a live breath voice
at a level the render gate can measure. He has not heard the new ones. Per
EVERYTHING IS A THUMB they are NOT queued for his approval -- they are in the
game, and he corrects them if he hates them.
