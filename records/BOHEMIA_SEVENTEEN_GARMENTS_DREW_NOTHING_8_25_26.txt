SEVENTEEN THINGS HE COULD PUT ON DREW NOTHING AT ALL
CHARACTER lane, 8/25/26.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT WAS FOUND, WHICH IS THE POINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The hair audit built for clause 1 ("a haircut is one haircut from every angle")
found two of fifteen styles that were a mane from the front and a crop from the
side, sitting inside a green gate. The obvious next question was whether genHair
was special. IT WAS NOT: every generator in the wardrobe branches on facing, and
nothing had ever asked the same question of the 204 canon garments.

tools/bohemia_garment_identity_audit.js is that question, ported. It was looking
for a coat that changes shape when you turn round.

IT FOUND SEVENTEEN GARMENTS THAT WERE NOT THERE.

    every KNIT CAP, WATCH CAP, FIELD CAP, WORK CAP and SLOUCH BEANIE
    CHINESE RICE FARMER HAT
    WRAPAROUND SHADES and GREY WRAPAROUNDS

MEASURED ON THE REAL FRAME, wearing them, not on a generator's return value:
equipping one changed ZERO PIXELS. Not thin, not misplaced, not subtly wrong.
ABSENT. He could pick one in the CHARACTER tab and nothing happened.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE CAUSE: ONE LITERAL 56, TWICE, IN TWO DIFFERENT GENERATORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BAKED is RIG2X'd. Its layer indices pack as y*BAKED.W and BAKED.W IS 112. Both
call sites divided by a hard-coded 56, which returns DOUBLE THE ROW.

genHat, and this one had a SECOND error stacked on the first:

    var ry=(L[li]/56)|0;                    // 20 for a head whose top row is 10
    hl = hTop + (HAT_MAX_Y[gd2] - rt*S);    // and then doubled AGAIN
       = 10 + (18 - 40) = -12

put() refuses every pixel below the hat line, and the hat line was TWELVE ROWS
ABOVE THE TOP OF THE CANVAS. Every hat drew nothing, on every facing.

The comment on that line reads "HAT_MAX_Y is RIG space, rt is 56 space -- scale rt
or the durag line lands at half depth". Half of that is true: HAT_MAX_Y is rig
space. rt was NOT 56 space; it only looked like it because of the literal 56 in
the expression directly above. A FIX APPLIED TO A MISREAD MEASUREMENT MADE IT
TWICE AS WRONG, and the comment then made the mistake look considered.

genAcc's shades, same two errors in one expression:
    ry3 = ((+fk2/24)|0)+G24_OY;             // 56 space, never scaled to the rig
    yy3 = (L3[lj3]/56)|0;                   // double the row again
dHead came out at -10, the lens band was pushed ten rows above the eyes and off
the head, and put() dropped all of it.

FIXED: BAKED.W is the only correct divisor and no scale factor belongs on top.
    hl is 18 facing S and E, 24 facing N -- eight rows down a 22-row head, deeper
    at the back, which is exactly what a durag does and exactly what the comment
    below it has always said it should be.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*** AND THE HEADWEAR GATE WAS GREEN THROUGH ALL OF IT, FOR TWO REASONS ***
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. IT LIFTS genHat OUT OF THE ALPHA and runs it against a synthetic grid, so
   BAKED and HAT_MAX_Y are undefined in its harness and `hl` falls back to the 30%
   band. THE BROKEN PATH NEVER EXECUTES THERE. A gate that lifts a function out of
   its environment cannot see a bug that lives in the environment.
2. WHAT IT HOLDS IS "a hat never crosses the durag line", AND A HAT THAT DRAWS
   NOTHING CROSSES NOTHING. A check a corpse passes is not checking for life.

Both halves are the same failure the pictures kept making this week: an
instrument that cannot tell "no defect" from "nothing ran" always reports the
comfortable answer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE NEW GATE, AND MY FIRST VERSION OF IT WAS ALSO VACUOUS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
gates/one_garment_per_slot_gate.js now sweeps all 221 canon garments on the REAL
worn path -- the only surface that could ever have caught this -- and fails if any
of them changes zero pixels.

FIRST CUT PASSED THE MUTATION THAT REINTRODUCED THE BUG. It compared "wearing it"
against "wearing nothing", and putting a garment on SUPPRESSES the painted layer
of that slot (the 8/21 rule), so the frame differed by his DURAG DISAPPEARING even
when the hat drew nothing. It was measuring "something changed", not "the garment
appeared". The painted layer of the slot under test is now pulled in BOTH frames,
so the only thing that can differ is the garment itself.

MUTATION PROOF, with both original errors restored:
    > FAIL EVERY GARMENT HE CAN PUT ON ACTUALLY APPEARS (206/221 -- DRAWS NOTHING:
      CHINESE RICE FARMER HAT, CHARCOAL WATCH CAP, RUST WATCH CAP, OLIVE FIELD CAP,
      STORM KNIT CAP, SAND KNIT CAP, SLATE WORK CAP, OXBLOOD SLOUCH BEANIE, ...)

WHAT THE IDENTITY AUDIT ALSO FOUND AND I HAVE NOT FIXED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Recorded rather than half-done, so the next session inherits the list:
    SMITH'S APRON / TRADES APRON   hem 0.188 facing S, 0.000 from behind. An apron
        that vanishes below the waist when he turns. Plausibly correct (an apron
        hangs at the front and its skirt hides behind the body) but it is a hard
        on/off across one notch, which is the shape of a bug.
    ROAD CAPE / SHOULDER MANTLE / RUCK PACK   sleeve coverage 0.03 facing E and
        0.97 facing NE. Probably view geometry (from behind, a pack is between you
        and the arms) rather than a defect -- WHICH MEANS THE RULER IS WRONG FOR
        back-LAYER ITEMS, and that is the thing to fix before judging them.
    52 garments change by a tenth of the body or more in a single notch. Most of
        that is the same back-layer ruler problem. Triage it before chasing any of
        them; the hair work says an area-shaped ruler will lie to you.
