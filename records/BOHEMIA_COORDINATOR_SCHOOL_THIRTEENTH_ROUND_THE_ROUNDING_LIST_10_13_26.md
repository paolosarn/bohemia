# THE COORDINATOR'S SCHOOL, ROUND THIRTEEN: THE ROUNDING LIST (10/13, sweep AK; rule 90 continues one round per sweep)

## The world outside game development

The hospital ward at night. A nurse does not wait for the call light; she rounds on every bed on a schedule, whether or not a bed has asked for anything, because the patient who has gone quiet is the one who falls. A 2022 analysis of 31 military treatment facilities (FY2017 to FY2019) linked hourly rounding to about 21 percent fewer falls (incidence rate ratio 0.79). An industry study across 27 units in 14 hospitals reported fewer call lights when rounding was hourly: bathroom requests down 40 percent, pain 35, positioning 29. The lesson is not the numbers; it is the shape: silence is checked on a schedule, not trusted.

Sources (search text): https://www.ingentaconnect.com/content/wk/phh/2022/00000028/00000002/art00038 ; https://www.studergroup.com/hardwired-results/hardwired-results-07/improve-clinical-outcomes-with-hourly-rounding/ ; https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT00632944

## What the coordinator does not do

It reads commits. A sweep is built from what landed; a lane that landed nothing is invisible in it, and a quiet board reads as a calm board. This sweep measured the silence instead: since 10/10 at about nine in the evening, PLUMBER, TUNING, MODS, QUESTS, FACTIONS, PEOPLE and ECONOMY have not committed once (DYNASTY, WORDS and LIFE+CITY are held and quiet by rule), while the whole art pipe written on 10/11 waits on PLUMBER's gates and stopwatch. Nothing is wrong on any of those boards. Nobody sent them the word. He asked once before 'which chats should I be talking to' (10/4) and the coordinator answered once; it never made the answer a standing line.

## The one change, applied this sweep

THE ROUNDING LIST: every sweep record carries, under THE LINES, the lanes with no commit since the sweep before last, in the order of what waits on them, with one clause each saying what; and the reply to him carries the same list in one line, so the word reaches the chats that have gone quiet. A held lane is listed as held, never as owed. The list is a count he can act on, never a complaint about him; he sends the word to whoever he likes (rule 40h).

## Why it is a round and not a confirmation

The board already had MODE lines, open-row counts and the plate's outs, and all of them describe a lane's state when it runs. None of them say that a lane has not run. The rounding list is the one instrument on this board that measures silence.
