# PART 1 — WHAT "THE DOLLAR CRASHED" ACTUALLY MEANS

## The dollar is not America's money. It is the world's settlement layer.
Pre-collapse the USD was ~58% of global central-bank reserves, sat on one side of
roughly 88% of all foreign-exchange transactions, priced essentially all oil and
traded commodities, and denominated about half of world trade invoicing. The
world's ships did not move because of American demand; they moved because
**letters of credit cleared in dollars.**

So killing the dollar does not merely impoverish Americans. It **removes the
promise layer that made strangers trade with strangers.** A ship's cargo changes
hands four times between loading and unloading, and every one of those handovers
is a bank promise, not a physical transfer.

**OUR CANON ALREADY NAILED THIS.** The GDD, Logistics section: *"Modern logistics
dies of broken PROMISES, not broken machines (dollar settlement fails → letters
of credit freeze → cargo stops changing hands → shelves empty in 72 hours)."*
That is exactly right and it is the single most important sentence in the
economic canon. Everything below is downstream of it.

## THE 72-HOUR NUMBER IS REAL
Modern grocery retail runs 1-3 days of shelf stock on just-in-time replenishment.
The UK's 2000 fuel protests emptied supermarket shelves in **four days** with the
banking system fully intact — the trucks simply stopped. Our GDD's "shelves empty
in 72 hours" is if anything generous.

## BUT AT TEN YEARS OUT, THE CRISIS IS OVER
This is the thing most post-apocalyptic fiction gets wrong and it changes
Bohemia's whole tone.

**Hyperinflations are SHORT.** Weimar Germany's ran roughly 14 months and ended in
November 1923 with the Rentenmark. Zimbabwe's ended in 2009 when the country
simply abandoned its own currency and dollarized. Yugoslavia's 1993-94 episode —
the second-worst in recorded history, prices doubling every ~34 hours — lasted
about 24 months. **Nobody stays in hyperinflation for a decade.** Money either
gets replaced or gets abandoned.

Ten years out you are not in a currency crisis. **You are living in the poor,
stable, permanent thing that replaced it.** That matches the GDD's own clock —
*"collapse, the hell years, first stability"* — and it should govern the feel:

> **BOHEMIA IS NOT CHAOS. IT IS A FUNCTIONING BAD NORMAL.** People have jobs.
> There are prices. There are schedules. Everyone knows the rules. The rules are
> just much worse than they used to be. Panic is a memory; competence at poverty
> is the culture.

That is a far more interesting register to write NPCs in than survivalist frenzy,
and it is what actually happens.

## WHAT THE DECADE COSTS, IN REAL NUMBERS
| case | span | what it did |
|---|---|---|
| Venezuela 2013-21 | 8 yrs | GDP down ~75-90%; **~7.7M emigrated — a quarter of the population** |
| Soviet collapse 1991-99 | 8 yrs | Russian male life expectancy fell ~6 years; millions of excess deaths, mostly **alcohol, untreated chronic disease, violence, despair — not starvation** |
| Lebanon 2019-present | 6+ yrs | currency lost ~98% of value; state electricity fell to 1-4 hrs/day; middle class erased in ~24 months |
| Zimbabwe 2008 → | 15+ yrs | acute phase ended in months; the country is still broken now |

**TWO LESSONS FOR OUR DEATH MATH.** First, Venezuela's quarter-of-the-population
exodus is a real-world upper bound reached WITHOUT a total collapse — our GDD's
70-80% exodus is aggressive but defensible for a desert city with one road out
and no water of its own. Second, and more useful: **the die-off is not
starvation.** It is untreated diabetes, no dialysis, no insulin cold chain, drink,
heat, childbirth, infected wounds, and violence. That should be how survivors talk
about who they lost — not "we ran out of food," but "she ran out of the pills."

# PART 2 — PERMANENT BROWNOUTS: WHAT AN UNRELIABLE GRID ACTUALLY DOES

Our CLUSTERED POWER LAW already says 12% of circuits are live and all of them are
owned. This part is about what that does to *industry*, which is the part he
asked and the part nothing in our files has answered yet.

## THE REAL CASES
- **Lebanon.** State power (EDL) fell to 1-4 hours a day. The gap was filled by
  neighbourhood diesel generators sold by **subscription, priced per ampere** —
  and the operators became a real political force the press calls the *generator
  mafia*, with cartel pricing and enforced territories. **This is our CLUSTERED
  POWER LAW happening in real life, and it already tells us what the faction that
  owns power behaves like: not a utility, a landlord.**
- **South Africa (Eskom load-shedding).** Scheduled rotating outages, up to ~10
  hours/day at the worst stages, costing an estimated ~5% of GDP a year.
  Businesses that could move to a schedule survived; businesses that could not
  closed permanently.
- **Venezuela, March 2019.** A five-day national blackout. **Water pumping failed
  within hours** — people drew from the Guaire river. That is the lesson our GDD
  already half-has: water is a *power* problem before it is a water problem.
- **Ukraine 2022-23.** Deliberate grid destruction; within weeks the population
  had reorganised its entire daily life around the published outage schedule.
  **People adapt to unreliable power astonishingly fast — the schedule becomes
  the clock.**

## THE FINDING: INDUSTRIES SORT INTO THREE TIERS BY INTERRUPTION TOLERANCE
This is the most useful thing in this document. It decides what can exist in
Bohemia and what cannot, permanently.

### TIER 1 — DIES ON THE FIRST OUTAGE, AND DIES FOREVER
Continuous-process industry with enormous thermal mass. An unplanned shutdown
does not pause these plants, **it destroys them.**
- **Aluminium smelting.** Molten cryolite freezes in the pot line within hours;
  a frozen potline is usually a total loss requiring a rebuild.
- **Glass float lines.** The tin bath and furnace run continuously for 10-15
  years. Cool one down unplanned and the tank is scrap.
- **Cement kilns, steel blast furnaces, oil refineries, chlor-alkali plants,
  semiconductor fabs.** Same physics, same outcome.

**CONSEQUENCE, AND IT IS HUGE: Bohemia can never make new glass, new cement, new
steel from ore, new fuel, or new chips. Not ever, not by any faction, at any tech
level available in the valley.** Every pane of glass and every bag of cement in
the game is *salvage from the old world and is therefore finite and depleting.*
That is a permanent, physical justification for the entire scavenge economy — it
is not a game convention, it is thermodynamics.

**AND ONE THAT SHOULD BE CANON: NO CHLORINE.** Municipal water treatment runs on
chlorine gas or hypochlorite from chlor-alkali plants — Tier 1, gone. So water
treatment reverts to **settling, sand filtration, and boiling**, which needs fuel,
which is scarce. Our GDD already says *"cities that lose sewage die of cholera in
months"* and calls the reclaim plant THE survival event. This research says the
chlorine supply is the specific thread it hangs from. **Whoever holds the last
pool-chemical warehouse in Las Vegas holds the city's water.** In a city of
drained swimming pools, that is a beautiful, very Vegas piece of leverage.

### TIER 2 — RUNS ONLY IF YOU OWN YOUR OWN POWER
Batch processes: they stop and restart fine, but need steady power *while
running*. Machine shops and welding, water pumping, refrigeration and the cold
chain, milling grain, medical equipment, battery charging, radio transmission.

**These are exactly the 12% clusters.** This tier IS the Bohemian economy, and it
explains why power is a currency rather than a utility: owning power does not
just light your street, **it is the only way to do productive work at all.**

### TIER 3 — DOES NOT CARE ABOUT THE GRID
Muscle, daylight, fire, animals. Farming and irrigation by gravity, hand
salvage, carpentry, blacksmithing at a charcoal forge, brewing and distilling,
hand textiles, cart and bicycle transport, butchery, tanning.

**Everybody not inside a cluster lives here.** And it is where the craft skills
come back — not as nostalgia, as the only tier that works.

## WHAT BROWNOUTS DO TO PEOPLE, NOT MACHINES
1. **The day inverts.** With intermittent power and 40C+ afternoons, productive
   work moves to dawn and dusk. Our agents already do this (`bohemia_agents.js`
   runs desert shifts ~07:00-15:00 with midday shelter). **Correct, and now it has
   a second reason.**
2. **The refrigerator is the status symbol.** In every brownout economy, cold is
   the visible marker of wealth. A working fridge means meat, medicine, and the
   ability to store surplus — which is the difference between a household and a
   business.
3. **The generator schedule becomes the social calendar.** Lebanon and Ukraine
   both did this. Markets, meetings and radio broadcasts happen when the power
   is on. **Design gift: "the hours" is a real in-world noun.**
4. **Batteries are money.** Highest value-to-weight of any salvage after
   medicine, degrade over time, everyone needs them.

# PART 3 — INTERNATIONAL TRAVEL AT 1%, AND WHAT TRADE ROUTES DO

## FIRST, THE THING NOBODY SAYS OUT LOUD: THIS DELETES VEGAS'S REASON TO EXIST
Las Vegas pre-collapse ran on roughly **42 million visitors a year**, a tourism
economy near **$80 billion**, and something like **one job in four** tied directly
to visitors. Harry Reid International handled about **50 million passengers a
year**. At 1% that is a few hundred thousand — realistically, **at ten years there
is no scheduled aviation at all**: no fuel supply chain, no parts, no
certification, no controllers.

**Las Vegas is the most travel-dependent, most import-dependent large city in
North America. It grows no food, has no port, no heavy industry, and its water
comes from a river 30 miles away through pumps that need power.** It exists
because people fly in. That is precisely why it is the right setting for this
game — the apocalypse does not have to destroy Vegas. **It only has to stop
people from arriving.**

## THE BEST REAL ANALOGUE IS CUBA, AND IT SHOULD BE OUR REFERENCE
The Special Period (1991-95), after Soviet trade support vanished:
- Imports collapsed from ~$8.1B to ~$1.7B in four years. **Oil imports fell ~90%.**
- Average Cuban adult lost an estimated **5-9 kg of body weight.**
- Havana fed itself with **organopónicos** — intensive raised-bed urban
  agriculture on vacant city lots. At peak, urban agriculture supplied a very
  large share of the capital's fresh vegetables.
- **1.2 million bicycles** were imported from China. The bicycle became the
  national vehicle.
- Oxen came back, because tractors need diesel and oxen eat grass.

**Every one of those is already in our canon or adjacent to it**: grass-to-food
conversion, the golf courses as crown jewels, the bicycle in the vehicle ladder.
**Cuba is the model. It should be named in the GDD as the reference case**, the
way Chernobyl was correctly rejected — ours is an economic collapse, and Cuba is
the best-documented economic collapse of a car-dependent, import-fed, tourism-
funded island. Vegas is an island in a desert.

## THE HIDDEN IMPORT THAT KILLS MORE PEOPLE THAN FUEL: FERTILIZER
North Korea's Arduous March (1994-98) is the cautionary case. The famine was not
primarily caused by losing food imports — it was caused by losing **fuel and
fertilizer** imports, which collapsed domestic yields. Modern agriculture is
substantially a machine for turning natural gas into food via Haber-Bosch
ammonia. Cut synthetic nitrogen and yields fall on the order of 40-60%.

**OUR CANON ALREADY SOLVED THIS AND SHOULD GET CREDIT FOR IT.** The GDD:
*"The soil economy runs on compost from the landfill and reclaim-plant
biosolids: every calorie in Bohemia has passed through the reclaim plant twice."*
That is the correct answer, it is the historically real answer (biosolids and
night soil are how every pre-industrial city fertilised itself), and it makes the
landfill and the reclaim plant load-bearing infrastructure rather than set
dressing. **It is the single best piece of grounded design in the GDD.**

## WHAT TRADE COLLAPSE LOOKS LIKE, FROM THE DEEPER RECORD
- **Post-Roman Britain, 5th century.** Within roughly one generation: coinage
  stopped circulating, **wheel-thrown pottery vanished entirely** and did not
  return for ~250 years, and villa economies ended. The visible marker of trade
  collapse was not hunger — **it was the disappearance of ordinary manufactured
  things that nobody local knew how to make.**
- **The Bronze Age Collapse, ~1200-1150 BC.** Bronze needs tin, and tin came from
  thousands of kilometres away. When the routes broke, the bronze economy died —
  and **iron won, not because it was better, but because iron ore is everywhere.**

**THE LAW THOSE TWO CASES GIVE US: COLLAPSE SELECTS FOR THE LOCALLY AVAILABLE,
EVEN WHEN IT IS WORSE.** Applied to Bohemia, that is a materials palette:
- **NO**: new glass, cement, plastics, ammunition primers, synthetic fabric,
  rubber tyres, refined fuel, semiconductors, chlorine.
- **YES**: salvage steel and aluminium (recut, never resmelted), adobe and rammed
  earth (the GDD already says *"there is no lumber; every rebuild is salvage
  steel, concrete, block, rammed earth"* — correct), charcoal, leather, wool and
  cotton by hand, glass *reused* as panes and bottles, gypsum from our own quarry
  cell for plaster.
- **The tell**: the further you get from the crash, the more the new-built world
  is EARTH-COLOURED, because earth is what is left. That is a direct art brief
  and it agrees with the 85/15 tan wall law.

## THE ROUTES THAT SURVIVE, AND WHY
| route | status at 10 yrs | why |
|---|---|---|
| **I-15 NORTH to Salt Lake** | the live artery | our canon: the surviving regional refining. **The fuel road.** |
| **I-15 SOUTH to LA** | the ghost road | the exodus route. 40 miles of open Mojave. Ports are dead — no settlement, no containers. **People went this way and did not come back.** |
| **UNION PACIFIC RAIL** | **the sleeper, and we are under-using it** | rail moves ~4x the tonne-miles per litre of diesel that trucks do. In a fuel-starved world **rail is the only efficient bulk mover.** A single train that runs once a month matters more than a hundred trucks. We have 90 rail cells and a railyard on the map doing nothing. |
| **the Colorado / Lake Mead** | ours already | water, and 2GW at the dam |
| **AVIATION** | gone | not 1% — zero scheduled. The airport is a **wind-shadow salvage yard**: aluminium, hydraulic fluid, oxygen bottles, tyres, avionics, and the best flat concrete in the valley |

## AND THE CONSEQUENCE FOR THE CARAVAN
Our GDD already locked the shape: THE CARAVAN, THE CARAVANSERAI (the truck stop,
same building same job 900 years apart), THE GUARANTOR. Research confirms and
sharpens it:

**Protection of trade routes is how states are born.** The Silk Road caravanserai,
the Hanseatic League, the East India Companies — every one began as merchants
paying for escort and ended as the escort becoming the government. Our GDD says
exactly this (*"the escort becomes the army"*). **The guarantor seat is the most
powerful unassigned position in the game and it should probably not stay
unassigned much longer** — whoever holds it is the closest thing Bohemia has to a
state.

**A convoy is a rare event.** Our canon proposes 1-3 a month. Research says lean
LOW: at 1% of former movement, and with the escort economics of a 400-mile
desert run, **one a month is right, and it should be a date everyone knows.**

# PART 4 — WHAT THIS CHANGES, IN ONE PAGE

**THINGS OUR CANON ALREADY GOT RIGHT** (do not touch): broken promises not broken
machines; the caravan/caravanserai/guarantor triad; biosolids closing the
nitrogen loop; the 12% clustered power law; medicine as the highest
value-to-weight good; no lumber, rebuild in earth; the 50-80k food ceiling.

**THINGS THIS RESEARCH ADDS**
1. **THE TIER LAW** — industries sort by interruption tolerance, and Tier 1 is
   gone forever. Glass, cement, steel-from-ore, fuel, chips and **chlorine** can
   never be made again in the valley. This is the physical basis of the scavenge
   economy.
2. **THE TONE CORRECTION** — at ten years the crisis is over. Bohemia is a
   functioning bad normal with prices, schedules and competence, not chaos.
3. **THE DIE-OFF IS MEDICAL, NOT NUTRITIONAL** — people talk about who ran out of
   pills, not who starved.
4. **CUBA IS THE REFERENCE CASE**, and should be named the way Chernobyl was
   rejected.
5. **RAIL IS UNDER-USED** — 90 cells and a railyard sitting idle in a fuel-starved
   world where rail is 4x more fuel-efficient than trucks.
6. **CHLORINE IS A STORY** — the last pool-chemical warehouse in Las Vegas is the
   city's water supply.
7. **POWER OWNERS ARE LANDLORDS, NOT UTILITIES** — Lebanon's generator cartels are
   the behavioural model for whoever holds the 12%.
