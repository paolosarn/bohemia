THE TWO DEFECTS HE NAMED, AND BOTH WERE REAL

1. DOORS MESHING WITH WINDOWS — on all sixteen, and the cause was structural rather
   than cosmetic. The front door was placed at one offset and the windows by a
   SEPARATE loop, and nothing ever compared the two. Two independent placers on one
   wall will collide sooner or later; here it was every single time. I had a
   collision check for a window running past the END of its wall and no check at all
   for a window running into another opening.

2. THE TWO-STOREY HOUSES — a 5.3 m plate with ONE row of windows near the floor and
   a blank wall above it. That is not a two-storey house, that is a warehouse, and it
   reads as one because that is exactly what a warehouse is. A real two-storey has a
   floor line and a SECOND ROW of windows on the upper storey. I raised a number and
   called it a storey.

BOTH WERE FIXED IN THE GENERATOR BEFORE HIS SECOND MESSAGE LANDED (one occupancy
list per wall that every opening must claim from, an opening that cannot find clear
wall simply not drawn, plus a real floor band and an upper-storey window row with
its own occupancy list). THE FIX DOES NOT RESURRECT THE SET. He thumbed all sixteen
down; GRAVEYARD IS FINAL, and re-showing sixteen repaired houses is precisely the
"finding a legal way to ship anyway" that the STOP PRODUCING law names as the
violation itself.

================================================================================
THE LESSON, AND IT IS ABOUT THE BATCH, NOT THE HOUSES

I generated sixteen and looked at them as a CONTACT SHEET at 330 px wide. At that
size a door merging into a window is invisible, and so is a blank second storey. I
verified the batch page rendered, that sixteen images painted, that thumbs worked —
every check about the MACHINERY, none about the ART. The one house he approved got
looked at closely, four times, at full size. The sixteen never did.

VOLUME DOES NOT MEAN LOOKING LESS. A factory multiplies whatever you did not check.

WHAT THE MACHINE NOW HOLDS (gates/house_factory_gate.py, extended this turn): no two
openings on a wall may overlap, and any mass over 4 m of plate must carry a second
row of windows. Both are checkable, both would have failed this batch, and neither
existed when it shipped.
