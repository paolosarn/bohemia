# WHO RUNS THE CLONE FARM (Paolo 11/3; VAMILY rule 359 amended, rule 374; ask line 401; his pick pending, the lead is the default)

## HIS WORDS
See laws/BOHEMIA_LAW_THE_CHEAP_CLONE_11_3_26.md section 7. Short: cheap, but power hungry (cold, energy, light); use the factions we
have, do not invent one; give him options.

## WHAT A CLONE FARM NEEDS AFTER THE CRASH (the realism test)
1. Steady power day and night (the vats, the machine wombs, the cold rooms): an outage of hours kills a batch.
2. Cold storage (embryos, cell lines, the meat).
3. People who can keep machines and sterile rooms alive.
4. Buyers, or a reason worth the power.

## THE OPTIONS (the coordinator's; realism first)
A. THE MOB (the lead, and the default until he picks). The most realistic AND the most fun. Real: MGM Resorts built a 100 megawatt
   solar field north of the city in 2021 that ran most of its Strip resorts by day; every casino has walk-in freezers, kitchens that
   fed thousands, engineers on staff, and the Strip already sold the spectacle (the lore's mammoth sliders and casino zoos). After the
   crash the Mob keeps one line running on the casinos' solar: clone meat sold to the valley as tribute and trade, and the beast PITS
   on the Strip, where clones are fought for bets (Battle Brothers' arena, our skin). Story: the Mob is the only one with meat to sell,
   so it owns every hungry block; kill or free the farm and the valley's meat price changes.
B. THE TRADES. The most practical: the battery plant, the solar know-how, the industrial blocks. They run it as a working farm, meat by
   the battery, no show. Solid and real, less to fight over.
C. THE NETWORK. The dark one: the only faction with a manufactured origin, holding the substations and the radio. They grow clones and
   let them loose on purpose to keep the valley afraid and listening. The horror twist; it bends the 9/29 law's 'they broke out when
   the dollar died' (new ones would be let out on purpose).

## RULE 374. USE THE FACTIONS WE HAVE (Paolo 11/3: "we don't need to keep creating factions... we're trying to make these factions
## interesting with storylines")
When a story needs a group to do something (run a farm, hold a well, sell a thing, start a fight), it goes to one of the factions we
already have, chosen by who could really do it; a new faction is never invented for it. Every such pick deepens that faction's story.
