## WHAT THIS IS
Three more of the eighteen real places (records/target/bb/real_places.json) wore the kit art of the kind each is like: THE
BANK and THE MOB MUSEUM a courthouse like any courthouse, the NATURAL HISTORY MUSEUM a library like any library. Each has
its own piece on the map now, through the map's rig, in the same frame as the bus yard, the greenhouses, the cooling center
and the dome, drawn on its block by slices/bohemia_place_pieces.js (NO CODE in this build: three entries in
slices/place_pieces/place_pieces.json and six pictures). What each shows is what WORLD's own list says of it:

THE BANK (Valley Bank Plaza, 1975, 'a tower of paper nobody wants over the strongest room in town'): fourteen floors of dark
glass in concrete bands over a double-height banking hall whose glass is out (sixteen in all, the real tower's count); a few panes gone; the name's letters on the parapet, one
missing; the money blown into drifts against the doors and the kerb, banded bricks of it dropped, and two people who came
to look; the plaza clock, stopped. On the fourth floor 'a stove fed with hundreds': one window lit, the soot up the face
above it. On the beat: the stove, and its smoke.

THE MOB MUSEUM (the 1933 federal courthouse and post office): a stone ground floor, two storeys of brick, the cornice, the
broad stair, four columns and a stepped pediment, the sash windows in ranks. The family keeps it: two men on the steps, a
black car at the kerb. One window is lit, the hearing room ('everybody who testified in there is dead now, so the room is
very quiet'). On the beat: that window, and a cigarette on the steps. No banner and no faction colour was put on it
(COLOUR IS TERRITORY: that is FACTIONS' to say).

THE NATURAL HISTORY MUSEUM (by Cashman Field): a low tan block, its taller entry hall with the glass gone and dark inside,
the sign board with two letters missing, and out on the forecourt on his plinth the animatronic T. rex, stood upright as
the old museums stood him, 'the one thing in this valley that is never coming back'; the keeper of the bones at his feet;
the crates the lab's people left. On the beat: 'some nights his motor still roars on the backup battery': his eye comes on.

The folder is 174,891 bytes now (fourteen lossless WebP pictures and the list). Seven of the eighteen real places have their
own piece.

## WHAT WAS TRIED AND THROWN OUT
THE BANK AS A CUBE (eight wide, ten tall) read as an office block; a slab 6.6 by 5.4 reads as
the tower. FIFTEEN FLOORS AND 230 NOTES weighed 31,320 bytes, over a place's 30,000 (the gate's S4 bit): fourteen
floors over the hall, and 140 notes. THE MONEY PALE (the paper's own faded green) could not be seen on the pale plaza; darker, and 230 notes.
THE T. REX IN THE HALL'S OPENING, head to the map's eye, was a green lump; side-on he was still a lizard lying down, lost
against the hall's dark; upright, side-on, out on the forecourt, 1.15 times life, he is a dinosaur.
