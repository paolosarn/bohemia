# THE LORE-YAP TEST ON TWO VIA-GROK RULINGS
DYNASTY, row [grok lore]. 10/9/26. MODE: SCHOOL (no code). Rule 49b: test what came out of a Grok talk against canon (newest date wins), the real world, and the technology line, and report holes in plain words.

## 1. THE GROUND PICKS WHO SHOWS UP (WORLD, records/BOHEMIA_WORLD_THE_GROUND_PICKS_WHO_SHOWS_UP_10_9_26.md)
HOLDS: the ground is the board's terrain kind (one list, not two); a faction's crews show up where the faction is. Real Mojave land use agrees: a wash, a playa and a bajada are different places and different people work them.
**THE HOLE, MEASURED IN CANON:** the pool is "which factions hold cells of a ground", read from the turf ledger. The third votes (9/28, s5, newer than the turf ledger) rule "HOME BASES AND ROAMING PARTIES, NOT TERRITORY... nobody fights over lines". So the pool reads a territory map canon has retired. The record's own number shows it: the Strip "250 cells, one outfit holds 92%" is exactly a territory map. Suggested fix, plain: seed a pool from the 14 home bases (a base's party pool is the grounds within its reach, weighted by distance), so a raided or ruined base stops sending parties without any turf edit.

## 2. THE CRISIS CLOCK (the 10/9 Grok sheets, the wiki: foretold about day 50 to 70, starts about day 80 to 100, runs 30 to 100 days)
HOLDS: the repo's own 8/28 law already says a crisis is foretold 20 to 50 days out and not optional, and that the world escalates by coalition (factions that fought each other stop), so a foretold lead of about 30 days is inside our range. The same law bans a rolled or chosen crisis, and a fixed clock is neither.
**THE HOLE:** our act is about 130 lived days (DYNASTY Q22: 400 days over three generations). A crisis that starts at day 100 and runs up to 100 more days outlasts the act by 70 days. Either the crisis ends with the act (the three-acts law already lets a past act's world be rewritten by later play) or the act's length is longer than 130. Nobody has ruled which, and it decides whether the first crisis is the act's ending or its middle.
