## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
What you sell to a merchant shows up in his stock, and you can buy it back. His word beats the price: here a buyback costs what
the shop paid you, never a markup.

## THE RULING (rule 248)
(a) WHAT HE SELLS SHOWS ON THEIR SHELF: every piece he sells (one by one or SELL THE JUNK) appears at once on that shop's shelf,
    first, marked as his ('YOURS, SOLD'), in the shop and the stall alike (supplies sold at the stall, rule 242, go back on the
    stall's count the same way).
(b) THE BUYBACK COSTS WHAT THEY PAID: buying back his own piece costs exactly the batteries he got for it, never more, for as long
    as he is in that town [COORDINATOR'S DEFAULT, rule 205, his to change]. When he leaves, it stays on the shelf as the shop's
    stock at the shop's normal price.
(c) A mistake is one tap to undo: buy it back and it goes to the man or the stash it came from, as it was (its KEEP mark, its
    slot).
(d) THE GATE: on the phone and computer: sell three pieces and a supply; each is on the shelf at once, marked his; buying each back
    costs exactly what it sold for and puts it back where it was; after leaving and coming back, the price is the shop's normal one.
