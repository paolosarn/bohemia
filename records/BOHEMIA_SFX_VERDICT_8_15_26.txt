UP = this IS the sound. DOWN = dead. blank = never judged.

EXPORTED BY PAOLO 8/15/26. His fourth full sweep in four days. 118 UP / 247 DOWN
across 39 moments that now have a sound.

WHAT THIS SWEEP SETTLED: FRICTION IS NOT A FLUKE. It was 6 UP / 9 DOWN on
fifteen judgements at 330 and that was a thin finding. SFX-05 put twenty more
friction candidates in front of him and he kept ten, including TWO CLEAN
FIVE-OF-FIVE SWEEPS -- miss_past and sleep_sink, the only 5/5 moments in any
batch since the original demo set. Friction now sits at 16 UP / 19 DOWN over
thirty-five judgements and it is, by a clear margin, the best method in the
game.

THE FOUR THAT DIED ARE CLOSED. Every one died whole on its FIRST outing, and
STOP PRODUCING (Paolo 7/26) ends a feature on a second rejection -- but the
shape of these four says something sharper than a count does. The three
biggest ROOMS in the batch are three of the four deaths: clear_still (space
0.8, room 1.875), cross_in (space 0.72, room 1.25), done_ring (space 0.78,
room 1.5). The two he swept clean are the two with almost no room at all
(miss_past space 0.14, sleep_sink space 0.16). Both 5/5 sounds are things
happening AT you; all three big-room sounds are a SPACE being described. He
does not want the room announced. That is a real reading of thirty-five fresh
thumbs and it is recorded as an observation, not promoted to a cap -- the last
time a small sample became law it cost the whole material finding.

The vectors are not copied here: a candidate is a pure function of (event,
index) through BOH_SFX.cook, and records/BOHEMIA_SFX_FINGERPRINTS pins what
each one measures as.
