## WHAT IS THERE NOW (coordinator, measured on main)
The town's stall (slices/BOHEMIA_SETTLEMENT_SCREEN.html, 'THE STALL: buy, sell') sells him supplies (a dose of meds, six rounds,
food and water, tape) and buys his gear (SELLING IS ONE TAP, RUN TWO 10/25: the price on every piece in his bag, KEEP, sell the
junk). It never buys his SUPPLIES back: a can, a round, a dose or a roll of tape can only be bought.
The buy and sell knobs exist (the town's traits move buy and sell apart: buy_price_mult, sell_price_mult).

## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; the wiki copy where it has it, else recall, marked)
Every marketplace buys what you carry at a sell price well under its buy price (the spread is the merchant's cut), and a town's
situation (a famine, a market day, a raid) moves the two; food you carry is an item you can sell. Ammunition, medicine and tools
go into a pool the moment you buy them and cannot be sold back (recall). His words beat it: here every supply can be sold.

## THE RULING (rule 242)
(a) THE STALL BUYS HIS SUPPLIES: food and water (the one count, rule 209), rounds, meds and tape can be sold at the stall, one
    unit at a time or a handful at once (the same unit the stall sells: a dose, six rounds, a night's food), from the company's
    count, never below what the next night needs without a warning.
(b) BUY AND SELL ARE TWO PRICES, AND HE SEES BOTH: each line shows what it costs to buy and what it pays to sell, side by side, as
    battery icons and the count (rule 224); the sell price is under the buy price by Battle Brothers' spread (ECONOMY's number,
    from the wiki copy), and the town moves both (a famine pays more for food, a market day pays less, rule 71's traits).
(c) THE TOWN'S NEED IS THE STORY: a town short of a thing pays more for it and the stall says so in a word ('they need meds
    here'); selling to a town in need is how a smart company makes money (the realistic crash simulator: buy cheap where there is
    plenty, sell where there is none).
(d) THE GATE: at a stall on the phone and computer: sell one of each supply: the company's count drops by the unit, the batteries
    rise by the shown sell price; the sell price is under the buy price everywhere; a town with a famine pays more for food than
    one without; the warning shows when a sale would leave less than a night's food.
