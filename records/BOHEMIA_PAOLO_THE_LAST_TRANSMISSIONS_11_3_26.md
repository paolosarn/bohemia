## THE LAW (rule 353)
1. THE FEEL: SOMETIMES, the game's communication feels like a last transmission: radio and phone calls in the plain, clipped, procedural
   voice of people doing their job while it goes wrong (call signs, numbers, a calm voice getting less calm, a request repeated, the
   answer that does not come, the open mic, the cut). The horror is in what is not said and in the silence after. His word: 'this is
   Bohemian, this is my swag.'
2. A NEW REFERENCE HE NAMED: MODERN WARFARE 2 = THE COMMUNICATION'S DREAD ONLY: the Washington DC defence, where if you do not hold, you
   hear the people you did not help over the radio. Nothing else of that game (CLAUDE.md's reference list carries it).
3. THE REAL SOURCE IS THE FORM, NEVER A REAL PERSON: real air traffic control and cockpit voice recorder transcripts (as investigators
   publish them: time stamps, who spoke, '[unintelligible]', '[sound of impact]', 'end of recording'), emergency dispatch and police
   scanner traffic, wartime radio logs: we study how they read and sound. We never use a real recording, a real transcript or a real
   person's last words, and never anything from a real recent crash: every line is our own, written for Bohemia's people.
4. WHERE IT LIVES FIRST (the coordinator's defaults, his to change):
   (a) THE TOWN YOU DID NOT DEFEND: when a town is raided and you did not take its defence (or failed it), your phone picks up its last
       transmissions as it falls: the gate, the man on the roof, the clinic, then the open channel. The direct twin of his Washington
       example, on our raids.
   (b) A CREW ON THE ROAD CALLING FOR HELP on the scanner, a few blocks away, while you decide.
   (c) THE SCANNER on the phone (the feed already has a scanner voice, records/target/bb/feed_kinds.json): sometimes the post is a
       transcript, time-stamped, cut off.
   (d) Later, his to approve: a boss's crew calling him on the radio during the fight; the Amalgamation's ghost on the channel (act one
       presents it as a ghost).
5. RARE AND QUIET: 'sometimes'. Never in the first minute (9/23), never forced, never a pop-up card (the 9/20 law: from a mouth, a
   radio is a mouth). Quiet underneath (rule 348(d)).
6. SOUND FROM REAL MATERIAL: real radio static, squelch, key clicks, a real handheld's hiss (the 10/9 rule: a recording of the real thing
   or its real physics, never noise). THE VOICES are the hard part: research the honest options (voice performers, recorded lines, a
   heavily band-limited radio voice) and bring him two or three to hear before any voice ships; text transcripts can ship first.
