## POST-MORTEM: WHY THIS LANE BUILT THREE ROUNDS ON IT
The row said "the one-cell sprite and its clips, cut from the 112 assets", the coordinator
marked it FIRST LINE, and the law said "about 28 px". This lane took the size as given and
measured everything at it. What it never did was SHOW HIM THE SIZE FIRST, as a thing to
knock down, before building three rounds on it. His objection was not to any clip; it was to
the premise ("a whole new character"), and the first item in front of him was the first
chance he had to say so.
THE LESSON: WHEN A RULING CHANGES WHAT THE PLAYER SEES OF THE BODY, THE FIRST THING IN
VOTE IS THE BODY AT THAT SIZE, BEFORE ANY WORK THAT ASSUMES IT. Nothing cheaper exists
than a thumb on the premise.
