## FINDING 4 — TOBY FOX'S DISCIPLINE: PROTECT THE HIT, AND REWRITE FOREVER
=============================================================================
From his own interviews: he polices TIMING above all — characters must not
smile in the wrong scene, serious scenes are PROTECTED from jokes... and he
reserves the right to break that rule ON PURPOSE when a joke is worth it
(the rule exists so the exception detonates). He invents characters only
when "this story needs a character like this," then rewrites their dialog
"about 10,000 times over 6 years." And his famous voice-blips are not
random flavor — they are CRAFTED for comedic timing, sound doing the work
of delivery. THE LESSONS: (a) comedy is a REWRITING practice, not a first
draft — which fits us exactly: make-an-attempt drafts are written to be
edited live by Paolo, so draft lines should be SHORT and SPECIFIC, built
for his red pen; (b) squiggle voices are a delivery instrument — their
timing against the text IS the joke's rhythm.

=============================================================================
## FINDING 5 — FEAR & HUNGER'S SILENCE: EVERY DEATH IS A LESSON
=============================================================================
F&H explains nothing and that is load-bearing three ways: (1) DREAD —
behind any door could be treasure or a terrible death, and uncertainty is
the horror; (2) LEARNING-THROUGH-DEATH — every death teaches something you
carry to the next run (combat is a puzzle, not a DPS wall: bait enemies
into your own bear traps); (3) COMMUNITY — the obscurity BIRTHED the wiki
culture; shared excavation of the game's secrets is part of the game's
body. This is roguelite-native and fits our seeds: a death that teaches is
a run that mattered.

=============================================================================
## FINDING 6 — THE CRAFT SCIENCE OF FUNNY (writers-room consensus)
=============================================================================
- SPECIFICITY WINS: "Jujufruits," never "candy" — the specific noun makes
  a sharper image and a stronger reversal. Generic details kill jokes.
- CHARACTER OVER JOKES: a character who is consistently, believably
  THEMSELVES in a ridiculous situation is funnier than a joke-teller.
  One quirk, deep, not wide.
- THE RULE OF THREE: first instance is groundwork, second establishes the
  pattern, third BREAKS it and earns the laugh. Callbacks escalate.
- THE STRAIGHT MAN: comedy needs a normal person's reaction to bounce off.

## THE R2 CHALLENGE FINDING — EXPLAIN THE HANDS, NEVER THE GHOSTS
=============================================================================
Tension caught between two of our own rulings: NORMIE-EASY (the lab's PZ
brief — accessibility, no hostile complexity) vs F&H's proven power of
withholding. These only conflict if applied to the same layer. THE
RESOLUTION, as a pattern: CONTROLS AND SURVIVAL BASICS get normie-easy
clarity (never F&H's cruelty about the interface — that part of F&H we do
NOT copy); LORE, THE UNCANNY, AND THE WORLD'S TRUTH get F&H silence (the
Amalgamation is never explained — which is ALREADY canon: the 5D ambiguity
is "intentional and permanent" per GDD v2 — the purple, the perfect
lights, the robotaxis stay unexplained; wiki culture is the intended
outcome). And DEATH TEACHES WORLD-TRUTHS: death=reload plus seeded worlds
means a death can always leave the player knowing something the last run
did not. Explain the hands. Never the ghosts.

## ROUTED — THE DIALOGUE CRAFT CARD (citable pattern, all draft-writing
## lanes; extends PEOPLE 0t; composes with ALWAYS-MAKE-AN-ATTEMPT and the
## catalogue law — craft rules for HOW attempts are written, corpus still
## cited for WHAT)
1. SPECIFICITY: never the generic noun. Vegas hands us free specificity —
   named analogs, real streets, real desert things. "A casino chip from
   the Sahara's last night" beats "an old chip."
2. CHARACTER OVER JOKES: every speaker has ONE quirk, played deep and
   consistently — funny is being THEMSELVES in the apocalypse.
3. RULE OF THREE + MECHANICAL CALLBACKS: our witness/memory systems can
   REMEMBER a bit and bring it back — the third appearance breaks the
   pattern. A callback the world performs is comedy compounding into
   attachment.
4. THE STRAIGHT MAN IS CAST: Marco, hardcore realist, happy to help — the
   valley's normal-person reaction shot. Scenes with a weirdo want Marco's
   deadpan nearby.
5. PROTECT THE HIT: no jokes where trauma lands (the cold open's sister).
   Breaking this rule is legal only ON PURPOSE, rarely, for a joke worth
   the damage.
6. WRITE FOR THE RED PEN: draft lines short and specific — Paolo edits
   live; a tight draft is an editable draft.
