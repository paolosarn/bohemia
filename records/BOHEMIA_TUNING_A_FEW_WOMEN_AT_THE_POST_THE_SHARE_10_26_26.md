## 2. DOES A CAUSE'S OWN CREW RUN HIGHER? YES FOR ONE KIND OF CAUSE, NO FOR THE OTHER
The real world splits causes in two, and the split is the finding:
- A CAUSE THAT PREACHES EQUALITY (the left-revolutionary and national-liberation rebels) runs 20 to 40 percent women:
  FARC 30 to 40, the ELN about 20 (the coordinator's sources); the Tamil Tigers 20 to 30 over the war (Wikipedia's article on
  women in the Sri Lankan civil war; Stack-O'Connor gives 15 to 20 of the guerrillas and a third of the Black Tigers; the INSS
  paper: a third often claimed, the LTTE claimed half, 20 to 30 more likely); the Kurdish forces 35 to 40 (TIMEP, 2017, rough
  and advocacy-sourced, repeated by NPA Syria). Wood and Thomas (Journal of Peace Research 2017, rebel groups 1979 to 2009): a
  Marxist-leftist ideology goes with more women fighters.
- THE ANARCHISTS' OWN WAR runs lower than that band: Spain, 1936, about a thousand women at the Republican front in the first
  months, mostly from the anarchist and communist militias, and several thousand more in armed rearguard units, before the
  Republic took them off the front (Wikipedia, Milicianas in the Spanish Civil War; Lisa Lines, Milicianas). No source found
  gives the share; the militias counted their men in the tens of thousands, so the front was a few in a hundred at most. The
  modern anarchist-inspired case (Rojava's confederalism) is the 35 to 40 above.
- A FAITH runs FEWER than the post: Wood and Thomas 2017, an Islamist ideology and higher religiosity go with fewer women
  fighters; Wood's Female Fighters (2019, over 250 rebel organisations): religious fundamentalist rebels consistently avoid
  women fighters even when short of men. No study found counts a Christian militia's share.
