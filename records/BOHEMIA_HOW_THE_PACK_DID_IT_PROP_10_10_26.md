## *** THE CONTOUR, AND IT IS THE OPPOSITE OF THE ROAD PAGE ***

The road page (10/10) measured his SURFACES and found **no dark ring anywhere: his edge
runs +15 LIGHTER than his inside**, because a surface has no edge, it just keeps going.

His OBJECTS go the other way. Edge minus inside is **-49.9**, and **100% of them carry
a dark contour** -- all 784 of them, without one exception. An object is cut out of the
world and has to say where it stops.

THIS LANE SHIPPED FIVE MARKERS AS A DARK BODY WITH ONE BRIGHT ACCENT AND NO CONTOUR, and
another lane had to draw a rim round them to make them usable on the demo. **That rim is
this number, and he already owned it.** A law read off the wrong family is how that
happened: the no-outline rule is true of his ground and false of his objects.

## THE SIX RULES A MARKER TAKES FROM THIS PAGE

1. **93 x 91, not 9 x 20.** The floor is his, and 40 x 40 is the minimum, not the target.
2. **A dark contour, one pixel, all the way round.** 100% of his objects have one.
3. **The body is mid, the contour is the dark.** His body sits at 50 of 255 with **16 of
   16 value steps inside it**. Ours was a dark body with one bright accent on a four-rung
   ladder, which is the shape of the complaint.
4. **Hundreds of colours, not a seven-step ramp.** 3728 median.
5. **The sun is high and north-west**, +12 top over bottom.
6. **A silhouette, not a rectangle**: 74% of the box is filled, and the rest is shape.
