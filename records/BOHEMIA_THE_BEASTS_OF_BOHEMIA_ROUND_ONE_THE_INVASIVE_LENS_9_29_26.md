## 1. WHAT MAKES AN INVADER (the biology, five traits; the more, the worse)
1. BREEDS FAST AND YOUNG (the feral hog: two litters a year of six, breeding at eight months).
2. EATS ANYTHING (hog, rat, hyena, raccoon: garbage, crops, carrion, eggs, small animals).
3. TOLERATES A WIDE CLIMATE (the Argentine tegu lizard sleeps through a frost; a python does not).
4. NOTHING HUNTS IT where it lands (no predator, no disease, no hunter with ammunition).
5. LIVES WELL BESIDE HUMANS (the edge of a dead city is a buffet: dogs, cattle, gardens, dumps, water tanks).
A large animal can still take over if 4 and 5 hold: it just takes decades instead of years.

## 2. THE REAL CASES (each one is a precedent a lane can cite)
- ESCOBAR'S HIPPOS (Colombia): four escaped a private zoo in 1993; about 170 by 2023, the largest hippo
  population outside Africa, breeding in rivers with no predator. A dead drug lord's pets became a national
  problem in thirty years. THE MODEL FOR ONE BIG ANIMAL AT ONE WATER.
- AUSTRALIA'S FERAL CAMELS: dromedaries let go when trucks replaced them; about a million by 2008 across the
  outback, culled to a few hundred thousand. THE MODEL FOR CAMELOPS: a desert camel with no predator owns a desert.
- THE UNITED STATES' FERAL HOGS: six to nine million across 35 states, the most destructive invasive mammal on
  the continent, breeding faster than any hunt. THE MODEL FOR ANY FAST-BREEDING OMNIVORE.
- NEVADA'S OWN MUSTANGS AND BURROS: tens of thousands of feral horses and burros on Nevada range right now, in
  the Mojave and the Great Basin, protected by law and outgrowing it. PROOF that horses and donkeys go feral in
  this exact valley without any lab.
- FLORIDA'S PYTHONS AND TEGUS: Burmese pythons emptied the Everglades of small mammals in twenty years (pet
  releases and a hurricane-wrecked breeding facility, 1992: A LAB THAT BROKE OPEN IS A REAL EVENT); the tegu
  spreads because it survives cold. THE MODEL FOR THE PET-TRADE REPTILES.
- EUROPE'S RACCOONS, BRITAIN'S GREY SQUIRRELS, THE STARLING: small, fast, city-loving; the sky and the attic.

## 3. THE TABLE, INVASIVE LENS (score the five traits, 0-3 each, max 15; 'made' from round zero)
S TIER: TAKES THE VALLEY IN ONE ACT
- CAVE HYENA / SPOTTED HYENA LINE  14/15. Every trait. Clans of 20 to 80, eat bone and garbage, laugh at
  night, kill dogs, take children; the only large carnivore that grows its numbers next to a human town. The
  road's true owner by act 2.
- FERAL HOG (not a lab animal; already in the river states, arrives by act 2)  15/15. The real champion. Digs up
  every garden and grave, breeds like rats, 200 kg boars charge. The valley's food and the valley's plague.
- CAMELOPS and feral dromedaries  12/15 (slow breeder, but nothing else in the desert can touch it). The caravan
  animal and the desert's cattle; a bull camel in rut kills men.
- DIRE WOLF  12/15. Packs of 6 to 15, follow the cattle and the dogs, cross a mountain in a night; competes with
  coyotes and feral dogs and wins. The lesson beast (they flank).
- FERAL HORSE (already here)  12/15. Herds on every dry lakebed; mounts for whoever can catch one.
A TIER: TAKES A PLACE
- FERAL CATTLE / AUROCHS  11/15 (needs grass and water; the golf courses and the river).
- THE HIPPO AT LAKE MEAD (a zoo escapee, the Escobar model; the lake's water is 10 to 27 C, inside a hippo's
  range)  10/15. One pod, one water, the deadliest animal in Africa now the deadliest at the reservoir. The
  Battle Brothers Kraken, alive.
- ARGENTINE TEGU and pet-trade MONITORS  11/15. Two-metre lizards that sleep through the winter and eat eggs,
  chickens, dogs; the lindwurm at small size, in numbers.
- BISON  10/15. Herds at the water; a stampede across a home base is a raid by nature.
- THE PASSENGER PIGEON  12/15 for spread, 1 for danger. Billions in a decade, strips a field, fouls a water
  tank, blackens the sky: THE FEED'S FIRST WEATHER EVENT.
B TIER: A LANDMARK, NOT A PLAGUE (slow breeders; the bosses)
- AMERICAN LION 7/15, SMILODON 6/15, SHORT-FACED BEAR 6/15, MAMMOTH 4/15 (one herd, one shore), GROUND SLOTH
  4/15, MOA 6/15. Each is one fight you remember, never a hundred.
C TIER: DIES HERE
- The cold animals (woolly rhino, cave bear, musk ox), the pythons (Vegas frosts), the thylacine (no cover, no
  small game left once the hyenas arrive).

## 4. WHAT IT DOES TO A HURT HUMAN CIVILIZATION (the game's lesson, never preached)
Invaders do not attack cities; they EAT THE MARGINS a city lives on. Hogs take the gardens and dig the graves.
Hyenas take the dogs, then the goats, then the weak. Wolves take the cattle. Pigeons take the grain and foul the
water. Camels and horses take the grass and become the transport. Hippos close the shore. The realistic economy
follows: the first thing a settlement builds is a WALL and a WATER TANK with a lid (LIFE+CITY [build a lot]), the
first contract is a HUNT (QUESTS' contracts), the first trade good is HIDE and MEAT (ECONOMY), and the man who
can catch a horse is rich (PEOPLE backgrounds).
