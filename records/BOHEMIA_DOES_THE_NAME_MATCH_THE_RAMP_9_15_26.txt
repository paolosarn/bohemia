DOES THE NAME MATCH THE RAMP?  --  CHARACTER lane, 9/15/26, VAMILY [names lie]
Every canon garment whose NAME names a colour, measured against the ramp it renders.

THE ROW: COPPER WORK SHIRT is green, "and that one lie is the whole reason Trades
measured 121 degrees off his chosen orange." A lying name is not cosmetic: this lane
dresses factions BY NAME, so a garment that lies puts the wrong faction's colour on
a body, and COLOUR IS TERRITORY says the colour is the statement.

*** THE ROW NAMES SEVEN COLOUR WORDS. THE RAIL USES 26. ***
Rule 12: a dependency is a premise, not a gate. Sweeping only copper, oxblood, bone,
steel, teal, dust and charcoal would have cleared the wardrobe and left the rest in.

HOW EACH KIND OF WORD IS CHECKED, because one test for all of them is wrong:
  CHROMATIC  the word names a hue      -> hue, within 40 degrees
  EARTH      dust, sand, khaki        -> an earth hue AND chroma under its ceiling
  NEUTRAL    the word names greyness  -> SATURATION at or under 0.34
  DARK       the word names a value   -> VALUE at or under 0.42
40 degrees is generous on purpose. This hunts LIES, not shades: the garment the row
was opened about is 121 degrees out.
