## THE FOUR CATEGORIES

### 1. THE HALL: NEGOTIATION IS A HIDDEN NUMBER READ THROUGH A FACE, NEVER A CARD
Contracts come from a speaker with a portrait (elder, magistrate, steward, merchant). Haggling
runs on a HIDDEN variable, Annoyance: every ask adds a random 3-6; at 9 or higher the client
throws you out and cuts reputation. The player never sees the number. What the player sees is
the SAME three-word mood ladder repeating across every client: pleased, annoyed, angry. THIS IS
ALREADY THIS LANE'S OWN RULE, CONFIRMED IN THE REFERENCE, NOT DISCOVERED HERE: no card, no
number, a face that shifts. [horror ask] and [the surer the wronger] already ship this shape for
us. What is NEW here is the mechanism BB hangs under it: a hidden threshold, invisible to the
player, that a face's mood is the only report of. That is a second, portable technique --
NOT for this row's writing, since rule 21 (dial/numbers) and the gambit systems are COMBAT's,
not WORDS' -- named here so it reaches COMBAT/TUNING if they want a haggle-shaped mechanic:
the number stays hidden, the face is the whole interface.

### 2. THE TAVERN: A PROCEDURAL STORY PER RECRUIT, NOT A HAND-WRITTEN LINE
Dev Blog #33 (Character Generation, Hiring and Shopping, cross-confirmed via ModDB/IndieDB
mirrors, MEDIUM-HIGH): each volunteer in the tavern comes with "a procedural background story."
Wage and hire cost are DERIVED from the background (06_BACKGROUNDS.md, this library, already
recall-tagged), and traits stay HIDDEN until a Barber visit or an event reveals them -- the text
a recruit carries at the moment of hire is deliberately incomplete. TWO THINGS THIS LANE ALREADY
HAS THE ENGINE FOR, NOT NEW: our own deed ledger already gives a person a real, generated past
(06_BACKGROUNDS.md's own OURS line says so: "a person's background is what they did before the
money died"), and the naming screen's roster() already generates a person before the player
touches them. BB's practice CONFIRMS the shape rather than inventing it for us. WHAT IS ACTUALLY
NEW: BB deliberately withholds part of the truth at the hire moment (traits are hidden) so a
recruit is still being discovered after he joins -- a found-out person, not a fully-read one.
That is a usable craft note for any future roster/recruit text this lane writes: say less than
the engine knows, on purpose, at first contact.

### 3. THE TOOLTIP: TRAITS ARE STATED, NEVER STYLED
Traits are two-edged pairs (Strong/Weak, Brave/Coward, Lucky/Unlucky) and every source that
names one states it as a bare stat modifier ("Brave: +5 Resolve") with no adjective, no flavor
sentence, no voice. This is a REGISTER FINDING, not a content one: the tooltip's job is to be
correct and fast to read, and BB spends zero words making it charming. THIS MATCHES A REGISTER
THIS LANE ALREADY BUILT FOR A DIFFERENT REASON: rule 20h's dead-institution voice ("too even," no
exclamation mark) and the notice/alert module (THE FIRST NOTICE, 9/21) are BOTH plain,
declarative, unstyled text for a mechanical fact. BB's tooltip register and our institution
register are the same shape for a different reason (efficiency vs horror) -- worth knowing
they agree, so a future stat/injury readout does not accidentally reach for flavor a bare number
already does better.

### 4. THE FIGHT ITSELF IS A TEMPLATE, NOT PROSE, AND THIS IS THE ROUND'S BIGGEST FINDING
Cross-confirmed across independent sources (Steam discussion, dev-blog forum thread on the
combat log) to the exact wording: every combat-log line is ONE FIXED TEMPLATE --
"[Attacker] swings/shoots at [Target] and misses!" or "...and hits [BodyPart] for [ArmorDamage]
and [HealthDamage]!" -- filled in every single time, no variation, no adjective, no reaction.
Death and wound lines are the same shape: "[Target] was killed by [Attacker]!" A four-hour fight
and a four-second fight report themselves in the identical sentence shape a thousand times over.
*** SO ALL OF BATTLE BROTHERS' ACTUAL WRITING -- THE HALL, THE TAVERN, THE ROAD, THE CAMP --
HAPPENS BEFORE AND BETWEEN FIGHTS, AND NONE OF IT HAPPENS DURING ONE. *** The fight is the one
place in the whole game BB spends no craft on prose at all. THIS VALIDATES A SPLIT THIS GAME
HAS ALREADY RULED, FROM A DIFFERENT DIRECTION: rule 21 (no damage before the dial; a routine hit
costs one, no words needed) and "no chart chrome" already keep the fight numeric and quiet;
[horror fight] is still an OPEN school row asking what the fight looks like under the bible,
never what it SAYS. BB's own practice is independent confirmation that this is the right split,
not just our own horror preference: even a game built entirely around its combat spends its
whole word budget on the parts that are not the combat.

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THIS LANE, GOING FORWARD (NOT A ROW, A STANDING NOTE)
No new test lines this round (school only). Four things to carry into future rows:
1. Haggle-shaped systems (if COMBAT or TUNING ever build one) should hide the number and let a
   face carry the mood -- already proven here, not invented, WORDS' to remind not to own.
2. Any future recruit/roster text should hold something back at first contact on purpose (trait
   reveal shape), the way DYNASTY's roster and this lane's naming screen already can.
3. A stat or injury readout should stay a bare fact, not a flavored line -- the tooltip register
   and the institution register already agree without having been told to.
4. THE FIGHT GETS NO WORDS FROM THIS LANE, EVER, AND THAT IS NOW CONFIRMED BY THE GAME WE ARE
   MEASURING OURSELVES AGAINST, NOT JUST BY OUR OWN RULES. If [horror fight] or COMBAT ever asks
   WORDS for in-combat text, the answer is BB's own practice, cited: no.
