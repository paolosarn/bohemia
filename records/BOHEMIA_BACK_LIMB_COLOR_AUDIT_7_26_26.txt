THE TEST: a garment is ONE garment, so the back limb should show the same colours
as the front limb. Colours EXCLUSIVE to the far side mean something is shading it
separately, baked into the clothing pixels where he says it must never be.

garments store ramp INDICES that get tinted, so legal tinting invents RGB by
design and the test claimed 67% of the character was invented. Discarded.)
