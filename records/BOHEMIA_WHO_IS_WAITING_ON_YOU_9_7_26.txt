================================================================================
WHY IT SHIPS WITHOUT PAOLO RULING A SINGLE NUMBER
================================================================================
NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL froze every stakes conversation, and correctly: a
hunger meter needs a RATE and rates are his. The row's own unlock is that a
SOCIAL burn is not damage -- "Hunger needs a rate. 'Three people are waiting on
you' needs nothing but the truth."
So this states truths and applies no rate:
  how many people are waiting on you      counted, not tuned
  which of them you showed up for today   read off the deed ledger's own turns
  who has stopped waiting                 the standing web's answer, not a
                                          threshold this module picked
THE PLACEHOLDER NEGLECT NUMBERS ARE DELIBERATELY NEVER READ, and the gate proves
that by parsing the module. Consuming a number tagged as a placeholder is how a
guess becomes canon by accident.

AND THE LEAVING RULE INVENTS NO THRESHOLD EITHER. Battle Brothers is the named
study and its punishment is loneliness rather than death -- miss what you owe and
men desert -- but it does not hand us "after how many days", so that is not
copied. Somebody stops waiting only when BOTH things the game already computes
are true: everything they have seen of you comes out negative, AND you did not
show up for them in the day just reckoned. A person who thinks well of you gets a
quiet day for free, which is true of people. The one you wronged and then ignored
is the one who goes.

THE FAMILY YOU WERE BORN INTO IS NOT ON THE LIST, and that is a reading of the
story rather than an oversight: act one opens by losing them, and the ones who
walk out walked out WITH you. What you TOOK ON is the marriage and the children.

IT SCALES BY COUNTING, NOT BY A CURVE. Every outfit you swear to adds exactly
one, and there is no weighting, because weighting is exactly where a number
nobody ruled would sneak in.

TWO THINGS I GOT WRONG, BOTH CAUGHT BY DRIVING IT
================================================================================
1. *** THE ENTRY WAS NEVER IN THE SOCKET AND EVERYTHING LOOKED FINE. *** The
   first cut pushed it beside `const DAY = BohemiaDayLoop.make()`, which runs
   about nine thousand lines BEFORE the module is defined. The guard meant to
   make that safe -- `if (typeof BohemiaObligation !== 'undefined')` -- did its
   job perfectly and silently: no throw, no log, an empty STAKES table, and a
   reckoning that would have reported nothing forever. A GUARD AROUND AN ORDERING
   BUG HIDES THE ORDERING BUG. It is pushed directly under the module now, where
   there is no order to get wrong, and the gate checks the socket's CONTENTS on
   the played surface rather than that the code exists.
2. And the same shape bit this lane on the previous row, where a button was bound
   to the wrong card. Twice in two rounds, the same failure mode: a thing that is
   present, reads correctly, throws nothing, and does nothing.

[PENDING Paolo] AND ONE HOLE I AM NAMING RATHER THAN PAPERING OVER
================================================================================
1. *** YOUR FAMILY IS ON THE LIST AND THE GAME GIVES YOU NO WAY TO BE THERE FOR
   THEM. *** Kin are not bodies on the street, so they witness nothing (ctDeed
   collects minds from what is DRAWN) and they hear nothing (the gossip pass
   needs two drawn people standing together). So they are counted as waiting and
   they can never leave. That is the honest state, not a bug I hid: punishing a
   player for failing at something the game offers no way to do would be a lie.
   The fix is a real one and it is the next thing this lane should take: kin need
   minds and a way to be shown up for -- going home, being seen.
2. THE COMMITMENT LADDER'S NEGLECT NUMBERS ARE STILL PLACEHOLDERS. Nothing reads
   them and nothing here needs them. If he ever rules them they become a second,
   sharper answer to the same question, and that is his call.
3. WHAT HAPPENS AFTER SOMEBODY GOES. Today they are simply off the list. Whether
   an outfit that stopped waiting turns hostile, or a spouse who leaves takes the
   children, is a story decision and it is his.
