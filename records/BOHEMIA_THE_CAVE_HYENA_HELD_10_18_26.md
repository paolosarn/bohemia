## WHICH HYENA
The CAVE HYENA (Crocuta crocuta spelaea): its genome was read from Ice Age bone (Westbury et al. 2020), so a lab can make
it on its living cousin, the spotted hyena, a surrogate of the right size. America's own hyena (Chasmaporthetes, the
running hyena of Arizona) is about a million years too old for any DNA, so it is not the one. 1.6 m nose to tail tip.

## WHY IT CANNOT PASS BY MORE PASSES (the finding)
The rig's grain reads as an edge on nearly every pixel of a bright coat, so a rest area (D5) can only be a DARK part each
view sees most (the crest from above, the face from the front, the brush and the stockings from behind). Dark parts over
a sand coat average to the same dark brown the pit owns, and the rig's dust desaturates any warm coat toward it (the
mustard pass rendered 99, 82, 64). With the pit at 86, 77, 70 and the shepherd at 106, 90, 75, every coat that passes
D5 lands between them.
