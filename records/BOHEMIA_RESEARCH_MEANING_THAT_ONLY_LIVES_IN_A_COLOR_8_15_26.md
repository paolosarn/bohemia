## AISLE 1 — GAMES (practitioner standard, not opinion)
- Game Accessibility Guidelines, a basic-tier item (the tier meaning
  "cheap and everyone should"): ENSURE NO ESSENTIAL INFORMATION IS
  CONVEYED BY A FIXED COLOUR ALONE. Colour is a BACK-UP channel for text,
  symbol, pattern or shape — by default or by setting.
- Xbox Accessibility Guideline 103 and WCAG 1.4.1 say the same in
  different words: colour is never the only visual means of conveying
  information or distinguishing an element.
- THE NUMBER: red/green deficiency affects roughly 8-10% of males. When
  colour is the sole container of meaning, IT FAILS ABOUT ONE IN TWELVE
  MEN. For an audience that skews male, that is not an edge case, it is a
  chunk of the room who cannot read our canon.

## AISLE 2 — THE REAL WORLD: SAFETY LAW MADE THIS NON-OPTIONAL
Safety signage is the discipline where getting this wrong kills people,
and it settled on REDUNDANCY BY LAW:
- ISO 3864 requires every safety symbol to sit inside a defined SHAPE —
  yellow triangle for hazard, blue circle for mandatory action, red
  circle with a 45-degree slash for prohibition. Colour AND shape AND
  symbol, three channels for one meaning.
- ANSI Z535 standardises colour, shape and symbol together so a person
  recognises the message "regardless of training or language", including
  at distance and in bad light.
The principle transfers exactly: a signal that matters must survive the
loss of any one channel. Bad light, a cheap screen, sunlight on a phone
and colour blindness are all the same failure, and only one of them is
about eyes.

## THE CHALLENGE FINDING (aimed at a law we love and enforce)
PURPLE RESERVATION is a good law and it is SINGLE-CHANNEL. Its gate
(gates/bohemia_purity_gate.py) enforces the letter perfectly — nobody but
the Amalgamation may use purple — and it CANNOT ENFORCE THE PURPOSE,
because the purpose is that a player SEES "that is them." A deuteranope
does not reliably separate purple from blue. So we have a machine
guaranteeing exclusivity of a signal we never verified is receivable.
The law is not wrong. It is INCOMPLETE: exclusivity without a second
channel is a lock with no door.
THE GOOD NEWS, and it is worth saying because it is most of the world:
LIGHT = TERRITORY is LUMINANCE, not hue. Dark versus lit reads for
everyone, in every deficiency, on every screen. The biggest signalling
system in the game is already safe by construction.

## THE DECISION / WORK ORDER (routed, and it names owners this time)
1. EXTEND STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR FROM PROGRESS TO IDENTITY (CHARACTER):
   every faction must be identifiable by SILHOUETTE — garment shape,
   proportion, headwear — with colour as the back-up channel, never the
   carrier. The wardrobe already ships shapes; this is a placement of
   existing pieces plus a rule about how new ones are assigned.
2. PURPLE NEVER CARRIES MEANING ALONE (ART + RUN, and it binds the
   REWIND before it is built): every Amalgamation signal pairs purple
   with a non-colour channel. The rewind already has one for free — the
   reverse-tape MOTION is unmistakable and hue-independent — plus the
   sound SOUNDS will make. Write the pairing into the VFX spec now.
   PURPLE RESERVATION itself is untouched and stays exclusive.
3. THE GREYSCALE / SIMULATOR GATE (SHARED, and it is the machine half
   the 8/2 finding never got): render every identity-bearing surface —
   faction dress, the map, the Amalgamation signals, UI states — in
   greyscale and under deuteranope/protanope simulation, and assert they
   remain distinguishable. Our own research says we have NEVER run either.
4. OWNERS ARE NAMED HERE ON PURPOSE. The 8/2 version of this finding died
   because it named nobody. A pending that names no person is not a

Our own colour dependencies: read from the build and our laws, high.
