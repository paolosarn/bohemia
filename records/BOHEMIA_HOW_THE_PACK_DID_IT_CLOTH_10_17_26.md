# HOW THE PACK DID IT: CLOTH (CHARACTER 10/17, [the texture set], rule 100c)

The packs hold no people (COOK [the two holes] 10/16, 0 of 8,674 tiles), so a worn coat has no pack twin. His packs do hold CLOTH: the three packs named below, 78 tiles, decoded from the re-cut source of truth and measured with the card's own rulers. The formula's garments (327, the S facing of the texture set cut this round, hair and faces left to PORTRAIT) are measured with the same rulers, so the gap is a number.

## WHAT THE PACK DOES WITH FABRIC, READ OFF THE NUMBERS
- A hanging cloth in his packs is drawn in about 15 value bands, with 2459 colours in one piece, over a value span of 172: the fold is a band, never a line (GARM-01 says the same in words; here it is in pixels).
- The light is from above in 71% of his cloth tiles; the shadow side is a band, not a gradient.
- The grain holds 3.8 px per light-dark step along a row; his cloth is never a flat fill.
- The outline is the outer ring 56.9 value darker than the inside: a soft ring, not a black line.

## THE GAP, MEASURED
- Colours: the formula paints a garment in 9 colours where his cloth carries 2459. That is the whole of what "looks nothing like the assets we downloaded" means at the pixel, and it is the paint layer COOK THREE owns (rule 87); the texture set carries whatever paint lands on it, because it is cut from pixels, not from the formula's names.
- Bands: 5 against 15. The formula's cloth has a lit side and a dark side and little between.
- Grain: 5.5 px per step against 3.8. The formula's cloth is flatter along a row.
- Light: from above in 64% of garments against 71% of his tiles.

## WHAT THIS PAGE DECIDES FOR THE CUT
The texture set is a CUT, not a paint: it carries the formula's pixels as they are today, marked placeholder (rule 100e), with the attach points the rig needs. When COOK THREE's repaint lands on the paint layers, the same tool cuts the repainted cloth into the same pieces with the same attach points, because nothing in the cut reads a colour. The numbers above are the target the paint has to reach: about 15 bands, hundreds of colours, grain over 3.8 px, the light from above.
