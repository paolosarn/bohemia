# WHY YOU CANNOT SEE THEM: THE HOSTILE THAT IS THE CROWD (coordinator swing, this round)

## THE RESEARCH
Vision finds a target instantly, with no searching at all, when it differs from
its surroundings in a preattentive channel: colour (hue and intensity), form
(orientation, length, width, size, curvature), or motion (flicker and direction).
THE FINDING THAT MATTERS AND THAT WE HAD BACKWARDS: performance is driven by
FEATURE CONTRAST, not by the feature. A thing is not findable because it is red;
it is findable because it is red among not-red. Colour is the one channel where
the hue itself also counts. Motion is its own channel and it is instant.
Sources: [Nothdurft, the role of features in preattentive vision (PubMed)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8249312/),
[ScienceDirect, same paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/004269899390020W),
[Bartram, visual attention and things that pop out (SFU)](http://www.sfu.ca/siatclass/IAT814/Spring2009/Lectures/IAT814-week5A.pdf),
[Feature analysis and the role of similarity in preattentive vision (Springer)](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/BF03206697.pdf),
[Psychophysical evaluation of low-level feature influences on attention (arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.06458).

## WHY OUR PLAN WOULD HAVE FAILED
Our answer to "make hostiles readable" was going to be COLOUR IS TERRITORY: a
hostile wears its faction's colour. In a Cartel block full of Cartel people, that
is the one thing that makes a hostile INVISIBLE. We would have shipped it, judged
it by eye on an empty street, and never seen the failure until Paolo did.

## THE RULE (mine, correct-after)
A hostile is found by CONTRAST WITH WHAT IS AROUND IT, in this order:
1. **MOTION FIRST, AND IT IS FREE.** Everybody else walks past; a hostile walks AT
   YOU. Direction of motion is preattentive and it costs no pixels, no palette and
   no new art. This is the whole fix on its own and it is the cheapest thing on
   this board.
2. **CONTRAST SECOND, MEASURED AGAINST THE LOCAL CROWD, NOT THE PALETTE.** A
   hostile carries one value step away from the bodies within a few cells of it.
   In a dark crowd it is light; in a light crowd it is dark. COLOUR IS TERRITORY
   still says WHICH colour; this says HOW FAR from its neighbours, and the gate
   must measure the frame, not the sprite.
3. **SILHOUETTE THIRD.** One shape rule at the edge (the shoulder line, the
   carried thing) so it survives greyscale and a small screen.
4. **NEVER A HEALTH BAR, A FLOATING NAME, OR AN OUTLINE.** Those are the honest
   test's opposite: they announce what the world failed to show.

## ROUTED
- ANIMATION [walks at you]: the approach is the tell, and it is free.
- CHARACTER [stands out]: one value step from the local crowd, not from the palette.
- DIRECTION [contrast rule]: the rule goes in the style card with a number.
- EYES AND EARS [find them]: a machine that opens a real frame and asks whether a
  hostile is findable in it, the same way this round's judgment did.
