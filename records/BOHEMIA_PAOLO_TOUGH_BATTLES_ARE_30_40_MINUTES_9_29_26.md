"WELL KEEP IN MIND TOUGH BATTLES CAN BE LIKE 30 40 MINS SOMETIMES ITS INSANE SO IDK ABOUT 90 SECONDS WHO TOLD U
THAT BUT YEAH SHORTER THAN 30 -40 MINS EVEN FOR TOUGH BATTLES I GUESS EITHER THE ENEMY IS HIGHER LEEVEL OR BETTER
EQUIPMENT OR U FUCKED UP"

## THE READING
- WHO TOLD ME 90 SECONDS: nobody. It was the manager's default, invented, and the "5 to 20 minutes" figure was
  recall, not a measurement. Both are struck. His number is the measurement: a TOUGH Battle Brothers battle runs
  30 to 40 minutes.
- THE RULING: ours is SHORTER THAN 30 TO 40 MINUTES EVEN FOR TOUGH BATTLES. Not a stopwatch on every fight; a
  ceiling on the worst case, and routine fights well under it.
- WHY A FIGHT IS TOUGH, his three reasons, which is the difficulty design in one line: THE ENEMY IS HIGHER LEVEL,
  or THE ENEMY HAS BETTER EQUIPMENT, or YOU FUCKED UP (formation, the wrong shot, walking into it). Nothing else
  makes a fight long. TUNING's table has three dials, not thirty.

## THE NEW DEFAULTS (manager's, in VOTE, TUNING [fight length] refines with measured play)
- A ROUTINE fight (even odds, you did not fuck up): 2 to 4 minutes.
- A TOUGH fight (higher level, better gear, or your mistake): 8 to 15 minutes, never past 15. Half of Battle
  Brothers' worst case or less.
- The speed comes from gambits carrying the routine, a turn as a beat, and the dial only for the shot that
  matters (9/22), not from cutting the depth.
