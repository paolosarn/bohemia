WHAT CAME OUT, MEASURED TWO WAYS. The first column is the BODY the skeleton made,

instead puts her where the stage says she is. The lesson is the same one this lane
keeps relearning: measure the thing you are claiming, not the thing that is easy.

one ruler measured all three bodies, so the drop from 4.36 to 3.59 is real.
