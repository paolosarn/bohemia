## THE ANSWER IN ONE LINE
**The genre's fear is that the world is running normally and nobody is left who should be
running it. We have written the "nobody is left" half for months and almost never written
the thing that should have been running it: 8 lines in 3,014 name an institution at all.
The voice this row asks for has nothing to be the voice OF yet.**

## 1. TWO LAWS LANDED THE SAME ROUND AND THEY PULL OPPOSITE WAYS ON THIS LANE
=================================================================================
Rule 20 says the text on screen is **a dead institution, too calm, in its own typeface**.
That voice is by definition FACELESS. It is a broadcast, a notice, a readout.

Rule 19, locked the same round, says **no player-facing sentence outside the phone exists
without a named speaker and their portrait on screen.** No narrator. No system voice.

Taken together, the most natural way to obey rule 20 is illegal under rule 19. If a lane
reads only rule 20 it will write creepy institutional prose onto the screen, and rule 19
kills it on arrival.

**THIS IS NOT A CONTRADICTION AND IT DOES NOT GO TO PAOLO.** Rule 19 leaves exactly two
doors open, and they are named in its own section 3 and section 2(a):

    a LABEL ON A MACHINE          rule 19 section 3 exempts it in those words
    THE PHONE HE OPENS HIMSELF    rule 19 section 2(a) moves the night summary, the
                                  ledger, the rent, the batteries and the deeds there

**SO THE INSTITUTIONAL VOICE HAS ONE HOME AND IT IS THE PHONE**, plus the words printed on
machines. And the phone just inherited all the bookkeeping, so the home is bigger than it
was an hour ago, not smaller. FALLOUT 1 already owns the interface and rule 20 section 4
says to read it as "the dead institution's screen", so the named reference set already
covers this and no new one is needed.

MEASURED, so the size of each door is a number and not a feeling. Of 3,147 authored lines:

**THE INSTITUTIONAL REGISTER IS LEGALLY AVAILABLE ON ABOUT FOUR PERCENT OF OUR WORDS.**

## 2. THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG
=================================================================================
The assumption, and rule 20 section 2 states it as the reason this is a direction and not
a costume: our premise IS the genre's premise, a Las Vegas ten years into a dead grid, so
our words must already be most of the way there.

I measured it over all 3,014 spoken lines:

    nobody / no one / nadie                     194 lines   6.4%
    stopped / went dark / gone / shut down       59 lines   2.0%
    used to / any more / since the lights        22 lines   0.7%
    nobody checks, nobody asks, nobody came      20 lines   0.7%
    something still running                      10 lines   0.3%
    *** AN INSTITUTION NAMED AT ALL               8 lines   0.3% ***
    any of the above                            282 lines   9.4%

**WE HAVE THE "NOBODY IS LEFT" HALF AND WE DO NOT HAVE THE OTHER HALF.** Six percent of
our lines say nobody. Three tenths of one percent name a city, a county, a company, an
office, a board or a utility. The fear in this genre is not that people are gone. People
are everywhere in our text and they are the best thing in it. The fear is that **the
system is still running and nobody is at it**, and we have eight lines of that.

And reading the ten "still running" hits by hand, because a count is not a classification:
nine of them are a PERSON still standing, still working, still alive. Exactly one is the
genre's own sentence, and it is worth quoting because it shows the lane can already do it:

    "Didn't go in. It's still on the wall, still true, still deciding which streets have
     light tonight, and I still could not..."

A document, on a wall, with nobody left to change it, allocating power tonight. In one sentence, that is
the genre, and we wrote it once.

**SO THE ROW'S OWN PREMISE IS BACKWARDS.** It asks for the voice the world's text is
written in. There is barely any world's text. The job is not a voice pass over an
institutional corpus. The job is that **the institution has to exist before it can have a
voice**, and the phone is where it gets to exist.

## 3. THE OTHER MEASUREMENT: OUR FLATNESS CANNOT CARRY A SIGNAL
=================================================================================
"Too calm" is the second half of the row. Calm relative to what?

    exclamation marks in 3,014 spoken lines                     1
    lines using capitals for stress                            31   1.0%
    exclamation marks in the 133 interface lines                0

**OUR VOICE RAISES ITSELF ABOUT ONCE IN A HUNDRED LINES.** That is not a defect on its
own; the lane worked for it and the lines are better for it. But it means the flat
register is nearly total, and **a register that covers 99% of the corpus cannot mark
anything.** Analog horror's calm is a CONTRAST effect: the announcer reading the casualty
list in the same voice as the weather is frightening because you know what the other voice
sounds like.

So the mistake round two must not make is "write it flatter." There is nowhere flatter to
go. The thing that does the work is not the punctuation, it is **what the flat sentence is
flat ABOUT**: alarming content in the syntax of a readout, sitting next to a person who
does react. The 31 lines that do raise their voice are the contrast the other 2,983 need,
and they are an asset rather than an inconsistency.

=================================================================================
Rule 19 section 3 ends with an example, and the example is one of this lane's shipped

Here are all twelve narrator-prose lines, read by hand, so the lane owns the list:

    Something came in on your phone overnight.
    nobody has picked it up yet
    walking your own block.

    Bohemia lives in your browser. Put it on your home screen and it opens like an app...

    Nobody holds this ground yet. No faction has claimed this district, so there is
      nobody here to ask.

The last three are the worst of it: paragraphs explaining the world to the player in a
voice that belongs to nobody. Two of them are also, ironically, the closest thing we have
to the institutional register, which is why they read as authoritative and why they have to
move rather than simply be deleted.

Note also which ones are about the BROWSER rather than the world. "Saved to this device"
and "Put it on your home screen" are the product talking, not the game. Rule 19 does not
have a category for those and neither did we; they are a third thing and they belong
wherever the save panel lives, which is a machine.

## 5. THE REAL WORLD: AN INSTITUTIONAL NOTICE HAS A SKELETON, AND IT IS THE LAW'S
=================================================================================
This is the half the genre is imitating, and it is not a mood. Real notices have legally
required parts, which is exactly why they read the way they do.

**A UTILITY DISCONNECTION NOTICE** must carry, across the jurisdictions that regulate it:
the exact date and time service will be cut; the total owed, broken into past due, current
and fees; the customer's right to dispute; how to ask for a payment plan or assistance; the
reconnection procedure AND its fee; and contact details for an appeal. Typical notice
periods run 10 to 20 days, with a final notice 48 to 72 hours out. The language must be
clear and understandable to a layperson.

**AND IN MANY PLACES THE NOTICE MUST BE IN ENGLISH AND IN SPANISH.** That is real law about
real notices, not a stylistic choice, and it means **THEY SPEAK SPANGLISH reaches the
institution too**, by a completely different route than it reaches a person. A person
code-switches. A notice is issued twice.

**AN EMERGENCY ALERT** is built from five slots: the SOURCE sending it, the HAZARD, the
LOCATION affected, the PROTECTIVE ACTION with when and how to take it, and when the threat
ends or new information comes. Longer alerts exist so the writer can drop jargon and
abbreviations rather than to say more.

**THE HORROR IS NOT IN THE WORDS. IT IS IN THE SKELETON SURVIVING ITS AUTHOR.** A notice
that still has a reconnection fee, a dispute procedure and an appeal contact, ten years
after the office that would answer any of them closed, is the genre exactly. That is a
structure our phone can carry and it costs nothing to write.

And it has an edge our version gets for free: **the amount owed is one battery** (EVERYTHING
COSTS ONE), so the notice's own arithmetic is absurd while its form is immaculate.

## 6. WHAT ANALOG HORROR DOES TO THE 96% THAT IS IN A MOUTH
=================================================================================
Nothing about register. A person is not a broadcast.

What the law asks of a person is one thing, from its own section 2: **a voice that is too
even**, and **the frame is ordinary and one thing in it is wrong**. Translated to a mouth,
and this is the part round two has to write:

- the person is calm about the part that should frighten them, and particular about the
  part that does not matter
- the wrong detail is said PLAINLY, in passing, and not returned to
- nobody explains the world; they refer to it as though it has always been like this
- the institution appears the way it appears in real life after it dies: as a form, a
  number, a rule somebody is still following, a fee

That last bullet is the one that fixes section 2's measurement. The institution does not
need a narrator to exist in our text. **IT CAN LIVE IN PEOPLE'S MOUTHS AS A THING THEY
STILL OBEY**, which is both legal under rule 19 and, from the real record of collapse, more
accurate than a broadcast.
