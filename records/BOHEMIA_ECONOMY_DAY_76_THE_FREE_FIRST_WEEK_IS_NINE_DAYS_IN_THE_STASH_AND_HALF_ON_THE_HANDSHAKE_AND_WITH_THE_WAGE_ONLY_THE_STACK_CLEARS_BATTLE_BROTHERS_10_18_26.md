## 1. HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES THE FIRST WEEK
Sources: bb_all/0002_A New Company.txt, 1651_Rebuilding a Company.txt, 0827_Game Guide.txt (Economy), 0441_Contracts.txt
(Haggling), 0427_Companion (Background).txt, 1608_Provisions.txt (reference/library/grok/wiki/PAGES_ALL.tar.gz);
records/target/bb/origins.json, price_table.json (foodSpoilDays, foodStackBatteries), contract_terms.json.
- **IT PREPAYS THE FOOD, NOT THE WAGES.** A New Company starts with three Companions and '2x Ground Grains' in the cart
  (50 provisions) on every funds setting, plus the funds column's tools, ammo and medicine (Medium: 2,400 crowns, 20
  tools, 40 ammo, 20 medicine). The wage (14 crowns a Companion a day at level 1) is paid from the first day. No page
  in the library offers a wage holiday, for any origin.
- **THE CART READS 8 DAYS ON THE BAR AND LASTS 7.** 50 provisions at 2 a man a day for three is 8.33 days, but Ground
  Grains spoil in 7 (price_table.json foodSpoilDays groundGrains 7, from the Provisions page): the crew eats 42 by the
  seventh day and 8 go off. Worked day by day, Battle Brothers' 46 does not move (the 8 lost are 16 crowns).
- **THE ECONOMY SLIDER LEAVES THE START ALONE.** The Game Guide's tables: Beginner and Veteran both pay 100 percent of
  contract rewards and selling prices; Expert pays 90 percent and its deserters leave with their gear. Beginner only
  raises the carry caps (200 / 500 / 150 against 150 / 300 / 100). The starting money is a separate pick (High /
  Medium / Low). So Battle Brothers' own easy end never touches the first week; only the purse does.
- **A JOB THAT PAYS AT ONCE EXISTS: THE ADVANCE.** Haggling's 'payment in advance' moves 25 percent of the completion
  and per-head pay to the advance (contract_terms.json payInAdvance 0.25); the wiki's real example: 150 crowns up front
  of a 735-crown contract (15 batteries of 73). It must be ASKED for: every ask costs 0.5 relations, and a failed one
  can lose the contract (annoyance 3 to 6 an ask, out at 9). Nothing scripts the first job: A New Company 'starts without any
  tutorial'; only Rebuilding a Company carries the Hoggart start.
- **BATTLE BROTHERS' RUNWAY (Day 74's rule, worked day by day, A New Company, plains):** Medium 46 (its default), Low 37,
  High 55; on the desert's food rate (x1.5) Medium is 41. Its burn: 4.2 batteries of wages and 1.2 of grains a day,
  5.4 in all.

## 2. THE REAL RECORD: THE REAL FIRST WEEK IS THE LEANEST ONE, AND IN A CRASH THEY MADE IT FREE
- **THE WAITING WEEK.** Before the pandemic 43 US states made the first week of unemployment unpaid, a one-week
  waiting period (NELP's toolkit on 2015 Department of Labor data; Wisconsin's DWD put it at 'more than 40 states'
  in 2020). Realism-first says the first week is the hardest week: the money arrives after it.
- **AND IN THE 2020 CRASH THE GOVERNMENT PAID TO DROP IT.** CARES Act section 2105: 'full Federal funding of the first
  week of compensable regular unemployment for States with no waiting week' (DOL, UIPL 20-20). Wisconsin, by the law
  firm Littler's account one of the very last states to do it, suspended its wait in April 2020 (2019 Wisconsin Act
  185, benefit years from 12 March 2020 to 7 February 2021, paid back to people who had already sat it out, funded with
  CARES money). When the economy broke, the people in charge made the first week free. Rule 117(d) is his trade of realism for fun; this is the case where the trade
  and the record point the same way.
- **ARRIVAL: FOOD IN THE HOUSE AND MONEY IN THE HAND.** The US Reception and Placement program: a per-capita grant
  ($1,225 of direct assistance per refugee in the FY2023 notice) for the first 30 to 90 days; agency guidance spends it
  on groceries for the first few weeks before food stamps start (two to three weeks) and a hot meal on arrival (local
  practice, not a federal rule; a search extract, to be checked against the current notice). The CLAP box (Day 72) was
  food too: dry goods and cans, things that keep.
- **THE CAPTAIN'S DOWN PAYMENT.** The condotta, the Italian mercenary contract, opened with a prestito, a down payment
  paid on signing (Wikipedia, Condottiero; a general source, flagged). The real twin of Battle Brothers' advance.
- **WHY THE FIRST WEEK IS THE ONE THAT MATTERS.** Binswanger and others, NEJM 2007: 30,237 people released from
  Washington prisons, 1999 to 2003; in the first two weeks out the risk of death was 12.7 times the state's (95% CI
  9.2 to 17.4), overdose 129 times. The first fortnight is where people are lost. What release money each state hands
  over was not found from here; nothing in this page cites a figure for it.
THE LESSON: when the real world decided a start had to be survivable, it put FOOD IN THE HOUSE THAT KEEPS and MONEY
IN THE HAND ON SIGNING, and took the wait out of the first week. Nobody's answer was a week where the boss skips
payroll: pay in arrears only moves a wage to the end of the week, it never forgives it.

## 5. THE PICK, AND WHY
**THE FREE FIRST WEEK:**
1. **NINE DAYS IN THE STASH.** The stash opens holding nine days of food and water for the crew you start with: for
   three men, 54 food units and 108 litres. The food is a KEEPING kind, 12 days or more on main (smoked ham 12, master
   rations 16), never grains (7) or bread (8), or two of the nine days rot on the shelf. Water does not spoil. On the
   live map, where a day eats ONE, it is nine FOOD.
2. **HALF ON THE HANDSHAKE.** The first contract the crew takes, whatever the world puts on the board, pays half its
   pay the moment he says yes, and the half is his if the job goes wrong. 15 at THIN, 22 at FULL. No haggle, no ask.
3. **NO WAGE HOLIDAY.**

**WHY NINE.** Battle Brothers' bar reads 8 on day one and its grains really feed 7. Rule 117(a) wants ours easier on
the number he sees: nine is the first whole day past Battle Brothers' 8, and two past what its cart really lasts. A
plain WEEK is a day short of Battle Brothers on the bar (this lane's own Day 72 and Day 74 'seeded week' were harder
than Battle Brothers, corrected here). It is also Battle Brothers' own move (the cart) and the record's (the box, the
groceries before the food stamps), so it is HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DID IT first (rule 41), our swag second.
**WHY THE HANDSHAKE, AND WHY HALF.** It is the only option with a choice behind it, so its win is earned: the battery
count jumps the moment he takes a job (117(c)), and taking a job is the one clear next thing on the first screen
(117(b)). It is Battle Brothers' advance and the condotta's down payment, made easier: offered without the ask, the
relations cost or the risk (Battle Brothers' quarter, asked for; ours half, offered). It is legal under the fifth
votes (3) only as payment terms: 'a starting job' authored by us is the authored first fight he killed ('Who are you
to decide what the first fight is?'). Half, not all: the whole pay up front is the easiest rung (section 6).
**WHY NO WAGE HOLIDAY.** No Battle Brothers twin (rule 41: it pays from day one). No real twin either: pay in arrears
moves a wage, it never forgives it. It makes a cliff: the burn jumps from 6 to 9 a day on day eight with no choice
behind it, a loss he did not cause (against 117(c)). It is worth 2 days. And it is worth nothing on the live map's
books, which have no wage.
**'SLIGHTLY HARDER THAN THE EASIEST VERSION'.** The easiest rung is all three at their most generous: ten days seeded,
the whole first job on the handshake, no wage for a week. The pick drops the wage holiday, halves the handshake and
takes one seeded day off. Measured (section 6): 7 days under the easiest, 2 over Battle Brothers, where the stack is
on the shelf. The same shape as his word on the fight (78 in 100 against Battle Brothers' 46; his 'slightly harder';
the coordinator's about 70): under the easiest, over Battle Brothers.

## 7. WHAT PROVES US WRONG
1. **THE ROW'S PREMISE IS THE NO-WAGE READING.** '250 starting batteries last 41 days' is Day 74's no-wage number. On
   the canon books 250 lasts 27, and no free week on main clears 46 at any purse on main.
2. **TUNING'S START WAS SET WITHOUT THE WAGE.** TUNING's rule-117 page lists 'already easier, his rulings: ... a daily
   wage -> bought once'. His words 'one time purchases, no daily wage' are on the PEOPLE RETINUE card (the followers);
   the fighting men's wage is his 9/29 ruling and is on the roster screen. TUNING's 250 / 290 start, measured on its
   own page as easier than Battle Brothers in the fight, is harder than Battle Brothers in the purse by 14 to 19 days
   once the men are paid.
3. **A WEEK IS A DAY SHORT OF BATTLE BROTHERS' CART.** This lane's own seeded week (Day 72's CLAP box, Day 74's 'plus a
   seeded week') reads 7 on the bar where Battle Brothers reads 8. Corrected to nine.
4. **A SEEDED STASH OF GRAINS IS A WEEK WHATEVER IT IS CALLED.** price_table.json spoils grains in 7 and bread in 8;
   nine days needs the keeping kind.
5. **THE LIVE MAP IS FIVE TIMES EASIER TODAY, AND THE CLIFF IS COMING.** On the live map a day eats ONE food for the
   whole crew and a battery buys ONE, with no wage and no water: the door's 240 is about 240 days of food, against
   Battle Brothers' 46. The free week changes nothing there except the first second (FOOD starts at 0, so the bar is
   red and the crew hungry on night one; nine FOOD at the door fixes Day 74's red-from-the-first-second). The day RUN
   TWO's six piles and the wage reach the map, 240 falls to 26: minus 89 percent in one cut. The free first week and
   the stack ship in the same cut as the wage, or he falls off it.
