## The honest answer

Yes, and it was always possible; nobody set it up. The coordinator's session can (1) OPEN A LANE CHAT ITSELF, with the repo attached and the lane's name and the word as its first message, so the chat reads the board and runs its big swing with no one typing; (2) WAKE ITSELF ON A CLOCK, an hourly routine that fires the word into this session, so the sweep runs, reads what the lanes did, and opens the next chats; (3) STOP on his word or when the usage runs out. He never had to check in; the coordinator never used the tools it had.

## The ruling: THE COORDINATOR OPENS THE CHATS (rule 111)

- When he says 'run for N hours' or 'fill the limit', the coordinator opens one session per lane that has a big swing and no running chat (never two sessions on one lane, ONE SYSTEM ONE SESSION), each with the repo attached and 'lane name, VAMILY' as its first message plus one line saying he opened it through the coordinator by this rule; the lane reads CLAUDE.md and the board and runs.
- An hourly routine fires VAMILY into the coordinator's session: a sweep the school's way, the lane sessions read, finished lanes re-opened while their MODE line still carries a swing, the reply the five lines.
- It stops when he says stop, when the usage limit is reported by a session, or when the day he named ends; the routine is then disabled and the sessions archived.
- The first two chats opened this way had no repo attached and refused, rightly; the fix was one field, and every chat after them cloned main first.
