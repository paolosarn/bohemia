================================================================================
AND EVERY PIECE OF THE ANSWER WAS BUILT ON 8/26 AND NEVER CALLED
================================================================================
The alpha's own face block says it about itself, in its own words:

  "THE PERFORMANCE WAS BUILT ON 8/26 AND COULD NOT BE USED, BECAUSE ONLY ONE
   PERSON IN BOHEMIA HAD A FACE ... So 'their portrait pops up' had nothing to
   pop up. That is the hole this fills, and it is why the 8/26 turn ended with a
   gated feature nothing called."

faceFor(id) has been sitting there since, deterministic, "same id, same face,
forever, on any device, with nothing stored". THIS ROUND IS THE CALL. It borrows
the player's own portrait path end to end -- faceFor, the ramp, renderFace,
packIdx, decodePlayerFrame -- so a stranger's head is made and unpacked exactly
the way his is, and nothing new draws a face.

ONE ID, ONE WHOLE PERSON: the id asked for is the id the BODY is drawn from.
ASKED ONCE PER PERSON, because a bake costs a frame and a face is a fact.
NO FACE, NO SPACE: a person whose head has not arrived yet gets exactly the
bubble this file drew before today, to the pixel.

ON THE GLASS: a 64x64 head, drawn at 20px inside a 20px round clip, in the left
of their own speech bubble, pixelated rather than smoothed. Watched on the real
canvas, not recomputed, because a cached face that never reaches the screen is a
feature that silently does nothing.

AND IT ONLY FITS BECAUSE OF [a human being]: the bubble used to be painted ON the
speaker's face.

RULE 19(e), DATED THIS ROUND: A TEXT PROMPT IS NOT AN EVENT
================================================================================
  "The road and walk directors stop opening cards THIS ROUND; a moment fires only
   if it has a body on screen or not at all; the tables stay as content waiting
   for bodies."

THE WALK DIRECTOR OPENED A CARD AND DOES NOT ANY MORE. It still picks the moment
off his own approved roster, with the words that roster already held; what changed
is WHO SAYS IT. A person who is actually drawn says it, in their own bubble, with
their own face beside the words, which is the whole of his sentence.

AND WITH NOBODY ON SCREEN IT FIRES NOTHING AT ALL, which is the other half of the
rule. Measured both ways on the alpha: spoken by 12:12:900 with a body there,
refused with the drawn list emptied. A moment with no body was a caption
pretending to be an event.

*** AND MY OWN OTHER GATE CAUGHT A BUG I HAD JUST WRITTEN: TWO CLOCKS ***
================================================================================
The bark slot runs on performance.now(): barkTick is called with it and BARK.next
is set with `now + 4000`, so that organ counts MILLISECONDS SINCE PAGE LOAD.

The first cut of walkSpeak set BARK.until from ctMinuteNow() -- GAME MINUTES, a
number like 360 against a wall clock already past 15,000. So every moment it
spoke was ALREADY EXPIRED when the next tick looked, and on its way out barkTick
pushed the next bark 1,500 ms away. The moment was silent AND it silenced the
next person too.

NOTHING THREW AND NOTHING LOOKED WRONG. [a human being], this lane's own gate
from five rounds ago, is what found it, because it counts speech bubbles on the
real canvas:
  clean origin/main   2, 3, 1 bubbles over three runs
  this tree, before   0, 2, 0
  this tree, after    3, 3, 3
TWO CLOCKS IN ONE FILE, and the one I reached for is the one the rest of the
street uses for everything except this. It is a claim in the new gate now.
