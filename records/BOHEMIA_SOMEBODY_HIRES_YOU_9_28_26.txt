================================================================================
MEASURED BEFORE A LINE WAS WRITTEN
================================================================================
A site district (commercial/industrial/medical/solar) has always offered the
same 'site' work to anybody who stands on it, for ever. A stranger and a
faction's own closest ally got the identical job. The reputation system this
lane already built has never once been asked a question about hiring.

TWO SYSTEMS ALREADY ANSWER "WHO ARE YOU TO THIS OUTFIT":
  THE BELONGING LADDER (bohemia_belonging.js) -- how many times you have done
    what an outfit wants. Starts at 'stranger' on a fresh save. Moves the
    moment a favor is answered through the asks screen, which is ordinary play.
  THE STANDING WEB (bohemia_standing.js, whoVouches) -- named people who are
    warm on you because they personally saw or heard something you did.

*** AND A FALSE PREMISE CAUGHT BEFORE ANYTHING WAS BUILT ON IT. *** The
standing web's DEED_WEIGHT table ships EMPTY in the module, which read as "dead
until Paolo rules it" and would have been the wrong foundation for half this
design. MEASURED on the real page: it is NOT empty. 84 rows, filled at boot
from the quest corpus (152 quests, mined the same way the questbook always
has). The web is genuinely live; a fresh save just has not touched a quest
yet, which is why the belonging ladder is the axis that actually moves in
ordinary early play and the standing web is the one that lights up later.
Neither is faked to look reachable; both are real.

A third fact closes doors: ctRelToMine already answers 'hostile' for a faction
your own outfit has fought, the SAME fact ctAgainstMe reads to decide who
attacks you on sight. Nothing new was invented for "a bad name."

================================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================
engine/bohemia_work.js gained ONE pure function, doorFor(who), and offer() now
accepts an optional fourth argument. `who` is three facts, never a person:
  rel      the faction relationship's sign: hostile / warm / neutral / unknown / null
  rung     the belonging ladder's key for this faction: stranger / peripheral /
           useful / ... or null
  vouched  true if somebody warm on the player also belongs to this faction

THE DOOR CAN ONLY DOWNGRADE OR CLOSE, NEVER INVENT A JOB THE GROUND NEVER HAD.
A stranger at a scav district still just scavenges. Standing cannot conjure a
site job out of nowhere; it can only mean you get the real job a site district
already offers instead of being quietly downgraded to the worst of it.

`who` is OPTIONAL and omitting it reproduces the exact old behavior, byte for
byte, which is how every existing caller (a_days_work_gate.js) stays green
with zero changes.

THE CITY WIRES IT WITH THREE DOORS IT ALREADY HAD:
  ctHiringWho(faction) reads the belonging ladder for that faction, the same
  ripple-sign ctAgainstMe already reads, and the standing web -- with a
  mind-to-faction lookup (ctFactionOfMind) that reuses this lane's own 8/11
  lesson rather than guessing a person back out of an id.
  workOffer() finds the ground's holder through the SAME door doWork() already
  pays through, so what block hires you and what treasury pays you can never
  quietly disagree about whose ground this is.

================================================================================
*** AND THE GROUND MOVED MID-ROUND, THE SAME ROUND THIS ROW WAS CLAIMED ***
================================================================================
Rule 38 (Paolo 9/28, LOCKED): "your character moving tile to tile throughout
the city, it's not gonna be like that anymore, that has to change immediately."
Places become SETTLEMENT SCREENS you tap into; the eight-button pad and
tile-to-tile walking through the city are dead, effective immediately, for
everywhere except the fight and named special places.

THIS DID NOT COST THE MECHANISM ANYTHING. doorFor() and offer() have always
taken a faction's facts and (x, y) coordinates, never "where his feet are" --
nothing about them assumed a walked pad, so nothing about them had to change
under rule 38. The ONE piece that is tied to the pad is workOffer()/workCell(),
the city's reference caller, wired to the only surface still live on the alpha
today. Whoever builds the settlement screen calls ctHiringWho(faction) and
BohemiaWork.offer(...,who) the identical way: find the tapped building's
ground holder, build a who, hand it to offer(). That is the whole re-wire, and
it is a few lines, not a redesign.

================================================================================
MEASURED AND NOT FIXED
================================================================================
THE VOUCH HALF OF "WHO WILL GIVE YOU WORK" CANNOT BE DEMONSTRATED ON A FRESH
SAVE TODAY, ONLY FABRICATED. The standing web is real and wired (proven: 84
weighted deed kinds exist and a hand-built mind with one of them correctly
produces a vouch), but reaching it in ordinary play requires touching the
quest corpus, which nothing in an early save has done yet. The belonging
ladder is the axis that actually moves for a new player; this is named rather
than quietly relied on.

WHAT "WHAT YOU USED TO BE" ([former jobs]) MEANS FOR THE PLAYER, NOT BUILT.
The row cites [former jobs] (BB-WHAT-YOU-WERE) alongside [your reputation], and
that system is entirely about NPCs' pre-collapse trades (bohemia_people.js
WAS_WORDS), derived from a person's own key. The player has no equivalent
identity anywhere in this repo, and assigning one would be inventing an
IDENTITY decision that is his to make, not a mechanism this lane should build
unasked. This row is built on the reading that "your standing" covers both the
faction ladder and the personal deed web, and "what you used to be" is left
untouched rather than guessed at with an invented player background.

THE WORK BUTTON DOES NOT YET SAY WHY IT DOWNGRADED OR WHY A DOOR CLOSED.
Rule 19 (text from a mouth with a portrait, nothing pops up) means the right
answer is a bark, not a card, and building that bark well needs its own round;
today the downgrade is only visible as WORK becoming SCAVENGE, which is honest
but silent about the reason. Named rather than rushed.

THE CITY WIRING (workOffer/workCell) IS ON GROUND RULE 38 JUST DECLARED DEAD.
It is the only surface live on the alpha today, so it is the honest place to
prove the mechanism works, but it is not where this belongs going forward. The
re-wire onto a settlement screen, once one exists, is named above as the
pattern to copy, not built here -- that surface is not this lane's to build.
