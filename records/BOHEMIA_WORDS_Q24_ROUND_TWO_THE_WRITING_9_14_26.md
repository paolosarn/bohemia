## THE CARD HAS TWO KINDS OF ROW AND THEY ARE TWO DIFFERENT SPEECH ACTS
Read out of the code rather than guessed, the first card already carries four pieces
of text:

| | |
|---|---|
| the take row | `I'LL TAKE IT` (PEOPLE, b39c33cb) |
| the confirmation | `You took it. It is yours until the day is out.` (PEOPLE) |
| a haggle row | `Make it a <currency> instead` (bohemia_haggle.js) |
| a haggle row | `Half of it now, before I go` (bohemia_haggle.js) |

School's finding 1 says what those two groups are: under the mirror image rule an
acceptance must be unconditional, and a reply that accepts on condition of different
terms **is a counter-offer**. So **one of those rows CLOSES the deal and the other two
RE-OPEN it**, and the family below writes them apart on purpose, because a player has
to be able to tell at a glance which button ends the negotiation.

That distinction is the whole round, and it was not visible before the school round.

## WHICH FINDINGS CHANGED THE LINES, AND HOW
**1. A CONDITION INSIDE THE YES RE-OPENS IT.** Every take row is unconditional and
**none of the four carries an "if"**, checked mechanically. The sharpest haggle line
in the set is the finding turned inside out: *"Not for that. For that and the tank,
yes."* It refuses first and then names its own terms, which is what a counter-offer
actually is, and no player could mistake it for agreeing.

**2. THE ACCEPTANCE THAT MEANS SOMETHING IS A READBACK, SO THE BUTTON STAYS SHORT AND
THE CONFIRMATION DOES THE WORK.** Aviation requires verbatim repetition because
"roger" hides a misunderstanding. PEOPLE had already built exactly this shape without
the research, so the design here is theirs and the family extends it rather than
replacing it.

**3. WHICH TERM HE READS BACK IS THE CHARACTER, SO THERE IS ONE CONFIRMATION PER TERM
RATHER THAN ONE CONFIRMATION.** Time, place, pay, load. *"It goes to the tank, not the
gate."* *"One battery, on the far side, not before."* **All five read a term back**,
checked. A job with nothing to read back keeps PEOPLE's original, which is the safest
of them because a day is the one term every job has.

**4. NOTHING BINDS**, so not one line swears loyalty. The confirmations name the first
checkpoint instead, which in a valley with no enforcement is the thing that actually
governs.

**5. SOCIAL YES IS FAST AND BARE**, measured at 1.75 words in real recorded speech, so
the button was not made ceremonial and **PEOPLE's "I'LL TAKE IT" is kept as one of the
options** rather than replaced for the sake of having written something.
