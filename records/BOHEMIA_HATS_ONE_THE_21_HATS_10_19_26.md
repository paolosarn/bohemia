## 2. THE METHOD

tools/bohemia_character_hats_one.py, called by the barber tool: each shape modelled ONCE as clay on the head bone of
PORTRAIT's starred head on the barber's mannequin, each hat in its own ramp's mid (the runway paint, the way the thirteen
gang bodies are coloured), short black hair painted on the scalp under it so a hat sits on a man. The hair 1 rule holds:
relief runs the way the thing is made (knit ribs down a cap, straw down a cone, seams to a button), never one size of bump
on a grid. Rendered through DIRECTION's rig unedited; the twin is the game's flat body wearing the same hat on the TEMPLE
TAPER (tools/bohemia_character_the_barber_on_the_skull_flat.js, CUTS=HAT:<name>).

WHAT LOOKING CHANGED (cycle 1 renders of one hat per shape):
- FIELD CAP read as a helmet: a thickness ramp on the dome is still a dome. Now a patrol cap: the crown stands out to one
  radius at every height and is cut flat at 176 mm above the head's centre.
- SLOUCH BEANIE: 34 mm of slumped length did not read from behind; now 52 mm, lower.
- SCRAP HELM: the rust mid sat on the mannequin's tan at one value and read as a bald head with seams; now its ramp's dark,
  plates 15 mm with 6.5 mm gaps and a rolled lip at the edge, rivets sparse along the seams.
- KNIT, WATCH, WORK, CONE, WRAP, SHEMAGH read as what they are on the first pass.
