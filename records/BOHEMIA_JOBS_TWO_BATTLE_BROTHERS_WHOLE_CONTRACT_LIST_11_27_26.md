## THE GAPS, EACH A BOHEMIA VERSION (map ones first; each a new way to play on the map, rule 494(d), never only a card)
GAP 1 TOWN-TO-TOWN PATROL (Patrol): 'walk the line': the client's town, two other towns, back, on the clock; the roaming crews on those
  roads come at you (WORLD's roaming parties); paid at the end, half if a town says you never came. Realism: a faction showing its
  colours on the trade road so the crews know who it pays. Uses PATROL's play with towns for posts.
GAP 2 THE ROAD CREWS (Intercept Raiding Parties, Marauding): a town names the crews working its roads; they are the map's own roaming
  parties near it (their tracks, rule 9/24); run down every one within a few days, each counted when it falls. The first kind that
  hunts what is already on the map rather than a party made for the job.
GAP 3 DRAW HIM OUT (Confront Warlord): break his crews and his camp and he comes out himself; a named man with his band (BESTIARY 4's
  champions, 11/27zzb). BOUNTY is the second half; the first half is new.
GAP 4 THE DIRTY WORK (Privateering, Raid Caravan made live): a faction pays you to hit a rival's caravans and posts on the rival's roads
  for a few days (WORLD 16's caravans drive now); the rival's bar falls and its crews start looking for you. Realism: in a crash the
  factions fight over supply, not land. Touches FACTIONS (the bars) and WORLD 16 (the caravans).
GAP 5 HOLD IT OUT THERE (Hold Fortress, Last Stand): hold a place that is not your client's gate: a ruin, the dam, another town that
  asked for help; the walk there is part of the job. Uses HOLD's waves at a place the map names.
GAP 6 A HUNT A NEW CREW CAN TAKE: every hunt on the shelf reads under the day's floor for a new crew of three (the smallest is eleven
  dogs), so hunting reaches him only near day 10; one small hunt (two or three animals) so the kind comes on day 1.

## THE REASONS (not built, and why)
REASON A THE WAR CONTRACTS (Decisive Battle, Siege Fortification, Conquer / Defend Holy Site): each is a battle inside a war between
  powers (Battle Brothers' noble houses, north and south). Ours has no war layer yet (FACTIONS owns the factions and their bars; TAKE A
  SIDE is a one-job choice); a decisive battle on our map is a FACTIONS build first, then a job. The holy sites have no place in our
  valley until his story names one (the dam, the Strip's church?): his call, never ours.
REASON B THE UPRISING (Slave Uprising): Battle Brothers pays you to beat down the Indebted. Ours already made debt the story (COLLECT:
  the debtor's own choice, take it all, take half, pay it yourself). Paid violence against people in debt bondage is a moral line in a
  game about an economic crash; whether the company may take that work is his, never a default.
