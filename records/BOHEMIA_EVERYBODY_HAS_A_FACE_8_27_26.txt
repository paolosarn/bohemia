BOHEMIA — EVERYBODY HAS A FACE, AND IT TALKS
CHARACTER lane, 8/27/26. Ruling: Paolo, 8/26. Built 8/27.

WHAT HE ASKED FOR
  "every time you speak to someone, their portrait will pop up on screen so you
   feel like you're relating to them... facial animations too, bro, like talking
   and shit... from eyebrows moving."

WHY THE 8/26 TURN COULD NOT DELIVER IT, AND IT WAS NOT THE ANIMATION
  facePerform() shipped 8/26 and was correct: four visemes off the letters, blinks
  on measured human timing, brows, deterministic, gated. NOTHING CALLED IT.
  ONE GREP EXPLAINS THE WHOLE THING: renderFace has been invoked exactly one way in
  this entire codebase -- renderFace(buildSpec()) -- and buildSpec() clones `pface`,
  THE PLAYER'S FACE. The family cast had dials and worn and no face. Every stranger
  in the valley had a body and no face.
  ONLY THE PLAYER HAD A FACE. "Their portrait pops up" had nothing to pop up.
  THE MISSING PIECE WAS NEVER THE ANIMATION. IT WAS A FACE FOR SOMEBODY WHO IS NOT
  YOU.

WHAT WAS BUILT
  faceFor(id, over) -> a full face spec, rolled from the id.
  IDENTITY AT 64x64 IS SIZE AND SPACING, NOT DETAIL -- which is also what the face
  recognition literature calls the identity channel (shape, and the relative
  position of the organs). Every field it dials already existed:
      head shape   craniumH foreheadW cheekW jawW chinW len
      spacing      browY eyeY noseY mouthY eyes.gap
      weight       brow thick/len/angle -- the loudest feature this small
      colour       skin iris lips brow hair
  NOTHING ABOUT renderFace CHANGED TO MAKE THIS WORK.

  GROUNDED (REALISM FIRST). A rolled face must be a PERSON:
    - vertical THIRDS held; browY/noseY/mouthY jitter inside their third, never across
    - the gap between the eyes is about ONE EYE WIDE
    - the mouth is a FRACTION of the face (~1/3 cheek width), never a fixed number
    - jaw <= cheek, chin <= jaw - 6, or the dice eventually roll a triangle
      standing on its point
    - A CHILD IS NOT A SMALL ADULT: bigger cranium, rounder jaw, features lower.
      FAMILY_CAST already carried `age`, so it drives it.

  DETERMINISTIC. Same id, same face, forever, any device, nothing stored. No
  Math.random in the face or the performance -- a person who shimmers is not a person.

  MECHANISM-MINE / CONTENTS-PAOLO'S. This decides how a face is BUILT, never who
  anybody IS. faceFor(id, over) lets an explicit spec beat the roll outright, so a
  `face` block on a FAMILY_CAST row lands with no code change.

  speakingPortrait(canvas) -> the thing that runs it: rAF, facePerform, a render
  cache keyed on (mouth, blink/8, brow/4) so the whole performance is a couple of
  dozen images rendered once, not sixty a second.

WHERE IT SHOWS UP
  THE COLD OPEN -- the first thing anybody sees in the demo. It already had a
  speaker and a line and no face.
    - the face sits BESIDE the words: on a phone in portrait the caption is already
      near the bottom and a head on top pushes the words into the thumb
    - A TITLE CARD HAS NO SPEAKER, SO IT HAS NO FACE. A head next to "TEN YEARS
      LATER" is the game claiming a line has an owner when it does not
    - A REPAINT IS NOT A NEW LINE. The caption repaints several times a beat (the
      SOUNDS lane measured it speaking 58 times for 7 lines), so say() dedupes on
      speaker+text the way cutVoice does. Same bug, same shape, third time.

THREE THINGS LOOKING CAUGHT THAT MEASURING DID NOT
  1. A CLOWN PARADE. HAIR_COLORS lists pink and violet beside black and a uniform
     pick treats them as equally likely: sixteen people, THREE PINK HEADS. That is
     the trenchcoat bug of 8/27 EXACTLY, ONE DAY LATER -- uniform over a list whose
     contents are not uniform in life. Weighted to real distribution now; dye ~5%
     (measured 6.0% of 600) and therefore a statement, which is COLOUR IS TERRITORY
     applied to a head. Grey is dealt from AGE, because grey is not a colour
     anybody picks.
  2. DIALS THAT COULD NOT MOVE THE PIXELS. The first cut rolled FIVE hair-style
     names when renderFace tests for TWO ('wavy_mid','curly') -- so 'straight',
     'curls', 'crop' and 'swept' were four spellings of one haircut -- and jittered
     eyeY by +-0.7px so all forty people had IDENTICAL eyes. Both looked like
     variety in the source and were nothing on screen.
     A DIAL THAT CANNOT MOVE THE PIXELS IS NOT A DIAL, IT IS A COMMENT. That
     sentence was written eighty lines above and broken in the very next block.
     Rolling a name the renderer ignores is worse than not rolling it: it makes the
     variety look solved.
  3. A STRAIGHT LINE DOWN EVERY HEAD. The hair part was a perfectly ruled dotted
     column -- HOW HAIR AND SHAPE WORK (8/1) clause 3 broken in one line. It
     survived because the player is ONE face; the day everybody grew a face it
     became the same mark repeated hundreds of times, which is what a machine tell
     looks like. Same fix as the 8/25 strand barcode: drift a pixel in short
     segments, deterministic off the head it is drawn on.
     COST TO THE APPROVED PLAYER FACE: 8 PIXELS OF 4096 (0.20%), all of them the
     part line. Measured against origin/main, not estimated.

AND THE WORST ONE, WHICH ONLY A GATE FOUND
  *** faceHash ALREADY EXISTED. *** One argument, eighty lines up, and it is what
  the 8/26 blink scheduler calls. This turn declared a SECOND function faceHash(id,
  salt) below it. Two function declarations with the same name in one scope is not
  an error and not a warning: THE LAST ONE SILENTLY WINS FOR THE WHOLE FILE. So my
  hash became the one facePerform was calling, with `salt` undefined -- still
  deterministic, still no crash, EVERY CHECK STILL GREEN, and every person in the
  game blinking to a clock nobody measured.
  NOTHING READS AS A BUG WHEN IT IS SPELLED CORRECTLY AND SITS IN THE WRONG PLACE.
  That is the 8/16 border lesson (a pass can be individually right and wrong for
  WHERE IT IS) in a different hat. Renamed faceRollHash, and made mechanical:
  NO TWO TOP-LEVEL FUNCTIONS SHARE A NAME.
  On its first run that check also found TWO PRE-EXISTING collisions -- CombatBridge
  and clampPkg, each inlined TWICE, byte-identical, another lane's. Identical copies
  make last-wins a no-op so nothing is broken today, but the day somebody fixes one
  copy and not the other, THE FIX SILENTLY LOSES -- the ENGINE SYNC failure mode
  with no gate on it. Pinned at 2, named, handed over as a row. Not rewritten by
  this lane mid-turn.

THE GATE -- gates/talking_portrait_gate.js, 23 checks, registered TALKING PORTRAIT
    closest of 60 faces      0.0135   (floor 0.012)
    mean distance            0.0914   (floor 0.080)
    dyed hair                6.0% of 600   (cap 8%)
    ruled parts              4 of 60  (cap 6)
    mouth shapes used        closed mid open wide
    pixels moved             mouth 16/21/22, blink 56, brow 34
    same id twice            byte-identical
    nothing asked            === the approved face
  MEASURED ON RENDERED PIXELS, not on the dials that produced them -- a face is
  distinct if it LOOKS distinct, and the dials are only how it got there.
  SAMPLE SIZE IS PART OF THE CLAIM: the pairwise distance is O(n^2) over 4096
  pixels so it runs on 60, but the DYE SHARE runs on 600, because the first cut
  measured 10% on 60 against a true 6% and would have failed a correct build.
  MUTATION-PROVED THREE WAYS: flatten the roll -> "everybody is the same person"
  fires; stop showing the canvas -> "the portrait pops up" fires; restore the ruled
  part -> "no straight line down the crown" fires at 60 of 60.

AN HONEST NOTE ON THE PICTURE
  The mouth row first drew seven whole faces and the shapes were REAL AND INVISIBLE
  -- 16 to 22 pixels of 4096 is a genuine movement and a useless picture. Rebuilt as
  one face at actual size plus the SAME PIXELS around her mouth magnified 11x.
  Nothing redrawn, nothing exaggerated, just a crop at a bigger zoom.
  And before that it drew ONE face captioned "undefined", because the tool assumed
  visemesOf() returned {ch,v} objects when it returns a flat array of strings. A
  picture is a claim; a tool that guesses at an API it never read makes a false one.
