BOHEMIA MUSIC — BATCH 20: VOLUME ON THE ZERO-POPULATION FACTION POOLS
==============================================================================
Part of the marathon cook (Paolo 7/25, "APPROVAL UNLOCKS VOLUME... VOLUME on
approved music styles: more songs per approved category pool, same
instrumentation DNA as the approved batches, tagged into the pools").

RESEARCH FIRST: swept every faction/overworld/menu category via CAT_DEFAULTS.
9 of 18 categories sat at ZERO tagged vibe songs: REDS, BLUES, ANARCHISTS,
COLORFUL, TRADES, VOLUNTEERS, CARTEL, MOB, CUSTOM. (Their own baked SONG 1/
SONG 2 hymns exist and are canon -- this is specifically about the EXTRA
"vibe" pool a faction's encounter rotation draws from beyond its own two
hymns.) These 9 were the highest-value volume target.

WHAT SHIPPED: one new song per empty pool, each reusing that faction's own
root/scale/wave/kit family (same instrumentation DNA as its hymn) with a
fresh kick pattern and one newly-birthed voice (9 new synthV topologies:
evictionbell, ledgerscratch, pockettwitch, faucetdrip, shatterspark,
confettipop, weldflash, kindlingchime, blankslate -- short, punchy, mostly
percussive accents/hits rather than sustained leads, since the ask was
volume within an established identity, not new sonic ground):
 REDS         -> THE FORECLOSURE NOTICE
 CARTEL       -> THE COUNTING ROOM
 MOB          -> THE BOSS TAKES HIS CUT
 BLUES        -> THE LAST GOOD CHECK
 ANARCHISTS   -> BROKEN WINDOW ANTHEM
 COLORFUL     -> PARADE OF LOST BALLOONS
 TRADES       -> WHAT THE APPRENTICE BUILDS
 VOLUNTEERS   -> HANDS THAT STILL BUILD
 CUSTOM       -> A NAME NOT YET CHOSEN
CAT-tagged directly into CAT_DEFAULTS so every fresh install ships with the
pool already populated (matching the durable-canon pattern, not relying on
a local device's own tags).

TWO REAL BUGS CAUGHT AND FIXED, not routed around:
1. music_gate.js's single-quote-only name regex silently failed to parse a
   song titled with an apostrophe ("THE APPRENTICE'S HANDS") -- renamed the
   song (WHAT THE APPRENTICE BUILDS) rather than fight the parser; simpler
   and avoids the same landmine in every other quote-sensitive regex in the
   file (NEW_VIBES, CAT_DEFAULTS).
2. 'osc' is a real sentinel (CUSTOM's own bass, handled directly in
   playStep as a raw oscillator using the song's own wave, not a synthV
   voice) that had simply never been exercised by music_gate.js or
   combat_pool_gate.js before -- no prior MLOOPS song used it. Both gates
   fixed to recognize the sentinel instead of flagging it as a missing
   voice body.

COMBAT POOL DISCIPLINE (per the marathon's own instructions: "Decode
COMBAT_B64 fresh from HEAD if you touch it"): combat_pool_gate.js caught
that combat's synth is its OWN FORK, separate from the alpha's MUSIC tab
engine -- tagging these 9 songs to their factions puts them straight into
combat's encounter rotation (MUSIC CATEGORY LAW pool clause), and combat's
fork didn't have the new voices. Re-decoded COMBAT_B64 fresh from the
CURRENT origin/main tip (main had moved since this session's last sync --
"COMBAT v63: overworld music in fights" had landed) rather than reusing a
stale local decode. Ported all 9 new voices plus 3 existing-but-missing
ones (ivalicebrass, ashchoir, farbell -- reused as bass/am by these songs
but never previously needed in combat's fork) verbatim into combat's own
synthV, matching the V63 session's own "ported VERBATIM from the canon
alpha synthV" convention already established right above the insertion
point.

VERIFIED LIVE (not just gate numbers): headless Playwright against the
real alpha -- all 9 songs render as rows in the MUSIC tab and play for
real (AudioContext state 'running', zero console/window errors). Separately
verified inside the REAL combat iframe (its own genuine ~30s boot, not
shortcut): called audio() to init the AudioContext, then called all 12
ported voices (9 new + 3 reused) directly through combat's own synthV --
every one executed cleanly, zero errors.

ALSO FIXED IN PASSING (found while re-verifying after the fresh combat
decode, unrelated to this batch but caught by the same full-suite pass):
combat_lab_gate.js had a stale assertion for the aim readout's "SHOT n/
skill" string -- an upstream "V62 weapon-gated" change (a different,
already-shipped parallel session) replaced the flat G.chainSkill readout
with a wpnCap() weapon-capped value, but never updated this gate's literal-
string check. Updated the assertion to match the real, current, legitimate
behavior.
