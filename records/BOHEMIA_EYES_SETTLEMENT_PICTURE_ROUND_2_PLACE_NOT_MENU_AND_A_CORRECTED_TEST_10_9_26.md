## A REAL CORRECTION TO ROUND ONE'S OWN CAUTION

Round one borrowed the point-and-click genre's "pixel hunt" lesson and asked round two to check
for a hover affordance. Measured first, the right way, before trusting the plan: mouse hover was
tested on all eight buildings and NONE of them change on hover. But this is the WRONG TEST for

TOUCH-first phone screen, which has no hover state for a real finger to produce at all. Testing
for hover here was transplanting a genre lesson without checking whether its mechanism still
applies, and it does not. CORRECTED: what actually matters on a touch screen is a STATIC visual
signifier a player can recognise without touching anything. Looking at the real picture (rest.png)
rather than only the hover test: at least one building carries exactly that -- a red cross symbol
painted directly onto the clinic's awning, a real, diegetic sign, not a UI hint. NOT CHECKED THIS
ROUND, named rather than assumed: whether all eight buildings carry an equally clear signifier, or
only some (the stall's red-and-white awning reads as a stall by genre convention; the hall, board,
