==============================================================================

THE ROW: "RUN painted it procedurally from twelve texels a block; a hand-painted
far end needs COOK." Pinch all the way out on the demo and the valley is a tan
dome with a repeating ripple in it.

THE FIRST THING THIS ROUND DID WAS REFUSE TO INVENT THE GEOGRAPHY.
  The valley is not a mood, it is a map the game already builds. This tool shells

    and Lake Mead         92 tiles
  The two depots are the layout's own ind1 and ind2, by name: {'x': 20, 'y': 28} and {'x': 78, 'y': 69}.
  If the engine's layout changes, this sheet changes with it.

WHAT "PAINTED BY HAND" MEANS, AND IT IS NOT RESOLUTION:
  RUN's far end is noise. The ripples ARE the noise function, and no amount of
  it becomes a mountain range. Plain noise makes dunes: smooth humps. FOLDING it
  -- one minus the distance from the middle -- turns every zero crossing into a
  CREST, and stacking those at halving sizes gives ridgelines with spurs off
  them, which is what a range is. Then the height field is shaded BY SLOPE
  against one sun, so a ridge has a lit west face and a shadowed east face.
  The basin gets ALLUVIAL FANS computed off the mountain mask, so the floor is
  brightest at the mouth of each canyon and fades into the middle, the way a
  real desert floor is the spread of what came down off the ranges.
  The washes braid. The city keeps its grid grain at one step off the fabric.

ONE LIGHT (rule 70a, Paolo 10/4): there is one sun in this file, north-west, and
the ranges, the fans, the city blocks and the lake all take their shading from
it. Nothing is lit on its own. RUN composites the sheet under its own hour; the
sheet is the LAND, not the lighting, and it is painted in the same sun the city
uses so the two cannot split.

THE POWER AUTHORITY'S CAMERA GRADE (the row's words; rule 20): the last aerial
survey the valley's power authority ever flew, on a camera already old. One
channel sits a pixel west, a scan structure in the horizontal, grain, and the

because one camera photographed the land and the city together. It runs AFTER
the palette check on purpose: the paint is his, the camera is the horror, and
the two are not allowed to get mixed up.

*** THREE SHEETS, AND ONLY LOOKING CAUGHT THE FIRST TWO. EVERY GUARD WAS GREEN
*** OVER BOTH OF THEM.
================================================================================
SHEET 1 passed fifteen checks and read as a PRINTED CIRCUIT BOARD. Every road tile
was filled solid, so at this scale all 2,543 arterials came out as wide as a city
block and the grid drowned the valley. THE FAULT IS A CATEGORY ERROR, not a bug:
the engine's grid is a TOPOLOGY. A tile is 96 m of world and it says "a road runs
through here", not "this 96 m is tarmac". A real arterial is about 30 m against a
400 m block. Roads are drawn as a line down the middle of their own tile now, wide
for a freeway and a hairline for an arterial, with the city fabric painted through
underneath. The road net went from most of the sheet to 3.6% of it.

SHEET 2 passed again and the city SAT ON THE LAND AS A GREY SLAB, which is the
thing rule 70 forbids in those words. Three faults under it:
  * the margin was a one-dimensional smear. I grew the land past the engine's grid
    by copying each edge row and column outward, which gave the sheet a pale band
    left and right and a dark band top and bottom: a striped frame, not land. A
    range does not continue by repeating a row, it continues by being further
    range, so the padding is grown from the distance to the nearest real mountain,
    broken with noise.
  * the ranges were flat. I multiplied the ridge field by the bulk of the mass, and
    deep inside a big mass the bulk saturates, so every crest washed out and the
    mountains came back as a dark crust. The bulk is only there to stop a cliff at
    the mask's edge; it is a clipped skirt now and the crest keeps its full range.
  * the city was one flat step per district with a fine fuzz on top. From altitude a
    city is a mosaic of blocks that DIFFER. One value per block, not a fine grain.

AND THE GUARDS THAT WOULD HAVE CAUGHT BOTH, now written, because numbers caught
neither and a green check over a worse picture is the most dangerous state there is:
  THE ROADS DO NOT SHOUT          their share of the sheet, and their value band
  THE CITY DOES NOT FLOAT AS A SLAB  the spread across its blocks, and its share

*** AND THE ROADS GUARD'S OWN FIRST VERSION WAS STRICTER THAN THE APPROVED BANK. ***
I wrote "within one ramp step of the fabric" from memory of this lane's own 9/27
sentence. The bank says something different and the bank is what was approved: route
gets its OWN value band, 108 to 132, above city's 74 to 104, on purpose, because 38%
of the valley's cells are road and the grid is how you read a city from altitude. A
guard that refuses his own bank refuses his art. This lane has now written that
mistake three times across three rounds; it tests what the bank says now.

*** ONE THING I AM NOT GOING TO PAINT AROUND, BECAUSE IT IS NOT A PAINTING FAULT ***
THE CITY IS A RECTANGLE. The engine fills its whole 96 x 96 with city out to the
grid's edge, so from altitude the valley holds a city with four straight sides and
square corners. No real city in a basin does that; it fingers up the washes and
stops where the ground rises. I could feather the rim and hide it, and that would be
a painting that lies about the map underneath, so the edge is painted exactly where
the engine puts it. WORLD's or RUN's call, with this sheet as the evidence.
