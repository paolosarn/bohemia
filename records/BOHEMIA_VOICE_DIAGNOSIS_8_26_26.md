## TELL 7 -- THE SPANGLISH NEVER REACHES A CONVERSATION

**532** lines carry a register other than plain English (21.8% of the build, at
the cap he set on 8/26). Inside the 27 quest scenes: **0**.

All of it is barks, quirks and street noise. The headline register is decoration
you walk past, and it is absent from every conversation that matters. This is a
DISTRIBUTION problem, not a volume one -- ENOUGH IS ENOUGH (8/26) capped the
count and that cap holds. Nothing here asks for more Spanish. It asks for the
Spanish we already paid for to be in a scene instead of on a wall.

## WHAT IS ALREADY GOOD, SO WE DO NOT BREAK IT

- **Zero em dashes** in 2442 lines. That law is holding.
- **Zero proper names** in the quest scenes, which is why a quest can cast
  against whoever actually exists on that block.
- **The barks already sound like people.** 75.0% contraction rate, short, rude,
  specific. The voice we want is already in the build; it just is not in the
  scenes.
- **The choices are lean.** Player replies run five or six words and 102 of them
  are a silent action in parentheses instead of a speech.
