# THE LOCKED-RULINGS SWEEP, PASS FOUR (coordinator, 9/7): PERKS WERE MEANT TO BE EYES

## THE LOCK (7/1, "LOCKED intent")
laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_CAMERA_TIMESTEP_FUSED_7_1_26.md, section 3: "Perks become a
PERCEPTION system on the zoomed-out layer, not just combat stats. Better perks = you
see more of what's happening around you, read the map deeper, catch things."

## THE BUILD
The shipped perk tree is 23 perks, saved, and every one of them is a fight stat
(COMBAT's own STATE line). Searched the alpha and every engine module for a perk
that touches sight, hearing, noticing, reading the map or spotting: the only hits
are an unrelated quest id, an encounter id and a quirk. That is a text search and it
is not proof, but it agrees with the lane's own description of the tree. As far as
anything can tell, the perception half of the perk system was never started.

## WHY THIS ONE MATTERS NOW AND DID NOT IN JULY
The zoomed-out layer he described in that lock is being built THIS WEEK: the travel
map with a sight circle and fog, tracks that differ by faction, the danger tell on a
block, the phone feed, borders you can see. Every one of those is a thing a perk
could make you see MORE of. The lock is not a perk feature; it is the reason those
systems have a progression at all. Without it, sight range, track reading and the
danger tell are fixed numbers forever, and a hundred hours of play never sharpens
your eyes.

## THE SHAPE (mine, correct-after; his lock is the contents)
- Perks split into two branches on the same tree: what your body does in a fight
  (the 23 that exist) and what you NOTICE (new): sight circle radius on the travel
  map, how far away a hostile reads as hostile, whether you can read whose tracks
  those are, how far the danger tell reaches, whether the feed shows you a rumour
  before it reaches you.
- Everything costs one; a perception perk is bought the same way as a fight perk.
- No perception perk changes how a fight plays. Combat stays RF4 on the beat.

## ROUTED
- COMBAT [perks see]: the tree gets its perception branch, wired to the numbers the
  travel map, the tell and the tracks already use.
- UI [what you notice]: the perception branch shown as options on a sheet (that
  lane's shape), not a stat list.
