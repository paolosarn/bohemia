  the fee locks at accept (Q126.N5, Q148.W1). Pay in goods or a favour is fine if said plainly on the
  screen. Numbers are TUNING's; the page gives the shape. 10 contracts (5 with no twist, 5 with one twist
  per cost band).
- QR-M THE COMPANY ON THE ROAD. The library argues against the loyalty meter from inside the games that
  used one (Q076.X3, Q132.X8, Q137.X2); loyalty shows as behaviour in the fight (Q137.W3) and as people who

  a priced sit-out); a declined offer stays silent. 8 designs.
