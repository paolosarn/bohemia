## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
Battle Brothers never saves inside a battle. It saves the campaign the moment a battle begins. Quit, crash or close the game
mid-battle and the next load puts you at the start of that same battle (in Ironman too: there is no way out of a fight by
quitting, only back to its first turn). The battle's results (the dead, the injuries, the loot, the spent gear) are written only
when you are back on the world map.

## THE RULING (rule 233)
(a) ONE SAVE AT THE FIGHT'S START: the moment a fight begins (the board cut and the men placed, before his first turn), the game
    writes one save of everything as it stood: the company, each man's hitpoints, armour and injuries as he walked in (rule
    226(a)), the stash, the clock, the job, the board's seed and the enemy he met.
(b) NOTHING INSIDE THE FIGHT IS SAVED: no turn, no death, no wound, no round spent, no loot.
(c) A RELOAD PUTS HIM AT THE START OF THE SAME FIGHT: refresh, close, crash, the phone killing the tab, a new build deploying:
    he comes back to turn one of that same fight, the same board and the same enemies, his men as they walked in. Never back on
    the map as if the fight did not happen, never a lost company, never a fight that is gone (no way out by quitting, Battle
    Brothers' way).
(d) THE FIGHT'S RESULTS SAVE ONLY BACK ON THE MAP: the dead, the struck down (rule 234), the wounds, the loot, the pay are written
    the moment he is back on the map, and from then a reload keeps them.
(e) It works on the demo and the alpha, phone and computer, and on the iPhone-proof save (engine/bohemia_save.js: probe the
    real size, verify the write, never a time machine).
(f) THE GATE: on the phone and computer profiles, live path (a save, the map, a road fight): start a fight, play three turns, let
    a man fall, reload: turn one, same board, same enemies, every man as he walked in. Finish the fight, reach the map, reload:
    the results stand. Reload twice in a row mid-fight: still turn one. Kill the tab during the save write: the save before it
    loads whole.
(g) CAMP [the fight saves only at its start] (it built the map's road door to the fight and the damage handoff), AFTER the COMBAT
    emergency [the fight is broken live] lands on main (same door, one system one session); a NOTE on COMBAT's board; line 155
    on his list; EYES checks.
