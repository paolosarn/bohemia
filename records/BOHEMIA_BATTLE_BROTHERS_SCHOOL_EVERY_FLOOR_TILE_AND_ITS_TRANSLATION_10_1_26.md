# BATTLE BROTHERS SCHOOL: EVERY FLOOR TILE, WHAT IT DOES, AND ITS BOHEMIA TRANSLATION (coordinator 10/1/26, his order)
Sources: the wiki's Combat Mechanics and Hit Chance pages and Dev Blog 4 (tactical combat mechanics) as search returns
them (our sandboxes cannot open them); (recall) marks a number from memory; Grok ask 15 confirms every line.

## THE RULES THAT EVERY TILE PLUGS INTO (Battle Brothers)
- Nine action points a turn for most men. Flat ground costs 2 AP a tile. Rough ground 3 AP (some rough tiles still 2).
  Murky water 4 AP. Changing height adds +1 AP per level and costs more fatigue. The Pathfinder perk makes every tile 2 AP
  and no extra fatigue.
- HEIGHT: standing a level above your target, +10% to hit; the one below, -10% to hit; melee and ranged alike. Ranged
  weapons gain one more tile of range per level of height and see past obstacles that would block them on the flat.
- SWAMP: a man standing in murky water takes a malus to melee defence (recall: -10) and pays the 4 AP.
- OBSTACLES (trees, rocks, walls): no movement through them; they block line of sight for ranged; a shot past a friend
  risks hitting the friend.
- ZONE OF CONTROL: leaving a tile next to an enemy gives him a free swing; it is terrain-free but every tile shape sets
  who is adjacent.
- NIGHT: ranged hit chance and vision fall (recall: -? at night); not a tile, but the board carries it.

## WHAT WE ADD (ours, not Battle Brothers')
- LIGHT as a tile property at night (analog horror: the fight is in the dark unless something is lit).
- A ROAD CLASS width (46g): small street one tile, big street two, freeway four; the width changes who is adjacent.
- INDOORS: a house tile is cover and a wall at once (A COMBAT TILE IS A HOUSE); its door is the one way in.
