BOHEMIA — THE FIRST CONNECTED RUN (RUN lane, 7/26/26)
=====================================================
THE RECORD OF WHAT SHIPPED. Not a proposal: this is live in the alpha's RUN tab.

WHAT IT IS
----------
One loop, start to finish, one thumb, iPhone portrait, inside the one alpha:

  1. You wake up INSIDE your own house — a real generated floorplan of a real
     home on the approved Campana block (seed 7), not a spawn point on a curb.
  2. You walk out the real front door onto the real dead street.
  3. Your neighbour the LINEMAN is standing on the road. Step next to him and the
     action button offers the talk (the loop's own talkablesNear).
  4. He gives you S01 THE METER READER — the real canon .bq bytes, the real
     dialogue, the real forks, the real silence options.
  5. "Follow the skimmed line" is an actual walk: the quest re-binds to the FIXER
     at the far end of the lineman's own street, ~57 tiles down the block.
  6. You resolve it. Three real endings, three real clout weights:
       put it back quietly   -> #quiet    (+8 followers)
       hand it to the trades -> #notable  (+25)
       cut it in front of everyone -> #reckless (+110)
  7. IF IT GOES LOUD (a #risky or #reckless resolution), the run hands off into
     the REAL combat frame over the existing BOHEMIA_ENCOUNTER bridge, you fight
     the Dead Eye Dial, and BOHEMIA_COMBAT_END puts you back on the block exactly
     where you stood, with the outcome (dead / spared / fled) reported.
     A QUIET fix starts no fight at all — the pacifist path is a whole run too.
  8. You walk home, step through your own gold-ringed door, and your phone comes
     up with the post the ENGINE built: one post per completed quest, the real
     clout tag, the real follower math.

THE GATE (69 assertions, green)
-------------------------------
It does not read strings. It opens the real page in a real browser at iPhone size
and PLAYS THE WHOLE RUN BY TAPPING THE REAL BUTTONS, twice — once down the loud
fork, once down the quiet fork — asserting every link: start inside your house,
out the door, NPC placed and talkable, dialogue real, mid-quest re-bind, quest
COMPLETE, feed post with the right clout, followers from the engine's math, and
zero console errors. Then it does the whole thing again INSIDE THE REAL ALPHA,
where the loud fork opens the real combat frame, a real encounter is pushed into
the dial, and the combat frame's OWN window sends the real BOHEMIA_COMBAT_END —
proving the return leg on the surface Paolo actually taps.

[PENDING PAOLO] — NPC PLACEMENT IS A MAP-LAW CALL
--------------------------------------------------
MAP LAW: Claude never designs map layouts. Two placements were picked
MECHANICALLY off the block the generator already made, straight out of the
quest's own text, and they are HIS to rule after he feels the run:

  LINEMAN — the road tile nearest your own front door that fronts a NEIGHBOUR's
    driveway. Reason: his own line is "I have a name and a face and a family on
    this block." He is the neighbour one door down. Nothing closer is possible.
    (seed 7: your door [77,112], the lineman [77,102] — ten steps.)

  FIXER — the far end of the lineman's own street (its last contiguous road
    tile). Reason: "It splits somewhere past the dead storefronts. Follow the
    cable that runs warm." You follow it down his street.
    (seed 7: [20,102] — 57 steps down the block.)

Both are deterministic: same seed, same tiles, forever.

[PENDING PAOLO] — DOES EVERY LOUD DEED DRAW A FIGHT?
-----------------------------------------------------
The run fires the combat handoff when the completing stage's own CLOUT tag is
#risky or #reckless (canon: loudness IS exposure; the Amalgamation watches viral
moments most carefully). Roster size rides the same signal: reckless draws three,
risky draws two. That is a MECHANISM keyed to a signal Paolo already locked — but
whether loud always means someone shows up, and who shows up, is his call.

WHAT THE RUN DOES NOT DO YET (honest list)
-------------------------------------------
- The player is not registered in ctx.scheduler, so the run's grid clock is the
  block sim's (one world-minute per step), not the loop's turn scheduler. Wiring
  the player into the real scheduler is an engine request for the WORLD lane.
- Only S01 is wired. The other eight canon quests are placed by the casting
  bridge into the real valley, not onto this block.
- The block is one seed-7 suburb block, not the whole valley: you cannot yet walk
  off it into the next district.
