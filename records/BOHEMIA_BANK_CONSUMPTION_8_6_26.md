# WHICH OF THE THINGS HE OWNS ACTUALLY REACH THE SCREEN

8/6/26 · ART LANE · measured, not read off a plan

Every real win this week came from asking one bank whether it was actually
drawing. The buildings were flat starter tile (8/3) and the valley had zero
objects (8/5) -- 8,674 purchased tiles, 1,927 personally thumbed UP, not one
pixel on screen. Nobody had ever asked it of the whole library. This does.

a finding, and that already cost a turn on 8/3. So the control runs first:

## READ THIS BEFORE TREATING ANY LINE BELOW AS A TASK

**A dark bank is not a bug.** Some are dead on purpose (the graveyard is a
law), some are masters that deliberately feed a small curated subset, some
are candidate sheets that were judged and mostly rejected. This is a list of
places to LOOK, and nothing here is a licence to wire anything up. Four
separate times today I turned an approved tile into a defect by deciding
where it went: **a verdict is about the object, never about where or what for.**


## AND IT IMMEDIATELY QUALIFIES SOMETHING I TOLD HIM TODAY

I concluded, out loud and in a record, that "there is NO seamless art in this repo
for ballast, field soil or desert floor" -- after opening TWO banks.

`BOHEMIA_GROUND_VARIANT_BANK_7_10_26.txt` is 1,211 images, 7.5 MB, and has NEVER
DRAWN A PIXEL. I never opened it. `BOHEMIA_TILE_REPO.txt` is another 875. So that
conclusion is UNSAFE and it is written here rather than left standing, because it
is exactly the assume-instead-of-check error this whole file exists to catch, made
by me, twice in two days, in the middle of documenting it.

I am not opening them this turn. STOP PRODUCING says a fourth attempt at a thing
means you already failed, and ground was four attempts. The next session (or he)
gets a clean run at it with this list in hand.

## WHAT NOT TO DO WITH THIS FILE

Do not wire anything because it appears here. 89.5% dark is not 89.5% waste:
  - the graveyard is dead ON PURPOSE and that is a law
  - candidate sheets are supposed to be mostly rejected -- that is what judging is
  - a master feeding a curated subset SHOULD be mostly unused
  - variant banks may be alternates, not additions
The number that matters is not the percentage. It is that TWICE this week a bank
turned out to be dark for no reason at all, and nobody would have known.
