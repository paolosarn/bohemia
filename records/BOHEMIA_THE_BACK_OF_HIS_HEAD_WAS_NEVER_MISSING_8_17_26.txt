BOHEMIA — THE BACK OF HIS HEAD WAS NEVER MISSING ART
CHARACTER lane, 8/17/26

TWO GATES HAD BEEN RED FOR WEEKS AND BOTH WERE DEMANDING A FACE ON THE BACK OF A
SKULL. Every handoff since carried it forward as

    "STILL HIS, STILL OPEN: NE and NW have no face painted at all"

which filed a debt against PAOLO for art that is correct. He was never going to
"finish" it, because there was nothing to finish.


===============================================================================
THE MEASUREMENT THAT SETTLED IT
===============================================================================
Painted pixels per part per facing, straight off his rig:

    dir    head   FACE          dir    head   FACE
    S       24     76           NE      92      0   <- flagged
    SE      31     64           N       92     12
    E       26     48           NW      92      0   <- flagged
    W       26     48           SW      31     64

NE, N and NW carry a NINETY-TWO PIXEL head against 24-31 on the front views.
That is not a head with its face deleted. That is THE BACK OF A SKULL: roughly
three times the mass, because a rear three-quarter shows the whole cranium and no
features. The two "empty" facings are the two with the biggest heads in the rig.

Then I rendered all eight through drawChar and LOOKED (VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE,
7/18). The face turns toward you through S/SE/E and away through NE/N/NW. The
rotation reads exactly the way a rotation should. THE ART IS RIGHT.


===============================================================================
WHAT WAS ACTUALLY WRONG — THE SAME MISTAKE, TWICE
===============================================================================
A fixed list of twelve parts, demanded on all eight facings.

  parts_are_painted_gate.js    for (let q = 1; q <= 12; q++)   // every direction
  bodyvar_gate.js              const PARTS = keys(BAKED.layers.S)

bodyvar's is the sharper one and worth keeping in mind: it derives "the parts that
must exist" from SOUTH -- the single facing that shows the MOST face -- and then
requires that set everywhere. A back view can never satisfy it. The gate was not
measuring the dials at all on those facings; it was measuring the difference
between the front of a head and the back of one.


===============================================================================
THE FIX, AND WHY NEITHER HALF IS A HOLE
===============================================================================
1. bodyvar now asks its OWN question. Its rule is "a DIAL must not empty a part",
   and emptying means the rig painted something and the dial took it away. So it
   compares each facing against ITS OWN neutral rig instead of against South's part
   list. This is STRICTER, not looser: every real emptying on every facing is still
   caught, and a part that was never painted there can no longer be reported as
   removed.

2. parts_are_painted still demands all twelve parts, with ONE anatomical carve-out
   THAT HAS TO EARN ITSELF: the FACE may be absent only on a facing that PROVES it
   is a rear view, by carrying at least twice the front-view head mass.

   *** THE PROPERTY THAT MAKES IT SAFE: the exemption cannot be bought by REMOVING
   ART. *** Delete the skull and the proof goes with it, so the missing face becomes
   a failure again. That is the whole design.


===============================================================================
MUTATION-TESTED, BECAUSE AN EXEMPTION IS A HOLE UNTIL SOMEBODY PROVES IT IS NOT
===============================================================================
tools/bohemia_backhead_mutation_check.js — 8 passed, 0 failed:

  0. the untouched rig PASSES (his rear facings are not a defect)
  1. face deleted from S (a front view)            CAUGHT
  2. face deleted from every front view            CAUGHT
  3. rear view stripped of its skull (NE head)     CAUGHT  <- cannot buy the exemption
  4. non-face part deleted from a rear view        CAUGHT  <- face only, never blanket
  5. a DIAL wipes a part the rig paints            CAUGHT  <- bodyvar's real rule
  + both mutated files md5-verified back to byte-identical before exit

AND THE FIRST VERSION OF TEST 5 WAS WRONG, in the way worth writing down: it
emptied the face OUT OF THE RIG and expected bodyvar to shout. With the rig empty
there, no dial removed anything, and staying quiet was the CORRECT answer --
parts_are_painted is what catches a rig hole, and it did, in test 1. A test that
fails a gate for answering its own question correctly is a broken test, not a
broken gate. The mutation was moved to where the rule actually lives: the dial
module.
