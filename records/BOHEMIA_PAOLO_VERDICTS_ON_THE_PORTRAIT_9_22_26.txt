   being all white around it ... everyone's looking less of a frog like that ...
   the eyes are going pretty good." That is the sclera fix from 9/21: the white

   it. NOTES ARE RULINGS -- it goes in, and it stays in.

4. THE SHADES ARE APPROVED, clean, no complaint.

5. THE LIGHT IS DEAD FOR THIS SESSION. Eight cards, two different lighting
   passes, rejected in one sitting. STOP PRODUCING: "a second rejection ends the
   feature for the session." There is no third lighting version. Anything that
   survives of it survives because he named it (the eyes), not because I liked it.
