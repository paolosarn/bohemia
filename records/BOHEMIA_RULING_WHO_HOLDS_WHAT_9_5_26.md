## THE RESEARCH (both aisles, this round's swing)
- **Real borders are not lines on a map, they are things you can see.** Gang and
  armed-group territory runs along physical landmarks: highways, rail lines,
  parks, washes. Chicago's Dan Ryan Expressway splits the South Side factions.
  Boundaries get MARKED, in colour, on walls and fences, and they move when
  alliances move. The strong hold the ground that earns; disputed ground is where
  the violence concentrates. Sources: [ICRC Review, Hazen: understanding gangs as
  armed groups](https://international-review.icrc.org/sites/default/files/irrc-878-hazen.pdf),
  [PoLAR: controlling movement and territory, Honduras](https://polarjournal.org/2021/02/10/controlling-movement-and-territory-gangs-and-the-state-on-honduras-urban-margins/),
  [Global Initiative: when gangs and militias run the streets](https://globalinitiative.net/analysis/urban-security-when-gangs-and-militias-run-the-streets/),
  [DOJ NDIC: growth of gangs in suburban areas](https://www.justice.gov/archive/ndic/pubs27/27612/growth.htm).
- **Games:** territory that pays the holder and can be taken is the oldest working
  loop there is; the tension is that holding must cost less than the land pays.
  We already have both halves: [block rent] (the land pays) and [lights bill] (the
  land costs), both shipped or open this round.

## THE RULING (mechanism mine, names already his)
1. **EVERY DISTRICT HAS AN OWNER.** No empty ground except what is explicitly
   deserted. `FACTION_ASSIGN` is filled by a RULE, not by hand.
2. **THE RULE:** rank the factions by `act1_power` (the graph already carries it,
   Remnants 14 down to Colorful 1) and rank the districts by what they are worth
   (the Strip and downtown first, then the arterials, then the industrial edge,
   then the desert). The strongest faction holds the most valuable ground it can
   reach. Ties go to whoever is closest to their seat.
3. **BORDERS RUN ALONG WHAT YOU CAN SEE.** A territory edge is never an invisible
   line: it follows a big road, a rail line, a wash, or a wall the city already
   generates. If you cannot see why the border is there, it is in the wrong place.
4. **THE BORDER IS MARKED.** Colour on the wall at the edge, in the holder's
   colour (COLOUR IS TERRITORY 8/26, and the one-pixel border law 8/16).
5. **ACT 3 REDRAWS IT.** `act3_power` is already in the graph; the same rule run
   again is the endgame map, so the world visibly changes hands across the game.
6. **HIS TO CORRECT, NOT TO AUTHOR.** Named characters' seats stay his where he
   has ruled them. Everything else is generated, and he fixes what he hates by
   playing it. This is EVERYTHING IS A THUMB applied to the map.
