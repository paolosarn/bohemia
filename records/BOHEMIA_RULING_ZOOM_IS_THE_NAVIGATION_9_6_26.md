# ZOOM IS THE NAVIGATION, AND THE TUTORIAL IS OFFERED (Paolo 9/6/26, LOCKED)
# "I saw a tutorial try to make the action button say that's how you zoom out the
# city, but no bro. Honestly I don't even want a button for city and map, like you
# have them wired all wrong. How you get to the cities is zooming out, so the game
# must explain that very well in the tutorial as well. And then have the tutorial
# pop-up after the other pop-up or something, like do you wanna skip the tutorial
# or have the tutorial or whatever."

## 1. THERE IS NO CITY BUTTON AND NO MAP BUTTON. THERE IS ONE ZOOM.
- **CITY and MAP stop being places you go to and become how far you are standing
  back.** One continuous zoom: the street you walk, pull back and you are looking
  at the city, pull back further and you are looking at the valley. Pinch in and
  you are back on the street where you were.
- **THE BUTTONS ARE WIRED WRONG AND HE SAID SO.** `data-p="city"` and
  `data-p="map"` are tab buttons today, sitting beside anim, rig, vote and the rest
  of the workshop. That is the workshop's furniture leaking into the game.
- The workshop tabs (rig, vote, look, words, clothes, anim, direct, slice, art, ui)
  are NOT in scope. They are our tools. This is about the two that pretend to be
  the game.
- **THE ACTION BUTTON IS NOT A ZOOM BUTTON.** The tutorial tried to relabel the
  action button to teach zooming; that is the exact thing he rejected. The action
  button does the action. Zoom is a pinch, and on a phone that is the gesture
  everybody already owns.

## 2. THE TUTORIAL EXISTS, IT IS OFFERED, AND IT TEACHES THE ZOOM
- After the opening pop-up, a second pop-up asks, in his words: **do you want the
  tutorial, or skip it.** Two choices, no third, no "later", no settings menu.
- **THE ZOOM IS THE THING IT MUST EXPLAIN WELL.** If a player never discovers that
  pulling back is how you reach the city, they never see the city at all, so this
  is the one lesson the tutorial cannot fumble.
- **THIS DOES NOT CONTRADICT "NO TEXT BOX IN THE FIRST FIGHT" AND THE TWO MUST NOT
  FIGHT.** The no-words rule (UI [no text box]) was written for COMBAT and stays
  exactly that: nothing explains the beat in words during a fight. Navigation is
  different from a mechanic learned by failing, he asked for it explicitly, and an
  OFFERED tutorial a player chose is not an interruption. Amended on the board so
  no lane reads the two rules as a conflict.

## 3. WHY HE IS RIGHT, BEYOND THAT HE SAID IT
A button that changes what you are looking at teaches the player that the street,
the city and the valley are three separate screens. A zoom teaches that they are
one place at three distances, which is what the game actually is: the same valley,
the same block, the same house, from further away. The 9/4 tile ruling already
said this about combat ("the size of the ground changes, the player stays the same
size"). The same idea, applied to how you move around, and it removes two buttons
from a UI he just ordered halved.

## ROUTED
- UI [no tabs]: kill the CITY and MAP buttons; zoom is the only way.
- RUN [zoom teaches]: the tutorial, offered after the opening pop-up, teaching the
  zoom before anything else.
- UI [tutorial ask]: the second pop-up, two choices, tutorial or skip.
- UI [no text box]: amended to say it is the FIGHT only, so it cannot be read as
  banning the tutorial he just ordered.
