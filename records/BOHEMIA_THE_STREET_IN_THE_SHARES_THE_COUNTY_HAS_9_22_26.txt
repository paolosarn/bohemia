AND THE THREE PER CENT IS NOT A BUG, WHICH IS WHY THIS IS WORTH WRITING DOWN. Real
people lose about two to five centimetres of height over a lifetime -- on 175 cm that
is one to three per cent -- and the rig's own note says exactly why it does nothing
else: "The ELDER narrows nothing: old age compresses the spine, it does not narrow the
shoulders." THE STAGE IS ANATOMICALLY CORRECT AND VISUALLY USELESS. You cannot reach
an old man through height, because old men are not short. He reads by his BACK (a
stoop), his HAIR and his FACE, and the rig does not bend a spine today.

*** THE FINDING, AND IT IS ARITHMETIC RATHER THAN ART. *** There is a real objection to
using the LIVE county's shares for a valley TEN YEARS COLD: a decade without power in
the Mojave kills the old first, and anybody now under ten was born after the crash.
Halve the children, cut the elders to a third, and the shares become child 6.5, teen
10.7, young adult 16.8, adult 59.3, elder 6.7. Then:
    on TWELVE people        county 1/1/2/6/2   ten years cold 1/1/2/7/1   ONE PERSON APART
    on the 183 his phone draws   county 20/16/26/90/31   cold 12/20/31/108/12
                                 NINETEEN FEWER ELDERS AND EIGHT FEWER CHILDREN
SO THE CHOICE IS INVISIBLE ON ANY VOTE SHEET AND LOUD ON THE STREET. A picture cannot
settle it; a crowd can. That is why this ships ONE row of shares instead of two nearly
identical ones dressed up as a choice. The halving and the third are REASONING, not
measurement, and they are labelled that way everywhere they appear. No canon is
invented here: nothing says what the valley's shares ARE, only what two rules produce.
