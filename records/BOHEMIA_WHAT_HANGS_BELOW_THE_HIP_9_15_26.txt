WHAT HANGS BELOW THE HIP?  --  CHARACTER lane, 9/15/26, VAMILY [long coats]
Asked of the pixels and the rig's own part grid, never of the garment's name.

HIS WORDS THIS ANSWERS (Paolo 9/7): the coat problem "will be the nature of any
long jackets and coats". The row asks for the DATA the rig needs: for every
garment that hangs below the hip, which leg segment carries it.

THE RULER. buildFrame returns a per-pixel grid of the rig's PART IDS. Each garment
is rendered on the body and diffed against the same body naked; every pixel it
changed is looked up in that grid. A garment hangs below the hip exactly when it
changes pixels the rig calls LEG. The hip and the knee come off the rest skeleton
hip row 66, lower leg starts at row 98, foot ends at 105, in a frame 112 x 112.
