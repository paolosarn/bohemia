THE WAGE IS A TRANSFER, NOT A FIFTH FROZEN VERB
================================================================================
The purse's own upkeep() function spends through exactly four named verbs
(day:ate, fight:plate, night:power, ask:leaned) and refuses a fifth by name:
"the four verbs are frozen; a fifth is a design change, and design changes
are Paolo's." [block rent] (9/12) already solved this exact problem for a
different cost (what you owe the faction whose block you stand on) by using a
plain transfer instead of a new verb: "IT IS A TRANSFER, NOT A FIFTH VERB...
rent MOVES, to somebody with a name." Follower wages are the same shape: a
flat debit() call with its own reason string, called from the SAME nightfall
sequence, right after loanNight() -- after your own debts, because what you
owe somebody else comes before what you pay yourself for help.

UNPAID LEAVES THE SAME NIGHT, NO INVENTED GRACE PERIOD. An unpaid circuit
already goes dark immediately (nightPower()); an unpaid follower leaves the
same way. A grace period is a real tuning question with no defensible default
to guess at, so none was invented.

THE BUG THIS ROUND FOUND IN ITS OWN FIRST DRAFT, AND HOW IT WAS CAUGHT
================================================================================
*** A SINGLE DECLINE WOULD HAVE BLOCKED EVERY LATER ROLE, FOREVER. *** The
first version of offerFor(hired) only skipped roles already HIRED. Since
offerFor always returns the first unfilled slot, and a DECLINED role is
never filled, declining the very first offer (the mechanic) would have made
offerFor return 'mechanic' again on every future call -- and because the bark
only asks a role once ever (CT_FOLLOWER_ASKED), the ask-guard would then
refuse to speak it again, silencing the whole retinue mechanism for the rest
of the session. Caught before this went anywhere near the gate's real-city
section, by writing the gate's own decline-then-advance test first and
watching it fail. FIXED: offerFor now takes BOTH hired and asked, and skips a
role that is either. NEGATIVE-CONTROLLED in the gate itself: section B
reconstructs the exact buggy function (skipping only `hired`) as a [self-test]
and proves it reproduces the block, right beside the real function proving it
does not.

right after loanNight(): a plain purse debit per hired follower who is due
tonight, skipping a relieved one, removing anyone who cannot be paid. The
driver's effect is one extra multiply inside cityStepMins, defaulting to 1
(a no-op) when no driver is hired.
