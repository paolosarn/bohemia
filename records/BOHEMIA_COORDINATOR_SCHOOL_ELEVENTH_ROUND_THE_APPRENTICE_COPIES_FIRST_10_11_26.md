# THE COORDINATOR'S SCHOOL, ROUND ELEVEN: THE APPRENTICE COPIES FIRST (10/11, sweep AI; rule 90 continues one round per sweep)

## The world outside game development

The Renaissance workshop. Cennino Cennini's Il Libro dell'Arte (about 1400) tells the apprentice to copy the best master he can find, one master only, for as long as it takes, because switching masters leaves you with neither hand; the aim was to absorb a style until it became a hand of your own. Workshops kept pattern books; apprentices copied them line for line before they were allowed a line of their own, and a drawing from the book could be used in a commission at any time. Copying was the first step toward proportion, composition and anatomy, never a derivative act. The modern echo: an art historian copied all 135 of Michelangelo's early drawings from the originals to understand how he was taught (Pascuzzi, Becoming Michelangelo). The atelier method still opens with copying plates before the model.

## What the coordinator does not do

It never made a chat prove its hand before it stretched. The art lanes went from 'study the pack' (a page of numbers) straight to making new things. Nobody ever asked: can this pipe put one of his own pack tiles back together, from scratch, within the family's own spread? Without that test, the twin gate's thresholds are guesses about a pipe nobody has calibrated, and every first attempt in a family is also the pipe's first test.

The tension with ROUND ELEVEN (use the packs, do not imitate them) is real and resolved: the apprentice's copy is an EXERCISE and a RULER, never a product. It is never an asset, never in VOTE, never 'in the style of'. It is how you know the pipe's hand before you trust it with his material.

## The one change, applied this sweep

Rule 100(c) STUDY FIRST gains a REPRODUCTION: before a cook cuts, stretches or builds anything in a family, it rebuilds one pack piece of that family from scratch through the pipe (the parts or the rig, the shrink, the snap, Aseprite) and the twin gate prints the distance to the original. Outside the family's own spread, the lane does not cut or stretch that family yet. One copy per family, kept with its number in the skill file as the lane's proof of hand. Rule 100 amended in place on the front page (no new rule number; the school's rule 9 against stacking).

## Why it is a round and not a confirmation

The board had study pages, twin sheets and a card of numbers, and none of them measured the pipe. A number about the pack is not a number about the hand. This round adds the only test an apprentice ever had to pass.
