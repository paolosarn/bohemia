# THE CLOSED PLAYTEST PROTOCOL (coordinator, 8/11/26 — pre-written per sweep
# 2 so it is READY the day the demo gate goes green. Grounded in the sweep 1
# research: film test-screening practice + Next Fest conversion data. The
# demo is an instrument before it is a launch.)

## ROUND 1 — FRIENDS AND FAMILY (the soft open)
- WHO: 5-8 people Paolo trusts to be honest (the Alexis circle). Mix at
  least: 2 non-gamers, 2 gamers, 1 who plays phone games daily. NOT other
  developers.
- HOW: the one link, their own iPhones, no explanation from Paolo beyond
  "play this" — the demo must self-explain or that IS the finding.
- INSTRUMENT: the in-demo feedback card (RUN 0f: fun? confusing? play
  again? + words) + the telemetry paste. Each tester sends Paolo ONE paste;
  Paolo forwards pastes to any chat; the coordinator compiles all rounds
  into one digest (where they quit, what confused, what they said).
- PAOLO'S JOB: nothing but forwarding. He does not defend the game, does
  not explain controls, does not watch over shoulders (a watched tester
  performs). One question allowed after they finish: "would you play more?"

## BETWEEN ROUNDS — REVISE
Findings route to lanes as corrections (correct-after law). No re-test
until the named issues are fixed. Target: days, not weeks.

## ROUND 2 — FRESH EYES (the test-screening law)
- WHO: 5-8 NEW people. NEVER re-screen a revised cut to the same audience —
  they compare to memory, not to nothing, and first impressions are the
  entire product of a demo.
- Same instrument, same silence from Paolo. If round 2's quit points moved
  and "play again" rose, the demo is converging; a third round only if
  round 2 surfaced NEW confusion (not residue of round 1's).

## THE PUBLIC DECISION (Paolo's, taken only after round 2)
The data says a public demo with no audience converts nothing: visibility
amplifies momentum, it does not create it. So the public question is not
"is the demo done" but "does anything exist for it to amplify" (a store
page, a following, a fest date). Options land as A/B/C when we get there.
NOTHING public before round 2 passes. First impressions spend once.

## STANDING RULES
- A tester who stops playing is a FINDING, never a failure — where and why
  is the whole point of the instrument.
- Quotes are recorded verbatim like Paolo's rulings; a tester's confusion
  outranks any lane's intention.
- The protocol composes with UNJUDGED-IS-DEAD: nothing here re-surfaces to
  testers; each round is fresh-eyes by construction.
