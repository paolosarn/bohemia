## THE JUDGMENT, AND WHY ALMOST NOTHING DIES
The oldest 60 are the wasteland staples — jeans, flannels, work shirts,
dusters, leather. Their SHAPES are sound and half of them are literally
the register's own material list (layered jersey and leather, heavy
boots, destroyed on purpose). Their disease is TINT: cloth mid-tones one
notch too saturated (0.27-0.54) for the monochrome-and-dust register.
KILLING A SHAPE OVER ITS TINT would invert STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR — the law
says the shape is the value and the colour is the cheap part. So:

- **RETINT x48 (the verdict for the muddy cloth):** keep the hue
  direction and the value, clamp the cloth mid saturation to 0.22-0.25.
  The card names a recolour inside the palette MAINTENANCE, not
  progress — this is that clause doing its job. Mechanical, specified
  per garment in the docket; COOK executes (DIRECTION does not cook),
  and each retinted garment then counts in the register and the ratchet
  climbs toward 100% honestly.
- **PASS-AS-ACCENT x12 (sat >= 0.55):** these are the accent channel's
  INVENTORY — legal only as the single faction-coloured piece on a body
  whose faction owns that hue (COLOUR IS TERRITORY; the picker
  enforces, per the hostiles verdict 9/6). They stay canon and stay
  saturated on purpose.
- **KILL x0, and that is the honest count.** Nothing in the oldest 60
  deserves the graveyard: no purple, no broken shape, no orphan. The
  recolour families (four plaid flannels, three hoodies, three scarves)
  are VARIANTS of one shape each — legal as variants, counted as one
  shape by the 7/19 law, and exactly what a wardrobe needs for a crowd.
  A kill wave here would have been the fourth-version disease wearing a
  judge's coat.
