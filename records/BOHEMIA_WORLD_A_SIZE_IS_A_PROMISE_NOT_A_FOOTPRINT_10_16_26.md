## 1. **BATTLE BROTHERS DOES NOT ANSWER IN SIZE. IT ANSWERS IN GUARANTEES.**

From the library (`reference/library/grok/wiki/`, the Settlements page), its
civilian ladder is three rungs, and what separates them is **what is certain to
be there**:

| | **Small Village** | **Medium Village** | **City** |
|---|---|---|---|
| guaranteed | nothing | **a tavern** | **a tavern and a temple** |
| military attached locations | **none allowed** | possible | possible |

And a second ladder, military, also three rungs:

| | **Wooden Keep** | **Stone Keep** | **Citadel** |
|---|---|---|---|
| guaranteed | nothing | **an armourer and a weaponsmith** | **an armourer and a weaponsmith** |
| kennels | more likely | can appear | **never appear** |

In its own words, *"size has an influence on prices"*, military settlements are
*"more likely to offer high grade weapons and armor"* and *"house stronger
regiments"*.

**So a size there is not an area. It is a promise.** A small village promises you
nothing and cannot hold a watchtower. A city promises you a tavern and a temple
before you arrive. That is why a player learns the ladder in one campaign and
reads the map without a legend afterwards: **the word tells him what he will find,
not how many tiles it covers.**

## 4. **BUT HIS RULING ASKS FOR THE HALF BATTLE BROTHERS NEVER HAD TO ANSWER**

Rule 102 is LOCKED and it is not BB's model: *"if a settlement is a 2 by 2 block
it should just be large on the map like that."* BB never needs a footprint because
its settlements are **pins on a painting** — an icon and a menu, with no extent to
get wrong.

He has ruled ours are real pieces of the city at true world size. **So the
footprint is ours to invent, and the library has no answer for it.**

The one real anchor we own, measured, in our own repo
(`engine/bohemia_tilekinds.js`, from `bohemia_suburb.js` at seed 1337):

- a block is **128 cells × 0.75 m = 96 metres a side**
- a lot pitch is **19.5 m** (range 18 to 21.8)
- a house footprint is **21 × 12 cells = 15.75 × 9 m**
- and that block holds **20 houses**

**So one block is about twenty households.** That is the unit a footprint can be
honestly stated in, and it comes from our own measurement rather than a feel.
