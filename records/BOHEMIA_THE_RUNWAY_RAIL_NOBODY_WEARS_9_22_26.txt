*** THE COLOUR GUARD WAS TOO WEAK AND THE TABLE CAUGHT IT. *** The first run refused a swap
only if it moved the body's AVERAGE cloth colour more than 26 of 255, and it happily
put REDS IN AN OLIVE COAT AND A GOLD BOOT and REMNANTS, the olive faction, IN BRICK.
Trading one strong colour for another barely moves an average, so the guard never
fired while the picture showed two factions wearing each other's territory -- exactly
what COLOUR IS TERRITORY exists to stop. A mean is the wrong instrument for it.
NOW A COLOURED GARMENT IS ONLY OFFERED TO THE FACTION WHOSE RAMP IT IS CUT IN. The
neutrals stay open to everybody, because a neutral is nobody's territory, and the
mean-shift guard stays underneath as a second net.

AND BOTH POLES TURN OUT TO BE REACHABLE FROM THE FEET ALONE -- a platform widens the
base into Rick Owens, a pant-boot narrows it into Balenciaga -- which is why the
factions with no coat at all can still be moved.

horror. It is the register he named, and the bible's rule 1 still wants one wrong
thing, which a better-cut trouser does not supply. So this answers "Rick Owens meets
Balenciaga" and it does NOT yet answer "analog horror meets". RNWY-16 is the bridge
and it is the next thing this lane builds.
