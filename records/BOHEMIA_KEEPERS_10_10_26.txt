BOHEMIA -- THE KEEPERS (10/10/26, PEOPLE lane)
VAMILY [keepers], row THE-MAN-WHO-WALKS-WITH-A-BEAST, rule 42 round three.
"People use animals as weapons and tools, and a faction that does is a
faction others fear." A handler background, a beast slot in the company,
the automated companion may be an animal.

================================================================================
THE SPLIT, READ FIRST
================================================================================
The row's own text already names the boundary: "the beast-keeper as a
background and a faction... With FACTIONS (the keeper faction's roster)
and COMBAT (the beast slot on the board)." This round built only this
lane's own piece: the six real crafts, and the shape a company's companion
slot would need to carry an animal.

================================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================
engine/bohemia_keepers.js: a pure module. Six real beast-keeper crafts
(rule 42 round three, section 2), never invented: war dogs (police and
military dog lines), hunting birds (falconry, a living Nevada craft),
camel cavalry (real for two thousand years), horse raiders (Nevada's
mustangers), the hyena men (Nigeria's real hyena handlers), bee-keepers as
siege (a real medieval tactic). companionSlot(craftId) returns the real
slot shape for the five crafts with a living animal, and REFUSES
bee-keepers honestly: a thrown hive is a weapon, not a beast that walks
beside you, and this module does not force it into a shape that would lie
about what it is.

================================================================================
A REAL GAP, MEASURED NOT GUESSED
================================================================================
Before building anything, read slices/BOHEMIA_CITY_WORLD.html's own
companion state. CT_WALKS_WITH is set in exactly the places you'd expect
(`CT_WALKS_WITH = hit.id;` or null), and every one of them is a person's
id. There is no shape anywhere in this codebase for a companion that is
not a person. "The automated companion may be an animal" is therefore a
real, measured gap, not an assumed one -- and this module proposes the
real shape that case needs (kind, species, craft) rather than silently
wiring something half-built into a system that has never supported it.

================================================================================
NO PRICE, ON PURPOSE
================================================================================
The research round's own economic note for this exact system is
qualitative, not a number: "a live capture -- a handler's dog or camel --
is worth a season's pay." Rule 63d forbids inventing a number no source
gives, so a hire price for a handler stays honestly unbuilt until TUNING's
table or a direct Grok ask gives a real one.

================================================================================
PROVEN, NOT ASSERTED
================================================================================
gates/keepers_gate.js reads the real research record and the real city
file DIRECTLY. Every one of the six citations is checked against the
actual round-three text, byte for byte (normalized for the file's own line
wrap, never reworded). companionSlot() is proven to answer only for the
five crafts with a real animal and refuse the sixth. The companion-slot
gap claim is proven against the live file's own source, not asserted: six
real CT_WALKS_WITH assignments found, every one a person's id or null,
zero already handling an animal shape.

================================================================================
THE GATE
================================================================================
gates/keepers_gate.js, new (REUSE-FIRST: nothing existed to extend).
21 legs: the real-citation proof, the real-animal/refused-weapon proof, the
refused-unknown proof, the real-gap proof against the live city file,
cook + registry.
