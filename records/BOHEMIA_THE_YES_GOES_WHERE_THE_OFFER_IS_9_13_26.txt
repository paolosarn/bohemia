*** THERE WAS NO WAY TO SAY YES. ***

The game shows him a job, tells him what it pays, tells him how long the walk is,
and gives him THREE WAYS TO ARGUE ABOUT THE TERMS -- and no way to take it.
Accepting went through exactly one door: a postMessage from the phone. A player
who never found the phone could haggle a job he could not have. He argued, the
terms really moved, the card really answered him, and the job never started.

That is not "a button that does nothing". It is worse and more exact: every
button worked, and the one that mattered was not there.

================================================================================
AND EVERYTHING ELSE ON THAT CARD ALREADY WORKED, WHICH IS WHY IT WENT UNSEEN
================================================================================
Pressed every button on it, one at a time, re-reading the page after each press:
  Make it a bag instead        the terms moved, "Fine. A bag, then"
  Make it a favour instead     the terms moved, "Fine. A favour, then"
  Half of it now               third ask, "They take it back. Somebody else
                               will do it", and the offer really ends
  GET UP                       the day starts
Four for four. A card where every visible control works is exactly the card
nobody checks for a missing one.

THE FIRST READING OF THIS WAS WRONG AND THE INSTRUMENT WAS THE REASON. A first
sweep pressed all the buttons in one pass and reported three of them dead. They
were not: the card REDRAWS after a press, so every handle after the first was
pointing at an element that no longer existed. Same lesson as the healing probe
last round -- CHECK THE INSTRUMENT BEFORE CONCLUDING THE FEATURE IS BROKEN. The
honest version re-queries the page after every single press.

================================================================================
NOTHING WAS BUILT TO FIX IT, AND THAT IS THE PART WORTH BEING ANNOYED ABOUT
================================================================================
offerAccept() has always worked. Called straight, it takes the job, the quest goes
live, the phone badge clears and the sting plays. It had no door on the card the
offer was already on.

So the fix is one row, and deliberately the SAME function the phone has always
used. Two ways to accept a job is how two rules about one thing start to disagree.

IT IS NOT SHOWN WHEN THERE IS NOTHING TO TAKE. A job already taken has no yes, and
neither does an offer argued until they took it back -- that is __ASK_FOR_MORE__'s
whole cost and a live button would have quietly refunded it.

================================================================================
AND IT UPDATES IN PLACE, WHICH THE FIRST CUT GOT WRONG
================================================================================
The first version redrew the card the way an ask does. MEASURED ON THE PAGE: the
quest went live and the card came back still offering the job and still saying
"nobody has picked it up yet". showWake() runs offerRing(), whose first line is
`OFFER=null; OFFER_TAKEN=false;` -- so redrawing the morning RE-RINGS the phone
and wipes the acceptance off the card that just took it.

The road card had already solved this shape: the outcome REPLACES the options, in
place. Leaving them up after a press lies about what just happened. Now the ways
to argue and the yes both go, the "nobody has picked it up" line goes with them,
and where the yes was it says: "You took it. It is yours until the day is out."

  and zero page errors while doing it
