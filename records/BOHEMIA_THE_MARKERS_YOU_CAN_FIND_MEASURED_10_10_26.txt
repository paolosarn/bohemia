WHAT CHANGED AND WHAT DID NOT. The silhouettes stay, pixel for pixel: 9/24 proved no two
share more than 62% of a shape, and at fourteen pixels the shape is what reads. The
moving parts stay: the banner stirs while you travel and hangs dead when you stop, the
shed's loose sheet lifts, the pump's beam nods. WHAT MOVED IS THE VALUE, and the values
are measured off HIS OWN APPROVED TILE FOR THE THING EACH MARKER IS:
  YOU            none: the packs hold no people (rule 82); this lane's own hue, same spread
  THE SHOP       pack:8. Market Stalls (14 approved tiles read)
  THE SHED       pack:5. Roof tiles (14 approved tiles read)
  THE PUMP       pack:18. Pipes and cables (14 approved tiles read)
  THE FORTRESS   pack:3. Broken wall tiles (14 approved tiles read)

A ladder is taken off every approved tile in that pack at once: all their pixels sorted
by light and cut at even steps, so the rungs are his tile's own shadow, midtones and
light. A colour picked by eye out of a photograph is how a lane invents a colour and
calls it his.

AND NOT ONE LINE OF ANOTHER LANE'S CODE HAS TO CHANGE. RUN's page looks a character up
in ONE shared palette, so this round ADDS letters to that palette instead of giving each
marker its own. Every old key still points at exactly the colour it did; the new letters
resolve on their own. 7 keys before, 20 after.

THE ONE THING THAT MAKES A FOURTEEN-PIXEL SHAPE READ AS AN OBJECT: the top row of every
solid run is lifted one rung, so the sun lands north-west the way the bible says and the
marker has a lit edge instead of being a sticker. That is why the rim was doing the
work before: nothing inside the art was.
