- RETREAT: the retreat button; men on the board's edge leave safely, the rest run for the edge with a zone-of-control defence
  bonus (records/target/bb/rules.json, rule 144(c)). Leaving an enemy's zone of control gives that enemy a free attack.
- THE ROUT (recall): a man whose morale breaks is FLEEING: he runs for the edge on his own turns, he can be caught and killed,
  and every enemy whose zone he leaves gets a free swing. A fleeing man can rally. When every enemy left is fleeing, the
  player can end the fight and it counts as won; the ones who got away are gone.
- ALLIES (recall): Battle Brothers does NOT let a man walk through his own men (only the Rotation perk swaps two). His ruling

(a) HIS RETREAT WORKS IN EVERY FIGHT (rule 144(c), still not in his hands): the RETREAT button on the fight's own bar; his men

    chases; no pay; a retreat is never a wipe.
(b) A FLEEING ENEMY RUNS, AND THE FIGHT GOES ON: he spends his turns running for the edge; he can be caught, hit and killed;
    he can rally (Battle Brothers' morale). Nothing ends the fight by itself the moment someone breaks.
(c) FREE HITS ON A MAN WHO RUNS (his words): a man of either side who leaves the zone of a melee man takes that man's free swing
    (Battle Brothers' attack of opportunity); a man who runs across the reach of a gunman who has a round in and has not

    per turn; the odds and numbers through TUNING's one table, Battle Brothers' first.
(d) THE END: the fight ends when every enemy is down or off the board; when every enemy left is fleeing, an END THE FIGHT
    button wins it (Battle Brothers'), the runners gone, the loot of the fallen yours.
(e) AUTO and the calculator play all of it the same round (rule 178(c)): AUTO chases a runner worth catching and never leaves
    a zone it does not have to; its own retreat runs the safest line to the edge.
