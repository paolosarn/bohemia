## RULINGS (NOTES ARE RULINGS)
1. A text prompt is not an event. THE SNATCHER has no kid on screen, nothing leaves his
   belt that he can see, nothing runs forty feet. It is a sentence pretending to be a
   thing that happened. Every card of this kind is DEAD on the walked surface THIS ROUND,
   not moved to a mouth later: the road and walk directors stop opening cards.
2. A moment fires only if it has a body: the kid is a sprite that comes off his blind
   side and runs, the thing leaves his belt on screen, or the moment does not fire at
   all. The tables of moments stay as content waiting for bodies.
3. Why they were done, answered honestly: the road moments were written 9/5 as cards
   standing in for encounters that had no bodies yet, under a law that says every piece
   of player-facing text ships as an attempt. The chats read "an attempt" as "text can
   stand in for the thing". The manager let text count as shipped. That law is amended:
   words are an attempt AT WORDS for a thing that exists on screen; words never stand in
   for the thing.
4. "You don't have shit for real." Recorded as the measure: nothing on the board counts
   as real until it is people, places and things on the deployed cut.
