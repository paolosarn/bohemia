## The ruling (rule 112)

(a) HE TALKS TO ONE CHAT. The coordinator is the only chat he types into. His words here are rulings, VAMILY is a sweep, and everything else on his side happens in the VOTE tab. Rule 40h (he sends the word to whoever he likes) is DEAD: the coordinator sends it.

(b) THE COORDINATOR RUNS THE FLEET. Every sweep, not only the big swing day: the coordinator reads the lane sessions it opened, archives every finished one the same sweep so his session list stays short, and opens a fresh session for every running lane whose board carries an OPEN row and no running session, with 'lane name, VAMILY' as its first message. Never two sessions on one lane: a lane chat he opened by hand before this ruling is archived when the coordinator's session for that lane exists.

(c) THE WAKE-UP IS STANDING. The hourly routine that fires VAMILY into the coordinator's session is permanent, not a day's. It stops only on his word or a reported usage limit, and it re-arms when he says go. Rule 111(c)'s 'end of the day he named' is dead.

(d) NO APPROVAL ANYWHERE. Rule 111(d) stands for every tool; a lane that hits a prompt reports the tool name in its reply and the coordinator adds it the same sweep. The lane sessions run with the repo attached and the allow list read at their start, so they never ask him.

(e) THE REPLY IS HIS WHOLE VIEW. Since he reads no lane chat, the coordinator's five lines are the only place he learns what the fleet did. The reply names what landed where he can see it (the tab), the rounding list of quiet lanes stays as a count he reads and does not act on, and the plate's state. Nothing that only lives in a lane chat reached him.

## Honest limits

- The routine fires into this one session. If this session is ever archived or lost, the routine fails to deliver and the fleet stops waking. The coordinator's reply tells him when that happens; the fix is one word from him in a new coordinator session, which re-creates the routine.
