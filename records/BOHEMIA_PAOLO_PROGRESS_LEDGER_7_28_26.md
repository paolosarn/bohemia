STANDING READS FROM THE NUMBERS (coordinator, mechanical, not canon):
1. The ZEROES AND FIVES are the named holes: sound effects (0), NPCs (5),
   items (5). Everything else has an engine and a lane grinding it.
2. His quest number is a warning about SURFACE, not text: content that is not
   REACHABLE IN THE GAME counts near-zero to him no matter how gated and
   parseable it is. (Same lesson as the engine reality map's 1-of-21.)

   upsetness down the line" — the friction he names is ART JUDGMENT LOAD.
   Anything that produces progress WITHOUT adding to his judge pile is worth
   double. (The tile-form system exists for exactly this; see the 7/28
