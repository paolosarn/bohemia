## What it is

Rule 108's eighth round, and the clearest statement of the goal: not a vendor, not a purchase, but THE CEILING. The chats' art has a ceiling ('coded by a seventh grader') and he will pay once, or leave a computer on, to raise it. Two constraints inside it: one-time purchases only (standing), and an always-on home computer MAY exist but CANNOT be depended on (so every pipeline must also run on GitHub's free runners).
