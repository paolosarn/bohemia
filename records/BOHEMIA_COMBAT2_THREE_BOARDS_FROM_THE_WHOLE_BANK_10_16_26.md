## HOW THE PACK DID IT (rule 100c, the page before the drawing; read from his tiles, measured, not remembered)

1. **The pack's camera is square ground from above and three-quarter objects.** Every ground tile he owns is a square
   plan (the cracked street, the slabs, the soil); every object (his cars in '9. Abandoned cards', the lamps, the fences,
   the pack house 5. Roof tiles #30) is seen three-quarter with a lit top and a front face. COOK FOUR's ranch is made the
   same way (the front slope and the front face). The fight's old cos45 squeeze of the ground (364 for 515) was OURS;
   the pack never had it. So these boards are square cells with standing objects, the pack's way.

2. **The pack's props are drawn in one 96-px box, not at world scale.** Measured over the 16 packs used: a car (96 x
   56), a traffic cone (62 x 96), a lamp post (33 x 96), a bin (75 x 96) and a chain-link run (96 x 50) all fill the same
   box. At the body's ruler (57 px a metre: the 112-px man is 1.75 m, COOK FOUR's measure) a 96-px car is 1.7 m long and
   a 96-px cone is 1.7 m tall. The ground tiles and the house pieces do not care (texture, and parts laid at size);
   the props do. THE RULE WRITTEN INTO THE MANIFEST, per kind: a prop is placed at the integer scale nearest its real size,
   never under 1x (rule 105, the floor), nearest neighbour (whole pixels): cars x2 (3.4 m), dead trees x2, lamps x2,
   chain link x2; walls, junk, rocks, barrels, signs, the dumpster, jersey barriers x1. NOT PLACED: cones, hydrants,
   bins and trash bags, giants at 1x with nothing under the floor. A 2x prop has 2-px pixels on a 1-px ground; that
   is on the sheet for DIRECTION to judge.

3. **A pack tile is one pace.** COOK TWO's cut cell (82 x 81 px, his street and slab tiles minus their baked frame) is
   1.44 m at the ruler. Eight cells are 11.5 m: COOK FOUR's ranch front to the pixel (656 px), a Las Vegas local
   street's roadway (37 ft = 11.3 m, the city code on Grok's page) and, as one cell, a Vegas sidewalk (4.7 ft). So the
   house tile is 8 x 8 cells = 656 x 648 px, the street one tile, the sidewalk one cell, the house two tiles, and no
   number of ours is in it.

4. **The pack's ground tiles are near-tileable, never exact, and some are not tiles at all.** His soil tiles (2. Soil
   and dirt, UP) lay clean with their 3-to-5-px rim cut. The desert pebbles of '1. Ground Tiles' (never judged by
   anybody until this round) looked like the Vegas yard and are not: 96 x 70 PATCHES with a 7-px dark rim and a lit top
   row. Laid as cells they striped every yard (the first run's sheet, thrown away). Measured and left out. THE HOLE
   (82a: declared after a count): the bank holds no gravel-yard tile and no street light taller than a garden post.

5. **Density.** Median distinct colours per 96 x 96 window: his ground 4,140 (the 10/10 page); these boards 3,820 /
   3,362 / 3,575 (small / middle / large); our old cooked blocks 18 to 29. Every pixel of the board is his.
