THE COLD HAND
=============

Rather than trust the row's description, I built a probe that behaves like the
tester it describes. It scores every visible control by WHAT ITS PIXELS ACTUALLY
DO -- fill brightness weighted hardest, then border, then text colour, then area
-- refuses anything it could not reach with a thumb (elementFromPoint at the
control's own centre, so it cannot press through a modal), presses whatever wins,
and never reads a word.

BEFORE, from a cold boot:

    1. WATCH      gold, score 89     correct, and the only good tap in the run
    2. GET UP     score 43           correct
    3. DROP IN    score 33           <-- the game ends here
    4. CITY       score 33
    5..12         DROP IN / CITY / DROP IN / CITY ...

    phone opened : 0
    job taken    : 0
    clock        : 06:00 at tap 1, 06:00 at tap 12

*** HE NEVER EVEN REACHES SLEEP. *** The row's version has him ending day one by
accident. The truth is worse and quieter: he flips the camera between the street
and the map until he puts the phone down. Nothing he can find spends a minute of
the day.

And `__OFFER_RANG` is 1 for the whole run. THE PHONE DID RING, at 06:00, and the
hand never heard it -- because a ringing phone was a dark chip with a hairline
border and a 14-pixel red dot in the corner.


DON NORMAN, WHICH THE ROW ALREADY CITED
=======================================

AFFORDANCES are what actions are possible. SIGNIFIERS are what tells you where
the action goes. And: WHEN YOU HAVE TO PUT A SIGN ON A DOOR, THE DESIGN ALREADY
FAILED.

The badge is a sign on a door. So the fix could not be a tooltip, an arrow, a
tutorial line or one more sentence of copy -- every one of those is a bigger sign
on the same door. It had to change which thing LOOKS like the handle.


THE FIX, THREE PATCHES
======================

1. bohemia_the_phone_is_the_handle_patch.py
   While a call is unread, the phone chip wears the SOLID FILL the opening's
   WATCH button already wears (#d8b45a on #191308).
   NOT A NEW SIGNIFIER: it is the treatment on the very first thing the player
   ever presses, so the language is taught before he needs it.
   AND IT HANGS OFF THE OWNER THAT ALREADY EXISTS. phoneBadge() computes
   `(OFFER && !OFFER_TAKEN)` and is the one place in the file that knows there is
   an unread call. The class is toggled THERE. A second thing polling "is the
   phone ringing" is the bug I shipped into the population card on 8/24 and had
   to take back out.
   NOT DAY ONE ONLY, ON PURPOSE: the honest rule is "an unread call is the game
   asking for you", true every morning, with no date check to rot.
   IT SWITCHES ITSELF OFF the moment the job is taken.

2. bohemia_a_panel_outranks_the_chrome_patch.py
   *** AND THE COLD HAND FOUND A SECOND BUG THE ROW DID NOT KNOW ABOUT. ***
   With the phone OPEN and covering 378x763 of a 390x844 screen, three world
   buttons were STILL the topmost element at their own centres:

       sleepbtn    bikebtn    rungbtn

   SLEEP ENDS THE DAY. He opens the phone, his thumb is already at the bottom of
   the screen, and the button under it finishes day one with the job never taken.
   That is the row's failure with a real mechanism under it.

   *** IT WAS MINE, FROM TWO DAYS EARLIER. *** __HUD_NEVER_OVERLAPS__ (8/24)
   joined the day-loop chips into #blstack so they could not collide, and gave
   that column z-index 39. #phonewrap has been 30 since it was built. Before the
   column SLEEP was z-index 7 and correctly underneath; the column lifted it over.
   A LAYOUT FIX THAT CHANGES A STACKING CONTEXT IS A LAYERING CHANGE. Same shape
   as the popcard's offsetTop and the see-through's playerBox -- MOVING WHERE
   SOMETHING SITS IS NOT A COSMETIC CHANGE -- and that is three times in one week.

   The city had twenty-two z-indexes and no rule, which is why it was possible:
   #ctcard 41, #cttalk 40, #cttell 39, #blstack 39, #phonewrap 30, #daycard 20,
   #savepanel 9, #devtray 9, #pfpanel 8, #buildpanel 8, #tlstack 7, #sleepbtn 7.
   A takeover panel sat below the chip columns AND below the conversation cards.
   The order is now written down next to the one number that moved:

       world / canvas      under everything
       chrome              6 .. 45   buttons, chips, the two columns, cards
       TAKEOVER PANELS     50 ..     things that own the screen while up

   ONLY #phonewrap MOVED. It is the only panel that actually covers the stage

3. bohemia_take_it_is_the_yes_patch.py
   With the first two in, the cold hand got four taps deep and stalled at the
   button that TAKES THE JOB -- `.mapgo`, background #2a2418, a dark chip built
   for the map's small "GO" link. Measured: TAKE IT scored 35 on its own screen
   and was beaten by an unread-post counter at 46. Everything else on that screen
   is text.
   `LIVE.offer` is true for exactly one state -- a job has come in and has not
   been taken -- and in that state TAKE IT is not one option among several, it is
   the single reason the screen is open. It wears the same fill now, so the whole
   first morning is written in one sentence he has already been taught:

       WATCH -> GET UP -> [gold] PHONE -> [gold] TAKE IT

   `.mapgo` itself is untouched, so the map's GO links stay the quiet chips they
   were designed as.
   AND THIS EDITED THE SOURCE, NOT THE BUILD: BOHEMIA_CURRENT_SLICE.html is
   generated by tools/build_current_slice.js from
   BOHEMIA_SOCIAL_PHONE_DEMO_7_20_26.html. I edited the generated file once
   before, on a different job, and the CURRENT SLICE gate caught it with
   "regenerating changes nothing" going FALSE. BEFORE EDITING A FILE, ASK WHO
   GENERATES IT.

WHAT THE PROBE COST ME, WHICH IS THE REUSABLE PART
==================================================

The cold hand was wrong four times before it was right, and every one of the four
was a version of the same bug the game had:

  1. IT CROWNED THE VIEWPORT. The first cut swept `[id]` and ranked #cityFrame --
     the iframe holding the entire world -- as the loudest button on screen, then
     "pressed" it twelve times. A scorer that will rank a viewport as a button is
     measuring nothing.
  2. IT PRESSED THROUGH A MODAL. Clicking by JS reached the phone chip through
     the wake card's scrim and reported an order no human can perform. That is
     this repo's own thumb-sweep finding -- "a Playwright click lands anywhere
     with equal ease, so reachability is invisible to our whole apparatus BY
     CONSTRUCTION" -- and it applies to a hand-rolled probe first of all.
  3. IT COULD NOT SEE THE BUTTON THE WHOLE JOB WAS ABOUT. It swept a fixed
     selector list, and nobody had put `.lv-take` in the list. A PROBE THAT
     ENUMERATES ITS SUBJECTS IS ONLY EVER AS COMPLETE AS ITS ENUMERATION -- the
     same lesson the panel gate learned pressing close buttons BY NAME and calling
     a panel stuck because it had never heard of #sv-close. Fixed by asking what
     BEHAVES like a control: a click handler, a button role, or a pointer cursor.
  4. IT REPORTED THAT TAKE IT WAS BROKEN, FOR FOUR RUNS. It pressed a handle
     recorded by an earlier evaluate; the phone re-renders on every state push, so
     the tagged node was gone by the time of the press. A real pointer at the same
     coordinates takes the job (measured: taken 0 -> 1). THE BUTTON WAS NEVER
     BROKEN. THE PROBE WAS MEASURING ITS OWN STALENESS -- and I nearly wrote up a
     working button as a demo-blocking defect.

Number 4 is the one to remember. Three of these made the probe blind; that one
made it CONFIDENTLY WRONG about the game, which is worse. VERIFY ON THE REAL
SURFACE (7/18) is about the gesture as much as the pixels: a synthesised click
event is a side-door press, and the phone routes taps through a delegated handler
that wants the whole pointer sequence.

WHAT IS STILL TRUE AND NOT FIXED
================================

The phone opens on a LOCK SCREEN of notifications, and the job is one tap further
in. That is left exactly as it is: tapping a notification on a phone lock screen
is the most universally understood gesture there is, and it is the one piece of
this flow that needs no teaching. The gate performs it rather than removing it.
