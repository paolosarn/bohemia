HOW A SHAPE IS COUNTED: each garment is rendered ALONE on the same body, diffed
against the bare body, and the pixels it CHANGED are hashed -- indices only,
colour thrown away. Two garments that change exactly the same pixels are one cut
wearing two paints. This is not a rule invented here: it is the ruler hair_gate
has used for haircuts since 8/1, pointed at the wardrobe for the first time.

AND WHY IT MATTERS ON THE STREET, measured on the real picker:

  3000 citizens wore 3000 DIFFERENT OUTFITS. Not one repeat, every garment used,
  no layer starved. By that count the rail is not short at all.
  Then the same crowd was RENDERED and the picture disagreed:
     300 citizens produced 223 different silhouettes
     of the 46 wearing nothing over the base, only 32 looked different
  THE OUTFITS ARE UNIQUE AND THE PEOPLE ARE NOT. Half the crowd wears nothing
  over the base, and those are the ones who repeat -- with three extra layers a
  citizen is 98% likely to be one of a kind, with none it is 70%.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY, because the difference decides who acts:
  - It does NOT say any pair is lazy. A plaid shirt and a solid shirt of the same
    cut ARE the same cut, honestly. Whether that is enough volume is DIRECTION's
    call against the style card, never this lane's.
  - It DOES say, with a number, how much of the rail is CUT and how much is
    PAINT: three garments in four add no new shape to the game.
  - The one layer that passes its own law outright is HAIR, at 100%: every
    haircut is a shape no other haircut draws. That is what the law looks like
    when it is enforced, and hair is the only category with a gate holding it.

ROUTED, and this lane did not do it on purpose: COOK cooks, so the next batch
should be CUTS and not colourways -- the shape count is the number to move, and
it is now held by a machine so it cannot quietly fall. Nothing here retunes the
picker either: making plain citizens rarer would hide the finding rather than fix
it, and how often a coat appears is a look decision for the card.

READ THIS BEFORE DRAWING A CONCLUSION. A NUMBER IS NOT A FINDING UNTIL YOU KNOW
WHAT IT IS COUNTING. "Same shape" here means the game paints the same pixels, on
one body, at one facing, at 112. A garment that differs only where this body does
not show it would read as a twin and is not one.
