## 1. THE DOOR NOBODY OPENED: ANIMALS THE LAB MADE NEW (real, today)
- ORGAN PIGS: pigs edited with human genes so their hearts and kidneys can go into people; real transplants
  happened in 2022 to 2024; farms of them exist under pharmaceutical guard. Let loose, they are hogs (S tier)
  with a story: every one carries a human gene, and people will not eat them. The horror bible likes that.
- MUSCLE-DOUBLED LIVESTOCK: cattle, pigs and dogs edited to lack the gene that limits muscle (myostatin;
  Belgian Blue cattle have it naturally; edited beagles were made in China in 2015). A 1,200 kg bull with
  double muscle is the aurochs made worse. Feral in a decade.
- HUMANIZED MICE AND RATS by the million in every research town; released, they are rats. Rats are the most
  successful invasive mammal in history and no game beast; but a plague rides them (PEOPLE [the plague]).
- LAB PRIMATES: Florida has a wild colony of rhesus macaques since the 1930s (a tourist attraction's release)
  that carries a herpes virus deadly to people; research macaques number in the tens of thousands. A monkey
  troop in a dead casino is real, funny, and dangerous. Not a beast to fight; a place's hazard and a rumour.
- THE ALMOST-HUMAN LINE: human-monkey and human-pig embryo chimeras have been grown in labs (2019 to 2021) for
  organ research and stopped by rule at a few weeks. Bohemia does not put a half-man on the road (his beasts
  are animals; the Amalgamation is the villain), but a rumour that the lab 'went further' is exactly the horror
  bible's institutional lie, and WORDS may use it as a lie.
- GLOW AND MARKER ANIMALS: fluorescent fish (sold in pet stores now), glowing rabbits and pigs; harmless, and a
  night-time tell on the map: a green shine in the dark is a lab animal. DIRECTION's analog horror gift.
- DE-DOMESTICATION: dogs are the realest beast of every collapse. A pack of former pets turns wild in one
  generation (India's 30 million street dogs kill ~20,000 people a year by rabies). Feral dogs are act 1's first
  fight and never stop being one.

## 2. THE KEEPERS (the Battle Brothers beastmaster, made real)
People use animals as weapons and tools, and a faction that does is a faction others fear:
- WAR DOGS (the noble houses' wardogs; police and military dog lines exist by the thousand); HUNTING BIRDS
  (falconry is a living craft in Nevada; Haast's eagle on a glove is a boss's pet); CAMEL CAVALRY (real for two
  thousand years, the desert's own); HORSE RAIDERS (Nevada's mustangers); THE HYENA MEN (Nigeria's hyena
  handlers are real: chained hyenas as street performers and guards); BEE-KEEPERS AS SIEGE (hives thrown
  over walls is a medieval tactic and a real one).
- FOR THE GAME: the beast-keeper faction (round zero's 'barbarians with unholds') is a roster PEOPLE and FACTIONS
  write: a handler background, a beast slot in the company (the player can hire a handler and his dog, camel or
  bird; the automated companion may be an animal), and the raid where the enemy brings a hyena.

## 3. SIZE ON THE FIGHT BOARD (a tile is a house, 40 metres or so; rule 38h)
A house-sized tile holds a whole pack, so beasts are GROUPS on a tile, not single figures, and the biggest are
still one tile: a dire wolf pack (6 to 10 animals) = one tile of wolves that can split; a hyena clan = one to two
tiles; a hog sounder = one tile; a mammoth herd = two tiles; a hippo pod = one tile at the shore that never
leaves the water tile; the eagle = no tile (it strikes the roof and leaves); the swarm = a tile that spreads one
tile a beat until fire or water; the dino-bird = one tile, moves two a beat (the runner); the lion = one tile,
one animal, hits once and hard (the dial shot's target). COMBAT [bestiary] turns this into the data file with
the 9/22 reach rule; TUNING numbers it.

## 4. WHAT EACH IS WORTH (ECONOMY, batteries are the money, everything costs one)
Hide and meat (hog, cattle, camel, horse: the valley's food), ivory (mammoth tusks are a real trade today from
thawing Siberia), camel milk and burden, horse transport, honey (the swarm's upside), eggs (moa, a meal for a
company), feathers (the eagle), a live capture (a handler's dog or camel is worth a season's pay), bounties (a
hyena clan's contract). A beast is a contract and a market, not only a fight.

## 6. WHAT CLOSES (rule 41)
The beast research is DONE at four rounds. From here it is data (WORLD), lessons (COMBAT), numbers (TUNING),
art (COOK to DIRECTION's card), prices (ECONOMY), keepers (PEOPLE, FACTIONS) and words (WORDS). A fifth round
is a lane's page, not the manager's.
