## THE RULINGS (LOCKED)
1. NARRATION, YES, AND ITS VOICE IS AI SLOP ON PURPOSE. Take the machine voice (the phone's own synthetic reader,
   SOUNDS [read aloud]'s default) and make it creepy as fuck: too even, slightly wrong, tape and room on it, the
   analog horror bible's 'a voice that is too even' made literal. It NARRATES EVENTS and READS TEXT (the feed,

   voice is not hiding what it is; the world is run by a machine nobody is left to switch off.
2. PEOPLE SPEAK IN THE SQUIGGLE. Any time a PERSON speaks, it is the Animalese-style gibberish voice chosen 7/21

3. VARIATIONS OF THE SQUIGGLE, OF COURSE: per person (pitch, speed, grain by sex, age, size, faction, mood);

   output looks and sounds machine-made, too smooth, a little wrong, and the world around it is hand-made and
