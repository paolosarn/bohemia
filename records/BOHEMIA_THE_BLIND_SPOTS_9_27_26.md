# THE BLIND SPOTS (coordinator 9/27/26, at Paolo's ask: "is there any blind spots of my whole enchilada?")
Pulled against the canon, Battle Brothers on a phone, and the real world. Each with what is done about it.
1. THE STORY DOES NOT EXIST. "A better story" is the pitch and THERE IS NO STORY YET (9/11) is still true: the
   60-boss ladder is a list of verbs, the 58 quests are a bank, and with QUESTS research-only nobody's row is
   "the main quest". Battle Brothers gets away with no story because its company writes its own; we promised
   better. ROUTED: QUESTS question (g): what the 152 say a MAIN LINE is when side content is contracts; then a
   WORDS/DYNASTY row for the spine across three acts. His to name, ours to shape.
2. HOVER DOES NOT EXIST ON A PHONE. Battle Brothers shows every number on hover; a thumb has no hover. One
   HUD (rule 24) needs a "look at" that is not "act on" (a hold, a second tap) or the fight is blind. ROUTED:
   UI [bb interface] must answer it before the fight moves onto the grid.
3. THE CLOCK ACROSS TWO SCALES. On the map time passes as you travel (days); on the street it passes on the
   beat (seconds). A day is one battery; a fight is thirty seconds. Which clock is the debt on? ROUTED:
   ECONOMY Q53 [two clocks]: the day on the map and the beat on the street, one ledger.
4. THE ASSET CLIFF. Everything drawn at 112 px is now source, and the one-cell sprite pipeline does not exist
   yet; the last time the body changed size the street had nobody with a face for two weeks. ROUTED: CHARACTER
   [small body] + ANIMATION [small clips] are first lines; DIRECTION judges the first small sprite before a
   second is drawn.
5. NOTHING TO HAND A FRIEND FOR WEEKS. The demo is a revamp in progress and the rule is "the first sixty
   seconds are a game"; a friend given the link this month plays a construction site. ROUTED: RUN cuts nothing
   until the grid walks; the old demo link stays as it is; nobody sends it.
6. FIVE REFERENCES PULLING. Battle Brothers (map, company), Rogue Fable 4 (fight), FFX and Fallout 1
   (interface), analog horror and Newgrounds (look), Ocarina (the flip). Each is inside its department, and
   the bible is the referee, but a phone screen is small and five voices on one screen is the "overwhelmed"
   complaint by another road. ROUTED: DIRECTION's [two scales look] card decides which reference is allowed
   to be visible on which scale.
7. SAVING THREE ACTS. One save, one seed, three slots (DYNASTY school round one) is a design, not a file yet;
   the treasuries did not save until this week. ROUTED: WORLD [act stamp] is first; PLUMBER [grid budget]
   measures the save.
8. DECLINING IS FREE COLLIDES WITH A WORLD THAT REMEMBERS. Our people remember everything (rumours, standing,
   the creditor). Battle Brothers docks relations for a refused contract. His rule wins: refusing costs
   nothing and nobody talks about it; only a contract TAKEN and dropped is a deed. ROUTED: PEOPLE [weights
   shape] and QUESTS question (h): what the 152 do when you say no.
