## 2. WHAT REALLY HAPPENS WHEN SOMEBODY DIES WITH NO INSTITUTIONS (recall)
- THE BODY IS WORK: washing, carrying, a place, a rite. In the Mojave ground you do not dig (caliche hardpan; the chapel module already says 'you build a wall and fill it'), so burial is a build job: stone, a niche, a day of labour for two or three.
- WHO DOES IT: family first, then the nearest people with a debt of obligation (neighbours, the congregation). The cost falls on whoever is closest and least able to refuse.
- THE DEBTS: no court; the creditor comes to the house and either forgives, takes the house, or takes a promise. The standing system already models the last one.
- THE HOUSE AND THE CHILDREN: someone has to be taken in; a house with nobody in it is taken by the nearest strong party.
- THE REST OF THE COMMUNITY WATCHES: how a death is handled is the only public ledger there is.

## 3. WHAT THE HEIR'S FIRST DAY IS MADE OF (proposal, draft:true; the best games make a funeral something you DO)
The heir arrives (the offer row from [heirs made on entry]), and the first thing the game asks for is the work, in the order the real world asks:

4. THE NAME: said once, at the wall (the legend line from Q20 is written here).
