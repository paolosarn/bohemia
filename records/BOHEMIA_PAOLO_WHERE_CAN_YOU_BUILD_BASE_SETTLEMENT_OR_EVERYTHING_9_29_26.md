## WHAT THE CANON ALREADY HOLDS
- The early framework he means: the rung system (WORLD [rung unlocks], 9/6: 'rung two grants a DOOR, not a dial:
  you can build on lit ground'), the boss ladder (60 bosses each handing a VERB), and 'things you do help you
  build different parts of the city.'
- 37e: fourteen parts of Vegas are HOME BASES, generated, yours or raided. 40b: building is a thing you do inside
  the settlement screen of a base you own. 31: the future city is derived from what you built.
- POCKET CITY 2 is a reference for the city-mode DROP-IN TRANSITION ONLY (7/1); citing it for 'edit everything'
  is outside its department. Rule 24: one game mode; a builder mode is dead.
- Battle Brothers: you never build; towns get better or worse from what you did near them.

## THE THREE SHAPES (his question; the realistic one leads and wins by default)
A. YOU BUILD WHAT YOU HOLD, AND WHAT YOU HOLD GROWS. Your base is one of the fourteen parts. You build on its lots.
   When you take the next part (a raid, a contract, a boss, a deal) it becomes yours too and you build there. A
   base is not one block: it is every block you hold, and by act 3 it can be half of Las Vegas. Bosses, contracts
   and deeds do three things: they hand you PARTS (a boss's verb opens a district), they unlock KINDS of building
   (the rungs: lit ground, water, a wall, a market), and they make the places you helped get better ON THEIR OWN
   the Battle Brothers way, without you touching a lot. Realistic (in every real collapse, rebuilding happens
   where someone holds the ground), one mode, and the derive already knows how to draw it.
B. A SETTLEMENT THAT IS BLOCKS AND BLOCKS FROM THE START: one big base, everything else is scenery. Simpler, but
   the fourteen parts and the raids stop meaning anything, and act 1 has nothing to take.
C. THE POCKET CITY 2 ROUTE: at some point you can touch and edit nearly everything. A second game (a city
   builder) inside the first, a builder mode by another name (rule 24 kills it), and nobody in a real collapse
   edits a block they do not hold.
DEFAULT: A. In VOTE.

## WHAT A MEANS ON THE BOARD
- LIFE+CITY [build a lot]: the lots you can build on are the lots of parts you HOLD; holding is FACTIONS' ledger.
- FACTIONS [home bases]: TAKING a part (raid, contract, boss verb, deal) is how the base grows; losing one is how
  it shrinks (37c, the future goes both ways).
- WORLD [rung unlocks] re-read: the rungs are KINDS of building unlocked by deeds and bosses, not places.
- The Battle Brothers half: a place you helped improves on its own (WORLD [bb places]); a place you hurt decays.
- COMBAT [sixty bosses]: a boss's verb can be a PART (the boss held it; beating him hands it to you).
