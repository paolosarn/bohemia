## MY READING (rule 70a, LOCKED)
ONE LIGHT OVER THE WHOLE MAP. The land past the map's edge and the city inside it are lit by the same clock, the same sun
angle, the same night, the same shadows, the same colour of the hour; the land is never a separately shaded picture laid
under a city shaded another way. The valley-edge picture is baked unlit and lit live by the city's own light pass (or the
city's light pass is baked into it at the same hour), so a sunset is one sunset edge to edge; a gate samples a city block and
the land beside it at four hours and fails when their lighting diverges. RUN [the valley edge] round two.
