'Black and Gold Armor', a 'Golden Feathered Helmet' or 'Feathered Nasal Helmet'; the orc warrior's in 'Looted Plate Armor' and a

THE FINDING THAT PROVED US WRONG: the plan said 'reads as the boss at fight size' and I first reached for size. Measured: the rig sets
every man's height (real_height_for_rig 1.72 to 1.79 m whatever the height dial: the kids' runners at height -0.85 are 1.752 m), and
THE GROUND MAY ZOOM, THE PERSON MAY NOT forbids scaling him. So he reads by colour and shape alone, as Battle Brothers' does.

## 7. THE METHOD (rule 495)
One owning module each: tools/bohemia_character_four_the_champions.py adds three types IN MEMORY to the nine enemies' pipe
(tools/bohemia_character_the_nine_enemies.py: the barber's roll, the body build, RUN TWO's render on DIRECTION's master rig, COMBAT's
fight poses, its pack) with its own roll (records/character/BOHEMIA_THE_CHAMPIONS_ROLL.json); the new cloths are copies of existing
cuts in a new colour registered in memory (as the nine add the hi-vis vest); the heavies' frame and bar are the three enemies' own
pieces and the powered arm CHARACTER 3's, called through the same pieces' door. Changes at the root, in the owners: the nine's pack
takes its strip, its set label, its colours and keep (no copy of the pack); the welder's mask takes up or down in its one builder;
the powered arm's door takes either forearm; the gambler hat takes its plume. The data in its files: ours.json people_looks.band
champion_road, champion_kids, champion_heavy and bestiary.look_pools pointing the three kinds at them (no fight code changed).
    python3 tools/bohemia_character_four_the_champions.py roll | model | look | make | pack | data | card
