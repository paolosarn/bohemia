## WHAT IS THERE NOW (coordinator, measured on main)
The meet card (MEET BEFORE YOU FIGHT, rule 151(e); slices/BOHEMIA_CITY_WORLD.html) offers two buttons: ENGAGE and FALL BACK (two
blocks away). Nothing tells him at camp where danger is.

## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
Meeting an enemy on the map gives a dialogue to engage or not; a stationary camp or location waits for you, so a player often camps
beside it to heal and repair first. Nothing on the screen points to danger while camped; you watch the map.

## THE RULING (rule 269)
(a) A THIRD CHOICE BEFORE A STILL ENEMY: when the enemy does not roam (a camp, a lair, a job's fixed target), the meet card gives
    ENGAGE, FALL BACK, and MAKE CAMP NEARBY (the words his to better; draft:true): the company pulls back a block and makes camp
    there, the enemy stays where it is, he heals until he taps ENGAGE on the camp or breaks camp. A roaming enemy never offers it
    (they would find the camp).
(b) THE COMPASS OF DANGER AT CAMP: while camped, each of the eight compass directions owns a piece of the screen's edge; a party
    coming from that way lights it, brighter as it closes, its colour from the AUTO calculator against his crew as it stands (rule
    254, one scale): the deep red of a party that would beat him, a weak glow of a party he would beat; a party he would beat easily
    barely shows. It reads at a glance, with the warning of rule 267(a).
(c) HE WATCHES THEM PITCH IT: making camp plays a short scene first, a few seconds and a tap skips it (nothing forced, the second
    votes): his men pulling the camp out of the cart (the hand-hauled shopping cart first, rule 260(c)) and building it together,
    tents up, the fire lit, the gear down, scaled to what he has (a lone man and a cart, a big crew and a wagon), then the camp. It is
    a placeholder first and better each round (his words).
(d) THE GATE: on the phone and computer: a still enemy's meet card shows three choices and MAKE CAMP NEARBY makes camp a block back with
    the enemy unmoved; a roaming one's shows two; at camp a party closing from the north-east lights the north-east edge, deep red
    for a party AUTO reads as a loss and a weak glow for one it reads as a clean win; making camp plays the pitching scene, a tap
    skipping it.
