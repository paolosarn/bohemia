# PART 1 — THE LAND HE ASKED ABOUT IS REALLY THERE

Clark County holds about **949,000 housing units, 60.2% of them detached
single-family houses** — call it **571,000 detached homes**. Vegas tract lots
run ~6,000-7,000 sq ft; take off the house, garage and driveway and roughly
**3,000 sq ft per lot is convertible ground**.

    571,000 homes x 3,000 sq ft  =  1.71 billion sq ft  =  ~39,300 acres

Against the canon's 10,000 acres of parks, golf and school turf, the houses
are **four times the land the city was already counting.** He was right that
it is there.

Add the rest of what a desperate city would look at: flat commercial and
warehouse roofs, big-box floors for indoor growing, vacant inholdings, the
medians. Round the theoretical maximum to **~50,000 acres**.

At the canon's own yield band (4-9 people per acre), 50,000 acres would feed
**200,000 to 450,000 people.** If land were the constraint, the ceiling would
go up five-fold.

It is not the constraint.

---

# PART 2 — WHY THE YARDS DO NOT ACTUALLY FEED ANYBODY

## THE VALLEY FLOOR IS CALICHE, AND A LAWN IS NOT SOIL EITHER
The 10,000 acres of turf are the crown jewels for a reason the canon already
states and this research confirms: decades of irrigation, mowing, clipping
return and fertiliser built **real topsoil** under the fairways and the big
parks. A golf course is a farm that has already been made.

A xeriscaped Vegas front yard is the opposite. Under the decorative rock is
weed fabric, and under that is caliche — a cemented calcium-carbonate hardpan.
It is not poor soil. It is not soil. You cannot till it, you build ON it.

## SO SOIL HAS TO BE MADE, AND HERE IS THE RATE
The city's own renewable soil input is biosolids from the water reclamation
plants — which is already canon ("every calorie in Bohemia has passed through
the reclaim plant twice"). Wastewater biosolids run roughly **20-30 kg of dry
solids per person per year**.

    65,000 people x 30 kg  =  ~1,950 tonnes of dry biosolids a year

Building a workable made-soil bed takes on the order of **100 tonnes of
compost per acre** (or, per household, about **1 tonne for a 400 sq ft raised
bed** — the two figures agree, which is why this number is trustworthy).

    1,950 tonnes / 100 tonnes per acre  =  ~20 NEW ACRES PER YEAR

**Twenty acres a year.** Going from 10,000 acres to 49,000 acres on the city's
own waste stream would take roughly two thousand years. The food ceiling is
effectively FIXED inside a human lifetime.

## THE ONE WAY TO CHEAT IT: THE LANDFILL
Apex is decades of Las Vegas's buried organics. It is not a flow, it is a
MINE — a one-time stock that could jump the soil budget by orders of
magnitude. It is filthy, slow, dangerous and worth fighting over. Whoever
holds Apex holds the only lever on the food ceiling that exists.

## AND WHAT THE YARDS ACTUALLY GIVE YOU
A 400 sq ft household bed of intensive vegetables is roughly **10-15% of one
person's calories** — and that is being generous. What it reliably provides is
green vegetables: vitamin C, folate, the things whose absence is scurvy and
anaemia. That is not nothing. In the real siege economies it is the difference
between surviving and surviving intact.

**A YARD KEEPS YOU FROM GETTING SCURVY. A GOLF COURSE KEEPS YOU ALIVE.**

## HYDROPONICS IS THE SAME ANSWER WITH AN EXTRA BILL
Hydroponics is superb at leafy greens and herbs and hopeless at calories — you
do not grow wheat, corn or potatoes in nutrient film at any sane cost, which
is why every commercial vertical farm on earth grows lettuce and basil. It
also needs pumps, lights and mineral nutrients, so it can only exist inside
the 12% lit clusters, competing for the same power the water pumps need.

Hydroponics in Bohemia is therefore a **luxury of the powered clusters**: fresh
green food is a marker of who owns a circuit, not a way to feed a city.

# PART 3 — WATER IS AN ELECTRICITY PROBLEM, AND HERE IS THE BILL

The canon already establishes that converting turf to crops uses LESS water
than the turf did (turf ~7 acre-feet per acre, vegetables 3-4). That still
holds. What the canon did not price is that in Las Vegas, **water has to be
lifted**.

Measured, from SNWA's own reporting: Southern Nevada used about **853.8
million kWh to move 439,187 acre-feet** of water. That is:

    1,944 kWh PER ACRE-FOOT

Now the city's actual bill:

| what | volume | energy | continuous load |
|---|---|---|---|
| irrigating 10,000 acres @ 3.5 ac-ft | 35,000 ac-ft | 68.0 GWh/yr | **7.8 MW** |
| drinking water, 65,000 people @ 50 gal/day | ~3,640 ac-ft | 7.1 GWh/yr | **0.8 MW** |
| **TOTAL** | | **~75 GWh/yr** | **~8.6 MW** |

**Call it nine megawatts, continuously, forever, or the city starves.** That
one number is the physical spine of the whole setting: it is why power is a
currency, why the clusters matter, and why a pump house is worth more than a
casino.

(Note the 50 gallons per person per day: that is a hard post-collapse figure,
roughly a third of what real Las Vegas used. Nobody is watering anything they
are not eating.)

# PART 4 — HOOVER DAM, AND WHY THE 12% IS A DISTRIBUTION STORY

The dam is thirty miles away.

- Nameplate **2,080 MW**.
- Drought-crippled: below Lake Mead elevation 1,035 ft, twelve older turbines
  that cannot run at low head shut down and only five modern units keep
  going — capacity falls from 1,302 MW to **382 MW**.
- Nevada's historical share of Hoover power is about **23%**.

Nine megawatts against a worst-case 382 MW is **2.4%**. Against Nevada's share
of that worst case, it is 10% of 88 MW. There is not a generation problem.
There was never going to be a generation problem.

**SO WHY IS THE CITY 12% LIT?** Because a grid is not a battery you plug into.
It is a synchronised machine: frequency has to be held, load has to be
balanced against generation second by second, protection relays have to be set
and coordinated, and transformers that sat dead for a decade fail when
re-energised. Generation is the easy half. **Distribution is what died.**

This is a WIN for the CLUSTERED POWER law, which now has a physical mechanism
instead of a vibe. The 12% are the places where somebody re-energised a
distribution substation and can hold an island stable. It explains, exactly,
the thing the law already asserts: the network is **eerily perfect inside its
boundary and dead one street over.** An island either works or it does not —
there is no gradient. You cannot half-energise a feeder.

It also means the dam is the single most valuable object in the world and its
value is not the electricity. It is the **right to decide which islands get
fed.**

# PART 5 — CAN IT IMPORT? YES. A LITTLE. SEASONALLY.

## THE ONE PLACE THAT CAN FARM WITH NO ELECTRICITY
**MOAPA VALLEY / LOGANDALE**, about 60 miles northeast. This is the important
one and the reason is specific: the **Muddy River is spring-fed**, warm
artesian springs feeding a river that runs downhill to the fields. Its
settlers dug over 18 miles of irrigation canals and grew cotton, grain,
alfalfa, orchards and vineyards. **Gravity does the work.** No pumps, no grid,
no 1,944 kWh per acre-foot. In a valley where every drop of Vegas water costs
electricity, Moapa is the only nearby agriculture that keeps running when the
power does not.

It has a bitter modern twist that is free canon: **much of the Moapa Valley's
water was already sold to Las Vegas** before the crash. The city bought the
water rights off the one place that could have fed it.

## THE OTHERS, ALL WORSE
- **PAHRUMP**, ~60 mi west: real farm valley, but on GROUNDWATER. Needs its
  own pumps and its own power. It can farm only as well as it can generate.
- **AMARGOSA VALLEY**, ~90 mi northwest: alfalfa and dairy, groundwater again.
- **VIRGIN RIVER (Mesquite, Bunkerville)**, ~80 mi northeast: river water,
  further out, more exposed.
- **THE REAL BREADBASKET, IMPERIAL AND YUMA**, 300+ miles southwest: where
  America's winter vegetables actually come from. That is not a trade route,
  that is an expedition, and it is act-3 scale.

## WHAT IMPORTS ACTUALLY CONTRIBUTE
Moapa's historical irrigated acreage is in the low thousands. Even farmed
hard, it feeds its own people first and trades a surplus. Honest number:
**5,000 to 10,000 people's worth of calories in a good year, ZERO in a bad
one, and only if the road is held.** Which is precisely what the CARAVAN /
CARAVANSERAI / GUARANTOR triad already exists to model. A caravan is not
flavour; it is the difference between 65,000 and 75,000 people eating.

## AND THE RAIL IS STILL SITTING THERE
The Union Pacific mainline runs Los Angeles - Las Vegas - Salt Lake City,
straight through the valley, and our own world has 90 rail cells and a railyard
doing nothing. In a fuel-starved world rail moves freight several times
further per gallon than trucks, and a gravity-fed farm valley 60 miles up a
rail line is not a coincidence, it is a route. Flagged as under-used for the
second research round running.

# PART 6 — THE NUMBER

| line | people fed |
|---|---|
| the 10,000 acres of inherited turf, fully converted | 50,000 - 80,000 |
| + every yard and every hydroponic rig in the valley | **+0 calories** (greens, vitamins, morale) |
| + soil built from biosolids, per year | +100 people/yr, and that is not a typo |
| + imports, good year, road held | +5,000 - 10,000 |
| + imports, bad year | +0 |

**WORKING NUMBER: 65,000 in the valley.**
Floor 45,000 in a bad year. Ceiling 80,000 in a good one with the caravan road
held. This confirms the 7/5 canon band rather than moving it — which is the
result you want, because it means the original grass-to-food math was right
before anybody checked it.

**IN THE WALKABLE WORLD: ~300 living bodies.** The walkable valley holds
~10,600 people of pre-collapse capacity against the real valley's ~2.3
million, so it is 1/217th of Las Vegas. 65,000 is 2.83% of pre-collapse, and
2.83% of 10,600 is 300.

Distribution per his ruling 2 (clusters + spread + no man's land) is in
laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_HOW_MANY_PEOPLE_7_29_26.md.

# WHAT THIS CHANGES

**CONFIRMED, DO NOT TOUCH:** the 10,000-acre inventory; crops using less water
than turf; the 50-80k band; golf courses as the crown jewels; biosolids
closing the loop; the caravan triad; the 12% clustered power.

**ADDED:**
1. **SOIL IS THE CEILING, AND IT IS ~20 ACRES A YEAR.** The food supply cannot
   grow meaningfully in a lifetime. The Apex landfill is the only lever.
2. **YARDS AND HYDROPONICS ARE VITAMINS, NOT CALORIES.** A yard prevents
   scurvy; a fairway prevents starvation.
3. **NINE MEGAWATTS IS THE PRICE OF EATING.** Water is an electricity problem
   because Vegas water is lifted.
4. **THE 12% IS A DISTRIBUTION STORY, NOT A GENERATION STORY.** The dam has
   forty times the power the city needs. What died was the grid, and that is
   why a lit island is perfect inside and dead outside.
5. **MOAPA VALLEY IS GRAVITY-FED AND THEREFORE THE ONLY NEARBY FARMLAND THAT
   SURVIVES THE COLLAPSE INTACT** — and Las Vegas had already bought its water.
