## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked; CAMP checks it in the wiki copy, reference/library/grok/wiki)
Hitpoints lost in a fight stay; they come back slowly with time on the map; armour stays broken until repaired, which spends tools
(our TAPE) over time; an injury lasts days; making camp (the T key) speeds the repairs and the healing while the clock runs and the
company is not moving; the Retinue's followers (a surgeon-like healer, a quartermaster, a scout who makes the party faster) are the
camp's upgrades.

## THE RULING (rule 226)
(a) DAMAGE STAYS: after every fight each man keeps the hitpoints and the armour he ended with, his injuries (the fight-gets-deep
    law: struck down, 20 percent dead, else laid up 30 to 40 days) and the dead stay dead; the next fight starts from that.
(b) HEALING TAKES TIME: hitpoints come back slowly as the clock runs, armour is repaired by spending TAPE over time, an injury heals
    in its days; the rates are Battle Brothers' from the wiki copy, in TUNING's table.
(c) THE PHONE'S CAMP APP IS THE HEAL BUTTON (amends 212(b)'s CAMP): its icon a heal mark (a cross or a heart in the Crashbox tin)
    with ONE BAR under it: the company's whole condition, every man's hitpoints and armour added into one bar (red for the blood,
    grey for the armour, the empty part showing what is missing), so one look says 'I need to heal' (rule 224: a bar, not numbers).
(d) TAP IT TO MAKE CAMP: the party stops, the clock runs faster, hitpoints and armour come back faster and the tape is spent; the bar
    fills as he watches; tap again or travel to break camp. While camped the party is still on the map: a roaming party may find
    it (Battle Brothers' risk).
(e) THE CAMP GROWS: upgrades at the camp (the followers and the wagon, rule 212's CAMP screen, Battle Brothers' Retinue): faster
    healing, faster repairs, more stash, a quicker march; the coordinator leaves what comes after camp to him ('we'll decide what
    happens after').
(f) THE GATE: on the phone and computer profiles: two fights in a row and the second starts with the first's wounds and broken
    armour; the dead stay dead; the HEAL bar equals the company's summed hitpoints and armour over their maxima; making camp fills
    it at the table's rates and spends tape; breaking camp stops it.
