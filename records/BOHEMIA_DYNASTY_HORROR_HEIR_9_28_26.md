## THE ONE LINE
**All three images are already built, and none of them needed a new prop.** The machine still
talking is the loading screen. The bill still coming is a prop that costs zero and cannot be
paid. The tape the parent left does not exist as an object, and it should not become one --
what we have instead is better and already fits the bible without a single new asset.

## 1. THE MACHINE STILL TALKING -- ALREADY SHIPPED, AND IT DOES NOT KNOW WHICH ACT YOU ARE IN
`slices/bohemia_loading_screen.js` boots every game with an ordinary institutional log --
READING THE VALLEY, COUNTING WHAT STILL STANDS, CHECKING STREETS FOR LIGHT -- and the one
wrong line, bible rule 1, is **NO OPERATOR ON DUTY**, printed calm, in the same register as
the five true ones. It is not written for act 1, act 2 or act 3. It is the world's own
plumbing, and it says the identical sentence whichever descendant is about to stand up.
**That IS the horror this row asks for, for free**: the heir does not inherit a warning
addressed to them, they inherit an institution that has been running its own checklist since
before they were born and will keep running it after. No new lore needed -- the line is his
own words already shipped (Q28, 9/23), reused, not rewritten, which rule 8 asks for.

## 2. THE BILL STILL COMING -- ECONOMY ALREADY RULED IT A PROP, AND THE FOLD AGREES
Round 48 (`BOHEMIA_ECONOMY_DAY_48...md`) measured that a bill from a dead office has **no
sender by definition**, costs zero, cannot be paid and cannot be argued with -- "the dead
institution's bill is not an upkeep line. It is not a cost. It is a PROP." And `bohemia_fold.js`
already rules `debt: {ruled:true, dies:true}`: **`whyDies()`** says it in the fold's own words,
*"a child is not personally liable for a parent's unsecured debts... YOU DO NOT INHERIT A
BILL, YOU INHERIT LESS AND YOU INHERIT THE PEOPLE HE OWED, still standing there."* Two lanes,
two rounds apart, arrived at the same shape from opposite doors: the paper dies with the
office that mailed it, and what actually crosses a generation is a person who remembers, not
a number. The bill an heir meets is not THEIR father's bill reappearing -- it is the valley's
own dead utility, still printing, addressed to no one in particular, same in every act.

## 3. THE TAPE THE PARENT LEFT -- IT DOES NOT EXIST, AND BUILDING ONE WOULD BE THE VIOLATION
Swept `engine/*.js` and `slices/*.html` for a literal recorded relic (voicemail, answering
machine, a message left, his own voice, her own voice): **zero hits that are a person's own
voice speaking to their child.** What exists instead is `bohemia_standing.js`'s **`inherit()`**
and **`legendOf()`**: a father's deeds survive only through **living witnesses who retold
them** (HEARSAY_LOSS 0.55 a hop, GEN_LOSS 0.45 a generation), and an eyewitness who is dead and
never retold is gone for good -- *"the eyewitness is dead. Only what was RETOLD is still in
the valley."* **That is a truer tape than a tape**: real spoken memory does not survive as a
perfect first-person recording, it survives as thirdhand and it decays, which Q8 (Day 8,
9/5) already measured at 80-110 years and three to four generations -- almost exactly
Bohemia's own hundred years and three acts. A literal cassette of the father's own voice would
be the ONE WRONG THING done twice: rule 2 already forbids two ONE WRONG THINGs on a screen,
and here it would be a second first-person channel competing with the loading screen's own
line, plus new lore (who recorded it, why, what it says) that is his to confirm, not mine to
invent. **Refused on purpose, same as section 8 withdraws "does the mystery get solved."**
