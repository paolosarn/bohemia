## THE RESEARCH (coordinator, 10/25; web search, sources below; realism first, rule 4)
What share of people who fight for pay or for a crew are women, in the real world:
- PARAMILITARIES, the closest thing to a mercenary company: Colombia's AUC about 2 percent women (2000 to 2003 figures, compiled
  in a Leiden thesis from other sources; unreviewed).
- STREET GANGS, law enforcement counts (National Gang Center, 1998 to 2010): 6 to 8 percent women; the 1996 youth gang survey
  10 percent; self-report surveys of youth 8 to 38 percent.
- THE US ARMY: about 16 percent of the whole Army (2023); in infantry and armor about 2 percent in the years after the 2016
  opening (NGAUS; 601 enlisted women in infantry in 2020), a compilation claims 9.7 percent by 2023 (unverified, does not fit
  the counts).
- PRIVATE MILITARY AND SECURITY COMPANIES: no firm armed-role number; women are a fifth to a quarter of the whole industry
  (mostly unarmed: guards at gates, search, customer service, intelligence), and few in armed roles (ICoCA; no figure found).
- REBELS WITH A CAUSE: FARC 30 to 40 percent women (Colombian government's estimate), the ELN about 20 percent.
THE READING: people who fight for money run about 2 to 8 percent women; people who fight for a cause run far higher (20 to 40).
His 1 percent sits under the real floor for a crew that hires for pay; 3 percent is the honest middle for a hiring post.
- ADDED 10/25, A WAR NOW (Ukraine): women about 8 percent of the whole army (about 68,000 to 70,000), but 13,487 with combat
  status in March 2024 (Military Media Centre) of about 880,000: about 1.5 percent of the army are women who fight; about 5,000
  in the combat zones. THE READING CORRECTED: among people who actually fight (paramilitaries 2, a modern army at war about
  1.5, front-line infantry about 2), about 2 percent; gangs run higher because most of a gang is not fighting. The realistic
  number for a hiring post is 2 percent; his 1 percent is close to it.
BATTLE BROTHERS (its department, the company): the base game hires men only; a popular community mod adds women as an option.

## THE RULING (rule 203)
(a) WOMEN CAN BE HIRED: a share of the men at the post are women, each a whole person (rule: ONE ID, ONE WHOLE PERSON): her own
    body on the rig, her own face, her own voice, her own clothes on the runway (the runway law), never a man's sprite renamed.
(b) THE SHARE IS ONE NUMBER in TUNING's table, his to set. UNTIL HE PICKS IT PLAYS AT HIS WORD: 1 percent at the post (NOTES ARE
    RULINGS). The realistic reading (3 percent at a hiring post, more inside a cause) is put to him once.
(c) She is rolled the same way as any hire (her background, her stars, her traits, her price); nothing about her numbers
    differs by being a woman; the game never comments on it.
(d) THE GATE: in 10,000 rolled hires the share matches the table's number within its noise; every woman rolled has a woman's
    body, face and voice on the post, the crew screen and the fight.
(e) Rows: RUN TWO [a few women at the post] (the roll, the whole person on its screens); NOTEs to ANIMATION (her body on the
    rig), PORTRAIT (her face, matched to her body), COOK (her clothes and gear on her body), SOUNDS (her voice lines in the
    fight's barks), TUNING (the number in the table); line 124 on his list.
