================================================================================
THE LAW WAS TRUE BY ACCIDENT, WHICH IS THE WORST WAY FOR A LAW TO HOLD
================================================================================
Measured before a line was written, in the two files that ARE the game, with
comments stripped:
  a company roster        none
  companion state         none
  a downed state          none
  an injury model         none
  anything that can kill  nothing, anywhere, that can kill a person you keep

So nobody you keep dies today -- not because the promise was built, but because
there is nobody to lose. A law that holds for that reason is not enforced, it is
merely undisturbed. The first system that can hurt a companion breaks it silently
and no check says a word. That is what this round fixes: the promise, built, and
a check that would notice.

================================================================================
EVERY NUMBER IS HIS OWN EXAMPLE OR A CALENDAR FACT
================================================================================
He named two lengths himself: "a leg that takes a SEASON, a hand that takes a
YEAR of the valley's time". A season is about ninety days and a year is three
hundred and sixty-five; those are facts, not dials.
The third is a WEEK. His sentence is "debuffs that are a lot longer than others",
and "a lot longer" needs a bottom rung to mean anything against. A week is the
shortest thing a person calls an injury rather than a bad day.
AND THE LADDER REALLY MEANS IT: the longest is fifty-two times the shortest.
All three are days on the clock the city already spends, so nobody invents a
second calendar.

================================================================================
*** THE ONE THING THE MODULE CANNOT DO IS THE POINT ***
================================================================================
There is no way to make a kept person dead. No flag, no option, no length that
runs forever. fall() has exactly one outcome, takes no caller-supplied length,
and cannot be passed permanent:true because the parameter does not exist.

A LAW ENFORCED BY A MODULE THAT COULD BREAK IT IS A LAW WAITING FOR THE NEXT
CALLER IN A HURRY. So the only honest shape is one where the bad state is
unreachable, and the gate proves it BY ATTEMPTING THE FORBIDDEN THING rather than
by reading a comment that says it cannot happen:
  a fourth argument that might be a length     refused
  an options object asking for permanence      refused
  an invented kind called "forever"            refused
  fall() arity                                 3, no slot to smuggle one through
  the word Infinity in the module's code       absent
  a dead/died/corpse flag on a kept person     absent

AND BEING DOWN IS A QUESTION AGAINST THE CLOCK, NOT A STATE ANYBODY MUST
REMEMBER TO CLEAR. Nothing ticks it and nothing clears it: "are they down" is the
game day against a number written when they fell. A save that sat on a shelf for
a year comes back with everybody healed and nothing to tidy up. 5000 falls across
every kind and every day: 0 that did not heal, 0 that ran forever.

Which injury it is comes off the seed the same way the heir does, so a reload
cannot change how badly somebody was hurt.

================================================================================
THE SEAM IS ONE LINE FOR COMBAT
================================================================================
COMBAT's [downed body] row owns the body on the floor: out of the fight, reached,
carried, never a corpse. ctFall(id) is what that call lands in, and it is
deliberately the ONLY door -- a second way in is how two rules about the same
thing start to disagree.

WHO CAN FALL TODAY is the people you KEEP: the one you married and the children
you had, because the family tree is the only roster of kept people the played
game has. The companion joins the same seam the round COMBAT ships the downed
body, and nothing here changes for it. That is why it is a seam and not a
special case.

TWO THINGS I GOT WRONG, BOTH CAUGHT BY DRIVING IT
================================================================================
1. THE FIRST HEALING CHECK LIED, AND THE BUG WAS IN MY PROBE, NOT THE CODE. I set
   T.day and called daySync(), which copies the day DOWN from the day loop and
   quietly undid the jump -- so "they come back" read false while the code was
   right. THE CLOCK LIVES ON DAY, NOT ON T. Worth knowing before concluding a
   feature is broken: check the instrument first.
2. AND THREE EXPORTS NOTHING CALLED, in a brand new module, for the second time
   in this lane. healed() is exactly !isDown, downNow() re-walked a list the city
   already filters by family, and hurtOf() answered a question no surface asked.
   THEY COME BACK IS NOT A FUNCTION, IT IS THE ARITHMETIC: `until` is always
   `from` plus a finite length, so isDown goes false on its own. Cut, and
   bohemia_down registered in the organ sweep so it cannot happen quietly again.
   The sweep now reads BohemiaDown 4 fns, dead 0.
