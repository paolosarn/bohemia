## THE ANSWER, MEASURED
Because the zoomed-out city has never been made as ART. It is the builder's diagram.
- The alpha's city mode is drawn with 19 filled rectangles against 9 image draws:
  coloured slabs (brown, grey, blue) with green and yellow edge lines for the
  streets and the borders. It reads as a block chart, because it is one.
- His own lock, 7/1 (THE TWO-SCALE CAMERA, LOCKED): "ZOOMED OUT -- the living city
  view. NOT chibi. SAME PIXEL STYLE but reading as a REALISTIC CITY FROM ABOVE."
  Modeled on Pocket City 2 for the drop-in only.
- Nobody owns the picture. LIFE+CITY owns the builder logic and draws the diagram
  it needs; COOK cooks districts at street scale; DIRECTION has judged the first
  screen and the hostiles, never the aerial. The one view that the feed, the
  factions, the borders and the travel map all live on has no artist.
So it looks like this because the same slabs have been standing in for a city since
the builder was first wired, and every ship since has been logic on top of a
placeholder. That is the fourth locked ruling this week the build never read.

## WHAT IT HAS TO BECOME (his lock, made specific)
- The same pixel tiles the street is made of, drawn at the coarse scale: real roofs,
  real roads, real lots, the runway-dressed crowd as dots that move, the burned car
  as a burned car. One valley at two distances, not a map and a game.
- Value first, colour second (COLOUR IS TERRITORY): the faction colour rides the
  borders and the marked walls, not the whole slab.
- It meets the street at one exact scale (his 9/7 zoom ruling), so a house is the
  same house on both sides of the switch. That means the coarse tiles are derived
  from the fine ones, never a second hand-drawn set that drifts (NOTHING IS BAKED
  ONCE).
- The diagram stays as a LAYER a builder can turn on (the skeleton, the plots,
  "protected"), off by default. The player sees a city; the mayor toggles the chart.
