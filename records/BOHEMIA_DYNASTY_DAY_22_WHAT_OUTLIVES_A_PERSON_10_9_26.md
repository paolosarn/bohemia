## 0. THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG, MEASURED ON THE REPO
bohemia_standing.inherit() is the machine that carries a deed across a generation, and it IS in the walked city (called twice). I ran it twice on ten witnesses, three of whom saw a deed first-hand and seven who only heard it retold. Measured: **generation one to two: 7 carried, 3 died (the three eyewitnesses). Generation two to three: 7 carried, 0 died.** The weight fades (GEN_LOSS 0.45 per fold: 0.45, then 0.20) but **not one retold deed is ever forgotten**. A deed fades to a fifth of its weight and then stays on the books for ever. Real memory does not work like that, and the game's own comment says it wants it to ('by the third generation only the very loudest thing your grandfather did still registers at all'): a count of 7 is not 'only the loudest'.

## 1. WHAT REALLY OUTLIVES A PERSON WHERE NOTHING IS WRITTEN (recall)
- THE FLOATING GAP (Vansina, oral tradition in Africa): a community remembers the founders and the last two or three generations well, and the middle is a gap that slides forward as time passes. What is kept is the start and the near past.
- NAMES OUTLAST STORIES: the name and one kind of thing the person was ('the one who brought water') last; the details drift or are given to a better-known figure (telescoping, many deeds merged into one).
- DEBTS AND GRUDGES outlast praise because somebody still holds them (a family feud runs for generations; a favour is forgotten when the person owed it is dead).
- PLACES HOLD MEMORY: a name on a place (a well, a wash) is the most durable record of all.
- WHAT A GAME CAN LEARN: Battle Brothers' own legacy is a name and a ledger (renown and house relations outlive a company: our [bb legacy] record). Memory feels real when the player hears a story about his grandfather that is a little wrong.

## 2. WHAT THE VALLEY SHOULD STILL KNOW IN GENERATION THREE (proposal, draft:true)
1. THE NAME, always (a person who did anything notable).
2. ONE LEGEND LINE, not a ledger: the loudest deed, merged and slightly wrong (the 'telescoped' version), from the top deed by weight at the fold. Everything else lapses.
3. THE DEBTS AND GRUDGES held by someone still alive (they already survive; stood counts).
4. A PLACE NAME if the family built or took something there.
5. NOTHING ELSE. The three eyewitnesses die with the generation (already built).
Mechanically a small change to inherit(): after the second hop, keep only the loudest K deeds per person (default K=1) and mark the carried line as 'told', and an unrecorded witness list cannot rebuild it. This is also the ending's gauge: the machine's records keep ALL seven deeds exactly; the valley's memory keeps one, a little wrong. The difference between the two is the blind spot.
