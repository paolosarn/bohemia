THE FAR END'S GROUND, RE-CUT FROM HIS OWN TERRAIN PICKS -- MEASURED
(COOK [the pack is the twin] part 3, 10/10/26; rules 82a, 87, 89)
==============================================================================

THE ROW, part (3): "the far end's 16 px tiles from the terrain picks where they exist."

*** AND THE PICTURE SAID IT BEFORE ANY NUMBER DID. *** His approved terrain is gravel
with stones in it, dirt with grit, grass tufts, cracked rock. The far-end basin tile
this lane shipped is A FLAT TAN SQUARE: fifty near-identical tans over 256 pixels, a
smooth wash with no grain at all. Laid across a 96 by 96 valley, that is what makes the
map read as paper.

THE ONE DECISION OF THIS ROUND, AND IT IS ONE LINE OF CODE. A far-end cell is 16 px and
his master is 96. Shrink his tile the ordinary way and the MEAN THROWS THE GRAIN AWAY --
averaging gravel gives you back exactly the flat tan square we already had. So every one
of the 256 pixels is TAKEN from his tile, nearest neighbour, no filter, at a stride that
walks the whole master. His palette and his speckle arrive at map size intact.

MEASURED, ON THE SAME 48 CELLS BEFORE AND AFTER:
  the spread of light inside a tile      4.93  ->   21.63
  the jump from one pixel to the next    3.42  ->   22.27
  colours in one 16 px tile                74  ->     242

NOT ONE KEY MOVED. A far-end tile is stamped by its self and its four edge types, and
those are this lane's own layout. Only the pixels changed, so the map needs no change at
all to show his ground instead of ours.

WHAT WAS LEFT ALONE, AND WHY IT IS NAMED INSTEAD OF FAKED: road, spine and water. His
terrain picks hold no road tile and no water tile, so there is nothing of his to cut them
from. Cooking a substitute for an asset that is not in the index is the 7/27 shopping
law's own violation; inventing one and calling it his would be worse. They stay this
lane's paint and the bank says so on every one of them.

*** AND THE CUT BEFORE THIS ONE CAME OUT A CHESSBOARD. *** It walked his whole desert
pool cell by cell, and his nine picks run from near-black burnt rock to pale open sand,
so neighbours flipped light-dark-light and the far end read as a checked tablecloth. A
basin cell should look like a basin cell; the difference between neighbours belongs in
WHERE IN HIS TILE the cell was taken from, which is rule 82a's own word, "placed,
flipped", and not in which tile it came from.

RULE 89 IS THE SHIP TEST HERE, NOT A FOOTNOTE. The sheet is the same 24 by 8 cells of
the valley before and after, side by side, at one art pixel to one phone pixel, nothing
scaled, and the numbers sit UNDER it as the proof line. If he cannot see it in that
picture it did not ship, whatever the numbers say.
