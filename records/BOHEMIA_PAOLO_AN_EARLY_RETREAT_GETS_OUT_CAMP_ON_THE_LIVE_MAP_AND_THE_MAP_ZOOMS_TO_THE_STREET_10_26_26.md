## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; rules.json where it has it, else recall, marked)
- Deployment: each side's line starts at its own edge with a gap between the fronts (rules.json
  deployment.min_gap_between_lines_hexes, the wiki's five tiles); a retreat sends men on the edge out at once and the rest run for
  it (deployment.retreat_from_map_edge). Ordered on the first turn, the head start nearly always gets everyone out.
- Camp: the company makes camp ON the world map; time runs, the map stays live, parties keep moving, and a roaming party may find
  the camp (recall).
- The world map zooms within one view, no screen change (recall).

## THE RULINGS
256. AN EARLY RETREAT GETS OUT
(a) A retreat ordered in the first round (his first turn) gets his men out nearly always: the head start is real. The deployment
    keeps Battle Brothers' gap; an enemy that moves farther than a man in a turn (the beasts: dogs, wolves, the fast ones) starts
    farther back by its extra reach, so its first turn cannot close a gap a man's first turn opens.
(b) The board's edge behind his line is near: his line deploys within a turn's run of his own edge.
(c) Later in the fight a retreat stays the chase it is (rule 198); a man surrounded or ambushed may still be caught.
(d) After a retreat: the job is his to drop (the X, rule 227) or keep; he runs and heals at camp (rule 226).
(e) THE GATE: 1,000 rolled fights of every enemy kind, the beasts among them, retreat ordered on his first turn: 95 in 100 or more
    of his men get out, and every man who started within a turn of his edge gets out; no fast enemy reaches a man on its first turn.
(f) COMBAT [an early retreat gets out] after [retreat from the lineup]; TUNING NOTE (the fast kinds' start); line 183.

257. CAMP ON THE LIVE MAP, THE CITY GOES ON
(a) Making camp (the phone's HEAL, rule 226(d)) happens ON THE MAP: the party's marker becomes the camp (a fire, the tents, the
    wagon when he has it) where it stands; the clock runs faster; the map stays live: roaming parties move, the feed scrolls on the
    phone, events and the news happen, the jobs' clocks run; he can pan and zoom the whole city while his men heal.
(b) The camp's own things (the HEAL bar, the followers, the wagon, the stash) live in the phone's CAMP app, small, never a screen
    that hides the map (rule 238: all buttons on the phone, the phone one size).
(c) A roaming party that finds the camp is seen coming on the map, and an event asks him (2 or 3 choices, a face) before anything
    happens.
(d) THE GATE: make camp on the phone and computer: the map stays on screen and live (a roaming party moves, the clock runs faster,
    a feed post arrives); the camp is drawn at the party's spot; the camp's things are in the phone app; breaking camp or a travel
    tap ends it.
(e) CAMP [camp on the live map] (its camp and its heal); NOTEs UI TWO (the CAMP app), RUN TWO (its camp screen folds into the
    app), RUN (the camp drawn on the map); line 184.

258. THE MAP ZOOMS IN TO THE STREET, ONE PINCH, NO NEW SCREEN
(a) THE CLOSE END GOES MUCH CLOSER: on the main map, with PLAY running or paused, one continuous pinch goes from the whole city on
    one screen (rule POCKET CITY 2's zoom) down to the street: close enough to watch his men walk through the city in detail,
    almost the fight's own look.
(b) AT THE CLOSE END THE GROUND IS THE FIGHT'S GROUND: the street, the houses and the props are drawn from the fight board's own
    house tiles and assets for that part of the city (reuse first), and his men are his real men in their real gear (the crew
    figure's sheets, rule 177) walking at their true size (THE GROUND MAY ZOOM, THE PERSON MAY NOT); other parties the same.
(c) NO NEW SCREEN, NO PHASE: it is the same map at every zoom; pinch back out and the city returns; the phone, the bar and PLAY stay
    where they are (rules 213, 238). It is watching, not the dead exploration walk (9/28): he still moves by tapping a place.
(d) IT HOLDS UP CLOSE: the map's detail must look good at every zoom, the close end above all; his words: the map is very far from
    final. Inside the phone's memory and frame line (rule 173): the close ground streams in only around the party.
(e) THE GATE: on the phone and computer: the closest stop shows his men at their fight size walking a street made of the fight's
    tiles; one pinch from the closest stop to the whole city and back with no screen change and the frame time inside the line;
    memory inside the line at the closest stop after a two-minute walk.
(f) RUN [the map zooms in to the street] (the zoom and the painting); COOK NOTE (the close ground from the fight board's tiles, what
