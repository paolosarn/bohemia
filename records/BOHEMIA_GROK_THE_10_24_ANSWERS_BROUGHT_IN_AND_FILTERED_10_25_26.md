   the target's defence. **The wiki copy (Combat Mechanics, Surrounding) supports ASK_45**: "a bonus of +5% melee hit

5. **Followers in a fight.** ASK_109 says followers "can join certain contracts". The wiki copy's Retinue page: "non-combat
