## 492(d) HOW IT WAS DONE, FIRST
- BATTLE BROTHERS: a town on the world map says what it has on the map itself: its size by its picture, its important buildings
  shown on it and listed in its tooltip (every specialised building in its tooltip since an update; once visited before that), its
  attached locations around it (farms, mines, a lumber camp: what it makes, which sets what it sells); roads are the fastest way and
  not always the safest. Nothing is a page of numbers you read somewhere else. Sources: [Steam discussion on settlement tooltips](https://steamcommunity.com/app/365360/discussions/0/412448792370167331),
  [Dev Blog #56, attached locations](https://www.moddb.com/news/adding-attached-locations-to-the-worldmap), [loading screen tips](https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Loading_Screen_Tips),
  [Dev Blog #55, region labels and exploration](https://www.moddb.com/news/adding-region-labels-and-an-explorable-worldmap).
- STARDEW VALLEY (rules 133, 343): the place is chosen once, on a picture of it, before the game; after, it is yours and it changes
  because of you; the game shows you one thing at a time.
- VALHEIM (rule 243): the map fills in where you have been, and your own pins on it say what you found there.
- PHONE DESIGN: one primary action per screen, under the thumb (44 points or more), in the bottom third upright and at the side
  sideways; a picture over a word, a word over a number.
- THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG: every one of Battle Brothers' answers is ON THE MAP. Ours is on a 100-pixel phone page in words
  and squares, and the map, which already knows the water, the power, the road and the danger of every block, shows nothing.

## THE PLAN (for the next HOME BASE; each fix a gate leg by a real tap, both ways; out of WORLD 16's road and route code)
A. THE SPOTS ON THE MAP (492(b)): when PLANT is pressed, the map itself shows the plantable blocks near him, each with a mark that is
   stronger where the spot is better (BohemiaHome.grade's score, from homeProbe's real water, power, road, danger; add scrap and food
   from the blocks' own districts, whose streets from the turf, how often raiders pass from the parties' tracks, the distance to the
   nearest town and job); a tap on one shows what it gives and costs in pictures (a tap, a bolt, a road, a skull, a house, a job), and
   the crew's best man (the highest level, his face) says which he would pick and why, one line, draft:true.
B. ONE NEXT STEP, BY A FACE, AT EVERY MOMENT (492(a)): the best man's face says the one thing to do now at 2, 6, 8, 9 and 12 above
   ('There. Water and a road. Walk us there.' / 'This is it. Plant it.' / 'Ours now. Go in, and put something up.' / 'Tap an empty lot.'
   / 'Home is that way.'), never a pop-up: in the map's own speech line (where DESI speaks now), each line said once.
C. THE BUTTON UNDER THE THUMB: the flow's buttons on the map's glass, 44 points or more, bottom third upright, the phone's side sideways;
   the in-game phone keeps its small page as the record (the bill, who lives there).
D. ONE NUMBER FOR A TRIP (5): the spot's distance said the way the trip will be (the road's blocks and hours), not a straight line.
E. ARRIVED AT THE SPOT (6): PLANT HERE on the glass where he stands when he arrives at the spot he picked; 'Are you sure?' as now.
F. AFTER PLANTING (8, 9): he goes in by one tap on his place (the map's own door); inside, the first time, the face says 'tap an empty
   lot' and the empty lots breathe once (the MOVE brackets of BUILD 11/27za).
G. THE FIRST BUILD (10, 11): the desk the first time shows three (the cheapest food, water, a wall) and MORE; the sheet steps aside so
   he sees it go up.
H. THE WAY BACK (12): YOUR PLACE stays in the band once planted (a tap walks him home).
