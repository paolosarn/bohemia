## THE RESEARCH (coordinator, 10/26; sources below)
- HOW PEOPLE HOLD A PHONE (Steven Hoober, about 1,300 people observed, 2013): one hand 49 percent, cradled 36, two thumbs 15;
  thumbs do about three quarters of the taps; the thumb's base anchors in a bottom corner (right-handed about two in three), and
  only about a third of the screen is effortless to reach. So: the buttons that matter go in one bottom corner, few and big, and
  the player picks the corner.
- BATTLE BROTHERS' WORLD MAP (its keys, a Blazing Deserts controls guide; Dev Blogs 127 and 128): few buttons: T makes and breaks
  CAMP, C or I the company and inventory, R factions and relations, Escape closes; the Retinue is a CAMPFIRE where five followers
  sit and a donkey is upgraded to a cart, a wagon, then a bigger wagon, each step raising the stash (players report 99 to 126 to
  152 slots). That is the 'increase your storage capacity' he means.
- THE FEED (laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_THE_FEED_ON_THE_CITY_SCREEN_9_4_26.md, his 9/4 law): the phone scrolls posts of what you did,
  what the world did and the city's life; reading it teaches the world.

## THE RULING (rule 212)
(a) WHERE IT LIVES: on the map only (rule 211, the second votes), in a bottom corner, LEFT or RIGHT by a setting (his hand), and
    it follows the screen when he turns the phone or iPad sideways (portrait and landscape both). On the computer, a corner too,
    the same setting. It never covers another press (211(b)).
(b) FEW BUTTONS, THE ONES THAT MATTER, AS APPS (icons people already understand, the Crashbox tin look of rule 130, never a pile
    of words). The home screen [COORDINATOR'S DEFAULT for the set, rule 205, his to change; the research above]:
    1. PLAY / PAUSE: one button, the big one, time on the map (Battle Brothers' pause); the speed he last chose is kept as a small
       mark on it and a hold changes it [COORDINATOR'S DEFAULT].
    2. CREW: looks like a social app (a friends or people icon), the one he presses most: opens the company screen (rule 172).
    3. CAMP: Battle Brothers' camp and campfire: make camp and break it (rest, repair, heal), the followers, and the wagon (the
       donkey to a cart to a wagon: more stash), the camp RUN TWO built (rule 151) moved here.
    4. FEED: the social app: his company's own page at the top (its name, tap to rename it right there; its posts: what you did)
       and the city's posts (what the world did, the city's life) scrolling at a glance (the 9/4 feed law).
    5. RELATIONS: Battle Brothers' R: who likes you, who hates you, and why (the factions).
    No more than these five; a sixth only when a lane shows it is pressed often.
(c) THE PHONE'S SCREEN SHOWS WHERE YOU ARE: behind the apps (its wallpaper) the block or street he stands on, live, and at camp
    the camp; the feed's newest post slides over it at a glance.
(d) THE TWO JOBS ARE OFF THE PHONE (rule 148(a) is DEAD by his words 'I also give up on the phone displaying the top two jobs'):
    jobs live on the town's board (197) and on the map's job markers.
(e) HE CAN CHANGE IT AFTER: a settings page lets him move, hide or reorder the phone's apps (his 'they can always choose the UI
    after'), and the corner.
(f) THE GATE: phone and computer, portrait and landscape: the phone sits in the chosen corner and moves when the setting flips;
    five apps at most, each opening its screen in one tap; PLAY/PAUSE stops and starts the clock; the company renames from the
    FEED; no jobs on the phone; nothing covered; the wallpaper is the block he stands on.
(g) UI TWO [the phone overhaul] (opened for it, the phone alone; UI keeps the kit and the bar); NOTEs to RUN (the map's speed pad
    and the old phone come out where the phone takes over), RUN TWO (the camp's screen moves under CAMP; the company name), WORLD
