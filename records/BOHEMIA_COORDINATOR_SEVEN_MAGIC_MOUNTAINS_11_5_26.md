## THE REAL PIECE
Ugo Rondinone, 2016: seven stacks of 33 locally quarried limestone boulders, three to six a stack, about 25 to 35 feet tall, painted
day-glo, beside Las Vegas Boulevard between Sloan and Jean near Jean Dry Lake, about 35.839 N, 115.271 W (sources in
records/target/bb/landmarks.json: Clark County, the Nevada Museum of Art, Travel Nevada, a visitor guide's GPS).
