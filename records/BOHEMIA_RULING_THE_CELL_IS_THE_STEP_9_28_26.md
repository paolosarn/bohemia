## THE RULING: ONE CELL = ONE STEP = 0.75 m
Five reasons, the first one decides it alone:
1. THE HONEST GRID CANNOT BE HONEST AT 3 m. Law s2: "what is drawn on a cell
   IS what the cell is." At 3 m a wall cell is three metres thick, a door is a
   garage-width hole, the mound is a 3 m hill and the person fills a 3 m
   square - the drawing must lie about every one of them. At 0.75 m a wall, a
   door, a person, a mound and a prop are each one cell AS DRAWN. Only this
   scale lets the drawing and the grid agree cell for cell.
2. HIS OWN SENTENCE: "move one grid at a time, that we had originally" - a
   step. A 3 m cell is four real steps per press.
3. HIS OWN ART: the 7/28 bank is drawn at 0.75 m (its own method line), and
   his car is 4x2 cells at it. At 0.75 the bank CUTS straight across with
   every colour kept (COOK measured); at 3 m every approved tile is redrawn
   four times smaller (REUSE-FIRST; "nothing drawn is thrown away", 34c).
4. HIS FIGHT REFERENCE: Rogue Fable 4's grid is a person per tile, walls and
   doors a tile each. Rule 33d: RF4 leads the fight.
5. "A HOUSE HAS WALLS, A DOOR AND AN INSIDE" (34, s1): a 4x4-cell house at
   3 m has a 1.5 m room inside; at 0.75 m a real suburb house (~15x17 cells,
   COOK's measure off the dossier's 14x8 m body) has rooms.
