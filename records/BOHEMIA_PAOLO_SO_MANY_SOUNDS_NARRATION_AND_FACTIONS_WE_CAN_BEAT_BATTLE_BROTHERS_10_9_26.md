## WHAT IT MEANS (rule 80, with a, b, c)
80. BETTER THAN BATTLE BROTHERS is a standing list (records/BOHEMIA_BETTER_THAN_BATTLE_BROTHERS_10_9_26.md): every lane names, in its domain, one thing Battle Brothers does badly or thinly and our fix, and keeps a [better than bb] line on its board the way it keeps a [bb ...] school line. Recreate with our swag (9/29) was the floor; this is the ceiling. His first three:
80a. THE SOUNDSCAPE. Battle Brothers has a sound for everything: the town's ambience, the smith's hammer, the tavern's murmur, the market, the road, the forest, each screen and each building; we have a handful. Grok ask 23 catalogues every sound and when it plays (the wiki, the dev blogs, the game's own file list players have posted), and SOUNDS builds our soundscape from real material, screen by screen, building by building, until a stranger hears a place before he reads it. SOUNDS [the soundscape] FIRST.
80b. NARRATION ON DEMAND. The text on screen is cool; it would be cooler read aloud when he wants it. Every player-facing text (the recap, the keepers' lines, the board's contracts, the events, the end screen) can be read by the machine voice (rule 45: the phone's speech engine, even and dead, a machine not a person) with ONE toggle in SETTINGS, off by default, a tap stops it; people keep their portrait mouths. SOUNDS [the narrator] widened; UI the toggle.
80c. A HOUSE YOU GIVE A FUCK ABOUT. In Battle Brothers no faction or noble house earns a feeling. Ours do: each faction has a face you meet more than once, a voice, a want, a memory of what you did (FACTIONS [beef]), a place that is theirs (the home base), people who recur at the posts and the board, and a reason to be hated or loved by the valley; the story lanes (DYNASTY, QUESTS research, WORDS) feed it. FACTIONS [a house you give a fuck about] FIRST; Grok ask 24 (why Battle Brothers' houses feel flat, from player writing, and what the quest library's best factions do), no new reference game.
