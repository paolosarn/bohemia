BOHEMIA -- WHEN DOES A PERSON STOP BEING SOMEBODY?
CHARACTER lane, 8/19/26. Measured on the rungs the city actually draws.

  The city blits a person at exactly one of four sizes, 1:1, never in between:
      C >= 64 -> 224     C >= 32 -> 112     C < 17 -> 28     otherwise 56
  Default walk zoom is the 112 rung, which is where every claim I have made
  about telling people apart was measured. These are the two rungs below it.

   rung   body   closest pair    mean   who is closest
   56px   51px         0.0150   0.089   Blues/Remnants, Cartel/Volunteers, Trades/Remnants
   28px   25px         0.0144   0.087   Caravans/Trades, Church/Mob, Church/Network

  FOR SCALE: the gap that actually failed -- two city residents reading as one
  person -- was 0.014, and gates/faction_outfit_gate.js holds 0.035 at 112.

  *** IDENTITY DOES NOT SURVIVE THE 56 AND 28 RUNGS. ***
  At that size two factions are closer together than the pin the build holds
  at 112, which means zoomed out they are not thirteen people any more.
  THAT IS NOT A BUG TO FIX BY WIDENING COATS: the body is only 25px tall there.
  It is a LIMIT to know and to say out loud -- at the wide zooms the cast is
  crowd, not characters, and anything that needs to be recognised (a faction
  holding a street, somebody you are looking for) needs a channel that is not
  the silhouette: a marker, a name, a colour, or the camera being closer.
