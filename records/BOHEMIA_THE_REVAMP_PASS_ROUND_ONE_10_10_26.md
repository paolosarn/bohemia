## COOK FOUR [the settlement from the packs] round 1 (6f070cc1): PASS. Register it in VOTE.
slices/vote/COOK4_THE_SETTLEMENT_FROM_THE_PACKS_TWIN.webp: camp, town and fortress, the cooked pictures beside the same
three rebuilt from 167 of his approved pack tiles, same hotspots. It is the first thing in the revamp that looks like the
pack he bought, and he will see it in one glance (rule 89 met: same three places, before and after). THE THREE FOR
ROUND 2:
1. THE ROOFS ARE STRIPES, NOT HOUSES. In the town the roof tiles are laid as long bands (orange, slate, orange), so a
   row reads as a pattern, not as houses. One roof per building, its ridge and its two ends, the house's walls under it.
2. EVERY PROP EVERYWHERE, EVENLY, AND A FIRE IN EVERY CORNER. Eight burning barrels in the camp; crates, sacks and tyres
   scattered at one density over the whole ground. Cluster the life at doors and stalls, wear a path between them, leave
   bare ground bare; ONE lit fire per scene by night, the others cold (R4: the light was in the room; R7: lights trace to
   who is there).
3. THIS IS LAS VEGAS. No thatch and no straw (the twin panel's thatched roofs, the camp's crop rows); tile, slate, tin
   and tarp. The palisade of sharpened logs fits act one's medieval (42c) and stays for the fortress only.
(The magenta behind the twin panel is the sheet's key colour, never a ship colour.)

## COOK TWO [the street kit from the packs] round 2 (f31b7472): THE ROAD PASSES, THE SHEET GOES BACK.
slices/fight_ground/kit_street/before_after.webp. The road is his: cracked asphalt, the worn arrows, the zebras, no
repeat. But the sheet breaks rule 89 and the kit misses one piece:
1. NOT THE SAME THING. BEFORE is a street with houses; AFTER is a bare crossing with sidewalks. He cannot compare two
   different places. Show the same crossing, same crop, before and after.
2. NO KERB. Road meets sidewalk in a hard cut; the pack draws every edge with a lit top and a dark shadow line. Add the
   kerb: a lit edge along the sidewalk, a 1-2 px shadow on the road side.
3. THE WEEDS ARE STAMPED. Every sidewalk slab carries the same weed clusters at the same density across hundreds of
   pixels (AH-03 T8). Weeds grow in seams and corners, thin and few; most slabs are bare and cracked.
Re-shoot the sheet with 1 and send it back; 2 and 3 go into the kit.

## COOK THREE [the thirteen repainted] round 3 (94dd431e): BACK. Keep the palette, change the cloth.
records/cook3/thirteen_repainted.png. The paint moved the right way (Blues, Church, Homeless, Cartel, Volunteers now read
in the runway's black, concrete, bone and oxblood), and the before and after are the same thirteen, so he can see it. But
against the twin:
1. TWO BRIGHTS STILL SHOUT. Colorful (green coat and trousers) and Network (teal coat and trousers) carry their colour
   head to toe; the card's rule is ONE accent above the waist. Their trousers go to concrete or black.
2. EVERY GARMENT IS THE SAME DOT SPECKLE. A noise of light dots over every coat (Caravans, Blues, Network, Remnants): a
   stamp, not cloth, the noise recipe his second votes killed. Cloth reads by FOLDS: 2-3 fold shapes per garment, lit from
   one side, dark in the crease, like the pack paints a sack or a tarp.
3. THE RUNWAY IS A SHAPE. Rick Owens, Balenciaga and Bottega read by drape, length and volume (long coats, oversized
   shoulders, layered hems) before colour; a repaint cannot give that. CHARACTER's rig owns the silhouettes: that is the
   ask for CHARACTER, not for COOK THREE. (The faces at body size are blank: PORTRAIT's row, noted, not judged here.)
Round 4: the folds and the one-accent fix; then here before VOTE. The runway photographs are still owed from him.
