=== TWO RULERS THROWN AWAY GETTING HERE, BOTH IN THE FLATTERING DIRECTION ===
1. THE FIRST CUT INVENTED THE SIZE. It hashed bodies at 22 pixels because a variable I
   never set defaulted to it. The real on-screen size is read off the game's own ladder
   from HC: 44px cells, 32px bodies. A made-up number in the middle of a ruler is how
   this lane has been wrong nine times.
2. THE FIRST CUT COUNTED BREATHING AS VARIETY. ctBody returns a frame chosen by the beat
   and the person's own offset, so two people wearing the IDENTICAL body hash differently
   just because they are mid-breath at different moments. That read 112 distinct and 38%
   repeats. Comparing everybody at ONE breath phase gives 47 distinct and 74% repeats.
   THE FLATTERING READING WAS THE WRONG ONE, WHICH IS THE DANGEROUS DIRECTION.

=== WHY THE OBVIOUS FIX IS WRONG, MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED ===
  cost of one more baked body   530 ms of frozen page
  six bodies today              ~3.2 s
Doubling to twelve would add another 3.2 seconds of freeze at boot, and a longer freeze
on a phone is ITSELF one of the things he named ("glitchy, buggy"). So raising the number
trades one of his complaints for another. THE FIX HAS TO BE CHEAPER VARIETY, NOT MORE
BAKES, and that is design work with a real tradeoff rather than a constant to edit.

WHAT THE SIX ARE, and they were a good decision when they were made: the city used to draw
every resident as the PLAYER's body under a colour shift, so the whole valley was him in
different hues. Six real bodies with six different silhouettes, gated by
city_cast_silhouette_gate, was a large step up. It is simply not enough for 183 people on
one screen.
