WHAT WAS ACTUALLY TRUE
  Measured the day he said it, on 5,000 people through the real generator
  (BOH_PERSONLOOK.lookFor), the same function the crowd on the street uses:

      long coats in the outer slot      16 of 35 garments        46%
      factions wearing one              5 of 13
      every person in the city          20.6% in a trenchcoat
      of everybody wearing any coat     45% wore a long one

  He said "everyone". He was describing a real distribution and he was UNDER-stating
  it if anything, because a coat is most of a person on screen: the share of people
  who READ as "guy in a duster" is higher than the share who are technically in one.

  AND FOUR OF THOSE SIXTEEN WERE MINE, cooked the day before he said this. The colour
  pass on 8/26 needed a saturated outer garment for two factions and reached for a
  duster four times, because a duster was what the shelf held.

THE CAUSE WAS A HOLE, NOT A WEIGHT
  Every long coat in the game is len 0.80-0.90. Everything else in the outer slot
  stops at the WAIST (vest:true or jacket:true). Between the waistcoat and the floor
  there was NOTHING.

  That is the entire bug. The picker is uniform over what exists. Give it a waistcoat
  and a floor-length duster and nothing between, and every person who wants "a coat"
  and not "a waistcoat" comes out in a duster. Nobody chose that. THE WARDROBE CHOSE
  IT.

  He named the cause himself, in the same breath as the complaint:
      "I know we still need to make a lot more clothing so this is like very early on"

WHAT WAS BUILT — TWO HALVES, AND THE FIRST ONE IS THE REAL ONE

  1. FILL THE MIDDLE. Seventeen new outer garments in two new length bands:
       HIP    len 0.34   CHORE COAT, BARN COAT, OLIVE CHORE COAT, STORM CHORE COAT,
                         RUST WORK COAT, COBALT CHORE COAT                    (6)
       THIGH  len 0.56   CAR COAT, SAND CAR COAT, SAGE CAR COAT, SLATE CAR COAT,
                         GRASS CAR COAT                                       (5)
       WAIST            WORK / STORM / KHAKI / OXBLOOD JACKET, SAGE + BONE VEST
     A new length is a new SHAPE, so STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR (7/19) is satisfied by
     geometry and not by argument. Verified drawing real pixels, not just registered:
     CHORE COAT 1245px, CAR COAT 1359px, WORK JACKET 1025px, vests 326px.

     RESERVING THE COAT WITHOUT THIS WOULD NOT BE A FIX. It would strip the coat off
     half the city and leave those people in a waistcoat. That is a subtraction, not
     a repair, and it is the version of this work that a share-cap gate would have
     happily certified.

  2. RESERVE THE LONG COAT. All 17 floor-length coats carry hard:true. The picker
     holds the reservation in exactly the shape the existing `lux` flag already used:

         var _hardOdds = 0.10;
         var _soft = pool.filter(function (x) { return !x.hard; });
         pool = (_soft.length && unit(id, 'hard:' + cat) > _hardOdds) ? _soft : pool;

     DATA, NOT NAMES. The picker module names no garment anywhere, so a new duster is
     reserved the moment somebody tags it, with no edit to the picker.

     A NAMED CHARACTER IS NOT A CROWD MEMBER. A faction, a boss, a killer, a quest NPC
     wears whatever its FACTION_LOOKS row or authored look says. The reservation
     governs the RANDOM population only. That is the whole point: once the coat stops
     being background noise, putting one on somebody becomes a statement.

  TEST 3 AND 4 ARE THE ONES THAT MATTER AND THEY ARE EASY TO LEAVE OUT. Tests 1 and 2

  looking at. A cap without the band floors is a cap on the symptom.

  WHAT THE GATE DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO: read FACTION_LOOKS, or decide who is a
  badass. A machine must never overrule a ruling (8/1).

  MUTATION-PROVED BY HAND the same turn: deleting the reservation line from the picker

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
  It does not decide who is a badass. It does not touch a faction's clothes. It does
  not delete a single coat. All 17 long coats are still in the game, still rendered,
  still wearable in the CHARACTER tab. The only thing that changed is how often a
  stranger on the street is wearing one.

THE LESSON UNDERNEATH
  WHEN SOMETHING IS EVERYWHERE, LOOK AT WHAT IT IS COMPETING AGAINST BEFORE YOU CAP
  IT. The trenchcoat was not over-represented because it was over-weighted. It was
  over-represented because it had no neighbours. The fix for "too much of X" is
  usually "not enough of everything else", and capping X without checking that is how
  you end up with a city where nobody owns a coat.

  Same family as the 17 invisible hats (8/26) and the four bright clothes worn by
  nobody (8/26): the material existed, or failed to, and nothing in the machine was
  watching whether it reached the player. Every one of those was found by MEASURING
  THE POPULATION, not by reading the catalogue.
