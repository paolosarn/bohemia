THE FOUR CROSS-CUTTING RULES HE GAVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE BACK IS NOT THE FRONT. A front shape must differ from the back shape.
   Right now they read the same, and he named it as the first thing.
2. COVER THE HEADSPACE. Backs of heads are showing bare skin that hair should
   own. Named for BUZZ CUT, CROP, BOWL CUT, SIDE PART, TOP KNOT, HIGH TOP.   [KILLED]
3. NO STRAIGHT LINES. "a lot about hair is about just the little off shapes that
   it makes so even something with the shag ... I'm seeing you make like a lot of
   straight lines and that's not realistic at all"
4. ONE PIXEL, NOT THREE. Said twice, for LOCS and CORNROWS: "just have it. The   [KILLED]
   difference is just one pixel not like two or three" -- one pixel hair, one
   pixel skin. At 56px there is no real estate for a 3px gap.
