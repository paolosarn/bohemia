HE FOUND TWO PIPELINE BUGS IN ONE SENTENCE, and neither was content.

1. THE ROOF OF EVERY CIRCLE. Scene.prism's top cap stepped by TWO (so half the
   fan was never emitted -- wedge-shaped HOLES) and repeated its third vertex as
   its fourth (so every quad it did emit was a degenerate SLIVER). Holes plus
   slivers at 2:1 iso is exactly a tarp pegged over a drum. It was in EVERY
   circular thing this factory has ever baked: the library drum, the city hall
   council chamber, the courthouse rotunda, the terminal concourse, every silo,
   every tank, every dry basin.

2. DOORS OFF THEIR WALLS. _door took a bare plane and a bare span and drew a leaf
   there; nothing checked a wall was behind it. SEVEN heroes had floating doors --
   city hall's ran 1.2 units past the end of its own block, the courthouse's
   floated clear of the rotunda, the campus library's overhung its colonnade, and
   both of the school's missed.

WHAT I SHOULD HAVE CAUGHT AND DID NOT
  VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE (7/18) says in as many words: A SYMPTOM THAT SURVIVES
  CONTENT CHANGES IS A PIPELINE BUG. I had that law. I still spent four district
  rebuilds adjusting drums instead of reading the primitive that draws drums. He
  had to tell me the circles were wrong before I looked at the code that makes
  circles.

  And both faults are the same shape as almost every bug in this repo: A VALUE
  PASSED BY HAND WHERE A VALUE COULD BE DERIVED. Doors now derive their face from
  the solid (_door_face), so a door CANNOT be off its wall.

THE THIRD THING THE FIX SURFACED
  Closing the prism cap turned the STADIUM icon monochrome, and the hue gate caught
  it the same turn. The bowl had only ever read as a ring because the HOLES in the
  broken cap let the field show through. Prisms take an inner radius now, so a bowl
  is a real ring -- which is what a stadium bowl, an oculus and a tank curb all are.
