## WHAT THE PIXELS SAY

**96 of 1506 purchased tiles are act-1 legal as bought. 1410 are not** (pure black, white, or
reserved purple above 1% of the tile). That is not a reason to reject them; it is the
reason they cannot be pasted straight in, and the number nobody had before.

### Colour is the half a machine can see. Subject is the half it cannot.

I rendered his wall and roof packs and looked at them (`records/target/BOUGHT_WALLS.png`,
`records/target/BOUGHT_ROOFS.png`). What the names promise and what the tiles are:

- `4. House wall tiles` (27) is a **medieval ivy cottage** — lime stucco, timber, arched
  plank doors, leaded glass. It is not a Las Vegas house wall.
- `wall tiles` (41) and `2. Wall tiles (1)` are **dungeon masonry** — mossy castle stone,
  irregular rubble, barred windows, near-black mortar.
- `3. Wall panels and details` (28) is **sci-fi control panels**, lit blue screens.
- `Rooftop and building tops` (46) is **cyberpunk skyscraper tops** — HVAC plant, helipads,
  neon signage. Exactly ONE of the 47 roof tiles (`5. Roof tiles#26`) is a pitched terracotta
  roof.

So the ruling has a SHAPE his library gives it, and it is not uniform:

| surface | is what he bought usable for act-1 Vegas? |
|---|---|
| ground, street, concrete, path, water | **YES, directly.** Cracked concrete and cracked street are exactly right for a dead Vegas. The RUN lane already draws these ahead of painted tiles. |
| walls | **NO for houses.** Fantasy and sci-fi subject matter. No stucco, no CMU, no corrugated metal. |
| roofs | **NO.** One usable tile in 47. |

### The fix is not to reject his art. It is to move its BLACK POINT.

Measured on the exact bytes the run ships today (tier S/A, pure, 44x44 - not the HD source):

| surface | tiles shipped | mean pure-black % | worst | over the 1% line |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| sidewalk + driveway | 20 | 2.2 | 8.4 | 16 of 20 |
| road | 13 | 5.0 | 15.0 | 9 of 13 |

So the street he walks on right now breaks the act-1 palette law, because his tiles were
shipped RAW and nothing measured them. `bought_beats_painted_gate.js` checks that his art
SHIPS and ships FIRST - both correct, both green - and never what colour it is.

`tools/bohemia_bought_conditioner.py` closes it without touching his art: a monotone
luminance remap compresses the illegal black and white tails into [17,232]. Ordering is
preserved so no detail is crushed, and RGB is scaled uniformly so HUE AND SATURATION ARE
UNCHANGED. Result: 33 tiles, mean illegal 2.2%/5.0% -> **0.00%**, and side by side the before
and after are near indistinguishable - which is the point. See `records/target/BOUGHT_CONDITIONED.png`.
`gates/bought_first_gate.py` proves it is still HIS: every conditioned tile must trace to a
tile he bought, hold its size, hue and saturation, and land inside act-1. Sabotage-tested
against a repaint, a leftover black pixel and an invented tile; it caught all three.
