## What it is

The ninth round of rule 108, and the filter for the eighth round's research while it is still running. Two things, both locked:

1. THE GOAL IS THE CEILING, NOT PERFECTION. He does not want 'the best art'. He wants the chats to be the best artist THEY can be, and he will support that once (a purchase, a computer left on). A tool is judged by whether it raises the chats' ceiling.
2. THE WORKFLOW IS THE GAME. If making a graphics asset costs him one more step each time, the game does not ship. His steps per asset can only go DOWN from here.

## The ruling

- HIS WORK PER ASSET IS ONE TAP, in the VOTE tab he already opens: yes, no or final on the result card. Nothing else. No prompt, no site, no file, no key per asset.
- THE REQUEST-CARD YES IS GONE FOR THE FREE ENGINE. Step 4 of the ten steps existed to stop money moving before his approval. The gun in the repo costs zero, so there is nothing to approve: the chat writes the card, the gun fires, the result lands in the VOTE tab, he taps once. The YES step survives ONLY on a card that costs money (Retro Diffusion, engine two), and those are rare by design.
- EVERY TOOL FROM THE RESEARCH IS FILTERED FIRST BY HIS STEP COUNT. Zero new steps for him, or it is out, however good it is. A tool that adds a step for the CHATS is fine; chats have time, he does not.
- ONE-TIME SUPPORT IS WELCOME, DEPENDENCE IS NOT. A purchase or an always-on computer may raise the ceiling; nothing may stop working when the computer is off (the free runners are the floor).
