## WHAT IT MEANS (rule 77a)
1. ONE ROAD KIT. The freeway and the street are the same road drawn at two widths: one asphalt, one lane paint, one curb, one lamp, one shoulder, one compass; a freeway tile beside a street tile must read as the same city. Today they look like two games.
2. THE DIRECTION. Rule 77's typed edges exist (COMBAT TWO 9fc51b2c); the freeway is its test case and it fails him: the lanes must run the board's long axis left to right (the sixth votes), every freeway tile's lane direction agreeing with its neighbour and with the street grid's compass.
3. NO WALL ON TOP OF A ROAD. The thing he sees as a gate wall on the freeway (a sound wall, a guard rail or an overpass parapet drawn as a wall across the road) is gone; a real freeway has a low concrete Jersey barrier in the median and a chain-link fence or a low sound wall at the shoulder's edge, never anything across the lanes; cover on a freeway board is the barrier, a stalled car, a truck's trailer.
4. JUDGED AGAINST THE REAL THING: a photo of I-15 through Las Vegas (reference/) beside the board at the phone profile; EYES counts what reads wrong.
