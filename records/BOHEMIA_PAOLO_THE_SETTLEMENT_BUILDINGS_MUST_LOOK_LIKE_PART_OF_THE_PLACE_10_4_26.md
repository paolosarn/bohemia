the land at their own angles and sizes, roads and people between them, smoke, a well, a wall; the ones you can use are
IN the picture and light up under the finger; nothing is a card, a tile row or a shelf. Ours the same: the block as one
45-degree picture cut from COMBAT TWO's boards and the banks (the houses, the store fronts, the lot, the street, the cars,
the sign, the people standing about), the usable buildings part of that picture (the barber IS a house with a pole, the
stall IS a stall on the sidewalk, the posts IS a wall with paper on it, the clinic IS a storefront with a cross, the board
IS a board nailed to a pole), lit under the finger, named only when touched; the size tier (camp 4, town 6, fortress 8)
decides how many stand there; the traits (71) change what is lit, boarded or burnt. A thumb shelf may exist only as a
second way in, below the picture, never as the picture. RUN TWO [settlement screen] round three, the first thing; EYES
judges it against a Battle Brothers settlement screenshot: does it read as a place or as options.
