# UI STUDY (rule 100c, before drawing): HOW THE PHONE'S TYPE IS BUILT (10/10/26, ui-kmqmrf, row [the phone's font])

The family being cooked is the phone's type, not a tile, so the study is of the two faces themselves (the pack holds no
type). Measured in the browser at 100 px: BohemiaROM advance 60, cap height 68, x-height 49; BohemiaCasing advance 50, cap
height 70. Both are dot grids of TEN DOTS PER EM (ROM the 5x8 character-cell register of the 9/11 font research, CASING the
5x8 stamped cut): one dot is a tenth of the font size.
On his phone (3 device pixels a point) a dot sits on whole pixels only at sizes that are multiples of 3.333 pt:
  10 pt = 3 pixels a dot (the posts now: sharp), 6.667 pt = 2 pixels a dot (sharp), 3.333 pt = 1 (unreadable).
  Everything between blurs the dot: 9 pt (the handles now) = 2.7 pixels a dot, 8 pt = 2.4, 7 pt = 2.1.
So 'tinier' has ONE honest answer that keeps the pixel look: 2 pixels a dot. Letter spacing must also be whole dots (0 or
0.667 pt); line height whole pixels (9.333 pt = 14 dots of 2 px).
