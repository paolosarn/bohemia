# THE TOP IS ONLY RESOURCES, THE CREW SCREEN SEES THE CAMP, AND THE DEAD ARE LORE-FRIENDLY (Paolo 11/3; VAMILY rules 368, 369, 360 amended; ask lines 392 to 394)

## HIS WORDS (verbatim, transcription kept; voice to text)
"OK, so first off keep the bodies lore friendly, right if it's a busy civilized settlement or part of the town, which then obviously
they don't have [corpses], but the gameplay should be very simplified. The sun in the top left corner. I hate the time... and then your
location settlement for example and the button at the top of the screen, I don't like that we have two crew visuals... the phone
doesn't need to be at the top. The only thing that really should be at the top of the screen, UI wise, is the resources to be honest.
Do you wanna look at the time? Look at your phone. If you wanna look at the crew, look at your phone. And also I wanted to provide an
upgrade towards essentially the visuals in the crew [screen]. I'm tired of all that brown backdrop shit. I wanna see more of the camp,
of the settlement, when you're in the crew screen, so I'm wondering if we can set up a smart system, the screen looks really clean.
It's your people in the camp and... right now it's hard to see them. There's so much brown background gunk. The UI is not optimized at
all for the visuals for the menu... especially in the crew menu, you click on a character, it centers the camera on that character,
to make it that much more like walking around."

## RULE 368. THE TOP IS ONLY RESOURCES
a. The top of the screen shows the resources row and nothing else: no DAY plate, no sun, no crew plate, no place name.
b. The time and the day live on the phone; the crew lives on the phone's crew button; the place's name is already the phone's first line.
c. One button for one thing: never two crew buttons on one screen.
d. Gate: the top band holds only the resource counts on the map, in a town, in the camp and in the fight; nothing the phone already has
   appears twice.

## RULE 369. THE CREW SCREEN SEES THE CAMP
a. Your people standing in the camp ARE the crew screen: the camp (or the settlement, or the town you are in) stays in full view,
   lit, with your men in it, walking around.
b. The brown backdrop is gone: the sheet, the gear slots, the stats and the stash sit in a clean, light, slim layer that leaves the
   camp visible (rule 357's text law: placed with the picture, never a box over it), on the phone where they can live there.
c. Tap a man (in the line-up or in the camp) and the camera glides to him and centers on him; his sheet shows; tap another and it
   glides on. It feels like walking around your camp.
d. Battle Brothers keeps its company screen over a picture of the camp's fire; our twist is the camp itself, live.
e. Gate: on the crew screen the camp is visible past a floor of the glass, every man of the company is drawn in it, and a tap on a man
   centers him within a beat.

## RULE 360 AMENDED: THE DEAD ARE LORE-FRIENDLY, AND SIMPLE
a. Where people live and keep order (a busy, civilized settlement or part of town) there are NO bodies: the living bury their dead.
   Bodies lie where nobody is left to move them: the empty blocks, the old camps, the roads between, the desert, the fallen towns.
b. The gameplay of the dead stays very simple: scenery that tells the story; at most what the board already does with a thing on a
   tile (cover, a blocker); no new system.
c. COOK DEAD's record and COMBAT MAPS 3's placing follow this; WORLD's map too.
