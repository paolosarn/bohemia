## WHAT IS THERE NOW (coordinator, measured on the rules and the board)
Rule 208(a) already plays the job's sure fight and its chance fights out on the AUTO calculator against his crew. But it maps the
result its OWN way: one to three stars at cut-offs 0.95 and 0.50, and a coordinator's softening (10/26, rule 205) counted a trip
'clean' with up to one man in six laid up. The fight's AUTO colour (rule 144(e)(f)(g)) reads the same runs on another scale: a
continuous red-to-green on the chance of a CLEAN win, a death pulls red, a man down sits between, and no neutral middle (even reads
red). Two scales off one calculator: the button and the fight can disagree. That softening was the coordinator's, not his.

## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked; TUNING checks the wiki copy)
Contracts carry one to three skulls; enemy parties grow stronger as the days pass and as the company grows; the first weeks mostly
offer what a new company can handle, and the late-game crises come long after. Its weak point, his words: the skulls are not
accurate.

## THE RULINGS
254. ONE CALCULATOR, ONE SCALE: THE JOB'S BLOOD IS THE AUTO COLOUR
(a) The job's blood and its red stars are read off the SAME runs and the SAME scale as the fight's AUTO colour (rule 144(e)(f)(g)):
    the chance of a clean win for his crew as it stands, a death pulling red, a man down between, no neutral middle.
(b) MAX BLOOD WHERE AUTO IS DEEPEST RED: a job on which the calculator expects him to lose men, or men to fall, wears the bloodiest
    button and three stars; a job AUTO would tint green wears clean tin and one star; between, the blood deepens on the same curve.
(c) The coordinator's 'up to one in six laid up is clean' (208(a), 10/26) is STRUCK: a fall counts as the AUTO colour counts it.
(d) THE GATE: for 200 rolled jobs, the button's blood level and the AUTO colour of the job's sure fight played from its lineup are
    one number within a hair; every job whose runs lose a man reads the deepest blood.

255. THE DANGER RAMPS TO DAY FIFTY, AND NO SIX WOLVES FOR A LONE WOLF ON DAY THREE
(a) THE JOB BOARD GROWS WITH THE DAYS AND THE COMPANY: in the first days the board offers what his company can do (mostly clean
    tin and light blood by rule 254), with at most one bloody job in sight as a warning; the dangerous kinds come in as days pass,
    the full danger by about DAY 50 (his words: 'up to 50 days is when shit starts getting difficult'); after that, the world is
    as hard as it is.
(b) SCALED TO WHO HE IS: a job is offered for the company he has: a Lone Wolf alone is never offered a pack of six wolves in his
    first weeks; a job that would kill him outright is not on the early board at all.
(c) Roaming dangers on the map ramp the same way (Battle Brothers' parties grow with the days).
(d) The curve: the coordinator's default, TUNING's numbers from Battle Brothers' day scaling: day 1 to 10 the board's worst job is
    one AUTO would read 'could go either way'; it widens to day 50; the first-week squeeze (rule 117) still bites through money, not
    death.
(e) THE GATE: rolled boards on day 1, 10, 25, 50 and 80 for three origins (the Lone Wolf among them): before day 10 no job reads the
    deepest blood except at most one; by day 50 the full range is offered; the Lone Wolf's boards in the first 20 days never hold a
    job his runs lose outright.
