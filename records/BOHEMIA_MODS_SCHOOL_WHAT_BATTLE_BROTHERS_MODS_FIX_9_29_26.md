# MODS [what mods fix] -- THE BATTLE BROTHERS MODS PLAYERS INSTALL, AND WHICH ARE VANILLA HERE (9/29/26)

Lane 22 MODS, session mods-59jyd6. Row: THE-MOD-FEATURES-THAT-ARE-VANILLA-HERE.
Ruling answered: Paolo 9/29, rule 40e ("instead of needing to use mods in Battle Brothers"); rule 39b
("modded vanilla++"). MODE: RESEARCH. Nothing in the game changed, no gate added. Every KEEP is a DEFAULT
for a lane to build one at a time; every MAYBE is a proposal for VOTE; every NO fights one of his rulings.
Battle Brothers is the campaign-layer reference and is named by him; each row ends with what WE do
differently (rule 39b), so none of this reads as a copy.

## 0. THE ANSWER IN ONE LINE

**Nineteen mods, not twenty: 11 KEEP, 6 MAYBE, 2 NO. And 13 of the 19 cost the base game's balance nothing at
all**, because they only speed the game, pause it, show you a number, or automate a chore. What players
install Battle Brothers mods to get is mostly INFORMATION and TIME, not a different rulebook. That is good
news: the KEEPs are cheap.

## 3. THREE THINGS THE LIST SAYS

1. **Speed is the loudest want, and our one hard law is against the obvious fix.** Every "must install" list
   opens with a game-speed mod. Our fight is on the beat at 120 BPM (a pillar), so we cannot speed the beat.
   What the players really hate is WAITING between turns that do nothing; that waiting we can cut. This is
   the one KEEP-shaped row that is a MAYBE, on purpose.
2. **Information mods are all the same request: show me what I cannot work out.** Effective Hitpoints,
   attribute ranges, named-item ranges, the stat forecast, real numbers instead of "many". The fleet's own
   rule RF4-68 says the same thing (tell what the player cannot derive, never explain what the floor can
   show). Seven of nineteen are this. Nothing in the game is asked to change; a face card and a settlement
   screen just print more.
3. **Autopilot is our gambits, and it is the rare mod that changes how much you play.** BB players install
   it to stop doing routine turns. FINAL FANTASY XII's gambit idea (combat only, rule 36a) is the same relief
   built into the design, so this is the strongest KEEP: a mod the base game of another studio needed, and ours
   already has as a first-class system.

## 4. THE SIX MAYBES, AND WHO TAKES EACH (a proposal in VOTE, one default at a time)

- **Swifter**: COMBAT, "skip the dead wait between the company's automatic turns", never the beat.
- **Bro Planner's stat forecast**: PEOPLE at the barber and RUN's training ground.
- **Tweaks and Fixes**: TUNING reads it item by item; "locations start discovered" is refused.
- **Legends' camping**: LIFE+CITY, inside the settlement screen of a home base (rule 40b). Alternate starts
  are already the origins.
- **Reforged's spawn scaling and reach**: TUNING's three difficulty dials (enemy level, enemy gear, your
  mistake, rule 40a). Reach is already the reach on the tiles.
- **Sato balance**: TUNING reads it as evidence, never installs it.
