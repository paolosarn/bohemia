THE COMBAT VOICE IS ASH AND DRIVE. Both survivors are ash, both are LOW (65.6 and
67.0 Hz, the bottom of their jitter ranges), and both are the HARDEST DRIVEN of
their five (0.682 and 0.712, the top of theirs). Nothing else lived.

WHAT DIED, AND IT IS NOT RANDOM:
  vital was CRYSTAL at 880 Hz. Crystal went 8/10 with him on 7/30, so the
    material was not the problem -- the REGISTER was. High and bright is wrong
    for a body being hit.
  clear was BELL with a 1.5-beat tail. Bell went 10/10 on 7/30 and SAVED is
    built from it. So bell is not dead either: a long ringing resolution is
    wrong FOR COMBAT. He does not want a fight to end like a save chime.
  miss WAS ash -- and still died 5/5. It was the only ash in the batch that was
    high (320 Hz) and barely driven (0.12). That is the control case, and it
    confirms the pattern is not "ash wins", it is LOW AND DRIVEN wins.

SO THE RULE THIS BATCH BOUGHT: combat is dry, low and saturated. Not bright, not
ringing, not airy. Two materials that scored 10/10 and 8/10 in other contexts
were both rejected here, which means CONTEXT beats material -- the 7/30 material
table does not transfer to combat unexamined.

DO NOT RE-COOK miss / vital / clear THIS SESSION. That is one rejection, not two,
and STOP PRODUCING says a lane that has just been told its surface is wrong does
not answer by producing more. The rule above is what a future batch would be
built on: low, ash-or-stone, driven hard, short. His call whether that batch
happens.
