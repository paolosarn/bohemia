## THE HEADLINE FINDING: THE RAW DATA'S "BEASTS" FACTION IS MOSTLY NOT ANIMALS
The locked law (rule 42) says beasts are de-extinct real animals, lab escapees, never
radiation, only what real DNA allows. Battle Brothers' own "Beasts" faction is NOT that: of
its 34 rows, checked one by one against the real notes field, only 9 are anything an Ice-Age
surrogate could be (Direwolf, Frenzied Direwolf, Frenzied Hyena, Hyena, Serpent, Unhold, Bog
Unhold, Frost Unhold, Armored Frost Unhold -- a wolf, a hyena, a snake, and what reads
cleanly as a short-faced bear at every tier). The other 25 are folklore monsters in the source
material itself: a nightmare demon, a witch, a fire spirit in three sizes, a lake monster
with tentacles, a dragon, a flesh-eating ghoul in three sizes, a forest spirit, a walking
plant, a giant spider, a regenerating deer-spirit, and a vengeful ghost. NONE OF THOSE ARE
ANIMALS, so the locked law cannot name them without being stretched past what it actually
says. THIS IS NOT A WORDING PROBLEM, IT IS A LAW-VERSUS-SOURCE MISMATCH, flagged for a ruling,
not decided here. Six more rows are a straight DATA BUG, not a words problem at all: Lower
Goblin Ambusher, Lower Goblin Skirmisher, Lower Mercenary, Lower Necrosavant, Lower Orc
Warrior and Lower Orc Young are all tagged faction:"beasts" in enemies.json, and every one of
them is plainly a weaker copy of a goblin, mercenary, ancient-dead or orc unit by name, gear
and skill set alone -- they are not beasts, the faction tag is wrong, flagged for whoever
owns the enemies.json factory (not a words fix).

Per MECHANISM-MINE/CONTENTS-PAOLO'S (words get an attempt, decisions wait, rule 8.11), every
one of the 25 real folklore rows below still gets a draft:true name and line, grounded the
