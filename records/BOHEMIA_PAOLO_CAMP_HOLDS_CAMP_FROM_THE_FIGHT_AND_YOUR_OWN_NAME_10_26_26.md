## WHY CAMP THROWS HIM OUT (coordinator, measured on main, slices/BOHEMIA_CITY_WORLD.html campHour)
Every camp hour checks three things and breaks camp on any of them:
1. the camp's settlement frame is not open (any close of the camp panel breaks it: 'left');
2. a fight is running, a meet card is up, or CITY_CONTACT_POSTED is set ('contact'); the flag can stay set after a contact is over;
3. a hunting party is within 2 blocks ('warned': 'X are coming. Camp's broken.'): a warning ends camp even if they never arrive.
Any of these sends him back to the city mode while he is trying to heal. And the HEAL/CAMP button is not on the live phone yet (UI
TWO's new phone is not merged).

## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
Camp is a toggle on the world map; it holds until you move or an enemy party actually reaches you and the fight begins. After a battle
you are back on the map; there is no camp-from-battle button (ours, his words).
