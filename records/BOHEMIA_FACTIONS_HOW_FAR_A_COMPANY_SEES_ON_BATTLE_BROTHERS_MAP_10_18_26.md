# HOW FAR A COMPANY SEES ON BATTLE BROTHERS' MAP (the sight numbers)

## The answer in one breath
Battle Brothers never prints the base radius. It prints what MULTIPLIES it: hills 1.25, mountains 2, every other ground 1; a Lookout follower +25%; night "reduced" with no number; forest does not shrink YOUR circle, it hides the parties standing in it until you are closer. No background, perk, trait or ambition widens the world map circle (Eagle Eyes, Night Owl and Bow Mastery are the COMBAT board's vision). The Scout follower is speed, not sight. Enemies never see farther than you by day; at night most of them lose sight like you do, but the undead and werewolves do not. Outside your circle a party is not drawn at all; the ground you have seen stays on the map; the Brigand follower is the one exception (some caravans shown everywhere). So the one number TUNING has to pick is the BASE, and this page gives it as a fraction of the map's width so it survives WORLD's and RUN's scale answer (the region is 496 blocks today; I inherit that number).

## Measured first (rule 12): what our map does today
- **engine/bohemia_parties.js `near(parties, x, y, radius)`** is already THE function: "the ONLY function that takes the player's position, and it only LOOKS." It takes a radius in cells (Chebyshev distance, a square not a circle) and defaults to 1. It is the seam RUN's row [sight radius] plugs into.
- **Nobody calls it.** The demo (slices/BOHEMIA_DEMO.html) and the alpha (slices/BOHEMIA_ALPHA_0_9.html) reference BohemiaParties zero times; the map's moving parties are RUN's own and are all drawn (rule 102d's defect, RUN's row at VAMILY.md 01 RUN [sight radius], OPEN).
- **The close scale has its own "see" number and it is not this one:** engine/bohemia_standing.js `SEE_RANGE = 9` tiles ("you have to be able to SEE it to have seen it"), read by bohemia_against.js inSight and bohemia_deeds.js. That is a street number for who saw a deed, at the walk's scale; the map circle is a different number at a different scale and must not borrow it.
- **The map's scale (RUN, records/BOHEMIA_THE_REGION_RUN_10_16_26.md):** the region is 496 x 496 blocks beside Battle Brothers' about 140 x 140 tiles, so about 3.5 of our blocks stand where one of their tiles does. A crossing: 1.9 days rim to rim on a road, 3.7 across open ground, so about 260 blocks a day on a road and about 135 on open ground. The far stop shows 202 blocks across the glass. These are the numbers the candidates below are built from.
- **Our terrain kinds already exist** on the region (RUN's placer: road, open, hills at 1.8 cost; the city's blocks) and in factions.json's grounds, so every multiplier below has a kind to hang on.

## One finding that proves us wrong (rule 6)
Our own library said the opposite of the wiki on forest: reference/library/battle_brothers/01_WORLDMAP.md line 12 read "Sight radius shrinks in forest and at night." The terrain table gives forest a visibility radius of 1, the same as plains; what forest does is HIDE THE PARTY STANDING IN IT (the developer, post 52). The two rules draw differently: a shrinking circle punishes you for walking under trees; a hiding tile punishes you for walking PAST trees, which is why the brigands wait in the forest beside the road. Fixed in the library this round with the source (rule 33j).

## ROUTED
- **RUN [sight radius]** (VAMILY.md 01 RUN, OPEN): the draw. `BohemiaParties.near(parties, x, y, radius)` is the seam and has no caller; radius = base x ground-under-you x night x follower, in blocks, from the table above; a party standing in a hiding kind is tested against 0.5 x radius; outside, zero pixels. The gate owed by rule 102d: at three zoom stops a party just outside the edge has zero pixels. Nothing about movement or tracks changes (the row's own constraint).
- **TUNING [the one table]**: the fourteen ids above go in as map.sight.* with the wiki page beside each and 'the wiki does not say' kept as the source kind where it is true; the base fraction gets the two neighbours as its slider.
- **WORLD [the region] and RUN [the map at Battle Brothers' size]**: the base is a fraction of the width on purpose; when the grid changes, the blocks change and the fraction does not. The region's scale question stays theirs.
- **EYES [the map's four numbers]**: Battle Brothers' crossing days and zoom stops are still owed; this page adds the fifth number they can measure on a real screenshot, the circle's width as a fraction of the map at default zoom, which would turn the base from a pick into a measurement.

## [bb the sight numbers] the school line
Battle Brothers hides a party outside your circle, grows the circle on hills (1.25) and mountains (2), shrinks it at night, lets the Lookout add a quarter, hides parties in forest until you are closer, never lets an enemy see farther than you by day, and never prints the base. Ours copies every multiplier and picks the base as a tenth of the map.

## [better than bb] the sight line
Battle Brothers' circle is a flat disc that knows nothing about who is looking. Ours can: the faction whose ground you stand on sees you first (their patrols get the home-ground 1.25, the stranger does not), and the man in your crew who grew up here is your Lookout without a hire. Both are rows for later, under the hold; neither is built.
