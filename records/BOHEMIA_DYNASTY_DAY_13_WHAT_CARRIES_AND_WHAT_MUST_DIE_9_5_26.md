## 1. *** THE FINDING THAT PROVES US WRONG: DEBT DOES NOT INHERIT ***
The row's own brief names **the debt** first in the list of what a child of a
debtor inherits. It is the one item on that list that legally does not carry.
In most jurisdictions a child is **not personally liable** for a parent's
unsecured debts. Creditors have a claim against the **ESTATE**, and if the estate
is insolvent **they lose out**. The exceptions all prove the rule:
- **You co-signed it** -- then it was always your own debt, not an inherited one.
- **Community property** -- which reaches a SPOUSE, not the children.
- **Filial responsibility laws**, on the books in about two dozen US states for
  a parent's medical and nursing costs, and described as **rarely enforced** in
  practice.
> **YOU DO NOT INHERIT A BILL. YOU INHERIT LESS, AND YOU INHERIT THE PEOPLE HE
> OWED, STILL STANDING THERE.**
### AND THIS IS WHERE IT GETS SPECIFICALLY OURS
Round 3 quoted our own standing web's anthropology: *"in stateless societies a
family is treated as a CORPORATE ENTITY whose reputation carries its economic
viability... You are born owing what your father owed."* Both things are true and
the gap between them is the whole point:
> *** DEBT STOPS INHERITING ONLY BECAUSE THERE IS A COURT TO STOP IT. TAKE THE
> COURT AWAY AND IT INHERITS AGAIN. BOHEMIA HAS NO COURTS. ***
So our standing web is not a fantasy mechanic and it is not a soft version of the
law. **It is what the law is there to prevent, running in a valley where the law
is gone.** That is REALISM FIRST, and it is a stronger justification than the one
the module already gives itself.

## 2. THE REAL AISLE -- WHAT ACTUALLY CARRIES, AND THE NUMBERS ARE STRANGE
### THE TRADE CARRIES AS A PULL, NOT AS A DESTINY
```
UK        a worker is 72% MORE LIKELY to be in an occupation if his father is in it
MILITARY  a son of a military father is FIVE TIMES more likely to enlist
          ... and only ONE IN FOUR of them does
FARMING   only 3% of farmers' sons stay in it
          ... and that 3% is SEVEN AND A HALF TIMES the base rate
BRAZIL    about 39% follow the parental occupational legacy
SAME SEX  33-40% larger inheritance rate from the same-gendered parent
```
> **READ THOSE TWO COLUMNS TOGETHER. THE MULTIPLIERS ARE ENORMOUS AND THE
> ABSOLUTE RATES ARE SMALL. FIVE TIMES MORE LIKELY, AND STILL THREE IN FOUR WALK
> AWAY.** The trade is the strongest pull in a child's life and it usually loses.
That is a mechanic, not a statistic: **the heir starts pointed at the father's
trade, and the game should expect them not to take it.**
### AND THE HOUSE IS THE REAL CHANNEL
Property is the thing that actually moves wealth between generations, and round 3
already measured the rest: status persists at roughly 0.79 a generation while
wealth and occupation persist at roughly 0.3 to 0.4. **The name outlasts the
money by about two to one.**

## 3. THE GAMES AISLE -- THE TWO KINDS, WHICH THE BRIEF NAMES CORRECTLY
- **THE CLEAN HEIR.** Everything material transfers and the new character is the
  old one with a different portrait. **Dying is a costume change.** The known
  outcome is the one its own players report: carry the items, the skills, the
  money and the shops across, and *the biggest thing you lose is your family,
  because all of them disappear.* Nothing was taken, so nothing was survived.
- **THE BURDENED HEIR.** Something real did not come across, and the player has
  to live inside the gap. The brief's own sentence is the finding:
  **the generation it went wrong is the one players remember.**
And the constraint that stops "burdened" becoming "punished" is also documented
(round 3): an heir must arrive with a real leg up, **or the handoff reads as
deleting a high-level character**, which players will not accept.
> **A CLEAN HEIR IS UNMEMORABLE AND A STRIPPED HEIR IS UNACCEPTABLE. THE ANSWER
> IS NOT A MIDDLE AMOUNT, IT IS THE RIGHT FIELDS.**
