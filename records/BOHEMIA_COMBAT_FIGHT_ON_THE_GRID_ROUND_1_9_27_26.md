## 2. THE SAME BOARD IN RULE 34's CELLS

At the law's default cell of 32 CSS px:

```
  the same canvas holds     12.2 CELLS ACROSS, 20.0 DOWN
  a house at 4 x 4 cells    3 houses across, 5 down
  a person                  ONE CELL, 32 px, against 112 today
```

**And a cell is 3 metres, derived rather than picked:** the fight's house is 12 m and the law
puts 4 cells in a house, so 12 / 4 = 3. Nothing in the world changes size. Only what a step
is changes. It also lines up with the walked street's own fine cell of 0.75 m: one fight cell
is four of them.

## 3. REACH, RE-DERIVED (the row's ask, and it is in VOTE)

His 9/22 numbers were pistol 1 tile, rifle 2, scope 3, when a tile was a house. **Keep the
metres, change the unit:**

```
  pistol   4 cells    12 m     unchanged
  rifle    8 cells    24 m     unchanged
  scope   12 cells    36 m     unchanged
```

**And drawing them on the real board is the finding of this round: only the pistol fits on
the phone.** A rifle's 8 cells fills the whole board edge to edge and a scope's 12 runs off
it. That is not an error, it is what a rifle is, and the fight already owns the answer: the
auto frame pulls back to hold every living enemy, so a rifle fight opens the shot by itself
and a knife fight stays tight. **Nothing new has to be built for that; it is V225's camera
doing the job it already does.**

The alternative, shrinking reach so the longest one fits at rest, would quietly halve
distances he has already approved. Realism first, and his numbers stand.

## 4. THE SCHOOL — HOW RF4 RUNS A FIGHT ON ITS GRID AT SPEED

Rogue Fable 4 is the reference for the fight (rule 33d, RF4 first). What makes its grid fast,
read against what we have:

| RF4 does | we do | keep |
|---|---|---|
| **One input is one turn.** No action points, no confirmation, no menu. A move, an attack and a quaff all cost the same thing: your turn. | **EVERYTHING COSTS ONE** is already his law, and the beat is already the turn at 120 BPM | **Already ours.** The grid does not change it. |
| **The board is small and entirely on screen.** You never scroll to understand the fight. | 2 houses today; **12 x 20 cells** on the new grid | **The grid buys this.** A 12 x 20 board is a real roguelike room. |
| **Line of sight is the tactic.** Walls and corners are how you break contact and pick your fight. | the fight has cover and pillars, but the walked world's **walls** never reach the board | **The honest gap**, and rule 34(b) closes it: every cell is FLOOR, WALL, DOOR, COVER or PROP, so a fight on the close grid inherits the block's real walls. |
| **Monsters move after you, visibly, one step.** You always see the step that will kill you before it lands. | the beat already does this; rule 23 asks for the enemy's beat visible before it lands | **Keep**, and it gets easier at one cell per step. |
| **Retreat is a real move** through a door or a corridor. | the way out exists and is placed off `sightTiles` | **Re-derive in cells** when the board is built. |

**And the one thing to steal from Battle Brothers is ZONE OF CONTROL** (cited:
`reference/library/battle_brothers/02_COMBAT_RULES.md`): stepping away from a man next to you
gives him a free swing. It was named last round and it gets cheaper here, because on a grid
of one-cell steps "next to you" is a fact the board already knows.

## 5. WHAT DIES AND WHAT SURVIVES OF THE LAST FIVE ROUNDS

Said plainly, because five rounds of this lane were built on the house tile:

- **Dies:** the house board as the fight's scale, the lot of sixteen street cells, the body at
  112 as the *walked* sprite, and the reach numbers expressed in houses.
- **Survives:** everything that was about honesty rather than size. The fight's canvas is 1:1
  with the glass (V224). The camera moves the ground and not the person (V225), which rule 34
  explicitly re-reads rather than repeals: one size at every zoom, and the size is now one
  cell. The floor is the street's own cooked art (V223). The mover is the walk's one cut ring
  (V226). The 112 art is not thrown away; it becomes the source the small sprite is cut from,
  and the portraits and vote pages stay at that size.
