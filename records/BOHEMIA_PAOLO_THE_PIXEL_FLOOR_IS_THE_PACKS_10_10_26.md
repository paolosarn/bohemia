## THE FLOOR, BY KIND (rule 105)

- a floor tile (street, sidewalk, ground, road, marking): 96 by 96, the pack's tile, never smaller;
- a building piece, a door, a wall: 96 on its long side;
- a lamp or pole: 96 tall;
- a prop: never under the pack's smallest prop, 29 by 36; a car or a crate at the pack's median, about 96 wide;
- an icon (the stash, the bar, the phone): never under 32 by 32 (the pack's smallest prop, rounded up; today they are 5 by 5);
- a map marker: never under 40 by 40 (the pack's smallest ground piece; today 8 by 8);
- a person: the 112 box, about 100 painted, already above the floor and unchanged;
- a portrait: never under the pack's tile, 96 by 96, at its own pixels.

The floor is a minimum, not a target: a thing may have more pixels, never fewer. On the map at the far stop the camera may shrink the world (THE GROUND MAY ZOOM), but the drawn asset in the file is never under its floor.
