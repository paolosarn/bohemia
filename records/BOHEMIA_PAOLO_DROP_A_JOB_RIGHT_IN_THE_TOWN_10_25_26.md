## WHAT IS WRONG
The only place to drop a job is the phone, and the phone lives on the map only (the second votes: THE PHONE IS IN THE CITY VIEW
ONLY). The town holds one contract at a time (RUN TWO 9d7feb3b, 'one contract in one place'). So to swap one job for another
in the town he is standing in: leave the town, open the phone, drop the job, walk back in, open the board, take the new one.
Five steps and a trip for one decision. Nobody measured that path; it is the coordinator's miss.

## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
One contract at a time. The active contract sits in a panel on the world map and can be cancelled there; cancelling costs
relation with whoever hired you (our numbers already carry it: cancelled -10, slices/BOHEMIA_SETTLEMENT_SCREEN.html).

(b) TAPPING A NEW JOB WHILE YOU HAVE ONE asks once: 'You are on a job for {who}. Drop it and take this one? ({cost})'. YES
