================================================================================

Same man, same injury, same second. The only difference is one tap.

WHY THE CARD SAID NOTHING. The promise built by [down not dead] covers two lists
and both of them are DERIVED. The family loop takes only the person you MARRIED
and the children you HAD (rel spouse or child), so the father you were born to is
not on it. The other list is ctYours(), and bohemia_company.js says in its own
words why there is no roster: "NO ROSTER, NO STATE, NO ADD, NO REMOVE. Pure
functions of a snapshot." That is right for a company. It is a hole in a promise.

================================================================================
AND THE HOLE GETS BIGGER, NOT SMALLER, THE LONGER YOU PLAY
================================================================================
ctYours() is recomputed out of BONDS and WITNESSES on every call. A witness is
somebody carrying a deed of yours in their head, and a deed fades on a three-week
halflife (DEED_HALFLIFE, 21 days, in the standing web). So the person the game
called yours this morning can quietly stop being yours, and the moment they do
the protection stops, with nothing said and nobody at fault.

A NAME HE TAPPED DOES NOT DECAY. That is the whole reason this is a MARK and not
another computation, and it is deliberately the opposite shape from the down
module, where "are they down" IS a question against the clock. One cannot help
decaying. The other cannot decay at all.

================================================================================
WHAT IT COSTS, WHICH IS WHAT MAKES IT A CHOICE AND NOT A FREE WIN
================================================================================
The coordinator armed this row off the craft: in the games that made companions
unkillable, the weight of a fall is WHAT YOU LOSE WHILE THEY ARE DOWN. So:

  the length      the injury the down module already rolled. Nothing new.
  the thing lost  their share of the carry, which in this world is the crew:
                  a hurt person leaves it and comes back when they are well
  on the card     RAY COSTS YOU / GIVING YOU NOTHING, 7 DAYS

Measured on the glass: crew 46 -> 45 the moment somebody fell.

NOT ONE NUMBER IN THIS FEATURE IS NEW. No cap on how many he can lock (a cap is a
number nobody ruled and it is the endgame shape he named). No price to lock. No
rung. The lengths are the down module's three, from his own examples.

IT IS NOT A LOCK-ONLY COST, on purpose. A hurt person carries nothing whether or
not he ever tapped their name; what the lock buys is that they COME BACK. A cost
that only bit the people he protected would punish the promise.

THREE THINGS I GOT WRONG, ALL THREE CAUGHT BY DRIVING IT

1. THE ROW RENDERED THREE PLAIN NAMES AND NOT ONE TAP TARGET. famLiving() hands
   back the STORED member, whose keys are role, name, age, draft, alive. THERE IS
   NO ID ON IT. The tree is the id authority. The fix asks the tree and refuses

   place in the whole file -- spelling it twice is the same defect as the body
   ladder written twice that put a speech bubble on somebody's face last round.

2. THE TAP DID NOTHING, AND NOTHING THREW. #daycardIn is ONE PERMANENT ELEMENT
   that every card refills with innerHTML, which is why cardShow assigns
   i.onclick, a property, instead of adding a listener. addEventListener STACKS.
   Every redraw added another handler, one tap ran all of them, and the lock
   toggled once per listener: on, off, on, off. With an even number of redraws
   behind him the tap did nothing at all, silently. One listener, for good.

3. AND THE GATE'S OWN RESET DID NOT RESET, which cost four claims. Clearing
   localStorage does NOT clear the book: ctLockLoad only overwrites the in-memory
   copy when storage HAS something, exactly like the ctDownLoad it was modelled
   on. So the tap under test was toggling a lock that was already on, and the
   whole block measured the feature running backwards. It resets through
   ctLockTap now, the player's own door, and THE RESET HAS TO PROVE ITSELF before
   the block that depends on it runs.
