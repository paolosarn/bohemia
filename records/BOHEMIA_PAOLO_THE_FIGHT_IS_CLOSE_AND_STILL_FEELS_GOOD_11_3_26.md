## WHAT THE COORDINATOR FOUND (DIFFICULTY's own measure, records/BOHEMIA_DIFFICULTY_ROUND_1_THE_CURVE_11_3_26.md, BUILD 11/3zzzn)
- The curve was measured as WINS ONLY (the live fight headless, AUTO both sides, 20 fights a point). On the hardest level a Lone Wolf
  won 95/85/45/75/85 of 100 over days 1 to 10 after the retune; on the easiest you win everything to about day 20.
- Nobody measured HOW CLOSE a fight is: men struck down, health left, turns a man stood one hit from death, how often one enemy hit takes
  half a man. A fight you win with every man at 90% is not a fight. That is "I'm unstoppable".
- Cheap gear on a rare man wins: the price of a piece does not match what it does, or the enemy's hits do not threaten armour at all.

## THE LAW (rule 370, his words; newest date wins over 351(c)(d) where they differ)
1. CLOSE, AND IT FEELS GOOD: on every difficulty a routine fight is won with cost (a man hurt badly, a man down now and then, the
   medicine spent); a tough one can be lost; the player wins by playing well, never by default. Even the easiest level bites (Battle
   Brothers' easiest still felt hard). The hardest early game is still not hell (351(c) stands for that).
2. ONE-SHOT FEAR IS REAL: some enemies can take half of an unarmoured man, or kill him, in one hit (Battle Brothers' two-handers and
   crossbows: in our skin the sledge, the deer rifle's headshot, the shotgun at one tile, a beast's charge). Armour is what saves you,
   so armour matters.
3. PRICE IS POWER: cheap gear is cheap because it is weak; the good piece costs what it is worth (Battle Brothers' price-to-protection
   curve as the floor). A rare man in cheap gear is a rare man in danger.
4. RARE MEN ARE GOOD, NOT GODS: a rare hire's edge is a few points where it matters, not a fight won alone.
5. THE ENEMY KEEPS UP: the enemy's gear, numbers and champions grow with the day and with your company's strength, and they fight
   smart (rule 366, COMBAT 7).
6. MEASURED BY CLOSENESS, NOT WINS: the gate simulates fights at each level and day and reads: win share, men struck down, health left,
   near-deaths (a man one hit from death), the share of enemy hits over half a man; each inside a band per level. Battle Brothers'
   numbers from the library are the floor for every band.
7. MORE TO FIGHT (his 'more content'): enemy kinds that each threaten in their own way (the hitter, the shooter, the swarm, the
   champion), first from Battle Brothers' roster lessons we have not used yet.
8. ALL IN TUNING'S ONE TABLE (rule 21, the 9/27 law): every number a player feels lives in the data, a modder can edit it.
