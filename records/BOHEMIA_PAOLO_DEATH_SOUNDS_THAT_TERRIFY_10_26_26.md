## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
Every unit type has its own death sounds (several per type so the same scream never plays twice in a row) and its own hurt
sounds, and the killing blow plays the weapon's flesh sound under it. A fatality (a head off, a skull smashed) adds its own crunch.
The sound tells you who died before you look.

## THE RULING (rule 235)
(a) A DEATH IS HEARD, AND IT IS TERRIFYING: every death in a fight plays a death sound that is frightening: a real person dying,
    inside the game's own analog horror sound (rule ANALOG HORROR; never cartoon, never a stock game grunt).
(b) MANY KINDS, NEVER THE SAME TWICE IN A ROW: at least these, each with several takes: the bone that breaks (the blunt blow, the
    fall), the blade (the wet cut, the breath that goes out), the gun (the hit, the body dropping, the gear on the asphalt), the
    beast (the bite and the drag), the slow one (the gasp, the choke, the last breath), the armour that gives (a car door or a
    plate cracking, then the man). The killing weapon picks the kind.
(c) FROM REAL MATERIAL (the second votes: no sand, no noise recipe): real recordings or real foley (celery and a wet towel for bone,
    a voice actor's breath), processed through the game's machine (the tape, the warble, the room); SOUNDS picks the sources and
    their licences (rule 165: no paid packs without his word).
(d) ON THE BEAT (rule 221(b)): the hit lands on the next sixteenth like every effect; the death's tail may ring past it.
(e) HIS MEN AND THE ENEMY: his own men's deaths are the worst sound in the game; an enemy's is still horrible; a beast's is the
    animal's.
(f) THE GATE: every death in rolled fights plays a death take from its kind, never the same take twice in a row, in the fight's
    mix, on the beat; at least six kinds with at least three takes each; each take is real material (its source line in the
    record).
(g) SOUNDS [death sounds that terrify] after its current row; line 157 on his list; EYES listens.
