## THE FINDING: GET UP WAS WEARING THREE EDGE TREATMENTS AT ONCE
.dcgo is named in the city's own object rule near the top of the file: a bevelled box,
border 0, radius 0, a clip-path cut corner, the same shape the street chips wear. Then a
later rule, same specificity, further down the file, put border-radius:9px and a 1px
hairline straight back on.
MEASURED ON THE REAL CARD BEFORE A LINE WAS CHANGED: clip-path YES, border 1, radius 9.
The bevel was being drawn underneath a rounded hairline box nobody meant to keep.

THE CARD'S OWN BORDER WAS THE SAME ROT IN REVERSE. #daycardIn declared
`border:1px solid var(--line); border-radius:12px`, and the skin has cancelled both with
!important since round three. They have painted nothing for days. They still counted as two
tells, and they still told the next person to read this file that the card is a rounded
hairline box. Round three's own note said DELETE rather than override; this one was missed.

A DECLARATION THAT A LATER RULE CANCELS READS EXACTLY LIKE A DECLARATION THAT WORKS.
That is why the gate asks the rendered card and never the stylesheet.

## THE DASHED EDGE WAS CARRYING MEANING, SO IT WAS TRANSLATED AND NOT DELETED
.rpend is the pending row and its border was DASHED. That is two tells at once, but it was
not decoration: dashed against solid is how the card said "this one is not settled yet".
Deleting it would have removed a tell at the cost of a distinction the player can see,
which is a worse trade than keeping the tell.
In the object language a thing that is not there yet has no BODY. The settled rows stand
proud (lit rim above, dark base below); the pending row is RECESSED, a slot with nothing in
it, using the same two skin values the pressed state already uses, turned over. Nothing new
was invented, and the two still read as two different things at a glance.
THE GATE HOLDS THE DIFFERENCE, NOT THE DECORATION: a pending row must not look like a
settled one. Mutation-proved by putting the dashed border back.

## THE MUTATION THAT DID NOT BITE, AND WHY IT IS THE BEST NEWS IN THE ROUND
The first mutation put the hairline and the 9px radius back on .dcbtn, and the gate stayed
12/0. That is not the gate being weak. .dcbtn is now in the SKIN's rule, which sets border:0
and the skin's own corner with !important, so putting a hairline back in the plain rule
changes nothing that renders. The skin is protecting the row from exactly the regression the
mutation tried to cause.
BUT A MUTATION THAT DOES NOT BITE PROVES NOTHING ABOUT THE LEG, so it was replaced rather
than counted: .dcx is a class the skin does NOT cover, which is the honest case (somebody
adds a control to the card and does not wire it to the skin). Putting a rounded hairline on
that one turns both legs red and names it.
  .dcbtn mutated  ->  12/0, because the skin overrides it
  .dcx mutated    ->  10/2, "nothing on the card is drawn with a one-pixel border [.dcx 1]"
Two different facts, and reporting the first as a proof would have been a lie about the leg.

## AND PLUMBER'S FRONT-PAGE FINDING, MEASURED AND ANSWERED (rule 12)
The front page carries: "#daycard is inset:0 and SITS OVER ALL EIGHT DIRECTION BUTTONS ON
BOOT, so a driven thumb has nothing to press and the alpha never reaches its first step."
REPRODUCED ON THE GLASS: on boot, #daycard wins the tap at every pad control's centre, 0 of
6 reach. So the observation is real.
IT IS NOT A GAME BUG. The wake card is a MODAL BY CONSTRUCTION -- the morning is a place you
are, and you leave it by pressing GET UP. Covering the pad is what a modal does. The only
question worth asking is whether the pad comes back when the card is answered:
    press GET UP  ->  card display: none  ->  5 of 6 pad controls reach
The one that does not is #pad itself, whose centre is the face, which IS the action button.
So the speed gate's driven thumb has nothing to press because the driver never dismisses the
card. That is the instrument, not the game, and this lane has made the identical mistake: my
own [one door] probe reported a row missing for exactly this reason on 9/14.
NAMED FOR PLUMBER AND RUN, not fixed here, and no board row was touched.
