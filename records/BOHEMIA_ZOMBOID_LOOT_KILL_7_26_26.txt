WHY IT FAILED — THE REAL CAUSE, NOT THE SYMPTOM
1. I EMULATED A PACE HE HAD ALREADY IMPLIED HE DID NOT WANT. The whole page is
   built on Zomboid's per-item time economy: eleven separate timed takes to empty
   one kitchen counter, then eleven more to pack them. His reaction to it was
   "imagine if that went by really quick instead of really slowly I might give a
   fuck about it." The mechanism I found most interesting (weight is time,
   organising costs more than grabbing) is EXACTLY the thing that made it not
   fun. I mistook "this is a clever rule" for "this is a good rule for us".
2. I TREATED HIS BACKLOG PHRASE AS A SPEC. He wrote "containers, search, weight,
   the tension of rummaging a house" weeks of context ago. I built precisely
   that, faithfully, without asking whether the TENSION he meant was slowness.
   It was not. He meant the interest of what you find, not the cost of picking
   it up.
3. I SHIPPED VOLUME WHERE HE WANTS MINIMALISM. 65 real loot tables and hundreds
   of distinct named items, presented as a feature ("the game's own loot data is
   in the page"). He wants FEWER kinds than State of Decay, expressed as
   resources with a count. The thing I was proudest of is the thing he does not
   want in Bohemia at all.
4. THE PAGE WAS A FLOOR PLAN, NOT A GAME. Noted in its own honest limits before
   he ever saw it, which means I knew the presentation was wrong and shipped it
   anyway because the mechanics were green.

WHAT THE GATES DID NOT CATCH, AND CANNOT
  245 green checks proved every rule was faithfully ported and every loop closed.
  Not one of them could ask "is this fun" or "is this the pace he wants". This is
  the third time this week a lane has shipped something green and unwanted. The
  gates are not the problem; using green as evidence of quality is. GREEN PROVES
  NON-VIOLATION AND NOTHING ELSE (autonomy doctrine 4).

THE LESSON I AM WRITING DOWN SO IT BINDS ME
  A REFERENCE IS NOT NEUTRAL. Choosing which game to emulate is already a design
  decision, and emulating a game whose pacing is the opposite of what Paolo has
  asked for produces a faithful, gated, worthless artifact. Before the next
  emulation: state in one line what FEEL the target game has, and check that line
  against his standing rulings. If it conflicts, the emulation is dead before it
  is written.

WHAT SURVIVES
  - THE CONTAINER IS THE CONTRACT (yield keyed on district + container kind).
    Compatible with the new law and worth keeping.
  - The measured facts in the teardown, as a record.
  Everything about the time economy is dead for our purposes.
