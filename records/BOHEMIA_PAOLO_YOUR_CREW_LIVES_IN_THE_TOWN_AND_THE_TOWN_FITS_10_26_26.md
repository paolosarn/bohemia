## THE RULING (rule 218)
(a) YOUR WHOLE CREW IS IN THE TOWN: when he enters a settlement, every man (and woman) of his company is there in the picture,
    each in his own look and gear (rule 177), doing something a person does in a town: drinking a beer at the bar or a stall,
    flirting with someone, talking with the townsfolk, walking the street, sitting, smoking, looking at the stalls; never standing
    in a line like chess pieces (rule 130(a)). Each one his own mix of activities at his own offsets; his traits may lean it (the
    drinker drinks). Tapping one of them opens him on the crew screen.
(b) THE TOWN HOLDS THEM ALL: the picture fits the whole crew (up to the biggest company an origin allows) plus the town's own
    people without crowding a door, a stall or a button; the walk trace (rule 169) keeps them on the ground; inside RUN's memory
    line (rule 173) and at frame time on a phone.
(c) NO SLIDING: the settlement screen shows the WHOLE town at once, fitted to the screen ('zoomed out perfectly'), in portrait and
    landscape, with the phone's corner as a margin (rule 213); no swipe or scroll is needed to reach anything; 10/25's 'a swipe
    moves the town' (EYES E30) is replaced by the fit. A pinch out still leaves for the map (211(c)).
(d) THE GATE: phone and computer, portrait and landscape, every town: every crew member is on the picture doing an activity (none
    idle in a row, none on a roof); every door, stall and person is on screen without a pan; frame time and memory inside RUN's
    lines with the biggest company; tapping a crew member opens him.
(e) RUN THREE [your crew lives in the town] (the crew in the picture and what they do; the hall is beside it); RUN TWO [the town
    fits the screen] (the fit, with its town-frame row); ANIMATION [the town's activities] (drink, flirt, talk, walk, sit, smoke
    clips through the rig); line 140 on his list; EYES checks.
