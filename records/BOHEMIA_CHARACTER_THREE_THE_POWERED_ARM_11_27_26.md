## WHAT IT IS (real; the research is CHARACTER 2's record and ECONOMY THREE's table)

socket below the elbow, no surgery. Salvaged and refitted: the socket was made for another man's arm, so it is taped where it meets
his; the battery is not its own, an outside pack taped on with its lead run into the socket. Never chrome.
