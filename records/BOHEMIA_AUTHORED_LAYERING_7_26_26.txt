=== BOHEMIA — HIS AUTHORED LAYERING WAS BEING THROWN AWAY (7/26/26) ===
Lane: CHARACTER. Paolo's report, and the bug it found.

HIS WORDS
  "It didn't used to be like this... we made the rig and we put the layers in
   the positions and how they layer, why can't you just refer to that? How do
   you not see this shit tweak out when it's facing different directions,
   facing east and west, not just in particular east, like the pixels on the
   torso tweak out."

HE WAS DESCRIBING A REAL BUG AND HE NAMED THE CAUSE HIMSELF.
Measured on the shipped alpha, every clip x 24 phases x 8 facings, counting how
many times the DRAW ORDER changes mid-animation:

    facing   order changes    clips affected
    S               0               0
    N               0               0
    E             150              48
    W             106              38
    SE            141              46
    NE            144              47
    NW            164              47
    SW            151              48

His authored layerOverride was honoured on exactly the two facings where
nothing re-sorted -- N and S -- and discarded on the other six, where
handOrder() rebuilt the draw order from scratch on EVERY FRAME. Two rules did
it, and both INFER depth from how far a joint has drifted past a +-2.5px
deadband:
    - the rest-relative hand rule (E, W, and all four diagonals)
    - the NE/NW ARM-UNIT DEPTH rule
Mid-swing a joint crosses the deadband and the order FLIPS: an arm or a hand
jumps from behind the torso to in front of it between one frame and the next.
On E and W both arms sit INSIDE the 8px torso footprint, so that flip repaints
a band of torso. That is the tweak-out, in the exact place he pointed at.

"IT DIDN'T USED TO BE LIKE THIS" -- also true. These are accretions. The
rest-relative rule dates from 7/2/26 and the arm-unit rule from 7/20/26, each
added to fix a specific complaint, each adding another per-frame guess on top
of the layering he had already authored by hand.

THE FIX: both guesses retired. Draw order is his paoloOrder(d) -- the
layerOverride he painted -- and it stays put for the whole clip.
    order changes after:  S 0 | N 0 | E 2 | W 4 | SE 4 | NE 4 | NW 4 | SW 4
The residual 2-4 are deadeye and crouch-aim-2h only: the GUN-UNIT rule, which
survives because a clip DECLARES it holds a weapon. A declaration cannot
oscillate; a guess can. (Its aim threshold can still cross mid-sweep on those
two clips -- named here, not hidden, and small enough to leave alone until he
says otherwise.)

TRUTH HIERARCHY: this supersedes the dynamic-hand-layer ruling (7/2/26) and the
NE/NW arm-unit ruling (7/20/26) on the six affected facings. Both were his;
this is his too, and NEWEST DATE WINS.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT FIX: the profile body is still 8px of painted torso (see
records/BOHEMIA_EAST_PROFILE_FINDING_7_26_26.txt). Stopping the layer flip
stops the popping. It does not give the side view depth it never had.
