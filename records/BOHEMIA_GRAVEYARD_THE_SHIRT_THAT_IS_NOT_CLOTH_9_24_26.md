## WHY IT WAS BUILT, SO THE MISTAKE IS LEGIBLE

His instruction was "analog horror direction MEETS Rick Owens meets Balenciaga meets
Bottega Veneta". Two rounds of runway work answered the middle two names and BOTH
RECORDS SAID PLAINLY that neither answered the analog-horror half, because a
better-cut trouser is not a wrong thing. RNWY-16 is Bottega's real house code:
FW22 opened on a white tank and straight jeans made entirely of leather, tooled to
look like cotton. On paper it is the bible's rule 1 wearing clothes — the ordinary
frame with one wrong thing — and the sentence naming the wrong thing was six words:
that shirt is not cloth.

## WHY IT FAILED, AND THE REASONING WAS WRONG AT THE ROOT

**1. THE WHOLE IDEA LIVED IN A SENTENCE, NOT IN THE PIXELS.** The garment only reads
as trompe-l'oeil IF YOU ALREADY KNOW THAT IS WHAT IT IS. Without the caption it is a
plaid shirt with harder contrast. I even measured that and shipped it anyway: 12.3%
of the painted body moved, worst pixel 122 of 765. I read those numbers as proof it
worked. THEY ARE EQUALLY CONSISTENT WITH "a slightly darker shirt", and I never asked
which of the two a person seeing it cold would say.

**2. I SUBSTITUTED THE MECHANISM FOR THE EFFECT.** My own RNWY-16 line says the
sprite-scale tell is SHEEN — a hard specular band where cloth diffuses. I could not
build a sheen renderer because garment ART is COOK's, so I reached the *same numbers*
through the ramp and told myself it was the same read. It is not. A value spread is
the mechanism; the specular band is the effect; I shipped the mechanism and called
the job done. **When you cannot build the thing, the honest move is to say so and
hand over the spec, not to ship the nearest thing you are allowed to build.**

**3. THE HORROR WAS AN ARGUMENT, NOT A FEELING.** Every line of the record argued
from the bible's rule 1 to the conclusion that this is analog horror. None of it was
a person looking at the picture and feeling anything. He looked at it and felt
nothing, which is the only measurement that counts on a taste item.

**4. AND IT BROKE A RULE HE HAD ALREADY GIVEN ME.** Rule 32(f), from the same vote:
"this game isn't in first person, when would I see this?" — a VOTE item is a frame
off a play surface or a tile at game scale. I sent a four-up contact sheet of bodies
on a tan background. Even if the garment had been good, the PICTURE was a study, and
a study belongs in records.

  different garment name.
