## THE FINDING IN ONE BREATH

The chats were failing at the one thing a language model is worst at: placing pixels as numbers in code. Every road that works moves the pixel-placing to a machine built for it and leaves the chat doing what it is good at: naming parts, writing shape code, measuring, and comparing. Nothing on this page needs a graphics card, a subscription or a per-call bill, and nothing adds a tap for him.

## THE RULES ADOPTED (rule 108 ROUND EIGHT)

- NO CHAT DRAWS A PIXEL OR A CURVE IN CODE. A chat names parts, writes shape code, measures and compares. The pixels come from Aseprite's scripts, the vector shrink, the 3D render, the pack cut, or the gun. A pixel written as a number in JavaScript is a gate failure (PLUMBER [the art pipe gate]).
- ONE CAMERA, REAL METERS, THE MODEL TURNS. DIRECTION owns the master rig; a sheet whose meters-per-pixel does not match the floor is refused.
- THE PACKS ARE THE RULER. Every threshold in the twin gate is measured from the packs against themselves; RED means further from the pack than the pack is from itself.
- THE BOX IS NEVER THE ONLY RUNNER, and its label never appears in the public repo's workflows.
- HIS STEPS ARE ONE TAP. Every row above was admitted because its first column is zero.
