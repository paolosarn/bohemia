## What it is

The fourth time he has said the map is too small (9/27 'how many pixels the Battle Brothers map is', 10/1, 10/2 'bigger and better zoomed out, for a fat minute', 10/9 'how many pixels, we haven't made any progress'), and the first time he has named the cause: two references pulling the map two ways. Rule 38a chased Battle Brothers' pixel count; rule 50 chased Pocket City 2's zoom range (one pinch from a bench to the whole city). The map is a 96 by 96 grid of cells drawn to fill his glass, so it is as big as his glass and no bigger: not Battle Brothers' size, not Pocket City 2's either.

## The ruling

1. PICK ONE: BATTLE BROTHERS. The map's SIZE, its ZOOM RANGE and its TRAVEL are Battle Brothers': a world the party crosses in days, many places, roads, the party marker small against it, the camera zooming the way Battle Brothers' does. Rule 50 (THE ZOOM RANGE IS A PILLAR, from Pocket City 2) is SUPERSEDED for the map: Pocket City 2 goes back to the drop-in transition only (7/1). The person-on-a-bench end of the zoom is the settlement screen and the fight, not the map.
2. THE GRID GROWS OR BREAKS: 'a larger grid or more grids' are his words and both are allowed. The 96 by 96 valley is one neighbourhood of what the map must be: the whole Las Vegas region (the valley, Henderson, Boulder City, Lake Mead, the Strip, the corridors out, I-15 south toward the dam and north toward the test site, US-95), with the city as the dense middle and desert, hills and shore around it (rule 46's fifteen terrains are the ground), so that a crossing takes Battle Brothers' days and a settlement is a destination, never a tap away.
3. THE NUMBERS ARE MEASURED, NOT FELT: how many tiles Battle Brothers' map is across, how many days a crossing takes at walking speed, how many settlements and locations it holds, and how far the camera zooms in and out; from the library (reference/library/battle_brothers/01_WORLDMAP.md) and Grok ask 26; ours meets or beats each number as the bare minimum, the same shape as rule 38a's pixel floor.
4. WHO: RUN [the map at Battle Brothers' size] FIRST, above everything on its board, with WORLD [the region] (the hold on WORLD is lifted for this one row: a rule he gave is never held); FACTIONS' home bases and roaming parties scale to the region; EYES measures our map against Battle Brothers' by the four numbers; PLUMBER's native map budget (rule 40g) carries the larger grid at 60 on a phone.
