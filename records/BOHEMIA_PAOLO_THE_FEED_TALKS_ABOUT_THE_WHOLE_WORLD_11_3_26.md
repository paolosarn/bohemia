## THE LAW (rule 352)
1. THE FEED TEACHES THE WHOLE WORLD, not only the valley: a quick glance at the phone teaches you the world he built (the 9/4 law of the
   feed: 'reading it teaches the world'). A real share of posts is about the world beyond Las Vegas: the rest of the country, Mexico,
   and the world (coordinator's default: about 4 posts in 10; the rest stays local).
2. INTERNATIONAL, THE WAY TWITTER IS: accounts from other places, their own voices, a word of their own language now and then, their own
   prices, weather, rumours, griefs and jokes; news travelling late and wrong.
3. FROM HIS CANON FIRST: the world he built lives in the project files (the collapse's many causes and the immortality bubble, laws/
   BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_COLLAPSE_ORIGIN_AND_DEATH_MODEL_7_18_26.md; the dollar's last month and the world going quiet, the ECONOMY rounds; the
   Amalgamation, the Network and the machine, records/BOHEMIA_AI_SLOP_THE_MACHINE_AND_THE_WORLD_9_30_26.md; the factions, the beasts' labs,
   the three acts; the GDD and the laws masters). Every world post is grounded in that canon and in what really happens when a currency
   dies (Argentina 2001, Zimbabwe 2008, Venezuela, Lebanon, the Soviet collapse: real, cited in the record).
4. NO NEW LORE BY THE BACK DOOR: a post may carry the world's texture freely, and rumours may be wrong (his 9/24 YES, 'the surer, the
   wronger'), but no post states a NEW big fact as true (who rules a country now, what caused a thing) unless the canon has it; anything
   that would be a new pillar is listed in the record as a question for him, not posted.
5. THE NETWORK LIES: some 'international' accounts are the Network's manufactured voices (the Amalgamation's psyop in act one, already in
   feed_kinds.json's accounts); analog horror at the source, never said outright.
6. THE MAX HOLDS: 120 characters (rule 350); draft:true; Spanglish only where a person would, never the player.
