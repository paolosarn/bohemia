## AND THE PHOTOGRAPH CAUGHT TWO THINGS THE SOURCE COULD NOT

The first build looked correct in every source check and was **broken on the glass**:

1. **The build stamp printed straight THROUGH the WAIT button.** `#loadwrap` is
   absolutely placed over the splash, but `#fronttap` and `#buildstamp` are siblings
   in the splash's own flow, so they landed on top of the terminal. "DEMO - BUILD
   9/23a - ONE FIGHT IS ONE SONG" ran across the middle of the button and the button
   was unreadable.
2. **The button's top edge sat on the bar**, by two pixels.

The stamp is **not** hidden -- he needs to see which build he is on (7/20, after "I
didn't see nothing new" twice) and a loading screen is the first place he looks. It
sits under the bar now as a line of the report instead of floating across it.

**BOTH ARE NOW GATE LEGS, MEASURED AS BOXES AND NOT AS OPINIONS**: the button may not
overlap the bar, the stamp may not overlap the button, and the screen must carry
exactly ONE wrong thing -- not zero, which is a screenshot, and not two, which the
bible calls a haunted house.
