## THE TWO RESEARCH ANSWERS

### PRINTED MEAT: THE BOTTLENECK IS BIOREACTOR VOLUME, NOT ANIMALS

Cultivated meat today costs **far more** than conventional, not less — one producer sells around
**$34/lb against under $5/lb for ground beef.** The reason is capacity: hitting even **1% of the
protein market needs 220-440 million litres of bioreactor volume, and total world capacity tops
out near 200 million.** Cost falls only with **very large vessels** — 100,000-litre class.

**So he is right that it gets cheaper than real meat, and the research says WHY: at scale.** That
makes it a perfect act-2 city-builder mechanic, because the boss is not an animal problem, it is a
**volume** problem. And it inverts the obvious:

**REAL MEAT BECOMES THE LUXURY** (ten years on, herds are gone) **and printed protein becomes the
staple** — but only once you have the vessels running. A real steak is a high-roller item in a
city that runs on vat protein, which is about as Vegas as an economy gets.

So THE HERD is replaced by **THE VAT**, and its lock is capacity.

### PLASTIC TO OIL: HIS INSTINCT IS SOUND, AND LAS VEGAS IS THE OIL FIELD

Pyrolysis converts **60-85% of waste plastic into liquid fuel** at 450-600°C, and the output is
roughly **diesel-equivalent (~45 MJ/kg)**. It **works at small scale**, and the thing that decides
whether you get consistent, usable oil rather than sludge is a **catalyst** (ZSM-5 zeolite is the
worked example). The honest catch is that it is **energy-hungry**.

That splits exactly the way he called it:
- **ACT 1: crude, low-yield, no catalyst.** You can crack plastic into a dirty oil in small
  batches. It runs an engine badly and it costs you fuel to make fuel.
- **ACT 2: with a catalyst from THE CHEMIST, yield and quality jump, and it mass-produces.**

**AND THE SETTING MAKES IT SING: LAS VEGAS IS MADE OF PLASTIC.** Every sign face, every slot
housing, every hotel fitting, every one of the acres of casino interior. **The city is the oil
field**, and that is a far better fantasy than finding a tanker. THE REFINERY is renamed
**THE CRACKER**, because pyrolysis is literally cracking.
