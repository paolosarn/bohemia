## THE WORKFLOW, FIXED (rule 108, amended)
1. REQUEST CARD, FREE. A chat puts a card in the VOTE tab before anything is made: what it will make (one street lamp), the nine pack tiles it will use as the reference (shown), the palette, the size, and the exact cost from the tool's free cost check (for example 'about 18 cents'). Making the card costs nothing.
2. HIS YES. He votes YES on the request card. A NO kills it for free.
3. THEN THE CALL. His YES lands in the registry (through his export, as every vote does); the repo's Action reads the approved requests and only then calls the tool, once per approved card, never more than the card said.
4. THE RESULT CARD. The sheet (pack tile left, new asset right, true size) comes back to the VOTE tab for his YES, NO or FINAL on the result. A NO here costs nothing more; the card said its price and that was all.
5. THE FREE CREDITS FIRST. Retro Diffusion gives free credits on a new account; the first request cards use those, so the first sheets cost nothing while he judges whether the tool is worth the $30.

Two taps per asset instead of one, and no cent before his word. His $30, or $100, can only be spent in the amounts he approved, card by card.
