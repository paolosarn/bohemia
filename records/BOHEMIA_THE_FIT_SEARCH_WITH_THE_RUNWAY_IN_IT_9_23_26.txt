HONEST LIMITS, all three stated rather than discovered later.
  1. THE HEAD AXIS IS GONE. The original ran heads and masks; I spent that budget on
     a FEET axis instead, because last round measured that both runway poles are
     reachable from the feet alone and the original was blind to that lever. COST: a
     set that separates on hats is not reachable from this run, so this is NOT a
     strict superset of the original.
  2. GREEDY IS A FLOOR, NOT A CEILING. Farthest-point does not prove the true maximum
     -- that is set-packing and NP-hard -- so the count is a floor on the answer. The
     original says the same about itself and it is repeated here rather than dropped.
  3. THIS IS THE REGISTER, NOT THE HORROR. A runway silhouette answers "Rick Owens
     meets Balenciaga". It does NOT answer "analog horror meets", because the bible's
     rule 1 wants one wrong thing and a better-cut coat is not one. RNWY-16, the
     garment that is not what it looks like, is the bridge and it needs new art.

COLOURWAYS ARE DELIBERATELY NOT IN THE POOL. STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR: the search is on
SILHOUETTE, so a cocoon coat is ONE candidate however many ramps it exists in. The 12
faction colourways added last round are what lets the winner be dressed in a faction's
own colour afterwards. Putting them in the pool would inflate the count with identical
shapes and make the answer look better than it is -- the original tool's own warning.

NOT WIRED (rule 18): this proposes a set and edits no faction. Which shape belongs to
which faction is HIS, exactly as the original said: "WHICH SHAPE BELONGS TO WHOM IS
HIS." The picture is in VOTE.
