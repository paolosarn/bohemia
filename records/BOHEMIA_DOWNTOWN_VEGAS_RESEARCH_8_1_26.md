# BOHEMIA — DOWNTOWN LAS VEGAS, RESEARCHED (8/1/26)

Paolo, 8/1: *"Do some big brain online research of what downtown Las Vegas looks like
I know it's gonna be a couple districts wide and we're gonna make different downtown
districts but yeah like for example in Vegas there's no roundabouts in downtown. I'm
pretty sure so yeah I would even go as far as to say you're like 15% done with that."*

**DOWNTOWN: 15%.** He is right, and the research says the reason is worse than a
roundabout. Sources at the bottom.

## THE FINDING THAT CHANGES EVERYTHING — a cell IS a block

The Fremont Street Experience canopy is **1,375 ft over four blocks**. That is
**344 ft per block**, or **105 m**.

A Bohemia district cell is **128 tiles × 0.75 m = 96 m = 315 ft.**

**ONE DISTRICT CELL IS ONE DOWNTOWN BLOCK.** Not a downtown. A block.

So the module I shipped — four quadrant blocks, an internal street grid crossing in
the middle, a roundabout, a skybridge between two of them — was **an entire miniature
downtown squeezed into a single city block.** At the real scale those "streets"
crossing inside it are alleys the width of a boulevard, and the four "blocks" are
four buildings. That is the 15%, and it is a scale error, not a decoration error.

**And this is exactly what he already said:** *"it's gonna be a couple districts wide
and we're gonna make different downtown districts."* He was describing the correct
answer. A downtown is BLOCKS ACROSS THE MAP, each cell one block, each block a
different kind of block. Not one cell trying to be a city.

## NO ROUNDABOUTS. He is right, and here is why

Downtown Las Vegas is a **1905 railroad townsite**. Senator William Clark's railroad
auctioned **1,200 lots** inside the townsite in a single day in May 1905, on a
surveyed rectangular grid laid out to serve the tracks. A townsite grid platted for
lot sales is orthogonal, block after block, with signalised intersections — a
roundabout is a 20th-century traffic device that got retrofitted into suburbs and
campuses, not into a 1905 lot-auction grid. The roundabout was invented by me and it
is gone.

## THE DEFINING PHYSICAL FACT: A THIRD OF IT IS PARKING

**About 33% of downtown Las Vegas is off-street surface parking** (Parking Reform
Network study, cited in the Las Vegas Weekly compact-city piece). One third. That is
the single most characteristic thing about the place from above, and my rebuild had
almost none of it — a fragment of surface lot in one corner. In the real thing the
lot is not a leftover; it is HALF THE BLOCK, and the buildings crowd onto what is
left.

## WHAT A DOWNTOWN VEGAS BLOCK ACTUALLY IS

- **A STREET WALL of NARROW LOTS.** The 1905 auction sold lots, not superblocks, so
  the frontage is a ROW of separate narrow buildings sharing party walls, each a
  different width, height and colour, built right out to the sidewalk. Arts District
  storefronts still standing date to the **1930s**. This is the single biggest visual
  difference from what I built: a downtown frontage is a row of many buildings, never
  one mass with a tower on it.
- **A MID-BLOCK ALLEY.** Townsite blocks are two rows of lots back to back onto a
  service alley — that is where the deliveries, the dumpsters and the back doors are.
- **LOW, with a few tall.** Mostly one to three storeys, punctuated.
- **BLADE SIGNS AND MARQUEES over the sidewalk.** Downtown is where the neon started.
  The shapes are ours; every word on them is Paolo's.
- **VACANT PARCELS.** Buildings came down and were never replaced, and the gap is
  either dirt or, more often, more parking.

## THE DOWNTOWN SET — his "different downtown districts"

The city's own master plan runs **12 downtown planning districts**. Ours, proposed,
each one cell = one block:

1. **`downtown`** — the commercial block. Street wall of narrow storefronts, a
   mid-rise, mid-block alley, and the surface lot that is a third of it. *(rebuilt
   this turn)*
2. **`downtown_arts`** — the Arts District block: an **18-block** stretch of 1930s
   storefronts and low warehouses, saw-tooth roofs, roll-up doors, murals.
3. **`downtown_civic`** — the courthouse / city-hall block: one civic mass set back
   behind a plaza, which is the ONE downtown block type that is not a street wall.
4. **`downtown_lot`** — the parking block. A whole block that is nothing but surface
   parking with a kerb wall around it. Real, common, and it makes the built blocks
   read as built.

**RESERVED FOR PAOLO, NOT PROPOSED:** the Fremont Street casino core. Casinos,
resorts and the Strip landmarks are his hand by law and are never auto-generated. The
Fremont Street Experience — a five-block pedestrian mall under a barrel-vault canopy
90 ft high — is the most recognisable thing downtown and it belongs to him.
