et al. 2021, Nature: the Columbian mammoth's genome, a hybrid of two lineages). Mother: an Asian elephant. Mojave: a cow and her calf walk
the wash at night to the springs. 3.6 m at the shoulder, 8 to 10 tonnes. A giant (one animal, a rare hunt, never a pack; WORLD 9's rule).

forward, out, up and in) and a trunk tube from the muzzle to the knees; grey skin under sparse dark hair (a warm-country mammoth, the
surrogate's skin), the lab's plate, collar and stitched patch. Sheet 2.7 MB (slices/fight_rig/beast_columbian_mammoth.webp) and its packed
atlas. Its outline is its own at the fight's size and the map's fifth (THE BESTIARY FLOOR F4). Strip and kin card:
records/target/bestiary_lab_seven/columbian_mammoth_strip.png, the_mammoth_beside_its_kin.png.

## 3. THE ONE THING: THE TRAMPLE (slices/BOHEMIA_FIGHT.html trampleLine() and trample(); bohemia_enemies.json numbers.trample, brain trampler)
In a straight line it walks THROUGH your men, up to three tiles: every man on the line is struck (+10, the charge's bonus), knocked down and
shoved aside, and it ends on the first free tile past the last of them (6 AP, 25 fatigue: the Gore's page). Nothing else in the valley
walks through a line (the bull and the bison stop at the first man). Its lesson, the fewest words: 'It walks through a line. Spread out.'
MEASURED (10 AUTO fights, day one): about six tramples a fight, up to two men each; a crew of 3 beats it 4 in 10, of 6 6 in 10, of 9 10 in
10. Like the bison, longhorn, bear and sloth (row 574), one animal is too much for a new crew of 3: a giant a new company should not be sent
at (WORLD/JOBS, TUNING).
Rule 431(f) ('just another animal') and rule 453 ('one thing no other does'): the newer rule asks for the trample; THE BESTIARY FLOOR's F5
lets it through (its brain's keys are in the file) and F10 checks it tramples.
