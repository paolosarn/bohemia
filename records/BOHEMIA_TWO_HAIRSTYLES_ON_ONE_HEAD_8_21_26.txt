*** 1. HIS PAINTED BOB WAS DRAWING UNDER EVERY HAIRSTYLE ***
------------------------------------------------------------
hair/curtain-bob is one of HIS PD layers. A generated hairstyle is stamped after it,
so front-on the generated hair simply covered it and the double was invisible.

IN PROFILE IT IS NOT COVERED. genHair takes its span from the PART GRID, and his paint
reaches two cells PAST that grid on each side at the crown:

    facing E, row 10:   part ids run x52-59        his paint runs x50-61

His bob's ramp holds exactly two colours -- [27,26,32] and [237,232,220] -- so what
showed through those four cells was the near-white one. A bright blob over the
forehead, on both profiles, in every canon style.

    MEASURED, BEFORE:  1,349 leaked pixels   (15 styles x 3 facings)
    MEASURED, AFTER:   0                     (and 3,677 -> 0 across all 8 facings)

THE FIX IS NOT MORE HAIR. Widening genHair's span to cover those cells patches the
symptom and leaves TWO HAIRSTYLES STACKED ON ONE HEAD. If he is wearing a hairstyle,
that IS his hairstyle: the PD hair layer is not drawn while a hair garment is worn.
RIG LAW IS UNTOUCHED -- his painted bob is not edited, it is not drawn, and it comes
straight back the moment no hair garment is worn. The PREVIEW path already cleared
every equipped slot, so this only ever concerned the worn body.

*** 2. THE FADE STOPPED IN THE MIDDLE OF THE HAIR ***
------------------------------------------------------
    sideBot = (back||prof) ? hBot : halfway      <- profile added 8/2
    fadeBot =  back        ? hBot : halfway      <- profile never added

fadeBot was a HAND COPY of sideBot, written before the 8/2 profile fix and never
updated with it. That fix taught the hair MASS to cover the whole skull side-on; the
fade's bottom stayed at the old halfway line. So in profile the taper stopped in the
middle of a mass that now ran to the jaw.

    tinted pixels, mean height in the hair (0 = top, 1 = bottom)
        before   0.72 - 0.79, and roughly twice as many of them
        after    0.91 - 0.95
His law: "near the crown hair is solid, and toward the taper more of the skin
underneath shows through". The taper is the bottom. It is not a copy any more, it IS
sideBot -- one edge, so the two can never drift apart again.

HOW I FOUND IT, INCLUDING THE WRONG TURN
-----------------------------------------
I GUESSED THE FADE FIRST AND I WAS WRONG. The blob looked like a fade misfiring, I
found the sideBot/fadeBot divergence, fixed it, and THE BLOB WAS STILL THERE. Then I
measured instead: zero hair pixels above luminance 120, on a style whose ramp tops out
at 50 -- so the bright pixels were not hair at all. Pulling PD layers one at a time
named hair/curtain-bob in one pass.
The fade fix stayed only because I then measured it ON ITS OWN and it holds up. A
change that survives because it was made on the way to something else is not verified,
it is just present.

AND THE FIRST PICTURE COULD NOT HAVE SHOWN THIS. tools/bohemia_hair_profile_look.js
stamped the generators on top of buildFrame's output by hand. That happens to match
the render order, so it looked right -- but it SKIPS THE PD LAYER LOGIC ENTIRELY, so
it could never have revealed whether his bob was suppressed. It drives G_WORN now, the
same path the game draws him with. VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE means the real path, not
a composite that resembles it.
