# WHAT HE HAS SAID ABOUT HOW THE GAME TALKS

Every quote of his, found in the three named sources, about how people in the game should talk: dialog, text, words,
tweets and posts, the feed, the radio, Spanglish, funny and morbid humour, names, reading level, "boring", "not human",
"swag", captions. Newest first. Built by `make_about_talk.py` from `corpus.jsonl`; no quote is retyped.

How to read an entry:
- **VERBATIM** = a raw note from the VOTE tab, or a block a record itself labels as his words (voice to text, so garbles stay).
- **CONDENSED** = the coordinator or a lane quoting him: words cut (their `...`), fillers dropped, commas and semicolons added.
  Use these for WHAT he wants, not for HOW he sounds.
- `[cut]` = I trimmed a long verbatim block to the part about talk; the words between two `[cut]` marks are one unbroken span.
- `...` inside a quote was already in the file (the recorder's cut).
