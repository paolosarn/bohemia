# BETTER THAN BATTLE BROTHERS (rule 80, Paolo 10/9: 'there's so many ways we can have a game that's better than Battle Brothers, so come on')

The standing list. One line per entry: what Battle Brothers does badly or thinly, and our fix, and the lane that owns it. Every lane adds its own line the first round it runs under rule 80 and keeps it current. Recreate with our swag (9/29) is the floor; this is the ceiling. Never a new reference game: the fix comes from our own idea, real life, or the quest library.

1. THE SOUNDSCAPE (his, 10/9). BB: a sound for everything, a place heard before it is read. Ours today: a handful. Fix: every screen and every building has its sound from real material, in analog horror; the valley at night is heard. SOUNDS [the soundscape]; Grok ask 23.
2. NARRATION ON DEMAND (his, 10/9). BB: you read everything. Ours: one toggle and the machine voice reads any text aloud; people keep their mouths. SOUNDS [the narrator]; UI the toggle.
3. A HOUSE YOU GIVE A FUCK ABOUT (his, 10/9). BB: no faction earns a feeling. Ours: a face you meet again, a want, a memory of what you did, a place that is theirs. FACTIONS [a house you give a fuck about]; Grok ask 24.
4. THREE GENERATIONS THAT INHERIT (Paolo 9/7, 9/23). BB: one company, one lifetime. Ours: the future computed from the past, the flip between acts. DYNASTY.
5. A LIVING CITY, NOT A STILL (Paolo 9/24). BB: a map that is a painting with a marker, towns that are menus with a picture. Ours: the map moves, the place is a painted scene with people in it. LIFE+CITY, RUN TWO.

7. FIGHTS SHORTER THAN THEIRS (Paolo 9/29: 2 to 4 minutes routine, never past 15). BB: 30 to 40 minute tough battles. COMBAT, TUNING.
