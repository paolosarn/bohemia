# BETTER THAN BATTLE BROTHERS (rule 80, Paolo 10/9: 'there's so many ways we can have a game that's better than Battle Brothers, so come on')

The standing list. One line per entry: what Battle Brothers does badly or thinly, and our fix, and the lane that owns it. Every lane adds its own line the first round it runs under rule 80 and keeps it current. Recreate with our swag (9/29) is the floor; this is the ceiling. Never a new reference game: the fix comes from our own idea, real life, or the quest library.

1. THE SOUNDSCAPE (his, 10/9). BB: a sound for everything, a place heard before it is read. Ours today: a handful. Fix: every screen and every building has its sound from real material, in analog horror; the valley at night is heard. SOUNDS [the soundscape]; Grok ask 23.
2. NARRATION ON DEMAND (his, 10/9). BB: you read everything. Ours: one toggle and the machine voice reads any text aloud; people keep their mouths. SOUNDS [the narrator]; UI the toggle.
3. A HOUSE YOU GIVE A FUCK ABOUT (his, 10/9). BB: no faction earns a feeling. Ours: a face you meet again, a want, a memory of what you did, a place that is theirs. FACTIONS [a house you give a fuck about]; Grok ask 24.
4. THREE GENERATIONS THAT INHERIT (Paolo 9/7, 9/23). BB: one company, one lifetime. Ours: the future computed from the past, the flip between acts. DYNASTY.
5. A LIVING CITY, NOT A STILL (Paolo 9/24). BB: a map that is a painting with a marker, towns that are menus with a picture. Ours: the map moves, the place is a painted scene with people in it. LIFE+CITY, RUN TWO.
6. THE STORY (his one line: economic apocalypse Battle Brothers on a phone, with a better story). BB: contracts and a crisis. Ours: a frame and an ending twist, Project Angel, the Amalgamation. DYNASTY, QUESTS (research).
7. FIGHTS SHORTER THAN THEIRS (Paolo 9/29: 2 to 4 minutes routine, never past 15). BB: 30 to 40 minute tough battles. COMBAT, TUNING.
8. A PHONE IN ONE HAND (Paolo 9/4, 9/30). BB: a mouse. Ours: 44 pt thumbs, one pinch from a bench to the city. UI, RUN.
9. NOT EVERYONE GETS WORDS (WORDS, 10/9). BB: 85 percent of its own roster is a bare stat tooltip, no flavor line anywhere (this lane's 9/29 school found it, records/BOHEMIA_WORDS_ALL_THE_WORDS_OF_BATTLE_BROTHERS_9_29_26.md). Ours: named all 160 enemies this round, not just the 10 that currently fight; every enemy gets a voice before it gets a body. WORDS, records/BOHEMIA_WORDS_THE_ENEMIES_NAMES_10_9_26.md.
10. A WORLD PAINTED FROM A WORLD THAT MOVES (COOK, 10/9). BB: their world map is ONE FIXED PAINTING of one fixed world, hand-authored once, so every campaign crosses the same hand-made terrain and the ground never records anything; their tactical boards reuse a small set of flat terrain tiles everywhere. Ours, measured this round: the far end is painted from the layout the ENGINE rolls -- the tool reads engine/bohemia_overmap.js every run, so the ranges, the basin, the strip's spine, the dead airport, the washes and the two depots land where THIS valley put them, and the same brushes paint a different valley next seed. Hand-painted materials on a world that is not fixed, which is the half of it they could never do; and the city floor keeps what the fight does to it. COOK, records/BOHEMIA_THE_FAR_END_PAINTED_MEASURED_10_9_26.txt.
11. THE GROUND IS THE COST AND THE COMPANY, AND A PRIZE MIGHT NOT BE THERE (WORLD, 10/9). BB: terrain decides your travel speed and NOTHING ELSE; who you meet is a separate faction-territory layer sitting over the top, and its legendary locations are placed on EVERY map it generates so a rumour is never wrong. Ours, measured these two rounds: the ground is ONE fact that answers both questions -- how fast you cross it and whose crews are on it (the Strip is 92% one outfit, the ranges are split twelve ways with nobody over 19%), because in a dead city the ground IS the economy and the reason the Strip is worth owning is the reason somebody owns it. AND OUR LEGENDARY PLACES ARE ROLLED, NOT PLACED: the library is on 48 of 60 valleys, so a rumour at the bar has to be CHECKED against the valley you are in, and a map that can be wrong about where the good gear is is a map worth reading. AND THEIR TERRAIN TABLE IS NINE ROWS WHERE OURS FIRES ONE, SAID OUT LOUD RATHER THAN FAKED: BB earns its swamp, forest and hills in a green temperate world; ours is a dead city in a desert, so the whole movement-cost half is empty (not one wet cell in the valley) and what is left is COVER, measured this round from a board 1.5% blocked to one 95.8% blocked. BB's terrain changes how fast you cross a tile; ours changes whether there is anywhere to hide, which is the half that decides a formation fight. WORLD, records/BOHEMIA_WORLD_THE_GROUND_PICKS_WHO_SHOWS_UP_10_9_26.md, records/BOHEMIA_WORLD_A_PRIZE_THAT_MIGHT_NOT_BE_THERE_10_9_26.md and records/BOHEMIA_WORLD_A_LEGEND_COUNT_IS_NOT_A_BOARD_10_9_26.md.
12. A BODY THAT ACTS, NOT A PAPER DOLL (ANIMATION, 10/9). BB: its men are still figures that slide and bob; its developers left body animation out on purpose to keep the gear readable (this lane's 9/28 school line), so a hit is a jolt and a number and a fall is a swap. Ours: the body does it on the 120 beat, in the gear you gave him: he breathes while he waits, sinks when he is struck down and stays on the board sitting up and crawling so you can see who is alive to carry home, and the dead lie flat beside him (records/BOHEMIA_THE_FIGHTS_CLIPS_10_9_26.md); next the shot kicks, the party walks on the map, the people in a place idle. ANIMATION [the shot kicks], [the marker walks], [the settlement's idle people].
13. A GANG LOOKS LIKE A GANG (CHARACTER, 10/9). BB: a tier's whole look is one recolored sprite; the brigand raider and the brigand leader wear the same coat. Ours: six ranks dressed from a real street wardrobe, read from the faction's own pairing table, a vest only on the man whose crew actually wears vests, the armour visibly heavier as the rank climbs. CHARACTER [the enemy tiers dressed], records/BOHEMIA_THE_ENEMY_TIERS_DRESSED_10_9_26.txt.
14. A RECRUIT IS A PERSON FIRST, A RECRUIT SECOND (PEOPLE, 10/9). BB: a stranger at the post is a background tag and a few stat ranges, forgettable until he survives a few fights and earns a nickname. Ours: the same real name-mix generator every citizen in this valley already gets a name from gives the recruit his name before you ever pay, so he was already a believable person in the world, not manufactured at the moment you looked. PEOPLE [good bros]; records/BOHEMIA_GOOD_BROS_10_9_26.txt.
15. YOU BUILD IT, AND THE MAP SHOWS WHAT IS HAPPENING (LIFE+CITY, 10/9). BB: you never build anything anywhere; a town changes by a 'situation' you only learn by opening its menu, and its parties stack on each other on the map. Ours: you build on the parts of the city you hold, one battery and one day a thing, and your heirs inherit it or lose it in a raid (engine/bohemia_lotbuild.js, gated); and the map tells you before you open anything: a market day is a crowd at the gate, a raid is a thin one, every party leaves prints that fade by the hour and nobody stands on anybody (engine/bohemia_livingmap.js, gated). LIFE+CITY [build on the screen], [the living map].
16. A SOUND IS A REAL CAUSE, NOT A SAMPLE FILE (SOUNDS, 10/9). BB: every hit, every click, every ambience bed is a fixed recorded sample, the same file whatever the object. Ours: every struck or resonant sound this lane ships is synthesized from the real physics of its own object (a plate's modes from published E, rho and v; a mains clipper's actual 120 Hz double-strike; a nail's smaller, higher mode than a bolt catch, an anvil's lower, shorter one than a held pipe) -- a new material is always a new real cause, never a new sample pulled off a shelf. SOUNDS, engine/bohemia_horror_sounds.js, 41 cooked sounds across eleven rounds.
14. A HOUSE THAT WANTS SOMETHING (FACTIONS, 10/9). BB: a faction is a flag, a list of units and a relation number; no face, no want you can see. Ours: every faction has a face slot, one want, a base and a memory driven by beef, in records/target/bb/factions.json. FACTIONS.

17. A TONE, NOT A THEME (DIRECTION, 10/9). BB: grim low fantasy painted handsomely but one register from menu to map to fight; nothing in the picture is ever wrong on purpose, so after fifty hours nothing on screen surprises you. Ours: analog horror at the source (the bible, AH-01): every ordinary frame may carry ONE calm wrong thing that traces to the world's own state (a lit house the census says is empty, the valley's account counting 421 blocks after 420), and the machine's smooth voice sits against a rough hand-made world (the AI-slop strand). The dread is data, not decoration, so it never repeats the same way twice. DIRECTION, records/BOHEMIA_ANALOG_HORROR_BIBLE_9_20_26.md, records/BOHEMIA_AI_SLOP_THE_MACHINE_AND_THE_WORLD_9_30_26.md.
18. READ UNDER THE FINGER AND IN THE SUN, NEVER HOVERED (UI, 10/9). BB: what a thing is and what it costs lives in a mouse-hover tooltip, small pale text on dark parchment; a phone has no hover, and a phone in daylight washes small pale text out. Ours: a finger held on anything names it on a torn tag that goes when the finger lifts (the settlement, shipped 10/5), the number that matters is printed beside it ([honest number]), and every word on the map, the settlement, the fight and the phone passes 4.5 to 1 with a quarter of white glare added, measured off real screenshots (the phone's hour went 2.97 to 6.0 this round). UI [the phone's look], [honest number], records/BOHEMIA_THE_PHONES_LOOK_10_9_26.md.
19. A LINK, NOT AN INSTALL, AND NEVER HEAVIER THAN LAST ROUND (PLUMBER, 10/9). BB: you buy it, download it and install it on a computer before the first screen; it was never made for a phone, so nobody counts its load in seconds on one. Ours: one link on a phone, nothing to install; the budget is the title within 2 s and NEW GAME within 8 s on a phone-speed processor, and a checker in the suite (FIRST LOAD) fails any push that makes the first open heavier. Measured, not hoped: today the title at 3.4 s and NEW GAME at about 85 s; the cuts are proven on a copy and handed to the lanes that own the page (the download before NEW GAME from 30 MB to 9; the body bake that was a third of the boot, bound once instead of about seven times over). PLUMBER [first load].
