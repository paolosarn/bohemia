## WHAT THE COORDINATOR SAW (the 15 places side by side, records/target/combat_maps_11_2c/COMBAT_MAPS_EVERY_GROUND_SHEET_11_2.webp)
- He is right, measured by eye on the sheet COMBAT MAPS 2 shipped: all 15 boards are the SAME BOARD. Horizontal bands of the same tan
  houses and roads (the suburb's block plan), the same two columns of men at the left and right edges facing each other, the same
  empty middle. COMBAT MAPS 2 changed the floor's colour under that plan and measured its own success as "old sand %": it measured
  colour, the thing he did not complain about, and never measured the plan, the thing he did.
- THE LAB YARD is literally THE WORKS' picture with a new floor (the record says so: "lab.0 is its own ground on the works' sheds").
  The desert, the pass, the wash and the landfill are a flat speckled grey with almost nothing on them.
- THE GREEN: the suburbs' lawns came out olive green and the golf course's fairways green stripes ("the green still green"). In an
  economic apocalypse in Las Vegas nobody is watering a lawn: the pumps are off, and even before the crash Nevada's AB 356 (2021) banned
  Colorado River water for decorative turf in the valley from 2027. A Vegas lawn left in a summer goes brown in weeks. Green lives only
  where water still is (a spring, a leaking main, the wash after a storm, the marina's last pool).
- Nothing on any board changes how you fight. No height you can take, no pass to hold, no ground that slows you, no wall to hide behind,
  no landmark you would know in one look. That is "nothing made them fun to do".

## THE LAW (rule 341, LOCKED by his words)
1. EVERY PLACE'S FIGHT BOARD IS ITS OWN BOARD: its own plan built from that place (never the suburb's rows of houses under a new floor),
   its own landmark you know in one look, its own things standing on it (rule 337: real models through the rig, never a recolour).
2. EVERY PLACE PLAYS DIFFERENT: one tactical twist per place that changes what a good turn is there, Battle Brothers' terrain lesson
   first (its hills you climb for the hit, its forest that blocks the shot, its swamp that makes a man fight worse standing in it, its
   ruins' walls, its camps' palisades), built from rules the fight already has (height is accuracy, rule 321; cover; blockers; burning
   ground; smoke; the climb-or-attack rule 328); a new rule only when a place truly needs one, named in the record.
3. WHERE THE TWO SIDES START comes from the place and the job (Battle Brothers' open-field line, its ambush, its camp you walk into),
   never always two lines at the two edges.
4. NO GREEN WHERE NO WATER IS. Dead lawns are brown, dust and weeds; green only where the fiction has water, and it reads as rare.
5. SEEDED: two fights at the same place are never the same board (records/BOHEMIA_THE_COMBAT_BOARD_ASSETS_WHAT_WE_HAVE_AND_WHAT_WE_NEED_9_29_26.md).
6. THE HONEST GRID: what is drawn, the cell's cost and what it does agree (laws/BOHEMIA_LAW_TWO_SCALES_ONE_GAME_9_27_26.md).
7. THE MACHINE GATE (a law without a gate is not enforced): every place's board measured on its PLAN, not its colour: open cells,
   cover cells, blocking cells, height cells, slow cells, chokepoints (a lane one or two cells wide), start positions. Every pair of
   places must differ past a floor; no place may match the suburb's plan; the suburbs' and the golf course's green share near zero;
   every place names its twist and its landmark in the data file. COMBAT MAPS 3 builds it into the suite.
8. ANALOG HORROR at the source, every board (the law of 9/20).
