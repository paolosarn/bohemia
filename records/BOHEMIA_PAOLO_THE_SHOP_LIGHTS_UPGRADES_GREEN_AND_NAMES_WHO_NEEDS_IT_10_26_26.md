## THE RULING (rule 282)
(a) THE SHELF LIGHTS UPGRADES: in every shop and stall, any piece that is better than what at least one of his men has in the same slot
    and of the same kind (a one-handed weapon of the same type and hands, a two-hander for a two-hander, a shield for a shield, a helmet
    for a helmet, body armour for body armour, every category) wears a green mark on its box (rule 249's box, rule 224's look); he sees
    it at a glance while he taps through the town's buildings.
(b) WHO NEEDS IT MOST: tapping a lit piece names the man it helps most, the one worst served in that slot first (the eight-men,
    seven-good-helmets case: the one with the bad helmet), with his arrows (rule 251(a)); a double tap buys it and he wears it (rule 249
    and [buy and wear]); the others it would help are listed after him.
(c) 'BETTER' IS MEASURED, NOT GUESSED: for armour and helmets the protection (and its weight against his fatigue), for weapons the
    damage and its effect for that man's stats and his kind (the recruit ceiling's weights, rule 216), one score per slot, the same one
    the shop's 'who it fits' uses.
(d) THE GATE: a company where one man has a far worse helmet than the rest: the shop's better helmet shows the green mark; tapping it names
    that man first; buying with a double tap puts it on him; a piece worse than everyone's in its slot shows no mark; every category.
