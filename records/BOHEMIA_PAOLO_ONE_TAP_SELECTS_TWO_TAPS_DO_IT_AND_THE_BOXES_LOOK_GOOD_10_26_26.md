## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
Left click (or hover) on a piece selects it and shows its tooltip in place; RIGHT CLICK buys it, sells it or equips it at once, no
confirm, no dialogue; drag moves it. The screen never changes for a single item. On a phone the double tap is that right click.

## THE RULING (rule 249)
(a) ONE TAP SELECTS: one tap or click on any piece (the shop, the stall, the stash, a man's bag and gear, the loot) lights it: a
    bright outline round its box at least, in the kit's light; its facts show IN PLACE (the screen's own info strip: what it is, who
    it fits, the stat arrows of rule 223); never a second menu, never a sheet over the screen, never the keeper talking or asking.
(b) TWO TAPS DO IT: a double tap or double click does the piece's one action at once: in the shop BUY it (and he wears it, [buy and
    wear]); his own piece in the shop SELL it (it goes on their shelf, rule 248); in the stash or the crew EQUIP or take it off; on
    the loot screen TAKE it. No confirm, no dialogue. On a computer a right click does the same (Battle Brothers').
(c) THE UNDO IS THERE: a buy, a sale, an equip, a take is undone the same way (the buyback at the price, rule 248; equip back; the
    loot screen's UNDO, rule 244).
(d) THE BOXES LOOK GOOD (his 'the interior of the boxes looks like dog shit'): the item box remade in the kit (the Crashbox tin and
    board, built with light and form, ALL THE UI LOOKS 3-D): a recessed well with a soft inner shadow, the piece lit inside it, the
    piece's quality read by the frame, never a flat dark fill; held against the world before it ships (COMPARE EVERY PIECE OF ART TO
    THE WORLD).
(e) THE GATE: on the phone and computer, in the shop, the stall, the stash, the crew and the loot: one tap lights the outline, shows
    the facts in place and opens no sheet or dialogue; a double tap does the action (the count and the batteries change), no
    dialogue; a right click on the computer the same; every item box draws the new well.
(f) Rows: UI THREE [the item box] (one slot component: the outline and the well) after its stat rows; RUN TWO [one tap selects, two
    taps buy] in the shop, the stall and the crew, with [buy and wear]; RUN FOUR the loot screen's taps; lines 174, 175; EYES checks.
