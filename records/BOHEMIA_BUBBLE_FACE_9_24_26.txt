IT IS A FACING. A field was read by its name. There is no face index to hand
across, and there is nothing on the other end to hand it to: the street draws a
baked CLOTHING FIT picked by trade (longcoat, skirt, poncho, cape) and a clothing
fit has no portrait in it. So "the bubble draws the face the body carries" cannot
be built as written, because the body does not carry one.

AND THE PORTRAIT WAS ALREADY DOING THE HARD PART. faceFor already dresses the
face from BOH_PERSONLOOK with the canon garment pool, so the cut is a real
per-person answer and not an unconstrained roll. PORTRAIT and CHARACTER built
that and it works.

*** AND MY FIRST PROBE OF THAT SAID IT WAS DEAD. *** It called a bare global
`lookFor` and read `.hair`; the module is behind BOH_PERSONLOOK and the hair is at
`.worn.hair`. It returned null for everybody with no error, and I was one step
from "fixing" a system that is alive. Before believing a negative, prove the
instrument can produce a positive: handed the pool, it dresses 20 of 20.

================================================================================
WHAT IS ACTUALLY WRONG, AND IT IS SMALLER AND DEEPER
================================================================================
*** NOTHING IN THIS GAME SAYS WHO IS A MAN AND WHO IS A WOMAN. ***
Not the person record. Not the derived identity (key, tier, name, role, lookSeed,
idSeed, lang, household, home, work, faction). Not the name bank. Not the face
spec. ONE RIG (Paolo 7/25, LOCKED) settled the BODY question -- one rig, sliders,
the separate female rig is dead -- and nobody ever came back for the reading.

THE NAME IS THE ONLY THING IN THAT SPEECH BUBBLE THAT SAYS "SHE", AND NOTHING WAS
LISTENING TO IT.

================================================================================
WHAT WAS BUILT
================================================================================
A. THE NAMES SAY WHAT THEY READ AS. 87 of the 90 given names Paolo approved on
   9/21 are marked she or he. This is a FACT about names already in the bank, not
   a new ruling about the world, and it is draft:true and his to overturn one line
   at a time without touching code. Three are left reading EITHER on purpose --
   Kai, Sunny, Juniper -- because a name that really does go both ways must not be
   forced, and forcing it is the one place this table could invent instead of
   report. The gate checks every marked name is really in the bank, so the table
   cannot drift away from the thing it describes.

B. THE CITY ANSWERS, because it is the only side that can turn an id back into a
   person. The shell gets an id string and had nothing else to build from.
   AND A NAME HE HAS NOT EARNED STEERS NOTHING: until you ask somebody their name
   they are their trade, and a face built on a name the player has never heard
   would be the game knowing something he does not. That is this lane's own 9/21
   rule kept rather than quietly broken for a face.

C. THE POOL IS NARROWED, NEVER THE PICK. Cuts that would contradict the name are
   taken out and the generator chooses freely from everything left, so the luxury
   odds, the trenchcoat rule, the colour, the skull and every other dial are
   untouched. HAIR_READS marks only 5 of the 11 canon cuts: the three clipped
   tapers and the two long falls. Locs, coils, a shag, a weave, short ropes and
   the curtain cut stay open to everybody, because they are worn by anybody and
   marking them would be inventing a rule.

D. AND THE FACE FOLLOWS THE NAME THE MOMENT IT IS EARNED. The face is cached by id
   and never asked twice, so the first time somebody speaks they are a stranger
   and their face is steered by nothing. Without this she would keep a stranger's
   face for ever under a plate carrying her name.

MEASURED AND NOT FIXED
================================================================================
IT IS ONLY THE HAIRCUT, AND THE FACES STILL DO NOT READ AS CLEARLY AS THEY SHOULD.
The portrait has no other masculine or feminine dial to turn: one rig, one skull
generator, and no jaw, brow-weight or lash that knows about any of this. Saying
the faces now "read as women" would be selling something I did not measure. What
I measured is that no face wears a cut that fights its own name.

AND WHAT ACTUALLY DOMINATES THESE PORTRAITS IS EYEWEAR. Most of the faces on the
sheet are wearing a wraparound band across the eyes, which is a known row already
named elsewhere (70 of every 200 people wear shades you cannot see through). It is
not this row and it is not fixed here, but it is the first thing you see.

THE ROW'S OWN SHIP TEST IS NOT MET AND CANNOT BE AS WRITTEN. "The bubble draws the
face the body carries" needs the body to carry a face. It carries clothes. Naming
that is the honest close rather than marking a line SHIPPED against a test the
game cannot satisfy.
