## WHICH FINDINGS FROM SCHOOL CHANGED THE LINES, AND HOW
Five, and each one is visible in the bank.

**1. THIRTY-THREE WORDS IS BELOW THE IDIOLECT FLOOR, SO NOT ONE PAIR IS BUILT ON
HOW ANYBODY TALKS.** Reliable authorship work needs 1,000 to 2,000 words and the
forensic low end is 105 to 341 tokens; three lines at our median is about 33 words.
The 9/4 round wrote pairs that leaned on word choice and rhythm. **Every pair in
this round is built on what a person will not say, what they want from the listener,
or what they see the world through, and none of them on vocabulary.** That is the
single biggest difference between the two rounds and it came straight out of school.

**2. THE ORDER IS THE REVERSE OF THE STANDARD ADVICE, SO NEGATIVE SPACE GOES
FIRST.** School ranked the channels by what survives 33 words and put vocabulary
last. **Pair one is nothing but negative space**, and it is the first thing in the
section rather than an afterthought at the end, which is where the 9/4 round put it.

**3. A TIC IS THE WRITER'S FINGERPRINT AND IT SPREADS, SO THERE ARE NO TICS.** Our
own build has five speakers in five quests independently using one rhetorical move.
**Not one line in this round carries a catchphrase**, and the section ends with the
same tic handed to two characters to show it MERGES them rather than separating
them. The 9/4 round did not have that demonstration because it had not yet measured
the failure.

**4. INTIMATES CONVERGE, SO ONE PAIR IS DELIBERATELY NOT TELLABLE APART.** Language
style matching predicts closeness on exactly the dimension stylometry uses: speed
dates 33.3% mutual interest above median against 9.1% below, couples 76.7% against
53.5% still together at three months. **Pair four is two people who have shared a
kitchen for thirty years and you cannot sort them, and that is the correct writing.**
Then a third person is added who has stopped matching, because divergence is the
drama. **The 9/4 round would have marked pair four as a failure. School says it is
the most accurate thing in the section.**

**5. THE REAL NUMBERS ARE A DIAGNOSIS, NOT A TARGET.** School's counterweight from
the craft side is that mimicking real conversation reads flat. So these are paced
for a phone at 120 BPM rather than pushed toward the telephone corpus, and the
fragments in pair four are short because the scene wants them short, not because a
corpus said so.

## THE MEASUREMENT THE ROW ASKED FOR, WITH THE RIGHT INSTRUMENT
The row says "measure our 504 NPC lines for it." The 9/4 round measured them with
function-word stylometry, which school established is the instrument built to ignore
what makes a character a character. **So this round measured the channels school
ranks above it instead.**

Across the 527 quest `@SAY` lines, taking the 37 speakers with 8 or more lines
(455 lines):

    refusals, by pattern                 6
    refusals, after reading every hit    4
    speakers who never refuse anything   31 of 37
    wants aimed at the listener          35
    speakers who never ask for anything  13 of 37

**NEGATIVE SPACE IS THE CHANNEL SCHOOL RANKS FIRST AND IT IS THE ONE WE HAVE LEAST
OF.** Eighty-four percent of our speaking cast will answer anything you ask them.

**AND THE RULER WAS CHECKED, WHICH IS WHY THE NUMBER MOVED.** Seven pattern hits,
read by hand: three were false. "I'm not saying it isn't" is a concession, "I'm not
telling you to do anything" is a disclaimer (and is the Q10 tell again), and "what I
can't tell you is why" is an admission of ignorance. **Four real refusals in 527
lines.** The four that are real are good, and the best of them is a medic saying
"that part is mine to carry", which is the whole technique in six words.

The other one worth naming is an elder saying "we don't ask about the hum." **A
GROUP refusal is stronger than a personal one**, because it tells you about the
place at the same time, and we have exactly one.

## WHAT IS IN THE BANK
Six sections, all `draft:true`, none in the game.

    PAIR ONE    what they will not say. One guards a subject and advertises the
                guard; the other gives it away to avoid being asked twice.
    PAIR TWO    what they want from you. One wants you gone and says so three
                ways; the other wants you to stay and never says it.
    PAIR THREE  what they see through. Two trades describing the same family,
                neither mentioning their trade.
    PAIR FOUR   two intimates who CANNOT be told apart, on purpose.
    THE THIRD   the person at that table who has stopped matching.
    THE TIC     one catchphrase given to two people, to show it merges them.

**THE TEST EVERY PAIR HAD TO PASS, WHICH IS NEW THIS ROUND:** the distinguishing
feature must be one that CANNOT BE HANDED TO THE OTHER SPEAKER. An absence, a
direction of want, and a way of seeing are not portable. A phrase is, and that is
why the corpus caught five characters sharing one.
