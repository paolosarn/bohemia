### THE PRIMITIVE UNDERNEATH: THE BOX
Everything in the vocabulary is one shape — an outer element that IS the edge
(its background is the line colour, its padding is the line weight) wrapping an
inner element that IS the fill. Corner, weight and cut all fall out of that one
pair. That is why seven letters can re-skin a whole page live, and it is what
RUN would implement when a pick lands.

## 4. THE GATE, AND THE HOLE IT FOUND IN ITSELF

`gates/ui_vocab_gate.js` — 57 checks, registered in the suite as **UI VOCAB**.

**A page of design options has one classic way of lying, and it is not a crash:
THE OPTIONS ALL LOOK THE SAME.** Three labels, three identical rectangles, and a
director being asked to choose between things that are not different. Every
source check ever written passes on that page. So the centre of this gate is a
RENDERED-STYLE FINGERPRINT of every option — the box, its fill, the second
accent, the body sentence and the feed art, read off the elements the browser
actually painted — asserted distinct within each fork.

**It caught two real ones on its first run.** COLOUR A and COLOUR B rendered
pixel-identical, because the sample only used the first accent and the whole
difference between them is the SECOND. TYPE A and TYPE B did too, because there
was no body text anywhere in the sample. Two of seven forks were asking him to
choose between the same picture. The samples now carry a second-accent tag and a
real sentence in the body face.

**PRESSED is the one fork that does not exist standing still.** Measuring it the
way the others are measured says "all three are the same", which is true and
useless. It is pressed with a real mouse-down and read while the thumb is down,
and dropping it from that measurement turns a separate leg red.

**AND THE GATE'S READING-LEVEL LEG FAILED TO FAIL.** Mutation test 7 dropped a
full paragraph of college jargon into the page and the gate stayed GREEN: 1,400
plain words drowned one bad sentence in the average. *A checker that cannot tell
a good page from a good page with one unreadable sentence in it is the broken
one* (8/1). The worst sentence is now measured on its own, twice — how long it
is, and how hard its WORDS are (syllables per word, which does not care how
short you make the sentence). Measured on the shipped page: worst 11.6 against a
cap of 16, worst 1.90 syllables a word against a cap of 2.30, whole page 6.3.

## 4b. SUN MODE LIGHTENS THE PAGE, NOT THE SAMPLES

The samples and the live preview are SHOWING THE GAME, and the game is dark.
Lightening them shows him a thing that does not exist. This is what the ART judge
page already does — the page turns to paper, the screenshots on it do not.

**The first cut got this wrong in both directions and only LOOKING caught it
(7/18, VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE).** It flipped the theme tokens on `<body>`,
which:

1. left every sample panel black on a cream page anyway, because `--fill` is
   declared as `var(--surface)` on `:root` — **a custom property that references
   another one resolves where it is DECLARED, not where it is used**, so it had
   already computed to the dark value and inherited down substituted. The rule
   read correctly and did nothing; and
2. once that was "fixed" by restating `--fill`, it turned the **BONE** option
   invisible — a bone accent on a cream panel. The option he is being asked to
   judge vanished in the mode he judges in.

Neither is visible in the CSS. Both are obvious in a screenshot. Sun mode now
touches page chrome only, by element, and never a vocabulary token, so nothing
it does can reach a sample.
