## 3. THE FINDINGS

### 3.1 A LEVEL NEVER RAISES A STAT IN THE FIGHT, AND THE MAP SIZES THE ENEMY BY YOUR LEVELS (HARDER THAN BATTLE BROTHERS, NO WORD)
The fight builds every man of his from a fresh roll of his background's ranges (crewUnit: hp, skills and defences from bg.stats); his
level only picks his perks. Battle Brothers raises three stats every level (rules.json experience.per_level: three attributes; stat_gains:
melee skill and defence 1 to 4, ranged skill, hp, breath and nerve 2 to 5). Meanwhile the map sizes a job by the company's strength,
10 a man plus 2 a level past one (records/target/bb/party_math.json): so every level ADDS ENEMIES and adds nothing to his men.
Measured where the gains are the only difference (the grown company and the tough one; in the first fight TODAY also meets the fixed
nine where Battle Brothers' job math sends ten, and the new crew is level one, so neither isolates it):
| fight | won in 100, today | won in 100, with the gains | struck down a fight, today | with the gains | your swings that land, today | with the gains |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| THE GROWN COMPANY (eight men at level 3, two skulls, day 22) | 4 | 8 | 7.83 | 7.69 | 48 | 53 |
| A TOUGH ONE (the twelve at level 4, three skulls, day 65) | 0 | 0 | 12 | 12 | 41 | 49 |
THE SIZE: small at level three or four (two or three level-ups: +5 to +7.5 melee skill and defence), and it grows every level: a level-11 man
in Battle Brothers carries +25 melee skill, +25 melee defence and +35 hp over his roll, while ours carries none and the map has added
20 strength for him (two more enemies on a job for every ten such men). The longer he plays, the further under Battle Brothers he falls.
Owner: COMBAT (crewUnit reads the stats the roster hands, or adds the level's gains) and RUN TWO (the roster hands each man's stats).
Until it lands, every grown company plays harder than Battle Brothers, which rule 117 makes a NO.

## 4. THE LIMITS (said once)
- AUTO plays his side: the numbers are the fight's own brain on both sides. A good player wins more at every setting; the CHANGE between
  settings is the measurement, not the level. The fights are seeded, so every setting meets the same men on the same boards.
- Battle Brothers' level gains are taken at the middle of the wiki's range, three stats a level, no stars (a melee man: skill, defence,
  hp; a shooter: ranged skill, defence, hp).
- The money rows are not in a fight; they are compared row by row (section 5), not played. ECONOMY's loop would measure them.
- The experience lever (-0.05) is in the start; it moves only the experience number.
- Every man wears the kit of his place in the line (ours.crew); by day 65 a Battle Brothers company has bought better armour, so A TOUGH
  ONE at Battle Brothers' numbers (0 won in 300) reads harder than Battle Brothers' real day 65. The change between settings still holds.
- CONTROL: with the hooks set to change nothing, the bench replays the unhooked fight event for event (node tools/bohemia_tuning_easier_measured.js
  --control: six seeded fights, the same result, the same rounds, the same number of events).
