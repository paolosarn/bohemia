# Paolo: 'what type of guns would fit their type of melee weapons... Battle Brothers has a handgun; is that a bazooka now?'
# Each line: BB CLASS | ITS RULE (what makes it that class) | OUR WEAPON THAT FITS | notes. Reach is in house tiles (9/22: pistol 1, rifle 2, scope 3).

MELEE
- SWORD (riposte, precise, low fatigue, weak vs armour) | a RIPOSTE stance and a sure hit | the MACHETE and the BAT: the sure-hit close weapon; riposte is the counter-swing
- AXE (splits shields, heavy) | breaks SHIELDS/cover | the FIRE AXE and the SLEDGE: breaks cover tiles (cars, barriers, doors) in one swing
- MACE (stuns, ignores some armour) | STUN, a lost turn | the TASER BATON and the PIPE: stun; the riot baton
- HAMMER (crushes armour first) | ARMOUR-FIRST damage | the SLEDGE and the CLAW HAMMER: strips plates before flesh
- CLEAVER (bleed, decapitate) | BLEED over turns | the KUKRI, the BUTCHER'S CLEAVER, the CHAINSAW (bleed and fear)
- FLAIL (ignores shields) | HITS PAST cover | the CHAIN and the MORNING-STAR (a padlock on a chain): hits over cover
- DAGGER (puncture ignores armour, cheap, fast) | ARMOUR-IGNORING stab | the KNIFE and the ICE PICK: the puncture, the finisher
- SPEAR (spearwall: stops the charge, +hit) | ZONE CONTROL, a line nobody crosses | the BAYONET RIFLE and the REBAR SPEAR: spearwall; the rifle's butt and blade as one weapon
- POLEARM (reach 2, hooks, knocks back) | REACH 2 in melee, hook and push | the HOOK POLE and the SCYTHE: reach 2, pull a man off a roof
- TWO-HANDED SWORD / GREATSWORD (sweeps 3 tiles) | HITS THREE TILES | the MACHINE GUN sweeping a line (33i, his ruling)
- TWO-HANDED AXE / GREATAXE (splits, round swing) | HITS ALL AROUND | the FLAMETHROWER (a round of fire) or the chainsaw sweep
- TWO-HANDED MACE / HAMMER (knock down, crush) | KNOCK-DOWN | the SHOTGUN'S BUTT / the BREACHING RAM
- WHIP (disarm, reach) | DISARM | the BOLA / the CATTLE WHIP (the keepers')
- THROWING (javelins, axes: short-range volley) | RANGE 1-2 thrown | THROWN KNIVES, HATCHETS, MOLOTOVS (fire)
RANGED
- BOW (reach 6-7, fast, weak vs armour) | many shots, far, weak on plate | the COMPOUND BOW: quiet, ammo you can make; reach 3 like the scope
- CROSSBOW (armour piercing, slow reload) | ONE HEAVY BOLT, reload | the HUNTING RIFLE (scope 3) and the CROSSBOW itself: one shot, plates cracked
- SLING (cheap, stuns) | STUN at range | the SLINGSHOT and the ROCK: the kids' weapon
- HANDGONNE (Blazing Deserts: one shot, a CONE that hits everyone in front, long reload) | THE CONE | THE SHOTGUN: reach 1, everybody in the spread, one shell, reload
- FIRELANCE (a one-shot fire burst forward, then a spear) | ONE BURST then melee | the FLARE GUN / the improvised pipe gun; then the bayonet
- MORTAR (city-states: long arc, splash, minimum range) | SPLASH ON A TILE, useless close | THE BAZOOKA / RPG and the mortar: one shot, a tile and its neighbours
- BOMBS (net, smoke, acid, fire, holy water) | UTILITY THROWS | the NET (the keepers), SMOKE (the swarm's answer), FIRE (molotov; the swarm's other answer), the fire extinguisher
OURS WITH NO BB PARENT (allowed: the mechanic is new and named)
- THE PISTOL (reach 1, cheap, common; a routine hit costs one) | the sword's role at range: the sure short shot
- THE RIFLE (reach 2) | the polearm's role at range
- THE SCOPE (reach 3, the dial's weapon) | the crossbow's role, the shot that matters
SHIELDS AND ARMOUR
- SHIELDS (block, shieldwall) | the RIOT SHIELD and the CAR DOOR; shieldwall is a line of doors
- ARMOUR by weight (fatigue vs protection) | plates, vests, helmets, the attachments (36c): the same trade

AMMUNITION (the Bohemia rule Battle Brothers never needed): bullets are scarce in act 1 (a bullet costs one), reloading
is a craft, bows and blades are real; TUNING [ammo] sets how scarce; ECONOMY prices a round; by act 3 the reclaimed
future makes bullets again. This is why act one feels medieval (42 s5).
