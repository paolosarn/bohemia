# BOHEMIA: THE WHOLE GAME (the living map of the final game)

Paolo 10/24 (rule 153): 'you don't even understand how many options we're gonna have for the game... I need you to understand what is going to be in our game and always keep that in sight... sometimes you do one thing and think that's the final and edit around it... you need to know what this game could look like at the final, and always be flexible.'

THE COORDINATOR REWRITES THIS PAGE whenever a ruling adds or changes a system. EVERY LANE READS ITS SECTION BEFORE BUILDING, so nothing is built as if one version were the final. EYES AND EARS uses it as the playtest's checklist. Newest ruling wins; the rules named are in VAMILY.md.
Status as of 10/24: BUILT = in the demo and working; THIN = a first version, far from the final; BROKEN = in, but wrong; MISSING = not in the game.

## 1. THE FRAME
- Three acts that are eras (Animal ~2060 the ruin; Human the rebuild; Angel the cyberpunk healing and the Moon), all three at once with the flip, unlocked at the first crisis; about 100 hours; a dynasty that inherits (the heir starts from how you made the last). Act one: THIN. Acts two and three: MISSING by his rule (act one's assets first, rule 128). The flip: MISSING in the demo.
- THE POINT (rule 133): Battle Brothers, quicker and funner for normies, hardcore underneath; the Stardew Valley player is the bar.

## 2. THE START
- Origins at their difficulties (Battle Brothers' origins as the floor), customize one person, names prepared, the easy end the default (TUNING change 4), the first minutes that make you care (rule 146, research running), A WORLD MADE FRESH every new game and by origin (where you start, the world around you, a first event only this world has). Crew pick: BUILT. Origins: THIN. World made fresh: MISSING (you always start by the church).

## 3. THE MAP (THE OVERWORLD)
- Battle Brothers' map: one 3D map at every zoom (Pocket City 2's pinch, rule 142), in colour with no sand flood (137), the cities built into the ground (143f), your crew's own models as the marker, no flags, no route lines (151f), sight only down the streets near you (151g), default speed 1.22x with no fast buttons (151c), travel and time, roads faster than dirt, road events (a face, two or three choices), the encounter card before every fight with ENGAGE and FALL BACK (151e), the flee road, tracks, the camp button (151d), night. 3D map: BUILT but BROKEN (three maps at three zooms). Colour: in progress. Encounter card, sight, camp: MISSING. Road events: THIN. Route lines and flags: present and to be removed.

## 4. PLACES

Home bases you build inside (building happens in a home base's settlement screen). Faction towns. Camps and fortresses. Special places. The camp (151d). Ghost graveyards (134). The Backrooms twice an act (132, later). Settlement screens, walkers, talk: BUILT. Walk-up: MISSING. Home-base building: THIN. Camp: MISSING.

## 5. JOBS (CONTRACTS) -- THE BIGGEST GAP
- About forty Battle Brothers kinds, eight verbs, already designed for our valley in questbook/research/QR_AF_THE_CONTRACT_KINDS_10_9_26.md: HAUL (escort a caravan, escort an envoy, free prisoners), CARRY (deliver an item), CLEAR (drive away a gang, destroy a camp, intercept raiders, raze or raid), HUNT (roaming beasts, hunt a beast kind until you have N, the big game hunt), PATROL (walk a route and back), HOLD (defend a settlement, last stand, siege), RECOVER (return a stolen thing, obtain an item, restore a place), FIND (discover a location, investigate, a missing person), plus the arena and the crisis clients; twists per kind; partial success; one to three stars (146e); the phone shows the two best-paying (148). Today ONLY 'go to a party and kill it' is BUILT; every other kind is MISSING.
