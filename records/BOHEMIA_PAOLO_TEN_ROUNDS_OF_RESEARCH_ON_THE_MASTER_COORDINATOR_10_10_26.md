Context: the coordinator had started a VAMILY sweep; he stopped it ("Stop") and said instead:

> "10 rounds of research please"

## What it is

A ruling about the coordinator itself, in the shape of rule 36 (big-brain research, how other studios do it) turned on the manager. The coordinator goes to school: ten research rounds, each on one part of the coordinator's job, each with real sources, each ending in ONE RULE the coordinator binds itself to. The school is a standing thing, not a one-off: when the coordinator finds itself making the same mistake twice, the answer is a school round, not a fourth attempt (STOP PRODUCING, 7/26).

## The ten rounds (the coordinator's cut of the job)

1. THE PRODUCER: what the best small-studio producers do day to day (Overhype, Supergiant, Ludeon, Klei; Clinton Keith, Cerny).
2. LOOKS FIRST, THE ART PASS: how studios run an art revamp mid-project and get to "looks right" before features (rule 87, rule 88).
3. SERVING THE DIRECTOR: turning a director's raw notes into work, never wasting his attention (Pixar's Braintrust, Nintendo's directors and producers, one-way and two-way doors).
4. MANY LANES, ONE TRUNK: coordinating 26 autonomous lanes on one main branch (WIP limits, ownership, trunk-based development, multi-agent failure modes).
5. THE VERDICT LOOP: how the best studios run playtesting and director review so feedback is fast, honest and actionable (Valve, Supercell, Nintendo, dailies; rule 89).
6. THE DEMO LANDS: shipping a demo that lands, the first 60 seconds, load-time abandonment, the release line (rule 52).
7. HONEST GATES: automated checks that stay honest for things you see and feel (visual regression, mutation testing, Goodhart's law, fast CI).
8. ONE LOOK ACROSS MANY HANDS: style bibles, paint-overs, purchased packs that do not look stitched, measuring consistency (rule 82, the reference twin).
9. THE MEMORY: keeping design knowledge current and small (living docs, decision records, consolidation, memory for forgetful agents).
10. TALKING TO THE BOSS: reporting up to a busy non-technical director on a phone (BLUF, plain language, show the thing, bottom-up reading).

Each round is its own page: records/BOHEMIA_COORDINATOR_SCHOOL_ROUND_NN_*_10_10_26.md. The ten ONE RULE lines are gathered into laws/BOHEMIA_LAW_THE_COORDINATORS_SCHOOL_10_10_26.md, which binds the coordinator, and rule 90 on the VAMILY.md front page points at it.

## What the coordinator did with it (the same turn)

Ten research rounds run in parallel, each with web sources and real URLs; ten pages written; the school law written from the ten rules; rule 90 on the front page; this record; handoff THEN (kd); all committed to main.
