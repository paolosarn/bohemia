AND HERE IS THE FAULT THAT IS MINE TO FIX, WHICH NEEDED NO MEASUREMENT AT ALL:
  THERE WAS NEVER A HOUSE-SIZED GROUND TILE. Not one. The banks hold 44 px cells
  of paving and the board holds a 12 m house tile, and nothing in the repo ever
  said how many of one go in the other. No metre contract, so the board was free
  to paint a paving cell at whatever size it liked, and it did. That is why the
  ground reads as a smear with a faint grid instead of a street.

THE FIX, AND IT IS ONE CONTRACT: a house tile is a whole number of his cells,
authored at HIS OWN DENSITY, pasted at 1:1, never scaled.
  a house tile is 12 OF HIS PAVING CELLS, exactly, which is why it tiles at all
  a house tile is 12 m  =  528 painted pixels  =  42.9 px a metre
  the floor DIRECTION set is 39 px a metre. His street is 42.9. We are over both.
  the phone gives a house tile 588 device px (196 CSS x 3). 528 fills 90% of it,
  so the board DOWNSCALES at worst and never blows anything up.

THE GUARD THAT GOT REWRITTEN TWICE, BECAUSE IT IS THE LESSON OF THIS ROUND:
  To prove nothing had been resampled I measured the repeat of each tile and
  demanded it be 44. IT FAILED MY OWN GOOD ART, TWICE, FOR TWO DIFFERENT REASONS.
  First: a MULTIPLE of a period is a louder peak than the period, so three paving
  variants answer 132 and the jitter answers 176, both perfectly honest. Second,
  and worse: the YARD has almost no repeat at all, because his yard cells meet
  edge to edge with nearly no contrast. THAT IS GOOD ART. A ruler that demands a
  visible grid is a ruler that rewards a worse picture.
  The proof is EXACT now and needs no ruler: every cell this build pastes is
  compared, pixel for pixel, with the bank cell it came from, at the moment it is
  laid. 720 cells, zero differences, and 81 to 100 per cent of each finished tile
  is still his untouched pixels.

THE CONTRACT FOR COMBAT, IN ONE LINE:
  THE FLOOR IS A DENSITY, NOT A TILE SIZE: 42.9 painted pixels per metre, laid on a 44 px cell lattice that tiles seamlessly, so a house tile is TILE_METRES x 42.9 pixels, whatever TILE_METRES turns out to be. The pieces ship as 528 px patches (12 cells, 12.31 m). Never draw a metre of this into fewer than 42.9 device pixels on the phone: a downscale is fine, a blow-up is the fault he saw.

*** AND A CONTRADICTION I FOUND ON THE BOARD THE SAME ROUND, WHICH IS A BUG, NOT A CHOICE ***
HOW BIG IS A HOUSE TILE? There are two live numbers in the repo right now:
  12.0 m   the fight's own code: TILE_WIDE 1.75 x the 112 fighter box = a 196 px tile, and
           gates/no_atari_gate.js carries 12 m as the DEFAULT he chose.
  ~20 m    WORLD, 10/1, measured off our own suburb kit: a house footprint is 15.8 x 9.0 m
           and THE LOT PITCH, house centre to house centre, is 19.5 m over a range of 18.0
           to 21.8. WORLD's reading is that what tiles a city is the house PLUS its yard and
           half its driveway, so "a house tile is about twenty metres square".
That is a 1.7x disagreement about the single most basic measurement on the fight board, and
it is newer and better evidenced on WORLD's side: theirs came off our own drawn city, the
12 came off a sprite-width convention.
THIS FLOOR DOES NOT HAVE TO PICK, and that is on purpose. It is authored as a DENSITY on a
seamless lattice, not as a fixed tile: 42.9 painted pixels a metre, laid on his 44 px cell,
wrapping on both axes. Lay 12 cells for a 12.3 m tile or 20 for a 20.5 m one; the art is
identical either way and nothing is resampled in either case. The pieces ship as 12-cell
patches because that is the smaller of the two, and a patch is cheap to repeat.
WHOSE CALL IT IS: COMBAT's, with WORLD's number in front of them. It is flagged here rather
than decided here, because picking it would change how far a man walks in a turn and how
many tiles a board is, and neither of those is this lane's.
