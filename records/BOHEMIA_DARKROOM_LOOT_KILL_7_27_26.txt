ROOT CAUSE — WHAT I ACTUALLY DID WRONG
--------------------------------------
Three things, and the first is the big one.

1. I PRODUCED SOMETHING HE DID NOT ASK FOR.
   His message asked for RESEARCH. The only thing in it that pointed at building
   was "we can try it again except it could be faster. You could try it something
   else." I read a shrug as a commission. A lukewarm "you could try" is not a
   request, and STOP PRODUCING is explicit that surfacing unasked work while he is
   unhappy with the baseline is itself the violation. The research alone was the
   turn. I should have delivered it and stopped.

2. I ANSWERED "TOO SLOW" BY DELETING THE ACT INSTEAD OF SPEEDING IT UP.
   Zomboid died for being an item-by-item time economy, so I went to the far
   opposite end of the axis and picked the most minimal loot system that exists.
   What that produced is a paragraph and one button in a modal. There is no
   search in it — no space, no container, no moment, no risk, nothing to be good
   at. He said State of Decay is "decent AS A LOOTING EXPERIENCE." An experience
   is the thing I removed. Faster was a note ON an experience, not permission to
   delete the experience. Two taps in a menu is not a fast search; it is no
   search.

3. IT LOOKED LIKE NOTHING, IN THE MIDDLE OF A LOOK PROBLEM.
   Grey squares with letters stamped on them, on a fleet where the standing
   complaint is that the world does not look consistent enough for him to approve
   anything. "PLACEHOLDER ART" being a legal label for a reference page does not
   make it a thing he can judge or enjoy. It is the wrong thing to put in front
   of him right now, whatever the header says.
