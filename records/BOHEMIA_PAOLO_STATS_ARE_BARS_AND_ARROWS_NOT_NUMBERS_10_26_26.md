## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; his words and recall, marked)
Every attribute on the character screen is a BAR: the bar filled to where the number sits against the attribute's maximum, the
number written on the bar, the fill behind it; the stat's own icon beside it. Comparing gear shows the change on the bar.

## THE RULING (rule 223)
(a) EVERY STAT IS ITS ICON AND A BAR: everywhere a stat shows (the crew screen, the shop's cards, the hire hall, the level-up, the
    fight's stat card), each stat is its icon (UI's stat icons, rule 189) and a bar filled to where his number sits against the
    stat's MAXIMUM, the number on the bar, the fill a translucent colour behind it (Battle Brothers' way, our Crashbox tin).
(b) A CHANGE IS AN ARROW AND THE AMOUNT: comparing (an item on a man, a level-up's choice): the stat's icon, a GREEN UP arrow or a
    RED DOWN arrow, and the amount (+5, -4); the bar shows the new fill with the changed part lit green or red. Never '63 to 68' in
    words; never a sentence of numbers.
(c) THE MAXIMUM IS ONE NUMBER PER STAT, from the game's own numbers (the highest a man can reach: his base, ten levels at the best
    roll, three stars; TUNING checks), the same everywhere so a bar means the same on every screen.
(d) WORDS ONLY WHERE A PICTURE CANNOT: a trait shows as its icon (a logo) with its name on a long press or hover; plain lines stay
    for what something does (rule 202(a)'s 'what it does' becomes icons and bars where it can).
(e) THE GATE: on the crew screen, the shop, the hall, the level-up and the fight's card, phone and computer: every stat is an icon and
    a bar, no 'X to Y' text anywhere, every comparison an arrow and an amount, the bar's fill equals number over maximum within a pixel.
(f) UI [stats are bars and arrows] (the one stat component every screen uses; the crew screen and the fight's card); RUN TWO NOTE
    (the shop's cards use it), RUN THREE NOTE (the hall's cards and the ceiling use it), TUNING NOTE (the maxima); line 145; EYES.
