=== BOHEMIA — WHERE THE BAKED LIGHT ACTUALLY LIVES (7/26/26) ===
Lane: CHARACTER. Evidence for laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_SHADOWS_ARE_SEPARATE_7_26_26.md
(Paolo 7/26, LOCKED). That addendum is the law; this is the mechanism under it,
found by reading the shipped code after he described the symptom.

HIS WORDS (this session's phrasing of the same ruling)
  "lighting and shadows is just a completely different layer... shadows that you
   might have drawn aren't a permanent fixture on clothes... when it's animation
   time and I just see what was supposed to be shadows fucked up with the
   animation, it's really upsetting."

THE FUNCTION THAT DOES IT
  function bshade(inG,x,y,r){
    if(!inG(x,y-1)) return r.lt;                  // nothing above -> LIT
    if(!inG(x,y-2)) return r.lt;                  // near the top  -> LIT
    if(!inG(x,y+1)) return r.dk;                  // nothing below -> SHADOW
    if(!inG(x-1,y)||!inG(x+1,y)) return r.dk;     // side edge     -> SHADOW
    return r.mid;
  }
That is a lighting model: top-lit, shadowed underneath and along the edges. It
runs AT COOK TIME on the REST silhouette and the answer is frozen into the
garment's pixels.

THE RAMPS ARE LIGHTING, NOT MATERIAL
  var BLK={dk:[20,20,24], mid:[42,42,50], lt:[74,74,86]}
  var RED={dk:[96,40,38], mid:[150,60,54], lt:[190,88,78]}
Three steps, and the three steps ARE lit / neutral / shadowed. There is no
separate material channel at all.

THE SCALE, MEASURED
  bshade call sites in the clothing factory : 7
  garments in the shipped bank              : 9
  garments carrying 3+ ramp steps           : 7 of 9

WHY IT BREAKS IN MOTION
A sleeve's lit pixels are lit BECAUSE they were the top edge of a hanging
sleeve. Rotate the arm and that lit edge rotates with the cloth -- now pointing
sideways, or down -- while the shadow that meant "underside" ends up on top.
Nothing about it is an animation bug; the light is nailed to the fabric.

THE PROOF IT IS THE CLOTH AND NOT THE RIG
The BODY has never had this problem, and it is the same renderer: the body's
shading (anatomy border, sky top-light, neck shadow) is recomputed EVERY FRAME
from the deformed grid. Cloth was the only thing carrying frozen light. That
asymmetry is the whole bug in one sentence.

THE FIX, SPECIFIED (no repainting required)
Because a garment ramp is exactly {dk, mid, lt}, a pixel's current step is
recoverable by matching its colour. So:
  - move bshade OUT of the cook and INTO the render, running it on the DEFORMED
    silhouette instead of the rest one
  - the cook emits form + material only (every pixel `mid`), so nothing freezes
ONE HONEST COMPLICATION, named rather than papered over: patc() currently makes
PATTERN by choosing a ramp step (a stripe is a run of `dk`). Pattern and light
share one channel. Pattern must become its own channel first, or a relight will
erase the plaid along with the shadows.

SCOPE, PER THE LAW'S POINT 4
Approved garments with baked shading are NOT re-cooked wholesale. The separation
binds all NEW cooks now, and any approved asset the moment it is re-touched.
This record does not change that.

THE GATE THIS TURN BUILT (the law's point 5, assigned to the CHARACTER lane)
gates/shading_separation_gate.js freezes the baseline as a RATCHET: the number
of bshade call sites and the number of garments carrying baked light may go
DOWN, never up. A new cook that bakes light fails the build. Grandfathered
assets stay exactly as the law says they may.
