## THE READING
In Battle Brothers the slow chess fight is where most of the hours go, and the map, the contracts and the
exploring are the smaller part. BOHEMIA FLIPS THAT SPLIT: the fight is FASTER (rule 36: quicker, less of a chess
puzzle; gambits automate the routine) and the hours go to a DEEP, RICH, INTERACTABLE, BUILDABLE WORLD: the map,
the settlements, the contracts, the people, and BUILDING (your home bases, 37e, are yours to build up; the
future city is your fingerprints, rule 31).

## WHAT IT CHANGES
- THE FIGHT HAS A CLOCK. A routine fight should be short and a big fight should feel big but never long. TUNING
  researches how long a Battle Brothers fight really takes (players report 5 to 20 minutes for a full brawl) and
  proposes our target; the manager's default until then: A ROUTINE FIGHT ENDS INSIDE 90 SECONDS OF BEATS, a boss
  fight inside 4 minutes; gambits (FF12) carry the routine so the player only touches the shots that matter (9/22
  dial rule). In VOTE.
- BUILDING IS BACK, INSIDE THE SETTLEMENT SCREEN. The separate city-builder screen was CUT 9/24 (rule 24, one
  mode) and stays cut; BUILDING lives in the settlement screen of a home base you own: tap a lot, put something on
  it (a shed, a pump, a wall, a shop, a roof), it costs batteries and time, it shows up on the map, in the fight
  board cut from that block, and in the derived future. LIFE+CITY owns what can be built and what it does; RUN
  owns the screen; WORLD owns the block. Everything costs one.
- INTERACTABLE means people and things you can act on in a place (talk, hire, buy, sell, scavenge, build, raid),
  never text popping up (rule 19).
- THE TIME SPLIT IS A MEASURED NUMBER: EYES measures where a play session's minutes go (map, settlement, fight,
  menus) once the shape exists; the target is the manager's default, in VOTE: fight under a third of playtime.
