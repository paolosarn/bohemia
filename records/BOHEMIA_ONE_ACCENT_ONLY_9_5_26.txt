WHAT THIS NUMBER IS AND IS NOT

  It is NOT a verdict on the card. Brown reads as highly saturated in HSV (a mid
  brown is about 0.59), so this count calls ordinary leather boots an accent --
  and the card explicitly lists dust, ash, bone, lead and oxblood-grey as LEGAL
  cloth. A NUMBER IS NOT A FINDING UNTIL YOU KNOW WHAT IT IS COUNTING.

  What it honestly says: THE WARDROBE PREDATES THE CARD. That is COOK's
  WARDROBE-REMAKE, still OPEN, and not a defect for CHARACTER to fix.

  THE REAL HOLE, AND IT IS THIS LANE'S: not one of the 280 garments carries
  any mark saying it is a faction accent. Fields on a canon garment are
  n, st, layer, lux, gen, hard, cw, fresh, hoodDefaultUp -- and nothing else.
  So "at most ONE piece per body carries the faction's saturated colour" was
  UNENFORCEABLE BY ANYTHING, whatever the pixels said.

  FIXED THIS ROUND: engine/bohemia_personlook.js now honours an `accent` flag
  and will never put a second one on a body -- data, not names, exactly like
  the `lux` and `hard` flags beside it, so COOK tags a piece as it cooks it and
  no code changes. IT SHIPS WITH NOTHING TAGGED (MECHANISM-MINE /
  CONTENTS-PAOLO'S): measured, 0 of 4000 people's looks changed. Held by
  gates/wardrobe_wired_gate.js, which drives the mechanism on a synthetic
  wardrobe rather than on the empty one, and by stripping the flag proves it is
  the RULE holding and not a pool that cannot break it.
