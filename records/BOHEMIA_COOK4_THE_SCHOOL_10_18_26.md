# COOK FOUR, round 10 (10/18): THE SCHOOL UNDER THE STRAIGHT ROOF, IN THE VALLEY'S TANS

Lane: cook 4. Rows: [one at a time], [the six buildings from the packs]. Rule 101d: one asset a round.
Opened by the coordinator (rule 112), run to the end, no question asked.

## Why this asset
Round 9's NEXT line: "his word; then the school, one round." His word came in the fourteenth votes: UP on the
warehouse, the gas station and the motel, and on the family in the tans ("That's way better holy shit"). Of the
six building types (church, school, gas station, motel, warehouse, casino) the family had drawn the motel, the
gas station and the warehouse; the school is the fourth. School versions: one (rule 95).

## What it is
The 1955-1970 Clark County finger-plan elementary school, at 57 px a metre, 38 m:
- the classroom wing: three classrooms of 9 m at a 3.3 m plate, his flat tan render (Wall tiles 1 #7), each
  with its own door straight outside (rooms 1 and 2 boarded, 4. #2; room 3 his wood door #7) and a ribbon of four
  of his windows laid frame to frame (broken #11, #12, boarded #6, #18; room 3 his wire glass #16), the party
  walls as seams in shade, under one measured tan roof;
- the covered walk: the motel's flat walk roof in his grey-tan slate at a 2.5 m beam, a post every half
  classroom (seven), the shade under it on the wall;
- the multipurpose room on the end: 11 m of his light stone panels (Wall tiles 1 #5) to 5.2 m, four small high
  windows (#0, #6, #3, #0), two of his steel doors (4. #9) side by side under a transom, its own measured tan
  roof; one bin (15. #21); the man at the double doors;
- the yard fence past the end: three panels of his chain link with his KEEP OUT.

## The one wrong thing (analog horror)
Every classroom is broken or boarded, but room 3's wire glass is whole and lit the cold grey-blue of a
television left on: the dark panes repainted toward grey-blue, the mesh, frame and sill left as his. Light is
paint we are allowed. Distinct from the warehouse's warm window on purpose: one cold, one warm.

## What it is not
No swing, no flagpole, no school bus, no sign: the packs hold none, and every pack prop is a 96 px box (rule 114).
WEAK: the walk's posts are his wooden palisade where the era used steel pipe; the gym's high windows are his
house windows cropped (no clerestory strip in the packs); the classroom doors are house doors.

## The not-vibe-coded gate's N3 (measured, for PLUMBER and DIRECTION)
This round the school, warehouse and gas station cards name their sprites, class and look (rule 114c), so N3
measures them instead of refusing them unread. All three fail D3 (one light) and D2 (colour count against the
ground packs, the band a 'tile' card is held to; the VOTE tab has no 'building' kind, which the gate maps to the
settlement band). MEASURED, the same rulers on his own approved pieces:
- D3, declared light 'top': his pack house 5. Roof tiles #30 is 139 degrees off; his YES tan ranch 106; the
  tan garage ranch 98; the motel 41; the gas station 38; the warehouse 'no light'; the school 80. On a house the
  brightness centroid reads the material (a dark roof over a pale wall), not the light, so the ruler fails the
  pack it is meant to follow.
- D2: one pack piece holds up to 5,830 to 6,560 colours; a building laid from about thirty of his pieces plus
  a soft shadow holds 27,000 to 36,000. I tried snapping every channel to a 4 or 5 step: D2 passes, but D4 (a
  ramp that only darkens) goes red, a real tell traded for a counted one, so the snap is NOT shipped.
ROUTED: PLUMBER (the gate) and DIRECTION (the style guide's building row): a building needs the settlement band
per 96 px window, and a light test that compares like surfaces (roof against roof), or its own band. Until
then N3 stays red on these three cards, named here, not hidden.
