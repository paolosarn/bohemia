## THE RULINGS (LOCKED; amends rule 35c and the quests-research addendum s3)
1. ONE MAIN QUEST AT A TIME. The main line is a single thread; you are never juggling two chapters of it.
2. TWO CONTRACTS AT A TIME. Battle Brothers holds one active contract; he calls that wack, because a caravan
   contract that carries you to a city should let you pick up a hunt near that city on the way. Two slots;
   once taken you finish (35c); declining is free (35c); a third offer waits until a slot opens.
3. THE PHONE SHOWS, THE PLACE ACCEPTS. On the cracked phone (city view only, 32c) you can SEE which settlements
   are offering which contracts right now, like a board, and plan a route. You cannot accept from the phone: a
   contract is taken from a mouth at the place (rule 19, 35c), on the settlement screen's contract offer (QUESTS
   round two put it there). The phone is the reason to travel; the place is where the deal happens.

## WHAT THE MANAGER ADDS (rule 41)
- The phone's contract list is the FEED's third kind of post ('what the world did', 9/4 law): a settlement posts
  its ask; the post ages; a taken contract disappears from the board; a place you helped posts more.
- Two contracts that can be done on one road is the DESIGN: QUESTS' research pairs them (a haul to X plus a hunt
  near X plus a delivery back), and the map's route line shows both destinations (RUN [bb map]).
- The time clock is what makes two at once a decision: a caravan has a deadline, a hunt has a beast that moves;
  ECONOMY [contract pay] prices the pair; TUNING holds the slot count (2) as a felt number.
