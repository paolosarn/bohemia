## 1. THE REAL AISLE -- WHAT AN INTERCESSOR ACTUALLY DOES
Across the traditions the job is remarkably consistent and remarkably narrow.
- **THEY CARRY THE RECORD.** In Tobit the angel's own line is *"it was I who
  brought the record of your prayers before the glory of the Lord."* **The angel
  does not answer the prayer. It carries the record of it upward.**
- **THEY WRITE DOWN WHAT YOU DID.** Islam and Sikhism both hold that every person
  has **two** angels who record the good and the bad. The recording angel does
  not act. It witnesses and writes.
- **THEY STAND BESIDE AT THE END.** Michael accompanies the faithful at their
  final hour and to judgment.
- **AND THE TRADITIONS DISAGREE ABOUT WHETHER THE GUARDIAN INTERCEDES AT ALL.**
  The Orthodox position holds guardian angels are *"Shields of Protection"* and
  **not** intercessors; the Catholic catechism says human life is surrounded by
  their *"watchful care and intercession."* Two answers to one question, which is
  itself the material: **watching and intervening are separable jobs.**
### AND THE COST, WHICH IS MY ARGUMENT AND NOT A SOURCED FINDING
I searched specifically for what the role costs the one doing it and **found
nothing usable**; the search returned material on angelic free will instead. So
this is reasoning from the role rather than a citation, and I am labelling it as
such: **every verb in the list above is performed on somebody else's business.**
Carrying a record, writing a deed, standing beside a person at their end. The
intercessor is never the party. **To stand between two things you have to stop
being one of them.**
That is round 10's measured finding (power costs perspective; at planetary zoom a
person is not visible) reached from an entirely different direction.

## 4. THE VERB LIST, AND WHY IT DOES NOT BREAK ROUNDS 10 AND 16
The nine built verbs are all things you do **to the world**. The intercessor's
verbs are all things you do **about what somebody else did**. That is a different
layer, not a tenth combat move, so the rules the player spent ninety hours
learning are untouched.

## 5. WHAT WE TAKE AND WHAT WE REFUSE
**TAKE**
- **SETTLE as the Angel's defining verb.** It is the exact inverse of everything
  the first two generations do, it is what an intercessor is for, and it is the
  hole four separate rounds of this lane found independently.
- **The two ledgers as the thing that gets weighed.** They exist, the canon for
  them exists, and nothing compares them for a person.
- **Watching and intervening as separable**, which the traditions themselves
  disagree about. SPARE is not the absence of a verb; it is a choice.
- **The cost, stated as an argument**: to stand between two things you stop being
  one of them.
**REFUSE**
- **A tenth combat verb.** Rounds 10 and 16 stand, and section 4 is why they can.
- **Undoing anything.** SETTLE closes a line; it does not erase it. A deed that
  never happened would break the whole witness web and round 11's rule that a
  scar is a repair rather than a deletion.
- **Making the Angel a judge with a right answer.** The traditions carry an
  intercessor and a shield and disagree about which; the choice is the mechanic.
- **Any canon.** What the Angel is, what it does on the moon, and what settling a
  line means in the story are HIS and locked. This round supplies six verbs and
  not one event.
