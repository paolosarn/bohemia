WHAT HE KEPT
------------
- The camp as a place that makes you stronger. Approved outright, and RENAMED by
  him in the act of approving it: it is a MOBILE camp.
- COMFORT: what is around your rest spot extends the buff. Approved as a mechanism.
- Chilling at camp, with SLEEP as a separate option.
- The payoffs: health regen, stamina regen, more stamina points.

WHAT HE CHANGED, AND EVERY ONE IS A RULING
------------------------------------------
1. MOBILE, not a fixed base.
2. THE TIMER IS IN TILES MOVED, not seconds. A buff may not burn while the player
   stands still.
3. NO FOOD ITEMS AND NO FOOD CRAFTING. One clumped pool (water + food + build),
   camp actions spend from it, loot adds to it.
4. THE MAGNITUDES ARE TINY: "+1 or 2 or 3 stamina points type shit", Rogue Fable
   IV's register. Valheim's 25 -> 148 health is explicitly the wrong scale.
5. THE CAMP IS A MEDICAL STATION: bandage, gauze, and a COMPANION pulling a bullet
   out of you. That last one is the first ruled mechanical role a companion has.
6. IGNORING THE CAMP MUST STAY PLAYABLE: "if people dont want to give a fuck about
   that its okay too."
