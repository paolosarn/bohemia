## THE THREE DIFFERENCES FROM THE TWIN (rule 82, on the card)
1. MASSING: his pack has one house shape; ours is five, so a street is never one house copied nine times.
2. ROOF: his is 1.49 walls tall with 14 percent eaves and a 6 percent lean; all five take those numbers.
3. GROUND: his house floats on a clear tile; ours sit on a contact shadow and a cast shadow.

## WHAT IS NOT DONE, SAID PLAINLY
- A prop at the body's scale does not exist in the packs: his cars are 96 px long (a 4.5 m car at 57 px a metre is
  256 px), so the carport is empty and no yard holds a car. The car at true size is its own asset, one round.
- The fence tile at 1:1 is 1.2 m tall, right for chain link; the KEEP OUT sheet at 1:1 is 1.4 m, big for a sheet,
  kept because scaling is banned (rule 105) and a plywood sheet that size is real.
- These are PLACEHOLDERS (rule 101) until his moodboard (rule 108 ROUND THIRTEEN); the card says so in its title.
- The wing's roof is drawn at 72 percent of the main roof's height so it reads as the nearer, lower mass; that
  number is this lane's call, not measured, and is the first thing to re-read if he says the L looks wrong.
