## FINDING 1 — UNDERTALE'S TRICK: THE COMEDY IS THE AMMUNITION
=============================================================================
Undertale's devastation works BECAUSE of its comedy, not despite it. The
craft chain: dynamic, endearing flavor text + characters who are weird and
funny and neither good nor bad -> the player forms REAL attachment ->
the game then puts what you love in harm's way (or makes YOU the harm —
the genocide route makes the player the villain, and every save point
counts the remaining kills like a butcher's ledger). The pacifist route's
warmth is what makes the genocide route unbearable. TRAUMA = ATTACHMENT
FIRST. A character nobody laughed with is a character nobody mourns.
[practitioner consensus across analyses]

=============================================================================
## FINDING 2 — FEAR & HUNGER'S TRICK: DREAD IS ANTICIPATED PERMANENCE
=============================================================================
F&H's horror is not jump scares — it is meagre resources + a sadistic but
CONSISTENT world + PERMANENT bodily consequence (limbs come off, in or out
of combat, and stay off) + gods whose indifference is visible (the coin
flip: your survival decided by something that does not care). Termina adds
the dramatis personae: 14 strangers, each fully realized, forced into the
same nightmare — the cast IS the stakes. Dread = the anticipation of
irreversible loss, priced in advance. [wiki/press + creator interviews]

## FINDING 3 — THE SCIENCE: FUNNY AND SCARY ARE ONE DIAL, NOT TWO
=============================================================================
Benign violation theory (McGraw/HuRL; the 2024 scream-then-laugh paper):
humor happens when something is WRONG (a violation) but appraised as SAFE
(benign), both at once. Fear and laughter are the SAME event with a
different safety appraisal — primates laugh at mock attacks. Psychological
DISTANCE (spatial, social, temporal, mental) is what flips a horror into a
joke and back. THE DESIGN CONSEQUENCE: funny->traumatizing whiplash is not
mixing two tones, it is MOVING ONE DIAL — perceived safety/distance — and
the designer owns that dial. Bonus: laughter floods the brain with
dopamine and lowers stress, which keeps players exploring — the comedy
literally funds the next scare. [peer-reviewed + lab sources]

## WHAT THIS MEANS FOR BOHEMIA (the challenge finding + applications)
=============================================================================
THE CHALLENGE FINDING (vs what we believe): our tone canon to date is
REALISM FIRST, and we have implicitly treated "grounded" as the opposite
of "goofy." Round 1 says that is wrong: comedy is not decoration on the
dark game, IT IS THE ATTACHMENT MACHINE THE DARKNESS RUNS ON. A Bohemia
that is only bleak will produce players who feel nothing when the sister
dies. Realism stays sovereign over WHAT happens; comedy is HOW the player
falls in love with who it happens to. The two do not compete.

WE ALREADY OWN MORE OF THE UNDERTALE SPINE THAN ANYONE NOTICED:
- Mercy states exist IN THE SHIPPED DIAL (dead/downed/broken/fleeing);
  kill-anyone is law; bosses are kill-or-spare with distinct grants; the
  witness organ makes the world SEE what you chose. That is the genocide/
  pacifist axis, already built. What is missing is the OTHER half:
  characters funny enough to make sparing feel like friendship and
  killing feel like loss.
- SQUIGGLE VOICES (already routed) are Animalese — a comedy instrument by
  construction. The ask-their-name system is a built-in JOKE DELIVERY
  SLOT: what a stranger says when you ask their name is where Undertale
  would put the first laugh.
- THE SAFETY DIAL IS OUR MAP: comedy lives where you are safe (your
  block, Marco the deadpan realist, the feed's absurd posts — apocalypse
  social media is benign violation by construction); dread lives where
  the light ends (nobody-patrols-the-dark + ambient danger = our F&H
  zone). WALKING BETWEEN THEM IS THE TONE TRANSITION. Composes perfectly
  with LIGHT=TERRITORY — tone is territorial, like everything else here.
- F&H PERMANENCE: cleared-stays-cleared, corpse-persist, companion
  permadeath — already law. The UNCANNY slot is already cast: the
  Network's eerily perfect lights, the ghost robotaxis, the purple — the
  uncanny is a familiar thing running on alien logic, never a jump scare.
  Lean in, never explain it.
- WHEN THE HIT LANDS, THE GAME MUST NOT WINK: distance collapses at
  trauma (the cold open's sister). No jokes in that room. The
  fireworks-or-muzzle-flash ambiguity already written into the opening is
  exactly the uncanny bridge between the two poles.
