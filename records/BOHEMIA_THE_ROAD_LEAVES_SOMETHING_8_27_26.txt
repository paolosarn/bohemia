--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOOT IS NOT A NEW SYSTEM, AND THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The obvious build is an item table, a rarity roll and an inventory. That build
would have been wrong, and this repo has five weeks of receipts for why.

BOHEMIA ALREADY HAS AN ECONOMY:

    engine/bohemia_economy.js   GOODS -- water (L, 4.0/adult/day), food (ration,
                                ~2000 kcal), meds (dose), fuel (L), power (kWh),
                                and SALVAGE (kg), whose own note reads "the
                                numeraire until Paolo names the money". Every one
                                carries a researched anchor.
    engine/bohemia_purse.js     the ledger, his three LOCKED currencies
                                (resources, electricity, clout), and four honest
                                entry kinds: source, drain, convert, transfer.

So loot is SALVAGE, in the currency the game already counts, landing in the
ledger the game already keeps. Which means the moment something drops it ALREADY
MEANS SOMETHING, instead of being an orphan number waiting for a system to give
it a job.

That is the invisible-hats failure prevented in advance instead of discovered in
five weeks. Seventeen hats nobody could see. Four Colorful garments worn by
nobody. An encounter director with zero callers, wired this morning. The pattern
is always the same: a finished thing with no connection to the rest.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MOST OF IT IS NOTHING, AND THAT IS THE FEATURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TEN YEARS COLD (Paolo 7/31, LOCKED): the crash is a decade in the past.

The research on real collapse looting is unambiguous about the order. Food and
water go in the first 24 to 48 hours. Then organised crews take the pharmacies
and the gun shops. IN THIS WORLD THAT HAPPENED TEN YEARS AGO. Anything a
desperate person could carry has been carried, twice.

And the game-design research lands in the same place from the other side:
scarcity is what makes a find feel like anything, and survival designers
deliberately leave most containers empty for exactly that reason.

REALISM AND FUN AGREE HERE, WHICH IS RARE, SO NOTHING HAD TO BE TRADED. Under
REALISM FIRST as amended 8/25 (fun carries the tie), there was no tie to break.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTHING IS EVER TAKEN FROM HIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

the_snatcher's approved verb is "grabs an item and RUNS -- a beat-timed chase"
and its ends is "loss without death". So A LOSS IS THE APPROVED DESIGN and I am
not overruling it.

It is not built here because THE CHASE IS NOT BUILT. An unavoidable loss with no
way to win it back is not the approved mechanic; it is a tax wearing its name.
When the chase exists, the snatcher takes something.

Today he takes nothing, and this is written down rather than half of an approved
idea being shipped quietly. The gate holds it: zero debits, ever.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A TAG IS FINE, A MARKET IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TEN YEARS COLD clause 2 bans the whole CATEGORY of economic gameplay: "exchange
rates. inflation. prices that move on a clock ... any market the player reads or
plays." And it draws the boundary itself:

    "What is banned is a price that MOVES BY ITSELF, not a price that exists.
     A tag is fine. A market is not."

This adds a FAUCET and nothing else. Every entry is currency `resources`, kind
`source` -- the ledger's own words for "created from nothing and injected". No
rate, no drift, no multiplier, no conversion, nothing the player can watch move.
The PRICES and PAYOUT tables stay empty and stay his.

THE NUMBERS 0 TO 3 ARE MINE. EVERYTHING COSTS ONE (8/15) reaches "any future
resource price anybody is tempted to invent", so these are small whole numbers
with a stated derivation instead of a curve, and they print in the gate every run
so he can move any of them with one word.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

There is no inventory, because the purse is the inventory and it already exists.
There is no rarity roll, because the amount is derived from the fiction.
There is nothing to spend it on yet: PRICES is empty and it is his.
The snatcher does not steal, and will when the chase is built.
