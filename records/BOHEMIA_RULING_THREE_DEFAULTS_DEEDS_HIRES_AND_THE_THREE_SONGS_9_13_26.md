## 1. A DEED WEIGHS WHAT IT COST YOU
THE HOLE: the standing table DEED_WEIGHT has 83 rows, every one for an authored quest
stage, and NOT ONE of the five deed kinds the walked city actually publishes (claim met,
claim refused, commit, favour, loan short) is in it. WORLD [someone lends] measured it:
"every act this surface publishes is witnessed, retold and weighs nothing." Two gates
(CITY DEEDS, CITY DIAL) are red on purpose "because he has not ruled", and the sweep
that fixes them by typing numbers would be the violation.
THE RULING: no table. A deed's weight is DERIVED from what it cost the doer, which the
game already measures: the minutes it spent (asking a name is free, showing up is 60
minutes, RUN measured both) and the batteries that moved. A free act weighs nothing; an
hour weighs an hour; a night's battery weighs a night. That is MECHANISM-MINE with no
contents to type, so the 83-row table stops waiting on him and the two red-by-design
gates get a real invariant to hold. Realism first: standing in a real street is earned
by what you gave up, not by a designer's score. He tunes nothing until he plays it and
hates it. -> FACTIONS [deeds weigh].

## 2. TAKING SOMEBODY ON COSTS ONE, LIKE EVERYTHING
THE HOLE: FACTIONS [recruit anywhere] shipped who is available where you stand and
deliberately did not build the act of taking them on, "a price is his."
THE RULING: EVERYTHING COSTS ONE is a pillar. Taking somebody on costs one battery a
night, on a handshake, collected at nightfall after rent and after the loan, the same
shape the night already has. Miss a night and they are owed, on the same card, and the
lender-stands rule applies at the fold. No roster: whether somebody is yours is computed
from the nights you paid, the way the company module already refuses a list. Realism
first: a day labourer is paid by the day and leaves when you stop. -> FACTIONS [take
them on].
