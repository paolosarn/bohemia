## HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT (rule 33; recall, marked)
The shop's grid, the stash and the brother's paperdoll never move. Hovering a piece shows its tooltip and the brother's numbers
show what it would change; you sweep the mouse across the shelf and read piece after piece without anything on the screen
shifting.

## THE RULING (rule 250)
(a) NOTHING MOVES: selecting a piece (rule 249's one tap) never moves, resizes, adds or removes any box or button on the screen: the
    shelf, the stash, the crew, the buy and sell buttons, the bar, the phone all stay exactly where they were. The facts show in a
    strip that is ALWAYS there, the same size, whatever is selected (empty when nothing is).
(b) COMB THROUGH THE SHELF: tap piece after piece and each one's effect shows at once in the same places; nothing has to be closed
    first, nothing scrolls on its own.
(c) THE TEAM SHOWS WHAT EACH PIECE DOES: the crew row stays on screen in the shop, and for the selected piece each man shows its
    effect on him (rule 223's icon, green up or red down arrow and how much; rule 202's 'what it does to your man'), the best fit
    marked; it changes in place as he taps the next piece.
(d) THE GATE: on the phone and computer, in the shop, the stall and the stash: tap ten pieces in a row: every box's and button's
    rectangle is the same before and after each tap (within a pixel); the facts strip has one fixed rectangle; the crew row shows a
    per-man arrow for each selected piece and updates without a scroll or a sheet.
(e) RUN TWO [nothing moves while he browses] with [one tap selects, two taps buy]; UI THREE NOTE (the facts strip and the item box
    keep one size); line 176; EYES checks.
