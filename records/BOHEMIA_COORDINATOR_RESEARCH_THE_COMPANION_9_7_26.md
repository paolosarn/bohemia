# THE COMPANION: A CHARACTER IS ONLY REAL IF SHE CAN REFUSE YOU (coordinator swing, 9/7)

## WHY THIS ONE
Nothing shipped on main since the last round, so the round is mine. On the board,
PEOPLE [walking companion] has sat for weeks as "needs Paolo: ROSA is a draft he has
not ruled on", while COMBAT already ships a companion who takes her own turn and

"needs Paolo" line with a defensible default is a line I failed to decide.

## WHAT THE RESEARCH SAYS MAKES A COMPANION LOVED OR HATED
- **Competence is the floor, not the ceiling.** Players forgive a lot if she is
  useful in the fight and never in the way; nothing else lands if she is not.
- **The hated companion interrupts.** The famous failure is a helper with a small
  set of lines who talks constantly. Frequency kills, not content.
- **A companion who only responds is furniture.** The one who feels alive WANTS
  something and works toward it whether or not you are watching -- concrete
  goals, standing grievances, lines she will not cross. A personality made of
  refusals lasts; one made of adjectives does not.
- **Emotional embedding covers technical sins.** A companion the story owns can be
  clumsy; a clumsy stranger is just a bug walking beside you.
- A validated design scale exists for this and it weighs exactly these things.
Sources: [AI RPG companions: a character is only real if they can refuse you](https://arcanumrpgs.com/blog/ai-rpg-companions/),
[an ode to AI sidekicks (The Ringer)](https://www.theringer.com/2020/06/30/video-games/last-of-us-part-ii-ai-sidekicks-non-player-characters),
[the Companion Design Scale (validated)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/382231173_What_does_the_perfect_Companion_in_Games_look_like_Development_and_Validation_of_the_Companion_Design_Scale),
[player relationships with AI game characters (CHI)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3706599.3720207).

## THE DECISION (correct-after; the NAME stays his)
1. ROSA walks with you on the street, under the draft name, tagged draft:true, per
   ALWAYS MAKE AN ATTEMPT (8/11): a name ships as an attempt, a decision waits.
   He renames her in one word if he hates it.
2. She wants something of her own, and it is already in the world: she is owed by
   somebody, or owes somebody, in the debt ledger that now names lenders. She
   works toward it whether or not you are watching. That is the whole difference
   between a companion and furniture, and it costs no new system.
3. She refuses. One thing she will not do (a faction she will not cross into, a
   person she will not rob), set from her standing, which already exists.
4. She talks rarely. A hard cap on lines per in-game hour, and never during a
   fight beat, because the fight teaches the beat alone.
5. She never blocks a doorway or a tap target. Competence is the floor.
