MEASURED: 66% of the world region at moon zoom is DEAD FLAT - 8x8 blocks
with zero texture variation - and one value (0.48) owns the frame from
median to 90th percentile. The valley diamond floats on empty beige.

IN COVERAGE ORDER:
1. THE SURROUND VOID (about two thirds of the screen). A real aerial at
   this height shows a BASIN: desert bajada texture on the valley floor,
   dark wash lines, the mountain ranges closing the horizon, and the
   interstate's dark line running in and out. Ours shows cardboard.
   AND THE ART ALREADY EXISTS: desert pavement #6e6045, wash #4a422f,
   mountain bedrock #5b5346 are all in the game's own terrain legend -
   this is placement at the coarse zoom, not new pixels. -> COOK
   [city from above]: the surround at moon zoom, from the existing
   terrain families. The aerial card now carries the rule (section 2B:
   THE VALLEY HAS A RIM).
2. THE CITY BLOCK READS DARK-NOISY AGAINST THE BRIGHT VOID (about 15%).
   The polarity problem the aerial verdict measured, amplified: with a
   bright void around it, the dark city grid reads as a smudge. Fixing
   liar 1 fixes most of this by giving the grid a mid-value basin to sit
   in; the card's band rules for ground/road/roof stand unchanged.
3. LABELS AND MARKERS DO NOT SCALE (a few percent, but loud). CHURCH and
   CUSTOM read wider than the districts they name; the white ring
   markers hold chart size at every zoom. A label wider than its
   district is chart chrome. -> the lane that owns the city labels, via
   the coordinator.
