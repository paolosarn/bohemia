## THE RULING (rule 191)
(a) A SHOOTER TAKES THE BEST SHOT: AUTO's ranged men choose the enemy they are most likely to hit; never a low-chance shot
    while a much better one is in reach. The kill-this-turn and the key man (rule 182) win only when their chance is close to
    the best one. (Sharpens rule 180(h) for shooters.)
(b) GEAR NEVER VANISHES SILENTLY: what a man carries into a fight he carries out, fight after fight, unless something in the
    fight took it (a shield smashed, a weapon broken, Battle Brothers' durability), and then the end screen says so in plain
    words ('his car door broke'). A spear and a car-door shield that were drawn in fight one and gone by fight three is a bug
    until proven a break: find which (the saved company, the bag swap, the layers, durability) and fix it at the source.
(c) THE GATES: in the stupid-move watch (rule 188), the count of shots taken below the best available chance by more than a
    set margin goes to zero; across ten fights in a row (173(e)), every man's gear before and after each fight matches unless
    the end screen named a break.
(d) COMBAT [a shooter takes the best shot] and [gear never vanishes] (with RUN TWO if the saved company or the stash is the
    cause); EYES checks ten fights' gear by eye.
