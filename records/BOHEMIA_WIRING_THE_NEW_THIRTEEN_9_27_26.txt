WIRING THE APPROVED THIRTEEN INTO THE FACTIONS  --  CHARACTER, 9/27/26, [runway redo]

HE APPROVED THE SET: "Fire make sure shit doesnt clip into places it shouldnt but yeah."
NOTES ARE RULINGS, so it goes into the game. The one real decision left is WHICH SHAPE
GOES TO WHICH FACTION, and the original search reserved that to him. EVERYTHING IS A
THUMB bans blocking on a thumb, so it is decided here BY A RULE, and the rule is
printed so one word can knock it down.

THE RULE: EVERY FACTION KEEPS ITS OWN BODY. A faction's dials are who it is, and they
were chosen long before the runway. So a faction may only take a shape built on ITS
OWN DIAL, and among those it gets the one whose silhouette is CLOSEST TO WHAT IT WEARS
TODAY. The valley changes register; nobody changes body.

*** AND THE COUNTS DO NOT MATCH, WHICH IS A REAL CONSEQUENCE AND NOT A ROUNDING
ERROR. *** The approved set has broad 3, small 4, lanky 3, tall 2, plain 1; the
factions need broad 4, small 4, lanky 2, tall 3. So these factions could NOT be
served on their own body and take the nearest one instead:
    Network: wanted tall, got small
    Remnants: wanted broad, got small
    Cartel: wanted small, got plain
    Volunteers: wanted small, got lanky
A faction quietly changing body is the kind of thing that gets noticed later and
blamed on nobody, so it is named here.

COLOUR IS TERRITORY, AND THIS IS WHERE IT WAS NEARLY LOST. Every garment in the
approved set is a NEUTRAL, because the search was on silhouette. Wiring them as they
came would have stripped thirteen factions of their colour in one commit. The twelve
faction colourways this lane added on 9/22 exist for exactly this moment, and the hair,
the head, the base shirt and the waist are kept untouched because they carry the rest
of the faction's colour and the search never touched them.
  factions wearing at least one garment in their own colour: 11 of 13
  wearing only neutrals in the three swapped slots: Homeless, Cartel  (their colour now rides on the shirt, hair and head alone)

TRENCHCOATS ARE RESERVED (Paolo 8/27, his number): thirteen factions times his ten
per cent is ONE, and the approved set carries TWO split-tail dusters. The floor-length
coat goes to Reds and the second one is not offered at all.
