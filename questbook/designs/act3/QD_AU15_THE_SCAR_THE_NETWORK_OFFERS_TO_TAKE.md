## THE SITUATION
Act 3. Iker (QD-AU05) or any veteran with a scar on their sheet is offered, by the dying Network, one last service before the hall goes dark: the memory of the fight that gave the scar, removed. The scar on the face stays.

## THE PERSON, THE PLACE, THE THING (never a card)
A Network attendant (a person, paid by the hall, tired) stops the company at the hall door. ATTENDANT: "The chair still works. It can take one memory before the power goes." / ATTENDANT: "He asked for it. I need someone to sign." / ATTENDANT: "The hall pays the escort. 8." The veteran stands behind her and says nothing.
- THE WANT, ONE LINE: The veteran wants to sleep; the attendant wants to do one last good thing; the Network wants the memory it takes.
- PAY: 8 BATTERIES from the hall, locked before the yes. SKULLS: 0. Declining is free and writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. SIGN: the memory goes. The veteran comes out lighter; he no longer knows why he hates Railroad Pass; he asks once why he has the scar.
B. DON'T SIGN, STAY WITH HIM: the company sits with him in the dark hall until the power dies; he tells the story out loud once; nothing is taken.
C. TAKE A COPY: the attendant can write the memory to a card before it is taken; he gets the card to keep in his pocket; he can choose to remember it later.
D. WE DON'T SIGN FOR OTHERS (`Q131.X7`, `Q139.P2`): the attendant nods; the hall pays the escort fee anyway; the veteran is angry for one night.
IF A FIGHT HAPPENS AND IS LOST: no fight is planned.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36); a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10% (the third votes). Followers never fight.

## WHAT THE WORLD SHOWS AFTER
THE WIN YOU SEE AND HEAR: the hall's lights going dark floor by floor on the settlement picture as you leave, with the hum dropping in steps; the veteran's line at camp after.
The veteran's portrait (the scar always stays); his camp line about Railroad Pass changes (A), is told once (B), or the card is in his pocket on his sheet (C). The Network's last records (A) feed the crisis.
