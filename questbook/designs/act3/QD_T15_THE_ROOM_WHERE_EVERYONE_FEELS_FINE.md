## THE SITUATION
The data centre pays sitters to watch the racks overnight. For a month, the sitters of hall B have not left. Their families bring food to the door. The sitters eat, smile, say they feel fine, and go back in. Elena Rosas has been in nineteen days. Her husband wants her home.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the data centre. Buildings: the hall (a security desk with one working screen), the market (closed). The hum is louder than it should be; the screen shows a queue of families at a side door with food.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Tomas Rosas, bus mechanic, grease in the lines of his hands, his wife's scarf round his neck.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Nineteen days. She says she's fine. She sounds fine." / "I want her in our kitchen saying it." / "The desk says it's voluntary. Everybody says it's voluntary."
- PAY: 20 batteries. SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (23); the ceiling: "I sold the bus parts for this." No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the families keep bringing food.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Elena, at the door of hall B, smiling, when the company arrives: "Oh, hi. Tell Tomas I'm fine. Really. I've never been this fine."

## THE CHOICES
A. CARRY HER OUT. The other sitters do not fight; they stand in the way, holding hands. A routine fight on the fight board, 2 to 4 minutes, where no one strikes back; the company pushes through. Elena comes home, angry, and is herself in ten days.
B. TALK HER OUT. One company member sits the night with her. She comes out in the morning. So does he, and he feels fine: for 30 to 40 days he is off the roster the way an injury is (rule 36), smiling.
C. PULL THE POWER. Cut hall B's feed at the breaker. Every sitter walks out blinking. The data centre's board posts nothing for a refresh, and the brownout next door lifts for a week.
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: Tomas is a client again later; Elena never speaks to the family. B: the company member's one wrong detail rides with him until he heals, then leaves. C: the data centre drops a tier in the derived future a little sooner; the Network's crumbling is one step faster at this base. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
