## THE SITUATION
Act 3. The Network is crumbling, and the towns outside the valley that ran on it are failing faster than the valley
is. Word has spread that the valley has water, work and light. A column of about forty people is walking in on the
interstate. The map drew the column two stops out before the stop, moving at walking pace toward the nearest
settlement. They are right about the answer this time: the valley is better. They are wrong about one thing: the
settlement they are heading for has a full hall and is turning people away, and the map knows it (its population
number sits at its cap).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The woman who talks for them, fifties, a dusty cream Bottega Veneta coat with the sleeves rolled, a child's backpack
on her front. She was born in the valley and left in the ruin.
First line: "I was born off Flamingo. Is it true there's work?"

## THE CHOICES (price on the button)
1. GIVE THEM WATER AND THE ROAD. Price: 1 water and 1 battery. They reach the settlement they chose. It turns half of
   them away; the other half stay. The valley's population number moves by the real amount.
2. TELL THEM WHERE THE WORK IS. Price: an hour of talk, and a little standing with the full settlement (shown), whose
   door you just talked people away from. They turn toward the settlement whose hall is hiring. All forty arrive.
3. KEEP MOVING. Price: nothing. They keep walking to where they were going.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Water and batteries, or an hour and a line of standing.
- The valley's population, a real number that moves either way.
- The group does not dissolve (`Q107.X6`): she appears again, on the settlement screen of whichever settlement she reached,
  working, and she nods. If the company pointed her, the next time the player opens THAT settlement's menu she is a
  client portrait in its hall with one small contract; accept or decline, and declining writes nothing.
- THE FLIP: if the player met QD-B06's families walking out in act 2, one face in this column is one of them,
  twenty years older. If that act was not played, the column is strangers. Both reads are written.

## THE NARRATOR
