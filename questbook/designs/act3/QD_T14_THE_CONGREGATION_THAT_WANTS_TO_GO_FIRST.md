## THE SITUATION
The family is building the real rocket. Everybody knows. The Deacon's people, forty old dealers and cocktail waitresses thrown out when the Strip shrank, built theirs out of water heaters and a billboard. The fins are painted by a nine-year-old, Yesi, the Deacon's granddaughter, who is not going. They need eight drums of hydrazine from the depot. They have saved for two years.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the chapel town. Buildings: the hall (the vestry), the clinic, the barber. In the yard, the rocket, visible from the screen, taller than the chapel.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: the Deacon, Ruperto Sol, 74, a dealer's vest under a white suit, gold cufflinks shaped like moons.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Eight drums from the depot. We have the money. Two years of it." / "Your family is going up. We are going first." / "Don't tell me what it is. I know what it is."
- PAY: 30 batteries. SKULLS: 2 (fuel is worth robbing).
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (33) and the ceiling: "That is the last of the tips." No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the rocket stands in the yard until the family's own launches.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Yesi, painting a fin, when the company comes to the yard after accepting: "Grandpa says the Moon is quiet. Is it quiet?"

## THE CHOICES
A. REAL FUEL. Bring eight good drums; a routine fight with fuel thieves on the depot road, 2 to 4 minutes on the fight board. They launch. It clears the power lines and comes apart over the lake. Nobody inside lives. It is what they asked for.
B. CUT FUEL. Bring drums cut with water. They launch; it lifts forty metres and falls in the wash. Most live, some are hurt. The Deacon knows who cut it.
C. SHOW HIM THE REAL ONE. Before the drums, take the Deacon to the family's launch site and let him see what one-way means. He decides. The sim decides with him, from his lines.
Same pay on every road; B's cost is the Deacon's face, not batteries.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: the yard is empty; Yesi paints the fins of the family's rocket instead, and her name is on it. B: the congregation is at the clinic; the Deacon is a client who never speaks to the family again. C: the rocket stays; it becomes the chapel's bell tower in the derived future. The feed on the phone carries each. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The billboard on the rocket's side is an ad for a casino that closed before any of them were born.
