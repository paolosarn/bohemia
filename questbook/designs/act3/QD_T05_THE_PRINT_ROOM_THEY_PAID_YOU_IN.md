## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the healing years. The towers have printed organs. The chapel clinic has a list of forty people who need one. A tower technician, Ines, will give the pattern file to one person, face to face, never over a wire, because the Network reads wires. The clinic cannot pay in batteries. It can build.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the chapel town. Buildings: the hall (the vestry), the clinic, the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Nurse Dalia Ferro, grey braid, a white Bottega coat with a clinic badge pinned upside down.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "The tower has the pattern. Ines will hand it over. To a person, not a wire." / "We can't pay you. We can build you a print room. On your lot." / "It prints for whoever you let in. That's yours to carry."
- PAY: the print room (a building on the family's lot, worth about 40 batteries of work). SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for batteries on top: 5, and the ceiling: "The room IS the money." No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the clinic's list stays forty long.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Ines, in a stairwell at the tower, after accept: "Don't write this down. Remember it."

## THE CHOICES
A. A DRIVE. She loads the file on a drive; the run home is one map day. A drive can be read: the Network's people meet the company at the tower gate, a routine fight on the fight board, 2 to 4 minutes.
B. HER HEAD. Ines walks out with the company and recites the file to the printer herself. Three map days; nobody can read a person.
C. BUY IT. The clinic buys a licence from the tower with its whole savings. No fight. The room prints only for patients the tower approves.
Same pay on every road: the room gets built. What differs is who it prints for.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
The print room stands on the family's settlement screen and on the map. A or B: a queue appears at the family's base (a picture, and the sound of forty people waiting), and the base's hall posts escort jobs for patients; the base draws a raid one time in three, because the room is worth stealing. C: the queue is short and well dressed. The clinic's list shrinks by the number the family lets in. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The first kidney the room prints has a birthmark on it.
