## THE SITUATION
Act 3, cyberpunk healing, but a local bust: the glass buyers in the reclaimed quarter defaulted, and the kilns fired a whole run of heat-shield tiles for the rocket on credit. The kiln master has one buyer left who pays on delivery: the rocket yard itself. Two days across the salt. He sends his own scout, Rafa, who has guided the company twice before. Rafa owes a debt crew. They hold his sister until he pays, and his price is the glass.

## THE CHOICES
The world testifies before the fork: at camp Rafa does not eat; he wears a woman's scarf; on day two he asks for the third time which side of the salt ridge you want. At the ridge, a debt crew of five is waiting, and Rafa steps off the cart and stands with them, not armed. Their leader says it plainly: "The glass pays his sister's debt. Walk away and nobody bleeds."
1. **Fight for the glass.** The fight is on the fight board at the ridge (the ridge is the mound). Rafa does not fight. After, he runs to find his sister and is gone from the company's life: the person lost. DONE; the glass is signed for; the fee. Any brother struck down rolls rule 36 (20 percent dead, else 30 to 40 days out).
2. **Pay Rafa's debt yourself.** Your own batteries, the amount the leader names. The crew leaves, the sister walks to the cart the next dawn. DONE; the fee; you are poorer and Rafa stays a scout the company can hire.
3. **"I won't fight over glass with a boy's sister in it."** Turn the cart back to the kilns. The priced refusal: no pay, the glass is Ezequiel's again, and he does not hire you again. Not a drop. Rafa is still lost to the crew.
