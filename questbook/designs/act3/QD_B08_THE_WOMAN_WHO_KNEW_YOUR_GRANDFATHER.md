# QD-B08: THE WOMAN WHO KNEW YOUR GRANDFATHER
ACT: 3 (reads the act 1 ledger: 1->3)
KIND: event
CRISIS: the Network crumbling
ECONOMY: boom
PLACE: an unlit service road under the new maglev line, between two pylons where no camera reaches
STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35)
SHAPE: E8, THE ONE WHO KNOWS YOU, crossed with E9, THE BILL COMES DUE (QR-B)

## THE SITUATION
Act 3, as the Network crumbles. On a dark service road, the one kind of place nothing records, the party finds an old
woman sitting on a cooler. She knows what the Gen 1 ancestor did on a road in act 1: the one choice, stop or pass, at
a stranger on a shoulder (QD-B01 or any E1 the player met). If the ancestor stopped, she is the one who was helped,
or her daughter. If the ancestor passed, she is still on the road. She also knows something no living person could
know: the exact words the ancestor said, which were never recorded anywhere.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The old woman, a threadbare Rick Owens coat, a face drawn in the same family style as the stranger in act 1 (older).
First line, if the ancestor stopped: "Your grandfather said 'just the leg' back to me. I kept that."
First line, if the ancestor passed: "He didn't stop. I'm not angry. I'm asking what you'll do."

## THE CHOICES
1. ASK HER HOW SHE KNOWS THE WORDS. No cost. She answers with something that is almost right and then is wrong in a
   way only the Network could be wrong. A private flag (the Network's reach, fading).
2. PAY THE OLD DEBT. If the ancestor stopped: she gives you something (a key to a cache from act 1). If he passed:
   you give her 10 batteries and a seat to the next town. No reward difference in meaning; different in what.
3. KEEP MOVING. She says "Many did." Nothing is written except that you passed, again.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- A private flag the Network's endgame reads (choice 1).
- Batteries or a cache (choice 2).
- The family ledger across acts: stop or pass, twice.
- The echo: the rest stop on the act 3 map at the act 1 shoulder exists only if the ancestor stopped (a road
  feature written across acts).

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Her shadow falls toward the pylon light, not away from it.
