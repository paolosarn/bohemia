## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the valley healing, and the council has done what healing economies sometimes do: it cancelled the old debts. The law says the debts are dead. The paper that proves it is in a box at the tower. Collectors who bought the debts at a discount have the list too, and they know a debtor who has not seen the paper will still pay.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the tower offices, half its floors now flats. Buildings: the hall (the old foreclosure counter), the market, the barber, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the foreclosure counter, its last day. Two portraits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Imelda Paz, the tower's last foreclosure clerk, glasses on a chain, a Balenciaga boxy blazer two sizes big.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Two hundred and twelve papers that say you owe nothing. The collectors got the list before I did." / "The council says the debts are dead. The collectors say, not till it's in your hand." / "Four stops. Get there first."
- PAY: 10 batteries. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 12. A second ask: "Twelve. It's my last job, not my last battery." Advance: 3 now, 3 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. RACE THEM ON THE PAVED ROAD. Fast. One collector crew waits at the second stop; a short fight on the fight board in a courtyard, or show them the council stamp and watch them do the math.
B. BLOCK BY BLOCK, THE BACK WAYS. No fight. Slower: two blocks are reached after the collectors, and those families paid a debt that no longer existed. Imelda writes their names down to refund them.
C. HAND THE BOX TO THE CO-OP. Half the stops; the co-op carries the rest. The co-op gets the thanks and the family is less known in the outer blocks.
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: rent at the outer blocks' markets falls one; the collectors become a roaming party that thins and then leaves the map. B: the tower's next board posts a refund run to the two late blocks. C: the co-op's next board is one offer richer. The tower's board never posts a collection job again. Declining leaves nothing; Imelda walks the papers herself and the collectors win most of the race.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
There are 213 papers in the box, not 212, and the last one is stamped cancelled with no name on it.
