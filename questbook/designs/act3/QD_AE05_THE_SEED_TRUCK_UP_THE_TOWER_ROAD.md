## THE CHOICES
A. PAST THE FEED STORE. Pavement, one day. The feed store crew (five, farm tools and two rifles) stops the truck: fight on the frontage block, routine, 3 minutes, or talk.
B. GIVE THEM FOUR TRAYS. They need seed as much as the tower. Priced and said: the tower gets 36, Wendell pays 18; the feed store later posts its own small contract on the phone's board (a new client the player made).
C. THE LONG WAY. Dirt, plus one day, no crew, the trays wilt: arrive on day 2 with a third gone, or on day 3 if 3 WATER is spent misting them.
