## THE SITUATION
Shaped from the ACT3 MOONSHOT law: the Network's god has gone silent, its leaders want the
severance reversed, and members WITHOUT NeuroLink implants can be turned while CARRIERS cannot. The
path (conversion or war) is set by act 2. This beat puts that moral line on one family, so the
split is felt, not read: a Network member without an implant comes to the family, asking for help
getting her brother, who is a carrier, away from the mast before the leaders send him to it. It
plays on either path; on the war path she is a defector, on the conversion path she is the first of
many.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The sister, a Network member in the faction's clean uniform with one sleeve torn off. She waits at
the foot of the mast and speaks only when the family taps her there, on the rooftop's settlement screen, where no one else can hear (rule 38). First line: "My
brother is on their list for tonight. He doesn't know he's on it. He thinks it's his idea."

## THE CHOICES
1. TAKE HIM OFF THE MAST BY FORCE. Costs: a fight on the fight board against Network guards (and her
   brother among them), MEDS for the medic after, and the Network's leaders learn the family
   guards the relay. Gain: the mast holds. Her brother lives, wired, and cannot be turned; she stays.
2. TALK TO HER, NOT TO HIM. Costs: time; the family lets the attempt on the mast happen and meets it
   there, so the relay is damaged and must be repaired with TAPE and batteries (rule 47). Gain: she and the un-implanted
   people she knows come over; on the conversion path they later help build the rocket.
3. LET HER TAKE HIM HOME HERSELF. Costs: the mast is guarded only by the family's own people that
   night, and one named companion is posted there instead of on rocket work. Gain: nobody in her
   family is fought; the family owes nothing and is owed a quiet favour, off the record.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Relay condition; Network standing; whether she and her people converted; the brother alive or not.
The act 3 city shows it (a repaired mast, a family of Network deserters on the family's street).
