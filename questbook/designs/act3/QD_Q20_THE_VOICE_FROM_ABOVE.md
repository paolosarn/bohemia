## THE SITUATION
Shaped from the story master: the Angel heir goes and does not return; the dynasty ends looking down at the planet; LIBERATE, RESPECT or BECOME resolves at the Moon. What the Moon IS stays Paolo's. This beat is the ground side, so the rocket never lands offscreen. The family and the company who stayed (QD-Q19) sit in the kitchen. The radio carries the heir's voice from above. The player plays the people at the table, listening.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The heir, on the radio, crackle and delay: "I can see the valley. It's so small. Is the kitchen light on?"

## THE CHOICES
1. TURN THE LIGHT ON. Someone at the table flips it; the heir says they can see it (they cannot; it is too small; they say it anyway).
2. ANSWER. The player picks one line from what the family said on the ridge (QD-Q15), if it was said, or from the heir's own act 3 deeds.
3. SAY NOTHING. The table listens. The heir talks for a while, then signs off.
Then the roll: the heir, from above, names who stood where on launch day, and the people at the table answer "here" or are silent for the ones who are not.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
This is the ground-side roll call: every post from QD-Q19, every home base standing or fallen, read aloud as places the heir can see lit or dark from above. The stance (LIBERATE, RESPECT, BECOME) is one line of the heir's, written three ways, Paolo's to set. DEFAULT: the heir names only the kitchen.
