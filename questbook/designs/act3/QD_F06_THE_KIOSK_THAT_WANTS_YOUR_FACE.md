# QD-F06: THE KIOSK THAT WANTS YOUR FACE
ACT: 3 (the first minute after a flip from 1 or 2)
KIND: main-beat
CRISIS: the rocket
ECONOMY: boom
PLACE: the same family block, reclaimed: rewired, lit, screens on old walls; above the rooftops, far off, the rocket's gantry
STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35)

## THE SITUATION
He taps the Gen 3 face on the phone and squeezes in. Act 3 is the Angel era: cyberpunk healing, the
rocket being built. The block is derived from acts 1 and 2 (three acts law s2a) and reads techier than
anything before it where it was reclaimed (32b). The clock restarts: sixty seconds of silence.

SECOND BY SECOND:
- 0-10 s: one cell per press on a sidewalk that is now smooth. A delivery cart (2x1) rolls along its
  fixed cell route on the beat; walking into its path stops it, a PROP that yields. Hum of screens.
- 10-30 s: the gantry is visible over the roofs from every open cell on the block, the compass. Where the
  family house stands (derived), one thing the past left is on the path (the socket block, or the painted
  cable, or the lamp, as in QD-F05). A public charge kiosk on the corner: a screen and a lens, no words,
  a face icon and a battery icon.
- 30-60 s: the silent choice. STAND on the kiosk's cell for one beat: the lens looks at him and a
  charged cell drops into the kiosk's tray, a thing he can take (no counter moves in the first minute).
  Or WALK PAST it. People go by in runway coats; some stop at the kiosk, some cross the street
  to avoid it. Nobody looks at him.
- 60-180 s: two workers in harnesses, walking toward the gantry, to each other: "Dicen que sube en tres
  semanas. Que no lleva a nadie que tenga cara en el sistema." (Overheard. Names the gantry.)

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
After 60 s, within two cells, an old woman sitting on the charge kiosk's step who never uses it: "Free
power. Free. Everybody forgets what free costs." One line. She does not explain.

## THE CHOICES
1. USE THE KIOSK: +1 battery. His Gen 3 face is now recorded. Nothing happens now.
2. WALK PAST: 0. His Gen 3 face stays unrecorded, the Amalgamation's blind spot.
3. SIT WITH THE OLD WOMAN: nothing now; she is on that step every time he comes by.
