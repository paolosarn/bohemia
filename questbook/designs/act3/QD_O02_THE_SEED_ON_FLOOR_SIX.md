## THE SITUATION
The future goes both ways. If the player raided the farm tower in act 1, act 3's tower is a raided base: one
offer on its board, pay in kind, windows boarded on the lower floors. (If the tower was never raided, this
contract appears at a town-tier tower with batteries as pay; the default read for an act not played is "never
raided".)

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the tower, three lit floors of fourteen. Buildings: the hall, SCAVENGE, a market with one stall.
  No barber. No clinic.
- BUILDING: the hall, one portrait.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Abuela Reyes, a grey Bottega knit, soil on the cuffs.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "The seed store is at the co-op. They'll sell. Nobody here can carry it back. Pago en comida,
  one crate a week, a year."
- PAY: food, one crate a week for a year of map time (no batteries). NEGOTIATION: advance only (the first crate now).
  Ask for more: "There isn't more." DECLINE: nothing written.

## THE CHOICES
1. Buy and carry the seed on the map road (two stops); a roaming party on the dirt road may stop the party.
2. Take the long road, safer, three extra map days; the tower's lights come on one floor later.
3. Sell half the seed at the co-op for batteries: the contract finishes, the tower grows half.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Declining leaves nothing. Done: one more lit floor, and the market adds a vegetable. The abuela never names the
raid. Half: half a floor, a line on the feed. Dropped: a deed.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
On floor six, one tray still has the act-1 raiders' boot print dried into the soil, and nobody has watered over it.
