## THE SITUATION
The council wants a trial. The wash camp says Güero is the reason their children do not get the runs. He does not hide. He sits outside his shed with a filter in his lap every morning.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the co-op blocks. Buildings: the hall (the laundry room, now with a real noticeboard), the market, the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Councillor Irene Paz, 58, whose father died in the fire, a pressed grey suit with a mourning band.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Güero Mata. The grain store. Eleven names; one is my father's." / "He fixes filters now. I know. Everyone tells me." / "Bring him to the council. Alive. We decide."
- PAY: 30 batteries. SKULLS: 1 (the wash camp may not let him go).
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (34); the ceiling: "This is the council's money. Count it." No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; Güero keeps fixing filters.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Güero, not getting up: "Took you twenty years. Let me finish this one filter."

## THE CHOICES
A. BRING HIM. He comes willingly. The wash camp's young men block the road: a routine fight on the fight board, 2 to 4 minutes, where the family can pull every shot.
B. BRING HIS CONFESSION. He tells it; a company member carries it down in his own words. A priced refusal: the fee halves; Güero stays at the camp.
C. BRING THE COUNCIL TO HIM. Irene and two councillors ride with the company to the wash camp and watch him work for a morning. Same fee. The council decides there.
The verdict in every road is the council's (QR-S rule 12), drawn by the sim from what they saw and heard.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
The council's verdict, whatever it is, sets the next boards: if he hangs, the wash camp's water sickness returns next refresh and its board posts a filter job; if he goes free, Irene resigns and the co-op's board changes client. Either way, the eleven names go up on the co-op's wall in the derived future. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Every filter Güero has ever fixed has eleven holes drilled in its rim.
