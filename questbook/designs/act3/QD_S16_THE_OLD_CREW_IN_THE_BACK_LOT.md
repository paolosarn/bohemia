## THE SITUATION
Act 3. The cartel yards became a freight house with a council licence and a new boss, Lupe Salas, the old boss's daughter. Her father's crew of seven old gunmen still sit in the back lot where the product used to be kept, with the guns, and they think it is 2060. The inspectors come in six days. If they find the guns, the yards are a cartel again.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the freight house, painted, a council plaque on the gate. Buildings: the hall (a real office now, with a plant), the market, the barber, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the office. Two portraits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Lupe Salas, a sharp Bottega coat over coveralls, her father's watch loose on her wrist.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "My father's men are in the back lot and they think it's 2060." / "Inspectors in six days. If they find guns, we're a cartel again, and I'm done being a cartel." / "Get them out. I care that they're alive. They held me as a baby."
- PAY: 12 batteries. SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 14. A second ask: "Fourteen. My father would have shot you for the first one." Advance: 4 now, 4 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. MOVE THEM BY FORCE. A fight on the fight board in the back lot among stacked crates. Lupe asked for them alive: struck-down old men get the law's injury, not a grave, unless the dice say otherwise, and she will know.
B. BUY THEM IN. Carry Lupe's offer: licensed night guards for the freight house, her wages, no guns but the licensed ones. The oldest says yes if the family's truck is the one he remembers from the tyre run (QD-S04); otherwise it takes one more night of talk.
C. WALK THEM OUT. Escort the seven, with their guns, two legs south to the valley's edge. They leave the way they came in.
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: the inspectors pass the yards; the board posts haul jobs from now on. B: the old crew stand at the gate as the freight house's night guard, portraits the player can meet on the next board. C: a roaming party of seven old men exists south of the valley. A: the clinic at the chapel holds some of them for 30 to 40 days, visibly. Declining leaves nothing; Lupe fails the inspection and the yards' board goes back to gunman jobs for an act.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The back lot's old radio calls the cartel's convoy call signs every night at eleven, on a frequency nobody has used in thirty years, and gets an answer.
