## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the valley healing and techier than the start. Brownouts still happen (never total dark), but now they come
from demand, not collapse (`Q088.W4`: the grid is a process with a clock). Tonight the rocket yard runs an engine
test, and the feeder it shares with the solar road sags. As the party passes, the night school's lights go brown.
The map shows the cause before the stop: the test stand is lit red and the feeder line between them is dimmed.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The teacher, forties, reading glasses on a chain, a pale Balenciaga hoodie with chalk on both sleeves.
First line: "Every test night. Twenty grown people learning to read, in the dark."
