## THE SITUATION
Act 3. The rocket's first test fire is set at the launch yard on the dry lake. A pad needs a flood of water under it when the engine lights, or the sound shakes the pad apart. The depot has three tanker trucks of chilled water and one driver who has done the road.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the depot. Buildings: the hall (dispatch window), the yard (trucks for hire), the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Ines Garza, dispatcher, a Bottega Veneta weave jacket over coveralls, a clipboard of paper because the screens lie now.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Three tankers to the dry lake. Four days, then they light it." / "If it's late they light it dry and the pad cracks." / "The overpass crew wants trucks. They don't care what's in them."
- PAY: 30 batteries and 8 FOOD. SKULLS: 2. Late (day 5 or after): 20 batteries, said now, not later.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (34); ceiling: "That's what the yard pays me in a month." Advance: 8 batteries, shown as 8 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; she sends the trucks with the driver alone and the phone shows how that went.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Ines, not looking up from the clipboard: "You came from the city side. Did you see the dry lake post? They want the dogs gone too."

## THE CHOICES
A. THE OVERPASS. Pavement, one day. The overpass crew (six, scrap rifles) blocks it: talk (they want 6 ROUNDS), or the fight board on the overpass block, routine, 3 to 4 minutes, the trucks behind your line, the mound is the on-ramp.
B. THE RAIL BED. Dirt beside the old tracks, plus one day, no crew. One tanker splits a seam on the second night: spend 5 TAPE or arrive with two of three.
C. "TWO TRUCKS, NOT THREE." Leave one at the depot. Priced and said: 18 batteries; Ines: "Two is a wet pad. Two is not a dry one."

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Delivered whole and on time: the test fire happens on the date, the feed posts the plume, and the depot and the launch yard share a trade line (water at cost). Late or two trucks: the fire slips four days and the launch yard says so on its board. The driver tells the pad chief where the hyenas drink (the dripping valve on the yard's tank farm), which makes QD-AE02 easier if the player took it; it is never needed. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
After the seam splits, the tanker's gauge still reads full, and stays full when it is empty.
