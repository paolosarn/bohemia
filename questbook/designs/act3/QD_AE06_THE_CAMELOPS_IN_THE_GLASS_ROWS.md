## THE SITUATION

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE

## THE CHOICES
A. DRIVE THEM. A road event, not a fight: walk the herd to the horse ranch west (one map day); the ranch pays 4 batteries a head, which the company keeps. Same total as B.
B. PUT THEM DOWN. The fight board on the greenhouse block, routine, 3 to 4 minutes, a kick knocks a man down. The meat sells for the same 16 batteries as the ranch pays; no more.
C. "I WON'T TOUCH THE HERD. I'LL FENCE THE ROWS." 6 TAPE and a day. Priced and said: half pay; the herd moves to the next farm on the board.
