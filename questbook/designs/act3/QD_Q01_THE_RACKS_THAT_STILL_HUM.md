## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the Network is crumbling and its local halls are going dark one by one. The salvage yard buys the racks for the rocket: good copper, good cooling pumps, good glass. A second hall out past the wash still has power, and the salvagers will not go in because "it is still running". The job: strip it. It pays in parts, which is the late scarcity (QR-Q rule 1). The Amalgamation's threat follows proximity to the secret (`Q085.P7`), so this is a plain job unless the player reads what is on the screens.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the salvage yard. BUILDING: the hall (a shipping container with a desk and a fan).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: TOMÁS, sixties, cut-off Balenciaga work coat, safety glasses pushed up, grease on one lens.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Hall two past the wash. Still got power." / "Pull the racks, bring me the pumps." / "Don't read the screens. Just pull."
- PAY: 6 batteries and one rocket cooling pump credited to the family's share of the build. NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 8; push again, he says "then pull it yourself and sell it yourself" and holds at 8. Advance: 2.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. DECLINING writes nothing. Somebody else strips it next month and the feed says the hall went dark.

## THE CHOICES
A. PULL AND DON'T LOOK. The fight board: the hall is a few house-sized tiles, the racks are cover, the loading door is the way in. A scavenger crew arrives on the second night: a group fight on the beat. Full pay.
B. READ THE SCREENS. The screens show a queue of portraits of the dead, updating. Reading costs half a night and moves the family one step closer to the secret; a roaming party starts following on the map. Pay the same (`Q121.X2`: the crueller or riskier branch never pays more).
C. "I WON'T PULL THE LAST RACK." One rack runs a grief app a whole street still uses (`Q071.X1` guard: the player hears it from a woman at the door). Leave it. Tomás pays 4, not 6, "for the rack you didn't pull". Finished, not dropped.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: a deed; the rocket's cooling line lists the family. B: the proximity line ticks. C: the street keeps its app until the Network's end, and the feed shows a post from the woman. Declined: nothing.
