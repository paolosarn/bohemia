## THE SITUATION

The block where that first fight was cut from has been rebuilt by the reclaimers: glass, solar, a market. She wants to stand on the house tile where she first killed a man and see what is there now.

- PAY: None (she asks; the yes is free and the no is free). The shop owner gives the company 5 BATTERIES of goods if Pepa tells the story. SKULLS: 0. Declining is free and writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. STAND THERE: the company walks her to the shop; it is a children's clinic now. She stands in the waiting room. She says one line about the man she killed. Nobody in the room knows what she means.
B. TELL THE STORY: she tells the shop owner the whole fight; the owner puts a small brass plate by the door with the date.
C. DON'T GO IN: she looks through the window and turns back; 'it's better than what we had'.
D. NO (`Q131.X7`): she wipes the sand drawing with her boot and goes to sleep.
IF A FIGHT HAPPENS AND IS LOST: no fight is possible; this is the act where things got better (THE FUTURE GETS BETTER, the second votes).
