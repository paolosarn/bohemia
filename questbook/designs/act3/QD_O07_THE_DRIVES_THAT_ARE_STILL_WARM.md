## THE SITUATION
The Network's seat is losing its tier. Its board, under the fever rule, shows offers that pay too well. This one
is the only one a sane company takes.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the data centre, fans roaring, half the lights dead. Buildings: hall, market (empty shelves), SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the hall, one of two portraits (the other is blank, and stays blank).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Ines Oyelaran, archivist, a silver Rick Owens hood.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Forty drives to the dam's cold room before the power goes here. Don't plug them in. Don't
  let anybody plug them in."
- PAY: 12 batteries. NEGOTIATION: ask for more and she pays 14 without a face change (the wrong sign). Advance 4.

## THE CHOICES
1. Haul to the dam as asked.
2. Drop them in the lake from the dam road: finished at a price, 6 batteries, she does not argue.
3. Sell two drives at the Strip on the way: the Strip pays 4, and Ines counts thirty-eight and pays 8, not 12 ("I pay for
   what arrives"): the same 12 (QR-G line 30), and the Strip's board gets a strange offer next visit.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Declining leaves nothing. Hauled: the dam's cold room is a SCAVENGE find later. Lake: nothing is ever found.
Sold: a deed and a change to the Strip's board.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The drives are warm in the truck bed an hour after they were unplugged.
