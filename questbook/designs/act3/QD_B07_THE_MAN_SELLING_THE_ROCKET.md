ACT: 3

## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the valley healing, a boom. The rocket is being built, and its parts are the most valuable things in the
valley. A trader on the road has one: a guidance board, clean, in a padded case. It is exactly what the build needs.
It was stolen from the clinic network, where the same chip family runs the dialysis machines.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The trader, young, clean, a Balenciaga shell jacket in hospital white, very polite.
First line: "Genuine. Off a machine that didn't need it anymore. Mostly."

## THE CHOICES
1. BUY IT. Costs 40 batteries (boom price). The rocket's build ledger jumps a stage. Two clinics run short a
   machine each; a feed post later carries a named patient.
2. BUY IT AND RETURN IT. Costs 40 batteries and a day: you drive it back to the clinic. Standing with the clinic
   network rises; the rocket waits for the slower, legal part.
3. KEEP MOVING. Nothing. The board goes to someone else's hands, and the map shows where.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries.
- The rocket's build stage.
- Standing with the clinic network.
- A named person's health (choice 1), on the feed.
- Where the board went (choice 3): the party that bought it is on the map.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The board's serial sticker has the family's surname printed on it, the same as the player's, and the trader does not
notice.
