## THE SITUATION
Act 3, cyberpunk healing: the valley is reclaimed and techy, and the Network is crumbling as the rocket
rises. Data-portraits of the dead flicker in and out on old screens across the city. In this diner one
booth is set for two. On the booth's screen a man's portrait talks with no sound. An older woman in a
tailored white Balenciaga coat sits across from it, eating slowly, and every few beats she answers it.
The waiter steps around her. The player can eat at the counter and leave.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
INÉS CALLOWAY (name Paolo's). Tapped, portrait up. The screen's portrait does not speak to the player,
ever.
FIRST LINE (FL05, the truth said as a complaint): "He's better company than he was. That's the problem."
THE TERMS: "His box is in the back, a drive the size of a deck of cards. Take it to the relay at the
rocket yard and hand it to the loader. It goes up with the rocket, one way. One clout: I tell the whole
counter who carried him."
WITHHELD: that the relay at the rocket yard will wipe anything not on the manifest, and his name is not
on it. She knows. She has not decided whether that is what she wants.

## THE CHOICES
1. TAKE IT. One clout on return (she says his name and the player's to the diner, out loud; the feed
   carries it). The walk to the rocket yard is a map trip; the drive in the player's pocket makes the
   Network's attention rise with proximity, so the road is harder than it looks.
2. TAKE IT, AND ASK FOR A DIFFERENT ONE: one battery instead of clout. "Keep it quiet, then. Fine."
   Two asks land.
3. NO. "Of course." She turns back to the screen. Nothing is written; the booth stays set, and on later
   passes she is still there, and the portrait flickers a little more each time the Network loses ground.

THE ROAD TO THE YARD (QR-AC, lines 9, 12 and 23; `Q087.X4`, `Q139.P2`, `Q095.X3`): three ways: the paved road (fast; the drive draws the Network's attention, a fight on the fight board); the tunnels (a day slower; underground the pull drops, the blind spot of the unrecorded); or the registrar's gate first (the manifest side route).
