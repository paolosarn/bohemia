## THE SITUATION
Act 3. The rocket's blast wall needs fired brick, and the brickworks is firing its biggest kiln in twenty years. A firing takes one night and cannot stop. Last time, a crew stole half the stack while it cooled.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the brickworks. Buildings: the hall (kiln office), the kiln, the clay pit, the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Socorro Llamas, kiln master, a scorched Bottega Veneta leather apron, eyebrows gone from the heat.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "One night. The fire can't stop and neither can you." / "The launch yard buys the first ten thousand bricks." / "The rest I sell. Cheap, to whoever stood here tonight."
- PAY: 16 batteries and 4 WATER, and the brickworks sells the company bricks at cost for the act (a price, said now).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Socorro, a hand on the kiln door, not burned: "You can tell by the sound when it's right. Like a crowd far off."

## THE CHOICES
A. THE WHOLE NIGHT ON THE YARD. The crew comes at three in the morning: the fight board is the kiln yard, the kiln is hot cover, routine, 3 to 4 minutes.
B. MEET THEM ON THE ROAD. Wait at the clay pit road and turn them before they reach the yard; a road event, talk or fight, plus no damage to the stack.
C. "I'LL WATCH, I WON'T FIGHT." Light the yard and stand in it; the crew takes a quarter and leaves. Priced and said: 10 batteries, bricks at cost still.
