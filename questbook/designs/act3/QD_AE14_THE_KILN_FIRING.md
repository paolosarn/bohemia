## THE SITUATION
Act 3. The rocket's blast wall needs fired brick, and the brickworks is firing its biggest kiln in twenty years. A firing takes one night and cannot stop. Last time, a crew stole half the stack while it cooled.

## THE CHOICES
A. THE WHOLE NIGHT ON THE YARD. The crew comes at three in the morning: the fight board is the kiln yard, the kiln is hot cover, routine, 3 to 4 minutes.
B. MEET THEM ON THE ROAD. Wait at the clay pit road and turn them before they reach the yard; a road event, talk or fight, plus no damage to the stack.
