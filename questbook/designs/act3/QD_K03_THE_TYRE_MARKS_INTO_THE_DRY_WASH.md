## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the valley healing, the rocket under construction and its parts the most valuable things in the valley.
In act 3 the roads are smooth and a set of tracks stands out: three heavy trucks left the paving within the last
day and went down into the wash. The map has shown the marks since the last trip (`Q050.W9`: the world is seen
doing it). The tracks are real and belong to a real party on the map: the rocket yard's own night crew, pulling
copper off the old pylon line in the wash, the line two clinics east still run on. The reading is built to be misread
once (`Q148.W3`): heavy, fast and at night, it reads like raiders.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The company's companion, kneeling at the edge of the paving (the companion is the mouth; the tracks are the thing).
First line: "Three trucks, loaded coming out. Nobody loads up in a wash."
He says it plainly, so the clue is never too subtle (`Q038.X3`).

## THE CHOICES (price on the button)
1. FOLLOW THE MARKS. Price: three hours. At the end, the yard's crew and a cut pylon line. What happens there is on
   the wash's screen and at your pace: talk, watch, or leave. No fight is forced; the crew is not hostile unless the
   company draws first (then rule 36 holds for anyone struck down: 20% dead, else out 30 to 40 days).
2. MARK IT AND MOVE ON. Price: ten minutes. The wash goes on the map as a named place. The next time the player
   opens the menu of the settlement whose clinics run on that line, its HALL carries a contract offer screen about
   the missing copper (the client's portrait, the fee, accept or decline; declining writes nothing).
3. KEEP MOVING. Price: nothing. The marks fade from the map in two days, the way all tracks do.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Time, as priced.
- Follow: the player knows who took the copper. That knowledge is the whole reward; no battery sits in the wash.
- Mark: a place on the map and a contract in a hall, both optional from then on.
- Keep moving writes nothing. The world still runs (`Q065.W6`): a week later the feed carries a post that the east
  clinics are on generator hours. It names no one and grades no one.

## THE NARRATOR
Read over the event screen in the machine voice (rule 45: too even, tape and room, a little wrong; never in the first minute; people on the screen still speak in the squiggle): "Three trucks went in. The tracks are clear about that. They are less clear about the rest."

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
