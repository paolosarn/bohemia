## THE SITUATION
The plate was made in act 1 out of a road sign and car doors, patched in act 2, and it has hung in the family house since. The act-3 heir, generated from the one before, came out small. The plate does not fit her. The towers pay for anything from the crash, and they pay for dents.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the tower offices. Buildings: the hall (a white showroom), the market, the barber, the lifts.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Iker Montaño, collector, silver hair, a Balenciaga archive coat with the crash-era label outside.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Your family's plate. Road sign, car doors. I've seen the photos." / "Twelve nights on a mannequin, then it comes home. Twenty-five." / "Or I buy it. Ninety. Think about it on the way down."
- PAY: 25 batteries to lend it. SKULLS: 1 (the Strip leg of the show tour).
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (30); the ceiling: "I am the only one who wants a road sign." The sale price is spoken, never pushed.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the plate stays on the wall.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The heir, at the family house, looking at the plate before leaving: "It's heavier than it looks. They said it would be."

## THE CHOICES
A. LEND IT. Escort it to the show and back, a routine fight on the Strip leg (2 to 4 minutes on the fight board). It comes home.
B. SELL IT. Ninety batteries. The plate leaves the family; the heir buys new armour that fits.
C. CUT IT DOWN FIRST. The family smith refits it to the heir (10 batteries, 4 map days) BEFORE the show. The dents that record the old fights are hammered flat. Iker pays the same; the heir wears it after.
Same pay for A and C; B is the only sale and is spoken on the screen.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: the plate on the wall, unchanged, and the show's photo on the city-view phone's feed. B: in the derived future the plate is in a tower window; the heir can see it from the street on every visit. C: the heir wears the plate; its fight stats are the plate's, its look is flatter. TWIST: Iker keeps it; the job becomes a recovery (a routine fight in the showroom) at the same pay. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
On the mannequin, the plate is a perfect fit.
