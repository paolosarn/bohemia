## THE SITUATION
The Network is failing. Implants that talked to it go quiet. The clinic holds the player's own struck-down man for
his 30 to 40 days; in the next bed, a woman whose implant keeps asking for a server that is gone. This offer comes
from the clinic building, not the hall: the clinic's board is where transport contracts are born.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the chapel town, candles and fluorescent tubes. Buildings: hall, clinic, market, barber.
- BUILDING: the clinic, one portrait.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Dr. Ana Belmonte, a black Owens smock, a stethoscope wrapped in tape.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Bed eleven needs the old techs at the data centre. Two days on the road, she can travel.
  I'll keep your man here gratis while you're gone."
- PAY: 4 batteries and your injured man's care free (his FOOD and MEDS for the term, rule 47). NEGOTIATION: advance of 1. Ask for more: she will not move.

## THE CHOICES
1. Carry her by road to the data centre; the techs switch the implant off.
2. Carry her to the co-op where a man says he can keep it talking: she lives with it.
3. "I won't take her into the Network's house": finished at a price, 1 battery, the doctor finds another way.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Declining leaves nothing, and your man's care is still paid in batteries as before. Done: the clinic's board adds
one transport offer next visit. Dropped: a deed; the doctor still treats your man.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Bed eleven's heart monitor beeps in time with the chapel bell, not with her.
