## THE SITUATION
Act 3, after the severance. Canon: the thing can no longer talk to people the way it used to, the Network's god
has gone silent, and its NeuroLink carriers cannot be turned. A woman is pulling a hand cart east along the road.
Her husband walks behind her. He has a carrier's port behind his ear. Since the silence he has not said a word.
He walks when she walks and stops when she stops. She is taking him somewhere, and she is not sure where. Whether a
port can ever be taken out is not written in canon (OPEN, Paolo's); nobody on this road knows either.

The cause is on the map: families have been leaving the data quarter by this road for three trips.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The wife, fifties, in a long pleated Balenciaga coat gone grey with dust, a scarf over her mouth.
First line: "Do you know a doctor who takes them out? Somebody said east."

## THE CHOICES
1. GIVE THEM A RIDE EAST. Costs a map day. At the eastern clinic the doctor looks at the port for a long time and
   says she does not know how. The wife stays anyway, because the clinic town lets them. The husband sits on a
   bench in the sun. A hand, and a row in the unrecorded ledger.
2. SEND HER BACK TO THE COMPOUND. His own people are still there, and the Network's leaders still give orders.
   Costs nothing. She thanks you and turns the cart around, because at least somebody there wants him. What the
   leaders do with a carrier is the war path's business; the feed may show it later.
3. KEEP MOVING. She keeps pulling the cart east. He keeps walking behind her.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
