## THE SITUATION
Act 3. Old Anselmo Ruiz had his memory propped up by the Network for twenty years: it reminded him of his street, his wife, his son. Last week its voice stopped. Last night he walked out of bed four to find the house it used to tell him was his.

- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Bed four walked out at two in the morning. Seventy-eight years old." / "He's going home. He doesn't know where home is." / "The dogs in the wash don't care."

## THE CHOICES
A. FOLLOW THE TRACKS. Two map days into the wash; a feral dog pack at the culvert, routine, 2 to 3 minutes, or wait them out half a day.
B. GO STRAIGHT TO HIS STREET. Only if the player carried the paper backups (QD-AE03) and read box nine: his real street is in it. One day, no fight; he is sitting on the step of a house that is not his.
C. "I'LL FIND HIM. I WON'T BRING HIM BACK." He will not come; you sit with him where he is and send for Daisy. Priced and said: half pay; Daisy: "Then I'll bring the cart. You stay."
