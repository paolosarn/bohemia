## THE SITUATION
Shaped from three locked pieces: the one-way launch "with the player's chosen companions (the ones
who agree to go)" (ACT3 MOONSHOT), the overlook bookend Paolo approved 8/26 ("the same rock before
the one-way launch, looking at everything your family built, then leaving it forever"), and the
ridge as the menu (M03 in the bank). This is the launch eve. It is the last main beat on Earth and
the ending's earning, shown before the Moon resolves LIBERATE, RESPECT or BECOME. The valley below
is DERIVED from all three acts' ledgers (THREE ACTS AT ONCE), so it looks like exactly what this
family made of it, lit or dark.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The act 3 heir's oldest living relative (who that is depends on the family's ledgers; if nobody is
left, the grave itself is the only company and the heir speaks first). First line: "You can see
your great-grandmother's street from here. It had no lights when she was your age."

## THE CHOICES
First, the ASKING: the heir asks each companion, one at a time on the rock, whether they will go.
Each answer names its reason in the family's history ("Your father kept my kids fed in the Killing
Summer"). Those who refuse are not punished and are not asked twice.
1. GO DOWN AND SAY GOODBYE IN PERSON. Costs: a full day of travel to the people who stay, and the
   launch waits. Gain: each goodbye is written unrecorded; on the Moon, every one of them is a voice
   the counterfeit family cannot imitate.
2. LEAVE A THING, NOT A WORD. Costs: one valued item per person who stays (a weapon, a battery bank,
   the family box); no travel. Gain: the things stay in the valley and appear in its derived future.
3. LAUNCH AT DAWN. Costs: nothing more; the people below learn of it from the feed on their phones.
   Gain: the fewest days lost; the most recorded exit; the Amalgamation watches it all.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Who went, who stayed and why, what was left. The Moon's final beats read it. Nothing from a declined
contract is spoken on the rock.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The grave has fresh flowers on it, and nobody in the family brought them.
