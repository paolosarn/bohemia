## THE CHOICES
At the springs, on day two, the mammoth stands in the track.
A. KILL IT. The fight board is the spring-mound block; one animal, one tile wide, hits once and hard; routine hits barely move it; the dial shot is the kill. Tough, 10 to 14 minutes. The track is clear. Pay as stated; no more.
B. FOLLOW IT. Half a day off the track; it leads to a seep with bones of the lab's other animals around it. Benedikt marks the seep as a place nobody should live, for a different reason. Pay as stated.
C. LEAVE IT. Bend the track one mile east around its range; Benedikt does the sum aloud: "The rocket turns two degrees. They'll hate me." Pay as stated, and one day longer.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Any choice counts for the ambition (what the heir learned crosses, not the kill). A: a tusk in the base; the springs fall quiet. B: the seep is on the map, the lab's story one step nearer (the Network's attention rises one step, shown). C: the ground track on the map bends, and the launch date slips a week.
