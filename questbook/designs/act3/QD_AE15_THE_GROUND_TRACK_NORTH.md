## THE SITUATION
Act 3. If the rocket fails on the way up, it falls along its ground track. The track runs north over the Tule Springs beds, where the lab's one mammoth walks. The surveyor has to walk the track and mark where nobody should live.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the launch yard. Buildings: the hall (site office), the tank farm, the canteen tent.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Benedikt Sosa, range surveyor, a Rick Owens poncho over a survey vest, a theodolite on his back like a rifle.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "I walk the line north, you walk next to me. Four days there and back." / "Up at the springs there's something big. Big like a building." / "I mark where nobody should live. That's the whole job."
- PAY: 28 batteries and 6 FOOD. SKULLS: 2 (3 if the mammoth is fought; said: "If you shoot it, I'm not standing next to you.").
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (31); ceiling: "The rocket people count everything twice." Advance: 6 FOOD.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; Benedikt goes alone and marks less.
- THE COMPANY'S LINE (only if a prior generation saw the mammoth, and the ambition is open): "Your grandfather saw it once and never said a word after. Go see why."

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Benedikt, sighting along the dry lake: "Everything north of this line, if it falls, it falls on."

## THE CHOICES
At the springs, on day two, the mammoth stands in the track.
A. KILL IT. The fight board is the spring-mound block; one animal, one tile wide, hits once and hard; routine hits barely move it; the dial shot is the kill. Tough, 10 to 14 minutes. The track is clear. Pay as stated; no more.
B. FOLLOW IT. Half a day off the track; it leads to a seep with bones of the lab's other animals around it. Benedikt marks the seep as a place nobody should live, for a different reason. Pay as stated.
C. LEAVE IT. Bend the track one mile east around its range; Benedikt does the sum aloud: "The rocket turns two degrees. They'll hate me." Pay as stated, and one day longer.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Any choice counts for the ambition (what the heir learned crosses, not the kill). A: a tusk in the base; the springs fall quiet. B: the seep is on the map, the lab's story one step nearer (the Network's attention rises one step, shown). C: the ground track on the map bends, and the launch date slips a week. DEFAULT READ (no prior sighting): the company's line is not said and the mammoth is just the mammoth. Declining leaves nothing.
