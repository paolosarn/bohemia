## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the valley is healing and trade outward is starting again for the first time since the crash. A merchant family wants to run the first caravan west in thirty years. The road is not a level-up of act 1 roads: it is new places (the map extends past the valley edge for the first time), new parties that do not know the family's name, and the verb the escalation hands out is distance, not bigger enemies (`Q075.P7`).

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the truck stop. BUILDING: the hall (the old diner, booths intact).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: MARISOL AND HER SON, one portrait, both in cream Rick Owens drapes, sunglasses the same shape.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Three days out, three back. First run since before." / "Nobody out there knows your name." / "That's good and bad."
- PAY: 14 batteries and a standing trade line: the truck stop sells to the family at cost afterwards. NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 16; she agrees and cuts the advance. Advance: 4 (or 0 at 16).
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; she waits a season and goes with someone else.

## THE CHOICES
A. THE FAST ROAD. Pavement, faster, and a toll crew at the second stop who do not know the family: pay 3 batteries, talk, or fight.
B. THE DIRT ROAD. A day slower, no toll, one road event (a stranded family, QR-B shape).
C. "I TURN BACK AT THE SECOND STOP." Priced: half pay, the truck stop's trade line is not offered, Marisol says "we'll finish it" and does.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: the map's edge stays open west; roaming parties from outside the valley now appear. C: an attempt, recorded as turned back. Declined: nothing. If the truck stop was raided in act 2 (THE FUTURE GOES BOTH WAYS), the settlement is a ruin with a new diner in a tent and the job is offered from there with the line "Second try."
