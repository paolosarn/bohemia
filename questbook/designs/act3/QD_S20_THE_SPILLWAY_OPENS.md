## THE SITUATION
Act 3. The lake is full, the first time since the operators' grandmothers. The dam must open the spillway in four days or risk the structure. Below it, five camps live on the river's old banks, where the river has been low their whole lives. They think the riverbed is a road.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the dam, lit end to end. Buildings: the hall (the operations room), the market, the clinic, the barber, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the operations room. Three portraits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Engineer Abiola Chen, a white hard hat, a Rick Owens cargo skirt, a tablet with the lake level on it.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "The lake is full. First time since my grandmother. We open the spillway in four days." / "There are camps on the river below. They've never seen the river high. They think it's a road." / "Ride the river road. Tell every camp. Four days."

- DEADLINE, SPOKEN AND SHOWN: four days; the spillway gates are on the map and turn red on the last day.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; Chen sends her own crew, slower.

## THE CHOICES
A. RIDE THE WHOLE ROAD. Five camps in four days. Two camps will not believe it; the family takes their elders up the ridge to see the lake at the lip of the dam. It works, and costs a day.
B. ASK FOR A DAY. Chen will hold one more day if the family carries her letter to the council; the turbines lose a day of the boom. Slower, safer.
C. TELL THE BIGGEST CAMP. The biggest camp spreads the word. Faster; the smallest camp, farthest down, hears late and climbs out with what it can carry. Chen says the odds before the yes.
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: the river camps come up; the dam's next board posts boat and fishing jobs with their faces. B: the council's board posts a thank-you contract. C: the smallest camp's people arrive at the dam with nothing and its board has a shelter offer. Failed (late): one camp is gone from the map, shown as a line of debris on the bank. Declining leaves nothing.
