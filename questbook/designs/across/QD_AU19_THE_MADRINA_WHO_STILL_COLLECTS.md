## THE CHOICES
A. (act 2) TELL MRS OCHOA WHAT IT SAID: she pays a little each week to a kiosk; she is calm; the act-3 landing is gentle (her debt is closed before the rush).
B. (act 2) BURN THE MADRINA'S BOOK: her grandson has her paper ledger; burning it with him forgives every debt on the blocks; the kiosks have nothing to read in act 3.
C. (act 3) BREAK THE KIOSKS: one block at a time, a leg each, the enforcer drone on the last one (a fight on four house tiles); the block is free.
D. LET THE KIOSKS RUN (`Q131.X7`): act 3 lands as written: doors chalked, the block's people moving out, the blocks dark on the map.
IF THE FIGHT IS LOST (`Q095.X3`, `Q093.X2`): the drone keeps one block; the others are free; the grandson keeps the madrina's name on a wall there.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36); a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10% (the third votes). Followers never fight.
