## THE SITUATION
The driver's cart is stolen in act 1 by a family that needs it to leave the valley. In act 3, the family's grandson shows up at your home base with the cart restored, to sell it back. The act-1 job is to chase it; the act-3 job is his.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the home base; the hall (the shed, later the bay).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Act 1: THE DRIVER is the client. Act 3: the family's grandson, with THE DRIVER'S HEIR beside him.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: ACT 1, DRIVER: "My cart. A family. Heading for the north pass." / ACT 3, GRANDSON: "My grandmother took this from your family. I brought it back." / ACT 3, GRANDSON: "I need an escort out of the valley. 20 batteries, and it's yours."
- PAY: Act 1: 8 BATTERIES. Act 3: the cart and 20 BATTERIES. SKULLS: 1 each. The fee is locked before the yes.
- NEGOTIATION: Act 3: the grandson takes no "more": "It's a gift with a job on it."
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining is free, writes nothing, and the follower never mentions it. No loyalty meter exists.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
First line after accept: "My cart."

## THE CHOICES
A. ACT 1: chase them down, routine.
B. ACT 1: let them go (the driver's call, at the pass). (QR-AT, lines 12 and 30: letting them go had no stated pay and the only full fee was the cruel road, `Q108.X2`, `Q139.P2`.) The driver pays 4 for the ride out, "for the try."
B2. ACT 1: TRADE AT THE PASS: give the family the shed's old handcart, 2 TAPE to fix it out of your own stores, for the cart. No fight, the full 8, and they still leave; the grandson still comes in act 3, with the handcart's wheel on his belt.
C. ACT 3: escort the grandson; the heir decides whether to keep the cart.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36). Followers never fight.
IF ACT 1 WAS NEVER PLAYED (QR-AT, line 18: `Q096.X1`, `Q121.X1`): the grandson comes anyway; the driver's heir knows the cart by her grandfather's tag on the axle.
IF THE FIGHT IS LOST (QR-AT, line 23: a lost fight is a result, never a reload, and the tell is in the picture, `Q095.X3`, `Q098.X2`, `Q093.X2`): act 1: the family reaches the pass with the cart and the driver pays nothing, the shed picture is empty; act 3: the grandson's escort fails at the valley edge and he stays, the cart his.
THE FOLLOWER'S CALL: Act 1: the driver decides at the pass. Act 3: the heir decides about the cart.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
If you let them go in act 1, the act-3 offer exists; if you took it back, the grandson never comes. Declining writes nothing. Whatever the outcome, the follower's rule keeps working and they stay; a job never gates them.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
