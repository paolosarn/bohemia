## THE SITUATION
The lab is one door, seen three times. The game never explains it. It shows the door, and who is behind it, in each era.

## ACT 1: THE DOOR IS CUT FROM THE INSIDE
The lab is a ruin (QD-AA09, QD-AA10). Its front door is intact; the enclosure fences are cut, neatly, from the inside. The company can TAKE the block as a part: clear the yard (a routine fight) and hold it. Holding it costs 2 batteries a week (a man at the gate) and draws every beast on the northern roads, because the feeder still runs.
- A. HOLD IT. The block is the family's.
- B. LEAVE IT. Someone else will hold it.
- C. BURN IT. The block is ash; nobody holds it.

## ACT 2: WHO IS BEHIND THE DOOR (an event on the northern road)
If A: the heir's family still holds it. The heir's first visit: the settlement screen of the lab, a hall with Nell's clipboard (QD-AA10) on a nail. If B: the Amalgamation's faithful hold it (the cultists row of the bestiary count), and the northern roads have more lab beasts than the table lists. If C: a field of ash with one new green shoot, and the beasts of act 2 come only from the corridors (the law's s3).

## THE NARRATOR (act 2 event, first time the heir passes the block)
"The door is closed. The door was always closed. Someone has been oiling the hinges, which is a kind thing to do for a door."

## THE CHOICES (act 2, if B)
A. KNOCK. A faithful in a white coat opens, smiles, and offers to sell the company a pup "grown from the drawers". 10 batteries. The pup is a dire wolf that never barks.
B. TAKE THE BLOCK. A tough fight (8 to 12 minutes) against the faithful and their two plated bears (the armoured unhold). The block is the family's.
C. WALK ON.

## ACT 3: WHAT THE LEDGERS MADE OF IT
Held by the family in act 2 or 3: the block becomes the reserve's office (QD-AA18) or, if the heir chooses, a clinic that grows MEDS from the drawers (the two-sided knowledge, kept). Held by the faithful: the lab is lit, working, and the northern roads carry beasts nobody in the table named. Burned: a museum of bones, the fossil beds as they were before anyone dug.
Default read, if act 1 never visited: the faithful hold it (B).

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Which of three hands held the door in each act; how many beasts of which kind walk the northern roads in the next act; whether the family bought a pup.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
In all three acts the visitor sign out front says "COME MEET THE PAST", and in act 3 someone has repainted it.
