## THE SITUATION
In act 1 the player taps the house and chooses to go forward. The transition is long and plain: the settlement screen of the base ages in place, lot by lot. Then act 2. The same base, the same house. Whatever the first person built on the lots is there, weathered. Whatever was raided is a black shell. The heir stands at the window of the house, and the player sees him from the settlement screen: a tiny figure in a lit square.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Nobody speaks for a minute. Then, at the hall, the first face of act 2: the child of a company member from act 1 (if the act-1 company had a Mateo, a Chuy, anyone, the ledger picks the most recent), now grown: "You look like her. You walk different."

## THE CHOICES
A. STAY. Look around act 2. The hall has one offer, written from what act 1 left (QD-T13 is a default). Nothing is forced.
B. GO BACK. Flip straight back to act 1. Nothing is lost; the heir waits at the window. The act-2 screen will show the same base, changed by whatever act 1 does next.
C. WALK THE LOTS. Tap each lot and hear one line about it from whoever uses it now ("Your mother's wall. We sit on it at lunch."). No batteries, no time; this is the tour, and it can be skipped at any lot.
None of these costs anything but time; this beat's cost was paid in act 1.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
The flip is unlocked for good. Every lot line in C is read from the act-1 ledger, with a default for an empty lot ("Nobody built here. Kids play on it."). The first face's parent is named on the screen, so a player who never met that parent still learns there was one.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The heir in the window is holding the same cup the first person held in act 1, and it is still steaming.
