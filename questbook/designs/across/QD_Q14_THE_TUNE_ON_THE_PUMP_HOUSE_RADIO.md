## THE SITUATION
A song passed across three acts. In act 1 a road event: a street singer at the overpass market is being robbed of her speaker by a crew. In act 2 her song is the pump crews' work song (or it is not). In act 3 it is what the rocket yards play at shift change (or something else is). Cheap to build: one tune, three reads.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Act 1, the event screen, the singer, a sequined jacket over a hospital gown: "It's just a speaker. Déjalos. I'll sing louder."

## THE CHOICES
Act 1: 1. STOP THE CREW. A short group fight; she sings the whole time. 2. BUY THEM OFF. 2 batteries. 3. LEAVE IT. Nothing written; she sings louder, the crew takes the speaker.
Act 2 (landing one): at the pump house, by read: if 1 or 2, the crew sings her song at work, and one of them says "the lady at the overpass". If 3, they hum something else. DEFAULT: they hum something else.
Act 3 (landing two): the yards' shift-change song, by read: hers (with a mural of her face on the yard wall), or a Network jingle nobody likes.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Act 1: a deed if 1 or 2. Act 2: the pump crew's song is a world line the whisper network uses as a signal (Paolo's call how; the design only says the tune is known). Act 3: the yard's sound. THE FUTURE GOES BOTH WAYS: if the pump house fell in act 2, the song survives only if act 1's deed was done, because the market people carried it.

## THE NARRATOR
