## THE SITUATION
THE SIEGE THAT LEARNS, across two generations. In act 1 the Destroyers besiege the depot and the family defends it. In the preparation days the player chooses which side to wall: the EAST (a line of dead trucks) or the NORTH (the rail cut). Whatever happens, the fold writes where the wall held and where it broke. In act 3 the depot is rich and the rocket runs on its freight, which is exactly what draws a siege (prosperity draws raids). The new attackers are the grandchildren of act 1's. They read the old siege. They come at the wall that held, because nobody guards a monument.

## THE CHOICES
Act 3:
- A. HOLD THE OLD WALL. The attack comes where it held last time. The painted trucks are cover on the fight board; their roofs are the high ground. Fight as agreed.
- B. MOVE THE MONUMENT. Spend two preparation days cutting the painted trucks loose to wall the other side too. The depot's people hate it (standing with the depot down); the fight board has cover on both sides. Same pay.
- C. TALK TO THE GRANDCHILDREN. Their leader, TOÑO, will take a freight share for a year instead. No fight; the depot pays the share out of its rocket freight (the rocket build slows by a stage in the feed). Paid the same 16 (QR-L P7; QR-G line 30: the talk never pays less than the fight). Itzel asks whether the family will carry part of Toño's first year: the player may hand back 8 of the 16 (the priced refusal, his to choose) or not.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Act 1 taken: a deed and the fold entry (breach point, the wall chosen). Act 1 declined: nothing about the family; the depot's siege result is the world's. Act 3: HELD again, TAKEN or TALKED, each a different depot in the ending's read. Struck-down members roll rule 36; a veteran of about 20 fights at 10%; the main character never dies. The future is never worse than the start: a depot that fell in both acts is still a working yard in act 3, owned by someone else.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The painted trucks' hazard lights still blink, sixty years on, and nobody at the depot will say who pays for the batteries.
