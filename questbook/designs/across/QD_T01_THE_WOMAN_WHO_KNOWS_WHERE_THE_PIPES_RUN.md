## THE SITUATION
The wash camp has no hall. It has a fire barrel under the overpass, and the one offer lives there. Tere lies on a cot under a blue tarp, in a runway coat somebody traded her for water. The camp digs for water every summer because nobody remembers where the city's buried valves are. Tere does. She has about four days, and she wants two things: her grandson Nico home, and one more look at the pump station she ran.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the wash camp. Buildings: the fire barrel (the asks), SCAVENGE, the tarp rows. A black cloth hangs on one tarp; that is the situation, drawn.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Lidia Olmos, the daughter, hands wet from washing, a man's watch on her wrist.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Mamá wants Nico. He drives for the depot. Two stops." / "The nurse says four days. Maybe." / "It's what we have. Look around."
- PAY: 6 batteries and a jar of clean salt, counted as 1 FOOD (the camp pays in kind; rule 47). SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more and the ceiling comes at once: "That's all of it." No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing. The offer leaves when Tere dies, whatever the player did.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Tere, from the cot, when the company arrives after accepting: "You're not Nico. Sit anyway."

## THE CHOICES
A. BRING NICO. Ride to the depot. He is on a run and the dispatcher will not release him, so one company member drives his seat for three map days (that man is off the roster). Nico gets home in time.
B. BRING HER TO THE PUMP STATION. Carry her on a litter, two map days on the dam road. Scavengers squat in the station: a routine fight on the fight board, 2 to 4 minutes, the station's house tile has a flooded floor. She points at the valves.
C. SIT WITH HER. One night at the camp. She talks until morning. A company member writes the valves down in the back of a ration book.
Same pay on every road. She dies within the week on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: in act 2 Nico is the wash camp's water man; the camp's "short of water" situation cannot be drawn there. B: in act 2 the dam's board posts a valve repair at better pay, and the wash camp's water price is one lower. C: the ration book is GEAR; in act 2 the heir of the man who wrote it carries it, and it opens B's repair offer. If that man is struck down in act 1 and dies (rule 36), the book is lost with him. Declining leaves nothing; in act 2 the camp is still digging.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
On her last night her hands draw the valve map in the air, and her map has one more street than the valley has.
