## THE SITUATION
Act 3, the city healing, the rocket being built. A young driver posts a contract in the rail yard's hall: haul a truck of oxidizer across the dry lake to the pad. She is the granddaughter of a member of the act 1 company, from the branch of his family that did not ride on. WHICH member is read from the act 1 ledger (the member with the most days in the company who did not die in act 1; if all died, the one buried by a road, QD-M04 choice A). The contract is a plain job (no twist, `Q148.X3`); the payoff is her.

On the offer screen, below. On the road, she says one line the player has heard before: her grandfather's most-repeated road line from act 1, word for word, and does not know it was his (`Q010.W8`, the line that lands later).

## THE CHOICES
A. SHE DRIVES, THE COMPANY WALKS. She is a good driver and green in a fight; if the truck is hit she is in the formation. Rule 36 applies to her like anyone.
B. SHE RIDES, THE COMPANY DRIVES. Slower (half a day more), and she is out of any fight.
C. THE DETOUR. Offered only in the "died in act 1" read: the route passes his grave on the south road. Half a day. She has never seen it. She says nothing at the grave, and the player can say nothing too.
