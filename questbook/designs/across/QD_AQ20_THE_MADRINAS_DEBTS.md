## THE CHOICES
A. ACT 1: collect by force, routine; or forgive it (her call).
B. ACT 3: erase the cache at a failing node, a tough fight.

Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36). Followers never fight.
THE FOLLOWER'S CALL: The granddaughter decides whether the book dies with the Network.
