## THE SITUATION
In act 2 a girl of fifteen asks to ride with the company as an apprentice (a road event at the family's base: a face, three choices). If taken, she rides for the act. In act 3 she runs her own mercenary company, a roaming party on the map with her own banner. How she treats the family depends on how the family treated her.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Act 2, the event screen, NADIA, a hand-cut Rick Owens hoodie, boots too big: "I can cook and I can shoot a little. Mostly cook."

## THE CHOICES
Act 2: 1. TAKE HER. She rides. Rule 36 applies: if she is struck down, 20% she dies (then no act 3 landing, and her grave is on the map). 2. TAKE HER, KEEP HER OUT OF FIGHTS. She cooks; the company's rest heals faster; she learns less. 3. SEND HER HOME. Nothing written.
Act 3 (the landing, a road meeting with her party on the map):
- RODE AND FOUGHT: her company is strong and her banner copies the family's. She offers to ride together on one rocket-yard contract for free. "You taught me to ask for the advance."
- RODE AND COOKED: her company runs a canteen crew for the yards; she feeds the family's company for free on the road.
- SENT HOME / DEFAULT: a stranger's company with a girl's name on the banner; she does not know you.
