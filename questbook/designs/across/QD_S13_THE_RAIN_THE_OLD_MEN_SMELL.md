## THE SITUATION (ACT 1)
The wash camp lives in a flood channel because nobody else will. The old men say rain is coming from the east, two nights, maybe three. Flash floods in these channels are real and fast. Everything the camp owns (water jugs, blankets, the radio cart) has to go up the bank to the old casino garage, and the garage is rented out by a Mob crew.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the wash, tarps and cooking fires. Buildings: the hall (a tarp), SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the tarp. One portrait.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Don Esteban, camp elder, torn Yohji, a transistor radio on a string.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Rain in the east. Two nights, three. The wash runs." / "Everything we own goes up the ramp to the garage. The garage has a landlord." / "Carry it up and keep the landlord polite."
- PAY: 2 batteries, and a night's watch over the company's truck any time it camps at the wash. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: "Two is all the camp has." No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the camp carries what it can on its backs.

## THE CHOICES
A. PAY THE RENT. 4 batteries of the family's own buys the camp level two for a season. The landlord is polite.
B. CLEAR LEVEL TWO. The Mob crew on the ramp: a fight on the fight board on a slope with parked-car cover.
C. THE CO-OP INSTEAD. One map leg further. The co-op takes the camp into its gym in return for work.
Same pay on every road. The flood comes on the night the old men said. They were right.

## THE LANDING (ACT 3)
A: the camp bought level two, then three, over the years it paid rent. The garage is a housing block with a mural of the flood; the wash camp is a town, two offers. B: the Mob took the garage back after the family left; the camp went back down the bank; the board is one desperate offer again. C: the wash camp is gone; its children are co-op members and the gym is a school. NEVER PLAYED (the default read): the camp carried half up on its backs, lost the rest, and the garage stands empty.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
The act 1 choice is the act 3 settlement. Declining leaves nothing, and act 3 reads it as never played.
