## THE SITUATION
Act 1. A tailor at the colourful market bought the last bolt of real black wool in the valley, off a truck at the depot. Wool, not plastic. She needs it carried across the underpass, where people get robbed for less.

- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Mercedes Aoki, tailor, her own coat made of twelve other coats, pins in her lapel like medals.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "One bolt of wool. Real sheep. From before." / "Carry it from the depot to me. Under the underpass, not over." / "I'll make one coat from it. Only one. The best one in the valley."

## THE CHOICES (ACT 1)
A. UNDER THE UNDERPASS. A crew of four waits: the fight board is the underpass block, routine, 2 to 3 minutes.
B. OVER THE TOP. Plus half a day on the freeway deck; a road event: a family selling water asks for a strip of the wool for a baby's blanket. Give it (the coat will be a hand shorter) or not.
C. "I'LL CARRY IT. I WON'T FIGHT FOR IT." If the crew stops you, hand over a third. Priced and said: 5 batteries; Mercedes: "A short coat, then."

## ACT 3: THE SAME STALL
In act 3, Mercedes's granddaughter Sayuri keeps the stall in a glass shop. She offers a plain contract (guard a dye shipment, routine) and on its screen says: "Abuela started a coat for your family. It's in the back. It needs the last sleeve and the lining." Finishing it costs 6 TAPE and 10 batteries at her bench; the coat is named for the act 1 company's senior member, can never be sold or scrapped, and is worn by the heir. It is a coat, not a trenchcoat (trenchcoats stay reserved). DEFAULT READ (the bolt never came in act 1): Sayuri's screen lacks that line, and the stall sells ordinary coats.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Act 1: the bolt delivered, whole, a hand short (B, given), or a third short (C); Mercedes cuts it that season. Act 3 reads which. Declining in either act leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
