## THE SITUATION
The dairy lost its herd to the desert in the first years. It came back. Forty head, and in front of them a bull the size of a pickup, built like two bulls, from a ranch that edited its stock. Every week the herd runs through the dairy's fences at dawn to get at the water trough, and every week something breaks.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the dairy. Buildings: the hall (the office, a calendar still on a month in 2050), the market, one empty lot the company can build on if it takes the dairy's offer later.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Hiram Voss, an old dairyman, one arm in a sling from the last run.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "They were ours. Now they're his." / "He comes first at dawn, straight line, same fence." / "Don't kill the cows. Kill him, and the cows come home."
- PAY: 12 batteries, and the bull's meat. SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (14); ceiling: "The cows are the money. I'm paying you in cows coming home." Advance: none ("I don't have it till they're back.").
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Hiram, on the offer screen, lifting the sling a little: "Same fence. Every time. You'd think he'd get bored."

## THE SIGN AND THE LAIR
The sign is the flattened fence line on the map, straight as a ruler, pointing at the trough. No lair: the fight is at dawn on the dairy's own lots.

## THE CHOICES
A. TURN THE HERD, KILL THE BULL. The tell: the bull paws the ground one beat before he charges in a straight line across two tiles; anyone in the line is knocked down and thrown. Fire or noise on a flank turns the cows. Pay plus the bull: 300 kg of FOOD that spoils in 3 map days.
B. BUILD THE SMOKEHOUSE FIRST. The dairy offers its empty lot if the company builds a smokehouse there (8 batteries, 2 days): the bull's FOOD keeps for the act, and the lot is the company's PART (rule 43).
C. MOVE THE TROUGH. Drag the trough two lots over; the bull's line misses the fence. No fight, 4 batteries; the herd stays wild on the map, and the dairy stays poor.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: the cows come home; the dairy's FOOD price drops two steps. B: the company holds a lot in the dairy with a smokehouse on it; it draws the bear later (QD-AA06). C: nothing changes for the dairy; the bull becomes a NAMED beast if he ever throws a company man. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The bull never makes a sound, not even when he is hit.
