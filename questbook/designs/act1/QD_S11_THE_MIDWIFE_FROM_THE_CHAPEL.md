## THE SITUATION

## THE CHOICES
A. THE PAVED ROAD. Fast, and the midwife is carsick. She demands stops. One day more than the map says, said by her at the first stop.
B. THE DIRT ROAD THROUGH THE WASH. Shorter. A family at the culvert asks her to look at a sick child. She will not pass. Half a day, and a friend at the wash.
C. BRING THE KIT, NOT THE WOMAN. She will not come; she gives her kit and her instructions to one company member, spoken, once. The birth goes by odds she states: "Most go fine. Some don't."
Same pay on every road.
