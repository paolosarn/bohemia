## THE CHOICES
A. SIT WITH IT. The player's character sits on the step and waits. Cost: the party loses the rest of the map day. The dog eats from a hand and lies down; the handler is buried behind the wall; the dog stays on his grave and the building keeps it (QR-G line 30: the kind road wins no companion the others do not).
B. THE CREW, BY THE BOOK. The sergeant's crew with poles and a blanket, ten minutes, the street watching. Cost: the sergeant's standing rises, the company's does not; the dog goes to the post's kennel and works again.
C. REACH FOR THE KIT. Anybody who reaches for the handler's kit is struck down by the dog, and rule 36 applies to that company member (20% dead, else 30 to 40 days injured), said by the sergeant before it happens: "Don't take the kit. The kit is what it's guarding." After that, the dog is shot or chased off, and the stair is clear.
The fee is paid on all three. There is no fight scene: this is one animal, not a group, and C is the dial's business.
