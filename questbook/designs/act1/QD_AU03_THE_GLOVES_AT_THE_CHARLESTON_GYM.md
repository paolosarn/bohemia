## THE SITUATION
Act 1. Ruben fought amateur before he fought for batteries. He keeps his old gloves tied to his pack. At the Charleston gym a kid, Neto, trains alone and wants to fight in the crew's ring night for a sack of flour. Ruben wants to give Neto the gloves and teach him one thing: when to quit.

## THE PERSON, THE PLACE, THE THING (never a card)
At camp Ruben is taping his hands for no fight. RUBEN: "There's a kid at the Charleston gym. He's going to get hurt Saturday." / RUBEN: "I want two nights there. You get the ring night purse, 8 batteries." / RUBEN: "I'm not asking you to fight. I'm asking you to wait."
- THE WANT, ONE LINE: Ruben wants Neto to learn to quit; Neto wants the flour for his grandmother.
- PAY: The ring night purse, 8 BATTERIES, locked before the yes; Neto's flour is not yours to take. SKULLS: 1. Declining is free and writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. TRAIN HIM: two nights; on ring night Neto fights the crew's man and Ruben throws the towel in round two. Neto is angry; he is whole.
B. FIGHT FOR HIM: a company member takes Neto's place in the ring; a one-on-one bout on one house tile under the beat, 2 minutes. Win or lose, the flour goes to Neto.
C. BUY THE FLOUR: you pay the crew 6 batteries for the sack; Neto does not fight; Ruben gives him the gloves anyway.
D. NOT OUR BUSINESS (`Q131.X7`): Ruben goes alone for one night and comes back with a cut eye and no purse; he stays in the company.
IF THE BOUT IS LOST (`Q095.X3`, `Q098.X2`): the company member is hurt the usual way; the crew pays half the purse anyway because the fight was good; Neto gets the flour.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36); a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10% (the third votes). Followers never fight.

## WHAT THE WORLD SHOWS AFTER

The gym picture shows Neto training (A, B, C) or the ring empty (D). Ruben's gloves are off his pack. Declining writes nothing.
