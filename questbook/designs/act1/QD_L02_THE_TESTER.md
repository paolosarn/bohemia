## THE SITUATION
A short boom inside the anarchy decade: somebody found a dead solar farm full of cells, and the drive-in swap meet is where they get traded. Every trade needs one thing: the swap's battery tester, the old voltmeter rig that says a cell is really charged. It broke. It went out for repair to the only man who can fix it, two days away. Nobody at the swap trusts a trade without it, so the swap has stopped. The job is to carry the tester home.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: THE DRIVE-IN, a swap meet under a dead screen. In the settlement view: stalls in the old parking rows, a recruit fire, a tavern in the projection booth, and the hall at the snack bar.
- BUILDING: the tester booth, drawn with a queue of people standing in front of an empty table (PL03). Tap the queue.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Lalo Ferrán, who runs the booth, a quilted Balenciaga-style cropped puffer, a jeweler's loupe on a cord, battery acid burns on both hands.
- THE SCREEN:
  1. "Tester's at Chuy's trailer at the marina, fixed, paid for. Bring it back here, two days each way, {fee} baterías, and the swap opens again." (FL07, the full pitch)
  2. "The wash road is quicker. Five boys with a truck sit on it and charge what they like."
  3. "The high road is a day longer and nobody sits on it."
  4. "Half now if you want. It's a boom, I can afford to trust you."
- PAY: K x 4 days + R1 + S (boom, low). Batteries only.
- NEGOTIATION: ASK FOR MORE: two steps land (boom ceiling); his face goes from easy to tight; at the ceiling: "That's the swap's money, not mine." ADVANCE: he offers it before you ask; taking it makes half the fee a named debt if you drop. Third push: "Forget it, I'll send my cousin." Nothing written.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE: accept, and the marker goes to the map. Decline: "Sure." He turns back to the queue. Nothing written.

## THE CHOICES
The whole job is route and fight, stated on the screen:
1. **The wash road.** Two days each way. The five with the truck are there, as said: a fight on the fight board in a dry wash (a group, with the companion; the mound at the bend is the one terrain effect). Or pay their toll in your own batteries and pass.
2. **The high road.** Three days each way, no fight. The keep for the extra days (FOOD and WATER, rule 47) comes out of the same fee.
3. **Either way, the tester comes back.** DONE; the fee. If a brother was struck down on the wash road (rule 36: 20 percent dead, else 30 to 40 days out), that is the price the danger step was paid for.
The "I won't do this part" choice: at the wash, pay the toll instead of fighting. It finishes the contract at a stated price (your batteries), not a drop.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Declined: nothing.
- Done: "brought the tester home". The swap reopens; the queue is gone next visit and the stalls are busier. Prices at the drive-in move back toward normal.
- Dropped with the advance: a debt owed to Lalo, the swap's door closed, the story travels by mouth.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
When Chuy hands the tester over, its needle already points to full, and it is not plugged into anything.

## WHY A PLAIN JOB IS GOOD
The screen told the truth about both roads, the danger and the pay, so the only decision is the player's own: time against risk. That is the treadmill at its best, and it needs no twist.
