## THE SITUATION
Act 1. Dolores served at Nellis as an airman before the base emptied. She carries a second set of dog tags, her friend's, who died in the first riots. The families hang tags on the north fence. She wants to hang these, but the fence is now the edge of the Remnants' ground and they shoot at people who walk up to it.

## THE CHOICES
A. BETWEEN PATROLS: the company crosses the open ground in one move between the patrol's passes, on a board where the fence is the far edge; no fight if the timing is read from the patrol's lamp and the chirp of its handheld radio at each pass (QR-AV, line 15: the clue was a picture only, `Q141.X3`).
B. ASK THE REMNANTS: walk to their gate and ask. The sergeant lets one person hang one tag for 'one battery and a name'; Dolores gives her friend's name and it goes in their book.
C. HANG THEM ON THE COMPANY'S CART: she decides the fence belongs to the dead and the cart belongs to the living; the tags hang from the cart.
D. NOT TO THE WIRE (QR-AV, line 12: the old D was the no moved after the yes, a drop with no price, `Q131.X7`, `Q139.P2`): you walk her to the old range sign two hundred metres short and no further. She ties the tags to it, where the families who could not reach the fence tie theirs, and pays 5 of the 10. (The free no at the fire, not this road, is what crosses the act as QD-AU20.)
IF THE FIGHT IS LOST (`Q095.X3`, `Q093.X2`): the patrol drives the company back; Dolores throws the tags over the wire as they run. They are on the fence the next time you pass, hung by someone else.

## WHAT THE WORLD SHOWS AFTER
THE WIN YOU SEE AND HEAR: the fence picture on the map gains one bright pair of tags among hundreds; they clink in the wind when the camera is near.
The fence shows the tags (A, B, lost), the cart shows them (C), or the range sign does (D). B writes the friend's name into the Remnants' book, which a later act can read.
