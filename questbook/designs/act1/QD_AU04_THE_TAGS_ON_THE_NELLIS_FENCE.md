## THE SITUATION
Act 1. Dolores served at Nellis as an airman before the base emptied. She carries a second set of dog tags, her friend's, who died in the first riots. The families hang tags on the north fence. She wants to hang these, but the fence is now the edge of the Remnants' ground and they shoot at people who walk up to it.

## THE PERSON, THE PLACE, THE THING (never a card)
At camp she lays the tags on her knee and turns them over once. DOLORES: "North fence. Where the families hang them." / DOLORES: "The Remnants' patrol passes at the hour. We go between." / DOLORES: "I'll give you my next pay. 10 batteries."
- THE WANT, ONE LINE: She wants her friend on the fence with the others; the Remnants want nobody near the wire.
- PAY: 10 BATTERIES from her next pay, locked before the yes. SKULLS: 2. Declining is free and writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. BETWEEN PATROLS: the company crosses the open ground in one move between the patrol's passes, on a board where the fence is the far edge; no fight if the timing is read from the patrol's lamp.
B. ASK THE REMNANTS: walk to their gate and ask. The sergeant lets one person hang one tag for 'one battery and a name'; Dolores gives her friend's name and it goes in their book.
C. HANG THEM ON THE COMPANY'S CART: she decides the fence belongs to the dead and the cart belongs to the living; the tags hang from the cart.
D. TOO CLOSE TO THE WIRE (`Q131.X7`): she keeps the tags and her pay; next act her heir asks again (see across, QD-AU20).
IF THE FIGHT IS LOST (`Q095.X3`, `Q093.X2`): the patrol drives the company back; Dolores throws the tags over the wire as they run. They are on the fence the next time you pass, hung by someone else.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36); a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10% (the third votes). Followers never fight.

## WHAT THE WORLD SHOWS AFTER
THE WIN YOU SEE AND HEAR: the fence picture on the map gains one bright pair of tags among hundreds; they clink in the wind when the camera is near.
The fence shows the tags (A, B, lost), the cart shows them (C), or nothing (D). B writes the friend's name into the Remnants' book, which a later act can read.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL (analog horror, said plainly, never explained)
