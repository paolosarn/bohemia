# QD-J07: THE CARTEL PAYS FOR THE DOCTOR
ACT: 1
KIND: contract
CRISIS: the Destroyers

PLACE: a clinic town in an old strip-mall urgent care (the settlement); the job is one map day north, a burned residential block (the fight board if it comes to a fight)
STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35). Names and lines are attempts; Paolo's to change.
STAGE: GATHERING (QR-J): two or three bands, a road that is not safe
CHECKLIST: passes all 32 (QR-AM 10/10: lines 31 and 32 run, pass as written)

## THE SITUATION
Act 1, the anarchy decade, a bust. A Destroyer band burned a block north of the clinic town two days ago. People
are still alive there, hurt, in the houses that did not burn. The clinic has one doctor, and she will go if
someone keeps her alive. The one who is paying is the Cartel. Canon says every faction hates the Destroyers, even
the Cartel, because the Cartel "at least needs some civilization standing." A crisis is when enemies post jobs in
each other's halls. This is the strange client of the GATHERING stage.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the clinic town. Its screen: the pharmacy counter (market; medicine dear this stage), the parking
  lot fire (rumours: "they smashed every mirror on the block"), and the waiting room, which is the HALL.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: a Cartel lieutenant, thirties, a mint-green Bottega Veneta leather jacket, sunglasses indoors,
  sitting in a waiting-room chair with a number ticket in his hand like a patient.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES:
  1. "The doctor goes north for one day. You bring her back."
  2. "Nine cells. Cartel cells. Same as anybody's."
  3. "Business needs customers who are alive."
- PAY: 9 batteries, locked at the offer (the valley rate for an escort is 5; the Cartel pays for speed).
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more (up to 11; past that he laughs and waits; nothing written). Advance: 4 now.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The doctor, from the doorway behind him, pulling on gloves: "He's paying. I'm going. You're the part in between."
(They bicker the whole way, like cousins. That is the warm beat.)

## THE CHOICES (inside the taken contract)
1. KEEP HER ALIVE FOR THE DAY. On the burned block a Destroyer band comes back at dusk. Ways through: hold the
   pharmacy door while she works inside; move the hurt to the church two streets over by the back alley; or light
   the fuel pile at the block's end to pull the band away. Rule 36 applies to the company. Full pay.
2. SHE WANTS A SECOND DAY. Not a twist, a choice: she asks to stay. Staying costs one map day and 1 FOOD a head (rule 47); the lieutenant pays nothing more ("I paid for one day"). More of the hurt live; the feed says how many.
3. I WON'T TAKE CARTEL MONEY (the priced refusal). You do the job and hand the pay to the clinic. Finished, not
   dropped. The lieutenant's standing drops a step: "You think my cells are dirty? They're the same cells."

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries (or the clinic's cells, choice 3); standing with the Cartel and with the clinic town.
- The act 1 climax ledger: the company stood with the builders on this block (the Destroyers' line).
- The doctor, alive: in the act 2 derive she runs the clinic, and her waiting room still has a number ticket
  machine. If the family was here, she knows the heir's face in act 2 and says the family's name.
- Declining leaves nothing. The doctor does not go; or she goes with someone else; the world decides.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
On the burned block every car's mirrors are smashed, and nothing else on the cars is touched.
