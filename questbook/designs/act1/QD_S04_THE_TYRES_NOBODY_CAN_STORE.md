## THE SITUATION
Act 1 has pockets of boom, and this is one. A cartel convoy came up from the south with four hundred truck tyres in one run. The valley's tyre price fell by half in a week. The yards are full, a rival crew is already sniffing at the fence, and in the sun a tyre is garbage in a month. The co-op blocks want eighty and will pay in grain. The yards want batteries. The family is how grain becomes batteries.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the cartel yards, stacks of tyres higher than the fence. Buildings: the hall (a shipping container office), the market, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the container. Three portraits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: El Guero Salas, yard boss, sunburned, a Bottega woven vest over a bare chest.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Four hundred tyres and room for two hundred." / "The co-op wants eighty. They pay me in grain, I pay you in batteries, because I'm a gentleman." / "Don't sell them to anybody on the way, eh."
- PAY: 7 batteries. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 8 ("Now you're a businessman too."). A second ask: "Eight is the top. Seven was the price of my good mood." Advance: 2 now, 2 less at the end.
- DEADLINE, SPOKEN: "By the next moon they're garbage." The map shows the moon.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. THE PAVED ROAD. Fast. The Reds' checkpoint taxes ten tyres. The co-op gets seventy and says so.
B. THE DIRT ROAD. Slower. The rival crew's tracks cross the dry wash; they wait at the crossing. A fight on the fight board against six on a wash floor with two cars for cover.
C. "I'M NOT FIGHTING OVER TYRES." At the crossing, sell the rival crew twenty tyres at glut price. The co-op gets sixty. Salas pays 5, as he said he would if the count came short.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: the co-op market sells tyres cheap; the depot's next board has one more run. C: the rival crew roll faster on the family's tyres, and their party moves one map stop per day instead of half. Declining leaves nothing; the tyres crack or walk, and the yards' board shows a guard job for the fence.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Every tyre in the four hundred has the same date moulded into the sidewall, and the date is next month.
