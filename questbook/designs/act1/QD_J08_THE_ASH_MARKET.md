## THE SITUATION
Act 1, a bust. Two days after a Destroyer raid, scavengers are selling what came out of the burned houses on
blankets along the road: tools, pans, cells, a bicycle, a box of shoes. Prices are a third of the valley price,
because there is so much of it. This is what BB calls a settlement's "raided" situation, seen from outside: food
dear inside the walls, loot cheap on the road. One thing on the blanket belongs to someone the family knows.

The cause is on the map before the stop: the smoke over the block for two trips.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The seller: a grandmother in a sun hat and a cropped Balenciaga jacket two sizes too small, with her grandson,
twelve, doing the sums. First line: "Everything's cheap. Don't ask whose. Ask how much."

## THE CHOICES
1. BUY THE ONE THING BACK. Among the tools is a sewing machine with a name taped to it: a neighbour from the
   family's block (derived from the ledger: someone the player met; in a seed where nobody was met, a name on a
   mailbox the player passed). 3 batteries. If the neighbour is alive, the family can carry it home to them. If
   not, it stays in the family's house and crosses the ages as a thing.
2. BUY CHEAP. Cells and tools at a third of price, up to 6 batteries' worth for 2. The grandmother haggles
   against herself and loses on purpose ("You look hungry. Take the pan."). Nobody judges.
3. KEEP MOVING. The boy calls one more price after the truck.
