## THE SITUATION
Before the lookout, parties came out of the dark. Now a kid sits up there with a pair of broken binoculars and a whistle. Chuy, fourteen, saw a fire party camp in the dry wash, the same boots that burned the co-op, where his aunt died. The co-op wants the leader's boots as proof.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the farm tower. Buildings: the hall, the racks, SCAVENGE, THE LOOKOUT (the family's, a chair on the roof with a tarp over it).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Chuy, the watcher, binocular marks around both eyes, his aunt's scarf.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Six of them. Dry wash. They light fires at night like they own it." / "The co-op pays for his boots. I just want you to go." / "I can see them from here. Right now."
- PAY: 16 batteries, from the co-op, for the boots. SKULLS: 3 at their camp, 2 on ground the family picks (the screen shows both).
- NEGOTIATION: Chuy has no ceiling to show; he is not the payer. "More" is answered by the co-op's line: "Sixteen. Boots first."
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the party moves on in three nights on its own.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Chuy, on the roof, not turning around: "Left of the tower. See the smoke? That's them."

## THE CHOICES
A. THEIR CAMP. A tough fight in the wash, 8 to 15 minutes, six against the company.
B. YOUR GROUND. The lookout sees them move at dawn; meet them on the pump road where the family picks the tiles. A routine fight, 3 to 4 minutes, a roof tile with height (the mound).
C. TELL THE CO-OP WHERE. The co-op's men go themselves. A priced refusal: the fee halves, and two co-op men come back hurt.
Same fee for A and B.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A or B won: the party is gone from the map; its name goes to the barber's rumour line. Chuy asks to join the company once, walk-past-able; if taken, he is a recruit, and in act 2 his heir can be. C: the co-op's next board has one less guard. The lookout stays on the roof in every later act. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
