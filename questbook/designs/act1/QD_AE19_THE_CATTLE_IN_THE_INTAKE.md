## THE SITUATION
Act 1, the anarchy decade. A herd of the lab's loose cattle drowned against the intake grating in the spring melt. The bodies choke the flow, and hyenas come down the rocks every night to feed on them. The turbines are running at half. Half the city is in brownout.

## THE CHOICES
A. THE ROCKS AT DUSK. The hyenas (six) come down to feed: the fight board is the intake tower block, wet rock, the tower's ladder platform is the mound; routine, 3 to 4 minutes.
B. BURN THE BODIES. Drag the carcasses out on the access ramp and burn them (4 TAPE for the winch rope); the hyenas lose the reason to come. No fight; one day longer; the smoke reaches the city.
C. "I'LL KEEP WATCH. I WON'T GO ON THE ROCKS." Stand on the dam crest with rifles while the divers work fast. Priced and said: 8 WATER; the hyenas take one diver's arm and Graciela tells you which one.
