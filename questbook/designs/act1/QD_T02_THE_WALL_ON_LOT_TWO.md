## THE SITUATION
The family holds the tower's block now, but the growers who work it are not the company. Marisol runs the racks. The Salt Boys' tracks are drawn on the map, two stops out, bending this way. The tower is the brightest thing on this side of the wash, and that is why they come.

- SETTLEMENT: the farm tower. Buildings: the hall (a folding table by the pump), the racks, SCAVENGE, and LOT TWO, empty, with a stake and a string where a wall could go.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Marisol, grower boss, dirt to the elbow, a cropped Rick Owens jacket over overalls.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Salt Boys. Fourth night. You saw the tracks." / "I pay for the harvest standing on the fifth morning. How is your business." / "The lot's there. Just saying."
- PAY: 18 batteries for the harvest standing. SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" lands (21). The second hits the ceiling: "It's your tower too, jefe." Advance: 4 now, shown as 4 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing: the growers hold the night alone, and the board shows what that costs.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Marisol, at the table: "Fourth night. I already counted."

## THE CHOICES
A. HOLD IT AS IT IS. A tough fight on the fight board cut from this block, 8 to 12 minutes, seven against the company across open racks.
B. BUILD THE WALL. Tap lot two: 12 batteries and 3 of the 4 days. On the fourth night the Salt Boys look at the wall, and go. No fight here.
C. BUILD AND HOLD. The wall stands, they try the gap anyway: a routine fight, 2 to 4 minutes, the wall is a house tile of cover.
Same pay on every road. The wall is the family's money and stays the family's wall.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
The wall is built and lives on the settlement screen of the base, and nowhere else (rule 86), in every later act (the derived future). A or C won: the Salt Boys lose men; beaten twice, they get a name. B: their tracks go to the nearest camp within two stops, and THAT board posts a guard offer (the chain, QR-S rule 10). A lost: the racks burn; the tower's board goes thin and pays in seed (FOOD). Declining leaves nothing but the world's own result.
