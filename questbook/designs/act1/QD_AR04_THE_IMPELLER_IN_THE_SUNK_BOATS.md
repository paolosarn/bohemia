## THE SITUATION
The farm tower's main pump threw its impeller and the hydroponic rows have four days of water in the tanks. The only impellers that size are in the engines of the houseboats that sank at the marina when the lake dropped. The marina's camp knows which boats, and sells nothing to anyone from the valley since the water fight.

## THE ROAD (QR-AR rule 1)
THERE: 13 hours by road to the shore, then the shore itself at half pace. BACK: the same with a part on a handcart. WAITS: four days of water in the tanks; the tower's tech says it in days and the tank gauge on the settlement screen shows it (the clock is a real thing on a screen, `Q021.W4`). The offer expires with the tanks: miss it and the rows die, the tower's board changes, nothing is hidden (`Q128.X2`'s fix). One night out at the marina. Roaming on this road: the marina's own boat people; a Caravans run to the dam.
