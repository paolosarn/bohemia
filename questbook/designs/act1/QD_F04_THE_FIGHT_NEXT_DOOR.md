## THE SITUATION
The main beat that gives him his first companion, the older sibling (the 7/19 survivor seed, "leaning",
a role, not a name). He starts in his own yard. Over the low wall, on the next lot, the sibling is
already fighting three scavengers who came for the car's battery, and is winning, but slowly. The fight
does not need him. It is happening on cells he can see and walk to.

SECOND BY SECOND:
- 0-10 s: one cell per press. A press into the yard wall shows it. From over the wall: blows landing on
  the beat, no voices.
- 10-30 s: the yard gate is four cells away. On the way, a pipe length on a PROP cell (the thesis in this
  opening: the only weapon in the family is a pipe). The sibling is visible over the wall, one cell, one
  colour, holding the mound.
- 30-60 s: the silent choice. WALK THROUGH THE GATE into reach: the fight is on the slab's cells, where he
  stands, three against two, RF4 on the beat, routine hits cost one, the sibling fighting as the automated
  companion. Or STAY IN THE YARD: at about 50 s the sibling drops the last one without him, sits on the
  mound, and bleeds a little. Nobody calls him.
- 60-180 s: either way, after 60 s, if he comes within two cells the sibling speaks. Down the street, two
  people carrying a door between them, to each other: "Otra vez los del lot de al lado. Esa batería no
  vale lo que les va a costar." (Overheard.)

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The sibling, portrait on, after 60 s, within two cells, one line: "You watched. Fine. Help me get the
battery out before they come back with more."

(If he fought: "Took you long enough. Help me get the battery out before they come back with more.")

## THE CHOICES
1. HELP PULL THE BATTERY: the car battery comes out in two beats; it is the family's new seed capital
   (+2 batteries, shared). The sibling joins as companion. The main line opens.
2. WALK AWAY: the sibling pulls it alone and takes it home. The main line still opens later from the
   house; the companion joins later, cooler (one line different, never a lecture).
3. TAKE THE BATTERY FOR HIMSELF (only if he fought): +2 batteries, his. The sibling does not stop him,
   says nothing now, and the ledger holds it for a later act.
