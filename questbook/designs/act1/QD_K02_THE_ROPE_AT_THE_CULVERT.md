## THE SITUATION
Act 1, the ruin, a bust. After a flash flood the wash is a moat, and the culvert is the only dry way east. A
family has strung a rope across it and lives on the embankment above. The map drew the rope the round the flood
receded; the family's small holding is marked on the map as theirs. The rate is spoken, and it scales with what the
company carries (`Q065.X1`): a rich company pays more, so the toll never turns into nothing.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The mother, fifties, a rifle across her knees, a floor-length Balenciaga parka cut off at the knee for the mud.
Her son holds the rope. First line: "Two cells, or the long way. The water's not ours, the concrete is."

## THE CHOICES (price on the button)
1. PAY. Price: 2 batteries at a light load, 3 at a heavy one (the number is on the button). The rope drops.
2. WORK IT OFF. Price: two hours. The company clears the flood debris from the culvert mouth with them; no
   batteries. The mother says what the work is before you pick ("Clear the mouth. It fills every storm.").
3. KEEP MOVING (the long way). Price from the ledgers: nothing. The party turns off; the map shows the detour's
   length before the tap, and it is only road time. Nobody fires. Nobody remembers it.

A fourth, violent answer (cut the rope and run it) exists only on the fight board, never as an event button, as QR-B
rules for E2.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Pay: batteries; the family's standing rises a little.
- Work: time; the culvert's mouth is clear on the map. THE FLIP: in act 3 the crossing is a paved bridge, and if the
  company worked it in act 1 the family's name is on the rail (`Q089.W4`: strangers built the road you drive on).
  If nobody worked it, the act 3 bridge carries no name. Both reads are written; an unplayed act 1 reads "no name".
- Keep moving writes nothing.
- The feed, two trips later, from their side: "Somebody paid the rope today. Rice tonight."
