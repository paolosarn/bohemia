## THE SITUATION
The dam road has started buying the field's surplus, which makes this a boom week for the Trades. Then a dust storm put six rows under sand. Clean, those rows are the field's whole month. In rows four and five, thirty people from the wash sheltered from the storm behind the panels and have not left. The field's own crew will not sweep around them.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the solar field, glass to the horizon. Buildings: the hall (the inverter shed), the market, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the inverter shed. Two portraits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Ingrid Oyelaran, shift lead, sun goggles, a white Rick Owens sheet dress with sand in the hem.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Six rows under the sand. Clean, they'd pay the whole field's month." / "There are people sleeping in rows four and five. My crew won't sweep around them." / "Two days. Sand first. The rest, you tell me."
- PAY: 8 batteries. SKULLS: 1 (the refugees have two guns and are scared).
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 9. A second ask: "Nine. The sand isn't paying me yet." Advance: 2 now, 2 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing. The offer leaves with the next wind.

## THE CHOICES
A. HIRE THE SLEEPERS. Ingrid pays them one battery each out of the field's hours if the family asks. Thirty brooms; done in a day.
B. WALK THEM TO THE WASH. No fight if nobody reaches for the guns. The company walks them one leg to the wash camp, then sweeps. Two days.
C. SWEEP AROUND THEM. Three days, the company's own backs. The sleepers watch and then, on the third morning, two of them pick up brooms.
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: the field's charge price at its market falls one. A: the field's next board has a sweepers' crew offer, with a face from the rows. B: the wash camp is thirty people bigger, and its board shows it. C: two refugees stay on at the field; the barber's rumour line has a joke about the family's backs. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Down every buried row, the sand has been swept clean in one perfect line the width of a foot, and there are no footprints.
