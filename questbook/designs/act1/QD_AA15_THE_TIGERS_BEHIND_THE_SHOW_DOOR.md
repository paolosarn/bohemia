## THE SITUATION
Act 1. The Mob runs the Strip's working casinos on batteries and bets. One of their back-of-house wings, the old magic show's animal wing, is where the laundry is. The laundry has stopped, because the show's two white tigers never left. They live in the wing, eat what the kitchen throws out, and lately they have started eating the laundry staff.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the casino. Buildings: the hall (the cage), the floor (games), the bar (hiring), the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Sal Benedetto, floor boss, a black Rick Owens coat to the ankle, rings on every finger, sunglasses at night.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Two tigers. White ones. They were the show." / "They don't come on the floor. They stay in back. The laundry's in back." / "Don't shoot them in front of the guests. We have guests."
- PAY: 20 batteries, or 14 and a comped week at the casino (the company eats and heals for free for 7 days: 7 FOOD, 2 MEDS). SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (23); ceiling: "The house doesn't lose. That's not a saying, it's a policy." Advance: 5.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the laundry stays shut.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Sal, on the offer screen, not taking the sunglasses off: "They still come when the show music plays. Nobody's played it in ten years."

## THE WALK AND THE SIGN
On the Strip the company walks: through the loading dock (a forklift, a sign "ANIMAL WING: HANDLERS ONLY"), past the laundry (sheets still in the machines), to the animal wing's door. The sign is Sal's line and the scratch marks on the inside of the wing's door, at head height.

## THE CHOICES
A. KILL BOTH. The fight: the pair splits; one holds a man's attention on one tile while the other crosses (the tell: one tiger stops moving, the other's shadow moves). Pay. The pelts are the house's ("The house keeps the coats."); nothing on top for the kill (QR-AO, line 30).
B. PLAY THE SHOW MUSIC. Find the show's sound desk on the walk and play the cue. The tigers come to the stage and sit on their marks, as trained. The company walks them into the old cage. Sal pays, and keeps them ("Guests love a tiger.").
C. OPEN THE LOADING DOCK. Let them out onto the Strip's dead end and into the desert. No fight, half pay. Two tigers are a visible party on the map, south of the city.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: the laundry opens; the Mob's standing rises a step. B: the Mob has tigers on its floor for the act; a later contract on the Strip may be to feed them. C: the tigers roam; a settlement south posts them in act 1 or 2. Declining leaves nothing.
