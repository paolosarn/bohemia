## THE SITUATION
Four turbine techs have to be at the dam by the shift change the day after tomorrow or the crew up there works a third week straight and somebody falls asleep on a rail. The techs can walk. They cannot fight. The road past Boulder City has had a crew on it since the dollar died.

## THE ROAD (QR-AR rule 1)
THERE: 16 hours by road at the techs' pace (QR-AF finding 5: the march goes at the escorted's speed; call it 18 hours, one night out at Boulder City). BACK: 16 hours, free of the techs, one night. WAITS: the offer stands 4 days; the shift change is the only clock, said aloud. (QR-AT, line 3: the dam's pin on the map carries the shift change's day count from the yes, `Q095.X1`, `Q119.X3`.) Roaming on this road: a road crew between Boulder City and the dam; the Mob's collectors turn up here since the dam pays in hours of power (FACTIONS).

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the turbine hall's valley office, a counter with a wall of clipboards and one working clock.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: FOREMAN DUBE, hard hat on the counter, Rick Owens coveralls, eyes red from the night shift.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Four of mine to the dam. Two days. They walk, you watch the road." / "Shift change is dawn the day after tomorrow. Late is a man asleep on a rail." / "I pay in hours. Your base gets power for a week. Nobody else pays like that."
- PAY: a week of hours of power to the player's home base (QR-O base 1's own coin), plus 20 BATTERIES at the far end by the dam's gate. ROAD ALLOWANCE (QR-AR rule 2): 2 FOOD and 1 WATER a head for the two days out, in a crate on the techs' own handcart, plus the wage. SKULLS: 2 (the road and the night, not the fight).
- NEGOTIATION: "More" once: the power stretches to ten days; twice: "That's the top. Take it or don't." (`Q236.P2`)
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining is free, writes nothing.

## THE NIGHT OUT (QR-AR rule 5)
One night at Boulder City's edge. One event at most, from QR-AG's ten: the Traveler or the Broken Cart, a face, two or three priced buttons. A quiet night is common.

## THE CHOICES ON THE ROAD
A. THE ROAD: the crew between Boulder City and the dam, four of them, a routine fight 3 to 4 minutes on the road's house tiles; the techs stand behind the line (the companion rule: nobody stands on anybody, the first votes).
B. THE LAKE SHORE: longer by half (shore is slow, WORLD owes the cost), nobody on it, the techs arrive a shift late. The map shows the hours before you pick it.
C. PAY THE CREW: 15 batteries and they wave the handcart through. Standing with the crew up; nothing on the ledger but the batteries.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36). A tech struck down is a tech, not a member: the shift is short one and the hours of power are short two days.

## THE ONE TWIST (T18, A THIRD PARTY SHOWS UP; lands at the far end, QR-AR rule 11)
