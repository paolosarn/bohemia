## THE SITUATION
Moapa's camp has the only sweet well between the valley and Mesquite and a raiding party out of the north has hit it twice at night for the pump's battery bank. The camp knows the party's pattern: every fourth night, from the north, at the dark of the moon. They want a company at the well for the two nights that matter, and they can pay in food and water, which is what a well camp has.

## THE ROAD (QR-AR rule 1)
THERE: 15 hours by road. BACK: 15 hours. WAITS: the raid is due on the fourth night from the post's date; the phone's board shows the post ageing (QR-AE's board rule) and the keeper says the night; arrive after it and the well is hit or it is not, and the keeper says which. A HOLD job is an appointment (QR-AF finding 7): the clock is the job. Roaming on this road: the raiders themselves, who leave tracks south toward the well, readable inside the sight radius the night before (rule 102d).

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: Moapa's camp, the well house, sandbags and a solar bank under a tarp.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: NILES, the well keeper, a shotgun across his knees, a Bottega Veneta windbreaker zipped to the chin.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Every fourth night. From the north. They want the batteries, not the water." / "Two nights. You're here, they're not. That's the job." / "I pay in what a well has: water for a month, food for the two nights, and ten batteries that they'd have taken."
- PAY: 10 BATTERIES and 8 WATER at hand-in on the morning after the second night, plus the camp's water free to the company for a month after. ROAD ALLOWANCE (QR-AR rule 2): 2 FOOD a head a day for the two nights on station, from the camp's own stores, said as a line; the road days are the player's. SKULLS: 2 (three nights out, a raid at night).
- NEGOTIATION: "More" once: 15 batteries. Twice: "They're taking it anyway if you're not here."
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining is free; the raid comes or it does not; the well's situation changes and the post ages off the phone (`Q236.P3`).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
First line after accept: "Sleep in the day. The moon's the clock."

## THE NIGHTS OUT (QR-AR rule 5)
Night one on the road: one event at most (QR-AG's Raid Farmstead is the fit, a face from a farm the raiders already hit, who points at their tracks). Nights two and three on station: no road event; the raid is the event, and it is the twist.

## THE CHOICES AT THE WELL
A. HOLD: the company takes the well house's tiles, the sandbags as cover, the solar bank behind the line. The raid is six on the first night they come, a routine fight at night, 3 to 4 minutes, the board's light low (COMBAT's night board). Chased off counts at three down.
B. THE TRACKS: go north by day on the raiders' prints to their camp, a wash twelve blocks up, and hit them asleep: a routine fight by day, 3 minutes; the well is unguarded that night and nobody comes, because they are dead or running.
C. THE DEAL: the raiders' leader comes to the sandbags at dusk with a flag and offers to leave the well alone for a cut of its water, monthly; Niles will not hear it; the player may take the leader's word and walk, and the well pays the raiders instead of the player.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36).

## THE ONE TWIST (T10, THE CLOCK ARRIVES; built into the kind, lands on station)
The raid comes a night early: the third night, not the fourth, because the raiders saw the company's tracks on the road and know the well is held. A party that reads the prints on the road the night before (the Raid Farmstead face, or the sight radius) is awake; one that slept on the first night on station wakes to the fight already on the tiles, the first beat lost. The clock is the job, and it moves once, on evidence the player could read (`Q011.W2` with `Q018.W8`'s generosity: the sign is drawn on the map).

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Held: Moapa's situation flips from "raided" to "held" and its board offers a water run south and a pump job the next pass; the raiders' tracks stop coming (`Q236.P4`). Missed: the solar bank is gone from the well house's picture and the camp's water price doubles for the valley; Niles says "fourth night, I said" if the party was late, and nothing if it declined. The deed ledger writes the night it happened and what the company did (`Q024.W10`). Across the road: Mesquite's job (QD-AR10) reads the well's state when the party passes.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Niles's shotgun across his knees is not loaded, and the shells are on the shelf behind him next to the well's logbook, which lists the raids on the third night, not the fourth.
