## THE SITUATION
A boom pocket in act 1: the Strip wants to open a charge counter in the co-op's square. More batteries in, more Mob in. The co-op votes on it in three nights. Block seven, on the far side of a road the last rain took, has sixty votes in a box nobody can reach on foot in time.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the co-op blocks, laundry between balconies. Buildings: the hall (the co-op office, a ballot poster on the door), the market, the barber, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the co-op office. Two portraits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Abuela Tona, returning officer, knitting, gold Balenciaga trainers.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Block seven votes like everybody else. The road washed out. The box is still there." / "Bring it sealed. Not opened, not lighter, not heavier." / "Count is in three nights. After that it's just a box."
- PAY: 5 batteries. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 6 ("Six, and I knit you something."). No second ask. No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing. The offer leaves at the count.

## THE CHOICES
A. THROUGH THE WASH. The fast way. A small crew sits in the dry channel charging a toll; a fight on the fight board in the culvert, or pay 2 batteries of your own.
B. THE LONG ROAD ROUND. A day slower. Arrives on count night, with the hall already full.
C. BRING A WITNESS. Block seven's own oldest voter insists on walking beside the box. Slowest; arrives the morning of the count. Block seven trusts the count.
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done: sixty votes counted; the co-op's board next visit reads the result, whichever way it went. C: block seven posts its own small offer on the co-op board next visit (the road). Late: the count runs without block seven, and the barber's rumour line says so. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The box is heavier on the way back than on the way out, and the seal was never touched.
