## THE SITUATION
The depot runs one salt and medicine caravan up from the south each moon. This one is four days overdue. In a bust, four days late means ambush. The depot's board already shows it: two runs cancelled, one guard job for the yard. The caravan master wants it found. He wants it found more than he wants the load.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the depot. Buildings: the hall (the dispatch window), the market, the clinic, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the dispatch window. Three portraits; this one is not sitting down.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Old Kasper, caravan master, the south road tattooed down one forearm, a faded Rick Owens bomber.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Four days late. My drivers are never late, not with medicine." / "Road south: the old truck stop, then the dry town. They're somewhere on that." / "Find them. That's the job. The load, we talk after."
- PAY: 7 batteries for FINDING the caravan, locked at the yes, whatever state it is in. SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 8. A second ask: "Eight. Find them first and ask me again." Advance: 2 now, 2 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
At the old truck stop the tracks look like an ambush: the trucks pulled off the road in a hurry. They are not. At the dry town the trucks sit in a line, the drivers eat with the town, and half the medicine is already in the town's clinic. The lead driver says it: "They had nothing. Kasper would have done the same. Maybe."
A. BRING BACK WHAT'S LEFT, AND THE DRIVERS. The town keeps what it has. Kasper fines the drivers and pays the family.
B. TAKE IT BACK. Load the medicine back out of the town's clinic. The town cannot stop you. Same pay.
C. LEAVE IT, BRING THE DRIVERS, AND SAY IT TO KASPER. The drivers walk in beside the truck and tell him themselves. Same pay.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A or C: the depot's medicine price rises one for a refresh; the dry town survives and appears on the map as a stop with its own event next time the party passes. B: the depot's price holds; the dry town is marked a ruin the next time the map is opened (the future goes both ways). C: Kasper's next board has a run to the dry town, paid by the depot. Declining leaves nothing; the caravan comes in on its own a week later, lighter.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The four trucks are parked in a perfectly straight line with their engines warm, four days after the drivers say they switched them off.
