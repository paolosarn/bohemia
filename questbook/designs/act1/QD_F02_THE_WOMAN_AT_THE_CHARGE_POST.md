# QD-F02: THE WOMAN AT THE CHARGE POST
ACT: 1
KIND: contract
CRISIS: none
ECONOMY: bust
PLACE: a ruined street corner: a 3-cell street with 1-cell sidewalks, a crushed car (2x1), a public charge post with six sockets, a pump house 20 cells down the road
STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35)

## THE SITUATION
He starts on the sidewalk cell of a dead corner. Across the street a woman in a long draped coat (the
runway rule) sits on an upturned bucket beside a hand cart. On the cart: a water drum and a cart battery.
She is at the charge post, which has six sockets; five are dark. She is waiting for the one lit socket,
which somebody else's cable holds.

SECOND BY SECOND:
- 0-10 s: one cell per press. A press into the crushed car (PROP) rocks it on its springs. A generator
  somewhere down the block.
- 10-30 s: the post and its five dead sockets are in sight from the spawn. Within five cells, the car's
  open door: in the footwell, a cable with two good clamps. The wrong detail is on the post.
- 30-60 s: the silent choice: TAKE the cable (a thing that charges things), or leave it. Down the street,
  three scavengers pry at a pump-house shutter; they do not turn. The woman does not look up. Two floor
  paths pass her; one passes the scavengers.
- 60-180 s: if he passes within two cells of her and stops a beat, she speaks (below). If he passes the
  scavengers within their reach, the fight is on those cells; one step back and they return to the shutter.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Only after 60 s, only within two cells, portrait on. She is NINA (one of the four people the revamp list
keeps as people you meet; the name is Paolo's to change): "Soy Nina. That one works. The man whose cord
that is has been charging for six hours. I don't have six hours. Can you push?"

## THE CHOICES
1. TAKE THE CONTRACT: push her cart the 20 cells to the pump house (a slow push, two cells a beat, past
   the scavengers). Pays 1 battery. Time: about a minute of play. If the scavengers are still there, she
   waits behind him while he deals with them or walks the long way round (four more cells).
2. TAKE IT, AND USE THE CABLE: if he took the cable, he can jump her battery off the working socket
   himself instead. Pays 1 battery and she stays; the man whose cord it was will notice later.
3. WALK ON. Nothing. She is on her bucket the next time he passes, same cell, same line.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
The cable (taken or left) and, if taken, whether it was used on the post. The contract: taken and
finished, or taken and dropped (a deed: she is not at the post again). DECLINING LEAVES NOTHING: no row,
no line, no look.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The charge post's little screen shows the full-charge icon on all six sockets, five of them with nothing
plugged in.
