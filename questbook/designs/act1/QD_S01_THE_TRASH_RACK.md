## THE SITUATION
A week ago the wash above the lake ran and packed the intake's trash rack with half the desert. Turbine two is choking, so the dam rations shifts and sells fewer hours. The divers clear the rack at night. By morning a crew of eight or ten has pulled the loose steel bars and the copper banding off the deck to sell. They are not raiders. They are hungry, and they are taking the dam apart one bar at a time.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the dam. Buildings: the hall (the turbine hall's old visitor desk), the market, the clinic, SCAVENGE, the barber.
- BUILDING: the hall. Three portraits today; this is the one with wet hair.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Ofelia Ruiz, intake foreman, a wetsuit under a cropped Balenciaga parka.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Turbine dos is choking on half the desert." / "There's a crew on the intake deck. Hungry, not raiders. They're taking my steel." / "Get them off the deck for three nights. How is your business."
- PAY: 8 batteries on the third morning. SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more once: 10 ("Ten is what the dam has."). A second ask: "Ten or somebody else." Advance: 2 now, shown as 2 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing. The offer leaves the board when the rack is clear, whoever clears it.

## THE CHOICES
A. CLEAR THE DECK. A fight on the fight board: the deck is one long house-sized tile with a rail and the water below. The group of eight scatters when half go down. Full pay.
B. HIRE THEM. Ofelia pays the crew in hours of power out of the dam's own count if the family vouches for them. The crew works the rack as a night shift. Full pay, one more night on the deck to watch them.
C. "I WON'T FIGHT HUNGRY MEN." Hold only the land end of the deck. The rack is cleared a week late. Half pay, stated by Ofelia before the second night.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Done either way: turbine two turns, the market's power price at the dam drops one. A: the scattered crew's tracks lead toward the wash camp, whose next board shows one more hungry mouth. B: the dam's next board posts "the deck gang wants their pay carried to the wash", with a face you already met. C: the dam writes "late", nothing more. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Every morning, among the stripped bars, one steel bar has been put back, straight and bolted, where no diver could reach it.
