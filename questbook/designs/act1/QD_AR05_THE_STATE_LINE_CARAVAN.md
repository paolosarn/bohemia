## THE SITUATION
Three carts of "produce" go to Primm, where the state line market pays in batteries the valley does not have. The yards want a crew on the carts that is not theirs, because a cartel crew on I-15 is a fight at every junction and strangers are not. The carts go at a cart's pace. The road has crews on it who know what the yards ship.

## THE ROAD (QR-AR rule 1)
THERE: 19 hours by road, at the carts' pace call it a full day and a night at Jean. BACK: 19 hours, empty carts, a night at Jean. WAITS: the carts leave in two days and the offer leaves with them. Roaming on this road: the I-15 crews; the state line's own gate people at Primm (FACTIONS).

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the cartel yards, the loading bay, three carts under tarps and a man who does not stand up.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: EL CONTADOR, the yards' bookkeeper, a Rick Owens coat over a vest, counting nothing on a calculator with no battery.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Three carts to Primm. You don't look under the tarps." / "Two days there, two back, you eat from the carts. That's my allowance." / "Primm's market pays you, not me. I'm paying you to be strangers."
- PAY: 90 BATTERIES at the far end from Primm's market master, per cart that arrives (30 a cart, QR-AF finding 6). ROAD ALLOWANCE (QR-AR rule 2): the party eats and drinks from the carts, 2 FOOD and 1 WATER a head a day, four days, said as a line. SKULLS: 3 (two nights, the state line, the tarps).
- NEGOTIATION: "More" once: a fourth cart's worth if all three arrive. Twice: the calculator goes on the table, face down. (`Q236.W4`, `Q236.W5`)
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining is free; the Cartel says nothing and finds a crew (`Q236.P3`).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
First line after accept: "Strangers. Walk like it."

## THE NIGHT OUT (QR-AR rule 5)
Two nights at Jean, going and coming. One event at most each: QR-AG's Lawmen After Criminal (the state line's people asking what the carts carry) is the natural fit on the way down; the Melon Thief on the way back.

## THE CHOICES ON THE ROAD
A. THE ROAD: the crews at the two I-15 junctions. The first asks a toll per cart (priced button, 10 a cart) or fights (routine, 3 to 4 minutes, the carts behind the line). The second does not ask.
B. THE OLD HIGHWAY: the frontage road, open ground for a third of the way, 6 hours longer, no junctions, the carts' axles may break (one in four, a cart left behind, 30 batteries gone).
C. LOOK UNDER THE TARPS: at the night camp. The player may. What is under them is the twist's fuel.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36).

## THE ONE TWIST (T13, THE LOOT IS THE BAIT; built into the kind, lands on the road)
The second junction's crew does not want a toll; it wants the carts, because somebody at the yards sold the route. A tough fight at the junction's house tiles, 8 to 12 minutes, eight of them with a truck for cover; or the state line's lawmen from the night event, if the player answered them straight, arrive at the junction first and the crew scatters (`Q062.W8`: the detour was the answer). Under the tarps is produce; under the produce is the Cartel's cash from the Strip, which is why the route was sold. The player who looked knows what the fight is for; the player who did not learns at Primm's scale. (QR-AT, line 12, `Q131.X7`, `Q139.P2`: a free button at the second junction, LEAVE THE CARTS: the party walks on; Primm pays nothing, El Contador pays the road allowance only and the Cartel's standing drops one.) (QR-AT, line 23, `Q095.X3`, `Q017.X1`: a lost junction fight is a result: the crew takes what it can drag, a cart a down member, and Primm pays for what arrives.) (QR-AT, line 27: the twist is DRAWN, one draw in four (QR-AR rule 11, `Q148.X3`, `Q148.P4`); on the plain draw the second junction is empty and the tarps are only produce.)

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Primm's market situation reads "supplied" and its prices drop for a week; its board offers a return cargo (a CARRY north) the next pass (`Q236.W8`). The Cartel remembers three carts or two (standing), and who looked under the tarps if a man of theirs saw it (the yards' crew is not on the road, so only a night event can tell). The deed ledger writes "walked the Cartel's carts to Primm" and the Blues' co-op reads it the next time you stand in their hall (`Q008.W5`).
