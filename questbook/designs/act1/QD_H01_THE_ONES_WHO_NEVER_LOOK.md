## THE SITUATION
Shaped from the ACT1 AMALGAMATION IS A GHOST law: the tunnel people live directly above the thing
and are never harmed, because they never look. This beat teaches the player that rule, by a person,
before the player ever meets the danger. WHY the family came to the wash is Paolo's (the law's
PENDING reason to descend); this beat starts once they are standing at the mouth. It is the one
visible main thread of act 1 while it is open, and it waits forever (research rule 2).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
An old woman who runs the camp's water, phoneless, Spanglish, runway-dressed in scavenged layers
(name and look: Paolo's). She does not speak until the player taps her fire on the tunnel camp's settlement screen (QR-AC, line 11, rule 38: the walk is dead; not tapping is the free no). Her first
line: "You can sleep down here, mijo. Everybody sleeps down here. Nobody looks."

## THE CHOICES
1. GO DOWN AND LOOK. Costs: one proximity-heat step (the only thing in act 1 that raises it), a
   night of travel time, and the first rung of the canon's ladder answers later as a
   natural-seeming accident on the road home (a failing ladder, a stuck gate; never explained). The
   haunting begins: whispers, a room that is warm for no reason. Nothing is named.
2. SLEEP AND DO NOT LOOK. Costs: nothing. The camp becomes a safe place on the map; the woman's
   people start offering small contracts there. The mouth stays open, and she is still at her fire
   whenever the player comes back. The main line waits.
3. BRING THE SURVIVING SIBLING DOWN WITH YOU. Costs: the sibling's days (unavailable for contracts
   and fights until you return), and the same heat step. Gain: the look is witnessed by family and
   written to the UNRECORDED ledger; later, the sibling can say "I saw it too."

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Proximity heat (choice 1 or 3). Unrecorded: who went down together. The camp's standing if the
family slept there. A player who never looks is never hunted in act 1 (threat-logic addendum). This
is a main beat, so "declining" here is choice 2, and it leaves no penalty either.

FLAGS AND SAVES (QR-AC, line 28; `Q123.X1`, `Q116.X1`, `Q040.X3`): writes tunnel_look = looked | slept | looked_with_sibling; heat_step.
