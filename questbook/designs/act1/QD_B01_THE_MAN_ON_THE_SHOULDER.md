## THE SITUATION
The party is crossing dirt in a bust stretch. A man sits against the bus shelter, one leg out straight, a tourniquet
made of a designer belt. Before the stop, the map already showed a set of tracks leaving the road and ending near
here. Two versions exist, and the storyteller picks by the world, not by dice: if a hostile party is really within
reach on the map, he is bait; if none is, he is exactly what he looks like. The player never sees which until after
the choice.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The man on the shoulder, forties, a cracked Balenciaga runner jacket, a portrait with sweat on the lip.
First line: "Hermano. Just the leg. I'm not asking for the truck."

## THE CHOICES
1. GET DOWN AND TREAT HIM. Costs 1 MEDS (or 3 batteries at bust prices; rule 47) and two hours of map time. Honest
   version: he asks to ride with the company at the full wage (a recruit paid like any other, never a prize for the
   kind stop; QR-G line 30), or leaves with a debt he remembers. Bait version: the party
   is caught on foot, a fight on the fight board against a group of three, the companion first to the man.
2. CALL OUT FROM THE TRUCK. Costs ten minutes. You ask him one question ("Who did the leg?"). Honest version: he
   names the party that robbed him, and their tracks light on the map. Bait version: he gets impatient, looks
   behind the shelter once, and the player can read it and leave.
3. KEEP MOVING. Costs nothing. Nobody curses you. The party rolls on.

THE TELL IN TWO CHANNELS (QR-AC, line 15; `Q141.X3`): in the bait version the glance behind the shelter comes with a sound on the same beat, a bottle rolling on concrete back there.
A LOST BAIT FIGHT (QR-AC, line 23; `Q095.X3`, `Q146.W1`): the three strip a quarter of the batteries carried and leave on foot, and the man goes with them; a struck-down brother takes the rule 36 roll (20% dead, else the long injury); the party rolls on. A result, never a reload.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries or medicine spent (choice 1).
- People: a recruit, or a wounded brother (choice 1, bait).
- Time: two hours or ten minutes.
- An echo: two trips later the city feed carries a post from his side. Honest and treated: "Some crew stopped for a
  stranger on the east road. Still happens." Honest and passed: nothing, ever. Bait and survived: the robbing party
  becomes a named party on the map with a grudge (`Q087.W5`).
- Keep moving writes nothing.
