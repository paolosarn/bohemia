## THE SITUATION
A pack of coyotes with one big dog in it comes to Jean's cistern every second night and has taken a goat and a child's hand. The pack drinks at Jean, sleeps in the wash toward Primm, and moves a block a day south as the cistern gets guarded. The keeper will pay in water, which is the only thing Jean has.

## THE ROAD (QR-AR rule 1)
THERE: 13 hours by road to Jean (0.55 days); from Jean, a few hours on the tracks. BACK: 13 hours. WAITS: the keeper says "They'll drink here a week more, then they'll be Primm's problem." The pack moves a block a day toward Primm (QR-AR rule 6) and never expires silently: the keeper's line is the clock, and the phone's post ages. One night out, at Jean, if the party sleeps before the hunt. Roaming on this road: FACTIONS' I-15 crews; the state line's own.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: Jean's camp, the cistern yard; the hall is the old casino's loading dock.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: ABUELA PINA, the keeper, a hose coiled on her shoulder, a Balenciaga puffer with the sleeves cut off.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "They come every second night. One of them is not a coyote." / "I pay in water. Four skins. Jean has nothing else." / "Their prints go south. Read them before noon or the wind takes them."
- PAY: 4 WATER and 10 BATTERIES, paid at Jean on the big dog's ear tag (the trophy on top, QR-AO's fix). ROAD ALLOWANCE (QR-AR rule 2): 1 FOOD a head for the day and the cistern's water free while you hunt. SKULLS: 2 (the night and the water, not the five coyotes).
- NEGOTIATION: "More" once: a fifth skin. Twice: "You want blood from a cistern?"
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining is free; the pack goes on drinking; the post ages on the phone.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE

## THE HUNT ON THE REGION (QR-AR rule 6)
The pack's prints are drawn on the region's blocks inside the party's sight radius (rule 102d), a fresh set each day, the old ones fading (01_WORLDMAP TRACKS [R]). Two trails cross in the wash: coyote and the big dog's wider print; the dog's trail is the one with the pack at the end (`Q054.W8`, signal against noise). Read right, the pack is caught asleep by day on the wash's house tiles, five coyotes and the dog: a routine fight, 3 to 4 minutes, chased off counts at three down, the dog does not run. Read wrong, the party finds the drinking place empty and comes back at night to the cistern, where the pack comes to it: the same fight at night, the board's light low.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Jean's situation flips from "pack" to "clear": its cistern yard shows the goat pen rebuilt and its board offers a water run north (QR-O base 13's carry-water kind) the next pass (`Q236.W8`). The big dog's ear tag reads a lab's number (rule 42: every beast broke out of a lab), and the tag is a thing in the bag the rumour at the bar (QR-AH) will ask about. A pack chased off and not killed drinks at Primm in a week: Primm's board shows the same job at Primm's price.
