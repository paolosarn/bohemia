## THE SITUATION
Act 1, the ruin, a bust. The storyteller rule: this fires only after an event or fight that cost a person or a big
share of batteries, or after three costly stops in a row (`Q044.W7`: after a hard hit, send relief). The cause on the
map: a warm light on the casino lot, drawn at dusk, and the sound of a crowd laughing that reaches the party marker
before the stop. Kids are pedalling a bicycle generator to run an old projector, and a movie is playing on the
casino's blank wall. Forty people on salvaged car seats. It costs nothing to watch.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The girl who runs it, fourteen, a huge vintage Balenciaga sweatshirt as a dress, a bike chain grease mark on her cheek.
First line: "It's free. If you sit, you pedal a turn."

## THE CHOICES (price on the button)
1. STOP AND WATCH. Price: two hours, and each member of the company pedals a turn. Company morale rises a little.
   Two members who have been quarrelling sit together, or do not; a line each, if the roster has such a pair.
2. LEAVE A CELL. Price: 1 battery. The kids can rest their legs for the second reel. The girl says thanks once and
   goes back to the projector.
3. WAVE AND KEEP MOVING. Price: nothing. Someone in the crowd waves back.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Time or one battery; a small morale line. Never a big number, and nothing worth farming: the stop fires once per
  lot and the morale gain is the same whether the company watched or gave (`Q125.X9`).
- The feed, next city view: a blurry photo of the wall, "Movie night on the back lot. Bring legs." It names no one.
- THE FLIP: in act 3, the casino wall is a real outdoor screen with its own power, and a small plaque names the kids
  who pedalled. If the company left a cell, one of the names on the plaque is the company's. If the company never
  stopped at the wall in act 1, the plaque names only the kids.

## THE NARRATOR
Read over the event screen in the machine voice (rule 45: too even, tape and room, a little wrong; never in the first minute; people on the screen still speak in the squiggle): "Forty people are laughing at the same time. That is the correct number of people."

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The kid pedalling stopped ten minutes ago, and the projector is still running.
