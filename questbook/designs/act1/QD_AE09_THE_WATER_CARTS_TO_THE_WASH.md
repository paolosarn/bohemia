## THE SITUATION
Act 1, the anarchy decade, a bust. The pump house holds its share of the lake and sells it. The wash camp cannot pay the price and cannot walk the distance. Once a week the pump foreman sends two mule carts down as the share agreement says. The drivers have stopped going.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the pump house. Buildings: the hall (the pump room door), the tank, the barber (a chair by the valve wheel).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Abuelo Teófilo, pump foreman, a vintage Rick Owens leather over a hospital gown, a valve key around his neck.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Two carts to the wash camp. The share says weekly." / "The drivers say something follows the carts. I say they're lazy." / "Bring the mules back. Mules cost more than water."
- PAY: 10 batteries and 4 FOOD. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (12); ceiling: "That's the water's price, not mine." Advance: 2 batteries.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the carts do not go this week.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Teófilo, turning the valve a quarter: "Listen. That's the lake. Everybody wants it, nobody wants to carry it."

## THE CHOICES
A. THE WASH ROAD. Dirt, one day. Tracks shadow the carts on the bank side by dusk (the dire wolves of QD-AE10). They do not attack a moving cart in daylight.
B. NIGHT AT THE CROSSING. If the carts stop past dusk, the pack tests them on the dry crossing block: routine, 3 minutes, mules behind the line.
C. "ONE CART, NOT TWO." Priced and said: 6 batteries; the camp gets half the share and says so.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Delivered: the wash camp's board posts its hunt (QD-AE10) if it was not already up, and the camp mother greets the company by the mules. One cart: the camp's next post is "one desperate offer" (QR-O's bust shape). Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
