## THE SITUATION
Act 1. Tomas, a fighter with about 20 fights behind him, was a lineman before the dollar died. He still carries his climbing spurs on his belt in every fight; he never uses them. The scrappers at Sloan are cutting down the last poles on his old route for the copper. One pole still carries his crew tag, a tin plate with his number punched in it.

## THE PERSON, THE PLACE, THE THING (never a card)
At the night camp, after the fire is lit, Tomas unhooks the spurs and sets them in the sand. He speaks; his portrait is the one talking. TOMAS: "First pole I ever climbed was on the Sloan route. My foreman punched my number on a tin plate and nailed it up." / TOMAS: "One pole left with my tag on it. The scrappers want it down by the second night." / TOMAS: "I climb it, I take the tag, we go." The price is on the button, not in his mouth: [CLIMB IT: 12 BATTERIES from his share] / [NOT THIS TIME] (QR-AV line 33). The map shows the pole yard as a place with a pole on it; the second night is said aloud and shown as the pole leaning on the map picture.

- PAY: 12 BATTERIES out of his own share, locked before the yes. SKULLS: 1. No advance (it is his own money). Declining is free and writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. CLIMB AND TAKE IT: Tomas climbs at dawn while the company holds the yard; the scrapper crew (four men) arrives at the second leg; talk first (their boss wants 4 batteries for an hour) or a fight on four house tiles.
B. BUY THE POLE: pay the scrapper boss 10 batteries for the whole pole; he cuts it down gently and Tomas takes the tag off it on the ground. No climb, no fight.
C. LEAVE THE TAG UP: Tomas climbs, reads the tag, and leaves it; he says the pole is his grave marker now. The scrappers are paid 4 to leave this one pole. It stands on the map picture afterwards.
D. NOT THE YARD (QR-AV, line 12: the old D was the no moved after the yes, a drop with no price, `Q131.X7`, `Q139.P2`, `Q134.X2`): you walk him to the yard's edge and will not hold it. He waits for the scrappers' noon meal and climbs alone; the tag comes down with a cut hand. He pays 6 of the 12, says 'fair', and the pole is down on the map picture the next time you pass Sloan.
IF THE FIGHT IS LOST (`Q095.X3`, `Q098.X2`): the scrappers keep the yard; Tomas gets the tag off a cut pole in the scrap pile for 2 batteries the next morning, bent in half. He nails it to the camp's cart.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36); a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10% (the third votes). Followers never fight.

The cart picture carries the tag (A, B) or the pole stands at Sloan (C). Tomas's portrait gets the tag on a cord round his neck. Declining writes nothing. The job never gates Tomas staying.
