## THE SITUATION
The co-op's ration list has a family of five on it, the Salcedos, who walked north to Mesquite in the spring to a cousin's farm on the Virgin River and have not been heard from since the river came up. The co-op's vote is in ten days and the Salcedos' five ration shares go to whoever is in the hall. The co-op would rather have the family. It is four days there and back if nothing goes wrong, and the co-op says so.

## THE ROAD (QR-AR rule 1)
THERE: 20 hours by road, a night at Moapa, a second on the road or at Mesquite. BACK: the same, with five people who walk at a family's pace (QR-AF finding 5), a night at Moapa; four days on the clock, five with the family. WAITS: ten days to the vote; the post ages on the phone from the day it is pinned. Roaming on this road: Moapa's raiders (QD-AR09), and the Virgin River's crossing people at Mesquite (FACTIONS).

## THE CHOICES ON THE ROAD
A. THE ROAD: four days, the well on the way, the river at the end.
B. MOAPA'S WORD: the keeper says five people went north in spring and three came back south in summer, and two of them were children; a party that asks turns the search; one that does not asks at Mesquite and learns it there, a day later.
C. THE CROSSING: Mesquite's river is up; the cousin's farm is on the far bank; the crossing people charge a head to ferry, or the party fords by day at a risk of the bags (the road allowance washed, a real cost on the map).
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36).

## THE ONE TWIST (T08, THE CLUE IS MISREAD ONCE; lands on the road, at Moapa)
The family is not at Mesquite. The father and the two youngest came back south in summer and are at Moapa, working the well camp under the cousin's name because the co-op's list would have cut their shares for leaving; the mother and the eldest are at the farm, which the river has made an island. The first trail (the list, "Mesquite") sends the party the whole way; the second clue (the keeper's three who came back, or the children's names on the well's chore board) is there at Moapa for a party that reads it (`Q148.W3`). Looking again saves a day; not looking is not a failure, only a day, and the far end still holds the mother (`Q148.X2`'s corridor is avoided: the misread costs time, never the job).
