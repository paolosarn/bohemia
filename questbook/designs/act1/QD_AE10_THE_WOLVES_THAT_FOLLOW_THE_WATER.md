## THE CHOICES
A. THE CULVERT. Walk the tracks to the den by day: the fight board is the culvert block, the pack flanks from both mouths. Tough, 8 to 12 minutes. The dial shot is for the big female.
B. BAIT WITH THE CARTS. If the player holds QD-AE09, stop the carts at the crossing at dusk and fight there with the mules behind the line: the pack comes on the company's ground. Tough but shorter, 8 minutes.
C. "I WON'T GO IN THE CULVERT." Build a thorn line around the camp: 6 TAPE, one day. Priced and said: the guide and 4 batteries; the pack keeps following the carts.
