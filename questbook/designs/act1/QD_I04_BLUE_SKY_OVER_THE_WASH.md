## THE CHOICES
A. GO DOWN YOURSELVES. The party climbs down and walks the camp out, a hand on each shoulder. Cost: the rest of the map day; the party's marker waits at the crossing. The camp's grandmother marks the company's banner with chalk.
B. SEND YOUR FASTEST TO THE MARKET BELL. One company member runs to the nearest market and rings it; half the market comes to help, some come to look at what the camp left. Cost: that member is off the roster for one map day; the camp loses its goods, not its people.
C. KEEP MOVING. The party crosses and goes on. Nothing is written about the company. She limps down alone, early as always.
