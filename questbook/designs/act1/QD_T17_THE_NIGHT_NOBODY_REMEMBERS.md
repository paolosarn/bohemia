## THE SITUATION
Dawn. The fire is out. The goat is tied to the truck. Paco has "LUPITA" on his forearm, still red. The batteries are three short. A man is standing at the edge of the lot with his hat in his hands, waiting for someone to wake up.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Don Refugio, the still's owner, in a church shirt and cowboy boots: "Good morning. Your man married my niece. I came about the goat."

## THE CHOICES
A. ASK AROUND. Go back along last night: the still (Refugio says Paco paid in batteries), the dance at the lot (the niece, Lupita, says it was a joke and she is not laughing), the goat's owner (who wants it back). Half a map day. The company assembles the night. It can assemble WRONG: if the player trusts Refugio's version, the batteries were stolen by Lupita's brother; if the player asks Lupita, Paco gave them to her as a dowry. The game never says which is true.
B. PAY AND GO. Give Refugio two batteries for the goat and his trouble, and leave. The tattoo stays. So does Lupita, at the still, every time the map passes it.
C. PACO DECIDES. The company lead lets Paco sort it out. He walks off with Lupita for an hour and comes back quiet. The goat stays with the company.
Priced in time, batteries, and one company member's mood.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Most of it resets: the goat is eaten or returned, the batteries are gone. ONE grievance sticks, whichever road: Lupita. In A, if the player blamed her brother, the brother is a face at the co-op who will not work for the family. In B and C, Lupita is at the still whenever the party passes; if Paco dies later (rule 36), she is at his grave in act 2 with his tattoo on her own arm.

## THE NARRATOR
Read over the event screen in the machine voice (rule 45: too even, tape and room, a little wrong; never in the first minute; people on the screen still speak in the squiggle): "Nobody remembers the night. The night remembers everybody, in some detail."

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The goat is wearing Paco's watch, and it is set to a time zone nobody in the company has been to.
