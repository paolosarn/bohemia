## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a boom. In one trade town the price of plain paper has tripled this month. The printer has orders from
people he has never met, paid ahead, for blank reams, no printing. He does not ask. The salvage mill up the wash
still makes paper from old cardboard, but its last shipment was bought on the road by strangers who paid double
and were never seen again. The canon's whisper network (GDD v3, older) is "offline evidence hand-carried"; HOW
the broadcast goes out is Paolo's. This contract only knows that paper is suddenly worth a lot, and that is true
in either answer.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the outlet-mall town. Its screen: the market (paper listed at three times last month), the food
  court (rumours: "everybody wants to write letters again"), the print shop, which is the town's HALL (the
  printer is also its notary).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: the printer, fifties, ink to the elbows, a white Maison Margiela apron gone grey.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES:
  1. "Forty reams from the mill up the wash. Two days there and back."
  2. "People pay me for blank paper now. I don't ask what for."
  3. "The last load got bought on the road. Don't sell mine."
- PAY: 6 batteries, locked at the offer. If the load comes a day late by the day road, 4 (he says so).
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more (up to 7, and he will set your company's name in type for free). Advance: 3.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The printer, setting type by hand without looking up: "You read? Good. Nobody reads the price anymore. They just pay it."

## THE CHOICES (inside the taken contract)
1. BRING IT ALL TO HIM. On the way back, on the mill's loading dock (the fight board cut from the dock), a crew blocks
   the gate. Ways through: pay the mill's night watchman to open the back, carry the reams over the roof, or wait
   the crew out until the morning shift. Delivered: full pay. The reams go to people who carry things by hand.
   Heat does not rise: carrying paper is not looking.
2. SELL THE LOAD TO THE STRANGERS. On the road a quiet, well-dressed pair offers the same six, in cells, now, politely,
   and the mill road stops being your problem (QR-G line 30 and QR-L P16: on a contract the pull is convenience, never a
   bigger number). If
   you sell, the contract is DROPPED (the one deed): the printer stops offering, and the rumour follows you. A trip
   later the feed shows a burned pile of paper in a wash. Nobody says who burned it.
3. I WON'T RUN THE MILL ROAD AT NIGHT (the priced refusal). You take the day road, one day late; the printer pays
   4, as he said at the offer. Finished, not dropped.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries; the printer's standing; the paper price in this town drops one band if the load arrives.
- The broadcast's ledger: a delivered load is a stack of blank paper on the print shop's shelf, visible on the
  settlement screen until the night of QD-H04. It is one piece of the bridge, never a requirement.
- Choice 2 is a dropped contract: a deed, a rumour, the burned pile on the feed.
- Declining leaves nothing. Another company takes the run or nobody does; the price stays high.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The printer holds the first sheet to the lamp and there is a faint grid of yellow dots on paper that has never
been through a machine.
