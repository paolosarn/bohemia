## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a bust. The house lends batteries to families who cannot make the month. Every Tuesday a collector rides out to the co-op blocks. The last escort quit. The road gang at the car wash knows the collector carries what he collects.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the Strip casino block. Buildings: the hall (the cashier's cage), the floor, the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Mr. Lucero, collector, a perfect grey Bottega Veneta suit, one shoe resoled with tyre.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Ride with me to the co-op and back. Keep me breathing." / "I knock. They pay or they don't. You don't knock." / "The car wash boys think my bag is theirs."
- PAY: 26 batteries and 6 ROUNDS. SKULLS: 2. The ride is on Tuesday: said aloud, one fixed day.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (30); ceiling: "The house has a number for me. You're near it." Advance: 6 ROUNDS now.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; he rides alone and the phone's feed says whether he came back.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Lucero, straightening a cuff: "I'm not a bad man. I'm the man the house sends so the bad man doesn't come."

## THE CHOICES
A. THROUGH THE CAR WASH. The gang (six, pipes and two shotguns) on the fight board at the car wash block, routine, 3 to 4 minutes; Lucero hides behind the vacuum stands.
B. THE ALLEYS. Plus half a day, no gang; Lucero is late and the families have hidden their food (he says so, and collects less: not your pay, his).
C. "I'LL GET YOU THERE. I WON'T STAND AT THE DOOR." You wait at the corner while he knocks. Priced and said: 18 batteries; Lucero: "Then you're a driver, not a guard."

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
He comes back: the house's board posts better escort work; the co-op remembers the company's face at the corner (C) or at the door (A, B). THE COLLISION: if the player also holds QD-AE08, the thief's family is on Lucero's list; AE08's offer said so before the yes (see AE08). Declining leaves nothing.
