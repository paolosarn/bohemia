## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a bust year inside the clawing back. The depot runs the valley's parts exchange: every pump coupling, fuse and breaker is logged, lent and taken back. A brass pump coupling went out on loan to Arden Street and never came back. The depot wants it, or the man who signed for it.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: THE DEPOT, an old bus garage. In the settlement view: bays of parts on shelves, a market counter, a recruit bench under a timetable board, and the office.
- BUILDING: the office, drawn like any office: a clerk at a desk, a clipboard, a wall of signed loan slips, one slip in red (PL14). Tap the clerk.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Señorita Ines Quan, the clerk, twenties, a sharp grey Balenciaga-style tailored coat with the depot's patch sewn on crooked, a pencil behind one ear.
- THE SCREEN:
  1. "I need a pump coupling back from Arden Street. It pays {fee}." (FL02, the job first)
  2. "Signed out to a Julio Varga. Six weeks late."
  3. "Bring the part, or bring the man and he can explain it to my boss."
  4. "Half up front if you want. It's policy."
- PAY: K x 2 days + R1 (Julio's street has people who will stand with him) + S (bust). Batteries.
- NEGOTIATION: ASK FOR MORE: one step, then "That's the schedule. I don't set it." (bust, low ceiling). ADVANCE: half, by policy, before you ask; a debt if you drop. ASK FOR IT IN KIND: TAPE (a fuse of your choice from the shelf, the depot's repair stock) instead of batteries, at the depot's price (rule 47). Third push: "Next company." Nothing written.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE: accept, Arden Street is on the map. Decline: "Okay." She stamps the next slip. Nothing written.

## THE CHOICES
On Arden Street the coupling is in the ground, holding the block's only pump together. Fourteen houses drink from it. Julio says it flat: "Take it and they carry water from the canal." Three neighbours stand in the street. The twist: the kind way through costs your own batteries.
1. **Take the coupling.** The neighbours step aside; nobody fights a company over brass. DONE; the fee. The pump stops.
2. **Bring Julio.** He comes; the coupling stays in the ground; the depot's boss fines him. DONE; the same fee.
3. **Buy it for them.** Pay the depot the coupling's price out of your own purse, and the clerk marks the slip closed. DONE; the same fee, minus what you paid. You are simply poorer, and the pump runs.
The "I won't do this part" choice is (2): you finish, and Julio carries the price, as stated on the screen ("or bring the man").

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Declined: nothing.
- (1): "took Arden Street's coupling". Next visit, a line of people with jugs walks the canal road on the map.
- (2): "brought Julio in". Julio's fine is a debt in his name, not yours.
- (3): "paid for Arden Street's coupling". The pump runs; nobody on Arden Street mentions it, but the depot clerk's slip wall shows it closed in your company's name.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The red slip on the office wall is signed in Julio's hand, and dated two weeks before the coupling went missing.
