## THE SITUATION
Act 2 in a boom: generators are selling faster than they can be found. A dealer needs one carried to a clinic, and he
pays part up front because the road is long. The generator was taken from a school two streets from the market. The
player learns that halfway, from a person, not a screen.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Toño Villalobos, dealer, oversized leather bomber worn off one shoulder, sunglasses at night, sitting on the
generator itself.
"Seis baterías to walk this to the clinica on Maryland. Dos now, cuatro when la doctora signs. It's heavy, it's
worth a lot, and people will look at you."

## THE CHOICES
1. **"No."** "Ándale, next." COST: nothing; he carries it himself or finds another back.
2. **"Deal."** TAKEN; two batteries in hand. Halfway, at a stop on the map, a girl of about ten stands on a porch and
   says plainly: "That's ours. That's from the school." Now the player is inside a taken contract, and the three
   ways out are all in the world:
   a. **Finish.** Deliver; the doctor signs; four more batteries. The school stays dark. The deed is "delivered
      Toño's generator", nothing more.
   b. **Return it to the school, then tell Toño to his face.** DROPPED. The two-battery advance is now a DEBT owed to
      a named man. Toño stops offering. The market hears it within the week. The school's lights come on, a world
      event with no player name on it.
   c. **Return it to the school and tell Toño raiders took it.** DROPPED, and a lie: two entries (told: raiders;
      happened: the school). The advance is still owed, but Toño believes the player. If Toño ever walks past that
      school at night, the gap can surface.
   d. **Finish, and tell the doctor whose it was** (QR-AC, lines 21 and 30; `Q130.X9`, `Q108.X2`). Deliver; la doctora signs; four more batteries, the same as a. She runs a cable to the school two nights a week off the clinic's bank. The kind finish pays the full fee, so b and c cost only because they break a taken contract, never because they are kind.
   COST in every case: a map day and the carry. The drop costs the advance as debt, a closed door, and a rumour.
   One drop is a story, never a blacklist (C13).

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Declined: nothing.
- Finished: a deed; the girl on the porch is still there next time, and she does not speak.
- Dropped: a deed with a battery debt, the told/happened pair if the player lied, and a count toward "strangers cite
  it" at three drops.

THE LIE HAS A TELL (QR-AC, line 1; `Q128.X2`): if Toño ever learns the school story, the player meets it at the market: Toño at the stall with his sunglasses off, offering nothing.
