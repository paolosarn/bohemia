## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a boom on the Strip. The house runs a private table for the families who hold the dam's share. Tonight the table is worth more than the house can afford to lose, and the house's own guards have cousins at it.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the Strip casino block. Buildings: the hall (the cage), the floor, the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Miss Odalys, floor manager, a sequinned Balenciaga gown worn over a bulletproof vest, flats.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "One table, one night, third floor." / "Stand where they can see you. That's half the job." / "Somebody at that table cheats. I want to know who, not to see blood on my felt."
- PAY: 20 batteries and 4 ROUNDS. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (23); ceiling: "The house likes you. Don't make it count." Advance: 4 ROUNDS.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the house hires another crew.
- THE COMPANY'S LINE (only if THE NAME AT THE GATE is the open ambition): the senior member: "Every guard on this street walks past that table. Let them see us."

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Odalys, counting chips without looking: "The guards will remember your faces. Make sure it's for the right thing."

## THE CHOICES
The cheat is a dam family's nephew, palming chips with a mirrored ring.
A. TELL ODALYS QUIETLY. She settles it with the family at dawn. Full pay; the house's guards learn the company's name as the ones who saw it and said nothing out loud.
B. CALL IT AT THE TABLE. The nephew's bodyguards draw: the fight board is the casino floor block, routine, 2 to 3 minutes, slot machines as cover. Full pay, no more; the dam family remembers it.
C. "I'LL STAND HERE. I WON'T NAME ANYONE." Stand all night; the cheat wins. Priced and said: 12 batteries; Odalys: "Then you're furniture. Good furniture."

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A or B: the Strip's gate guards greet the company by name from the next visit (the ambition's step; their line changes). B also costs standing with the dam family. C: nothing but the pay. If no ambition is open, the guards still learn the name; it just is not a goal. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The mirrored ring shows the table one second late, and in it the company's faces are still sitting down.
