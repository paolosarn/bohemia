## THE SITUATION
The transformer hums on the corner lot. It has headroom for one line. The chapel clinic wants it for the cold room. The co-op's own racks want it for the grow lights. The Strip's charge room wants it and pays the most. Three offers sit on the hall's board, each a face. One contract at a time: take one, and the other two leave when the line is spoken for.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the co-op blocks. Buildings: the hall (the laundry room with a corkboard), the market, THE TRANSFORMER (the family's, with a switch box and three cable ends on the ground).
- OFFER 1, CLIENT: Sister Amparo, the clinic. "Insulin sits in a box that is warm. You know what that means." PAY: 14 batteries.
- OFFER 2, CLIENT: Beto Salas, co-op rack boss. "We feed this block. With lights we feed two." PAY: 12 batteries and food for the company for 10 days.
- OFFER 3, CLIENT: Mr. Lucero, the Strip. "I pay in batteries. Real ones. Thirty." PAY: 30 batteries.
- SKULLS: 1 each (the job is laying cable; copper thieves work the line).
- NEGOTIATION: each face has one "more" and a spoken ceiling. No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining all three writes nothing: the transformer feeds the co-op blocks only.
