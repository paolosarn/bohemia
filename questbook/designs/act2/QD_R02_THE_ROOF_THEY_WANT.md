## THE SITUATION
THE TAKE CONTRACT, the other hall. In a boom, power is what everyone fights over, and the tower's roof array sells the Cartel its own power back. The Cartel will not do its own assault (it needs the Reds' trade next year). It hires a company. The people inside the tower are ordinary: two floors of families, a school timetable taped to a door.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the cartel yards, container stacks, a forklift idling. Buildings: the hall (a shipping office), the market, the barber, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the hall, second of three portraits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: TEODORO VAZ, quartermaster, fifties, a Balenciaga puffer zipped to the chin in the heat.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "The tower on Paradise has the only real roof on the east side." / "The Reds sit on it and sell us our own light." / "Take the roof in five days. Keep the building standing. What you do inside is yours."
- DANGER: three skulls.
- PAY: 14 batteries. NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 16, and his face closes ("Sixteen is the number."); a third ask ends the offer and costs standing with the yards. ADVANCE: up to 4.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing. The yards post it again next refresh or hire someone the map can see.

## THE CHOICES
- A. STORM IT. One fight on the tower's fight board (a tough fight, 8 to 15 minutes, never past 15): the lobby, the stair core, the roof, each a house-sized tile. The roof is the high ground and the goal; a panel row can collapse under fire and stop being high ground. The families' floors are tiles on the board; a lane to the back street stays open for them the whole fight. Win: the roof held at the end.
- B. CUT THE WATER (the siege by waiting). Close the tower's water line at the pump for four map days. Nobody is fought. The tower opens its door on the fifth. Pays the same as A. The families were thirsty for four days, and the feed says so.
- C. OPEN ON TERMS (the talk option). The Reds' roof keeper wants out. He opens the stair if his floor walks out with what they carry. Costs 3 of the fee, paid to him.
At the end of any route: KEEP IT WORKING (TAKEN: the array powers the Cartel) or BREAK THE PANELS (RUINED). Same pay either way.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Declining leaves nothing. Taken: a deed for the company; the tower's state (TAKEN by the Cartel, or RUINED); the Cartel's favour up and the Reds' wrath up as separate numbers. The tower's board changes clients and pays in Cartel terms, or goes dark. Act 3 derives it signed: a ruined tower is a squatted shell; a taken one is a Cartel power station whose hall remembers who opened the stair (route C) or who cut the water (route B). Struck-down members roll rule 36; the main character never dies.
