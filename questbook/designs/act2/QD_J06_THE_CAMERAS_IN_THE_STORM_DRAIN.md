## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a bust stretch. The storm drains under the Strip are where the Homeless live, and the tunnels are the
valley's one road nothing records (the canon blind spot: face to face, tunnel talk). Someone has been wiring small
cameras along the east drain, a few every night, by a crew in clean hi-vis who say they are "maintenance." The
tunnel keeper wants them down. The canon's threat logic puts the tunnels on the trigger list: going near them is
looking. The screen says so plainly, as a danger, in the keeper's words.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the tunnel camp, reached on the map by a culvert mouth. Its screen: a junction chamber with a
  folding-table market (batteries cheap, food dear), a fire where people talk (rumours), and the HALL, which is
  a card table under one bulb where the keeper sits.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: the tunnel keeper, a woman near seventy, a quilted Rick Owens hood, a ring of keys on a
  bootlace.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES:
  1. "Somebody's putting eyes in the east drain. I want them out."
  2. "Four cells and a key to the east tunnels. That's what we have."
  3. "Down there isn't safe. For you, I mean. Not for us."
- PAY: 4 batteries and a key (the key opens a tunnel route across the map, faster than the dirt road above).
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more (she has no more: "Then don't."). Ask for an advance: the key now, the cells after.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The keeper, dealing solitaire under the bulb: "You came in through the front. Nobody comes in through the front."

## THE CHOICES (inside the taken contract)
1. TAKE THEM DOWN. One block of drain, twelve cameras. Ways through: go at the crew's shift change and unhook them
   quietly; drive the crew off (a group; rule 36 applies; they fight only if cornered); or offer the crew better
   paid work up top, which the keeper's nephew can arrange. Full pay. Heat rises one step.
2. TURN THEM TO THE WALL. Cameras stay up, facing brick. Faster, safer. She pays 2, as she tells you before you go,
   because "they'll notice in ten days." Heat rises one step.
3. I WON'T GO PAST THE GRATE (the priced refusal). You give the key back; she pays you 1 cell for coming down.
   Finished, not dropped. Heat does not rise.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries; the key (a map route); the keeper's standing.
- The east drain's state: dark (1), blinded for ten map days (2), watched (3 or never taken). The settlement screen
  shows it: the keeper's fire moves into the east drain when it is dark.
- The unrecorded ledger: the east drain is a road the whisper network can use (QD-H04 may name it).
- Declining leaves nothing. The cameras keep going up; the keeper keeps dealing solitaire.

## UNDER LIBERATE, RESPECT, BECOME (the ending's one line for this row)
- LIBERATE: "The east drain. Nobody filmed what went through it that night. That was you."
- RESPECT: the drain is kept dark by agreement, the one place in the valley nothing watches, and the keeper's
  granddaughter keeps the key.
- BECOME: the steward-heir knows every blind spot in the valley, because the family made some of them. In the
  epilogue the cameras go back up in the east drain, for everyone's safety, and they are kinder cameras.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The cameras' cables end in the drain water, connected to nothing, and their red lights are on.
