# QD-M04: THE BEDROLL NOBODY ROLLS UP

ACT: 2
KIND: event
CRISIS: none (fires in any crisis)
ECONOMY: either
PLACE: the first map camp after a fight in which a company member died (rule 36's 20% roll on a struck-down member); anywhere on the map.
STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35)

GRIEF AFTER A DEATH, shaped as a camp EVENT SCREEN. A template: it fires for ANY company member who dies, and reads his ledger. Filed in act 2 as the first shelf copy; the same screen serves all three acts.

## THE SITUATION
A member was struck down and the 20% came up. The fight ended. There was no loot screen for him, ever (`Q125.X8`). At the first camp, the company has not rolled up his bedroll. The screen does not fire in the fight and does not fire on the map mid-travel: it waits for the fire, because grief needs a place (`Q001.W6`).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The event screen's face is the member who stood NEAREST to him in the formation when he fell (the fight decides who speaks). The first line is THE RECEIPT: it names the cause, in that member's words, from the fight's own log (`Q137.W1`).
Template: "He held the [doorway]. I said the [left] was clear. It wasn't."
Example, filled: "He held the stairwell. I said the roof was clear. It wasn't."
If nobody stood near him: "Nobody was next to him. That's on all of us." (said by the member with the most days in the company).

## THE CHOICES
A. BURY HIM HERE. Half a map day. A grave becomes a permanent prop on this road on the map, with his name. His gear goes in with him.
B. CARRY HIM HOME. Only offered if his settlement is within three stops. One or two map days, wrapped in the truck. His people receive him; they keep his gear. The company is slower on the road while carrying him (duration as grief, `Q001.W6`).
C. HAND HIS THINGS ON. The speaker asks for one thing of his by name ("his jacket"); the rest is buried with him by the road. No time spent. The speaker wears it from then on, visible on her portrait.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Every branch: PEOPLE writes the receipt line, the fight, the formation tile, the date. Within two map stops or one city-view open, the world shows it (QR-G checklist line 1): a feed post from his settlement, or a person at his settlement who asks the company if it is true. Never a banner.
A: his grave on the road; the company's road line when passing it, once per pass, never repeated in the same act.
B: his settlement's hall offers the company nothing extra (no reward for grief, `Q125.X9`); his mother stands at the hall door on the next visit and says one line.
C: the jacket is on the speaker in every fight after; if she is struck down later, her receipt names it.
For ten map days after, the member who spoke fights one tile further back than her gambit says, and says why once: "Not today." (grief shown as behaviour, `Q041.W3`, bounded so it never spirals into busywork, `Q041.X2`).

## THE NARRATOR
Read over the event screen in the machine voice (rule 45: too even, tape and room, a little wrong; never in the first minute; people on the screen still speak in the squiggle): "His bedroll is where he left it. Nobody has asked it to move."

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
That night in the city view, the feed shows a new post from his account: a photo of the camp, taken from where his bedroll lies.
