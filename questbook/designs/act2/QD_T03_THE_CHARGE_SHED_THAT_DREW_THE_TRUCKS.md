## THE SITUATION
The shed changed the field. Three trucks idle at the pylons where there used to be none. A caravan master who never stopped here has a cup of coffee at the hall. The co-op two stops away is short of cells, and the shed has more than the field can use. That is a board that did not exist a month ago.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the solar field. Buildings: the hall (a shipping container with a window cut in it), the market, the barber, THE SHED (the family's, lit, humming).
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Osvaldo Reyes, caravan master, dust goggles pushed up, a Balenciaga parka with the sleeves cut off.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Your shed's the only honest plug between here and the lake." / "Twenty cells to the co-op. They pay me, I pay you." / "The Wash road is quicker. It's also the Wash."
- PAY: 22 batteries. SKULLS: 1 on the road, 2 through the wash (the screen says both).
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 25, then the ceiling ("I have trucks to feed too"). Ask for payment per cell delivered instead: the payment mode is the player's bet on his own company.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; Osvaldo finds another crew and the cells arrive later.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Osvaldo, not looking up from the coffee: "You built that? Good. Somebody had to."

## THE CHOICES
A. THE ROAD. Four map days, a routine fight at the overpass with cell thieves, 2 to 4 minutes on the fight board.
B. THE WASH. Two map days, the party's ground: a tough fight, 8 to 12 minutes, three house tiles of dry culvert.
C. TWO RUNS. Split the load, six map days, no fight; the co-op's cell price stays high three days longer (its people pay that, and the next board there says so).
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Delivered: the co-op's cell price falls one; Osvaldo returns as a client while the shed stands. The shed itself is now a reason to raid: the field draws a raid-expected situation one time in four (TUNING's number, QR-S rule 11). If the shed is ever raided, Osvaldo's offers leave with it. Declining leaves nothing.
