## THE SITUATION
Act 2. Abuelo Chuy fought in the first company in act 1 and is now old in act 2 (followers and fighters age between acts only, rule 84). He kept a pay book: every fight, every day, every battery he was owed by the faction the company fought for in act 1. That faction now runs Henderson and has a paymaster. The book says they owe him 41 batteries.

## THE PERSON, THE PLACE, THE THING (never a card)

Their paymaster sits in the old bank in Henderson." / CHUY: "Come stand behind me." ## THE CHOICES
A. READ IT ALOUD: Chuy reads the book line by line at the counter; the paymaster pays 20 'as a gift' to stop him reading. A queue of other old fighters forms behind him.
B. FIND THE OTHER BOOK: the paymaster says "our book says paid," and that is the pointer; the clerk who signed it works the next window in the same bank. One talk at her window, the same leg; she admits it; 41 paid. (QR-AV, lines 8 and 29: it was a two-leg talk and nobody said the other book existed, `Q095.X2`, `Q155.X1`, `Q155.P2`.)
C. TAKE THE PENSION: the faction offers a pension in place of the debt: 1 battery a week for life, no lump. Chuy takes it; he still pays the company its 6.
D. NOT BEHIND HIM (QR-AV, line 12: the old D was the no moved after the yes, a drop with no price, `Q131.X7`, `Q139.P2`): you walk him to the bank door and will not go in. He goes to the counter alone; the paymaster pays him 8 to stop coming back and he takes it; he pays you 3 of the 6, and the book stays open. In act 3 his grandson (a recruit) brings it to the counter.
IF A FIGHT HAPPENS AND IS LOST: no fight is planned; the paymaster's guards are a wall, not a fight. If the player starts one, the scene's first line says so, and losing means Chuy is barred from Henderson.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36); a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10% (the third votes). Followers never fight.
