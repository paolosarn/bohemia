## THE SITUATION
Neri kept the list of which co-op families lose their rooms next. Two people wanted her dead: Fabian, a squatter whose mother is at the top of the list, who shouted at her in public, and Mrs. Aldana, the towers' own collections chief, who needed the list to say something it did not. The towers' guards logged the body and went to lunch.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Fabian, cornered at the squat, before anyone asks: "I yelled at her. I didn't touch her. Ask me what I saw on the stairs."

## THE CHOICES
A. NAME FABIAN. Easy: the whole base already says it. The towers hang the case on him; the squat's board thins. Six days later Mrs. Aldana's list takes the next name, and that family's clerk is found in the same stairwell.
B. NAME ALDANA. Fabian saw her on the stairs, and her ledger has a torn page. The towers do nothing to her (being right is not power); Alma posts the name on the co-op's board. A routine fight when Aldana's guards come for the post, 2 to 4 minutes on the fight board.
C. BRING ALMA BOTH AND LET HER NAME ONE. The fee is paid for the evidence. The sim picks what Alma believes from what was found.
Same pay on every road. The case can be solved wrong, and wrong costs someone.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER

C: whatever Alma believes, the co-op carries for a generation: in act 3 a stairwell is named for Neri, or for nobody. Declining leaves nothing.
