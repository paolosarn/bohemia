## THE SITUATION
The recyclers mine the landfill for copper and glass. The hyenas mine it for everything else. Two pickers were taken this month, and what came back of them was bone cracked open lengthwise, which no dog can do.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the recyclers' yard. Buildings: the hall (the weigh station, the scale still reads 0.4 kg with nothing on it), the market (copper), SCAVENGE.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Marisol Quintero, the yard's forewoman, a welder's apron over a cropped Balenciaga jacket gone grey.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Hyenas. From Arizona, they say. They laugh all night." / "They bit through Tomas's plate like it was a lunch tray." / "The big female never comes out first. Kill her and they go."
- PAY: 14 batteries and 4 TAPE (the yard's own repair stock). SKULLS: 2.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (16 and 4); ceiling: "The tape is my crew's tape. Don't ask for more of it." Advance: 4 TAPE up front, shown as 4 less at the end.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Marisol, on the offer screen, holding up a chest plate with a half-moon bitten out of it: "This was the good one."

## THE SIGN AND THE LAIR
Two pointers: her line about the female, and hyena tracks that circle the yard's fence every night on the map. The lair: a trash ridge with a hollow dug under a sofa. Two quiet beats. Then the laughing.

## THE CHOICES
A. FIGHT IN PLATE. Armour protects for one hit; each hyena bite SHREDS a piece (the tell: the bitten man's armour icon cracks). Wounded hyenas flee a tile and come back two beats later. Kill the matriarch and the clan scatters. Costs 3 to 6 TAPE; pays 4 back.
B. FIGHT LIGHT AND FAST. Leave the plate at the yard; the company moves first and reaches the female behind the ridge. Less TAPE lost, more men struck down (each at the 20% roll).
C. MOVE THE YARD'S FEED. The hyenas come for the pickers' food scraps. Pay 3 FOOD to leave a carcass on the far side of the landfill each week. No fight; the full fee ("They're not eating my pickers. That was the job."), and the 3 FOOD a week is the company's own price for the kinder road (QR-AO, line 30; the house rule's reading a); the clan stays, fed, on the map.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A or B: the yard's TAPE price drops a step for the act. C: the clan grows; in act 3 the recyclers keep hyenas as guards (the keepers, round three s2). Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
The laughing stops, all of it, at exactly the same second, every night.
