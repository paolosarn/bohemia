## THE CHOICES
A. DO WHAT THE VOICE SAID: take the keys to Garza. He pays. He asks who told you. If you say 'the driver', he goes pale and pays anyway.
B. ASK THE WIDOW: the driver's widow lives two blocks from Garza; she says the truck was his and the debt was his; she gives you a second set of keys and wants half of Garza's pay for her kids. It pays the company the same.
C. TURN THE RADIO OFF: smash the CB or pull the antenna; the keys stay in the glovebox; the debt stays unpaid; the voice never speaks again.
D. LEAVE IT ON AND SAY NOTHING (`Q131.X7`): the voice repeats the ask once a week, the same words, the same pauses; it never changes and never threatens.
IF A FIGHT HAPPENS AND IS LOST: no fight is planned. Garza's yard has two dogs and a fence; a fight there is the player's choice and losing means Garza keeps the truck and pays nothing.
Fights are on house tiles; routine 2 to 4 minutes, tough never past 15. A member struck down: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt (rule 36); a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10% (the third votes). Followers never fight.
