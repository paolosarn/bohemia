## THE SITUATION
Late act 2, a bust stretch, while the whisper broadcast is being prepared somewhere else on the map and every spare
watt in the valley is being pulled toward it. A brownout drops the line as the party passes the pump house (never
full dark: the pumps slow, the lights go brown). The pump feeds two things downstream, the hydroponic shed and a
clinic, and at brownout power it can hold one.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The operator, an old man with a headlamp and a Bottega woven vest gone grey, one hand on a breaker.
First line: "Pick one. I've been picking all week, and I'm tired of it being me."

## THE CHOICES
1. LEND YOUR BATTERIES. Costs 6 batteries (scaled to what the company carries, never less than a painful share).
   Both stay up until the grid comes back. Standing with the faction running the line rises. The broadcast
   loses a sliver of power (its ledger notes it; nothing is said).
2. PICK FOR HIM. Costs nothing but the choice. Clinic or shed. Whichever loses has a cost that shows up later:
   clinic dark means a named patient on the feed; shed dark means a food price rise in the nearest town the next
   time you arrive.
3. KEEP MOVING. He picks. The player never learns which until the feed says.

One warm beat (QR-AC, line 26; `Q030.X2`): if the company lends the batteries, he laughs once, the first time all week, and turns his headlamp off to save it.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries (choice 1).
- Standing with the faction holding that line.
- The grid ledger: which load held.
- The valley's food price or a person's health, one trip later.
- The broadcast's power ledger (choice 1), a small debt the reckoning reads.

FLAGS AND SAVES (QR-AC, line 28; `Q123.X1`, `Q116.X1`, `Q040.X3`): writes brownout_pump = lent | clinic | shed | his_pick. A play-forward test sets each value and walks to every place that reads it (the feed's named patient or the town's food price one trip later, the broadcast power ledger); the save lands only at clean states: before the event screen opens and after it closes; never mid-choice and never mid-fight.

## THE NARRATOR
Read over the event screen in the machine voice (rule 45: too even, tape and room, a little wrong; never in the first minute; people on the screen still speak in the squiggle): "The pump can hold one of two things. It has held both for as long as it could."
