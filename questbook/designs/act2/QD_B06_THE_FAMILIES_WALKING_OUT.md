## THE SITUATION
Late act 2. A rumour that something is going to be dropped on the valley has reached one neighbourhood (it is the
earth-side nuke, and the rumour is half right). Thirty people on foot, with carts, are walking out into the desert
toward nothing. They are right about the risk and wrong about the answer: the walk kills more of them than staying.
The party meets them head-on.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The woman who speaks for them, fifties, a runway overcoat worn inside out so the lining shows, a child's hand in hers.
First line: "They say it's coming here. You're driving into it. Why?"

## THE CHOICES
1. GIVE THEM WATER AND BATTERIES FOR THE ROAD. Costs 4 batteries and 4 WATER (rule 47). They keep walking. Some make it;
   the feed later names how many.
2. TELL THEM WHAT YOU KNOW. Costs an hour and depends on what the player really knows (a deed flag from the main
   line). If you know the nuke can be stopped, you can say so; half turn back, half do not believe you. If you do
   not know, you can only guess, and they hear it.
3. KEEP MOVING. They watch you go the other way.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
