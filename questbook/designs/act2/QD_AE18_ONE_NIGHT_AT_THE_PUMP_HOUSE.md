## THE SITUATION
Act 2. The company owes the pump people for water drawn on credit through a dry season. A roaming party has told the pump house it will come on the night of the new moon. The pump people would rather have the company on the roof than the batteries in the drawer.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the pump house. Buildings: the hall (pump room door), the tank, the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Teófilo's daughter Amparo, now foreman, his valve key on her neck, a Rick Owens leather that was his.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "New moon. They said so. They want the pumps." / "Hold the roof one night and the book is clean." / "If you don't owe us, I'll pay you in water. Same job."
- PAY: the company's debt struck from the book (shown as its battery amount), and 6 WATER. If no debt: 20 batteries and 6 WATER. SKULLS: 2. The night is fixed: said aloud, shown on the phone.
- NEGOTIATION: none on the debt ("It's the book. The book doesn't haggle."); one "more" on the water (8).
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing: the debt stays a debt, no worse.
- THE COMPANY'S LINE (only if PAY THE LOAN OFF is the open ambition): the senior member: "One night and we don't owe nobody. I'd stand on a roof for that."

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Amparo, a hand on the valve wheel: "My father lent you that water. He'd want it paid like this. Not in batteries. In standing here."

## THE CHOICES
A. THE ROOF. The party (eight) comes at the tank at midnight: the fight board is the pump house block, the roof is high ground (accuracy), tough, 8 to 12 minutes.
B. THE ROAD. Meet them at the dam road bend before they reach the tank: a road event, three lines, then a routine fight, 3 to 4 minutes, if the talk fails.
C. "I'LL HOLD THE DOOR, NOT THE TANK." Keep the people safe inside and let the party drain some water. Priced and said: half the debt struck; Amparo: "Half a night, half a book."

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Held: the book is clean (the ambition's condition); Amparo's face greets the company at the pump house from then on; the dam road draws safer for a season. C: half the debt remains, and the tank's level on the map is lower. A struck-down company member is 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days. Declining leaves nothing.
