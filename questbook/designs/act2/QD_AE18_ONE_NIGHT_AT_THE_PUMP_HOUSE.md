## THE SITUATION
Act 2. The company owes the pump people for water drawn on credit through a dry season. A roaming party has told the pump house it will come on the night of the new moon. The pump people would rather have the company on the roof than the batteries in the drawer.

## THE CHOICES
A. THE ROOF. The party (eight) comes at the tank at midnight: the fight board is the pump house block, the roof is high ground (accuracy), tough, 8 to 12 minutes.
B. THE ROAD. Meet them at the dam road bend before they reach the tank: a road event, three lines, then a routine fight, 3 to 4 minutes, if the talk fails.
C. "I'LL HOLD THE DOOR, NOT THE TANK." Keep the people safe inside and let the party drain some water. Priced and said: half the debt struck; Amparo: "Half a night, half a book."
