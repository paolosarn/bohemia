## THE SITUATION
Wherever the company fights, a bear comes after. The medics noticed first: their gravediggers go out to bury the dead of every skirmish on the east side and find the graves already open. The bear is 900 kilos and has a nose that reads a battlefield from miles away. Lately it has been reading the company's.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the hospital. Buildings: the hall (the triage desk), the clinic (MEDS), the chapel.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Dr. Anwen Hale, head of the medics, scrubs under a Bottega leather coat, gloves on even at the desk.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "It follows fights. Yours, mostly. I'm not blaming you. I'm telling you." / "We shot it. It walked off. We shot it the next day in the same place. No hole." / "You have to finish it at once."
- PAY: 30 batteries and 4 MEDS. SKULLS: 3.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (34); ceiling: "The MEDS are the ones we'd use on you. Think about that." Advance: 2 MEDS.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the bear keeps coming to battlefields.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Dr. Hale, on the offer screen: "You left four on the Boulder road last week. It had them by morning."

## THE SIGN AND THE LAIR
The sign is on the map and it is the company's own: every fight marker the company left behind has a drag line leading away from it, long, toward the same wash. The lair is the last battlefield; the bear will come to fresh dead.

## THE CHOICES
A. BAIT IT WITH YOUR OWN FIGHT. Leave the dead of your next fight unburied and wait on that board. It comes. The tell: its wounds close at the start of every turn it was not hit hard. Focus every gun on it in one turn (the dial shot helps) or it heals; it grabs one man and throws him a tile. Pay. The pelt hangs in the hospital's chapel, over the graves it opened (QR-AO, line 30). The medics note what you did with your dead.
B. BAIT IT WITH MEAT. Leave 6 FOOD instead of bodies. Same fight; the medics say nothing.
C. BURY EVERY DEAD FROM NOW. For the rest of the act, the company spends half a day after every fight burying. The bear finds nothing and leaves the east side. The full fee at the act's end ("It left. That was the job."); the half day after every fight is the company's own price (QR-AO, line 30; the house rule's reading a).

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: the medics' standing drops a step (they saw); the east side's graveyards stay closed. B: neutral. C: the company's burial habit is a trait the heirs inherit (they bury their dead). Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
It never eats the company's dead. It only moves them, and lays them in a line.
