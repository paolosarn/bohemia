## THE SITUATION
Shaped from the story master's act 2 end: the whisper broadcast goes out, the family stops the
earth-side nuke, and the super-captcha severs the reach. Canon (threat-logic addendum) says the
finale is "truth released simultaneously," which traps the machine by its own secrecy, and Dubai is
the precedent for what it does when a secret spreads. HOW the broadcast is sent, how the nuke is
stopped and how the super-captcha works are NOT written and stay Paolo's. So this beat is shaped as
the one thing the library says makes such a night land: a CASTING. The mission is fixed; the player's
authorship is WHO carries the truth to which faction, who holds the pump house, and who goes with the
heir to the nuke. Every name on the list is a hand from QD-H03 and the contracts behind it.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The act 2 heir's closest companion (a person the player chose; identity Paolo's), at the pump house
door with the list of hands written on the inside of a cigarette carton, not on a phone. First line:
"Everybody on here trusts one of us. Nobody on here trusts all of us. So who goes where?"

## THE CHOICES
The player assigns names to three posts. Each post shows, in the companion's words, who that person
trusts and why (the contract that made them a hand). Then one final choice:
1. SEND EVERY HAND AT ONCE. Costs: every carrier is exposed the same night; some do not come back,
   and each loss names its cause ("she went to the Cartel alone; you never did a job for them").
   Gain: the fastest, widest spread; the canon finale shape.
2. KEEP ONE HAND HOME. Costs: one faction hears the truth last, and the heir's own post is weaker.
   Gain: one named person survives for certain, by staying out of it.
3. GO YOURSELF TO THE HARDEST DOOR. Costs: the heir is at the most dangerous post and cannot be at
   the nuke; the companion goes there instead. Gain: the faction that trusts no one hears it from a
   face.

One warm beat (QR-AC, line 26; `Q030.X2`): at the bottom of the carton the companion has written his own name, crossed it out, and written it again.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Who carried what, who lived, which factions heard first. This roll call decides the act 3 path
(conversion or war, per the moonshot law) and which stances stay reachable. Contracts that were
declined in any act simply are not on the carton.

THE FLOOR (QR-AC, lines 5 and 18; `Q022.X2`): if act 3 is opened before this night, the act 3 path reads as unset and takes the moonshot law's default; the carton is blank and nobody in act 3 names a carrier.

FLAGS AND SAVES (QR-AC, line 28; `Q123.X1`, `Q116.X1`, `Q040.X3`): writes carriers = post by post; casting = all_at_once | one_home | heir_hardest; lost = names. A play-forward test sets each value and walks to every place that reads it (the act 3 path, which stances stay reachable, every faction's first hearing); the save lands only at clean states: before the beat and after its choice is written; never mid-fight and never mid-screen.
