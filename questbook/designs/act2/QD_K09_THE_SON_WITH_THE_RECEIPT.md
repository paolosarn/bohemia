## THE SITUATION
Act 2, the world clawing back, a boom. This event reads the DEED LEDGER, and only one thing on it: an act 1
contract that the Gen 1 ancestor TOOK and then DROPPED (declining never counts; only a taken-and-dropped contract is
a deed, rule 35c). The client's family survived and now runs a stall on a paved market road. The map has shown the
stall under the family's name since the act opened, so the player can see the bill coming and route around it
(`Q149.P4`: the world testifies first). In act 1 the drop had a witness, the client's son, a boy then, and the
player saw him watch (`Q086.X7` is the flaw this avoids).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The son, thirty now, a tailored Balenciaga work jacket, a receipt in a plastic sleeve.
First line: "My father paid your grandmother an advance. This is the paper."
(The act 1 offer screen had an advance on it; that advance is the number on the receipt, `Q148.W1`: the fee locked
before the truth, twenty years ago.)

## THE CHOICES (price on the button)
1. PAY IT BACK. Price: the act 1 advance at act 2 prices (the number is on the button: e.g. 4 batteries), and it
   scales with what the company carries, so it is never nothing. He tears the receipt.
2. HEAR HIM OUT. Price: an hour. He says what the drop cost: his father walked the job alone and came back without
   two fingers. Then he offers a way to square it. Hand-off in settlement terms: the next time the player opens the
   menu of his settlement, its HALL carries his contract offer screen at a fair fee, and taking it and finishing it
   clears the debt. Declining that offer writes nothing, like any contract.
3. KEEP MOVING. Price: nothing. He does not follow, he does not curse, and nothing new is written. The old deed
   stays exactly what it was.

A violent answer exists only on the fight board, never as an event button. If it happens there, rule 36 holds for
anyone struck down (20% dead, else out 30 to 40 days), and a lost fight continues the story (`Q087.W1`).

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries, or an hour and a contract in a hall, or nothing.
- Paid or squared: THE FLIP, in act 3 the stall is a shop with the family name, and its owner greets the Gen 3
  heir by name, in past tense about the grandmother (`Q144.W7`).
- Keep moving: in act 3 the stall is a shop and nobody greets the heir. Nothing worse.
- It fires ONCE per dropped contract. No hidden counter builds behind it (`Q118.X1`).
