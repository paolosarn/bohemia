## THE SITUATION
Act 2. Mireya is a veteran shooter whose hand has started to shake after about 30 fights. She still carries her scoped rifle and has not fired it in five fights. She wants to sell it to the right person, not the highest bidder.

## THE PERSON, THE PLACE, THE THING (never a card)

- THE WANT, ONE LINE: Mireya wants the rifle to keep doing what it did; she does not want to say she is done.
- PAY: A tenth of the sale (2 to 4 BATTERIES; the three offers are said aloud at the range). SKULLS: 0. Declining is free and writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
A. THE GIRL AT THE RANGE: a fourteen-year-old who shoots tin cans for the town watch; she offers 18 batteries. Mireya teaches her one afternoon.
B. THE WATCH CAPTAIN: offers 30 batteries; the rifle guards the town wall. Mireya is quiet about it.
C. KEEP IT, CHANGE THE JOB: Mireya becomes the company's spotter; she keeps the rifle on her back and never fires it; her reach line changes (TUNING's number) from shooter to spotter.
D. NOT NOW (`Q131.X7`): she keeps it; she shakes; nobody says anything; the job comes back next act from her heir if she dies with it.
