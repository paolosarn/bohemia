## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a bust. A company member was struck down in a fight and lived (rule 36: 20% dead, else 30 to 40 days
out). He has 34 days left on the injury, and the map shows the count on his portrait. He eats, he rides, he
cannot fight. Two others in the company disagree about him, and the event fires ONLY when the roster holds an
injured member with more than 20 days left AND a pair whose histories clash on it: one who lost a unit by carrying a
wounded man, one who was once left behind (`Q043.W3`: composition decides the drama). Both of them have a point
(`Q126.X5`).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Two portraits, two mouths. The first speaks, then the second (`Q043.W2`).
The first, a woman in a battered Bottega Veneta intrecciato vest: "He eats a cell a week and he can't hold a line.
The clinic's right there."
The second, a man with a scarf over a scar: "I got left at a clinic once. Nobody came back."
The injured man says nothing. He is awake.

## THE CHOICES (price on the button)
1. LEAVE HIM AT THE CLINIC. Price: 5 batteries up front for his care, and he is off the roster until his days run
   out. Hand-off in settlement terms: he appears in that settlement's CLINIC building on its menu, and when his days
   are done the company can pick him up there. If the company never comes back, he is still there; the clinic says
   so plainly when the menu opens.
2. HE RIDES WITH US. Price: the truck is slower on dirt while he is aboard (the map shows the new travel time), and
   his upkeep continues (1 FOOD a day and his MEDS, rule 47). The first speaker's morale drops; she says why, once.
3. SAY NOTHING, DRIVE ON (keep moving). Price: nothing. Nothing changes: he stays aboard, as he was. The pair stops
   talking about it. This event does not fire again for this injury.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
- Batteries, travel time, one member's morale, a man's place on the roster.
- Leave him: if the company collects him, the second speaker's line changes the next time he talks ("You came
  back."). If it never does, the clinic keeps him, and in act 3 (the flip) he is an old orderly at that clinic who
  does not bring it up. The result is always said out loud (`Q043.X3`).
- No narration grades it. The pair's lines are the only verdict, and they disagree.
