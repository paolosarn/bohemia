## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a bust. Somebody takes one ration a night from the line's back table. Always one. The line has started to turn on itself.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the co-op blocks. Buildings: the hall (the ration office), the courtyard, the barber.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Carmen Ibarra, ration clerk, a Rick Owens wrap cardigan, a stamp pad on a string.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "One ration a night. Somebody we know." / "Find them before the line does." / IF THE PLAYER HOLDS QD-AE07, a fourth line: "And the Strip man comes Tuesday. Everybody hides food on Tuesday. Think about that."
- PAY: 14 batteries and 10 FOOD. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (16); ceiling: "We pay you in our own dinner." Advance: 4 FOOD.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing; the line keeps watching itself.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Carmen, stamping a card hard: "I don't want them punished. I want it to stop before somebody gets hurt for it."

## THE CHOICES
The thief is Beto, eleven, from the third floor. His family is on the Strip's list; the ration pays Lucero's Tuesday.
A. HAND HIM TO CARMEN. The co-op pays; Beto is barred from the line a month. If the player escorts Lucero (AE07), he still knocks on that door.
B. PAY THE DEBT. 20 batteries from the company's own pocket to Lucero (the player must meet him: AE07 held, or the Strip's hall). Both contracts finish clean; it costs the most and pays nothing extra.
C. "I FOUND HIM. I WON'T NAME HIM." Tell Carmen it stops, and it does. Priced and said: half pay; Carmen: "Then you carry it, not me."

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A: Beto's family appears at the wash camp a season later. B: the third floor's door has a flower in a can next visit. C: the line calms and Carmen never asks again. If AE07 is held, the touch lands at one door both clients named, two stops from where it began. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Every stolen ration is found later, unopened, in a neat stack behind the bricked-up door on the third floor.
