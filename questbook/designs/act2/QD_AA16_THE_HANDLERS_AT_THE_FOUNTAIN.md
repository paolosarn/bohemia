## THE WALK

## THE CHOICES
A. FIGHT. The keepers loose the hyenas; the chief whistles and they re-target (the tell: the chief's hand goes up). Drop the chief and the hyenas stop and lie down. Pay; the hyenas are left chained to the fountain; the Strip takes them.
B. HIRE THEM. Pay Baba Tunde 10 of the 28 to join the company with one hyena: a HANDLER follower and a beast companion (the kennel's building feeds it: 1 FOOD a day). The desk pays in full ("They're gone from my fountain. That's the contract.").
C. FIND THE ONE THAT BIT. Walk the keepers' camp in the parking structure; the biter is a young hyena the chief cannot control. Put it down (routine, 2 minutes) or take it (a handler in the company can). The keepers stay; the desk pays half.
