## THE SITUATION
Shaped from the story master: act 2 ends with the whisper broadcast, truth released at once through trusted people, and the Amalgamation's blind spot is the unrecorded self. HOW the broadcast works is Paolo's. This beat is only the COUNT before it: the people who will carry it, face to face, in the one room nothing records. Who comes is derived from every contract and event the family did in acts 1 and 2 with a person, not a faction (QD-H03 is the first of them returning; this is all of them at once).

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The tunnel elder from QD-H01, much older: "Nobody writes anything down in here. So say your names out loud."

## THE CHOICES
1. CALL THE ROLL YOURSELF. Each person the ledgers bring says their name and one reason, read from what the family did for or with them. One named abstainer stays silent and gives a reason too.
2. LET THE ELDER CALL IT. Shorter; the same people; the player hears fewer reasons.
3. ASK THE ABSTAINER TO STAY. A short talk. If the family did right by them once, they speak. If not, they leave, and their district does not carry the message.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
The count is the size of the broadcast's reach, district by district. It is read from people the family met face to face, never from batteries or standing numbers. DEFAULT (few deeds, or act 1 not played): the room is small, the elder counts "the people who came anyway", and the broadcast still goes; it reaches fewer blocks, which the act 2 end shows. THE FUTURE GOES BOTH WAYS: a person from a base that fell sends a child instead, who says "for my mother".

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
When the roll is done, one more voice from the dark end of the tunnel says a name, and it is the name of the sibling on the ridge.
