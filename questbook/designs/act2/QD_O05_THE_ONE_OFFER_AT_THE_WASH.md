## THE SITUATION
A camp board shows one offer. The whisper broadcast is being prepared face to face, unrecorded, and the wash is
where people who cannot be tracked sleep. The one offer on this board is small and matters more than it pays.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the wash, cooking fires, a shopping cart of radios. Buildings: the hall (a tarp), SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the tarp, one portrait.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Lalo, a torn Margiela coat, headphones with no cord.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "My sister went up to the co-op for the broadcast people. Three days. Bring her back or bring
  me back what she said."
- PAY: 2 batteries. NEGOTIATION: none possible ("that's all of it").

## THE CHOICES
1. Find her at the co-op and walk her home.
2. Find her working with the broadcast people and bring Lalo her message instead.
3. Tell the co-op's guard she was asking: she is safe and kept, Lalo is paid by nobody.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Declining leaves nothing. Done either way: the wash camp's next board has one more offer (a town-camp edge). Choice
3 writes a deed on the ledger, not a flag on the wash.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Every radio in the cart is tuned to the same dead frequency, and the volume knobs are all turned up.
