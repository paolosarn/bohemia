## THE CHOICES
A. HIRE HIM. Mateo joins the company for the run and after. He fights well. Paz takes the pay and does not speak to the family again this act.
B. DO NOT HIRE HIM. The run goes without him. On the canal road he is there anyway, following on foot; the routine fight (2 to 4 minutes on the fight board) has him in it, unarmoured.
C. ONE RUN. The family's lead takes him for this run only and lets him see it, the canal, the thieves, what the job is. After the hand-in Mateo decides; the game does not.
Same pay on every road.
