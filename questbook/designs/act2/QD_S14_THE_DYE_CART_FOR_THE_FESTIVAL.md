## THE SITUATION
Act 2, and the colourful market has lasted ten years, which in this valley is a miracle. It wants a festival. A festival needs colour, and the season's dyes come up the south road on one cart driven by Paco, who is never late. Paco is two days late.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the colourful market, bunting over a car park. Buildings: the hall (a folding table under an umbrella), the barber, SCAVENGE.
- BUILDING: the umbrella. One portrait.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Pim, organiser, hands dyed blue to the wrist, a patchwork coat.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Ten years of market and no colour. Imagine a festival in grey." / "The dye cart is two days late. Paco drives it. Paco is never late. Paco is never anything." / "Find the cart. Bring the colour. We make it loud."
- PAY: 4 batteries, and the company's first dye at the barber is free. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: ask for more: 5 ("Five, and you're on the poster."). No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE CHOICES
On the south road the cart's tracks turn toward the Strip, not into a raider's wash. At the Strip, the cart is behind a casino, and Paco is holding hands with a croupier named Luz. He says it aloud: "The casino pays triple for colour. We're getting married."
A. BRING THE CART BACK. Paco comes, sulking. Full festival.
B. SPLIT THE LOAD. Half sold to the casino, half to the market. A smaller festival. Paco and Luz stay on the Strip.
C. BRING LUZ TOO. The festival gets a wedding. The casino is short a croupier and a load of colour, and says so on its next board.
Same pay on every road.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER

Declining leaves nothing; the festival happens in grey and is still a festival.
