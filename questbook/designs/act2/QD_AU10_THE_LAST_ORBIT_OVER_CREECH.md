## THE SITUATION
Act 2. Wren is a veteran who flew drones from a trailer at Creech before the base was abandoned. One aircraft was left in a loiter pattern when the crews walked out; it has been landing, recharging on a solar pad and taking off again on the same orbit for years. Its shadow crosses the north-west road twice a day. People in Indian Springs call it the angel. Wren wants to go into the trailer and land it for good.
