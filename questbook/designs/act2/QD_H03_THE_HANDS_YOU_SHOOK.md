## THE SITUATION
Shaped from the story master (act 2 ends with the whisper broadcast), GDD v2 (the whisper network
"built across three generations," truth "released simultaneously through every trusted faction")
and the GDD v4 two-ledger principle (what is done in trust is never seen by the machine). This beat
makes the network visible for the first time: a person the family once did a contract for, face to
face, comes back. The WHO is derived from the ledgers (the acts at once rule): the most recent
finished, in-person contract whose client is still alive in act 2, in act 1's heir or act 2's own
work. If there is nobody, the beat still plays: a stranger comes, sent by nobody, and the network
starts from one.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
The returning client, older now, dressed better than when the family met them. They wait until the
brownout takes the street lights. First line: "Not on the phone. Never on the phone. You did right by
my people once, so I'm going to tell you something."

## THE CHOICES
1. TALK HERE, IN THE DARK, NOW. Costs: nothing in batteries; one proximity-heat step (this is the
   first time act 2 LOOKS). Gain: one hand joins the whisper network, written UNRECORDED; the person
   names one more trusted person in another faction (a new contract offer appears in that place).
2. MEET THEM IN THE TUNNELS LATER. Costs: a day of travel, a battery for the walk, and the same heat
   step. Gain: the hand joins, and the tunnel people (if the act 1 family slept at their fire) join
   as a second hand, because they saw you come down to listen.
3. SEND THEM HOME. Costs: nothing, and nothing is lost: they still count as a hand when the
   broadcast comes, and they will come back once. The family just does not learn what they know yet.
   The main line waits.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
Hands in the unrecorded ledger (the count the act 2 finale reads). Heat. The new offer. Declined
contracts from any act are never a hand and never mentioned; only work done in person counts.

FLAGS AND SAVES (QR-AC, line 28; `Q123.X1`, `Q116.X1`, `Q040.X3`): writes hands = n, unrecorded; visitor = derived_client | stranger; met = here | tunnels | sent_home. A play-forward test sets each value and walks to every place that reads it (the act 2 finale's count, the new offer in another faction's place, the heat); the save lands only at clean states: before the beat and after its choice is written; never mid-fight and never mid-screen.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL

typed draft of everything the visitor said, unsent.
