## THE SITUATION
Act 2. The dam road needs hauling: panels and pump parts, too heavy for men. Camelops came up the US-95 from the lab years ago and breed on the flats. A string of camels would carry what the company cannot. The water board at the dam posts the contract; the company's own home base is where the corral would go.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the dam's water board home base. Buildings: the hall (a control room with one working dial), the market (WATER), the dock.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Engineer Hana Liu, the board's haul master, a hard hat over a silver Balenciaga puffer.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Camels. Real ones, the old kind, taller than a horse." / "Catch six, build them a corral, haul for me for the act." / "Don't shoot the big bull. He's the one they follow."
- PAY: 8 batteries a month for the act, and 20 WATER now. SKULLS: 1.
- NEGOTIATION: one "more" (10 a month); ceiling: "It's a salary. Salaries don't climb." Advance: 10 WATER.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
Hana, on the offer screen: "They drink once a week. Once. I want to be a camel."

## THE CHOICES
A. BUILD THE CORRAL FIRST. On a lot of the company's held base: 10 batteries, 2 days. Then ride out and catch six (a handler makes it a day, without one it is three). The herd's bull is in rut: the tell is his head going low and his neck swinging; step off his tile or he knocks a man down. The string hauls; the pay runs all act.
B. CATCH THE BULL TOO. Rope the bull (a tough routine fight where nobody may kill). The whole herd follows him into the corral: twelve camels, not six. More haulage, more FOOD to feed them, and the corral is visible prosperity on the map.
C. SHOOT THE BULL FOR MEAT. The herd scatters to the desert. 200 kg of FOOD; no string, no salary; Hana pays 5 WATER for the scouting and does not post to the company again this act.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A or B: the company's held base has a corral; the dam road's hauls finish faster (a step toward the act 2 reckoning); the corral draws lions and wolves to that block (QD-AA05's kind). C: the flats have no herd in act 3. Declining leaves nothing.
