## THE SITUATION
Father Anselmo kept the chapel for forty years through the worst of it. He died in his sleep. He asked, a long time ago and in front of witnesses, to be buried in the farm town he came from, two days east, past the line the Destroyers held in the first act. The chapel is between priests and its services have stopped. The town is in mourning, and the board shows it: one offer, this one.

## THE SETTLEMENT AND THE OFFER SCREEN
- SETTLEMENT: the chapel town, black cloth on the door. Buildings: the hall (the vestry), the clinic, the market, the barber.
- BUILDING: the vestry. One portrait; the other frame has a candle in front of it.
- CLIENT PORTRAIT: Deacon Rui, young, a black Ann Demeulemeester coat too big in the shoulders.
- THE SCREEN'S LINES: "Father Anselmo wanted to go home. Home is two days east, past the old line." / "The chapel can't close for a funeral that far away. It can pay you to take him." / "Bring him there. Bring back whoever they send."
- PAY: 6 batteries. SKULLS: 2 (the old line).
- NEGOTIATION: "The chapel's plate is six. There is no more." No advance.
- ACCEPT / DECLINE. Declining writes nothing. The offer leaves when someone else takes him or the chapel buries him itself.

## THE CHOICES
A. THROUGH THE OLD LINE. Fast. A camp of what is left of the Destroyers sits on the road. They let a hearse pass if someone asks them properly, and some of them knew him. Or they do not, and it is a fight on a fight board of burned cars.
B. THE LONG WAY ROUND. A day more in the heat. The company buys ice at the dam for 2 of its own and keeps him cool.
C. "HE STAYS HERE." Bury him in the chapel yard. Priced, and Rui says the price: half pay, and the farm town sends no priest.

## WHAT THE LEDGERS REMEMBER
A or B: he is buried at home; the new priest rides back in the truck, and the chapel's next board has two offers again. A, asked properly: the Destroyers' camp shows one face on the chapel board next act, come to the grave. C: the chapel stays without a priest for one more refresh; his grave in the yard becomes a place people visit, on the settlement screen. Declining leaves nothing.

## THE ONE WRONG DETAIL
Every time they lift the coffin it is lighter, and at the grave it weighs exactly what an empty box weighs, and nobody opens it.
