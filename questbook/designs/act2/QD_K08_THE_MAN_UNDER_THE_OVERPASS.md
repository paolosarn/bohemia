## THE SITUATION
Act 2, a bust, the overpasses full of people with nowhere else. Rare: once or twice an act at most, and only after
the player has tapped at least one long trip on the map. It fires only where nothing records (the Amalgamation's
blind spot is the unrecorded): under a concrete overpass, in a dust storm, radio dead. The storm is drawn on the
map before the stop, and the overpass sits inside it; that is the cause the map shows. A man is sitting on a milk
crate, out of the wind, selling nothing. He knows one thing he cannot know: the destination the player just tapped
on the map. Nobody in the company has said it aloud.

## THE PERSON AND THE FIRST LINE
An ordinary man, any age, a thin black Rick Owens tank under a hi-vis vest, hands folded.
First line: "You're going to the dam. Take the low road."

## THE CHOICES (no price on any of them; that is the point)
1. "HOW DO YOU KNOW THAT?" He says: "Many ask." Nothing else.
2. LIE ("We're going north."). He nods, as if that is fine too.
3. SAY NOTHING, DRIVE ON (keep moving). The truck pulls out. He does not watch it go.

All three give the same reward, which is none (`Q004.W4`, `Q010.W3`). The low road is a real road; it is not
faster, not safer, not a trap. Taking it or not is the player's business.
