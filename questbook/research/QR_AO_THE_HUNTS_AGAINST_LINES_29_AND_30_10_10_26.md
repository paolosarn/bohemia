QUESTION: row [aa lines 29 and 30] in VAMILY.md. The 20 QD-AA hunt designs were stamped before THE CHECKLIST's lines 29 and 30 (QR-G) existed and were only read lightly against them. Run all 20 strictly, fix in place what fails without changing the premise, keep every citation valid, and say each design's result out of 32 on its CHECKLIST line.

## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

20 hunts run. 5 pass both lines as written (QD-AA13, AA16, AA17, AA18, AA20). 15 failed and are fixed in place: 15 on line 30, 1 on line 29 (QD-AA04 failed both). Every design now reads 32 of 32. No premise changed: no road was cut, no beast changed, no client changed. The commonest failure, 11 of the 15, is THE TROPHY ON TOP: a kill road paid its fee AND the body (pelts, canines, a second tusk, hides, meat), so killing out-paid every road that spared the beast. That is line 30's own worst case, the crueller branch paying more (`Q190.X1`, `Q191.X1`, `Q004.W4`). The fix is one sentence each: the trophy goes to the client's wall, and the fee is the fee. The second failure, 6 designs, is THE HALF FEE FOR THE KIND DONE ROAD: a road that ends the client's problem without killing (feeding the hyenas, burying the dead, waiting for the old bull) paid half, so the kindness was taxed (`Q108.X2`, `Q148.W9`, QR-L P7). Fixed by paying the one fee and letting the road's own cost (food, days, half a day after each fight) be the price the player chooses out of his own purse (the house rule's reading a).

- A CONTRACT pays one fee on every road that counts as done (QR-L P7). "Done" is the client's stated problem gone, by her own words. Loot taken off the beast is pay in another currency and counts (`Q103.X5`).
- A priced refusal (the problem stays or moves to someone else) may pay less for less work, if a road at least as kind pays the full fee (`Q139.P2`).
- A price the player pays out of his own purse for the kinder road is not a reward (`Q108.X2`).
- An EVENT may carry ONE stated temptation, with the number on the button (`Q166.W4`, `Q148.W8`); a second paid road, or a paid road with no number on its button, fails. A cartoon offer tempts nobody (`Q204.X3`).
- People and power count as pay: a follower who reads tracks faster is a reward.

## THE FIXES, BY SHAPE

1. THE TROPHY ON TOP (AA01, AA02, AA04, AA05, AA06, AA07, AA08, AA15, plus the hidden bonus shape in AA11, AA14, AA19): the body went to the company as well as the fee. Now the trophy hangs where the client lives: Ray's diner, the tack room, the chapel, the pro shop case, the house's coats. That keeps the "trophy on the wall" scene (`Q236.X3`, `Q003.W2`) and moves no number. QD-AA02's pelt MODE survives because it is chosen at the hire as a bet on the company (`Q236.W6`), and it falls back to the flat fee if the player takes the no-kill road, so the bet never punishes mercy. QD-AA11 still gets its named trophy through that mode.
2. THE HALF FEE FOR THE KIND DONE ROAD (AA03, AA04, AA06, AA07, AA08): now the one fee, with the road's own cost as the price (`Q108.X2`, `Q148.W9`, `Q088.W9`). Feeding, burying, and waiting were already priced in food and days; the half fee taxed them twice.
3. THE DARK SIDE PAID (AA09, AA12): the drawers and the organ pigs paid the company for the worst road. Now a stranger and a clinic still ASK (the temptation is said by a mouth, `Q092.W6`, `Q003.W6`) but the ask carries no price, so the road is chosen for what it does to the valley (`Q003.W5`).
4. TWO PAID ROADS ON ONE EVENT (AA10, AA19): one road keeps the stated temptation, the other keeps its memory and loses its pay (`Q166.W4`). Nell becomes a person who joins, not a tracking stat (`Q003.W8`, `Q144.W2`): her notes still cross to the heir.
5. THE UNSAID TRICK (AA04, line 29): the cow-turning rule is now Hiram's line, so the cheaper fight is reached by listening (`Q155.P2`, `Q018.W5`, `Q148.W3`).

## THE COMMONEST FAILURE

THE TROPHY ON TOP: a beast hunt treats the carcass as free loot, but in a contract with a no-kill road, free loot IS a tilt toward killing. The hunt shelf was written from Battle Brothers' taxidermist (recall) and the trophy buyer, where every kill drops parts; QR-G's line 30 came later and nobody went back. The rule for the next hunt: in a contract, the beast's body belongs to the client or to the pelt MODE chosen at the hire, never on top of the fee (`Q103.X5`, `Q067.X5`, `Q148.W8`). In an event, it is the one stated temptation, with the number on the button.
