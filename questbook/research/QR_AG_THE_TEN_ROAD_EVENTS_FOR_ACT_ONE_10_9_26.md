# QR-AG: THE TEN ROAD EVENTS FOR ACT ONE

Sometimes, while your party crosses the map, a real person you can see on the road stops you and asks for something, and you pick one of two or three answers with the price written on the button. Here are the ten best ones for the first era, each copied in shape from a real Battle Brothers event and rebuilt for our broken Las Vegas.

QUESTS LANE (chat 19), research only, round 10/9. Row [the road events]. Nothing here is built (rule 35). Written for RUN, who builds the event screen, and for WORLD, PEOPLE and WORDS, who feed it. Every Qnnn id below resolves in records/BOHEMIA_QUESTBOOK_LAW_INDEX.json. Battle Brothers facts come from the wiki pages Grok pulled (source: wiki dump, unverified, reference/library/grok/wiki/PAGES_ALL.tar.gz, page named in each entry) and from Grok pages marked PASSED FILTER (source: Grok, unverified). Money: 10 crowns to 1 battery (rule 75d, as QR-AF uses it). All contents (names, faces, lines) are draft:true and Paolo's to change.

BUILDS ON, DOES NOT REPEAT: QR-B (the ten SHAPES, E1 to E10; this page fills those shapes with ten concrete act one events), QR-K (how a shape repeats; its once-per-face rule is used here as is), QR-AA (the hunt; event 6 hands off into it), QR-AF (contract kinds; no event here is a contract), and the event designs on the shelf. Where a shelf design already holds a premise, the event below names it and differs: QD-B01 (the wounded man, possible bait) is not event 1; QD-B03 and QD-M03 (quarrels in the company) are not here at all, because every event on this page is a STRANGER who stops the party; QD-K02 (the toll at the culvert) is not repeated.

## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Battle Brothers' road events are short: one painted picture, one person, two to four buttons, a cooldown in days, and a requirement list that ties the event to where you are and what you carry (source: wiki dump, unverified, 0710_Events.txt; reference/library/battle_brothers/08_CONTRACTS_EVENTS.md). The library agrees with that shape and adds three hard rules. The event is CAUSED by something on the map you could have seen (`Q044.W2`, `Q050.W9`, `Q061.X4`). The price is ON THE BUTTON and the button does what it says (`Q107.W7`, `Q107.X1`). And it is RARE, because repetition turns a person into a vending machine (`Q066.X1`, `Q148.X3`). So the ten below are: a man with a dead trike, a posse with a photo, a runaway in a culvert, a blind man with a watch, kids with a dead trader's rings, a man who runs out of a storm drain, a grower whose shed you could rob, a seller with a thumb drive, a walker who wants your fire, and a crowd about to tie a thief to a light pole. Each has two or three answers, one of which always costs nothing and lets you keep moving. None starts a fight on its own (rule 68): a fight only ever comes from a party that is drawn on the map and can be fled.

## THE RULES ALL TEN OBEY (from the library, read once, applied ten times)

1. A FACE, NOT A CARD. The event opens on one person's portrait and their squiggle voice; the narrator reads only the one setup line (rule 45). A person with a name and a want is what makes the stop matter (`Q028.W2`, `Q001.W2`).
2. THE CAUSE IS VISIBLE. Before the stop, the thing that caused it is drawn on the map: the dead trike on the shoulder, the posse's dust, the greenhouse light. No visible cause, no event (`Q044.W2`, `Q050.W9`, `Q061.X4`).
3. THE PRICE IS ON THE BUTTON. In the six resources (BATTERIES, FOOD, MEDS, ROUNDS, TAPE, WATER), the company (a person gained, lost or hurt) or HOURS. Odds are shown when there are odds, like Battle Brothers' "70% ok, 30% injury" button (source: wiki dump, unverified, 0305_Broken Cart.txt). Frostpunk's crowd scene is the model: the cost sits on the option (`Q107.W7`).
4. THE BUTTON DOES WHAT IT SAYS. "Help push" never secretly means "get robbed" (`Q107.X1`). A twist may come later, never as a lie on the button.
5. NO DOMINANT ANSWER. If one choice is strictly better, it is a difficulty setting, not a choice (`Q126.X2`, `Q126.X1`). If kindness also pays best, it is not kindness (`Q108.X2`, `Q126.X7`, `Q125.X9`). Where the kind answer pays, it pays less or later.
6. KEEP MOVING IS ALWAYS FREE. One button always costs nothing and writes nothing (`Q126.W3`). The free no is the same law as the contracts' free decline.
7. NEVER GRADED. The narrator does not praise or scold (`Q126.X4`). Its one line is too calm and a little wrong (rule 45).
8. RARE. A cooldown per event, a once-per-face rule from QR-K, and at most one stop per two or three trips (QR-B). Wealth raises the danger of what stops you (`Q044.W4`, `Q151.W3`); after a hard hit, a kinder one comes (`Q044.W7`).
9. NEVER IN THE FIRST MINUTE, and in act one, never before the party's first full day on the map (Battle Brothers itself gates Lawmen and the Map seller at campaign day 10 or older; source: wiki dump, unverified, 1147_Lawmen After Criminal.txt, 1769_Shady Character Offers Map.txt).
10. THE CONSEQUENCE COMES BACK ELSEWHERE. A choice writes a ledger line that shows up later on the feed or on the road, not right away (`Q004.W5`, `Q024.W10`).
11. NO FIGHT FROM A POPUP (rule 68). Where Battle Brothers' button says "a fight starts", ours says a party is now on the map, drawn and moving, and you may flee it.

## THE TEN EVENTS

Each entry: the Battle Brothers event it translates; our person; where; the visible cause; the buttons with costs; the narrator line; what the ledgers remember; requirements and cooldown.

### 1. THE TRIKE ON THE SHOULDER

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Broken Cart". Cooldown 50 days. Requires: on a road, at least one brother without a temporary injury. Score 9. A trader with a broken cart and a defeated donkey says "Have you come to take my things? If so, you needn't kill me. Take what you want." Choices: "take everything we can use" (minus 2 reputation, 1 to 3 items of bread, tools, medical supplies at triple quantity); "let us help you get your cart on the road" (70%: plus 2 reputation and the items; 30%: the same plus one brother hurt, a sprained ankle, smashed hand or crushed finger); "we have no time for this" (nothing). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 0305_Broken Cart.txt.

OURS: a parts trader, fifties, sitting on the curb beside a three-wheel cargo trike with a dead battery pack.
FACE: a sun-cracked woman in a cropped black utility jacket worn inside out, a bike chain for a belt, one lens of her glasses taped.
WHERE: the shoulder of a highway segment on the map, between two settlements, never inside one.
VISIBLE CAUSE: the trike is drawn on the road tile one leg before the stop, its light blinking.
BUTTONS:
- STRIP IT. +3 TAPE, +2 MEDS. Standing with the settlement she trades into: minus 2.
- HELP PUSH IT. 1 HOUR. +1 TAPE, +1 MEDS (she pays you in what she has). "70% fine, 30% one of yours is hurt for 3 days."
- KEEP MOVING. Free.
NARRATOR: "A woman waits beside her vehicle. She has waited before. She is good at it."
LEDGERS: STRIP writes "stripped a trader's trike, [road]" to the deed ledger; she never appears again on that road, and her settlement's market shows one fewer parts stall for a season (the future goes both ways, rule from the third votes). HELP writes "pushed a trike, [road]"; her stall later sells you TAPE at a small discount. KEEP MOVING writes nothing.
WHY IT IS FIRST: the cheapest honest trade in the whole set. STRIP pays more now, HELP pays less now and a little later, so neither dominates (`Q126.X2`, `Q108.X2`); the injury odds are on the button (`Q107.W7`). The library's warning about scarcity bending mercy is the whole tension at low TAPE (`Q031.W3`, `Q108.W3`).
REQUIRES: on a road; at least one company member unhurt; not within one map leg of a settlement. COOLDOWN: 50 days (Battle Brothers'). Once per face.

### 2. THE POSSE WITH THE PHOTO

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Lawmen After Criminal". Cooldown 60 days. Requires: campaign 10 days or older, daytime, on a road, no late-game crisis. Score 5 per Thief, Graverobber, Raider, Killer on the Run or Nomad in the company. Riders of a noble house claim a wanted man is among your brothers. Choices: give him up (plus 100 crowns, lose the brother); refuse (fight a noble army, minus 30 relations with the house); pay 1000 crowns (only if over 1500); invoke renown (only over 3000 renown, free); ask for the drawing (50% keep him with a good mood, 50% the fight and minus 30). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 1147_Lawmen After Criminal.txt.

OURS: the road captain of whichever faction holds this road, with four riders.
FACE: a calm man with a shaved head under a heavy padded hood, a faction armband stitched into an oversized tailored coat (not a trenchcoat; trenchcoats are for badasses), holding a laminated photo printed on a home printer.
WHERE: on a road that faction holds. The faction's colour is on the riders (colour is territory).
VISIBLE CAUSE: the posse is a drawn party on the map, moving toward you at road speed, for at least one leg before the stop. You could have turned away (rule 68).
TRIGGER: you carry a company member whose background is a thief, a wanted man, a road crew who switched sides, or a highway camp hand (GROK_13's skins: "Thief is a thief... Raider is a road crew who switched sides... Killer on the run is a wanted man... Nomad is a highway camp hand"; source: Grok, unverified, GROK_13_BACKGROUNDS_2026_09_30.md).
BUTTONS:
- HAND HIM OVER. +10 BATTERIES. You lose [name] from the company.
- PAY THEM. -100 BATTERIES. Shown only if you hold more than 150. He stays.
- NO. Free now. Standing with [faction]: minus 30. The posse stays on the map as a hunting party; it can be fled.
NARRATOR: "The photograph is recent. Nobody in the company remembers it being taken."
LEDGERS: HAND OVER writes "gave [name] to [faction]"; his family, if the dynasty ledger knows one, remembers it in the next era. PAY writes the 100 to the faction's take. NO writes "refused [faction] on [road]" and a pursuit flag; the posse's tracks stay drawn. This is the library's consequence-with-a-hunter (`Q038.W9`) and the antagonist as a clock you can see (`Q109.W4`), held to rule 68: the hunter is a drawn party, not a timer.
WHY: a third party wanting one of yours is the rival-claimant beat (`Q016.W7`); no button grades you (`Q126.X4`). The renown and drawing buttons are cut: three is the cap, and a 50% coin flip on a person's life on the road is the RNG misery the library warns about (`Q041.X1`, `Q065.X4`).
REQUIRES: day 10 or later; daytime; on a road held by a faction; one matching background in the company. COOLDOWN: 60 days. Once per face.

### 3. THE MAN UNDER THE DIRT

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Deserter in Forest" (cooldown 200 days; a free roster slot, daytime, forest; the deserter hides, bounty hunters with dogs offer 200 crowns: "He's right there, take him" gives 200 crowns and bad mood for Deserter brothers; "What? Who? Where?" then lets you recruit him or send him on). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 0570_Deserter in Forest.txt. Also "Runaway Laborers" (cooldown 60, on a road: point them out for minus 1 reputation and 50 crowns, or deny, 70% they can join). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 1720_Runaway Laborers.txt.

OURS: a young man who ran from a compound where he was working off a debt on a tab (GROK_13's skin for the indebted: "a person a compound already owns on a tab"; source: Grok, unverified, GROK_13_BACKGROUNDS_2026_09_30.md).
FACE: thin, sunburnt, in a filthy designer track top three sizes too big, a plastic hospital bracelet still on his wrist with a compound's barcode.
WHERE: a flood culvert under a road, any dry wash on the map.
VISIBLE CAUSE: a compound's dog crew is a drawn party on the map, slow, sniffing along the wash; their tracks cross yours.
BUTTONS (after he crawls under the dirt and the dog men arrive):
- POINT AT THE CULVERT. +20 BATTERIES. Anyone in your company who ran from somewhere: bad mood.
- SAY NOTHING. Free. The dog men leave. If you have a free slot, he asks to come: a new hire who costs almost nothing a day until his debt is paid (GROK_13's mechanic).
NARRATOR: "The dogs are very interested in a spot where nothing is."
LEDGERS: POINT writes "sold a runaway to [compound]"; that compound's standing plus 5. SAY NOTHING writes nothing unless he joins; if he joins, "hid a runaway from [compound]", and if that compound's crew ever sees him in your company, the standing drops then (a delayed, displaced consequence, `Q004.W5`).
WHY: the Whispering Hillock's ambiguous petitioner (`Q004.W6`): you do not know what he did, only that he is owned. SAY NOTHING is free because a no is free (`Q126.W3`); POINT pays now, which is the honest temptation with a number on it (`Q148.W8`).
REQUIRES: daytime; near a wash; a compound faction exists within three legs. COOLDOWN: 200 days. Once per face.

### 4. THE BLIND MAN WITH THE WATCH

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Beat up Old Man". Cooldown 60 days. Requires daytime, on a road, a free inventory slot, and one brother not offended by violence. An old blind man with a jeweled ring "tilts his head as though to see you with his ears". Choices: "Travel safely, old man" (nothing) or "That jeweled ring. Hand it over" (minus 1 reputation, a signet ring). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 0226_Beat up Old Man.txt.

OURS: an old blind man walking the shoulder with a cane cut from a road sign post.
FACE: milky eyes, a gold watch far too expensive for everything else he wears, a Bottega woven bag gone grey with dust.
WHERE: any paved road on the map, never within a settlement.
VISIBLE CAUSE: a single walker drawn on the road, very slow, for one leg before the stop.
BUTTONS:
- TRAVEL SAFE. Free.
- TAKE THE WATCH. +15 BATTERIES (the watch's sale value at a market). Standing on this road: minus 1. Anyone in your company with a soft background: bad mood.
NARRATOR: "The watch is correct. He checks it often, by touch."
LEDGERS: TAKE writes "robbed a blind man, [road]" to the deed ledger and the feed later posts it as a rumour with no name; TRAVEL SAFE writes nothing.
WHY: the purest two-button event in Battle Brothers, and the library's case for it: zero mechanical reason to be kind, so the choice is about the person (`Q004.W4`); the reward for cruelty is real and small (`Q148.W8`); the narrator never comments (`Q126.X4`).
REQUIRES: daytime; on a road; one company member who would do it. COOLDOWN: 60 days. Once per face.

### 5. THE KIDS WITH THE RINGS

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Kids and Dead Merchant". Cooldown 999999 (once). Requires daytime, on a road, at least 3 brothers. A gang of children has stoned a wealthy merchant to death and taken his jewelry; forty or fifty come out of the trees with stones. Choices: take the goods (minus 1 reputation, a signet ring and 50 to 200 crowns, and a quarter of your men take brawl injuries); a Hedge Knight background gets it free; "Back off, men" (nothing). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 1114_Kids and Dead Merchant.txt.

OURS: a red-haired girl of about eleven, the leader of a street gang of thirty, wearing a dead battery trader's rings on every finger.
FACE: grinning, a man's tailored blazer to her knees with the sleeves cut off, a slingshot made of surgical tubing.
WHERE: a dry wash or an empty lot beside a road on the map.
VISIBLE CAUSE: a trader's cart is drawn stopped on the road and does not move for a whole day before you arrive; on the stop, the body is in the tall weeds.
BUTTONS:
- TAKE THE GOODS. +5 to 20 BATTERIES and a signet ring (an item). "1 in 4 of yours hurt by stones, a few days each." Standing on this road: minus 1.
- BACK OFF. Free.
NARRATOR: "The children have counted you. They counted correctly."
LEDGERS: TAKE writes "took a dead trader's goods from children"; the gang becomes a small roaming party of the road (it can rob your next caravan contract there). BACK OFF writes nothing.
WHY: the library's image is exact: a child throwing stones at the victim (`Q112.W2`). No clean option (`Q001.W11`, `Q004.W3`); the cost of taking is on the button (`Q107.W7`). The Hedge Knight background button is cut to keep two choices; PEOPLE may add a third later for a company member with a hard-man background, as Battle Brothers did.
REQUIRES: daytime; on a road; at least 3 in the company. COOLDOWN: once ever.

### 6. "PLEASE, NO MORE"

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Man in Forest". Cooldown 60 days. Requires daytime, forest, a free inventory slot. A man bursts from the bushes, says "Please, no more," and runs. Choices: follow him (45% a fight against brigands, 45% against direwolves, 10% a named dagger) or let him go (nothing). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 1237_Man in Forest.txt.

OURS: a man with twigs and dried mud in his hair who climbs out of a storm drain.
FACE: soaked to the waist, one shoe, a reflective jacket with a lab's logo half torn off.
WHERE: storm drains along the edge of the city, near the dry washes where the lab's beasts roam (rule 42).
VISIBLE CAUSE: tracks on the map lead to the drain from somewhere off-screen; the stop happens when the party crosses them.
BUTTONS:
- FOLLOW HIM. 2 HOURS. You find what he ran from and it is DRAWN on the map: a raider camp (45%) or a dire wolf pack (45%), which you may attack or leave, or a dropped named knife (10%). The odds are on the button.
- LET HIM GO. Free.
NARRATOR: "He apologised to the drain before he left it."
LEDGERS: FOLLOW marks the found party on the map and, if it is a pack, opens QR-AA's hunt (tracks, then the den). LET GO writes nothing; the tracks stay on the map for a day for anyone who wants to look.
WHY: this is the only event of the ten that leads toward a fight, and rule 68 shapes it: the button reveals a party, it never starts a battle. The library calls the loot that leads you on a trap (`Q110.P5`) and the dream that is bait (`Q085.W6`); here the bait is a frightened man and nothing about him is a lie.
REQUIRES: daytime; within two legs of a wash; a beast or raider party exists nearby (the cause is real, `Q044.W2`). COOLDOWN: 60 days. Once per face.

### 7. THE GREENHOUSE ON THE DIRT

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Raid Farmstead". Cooldown 30 days. Requires daytime, plains or farmland, on a road, 50 or less food, at least 5 brothers, 2 not offended by violence. Score 30. A brother reports the food is rotting and "gently suggests" raiding a farm in the distance. Choices: take only what is needed (minus 1 reputation, ham and bread); take everything (minus 2, more food); take everything and kill everyone (minus 5, more food, pitchforks, Bloodthirsty for a brother); move on (nothing). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 1625_Raid Farmstead.txt.

OURS: the grower at a plastic hydroponic shed on the valley edge; the event is started by your own low food, and the person who stops you is the grower who has seen you looking.
FACE: an old woman in rubber boots and a sheer Rick Owens-style smock over a sweater, a grow lamp's purple light on her face.
WHERE: dirt roads on the valley edge, near any small growing plot.
VISIBLE CAUSE: your FOOD is at 5 days or less (shown in the HUD in red) and the greenhouse glow is drawn on the map at night.
BUTTONS:
- TAKE WHAT WE NEED. +3 FOOD. Standing with the nearest settlement: minus 1.
- TAKE IT ALL. +6 FOOD, +1 TAPE. Standing minus 3. The plot is drawn as raided and grows nothing for a season.
- MOVE ON. Free.
NARRATOR: "The plants were not expecting visitors. The plants are fine."
LEDGERS: either take writes "raided [grower]'s plot" to the deed ledger; TAKE IT ALL also writes the plot as fallen, so in a later era derived from this one that plot is a ruin (the future goes both ways). MOVE ON writes nothing.
WHY: the event is caused by the company's own ledger, never rolled (`Q044.W2`); the library's case that scarcity turns compassion against your own (`Q031.W3`) is the whole thing. The "kill everyone" button is cut: a third act of cruelty adds nothing the second does not, and the library warns against the dominated option (`Q126.X2`).
REQUIRES: night or day; on dirt; FOOD 5 days or less; at least 4 in the company. COOLDOWN: 30 days.

### 8. THE MAN WITH THE DRIVE

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Shady Character Offers Map". Cooldown 50 days. Requires daytime, campaign at least 10 days. A man sells a map: pay 600 to 1400 crowns; with a Historian it always reveals a named item's location, without one 50% it is unreadable. A Peddler can halve the price; a Thief can steal it (same odds). "Not interested" does nothing. Source: wiki dump, unverified, 1769_Shady Character Offers Map.txt.

OURS: a seller of old data with one thumb drive on a lanyard.
FACE: a narrow man in a white Balenciaga-shaped puffer gone yellow, earbuds in that are not connected to anything.
WHERE: a roadside stall at a crossroads on the map, a beach umbrella over a folding table.
VISIBLE CAUSE: the umbrella is drawn at the crossroads; it moves to a new crossroads each week.
BUTTONS:
- BUY IT. -60 to 140 BATTERIES (the number shown). "50% it opens." With a library hand in the company: "it opens."
- HAVE [stall man] HAGGLE. -30 to 70 BATTERIES, same odds. Shown only with a stall-man background in the company (GROK_13: "Peddler is a stall man"; source: Grok, unverified, GROK_13_BACKGROUNDS_2026_09_30.md).
- NOT INTERESTED. Free.
NARRATOR: "The file on the drive was last changed tomorrow."
LEDGERS: a drive that opens marks a cache on the map with a named item. A drive that does not open is an item in the bag ("a drive that will not open"), which a library hand you hire later can read. NOT INTERESTED writes nothing; the stall is there next week somewhere else.
WHY: knowledge as the thing bought (`Q018.W3`), a rumour that becomes a place (`Q001.W2`); the odds are stated so a loss is consequence, not misery (`Q065.X4`). The thief button is cut to keep three. The narrator's line is the one wrong detail, never explained.
REQUIRES: day 10 or later; at a crossroads; more than 60 batteries. COOLDOWN: 50 days.

### 9. THE WALKER AT THE FIRE

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Traveler". Cooldown 14 days. Requires on a road, not in the far north or far south. A traveller asks to share the camp: "Join us at the campfire" (flavour text, minus 3 provisions) or "No, keep your distance" (nothing). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 2029_Traveler.txt.

OURS: a walker crossing the valley on foot who asks to sit at your fire for the night.
FACE: a tall woman with a shaved head and a hand-painted cardboard sign folded in her pocket, a sleeping bag worn as a cape.
WHERE: any night camp on the map, on a road.
VISIBLE CAUSE: a single walker drawn on the road at dusk, moving the same way you are.
BUTTONS:
- SHARE THE FIRE. -1 FOOD, -1 WATER. She talks: one place on the map is revealed (a cache, a den, or which faction holds the next road), and the feed later posts her line.
- KEEP YOUR DISTANCE. Free.
NARRATOR: "She knows the names of three roads that are not on any map. She says them in order."
LEDGERS: SHARE writes "shared a fire with a walker"; nothing else. Her reveal is the only reward. KEEP YOUR DISTANCE writes nothing.
WHY: this is QR-B's E10, the good hour, and the library's storyteller rule: after a hard hit, send relief (`Q044.W7`). It fires preferentially after a lost fight or a death in the company. Battle Brothers' 14-day cooldown is the shortest of the ten, and that is right for a relief event. The register is hospitality, kept a touch uncanny (`Q133.P6`).
REQUIRES: night; on a road; weighted up after a loss. COOLDOWN: 14 days. Once per face.

### 10. THE THIEF ON THE LIGHT POLE

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Melon Thief" (once; daytime, a free roster slot, within 4 tiles of an allied small northern settlement; drunken villagers carry a blindfolded man on a felled tree to tar him; you can stop them and he can join as a Thief, help tar him, or say not our business). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 1256_Melon Thief.txt. Also "Fake Witchhunter" (cooldown 99999, day 25 or later, near a small settlement, on a road: peasants want to burn a man; intervene for 50% 75 crowns or 50% a third of your men lightly wounded). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 0728_Fake Witchhunter.txt.

OURS: the crowd's leader at the edge of a small settlement, about to tape a man to a dead street light for stealing a battery.
FACE: a big man in a sleeveless hoodie with a tower of zip ties on his forearm, a church-key opener hung on a chain like a medal.
WHERE: within one leg of a small settlement on the map.
VISIBLE CAUSE: the crowd is drawn at the light pole, and the settlement's brownout is on (the stolen battery was theirs; brownouts are real on the grid).
BUTTONS:
- CUT HIM DOWN. Standing with [settlement]: minus 5. If you have a free slot, he asks to come (a thief background). "With 4 or more of you, they back off."
- NOT OUR BUSINESS. Free.
NARRATOR: "The light above him has not worked in years. They are using it for something else now."
LEDGERS: CUT writes "cut down a thief at [settlement]"; that settlement's hall offers you one fewer contract for a week. NOT OUR BUSINESS writes nothing; the next time you pass, the pole is empty and the feed posts nothing about it.
WHY: the mob verdict (`Q098.N4`) and the library's lesson that the violence is downstream of the crowd's own escalation (`Q107.P4`). The third Battle Brothers button (help tar him) is cut for act one; it may return in a crueller later rewrite. The cost of the kind answer is a real standing loss so mercy is not free money (`Q108.X2`, `Q108.W3`).
REQUIRES: daytime; within one leg of a small settlement in brownout; at least 2 in the company. COOLDOWN: once per settlement.

## HOW THE TEN ARE PICKED ON THE ROAD

Battle Brothers picks events by a SCORE: each event that meets its requirements gets a weight (Broken Cart 9, Lawmen 5 per matching background, Raid Farmstead 30, Traveler 15), and one is drawn from the eligible pile (source: wiki dump, unverified, the Score line on each page above). The weights show how it reads the company: Lawmen only rises when you carry criminals, Raid Farmstead only exists when food is low. That is the library's storyteller, not a dice roll (`Q044.W2`). Ours adds two rules from the library: a hard pacing cap (QR-B: at most one stop per two or three trips, and never two in one trip) because rarity is what keeps a face a person (`Q066.X1`, `Q084.X6`, `Q148.X3`), and the ebb (`Q044.W7`): after a loss, event 9 is weighted up and events 2, 5 and 7 are weighted down. Wealth works the other way (`Q044.W4`, `Q151.W3`): above a battery threshold, 2 and 8 rise.

The world runs without you (`Q055.W1`, `Q061.W2`) and its factions keep their own agendas (`Q055.W7`): the posse in event 2, the dog crew in event 3 and the gang in event 5 exist on the map whether or not they ever stop you.

## THE SPEC FOR RUN (one row per event, a data file a modder can edit)

```
event:
  id:                 road_trike_on_shoulder       # snake_case, unique
  draft: true
  act: 1
  bb_source:          "Broken Cart"                 # the Battle Brothers event translated
  bb_cooldown_days:   50
  cooldown_days:      50                            # TUNING owns the number
  once_per_face:      true
  score:              9                             # base weight; may name a modifier
  score_mod:          null                          # e.g. "+5 per company member with background in [thief, wanted, road_crew, camp_hand]"
  requires:
    min_play_seconds: 60                            # never in the first minute (hard floor for every event)
    min_day:          1                             # 10 for posse and drive
    time:             day | night | any
    terrain:          [road] | [dirt] | [wash] | ...
    near_settlement:  {max_legs: n, size: small}    # or null
    company_min:      1
```

## WHAT TO AVOID (the flaws, in one place)

- Pure random: `Q044.X1`, `Q041.X1`, `Q065.X4`. Every event needs a visible cause and stated odds.
- Seams that show: `Q061.X4`. No posse from nowhere; it was on the map.
- The vending machine: `Q066.X1`, `Q148.X3`, `Q084.X6`. Cooldowns, once per face, the pacing cap.
- The option that lies: `Q107.X1`. The label is the outcome.
- The dominated option and the paid kindness: `Q126.X1`, `Q126.X2`, `Q108.X2`, `Q126.X7`, `Q125.X9`.
- The graded choice: `Q126.X4`. The narrator never judges.
- The bait-and-switch resentment: `Q053.X1`. Event 6 says on the button what it might lead to.
- Early costs that outrun the reward: `Q067.X5`. No act one event can cost more than about two days of keep (`Q146.W8`, QR-L).
