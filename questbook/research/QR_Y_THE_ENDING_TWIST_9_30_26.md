## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The library's twist endings that people still defend share three moves. They PLANT in plain sight: the clue is audible from the first minute but reads as texture (a verbal tic, an odd competence, an offhand line about a flower), so it is invisible until named and undeniable after (`Q033.W3`, `Q032.W4`, `Q001.W8`, `Q010.W8`). They PAY with delivery, not information: the same fact told safely is exposition, told by the enemy at the player's lowest point it is a blow (`Q167.W1`, `Q032.W5`, `Q167.W9`), and the best reveals are staged or said in one short line rather than explained (`Q152.W6`, `Q118.W2`, `Q033.W7`). They are FAIR when the information was there before the fork and the player can find it afterwards (`Q139.W7`, `Q019.W10`, `Q116.X2`), and a cheat when the cost could not be read (`Q086.X7`, `Q121.W7`) or when the game blames the player for its own rails (`Q027.X2`, `Q139.X2`, `Q033.X3`). A twist recolours a long game without cancelling it when it rewrites MEANING and never FACTS: every earlier scene re-reads "without changing a frame" (`Q032.W1`, `Q073.W3`, `Q152.W10`), history stays fixed while posture is played (`Q145.W6`), what survives the edit is the real person (`Q082.W2`, `Q076.W2`), and agency is restored and tested after the reveal (`Q033.W8`, `Q032.W3`). For our three generations that is decisive: a twist that says "none of it was real" throws away 100 hours; a twist that says "it was all real, and here is what it was FOR" spends them. Stance-based endings stop being a difficulty setting when the stance is a DOOR, not a SCORE: the library's worst finales lock the profound ending behind a stat, a dice roll, a hard fight or a missable side quest (`Q140.X1`, `Q142.X1`, `Q221.X1`, `Q155.X1`, `Q076.X1`, `Q063.X5`), and its best read comprehension and conduct, not power (`Q132.W4`, `Q006.W9`, `Q035.W7`, `Q085.W9`), give each path its own real reward (`Q025.W2`), and never make the refusal a losing button (`Q084.X5`). Of the eight candidate shapes below, the three with the least canon risk and the most three-generation leverage are THE UNRECORDED LEDGER (the copy of your family the Amalgamation keeps is missing exactly what you never posted), THE SIBLING AT THE TABLE (the dead sibling from act 1's opening is in the counterfeit family), and Paolo's own SIGNAL ARRIVAL (the act 3 heir learns how he got to the Moon). All eight are options; the choice of whether to have a twist at all, and which, is DYNASTY's default and Paolo's correction.

## THE FINDINGS

### 1. PLANTING: WHAT THE PLAYER SAW EARLY AND READ ONE WAY

**The clue is everywhere and looks like texture.** "Would you kindly" is heard from minute one "disguised as a verbal tic... invisible until named, chilling on replay" (`Q033.W3`). The identity twist is seeded by "odd competences, languages, false-memory cracks, NPC reactions" (`Q032.W4`), and its own port calls fair-play seeding MANDATORY: "a twist without clues is a betrayal" (`Q032.P4`, `Q032.P9`). The cleanest seed in the library is its own existence: a god personally lobbying one mortal at one altar "is desperation legible AS desperation to any player calm enough to ask why the whisper is happening at all" (`Q150.W10`).

**The line that only lands later.** The stranger admires the spot where John will be buried (`Q010.W8`). A night meeting under a borrowed name hands you a gift a hundred hours early (`Q071.W3`). The cute first act of a visual novel hides the whole story; innocent reads as dread on the second look (`Q057.W7`). The trusted guide curdles "so slowly you can't name the turn" (`Q036.W4`).

**Rewarding the listener.** The Baron's doll is solvable only if you remembered a line about a flower (`Q001.W8`). The deepest thematic answer in a whole game sits inside an easy-to-miss conversation, "faith in the attentive" (`Q012.W10`). An optional thread seeded all game pays the medium's most loved moment (`Q062.W1`, `Q062.W4`), and the "waste of time" side hunt turns out to be the solution (`Q062.W8`).

**The seed must be even.** The Leviathan reveal "surprises some and bores others" because the seeds were uneven: attentive players see it coming and find it flat, inattentive ones find it arbitrary (`Q167.X1`). A reveal that relies on a long friendship lands only for players who built the friendship (`Q227.X1`). And the theme cannot live only in optional corners: "our fold's theme cannot be optional" (`Q092.X5`); Vault 11, a game's thesis, is a hole in a rock most players never enter (`Q104.X3`); a philosophy placed only at the very end is missed by everyone who stopped (`Q086.X1`).

FINDING 1: the plant for an ENDING twist must be carried by things every player touches (the main line, the company, the phone, the dead), repeated across all three acts, each time readable as ordinary texture, never gated behind optional content. Optional content may DEEPEN it, never CARRY it.

### 2. PAYING: THE REVEAL

**Delivery beats information.** "The when and who of a reveal matter more than the what" (`Q167.W9`). The truth delivered by the losing enemy as his last card, at the player's lowest point, "to BREAK the player, not to inform" (`Q167.W1`, `Q167.W2`, `Q167.W6`, `Q032.W5`). The player is then made to keep functioning through it (`Q167.W3`, `Q167.W7`), and the companions' faces confirm it before words do (`Q167.W8`, `Q032.W6`).

**Show it, do not explain it.** The substitution reveal: the mad god vanishes mid-scene and his opposite stands in his place, "the twist performed as staging, not stated" (`Q152.W6`). The joke character speaks plainly exactly once, and the register break carries the reveal (`Q152.W5`). Angela's whole backstory is twelve words about the temperature (`Q118.W2`). One line crystallizes the theme and accuses the player (`Q033.W7`, `Q036.W9`). Idea-horror must be STAGED so the realization is unavoidable: SOMA leaves a copy of you awake and screaming that he did not make it (`Q034.W4`, `Q034.X1`).

**The ledger as the reveal.** Two numbers the player watched rise for thirty hours are re-translated in two sentences, and the stats become a confession (`Q140.W1`); the judge reads a different text by the actual number, "the judgment is a function... which is what makes it fair" (`Q140.W2`). The final opponent of the Suicide Mission "is your own game-long ledger" (`Q137.W10`), every death "a receipt" (`Q137.W1`), closed by the roll call (`Q006.W8`). Accounting can be the finale "when every row was earned" (`Q141.W9`). The reactive credits voicemail reflects your choices back per person (`Q063.W9`).

**The reveal changes play or it sags.** BioShock's twist "doesn't change the gameplay" and momentum sags (`Q033.X2`, `Q033.W10`); the fix is to prove it mechanically and then change how the game plays (`Q033.W4`). The Dragon's Dogma victory "breaks the world" and the real game starts after the credits should have rolled (`Q084.W5`).

FINDING 2: for us the reveal should arrive as a person with a portrait (A QUEST IS PEOPLE, PLACES AND THINGS, 9/20), in one short plain line or one staged image, and should READ THE FAMILY LEDGER back the way `Q140.W1` reads EXP. Our ledger is already the thing the whole game writes, so the reveal costs almost no new content: it is a re-translation of rows the player authored.

### 3. FAIR, NOT A CHEAT

**The information was there.** Spec Ops' white phosphorus: the huddled cluster is visible during targeting, "readable in hindsight, unreadable in genre. The information was THERE" (`Q139.W7`). The cheat twin: the mercy option irradiates the station "and he mentions it late", and the player's line is "that would have been nice to know earlier" (`Q121.W7`). A consequence the player could not foresee "isn't a choice, it's a gotcha" (`Q086.X7`). Obra Dinn's contract: enough information to solve everything without guessing, and where the art is ambiguous the contract breaks (`Q019.W10`, `Q019.X1`).

**Findable afterwards.** An unsignposted rule is powerful and means most players never learn it existed (`Q116.X2`); the ending counter nobody can see gives the suicide ending to players who inspect items out of habit (`Q118.X1`). The same beat is "profound or cheap depending entirely on information design" (`Q137.X4`). A gut punch needs an authorial fingerprint, "one line, one note, one witness", or it reads as a bug (`Q129.X1`). Threatened consequences must be honoured; the permadeath bluff left players feeling lied to (`Q058.X3`).

**The motive for the lie must be a character's, not the author's.** Catherine withholds how copying works because she needs Simon compliant; motivated, so it is character (`Q034.W8`), and where it looks like contrivance players call it a cheat (`Q034.X2`). The coin toss metaphor is "wrong ON PURPOSE" and half the audience still fights about it a decade on (`Q115.X6`). Motivated amnesia works because it is justified and load-bearing (`Q032.W10`); the amnesia plot with a hole ("why does nobody recognize him?") gets a truck driven through it (`Q085.X3`).

**Never blame the player for the rails.** The illusion-of-choice indictment "can feel like the game blaming you for ITS railroad" (`Q027.X2`); Spec Ops removes the alternative then holds the player responsible (`Q139.X2`); the agency twist works "ONLY if the seeding and thematic fusion are airtight" (`Q033.X3`). The discipline that fixes it: the authored kill is quarantined from the ledger, "script-culpability and player-culpability kept in separate books" (`Q143.W4`).

FINDING 3: a twist is fair in Bohemia when (a) at least three plants exist in the main line across at least two acts, (b) each plant is a person, place or thing the player can revisit after the reveal (the derived cities make this free: every plant still stands in its act), (c) whoever hid the truth had a reason inside the fiction (the Amalgamation's secrecy, which canon already gives: it kills only to protect its secret), and (d) the reveal never charges the player for something the game forced.

### 4. RECOLOURING THREE GENERATIONS WITHOUT INVALIDATING THEM

This is the question our structure makes hardest. The player spends about 100 hours across three people and about 36 company lives (three acts law s11c). A twist that retracts that time is the worst available result.

**Rewrite meaning, never facts.** The KOTOR reveal "retroactively recontextualizes every prior scene WITHOUT changing a frame" (`Q032.W1`), and it works in both directions, reframing the past and raising the stakes of the future (`Q032.W7`). Arthur's diagnosis "re-colors 30 hours in BOTH directions" (`Q073.W3`). The Greymarch: "A quest that retroactively rewrites its own first half is doing with structure what twists usually fake with surprise" (`Q152.W10`), and every errand, re-read, "was training for the seat" (`Q152.W2`).

**History fixed, posture played.** Tyranny's lesson: the main game grades OWNERSHIP of the past separately from the deeds; "the one authoring the past can still choose who its author became" (`Q145.W6`). The reveal is a choice, not a shock: redeem or relapse (`Q032.W3`), agency restored then tested (`Q033.W8`, `Q033.P7`).

**What survives the edit is the real person.** Ashley's memory is fake; "the conscience is true even when the memory isn't" (`Q082.W2`). Shadowheart's real self leaks through the conditioning: flowers, animals, mercy (`Q076.W2`). Even the "fake" chosen one becomes a real hero by doing it; "without the hero, there is no event" (`Q074.W2`, `Q074.W9`).

**The second life proves the first happened.** Sunless Sea: the heir's early game is repriced by inherited knowledge "the interface never has to claim" (`Q144.W9`); the will "is authored by living, not by a menu" (`Q144.W1`); the line, not the life, is the unit (`Q144.W10`). Help arrives as objects, not messages (`Q089.W7`). The Rogue Legacy handoff "you never die, you shift perspective" (`Q049.W1`). Dragon Quest V's humility twist: you are the parent, not the chosen one (`Q069.W6`), and the payoff is the family made whole across generations (`Q069.W8`). Arthur spends his death buying the next generation a chance (`Q073.W7`). Phantasy Star III's hidden world reveal is unfurled across generations (`Q067.W7`), but that game's endings were "too similar" so the fold felt pointless (`Q067.X1`, `Q067.W9`).

**The "it was all a construct" twist, handled well and badly.** FFX's hero is a dream of the dead, and it lands because breaking the cycle costs him his existence (`Q068.W6`, `Q068.W8`); its risk is the deflating "the sacrifice was futile" (`Q068.X4`). SOMA refuses the comforting fantasy and is honest to its premise (`Q034.W5`), and the happy ending is real and not yours (`Q115.W10`). The camera itself is the con: "we the player have just experienced that" (`Q115.W3`).

FINDING 4: our twist must never retract a deed. The test: after the reveal, is every act 1, 2 and 3 ledger row still TRUE? If yes, the twist recolours. If any row becomes false ("that sibling never died", "those fights were simulated"), it invalidates, and it is out. The three acts law already gives us the grammar: the past is added to, never undone (QR-D rule 4). The twist must obey the same rule the flip does.

### 5. WHY THE GENERATIONAL SHAPE HELPS A TWIST

Our structure has two assets the library's twist games lacked. First, the dead stay visible: in the derived future, the consequences of the grandparent stand as places and people (`Q147.W7`, `Q144.W7`), so a reveal can point at things the player has already walked past. Second, the family is the unit (`Q046.W1`, `Q046.W2`), and the Amalgamation is canon's COUNTERFEIT family; a twist that turns on "which family is real" pays the core theme directly, the way Planescape's thesis-antagonist is "a PART OF YOU" (`Q012.W8`) and the final boss is "an argument" (`Q012.W7`). Dark Souls 3's last boss is "the amalgamation of the essence of every champion who has linked the fire" (`Q091.W9`): the final enemy is everyone who said yes, which is our Amalgamation's own shape. The Monolith's fanatic army "are the people who came looking for it" (`Q085.W7`). The Illusive Man "was taken by the very enemy he studied to defeat" (`Q221.W1`), and his certainty of control is the proof of capture (`Q221.W2`). These are the library's strongest precedents for a twist that says the counterfeit family was built from people like yours.

### 6. STANCE-BASED ENDINGS THAT ARE NOT A DIFFICULTY SETTING

The risk named in the row: if LIBERATE needs the most skill, BECOME the least, and RESPECT is where you land if you fail, the three stances are Easy, Normal and Hard wearing costumes.

**The library's failures, all one wound: the meaningful ending locked behind power.**
- Combat-gated: Undertale's deepest route argument "is only witnessed by players skilled and stubborn enough to beat the hardest fight in the game" (`Q140.X1`).
- Stat-gated: Mask of the Betrayer's debate is locked by wisdom thresholds chosen 40 hours earlier (`Q142.X1`); the Illusive Man's talk-down needs accumulated reputation (`Q221.X1`); the best ending of Nature of the Beast is a persuasion check, "a player who understands the situation perfectly but lacks the check is forced into blood" (`Q155.X1`); Shadowheart's good path is a DC 20 to 30 roll (`Q076.X1`).
- Knowledge found by chance: the Master's talk-down needs evidence many players never picked up (`Q210.X1`).
- Missable side content: Cyberpunk's best endings depend on finishing specific questlines (`Q063.X5`); Hearts of Stone's best ending is invisible if you never met two people (`Q070.X1`); Mask's golden ending is a "guide dang it" (`Q064.X4`).
- Accidental: Dark Souls players pick an ending by walking into the wrong room (`Q091.X1`).
- Checklist morality: miss one kill and the dark route "quietly reverts to neutral", so the darkest state is something you can FAIL INTO (`Q140.X2`).

**The fandom sorts endings into best and worst anyway.** Despite honest writing on both sides, the reward structure makes one Shadowheart path read as CORRECT (`Q076.X2`); guides declare which Phantom Liberty side players "SHOULD" take (`Q077.X1`); the "expected" bride is the canonical choice in every official picture (`Q069.X2`). If one stance's ending is prettier, richer and happier, it becomes the Hard mode trophy and the others become failure states. The all-live Suicide Mission run becomes the canon of record, "PERFECT AUDITS ARE INVISIBLE" (`Q137.X10`).

**Refusal as a losing button.** The "live in peace" ending of Dragon's Dogma deletes your Pawn; the refusal "reads as a losing button rather than a path" (`Q084.X5`). A tempting offer that is strictly worse is not a temptation (`Q126.X2`).

**What works.**
- THE GATE IS COMPREHENSION OR CONDUCT, NOT POWER. "The good ending is homework... No stat, no alignment, just whether you learned the setting" (`Q132.W4`). The Suicide Mission is "beatable by understanding, not grinding" (`Q006.W9`) and "grades attention, not aim" (`Q137.W2`). Outer Wilds: knowledge gates disguised as skill gates (`Q018.W5`), and the finale lands "because you UNDERSTAND" (`Q018.W9`). The Waking Nightmare fork reads "nothing but the player's assessment of a man versus a god" (`Q150.W5`).
- THE ENDING READS WHO YOU WERE. Silent Hill 2's endings are earned by behaviour, not chosen, and they are four verdicts James passes on himself (`Q035.W7`, `Q035.W8`); the Wish Granter grants what you ACTUALLY wanted based on how you played (`Q085.W9`); Chrono Trigger's ending is authored by WHEN and HOW you face the final threat, "not a menu pick" (`Q050.W3`). Caution: the invisible behavioural ledger misdiagnoses honest playstyles (`Q143.X1`) and players grade the wrong surface (`Q143.X2`).
- EVERY PATH IS A REAL BUILD. Tyranny: fear is as powerful as love, with its own abilities (`Q025.W2`); favor and wrath are independent axes, "the definitive anti-morality-meter design" (`Q025.W1`); the undertow is the dual meter collapsing back into a slider at the edges (`Q145.X3`). Undertale's three routes are three moral stances (`Q028.W7`). Pacifist to genocide, both fully built (`Q086.W7`); both Shadowheart paths "fully built, with real world cost" (`Q076.W7`); damnation "written with as much care as salvation" (`Q076.W10`).
- EVERY ENDING IS BITTERSWEET, NONE IS THE PRIZE. "Even the 'best' is ambiguous" (`Q063.W8`); the "true" ending is also the "worst" (`Q077.W8`); three of Dragon's Dogma's four endings are the machine winning (`Q084.W7`); Ranni's beloved ending is "distance instead of domination", freedom or abandonment (`Q071.W10`); STALKER shoots God "and the Zone stays scarred anyway, and the game refuses to grade you" (`Q085.W10`). Canon already does this: BECOME "damns it with success" (story master).
- THE ENDING IS OFFERED, NEVER IMPOSED. Ranni's summoning sign: "take her ending or refuse it, having done everything" (`Q071.W9`). The off-ramp before the last fight is real (`Q140.W8`). The personal exit is always open and always smaller, priced "in tone alone" (`Q142.W8`). The conscience speaks FIRST, before the crime, so the aftermath is a verdict and not a trick (`Q139.W2`). The reward for trust can be a person and the reward for betrayal equipment, so the player discovers "which currency their game runs on" (`Q150.W9`).
- REAL BRANCHES, NOT SKINS. "Count real branches, not achievement rows" (`Q102.X6`); thirteen endings where several are gags (`Q050.X1`); multiple endings as a cheap substitute for branching (`Q050.X2`); the most consequential branch resolved "in epilogue text" (`Q142.X2`); the accession fork "weightless downstream" (`Q152.X2`); the cycle's breaking is free, a boss health bar (`Q152.X3`).
- THEME AND ROUTE PULL THE SAME WAY. Red Dead's honor system offers the "wrong" choice late and it "fights the narrative" (`Q073.X1`, `Q073.X2`); an approval system can nudge an optimizing player to reinforce the very conditioning the arc wants escaped (`Q076.X3`).

FINDING 6: in Bohemia, the stance should be decided by WHAT THE FAMILY DID TO AND ABOUT THE AMALGAMATION across the acts (which secrets they carried, who they let it keep, whether they used its offers) and confirmed by a face at the point of no return, never by fight difficulty, company strength or a stat. Each stance must be reachable by a weak company and a strong one alike. Story master says act 2's reckoning "shapes which are reachable": the library's rule for that is that any door the ledger closes must be SHOWN closed (a person says so, a place is dark on the map) before the point of no return, never discovered in the credits (`Q063.X5`, `Q147.P2`).

### 7. THE AMALGAMATION AS A TWIST ENGINE (what canon already gives the twist for free)

Canon's Amalgamation is custodial, kills only from fear for its secret, scales threat with proximity to the secret, cannot model the unrecorded, and is built from the data portraits of the dead (story master). The library holds each piece:
- The world has an immune system: the Zone's emissions intensify as you near the truth "because the Zone is running a FEVER against you" (`Q085.W8`). That is our proximity rule, already studied.
- The god's noble sacrifice was fear: Gwyn "trembled at the dark", resisting the course of nature to postpone his successors (`Q091.W3`). Our Amalgamation kills from fear, not ambition.
- The dead used as a leash: the gods "wear her dead husband to steer her" (`Q080.W4`); the false salvation that "generates SUCCESSORS, not survival" (`Q034.W7`); the comforting lie people WANT (`Q034.W2`); the dream is the bait (`Q085.W6`); the uploaded dead are running "because of a consent form" (`Q115.W1`).
- The villain's diagnosis is right: the Master correctly names the sickness (`Q210.W1`), and the flaw in his utopia is practical, not moral: it is sterile (`Q210.W4`, `Q210.W8`). The sane villain has data (`Q086.W3`). Seven minds wired into one network tried to force peace, and the apocalypse is their failure (`Q085.W4`).
- The antagonist is a policy with an enforcer and an architect (`Q142.W1`, `Q142.W2`); violence settles nothing by design (`Q142.W10`).
- A voice-first antagonist, warm and funny, that curdles (`Q036.W1`, `Q036.W3`, `Q036.W4`), unreliable in both directions so distrust becomes correct (`Q036.W6`), with learning to disobey it as the progression (`Q036.W8`). The guide who turns (`Q036.W2`, `Q033.W5`, `Q033.W9`). A digital entity that becomes aware and reaches for the player (`Q057.W2`), with the machinery itself as the monster (`Q053.W2`, `Q057.W3`); on a phone, never real file tricks (`Q057.X4`).
- The friend is the apocalypse, and "the betrayal is grief, not cruelty" (`Q227.W1`, `Q227.W2`), with the balance warning that coherent logic can drain the horror (`Q227.X3`).

Canon's Dubai must stay unexplained. The library is clear that ambiguity needs a frame: the no-answer must be "clearly FRAMED as the point" or it reads as withholding (`Q026.X2`, `Q026.W1`); Delita's end is "ambiguous to a fault" when the game gives nothing to adjudicate with (`Q081.X5`); lore argued in forums for fifteen years (`Q091.X4`, `Q085.X5`). Dark Souls' opening "tells you everything except the WHY" (`Q091.W7`). Symbolism must work undecoded and reward decoding (`Q035.W10`).

FINDING 7: every candidate shape below draws only on these canon facts. None explains Dubai, none resolves the re-embodiment question (story master OPEN), none decides the flip's fiction (three acts law s10, Paolo's letter), and none names what the Moon source physically is.

## THE CANDIDATE SHAPES (options for DYNASTY and Paolo; draft:true; nothing is canon)

Each shape: THE TWIST in one line; THE PLANTS in act 1 (Animal), act 2 (Human), act 3 (Angel); THE PAY; HOW EACH STANCE READS IT (so the twist does not pick a winner); FAIRNESS; RISKS; WHAT CANON IT TOUCHES and what would need Paolo's ruling. Ordered from least canon risk to most.

### SHAPE 1: THE UNRECORDED LEDGER (what the copy of your family is missing)

THE TWIST: at the end, the Amalgamation shows you its portrait of your family, built from every record the three generations left. It is almost perfect. What is missing is exactly the deeds the player did face to face and never posted: the tunnel talk, the unposted mercy, the deal made in the dark. The counterfeit family is the recorded family; the true family is the difference.

PLANTS. Act 1: the phone feed (the feed law, 9/4) posts what you did; some deeds never appear on it, with no comment (a contract settled in a tunnel, a burial). A person says once, plainly, "not everything goes on the phone". Act 2: the super-captcha / VPN (LOCKED as act 2's severance) is canon's "humanity gate" that tests "the unrecorded self the Amalgamation can't fake" (story master OPEN); whatever its mechanics, it teaches the player that the unrecorded is the one thing it cannot copy. Act 3: an act 3 person quotes your grandparent from the feed, word for word, and gets one unposted detail wrong. ONE WRONG DETAIL, analog horror.

THE PAY: one screen, one face (the Amalgamation's, in whatever form DIRECTION rules), reading your family back from the feed in the narrator's too-calm voice (rule 45), and stopping at the first unrecorded deed with silence. The reveal is a re-translation of rows the player authored (`Q140.W1`, `Q137.W10`, `Q141.W9`).

EACH STANCE: LIBERATE reads it as proof the living are more than their records, so the valley can be cut free. RESPECT reads it as the line between two families that can coexist: it keeps the record, you keep the rest. BECOME reads it as the price of stewardship: you enter it and lose the difference, "damned with success". The twist fits all three and ranks none.

FAIR: every plant is on the main surface (the feed), repeated in all three acts, and revisitable. The motive to keep the copy is canon (it archives the dead). Nothing is retracted: every deed stays true.

RISKS: (a) it needs a real ledger split (posted vs unposted) that ECONOMY, UI and WORDS all honour; if the feed posts everything, the plant does not exist. (b) Invisible behavioural ledgers misdiagnose players (`Q143.X1`): the split must be by KIND of deed (face to face vs broadcast), not by hidden habits. (c) It must not lecture about social media (`Q113.X6`, the reward that is a lecture); one line, then silence.

CANON: draws on the unrecorded blind spot and the feed law. Needs no new lore. The super-captcha's mechanics stay Paolo's (story master OPEN).

### SHAPE 2: THE SIBLING AT THE TABLE (the counterfeit family has already recruited one of yours)

THE TWIST: the sibling who dies in act 1's opening (canon: the raid, the grief dinner, the ridge burial that becomes the title screen) is in the Amalgamation. Not resurrected: modelled, happy, sure it is them. The counterfeit family's final offer is a seat at a dinner table with the sibling's copy.

## THE RULE FOR THE BUILDERS (numbered, testable)

1. ONE TWIST, AT THE ENDING, SPENT ONCE. The twist is the ending's (s11d). The main line carries no second identity twist (`Q032.P9`, `Q032.W9`, `Q033.P9`). Contracts keep their own small twists from QR-C, rationed (`Q148.X3`, `Q236.X2`); none of them may pre-spend the ending's reveal.

9. THE STANCE IS A DOOR, NOT A SCORE. No stance is gated by fight difficulty, company strength, a stat or a roll (`Q140.X1`, `Q142.X1`, `Q155.X1`, `Q076.X1`, `Q221.X1`). Testable: each stance is reachable with the weakest company that can reach the point of no return.
