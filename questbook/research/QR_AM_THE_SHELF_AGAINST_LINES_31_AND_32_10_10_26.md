# QR-AM: THE SHELF AGAINST LINES 31 AND 32

QUESTION: row [lines 31 and 32] in VAMILY.md. Run all 210 designs in questbook/designs/ against THE CHECKLIST's lines 31 and 32 (QR-G), fix in place what fails without changing the premise, and move every CHECKLIST line from 30 to 32.

STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35). Author: QUESTS lane, round 10/10.

## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

210 designs run. 200 pass both lines as written. 10 failed and are fixed in place: 9 on line 31, 2 on line 32 (one design, QD-AE12, failed both). No premise changed; every fix adds a road or a price, and nothing was cut. The shelf's line 31 failure has one cause: the theme sat behind a fight the player had to WIN. It came in two shapes. Five beast and clearing jobs had every road end in a fight (`Q216.X1`, `Q183.X3`). Four jobs put a fight in front of the real choice, so the talk could only happen after a win (`Q095.X3`, `Q098.X2`, `Q017.X1`). The line 32 failure is smaller and older: a kind side that costs nothing, so the impossible choice turns easy (`Q226.X2`, `Q231.X3`, `Q157.X1`). The shelf is better on 32 than expected, because QR-X's house rule under line 30 already made most designs price their kind side (`Q108.X2`, `Q139.P2`).

## HOW THE TWO LINES WERE READ

LINE 31 (`Q216.X1`, `Q183.X3`, `Q199.X3`, `Q197.X1`): "Can the theme be met without winning a fight, and does a fight never decide what a conversation should?" Read as two tests.
- (31a) THE THEME HAS A ROAD WITH NO WIN IN IT. At least one road that reaches the design's point (its done, its landing, or its real fork) does not need a won fight. A priced refusal counts only if the theme is met by it, not when it simply walks away (a refusal that leaves the dogs in the yard does not meet "the kitchen gets its water"). A fight that is offered beside a talk road, a slow road or a paid road passes. A fight board without killing (netting, driving, a push where nobody strikes back) is still a fight and does not count as the other road on its own.
- (31b) NO FIGHT DECIDES A TALK. If the design's fork is a conversation (who to name, what to tell, whose side), a fight may follow a choice but never settle it. A fight the player chooses as his answer (the old champion who asks for one, `Q204.X3`'s honest offer) passes, as long as a spoken road sits beside it.
Line 31 is not line 23. Line 23 guards the emotional SCENE and the lost fight (`Q095.X3`, `Q098.X2`); line 31 guards the THEME. A design can pass 23 (the scene is talk, a lost fight is a result) and still fail 31 (every road to the result is a fight).

LINE 32 (`Q226.X2`, `Q231.X3`, `Q157.X1`, `Q161.X3`, `Q204.X3`): "Are both sides of a two-person choice, a bargain or a refusal equally built, so neither is the obvious pick?" Read as: no side is cheaper AND kinder AND as well paid at once. If one side costs nothing and also pays as well or better than the others, or carries no risk while the others carry some, it is the obvious pick and fails. A stated temptation with its number on the button passes under THE HOUSE RULE (c) (`Q166.W4`, `Q148.W8`), and a priced refusal that pays less for less work passes under (b) (`Q139.P2`). Three equal-pay roads that differ in what the world becomes pass when each road costs something different (`Q004.W4`, `Q097.W10`), which is the shelf's usual shape. A choice with no stakes on purpose (QD-K08, `Q004.W4`, `Q010.W3`) passes: nothing is easy where nothing is tempting.

## THE COMMONEST FAILURES

1. **THE BEAST JOB WITH NO ROAD BUT BLOOD (5 of 10).** QD-AA01, AA02, L03, L10, O01. A hunt or a clearing written as three fight variants: fight here, fight there, fight with a twist. The rest of the AA bestiary already had the fix, which is why the shelf could copy it: move the food (QD-AA03, AA06), move the trough (AA04), burn the reeds (AA07), wait for the old bull to die (AA08), tell the keeper (AA18). The library says the same: the theme swallowed by a firefight (`Q216.X1`, `Q183.X3`), and the cure is a second verb that is not a weapon (`Q087.X4`, `Q084.X2`). Our own law makes it sharper: with fights of 2 to 15 minutes and the hours on the world, a job that is only a fight is a job most players skip.
2. **THE FIGHT AS A TOLL BOOTH (4 of 10).** QD-AE12, AE13, T04, T13. The design's heart is a talk (a grave, a door, which place gets light, a mother's debt), but a routine fight stands in front of it and nothing says what happens if it is lost or skipped. Line 23 did not catch these, because the scene itself is talk; line 31 does. The fix is always the same two parts: a slower or paid way round (`Q087.X4`, `Q146.W1`) and a sentence that a lost fight costs time, never the choice (`Q095.X3`, `Q017.X1`).
3. **THE FREE KIND SIDE (2 of 10).** QD-C04, AE12. The kind road cost nothing, and in C04 it also won the best reward, which line 30 flagged and the old bend excused. The library calls this the impossible choice made easy (`Q226.X2`, `Q231.X3`): once one side is more likable AND cheaper, the player is not choosing (`Q157.X1`, `Q161.X3`). The fix is a real cost on the kind side, said before the pick: a person still put out (C04), a companion who will not ride (AE12), the grief shown as behaviour, not a number (`Q041.W3`, `Q125.X9`).

## WHAT PASSED, AND WHY IT PASSED

The shelf passes line 32 mostly because QR-X and QR-AC already did the work. Three equal-pay roads that cost different things (time, standing, a person) are the shelf's default shape (`Q004.W4`, `Q097.W10`), and the priced refusal is on nearly every contract (`Q139.P2`, `Q140.W8`, `Q142.W8`). The lie roads already net the same as the truth (`Q125.X7`, `Q121.X2`, `Q130.X9`). Line 31 passes on most of the shelf because the 9/29 rule of three ways through (QR-AC line 9) put a talk road or a slow road beside almost every fight; the 10 fails are the designs that rule missed, or that hid their fight in front of the choice rather than in the middle of it. Two designs looked like fails and are not: QD-T16 (the old champion asks for the fight himself; refusing and sparring are both spoken roads) and QD-B03 (the quarrel may end in a dawn fight only if the player chose not to judge it; hearing the accused out is the talk road). A fight a person asks for, beside a no, is the person's answer, not the game's (`Q126.X4`, `Q137.X9`).
