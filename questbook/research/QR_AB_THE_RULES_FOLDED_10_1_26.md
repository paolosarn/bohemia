## THE HOUSE RULE (the sentence now in both QR-G and QR-L)

CONTRACTS PAY ONE FEE ON EVERY BRANCH THAT COUNTS AS DONE; EVENTS AND MAIN BEATS MAY CARRY ONE STATED TEMPTATION, WITH
THE NUMBER ON THE BUTTON, AND THE OTHER SIDE FULLY BUILT.

Why it is the right reading, by id. The contract half: zero reward difference makes a choice purely ethical
(`Q004.W4`); the truth and the lie pay the same (`Q097.W10`); when the moral choice also pays more it is no dilemma
(`Q108.X2`); reward economies that tilt the fork are the flaw (`Q070.X2`, `Q130.X9`, `Q125.X7`, `Q190.X1`, `Q191.X1`,
`Q171.X3`, `Q184.X2`, `Q164.W4`). The event half: the miracle gets priced (`Q204.W5`); a cartoonish price tempts
nobody (`Q204.X3`); the temptation has a number on it, the only kind that tempts (`Q166.W4`). The "fully built" half:
the underbuilt other side is its own flaw (`Q226.X2`, `Q231.X3`, `Q157.X1`, `Q161.X3`).
