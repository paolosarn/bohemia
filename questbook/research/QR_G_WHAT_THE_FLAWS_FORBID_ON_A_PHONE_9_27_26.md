## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The library was studied on PC and console, and only 4 of the 693 flaws are about a phone directly (`Q060.X4`, `Q141.X3`, `Q092.X3`, `Q057.X4`). The phone does its damage by AMPLIFYING. A player who plays for five minutes, puts the phone in a pocket, and comes back two days later forgets everything the game did not show, so the families that hurt most are the invisible ones: things that break silently (28), clocks you cannot see (12), order traps (8), paths only a wiki can find (25), the point hidden in optional content (31), and state nobody can read back (6). Together that is 110 flaws, 16% of the library, and they share one cause: the game knew something and did not show it. On a phone the player never remembers what the game did not show. Bohemia's own rules make this harder, not easier: NOTHING POPS UP and TEXT COMES FROM A MOUTH, so every warning, every clock and every "you broke this" has to be a person, a place or a thing on the map or the grid, never a banner. The second big lesson is size. A contract must fit a sitting: one leg, one place, three ways in, and a loss that costs a day of map time, never a day of the player's life (`Q093.X2`, `Q128.X3`, `Q111.X2`). The third is the no. DECLINING IS FREE is already our law, and 21 flaws show what happens when a refusal is punished, empty or missing (`Q131.X7`, `Q134.X2`, `Q084.X5`); the library also holds the fix, the priced refusal inside a taken job (`Q139.P2`) and the free off-ramp before one (`Q140.W8`). The biggest family in the whole library, reward meters that turn a choice into math (58), matters less on a phone and is already killed by our no-karma-bar stance (`Q125.P17`).

## HOW THE COUNT WAS MADE

1. Every one of the 693 X ids (132 studies, `Q017` to `Q152`; studies `Q001` to `Q016` carry no X ids in the index) was placed in exactly ONE family. First pass: a keyword pass over each flaw's title, then its body. Second pass: all 693 titles and lessons were read by hand and 260 placements were moved. The full list, every id in its family, is the APPENDIX at the end, so any lane can re-cut it.
2. A flaw that fits two families went where it would hurt on a phone. Example: `Q129.X5` (the Tenpenny massacre on a hidden clock) could be "the point is optional" but went to HIDDEN CLOCKS.
3. PHONE BITE is a 1 to 5 score for how much a thumb, a small screen and a five-minute sitting make that family worse than it was on the machine it was studied on. 5 = the phone makes it fatal. 1 = the phone does not change it. The ranking is bite first, count second.
4. Thirty families. The first 25 are the ranked list below.

## THE FINDINGS

**F1. The phone is almost absent from the library, so its lessons are amplifications.** Four flaws name a device. `Q060.X4`: precise positioning on a grid needs touch designed first, not patched later. `Q141.X3`: every evidence class must exist in two channels (a name spoken must also be written), which matters on a phone played with the sound off. `Q092.X3`: platform decisions are permanent. `Q057.X4`: tricks that touch the real file system do not port to a phone. Everything else in this page is a PC or console flaw made worse by a thumb and a pocket.

**F2. "You cannot see it" is the phone's disease.** SILF, TIME, ORDR, WIKI, OPTL and JOUR together hold 110 flaws. The library keeps confirming it: `Q095.X1` calls a silent timer "a bug wearing a design costume"; `Q128.X2` says "players lose the chain without learning they lost it"; `Q112.X5` counts the sixth confirmation that silent sequencing is a bug; `Q071.X2` says a quest that needs a wiki has outsourced its design. On a PC a player can alt-tab to a guide in a long evening. On a phone, in a five-minute sitting, the player who hits the wall closes the app.

**F3. Our two hard rules collide here, and the collision is the design problem.** NOTHING POPS UP forbids the usual cure (a banner, a marker, a "quest failed" toast). So every tell must be DIEGETIC: a face on the road, a changed prop on the settlement screen, a line in the city-view phone's feed. The library has this cure too: `Q146.P4` (the world warns about itself), `Q037.P2` (flood the world with in-world direction), `Q128.X2`'s own law (one diegetic tell per broken link), `Q143.P3` (an NPC reads your conduct aloud, once).

**F4. Size is a phone rule.** `Q148.W10` praises the contract as "the genre's best-scoped quest container" because it runs notice to payment in one sitting. The worst loss flaws are all size mismatches: `Q093.X2` (days of progress lost in one raid), `Q128.X3` (one failed corridor wastes three real-time days), `Q111.X2` (failure costs a full loop), `Q094.X4` (the best content needs a second job). A five-minute sitting cannot carry an hour-long leg.

**F5. The refusal family is our rule's evidence.** 21 flaws are about a no that is punished (`Q084.X5`), empty (`Q134.X2`), missing (`Q103.X1`) or invisible (`Q131.X7`). Paolo's DECLINING IS FREE solves the offer. The library adds the other half: once a contract is taken, the refusal inside it must still be a path with a price, never a deletion (`Q139.P2`, `Q142.P3`).

**F6. The biggest family is not the phone's problem.** METR (58) is the library's loudest finding: a morality score or a juicy reward turns a dilemma into arithmetic (`Q121.X2`, `Q125.X4`, `Q078.X3`). It matters, but it does not get worse on a phone, and Bohemia already rejects a karma bar (`Q125.P17`, `Q149.P3`). It ranks 16th here for that reason, not because it is small.

### 2. TIME, hidden and hard clocks (12, bite 5)

IN BOHEMIA: the Battle Brothers map passes time as you travel, so every contract with a deadline is a clock the player spends by tapping. ## THE RULE FOR THE BUILDERS

1. SHOW IT OR IT DID NOT HAPPEN. Every fail, close, deadline and cross-act consequence has a diegetic tell the player meets within two map stops or one open of the city view. (Families 1, 2, 3, 17.)
2. NO WALL-CLOCK TIME. The world moves on travel and on the beat, never while the app is closed, never while a person is talking unless the scene says so. (Family 2.)
3. ONE SITTING, ONE LEG. A contract's work fits one place and one block of the city, cut as the fight board; a failure costs that leg only. (Families 5, 13, 15.)
4. TWO POINTERS PER OBJECTIVE, NO OUTSIDE HELP. (Family 4.)
5. THE NO IS FREE AT THE OFFER AND PRICED INSIDE. (Family 9.)
6. THE THESIS IS ON THE ROAD. (Families 10, 14.)
7. EVERY ACT STANDS ALONE; every plant has a default read. (Families 3, 11.)
8. THREE SHORT LINES, TWO CHANNELS. (Families 7, 8.)
