## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

The new 84 are mostly the library's heaviest single-quest dilemmas (the honest devil, the cycle between parent and
child, the hoarded cure, the allocation switch, the false paradise) plus ONE study that changes the most: `Q236`, a
full teardown of the Battle Brothers contract hall. `Q236` confirms almost every rule QR-A, QR-L, QR-N, QR-P and
QR-S wrote from secondary notes, gives the real haggle numbers, and names two places we deliberately differ (asking
for more costs relations there and nothing here; the advance loses money to rounding there and is an honest loan
here). It also names the one real threat to the free no: the daily wage makes declining expensive on the treasury
even when it writes nothing to the ledger (`Q236.X4`), so the free no needs a floor of income. Across the other 83,
the strongest single pattern is a FLAW that recurs ten times: the best ending hidden behind a stat check or
meta-knowledge (`Q155.X1`, `Q162.X1`, `Q170.X1`, `Q186.X1`, `Q207.X2`, `Q210.X1`, `Q221.X1`, `Q156.X3`, `Q175.X1`,
`Q179.X3`); the second is a mechanical reward tilting a moral fork (`Q190.X1`, `Q191.X1`, `Q171.X3`, `Q184.X2`). Both
become checklist lines. For our three generations the new shelf is rich: the parent racing the child's first kill
(`Q170.W1`, `Q219.W6`), the grievance witnessed in one generation and forgiven in another (`Q155.P3`), the heir who
answers for a self he cannot remember (`Q215.W9`), the verdict a town carries for decades (`Q234.W3`). For act three
the new shelf finally gives the rocket and the Network their own studies: a faith aimed at a destination nobody can
check (`Q190.W1`), a goodbye that is correct (`Q188.W3`), a paradise whose only exit is death (`Q212.W2`). One real
contradiction lands on QR-D: `Q187.W6` and `Q194.W2` say understanding a past choice never lets you choose again,
while our three-acts law lets an answering deed re-derive the future; the page keeps its rule and tightens it (the
answer moves the wound, never erases it).

## PAGE BY PAGE

### QR-A: CONTRACTS AND THE FREE NO

SHARPEN C4 and C6 (no is ungated, declining writes nothing). `Q236.W7`: the accept button is the only promise;
browsing, reading and haggling are free of commitment. `Q236.N2`: decline returns to the hall, the offer stays,
nothing is written. `Q236.P3` states it as a port: only accept, finish, fail and drop are deeds. This is the first
primary study behind C6; until now C6 stood on `Q126.W3` and Paolo's words.

SHARPEN C9 (one open contract; folded 10/1 from rule 51: QR-A C9 is now TWO slots, and the test below reads "while
both are TAKEN"). `Q236.N1` and `Q236.W2`: a second offer is readable but not takeable, and the game
teaches the one-at-a-time rule as a greyed button, not a speech. New test for C9: while one contract is TAKEN, every
other offer screen still opens and shows its terms; only accept is disabled.

CONTRADICT (the spirit of C6). `Q236.X4`: the daily wage burn makes refusing expensive in practice; the decline is
free in the ledger but not on the treasury. A no that means a missed meal for the company is not free, whatever the
ledger says. ADD C19: A NO IS NEVER A SLOW DEATH. A second income always exists (SCAVENGE on the settlement screen,
QR-O rule 11) that covers the company's keep at a thin margin, so one declined offer never forces the next yes. Test:
a company that declines every offer for ten map days and scavenges each stop does not go bankrupt at default tuning.

ADD C20: THE CLIENT WANTS SOMETHING BESIDES THE FEE. `Q236.X3`: the Battle Brothers elder has a portrait and a mood
but no want beyond the fee and no life on the map; the study's own law is a person in a place with a want the player
can see, whose place changes after the hand-in. QR-G line 25 already says this for every item; QR-A lacks it.

SHARPEN C3 (clean terms in one breath). `Q159.W7`: the terms are clear and taken anyway; the complicity chosen with
full information is worse than being fooled. `Q236.W3`: the skulls are a reading, so "too strong for me" is the
player's informed call. Clean terms are not only fair, they are what make the yes his.

SHARPEN C8 (the world keeps world-state, not a name). `Q158.W7`: ignore it and the man dies off-screen; the freedom
to fail by absence makes the choice to try meaningful. But `Q158.P4` and `Q158.X3` want a failure to LAND and be
recorded against the player. That is right for a TAKEN contract (C12, C13) and wrong for a declined one. Rule of
reading: the harm of a declined job lands in the world without the player's id (C8, `Q055.W1`); the harm of a taken
job that failed lands with it.

CONTRADICT C7 inside C17 only. `Q161.W5`: the refusal cracks the disguise; saying no reveals the monster under the
kindly face. On a contract this is banned (C7: the no is answered once, kindly). For the main quest's honest-devil
offers (C17) it is a tool: the counterfeit family's face may change when refused, and the refusal may be a recorded
stand (`Q126.P24` is the comfort-verb law this stays inside).

ADD C21: A CLIENT WHO REFUSES TO PAY. `Q236.N5`: at hand-in the client may refuse (a twist); take it by force or leave
unpaid, which makes the player price his own honour. QR-C's T11 and T12 remove the payer; this adds the payer who is
present and says no. Close it as DONE (the deed is the client's) unless the player takes the pay by force, which is
his own deed. It rides the twist ration (C15).

SHARPEN C12 (failing is not dropping). `Q236.N4`: in Battle Brothers, walking away after a twist is a FAILED
contract with a relations and renown drop. Ours stays sharper: a twist that proves the client lied VOIDS (QR-C rule
11, `Q003.W1`); a twist that did not lie and is walked away from is DROPPED, priced by C13.

SHARPEN C15 (most contracts are exactly what they say). `Q236.X2`: veteran players treat the contraband caravan and
the bigger camp as expected beats after the tenth time, the same law `Q148.X3` gave. `Q158.W8` names the best shape
for the rare twist: the small favour that hides the weight.

### QR-B: EVENTS ON THE ROAD

SHARPEN rule 11 (tone range per ten). `Q153.W10`: the comic quest placed among grim ones relieves and, by contrast,
sharpens the dread; range is the point. `Q163.W9`: the release valve with a blade. `Q185.W8`: the comedy itself is
the mercy. ADD rule 13: A COMIC EVENT CARRIES ONE STRAIGHT BEAT. `Q153.W6` (one witness in the farce whose grievance
aches) and `Q153.P3` (one grievance sticks) make the rule; `Q185.X1` and `Q204.X1` are the flaws (the joke swallows
the ache). Test: a comic event has one choice or outcome line whose words are not a joke.

ADD rule 14: URGENCY IS IN THE SITUATION, NEVER A TIMER ON THE EVENT SCREEN. `Q226.W4`: the clock removes
deliberation. `Q226.X3`: a clock that is the only thing forcing the choice reads as a gimmick; the impossibility must
be structural (geometry, stakes). `Q206.X2` and `Q206.P3`: a body-count clock must compel, not crush. On our event
screen that means no countdown; the choice "wait and see" exists and costs what the situation says (the water rises,
the party passes).

ADD rule 15: THE QUIET ENCOUNTER HAS NO REWARD, AND IT VARIES. `Q202.W1`: no loot, no plot; the reward is the person.
`Q202.W5`: walking away costs nothing mechanical and misses everything human. `Q202.W9`: the unflagged is the point.
`Q202.X2`: if every quiet encounter is a lonely person with a sad story, the player learns the pattern; lives must be
funny, prickly, boring, evasive. `Q202.X3`: listening that is only clicking through can be faked. On the event
screen, presence is a CHOICE with a cost in map time (stay the night, share the fire), never a gate on reading.

SHARPEN rule 4 (no dominant choice; the kind choice is never also the profitable one). `Q204.X3`: a price on a
person that is cartoonishly evil is no temptation; make the money matter to a desperate company. `Q161.X3` and
`Q157.X1`: a bargain or a seduction that is obviously wrong is not a choice. The greedy choice on a road event must
pay enough to hurt to refuse.

SHARPEN rule 8 (a lost ambush continues). `Q235.W2`: the loss belongs to the attackers, never to the player's
failure; grief without resentment. A lost road fight is written as what THEY did.

SHARPEN rule 7 (a delayed consequence is telegraphed). `Q187.X1`: a choice whose stakes are under-signalled lands as a
gotcha, not a tragedy. One thing in the scene must say that this matters.

### QR-D: PLANTED IN ONE ACT, PAID IN ANOTHER

SHARPEN rule 2 (displaced, not just delayed). `Q187.W1`: the choice detonates hours later and miles away. `Q187.W2`:
the player receives the outcome, discovering his own choice like a stranger reading a result. `Q187.W5`: it arrives
as particular children, not an abstraction. The landing names a small number of specific people.

SHARPEN rule 7 (one clue, never label the hinge). `Q187.X1`: when the stakes of the plant are under-signalled, the
landing feels like punishment for missing information. `Q187.P1` keeps the displacement and the half-knowledge. The
plant says plainly that something is being decided, even if nobody knows how it goes (`Q004.N1`).

CONTRADICT rule 4 and 5 (the answering deed). `Q187.W6`: understanding is not undoing; the removed verb is "choose
again". `Q194.W2`: the past is a walk, not a rewrite; you can see it clearly, never change it. `Q194.W5`: the
reckoning changes the walker, not the walk. Our three-acts law lets the player flip back and do a SECOND deed that
re-derives the future. The page keeps its rule, because the law is Paolo's, and the new studies tighten it: the first
deed stays in the record with its landing (`Q186.W5`: the deed stays real), the second deed moves the wound and never
erases it (rule 5), and `Q162.W3` shows the right shape: the power to undo arrives later and forces the choice AGAIN,
with the first choice's damage in view.

CONTRADICT rule 17 (no confession verb; the act 3 person does not know it was your family). `Q215.W1` and `Q215.W9`:
the player is answerable for a self he cannot remember; owning a crime without the memory of it is the truest kind of
accountability. `Q215.W2`: the victim is owed regardless. Rule 17 banned the heir buying relief with a line. The new
studies add the other side: the heir may CHOOSE to shoulder an ancestor's deed he learned of, and it must cost more
than lying or silence (`Q215.X3`). Amended reading: no comfort verb, but a costly shoulder verb (batteries, standing,
a person's trust), and nobody makes him.

ADD rule 19: THE GRIEVANCE IS WITNESSED IN ONE ERA AND FORGIVEN IN ANOTHER. `Q155.P3`: a prior generation's atrocity
and its refusal to forgive, seen in play, so a later generation's forgiveness carries a history the player lived.
`Q155.W9`: a crime centuries dead and still killing. `Q155.X2` is the flaw it fixes (the grief told, not felt). With
all three acts open, this is our unique strength: the player can stand in act 1 at the crime and in act 3 at the
forgiving.

ADD rule 20: THE UNTOUCHABLE GETS A LATER THREAD. `Q154.P2`: a power beyond reach now carries a discoverable thread a
later generation can pull, or a record that names it. `Q154.X1`: without it, untouchability reads as an unfinished
quest. Act 1 cannot touch the Amalgamation; every act 1 brush with it plants one named thread for act 2 or 3.

ADD rule 21: THE CYCLE RACE. `Q170.W1`: a parent races to stop the child becoming him. `Q219.W6`: the child's first
kill locks the path. `Q219.W2`: the son follows the template, not the sermon. `Q219.P2` and `Q170.P4`: the break is
costly, partial, and has an aftermath shown across time. A gen 2 heir can see a gen 3 descendant's road, and the flip
lets the player race it from both ends.

ADD rule 22: SOME ANCESTORS ARE GUARDED AGAINST. `Q199.W1`: the past wants to be the future. `Q199.W6`: not every
forebear is a foundation. `Q199.P3`: at least one descendant at risk is known. For Project Angel's uploaded dead this
is exact: an ancestor who wants to live again through the living. `Q199.X3`: it must not resolve as one boss kill.

SHARPEN rule 15 (one tell per landing, readable). `Q234.W3` and `Q234.W8`: the town carries an uncertain verdict for
generations, and the player returning years later sees it still shaping lives. `Q234.X3` and `Q234.P4`: if the long
consequence is thin, the verdict feels like a one-time event.

SHARPEN QR-G line 5 and 18 as they apply here. `Q224.X2`: a weighing that depends on a debt from an earlier game
lies flat for players who did not carry it; the debt must be legible in the quest. For a player who flips to act 3
first, every landing carries its own debt in words.

ADD rule 23: THE WAITERS AGED. `Q204.W1`: the child stayed, the love aged. `Q204.W3`: nothing saved the ones who
searched. `Q204.P3`: show the waiting, not a summary. With three acts open, a person or thing kept unchanged in an
earlier act, met again in a later one beside those who waited, is our cleanest time-horror.

### QR-F: THE FIRST SIXTY SECONDS

`Q235` (the founding morning) is the new shelf's opening study, and it cuts both ways.

SHARPEN rule 12 (one name, one contract in the first three minutes). `Q235.W6`: the first task has purpose tied to
the loss. `Q235.P4`: the task that pulls a grieving protagonist back must carry real purpose and be handed over by

SHARPEN rule 3 and the second votes (the game starts in the ruin). `Q235.W7`: the rise starts from absolute zero; the

happened before the first frame; it is a THING found in the first minute (a grave, a table set for one more), never a
scene played at the player. `Q235.W2` keeps what matters: the loss belongs to the attackers.

SHARPEN rule 14 (the first minute is not the loudest). `Q228.W4` shows the opposite design (a deadline in the opening
forces the change). We keep ours: no clock in the first hour; a clock on the main line is spoken first (QR-H rule 4).

STALE (not from the new studies, flagged for the page's owner): rule 2 (one cell per first press) and rule 10 (the
squeeze) describe the dead walk. The first sixty seconds now happen on the map and the first settlement screen.

### QR-G: WHAT THE FLAWS FORBID (THE CHECKLIST)

SHARPEN line 20 (every choice writes a different line; two lead to different states). `Q177.X2`, `Q215.X3`, `Q208.X2`

### QR-J: THE CRISIS IN THE BACKGROUND

ADD rule 19: A CRISIS STAGE MAY HAND THE PLAYER THE SWITCH. `Q169.W1`: infrastructure is a moral act; the console is a
verdict. `Q169.W2`: the output is finite, every allocation leaves someone dark. `Q169.W5`: the console judges nothing.
`Q169.W8`: the consequence is downstream and quiet. Power and water are held by SHARE between factions; a crisis stage
that breaks a share can put one allocation in the company's hands. `Q169.X1` (effects too diffuse) and `Q169.X3` (no
