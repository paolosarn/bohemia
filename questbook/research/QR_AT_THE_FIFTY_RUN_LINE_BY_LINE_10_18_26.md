# QR-AT: THE FIFTY RUN LINE BY LINE

QUESTION: row [checklist the shelf] in VAMILY.md. Forty designs (QD-AP01 to AP20, the picture-change shelf, QR-AP; QD-AQ01 to AQ20, the follower-with-a-job shelf, QR-AQ) and the ten region designs (QD-AR01 to AR10, QR-AR) were never scored line by line against THE CHECKLIST (QR-G). Twenty of them already said "passes all 32". Score all fifty strictly, fix in place what fails without changing the premise, and list them.

STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35). Pages only (rule 88), nothing registered. Author: QUESTS lane, round 10/18.

## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

50 designs run. 2 pass as written (QD-AP06 the chimney and QD-AP18 the light on the map, both events with no fight). 48 failed at least one line and are fixed in place; all 50 now read 32 of 32. The twenty QD-AP designs that already said "passes all 32" did not: 18 of them failed, which is the round's real finding. A stamp written by the author of the design, in the same round as the design, is self-attestation, and it is wrong nine times in ten. The commonest failure by far is THE LOST FIGHT WITH NO RESULT: 40 of 50 designs said what happens when the job goes well and what happens to a man struck down (rule 36), and never what the JOB becomes when the fight is lost, so line 23 ("a lost fight leads to a result, not a reload", `Q095.X3`, `Q098.X2`, `Q017.X1`) and, on the picture shelf, line 1 (the tell of a failure, `Q071.X1`, `Q128.X2`) went unanswered. The second is THE MISSING "I WON'T DO THIS PART" ROAD (line 12, 30 designs, `Q131.X7`, `Q134.X2`, `Q139.P2`). The third is the old one QR-AO found in the hunts: THE MONEY AT THE FORK (line 30, 13 designs, `Q190.X1`, `Q191.X1`, `Q108.X2`).

## HOW THE FIFTY WERE READ

Every design against all 32 lines, strictly; a line that does not apply (18 with nothing planted, 17 with no thesis) passes as n/a. Line 31 is read as QR-AM reads it (31a a road to the theme with no won fight in it; 31b no fight settles a talk). Lines 29 and 30 are read as QR-AO reads them, under THE HOUSE RULE: a contract pays one fee on every road that counts as done; a priced refusal may pay less for less work if a road as kind pays full; a price out of the player's own purse is not a reward (`Q108.X2`, `Q139.P2`); an event may carry one stated temptation with its number on the button (`Q166.W4`, `Q204.X3`). Line 23's "result, not a reload" is read as: the design names in one line what the JOB becomes on a lost fight (a half result, a later and worse road, the place left as it was and shown so), not only what happens to the man struck down.

LINE 8 FOR A FAR JOB (new reading, used on QD-AR and binding on the next far shelf): a far job's leg is the ONE destination's block, where the work and the hand-in happen. The road is travel: its fights, night stops and clues belong to the road (QR-AR rule 4, QR-AG, QR-AS), not to the job, so a night stop the road already sleeps at is not a second destination. All ten region designs pass on that reading; a far job that needs work at two blocks fails it (`Q095.X2`, `Q079.X1`, `Q083.X1`).

## THE THREE FAILURES, BY SHAPE

1. THE LOST FIGHT WITH NO RESULT (40). The shelf's shared template had a struck-down line (rule 36: 20 percent dead, else 30 to 40 days hurt) and nothing for the job itself, so a player who loses the fight on a phone has no idea whether the place stayed dark, the client walked or the board reloads (`Q095.X3`, `Q098.X2`, `Q017.X1`, `Q093.X2`, `Q111.X2`). On the picture shelf the same hole also failed line 1, because the picture only had an AFTER for the good ending (`Q071.X1`, `Q128.X2`, `Q140.X2`). The fix is one line each: the job lands as a half result or a worse road, and the picture shows it (the window stays dark, the mast stays bare, half the town lit). A lost fight is the story getting worse, never a reload (`Q144.X1`, `Q128.X10`).
2. THE MISSING PRICED REFUSAL (30). Most designs had a free no at the offer (line 11) and nothing once the job was taken, which is QR-G OPEN 4's read: the priced refusal FINISHES the contract (`Q131.X7`, `Q134.X2`, `Q134.X10`, `Q075.X4`, `Q103.X1`). The fix is a road D at a smaller fee for a smaller job, and on the picture shelf D is a PARTIAL picture (the pole stays dark, the garden stops at stage one), which keeps rule 86: the change lives on the base's settlement screen.
3. THE MONEY AT THE FORK (13 on line 30, 2 on line 21). Per-head pay on the crueller haul (AR07), a bribe bigger than the fee (AR08), a recruit who joins free on one road (AQ07), the kind road paid half (AQ10, AQ13, AQ17, AP07), the kindest no-fight road paid nothing (AR09). Each is now one fee on every road that counts as done, with kindness bought out of the player's own purse in FOOD, WATER or days (`Q190.X1`, `Q191.X1`, `Q171.X3`, `Q184.X2`, `Q164.W4`, `Q004.W4`, `Q097.W10`, `Q108.X2`, `Q130.X9`, `Q121.X2`).

## WHAT IS STILL NOT CLEAN, STATED
