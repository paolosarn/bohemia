# QR-AS: THE CORRIDOR NIGHTS. WHAT STOPS THE PARTY ON EACH OF THE SEVEN ROADS OUT

The map is now the whole Las Vegas region, and every job out of the city goes down one of seven roads (QR-AR). A road is not empty. It has its own people: the lawmen at the state line, the Mob's collector on the dam road, the boat families on the lake, the lab's man on the mountain pass, the nomads at the only water for a day. This page says which of the ten road events we already wrote (QR-AG) fit which road, and writes the five the region still needs, one for each road the ten leave bare. Every one is a person you can see coming, with a face, two or three answers, and the price written on the button. None is a card. None happens in the first minute.

[bb the corridor] research line: Battle Brothers ties an event to WHERE you are with a requirement list (on a road, in the mountains, on desert terrain, the nearest town 6 or 7 tiles away, daytime, snow or no snow) and to WHAT you carry (a background, a free slot, crowns, food) and picks one from the eligible pile by score (source: wiki dump, unverified, 0710_Events.txt and the pages named below). ## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

A corridor is a road out of the city plus the people who live on it, and a stop belongs to a corridor when its visible cause is something that corridor really has (`Q044.W2`, `Q050.W9`, `Q061.X4`). Sorted that way, the ten events of QR-AG already cover every road at least twice, but they cover the roads as roads (a trike on any shoulder, a blind man on any pavement, a walker at any fire) and miss what makes each corridor itself: the line at Primm where refugees are searched and you are not, the scale house at Railroad Pass where the dam road is taxed by weight, the boat at Boulder Beach that trades a day of shore for a price, the lab man on the mountain pass who made the beasts and wants them to remember him, and the nomads' water on the dry lake when the heat has already cost you a man for the day. Those are the five. Each is a stranger with a face and a visible cause, two or three buttons with the price printed, one button always free, odds printed where there are odds, the narrator never grading (`Q107.W7`, `Q107.X1`, `Q126.W3`, `Q126.X4`). The night camp (rule 113d: the party camps on the road at night, the Battle Brothers way) is where most of them walk in, one a night at most (QR-AR rule 5), never in the first minute, never on the party's first day out (rule 32a; QR-AG rule 9). Far means rarer company and harder stops (FACTIONS' quote of the wiki: trouble scales with distance to civilization), and after a hard hit the kind stop comes (`Q044.W7`).

## 2. THE RULES, READ ONCE (QR-AG's eleven stand; four more for a corridor)

QR-AG's eleven rules hold for every stop on this page without change: a face not a card; the cause is visible; the price is on the button; the button does what it says; no dominant answer; keep moving is always free; never graded; rare; never in the first minute and never before the first full day out; the consequence comes back elsewhere; no fight from a popup (rule 68). The region adds four.

12. A STOP BELONGS TO THE ROAD THAT HAS ITS CAUSE. An event may fire on a corridor only if its visible cause is a thing that corridor really has on WORLD's file or FACTIONS' rows: a greenhouse needs farmland (Moapa, Pahrump, Mesquite), a storm drain needs the city's edge or a wash, a boat needs the lake, a scale house needs the dam road's pass. The seam the library hates most is the thing from nowhere (`Q061.X4`); on a real map the fix is free: the cause is already drawn.
13. THE FAR BAND IS HARDER AND RARER COMPANY. Past a day out, the kind stops thin and the hard ones thicken (FACTIONS' quote: "difficulty of non-contract locations and roaming parties scales with distance to civilization and time"; `Q044.W4`), but the ebb still holds: after a lost fight or a death, the walker at the fire and the boat family's night are weighted up (`Q044.W7`).
14. THE NIGHT CAMP IS THE DOOR. Rule 113d decides the night question the Battle Brothers way: the party camps on the road at night [R] (01_WORLDMAP, NIGHT and CAMPING). A night stop walks INTO the camp, drawn on the map at the party's marker: the walker comes to the fire, the boat family's lantern comes along the shore, the lab man's receiver clicks from the ridge. A day stop is met on the road. The camp is the one place the player is standing still, so it is where a face gets its line out (QR-AR rule 5; `Q006.W10`'s quiet beat before the hard part).
15. ONE ROAD, ONE REGISTER. Each corridor's stops share a mood, so the road is recognisable by what happens on it before the player reads a sign: the dam road is BILLED, the lake road is HOSPITABLE AND WRONG, the state line is SEARCHED, the Searchlight road is THIRSTY, the mountain is WATCHED, the pass is TAXED BY ANIMALS, the north road is DEAD. That is the library's lesson that a place should read as a place with its feet (`Q147.W7`: the world wearing its open quests as terrain) and the analog horror law's one calm wrong thing per road (rule 20 s9).

### 11. THE LINE AT PRIMM (corridor 3, THE STATE LINE; register: SEARCHED)

BATTLE BROTHERS: "Civilwar Refugees". Cooldown 21 days. Requires: on a road, at least 2 brothers. Score 10. "You meet a large group of refugees that are huddled together." Choices: leave them alone (nothing); share provisions (+3 reputation, lose a stack of provisions); a Refugee brother talks to them (+3 reputation, lose provisions, gain an arming sword or falchion); search them for valuables (50%: minus 3 reputation, one brother takes an accident injury, loot; 50%: minus 3 and the loot). Source: wiki dump, unverified, 0392_Civilwar Refugees.txt. Also the library's checkpoint: the gate searches the poor and not the citizen (`Q100.W10`), and the border favour asked across a booth (`Q108.W1`).

OURS: the lawmen's line is drawn across I-15 at the state line, a row of sawhorses and a sheriff's truck with its bar lights on and no fuel to move; a column of people walking up from California is stopped at it and searched, one by one. The person who stops YOU is the column's leader, who has watched the lawmen wave your party through.
FACE: a woman in her forties with a California plate hung round her neck on a cable as a sign, a Bottega-shaped leather tote rubbed grey, one child's hand in hers that is not her child's.
WHERE: the state line at Primm, on the freeway, in the open, at any hour the line is manned (day).
VISIBLE CAUSE: the column is a drawn party walking the other way (QR-B's E7) for at least two legs before the stop, and the line itself is a map object at Primm's south edge, lit; the party passed through it without a search because it is armed and the lawmen take a company's chit (Primm's Caravans trade with everybody). The player saw both.
TRIGGER: the party crosses the line outbound or inbound; the column is present (it is drawn and moving on its own, `Q055.W1`).
BUTTONS:
- SHARE WATER. -1 WATER per four heads in the column (the number printed, e.g. "-5 WATER"). Standing with Primm (the Caravans): plus 1. Later: one of the column stands at Primm's post for hire with a refugee background (PEOPLE 10/17: the Refugees situation at Primm, +35, the biggest tilt in the book).
- SEARCH THEM FOR WHAT THE LINE MISSED. +1 or 2 items from the column's own list (a knife, a hood, a woodcutter's axe, tape, a tin of food: Battle Brothers' loot table C in our skin). "50% one of yours is hurt in the scuffle, a few days." Standing with Primm: minus 3. Anyone in your company with a refugee or soft background: bad mood.
- KEEP MOVING. Free.
NARRATOR: "The line is for people who walk. You did not walk."
LEDGERS: SHARE writes "gave water to the column at the line"; the feed later posts a post with no name from someone who made it to Primm. SEARCH writes "searched the column at the line" to the deed ledger and the lawmen's standing on this road drops (they saw you do what they do and did not like the mirror). KEEP MOVING writes nothing.
WHY: QR-B's E7 (the people walking the other way) finally has its act one event, and the state line is the one place on the map where it is caused by law, not weather. The checkpoint that searches the poor and waves the armed through is the Vault City mechanic exactly (`Q100.W10`), and it is the player's privilege made visible without a word of comment (`Q126.X4`). Scarcity bends mercy at low WATER (`Q031.W3`, `Q108.W3`); the kind button pays later and less (`Q108.X2`, `Q126.X2`); the cruel button's injury odds are printed (`Q107.W7`). The Refugee-background button from Battle Brothers is cut to keep three; PEOPLE may add it as a fourth line of the SHARE button's text when the company has one.
REQUIRES: day 10 or later; at the state line; the column present; at least 2 in the company. COOLDOWN: 21 days (Battle Brothers'). Once per face (the column's leader changes).
ASSET NEEDED (COOK FOUR, the place; PORTRAIT, the face): the line as a map object at Primm's south edge (sawhorses, a sheriff's truck with dead bar lights, a hand-painted LINE sign); the column as a party marker that reads as civilians (a dozen figures, bags, no weapons); her portrait with the plate on the cable.
SOUND NEEDED (SOUNDS): the column's feet on the freeway at a walking pace that is not the party's beat; the bar lights' dead relay ticking; her voice (a squiggle variation; she is from somewhere else and the Spanglish is not hers, rule from the first votes: the player does not speak it, the valley does).

### 12. THE SCALE HOUSE AT RAILROAD PASS (corridor 1, THE DAM ROAD; register: BILLED)

BATTLE BROTHERS: no event page translates a toll. The shape comes from the settlement situation "Ambushed Trade Routes" (a camp on the road raises prices until it is cleared; clearing it has a 50% chance to end the situation; source: wiki dump, unverified, 0670_Drive Off Nomads.txt and the situation page) and from the brigands who "hide beside roads" (FACTIONS' page, the wiki's dev posts). The library supplies the rest: the collector's visit as the world's first acknowledgment that you exist, arriving as a bill (`Q151.W2`, `Q151.N1`); a toll with exactly one bypass that costs something the ledger tracks, because a gate with four bypasses is a doorway (`Q126.P28`, `Q126.X9`); the checkpoint that searches the poor (`Q100.W10`).

OURS: the Mob's collector works the truck scale at the dead casino on Railroad Pass, the one place the dam road squeezes between two hills. Everything that goes to the dam or comes back from it crosses his scale, and he charges by weight.
FACE: a soft-spoken man in a Rick Owens-shaped black tunic to the knee over work boots, reading glasses on a chain, a clipboard whose paper is a casino's old comp sheets, a church-key opener for a pen clip.
WHERE: the casino's truck scale at Railroad Pass, on US-93, the pass between Henderson and Boulder City.
VISIBLE CAUSE: the scale house is a map object with its light on, drawn at the pass from the start of the game; the road crew's hideout (FACTIONS' wild location) sits in the hills above it and its crew is a drawn party; and the party's own HUD shows its cargo weight. The player knew the pass was held before arriving.
BUTTONS:
- PAY BY WEIGHT. -1 BATTERY per 20 kilos of cargo the party carries (printed, e.g. "-6 BATTERIES"); an empty party pays 1. The road to the dam is open to you today and the crew lets you by.
- SHOW THE MOB'S CHIT. Free. Shown only if the company has finished a contract for the Mob (the ledger has the line). He waves you through and writes your name down.
- GO AROUND THROUGH THE HILLS. Free at the scale; +4 HOURS of hills on the clock (printed), and the hills are the crew's (a drawn party you may meet or flee, rule 68). Nothing is written by the collector; the clock is the price.
NARRATOR: "The scale still works. It is the only thing in the building that does."
LEDGERS: PAY writes the batteries to the Mob's take and "weighed at the pass" to the deed ledger; three payments and he remembers your name, which halves the fee (the collector's recognition, `Q151.W2`). SHOW writes "known at the pass". GO AROUND writes nothing, but the crew's standing toward you drops if you are seen in their hills.
WHY: this is QR-B's E2 (whoever holds the road takes a cut) with the library's three fixes applied: the bill as recognition, not insult (`Q151.W2`); one bypass priced in the ledger (hours and the crew), never four (`Q126.P28`); and no button lies (`Q107.X1`). It also makes the dam road the one corridor that costs money to USE, which is why QD-AR02's relief shift pays the road as its own line (QR-AR rule 2). KEEP MOVING is the hills button: free of batteries, priced in hours, and that is the honest version of free on a road with one pass (`Q126.W3`).
REQUIRES: at Railroad Pass; day or night (the light is on); any company. COOLDOWN: none (he is always there), but the FACE speaks only once a trip, and the stop is skipped entirely when the party has nothing and knows him (the vending machine rule, `Q066.X1`, `Q148.X3`).
ASSET NEEDED (COOK FOUR): the casino at the pass with its truck scale and one lit window as a settlement-screen picture and a map object; PORTRAIT: the collector's face; the road crew's hideout marker above (FACTIONS' row).
SOUND NEEDED (SOUNDS): the scale's platform settling under weight (a low steel groan, real material, no sand); his pen on the comp sheet; the casino's one working slot machine somewhere inside, a jingle at the wrong speed.
AH: the casino's marquee still runs its last message on a loop, a buffet price in dollars, and the collector has never once looked up at it.

### 13. THE BOAT AT BOULDER BEACH (corridor 2, THE LAKE ROAD; register: HOSPITABLE AND WRONG)

## 5. THE NIGHT CAMP, AND WHAT COMES TO IT

Rule 113d (the coordinator's wave of 10/17) settled QR-AR's night question the Battle Brothers way: the party camps on the road at night. Battle Brothers' night is slower travel, shorter sight, likelier ambush, and a camp that can be attacked [R] (01_WORLDMAP, NIGHT and CAMPING). So on the region a far job of two days has one or two nights out, each night is a camp drawn at the party's marker, and each camp may hold ONE stop (QR-AR rule 5), or none, which is allowed and common (`Q044.W7`'s ebb). Of the fifteen stops now on the shelf, these are the night ones and they come TO the camp: 9 THE WALKER AT THE FIRE (any road), 13 THE BOAT AT BOULDER BEACH (the lake road), 7 THE GREENHOUSE (its glow at night, on the three farm roads), 6 "PLEASE, NO MORE" (dusk at the drains), and 12 THE SCALE HOUSE (whose light is on). The rest are day stops met on the road. A night stop never starts a fight (rule 68); if a party means to attack the camp it is drawn approaching and the player may break camp and move (Battle Brothers' own rule; `Q151.W7`: the flee option never closes, which is what makes staying a choice).

What the night costs without any stop: the clock and the food, and nothing else. The library's warning stands: survival that stops mattering is vestigial (`Q065.X1`), and a camp that charges only time is the honest fee (`Q146.W8`). TUNING owns whether the night is six hours or eight.

## 6. HOW A CORRIDOR PICKS ITS STOP

Battle Brothers draws from the eligible pile by score (QR-AG's section on the pick; source: wiki dump, unverified, 0710_Events.txt). The region adds the corridor as the first filter: only stops whose cause the corridor has (section 3's YES cells and section 4's WHERE lines) are eligible on it. Then QR-AG's weights, then three region modifiers: the far band raises the hard stops (2, 5, 12, 15) and lowers the kind ones (`Q044.W4`); a loss or a death in the last two days raises 9 and 13 and lowers 2, 5, 15 (`Q044.W7`); and the pacing cap holds: one stop a night at most, never two stops on one travel leg, and never a stop on the party's first day on the region (QR-AG rule 9; rule 32a). The world runs without the party (`Q055.W1`, `Q061.W2`): the column at the line, the collector, the boat family, the lab man and the nomads are all on the map whether or not they ever stop you, and the player can see every one of them before the stop fires.
