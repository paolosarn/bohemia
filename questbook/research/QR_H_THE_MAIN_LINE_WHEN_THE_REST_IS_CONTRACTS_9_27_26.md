# QR-H: WHAT IS THE MAIN QUEST WHEN EVERYTHING ELSE IS A CONTRACT?

STATUS: draft:true, research only, nothing built (rule 35). Letter H, question (g) of the QUESTS lane.
Answers blind spot 1 of records/BOHEMIA_THE_BLIND_SPOTS_9_27_26.md ("THE STORY DOES NOT EXIST").
Written for RUN, WORLD, PEOPLE, WORDS and DYNASTY. Every naming or identity choice below is Paolo's.
FOLDED 10/1 (QR-AB): rule 18 (QR-U, applied in QR-X), and a note on rule 8 (rule 51). Each edit is marked in place.
FOLDED 10/9 (QR-AK): QR-U rules 17, 19 and 20 (17 fills the number held since 10/1) and the sharpens of rules 3, 5 and 12, each marked in place.

QUESTION: What do the 152 say a MAIN LINE is when the side content is contracts? How does a main
quest stay alive and pull the player while they are free to do contracts at their own pace (no
timers that punish, no nagging)? How do the best main lines use the side content, how often does a
main beat land, how is the line carried by people and places and not a journal, and how is the
ending earned? Then: THE SHAPE of Bohemia's main line across the three acts that are open at once (swept 9/29, rule 39c: open together once unlocked; act 1 is played first with one person and the next generation unlocks later, default the first home base).

## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

In a contracts game the main line is not the thing you do most; it is the thing everything you do
is secretly FOR. The library is blunt about three facts. First, a game whose meaning lives only in
optional content loses most players' meaning: "theme in optional content is missed" is confirmed
around fifteen times (`Q104.X3`, `Q111.X5`, `Q118.X4`, `Q095.X5`), and pure-system games with no
spine read as "sound and fury" (`Q044.X4`, `Q061.X1`). So Bohemia needs ONE authored spine, and the
thesis (true family versus the counterfeit family) has to sit ON it. Second, the best spines that
coexist with free play do not push with clocks: they WAIT, like an offer that never expires
(`Q126.W3`, `Q131.N1`), and they pull with curiosity and people instead (`Q018.W3`, `Q090.W7`).
Bohemia already owns the perfect version of this in its own canon: the Amalgamation only moves
against you in proportion to how close you get to its secret, so the main line's danger is
something the player walks toward at their own pace, never something that chases them. Third, the
spines people remember turn side content into the ballot for the ending: the contracts you took,
the people you met face to face, and what you built are read aloud at the end (`Q006.W2`,
`Q136.W2`, `Q137.W1`). For Bohemia that means: every contract finished in person is a hand shaken
off the record, and the whisper network that frees the valley at the end of act 2 is literally
made of those hands. A main beat lands when the player reaches for it, not on a timer, but the
world must offer a fresh thread whenever the player drifts (`Q090.W7`, `Q046.X1`). The ending is
earned when the finale is a legible reading of the whole life, and the player could see the keys
being cut before the door (`Q102.P8`, `Q136.X1`).

## THE FINDINGS

### 1. A contracts game still needs one authored spine, and the thesis must live on it

- The library's most repeated warning: if the theme lives in optional content, most players never
  meet it. `Q104.X3` (the best-written content in a 100-hour game "is a hole in a rock you may never
  enter"), `Q111.X5`, `Q118.X4`, `Q095.X5` (a side quest doing main-quest work), `Q092.X5` ("our
  fold's theme cannot be optional"), `Q074.X2` (you can skip the entire theme and still win),
  `Q084.X1` (the real story was post-credits), `Q086.X1` (the philosophy is all at the very end).
  For Bohemia, where the side content is contracts that a player may decline for free, anything
  that must be felt (grief, the counterfeit family, the price of the unrecorded) cannot live only in
  contracts.
- Systems without a spine: `Q044.X4` (pure emergence "has no theme, no arc the designer intends"),
  `Q061.X1` (the living political world is "narratively THIN (no real questline/endgame)"),
  `Q055.X1` ("if I created my own narrative it'd be in my head, not the game"). The fix all three
  name is the hybrid: `Q056.W1` (a deep sim AND a hand-written main quest, "emergence for texture,
  authored story for meaning"). Battle Brothers' company writes its own story; the pitch "with a
  better story" is exactly the `Q056.W1` hybrid.
- The spine must be ONE question, not a list of events: `Q012.W1` ("a single thematic question
  posed in the opening, echoed by NPCs, and demanded by the finale; every quest is an ANSWER") and
  `Q012.W2` (side quests as case studies that DEEPEN the question). For Bohemia the question is
  already written in the family law: what is a family worth if it can be lost?
- The opposite failure also exists: `Q022.X1` (a main plot that is "follow orders, go here/there,
  a big machine attacks, repeat" while the side content carries the themes) and `Q022.P9` ("keep the
  MAIN spine driving; don't let side quests carry ALL the theme while the A-plot idles").

### 2. How a main line stays alive with no punishing clock and no nagging

- THE OFFER THAT NEVER EXPIRES. `Q126.W3`: "No ambush, no curse, no closed door... The offer never
  expires. Every hour you don't take it, you are choosing not to take it, again." `Q131.N1`: on
  refusal "the quest simply waits, forever... refusal is free." This is Paolo's DECLINING IS FREE
  rule, applied to the main line itself: the spine is the one standing offer that is always there.
- PAUSE, NEVER FAIL. `Q114.N2` ("walking away mid-substory pauses, never fails... a botched beat
  re-offers later") and `Q114.W3` ("you cannot 'miss' substories; failure costs the good item, not
  the story"). A main beat walked away from is exactly where you left it.
- THE SILENT TIMER IS A BUG. `Q095.P7` ("a silent timer on your best content is a bug wearing a
  design costume... our quest factory needs VISIBLE expiry"), `Q137.X3` (rewarding thoroughness for
  40 hours then executing hostages for it "is honest ONCE and vicious always"), `Q104.X6` ("side
  content that silently fails main content is a shipping failure"), `Q071.X1` (progress the main
  story too far and threads silently die). Hard clocks alienate: `Q038.X1` (the water-chip timer;
  the sequel removed it), `Q026.X1`. The generous version: `Q018.W8`, `Q019.W8` ("deduce at your
  leisure... respect the player's pace").
- THE LIBRARY'S COUNTER-CASE, NAMED SO WE DO NOT DRIFT INTO IT: `Q006.W6` and `Q087.P5` bank the
  hidden clock (wander and people die; ignore the warning and the town is sacked). Those are right
  for their games and wrong for ours: Paolo's shape is AT YOUR PACE. What we keep from them is only
  their honesty rule: if a clock ever exists it is stated in the fiction by a person (`Q137.X3`:
  "Joker knows; Joker could have said"), and it is audible (`Q119.X3`).
- THE MAIN LINE MUST NOT BE FINISHED BY SOMEONE ELSE. `Q085.W3`: the team cut "the possibility for
  an NPC TO COMPLETE THE MAIN MISSION BEFORE THE PLAYER" and shipped it only for side quests. The
  world runs without you (`Q055.W1`, `Q061.W2`, `Q051.W2`), but the spine waits for you.
- THE PULL IS CURIOSITY, AND DANGER IS OPT-IN. `Q018.W3` (the rumor web: "a growing map of
  known-vs-implied that always gives a thread to pull, with NO waypoints"), `Q023.W2` (the quiet
  thrill of stumbling on the next step). The danger model that makes this safe is the fever:
  `Q085.W8` ("emissions intensify as you near the truth because the Zone is running a FEVER against
  you") and its port `Q085.P7` ("the closer the dynasty gets to the truth, the harder the world
  pushes back, DIEGETICALLY"). This is already Bohemia canon
  (laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_AMALGAMATION_THREAT_LOGIC_7_1_26.md: "threat scales with PROXIMITY TO THE
  SECRET, not player power"; "What you build makes you loved. What you know makes you a target").
  Put together: the main line never punishes the player for being slow, because the only thing that
  raises the danger is the player choosing to look.
- THE PULL WHEN THE PLAYER DRIFTS. `Q090.W7`: the story arrives by radio, "JUST WHEN YOU MIGHT BE
  LOST FOR THINGS TO DO, IT'LL START PINGING OFF." `Q101.W3`: an automated distress call receivable
  from three districts. `Q088.W8`: the world explains itself through its own dying media. The pull
  is an offer placed in the world, never a nag: a thing you can hear, travel to, or leave untapped (swept 9/29:
  there is no walk through the city, rule 38).
- SEED THE HOOK EARLY. `Q047.X3` ("seed enough EARLY intrigue that players invest before the
  drip-feed pays off"), `Q067.X4` (the first generation of the first generational saga "is a boring
  stroll through monotony"). With three acts open together once unlocked, act 1 cannot be the boring generation, and since every player lives act 1 first (rule 39c) it is also the one generation nobody skips.

### 3. How the best main lines use the side content

- SIDE CONTENT IS THE WIND-UP FOR THE FINALE. `Q006.W1` ("structure the entire experience as
  PREPARATION for one finale that reads every prior choice; the side content becomes the point"),
  `Q006.W2` ("optional in name but load-bearing for the ending... your friends live or die"),
  `Q137.W3` ("LOYALTY IS ARMOR, LITERALLY... being KNOWN by your commander is ballistic
  protection"), `Q024.W9` (prep shapes options).
- THE BALLOT WAS CAST WEEKS AGO. `Q136.W2` ("side quests as silent constituencies... the finale is a
  ledger-read"), `Q042.W5` ("one late set-piece where the WHOLE game's accumulated
  reputation/alliances pay out at once"). This is the cleanest answer to "why take contracts?" that
  does not violate DECLINING IS FREE: nobody is punished for a no; a yes just becomes a voice later.
- THE CONTRACT IS THE BEST-SCOPED CONTAINER. `Q148.W10` ("notice to payment in a session, full
  formula every time... nothing has time to dilute it") and `Q148.W2` ("a job system that never
  begs"). Contracts should stay short and whole; the main line is what threads them.
- THE MAIN LINE THREADS THROUGH SIDE CONTENT. `Q001.W5` ("the quest pauses and threads THROUGH other
  quests, so its resolution feels discovered across the world"), `Q004.W5` (the payoff "lands
  quests later, in a DIFFERENT quest... the world feels like it has a memory"). A contract taken for
  pay can carry a thread of the spine inside it, once in a while, without becoming a main quest.
- THE MAIN LINE CHANGES THE POOL. `Q044.W2` (the storyteller READS your state and picks the event),
  `Q071.W6` ("gated by world-state, not markers... the gate is a cosmological FACT"), `Q089.W6`
  (paths smooth as people walk them: the city is an accumulated record). Note the contrast with
  `Q044.W4` (threat scales with WEALTH): Bohemia's canon explicitly rejects wealth-scaled danger
  from the secret; building is safe. So in Bohemia, what the main line changes is WHICH people are
  in the places and WHAT they ask for, not how hard the world hits a rich player.
- THE WARNING ABOUT FACTION ERRANDS. `Q074.X1` ("seven factions, each needing their own quest-chains,
  is dozens of hours of fetch-work") and `Q074.P9`. If the main line asks the player to win every
  faction, it becomes a checklist of contracts. It should instead READ the contracts the player
  chose to take.

### 4. How often a main beat lands (cadence)

- QUIET, LOUD, QUIET. `Q039.W4` ("score tension like music: long dread, sharp scare, release"),
  `Q044.W7` ("after a hard hit, send relief"), `Q013.W2` (swing from grim main-story to side-story and
  back; "the contrast makes BOTH hit harder").
- THE DEAD-AIR PROBLEM IS THE BIGGEST RISK FOR US. `Q046.X1` ("when the story engine isn't firing,
  actions feel rote") and its port `Q046.P7` (pace the drama AND make the moment-to-moment loop
  engaging so the gaps do not sag). In Battle Brothers shape, the quiet loop is contracts, travel
  and the fight; they must be good on their own, and the spine is the rare loud note.
- ONE DELIBERATE STEP AT A TIME. `Q029.W2` ("a paced cadence of committed, branching, mutually
  exclusive laws, slow enough that each is FELT").
- DRIP, DO NOT DUMP. `Q068.X3` (a late lore-dump "in big talky chunks"), `Q047.W3` (story earned
  through play), `Q035.W2` (slow-drip truth).
- DO NOT LET THE END DISSOLVE. `Q050.X3` ("don't let the finale dissolve into open-ended busywork"),
  `Q051.X4` (thin late content), `Q065.X1` ("the back half loses its tension... hit max wallet").
  In a batteries economy this is a direct warning: the main line's last beats must still cost.

### 5. Carried by people and places, never by a journal

- THE WORLD IS THE LOG. `Q023.W1` ("quests live in dialogue, item text, and the environment; the
  PLAYER assembles them"), `Q023.W10` (the fix is better in-world clues, not markers-or-nothing),
  `Q037.W1`, `Q037.W2` ("flood the world with diegetic direction"), `Q038.W3` (navigate by deduction).
- THE CAUTIONS. `Q023.X1` (cryptic works in small spaces; at open-world scale whole questlines are
  lost), `Q037.X2` ("taking many quests at once floods the journal"), `Q037.X3` (teach the literacy
  early). For us: a BB-scale valley is big, so the spine needs MORE in-world pointers than a single
  district, and the player holds only one main thread at a time.
- A PERSON IS THE TURN. `Q073.W6` ("a nun telling him he was kind is the axis of a 60-hour game, not
  a set-piece"), `Q074.W5` ("seven factions must each personally NAME you... destiny is a political
  campaign"), `Q022.W5` ("even the interface is a person").
- THE CITY SNAGS YOU. `Q013.W6` (substories start by walking past something), `Q114.W2` (not on the
  map; found by walking the streets). A main beat is a person standing in a place, not a pop-up. (Swept 9/29: the
  walked street is dead, rule 38; the snag now lives on the road, as events, and in the settlement picture.)
- PEOPLE REMEMBER ACROSS GENERATIONS. `Q144.W7` ("officers who cross remember the previous captain...
  the cheapest possible reactivity carrying the heaviest possible freight"), `Q089.W7` ("help arrives
  as objects, not messages"), `Q113.W1` ("the inheritance is a list of children").

### 6. How the ending is earned

- THE ROLL CALL. `Q006.W8` ("end by TALLYING the survivors"), `Q136.W10` ("the room counts aloud...
  the player HEARS their weeks of choices, one voice at a time"), `Q141.W9` ("ACCOUNTING CAN BE THE
  FINALE when every row was earned").
- EVERY OUTCOME HAS A RECEIPT. `Q137.W1` ("each casualty names its cause in player-history"),
  `Q137.W2` ("the finale grades attention, not aim"), `Q035.W7` (the ending earned by behavior, not
  chosen), `Q085.W9` (the machine grants what you ACTUALLY wanted based on how you played),
  `Q050.W3` ("WHEN + HOW you face the final threat authors the ending, not a menu pick").
- SHOW THE KEYS BEFORE THE DOOR. `Q102.X1` (unlock conditions invisible; players "never learn the
  other five existed"), `Q102.P8` ("show the player they're building keys"), `Q136.X1` ("political
  capital needs a LEDGER VIEW"), `Q137.X1` ("the rules are invisible and the wiki is mandatory").
- MAKE THE ENDINGS REALLY DIFFERENT, AND PLAYED. `Q067.X1` (endings too similar), `Q050.X1` (many
  endings are thin vignettes), `Q102.X6` ("count real branches, not achievement rows"), `Q142.X2`
  (the consequence is "TOLD more than PLAYED"), `Q152.X3` (the cycle breaks for free, as a boss
  health bar), `Q079.X2` (the payoff is offscreen).
- LET HOPE BE A PERCENTAGE. `Q123.W10` ("the best you can do is 12.5%"), `Q085.W10` ("I'll never know
  if I was right"), `Q073.W7` ("his last goal is someone else's future"). These fit the canon's
  endings exactly: LIBERATE saves one valley, "and that is enough."

### 7. The spine across generations, and across acts open together (once unlocked, rule 39c)

- PLANT IN ONE AGE, LAND IN ANOTHER. `Q004.P1` (choices whose true stakes land a generation later),
  `Q026.P9` (return across generations to see judgments ripen), `Q137.W8` (the dead stay dead across
  a sequel), `Q050.W5` ("actions in one era materially reshape another").
- THE LIFE WRITES THE WILL. `Q144.W1` ("every mid-run choice is secretly testament-drafting"),
  `Q144.W3` ("the refusals make the line real"), `Q144.W9` ("the second life proves the first one
  happened"), `Q046.W1` ("the unit is the FAMILY across centuries").

## THE SHAPE OF BOHEMIA'S MAIN LINE (shaped from the story master, nothing added)

Sources: laws/BOHEMIA_STORY_MASTER_7_18_26.md, the ACT1 PROCEDURAL ENDING, ACT3 MOONSHOT, ACT1
AMALGAMATION IS A GHOST, FAMILY CORE THEME and THREE ACTS AT ONCE laws, the THREAT LOGIC addendum,
and GDD v2/v4 on the whisper network and the two ledgers. Where the canon is silent, this page says
so and leaves it to Paolo.

### The spine is three strands braided, one question underneath

The one question (`Q012.W1`): what is a family worth if it can be lost? The counterfeit answer is
the Amalgamation's ("a family that never ends, nothing is ever lost"). The three strands:

1. THE FAMILY STRAND (always safe, always there). The grief, the heirs, the three descendants the
   player flips between. It never raises the danger. It is where the thesis is FELT, so it is on the
   spine, not in contracts (`Q092.X5`).
2. THE SECRET STRAND (opt-in danger). The ghost in act 1, the discovery and the reckoning in act 2,
   the Moon in act 3. It only moves when the player looks. Each look raises the proximity heat by a
   step, and the canon's own response ladder answers (natural-seeming accident, quietly accelerated
   faction conflict, untraceable hit squad, then carriers). That ladder IS the pacing device; no
   timer is needed (`Q085.W8`).
3. THE CITY STRAND (what the world does). The Destroyers in act 1, the clawing back in act 2, the
   Network's crumbling in act 3. It runs whether or not the player acts (`Q055.W1`), and it changes
   the contract pool: who is in which place, what they need, what they can pay.

### What the contracts feed, act by act (the ballot)

- ACT 1: every contract taken and finished writes the ledger that the procedural act 1 climax reads
  (whether you built, whether you raced for pseudo-mayor, which factions). The climax is the canon's
  generator over nine approved elements; this page does not pick one.
- ACT 2: every contract finished FACE TO FACE, off the feed, is a row in the UNRECORDED ledger
  (GDD v4 two-ledger principle). GDD v2 describes the endgame as a "whisper network built across three
  generations. Truth released simultaneously through every trusted faction." The story master places
  that "whisper broadcast" at the end of act 2. So the whisper network is made of the people the
  family did honest work for in person. This is `Q006.W2` and `Q136.W2` in Bohemia's own terms. A
  declined contract costs nothing and is remembered by nobody; it simply is not a hand.
- ACT 3: the war path or the conversion path is set by act 2 (story master). The contracts the
  family took for the Network's un-implanted members are the conversion path's roll call.

### How the three acts, open together once unlocked, change the spine

- Every main beat is tagged with the act it lives in. The player can reach the act 3 beats early,
  but the act 3 city they arrive in is derived from acts 1 and 2 (THREE ACTS AT ONCE, section 2).
  Early, act 3 is the ruin; the beat still plays, on poor ground. This is the Ocarina rule applied
  to the story, and it is how the main line uses the flip without a lock.
- The secret strand is the one strand with an ORDER: canon says the player "barely knows what it is
  even by the end of Act 2" and in act 1 it is a ghost, never named. So the word "Amalgamation" must
  never appear in act 1 even if the player has already met the act 2 reveal (a flip does not carry
  vocabulary backward into the mouths of act 1 people). How the flip handles knowledge the player
  has and the act 1 characters do not is DYNASTY's to rule; this page flags it.
- Planted beats cross acts as a THING, a PLACE or a PERSON (law section 4): a box, a grave, a hand.

### Cadence (a default for the builders, in VOTE if anyone wants it changed)

- A main beat is never delivered; it is found. The world keeps exactly ONE main thread visible per
  act at a time: one person in one place who can move the spine. Leave them untapped and they are still
  there (`Q126.W3`).
- After a heavy main beat, the next thing the world offers is light: a contract, a road event with
  a joke in it, a camp night (`Q013.W2`, `Q044.W7`).
- When the player has taken no main step for a long stretch, the world places one new pull in the
  street they are standing in: a broadcast, a stranger with a message, a thing left on the doorstep
  (`Q090.W7`). It never speaks first in the first sixty seconds, and it never repeats itself.

### The ending, earned

LIBERATE, RESPECT and BECOME resolve at the Moon in act 3; act 2's reckoning shapes which are
reachable (story master). The earning, from the library: the reckoning at the end of act 2 is a
roll call of the hands (`Q136.W10`), the launch eve at the end of act 3 is a roll call of who agrees
to go (the one-way launch "with the player's chosen companions (the ones who agree to go)"), and
both show their keys ahead of time (`Q102.P8`). The exact mapping of the three stances across the
act 2/act 3 boundary is listed as FLOATED/PENDING in the ACT3 MOONSHOT law; this page does not map it.

## THE RULE FOR THE BUILDERS (numbered, testable)

1. ONE MAIN LINE EXISTS AND CARRIES THE THESIS. Every beat of the family strand is on the spine.
   Test: a player who declines every contract still meets the grief, the counterfeit offer, and the
   choice among LIBERATE, RESPECT and BECOME. (`Q104.X3`, `Q092.X5`, `Q012.W1`)
2. THE MAIN LINE NEVER EXPIRES AND NEVER FAILS. No main beat has a hidden timer; a beat walked away
   from is waiting where it was left. Test: advance time by any amount on the map; every open main
   beat is still takeable. (`Q126.W3`, `Q114.N2`, `Q095.P7`)
3. DANGER ON THE SPINE IS OPT-IN. The secret strand raises proximity heat only on a player action
   that LOOKS (the trigger list in the threat-logic addendum). Test: a player who builds, trades and
   takes contracts but never looks has zero heat. (`Q085.W8`, threat-logic addendum)
4. IF A CLOCK EVER EXISTS, A PERSON SAYS IT OUT LOUD FIRST. No silent clock anywhere on the spine.
   Test: every time-limited main situation has a spoken line, with a portrait, that names the limit.
   (`Q137.X3`, `Q119.X3`)
5. NOBODY FINISHES THE MAIN LINE FOR YOU. The world may resolve contracts without the player; no
   main beat resolves without the player. (`Q085.W3`)
6. CONTRACTS ARE BALLOTS, NEVER TOLLS. No main beat requires a specific contract to have been taken.
   Contracts only change how a main beat plays out and who stands with you. Test: every main beat
   is reachable with zero contracts; its roll call differs. (`Q136.W2`, `Q006.W2`, `Q074.X1`)
7. DECLINING LEAVES NO TRACE ON THE SPINE. A refused contract never appears in any roll call, never
   lowers standing, never is spoken of. Only taken contracts (finished or dropped) are rows.
   (Paolo 9/27 shape, `Q126.W3`)
8. ONE VISIBLE MAIN THREAD PER ACT. At any moment, each act shows the player at most one person in
   one place who moves the spine. (`Q037.X2`, `Q023.X1`) (folded 10/1 from rule 51: Paolo 9/30 made it law, ONE
   main quest at a time beside TWO contract slots; the main thread never takes a contract slot.)
9. THE PULL WHEN IDLE IS A THING IN THE WORLD, NEVER A PROMPT. After a long stretch with no main step
   the world places one new in-world pull in the player's current street; nothing pops up; nothing
   speaks in the first sixty seconds. (`Q090.W7`, `Q013.W6`)
10. EVERY MAIN BEAT IS A PERSON IN A PLACE. Its first line comes from a mouth with a portrait. The
    phone feed may REPORT a main beat after it happened, never deliver it. (`Q073.W6`, `Q023.W1`)
11. QUIET AFTER LOUD. The first thing offered after a heavy main beat is light. (`Q013.W2`, `Q044.W7`)
12. THE FINALES ARE ROLL CALLS WITH RECEIPTS. Each act's finale names, one person at a time, who
    stood with you and why (the contract or the deed that bought it). (`Q136.W10`, `Q137.W1`)
13. SHOW THE KEYS BEFORE THE DOOR. Before any roll-call finale, the player can see, in the world
    (the people, the places), roughly who will stand with them. (`Q102.P8`, `Q136.X1`)
14. THE LAST BEATS STILL COST. The act 3 beats cost batteries, people and time in amounts a rich
    player still feels. (`Q065.X1`, `Q050.X3`)
15. ACT 1 NEVER NAMES THE MACHINE, EVEN AFTER A FLIP. (ACT1 GHOST law; DYNASTY rules the flip's
    knowledge.)
16. REVEALS DRIP. No main beat delivers more than one new truth about the secret. (`Q068.X3`)
17. EVERY STEP TOWARD THE SECRET HAS AN OFF-RAMP. (folded 10/9 from QR-U) The right to stop looking, and the record keeps the choice to
    not know (`Q154.P3`); a frame that punishes curiosity with no off-ramp removes the agency the theme is about
    (`Q154.X2`); the hostility rises in readable steps (`Q154.W9`). Proximity is the danger (`Q203.W2`), telling spreads
    the target (`Q209.W8`), the made people's safety is one revelation from ash (`Q218.W6`), whoever holds the truth
    holds the match (`Q218.W2`). Test: every main beat that moves toward the secret has a stop button that writes
    "chose not to know" and is never punished as failure.
18. THE COUNTERFEIT OFFER IS GENUINELY GOOD. (folded 10/1 from QR-U, applied to the designs in QR-X.) Every offer the
    Amalgamation or its copies make (the dead back on a screen, free power for a face, a grief that stops) must TEMPT:
    not a bad thing dressed as good, but the real wish (`Q174.W7`). "No one is ever lost, the dead are back" is its
    whole promise, made a place you leave by refusing (`Q174.W10`). BECOME must be genuinely tempting or LIBERATE is a
    difficulty setting (`Q156.P2`); the villain's diagnosis is right (`Q210.W1`); and refusing must never be the
    obviously correct, free pick (`Q161.X3`). This is the main-line form of QR-L P16: the temptation is stated and the
    other side is fully built (QR-G line 32). Test: a reader who does not know the canon can argue for BECOME in one
    sentence from what the game showed; every counterfeit offer shows the offer WORKING for someone the player has met.
