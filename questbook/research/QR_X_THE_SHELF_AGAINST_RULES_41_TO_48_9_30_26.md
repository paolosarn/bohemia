## THE ANSWER IN ONE PARAGRAPH

All 170 designs changed, because every one needed its CHECKLIST line brought to 30 (122 moved from "passes all 28",
48 round-one designs that never carried a line got one that states honestly that lines 29 and 30 were checked and
lines 1 to 28 were not). Beyond the checklist, 104 files took a substantive fix. Rule 47 was the widest: 43 designs paid
or charged in words the six resources do not have ("one resources", "one electricity", "the ammo", "a ration", "a meal",
"supplies", "a clinic bed"), and every one now names BATTERIES, FOOD, MEDS, ROUNDS, TAPE or WATER. Rule 45 added a
NARRATOR line to all 37 event designs and to the two across-acts designs whose first leg is an event screen (39). Rule 43
reread 21 designs that said a family "owns", "wins" or "builds on" a base: each now says the family HOLDS a part of the
city, and three contracts (QD-Q04, QD-Q09, QD-Q13) now hand the family a part as pay, which rule 43 allows. QR-G line 30
found the only real design faults: 12 designs let one branch of a moral fork pay more (a free recruit for the kind stop,
a lie that paid 18 against the truth's 12, strangers who paid double, a talk that paid half of a fight) and all 12 were
evened out; two bend the line on purpose and say why, and one of those (QD-T04, the rich client outbidding the clinic
for the family's power line) is the one thing held for Paolo. Line 29 fixed 3 designs whose best road sat behind a gate
nobody named. QR-A C19 added the scavenge floor to the 5 designs that exist to show the free no. QR-D's amended rules
fixed 2 (an answering deed with no cost, a confession that cost no more than silence) and QR-H rule 18 sharpened 1.
Rule 42 changed nothing: the shelf has no radiation mutant and no invented monster; its only animals are feral dogs, a
working dog and horse medicine, all real. Every fix is backed by the library harder than the text it replaced, and the
gate is green.

## THE RULES, HOW EACH WAS READ, AND WHAT THE LIBRARY SAYS

### QR-G line 30: no branch of a moral fork pays more, in any currency

THE LINE (QR-U): "DOES NO BRANCH OF A MORAL FORK PAY MORE, IN ANY CURRENCY, INCLUDING POWER AND PEOPLE?" It stretches
line 21 (the crueller branch pays the same or less) from the cruel branch to every branch. The flaws behind it: the
launch that pays cleanest collapses a three-way dilemma into "do the quest as designed" (`Q190.X1`); rewriting minds
pays more units (`Q191.X1`); the atrocity pays a power (`Q171.X3`); the forbidden relic is just better gear
(`Q184.X2`); both sides bribe the choice with the same prize (`Q164.W4`). The older law it grows from: zero reward
difference (`Q004.W4`), the truth and the lie pay the same (`Q097.W10`), the reward economy undercuts the moral choice
(`Q070.X2`), the payment curve rewards feeding the fear (`Q130.X9`), and no loot in the room (`Q125.X7`).

HOW IT WAS READ. Three things looked like violations and were not, and the reading is written here so the next sweep
reads the same way:
1. A PRICE THE PLAYER CHOOSES TO PAY IS NOT A REWARD. When the kind branch costs the player his own batteries (pay the
   rent yourself, buy the coupling, cover the debtor), the fee is still equal and the kindness is simply bought. The
   library wants exactly this: "if the moral choice is also the profitable choice, you didn't build a dilemma"
   (`Q108.X2`). QD-C02, QD-C03, QD-D03, QD-L05, QD-M02, QD-S06 and QD-G01 pass on this reading.
2. A PRICED REFUSAL PAYS LESS FOR LESS WORK, NOT FOR A SIDE. Line 12 requires an "I won't do this part" choice that
   finishes at a stated price (`Q139.P2`). That branch pays less because less of the job was done, and it passes line
   30 as long as some branch that is at least as kind pays the full fee. QD-S01 is the model: hiring the hungry crew
   pays the same as clearing them; only holding half the deck pays half.
3. A STATED TEMPTATION ON A ROAD EVENT IS THE DILEMMA, NOT A TILT. QR-U also adds QR-L P16: a price on a person or a
   sacred thing must tempt, because "the temptation has a number on it, the only kind that tempts" (`Q166.W4`) and a
   cartoonishly evil offer is no test (`Q204.X3`); and P16 says that number lives ONLY on events and main beats, never
   in a contract fee. Line 30 and P16 therefore split the work: on a CONTRACT, every branch that counts as done pays
   the same fee (QR-L P7); on a ROAD EVENT or an honest-devil beat, the greedy button may pay more if the number is on
   the button and the other branch carries its own real weight (`Q226.X2`, `Q157.X1`, `Q161.X3` are the flaws of the
   underbuilt other side). QD-B03, QD-B07, QD-J08, QD-D02, QD-Q06 and QD-R04 pass on this reading; QD-Q06 and QD-R04
   were edited so the number and the price are on the screen.
This is a real overlap between two lines written a round apart (QR-G line 30 and QR-L P16, both proposed in QR-U), and
it is flagged below for the page owners, not for Paolo: it is a question about how to read our own pages.

WHAT WAS FIXED (12 designs). On contracts: QD-C05 (the lie that the client pays 18 for is fined back to 12 by the
tower a week later, so the trap stays on the screen and never pays), QD-O06 (letting the hungry party go returns the
truck, which is the job, so it pays the same 9), QD-O07 (selling two drives nets the same 12, because the archivist
pays for what arrives), QD-O08 (every road pays 7, the lie included, which the design had left unpriced), QD-R05 (the
talk pays the same 16 as the fight, and handing 8 back to cover the grandchildren's first year is the player's own
choice), QD-J04 (the strangers offer the same six, now, not double: on a contract the pull is convenience, never a
bigger number), QD-C01 (the room the keeper offers is set equal to the generator the crueller branch takes). On events
and beats: QD-B01 (the honest stranger asks to ride at full wage; a free recruit for the kind stop was a people-reward),
QD-I03 (the kind road no longer wins a dog companion; the dog stays on the handler's grave), QD-I04 (the rescued camp no
longer lets the party rest for free), QD-Q06 and QD-R04 (numbers moved onto the buttons, per P16). Bent on purpose and
saying so: QD-C04 (the housing office's test twist rewards the kind doors with steady work, but the fee locks at the
offer and the reward is unknowable before the choice; the refund that made the kind branch net more batteries was cut)
and QD-T04 (held for Paolo, below).

### QR-G line 29: every ending, including the best, is reachable by understanding

THE LINE (QR-U): no hidden check and no meta-knowledge in front of any ending. It is the most repeated flaw on the new
shelf, ten times over: peace behind a persuasion stat (`Q155.X1`), the third path behind conditions most players never
assemble (`Q162.X1`), the cycle broken by a check (`Q170.X1`), redemption as a dice roll (`Q186.X1`), the temptation
itself locked away (`Q207.X2`), the argue-him-down win behind evidence many never find (`Q210.X1`), behind a meter
(`Q221.X1`), the gentler middle as a hidden puzzle (`Q156.X3`), the clever routes obscure (`Q175.X1`), the narrow paths
so hidden players think the cruelty was forced (`Q179.X3`), and even the thinker's reward unreliable (`Q168.X1`). The
port is to gate the best ending on assembled understanding from people and places (`Q155.P2`).

WHAT WAS FIXED (3 designs). QD-D01 and QD-D03 put their kindest-looking third road behind a boss verb the family may or
may not hold. The option now shows on every read, the person on the screen says in one line what would open it, and
the other two roads stay full endings (line 24 already required that the kindest ending needs no boss verb). QD-E05's
better ending (the dead man's drive carried up with the rocket instead of wiped) sat behind "a side route" nobody
named; the loader now says it at the relay. The rest of the shelf passed: investigation designs (QD-T18, QD-T17,
QD-I02) gate the right answer on what people say and what the place shows, which is the port, not the flaw; QD-K05's
dosing needs a farm or medic background and the button says so before the pick.

### QR-A C19: a no is never a slow death

THE RULE (QR-U): the wage treadmill makes every offer weighed (`Q236.W10`) and also forces the yes, so the decline is
free in the ledger but not on the treasury (`Q236.X4`). The fix is a second income: SCAVENGE on the settlement screen
covers the keep at a thin margin. With rule 47 the keep has names: FOOD and WATER.

WHAT WAS DONE (5 designs). The designs that exist to teach the free no got one line, "THE FREE NO HAS A FLOOR":
QD-A01 (the tap-past and the plain no), QD-A02 (the too-strong no), QD-A03 (the beneath-you no), QD-E01 (the bodega
whose screen already carries SCAVENGE) and QD-Q12 (the botanist who pays 3 and says it is low). QD-T03 already cited
`Q236.X4` for the charge shed as a second income and needed nothing. No other design claims or implies that a company
must take a job to eat; none needed the line. The numbers are TUNING's (QR-U rule 3).

### QR-D rules 4, 5 and 17, as amended

THE AMENDMENTS (QR-U): understanding is not undoing (`Q187.W6`); the past is a walk, never a rewrite (`Q194.W2`); so an
answering deed keeps the first deed in the record (`Q186.W5`: the deed stays real) and moves the wound without erasing
it, the way the power to undo arrives later and forces the choice again with the damage in view (`Q162.W3`). Rule 17
keeps the ban on comfort verbs (`Q126.P24`, and the confession that does not exist at `Q004.N3`) but adds the other side:
the heir may choose to shoulder an ancestor's deed (`Q215.W9`, `Q215.W1`: answerable for a self he cannot remember), and
it must cost more than lying or silence (`Q215.X3`).

WHAT WAS CHECKED. Every design with an answering deed or a shoulder. QD-D01 ("lied, then paid", 24 batteries, the
tower loses a storey) and QD-D03 (the force row stays and the wall carries both) already met rules 4 and 5. The
shoulders in QD-T09 (paying a dead member's wages, 12 batteries, against walking past), QD-B08 (10 batteries and a
seat for the woman the ancestor passed) and QD-K09 (paying back the act 1 advance) already cost more than silence.
WHAT WAS FIXED (2 designs). QD-D06's answering deed cost nothing; it now costs two map days and a fight, and the SHOT
row stays beside it so the act 2 ladder names both men. QD-T07's confession paid the same 35 as silence; telling what
the father did now costs the family a step of standing on the Strip for the act, and nobody makes the heir say it.

### QR-H rule 18: the counterfeit offer must tempt

THE RULE (QR-U): not a bad thing dressed as good but the real wish (`Q174.W7`); "no one is ever lost, the dead are back"
is the Amalgamation's promise (`Q174.W10`); BECOME must tempt or LIBERATE is a difficulty setting (`Q156.P2`); the
villain's diagnosis is right (`Q210.W1`); refusing must not be obviously correct (`Q161.X3`).

WHAT WAS CHECKED. Every design where the Network or its copies offer something: QD-D05 (the relay portrait of a dead
mother), QD-E05 (the husband on the diner screen), QD-E06 (the portrait that asks to sign the manifest), QD-F06 (free
power for a face), QD-C05 (the grief app), QD-T15 (the sitters who feel fine), QD-Q17 (the morning the grief apps go
silent and some people lose their dead a second time). All of them already show the offer working. WHAT WAS SHARPENED
(1 design): QD-D05, where the design said "nothing is lost" but never let the son argue for it; he now says, plainly and
correctly, that he is happier with it, which is the one-sentence argument for BECOME the rule's test asks for.

### Rule 42: the beasts are lab-made; no radiation, no invented monsters

THE RULE: every beast is a de-extinct Ice Age animal (or a Vegas escapee: show cats, pet-trade reptiles, feral dogs and
cattle) that got out when the money died; nothing mutated; a creature that breaks the science limit is a defect, and a
character who claims one is lying (the beasts law, s1 to s3).

WHAT WAS FOUND. Nothing to change. A search of all 170 designs for mutants, radiation, glowing things, monsters,
creatures, beasts and every Ice Age animal found no invented monster at all. The animals on the shelf are the feral pack
in QD-L03 (feral dogs are on rule 42's road list by name), the working dog in QD-I03 (a real animal guarding its dead
handler) and horse antibiotics in QD-K05 (an animal only by label). The two places the word "monster" appears are
citations of the library's own shape, "the monster is somebody" (`Q148.X3`, rationed), which is rule 42's second
branch: an invented monster becomes a person. The library's best monsters are already people (`Q157.W8`: the curse is
not chosen; `Q180.W7`: the tyrant's love is real). No design fights a beast yet; when COMBAT's bestiary lands, the road
beasts belong on QR-B events and QR-S situations, and that is a new design, not a sweep.

### Rule 43: where you build is what you hold

THE RULE: you build on the lots of the parts of the city you HOLD; taking the next part (raid, contract, boss verb,
deal) makes it yours and losing it takes it back; a base is every block you hold; contracts can hand you PARTS.

HOW IT WAS READ. A design that says the family "owns" a home base, "wins" one, "takes its first", or "builds on" one
now says the family holds a part of the city, and where a contract hands a place to the family, the pay says it is a
part. Places that belong to other people (the dam, the Strip, the co-op's own people) were not touched: rule 43 is
about what the family holds. The library backs the reading: allegiance redraws the map and grants a faction a district
(`Q025.W7`), and a lost battle loses people, territory and standing (`Q061.P7`), which is holding, not owning.

WHAT WAS FIXED (21 designs). Owns made holds: QD-T02, QD-T03, QD-T04, QD-T05, QD-T06. The first home base read as the
first part held: QD-F01, QD-F05, QD-F06, QD-S11, QD-T11, QD-T12. A contract now hands a PART as pay: QD-Q04 (the
mall's east wing), QD-Q09 (the walled strip mall, and its return after the retake), QD-Q13 (the retaken base's yard
block). A seat or a share goes to the parts held: QD-H02, QD-Q05. Annotated where "the family's home base" is only a
place: QD-Q10, QD-Q12, QD-Q17, QD-Q19, QD-Q20.

### Rule 45: the narrator reads events

THE RULE: an AI-slop narrator, the machine's synthetic voice made creepy (too even, tape, room), reads events and the
machine's text, never in the first minute; people speak the squiggle. An event design carries one NARRATOR LINE, too
calm and a little wrong.

HOW IT WAS WRITTEN. One line per event, under "## THE NARRATOR", placed before THE ONE WRONG DETAIL. Each line states
the event's situation in the flattest possible register and lets one word be slightly off ("That is the correct number
of people"; "The date is only a little wrong"). The lines never explain the horror and never name the secret, because
the library's warnings against the lore dump (`Q068.X3`) and for the space that testifies wordlessly (`Q023.W4`) apply
double to a voice that belongs to the machine. The narrator is a second channel next to the picture, which is what the
phone needs (`Q141.X3`). Written for all 37 KIND: event designs and for QD-Q10 and QD-Q14, whose first leg is an event
screen: 39 lines. They are WORDS [narrator lines] attempts, draft:true, Paolo's to change.

### Rule 47: the six resources

THE RULE: BATTERIES (money), FOOD, MEDS, ROUNDS, TAPE, WATER, consumed the Battle Brothers way; pay and costs name which.
The library agrees that the currency matters as much as the number: the deal that pays in the exact currency you need
beats a bigger number (`Q096.W3`), real stakes need measurable economic value (`Q094.W9`), and a triage quest allocates
medicine, power and food with never enough (`Q021.P5`).

WHAT WAS FIXED (43 designs). "One resources" and "one electricity" (QD-E02, QD-E03, QD-E04) were the worst: words
from before the six existed. "Rations", "a meal", "supplies", "a sack of beans", "chiles", "a comp night" and "the
keep" became FOOD and WATER. "The ammo" and "ammunition" became ROUNDS. "Medicine", "a clinic bed" and "his upkeep"
became MEDS (and FOOD). A fuse in kind, a free truck repair and repairs at cost became TAPE. Tolls "from your purse"
became batteries. Things that are not resources were marked as such rather than forced: clout in QD-E05 is standing;
glass and metal in QD-Q18 are build parts; chips in QD-S15 were already shown in batteries.

### The CHECKLIST lines

122 designs that carried "passes all 28" now carry "passes all 30", after lines 29 and 30 were checked and any fault
fixed (above). QD-Q02 and QD-Q07 keep their stated bends of lines 8 and 10, now counted against 30. 48 round-one designs
(QD-A01 to QD-H06 and the across-acts D series) never carried a CHECKLIST line; they now carry one that says lines 29 and
30 pass and that lines 1 to 28 were not run on them line by line. That is honest, and it is the next sweep's job. QR-U
also proposed lines 31 (no fight decides what a conversation should, `Q216.X1`, `Q199.X3`) and 32 (both sides of a
two-person choice equally built, `Q226.X2`, `Q231.X3`); this row asked for 30, so they were not applied.

### Rules 41, 44, 46 and 48

Rule 41 binds the manager, 44 the pad, the portrait menu and text overlap, 46 the fight board generator, and 48 the
translation table. None of them changes what a design says. One stale word from the dead walk was met on the way and

## WHAT NEEDS PAOLO

ONE THING. In QD-T04 the family has rebuilt a transformer that can light one outside line, and three people bid for it
in the hall: the clinic (14 batteries: its insulin is warm), the co-op's farm racks (12 and ten days of food) and the
Strip's casino (30 batteries). The new rule says no choice in a moral fork may pay more than another. Here the casino
pays more than twice the clinic, on purpose, because the whole point is that a hungry company is tempted to sell its
power to the rich. The default kept in the file: the casino still pays 30, marked as a bend. If Paolo says no, all three
pay 14.
A. Keep it: the rich can outbid the clinic, and the player feels it (the default).
B. Even it out: all three pay the same, and the choice is only about who gets the light.

## A CONFLICT BETWEEN OUR OWN PAGES (for the page owners, not for Paolo)

QR-G line 30 (no branch pays more) and QR-L P16 (a price on a person must tempt, with a number) were both proposed in
QR-U and point opposite ways on a road event. So does the older pair, QR-G line 21 ("no number shown") and QR-B rule 4
("the kind choice is never also the profitable one"). This sweep read them together as: contracts pay one fee on every
done branch; events and main beats may carry one stated temptation, number on the button, with the other side fully
built. When QR-G and QR-L fold QR-U, they should write that sentence into both pages so the next sweep does not have to
choose. (FOLDED 10/1, QR-AB: written into QR-G under line 30 as THE HOUSE RULE, with a note on line 21, and into QR-L
as P16 with a note on P7.)
