THE FIGHT IS THE GAME'S FACE. It is the screen a stranger sees first, and right now everything on it is a coloured outline: a box with a thin orange line round it, a box with a thin green line round it, a flat circle with a face in it. None of it is a thing. It is a web page sitting on top of a game.

SO HERE IS THE FIGHT BUILT OUT OF OBJECTS. The health is not a coloured bar, it is a lit strip sunk into a slot, or a needle on a dial. The verbs are not outlines, they are keys with height that go down when you press them. SHOOT is not a circle, it is the one hot key on the machine, and it sits under a guard.
