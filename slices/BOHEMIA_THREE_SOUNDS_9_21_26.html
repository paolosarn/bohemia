SEVEN SOUNDS

SOUNDS · 9/21 and 9/22 · every one of these plays on the beat · notes box at the bottom

1. A FOOTSTEP THAT LANDS ON THE BEAT

Your old footsteps were almost pure tones, like a beep. A real footfall is mostly noise with a little weight under it. This one is noise-first and its loudest instant is right at the start, so the step lands ON the beat instead of just after it. Play it against the beat above and it should sit exactly on the tick.

2. THE STEP LOSES CONTACT

The same step, but the sound drops out twice while you are mid-stride, like old tape losing its grip on the head. It goes dull first and then quiet, and it never goes fully silent, because real tape does not. The two drop-outs are timed to the exact moments the ground skips under you in the walk, so what you hear is what you see.

3. THE PHONE STILL TRANSMITS

What the phone sounds like when you open it. Two tones together that do not agree with each other, the way an old emergency alert did, coming through a dead radio band. Then the tones stop and the hiss keeps going, because the transmitter is still on and there is nobody at the other end.

4. A SONG THROUGH THE DEAD SPEAKER

This is your own sound call, changed by the analog horror rule, made into something you can hear. You said post-apocalyptic FFX. The horror rule now comes first, and the way to keep both is a warm tune arriving through a broken radio. Play the same tune twice: clean, then through the speaker. The tune does not change, the room it reaches you through does.

5. THE FOLD

A generation passing. Somebody stops, and the electrical hum of the house carries on at exactly the same pitch, because the grid does not care that a man died. Nothing swells. Nothing rises. The silence is switched on rather than switched off.

6. THE FIGHT'S CLOUD

You ruled that a cloud crosses the fight. A cloud makes no noise, so what you hear is what a cloud does to a place: the brightness goes out of the room and comes back. No whoosh, because a whoosh is a sound effect pretending to be weather. The darkness numbers are the ones the city's own weather already uses.

7. THE DOOR

Walking through a door. The real sound is not the hinge, it is one room becoming another: outside is wide, inside is closed in and narrower, and the same hum is on both sides because it is the same house. The latch is the small part.
