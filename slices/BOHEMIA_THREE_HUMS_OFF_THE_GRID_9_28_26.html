YOU ALREADY THUMBED THE OLD THREE, SO THEY CANNOT CHANGE. A rule in this game says once you have judged a sound it stays exactly what you heard. So I did not touch them. Instead each one below is a BRAND NEW sound, built to actually hit the real number: a running generator's alternator makes 60 Hz because of how fast its shaft turns, and a transformer lighting up a block (or a sign) hums at 120 Hz because its core gets pulled twice every power cycle. These three play through real oscillators instead, so they land on the number for real.
