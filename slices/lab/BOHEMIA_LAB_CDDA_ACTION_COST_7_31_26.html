THE FINDING — AND IT IS BETTER THAN A TABLE OF MINUTES

An action's cost is FIXED, in "moves". Your CONDITION converts moves into real time. And the conversion can never exceed 4x, because speed has a hard floor at 25%. So the cost table stays one stable number per action, the FELT cost varies with how wrecked you are, and a terrible day can never become an infinite one.

THE DAY

your speed

1. ACTION COST — every action costs MOVES

100 moves = 1 turn = 1 second (calendar.h:289). So a 5-minute job is 30,000 moves, always, no matter who does it. Press one and watch the day go.

2. CONDITION — what makes the same action eat more of your day

Drag these and watch every cost above change. These are THEIR penalties, off character.cpp:7604-7657 — carried weight, pain, thirst — plus the floor that stops the whole thing running away.

3. TRAVEL — a trip is a formula, not a guess

mission_companion.cpp:1358 — 20 minutes + distance x 10 minutes. A base cost for setting out, then a rate. That is the shape our own walking clock wants.

4. ERRANDS — send someone away for a DECLARED BLOCK

Their companion missions are fixed blocks — 1, 4, 10 or 20 hours — and the payout is a RATE x HOURS WORKED (merch_amount = 3/4/5 * hours). You commit the block up front and collect when it is done.

5. SLEEP DEBT — and the first rung is TWO WHOLE DAYS

character.h:245-256. Their sleep debt is a ladder measured in MINUTES OWED, and nothing at all happens until you have been up two days. Everything you press on this page feeds it. One rough night is free; a week of them is not.
Their per-minute accrual and the penalties each tier applies live in their effects JSON, which this page does not model — so read the LADDER, not a punishment curve.
