THE FINDING — NOTHING DISAPPEARS. IT GETS AN OWNER.

Zomboid switches the power off on day 14 and that is the end of the story. What actually happened in Lebanon is that the state grid fell to about four hours a day and private generator cartels sold you the other six and a half, by the ampere, for over a hundred dollars a month — priced in the money that was busy dying. The utility was not deleted. It was privatised at gunpoint.

1. THE MONEY DIES — and it dies on a clock you don't control

Lebanon's pound was pegged at 1,507.5 to the dollar for 22 years. By February 2024 the official rate was 89,500 — a 59x devaluation, over 98% of value gone. Bank assets went from $217bn to $104bn. That is the slow version. The fast version is measured in DOUBLING TIME, and it is the same mechanic with the dial turned up.

2. THE FREEZE — you have money you cannot reach

This is the part games never model, and it is the cruellest thing on the list. Your savings are not gone. They are RIGHT THERE, and you may have this much of them per period. Three real caps, all of them real laws that real people lived under:
in the bank (unreachable)
in your hand the only money that is real

3. THE GRID DIES ON A TIMER — Project Zomboid, real Lua

ElecShut = 2, ElecShutModifier = 14 and the same for water. Tier 2 is "0-30 days"; the modifier is the day it happens. So the apocalypse ships with the lights going out on day 14 and the taps on day 14. A timer, not an event you can affect.

4. THE CARTEL — the mechanic neither game has

When the state grid died, Lebanese households bought 5 amperes for about 6.5 hours a day from a private generator, for over $100 a month in Beirut — barely enough for one air conditioner. THE VICE: the subscription is priced in hard currency while your wages are in the dying one, so the same 5 amperes costs more of your life every single month you keep it.
THIS IS ALREADY OUR LAW. CLUSTERED POWER: 12% lit, OWNED, the network eerily perfect. LIGHT=TERRITORY. Nobody patrols the dark. Bohemia's power law and Bohemia's economic collapse are the SAME LAW, and Lebanon is the proof that it is how collapse actually works.

5. COMFORT — the only curve that goes UP

Valheim, reused from our own LAB-05 teardown. Rested lasts 480s at comfort 1 and +60s per comfort level up to 17, so 24 minutes at the top. Comfort is a property of what you BUILT, and nothing in the crash can take it. Every other number on this page falls on somebody else's clock. This one only moves when you move it.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DECIDE

NOT ONE NUMBER HERE IS BOHEMIA'S. The THREE CURRENCIES law stands untouched — resources, electricity, clout, and no fourth thing; this page adds no currency and no price. The purse and the settlement economy belong to the WORLD lane, which is building them right now. What this page is FOR is one question: should the collapse be a set of falling curves you cannot touch, with exactly one rising curve that is whatever you built? And its corollary, which is the real prize: when a utility dies in Bohemia, does it DISAPPEAR, or does it get an OWNER you have to deal with?
