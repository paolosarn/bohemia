Equip the hammer. Left of this is the whole build system.

THE ONE THING TO TAKE

A workbench requires a roof and 70% cover to CRAFT. It requires no roof and no cover at all to claim its build radius or to suppress spawns. Drop it on bare dirt and you already have a camp. The house is improvement, never prerequisite. You do not build a camp, you place one.

THE FIVE MECHANICS, EACH PLAYABLE END TO END

1 · BUILDING. The hammer is the menu — one tool, three verbs, no build mode to enter or leave. In the real game those three verbs share one stamina hook keyed on the hammer, which is how we know they are one system. Placement answers with a status, not a yes/no.
2 · CRAFTING. Stand in a bench's range, under a roof, with 7 of the 9 tiles overhead covered. Craft, and the materials leave your pack.
3 · DECONSTRUCTING. Take it down and get all of it back — but only for pieces you placed. Tear down the ruin and you get nothing. Being wrong is free, so nobody hesitates.
4 · UPGRADING. Park an extension within 5 m of the bench. Level goes up, the white circle grows by 4 m, five levels, 20 m to 36 m.
5 · SPAWN SUPPRESSION. Roll for monsters. Inside the circle nothing comes. Take the bench away and roll again — that is what the circle was actually for.

AND IT IS STILL PHYSICAL

Support has to reach the ground. A wall floating off the end of nothing draws red and says so. Fast to use is not the same as weightless, and that is why it reads as real rather than as a menu.

WHAT IS NOT HERE, ON PURPOSE

Snapping, rotation, roof geometry, wall-by-wall architecture, and every real number of Bohemia's. This page models the half of Valheim's hammer he praised and skips the half its own players complain about.
