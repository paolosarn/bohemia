THE FINDING — MONEY IS NOT THE CURRENCY. STANDING IS.

Ten years cold, there is nothing left to trade with. What is left is who owns the working thing and whether they will deal with you. New Vegas proved that works, and the reason it works is that fame and infamy are TWO SEPARATE COUNTERS — you can be loved and feared by the same people at the same time. One slider is a stat. Two counters is a person.

1 + 2. STANDING — two numbers, and the title comes from BOTH

Tap a faction to see it. Fame and infamy never cancel: they are tracked apart, and the combination names you. That is where Wild Child, Dark Hero and Soft-Hearted Devil come from — titles that only exist because the game refused to average you out.

3. THE SAME DEED IS WORTH DIFFERENT AMOUNTS

Their real thresholds are per-faction, and they are not close. The Brotherhood needs 3 to accept you; the Legion needs 100 to idolise you. A small, insular, desperate outfit is cheap to impress. A huge one barely notices. Same deed, different standing — which is how you make a small faction worth caring about without buffing it.

4. WHAT YOU DO — and one of these is the trap

Every deed moves BOTH counters, some of them on more than one faction at once. There is no move that is free with everybody, which is the whole point: standing is not a score you farm, it is a set of people you are choosing between.

5. YOU BUILD TO BECOME SOMEBODY THEY HAVE TO DEAL WITH

The city-builder half. What you put up does not make money — money is banned. It makes you worth dealing with, and it is the only curve on this page that rises, because it is the only one you build with your hands (that part is Valheim's comfort). Under the CENTURY RULE it compounds across three acts.
