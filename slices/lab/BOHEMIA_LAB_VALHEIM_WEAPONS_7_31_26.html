THE FINDING — IT IS NOT A WEAPON LIST, IT IS FOUR MULTIPLIERS

The damage printed on a Valheim weapon barely matters. What matters is four multipliers you EARN: know what you're fighting, stand behind it, time a block, and have used the thing before. The weapon's real job is deciding WHICH of those you can reach.

YOUR WEAPON

THE LAST HIT, ALL THE WAY THROUGH

Tap an enemy to attack it. Walk BEHIND one first and watch what happens.

1 + 2. DAMAGE TYPES vs RESISTANCES — the knowledge lever

Ten damage types; three physical (slash / blunt / pierce) do the work. Resistance is a per-type multiplier applied to each type SEPARATELY, then armour is applied to the total. So a weapon that splits its damage gets partial credit against a resistance instead of being shut out.
AND THE DESIGN STATEMENT: NO ENEMY IN VALHEIM IS WEAK TO SLASH. Slash is what swords do, and the sword is the default weapon. The generalist is never optimal and never bad. That is a choice, and it is why nobody ever feels stupid for carrying a sword.

3. BACKSTAB — the positioning lever, and it is enormous

Most weapons 3x from behind. Two-handed clubs 2x. Knives 10x. That single number is a whole playstyle: a knife is a bad weapon from the front and the best weapon in the game from behind. This is the lever Bohemia's own audit says we do not have.

4. PARRY into STAGGER — the spend lever

Parry at the right moment and the enemy is instantly staggered, and a staggered enemy takes double damage and cannot act. Parry strength is a property of what you are holding: shields 1.5x, a buckler 2x, swords 2x, knives 4x. So your defence choice is also an offence choice.

5. WEAPON SKILL — and it raises your FLOOR, not your ceiling

This is the subtle one and it is the best idea in the list. Skill rolls your damage between a floor and a ceiling that BOTH climb. At skill 0 you roll 25%-55% of the weapon. At 100 you roll 85%-100%. The ceiling is reached by skill 75, so late levels buy you nothing but CONSISTENCY — mastery means you stop getting robbed, not that you hit harder.
