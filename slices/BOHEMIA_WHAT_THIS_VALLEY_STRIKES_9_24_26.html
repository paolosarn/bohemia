WHAT THIS VALLEY STRIKES ON THE HOUR

SOUNDS · 9/24 · the map has a clock now · no noise in any of them
THE MAP NEEDS A CLOCK YOU CAN HEAR. You said the overworld is Battle Brothers, and that time passes while you travel. You also said their map is a bunch of still pictures and we put life into it. So: something strikes the hour out there.

None of these three has any noise in it. They are metal being hit, and the notes inside each one are the real ones: a bell founder tunes a bell to a minor third, which is why a bell sounds sad, and a struck pipe is not a chord at all, which is why it clangs.

A. A CRACKED BELL

A town bell that nobody has looked after for ten years. A crack stops the shell moving as one piece, so it goes out of tune and loses its ring: it thuds instead of singing. This is the realistic one for this valley, so it leads.

B. A BELL THAT STILL WORKS

The same bell, uncracked. The low note rings on for over a second and a half after the high ones have gone, which is why a real bell seems to warm up as it fades. That is not a setting: higher notes lose their energy faster, always.

C. A LENGTH OF PIPE

No bell at all. Somebody hits a piece of steel pipe, because that is what a dead town actually has. A pipe's notes are not a chord, they are spread out at odd spacings, and that is exactly why it clangs instead of ringing. It is more than twice as bright as the bell.
