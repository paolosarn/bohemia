FIVE WAYS THE MACHINE SPEAKS

The small words stamped on the machine: the top strip, the buttons, the chips down the side. They all used to be typewriter text. Now they are their own alphabet, and here are five ways to cut it. Each block is the real thing, not a drawing of it. 9/15
THE GAME HAD ONE ALPHABET DOING THREE JOBS. The phone screen, the writing people say, and the labels stamped on the machine were all the same typewriter letters. Screens and writing got their own alphabets already. This round the machine got its own, which was the last one left.

THE TOP BLOCK IN EACH ONE IS TEN PIXELS TALL, WHICH IS THE REAL SIZE. The bigger block under it is only there so you can see the shapes. Judge the small one.
