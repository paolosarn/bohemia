ONE RING, CUT — NOT EIGHT LOOSE CIRCLES

"I want the action button to be only surrounded by one other circle, and that circle is cut into how many parts of the directions that we need. I don't want them to be independent circles. Like a circle outside of a circle, maybe kind of looking like a key lock or something."

Five ways to cut one ring. Same action button in the middle every time, same one ring around it — what changes is how it is cut, how thick it is, and how much it reads as a lock. All drawn at the size it would really be.

The middle circle is the action button and it never changes. The ring is one piece with cuts in it, so there is nothing floating.
Eight or four: 1, 2, 4 and 5 give you eight directions. 3 gives you four and gets much bigger, easier segments for a thumb — that is the trade, and it is yours.
The keyhole idea is strongest in 4, where the cuts go all the way through and the outer edge is notched like a lock cylinder. 2 is the same idea done quietly.
