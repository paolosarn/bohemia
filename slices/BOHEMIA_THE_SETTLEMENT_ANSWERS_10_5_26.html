THE SETTLEMENT ANSWERS

SOUNDS · 10/5 · four taps that had nothing
The barber, the stall, the board and the posts had no sound when you tapped them. Four new ones, each built out of a sound this lane already made, not invented fresh.

A. THE BARBER'S CLIPPERS

A mains clipper's blade struck against its stop twice every cycle: 120 Hz, a buzzy stack of harmonics, not a clean motor tone.

B. A CAN ON THE COUNTER

A tin can's own thin ring landing on the stall's wood counter: the same contact, two materials at once.

C. THE BOARD'S OWN NAIL

A tack pinning a card to the board: the same strike the fight's turn-close already uses, smaller and higher.

D. PAPER AGAINST THE POST

Many tiny creases catching and releasing, broadband, the way real paper crinkle actually spreads.
