THE ONES NOBODY CAME FOR

A hundred years pass. A third of this valley changes hands. Not one courthouse, jail, hospital or cemetery does. 9/24
3,415 BLOCKS OF 9,216 GET A NEW OWNER BETWEEN YOUR FIRST LIFE AND YOUR THIRD. That is 37% of the valley. The Mob take the most and they were already the biggest.

And of the 21 civic blocks — the courts, the police, the fire house, the library, the jail, the prison, the hospital, the cemetery, the chapel, the radio station — exactly ZERO change hands. Every one of them is dark in your first life and still dark in your third, with the same landlord the whole way.
697 blocks went from the Network to the Mob. Nobody printed a new board. They stamped the old one.
Who holds what in your first life and in your third, off his own faction graph. Four crews change tier. The Reds come up to fortress, the Caravans fall to town, the Trades come up to town, and the Anarchists drop to camp. Nobody typed any of it: it falls out of the two power columns that have been sitting in that file since before this lane existed.
THE BLOCK YOU WAKE UP ON DOES NOT MOVE EITHER. 48,48 is Mob fortress ground in act one and Mob fortress ground in act three. A third of the valley changes hands around it and your own corner keeps the same landlord for a hundred years.
AND A NUMBER I GAVE YOU WRONG LAST ROUND. I said there were 18 civic blocks. There are 21. Eighteen is the count of the eight kinds I listed out loud; the table underneath it already had ten kinds and added up to 21, and I did not notice the two numbers were about different things. Same mistake as last round, one round later: the table was right and the sentence over it was not.
