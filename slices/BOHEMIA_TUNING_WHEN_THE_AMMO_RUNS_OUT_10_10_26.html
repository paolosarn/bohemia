WHEN THE AMMO RUNS OUT

In Battle Brothers ammo is not expensive, it is limited. Your company has one stock, a quiver holds 10 shots, and it refills from the stock after each fight. Pick your start and your guns and see how many fights you get before you reach for a blade.
