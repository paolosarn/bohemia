FIVE WAYS THE SCREEN READS

Your phone has its own lettering now, made of dots, drawn for this game. Same phone five ways. Option A is what is in the game now. Tap a thumb. 9/13
THE PHONE WAS WRITTEN IN THE SAME LETTERING AS EVERYTHING ELSE, and so was the writing on the buttons, and so was the writing in the cards. One typeface doing all three jobs is the thing people point at when they say a screen was made by a machine.

SO THE PHONE GOT ITS OWN. It is built out of dots on a five by eight grid, the way a real cheap display works, and I drew all one hundred and six letters and symbols for it. It is two kilobytes, smaller than one small picture. The gaps between the dots are left open on purpose so it reads as a thing made of little lamps instead of as small print.

AND IT ALMOST SHIPPED WRONG TWICE. First the letters were a quarter wider than the old ones, so the name at the top of your phone got cut off. Then the little signal bars turned out to be a symbol I had not drawn, so that one mark was secretly in the OLD lettering, sitting right next to the new. Nobody would ever see that. A test does now.
