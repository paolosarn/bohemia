HOW BIG IS THE UI ON THE RUN

You said make all the UI 50% smaller. Half is option C. Here is half next to what is there now and next to three other amounts, because the thing half costs is the size of the thing you press, and it is easier to pick when you can see them beside each other. Every one of these is a real size, drawn to scale.

The number under each one is how big the buttons are to your thumb. The phone standard is 44. Anything under that you have to aim at.

Drawn at 250px wide where the real phone is 390, so everything here is 64% of life size — but all five are shrunk by the same amount, so the comparison is true.
Half is built and measured. It is not in the game yet because shrinking a button shrinks what it will answer to, and on a real tap test the halved build answered 2 presses out of 11 where the one you are playing answers 7. That is the thing to solve, and which of these you want decides how hard it is.
