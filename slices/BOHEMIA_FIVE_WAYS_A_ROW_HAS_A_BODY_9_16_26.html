FIVE WAYS A ROW HAS A BODY

The six answers on the morning card. These are the first things you read in the game, every single time. Each block below is the real thing at the real size, not a drawing of it. 9/16
THEY WERE A THIN GREY LINE AROUND A ROUNDED BOX. That is two of the things on the list of stuff that makes a game look like a robot made it, on the screen you see first.

AND THE WAY OUT OF THE CARD HAD THREE EDGES AT ONCE. GET UP was already drawn as a cut-corner metal box, and a second rule further down the file put a rounded grey outline back on top of it. Both were being drawn. Nobody meant to keep the second one.

A IS WHAT IS IN THE GAME NOW. B is what it looked like before, so you can see the difference. The rest are other ways to give a row a body.
