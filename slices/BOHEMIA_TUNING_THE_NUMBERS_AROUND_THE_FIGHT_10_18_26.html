THE NUMBERS AROUND THE FIGHT

TUNING, research only. The fight reads every number from one table. The screens around it (the settlement, the roster, hiring, the camp staff) type theirs into the code: 48 numbers, 17 of them already sitting in the table unread, 10 with no source at all. Each one below was read off the live file. Full page: records/BOHEMIA_TUNING_THE_SCREENS_AROUND_THE_FIGHT_IN_THE_ONE_TABLE_10_18_26.md

What is wrong, in three lines

  1. A hired man levels worse than in Battle Brothers. There, every recruit has stars on three stats and the stars make his level-ups bigger. Here, a man from the hall has a star on one stat, and the star raises his starting number instead.
  2. Camp staff are free. The button says one battery a night. The cook says two hundred batteries, once. The tap charges nothing, and nothing charges at night.
  3. The right numbers already exist and nobody uses them. A price file with the wiki's own sell-back, hiring and contract maths was written; no screen loads it. So a level-one militiaman can cost 225 batteries, when the wiki's formula tops out near 49.
