THE SIGN STILL LIGHTS

Six dead businesses whose signs are still up and still running on whatever power is left. The block's owner put their colour where the notice used to go. Every sign below is drawn at the size it would be in the world, blown up so you can see it. 9/21
THE WHOLE IDEA IN ONE LINE. The scary part is not a monster. It is that the motel sign still says VACANCY, in the motel's own letters, and nobody has worked there in ten years. The only thing that changed is the little board underneath: that used to be where they wrote NOW HIRING, and now it is just a block of somebody's colour, so you know whose street you are on before anyone says a word.

1. WHAT THE OBJECT IS

Three parts and no more. The box up top with the name in it, lit from behind through dirty plastic. The board under it, the slot for the notice. The pole. A fourth part would just be mess.
  • THE BOX still says what the place was called. It never changes.
  • THE BOARD is the part that changed hands. Colour only, most of the time.
  • THE POLE is galvanised steel and it is the only clean thing out there.

2. SIX OF THEM, EACH ON SOMEBODY'S STREET

The names are my first try and you can rewrite any of them. Which crew holds which sign is not my call and is not fixed here: the map already says who owns every block, so in the game the sign asks the map. This is the colour doing the job.

3. THE ONE THAT MATTERS: BEFORE, AFTER, AND DARK

Same sign three times. Nobody holding it, somebody holding it, and the same sign on a block where the power finally went. The light is real: most of the valley is off, and the game already knows which blocks still have a circuit, so a sign that glows is telling you something true about where you are standing.

4. HOW BIG IT ACTUALLY IS, NEXT TO A PERSON

This is the part I had to check before believing any of the above. A real roadside sign is about two and a half times the height of a person, and a person on our street is drawn about a hundred pixels tall. So the sign lands around 257 pixels tall in the game, which is bigger than your whole thumb on the screen. The name reads. The little line under it reads if you walk up to it. That is why the type was worth fixing.
THE BAR ON THE LEFT IS HOW TALL A PERSON IS DRAWN

5. AND THE HARD ONE

Three of the crews barely have a colour at all, and this is what that costs: with the Mob holding it, "somebody holds this block" and "nobody holds this block" are the same picture. A grey flag is not a flag. So here is my fix in the third and fourth panels: the grey crews do not paint the board, they take it down. An empty frame with the bolts still in it reads from any distance and in any light, and it says something true about them, which is that they do not advertise. Tell me if you hate it.
Nothing here is in the game yet. It is drawn by one function that takes the business, the owner's colour and whether the power is on, so the day this goes in, the world calls the same function and the map answers the middle part. Names on the signs are drafts.
