THE FIRST NOTICE

WORLD · 9/21 · the machine keeps talking after its owner is gone
We wrote the survivors for months and never wrote the thing that stopped. Out of 3,014 spoken lines in this game, eight name a city, a county, an office, a board or a utility at all. So here is the first one that does. It is a real disconnection notice, with every slot the law makes a real one carry, filled in from the actual grid, the actual block and the actual money in the game. Nothing on it is typed by hand.

1. THE NOTICE, ON THE PHONE

Served on a lit feeder in this valley. The debt is one battery, because everything costs one. The reconnection fee is one battery. The form gives you a ten day dispute window and a right of appeal.

6. THE ONE THAT NEVER NEEDED ANYBODY

Every tract in this valley is walled, because the county made the wall compulsory. A wall like that comes with an association, and an association comes with rules recorded against the land itself. Those rules do not need a city, or a court, or an office. They run with the dirt forever. So the fines are still adding up, and the hearing you are owed is in front of a board that stopped meeting years ago.

7. AND THE ONE THEY BRING TO YOUR DOOR

Glasgow, 1915: twenty five thousand families stopped paying rent and won in nine months. It was never about the money. The landlord only wins if he can put somebody else in your flat, so the neighbours stood in the doorway and nobody could get in. This is the paper the man at the door was holding.
