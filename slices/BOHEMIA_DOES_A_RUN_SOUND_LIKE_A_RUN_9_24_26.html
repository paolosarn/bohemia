DOES A RUN SOUND LIKE A RUN

SOUNDS · 9/24 · measured in the real game first · 120 BPM
WHAT I FOUND IN THE GAME, BEFORE MAKING ANYTHING. One press of the pad carries you a whole house, 25 squares, and makes exactly one footstep sound. That is right, and it is your own rule: one step is one house. I walked 320 squares in three directions and counted 19 footstep sounds, so 94 out of 100 squares you cross are silent and that is correct too.

But holding the pad makes you run, and a run covers two houses in one beat and still makes one sound. So a run sounds exactly like a walk while you move twice as fast. That is the thing to fix, and these two buttons are the question.

A. WALKING, ONE HOUSE A BEAT

One footfall on every beat. This is what the game does now and it is right. Turn the beat on above and it lands exactly on the tick.

B. RUNNING, TWO HOUSES A BEAT

Two footfalls a beat, evenly spaced, so the run sounds twice as fast because you are going twice as fast. This is what running should sound like and does not.

C. WHAT RUNNING SOUNDS LIKE NOW

The same as A, because the second step of a run is thrown away. Play A then C and you will hear nothing change, which is the bug.
