THE DARK INSTITUTIONS

More of the one you liked, and the map handed me something worse than I was going to make up. 9/22
EVERY COURTHOUSE, POLICE STATION, FIRE STATION, LIBRARY, JAIL, PRISON, HOSPITAL AND CEMETERY IN THIS VALLEY IS DARK. Eighteen places. Not one of them has power. I did not write that, I counted it off the map.

The data fort has all six of its blocks lit.
The only building with its lights on is the one with no name on it.
Counted off the real map: every block of every kind, and whether it touches a live circuit. Nothing civic made the list.
