THE THING NOBODY HAD NOTICED

Your game does not know who is a man and who is a woman. Not on the person, not on the body, not in the name list, not on the face. A name was the only thing in that whole picture saying "she", and nothing was listening to it. So I marked the names, which is a fact about the names you already approved, and the face listens now.

It is only the haircut so far. The faces still do not read as clearly as they should, because there is one body in this game and no other dial to turn yet. If you want the faces to go further than the hair, say so and that is the next job.
