WHAT A FLIP SOUNDS LIKE

SOUNDS · 9/25 · one tap on the phone, three acts · 120 BPM
WHAT THIS IS. You said the three acts are open at the same time and you flip between them on the phone. This is the sound of that flip.

It is not a whoosh and it is not a swoosh. It is a radio changing station. When a radio loses one station and has not found the next, the hiss gets louder, not quieter, because the machine turns its own volume up hunting for something to receive. That is real, every radio does it, and it is the reason inter-station hiss is the loudest thing on the dial.

And that gap is the future you have not built yet. So the thinner the act you are flipping into, the longer the machine hunts and the louder it gets. The sound says the same thing your ruined city says. You tap once, so it is over inside one beat, every time.

A. FLIPPING INTO AN ACT YOU HAVE LIVED

A full ledger. The old station drops, a short hiss, the new one catches straight away. The gap is a tenth of a second and about twice as loud as the station, which is what a real radio does.

B. FLIPPING INTO AN ACT HALF BUILT

Half a ledger. It hunts nearly twice as long and gets louder before it catches, and the station it lands on has lost some of its body.

C. FLIPPING INTO A RUIN

Nothing built yet, which is where you start. The longest hunt and the loudest, and what it finds is a bare hum with none of its harmonics left. This is the one the tutorial is made of.

On the beat, in order, so you can hear the hunt getting longer.
