WHY THE REWARD IS BATTERIES, NOT A NEW SCORE

Battle Brothers pays these in "renown," a score this game doesn't have and isn't getting one for this. This game locked its money down to exactly three kinds a while back, and none of them is a reputation score. So the company gets paid the same way everything else in this game pays you: a few batteries, once, when it's earned.
