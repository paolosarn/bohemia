The very first screen, before anything loads. Every one below is 390 pixels wide, the real width of a phone, and nothing is blown up. A is what is in there right now. 9/21
YOU SAID MAKE ALL THE UI 3-D. So here is the first screen four ways, and three of them are built like real objects: one light coming from above and to the left, a lit edge where the light lands, a dark edge where it does not, and a side underneath so the thing has a body you could put a finger under.

AND IT IS ALL ANALOG HORROR AT THE SOURCE. Not a filter on top. The case is a colour an office picked in 1979 and nobody ever changed. The glass is lit from behind by something that is still running with nobody left to read it. The little green light beside the word is ON. That is the horror: the machine is fine, and the people are gone.

WHAT I DECIDED WITHOUT YOU: the word is BEGIN and it is one tap, never two. WHAT I NEED: which body the first screen has. One letter.
