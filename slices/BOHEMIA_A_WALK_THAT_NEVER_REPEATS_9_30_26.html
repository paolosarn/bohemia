A WALK THAT NEVER REPEATS

SOUNDS · 9/30 · the same footstep, played six or twelve times, never landing the same way twice
THIS PAGE IS ABOUT REPETITION, NOT PITCH OR MATERIAL. The old game plays one recorded sample for every sidewalk step and every wood floor step in the whole valley. The fix that already shipped for concrete, asphalt, dirt, sand and a wood floor is about getting the MATERIAL right. This page is about the OTHER half of the same complaint: even the right material, played the same way every time, is still one sample on repeat.

So each button below plays a real walk, not one footstep. Every single footfall in it is a little different: a heel never lands with the exact same force twice, so the timing and the loudness of the contact drift a few percent step to step, the same way a real gait does. There is no pool of samples to run out of; it never has to repeat.
ITEM ONE · A WALK ON THE SIDEWALK · six steps, one a beat

WALKING ON CONCRETE

Six footfalls, one every half second (120 BPM, one a beat). Listen for the small differences step to step, not a loop.
ITEM TWO · A WALK ON A WOOD FLOOR · six steps

WALKING ON A WOOD FLOOR

Same idea, the material that really does ring.
ITEM THREE · RUNNING ON THE SIDEWALK · twelve steps, two a beat

RUNNING ON CONCRETE

Twice the footfalls in the same time, which is what a run actually is: two strides in the time of one walked stride, never two jammed into one instant.

FOR COMPARISON: THE SAME FOOTSTEP, SIX TIMES, WITH NO VARIETY

What the old system actually does when its sample pool is one deep: the identical render, back to back. This is the thing the fix above replaces.
