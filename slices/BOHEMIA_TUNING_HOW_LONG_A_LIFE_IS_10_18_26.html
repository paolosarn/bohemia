HOW LONG A LIFE IS

TUNING, research only. You asked for a random, fun way for people to die of old age. An act is one year, so old age mostly takes people between acts, not while you play. Three ways to do it, each tested on 20,000 lives.

The pick: THE KIND ROLL

  • Nobody you know dies of old age before 60.
  • While you play an act, about one person in 40 dies of age every six acts.
  • 8 in 10 people you met at 40 are still there in the next act.
  • How you played shows: of the 20-year-olds you met, 55 in 100 reach act 3 if you rebuilt the city, 48 if you did not.
  • The body always warns you first: the stoop, the cough, the grey hair.
