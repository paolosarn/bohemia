THE ROOM ON THE TAP

SOUNDS · 9/21 · tap a sound, then a thumb · the notes box is at the bottom
What this is. When you press the door, the game used to make no sound for a long time. Now a room turns on: a low electrical hum with a faint hiss on top, like a place that still has power and nobody in it. Your heartbeat sound is unchanged and sits on top of the room. Listen to each on its own, then both together, which is what you actually hear in the game.

1. IS THE ROOM TOO LOUD OR TOO QUIET UNDER THE HEARTBEAT

I set it to carry 60% of the heartbeat's energy, so the heartbeat stays the thing you notice. That 60% is the one number I picked myself. Everything else is measured off your own approved heartbeat.

2. WHEN THE SONG STARTS, SHOULD THE ROOM STAY ON

Right now it gets quieter and keeps running, because a room that stops is a file that ended. You might want the song to have the place to itself.

3. IS THIS THE RIGHT KIND OF ROOM

It is built to sound like it came out of an old radio: nothing below 100 Hz and nothing above 5 kHz, which is the band a real AM broadcast passes. That makes the loading room and the music the same broken transmitter. The other option is a room you are simply standing in, with no radio in the way.
