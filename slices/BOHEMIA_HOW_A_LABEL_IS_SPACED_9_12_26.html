FIVE WAYS A LABEL IS SPACED

Same buttons, same metal, one thing different: how far apart the letters sit. Option A is what is in the game now. Tap a thumb. 9/12
TINY WIDE CAPITALS ARE ON THE LIST OF WHAT GIVES A BOT-BUILT SCREEN AWAY, and we had eighty of them on the walked city. Fifty-eight were the exact same number, typed out fifty-eight separate times. That is not a rule, it is a habit.

SO THE GAP NOW COMES FROM A MACHINE, NOT FROM TASTE. A stencil plate holds its letters where the plate cut them. A label-maker steps its wheel the same distance every time whether the letter is an I or a W. An old lit readout has a one-dot gap built into the screen itself. Each one below is a real machine, and the number under it is that machine's number, not one I liked the look of.
