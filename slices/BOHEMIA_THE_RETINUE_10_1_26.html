WALKING OFF IS A FREE NO

Ask for a medic, then walk up to somebody else before two beats pass: the offer is gone, no cost, and medic is never asked again this game. But the NEXT role still gets offered the moment you ask again — a single decline used to block every role after it forever, found and fixed this round (measured below).

WHY TWO OF THE FOUR AREN'T LIVE YET

A medic who heals faster needs a wound to heal; this game only has a ruling that a struck-down man is out 30 to 40 days (rule 36b), not code that tracks it. A mechanic who repairs cheaper needs a repair meter; this game only has a flat yes-or-no switch for a spent plate. Building a fake meter just to give these two something to discount would be inventing a system nobody asked for. They are hired for real, paid for real, and will do real work the day those two systems exist.
