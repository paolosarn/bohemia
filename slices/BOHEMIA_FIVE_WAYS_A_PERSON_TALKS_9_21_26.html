YOU SAID A QUEST IS PEOPLE, NEVER A CARD, AND THE WORDS COME OUT OF A MOUTH. That means this panel is the only place in the walked game where a sentence is allowed to exist. Every bullet list and every pop-up is dead. So this thing has to be right, because it is going to carry everything anybody ever says to you.

AND IT HAS TO BE A THING. Right now it is a see-through grey box with a tiny face next to it. Below, the face is behind glass sunk into the case, or it fills the top of the screen, or it is talking to you through a speaker. Your answers are keys with height, not outlines.
