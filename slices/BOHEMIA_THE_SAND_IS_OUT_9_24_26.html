THE SAND IS OUT OF THE ONES YOU LIKED

SOUNDS · 9/24 · three taps · each one plays the door, then the cloud, then the song
THE SAND WAS ONE FILTER, SHARED BY EVERY SOUND I MADE. It was supposed to cut the top off each sound at the limit that sound claims. It was cutting in the wrong place, so about a third of every sound was sitting above its own limit. That is the sand, and it was in the ones you liked too, not only the ones you killed.

Each button plays three sounds you voted yes on, one after another: the door, the fight's cloud, and the song through the dead speaker. Same three every time, so you are hearing the filter and nothing else.

A. AS YOU HEARD THEM

The old filter, unchanged, so you can hear what you approved. This is the leaky one. The door has nearly 16 percent of itself sitting above the limit it claims.

B. THE SAND OUT

The same three sounds with the filter fixed. A real one now: flat up to the limit, then it falls off a cliff, which is what a broadcast transmitter actually does because the law makes it. Nothing else about these sounds changed. This is what ships unless you say otherwise.

C. THE SAND OUT AND A DIFFERENT MACHINE

Same fixed filter, but the machine is a tape deck instead of a radio transmitter. A tape reaches about 14,000 and a radio channel only reaches 5,000, so C keeps a lot more of the top while still not leaking. This is the real question underneath: when you hear this world, are you hearing it off the radio or off a tape? B is boxed in. C is open.

WHAT MOVED, IN NUMBERS

How much of each sound sits above the limit it claims. Under 5 is the rule this lane set itself.
