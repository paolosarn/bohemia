THE WATER STILL RUNS

Somebody in this valley owns every drop of water anyone drinks, and the game already knows who. This is the sign that says it out loud. 9/21
I CHECKED THE REAL VALLEY BEFORE DRAWING ANYTHING. There are six water places on the map. Three still have power and three are dead. All three of the live ones are wired into ANARCHISTS ground. So one crew holds one hundred percent of the running water and nobody has ever been told. The game will even say the sentence if you ask it: "The pumps are running. ANARCHISTS hold the water." Nothing on any screen has ever said it to you.

1. THE SIGN

A water agency's sign at the gate of the plant. Low, concrete, built to last forever, and it did. The panel in the middle is a status board, the kind that says STAGE 2 WATER RESTRICTIONS at a real water district. Ours still works. It is wired to the pumps and it is still reporting, correctly, to nobody. That is the scary part. The agency is gone and its sign is still doing its job.

2. WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE IN THE FOUR REAL CASES

Every one of these is a state the game can actually be in. The colour band along the bottom is the crew that owns the wire feeding the plant, which is the same rule as the roadside signs: whoever can switch it off owns it.

3. THE SIX WATER PLACES, AS THEY REALLY ARE

Straight off the valley, not made up. The three dead ones are reservoirs, which sit out on open ground with no street to feed them. The three live ones are the pump station and both treatment plants, and they are all on the same crew's wire.
One more thing worth knowing: the pumps report they are lifting zero litres, because the valley's thirst is reading zero. That is not this sign's problem and I did not touch it, but somebody should look.

4. WHY THIS IS THE RIGHT SECOND SIGN

The first six signs were shops. A shop closing is sad. A water authority closing is the end of a city, and Las Vegas only exists because water gets pumped a thousand feet uphill out of the lake. That is already in the game as real physics. So this is the one sign in the valley that is about whether anybody lives here at all, and it is the one sign whose colour band actually matters, because the crew on that band can turn off your water.
Nothing here is in the game yet. The name on it is a draft and you can rewrite it. The crew on the band is not a choice I made: it is whoever the map says holds the wire.
