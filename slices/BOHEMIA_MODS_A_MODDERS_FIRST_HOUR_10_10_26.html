A modder's first hour

MODS. Every step is something that exists today. One is marked NOT BUILT.
5 min1 LOOK
Read the one page that lists the 15 data files, every field, and who reads each.
2 min2 SERVE THE GAME
cd the-repo
python3 -m http.server 8000
Open localhost:8000/slices/BOHEMIA_FIGHT.html. The fight fetches its data, so a double-click will not work.
5 min3 CHANGE ONE THING
In records/target/bb/weapons.json, find "knife". Change damage_min 15 to 20 and damage_max 25 to 30. Reload.
RAN: before 15 and 25, after 20 and 30, no errors, file put back.
5 min4 CHECK IT AS A MOD
node tools/bohemia_mods_merge_reference.js mymods
Prints CHANGED and which row moved.
NOT BUILT: the game does not load a mods folder yet. Step 3 is how you see it today.
10 min5 THE THREE MISTAKES
A word where a number goes: skipped, named. A field that does not exist: ignored, named. A new row missing fields: skipped, and it lists them. Nothing crashes.
15 min6 ADD SOMETHING NEW
Copy a whole row, give it a new id and name, change what you want. A worked example (a Moon Blade) is in tools/mods_reference.
1 min7 REFRESH THE PAGE
node tools/bohemia_mods_schema_page.js --write
BEFORE YOU TRUST A CHANGE
The old fight inside the demo ignores these files. A weapon plays only its first skill. Nothing checks how big a number is.
