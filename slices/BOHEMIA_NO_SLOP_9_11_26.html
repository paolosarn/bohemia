WHAT GIVES IT AWAY, AND WHAT WE HAD. The research names the same short list every time: a handful of default fonts, dark mode by reflex, a grey one-pixel line around every card, rounded cards around everything, gradients and glow, tiny wide-spaced capital labels, and emoji used as icons. I counted ours. We had all of it: 800 hits across the two files that draw your screen. That number is a floor I can re-run, and it only goes down.

THE RULE THAT REPLACES IT comes from the world, not from a component kit. An edge is a thing's edge: a lit rim on top, a dark base under it, never a grey hairline floating on nothing. A label is painted or stamped ON something. A panel has a body you can see. All five below follow that. They differ in what the object is made of.
