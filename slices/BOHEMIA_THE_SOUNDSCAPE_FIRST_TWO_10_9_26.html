THE SOUNDSCAPE'S FIRST TWO

SOUNDS · 10/9 · the smith and the armourer had nothing
The valley's working sounds, from Grok ask 23's floor ("the clinking of someone making disorder"): the smith's own hammer, and the armourer setting a rivet. Both out of the same real metal mode this lane already uses for a held pipe.

A. THE SMITH'S HAMMER

An anvil bolted to its stump: the same free-free bar series as a held pipe, lower and far shorter because the mass bleeds the ring into the ground almost at once.

B. THE ARMOURER'S RIVETS

A rivet's tiny, stiff metal rings even higher and shorter than a tack; a rivet gun sets it in several quick blows, never one.
