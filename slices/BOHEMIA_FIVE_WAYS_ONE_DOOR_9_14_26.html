You had two buttons that opened the same subject. Option A is what is in the game now. Tap a thumb. 9/14
SAME COMPLAINT YOU MADE ABOUT PRETTY MAP AND DROP IN, IN A NEW PLACE. OUTFIT up in the top strip and STANDING down the left both opened your faction business. Two doors, one room.

THEY WERE NOT ACTUALLY THE SAME SCREEN THOUGH, AND THAT CHANGED THE FIX. I opened both and read them. STANDING is where YOU stand: your rung, this ground, the rent, who would come with you. OUTFIT was the list of all sixteen crews: where each one is, whether you have met them, what they want, what they pay. Two useful things about one subject. So I did not delete the list. It moved inside STANDING, one tap further in.

AND HERE IS WHY STANDING IS THE ONE THAT SURVIVED. In this game OUTFIT means your CREW. The game says it plainly elsewhere: your outfit and theirs are at war. I still read it as clothes, with the whole game open in front of me, because there is also a CLOTHES tab. A stranger has the same collision and none of the context. STANDING says the same thing in words nobody can mistake.
