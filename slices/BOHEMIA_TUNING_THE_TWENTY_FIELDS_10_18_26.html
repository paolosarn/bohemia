THE TWENTY FIELDS, AS FOUR 2D STUDIOS SET THEM

TUNING, research only. For DIRECTION to read before it picks the style guide's numbers (rule 114a). Eighty cells: 51 from the studio itself, 2 secondary, 1 community, 4 inference, 22 not published by anyone. The one finding: three of the four studios never cap colours per sprite; the colour is decided at the source (a model, a palette file, a normal map) and the lights change it on screen. A colour count on a lit render or a screenshot would reject the rig's look. Full page: records/BOHEMIA_TUNING_THE_TWENTY_STYLE_GUIDE_FIELDS_AS_FOUR_STUDIOS_SET_THEM_10_18_26.md

#FieldBattle BrothersDead CellsBlasphemousEastwardOurs today
1Visual identity statementPainted busts, 'somewhat more serious and gritty, but still exaggerated' (moved there from 'light-hearted comic'); the detailed painted look kept by layering gear instead of animating.
bb_devblog88 bb_devblog5
Three pillars: 'a saturated color palette, Celtic architecture and the theme of alchemy'; 'a modern take on this art style' with particles, motion, a 3D lighting system and shaders.
dc_deepdive_colors dc_80lv
'Purist' pixel art: 'there is nothing in Blasphemous that is not pixel art'; no shaders, no 3D elements; Spanish religious art (Ribera, Velazquez, Zurbaran, Murillo) and Seville.
bl_thr bl_pczone
'Retro Pixel Art + 3D Lighting' (the studio's own line, 2015); 'a 3D game with a 2D perspective'.
ew_pixpil_2015 ew_gamedeveloper
Rule 114a's example line plus rule 115: modelled at the source, rendered to pixels through the rig; analog horror on every pixel.
2Native resolution and pixel sizeNo pixel grid: painted raster at the screen's native resolution, one painted pixel per screen pixel, with separate UI-scale and scene-scale sliders. Icons painted at 500x500 and resized to 48x48. A sprite's size is set by its brush metadata offsets (the 104x142 width/height in the docs' example 'do nothing').
bb_density_record bb_paul bb_modding_brushes
640x360 (a developer-adjacent list, not a studio page). The hero is 'only about 50 pixels tall'; the mesh is rendered 'in a very small size and without antialiasing'. (secondary)
dc_resolution_tweet dc_deepdive_3d
640x360 native, pixel-perfect at integer multiples; in development 'a virtual frame resolution of a fixed 360 pixel height' with the width from the monitor's aspect ratio, pixel-perfect or stretched upscaling as a player option.
bl_pcgw bl_kickstarter_tech
Not published by Pixpil. Players measure a 360p base, the PC build locked at 720p at launch with linear filtering on non-integer scales (a community thread, not a studio statement). (community)
ew_steam_res
The person is one size everywhere, a 112 box about 100 px painted; the rig draws 128 px a metre, a 1.72 m man 66x132 px; a 96 px tile floor. No internal canvas is declared; the demo draws at device pixels.
3Camera and perspectiveHex grid, busts only: 'the top half of each character, without animation', one facing, gear swapped in layers because 'drawing each character in several directions would have multiplied the workload immensely'; the world map painted from above.
bb_devblog5 bb_devblog5_moddb
Side view platformer; backgrounds drawn as pixel art and 'redrawn in normal maps' so 3D lights work on a flat scene.
dc_deepdive_3d dc_deepdive_colors
Side view, no 3D elements anywhere.
bl_thr
Top-down three-quarter; 'smart placing and orientation of the sprite to make it look like 3D'; each asset split by 'its natural structure' and rebuilt in a 3D environment before lighting.
ew_redbull ew_gamedeveloper
45 degree three-quarter (the 45 DEGREE ART LAW), a square grid (the third votes).
4Master paletteNot published. The only palette note is a development one: 'the strong colors are just for differentiating the regions better during development' (placeholders, not the look).
bb_devblog52
No palette file published. The rule is a direction: saturation pushed 'almost exclusively' in the midtones; complementary schemes indoors 'to give a confined feeling', analogous outdoors for depth; the dynamic light tints a level 'while keeping its base colors'.
dc_deepdive_colors
Not published (the 200-page art book is the likely home; not reachable from here). (not published)
bl_thr
Not published. The final colours are graded after lighting: the engine adds 'LUT texture' colour grading, SSAO and an optional CRT blur on top of the sprites.
ew_80lv
No master palette file yet (rule 114a owes it). The rig snaps a render's colours 0.6 of the way toward the pack family's colours; people and beasts have no family yet.
5Colour budget per asset classNone. Painted raster, thousands of colours per sprite. The one size rule is for icons: at 48x48 'it all comes down to colors and contrasts when you resize it'.
bb_paul
None fixed per sprite. Each frame ships with a normal map and is lit by a toon shader and dynamic lights, so on-screen colours are the light's, not a count the artist set.
dc_deepdive_3d dc_80lv
Not published. INFERENCE: hand-placed pixels with no shaders imply a hand-chosen palette per sprite, the only one of the four where a per-sprite count could even be measured on screen. (inference)
bl_thr
None published. Deferred lights plus a LUT change every screen colour, so a count on a screenshot is a count of the lighting.
ew_gamedeveloper ew_80lv
Rule 114a's example caps (12 / 6 / 5 / 24) are illustrative. The rig's round three renders measured 10,146 to 13,047 colours per sheet, and DIRECTION wrote 'his art is not limited-palette'. THE FINDING: see section 3.
6Light directionNot published for the busts (static paintings). The map has a day/night cycle that changes the light and the play (night slows parties and cuts sight range); its direction is not stated.
bb_devblog17
No fixed direction: dynamic 3D lights over per-frame normal maps, 'a torch can light a statue from the correct side'. The normal maps for backgrounds are drawn by hand.
dc_deepdive_colors dc_80lv
Baked into the pixels; no runtime light. Direction not published. (not published)
bl_thr
No fixed direction: 'general deferred shading, which enables our designer to put more lights into the level'; a hand-painted bump map per asset part; weather and time of day move the light.
ew_gamedeveloper ew_pixpil_deferred
One hard light in the rig (rule 115d); the gate measures light top-to-bottom per render (round three: light +8.7 on the truck).
7Shading stepsPainted gradients, no step count; after the resize an icon gets 'smart sharpen 35%' and '+20 brightness +20 contrast'.
bb_paul
Stepped by a 'basic toon shader' over the normal map; 'cell shading techniques when rendering models in low resolution and without applying anti-aliasing'.
dc_deepdive_3d dc_80lv
Hand pixel shading, frame by frame; steps not published. (not published)
bl_gameanim
Lit by the bump map and the lights, plus a simulated SSAO pass; steps not published.
ew_80lv
Clay shading in the rig: roughness at least 0.9, specular 0.15, metallic at most 0.2, grime from the model's own ambient occlusion (round three).

9Anti-aliasing and ditheringFully anti-aliased by nature (painted raster scaled by the engine).
bb_density_record
No AA: rendered 'without antialiasing'. The named leftover is 'flickering pixels', which 'could be eliminated manually, but the whole point of the process is speed'; 'we still haven't found any solution'.
dc_deepdive_3d dc_80lv

bl_thr
Pixel art in Aseprite; an optional CRT blur filter on top; dither rule not published.

The rig's round-three fault list: 'airbrushed at 4x' is a fail; grain finer than a pixel averages to grey; even grain is a leopard print.

18Tie-breakerReadability wins: faces must stay readable at the most zoomed-out level, so figures get more iconic and weapons leave real proportion.
bb_devblog5
Speed wins over cleanup: flickering pixels are left because 'the whole point of the process is speed'.
dc_deepdive_3d
Purity wins: nothing on screen that is not pixel art, even where it costs.
bl_thr
The light wins over the pixel's own colour: the sprite is split and bump-mapped so the lights, weather and time of day decide the final colour. (inference)
ew_gamedeveloper
Rule 114a's example: readability at 1x, then palette over detail, then silhouette over colour. Rule 115 adds the source: one rig, everything the same.
19Review procedureJudge at the final size: paint rough at 500x500, resize to 48x48, compare against the existing icons, then sharpen and punch.
bb_paul
Time the keys with the fewest frames until they convince, then interpolate; clean flickers by hand only where it is worth the time.
dc_deepdive_3d
Concept sketch, pencil animation draft, pixel frames, then tuning in the engine's sprite animator.
bl_kickstarter_chars
Asset in Aseprite, split by structure, rebuilt in 3D, bump map painted per part, then into the engine under the lights.
ew_gamedeveloper
Rule 114a's example (contact sheet at 1x, real background, greyscale, 8x8 silhouette row, in the game, the gate report attached); the rig has four sheets at 1:1 and a look sheet (round three).
