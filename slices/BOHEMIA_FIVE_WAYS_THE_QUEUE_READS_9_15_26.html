FIVE WAYS THE QUEUE READS

The one place you vote is built. This is how the list inside it could look. Option A is what is in the game right now. 9/15
WHAT YOU ASKED FOR IS IN. One tab, behind the gear, in the demo. Everything new from every chat lands in the same list, newest at the top. Up, down, or say why. The second you vote, the row leaves and it does not come back.

WHAT I HAD TO PICK WITHOUT YOU. How much of the thing you see before you tap. Right now a row is a name and one line, and the picture or the sound only loads when you tap LOOK AT IT. That keeps the list fast when forty things are waiting, but it means you tap twice to judge once. Here are five ways it could go.
