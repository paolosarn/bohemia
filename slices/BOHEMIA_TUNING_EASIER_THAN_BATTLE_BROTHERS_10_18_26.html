EASIER THAN BATTLE BROTHERS

TUNING, research only. Your words on the numbers card are now in the one numbers table. All 209 numbers carry a mark. Where a number decides how hard the game is, Battle Brothers' number is the HARDEST the slider goes, and ours starts easier.

Four places the game is harder than Battle Brothers right now

  1. how many of a hired man's stats carry stars. the hire screen stars ONE stat and applies it to his starting roll (TUNING [the settlement's numbers] finding): a hired man is worth less than a Battle Brothers recruit
  2. what a hire costs. the hall asks up to 225 batteries (2248 crowns): four and a half times the formula (TUNING [the settlement's numbers] finding)
  3. a level-up makes his man stronger in the fight. the fight rolls every man fresh from his background and his level only picks his perks, while the map adds two strength a level to size the enemy: every level makes the enemy bigger and his men no better (records/BOHEMIA_TUNING_EASIER_THAN_BATTLE_BROTHERS_MEASURED_10_18_26.md s3.1)
  4. what a cheap hire costs. the hall asks up to 41 batteries; the formula gives 29 (TUNING [the settlement's numbers] finding)
