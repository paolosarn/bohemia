FOUR WAYS THE GAME LOADS

The screen that covers everything before you can touch anything. 390 pixels wide, real size, nothing blown up. A is what I built and wired. 9/22
YOU SAID: no loading screen, just glitchy shit loading in and out before you can touch anything, and maybe even a loading bar if I am smart enough. So here it is, and the bar is real.

THE BAR CANNOT LIE. There is no timer anywhere in it. The only thing that moves it is the game reporting what it has actually finished, and it hits the end exactly when the pad works. If nothing has been loaded yet it sits at zero and says so. A bar that fills up on a stopwatch while the game is still chugging is the same lie as a button that does nothing when you tap it.

AND IT IS A DEAD MACHINE, NOT A SPINNER. It is the power company's terminal booting in a room nobody is in. It says what it is doing in the same flat voice all the way down. The words are the writing chat's, not mine: five lines, one per real stage, each one only showing up when that stage is genuinely working. One line in the list is about people instead of plant and it says NO OPERATOR ON DUTY, flat, as a status and not a warning. That is the only wrong thing on the screen, on purpose: two would be a haunted house.

AND THE COUNT IS REAL. 58 OF 68 is the actual number of things finished out of the actual number there are, printed off the same arithmetic the bar is made of, so the words and the bar can never disagree.

WHAT I NEED: one letter.

The progress is not part of the machine, it is the edge of your phone. A single lit strip across the very bottom, the whole width, no number at all. Quietest of the four. You feel the progress in the corner of your eye instead of reading it, and you never learn exactly how long is left.
A is built and it is a drop-in file the play chat wires the real numbers into. Its bar takes only what the game reports finished and has no timer in it anywhere, so it cannot run ahead of the game. Every edge, key and piece of glass above comes out of the same one file as the fight, the talk panel and the notes box.
