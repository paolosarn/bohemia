YOU CAN TODAY

Change damage, armour damage, range, durability, price (in crowns), the skill's AP and fatigue.

YOU CANNOT TODAY

Give a skill a shape (a sweep, a line, five tiles behind). It is a sentence, and nothing reads it.
Make a weapon offer a second skill. The fight plays the first one and the reload.
Point a weapon at its mastery. That link is a sentence in another file.
