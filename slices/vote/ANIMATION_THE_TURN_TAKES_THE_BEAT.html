THE TURN TAKES THE BEAT

Right now, when you change direction, he is just a different drawing on the very next frame. There is no turn. He is facing south, then he is facing east, with nothing in between.

He already has eight drawings, one for every direction. A turn is those drawings in order. Nothing new gets drawn; the turn is just allowed to take the same half second everything else takes.

Both are turning the same corner, south to east, on the beat. The top one is what happens today. The bottom one passes through south-east on the way, and it holds before it goes, the way the street does when he walks.
