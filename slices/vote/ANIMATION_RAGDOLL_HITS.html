PLACEHOLDER: THE RAGDOLL'S HITS

The same ragdoll now falls three more ways on the same bones: shot, he snaps and drops where he stood; clubbed, he is thrown sideways and travels; off a roof, no blow at all, he falls and lands. You see all three beside the blow already in VOTE, every direction, playing on the beat at the game's own size; nothing is in the fight until you say final.
