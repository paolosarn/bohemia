THE FIGHT IN THE PIT

FIGHT takes your three in. The pit fight is the fight we have, on a small piece of the Strip: no retreat button anywhere, not even on the phone, and nothing in the pit leaves. A beast or a man who breaks runs for the wall and gets cornered there, and the crowd boos him. When it's over nothing comes off the dead: the purse is paid at the rope.

THE CROWD

Real voices and hands from the library, through the Mob's PA, which cuts in and out. In that fight it gasped at five misses, cheered three big hits, booed the bull twice for running and gave the long cheer at the end.
