HONEST. The faces are the dim head the settlement screen shows when no face has been handed over, because PORTRAIT has not drawn these four yet. By the game's own rule they do not speak until a face is in the slot: the lines below are ready and unspoken. The day a face lands, the same scene speaks with no more work. The three states are the bar from the town card: wronged (20), neutral (50), kept their roads (80). The lines and wants are my first tries, Spanglish for the crews and the keeper. None of this is in the live game yet: NOT IN A TAB YET.
