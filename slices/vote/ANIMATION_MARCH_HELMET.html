THE HELMET ON THE MARCH

YOUR hard hat took a hit. As the crew walks, you lift it off, hold it at your chest and knock the dent out with the butt of your knife, three times, then put it back on. It is the hat you are wearing. Your legs never stop walking.

YOUR CREW ON THE MAPLOADING

In the game it happens by itself: a hurt or banged-up crew member works on it now and then, one or two at a time, never the whole crew. Walking away from you, nobody does it (you would only see a back).
