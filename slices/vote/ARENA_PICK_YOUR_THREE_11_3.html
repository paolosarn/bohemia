PICK YOUR THREE

Tap the pit, read tonight's card, then one screen of big faces: your best three are already picked, so it's one tap to fight. Tap another face to swap him in. Every man's accessory is crossed out in red: there's no collar to put on, his accessory just doesn't count in the pit. FIGHT takes only those three in, against exactly what the card said.

Measured in the real demo: FIGHT hands the fight only the picked men in your order, none of them wearing his accessory, against every foe on the card, on a small board (9 by 5 instead of 20 by 15). Next: no retreat, no loot, and the crowd.
