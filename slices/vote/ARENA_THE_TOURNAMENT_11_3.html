THE STRIP'S TOURNAMENT

Every few weeks the Mob runs a tournament for five days, and its account says so on your phone's feed. Tap the pit that week and the bookie offers it under tonight's card: five of yours, three rounds, each one tougher, no swapping men between rounds. Every round you win pays right away. Quit after any round and keep what you won. Win all three: 300 batteries and the pit-fighter helm.
