PICK YOUR MAN OUT

You made the overworld Battle Brothers. Their company screen is a grid of little faces and you find your guy in it without thinking. So I asked whether you can find yours.

First, a problem I found while asking. The game's own checker said these two are the same person. It has been saying so for four rounds and I kept repeating it.

THE GAME CALLED THESE ONE PERSON
  • the haircut
  • eye colour
  • shades
  • what they are wearing

That is 4 different things, and one of them is wearing sunglasses. They were never the same person. The checker was adding up brightness, which is not how anybody tells two faces apart.

So here is the real question, with the checker fixed. Nine of your people, at the size they actually appear on your phone. Can you tell them apart?

Measured across all 1,770 pairs of sixty people: not one pair shares every single thing you could name about them. The closest any two get is two things apart, and only 25 pairs are even that close.

These nine are picked to match the real crowd, not to flatter it: 3 of the 9 are in shades, because 29% of everybody is. That is worth your eye on its own. The eyes are the most identifying thing on a face this small, and nearly a third of the valley has them covered.
