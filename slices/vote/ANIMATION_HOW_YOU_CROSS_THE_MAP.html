HOW YOU CROSS THE MAP

Your call: the valley is crossed the Battle Brothers way, a marker you send somewhere while the clock runs.

First, what the map does now. I pressed the walk pad twenty-four times in all eight directions while zoomed out. He moved zero. On the street the same press moves him twenty-two lots and shifts the whole screen. There is no marker and the pad is dead out there.

So how should the marker travel? Battle Brothers slides it along smoothly in real time and you can pause. But your own rule says everything in this game moves on the beat, twice a second, street and city both, one game mode. Those two cannot both be true, so this is the fork.

I picked B and built it, because it is your own two rules agreeing: one beat is one lot, and it is the exact feel you already voted for on foot. Tell me A and I will switch it.

The marker itself is not mine and is not redrawn: it is the one the art chat already made, banner and all. What I built is the travelling. The banner hangs dead when he stops, which is their rule, and it holds on both.
