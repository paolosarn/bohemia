THE CAMP

When you make camp, you see your whole crew, each doing what he needs. The badly hurt one lies bandaged with a friend kneeling by him, a hurt one binds his cut by the fire, one hammers his car door straight, one cooks, one cleans his rifle, one sleeps. When the hurt one heals, he gets up.
