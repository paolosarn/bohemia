A BLOCK CHANGED HANDS

The first one, ever, in this game. The block right next to your front door. 9/24
UNTIL THIS ROUND, NO BLOCK IN THIS VALLEY COULD EVER CHANGE OWNER. Who holds what was worked out fresh from the map every time the game started, and nothing you did was anywhere in it. Take a crew's ground, burn their town, pay them or don't: the game came back the next morning with the same fourteen owners, forever.

Now it can change, and it is remembered, and it is remembered per life so your third generation can be handed what your first one took.

Both are photographs of your real game. In between them, the block one step west of your door stopped belonging to the Mob and started belonging to the Volunteers. Look for it. (There is one difference you can see and it is not that one: a walking hint was up in the left picture. Nothing about who owns the ground moved a single pixel.)
YOU CANNOT FIND IT, AND THAT IS THE HONEST HALF OF THIS. The ground changed owner and the street did not say a word.

I counted why. While you stand on the street the game asks two or three times a second who owns ground, and those are all asking about the one square you are standing on. And out in the city view it asks ZERO times. The screen where you would look at a map of who owns what never once asks who owns anything.
What the record kept, off the real game. The tier stays the ground's, not the taker's: taking a fortress block does not turn it into your fortress, because what a place charges is a fact about the place.
WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT. It is the memory, and the memory is the part that had to exist first, because the three lives only work if the future can be worked out from what the past did. It decides nothing about how you take ground or what it costs. Those are yours, and nothing in the game takes ground yet. Nothing here touches the demo.
