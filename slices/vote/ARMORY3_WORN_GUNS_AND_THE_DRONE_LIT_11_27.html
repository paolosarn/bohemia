YOUR DRONE IS LIT BEFORE YOU SEND IT

Pick a man with the drone kit: the five tiles it will hit light up, the button says SEND and how many of them are on it. No fake hit chance: a drone always flies.

BEAT-UP GUNS

Six guns now come worn too: about half the price, ten points harder to hit with. Same gun, same damage. In 24 shops of each size: camps sold the homemade and beat-up guns, towns the clean rifles and the drones, forts the rockets and the belt-fed.

THE DOUBLE SHOTGUN

Grandpa's side-by-side: two blasts, then a whole turn to break it open and load. 40 batteries, in the towns.

IN HIS HANDS, TODAY

Some new guns now look like their own kind in his hands: the switched pistol is a service pistol, the pipe guns are the homemade ones, the shotguns are a pump or a double, the night and heat sights sit on a scoped rifle. The AR, the Kalash, the belt-fed, the rockets and the drone kits still look like their family's gun until they get their own models.
