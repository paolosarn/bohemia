THE CREW SCREEN SEES THE FIGHT

Right after a fight, the crew screen now shows the fight itself, not the camp: the same street, the dead where they fell, and your men going through them, kneeling in their coats, checking on the one who went down. The man you picked stands in the middle of his sheet. Tap another man, in the line or on the ground, and the camera glides to him.

On your phone: win a fight, then tap CHOOSE HIS LEVEL-UP on the end screen. Leave the end screen and the crew screen is the camp again.
