EVERY SHOT IS FELT

Any time anyone fires a gun, yours or theirs, the barrel flashes and the whole screen goes white for a blink. A pistol is a light hit, a rifle is harder, a shotgun whites the street out. It can never strobe: shots fired together are one flash, and never more than three in a second. Watch the fight below play itself.

 

On your phone: any fight where somebody has a gun. SETTINGS has a FLASH switch (FULL or SOFT) for eyes that cannot take flashing; MOTION: LESS makes it soft too.
