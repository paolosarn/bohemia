THE GUNS GROW UP, AND THE DRONE

Their pilot sends a drone. It flies in this turn and hangs over the lit tiles, and hits on his next turn. Step off, or shoot it down (the square shows your chance).

Sixteen new guns and kits, all real ones a broken America would have. These are pictures of the real fight on a phone. A sword can't reach a drone.

Nobody moved, so it landed. A man in light armour goes down every time. Heavy armour lives about two times in three.

Your own pilot can send one too.

AUTOMATIC: THREE ROUNDS A PULL

One press, three rounds at one man. Each round rolls on its own and the later ones are harder to land. It eats your rounds three times as fast.

THE BELT-FED: THREE HOUSES AT ONCE

It sweeps the man you pick and the house on each side of him. The tiles light up before you fire.

THE ROCKET: A HOUSE AND THE EIGHT AROUND IT

Lit before you fire, then the blast. Two rockets, and a whole turn to reload.

ALL SIXTEEN

Each says one hand or two. You find them in the shops and in enemy hands.
