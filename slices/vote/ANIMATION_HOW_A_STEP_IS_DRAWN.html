HOW A STEP IS DRAWN

One press now moves him a whole house, 275 pixels on your screen, in half a second. His legs can only show one pace of that. So the question is not the legs. It is what the ground under him does in between.

What ships right now is the top one, and nobody chose it: a number written when a step was small was still sitting in the code, so the whole world jumps at once. The middle one is what the code thought it was doing.

All three are on the real beat, 120 a minute, and all three cover the same distance. The only difference is when the ground moves.
