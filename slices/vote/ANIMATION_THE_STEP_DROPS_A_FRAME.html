THE STEP DROPS A FRAME

One press moves him a whole house. His legs only have four drawings and they cannot cover that much ground, so I stopped pretending they could.

Top: his legs walk smoothly while the street jumps. That is the moonwalk. Bottom: he holds still, the picture drops two frames, and he is there. Same four drawings, same beat, same distance.

A short press does not skip. If something is in the way and he only moves a few feet, he just steps there on the beat, because a dropped frame is a way of showing distance and there is no distance to show. Measured while he walked: eleven presses out of fourteen had ground to cross, three did not.
