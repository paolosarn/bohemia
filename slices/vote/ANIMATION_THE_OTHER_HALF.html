THE OTHER HALF OF THE SAME PROBLEM

Still working off your one line: "some of the DIRECTIONS look like dog shit."

Last round I found the ones that were dead facing you. Then I asked it the other way round and found the opposite, which is worse. Nodding was invisible from the side. That is the one direction a nod should be easiest to see, because you are looking straight at his chin.

The reason is stupid and I will own it. His head was tilted forward a bit all the time, and then it swung eight degrees on top of that. His head is twenty-two pixels tall. Eight degrees is about a pixel and a half. It was a nod you could only find with a ruler. Now the tilt is gone, the swing is three times bigger, and his neck and shoulders go with it.

The other two are the same bug as last round, twice more. A thrust goes forward, and forward is straight into the screen. Turn him to face you and a spear drive and a knife jab both did nothing: 12% each. Now he sinks into the lunge, the back leg folds, the arm punches out low. 55% and 45%, and every side view untouched down to a tenth of a point.

Left is what you killed. Right is now, on the real clock.

That is twelve of the forty-seven you killed, and five of those twelve only got their movement fixed, not the hand thing you killed three times. The order is not my taste any more, it is the measurement: whichever one has the worst direction goes next.
