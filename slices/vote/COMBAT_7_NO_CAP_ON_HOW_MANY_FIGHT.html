NO CAP ON HOW MANY FIGHT

"Make sure there's no coded cap."

There was one. The fight lined his men up in two rows of nine, and the 19th man and up had nowhere to stand. With 30 of his men, 6 were left off, and the screen went black. Now every man stands: a third row and more behind, and the board grows taller when a side runs out of room. Their side has no cap either.
