PLACEHOLDER: THE DIRE WOLF REDONE

The grey wolf you called the ugliest was one flat colour on a fox's body, so this one was rebuilt in 3D: longer legs, a deep chest, short ears, a longer face, a rope tail with a black tip, and a tawny coat with a dark saddle and a cream belly. You see it here in all eight directions, playing its idle, step and bite on the beat at the game's own pixels, beside the fox you liked and the grey one, as a placeholder until your moodboard.
