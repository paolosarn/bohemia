FACING YOU, HE WAS DOING NOTHING

These are the last two clips from that old batch you never gave a thumb to. Before I put them in front of you I looked at them, and they were broken in a way nobody had ever measured.

A man who bends over disappears when he faces you. Bending forward is a sideways move, so it only shows from the side. Facing the camera there is nothing to show, and the code had no other plan. Picking something up changed 126% of his pixels from the side and 36% facing you. His whole body shifted ten pixels from the side and less than one facing you. Laughing was worse: facing you it moved him one pixel. He was standing still.

Facing you, a squat has to be spent where it does show: he drops, his legs fold, his arms go down and his head goes with them. A laugh becomes a bounce and a shake in the shoulders. Picking up now moves him 5.5 pixels facing you instead of 0.9. Laughing moves him 4.2 instead of 1.0. From the side both are untouched.

Left is what they were. Right is now. Both facing you, on the real clock.

This is not just these two. Every clip written as a forward bend has the same hole on the two directions you see most, and the fix pattern was already in the file on three other clips. These two are the ones on my list; I will name the rest with numbers rather than quietly change forty clips.
