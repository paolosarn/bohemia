HEAVY ARMOUR COSTS A TILE, NEVER A SWING

"You can only walk one time less... make sure that this does not make it impossible to do any sort of attack or abilities."

The heaviest armour (a coat of plates and a full helm) takes one house off his walk: 3 instead of 4. It never takes anything off his attacks or abilities. Standing still he has all 9 points for anything he owns, and he can walk one house and still strike. The lit ground is where he can walk.
