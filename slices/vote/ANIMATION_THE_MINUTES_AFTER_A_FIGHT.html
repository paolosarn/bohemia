THE MINUTES AFTER A FIGHT

When a fight ends, your men don't just stand there. This plays behind the loot screen instead of black: one goes through a dead man's pockets and finds a battery, one kneels by a hurt friend, one ties a rag round his cut arm, one is bent over catching his breath, and two smoke and talk about what's next.

Why these: soldiers' memoirs and the army's own manuals say the same thing about the minutes after. Men check each other, patch wounds, go through the dead, reload, and the shakes hit once it's quiet. Held keys on the beat, the same stop-motion as everything else.
