WHAT THE BUTTONS ARE MADE OF

Five ways the same five buttons could look. Same words, same places, same sizes — only what they are made of changes. This is the thing that has to be swappable, because you said the interface changes across the three acts.

Nothing here is a plan. Each one is drawn, so you can point at one.

The buttons and the ground behind them are identical in all five; only the surface changes. That is the point — if the look is one named set of shapes and colours, act one, act two and act three are three sets, not three rewrites.
Act one you called 2050 rustic: our own tech worn down and hand-repaired, not a 1950s idea of the future. That is 1, 2 and 4 pulling at it from three directions. 3 is what happens if the box goes away entirely. 5 is act two, the world working.
