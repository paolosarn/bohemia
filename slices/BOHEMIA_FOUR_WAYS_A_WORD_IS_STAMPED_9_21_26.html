FOUR WAYS A WORD IS STAMPED

The letters on every button in the game. The big line is blown up so you can see the shapes; the row of buttons under it is the real size. 9/21
THE LETTER N IN OUR BUTTON FACE IS AN H. Not nearly, not sometimes. I asked the machine to draw every capital letter at the size the buttons use and compare each one to all the others, and H and N came back as the closest pair in the whole alphabet. Only a tenth of the ink is different. W against H and A against H are right behind it.

AND I KNOW EXACTLY WHY, BECAUSE THE SAME LETTERS ARE CUT THREE WAYS IN THIS GAME. The screen face and the writing face are drawn from the same dots and neither of them has this problem. The button face squeezes its letters so the ink of one column laps over into the next. That is what makes it look stamped and heavy, and it is also what swallows the little diagonal in the middle of an N.

SO IT IS A TRADE AND IT IS YOURS. A is what is in the game now and it is the best looking of the four. D reads perfectly and it is the plainest. I picked C and I am building C unless you say otherwise: it keeps almost all the weight and the N comes back. Look at the word RUN in each one.

How this was found. Every capital letter drawn at 5 pixels, the size the buttons actually use, and compared to every other capital letter, for all three of the game's faces. The screen face and the writing face came back clean, which is what proved it was the squeeze and not the letters. Nothing in the game has been changed: all four of these are sitting side by side waiting on your letter.
