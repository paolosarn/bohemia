FOUR RULES

1 Never rename an id. A new name is a new row.
2 Retire, do not delete. The row keeps its id and says retired.
3 A mod names its own rows modname:thing. No game id has a colon, so they can never collide.
4 Ids stay lowercase letters, digits and underscore. All 725 already do.
