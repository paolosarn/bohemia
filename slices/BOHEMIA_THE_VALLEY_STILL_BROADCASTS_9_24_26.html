THE VALLEY STILL BROADCASTS

SOUNDS · 9/24 · 120 BPM · three options, one tap each
TURN YOUR VOLUME UP AND USE HEADPHONES IF YOU HAVE THEM. Half of this sound is hiss and a hum, and a phone speaker on half volume will not play it.
THERE IS NOTHING LIKE THIS IN THE GAME YET, AND IT IS THE ONE SOUND THE WHOLE LOOK IS BUILT ON. A dead authority left a transmitter running. Every so often it plays the two-tone signal that comes before an announcement, and then the announcement never comes. It does not know you are there and it never will.

The two tones are the real ones, 853 and 960 together, and the real rule says that signal runs at least eight seconds. Eight seconds at our tempo is exactly four bars, so nothing had to be bent. Then one bar of dead air, and it starts again.

A. AS IT LEFT THE STATION

What a transmitter that somebody still looks after sounds like. It has the band a real one has and a carrier under the quiet, and nothing else is wrong with it. This is here so you can hear what the wear is actually doing.

B. AS IT ARRIVES, TEN YEARS LATER

The same signal off a machine nobody has touched. More hiss, the head is not holding speed so the tone sags and recovers, and twice the signal drops out for 34 milliseconds and comes back dull. None of it lands on the beat, on purpose: a machine breaking is not playing along with you.

C. AND IT NEVER STOPS

B, on repeat, the way you would actually meet it. This is the real question: the repeating is supposed to be the scary part, and repeating is also the fastest way to get sick of something. Let it run a while before you decide.
