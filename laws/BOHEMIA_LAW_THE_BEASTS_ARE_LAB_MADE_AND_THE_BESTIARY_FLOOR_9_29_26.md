# BOHEMIA LAW -- THE BEASTS ARE LAB-MADE, AND BATTLE BROTHERS' BESTIARY IS THE FLOOR (Paolo 9/29/26, LOCKED)
# His words: records/BOHEMIA_PAOLO_THE_BEASTS_ARE_LAB_MADE_AND_THE_BB_BESTIARY_IS_THE_FLOOR_9_29_26.md. Rule 42 on VAMILY.md.

## 1. THE BEASTS ARE DE-EXTINCT ANIMALS, NOT RADIATION
Every beast in Bohemia is an animal humans brought back with genetics (the cool technology of the crash, 7/24
LOCKED #2) that broke out of its lab when the dollar died. Nothing mutated, nothing glowing. 37n's 'maybe' is canon.

## 2. THE SCIENCE LIMIT IS A LAW
Only animals whose DNA can really be read come back: Ice Age and after (about the last million years), from
permafrost, dry caves, museum skins and frozen tissue; a living relative of the right size must exist to carry it.
No dinosaurs, no giant reptiles of deep time. A beast that breaks this rule is a defect; a character who claims one
exists is lying, which is a line. The research and the table: records/BOHEMIA_THE_BEASTS_OF_BOHEMIA_DE_EXTINCTION_ROUND_ZERO_9_29_26.md.

## 3. THE VALLEY'S OWN DEAD (AMENDED THE SAME HOUR by Paolo: 'it didn't have to break out of a lab in Vegas, it could have been a state or two over'; records/BOHEMIA_PAOLO_THE_LAB_COULD_BE_A_STATE_OR_TWO_OVER_9_29_26.md). The Vegas lab is one source of many; labs a state or two over let their animals out the same week, and nature spread them along the real corridors (the I-15, the Colorado River to Lake Mead, the US-95). Nature's clock is the acts' clock: act 1 the local escapees and first arrivals, act 2 established populations, act 3 what the player's line let breed or hunted out, derived from the ledgers. WORLD's data file says which animal arrives when and from where; a beast's arrival is a road event and a rumour first.
The lab was north of the city at the fossil beds (Tule Springs, real) and the cave (Gypsum Cave, real); what broke
out is what once lived on this ground: dire wolf, camel, horse, lion, sabre-tooth, bear, sloth, bison, mammoth.
Vegas's exotic escapees (show cats, pet-trade reptiles, feral dogs and cattle) walk the same roads.

## 4. THE BESTIARY FLOOR
Battle Brothers' count is the bare minimum: 12 combat factions, about 75 enemy types, 17 beasts, 3 bosses, and
named champions (records/BOHEMIA_BATTLE_BROTHERS_SCHOOL_THE_BESTIARY_COUNT_9_29_26.md). Every Battle Brothers archetype has a Bohemia answer that keeps its LESSON (the
necromancer is a man reviving his robots: kill him first). COMBAT [bestiary] owns the count and the lessons; WORLD
[creatures] owns which animal lives where; PEOPLE and FACTIONS own the human rosters; COOK draws them to
DIRECTION's card; nothing ships under the floor (gate OWED: the roster data file counts factions, types and beasts
against the floor).

## 5. ACT ONE FEELS MEDIEVAL
"When civilization dies, especially our first act will kinda feel like that." No power, no law, men with what
they can carry, beasts on the road; the technology is the thing on the horizon, not in your hand. DIRECTION's
bible and WORLD's act-1 read this.

## 6. MECHANIC FIRST, SKIN SECOND (Paolo 9/29, the same night, LOCKED; records/BOHEMIA_PAOLO_THE_BEAST_ARCHETYPES_EXACTLY_AS_BATTLE_BROTHERS_9_29_26.md)
