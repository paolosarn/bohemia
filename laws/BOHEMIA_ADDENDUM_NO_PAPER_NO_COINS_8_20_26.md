# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM — THERE IS NO PAPER AND THERE ARE NO COINS (8/20/26, LOCKED)

> "THERE IS NO PAPER NO COINS COINS GET MELTED DOWN TO RESOURCE PARTS WHAT
> DONT U UNDERSTAND"

## THE RULING

Bohemia has no cash. No notes, no coins, no till, no counting money off. A coin
in this valley is not money, it is **METAL**, and metal gets melted down into
RESOURCE PARTS. When something changes hands in this game, what moves is
resources, medicine or electricity — the three-currency system that has been
canon since the tier-ascent law on 7/8 — and physically that means **parts with
weight**, not paper with a face value.

This is not a note about a sound. It is what the economy IS.

## WHY IT MATTERS MORE THAN THE SOUND IT KILLED

The sound engine has carried a MONEY MOVES moment since 7/29 and it has been
offered to him THREE TIMES under three ids, fifteen candidates, and every single
one died:

    money        "cash changes hands. in a post-economic valley this is
                  never neutral"                            0 UP / 5 DOWN
    cash_count   "paper and coin counted off. many small events, never one
                  tone"                                      0 UP / 5 DOWN
    hands_pass   "paper and coin counted off. in a post-economic valley
                  money moving is never nothing"             0 UP / 5 DOWN

Fifteen for fifteen. And the reason was never the synthesis, the method or the
voices — three different sources were tried. **The BRIEF was wrong.** Every one
of those candidates was cooked to be paper and coin in a world that has neither,
so the better the sound was at being what it was asked to be, the more wrong it
was. A perfect coin sound was always going to die.

THE LESSON, and it is bigger than this moment: a moment that dies with EVERY
candidate across MULTIPLE SOURCES is not a cooking failure. It is a BRIEF that
does not describe his world. Re-cooking it is the wrong move and I made that
move twice. The right move is to stop and ask what the thing actually is.

## WHAT THE MOMENT ACTUALLY IS

Something changing hands in Bohemia is **parts moving**: metal with mass, more
than one piece, dropped or handed or counted by weight rather than by number.
It is closer to a bag of scrap being set down than to a till. It has no ring, no
chime and no paper.

`money`, `cash_count` and `hands_pass` are DEAD, graveyard-final, and they are
dead for their BRIEF, not for their sound. Any replacement must be written from
resources and parts, and must never contain the words cash, coin, paper, note,
till or change.

## WHAT THIS TOUCHES BEYOND SOUND

Any lane writing a transaction — quest rewards, trade, the purse, a shop, a
bribe, a debt — writes it in resources, medicine or electricity. Nobody hands
anybody a bill. If a UI or a line of dialogue anywhere says cash or coins, it is
wrong and it is this addendum that says so.

## GATE

`gates/no_cash_gate.py` sweeps the sound engine's briefs and the run's
transaction text for paper/coin/cash language. A moment that describes money as
paper cannot ship again.
