THE RULING (extends NOTES-ARE-RULINGS 7/19 to its negative half):
1. BULK SILENCE IS A VERDICT. If a batch was presented and Paolo moved past it

   REJECT, even with zero individual thumbs recorded. Individual thumbs-down
   are not required for a batch to be dead.
2. STALE UNJUDGED = PRESUMED DISMISSED. A candidate bank that has sat
   unjudged after being surfaced is presumed passed-on. NEVER re-surface it
   for thumbs, never carry it as "pending Paolo," never count it as backlog.
   The move is confirm-then-graveyard: list it once as "presumed dead unless
   you say otherwise," then graveyard with a post-mortem. No re-cooks.
3. FRESH IS DIFFERENT. New, never-seen candidates still get ONE clean
   presentation (the verdict workflow is unchanged for fresh cooks — e.g. the
   7/25 marathon waves he has not seen yet are FRESH, not stale).
4. TASTE SIGNAL. The taste canon/filter treats stale-unjudged banks as a
   NEGATIVE signal (weak NO), not neutral. "Paolo saw this class of thing and
   didn't react" is information.
5. WORKFLOW CONSEQUENCE. Judges and roundup tools stop re-presenting old
   unjudged corpus for judgment. The asset-roundup pattern flips from "surface
   the unjudged for thumbs" to "sweep the stale-unjudged toward the graveyard
   with a one-shot confirm list."

This kills the false "pending Paolo" backlog: the shelf holds only rulings he
has never been shown and fresh batches awaiting their FIRST look.
