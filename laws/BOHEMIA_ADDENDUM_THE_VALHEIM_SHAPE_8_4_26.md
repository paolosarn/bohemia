## 1. THE WORLD STAYS BIG (LOCKED — nobody ever proposes shrinking it)
The locked valley (GDD v5): 96x96 cells x 128 tiles = 12,288^2 fine tiles,
~9.2km x 9.2km = ~85 km^2 (~33 sq mi) at 0.75m per tile. HONEST NUMBERS ON
RECORD so the fleet never bullshits him: that is ~2.3x SKYRIM'S ACREAGE
(Skyrim ~37 km^2) — and the "10x Skyrim" FEEL is carried by DENSITY, which
is where this map actually dwarfs everything: every cell is a Pelican Town's
worth of designed ground (16,384 tiles, the proven Stardew density unit),
9,216 cells of it. Skyrim's ground is mostly wilderness between ~9 cities;
ours is designed urban fabric edge to edge. Same class as Project Zomboid's
Knox Country: real-scale streets you could never exhaust, and PZ is the
proof players LOVE a map bigger than any run needs.

## 2. THE VALHEIM DISCIPLINE (LOCKED — the law that makes 33 sq mi legal)
You are NOT meant to explore every nook and cranny. Valheim's shape: the
world is far bigger than any playthrough, ~20% seen in a normal run, and
that is a FEATURE (the ocean of unexplored world is what makes it feel
real, and what makes the next seed a new game). Bohemia adopts it:
- EVERYTHING still meets the look bar — an unexplored district is never an
  excuse for placeholder art. (His words: "everything look very good.")
- But CONTENT DEPTH concentrates where the acts point players; the rest is
  the believable ocean of dead city, procedurally dressed to the bar.
- REPLAYABILITY comes from the seed + quest variety, not from forcing one
  run to eat the whole map: different seed, different valley, different
  quest options and facts = the comeback engine.

## 3. THE ACT REPAIR CAPS (LOCKED as directional targets — the first hard
## numbers of the city-builder loop)
- ACT 1: ~15% of the city repairable, WITHOUT a deathly grind.
- ACT 2: ~33-40% restorable, WITHOUT being boring as fuck.
- ACT 3: 80-100% fully repairable IF THE PLAYER WANTS — completionism is
  allowed to get a little boring at the tail, and that is accepted, not a
  bug to fix.
The anti-grind clauses are part of the ruling, same rank as the numbers:
the caps describe what a NON-grinding player can comfortably do, so the
economy/pacing tuning derives from them. This is the first concrete sizing
of the Pocket-City-2-feel build loop (7/31 ruling) and the city-builder
design conversation continues from these numbers.

## 4b. BUILDING IS OPTIONAL + THE UNITED FRONT (his answer to "who rebuilds
## in Act 3", same sitting, LOCKED)
His words (voice-to-text, quoted with garbles marked): "this is you could if
you wanted to. if you don't want to build a single thing in the game that's
OK too. It's just most likely the story in the progress of the world might
will definitely look different... maybe it's a combination of a lot of
things. Ultimately I see if you're able to rebuild 80% of the city...
this is supposed to be like the pseudo mayor vibe so you definitely have
your [GARBLED: "death in"] ... need [factions] to get along with you to
help you. absolutely [GARBLED: "affection" — likely "a faction"] can only
go so far. has to be like a united front."
TWO RULINGS:
(a) BUILDING IS FULLY OPTIONAL. A player can finish the game having built
NOTHING — legal, supported, never punished by a wall. The cost is
narrative, not mechanical: the story and the world's progress "will
definitely look different." The act repair caps (§3) are CEILINGS for
players who want to build, never floors anyone must meet. This is
comeback-canon design: the build-nothing run and the rebuild-everything
run are different games on the same seed.
(b) THE PSEUDO-MAYOR AND THE UNITED FRONT. Large-scale rebuilding is a
COMBINATION of many things, and the vibe is PSEUDO-MAYOR: at 80% you are
not laying every brick, you are the one the valley rebuilds AROUND. It
requires FACTIONS THAT GET ALONG WITH YOU — and one faction can only go so
far: the 80-100% tier takes a UNITED FRONT across factions. So the endgame
city-builder currency is not concrete, it is ALLIANCE — the faction
standing system and the build system are ONE system at scale, and the
faction dossiers + standing ledger just became city-builder
infrastructure.
GARBLES ON RECORD, NOT CANONIZED: "you definitely have your death in"
stays unresolved verbatim until he re-states it.
