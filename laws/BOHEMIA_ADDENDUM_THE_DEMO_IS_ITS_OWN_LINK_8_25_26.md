## THE LAW
**THERE ARE TWO SURFACES AND THEY ARE NOT THE SAME THING.**
1. **THE WORKSHOP.** `slices/BOHEMIA_ALPHA_0_9.html`. Seventeen tabs.
   RUN, CHARACTER, CLOTHES, ANIMATION, RIG, COMBAT, MUSIC, CITY, MAP,
   SLICE, LIFE, ART, LOOK, WORDS, DIRECT, CUTSCENE, VOTE. It is HIS bench.
   The ONE-LINK LAW (7/18) governs THIS and nothing else: one URL, never a
   cache-buster, always fresh. Unchanged, untouched, still law.
2. **THE DEMO.** A SEPARATE BUILD AT A SEPARATE URL. No dev tabs. No
   judge pages. No vote panel. No SLICE tab. A stranger opens it and the
   only thing there is the game.
**THE WORKSHOP LINK IS NEVER GIVEN TO A PLAYER.** Not to a friend, not to
a tester, not to anybody outside this project. Handing somebody the
workshop is handing them sixteen doors and asking them to guess.

## WHAT THIS CORRECTS, AND IT WAS THE COORDINATOR'S ERROR
Sweeps 14, 20 and 21 planned the whole friends round around "5-8 people,
their own phones, THE ONE LINK." That was wrong and it was wrong in the
worst possible way: it would have sent his friends into a developer
workbench and spent the one round that spends once.
The error underneath it: **"the demo plays" was being measured INSIDE the
workshop.** gates/the_whole_demo_gate.js drives the alpha and proves the
day runs end to end — which is true, and valuable, and is NOT the same
claim as "a demo exists." A build that only exists as a path through a
dev tool is not a build.
THE DEMO PLAN SAID THIS ON 8/4 AND WE READ PAST IT: item 9, "DEMO GATE:
one integration test that plays the whole day headless + deploy-verified
on the real link. **THE DEMO IS A BUILD, NOT A VIBE.**"
