# THUMB THUMB THUMB, EVERYTHING IS A THUMB

**Paolo, 8/9/26. LOCKED. This amends the VERDICT WORKFLOW for every lane.**

> "Thumb thumb thumb everything is a thumb"

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## WHAT HE IS SAYING

He is not complaining about one judge page. He is telling us what we turned him
into: **an approvals queue.** Nothing in this game moves until he taps a thumb,
so every lane converts its work into a ballot and hands it to him, and the
ballots arrive faster than any one person can vote.

The turn immediately before this one was the proof. He asked what was blocking
the demo and got **a numbered list of twelve things waiting on his thumbs**,
followed by a question asking him to choose how I should approach the next
build. Twelve votes and a process decision, in one reply, from ONE lane, on a
day he had already said five times he was tired.

He never asked to run an approvals process. He asked for a game.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## THE LAW

**THE DEFAULT FLIPS FROM APPROVE-BEFORE TO CORRECT-AFTER.**

Claude decides, builds the thing, and puts it in the game where he will meet it
while playing. He reacts to a thing that EXISTS. He does not authorise a thing
into existence.

    OLD:  cook -> surface as candidates -> he thumbs -> it becomes real
    NEW:  cook -> DECIDE -> it is in the game -> he corrects what he hates

A correction is cheap and he gives them freely and precisely: "the cornrows is
not one pixel of skin two pixels of hair", "that grenade throwing was dogshit",
"the roofs are all fucked up". Those are worth more than a thumb and they cost
him nothing, because he was *playing* when he said them.

### WHAT STILL GOES TO HIM, AND IT IS A SHORT LIST

1. **IDENTITY AND NAMES** — canon he has explicitly reserved. MECHANISM-MINE /
   CONTENTS-PAOLO'S is unchanged. Do not name his characters, his factions, or
   his currencies.
2. **A FORK WITH NO DEFENSIBLE DEFAULT** — genuinely two roads, both legitimate,
   where guessing wrong costs a week. Pick the one you can defend, SAY you
   picked it and why, and build it. Only stop if it is unsafe to be wrong.
3. **THINGS HE ASKED TO SEE.** If he says show me, show him.

That is the whole list. Everything else is ours to decide and live with.

### WHAT IS BANNED

- **A NUMBERED QUEUE OF PENDING VERDICTS IN A REPLY.** One thing at most, and
  only if it truly cannot be decided. Never a list of twelve.
- **ASKING HIM HOW TO DO THE WORK.** "Do I show you candidates or do you
  describe them first?" is our problem. Pick one.
- **BLOCKING ON A THUMB.** Nothing waits for approval. If it is good enough to
  ship, ship it. If it is not, it is not finished.
- **THE THUMB AS A SAFETY NET.** Surfacing something unfinished as a "candidate"
  so the risk of it being bad becomes his is cowardice wearing a process.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## WHY THIS IS NOT A LICENCE TO STOP CHECKING

The bar goes UP, not down. Under APPROVE-BEFORE a lane could ship something
mediocre and let the thumb sort it out. Under CORRECT-AFTER, **whatever you
decide is what he plays**, so the standard for "done" is now the standard for
"in the game".

That means MORE looking at the real surface, not less. The judging he was doing
for us is judging we now have to do ourselves before it lands.

**AND STOP PRODUCING STILL BINDS.** Deciding for him is not permission to make
four versions of something he never wanted. It is permission to make ONE version
and stand behind it.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## THE PART THAT IS OURS TO EAT

The existing verdict machinery is not the enemy and does not get deleted. The
judge pages, the export buttons, the thumbs already in the alpha stay exactly
where they are, because when he WANTS to sit and sweep a batch he is entitled to
a good tool. The 130-of-130 SFX sitting proves he will do it when he chooses to.

What changes is that **we stop making it the gate.** A judge page is a thing he
may open. It is never a thing the work waits behind.

And the backlog of already-surfaced verdicts does not get re-asked. Under STALE
UNJUDGED IS DEAD, silence already resolved most of it. Anything still sitting is
now OURS to decide, kill, or ship. Do not hand him that list again.

---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## THE TELL

If a reply ends with a list of things he must rule on, the turn failed, no
matter how much shipped. The correct ending is what was DECIDED and what is now
in the game.

**A turn he can answer with "cool" is a good turn. A turn that needs him to vote
twelve times is a turn that made him the bottleneck in his own game.**
