## 4. THE DIAL IS FOR THE IMPORTANT SHOT
"The Dead Eye Dial can be a great idea for important parts of the combat to make it more fun
and engaging." NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL is amended by that sentence:
- ROUTINE EXCHANGES RESOLVE ON THE BEAT WITHOUT THE DIAL. A hit inside reach on the beat lands
  and costs ONE (EVERYTHING COSTS ONE). No dial, no pause, no number to read.
- THE DIAL COMES UP FOR THE SHOT THAT MATTERS: the kill shot, the boss, the called shot, the
  one that turns the fight. There it decides what that shot does, on the beat, the way it
  already works.
- THE DIAL STILL OWNS EVERY NUMBER BIGGER THAN ONE. Nothing adds flat damage beside it; the
  routine hit is one, the dial is the multiplier moment. His to correct in VOTE.
- Which shots are "important" is a short list the fight can read (a kill shot, a boss, a
  called shot), never a mood; COMBAT ships the list, DIRECTION judges whether the fight got
  more fun, he corrects.
