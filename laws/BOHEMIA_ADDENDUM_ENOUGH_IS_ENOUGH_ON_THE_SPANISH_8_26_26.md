## WHAT IT SETTLES, AND IT IS TWO SEPARATE THINGS

### 1. THE FEATURE IS DONE. STOP SPENDING TURNS ON IT.
Three consecutive turns went into language: the registers, then the quirk
lines, then the reactions. Every one of them was defensible on its own and the
SUM was a third of a day of a fourteen-lane project spent on flavour, while the
demo he keeps naming in the same sentence sat still.

**HE SAID IT AT ROUND THREE. THE LAW THAT SHOULD HAVE CAUGHT IT AT ROUND TWO
ALREADY EXISTED**: STOP PRODUCING (7/26) -- *"a second rejection ends the
feature for the session"*, *"writing a fourth version of anything means you
already failed."* He was not rejecting the work, so no rejection counter tripped;
what he was rejecting was the SHARE OF THE PROJECT it was taking. That is a
failure mode the 7/26 law does not name, so it is named here:

**A FEATURE NOBODY ASKED TO CONTINUE IS FINISHED WHEN ITS FIRST TURN ENDS.**
Finding a real bug inside a shipped feature is not permission to spend the next
turn there. It is a row in the handoff for whoever picks it up.

### 2. THE NUMBER: PROPORTIONAL TO VEGAS, OR SLIGHTLY LESS.
This is a RULING ON A DIAL, and dials are his. It is also, exactly, a correction
of what shipped: the build was set to Clark County's real share.

| | Spanish-at-home share of the valley |
|---|---|
| Clark County, measured | **18.5%** (418,475 speakers 5+ of 2,265,461) |
| what shipped 8/25 | **18.5%** -- proportional, on the nose |
| **his ruling, 8/26** | proportional **or slightly less** |
| what it is now | **15.0%** (spanglish 11.8%, poor-english 3.2%) |

15.0% is 81% of the real number. That is the reading of "maybe slightly less"
this build takes, and it is written here so the next session corrects the LAW
rather than guessing at the code.

The three registers, the craft rules and the hard rule from
`BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_THEY_SPEAK_SPANGLISH_8_25_26.md` are UNCHANGED. He did not
reverse that ruling; he capped how much of the valley it covers and how much of
the project it eats.

## THE MACHINE HALF (a law without a machine gate is not enforced)
`gates/language_gate.js` gains two claims:
  (a) **THE VALLEY MIX IS AT OR BELOW THE REAL COUNTY SHARE.** Proportional is
      the ceiling now, not the target.
  (b) **THE REGISTER CORPUS DOES NOT GROW.** A hard count of register lines is
      pinned at what shipped today. Adding one turns the gate red and the
      failure names this law. To raise it, a session must have a ruling from him
      NEWER than 8/26 and must edit the ceiling with that quote beside it.

Claim (b) is the unusual one and it is the point: it is a gate against *doing
more work*, which is the thing that actually went wrong. A feature cap is the
only shape of check that can catch enthusiasm.
