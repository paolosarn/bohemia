## 1. WHAT HE RULED, AND WHAT HE DID NOT
TWO HALVES, AND THEY PULL OPPOSITE WAYS ON PURPOSE:
  THE AMOUNT    how OFTEN a person interacts with you should look like Battle Brothers,
                which is rarely, deliberately, and it matters when it happens.
  THE VARIETY   what they SAY should draw on everything this fleet has researched, so
                the same thing almost never comes back twice. Battle Brothers has
                nothing like our material and he knows it.

HE SAID "MAYBE" TWICE AND THAT IS PRESERVED HERE RATHER THAN ROUNDED OFF. The
DIRECTION is a ruling: fewer, bigger, never-repeating. The exact number is not set, and
"keep that in mind" is a standing consideration, not an order to go mute the town.

## 2. WHAT BATTLE BROTHERS' AMOUNT ACTUALLY IS
Battle Brothers has no street, so it has NO AMBIENT TALK AT ALL. Every interaction is a
discrete event you cannot miss: hiring at a tavern, an event that stops the party,
negotiating a contract. The amount is LOW and each one is a MOMENT. That is the shape he
is pointing at, and it means the number to compare is not "how many noises does our
street make", it is "how many times did somebody address HIM".

## 4. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE WORK, AS A STANDING RULE
(a) A POOL GETTING BIGGER IS NOT THE SAME AS HEARING MORE. Any lane that widens a line
    table MEASURES WHAT IS HEARD IN A PLAY, not what exists in the file. This law was
    written the same round a change made the pool five times bigger and the number of
    different things heard went DOWN.
(b) A STATE COLOURS A VOICE. AN EVENT REPLACES IT. A reaction to something somebody
    SAW or HEARD outranks the weather and should. A permanent fact -- you have met, you
    stand at this rung -- must never become the only thing a person can say, because a
    state is true for ever and a two-line bucket then owns that person for the rest of
    the game.
(c) THE STREET TAKES TURNS. Somebody who has just spoken stands down while anybody else
    is in earshot.
(d) THE AMOUNT IS ONE DIAL, NAMED, so one word from him moves it.
(e) VARIETY IS A CROWD PROBLEM BEFORE IT IS A WORDS PROBLEM. With one person in earshot
    at a time, no table is big enough. PEOPLE [honest crowd] and the enough-people
    measurement are upstream of every line anybody writes.

## 5. WHAT IS NOT RULED, AND STAYS HIS
The NUMBER of interactions per five minutes. He said maybe twice; nobody has picked it.
It is written as one dial with the measurement beside it and it waits for his word.
