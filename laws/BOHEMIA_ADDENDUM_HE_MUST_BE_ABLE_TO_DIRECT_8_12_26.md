# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM — HE MUST BE ABLE TO DIRECT IT, NOT JUST WATCH IT (8/12/26, Paolo, LOCKED)

> **"Bro this is the same fucking problem we had with the questing shit!
> I CANT DIRECT QUESTS OR CUTSCENES RN WTF IS WRONG WITH YOU."**

## THE RULING

**Every system he has to make decisions about ships with an INSTRUMENT for
making them, in a tab, the same turn the system ships.** A decision he cannot
express is a decision that blocks the build, and a lane that ships the system
without the instrument has shipped him a thing to watch.

## WHY THIS IS THE SECOND TIME

The questbook was 152 studied quests he could not touch, so the quests did not
get made. On 8/11 he named it — *"THATS WHY I HAVENT DONE QUESTS YET"* — and the
answer that turn was the WORDS tab: 946 lines he can retype.

**And that answered the wrong half.** WORDS lets him change what a person SAYS.
It does not let him change:

- who is in the scene at all
- what order anything happens in
- where it takes place
- whether a beat exists
- where a choice leads
- what a quest stage does

That is **DIRECTING**, and there was no surface for any of it. He watched two
cutscenes, had structural notes about both, and the only channel he had for them
was typing a paragraph at me and waiting. That is not a creative director, that
is a man filing tickets.

**AND IT MADE ME ASK HIM QUESTIONS.** The turn before this one ended with
*"Does the father live?"* — a structural decision I had no way to receive except
as an answer to a question. `EVERYTHING IS A THUMB` (8/9) banned the approvals
queue; this is the same disease with a different symptom. The queue came back
because the instrument was missing. **Build the instrument and the question
disappears: he opens DIRECT, adds the father, and plays it.**

## WHAT THE INSTRUMENT HAS TO DO

The test is not "can he give feedback". It is **can he change the thing himself,
see the change immediately, and keep it.**

For anything ordered (a scene, a quest, a conversation):

- **SEE** every beat/stage/line in order, in plain words
- **ADD** one, **DELETE** one, **MOVE** one
- **RETARGET** it: who speaks, who is present, where a choice leads
- **RELOCATE** it: which room, which building
- **PLAY IT IMMEDIATELY** — the thing he built, not a preview of a plan
- **KEEP IT** — survives a reload, and exports so a lane can land it as canon

## THE LINE THIS DOES NOT CROSS

The instrument does not make the decisions and it does not invent canon. It
hands him the controls. What he builds with it IS the ruling — that is
`NOTES ARE RULINGS` (7/19) with a steering wheel attached.

A lane still writes the words (`ALWAYS MAKE AN ATTEMPT`, 8/11) and still cites
the corpus (`DIALOGUE REFERS TO THE CATALOGUE`, 8/11). Directing is the third
thing, and it was missing.

## THE TEST FOR ANY FUTURE SYSTEM

Before shipping anything he has to rule on, answer in one sentence:
**"where does he change this himself?"** If the answer is "he tells me and I
edit a file", the system is not shipped yet.

## THE MACHINE

`gates/direct_gate.js` — the DIRECT tab exists, is reachable, loads every
authored scene and every canon quest, and every one of the verbs above is wired
to something real. It plays what he built rather than what shipped, and it
exports.

Gate it the same turn (A LAW WITHOUT A MACHINE GATE IS NOT ENFORCED).
