# "From visuals and anything that's audio-wise, from now on, end of story forever: I want us
# to lean more into an analog horror type of direction. I might have mentioned this before
# with Machine Party, and we talked about not putting a grim filter on things, but we need to

# to complement that. The whole enchilada. Analog horror for everything that is a pixel to

## 1. THE LAW
For the rest of the cycle, until the game is done, EVERY PIXEL AND EVERY SOUND IS THOUGHT
ABOUT AS ANALOG HORROR FIRST. Tiles, bodies, faces, clothes, the interface, the loading
screen, the fight, the phone, the music, the room tone, the voices, the silence. A piece of
art or a sound that was not thought about this way is not done, whatever else it passes.
This is the tone over every department's reference; it does not replace any of them.

## 2. WHAT ANALOG HORROR IS, FOR THE FLEET (plain words, the genre's own grammar)
Horror that looks and sounds like it came off old consumer recording gear and dead
institutions. The frame is ORDINARY and one thing in it is WRONG. The camera does not move
to help you. Stillness is long. The light is the light that was there (a fluorescent tube,
a screen, a sodium lamp, a sky too flat). The text on screen is from an institution that
should not still be broadcasting, in its own typeface, too calm. The sound is the room:
hum, hiss, tape drop-outs, a broadcast tone, a voice that is too even, and silence used as
an instrument. Nothing jumps. The fear is that the world is running normally and nobody is
left who should be running it. That last sentence is already Bohemia's premise (a Las
Vegas ten years into a dead grid), which is why this is a direction and not a costume.

## 3. WHAT IT IS NOT
- NOT A FILTER. The 8/3 debate ("not putting a grim filter on things") stands in its
  conclusion: the grime is a machine that runs at bake time (laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_NO_PAINT_8_3_26.md),
  and analog horror is not a VHS overlay dropped on a normal frame either. It is decided at
  the source: what is in the frame, how it is lit, how long it holds, what it sounds like.
  A scanline shader over a cheerful street is the violation, not the compliance.
- NOT A MONSTER. No creature design, no gore pass, no jump. A person a little too still is
  the face; a street with the lights still on and nobody home is the tile.
- NOT A NEW REFERENCE GAME. Analog horror is a GENRE. NEVER ADD A REFERENCE GAME HE HAS NOT
  NAMED stands in full: no named series, channel, film or game is studied or cited unless
  he names it. Machine Party (8/3) is the one named visual reference and the bridge.
- NOT AN EXCUSE TO DARKEN. The runway law stands (everyone dresses like a runway); the far
  view he approved stands; the colour-is-territory law stands. Analog horror is the wrong
  detail in a well-lit frame, not a brightness slider.
