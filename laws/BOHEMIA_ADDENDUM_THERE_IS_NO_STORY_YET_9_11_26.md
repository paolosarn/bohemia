# ADDENDUM: THERE IS NO STORY YET, SO STOP BLOCKING ON ONE (Paolo 9/11/26, LOCKED)

> "Instead of you getting stuck in fucking up the flow trying to work on some
> bullshit like this you know we don't have a story yet bro so I don't have the
> final answer for you. Your job is to make sure the rest of the game is
> playable and fun bro come on."

## WHAT CAUSED IT

The RUN lane measured the demo's first minute, found the ruled cold open has zero
callers, saw that `COLD_OPEN.cast` and `COLD_OPEN.place` are marked
`[PENDING, Paolo's call]`, and **put those two in front of him as the thing
blocking the row.** They are story: who is standing behind you, and what building
you are standing in front of. He does not have a story yet, so the question had no
answer and never would have.

## THE LAW

1. **A `[PENDING Paolo]` that is a STORY question is not a blocker, it is a hole
   in the story, and the story is not written yet.** Do not surface it as a
   decision. Do not park a row on it. Do not put it in WHAT I NEED FROM YOU.
2. **Work around story holes with mechanism.** If a scene needs a cast and there
   is no cast, the scene is not the row. Find the thing that is playable without
   it and do that instead.
3. **The standing job when a row stalls is PLAYABLE AND FUN**, in those words. Not
   "measure more", not "write a better record", not "ask him". A round that ends
   with a finding and no improvement to the thing he can play has to justify
   itself, and "it was blocked on the story" no longer justifies anything.
4. This binds EVERY lane, not just RUN. `[PENDING, Paolo's call]` markers that
   turn out to be story questions are to be treated as **deferred forever** until
   he starts writing a story, and nothing waits on them.

## WHAT IT DOES NOT CHANGE

Identity and names he reserved are still his, and a genuine creative fork about
the GAME in plain words is still legitimate. The difference is that a fork is a
choice between two things that both exist; a story hole is a blank page, and
handing him a blank page is not a question, it is the work not being done.

## THE ROW THIS KILLED

RUN `[drop in]` was about to sit on the cold open. It does not any more: the cold
open is set aside until there is a story to put in it, and the row is now only
about what can be made good with what already exists and runs.
