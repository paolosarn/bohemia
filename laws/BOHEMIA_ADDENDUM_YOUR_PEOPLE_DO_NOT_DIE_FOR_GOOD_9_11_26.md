# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM -- YOUR PEOPLE DO NOT DIE FOR GOOD (Paolo 9/11/26, LOCKED)
# "I know from Battle Brothers a huge part of their system is that people permanently
# die, and I don't think I want that. I do like the recruiting system, from different
# factions and cities, and how people in your company just get incorporated into quests
# autonomously, that's very cool. But I don't want anyone to permanently die, or even
# have permanent debuffs. There could be debuffs that are a lot longer than others. But
# there has to be a way in the game to make it easy for people to LOCK characters they
# don't want to die, companions, people in your company, and not endgame bullshit."

## 1. WHAT IS KEPT FROM THE REFERENCE (campaign layer only, per the 9/5 law)
- **Recruiting from factions and cities.** Who will join you depends on where you
  are, who holds that ground, and your standing there; a fortress offers different
  people than a camp. FACTIONS owns it.
- **Your company is written into the world's asks.** People in your company are
  not a roster; they show up in the asks the systems generate, with their own
  wants and debts, without anyone scripting it. That is the systems-do-the-asking
  rule (9/6) applied to the people who travel with you.

## 2. WHAT IS REFUSED FROM THE REFERENCE
- **NO PERMANENT DEATH FOR YOUR PEOPLE.** A companion or company member who goes
  down in a fight is DOWN, not dead: out for a long time, carried home, costing you.
  They come back.
- **NO PERMANENT DEBUFFS.** An injury can be long, and some are far longer than
  others (a leg that takes a season, a hand that takes a year of the valley's time),
  but every one heals. Nothing on a person you keep is forever.
- THIS IS HIS REALISM TRADE, AND HE MADE IT: realism first says people die; he chose
  fun and attachment over that, and the 8/4 law says that trade is his to make.
- **WHAT STILL DIES:** the story's deaths (the sibling in act one; whoever dies at
  the top of act two, parked), strangers and enemies, and everyone, eventually, of
  TIME across the generations (the fold). This law is about the people you keep,
  in the life you are playing.

## 3. THE LOCK
- **You can lock a person.** A locked companion or company member cannot be lost:
  when they would die, they go down instead, for the long timer. Locking is EASY and
  EARLY: available from the first hour, one tap on the person, no endgame gate, no
  rare item.
- **A lock costs something real, so it is a choice and not a default:** a locked
  person is carried when they fall, which slows the party; their long injuries still
  cost batteries to treat; and a locked person who would have died carries a scar in
  the standing web (the world saw them fall). The numbers are the coordinator's;
  the shape is his.
- Unlocked people still do not die for good (section 2). The lock is the promise on
  top: this one is never even close.

## ROUTING
- PEOPLE: [down not dead], [lock them].
- COMBAT: a downed body in the fight (out, carried, not a corpse).
- FACTIONS: [recruit anywhere].
- QUESTS: [company in asks].
- ECONOMY: what a long injury costs, as research.
