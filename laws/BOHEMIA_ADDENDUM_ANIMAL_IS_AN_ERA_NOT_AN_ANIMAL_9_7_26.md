## 1. THE RULING
Generation one is called the Animal because ACT ONE IS THE ANIMAL ERA: the anarchy
decade, surviving the ruin, instinct before law. Nobody plays a coyote. Nobody plays
a four-legged anything. The player is a person in a time when people lived like
animals. The same reading holds for the other two: HUMAN is the era when the world
claws back and rules return; ANGEL is the era above it, the cyberpunk healing and
the one-way trip. Eras, not creatures.

## 2. IT WAS ALREADY WRITTEN AND WE READ IT WRONG
The story master (laws/BOHEMIA_STORY_MASTER_7_18_26.md, THE THREE ACTS, 7/19) says
it in one line: "Generations Animal / Human / Angel; camera levels street / city /
planetary zoom", and then describes act one as THE ANARCHY DECADE, surviving the
ruin. The coordinator read "Gen 1 Animal" in the pillars as a literal animal, put
"the game opens as the coyote" on the board as his own call (9/5), and DYNASTY spent
two research rounds on playing as an animal and on a coyote in Las Vegas, and
CHARACTER got a job to prove a four-legged rig. All of that rests on a misreading of
a sentence that was on the shelf the whole time. Root cause: nobody re-read the
story master before building on its vocabulary. He said this round that the rough
draft of all the acts exists and he has not thought about it in some time; it does
exist, and it is the file above plus its act addenda.

## 3. WHAT THE ANIMAL ERA IS, IN TERMS A LANE CAN BUILD
- The verbs of act one are survival verbs: take, hide, run, fight, eat, keep your
  people alive. No courts, no contracts that hold, no prices that mean anything.
- The look of act one is the runway worn to the bone (already ruled), the 2050
  rustic interface (already ruled), and a valley that is running out (already
  built).
- The sound of act one is the beach vibe worn down (already ruled).
- The pillar's word survives as a feeling: instinct first. That is a direction for
  WORDS (how people talk when there is no law), COMBAT (the first era's fights are
  ambushes and scrambles, not duels), and DYNASTY (what a child raised in the
  animal era carries into the human one).
