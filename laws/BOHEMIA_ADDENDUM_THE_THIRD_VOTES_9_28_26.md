## 1. NO ATARI (37a)
Pixel detail is never reduced. "Tiny character" (rule 34) means SMALL RELATIVE TO BUILDINGS, drawn at the detail
of the zoomed-in roaming art we already made; a house is many cells because the cells are small, not because the
art is. The 32 px cell and the 28 px one-cell sprite are DEAD as defaults. BEFORE ANY GRID IS BUILT, he sees
OPTIONS: what a house tile and a street tile look like at real detail, made from assets we have (WORLD + COOK
[tile options], in VOTE). The map gets MORE pixels, not fewer: DIRECTION measures how dense the Battle Brothers
map is and the card follows. Eight-pixel figures and eight-pixel markers are refused by the cook gate.

## 2. PLACES ARE SETTLEMENT SCREENS (37b; amends 33b and 34)
A place on the map opens a SETTLEMENT SCREEN, Battle Brothers' way: you see the place drawn (alive, under the
bible: lights, people moving, weather), you tap a building to use it (the market, the barber, the shed, the
pump, the hall with the asks, SCAVENGE). You do not control a person "until the game thinks you should". Walking
on foot survives in TWO places only: THE FIGHT (the grid, RF4), and SPECIAL PLACES worth walking (the Strip,
named by DIRECTION and WORLD), where your company follows you. The eight-button pad is dead everywhere else. The
walked street becomes a set piece, not the game. RUN [two scales] is re-aimed: map <-> settlement screen <->
fight; the squeeze opens the map; a tap opens a place.

## 3. THE FUTURE GOES BOTH WAYS (37c; amends 32b)
"The future is a reflection of your past actions." Built rises (reclaimed, techier); raided FALLS (the blocks
you destroyed and the people you robbed are worse off three acts later). The derive is SIGNED. The start is
still the ruin; a do-nothing future is the ruin plus what the factions did; a raider's future is worse than the
start where he raided. DYNASTY [the derive], WORLD [future city].

## 4. THREE GENERATIONS, ALWAYS (37d)
"It will always be three generations." The acts are the generations (rule 31). A crisis, a boom, a bust happens
INSIDE an act. No lane re-frames the acts as anything else.

## 5. HOME BASES AND ROAMING PARTIES, NOT TERRITORY (37e; re-aims FACTIONS)
Territory colour on the ground is not a mechanic; nobody fights over lines. What the factions ARE on the map:
FOURTEEN HOME BASES (fourteen parts of Vegas, generated like Battle Brothers' settlements, each doing a different
thing, each powering and watering its own blocks) and MANY ROAMING PARTIES (far more than fourteen at a time;
DIRECTION/FACTIONS measure how many BB runs) you meet, fight, raid, trade with or watch pass. A home base can be
attacked: taken or ruined; the hard ones late in an act; a SIEGE is a research question. No building portraits;
no text on screen he did not ask for.

## 6. THE GRID IS SQUARE (37f)
Square cells. What is two tiles away is always obvious. Not hex, not diamond.

## 7. THE FIGHT (37g)
- DEATH: struck down = 20% dead, else out 30 to 40 days with a mark (rule 36b stands). AND: THE MAIN CHARACTER
  NEVER DIES; a veteran of about 20 fights dies at 10%; the struck-down keep their gear, damaged. TUNING.
- HIGH GROUND IS A ROOF: buildings get taller (two and three storeys); a roof still standing is the high ground;
  a collapsed roof is not. COMBAT with COOK.
- The movement pad's fate is decided by 37b: dead outside the fight and the special places; inside the fight,
  COMBAT decides how a tap moves a man on a square grid.

## 8. MOVEMENT GLIDES (37h)
On the map and wherever a body moves, the motion is SMOOTH: it slides, never block by block; the animation
inside the sliding thing plays to the 120 BPM. ANIMATION [glide].

## 9. PORTRAITS AND CUSTOMIZATION (37i)
Head only for now; head gear shows in the portrait; shoulders only if the clothing assets make it cheap;
backgrounds may change. Face and portrait customization happens at the BARBER in a settlement screen; many more
hairstyles and head shapes, Battle Brothers' count as the floor. Eyes rest slightly off-centre.

## 10. THE FLIP IS A BIG DEAL (37j)
A full transition: a filter, a cut, a sound; the phone and the world change look; the phone is cracked at the
start and gets "bougier and busier" with progress; the flip is UNLOCKED at some point in each act, never at the
start (when is his; a default goes to VOTE). "We can't do this cheap."

## 11. SCAVENGE (37k)
A SCAVENGE button in the settlement screen: spend time, test your luck, for materials; meant for when you are
down bad on food, medicine or batteries; a settlement may show a bonus (a recent battle: more ammo). WORLD +
LIFE+CITY, cited from the shift research.

## 12. SOUNDS (37l)
The footstep that is not sand: "the reverb of a glass jar." The space around a sound is wrong: dry, real, no
synthetic room. Three more noise-built items down; the recipe stays dead. The room at B, the valley strikes A.

## 13. LIFESPANS (37m)
