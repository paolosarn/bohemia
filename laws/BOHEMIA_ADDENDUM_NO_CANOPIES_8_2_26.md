## THE LAW

**A CANOPY IS A PARK THING.** No shelter-canopy on any district outside a park.

Where one stood, the building gets what a real entrance actually has: **STEPS**, **PIERS**,
a porch cut into the mass, a recessed doorway. Shade is not the point of a doorway.

## I WAS TOLD TWICE, AND THE FIRST TIME I DIDN'T LISTEN

Earlier the same day he wrote, about two of these icons, *"the icon is trying so hard to
have some shade shit like WTF."* My answer was to make the canopies **smaller** — a quarter
the area, with ribs and a fascia so they'd "read as built."

That is the failure the **STOP PRODUCING law (7/26)** names outright: *finding a legal way
to ship the rejected thing IS the violation.* He didn't ask for a better canopy. He was
telling me the canopy was the problem, and I heard a note about execution because a note
about execution was the one I knew how to fix.

The second message is not a refinement of the first. **It is a ruling, and a ruling is not
a design conversation.** So they are gone — not shrunk, gone — from all four districts and
all four icons, in the walking plot and in the map icon alike.

## WHAT COUNTS AS A CANOPY, AND WHAT DOESN'T

This distinction is the whole gate, and it has to be stated so nobody relitigates it later:

| | what it is | ruling |
|---|---|---|
| **CANOPY** | a roof you stand under that is *not part of a building's own volume* — entry canopy, boarding canopy, fuel canopy, stall tent, carport | **parks only** |
| **SPAN** | infrastructure that carries something *across a gap* — skybridge, freeway overpass deck, sign gantry, jet bridge, substation busbar | stays; nobody built these to stand under |

A **rooftop solar array** is equipment bolted to a roof, not a canopy. The terminal's
photovoltaics moved from over the platform onto the head-house roof — same signature, same
count, nothing left for a person to walk under.

## WHAT CAME OUT, AND WHAT REPLACED IT

| district | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| **city hall** | entry canopy on a 160-ft mast, spanning the plaza | a broad flight of **steps** onto the podium + a row of **entry piers** at the doors |
| **city hall** | 33 solar trees *over the plaza people walk across* | the same 33, standing in their **own gravel bed** at the plot edge — equipment, not shade |
| **courthouse** | the projecting cantilever over the plaza | **steps** up out of the plaza + the **piers** that carry the wall above them |
| **terminal** | photovoltaic deck over the boarding platform | the array **on the head-house roof** |
| **chapel** | covered walk from the lot to the doors | a low **path wall** either side, and the trees that were meant to shade it, dead in their grates |

## THE RATCHET

`CANOPY_DEBT` in the gate names every shelter canopy still standing in the valley. **It may
only shrink; nothing new joins it.** Districts he has already approved are on the list and
are **not** to be reopened for this alone (8/1: *"approved for now" is an approval*) — they
come off when they're next touched for another reason.
