## RULING 1 — THE REFERENCE SET IS THREE GAMES NOW
Bohemia's combat and campaign are aimed at: **ROGUE FABLE 4** (already
law, 8/17 and 8/26 — RF4's systems quantised to the 120 BPM beat),
**BATTLE BROTHERS** (new: the campaign layer, and as much tactical swag as
survives the pace test), and **FINAL FANTASY XII'S GAMBITS** (new: the
mechanism that makes depth fast).

## RULING 2 — *** THE SPLIT THAT MAKES THIS TRACTABLE, AND IT IS IN HIS
## OWN MESSAGE ***
He praised two different things and named a problem with only one of them.
- **WHAT HE LOVES AND IS SLOW:** the tactical layer. "Every time you
  enter combat it's a fucking chess match and I don't think normal people
  would like that."
- **WHAT HE LOVES AND IS NOT SLOW AT ALL:** the campaign layer. The
  world, the caravan, the city-states, faction holdings. Contracts,
  renown, moral reputation, relations between factions.
**SO WE TAKE BATTLE BROTHERS' CAMPAIGN WHOLESALE AND WE DO NOT TAKE ITS
TACTICAL PACE.** The depth he is describing lives mostly in the strategic
layer, and the strategic layer costs a player nothing per turn. That is
the whole answer to "too slow for normal people," and it is why this is
buildable rather than a contradiction.
The tactical layer keeps RF4's shape, which he already ruled twice, and
which is FAST by design — one hour, no stat bloat, information on the
field rather than in menus.

## RULING 3 — THE GAMBIT IS THE BRIDGE, AND IT ANSWERS HIS OWN COMPLAINT
FF12's gambits are "if A, then X" rules with a target, an action and a
PRIORITY, evaluated top down, first match wins. Practitioners describe it
as programming without knowing you are programming, and the pleasure as
**"a great feeling of triumph when you defeat a formidable enemy through
a fine-tuned setting of your gambits."**
**THAT IS EXACTLY THE MACHINE THAT CONVERTS A CHESS MATCH INTO A FAST
FIGHT.** A chess match asks the player to think hard EVERY TURN. A gambit
asks them to think hard ONCE, then watch the thinking pay off at speed.
The depth does not leave; it moves off the turn and into the loadout.
**AND THE KNOWN CRITICISM IS THE DESIGN CONSTRAINT, NOT A REASON TO
SKIP IT:** "gambits make the game play itself." Whatever we build, THE
PLAYER'S HANDS MUST STILL MATTER ON THE BEAT. The gambit handles the
obvious; the player handles the decision. If a fight can be won with the
phone face-down, it went wrong.

## RULING 4 — THE CITY SOUL IS THE CAMPAIGN LAYER
"It was missing a little bit of this, like, city soul." Battle Brothers'
map is alive because places WANT things, factions have relations, and
your renown and your moral reputation change what you are offered. Ours
has districts, factions, standing and a phone feed that offers work.
**THE PARTS ARE ALREADY HERE AND THEY HAVE NEVER BEEN WIRED INTO ONE
ECONOMY.** That is the shape of the next few days of research.
