# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM — THE THREE CURRENCIES + THE CENTURY RULE (Paolo 7/26/26, LOCKED)

Paolo's words: "buildings produce resources which the logo is displayed as
like an apple, duct tape, and maybe another important thing... then there's
electricity — batteries, kind of tech stuff... or a building can produce
clout which can help in a bunch of other ways... I would just really like to
simplify it... games like that are called spreadsheet simulators and I'm not
a fan... this game is about rebuilding the city over 100 years... when we
make buildings, whether to house more people or produce more resources, that
impacts the city of Bohemia over 3 acts... if you never participate in
building buildings that increase resources or clout or electricity, the
actual city in act three will be vastly different from a city where the
dynasty cared about rebuilding civilization."

LOCKED:
1. THREE CURRENCIES ONLY: (a) RESOURCES — physical goods, displayed as
   simple icons (apple = food, duct tape = materials; a possible third icon
   is [PENDING Paolo]); (b) ELECTRICITY — batteries/tech; (c) CLOUT —
   producible by buildings, spendable in many ways.
2. ANTI-REFERENCE: Civ-5 / Surviving-the-Aftermath multi-currency
   spreadsheet feel is BANNED. City-builder mode stays radically simple.
3. BUILDINGS: house people or produce one of the three. That's the economy.
4. THE CENTURY RULE: dynasty building choices COMPOUND across the three acts
   (~100 years). Neglect production/power/clout and act three's city is
   visibly poorer; invest and it's visibly rebuilt. The city is the game's
   long memory. Mechanism to be designed (research commissioned); numbers
   are Paolo's when the mechanism is ruled.
