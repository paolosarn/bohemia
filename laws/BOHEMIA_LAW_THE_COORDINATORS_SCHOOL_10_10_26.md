## THE TEN RULES

1. THE PRODUCER TRIAGES, THEN PLAYS, THEN REPORTS (round 1, the producer). Every sweep opens with the three biggest risks and the coordinator's own five minutes in the live build on the phone profile, and no lane's report is read before both are written down. What changes: the round record starts with THE THREE RISKS and THE FIVE MINUTES, above the lane-by-lane notes.

2. FINISH ONE SCREEN BEFORE YOU DRAW THE THOUSANDTH ASSET (round 2, looks first). One screen at final quality, with its rules written as numbers (pixel density, palette, light direction, outline, the HUD's material), is the contract every other piece of art is measured against, and nothing else ships until it exists. What changes: the look program (rules 82, 87, 88) names ONE screen first, the fight board on one Vegas block with its HUD, and DIRECTION's twin sheet for that one screen is the first thing judged; the cooks' kits are measured against it, not against each other.

3. TRANSLATE, DON'T RELAY (round 3, serving the director). Keep the director's sentence, turn it into a specific job, decide every door that can be walked back through yourself, and bring him only the one-way doors, shown as a thing he can see or hear, never as a list. What changes: a ruling record keeps his words verbatim AND the one-line translation ("what he means is..."); the WHAT I NEED FROM YOU block holds only one-way doors (identity, names, a creative fork with no defensible default) and is "Nothing, I'm good" otherwise, which it already should be (rule 8/15).

4. OWNERSHIP IS A FILE, THE GATE IS ON THE TRUNK, AND EVERY LANE HOLDS ONE JOB (round 4, many lanes, one trunk). Every path has one owner lane written in a file a machine reads, the checks that protect main run on main and not only on the lane's desk, and no lane may claim a second job while it holds one. What changes: PLUMBER [the owner file] (a CODEOWNERS-style file mapping engine/, slices/, tools/, gates/ paths to lanes, with a gate that refuses a commit touching another lane's path); the board's rule that a lane claims ONE row at a time becomes a gate leg (a second CLAIMED row in one lane section is red); the suite's trunk run (pages.yml already runs on main) is read as the truth over a lane's local green.

5. THE JUDGE SEES THE DIFFERENCE OR THE ITEM IS NOT READY (round 5, the verdict loop). An item reaches the director only when its one question is written on it, the change is big enough to flip and see in one tap at full size, and its own lane has already killed the weaker versions. What changes: this is rule 89 with teeth: every look item carries its one question, its before/after at one to one, and the lane's own kill list (what it tried and threw away). EYES [the before and after] reads them in that order; an item without the three does not register.

6. TEN SECONDS OR IT IS NOT A DEMO (round 6, the demo lands). A browser demo that takes longer than ten seconds to reach play on a phone is a download wearing a costume, and the release owner says NO GO in one line until the number is under ten. What changes: the RELEASE LINE (rule 52) gets a first line, TIME TO PLAY UNDER TEN SECONDS on his phone's profile at 4x throttle, measured by RUN's load gate; the 40-to-67-second new game is the single biggest red on the line and RUN [ten seconds to play] is its row.

7. A GATE THAT HAS NEVER GONE RED IS NOT A GATE (round 7, honest gates). Every automated check carries a broken input that turns it red, says how often it lies, and looks at the same surface the director looks at, or it is not enforcing anything. What changes: PLUMBER [the gate goes red] sweeps the suite for gates with no mutation proof and lists them; a new gate is accepted into the suite only with its red case named in the commit; visual gates read a screenshot of the phone profile, not the code.

8. THE LOOK IS A NUMBER BEFORE IT IS A DRAWING (round 8, one look across many hands). Nobody draws until the pack twin is named and its numbers are on the card (pixel density, palette count, light direction, outline width, luminance range), and nothing reaches the vote until its own numbers sit beside the twin's and match. What changes: DIRECTION's twin sheet carries the numbers per family, COOK TWO/THREE/FOUR put them on every item, and the pack gate (PLUMBER [the pack gate]) compares them; a cook item without its twin's numbers does not register.

9. RETIRE BY POINTER, NOT BY DATE (round 9, the memory). The round a rule changes, the old rule gets one line naming what replaced it and the live file gets shorter, not longer. What changes: every lettered amendment (22g, 54c, 64a, 65a, 66a, 73a, 73b, 77a, 77b, 82a) is folded into its parent rule the round it lands or the parent gets the one-line pointer; a sweep that adds a rule and does not retire or fold one says so in the round record; the START file's coordinator blocks older than (ka) are folded into one paragraph with pointers to the records.

10. THE LAST FIVE LINES ARE THE REPLY (round 10, talking to the boss). The change, the one number, the one red, the ask and the two-sentence bottom line are the last five lines he sees; the whole reply is capped at 150 words and eighth grade; every word above those lines is a word he will not read. What changes: the coordinator's replies shrink to the cap from the next reply on; the reply contract gate is asked for a word-count leg and a last-five-lines leg (PLUMBER [the reply leg]); long detail goes to the record, never to his screen.

## WHAT THIS LAW DOES NOT DO

It names no new reference game and changes no pillar. Production practice from other studios is method, not content: how a producer runs a review is not a game Paolo has not named. The research lanes' library is untouched. Nothing here lifts the rule-88 hold; rules 2, 5 and 8 are the hold's method.
