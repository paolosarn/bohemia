## CLAUSE 1 — THE RULING ITSELF: IT IS A DYNASTY, NOT A ONE-LIFE RUN

Bohemia spans **three dynasty generations — Animal / Human / Angel — across roughly
100 years**. Dynasty saves span all three. Heirs inherit the choice log. Companions
do not die; permadeath is named an anti-feature. "Roguelite" describes the run
structure, not a wipe.

Progress, standing, the city, the choice log: **they CARRY.** That was already true
in five separate law files before he had to say it again:

## CLAUSE 2 — NEVER MAKE HIM RE-CONFIRM HIS OWN LAWS

`laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_NOTES_ARE_RULINGS_7_19_26.md` already says never make him
re-confirm his own **words**. This extends it: never make him re-confirm his own
**laws**.

**BEFORE A QUESTION GOES IN FRONT OF HIM IT IS SEARCHED AGAINST CANON.** If the
answer exists, there is no question — there is a thing to go and read. A settled
question is worse than silence: it costs him a reply, makes him re-litigate what he
already decided, and it is the difference between an assistant and a tax.

**THIS FAILED TWICE IN TWO TURNS**, which is why it is a law and not a resolution:
- *"When a utility dies, does it disappear or get an owner?"* — settled by CLUSTERED
  POWER (the lit ~12% is OWNED) and LIGHT = TERRITORY.
- *"In a roguelite, what happens to standing when the run ends?"* — settled above,
  five times over.
