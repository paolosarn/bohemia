# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM -- THE VAMILY WORD (Paolo 9/4/26, LOCKED)
# "Nah have a standard like VAMILY. Make the key word vamily for all of the
# jobs u assign to it ever in the history itll search if it has a job
# assigned for its role and yeah. Make it VAMILY."

## THE RULING
- **VAMILY** is the one word. He types it in any chat and that is the whole
  instruction. It amends the AUTONOMY DOCTRINE (7/26) for coordinator-
  assigned work: "go" still pops a lane's own backlog; **VAMILY pops the
  coordinator's assignment for that lane.**
- The assignments live in ONE file at repo root: **`VAMILY.md`**. It is the
  ledger and the history. A chat that hears the word searches that file for
  its role, takes the first job marked OPEN, marks it CLAIMED and commits
  before building, marks it SHIPPED and commits when it is in the walked
  surface AND the demo. "Ever in the history" is the SHIPPED lines.
- A chat with no role yet takes the top of THE ORDER in that file and that
  lane becomes its role. So he never has to remember which chat is which.
- Only the coordinator adds jobs to `VAMILY.md`. Lanes change status words
  and nothing else. The full text of a job is its named row in
  BOHEMIA_BACKLOG.md, so nothing is written twice.
- Every job carries his 9/4 orders: 120 BPM friendly for everything, and
  re-cut the demo when you ship.

## WHY THIS SHAPE
He does not dig in files and he does not paste. The lane word already
started a chat, but it left him remembering what each chat was for and
trusting that a job was where the chat would look. VAMILY moves that
memory into one file the machine reads, and it gives him the thing he asked
for on 9/4 in three different ways: a shortcut, a history, and jobs that
are ready for the assigned chat without him carrying anything between
them.

## GATE
handoff_gate should assert `VAMILY.md` exists, parses (every job line is
OPEN / CLAIMED <date> / SHIPPED <date>), and every job name resolves to a
row in BOHEMIA_BACKLOG.md. A law without a machine gate is not enforced;
this one is routed to SHARED as a one-line gate row until it lands.
