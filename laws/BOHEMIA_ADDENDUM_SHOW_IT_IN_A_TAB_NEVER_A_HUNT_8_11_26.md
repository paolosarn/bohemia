## WHAT HAPPENED

The ART lane wired four approved tile families into the walked world in one
day (rail, parking stripes, the parapet ring, corrugated skins) and closed
every reply with "walk the RUN tab and correct anything you hate." That is a
HUNT: the RUN tab is a whole valley, the wired work is in specific districts
he was never given a door to, and "cell 55,29" is a coordinate, not a door.
He already made this law once (NAME THE TAB, 7/28: "the link is the door and
the tab is the room") and the lane obeyed its letter - a tab WAS named - while
breaking its whole point: he still could not SEE the work without wandering.

## THE LAW

1. ANYTHING SHIPPED THAT CAN BE SEEN gets PUT IN FRONT OF HIM the same turn:
   a card in the lane's tab (ART for this lane) carrying a REAL SCREENSHOT of
   the thing live in the game, captioned in plain words. He observes; he never
   searches.
2. "Walk the RUN tab and find it" is BANNED as a deliverable. The RUN tab is
   where he plays, never where he is sent on errands. If the work only exists
   somewhere in the world, the lane brings the world to the tab (screenshot),
   not him to the world.
3. This binds every lane and every session, on top of NAME THE TAB: the tab
   is the room, AND the thing must be IN the room, visible, no steps between
   the tap and the seeing.
