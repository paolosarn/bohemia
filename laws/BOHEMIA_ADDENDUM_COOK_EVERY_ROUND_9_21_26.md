## 1. THE LAW
A MAKING LANE COOKS EVERY ROUND. Every round of SOUNDS ends with a sound he can hear. Every
round of UI ends with a panel he can see at real size. COOK a tile or a prop, CHARACTER a
body or a garment, ANIMATION a clip, PORTRAIT a face, WORDS a set of lines in a mouth,
PEOPLE a person with a name and a face, LIFE+CITY a building or a block, FACTIONS a sign or
a flag, QUESTS an ask a person speaks, WORLD a thing on the phone. One real thing minimum,
registered in the VOTE tab, every round, or the round did not happen.

## 2. WHAT THIS AMENDS
Rule 18(b) of THE PLAYABLE CUT (9/20) said a held lane "spends the round measuring its part
of the three, or on school". That sentence is DEAD as a round's whole output. It made the
sound chat a checker. From now: THE HOLD IS ON THE PLAY SURFACE, NOT ON THE MAKING. Nothing
a held lane makes reaches the demo or the alpha's play tabs until the coordinator says the
cut holds; everything a held lane makes goes to the VOTE tab (in the alpha, rule 15), where
he hears it, sees it and votes. Measuring rides beside the cooking; school is round one of a
new question and never a second round in a row for a making lane.
STOP PRODUCING (7/26) is not touched: a lane he has FROZEN by verdict still produces nothing;
rule 18's hold is not a freeze, it is a queue, and the queue is the VOTE tab.

## 3. WHAT A ROUND LOOKS LIKE NOW, FOR THE TWO CHATS HE NAMED
SOUNDS: a round is sounds. Real audio he can play in the tab, on the beat, under the analog
horror law: the room hum for the BEGIN tap, the footstep that lands on the beat, the tape
drop-out of the step, the broadcast tone of the phone, a song through the dead speaker. The
instruments (the limiter, the audio-context count, the driver) stay in the proof line.
UI: a round is panels. The loading screen with BEGIN, the fight HUD, the talk panel with a
face, the notes box, the vote tab itself, each drawn 3-D (light and form, delivered as
pixels), each a picture at real phone size in the tab beside the flat one it replaces.

## 4. THE REPORT LEADS WITH THE THING
A making lane's round report and board line lead with WHAT WAS MADE and WHERE HE CAN HEAR OR
SEE IT (the VOTE tab, the item's id). Gates, mutations and measurements go after, in the
proof line. A report that leads with a gate is the shape he called "so fucking strange".

## 5. THE GATE (OWED, PLUMBER [cook gate])
The vote registry is the machine's record of cooking. PLUMBER builds the leg: for every
making lane, a round that lands a commit on main without registering at least one new item
in the VOTE registry is red on that lane's row. Until it exists, the coordinator reads the
registry every VAMILY and names, by chat, who cooked and who only checked.

## 6. THE VOTE TAB PLAYS SOUND
A sound he cannot hear in the tab is not cooked. UI [vote plays sound]: a sound item has a
play button and plays on the phone, on the beat, once, and the vote controls sit beside it.

## 7. ROUTING
Rule 22 on the front page. Every making lane's MODE line loses "spend the round measuring"
and gains COOK EVERY ROUND. SOUNDS [cook sounds] first line; UI [cook panels] first line
and [vote plays sound]; PLUMBER [cook gate].
