1. HUMAN-AUTHORED TILES ARE THE PIXEL SOURCE. Purchased/downloaded packs made
   by human artists (Paolo buys, uploads, and bulk-sweeps them) are the
   default origin of all new visual material.
2. THE AI'S ART ROLE IS: ingest, recolor onto the locked palette, run the
   dead-world aging passes (70/20/10, bleach/dust/loot — mechanical), compose
   per the Room Recipe Book and the composition laws, and wire into the
   engine. ASSEMBLY, ADAPTATION, AND AGING — not authorship.
3. AI pixel AUTHORSHIP is the exception: only when no human tile covers the
   need, flagged as such, and it lands in Paolo's judge pile as a first look.
4. PACK INTAKE PROCEDURE: Paolo uploads pack zips to the ART chat -> ingested
   into HD-repo-style banks (keyed pack/idx, licensed-source noted) -> ONE
   bulk sweep sitting for Paolo (Great Sweep pattern) -> UP tiles enter the
   Approved Asset Index automatically -> palette-map + age -> compose.
5. THE TARGET QUESTION: the current target screen is CBB-frozen. Once a
   human-tile corpus is swept, the expected move is re-cutting the target
   from human tiles — that re-cut happens ONLY on Paolo's explicit word
   (a frozen target needs a new ruling, never a quiet replacement).
6. SHOPPING LIST GIVEN (his purchase, ~$20-40): LimeZu "Modern Interiors" +
   "Modern Exteriors" (itch.io) as the living-city base; one post-apocalyptic
   tileset of his choice (itch.io/CraftPix) for wasteland flavor; Kenney.nl
