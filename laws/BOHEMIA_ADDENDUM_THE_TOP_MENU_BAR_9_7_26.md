## THE RULING
The walked game gets **ONE BAR THAT OWNS THE TOP OF THE SCREEN.** Not chips floating over
the world; a strip, pinned to the top edge, that the game's status lives in and the menu
hangs off. Everything that is currently a loose button in the top-left goes into it.

## WHAT THE BAR CARRIES, AND THE RULE THAT DECIDES IT
**Only what the game actually knows.** MECHANISM-MINE / CONTENTS-PAOLO'S: a readout with
an invented number in it is worse than an empty bar, because he cannot tell the two apart
by looking.

Today the walked city truthfully knows three things and they are what ships in the bar:
WHERE YOU ARE (district, on foot or riding, inside), THE DAY AND THE CLOCK, and the menu.

The three currencies are NOT in the bar yet and that is not an oversight: there is no live
player purse on the walked surface. `PURSE` exists as a module with three currencies and a
ledger, and nothing on the street holds one. ECONOMY's own round 27 says the loop does not
close. **The bar reads its money through one function, so the moment a purse is wired the
numbers appear with no further design.** Until then it shows no money rather than a zero
that means nothing.

## WHAT THIS PAIRS WITH
- **[no tabs]** (9/6, LOCKED): CITY and MAP stop being buttons. A bar is where the
  survivors of that cull live.
- **[half size]** (9/6, LOCKED): the bar is drawn at the same half scale as everything
  else on the run.
- **[skin swap]**: the bar is the biggest single piece of interface in the game, so it is
  the first thing that must come off a named skin when three acts land.
