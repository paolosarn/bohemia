## 1. THE THREE ACTS ARE OPEN AT THE SAME TIME
The three generations (Gen 1 Animal, Gen 2 Human, Gen 3 Angel; ERAS, Paolo 9/7) are no longer
played one after another. All three are open from the start of the game and the player FLIPS
between them at will, the way Ocarina of Time flips child Link and adult Link. The ~100 hours
are spent across the three, in whatever order he flips.
THE PILLAR LINE IS AMENDED: "three generations that INHERIT... the player lives all three"
stands; "lives all three AT ONCE, flipping" is added.

## 2. THE FUTURE IS THE PAST'S REPORT CARD, DRAWN AS A CITY
Flip forward and the later act shows what the earlier acts have earned so far. Early in the
game the future city is built like shit, because nothing has been done yet. "If you want to
see more awesome futuristic technology life, you gotta do more in the past." So:
(a) THE FUTURE IS DERIVED, NEVER AUTHORED. Act 2's city and act 3's city are computed from the
    earlier acts' ledgers (deeds, territory, batteries, standing, what was built, who lived)
    every time he flips forward. Nobody hand-places act 3.
(b) IT IS ALWAYS THERE TO LOOK AT. He may flip to act 3 in the first minute and see the ruin
    his nothing has made. The ruin is the tutorial.
(c) WHAT HE DOES IN THE FUTURE IS ITS OWN LEDGER, LAID ON TOP. A flip back to act 1 and a
    change there re-derives act 2 and 3's base; act 2 and 3's own deeds are re-applied on top
    of the new base. Nothing he did in the future is thrown away; it lands on different ground.
    (The Day of the Tentacle rule: the past rewrites the future's world, never the future
    character's hands.)
(d) THE MACHINE GATE: a checker that changes one act-1 ledger row, flips forward, and proves
    the act-3 city changed, and that act 3's own deeds survived the re-derive.

and used in another. QUESTS owns the shape; A QUEST IS PEOPLE, PLACES AND THINGS stands (the
thing that carries across ages is a thing, a place, or a person, never a card).
