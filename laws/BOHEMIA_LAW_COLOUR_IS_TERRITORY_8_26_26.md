## THIS AMENDS STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR. IT DOES NOT KILL IT.

The 7/19 law says a colourway is legal but never PROGRESS, and the 8/15 amendment
says a faction is told apart by its OUTLINE with colour as the back-up channel.
Both stand. The valley opens at 06:00 in the dark and a silhouette is what reads
there, so **silhouette remains the carrier of identity and the accessibility
answer, and the greyscale test still governs.**

What 8/26 adds is the other half, which nobody had asked for:

**A FACTION'S COLOUR IS NOT DECORATION. IT IS A STATEMENT OF WHO WOULD DEFEND
YOU, AND IT MUST BE COORDINATED, SATURATED, AND NOBODY ELSE'S.**

Colour is now a REQUIRED second channel. Silhouette says *what kind of person*;
colour says *whose*.

## WHY THIS IS REALISM AND NOT STYLE

His reference is exact and the history backs it. Researched 8/26:

- Colours signal allegiance, claim territory, and **identify rivals at a
  distance** — that distance is the whole function.
- The practice began as a **practical necessity** in dense urban areas: it lets a
  group project presence **without verbal confrontation**.
- Choices were **oppositional**: the Bloods took red deliberately to contrast the
  Crips' blue. A colour only means something against another colour.
- Origins were often **mundane and material** — one account of the Crips' blue is
  simply which bandanas the local surplus store had in bulk.
- And they are **easy to take off** if you are stopped. Colours are a decision you
  make every time you go out.

Every one of those is a mechanic waiting to happen in a valley where the economy
died. The last one especially: **wearing your colours is a choice with a cost.**

## THE FOUR TESTS

1. **COORDINATED.** A faction's worn set agrees with itself. Not one loud garment
   under a beige coat — a coat is most of a person and it decides the reading.
2. **SATURATED ENOUGH TO BE A SIGNAL.** A faction whose cloth is mostly grey and
   brown has no colour, whatever its name says. Drab is legal, but only when
   drabness IS the statement (the Volunteers, the Homeless).
3. **NOBODY ELSE'S.** Two factions may not own the same dominant hue. You read it
   at fifty yards or it is not doing its job.
4. **THE NAME IS NOT A LIE.** A faction named for a colour wears that colour.

## WHAT IT DOES NOT DO

It does not licence recolours as content. **A recolour is still never PROGRESS**
(7/19) — progress is still new SHAPES. What changed is that a recolour can now be
IDENTITY, which is a different job, and identity was the thing that was broken.

It does not hand me colour ownership. **Which faction owns which colour is HIS**
(MECHANISM-MINE / CONTENTS-PAOLO'S). What the machine may fix without him is a
CONTRADICTION — a faction named COLORFUL dressed in bone, a faction named BLUES
dressed in red. "The Mob should own charcoal" is taste, not a contradiction, and
it waits for his thumb.

The colours were built for it and never put on it. That is the same failure as the
seventeen hats that drew nothing on 8/25 — **the material existed and never reached
the player** — and this one had his own words attached to it for five weeks.

board has since 8/1 — one pattern in two places, not a second mechanism.
