## 1. NOTHING IS FORCED, AND NOTHING HAPPENS IN THE FIRST SECOND (32a)

force this on me." "Don't force interactions on the player." Said three ways in one batch.
- The first minutes of the game are HIS. Nobody speaks to him, stops him, or stands in his door
  until he has walked. The world arrives with time, on the beat of its own clock.
- A person who wants something STANDS THERE and can be walked past; an ask is a person in a place,
  never a stop. A rumour is OVERHEARD as he passes, never said to his face. The creditor who
  remembers his father is there when he walks by, one line, once, and never in the first minute.
- The demo is the four things (rule 18) and NOTHING ELSE fires in it.
- Amends A QUEST IS PEOPLE, PLACES AND THINGS: add "and none of them blocks him". Gate: every
  spoken line has a distance and a delay; nothing speaks in the first 60 s of play; nothing speaks
  unless he is within its reach; nothing halts the pad.
