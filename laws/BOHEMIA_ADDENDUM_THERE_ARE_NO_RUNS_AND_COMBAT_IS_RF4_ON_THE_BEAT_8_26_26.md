# ADDENDUM — THERE ARE NO RUNS, AND COMBAT IS ROGUE FABLE 4 ON THE BEAT
### Paolo, 8/26/26. LOCKED. Four rulings in one message, one of which corrects the top of the truth hierarchy.

---

## HOW THIS CAME UP

Four times across two days he said the combat does not feel like Rogue Fable 4.
The fourth time: *"OKAY WE NEED TO DO WAY MORE WITH COMBAT. THIS DOESNT FEEL LIKE
ROGUE FABLE 4 AT ALL. IT NEEDS A HUGE UPDATE I APPRECIATE ALL THE MECHANICS YOU
INTRODUCED. WE ARE VERY FAR OFF FROM THIS BEING THE COMBAT!"*

Then: **"YOU NEED TO ASK ME ALL THE QUESTIONS YOU NEED TO ASK ME NOW."** So the
one-question rule was suspended by him, four questions went up, and all four came
back with rulings.

---

## RULING 1 — "JUST IMAGINE ROGUE FABLE 4 WITH 120 BPM EVERYTHING"

> *"I DONT KNOW WHY YOURE MAKING SUCH A BIG WHOOP ABOUT IT JUST IMAGINE ROGUE
> FABLE 4 WITH 120 BPM EVERYTHING BRO LIEK THATS ALL?"*

**That is the entire design brief and it is one sentence.** I had built it into a
philosophical fork about whether the dial or the decisions should own a fight. It
is not a fork. **It is RF4's systems, quantised to the beat.** The 120 BPM law and
RF4 are not in tension and never were — the beat is the clock RF4's turns run on.

He also picked *"split it about evenly"*: the dial keeps mattering, but where you
stand and what you use matter as much. **Bad timing stops being a death sentence.**
Measured 8/25, which is what made the gap visible: a perfect dial clears 7 fights
of 10 and dies twice; a sloppy dial clears 2 and **dies 8 times of 10.**

**STOP TREATING THE DIAL AS A QUESTION.** It stays, everything quantises to it,
and RF4's depth is built on top of it.

---

## RULING 2 — ALL THREE BARS

The full trinity, not a subset:

- **PROTECTION POINTS** — a second bar above HP, unbreachable while one point
  stands, regenerating on a clock (RF4-05).
- **POWER** — one unified offensive stat (RF4-07), and *anything modifying Power
  modifies ALL power* (RF4-42).
- **SPEED POINTS** — mobility as a spendable resource that **BUYS ACTIONS**
  (RF4-08), deliberately hard to stack (RF4-09).

**Zero of the 15 teardown rows describing this economy were built.** Not partly.
Zero. That is the measured reason the mechanics shipped 8/20–8/25 (medic, car,
alarm, finisher, breacher, eyes ring, open ground, xp/loot) did not add up to the
feel: they are decisions layered on a fight with no economy underneath them.

---

## RULING 3 — A REAL KIT OF ABILITIES

Four to six, each **recharged by a verb, not a timer** (RF4-11, RF4-13). Do the
thing to get the thing back. This is where RF4's every-turn-is-a-choice comes from.

---

## RULING 4 — *** THERE ARE NO RUNS. *** THIS IS THE BIG ONE

> **"BRO THERE ARE NO RUNS. IT IS A FULL GAME THAT WILL TAKE YOU 100 HOURS TO
> COMPLETE BRO. LEVELING UP LEVELS AND GIVES YOU EXPERIENCE FOR EXPERIENCE TREE
> CYBER PUNK ELDERSCROLL PERK AND BONUS SHIT. WILL ALSO GO HAND IN HAND WITH
> ABILITIES AND THE 60 MINI BOSSES IN THE GAME THAT GIVE YOU A NEW WAY TO
> INTERACT WITH BOHEMIA BRO!"**

### THIS CORRECTS THE TOP OF THE TRUTH HIERARCHY

`CLAUDE.md`'s first line has called Bohemia a **"roguelite"** since the beginning,
and every session has read that line first, every session. **It is wrong.**

- **NO RUNS.** Not a run-based game. A **full game, ~100 hours to complete.**
- **A PERSISTENT EXPERIENCE TREE** — his words: *Cyberpunk / Elder Scrolls perks
  and bonuses.* Not talents drawn fresh each run. A tree you build across one long
  character.
- **THE TREE, THE ABILITIES AND THE 60 MINI BOSSES ARE ONE SYSTEM**, not three.
  Each mini boss **gives you a new way to interact with Bohemia** — a verb, not a
  trophy.

**WHAT THIS INVALIDATES IMMEDIATELY:** every design decision that assumed a run
resets. The XP question I put to him on 8/26 offered *"only counts between runs"*
as an option — a question built on a premise that does not exist. RF4-44 (tomes as
a per-run level-up currency) and RF4-31 (build variety across runs) must be re-read
against a single persistent character, not ported.

**AND IT VINDICATES V181's SHAPE.** Experience off the bodies, earned by walking to
them, feeding a tree that persists — that is a 100-hour game's economy, not a run's.

---

## WHAT IS NOW LAW

1. Combat is **Rogue Fable 4 with everything on the 120 BPM beat.** One sentence.
2. **All three bars** ship: Protection, Power, Speed.
3. **A real kit of abilities**, recharged by verbs.
4. **There are no runs.** One character, ~100 hours, a persistent perk tree, and
   60 mini bosses that each hand you a new way to interact with the world.

## WHAT IS STILL HIS AND NOT DECIDED HERE

The **numbers** on any of it, the **names** of perks and abilities, and **which 60
bosses** and what each grants. `NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL` is untouched: POWER is
built to modify **what the dial already does**, never to add flat damage beside it.
