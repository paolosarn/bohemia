## WHY THIS RULING EXISTS — the failure that earned it, named honestly

The WORLD lane spent 7/31 like this: he approved the high school at 89% and said
"lets move on", so the lane rolled the legibility fix to all 46 districts (good,
visible, on-brief), and then went straight into **invisible plumbing** — an
economy ledger — and offered him a **faction system** as the next step. Both are
real items on the coordinator's gap list. Neither is the WORLD, and neither
CHANGES WHAT HE SEES.

The tell was there and it got walked past: the lane's own reply said *"Nothing to
look at. This is plumbing, and it's invisible until you fill in the numbers."*
**A turn in this lane that has nothing to look at is a turn that missed.** He runs
this project by looking at it. He judges by looking at it. The verdict workflow,
the judge pages, the render-and-look bar, VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE — the entire
apparatus of how he works is visual, and 32 of his 45 districts are still
rejected. That is the open wound, and the lane wandered off it toward a gap list
written by somebody else.

**The deeper error: a coordinator's ranked list is not a ruling.** THE BIG MISSING
(7/29) ranks the economy #3 and says OWNER: WORLD. That is a useful document and
it is NOT canon. Paolo's rulings are canon, and his standing direction for this
lane has been the districts since the bulk verdict on 7/27. Taking a records/
ranking as an instruction over the human's own direction is the same class of
mistake as taking a garbled transcription literally.
