He is right on every count, and the failure is named plainly here so no session
repeats it: the run drew the player as an ORANGE DOT and the neighbours as
COLOURED RECTANGLES on flat squares, while the actual character — the rig, the
painted regions, the wardrobe, the face system, the walk cycle, six weeks of
approved work — sat one iframe away, already reachable over a bridge that already
existed. That is not a demo of Bohemia. That is a prototype wearing its name.

## THE LOCK

**1. THE RUN LANE'S JOB IS INTEGRATION, NOT FEATURES.** The lane exists to make
the ONE thing Paolo plays contain everything the fleet has built. A run turn that
adds a new mechanic while an approved asset bank still is not on screen is the
wrong turn. The measure of the lane is not "what did you add", it is "what is
finally IN it that we already made."

**2. QUESTS ARE THROWAWAY SCAFFOLDING IN THE RUN.** Paolo does not care about
the run's quest content and does not want to judge it. The run's quest is a cheap
errand whose only job is to ROUTE HIM AROUND THE CITY so the integration has
somewhere to show itself. It is disposable by design and gets replaced by real
quests later. Never surface the run's quest text for a verdict; never spend a
turn writing it.

**3. IT HAS TO LOOK LIKE THE GAME.** Not "eventually." Every run turn from here
closes visible gaps between what is on screen and what the corpus already holds:
real bodies, real buildings, the 3/4 iso view (45 DEGREE ART LAW), the real
valley, the music, the day cycle. A placeholder that survives a turn must be
declared out loud in the reply, not left for him to notice.

**4. NO MORE "WHAT DO YOU WANT ME TO DO WITH THIS."** Every run ship ends with
the INTEGRATION LEDGER score, machine-checked, so the state of the game is a
number that goes up instead of a feeling. He does not have to judge anything to
know whether the game got closer. That ledger is
`records/BOHEMIA_RUN_INTEGRATION_LEDGER_7_26_26.md`, enforced by
`gates/integration_gate.js`: a row may not be marked INTEGRATED without a machine
probe that proves the wiring is really there. Unproven claims fail the gate.

**5. THE THROWAWAY-QUEST COROLLARY.** Because the run's quest is disposable, the
run may freely place errands wherever the integration needs the player to walk.
Map-law is untouched: the run picks nothing canonical, it just needs a reason to
send him past the thing that was just wired in.

## WHAT THIS RULING DOES NOT CHANGE
- The gate discipline, the one-alpha law, the ship flow, REUSE-FIRST.
- The other lanes keep cooking. This is about where their output LANDS.
