realism for fun addicting gameplay, or if I think it's interesting enough,

the fun-trade or the interesting-trade, so the trade is visible. This is
the game's identity sentence: the most realistic economic crash simulator,
but fun. (It joins the educational mission in GDD v5: the infrastructure IS

## 2. THE QUESTION FORMAT (how ALL sessions ask him design/lore questions
## from now on — supersedes multi-question lists)
His words: "from now on you need to ask me one question at a time OK so
queue your questions up. provide more thorough explanation. try to come up
with two or three conclusions and I choose a conclusion."
THE FORMAT, LOCKED:
1. ONE question per reply. Sessions keep a visible QUEUE of what's next;
   the queue waits.
2. Each question comes with a THOROUGH EXPLANATION: why it matters, what
   hangs on it, what the real world says (researched, not vibes).
3. It ends with TWO OR THREE CONCLUSIONS (A/B/C) — complete, concrete
   candidate answers he can pick with one letter. Per the realism default,
   option A is the most realistic unless flagged otherwise; drama/fun
   trades are labeled as what they are.
4. He picks a conclusion (or writes his own); the pick is recorded as the
   ruling the same turn. "Other" is always legal and outranks the menu.
This extends the CLAUDE.md "ONE question max, bolded" rule with the
conclusions menu, and it is why the 8/4 seven-question list format is dead.
