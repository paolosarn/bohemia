## 1. THE CITY-BUILDER FEELS LIKE POCKET CITY 2 (LOCKED — the direction the
## whole missing half was waiting for)
His words: "I want the whole city builder aspect to feel exactly like Pocket
City 2 just more post apocalyptic."
WHAT THIS LOCKS: the FEEL contract. Pocket City 2 is the second time he has
named this game as a bar (7/28 icon readability, now the whole build loop) —
one-thumb mobile building, tap-to-place immediacy, satisfying instant
feedback, readable zones/services, progression that celebrates you. Bohemia's
build half plays like THAT, skinned post-apocalyptic: you are not zoning a
new city, you are re-lighting a dead one (salvage, repair, reclaim — the
adjectives change, the FINGER FEEL does not).
WHAT STAYS OPEN [HIS DESIGN, next sittings]: what the build verbs act ON
(repair a ruin vs place new), what the three currencies buy, which buildings
exist at act-1 tier (walls are already "a mechanic, not a rect" per GDD),
and how rebuilding advances the acts. The scarcity economy under the
PC2-smooth surface is the design tension to resolve NEXT — PC2 is
abundance-shaped, Bohemia is scarcity-shaped, and the answer lives in what a
tap COSTS, not how the tap feels.
ROUTING: big-missing item 2 downgrades from "no design" to "feel ruled, loop
open." No lane owns it yet; design continues in lore sittings.

## 2. MORE CORPSES, MORE SKELETONS (LOCKED as direction)
His words: "We need a lot more corpses a lot more skeletons in the game."
THE RULING: the dead become VISIBLE WORLD LANGUAGE, at real density — 2.2M
people are unaccounted for (death math, GDD v5) and the world currently
shows almost none of them. This is world dressing AND story-via-map: where
bodies lie tells what happened there (the tower die-off, the exodus road,
the hospital order — all pending damage-layer stories get their punctuation
from the dead).
THE LOOK, RULED (Paolo, same sitting): "ofc i want a realistic mix of
skeletons and husks." LOCKED: the dead follow the desert's real logic —
bleached, scattered, animal-worked SKELETONS in the open; desiccated
mummified HUSKS in the sealed places (cars, rooms, tunnels); the
already-canon DECAL corpse-persist layer for fresh kills. REALISTIC is the
placement law: where a body lies determines what a decade made of it, so
the dead tell you where they died. WORLD's tile forms are unblocked.
MECHANICS ALREADY CANON, REUSE: corpse collection is LOCKED as an in-game
system (GDD v5); the DECAL layer already holds corpse-persist the
Amalgamation can read. This ruling adds DENSITY and ART, not new mechanics.
ROUTING: WORLD files the tile forms (skeleton/husk prop families + corpse
decals, placement language per district story); ART cooks from the forms.

## 3. MARCO (named character — CANON, his clean re-statement)
His first sentence garbled; the coordinator's "king of the hobos" reading
was WRONG ("MARCO IS NOT THE KING OF HOBOS LMAO") and is dead. Paolo then
re-stated clean, and THIS is the canon, his words verbatim:
"Marco hardcore realist and neighborly. Happy to help."
MARCO: a HARDCORE REALIST — no illusions about what the world is now — who
is NEIGHBORLY and HAPPY TO HELP anyway. That contradiction is the whole
character: the man who believes nothing will save you and still shows up
with tools. First entry in the named cast.
STILL OPEN [Paolo's, when he feels like it]: faction or unaffiliated, where
he lives, his look, his role in quests/companionship. Nothing invented.
ROUTING: PEOPLE lane named-cast tier, personality canon as above. The
standing lesson from the garble round stays: a garbled sentence is a
question to ask, never a lore entry to complete.

## 4. THE MOB RUNS WITH THE CARAVANS (LOCKED as direction)
His words: "I can see the mob a huge role with the caravans."
WHY THIS IS PERFECT VEGAS: the Mob BUILT Las Vegas the first time —
providing money, muscle and trust where the official world would not. That
is exactly the caravan problem (GDD v5 logistics: trade restarts on
guarantors, escorts, and enforced promises). The Mob doing it AGAIN, a
century later, is the city's own history rhyming — and it puts a face on
the scariest seat in the canon: the GUARANTOR (whoever banks the caravans
slowly becomes the state; protection of trade routes is how states are
born).
OPEN [HIS DESIGN, likely next sitting]: whether the Mob IS the existing
Cartel faction, absorbs it, or is a NEW faction beside it; whether the Mob
holds the guarantor seat outright or is fighting for it; how far up the 15
North their protection reaches.
ROUTING: PEOPLE lane (faction canon note), WORLD (convoy/guarantor design
stays pending but now has a named contender).
