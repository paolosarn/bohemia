## 1. NO RESTART, AND WHY
The assets are good (he approved the far view, the clips are redone, the street tiles are
on his bank). The engine is not the problem: the fight, the walk, the crowd, the money,
the fold all exist. What is broken is THREE INTEGRATIONS, and the fleet shipping twenty
lanes of breadth into one surface while the manager measured the demo by a list instead
of by a machine. A restart keeps the assets and throws away the systems, then rebuilds
the same three integrations from zero with the same twenty chats. It would arrive at this
exact spot later. So: no restart. The fleet HOLDS, three things get finished, and the
ratchet becomes a machine.

## 2. THE GAME GOT WORSE, AND THAT IS THE MANAGER'S
The FIVE MINUTES law said "never worse than the last cut". It was a sentence. THE STEP IS
A HOUSE shipped a camera and a stride that made walking worse (he crashes into walls and
misses gaps; his person grows when he pinches), and the fight is still a different
universe. He is right that it is farther from playable than it has been. The fix is not
more rows; it is fewer, and a gate.

## 3. THE PLAYABLE CUT: THREE THINGS, NOTHING ELSE SHIPS
Until the coordinator says the playable cut holds, the ONLY changes that reach the alpha
are these three, each owned by one lane:
1. LOADING (RUN). A real loading screen when the alpha opens and when the demo opens.
   Nothing is tappable until the game is loaded; the alpha does not open straight into
   the RUN tab; the screen says what it is doing and holds every load, including the
   ones that today happen during the first minute of play. PLUMBER's freeze numbers
   (174 s of the first 300 frozen on a phone) are what it hides and then shrinks.
2. WALKING (RUN, with LIFE+CITY's lattice). ONE walking camera: a house fits on the
   screen, the body is drawn at one fixed size at that camera and NEVER changes while he
   walks or pinches (pinch out from the walk goes to the city; there is no in-between
   stop that resizes people). ONE stride: a press moves him to the next place he can
   stand toward the press, never past a gap and never into a wall; a press toward a wall
   slides along it; he can walk through any door or gap a body fits through. The house
   step of rule 16 stands as the ceiling of a stride, not its floor: the stride is as
   long as the ground allows, up to a lot.
3. THE FIGHT (COMBAT, DIRECTION judging, UI for the HUD). The fight starts ON THE EXACT
   TILES HE IS STANDING ON: the same ground art, the same bodies at the same size, the
   same light, the camera pulls back a little and the cloud passes. No arena, no second
   floor, no second body ladder, no workshop. Rule 17 stands and is sharpened by his
   words: "it doesn't even load on the tiles that you're on."
4. THE FIRST PERSON (QUESTS, PEOPLE, WORDS, PORTRAIT; added 9/20 by laws/BOHEMIA_LAW_A_QUEST_IS_PEOPLE_PLACES_AND_THINGS_9_20_26.md). Inside

   shows the result. No card pops up at the start, at wake, at night or on the road; the
   bookkeeping lives on the phone he opens.
