## 1. TWO SCALES OF ONE WORLD
- THE CLOSE GRID: the character is TINY, one cell, and moves ONE CELL PER STEP ("that we had originally").
  Buildings are many cells; a house has walls, a door and an inside; a car is two cells; the street is
  cells. This is Rogue Fable 4's own grid, and the fight happens ON IT, where he stands (rule 24, one
  mode; rule 33d, RF4 first).
- THE MAP: zoom out and the same world is the Battle Brothers map (rule 33): the party is a marker,
  tap to travel, the clock runs, places are destinations. Arriving zooms back in onto the close grid of
  that place.
- ONE GAME: the squeeze is the door between the two scales; the same buttons; the character never
  grows with the zoom ("your character stays tiny even as you zoom out"). No mode.

## 2. THE GRID IS HONEST
"Even when I'm large I don't have to worry about bumping into a wall I can't get past." Every cell is
exactly one of FLOOR (walkable), WALL (blocks movement and sight), DOOR (passable, a threshold), COVER
(the mound: the one terrain effect, accuracy) or PROP (blocks movement, not sight). What is drawn on a
cell IS what the cell is: no picture is pasted over cells that disagree with it. A person can always
reach any floor cell that connects to his; a sealed yard is a wall with a door, or it is not on the grid.
THE MACHINE GATE: for every block, the drawing and the grid agree cell for cell; every floor region
connects to the street; a pressed direction into a wall never reads as a dead pad (the wall shows itself).

## 3. ONE HOUSE IS MANY CELLS
"One house doesn't equal one tile, it's all fucked up." THE STEP IS A HOUSE (Paolo 9/15) is DEAD as a
rule of the walked surface and the fight: a step is one cell, a house is about 4 by 4 cells, a person is
one cell. The 9/15 law's file stays for the record with a SUPERSEDED header. A COMBAT TILE IS A HOUSE
(9/4, the batteries addendum's second half) is DEAD the same way; batteries stay the money.

## 4. THE PERSON IS ONE SMALL SIZE (rule 21 re-read)
THE GROUND MAY ZOOM, THE PERSON MAY NOT stands as a rule about consistency: the person is one size at
every zoom of the close grid, and a marker on the map. The size is ONE CELL (default about 28 px tall
on a 32 px cell), not 112 px. The 112 px bodies, the runway clothes at that size, the clips and the
portraits are ASSETS: the HD source the small sprite is cut from, the face in the bubble, the VOTE
pages. Nothing drawn at 112 is thrown away; it stops being the walked sprite.

## 5. THE DEFAULTS (in VOTE as one item; his to knock down)

with a door and an inside. A car 2x1. A street 3 cells wide with a 1-cell sidewalk each side. Reach in
the fight in cells (pistol, rifle, scope: COMBAT re-derives the 9/22 numbers on the new grid). The mound
