## 1. ONE VOTE TAB, IN THE DEMO
There were eight judge pages across the alpha and the workshop, one per lane, each
built when it was "one or two things". That was fine then and is not now. From this
ruling there is ONE place he votes: a VOTE tab in the DEMO (the thing he plays), reached
from the gear so a stranger never trips over it. Everything that wants his thumb goes
there and nowhere else: a new song, a new face, a new haircut, a new outfit, a new tile,
a new animation, a REDONE animation, a new line of words. Kinds are mixed in one queue,
newest first, each with the same three controls: up, down, a comment. Sun-mode readable,
export as .txt, the comment box always at the bottom (the verdict workflow stands).

## 2. A VOTE CONSUMES THE ITEM
"After I vote on something it needs to stop presenting itself like I didn't just vote
on it." A voted item LEAVES the tab the moment he votes, and never comes back under the
same id. The record of votes is written to disk (records/, .txt, the same turn, as
always) AND to a registry the tab reads, so a rebuild cannot resurrect a judged thing.
What needs his attention is exactly what is in the tab; what is old is not there. THE
GATE: an item with a recorded verdict never renders in the VOTE tab; a redo is a NEW
item with a new id that points at the old one and says "REDONE: you killed this on
<date> because <his words>" so he is never judging blind.

## 3. APPROVED GOES WHERE IT BELONGS, KILLED GOES TO THE GRAVEYARD, REDONE COMES BACK
An approved item goes into the game and into its own tab (MUSIC, CHARACTER, ANIMATION,
CLOTHES...) the same round, by the lane that owns it; the lane reads the registry, it
does not ask him twice (NOTES ARE RULINGS). A killed item goes to the graveyard with a
post-mortem, except clips, which are a REDO (his 9/7 ruling) and come back to the VOTE
tab as a new item. The old per-lane judge pages stop being where anything new is
presented; they may stay as archives, they present nothing.

## 4. WHO BUILDS IT
UI owns the tab shell, the queue, the registry and the "voted things vanish" gate. Every
lane that had a judge page (ANIMATION, CHARACTER, SOUNDS, COOK/DIRECTION, WORDS, and the
new PORTRAIT chat) REGISTERS its candidates into the queue and READS verdicts back; no
lane draws its own vote page again. RUN cuts it into the demo under rule 14a. Tab: VOTE,
in the demo, behind the gear.

## 5. THE PORTRAIT CHAT (20 PORTRAIT)
"A lot of the faces that you've been making [are] dogshit, very blank, and we need a
way where I can speak that along." A new chat, number 20, PORTRAIT: the zoomed-in HD
face of every person, and nothing else. Its laws are the ones that already exist
(EVERYBODY HAS A FACE, ONE ID ONE WHOLE PERSON, THE PORTRAIT WEARS THE HAIRCUT THE BODY
IS WEARING, HE CAN BUILD HIS OWN FACE, EVERY FACE COMES WITH A THUMB, A HAIRCUT READS
FROM EVERY ANGLE) plus three rulings from this message:
- THE PORTRAIT MATCHES THE BODY: eye colour, hair colour and the HAIRSTYLE are the same
  person on the small overworld sprite and on the HD portrait, "damn near the same".
  The overworld sprite is the zoomed-out, small, readable body; the portrait is the
  zoomed-in HD version of the SAME person. (His phrase "Pokémon vibe" describes the size
  relationship, small sprite vs big face; it is NOT a new reference game and nobody
  studies it. NEVER ADD A REFERENCE GAME HE HAS NOT NAMED stands.)
- BLANK IS THE DEFECT. A face that reads as nobody is the thing he is calling dogshit.
  The chat's first round is SCHOOL: what makes a small painted face read as a person
  (the eyes, the asymmetry, the light, the expression at rest), measured against the
  faces we have, before any face is redrawn.
- TRY 3-D. "We can try to make it look 3-D too while we're at it... we cook 3-D even if
  we're only eating 2-D." The portrait may be built with real light and form (a lit,
  shaded, turned head) and delivered as 2-D pixels. It is an attempt; it goes to the
  VOTE tab like everything else.
- HE SPEAKS ALONG: his per-face comments in the VOTE tab are the direction channel; the
  PORTRAIT chat reads them every round and answers each one with a new candidate.
