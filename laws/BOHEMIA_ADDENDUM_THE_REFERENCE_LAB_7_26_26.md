# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM — THE REFERENCE LAB (Paolo 7/26/26, LOCKED — new lane "lab")

Paolo's words: "does making a decent session out of making emulations of
other games like Stardew Valley... help us understand our own code, what to
implement, what to feel for, and how you even code when given certain
archetype code... I want it to feel like less that we're walking in this
dark cave together — understanding how other games have done it directly in
our session could be a good thing."

THE LAW: a standing lane, first word "lab", the fleet's prototype shop.
1. ONE SESSION = ONE SYSTEM = ONE NAMED GAME. The lab builds a minimal
   WORKING emulation of a specific system from a specific game (researched
   online first), in our stack (plain HTML/canvas), with placeholder art.
2. NEVER THE GAME: emulations never touch the alpha, any engine module, or
   Bohemia's art style. They are reference artifacts. Standalone pages under
   slices/lab/ (small, no giant embeds), clearly labeled REFERENCE.
3. EVERY EMULATION DELIVERS THREE THINGS: (a) a playable page Paolo can
   FEEL; (b) a FEEL LEDGER — every number that makes it feel right (speeds,
   accelerations, timings, sizes, camera behavior) recorded in records/lab/;
   (c) a PATTERN NOTE — plain-English + code-pattern write-up of how the
   master did it and what Bohemia should port.
4. THE VERDICT: Paolo plays the emulation and rules — "port this feel" sends
   the ledger/pattern into the relevant lane's backlog as a build item;
   silence or kill shelves it. The lab never ports anything itself.
5. WHY THIS WORKS (on record): AI executes memorized archetypes at expert
   level and invents poorly without references. The lab converts memory into
   playable references, so unknowns stop being guessed and start being
   copied-then-translated. This is the standing cure for the dark cave.
