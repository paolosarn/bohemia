## THE LAW
1. NO WALLS, EVER. The whole valley is walkable from day one (already true
   in the engine). Nothing is level-gated or fenced. What gates you is:
   DANGER (whose territory it is, how dark it is — LIGHT=TERRITORY, nobody
   patrols the dark, ambient danger: all already ruled) and WORKABILITY
   (below).
2. THE LADDER — THE INFRASTRUCTURE CHAIN. Restoration spreads OUTWARD from
   where you are, through the real chain the GDD already grounds: water
   before people, power before workshops, fuel before vehicles, materials
   before rebuilding. A district you can WALK is not yet a district you can
   WORK: it becomes repairable/farmable/restorable when the chain
   physically reaches it. Progress looks like utilities spreading on the
   map, because that is what it is. Nothing fake-gates; the gate is
   logistics. (This is the curriculum: the player learns how civilization
   works by re-plumbing it.)
3. THE LOCKS — KEYSTONE SEATS. Extending the chain past each rung runs
   through a KEYSTONE: a named, real, already-canon building somebody
   holds (the Granary, the Arsenal, the substations, Intake 3, the reclaim
   plant, the fuel depots, ultimately the dam). Opening a keystone is the
   "boss," and it can be a NEGOTIATION, A DEBT, AN ALLIANCE, OR A WAR —
   the player's choice of method is the roguelite fork. The big late
   rungs need MULTIPLE factions aligned (the united-front ruling): at
   scale, the locks stop being buildings and start being relationships.
4. REPEATING BIOMES, THE 20% DISCIPLINE. District types repeat across the
   valley like Valheim's biomes; you need to secure ONE of each kind that
   matters to your run, never all of them. This is why the map can be huge,
   the act caps can be ceilings, and a run only touches ~20%.
5. VEHICLES ARE THE BOAT MOMENTS. The bike and later the car are the
   scale-reopeners (locked ladder, GDD v5): each one makes the same map
   feel newly reachable, exactly like Valheim's ships.
6. DANGER SCALES BY PROXIMITY AND POSSESSION, NOT PLAYER LEVEL. Who holds
   a district and how dark it is sets its threat — composing with the
   Amalgamation's own threat logic (power is loud and safe; the truth is
   quiet and deadly).

## ROUTING (the four lanes this changes — flows via boot files, no pasting)

  (danger from territory/light, workability from chain reach), keystone
  registry (ships empty), chain-reach model on the existing organs.

  sits on — the dossiers and the progression system are now one fabric.
- RUN: the game day + travel pacing consume the tier axis (what reads
  dangerous, what reads workable, on the walk).
- COMBAT: danger-by-territory feeds encounter pressure when the director
  plugs in.
