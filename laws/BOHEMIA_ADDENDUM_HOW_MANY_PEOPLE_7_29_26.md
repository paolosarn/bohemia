TWO THINGS ARE LOCKED HERE AND THEY ARE SEPARATE.

**(a) THE CANON POPULATION IS THE CARRYING CAPACITY.** Not a die-off
percentage, not a survival-rate guess. The number of people in the valley is
the number the valley can FEED, with the survivors already doing their best.
This kills the 3%-versus-30% argument outright: neither was ever the right
question. The dirt decides, not the disaster.

**(b) DENSITY IS A FEELING, NOT A HEADCOUNT.** Every living person does not
get an NPC. The target is that the city FEELS equivalent to that population.
This is the ABSTRACTION LAW applied to people, and it is now explicit: the
simulated body count is chosen to produce the right FEELING of busyness, and
its only obligation to the canon number is to be consistent with it.

**THE NUMBER: 65,000 in the valley.** Band 45,000 (bad year) to 80,000 (good
year with imports held). Derivation in
records/BOHEMIA_FOOD_CEILING_RESEARCH_7_29_26.md; it lands inside the existing
50,000-80,000 canon band from BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_GRASS_TO_FOOD_MATH_7_5_26.md,
which the new research confirmed rather than moved.

**IN THE WALKABLE WORLD THAT IS ~300 LIVING BODIES.** The walkable valley
holds ~10,600 people of pre-collapse capacity against the real valley's ~2.3
million, so it is ~1/217th of Las Vegas. 65,000 is 2.83% of pre-collapse;
2.83% of 10,600 is 300. That is the whole living population of everything you
can walk to.

**`OCCUPIED_RATE=0.30` IS DEAD.** 300 people is ~115 households against ~4,070
homes: a 2.8% valley average, not 30%. But it is NEVER applied as a flat rate

## RULING 2 — ALL THREE DISTRIBUTIONS AT ONCE

> "both. why not both. some clusters. some no mans lands. some random spread."

LOCKED. Population is not a rate, it is a MAP. The 300 bodies split three
ways, and the empty part is as authored as the full part:

| zone | share of the 300 | share of the 177 neighborhoods | what it reads as |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CLUSTERS** | ~60% (180) | ~8% (14) | 12-13 people in one neighborhood. A real settlement. You hear it before you see it. |
| **SPREAD** | ~25% (75) | ~42% (75) | one household per neighborhood. You meet somebody every few minutes. |
| **LONERS** | ~15% (45) | ~25% (44) | one person, alone, dug in. A story, not a crowd. |
| **NO MAN'S LAND** | 0% | ~25% (44) | nobody. Not thinned out — EMPTY. |

A quarter of everywhere you can walk has nobody in it at all, on purpose, and
that quarter is what makes the clusters land. Emptiness is content.

CLUSTERS ARE NOT RANDOM. They sit on the three things people actually need:
the 12% lit circuits (CLUSTERED POWER), the converted farm ground (the golf
courses and big parks), and the water. A cluster that is not on one of those
is a bug.

## RULING 3 — RESEARCH ORDERED AND DELIVERED

> "do online research to answer this questions. how much more food can bohemia
>  make and shit and can 'import'"

Full record: **records/BOHEMIA_FOOD_CEILING_RESEARCH_7_29_26.md**. The four
findings that change canon:

1. **YARDS AND HYDROPONICS DO NOT ADD CALORIES.** The land is there — ~571,000
   detached houses in Clark County, roughly 39,000 acres of convertible yard
   against the 10,000 acres of turf. But the valley is caliche and a yard has
   no soil, and soil is BUILT, at about 20 acres a year from the city's own
   biosolids. Yards and hydroponics give greens, vitamins and morale. The
   fairways give calories. **A yard keeps you from getting scurvy; a golf
   course keeps you alive.**
2. **WATER IS NOT A VOLUME PROBLEM, IT IS AN ELECTRICITY PROBLEM.** Measured:
   Southern Nevada burned 853.8 million kWh to move 439,187 acre-feet — 1,944
   kWh per acre-foot, because Vegas water is LIFTED out of Lake Mead. Farming
   the 10,000 acres plus drinking water for 65,000 people costs about **9
   megawatts, continuously, forever.**
3. **HOOVER DAM IS NOT THE CONSTRAINT — THE GRID IS.** Even in its crippled
   low-lake state the dam makes 382 MW. Nine megawatts is 2.4% of that. So the
   12% CLUSTERED POWER law now has a physical reason and it is a better one
   than "not enough power": generation is trivially available and
   DISTRIBUTION is what died. A grid is a synchronised machine that needs
   operators, protection and load balance. The 12% are the places that
   re-energised a substation and can hold it. That is exactly why the network
   is "eerily perfect" inside its boundary and dead one street over.
4. **IMPORTS ARE REAL, SMALL, AND SEASONAL.** The one place near Vegas that
   can farm with NO electricity is the Moapa Valley: the Muddy River is
   spring-fed and gravity-flows to the fields, ~60 miles out. Add Pahrump and
   Amargosa (groundwater, so they need pumps of their own) and the Virgin
   River valley. Realistic contribution: 5,000-10,000 people's worth of
   calories in a good year, ZERO in a bad one, and only if the road is held.
   This is what the CARAVAN / CARAVANSERAI / GUARANTOR triad is for.

## RULING 4 — CUBA IS THE FLOOR, NOT THE MATCH

> "act 1 would be worse than cuba sure"

LOCKED. Cuba's Special Period is the NAMED REFERENCE CASE and act 1 is
DELIBERATELY WORSE THAN IT. The reference is a floor to measure against, not a
target to match. What Cuba kept that Vegas does not have:

- **A STATE.** Cuba kept a government, a ration book, and a functioning public
  health service through the whole thing. Vegas has factions.
- **THE REST OF THE WORLD.** Cuba lost its patron, not the planet; it still
  traded. Our collapse is global — the dollar was the settlement layer.
- **RAIN AND SOIL.** Havana's organopónicos grew in a place where things grow.
  Vegas is caliche and 4 inches of rain a year.
- **GRAVITY WATER.** Cuban agriculture does not have to lift its water 1,000
  feet with electricity that no longer exists.

Cuba lost 90% of its oil and its adults each lost 5-12 pounds, and almost
nobody died. **Vegas lost the same things plus the state, the world, the rain
and the pumps.** That is the calibration: when writing act 1, Cuba is how bad
it gets when the fundamentals still work. Ours is below that line.
