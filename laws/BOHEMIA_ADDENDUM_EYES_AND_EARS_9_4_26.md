## THE RULING
- **LANE 17, EYES AND EARS.** It is his double. It looks and listens BEFORE
  he does, on every visual and audio thing any lane ships, and tells him
  in two words whether it passes.
- **THE SEAM, so it does not collide with DIRECTION:** DIRECTION decides
  what the game should LOOK like (the style card, the runway). EYES AND
  EARS checks that what shipped is CORRECT and GOOD: nothing glitches
  (floating windows, wall gaps, sprites clipping, text overflowing, a
  frame popping, a sound clipping), it matches its reference (the 9/4
  compare law), and it is not worse than the best work of its kind. It
  never decides taste. It catches what is broken and what is weak.
- **IT LEARNS THE JOB FIRST.** Weeks if it takes weeks: how the best pixel
  art is judged, how game audio is judged, how studios catch glitches by
  machine (screenshot diffs against golden images, audio loudness and
  clipping checks), what makes a walk cycle read wrong. Its VAMILY section
  starts in RESEARCH mode with that queue and turns to a STANDING DUTY
  when the checklist exists.
- **ON THE REAL SURFACE, BY MACHINE WHERE IT CAN.** Screenshots of every
  tab at phone size on every ship, diffed; every approved sound rendered
  and measured; every clip played and watched. VERIFY ON THE REAL SURFACE
  (7/18) is this lane's whole religion.
- **THE ONE EXCEPTION TO "ONLY THE COORDINATOR ADDS JOBS":** when EYES AND
  EARS finds a defect on something a lane marked SHIPPED, it may write ONE
  line into that lane's VAMILY section, marked `[eyes: two words]`, naming
  the defect and the record that shows it. A bounce-back is a job by law.
  Nothing else in the board is its to touch.
- **WHAT HE GETS:** a verdict he can trust or spot-check, in two words, on
  every shipped visual and sound, without opening anything. On a day he
  has no time, the game still got looked at and listened to.

## AND HIS OTHER QUESTION: WHY NOT A FULL SWEEP EVERY ROUND
He is right that it can be. Every VAMILY round in this seat now reads
EVERY lane's newest commits and handoff block (cheap, and it was already
the rule), and sends a reader into any lane that shipped or moved since
the last round. The readers are not sent into a lane where nothing moved,
because a reading of an unchanged lane finds the same things it found
last time and puts them on the board twice. So every round is a full
sweep, sized to what changed. The only honest limit stands: a job that is
not grounded in something checked does not go on the board.
