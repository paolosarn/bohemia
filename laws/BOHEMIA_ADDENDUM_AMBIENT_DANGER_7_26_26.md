# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM — AMBIENT DANGER: COMBAT CAN HAPPEN ANY TIME (Paolo 7/26/26, LOCKED)

Paolo's words: "as you're walking the overworld... what happens when a crazy
psyched-out homeless person wants to attack you, or a stray dog wants to
attack you, or a drone piloted by a faction that has it out for you tries to
attack you — combat can happen at any time... you're being very black or
white with it."

LOCKED:
1. THE OVERWORLD CARRIES AMBIENT HOSTILES. While simply walking, combat can
   begin at any time from the world itself: crazed individuals, feral
   animals (stray dogs), faction drones sent by factions that have it out
   for you, and whatever else fits the dead world. This is the SPINE of how
   combat starts; social/territorial hostility layers on top of it, and the
   earlier all-or-nothing framings are dead (see the killed hostility-models
   post-mortem).
2. Ambient encounters route into the same encounter handoff the run uses.
3. PLAYER-INITIATED TOO (Paolo, same day): "you could start combat with
   peaceful people and just start murdering everyone — that's kind of the
   whole point." Attacking any peaceful NPC at will is a first-class combat
   entry, same encounter handoff.
4. WHAT spawns, WHERE, and HOW OFTEN are Paolo's dials [PENDING]: the
   mechanism (ambient encounter triggers on the walk surface) is buildable;
   the bestiary/threat list and frequency are his. Enemy-variety research
   commissioned 7/26 (Paolo: "I'm concerned this game could be very boring
   if not done right") — returns a thumbable act-1 starter roster.
