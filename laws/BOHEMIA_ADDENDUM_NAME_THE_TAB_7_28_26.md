# BOHEMIA — ADDENDUM: NAME THE TAB (Paolo 7/28/26, LOCKED)

> **"I need you to always tell me what tab I can find this shit in"**

## THE LAW

Every reply that mentions ANYTHING he can look at names **the tab it is in**, in
plain words, every time. Not the file. Not the path. Not "the judge page." The
tab — RUN, CHARACTER, CLOTHES, ANIMATION, RIG, COMBAT, MUSIC, CITY, MAP, SLICE,
LIFE — and where inside it if the tab is busy.

If a thing is **not in a tab**, the reply says so in those exact words:
**NOT IN A TAB YET.** That is not a caveat to bury, it is the most important thing on the line,
because a thing he cannot reach does not exist to him.

## WHY IT LOCKED

Because I spent a whole night shipping and never once told him where to tap.

The judge page went into the LIFE tab and I described it as "the top card, red
border" without ever saying the word LIFE in the same breath. The 42 re-cooked
tiles are in a bank file and are **in no tab at all** — I said they were "shipped"
and "live," which was true about the repository and useless about his thumb. He
had to ask "where can i see this?" once already tonight, and I answered with a
file attachment and a link instead of a tab name.

The ONE-LINK LAW gets him to the door. This law tells him which room.

## THE FORM

Say it like a person telling someone where something is:

- "It's in the **LIFE tab**, first card."
- "It's in the **CITY tab** — tap DROP IN, then walk out the front door."
- "**Not in a tab yet.** It's built and proven but nothing in the game shows it."

Never:

- "It's on the judge page" (which page? reachable how?)
- "It's at records/target/FOO.png" (he never opens files)
- "It's live" (where?)
- "It's in the build" (WHICH TAB)

- A verdict surface that is not reachable from a tab is **not a verdict surface**.

## THE GATE

`gates/name_the_tab_gate.py`, registered in the suite.

A gate cannot read my replies — replies are not files — and pretending otherwise
would be the fake-verification the doctrine already bans. So it holds the two
things that ARE files and that make forgetting harder:

1. **This law exists, is indexed, and still carries his words verbatim.**
2. **CLAUDE.md and the autonomy doctrine both carry the clause**, so every future
   session reads it in the first two files it opens.
3. **Every judging surface in `slices/` is reachable from the LIFE hub**, which is
   the tab that routes to things needing his eyes. A judge page nobody can tap
   from a tab is exactly the failure this law is about, so it fails the build
   rather than sitting there being technically shipped.

Clause 3 is the one with teeth: it is the machine half of "name the tab," because
you cannot name a tab for a thing that is not in one.
