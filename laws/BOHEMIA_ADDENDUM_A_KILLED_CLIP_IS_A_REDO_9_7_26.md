# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM -- A KILLED CLIP IS A REDO, AND THREE THINGS EVERY CLIP GETS WRONG
# (Paolo 9/7/26, LOCKED, with his full clip verdict: records/BOHEMIA_CLIP_VERDICTS_9_7_26.txt)

## 1. FOR ANIMATION CLIPS, THUMBS DOWN MEANS DO IT BETTER, NOT DELETE
"If I killed it I don't want it gone. I just think it could be done better. Make a
new one." So for CLIPS, and only clips, a thumbs down is a REDO: the verb stays on
the list, the motion is remade, and the remade clip goes back in front of him. The
export's own words "delete from sources, never re-add" are OVERRIDDEN by him for
this list. GRAVEYARD IS FINAL still governs garments, haircuts and shapes; a dead
SHAPE stays dead. A clip is a motion on a body, and he wants the motion fixed.
47 clips are redo (18 candidates, 29 originals). 56 are kept (23 new, 33 old). 2 are
unjudged (pickup, laugh) and get judged with the redo batch.

## 2. THE THREE DEFECTS HE NAMED, WHICH ARE THE RIG'S, NOT ONE CLIP'S
- **ELBOWS.** "You kind of suck at bending elbows at all, and if you do, sometimes
  they're going the wrong way." An elbow bends ONE way. A forearm never passes
  through the line of the upper arm. This is the ANATOMY LAW (locked) failing in
  motion, and it needs a limit in the rig, not a fix per clip: a joint that cannot
  be driven past zero the wrong way, in every facing.
- **LONG COATS.** "Glitching and popping out of place... tie it more to the legs, it
  feels like it's freestyling." The coat skirt follows the legs: it is attached to
  the hip and the thigh it covers, it moves with the stride, it never pops to a
  place the legs are not. This will be the nature of every long jacket in the
  runway wardrobe, so it is a wardrobe-wide rig rule, not a trenchcoat patch.
- **FACINGS.** "When it's facing north-east the hand was behind the head even though
  it's supposed to be in front." Draw order per facing is wrong on some of the eight
  directions: the near hand must draw in front of the head and body when the pose
  puts it in front. Every clip gets checked in all eight facings for what is in
  front of what, and the frame he sent (records/target/PAOLO_THE_COAT_AND_THE_
  ELBOWS_9_7_26.jpg) shows NE and SW with the coat and arm reading wrong.

## 3. THE ORDER OF WORK
Fix the rig first (elbow limit, coat-follows-legs, per-facing draw order), THEN redo
the 47 against the fixed rig, THEN bake the 23 approved verbatim. Redoing 47 clips on
a rig that still bends elbows backwards is 47 clips he thumbs down again.
