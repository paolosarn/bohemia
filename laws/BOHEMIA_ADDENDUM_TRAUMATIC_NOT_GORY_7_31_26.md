## CLAUSE 1 — TRAUMATIC AND GORY ARE TWO DIFFERENT DIALS

**GORY** is a texture: how wet it looks. **TRAUMATIC** is a structure: what it
costs and what it makes you do afterwards. Most games only have the first dial and
turn it up when they want the second.

Bohemia turns up the SECOND. Gore is permitted but it is never the mechanism, never
the headline, and never the reason a violent moment lands. **A violent thing that
would stop being upsetting if you drew it clean was never traumatic to begin with.**

## CLAUSE 2 — A HURT BODY IS A CLOCK, NOT A CORPSE

The finding from Crisis Response, and the reason it works: *"once someone is hurt
the player will be working against the clock to save them."* A shot does not
resolve when the body falls — **it opens a task.**

Bohemia already ruled the machinery for this and had no reason for it yet:
- the bleed trigger (`records/BOHEMIA_BLEED_TRIGGER_ANSWER_7_31_26.md`) — sharp or
  shot, severity from what got past your clothes, most hits do not bleed
- the mobile camp clause 8 (`laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_THE_MOBILE_CAMP_7_27_26.md`) —
  the camp is the medical station, a COMPANION pulls the bullet out, gauze, bandage
**The aftermath IS the trauma.** That is now the stated reason those exist.

## CLAUSE 3 — IT COSTS THE PLAYER, NOT THE CORPSE

If a killing takes nothing from you — time, standing, a companion's opinion, a
bandage you now do not have — it is spectacle, however it is drawn. The cost is
what makes it stick.

This is why it is not in tension with the north star
(`laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_WHAT_COMBAT_IS_FOR_7_27_26.md`): "deal the most damage and
take the least" is still the optimisation. This clause governs what the *aftermath*
of winning feels like, not whether winning is the goal.

## CLAUSE 4 — THE BODY IS A LEGIBLE SYSTEM YOU WATCH FAIL

Crisis Response simulates pulse, breathing and blood oxygen. A number you
understand going the wrong way in something that was a person a second ago is worse
than any amount of red, because **gore is a texture and a falling pulse is a story
with an ending you can see coming.**

Whether Bohemia shows a body's state as numbers, sound, or something else is
**[PENDING Paolo]**. That it should be LEGIBLE is the ruled part.

## CLAUSE 5 — THE CHEAPEST TOOLS ARE THE STRONGEST, AND THEY ARE NOT VISUAL

He named two, and neither is art: **people screaming, and people begging.** A
scream that stops mid-breath. Someone asking you not to, before you decide. Both
cost nothing to draw and both do more than a gore overlay.
