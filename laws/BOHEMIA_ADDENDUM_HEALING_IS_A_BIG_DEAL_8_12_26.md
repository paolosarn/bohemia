# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM — HEALING IS A BIG DEAL (Paolo 8/12/26, LOCKED — spoken
# watching his sister recover from surgery: "I'm watching my sister take
# pain pills and it's crazy bro, it's a serious thing getting injured. and
# in a post economic crash world too.")

Realism-first at its purest: this ruling came from real life, and the GDD
already agreed (v2 §15, LOCKED since June: "Getting shot is serious. It is
not fixed with a bandage in five seconds... days of recovery... field
medicine stabilizes, it does not heal"). Today the system got its soul.

## 1. HEALING IS LONG-TERM (LOCKED)
A serious injury means CONVALESCENCE: hanging out at the spot (home, camp,
a clinic) for something like A WEEK of game time at the bare minimum —
longer for severe. Healing is a real cost paid in the game's most honest
currency: time. "It's a serious thing getting injured."

## 2. THE HEALING MONTAGE (LOCKED — his image: "instead of rewinding tape
## it's a forward tape and you get to view it")
Recovery plays as a FAST-FORWARD CUTSCENE YOU WATCH — the anti-Pokemon-
Center: instead of a free instant heal, you SEE a week (or months, by
severity) pass over your character. The cost is made visible and felt.
MECHANISM ALREADY EXISTS: the cutscene machine (real rooms, derived seats)
+ the resolver's declared time-spend moments + sleep-save. This is
assembly, not invention.

## 3. LEAVE EARLY = THE CALM, DEEP DEBUFF (LOCKED, his Valheim grounding)
Leaving before fully healed is always LEGAL — and it costs: lower max HP
and a quiet, persistent drag (stamina regeneration reduced and kin). His
calibration, verbatim intent: like being WET in Valheim — "even if it's
just a little different... you cannot play in the most advanced areas
where you really need all that stamina." NEVER crazy, never a wall;
a calm, deep disadvantage that decides the hardest content. Numbers are
tunable by his playtest feel, not spec.

## 4. PAIN PILLS — MAYBE THE MOST VALUABLE THING (LOCKED direction)
"Honestly maybe the most valuable thing is pain pills really." The premium
good of the valley (a GOOD, not a currency — the three currencies stand).
- TWO TIERS, MAYBE THREE MAX — simplify, his word. No pharmacology tree.
- Pills MANAGE the wounded state (function while hurt, soften the debuff)
  — they never accelerate true healing. Only time heals.
- PROVENANCE IS QUESTIONABLE AND THE DESCRIPTIONS GET TO BE FUNNY (his
  word: "it's questionable where it's from... maybe they'll be in the
  description, it'll be funny") — item descriptions ship as draft
  attempts under the dialogue craft card (specificity: never "pills," 
  always the named sketchy thing).
- REALISM CHECK, CONFIRMING: sealed tablets genuinely keep potency for
  years-to-decades (unlike insulin's dead cold chain and the antibiotics
  runout already in canon) — opioid pills surviving as the valley's most
  precious good is chemically honest. The medicine ledger holds.

## 5. THE CARTEL'S CROWN (LOCKED direction)
"This is the actual number one awesome thing that the cartel does." The
Cartel's true economic role: THE PAIN PILL SUPPLY. The reason everyone —
including people who hate them — eventually deals with them. Composes
with: the Cartel's international supply chains (GDD v2), the permanent
Remnants war, the caravan/Mob route economy, and the faction dossiers
(the PEOPLE lane's Cartel dossier now has its spine: they sell relief).
The life lesson underneath, never preached: in collapse, whoever controls
relief from pain controls people.
