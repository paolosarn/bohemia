## WHAT THIS OVERRIDES, AND EXACTLY HOW FAR

GRAVEYARD IS FINAL says dead things stay dead, no remakes, fresh cooks answer dead
slots. That law is not repealed. It is **scoped**, by him, with one condition:

> **A kill may be reopened ONLY when the MECHANISM that caused the defect has since
> changed, and the kill reason names that defect.**

The reason this is not a loophole: his verdicts killed two different kinds of thing,
and they were never the same kind.

- **A SHAPE HE DID NOT WANT.** "was so bad not an Afro at all", "dog shit", "looks
  like a skinny beanie". No mechanism fix reaches these. They stay dead forever, and
  a future answer to that slot is a FRESH COOK under a new name, exactly as the
  original law says.
- **A GOOD SHAPE THE MACHINE RENDERED BADLY.** "I see the vision it just needs to be
  executed better", "there's part of the head that's not even with hair", "there's no
  hair in between the top and then the ponytail". These were never verdicts on the
  silhouette. They were verdicts on a bug — bare scalp from behind, a texture that
  only ran on half the drawing paths, a strip centred one pixel right because
  Math.round breaks .5 upward. **Those bugs are now fixed for every style at once.**

Killing a shape because the renderer was broken and then refusing to look again
after fixing the renderer is not discipline, it is waste.

## THE TEST, so this is checkable and not a matter of taste

A kill may be reopened only if ALL THREE hold:

1. **HIS KILL REASON NAMES A DEFECT**, not a preference. It must be quoted.
2. **THE DEFECT HAS A DATED MECHANISM FIX** that landed after the kill, and the fix
   is general — it changed the machine, not that one style.
3. **HE SEES IT AGAIN.** A reopened kill comes back as a CANDIDATE for a fresh
   verdict. It is never silently restored to canon. His thumb is the only thing that
   makes it live, and a second kill is permanent.

If a kill reason is a matter of taste, it stays dead. If it is a bug report, it gets
one more look on a fixed machine.

## WHY THIS GENERALISES BEYOND HAIR

The same distinction applies everywhere he judges: clothes, animations, sounds,
districts. Every rejection is either "I don't want this" or "this is broken", and
only the second kind is a candidate for reopening after a machine fix. Recording
which of the two a verdict was, at the time he gives it, is the actual discipline —
and the graveyard registry now carries his exact words for precisely that reason.

## WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE

- A reopened style still needs his thumb. Nothing returns to canon on my say-so.
- A second kill is final, with no third look, whatever changes later.
- Post-mortems are never deleted. A reopened kill keeps its tombstone, annotated
  with the date and the mechanism fix that earned it a second look.
- MECHANISM-MINE / CONTENTS-PAOLO'S: I may fix the machine and re-present. I may
  never decide that a shape is now good.
