# BOHEMIA ADDENDUM — COMPANIONS: THE BROTHERHOOD RULING (Paolo 7/26/26, LOCKED)

Paolo's words: "followers can help in combat and we're gonna integrate that
properly... I'm not a big fan of having to micromanage your followers...
followers permanently dying just inspires people to save scum and I really
want to prevent that... they might help with inventory, they push the smart
cart, or if it's not smart yet they just push a cart... Fallout 4 did a great
thing, they provide additional dialogue whenever you're doing anything in the
world... I want followers to be a deeper feeling — how Final Fantasy 15
handles it, they're your gang... they did camping and you really felt like
these were your brothers. I would want that feeling."

LOCKED:
1. COMPANIONS FIGHT: crew members join combat, integrated properly.
2. NO MICROMANAGEMENT, NO PERMADEATH: companions never permanently die
   (permadeath breeds save-scumming — banned by design). They can go down,
   never be deleted.
3. UTILITY: companions help carry inventory and push the cart.
4. AMBIENT DIALOGUE: companions comment on the world as you do things —
   Fallout 4 is the named positive reference.
5. THE FEELING IS THE POINT: FF15 brotherhood is the north star — camping
   with your gang, "these are your brothers." The camp ritual (survival
   direction addendum, same day) and companion bonding are ONE scene: the
   camp is where the brotherhood lives.
6. OPEN SEAM [PENDING Paolo, coordinator will re-ask]: the relationship
   between feed-followers (the crowd, clout counts) and traveling companions
   (the crew) — how the crowd becomes the crew is unruled. No session invents it.
