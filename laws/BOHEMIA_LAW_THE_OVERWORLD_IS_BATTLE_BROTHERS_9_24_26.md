## 1. THE OVERWORLD IS THE BATTLE BROTHERS MAP
Crossing the valley is done the Battle Brothers way: a MAP with your party on it as a marker, tap
where you want to go, the party travels there while time passes, roads are faster than dirt and
ruins, other parties roam and can be seen coming, places (blocks, towns, camps, plants, pumps) are
destinations you enter. "It's just so much easier to travel the world that way, quicker."
- THE MAP IS THE CITY VIEW WE HAVE (default, in VOTE). One squeeze out from the street is already the
  zoomed-out valley with the cracked phone on it (RUN [way back], UI [phone city only]); that view
  gains the Battle Brothers travel mechanics. No new screen, no new mode (rule 24): the same buttons,
  the same UI, the squeeze is the door.
- THE STREET IS WHERE YOU ARRIVE (default, in VOTE). The walked street at one step per house does
  not die; it stops being how you cross the valley and becomes the PLACE you enter from the map,
  where the people are and where the fight happens on house tiles. Battle Brothers enters a town as
  a set of menus; we enter it as a street. He corrects in VOTE.
- THE FLIP LIVES HERE TOO: the cracked phone on the map carries the three descendants (rule 31, 32c).

## 2. EVENTS ON THE ROAD
"We can still have so many cool things like pop-up events and stuff." Battle Brothers events are the
model: travel is interrupted by a situation, a face and a few lines, two or three real choices, a
consequence that lands in the ledgers. THIS AMENDS RULE 32a FOR THE MAP ONLY: an event may stop the
party on the road. It still never fires in the first minute of play, it still comes from a mouth
with a portrait (rule 19), it is never a card floating over the street, and the choice is real (a
Battle Brothers event with one option is not an event). On the street, 32a stands whole: nothing
forced, walk past.

## 3. THE FIGHT: ROGUE FABLE 4 FIRST, BATTLE BROTHERS SECOND, ON HOUSE TILES
"Each tile is as big as a mini block, like a building." "Very inspired by Rogue Fable 4, because if
Rogue Fable 4 touched the combat of Battle Brothers more, it'd be more fun."
- The tile is a building (rule 16, BATTERIES ARE THE MONEY AND A COMBAT TILE IS A HOUSE). Unchanged.
- ROGUE FABLE 4 LEADS THE FEEL: on the beat, quick, the grid, verbs; BATTLE BROTHERS IS THE FLAVOUR
  (the 9/22 addendum's reach on the tiles, a turn is a beat, routine hits cost one, the dial for the
  important shot all stand; they are read as RF4 speed with BB weight).
- TERRAIN: ONE EFFECT IN THE WHOLE FIGHT. "No terrain stand-on effect, really, other than a small
  mound on the floor you can get an accuracy bonus from." High ground = accuracy bonus. Nothing else
  on a tile changes a number: no cover values, no slow ground, no elevation ladders. COMBAT removes
  any other tile effect that exists and the gate refuses a new one.
