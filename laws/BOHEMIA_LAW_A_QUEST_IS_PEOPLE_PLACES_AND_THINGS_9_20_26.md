## 1. THE LAW
A QUEST EXISTS ONLY IF IT HAS: a PERSON with a face who says it to you (the talking
portrait law, 8/27: everybody has a face and it talks), a PLACE you walk to, a THING you
pick up or do there, and a RESULT you can see in the world when you come back. A line of
text on a card is not a quest. A progress readout ("0 of 8 toward the city backing you")
is not a goal. A bullet list of what happened is not a day. If it is not people, places
and things, it is not in the game.

## 2. NOTHING POPS UP
The demo opens on the world (after the loading screen). No card pops up at the start, at
wake, at night, or on the road. What the game has to tell him is said BY A PERSON with a
portrait, in that person's voice, standing where the player can see them, or it waits on
the PHONE until he opens it himself. The night summary, the ledger, the rent, the
batteries, the deeds: those are the phone's, and the phone is a thing he opens. The wake
card, the night card and the road card as pop-ups are DEAD.

## 3. TEXT COMES FROM A MOUTH
Every sentence a player reads that is not a label on a machine comes from a named person
with a portrait on screen while they speak (EVERYBODY HAS A FACE AND IT TALKS; THE PORTRAIT
WEARS THE HAIRCUT THE BODY IS WEARING; ONE ID ONE WHOLE PERSON). No narrator. No system
voice. "The Meter Reader: nobody picked it up" is a sentence with no mouth; it dies.

## 4. WHAT THIS DOES TO THE ASKS
The asks system (9/6) was ruled as "somebody standing in front of you who wants
something". It shipped as a card. The mechanism stays; the SURFACE changes: an ask is a
person at your door or on your street, with a face, who says it; the terms he can argue
are said back and forth in that person's mouth; taking the job is a word to that person;
the place is on the map and the thing is on the ground there; the pay lands in his hand
from that person's hand. THE METER READER is the first: a named person, a portrait, a
place six blocks east, a thing to read, one battery from their hand.

## 5. THE PLAYABLE CUT GAINS A FOURTH THING
THE PLAYABLE CUT (9/20) listed loading, walking, the fight. It has a fourth: THE FIRST
PERSON. Inside the first minute, one named person with a portrait speaks the first ask to
him at his door, in the game's words, with a place to go and a thing to bring back, and
the world shows the result. Nothing else about quests ships until that one person is real
on the deployed cut. The other pop-ups die in the same round.

## 6. WHO
RUN [no pop ups]: the start, the wake, the night and the road cards stop popping up; the
night's bookkeeping moves to the phone he opens. QUESTS [a person asks]: the first ask is
a person with a face at his door. PEOPLE [face at the door]: the body and the portrait
wired to that person (the talking portrait machinery exists). WORDS Q26 [the first ask
spoken]: what that person says, and says back when he argues. PORTRAIT [faces first]:
that face first (the chat is still unclaimed; PEOPLE uses the portrait that exists until
it is). UI: the talking panel is an object in the game's face, not a card of bullets.
DIRECTION judges the person at the door as a picture beside the street.

## 7. THE STANDING RULE FOR EVERY LANE
Before any lane ships a sentence to the screen, it answers: WHO SAYS THIS, WITH WHAT FACE,
STANDING WHERE. If there is no answer, the sentence goes to the phone or does not ship.
The gate: no element that draws player-facing prose outside the phone without a speaker
id and a portrait bound to it.
