dire_wolf.glb (ANIMATION [the beast in eight], 10/17/26): a derivative of the Khronos glTF sample Fox
(reference/models/khronos/Fox.glb). The fox model is CC0 by PixelMannen; its rig and the Survey, Walk and Run clips are
CC BY 4.0 by tomkranis (glTF conversion by AsoboStudio and scurest, CC BY 4.0). Attribution owed and given here.
What is ours (tools/bohemia_animation_the_beast_in_eight_prep.py): the recoloured coat, the wider body and heavier skull,
and the Bite clip posed on the same bones. Rendered through DIRECTION's master rig (tools/bohemia_direction_master_rig.py).
A placeholder (ROUND THIRTEEN): the real dire wolf model is a later cook, one at a time, after the moodboard.

cave_hyena.glb (ANIMATION, 10/18, THE CAVE HYENA, the bestiary's number 3): derived from the Khronos Fox, the same
attribution as dire_wolf.glb above. What is ours (tools/bohemia_animation_the_hyena_prep.py): the sloped shape (front

shape, and the Bite clip (the wolf's keys).
