# RUNWAY REFERENCES (the two houses, at the level of SHAPE)

THE RULE (9/4, LOCKED): every garment and haircut in Bohemia is modelled on
high fashion, and the named houses are BALENCIAGA and RICK OWENS. NO THIRD
HOUSE until he names it. This library holds their silhouettes as SHAPES
(shoulder, drape, hem, boot) — what a body wearing them IS, structurally.
Turning these shapes into pixel rules at 56 and 112 px is THE-STYLE-CARD's
job, not this file's. Nothing here enters the design vocabulary (8/28):
these names are rulers, and the register they describe is already Bohemia's
own — monochrome and dust, drape and asymmetry, destroyed on purpose.

## THE SHOULDER

### RNWY-01  The exaggerated square shoulder (Balenciaga)
- WHERE: https://www.anothermag.com/fashion-beauty/8602/the-evolution-of-balenciagas-hero-silhouette
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the shoulder line is CUT SQUARE and wider than the hips, with the waist narrowed against it; the drama is the RATIO of shoulder to waist, not padding for its own sake.

### RNWY-02  The dropped, rounded cocoon shoulder (Balenciaga, the house code)
- WHERE: https://grazia.sg/fashion/balenciaga-fw24-haute-couture-demna/
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the cocoon hides the shoulder point entirely — one continuous arc from neck to elbow, pulled back off the nape; a body inside it reads as one curved MASS, not a torso with arms.

### RNWY-03  The absent shoulder (Rick Owens)
- WHERE: https://antidotestyle.com/blogs/news/rick-owens-draping-and-textures-atlantas-guide-to-sculptural-style
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the opposite pole — the shoulder is soft and narrow so the SILHOUETTE'S width lives at the hem or the boot instead; height, not breadth, is the statement.

## THE DRAPE

### RNWY-04  Bias-cut coil (Rick Owens)
- WHERE: https://antidotestyle.com/blogs/news/rick-owens-draping-and-textures-atlantas-guide-to-sculptural-style
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: fabric cut on the diagonal WRAPS the body instead of hanging off it; the fold lines run diagonally around the figure, never straight down.

### RNWY-05  The elongated column (Rick Owens)
- WHERE: https://anastasiaboutique.com/blogs/the-shop/mastering-the-dark-a-guide-to-styling-rick-owens
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: elongated, stacked, skewed — the figure reads from a block away because every layer's hem sits LOWER than the layer over it, stretching the body into one vertical.

### RNWY-06  The maximal layer stack (Balenciaga FW18 outerwear)
- WHERE: https://www.ssense.com/en-it/editorial/fashion/balenciaga-brand-history-demna
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: layers are worn AS VOLUME — shirt, hoodie, trench, parka stacked until the body is an architecture; each layer must stay individually readable at the collar or the stack becomes a blob.

## THE HEM

### RNWY-07  The asymmetric hem (Rick Owens)
- WHERE: https://www.1stdibs.com/fashion/clothing/evening-dresses/rick-owens-fw19-asymmetrical-silk-blend-bias-cut-dress/id-v_25164572
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a hem is a DIAGONAL EVENT, not a horizontal line — one side rides the thigh while the other falls past the knee, and that single angle carries the whole garment's asymmetry.

### RNWY-08  The elongated tee hem (Rick Owens jersey)
- WHERE: https://aumifour.com/blogs/faq-your-questions-answered/rick-owens-faq-2026
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: jersey pieces are cut LONG so the under-layer's hem shows below every outer layer; the visible stagger of hems IS the layering, readable in silhouette alone.

### RNWY-09  The arched back fall (Balenciaga cocoon)
- WHERE: https://grazia.sg/fashion/balenciaga-fw24-haute-couture-demna/
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the cocoon's hem falls FORWARD — the back arcs over and down so the garment is longer behind than in front, the profile reading as a comma, not a rectangle.

## THE LEG AND THE BOOT

### RNWY-10  The pant-boot / continuous leg (Balenciaga)
- WHERE: https://www.anothermag.com/fashion-beauty/8602/the-evolution-of-balenciagas-hero-silhouette
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: trouser and boot fuse into ONE unbroken line to the floor; no ankle break, no cuff event — the leg is a single column and the silhouette's base is narrow.

### RNWY-11  The drop-crotch taper (Rick Owens)
- WHERE: https://aumifour.com/blogs/faq-your-questions-answered/rick-owens-faq-2026
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: volume pools LOW between the hips and the knee, then tapers clean to the ankle; the leg reads as a diamond, widest below the hip, never as two tubes.

### RNWY-12  The platform grill boot (Rick Owens)
- WHERE: https://rickowens.eu.com/
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the boot is the silhouette's PEDESTAL — a visibly thick, flat platform mass that widens the figure's base and makes the column above it read taller; heavy boots are a proportion tool, not an accessory.

## THE WHOLE FIGURE

### RNWY-13  The two poles, stated once
- WHERE: this file's entries RNWY-01..12, read together
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the register has exactly two whole-figure poles — WIDE AT THE TOP (square or cocoon shoulder, narrow leg, Balenciaga) and TALL AND STACKED (soft shoulder, staggered hems, heavy base, Rick Owens); every crowd body should commit to one pole, because a figure that mixes both reads as neither.

## THE THIRD HOUSE (added 9/22 by CHARACTER, [runway redo])
Paolo named THREE houses on 9/21: "analog horror direction meets Rick Owens meets
Balenciaga meets Bottega Veneta, bro" (rule 30). This file held twelve entries across
TWO of them and nothing at all for the third, so half of a locked instruction had no
ruler behind it and every lane dressing to rule 30 was guessing at one name in three.
Bottega's codes are not silhouette codes like the other two -- they are codes about
SURFACE AND ANNOUNCEMENT, which is why they sit in their own section and why they change
what a garment says rather than what shape it cuts.

### RNWY-14  Intrecciato, the woven lattice (Bottega Veneta)
- WHERE: https://www.1stdibs.com/blogs/the-study/bottega-veneta-intrecciato-50th-anniversary
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the house's signature is a WEAVE, strips of leather interlaced into a base so
  the pattern IS the structure and not a print on top of it. At sprite scale a weave
  cannot be drawn strip by strip, so what survives the shrink is a regular two-value
  lattice on one panel, at an angle, reading as texture at 100% and as a slightly darker
  block from across the street. It is the only ornament in this whole register that is
  earned, because it is structural.

### RNWY-15  No logo, nothing announced (Bottega Veneta)
- WHERE: https://www.selvane.co/blogs/knowledge/bottega-veneta-design-philosophy-decoded
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the house built itself on discreet, logo-free luxury -- the old line is that
  your own initials are enough. Nothing carries a mark, a badge or a name; the garment is
  identified by cut and material alone. THIS IS ALREADY OUR LAW IN ANOTHER HOUSE'S VOICE:
  COLOUR IS TERRITORY says a faction is read off colour, and STRUCTURE-NOT-COLOR says the
  shape must survive greyscale. A faction patch, a stencilled name or a painted sigil on a
  garment fails all three at once.

### RNWY-16  Trompe-l'oeil leather, the ordinary thing that is not (Bottega Veneta, Blazy)
- WHERE: https://chooserealleather.com/style/brands/bottega-venetas-illusion-leathers-have-to-be-seen-to-be-believed-from-leather-denim-to-lambskin-plaid-shirts/
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the FW22 opening look was a white tank top and straight-leg jeans made ENTIRELY
  of leather, tooled to look exactly like cotton; the SS23 checked shirt was printed twelve
  times over to reach flannel's depth. The garment is mundane at a glance and wrong on the
  second look.
  *** THIS IS THE ANALOG HORROR BIBLE'S RULE 1 WEARING CLOTHES. *** "The ordinary frame,
  one wrong thing... name the wrong thing in one sentence or the frame fails." A plain
  flannel shirt that is not cloth is exactly one wrong thing on an otherwise ordinary body.
  It is also the only one of the three houses that answers rule 20 directly rather than
  being decorated with it afterwards, which is why a re-cook under rule 30 should reach for
  this before it reaches for a bigger shoulder.
  AT SPRITE SCALE the tell is SHEEN, not pattern: leather holds a hard specular band where
  cloth diffuses, so a "denim" panel with a one-pixel hard highlight along the fold reads as
  the illusion. That is a value trick, which GARM-01 already says is how fabric works here.
