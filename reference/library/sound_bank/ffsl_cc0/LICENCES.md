# The Free Firearm Sound Library, windows (CC0 1.0)

By Still North Media (Ben Jaszczak and team), Kickstarter 2013-14. Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal. Mirror: https://github.com/buddingmonkey/FreeFirearmsSFXLibrary. Original: http://www.stillnorthmedia.com/firearm-sound-library.html. Listing: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library.

Every FLAC here is a window of one master track, cut, mono and resampled to 44.1 kHz by tools/bohemia_guns_heard.py --bank. CC0 asks for nothing; the credit is kept because the people who made it should be thanked.
