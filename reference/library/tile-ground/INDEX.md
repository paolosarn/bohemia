# TILE GROUND REFERENCES (the combat floor, one house per tile, from 45 degrees)

FOR: COOK's [combat ground] work under the 9/4 tile law - a combat tile is
a house (1.5 to 2 sprite widths), a house with a backyard spans 1x2, and
the fight happens on Vegas ground. THE SCALE IS REAL: Vegas single-family
lots run 3,300-6,100 sq ft and the median house is 2,000+ sq ft, so at one
house per tile a tile is roughly a 15 m square - the house nearly fills
its tile and THE YARD IS ITS OWN TILE, which is exactly what the 1x2
ruling says. Nothing here enters the design vocabulary (8/28).

### TG-01  The lot's real proportions (the tile law's own numbers)
- WHERE: https://www.reviewjournal.com/business/las-vegas-neighborhoods-are-gaining-more-elbow-room/ + https://files.lasvegasnevada.gov/planning/Title-19-Development-Standards.pdf
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a Vegas lot is barely bigger than its house - measured 3,300-6,100 sq ft lots under 2,000+ sq ft homes - so the HOUSE TILE is mostly roof with a thin apron, and a fat margin of ground around a house is a scale lie.

### TG-02  The house tile from 45 degrees
- WHERE: https://www.google.com/maps/@36.1146,-115.1728,120m (satellite, any block)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: from above-at-an-angle a house is ROOF PLANES FIRST (two or four pitched planes, one ridge), then the front face; at combat range the roof IS the house's ground read, and its ridge line gives the tile its orientation.

### TG-03  The yard tile
- WHERE: the same satellite anchor + the walked suburb (decorative gravel front yards, 8/8; TAN WALL 85/15 law)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a Vegas yard is GRAVEL OR HARDPAN inside a block wall - no lawn in act 1 - with one or two large objects (a dead tree well, a shed, a pool now empty); the wall runs the tile's full edge, so a yard tile's cover story is its WALL, not its middle.

### TG-04  The street tile
- WHERE: the walked arterials (double yellow, parkway mulch, SIDEWALK SANCTITY law)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: at house scale one street tile carries HALF the street - kerb, sidewalk band, parking lane, one travel lane - never the whole road; the kerb line is the tile's strongest edge and the fight's cleanest lane marker.

### TG-05  The lot tile (commercial ground)
- WHERE: https://www.udg.org.uk/publications/udlibrary/learning-las-vegas
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a commercial lot tile is STRIPED ASPHALT with seal-coat patches and stop bars - the most readable man-made ground there is; its stall stripes give a free tactical grid the fight can read without any overlay.

### TG-06  The rubble tile
- WHERE: banks/tileforms (the approved waste fill TF-ART-026 and riprap TF-ART-023 families)
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: our rubble already has approved grammar - earth base, worn-through patches, bleached flecks, off-shape clusters, never dot stipple; a combat rubble tile REUSES that grammar denser, and its walkability must read from silhouette alone (the clarity rule, CGRD-01).

### TG-07  Cover that reads (the 45-degree test)
- WHERE: reference/library/combat-ground/INDEX.md (CGRD-01, CGRD-02) + the 45 DEGREE ART LAW
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: at one house per tile, cover is HOUSE-PART SIZED - a block wall segment, a dead car, a dumpster, a porch pier - and each must break the ground's silhouette at the tile edge where it blocks; cover that only reads by its colour is not cover.
