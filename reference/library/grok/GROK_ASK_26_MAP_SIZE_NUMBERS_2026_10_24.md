# ASK 26. THE MAP'S SIZE IN NUMBERS

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Our map has to be as big as Battle Brothers. A larger grid or something.

## MY PROPOSAL
The Battle Brothers wiki page on the Global Map states that a typical setup involves a total of 17 settlements. It lists 2 City Halls, 3 Town Halls, 6 Village Halls, 2 Strongholds, 2 Castles, and 2 Fortified Manors. Settlement distribution among the three Noble Houses can range from roughly equal to lopsided. The wiki does not say the exact number of hex tiles across or down the world map at each map size setting. It does not say how many in-game days a company at walking speed takes to cross the map corner to corner or edge to edge. It does not say the camera's near and far zoom stops or how many tiles fit on a 1080p screen at each stop. It does not say the party marker's size in pixels against the map.

Modders and players on Reddit have measured generated maps. One post states that map size values range from just above 10,000 to just above 18,500. Diameter ranges from the 140s to the 200s. An older Reddit comment states that before the Beasts and Exploration DLC the map was 140 by 140 hexes and after it became 140 by 170. The wiki does not confirm these numbers. The developer blog describes the world map as covering the scope of a single continent with geography and climate similar to a middle-European country. It is procedurally generated at the start of every new game. Terrain types have different movement speeds listed on the wiki. Plains, farmland, and steppe move at 1. Mountains move at 0.25. Roads are faster. Fog of war covers unexplored areas except around the party and major settlements.

For Bohemia the map must match or exceed these scales so that travel takes real time and settlements are not on top of each other. The Las Vegas valley becomes the dense middle of a larger region that includes Henderson, the Strip, I-15, and US-95. The party marker stays readable at every zoom. Settlements, roads, and locations keep their true world size at every zoom stop. The camera never shows black. The far stop shows the whole valley the way Battle Brothers shows its continent.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Global Map page; Reddit r/BattleBrothers posts on map sizes and DLC expansion; Battle Brothers developer blog posts on worldmap generation and rework.
