# ASK 105: Keyboard Shortcuts, Dropped Contracts, Roster Scaling

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Find out all the keyboard button clicks that were on Battle Brothers and do the exact same for Bohemia. How do we turn a quest off. Battle Brothers matches you with opponents by the amount of people on your roster.

## MY PROPOSAL:

Battle Brothers uses a fixed set of keyboard shortcuts on the global map, in tactical combat, and in the inventory. These are documented on the Keyboard Shortcuts page of the wiki. Bohemia runs on iOS so the same actions map to taps, swipes, and button presses that match the functions exactly. Canceling or failing a contract costs renown and local relations as listed on the Factions and Relations page. Party strength for contract scaling is the sum of 10 + 2 times (level minus 1) for the 12 strongest brothers, with a specific multiplier formula from the Game Guide page.

### Keyboard Shortcuts from the Keyboard Shortcuts page

On the global map the keys are these. W A S D or the arrow keys move the camera. The mouse wheel up or Page Up or the plus key zooms in. The mouse wheel down or Page Down or the minus key zooms out. Shift or Return centers the map on the Battle Brothers unit and zooms in. Spacebar pauses or unpauses the game. F5 does a quick save. F9 does a quick load. Esc opens the game menus or exits menus or closes an open city or shop or inventory window. F toggles tracking footprints. T makes or breaks camp. R opens or closes the Factions and Relations window. C or I opens or closes the Inventory window. Alt plus F4 quits the game without saving for ironman mode. Ctrl plus click on a neutral party attacks neutral parties like mercenaries or caravans or peasants if you do not have an active contract.

On the tactical map the keys are these. W A S D or the arrow keys move the camera. Spacebar or End waits the turn which pushes the active character's turn to the end of the queue or ends the turn if wait is not possible. Enter or F ends the turn for the active character. R ends the round so all your men skip their turn until the next round. The number keys 1 through 9 activate or deactivate the current unit's respective skills. Alt shows or hides hitpoint bars and status effect icons. Shift centers the camera on the currently acting character. Mouse wheel up or Page Up zooms in. Mouse wheel down or Page Down zooms out. Plus raises the camera level. Minus lowers the camera level. C or I opens the currently acting character's inventory on your turn. B shows or hides highlights for blocked tiles. T shows or hides trees.

In the inventory the keys are these. Esc or C or I closes the inventory screen. Holding Alt and clicking an item repairs the item at a Weaponsmith or Armorer. Tab or D selects the next character. A selects the previous character. Drag and drop of a character icon into a formation slot sets the starting position. Drag and drop into a reserve slot keeps the character out of battles.

### Contract Abandonment and Failure from the Factions and Relations page

Canceling a contract costs 100 renown and 10 relation points with the settlement or house. Failing a contract costs 75 renown. A poorly completed contract costs 25 renown. Betrayal on a contract costs 200 renown and 100 relation points. Failing a civilian contract costs 20 relation points. Failing a noble contract costs 10 relation points. A poorly completed noble contract gains 2.5 relation points. A poorly completed civilian contract gains 5 relation points.

### Roster Strength Scaling from the Game Mechanics page and the Game Guide page

Roster strength or party strength is the sum of individual strengths for the 12 strongest brothers or 14 for the Peasant Militia origin. Each brother adds 10 plus 2 times (level minus 1). Gear does not change the score. If the party has fewer than 3 members each brother adds 10 regardless of level. Contract difficulty uses this roster strength along with the contract type base value from 40 to 200, the skull difficulty rating multiplier, and the combat difficulty setting. The final multiplier is 94 percent times (1 percent times roster strength) raised to the power of 0.89. The multiplier cannot be lower than 0.75 or higher than 5. It is then multiplied by the combat difficulty which is 85 percent for Beginner, 100 percent for Veteran, and 115 percent for Expert. Non-contract locations and most roaming parties scale mainly with days and distance to civilization. Roaming parties scale slightly with player strength as well. The wiki Game Mechanics page states that contract difficulty scales with the strength of your roster which equals a sum of individual strengths of 10 plus 2 times (lvl minus 1) of 12 strongest brothers.

The wiki does not say the exact keyboard bindings for every menu button beyond the listed shortcuts. The wiki does not say the precise cost in crowns or other resources for abandoning a contract beyond the renown and relation hits.
