# ASK 120: What I Found on My Own

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Read the whole master file. Write a page headed WHAT I FOUND ON MY OWN: what is missing from the design, what is weak, what contradicts what. Then research whatever you think we need that nobody asked for.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The design is strong on Battle Brothers translation of economy, roster, day loop, and combat basics. What is missing is a locked translation for the four late-game crises. The Late Game Crises wiki page states the first crisis is foretold around day 50 to 70 and starts around day 80 to 100. There is no renown requirement. A second crisis can start after at least 50 days of peace. Greenskin and undead crises can destroy settlements if permanent destruction is on. Bohemia has no equivalent crisis schedule, no party-strength check that gates them, and no rule for whether a destroyed settlement can be rebuilt under the mayor arc.

What is weak is the visual language for chip-body undead on the tactical map. Prior piles lock the analog-horror direction, but no file yet says how a body with a chip still in it looks when it is downed versus when it rises, or how long the body stays before the chip forces it up.

What contradicts is the map size. Paolo said the map has to be as big as Battle Brothers. ASK 118 shows the wiki does not print the exact hex count and player data lists diameters from the 140s to the 200s. Bohemia has not locked one size or three size options.

Nobody asked for the exact party-strength formula that gates the first crisis. Reddit and Steam threads list a base of 10 plus 2 per level past 1 for the top 12 or 14 men, with thresholds near 160 for the warning and 180 for the start. The wiki does not print those numbers. That formula is the underbelly number that decides when the world stops being a sandbox and starts being a crisis.

A LITTLE THING FROM the attached-locations system that turns a settlement into a unique market, from Dev Blog 56.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: First crisis window is roughly day 80 to 100. Permanent destruction is optional. Party strength is the real gate.
WHAT IS NOT: Exact party-strength thresholds on the wiki. Bohemia crisis skins.
WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: Which of the four crises gets the first Bohemia skin.
