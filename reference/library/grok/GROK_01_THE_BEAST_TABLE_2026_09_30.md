# ASK 1. THE BEAST TABLE, EXACT

Battle Brothers beasts named in the ask. Numbers are from the public wiki infobox and the skill pages it transcludes, pulled 2026-09-30. Where a skill line says "70% and 20%", the wiki prints two icons, not words. On that wiki the first percent is armor damage and the second is armor penetration. I label them that way. If a stat is missing from the infobox, I say so. I do not fill the hole.

Wiki root: https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Beasts

## The lines

1. Direwolf. HP 130. Head armor 30. Body armor 30. Melee skill 60. Melee defense 10. Ranged defense 10. Initiative 150. Action points 12. Fatigue 180. Resolve 50. Skills: Berserk, Executioner, Pathfinder, Direwolf Bite (4 AP, 6 fatigue, 30-50 damage, 70% armor damage, 20% armor penetration). Drops: Beasts and Exploration DLC, Unusually Large Wolf Pelt 35%, Adrenaline Gland 15%, or Strange Meat 16.5%. Beast Slayers origin rolls that table twice at 50%. No DLC: pelt 50% or Strange Meat 16.5%. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Direwolf

2. Frenzied Direwolf. HP 150. Head armor 30. Body armor 30. Melee skill 65. Melee defense 10. Ranged defense 10. Initiative 150. Action points 12. Fatigue 180. Resolve 70. Same Direwolf Bite as the normal wolf (4 AP, 6 fatigue, 30-50, 70% armor damage, 20% armor penetration). The page does not give a separate bite. Drops are on the same loot pattern as the direwolf page's frenzy note; the frenzy page itself is the source for the stats. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Frenzied_Direwolf

3. Hyena. HP 120. Head armor 20. Body armor 20. Melee skill 60. Melee defense 10. Ranged defense 10. Initiative 90. Action points 14. Fatigue 180. Resolve 50. Hyena Bite: 4 AP, 6 fatigue, 20-35 damage, 100% armor damage, 35% armor penetration, bleeding 5 damage per turn if at least 6 hitpoints are lost (the skill page says 10 per turn on Frenzied Hyenas). https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Hyena

4. Nachzehrer, three sizes, no armor listed. Small: HP 80, melee skill 60, melee defense 10, ranged defense 15, initiative 125, action points 9, fatigue 130, resolve 50. Medium: HP 200, melee skill 70, melee defense 15, ranged defense 10, initiative 110, action points 9, resolve 80. Large: HP 380, melee skill 80, melee defense 20, ranged defense 5, initiative 95, action points 9, resolve 110. Claws, 4 AP, 6 fatigue: small 25-40 with 75% armor damage and 10% armor penetration. Medium and large claw pages exist and were not fully quoted here; do not copy the small numbers onto them. Gruesome Feast: 6 AP, 25 fatigue, eats a corpse, grows a size if it can, clears injuries, heals toward max. Swallow Whole: 9 AP, 25 fatigue, removes a brother until the nachzehrer dies; the brother takes 10-20 damage. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Nachzehrer

5. Webknecht. HP 60. Head armor 20. Body armor 20. Melee skill 60. Melee defense 10 before day 50, 15 after. Ranged defense 20 before day 50, 25 after. Initiative 150. Action points 11. Fatigue 130. Resolve 45 plus 3 per ally. Also +5% direct damage on days 25-50 and +10% after, and 2x direct damage to webbed targets. Webknecht Bite: 6 AP, 10 fatigue, 20-40 damage, 70% armor damage, 30% armor penetration. Webknecht Poison: poison on any hit of at least 6 damage, 5 damage at end of the target's turn. Weave Web: 6 AP, 25 fatigue, roots, cuts melee skill, melee defense, and ranged defense by 50%, cooldown 3 turns. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Webknecht

6. Unhold. HP 500. No armor listed. Melee skill 70. Melee defense 10. Ranged defense 0. Initiative 75. Action points 9. Fatigue 400. Resolve 130. Same page also lists Bog Unhold (ranged defense 5, same HP 500) and Frost Unhold (HP 600, head and body armor 90, melee skill 75, initiative 85, resolve 150, ranged defense 0). Skills: Crippling Strikes, Pathfinder, Resilient, Steel Brow, Regeneration. Sweep: 4 AP, 20 fatigue, 40-80 damage, 80% armor damage, 40% armor penetration, knockback, stagger, up to 3 adjacent targets. Fling Back: 4 AP, 20 fatigue, tosses the target behind the unhold, 10 fatigue and falling damage. Unstoppable Charge: 6 AP, 30 fatigue, stun and stagger, range 3. Drops, regular origins: Giant Skull and Bones 40%, Unhold Hide 40%, or Unhold's Heart 20%; Frost drops Thick White Fur instead of hide; Strange Meat 33%; Deformed Valuables 20% (450 crowns). https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Unhold

7. Armored Unhold. The ask says Armoured. The wiki page is Armored Unhold. HP 500. Head armor 35. Body armor 35. Those 35s are worn armor, not a second natural layer. Melee skill 70. Melee defense 10. Ranged defense 0. Initiative 75, plus 40 on the first 2 rounds (3 in scenario mode). Action points 9. Fatigue 400. Resolve 130. 1.1x damage after day 90. Same three attacks as the Unhold. Used by barbarian beastmasters. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Armored_Unhold

8. Frost Unhold. The name redirects to Unhold. Stats are on that page, section Frost Unhold: HP 600, head armor 90, body armor 90, melee skill 75, melee defense 10, ranged defense 0, initiative 85, action points 9, fatigue 400, resolve 150. The Armored Unhold page has a second frost row: worn armor 400 head and 400 body, plus 90 natural, so the infobox shows 490 and 490, HP 600, melee skill 75, initiative 85 plus 40 early, resolve 150, XP 550, and 1.15x damage before day 90, 1.25x after. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Frost_Unhold

9. Alp. HP 100. Armor not listed. Melee skill not listed. Melee defense 10. Ranged defense 10. Initiative 60-115. Action points 10. Fatigue 100. Resolve 100. Night only. Skills: Underdog, Existing Partly in Dreams, Fade, Nightmare, Sleep. Nightmare: 4 AP, 10 fatigue, 25 damage minus 25% of current hitpoints, at least 5, ignores armor, never misses, only on sleeping brothers. Sleep: 4 AP, 5 fatigue, a stun that allies or Rally can break. Drops, regular: Parched Skin 40%, Third Eye 40%, or Petrified Scream 20%, plus 20% Soul Splinter (300 crowns). https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Alp

10. Hexe. HP 80. Head armor 0. Body armor 25. Melee skill not listed. Melee defense 5. Ranged defense 5. Initiative 100. Action points 9. Fatigue 80. Resolve 160. Charm: 5 AP, 15 fatigue, mind-control one brother for 2 turns. Resist chance is resolve minus 35 (minus 40 if adjacent). At 35 resolve or less, charm always lands. At 130 resolve, resist is 95%, and 95% is the cap. Hex: 3 AP, 5 fatigue, 2 turns, the brother takes the same damage the hexe takes, armor ignored, cannot be resisted. Drink Night Owl Elixir: 2 AP, 10 fatigue, clears nighttime penalties. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Hexe

11. Schrat. HP 600. Armor not listed. Melee skill 70-75. Melee defense -5. Ranged defense -5. Initiative 60. Action points 7. Fatigue 400. Resolve 200. Uproot: 5 AP, 25 fatigue, 70-100 damage, 85% armor damage, 50% armor penetration, stagger, 3 tiles in a row. Grow Shield: 5 AP, 50 fatigue, builds a new shield after the old one breaks. Saplings are a separate entry. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Schrat

12. Sapling. HP 75. Armor not listed. Melee skill 80. Melee defense 15. Ranged defense 15. Initiative 80. Action points 7. Fatigue 400. Resolve 150. XP 50. Weak Uproot: 5 AP, 25 fatigue, 40-60 damage, 85% armor damage, 40% armor penetration, stagger, 1 tile. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Sapling

13. Lindwurm. Head and tail are both on the page. Each: HP 1100, head armor 200, body armor 400, melee skill 75-85, melee defense 10, ranged defense -10, initiative 80, action points 7, fatigue 400, resolve 180. Acid Blood: a melee hit of at least 10 hitpoint damage sprays the next tile; acid eats 10% of head and body armor per turn for 3 turns, refreshes, does not stack. Tail Slam Split: 4 AP, 25 fatigue, 60-120 damage, 150% armor damage, 35% armor penetration, hits the target and the one behind, daze 2 turns or stun 1. Tail Slam Sweep: 4 AP, 20 fatigue, same damage, -5% to hit, up to 3 adjacent, daze or knockback or stun. Tail Slam Thresh: 4 AP, 25 fatigue, same damage, -5% to hit, up to 6 around the tail. Gorge and Move Tail are also on the page. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Lindwurm

14. Serpent. HP 130. Head armor 40. Body armor 40. Melee skill 65. Melee defense 10-15. Ranged defense 25. Initiative 50-100. Action points 9. Fatigue 110. Resolve 100. Serpent Bite: 5 AP, 5 fatigue, 50-70 damage, 75% armor damage, 30% armor penetration. Drag: 6 AP, 20 fatigue, pulls the target, ends Shieldwall, Spearwall, and Riposte, falling damage plus fatigue, stagger 2 turns, range 3, only if level or higher. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Serpent

15. Ifrit, three sizes. Small: HP 110, body armor 110, head armor shown gray at 110, melee skill 65, melee defense -5, ranged defense -5, initiative 60, action points 8, fatigue 400. Medium: HP 220, body armor 110, melee skill 75, melee defense 0, ranged defense -10, initiative 50, action points 8. Large: HP 440, body armor 110, melee skill 80, melee defense 5, ranged defense -15, initiative 40, action points 8. Size 1 Headbutt: 6 AP, 15 fatigue, 35-45 damage, 60% armor damage, 50% armor penetration. Size 2 and 3 have their own headbutt and throw pages. Merge Living Sands: 25 fatigue. Sand Golem is a perk, not a second animal. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Ifrit

16. Geist. HP 1. Armor not listed. Melee skill 65. Melee defense 30-35. Ranged defense 999. Initiative 95. Action points 9. Resolve 130. Incorporeal. Ghastly Touch: 4 AP, 0 fatigue, 15-25 damage, 100% armor damage. Horrific Scream: 6 AP, 0 fatigue, resolve checks in 3 tiles. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Geist

17. Kraken. Head: HP 3800, head armor 1600, body armor shown gray at 1600, initiative -300, melee defense -15, ranged defense -15, action points 9, fatigue 999. Tentacle: HP 300, melee skill 80, melee defense 25, ranged defense 25, initiative 100, action points 9. Enraged tentacle adds resolve 60. Devour: 6 AP, 25 fatigue, kills the brother if you end the turn next to the mouth. Ensnare: 5 AP, 10 fatigue, root, no skills, drag up to 6 tiles. Move Tentacle: 1 fatigue. Bite (10 fatigue) is also transcluded: 5 AP, 10 fatigue, 70-110 damage, 100% armor damage, 50% armor penetration, 5 bleeding for 2 turns if at least 6 hitpoints lost, range 2. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Kraken

18. Ijirok. The short page redirects to The Ijirok. HP 2200. Head armor 140. Body armor 140. Melee skill 95. Melee defense 20. Ranged defense 15. Initiative 95. Action points 9. Fatigue 400. Resolve not listed. Immune to stun, disarm, night, nets, forced movement, injuries, poison, and morale. Not immune to bleeding, fire, or fatigue. Gore: 6 AP, 20 fatigue, 100-130 damage, 75% armor damage, 40% armor penetration, knockback and stagger, range 3 to 6 (3 to 10 on turn 1). Spirit Walk: 3 AP, 10 fatigue, teleport, snow tiles, chill, range 1 to 30. Ijirok Regeneration: heal 6% of max at the start of each turn. Legendary hunting-ground boss, not a random pack. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/The_Ijirok
