# STUDIO ASSET PIPELINE FROM CRASHBOX

## MY PROPOSAL

The research is the handmade Crashbox look. Cuppa Coffee used clay, puppets, and junk. The studio can cook the same feel in pixels with a locked palette, stop-motion frames, and visible construction. This is the practical path.

### LOCKED PALETTE

Start with one palette for the whole game. Use Lospec. Soft & Dirty (15 colors) or Dry Sea (10 colors) or a custom mix of rust, dull metal, sick green, and clay. Limit every sprite to the same set. Value does the work. Shadows shift cooler. Highlights are scratches, not shine. Nothing glows. Grain is in the pixels.

### TOOLS

Aseprite or Pixelorama for sprites and animation. Both do frames, onion skin, tags, and sprite sheet export. Author at the target resolution. Do not scale down later. Export with 1 or 2 pixels of padding so edges do not bleed. Point filter in the engine. Integer scale only.

### SPRITE RULES

Bodies are assembled. Head sits slightly off. Arms are a different tone. Parts look patched. Clay faces are simple shapes with fingerprint dents. Robots are mismatched tin and rust. Drones look taped. Leave the pixel steps. Do not smooth curves.

### ANIMATION RULES

Stop-motion. 3 or 4 frames for a walk. Hold each pose. Do not tween. Frame duration around 100 to 150 ms. Idle is a slight shift that holds. Turns are snaps. The stiffness matches the chip bodies and the broken machines.

### TILE AND UI RULES

Tiles are junk. Cracked asphalt with scrap. Boards and rust with one green clay cartridge stuck in a wall. UI plates are cartridge shapes with rusty edges. Text sits a little off. Light is flat or from one hard source.

### COOK ORDER FOR THE STUDIO

One green clay cartridge. Soft, dented, fingerprint ridges. One rusty tin robot that loads it. Mismatched parts. One person sprite with a clay face and a 4-frame stop-motion walk. One street tile and one wall tile. One UI plate that is the cartridge shape. Lock these five. Everything else grows from them. The art coordinator can cook against this sheet. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light and the stillness, decided at the source.
