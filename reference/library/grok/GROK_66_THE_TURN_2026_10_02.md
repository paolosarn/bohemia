# THE TURN

No new names. No health numbers. Battle Brothers turn, his skins only.

9 action points. A step is 2. A one-hand swing is 4. One tile is a house. Melee is the next tile.

Pistol is the dagger. One tile. Spends bullets or nails.
Pipe is the mace. Stun. Spends nothing.
Car is the handgonne. Cone. Range 2. Shot spends gas from the ammo pile. Reload is 9.

A hit of 10 or more can leave the one pain line. If he dies, the run ends.

The ground picks who shows up. The score picks how many. No scripted fight.
