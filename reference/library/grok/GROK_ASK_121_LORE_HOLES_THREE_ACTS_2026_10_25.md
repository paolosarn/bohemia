# ASK 121: Lore Holes Against the Three Acts

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Read the whole master file. The lore has holes. Write a page headed LORE HOLES AGAINST THE THREE ACTS: where the story does not yet touch the systems.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The master locks three generations over roughly 100 years. Gen 1 is Animal. Gen 2 is Human. Gen 3 is Angel. The player lives all three. Buildings unlock by act. Act 1 is the baseline post-crash set. Act 2 is harder infrastructure. Act 3 is the grand modern structures. Threats reset only at the generational handoff, not as a simple respawn. The mayor arc sits on top of the city-building jewel.

What does not yet touch the systems is the day-to-day of a 50-to-100-day Battle Brothers campaign inside one act. The Late Game Crises page says the first crisis arrives around day 80 to 100. The master does not say whether that crisis is the end of Act 1, the start of the generational handoff, or a mid-act event that can repeat. The death math says roughly 2.2 million people left the valley. The master does not say how many of those bodies still walk with chips, or whether the chip-body count drops or rises when Act 2 begins.

The three-currency system (medicine, electricity, resources) is locked. The master does not say how the currency mix changes when Act 2 opens cars and EVs, or whether the old battery-only economy is still the only one the player sees in the first 100 days. The mayor seat is the jewel. The master does not say whether the mayor can rebuild a settlement that a crisis destroyed, or whether permanent destruction stays permanent across the generational handoff.

A LITTLE THING FROM the vehicle ladder that starts with man-powered travel and unlocks cars later, from the master.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Three acts, three generations, buildings gated by act, threats reset at handoff.
WHAT IS NOT: Where the first crisis sits inside Act 1. Chip-body population by act. Mayor rebuild power.
WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: Whether the first crisis ends Act 1 or sits inside it.
