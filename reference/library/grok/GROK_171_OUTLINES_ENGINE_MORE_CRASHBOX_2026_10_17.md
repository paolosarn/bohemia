# OUTLINES ENGINE MORE CRASHBOX

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants more analog horror and assets so the sprites and animations look way better. Claude makes a really bad graphic artist.

## MY PROPOSAL

Outlines and anti-aliasing. Outlines are optional. Use a dark near-black or a dark version of the fill color only on the outer silhouette if the background is busy. Do not outline every internal detail. Skip outlines if the background is controlled and contrast is high. Anti-aliasing is rare. Use one intermediate pixel only on long shallow diagonals on larger sprites. Never on 45 degree steps or straight lines. Never on small sprites. Over-AA makes blur. The Crashbox look is stiff and stepped. Leave the steps. Grain is intentional dither or baked noise, not blur.

Engine rules. Godot: Project Settings Rendering Textures Default Texture Filter Nearest. Viewport stretch mode. Integer scale. Unity: Filter Mode Point on sprites. Pixels Per Unit match. Pixel Perfect Camera with Reference Resolution. Compression None. No mip maps. Export Aseprite sheets with 1 pixel padding. Tags become animation names. Durations travel with the sheet. Point sampling keeps the pixels square. Integer scaling only. Nothing glows.

More Crashbox. Ten 2nds is an off-screen announcer and a digital countdown with rebus phrases that wipe. Riddlesnake is a coiled wooden snake with a turban and flute. A man rises from a basket and blows a horn to reveal the answer. Word Shake is black and white nickelodeon style. The chef mixes words and the kitchen explodes in flour. All stay mixed media and stiff. The machine crashes. The parts do not quite fit.
