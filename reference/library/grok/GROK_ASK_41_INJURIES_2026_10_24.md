# ASK 41 INJURIES THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
The negative pain trait can be the same. Do not invent a different hurt name per body part.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers uses temporary and permanent injuries. A temporary injury can appear if a hit deals at least 10 hitpoints and reaches a threshold of about 25 to 60 percent of maximum hitpoints. Type depends on the weapon (cutting, piercing, blunt, burning) and the part hit. Temporary injuries heal over days with medical supplies. One medical point is used each day per injury. Temples can halve the time for a price. Bandages stop certain hemorrhaging injuries only during the same battle. Permanent injuries occur when a character reaches zero hitpoints and survives (33 percent chance, 90 percent with Survivor). Fatalities prevent survival. Permanent injuries stay until a rare item removes them. The negative pain trait stays the same name. The wiki does not list every exact threshold for every injury.

In Bohemia the same system stays. Temporary and permanent injuries follow the same rules. The pain trait keeps one name. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Temporary Injuries, Permanent Injuries, and Medical Supplies pages, and developer blogs on injury mechanics. The wiki does not say every exact threshold.
