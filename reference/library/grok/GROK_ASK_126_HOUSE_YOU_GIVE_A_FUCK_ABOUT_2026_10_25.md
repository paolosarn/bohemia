# ASK 126: A House You Give a Fuck About

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Read the whole master file. A house you give a fuck about: a face you meet again, a voice, a want, a memory of your deeds, a place that is theirs.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The master locks the idea of a house the player cares about. It has a face that returns, a voice, a want, and a memory of the player's deeds. The place is theirs. The master does not lock which of the 14 factions gets the first house, or what the face looks like under analog horror.

Battle Brothers makes noble houses feel distinct through coats of arms, descriptions, and procedurally generated leaders. The Factions and Relations wiki page says each house has a unique coat of arms and description. Relations with the house and its settlements change prices and contract access. Dev Blog 57 says houses have traits that change the contracts they offer. Completing contracts raises relations. Failing them lowers relations. The wiki does not say a recurring named face that remembers specific deeds, or a voice that changes based on past contracts. The wiki does not say whether a house has a physical seat the player can visit outside a settlement screen.

Bohemia has no locked rule that says the first house is a settlement the player returns to after every contract, a faction leader who appears on the world map, or a portrait that only appears when relations cross 70. The master does not say whether the memory of deeds is a short log the player can open or a line of dialogue that only plays once.

A LITTLE THING FROM the relation score that already tracks how a settlement feels about the company, from the Relations page.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Face, voice, want, memory of deeds, place that is theirs. Colour is territory. Relations change prices.
WHAT IS NOT: Which faction is first. What the face looks like. How the memory is stored.
WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: Whether the first house should be the settlement the player starts near.
