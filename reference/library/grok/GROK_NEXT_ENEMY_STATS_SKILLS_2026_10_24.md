# NEXT ENEMY TYPES STATS AND SKILLS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
A combat tile is house-sized. Melee is likely the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows reach 2, 3, or up to about 12-13 tiles.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the units. Action points and fatigue costs stay the way Battle Brothers does them.

Direwolf has 130 health, 60 melee skill, 10 melee defense, 10 ranged defense, 50 resolve, 150 initiative, 180 fatigue, 20 fatigue recovery, and 12 action points. Direwolf Bite costs 4 action points and 6 fatigue for 30-50 damage. They rush even if they will have no action points left to attack. They can attack three times if they do not move. Frenzied Direwolf has 150 health and 65 melee skill.

Unhold has 500 health, 70 melee skill, 10 melee defense, 0 ranged defense, 130 resolve, 75 initiative, 400 fatigue, 30 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Regeneration heals 15 percent of max health at the start of each turn. Fling Back costs 4 action points and 20 fatigue and tosses the target behind the unhold. Sweep costs 4 action points and 20 fatigue, can hit up to 3 targets, and does 40-80 damage. Unstoppable Charge costs 6 action points and 30 fatigue, has a range of 3 tiles, and can stun. They cannot be stopped by Spearwall.

Goblin Ambusher has 40 health, 60 melee skill, 75 ranged skill, 10 melee defense, 20 ranged defense, 45 resolve, 140 initiative, 100 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Poison applies if the target loses at least 6 health from any of their attacks. It lasts 3 turns and reduces action points, initiative, and vision. They stay fragile but hard to hit with ranged weapons.

Goblin Overseer has 70 health, 75 melee skill, 80 ranged skill, 15 melee defense, 20 ranged defense, 70 resolve, 120 initiative, 130 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Captain is an aura that increases the resolve of other allies by 15 percent within 5 tiles. Whip costs 3 action points and 5 fatigue and sets one goblin's morale to confident within 5 tiles. They carry a heavy crossbow.

Fallen Hero has 180 health, 60-65 melee skill, 5 melee defense, 0 ranged defense, 130 resolve, 60 initiative, and 7 action points. Bite costs 4 action points and 0 fatigue for 15-35 damage. They have a 90 percent chance to rise again once with 50 percent health, even if headless. Necromancer has 50 health, 50 melee skill, 5 melee defense, 10 ranged defense, 90 resolve, 90 initiative, 90 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 7 action points. Raise Undead costs 3 action points and 10 fatigue and can be used twice a turn. Possess Undead costs 3 action points and 10 fatigue and sets action points to 12 plus other bonuses for one turn.

In Bohemia the same numbers and costs stay. The look changes to fast flanking drones, large slow machines that shove and heal, small poison drones, and chip-bodies that keep moving. Melee stays the next tile. Ranged reaches further. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Direwolf, Beasts, Unhold, Skills, Goblin Ambusher, Goblin Overseer, Goblins, Fallen Hero, Necromancer, and Zombies.
