## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers pays each brother a daily wage based on background, a small random factor, and level. Wages rise by a percentage each level. Missing pay lowers mood. Angry brothers can desert. Traits like Loyal, Disloyal, and Greedy change the chance. Hard difficulty makes deserters take their gear. Indebted and certain origin characters cannot desert. The wiki does not list every edge case for every background.
