# ASK 68 INVENTORY AND CART THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Inventory and the cart stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers starts the company with a limited inventory, usually around 99 slots depending on origin. Cart upgrades bought through the retinue add more slots in steps of 27. Equipped items and bag slots on brothers do not count against the main inventory. Resources like tools, ammunition, and medicine have separate caps. Food occupies inventory space and spoils. The wiki does not list every edge case for every origin or upgrade path.

In Bohemia the same inventory and cart system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
