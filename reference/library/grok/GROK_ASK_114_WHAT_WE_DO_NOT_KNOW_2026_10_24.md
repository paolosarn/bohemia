# ASK 114: What We Do Not Know

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

We keep missing the day loop. Camp, night, vendors, provisions, medicine, tools, ammo. If the wiki does not say it, write that.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The wiki states the following rules. Provisions: each man needs 2 per day by default. Terrain multiplies consumption from 1.0 on plains to 2.0 on mountains. Food spoils. Running out lowers morale and can cause desertion. Source: Provisions page.

Medicine: 1 medical supply point per day heals one temporary injury. Lost hit points heal without medicine. Running out stops temporary injury recovery. Source: Medical Supplies page.

Tools: 1 tools point repairs 15 durability. Repair rate is 3 durability points per hour. Camp or caravan escort doubles the rate. Blacksmith increases speed 33 percent. Source: Tools and Supplies page.

Ammo: global stockpile. Handgonne costs 2 units per shot. Refills after battle from the stockpile. Source: prior piles and Ammo pages.

Day cycle: Dawn to Night. Vendors and recruitment close at night. Only the tavern stays open. A full day is about 1 minute 45 seconds real time. Time pauses in town, events, and combat. Source: Game Mechanics page.

Camp: doubles repair rate. Reduces vision and increases visibility to enemies. Source: Tools page and Dev Blog 17.

The wiki does not say the exact night movement slowdown percentage, the exact camp vision reduction, or the precise tick interval for the 3 durability per hour rate. The wiki does not say how many hours define night versus day.
