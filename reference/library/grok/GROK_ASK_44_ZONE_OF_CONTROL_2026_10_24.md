# ASK 44 ZONE OF CONTROL THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Zone of control stays the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers gives every melee unit a zone of control on the six adjacent tiles. Leaving a controlled tile triggers free attacks from every hostile that controls it. The attacks cost the controller nothing. If every attack misses, the unit leaves. If any attack hits, the unit stays and the attempt fails. Ranged weapons, fleeing units, and stunned units do not create a zone. Smoke pots remove zones for one turn. Footwork and Rotation ignore the zone. Two or more levels of height difference also break the zone. The wiki does not list every edge case for every skill.

In Bohemia the same zone of control stays. Free attacks on leaving stay. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Combat Mechanics page and developer notes on tactical combat. The wiki does not say every edge case.
