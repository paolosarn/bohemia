## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers runs time on the world map in real time with a day and night cycle. Night slows movement and cuts vision. Most settlement buildings close at night. Wages pay at noon. Food is consumed, tools repair gear, and medicine heals injuries while time passes. Camping makes time pass faster and speeds recovery and repairs, but the company is immobile and easier for enemies to spot. The wiki does not list every edge case for every phase or location.
