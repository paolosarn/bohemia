## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Parties and roaming stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers fills the world map with parties that move on their own. Camps send out parties to raid, hunt, or patrol. Roaming parties scale with days, player strength, and distance from settlements. Parties leave tracks that the player can follow. Some parties are hostile and some are neutral. The wiki does not list every edge case for every party type.

In Bohemia the same party and roaming system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Game Mechanics and Global Map pages, and developer notes on world simulation. The wiki does not say every edge case.
