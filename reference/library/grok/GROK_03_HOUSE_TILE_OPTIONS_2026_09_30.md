# ONE PASS: WHAT A HOUSE TILE DOES TO A WEAPON

No lock. A pile. His scale: a tile is a house. Melee is the next tile, not the same tile. A bow might reach 12 or 13. Pistol is the dagger. Car is the handgonne and spends gas.

## The real distances

A Vegas compact lot is 35 feet wide. A local street right-of-way is 47 to 60 feet, and the driveable part can be about 37 feet. So one tile, house plus yard, is about 40 to 60 feet. Two tiles is across the street. https://online.encodeplus.com/regs/lasvegas-nv/doc-viewer.aspx?secid=377 https://clarkcounty-nv.elaws.us/code/coor_title30_30.52_sec30.52.030

A pistol is useful inside about 25 yards, and a stretch at 50. That is one tile, maybe two if you are good. A shotgun with shot is a cone inside about 30 to 40 yards, then it falls off. A rifle is the thing that still works at 7 to 13 tiles. https://www.pewpewtactical.com/effective-range-pistols-rifles-shotguns/

Battle Brothers, for the job not the yards: polearm is 1-2 tiles. Bow is 6-7. Handgonne is 2, cone, reload eats the turn.

## The pile

1. Pistol at 1 tile. Puncture. Ignores armor. Spends ammunition. Already his dagger. Best first-fight gun.
2. Shotgun at 1 to 2 tiles. The man-carried handgonne. Cone. Reload. Spends ammunition. The car stays the big version and spends gas.
3. Bolt rifle at 3 to 6 tiles. Crossbow job. One shot, rack, accurate. The roof gun if he later wants 7 to 13.
4. Semi-auto at 3 to 13. Bow job. Many shots, no reload beat. Fights the pistol if both are the fast gun. Pick one fast gun.
5. Pipe or bat at 1 tile. Mace. Stun. Spends nothing. The reward for stepping in.
6. Machete at 1 tile. Cleaver. Meaner when they are low. Spends nothing.
7. Sledge at 1 tile. Hammer. Breaks a door, a vest, a car panel. Tools repair it.
8. Spike strip on the tile edge. Spearwall. Stops the step. Not a weapon you swing.
9. Car door. Shield. Held. Or dropped as cover.
10. Bottles and bricks at 2 tiles. Throw. Five in a bundle, then empty, like the wiki.
11. Real crossbow at 2 to 4. Quiet. Analog horror. Worse than the rifle. A perk if you want silence.
12. Flare at 2. Firelance, one use, then the fight is done with it.
13. Trailer tube. Mortar. Not in the first fight.
14. Dog on a lead at 1. Spearwall or riposte. The beastmaster already has this job.
15. Tow strap at 1. Flail. Ignores a door someone is hiding behind.
16. Net at 2. Root. One use.

## Three ways the first fight can be cut

Cut A, guns first. Pistol, shotgun, bolt rifle, car. Melee is the dare. Fast to teach. Weak horror.
Cut B, both. Pistol and shotgun spend ammo. Pipe, machete, and sledge spend nothing, and only work at 1 tile. That is the reward. This is the one I would ship.
Cut C, melee first. Guns are rare. Strong horror, slow demo, and it fights his line that a pistol is the dagger.
