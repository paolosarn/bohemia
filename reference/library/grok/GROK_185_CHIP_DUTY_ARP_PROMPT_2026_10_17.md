# CHIP DUTY ARP PROMPT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants better 120 BPM 8-bit songs that play on HTML without the crushed-sand sound so Claude can make stronger tracks for the light game engine.

## MY PROPOSAL

Duty cycle. Square waves change character when the pulse width shifts. 12.5% is thin and reedy. 25% is the classic bright NES lead. 50% is hollow. Switch mid-note for life. This adds motion without mud or extra channels.

Arpeggios. One channel plays root, third, fifth fast enough that the ear hears a chord. Lock the speed to the tempo (eighths or sixteenths at 120 BPM). This fakes harmony on limited voices and keeps the mix clear.

Vibrato. Small LFO on pitch (a few cents at 4-6 Hz) warms sustained notes. Use sparingly on leads only. Too much turns it wobbly.

Drums. Noise only. Low short burst for kick on 1 and 3. Mid burst for snare on 2 and 4. Tiny high click for hats. Same channel, different pitch and length. Keep hits under 100 ms so they stay punchy and do not smear into sand.

Prompt for Claude. NES-style chiptune, 120 BPM, square lead with duty cycle changes, triangle bass, noise drums, arpeggios instead of chords, 3-4 channels max, gain envelopes, dry, no bit crush, no heavy compression, clean 16-bar loop, output as Web Audio JavaScript or simple note arrays.
