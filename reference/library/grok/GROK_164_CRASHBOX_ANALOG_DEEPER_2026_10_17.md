# CRASHBOX ANALOG HORROR DEEPER

## MY PROPOSAL

Crashbox is remembered as a creepy kids show. People call it the real Candle Cove. Segments that stuck are the Revolting Slob who explodes, Eddie Bull who gets eaten by zoo animals, Captain Bones the eternally dead skeleton pirate, and Haunted House Party with cardboard cutouts and lightning. The framing is a junk computer that crashes. Rusty tin robots load green clay cartridges. One loader is scrap metal that falls apart and puts itself back together. The whole thing feels barely held together. That is the analog.

### MORE LOOK TO STEAL

Eddie Bull is a clay figure with a real human mouth edited on. The body is stiff. The animals are clay and wrong. He gets swallowed whole and comes back out. The stiffness and the wrong mouth are the uncanny. Captain Bones falls apart into bones and rebuilds the math problem. The body is not solid. It is parts. Haunted House Party uses cardboard cutouts that appear after a flash. The reveal is flat and sudden. Sketch Pad is hand-drawn and the pictures do not quite make sense until the missing piece. The Scrap Metal Game Loader is rusted scrap held by a handle. It falls apart when it loads. That is the machine.

### FOR BOHEMIA PIXELS

Chip bodies can fall apart and rebuild like Captain Bones. A downed hire is still there. The parts can rearrange. Faces can have the wrong mouth or the wrong eyes like Eddie Bull. A green clay cartridge can be stuck in a wall or a robot and look soft and dented. A robot can have one loader arm that is scrap and falls off or reattaches. Tiles can have the scrap metal look. A street can have one piece that is clearly junk held on with wire or tape. Movement stays stop-motion. Hold the poses. The stiffness is the horror. Nothing glows. Light is flat or one hard source. Grain is in the pixels.

### COOK MORE

One Eddie Bull style face. Clay body, wrong mouth, stiff. One Captain Bones style body that can fall into parts and rebuild. One scrap metal loader that looks like it falls apart. One cardboard cutout reveal for a loading or a menu. One tile that has scrap metal and wire. The art coordinator can cook these against the earlier sheets. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light and the stillness and the parts that do not quite fit, decided at the source.
