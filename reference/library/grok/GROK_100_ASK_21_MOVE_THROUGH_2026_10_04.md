# ASK 21. MOVING THROUGH YOUR OWN MEN

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo did not state the move-through rule in this chat. The ask file quotes him on October 4: they can move past through one of your own people, they cannot move through two.

## MY PROPOSAL

The combat mechanics page does not state the one-versus-two rule. It does say a melee unit holds the tiles next to it, and leaving that tile draws a free strike. Footwork lets you leave that tile safely. Rotation swaps you with a non-hostile unit so you can leave. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_Mechanics The wiki does not say the action point cost or the fatigue cost of Footwork or Rotation on the pages I opened.

A player comment from October 2016 on the developer forum says you cannot move through two or more of your own units in a row, and you cannot move through allied units that are not your company. https://battlebrothersgame.com/forums/topic/ability-to-choose-the-path-of-movement-in-battle/ That is a player comment about an old build, not a current wiki number. I am not treating it as the live rule.
