# ONE PASS: THE CHIP-BODY FIGHT

His lock: the dead are bodies with a chip still in them. Analog horror. Not magic. Not an empty robot. The person with the controller is the necromancer.

Battle Brothers floor, wiki 2026-09-30. A wiederganger walks slow, bites for 15-35, and has a 66% chance to get back up once at half health unless the head is gone. A necromancer is a living person. Possess sets a body to 12 action points. Raise brings a body back if the head is intact and nobody is standing on it. He stays in the back. Two shielded bodies guard him. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Wiederganger https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Necromancer

## What the chip is doing

Real anchor, not a build: a pacemaker and a deep-brain stimulator already sit in a person and stop when the battery dies. The horror is the reverse. The chip outlives the person. I am not designing the chip.

## Options for the get-back-up

1. The chip reboots once. Head shot kills the chip. Same as the wiki. Smash or take the head and it stays down.
2. The chip reboots until the battery in the neck is pulled. A dagger job. Puncture becomes pull the chip.
3. The chip reboots only if the controller is alive. Kill the person, the bodies drop. Cleaner demo. Weaker horror.
4. Both 1 and 3. They get up once on their own. After that, only the controller can stand them up.

I would ship 4. It is the wiki, and it gives you two ways to end the fight.

## Options for the body

5. Slow walk, bite, no gun. They can pick up a pipe. They cannot shoot. Wiki rule.
6. They wear whatever they died in. A casino vest, a hospital gown, a work boot. Armor is leftover clothes, often already half broken. Wiki: 50% chance the armor is at half.
7. A fallen hero is a body in a good vest and a full helmet. More health. The controller can make it swing twice.
8. A geist is not a body. It is a speaker in a window that plays a voice and drops morale. Kill the speaker.
9. Ancient dead stay a maybe. Old Guard kits with a chip in a corpse. Do not put them in the first fight.

## Options for the controller

10. A living person in the back. Coat, hat, a knife. Two bodies with doors for shields stand in front.
11. Range is the whole fight. He does not need to see the body. The chip is the link.
12. He can stand a body up twice in a turn, if the head is there and the tile is empty.
13. He can juice one body for a turn. That body acts twice.
14. If your hire dies and the head is intact, the chip can be put in. That hire stands up on their side. Horror. Optional. Off for the demo.

## How you beat them

15. Head shot. The chip is in the neck or the skull.
16. Stand on the body. It cannot get up. If it does, it shoves you one tile.
17. Kill the controller. New bodies stop. Ones already up may still finish the turn.
18. A flare or a bright light does nothing. Do not invent a weakness the wiki does not have, unless he asks.
