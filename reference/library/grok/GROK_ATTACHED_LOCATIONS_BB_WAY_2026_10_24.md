# ATTACHED LOCATIONS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Lean into the Battle Brothers settlement menu.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers places attached locations outside a settlement on the world map. They change what the settlement sells, what it charges, and who appears in the hire list. The wiki lists them by group. Military ones are the wooden watchtower, the stone watchtower, the militia barracks, and the barracks. They increase militia, deserters, and retired soldiers. Fortified barracks also bring sellswords and hedge knights. Specialized shop locations include the blast furnace, the leather tanner, ore smelters, and the surface iron vein. These raise the quality and selection of armor and weapons. Supply locations are the workshop for tools, the arrow maker's shed for ammunition, and the herbalist's grove for medical supplies. Provision locations are the beekeeper for mead, the brewery for beer, fishing huts for dried fish, the gatherer's hut for roots and berries, goat pens for cheese or dried lamb, the hunter's cabin for cured venison, the mushroom grove for mushrooms, the orchard for dried fruits, the pig farm for smoked ham, the plantation for dates, wheat fields for grain and bread, and the winery for wine. Trade locations are the amber collector, the dye maker, the gem mine, the incense dryer, the lumber camp, the peat pit, the plantation for spices, the salt mine, the silk farm, the surface copper vein, the trapper for furs, and the wool spinner for cloth.

Most attached locations produce goods that appear in the marketplace. More attached locations raise the settlement's prices. Some attract specific backgrounds. A lumber camp brings lumberjacks. Wheat fields and pig farms bring farmhands. Fishing huts bring fishermen. The mushroom grove brings cultists. The wiki does not say an exact maximum number of attached locations for every settlement type. Small villages have no military ones and a tavern is guaranteed. Medium villages can have a wooden watchtower. Cities can have a wooden watchtower and militia barracks, and a temple is guaranteed. Fortifications follow a similar pattern with higher quality shops more likely. Attached locations can be destroyed. When one is destroyed the settlement loses its benefits.

In Bohemia the same system sits on the map. An attached location the player can see from the settlement changes what the settlement sells and who is available to hire. Destroying it removes the benefit. The player reads the settlement by looking at the icons outside it before they enter.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki page Settlements and attached locations; developer blog on attached locations.
