# ASK 39 GROUND EFFECTS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Ground effects stay restrained the way Battle Brothers does them.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers shows blood and corpses on the battlefield. A corpse spawns on the slain unit's tile if empty, then on an adjacent empty tile, then on the killer's tile if adjacent. If every nearby tile already has a corpse the new corpse does not spawn and non-droppable items such as armor are lost. Items on the ground appear in the post-battle loot if the fight is won. Retreating loses ground items. Fatalities include decapitation, head smash, and disembowelment. These prevent undead from rising. Blood and corpse visuals stay restrained. The wiki does not give exact blood particle counts or corpse sprite rules.

In Bohemia the same rules stay. Blood and corpses appear on the ground. Drops follow the same spawn order. Fatalities stay visual and mechanical. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
