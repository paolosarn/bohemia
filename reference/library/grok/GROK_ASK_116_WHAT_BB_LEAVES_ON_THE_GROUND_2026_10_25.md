# ASK 116: What Battle Brothers Leaves on the Ground

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

What Battle Brothers leaves on the ground. Hit flash, blood spray, decals, pools, corpses, dropped gear, severed heads. Variations for undead and beasts.

## MY PROPOSAL:

Battle Brothers shows damage through layered armor and injury icons on the character busts. Dev Blog 10 states that blood appears on weapons after kills and sprays onto characters who spill a lot of it. Fatality kills leave specific outcomes: decapitated, smashed head, or disemboweled. Destroying the head prevents a corpse from rising as a Wiederganger. Corpses stay on the field and can be looted for remaining gear during or after the fight. Destroyed armor does not appear as loot. The wiki Fatality page lists the types and the skills that cause them. The wiki does not list exact hit-flash timing, blood-pool size, decal duration, or whether severed heads remain as separate sprites. The wiki does not say how undead or beasts leave different visual remains beyond the Wiederganger rise rule.

A LITTLE THING FROM the blood-on-weapons system in Dev Blog 10.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Fatalities prevent some undead rises. Blood shows on weapons and characters. Loot comes from undestroyed gear.
WHAT IS NOT: Exact visual timing and decal rules. Precise corpse sprite differences for beasts.
WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: How long corpses stay visible after the fight ends.
