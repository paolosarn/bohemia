# ASK 57 RENOWN AND RELATIONS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Renown and relations stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers tracks renown for the whole company. Renown rises on finished contracts, won fights, and cleared camps. It falls on fails, cancels, and daily decay. Higher renown raises contract pay and unlocks harder jobs. Relations are per settlement or house. Good relations improve buy and sell prices. Relations drift toward neutral each dawn. Reputation is a third track that mostly changes which contracts appear. The wiki does not list every edge case.

In Bohemia the same three tracks stay. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Factions and Relations, Renown, and Relations pages, and developer notes. The wiki does not say every edge case.
