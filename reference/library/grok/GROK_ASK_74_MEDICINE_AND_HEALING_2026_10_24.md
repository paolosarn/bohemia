# ASK 74 MEDICINE AND HEALING THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Medicine and healing stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers uses medical supplies to heal temporary injuries. One point is required each day for every injury. Lost hitpoints heal over time on their own. Temples can treat injuries for a price and cut the healing time in half. Bandages stop certain bleeding injuries only during the battle they were taken. Permanent injuries do not heal except through rare items. Running out of medicine stops recovery from temporary injuries. The wiki does not list every edge case for every injury or follower.

In Bohemia the same medicine and healing system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Medical Supplies and Temporary Injuries pages. The wiki does not say every edge case.
