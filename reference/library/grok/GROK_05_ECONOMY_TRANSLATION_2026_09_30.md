# ASK 5. THE ECONOMY, TRANSLATED

Paolo's map, this chat, 2026-09-30. I do not rule. Battle Brothers is the floor. His words win.

## The six piles

1. Medicine = medicine. Same job.
2. Crowns = batteries / electricity. Money. Buildings make them (his 9/5 lock).
3. Provisions = resources. Food, gas, and water sit in this pile. Icons can differ. A car shot spends this pile (his words, this chat).
4. Renown = clout.
5. Ammunition = ammunition. Added pile.
6. Tools = tools. Added pile.

Every thing you pick up lands in one of these six, even if the icon changes.

## How Battle Brothers spends them, and the translation I am using until he says no

- Food: 2 provisions per hired man per day. Hard ground eats more (desert 1.5, mountains 2). Miss food and mood falls, then men leave. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Provisions
- Translation: 2 resources per person per day. Water and gas are the same pile, so a day in the open and a car shot both drink it. I am not inventing a second gauge.
- Medicine: 1 point per injury per day or the wound stays. Lost hitpoints come back alone. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_Supplies
- Translation: 1 medicine per injury per day.
- Tools: repair weapons and armor while time passes on the map. Player reports say about 15 durability per tool. (Steam discussion, not the wiki. Treat as recall-adjacent.) https://steamcommunity.com/app/365360/discussions/0/1836811737982905610/
- Translation: tools fix guns and cars the same way. One tool, a chunk of durability.
- Ammo: arrows, bolts, throwing, guns. A shot spends one.
- Translation: a pistol or rifle spends ammunition. A car does not. A car spends resources, because he said the shot is gas.
- Wages: crowns out every day. Level-up adds 2 crowns. Greedy adds 2. Miss pay and mood falls, then desertion. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Game_mechanics
- Translation: batteries out every day per hired person. His rule this chat: a hire can leave only a couple of days after batteries hit zero, not the same day the purse empties.
- Renown: a score, not a pile you drink. It opens bigger work.
- Translation: clout is the score. It is not spent like a battery.
- Start kit by difficulty, same page: high 2500 crowns / 50 food / 40 tools / 80 ammo / 30 medicine. Medium 2000 / 50 / 20 / 40 / 20. Low 1500 / 50 / 10 / 20 / 10.
