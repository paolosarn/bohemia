# ASK 27. FIGHT THE TILE-RPG VIBE

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
I'm trying to fight this tile turn-based RPG vibe. It has to be a lot better.

## MY PROPOSAL
Tactics games that do not feel like a board game use camera, momentum, reactions, and timing between the tap and the result. Valkyria Chronicles switches to a third-person action camera when a unit acts. The unit runs and shoots in real time for a few seconds before the camera returns. The board is still there but the action feels like a short movie clip. Gears Tactics rewards a finishing move with extra actions for the whole squad. The screen punches forward and the next move starts before the player has finished reading the numbers. Transistor freezes time so the player plans a string of moves, then plays the whole string at once as one continuous animation. The character dashes, strikes, and repositions in a single burst. Pokemon Mystery Dungeon moves every unit at the same moment the player moves. The floor never waits. Chrono Trigger's active time battle keeps the clocks running even while menus are open. The player feels pressure without a full real-time switch.

Our locks are square house-sized tiles, Rogue Fable 4 on the beat at 120 BPM leading the fight, guns first, and a routine fight lasting 2 to 4 minutes. The tricks that fit are short action bursts after the tap, momentum that carries a unit one extra tile when it kills, and simultaneous reaction flashes on the beat. The camera can punch in for the half-second the gun fires and then return. The beat keeps every animation locked so the fight never turns into a free camera movie. Simultaneous enemy reactions on the same beat stop the turn from feeling like a long queue. The board stays readable. The player still sees every tile and every number. The result arrives in less than one second after the tap so the fight never feels like a chess puzzle that lasts forty minutes.

Sources: DualShockers article on turn-based systems that feel like real-time; GameRant articles on tactical games and blended systems; developer descriptions of Valkyria Chronicles, Gears Tactics, and Transistor.
