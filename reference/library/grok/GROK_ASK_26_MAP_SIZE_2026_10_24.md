## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
The map must match Battle Brothers' scale. Grid expands to cover Las Vegas region.

## MY PROPOSAL
The wiki does not say a fixed hex count across and down for player map size settings because the game does not offer a map size slider to the player. Map size varies by seed. Modder data from seed samples shows Size values from just above 10000 to just above 18500 and Diameter values from the 140s to the 200s. A typical setup has about 17 settlements. The data does not say exact days to cross corner to corner or edge to edge at walking speed. Terrain modifies speed: plains move at 1, forests at 0.65, mountains at 0.25. The wiki does not say the camera near and far zoom stops in tiles on a 1080p screen or the party marker size in pixels. Night reduces sight radius. The party marker stays a fixed sprite while the world does not scale with zoom.

In Bohemia the same scale stays. The map matches Battle Brothers' pixel count and day-to-cross feel. No grey-yellow sand flood. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Global Map page, modder seed browser post, Dev Blog on worldmap, and Campaign Map Config mod notes. The wiki does not say exact hex counts, exact days to cross, exact zoom tile counts, or party marker pixel size.
