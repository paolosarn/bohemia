# ASK 34 THE FIRST MINUTES THAT MAKE A PLAYER CARE THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
We have a short amount of time to make people really give a fuck. Understand what's going on, each play unique. Whether we get a cutscene or a text box or straight into the journey. Depending on our origin. Search up online what people want.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers starts each origin with text that sets the company situation and then drops the player onto the world map or into a short guided fight. Rebuilding a Company begins after a lost fight against Hoggart the Weasel and gives a tutorial contract. A New Company starts with three men and funds. Lone Wolf starts with one experienced hedge knight and good gear but low funds. Peasant Militia starts with twelve poorly equipped peasants and allows sixteen in a fight. Most origins open with text and then the map. The game does not use a long cutscene. Studies and articles on first minutes say a strong open gives competence (the player can do one thing on purpose), a motive (a reason to do it again), and a horizon (evidence the game gets larger). Run-based and sandbox games often open straight into play or with short text rather than long cutscenes. Players form opinions in the first three to five minutes. Randomness and unique starts keep interest.

In Bohemia the same origin-based open stays. Text or short scene sets the company then the map. Each origin feels different. No long cutscene. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Origins and Peasant Militia pages, Dev Blog on company origins, and articles on first-minute player engagement. The wiki does not say exact first-minute timing or cutscene length.
