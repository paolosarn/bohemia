# BIG BEASTS AND LATE GAME UNITS STATS AND SKILLS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
A combat tile is house-sized. Melee is likely the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows reach 2, 3, or up to about 12-13 tiles.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the units. Action points and fatigue costs stay the way Battle Brothers does them. A boss is never an animal.

Lindwurm has 1100 health, 200 head armor, 400 body armor, 75-85 melee skill, 10 melee defense, -10 ranged defense, 180 resolve, 80 initiative, 400 fatigue, 30 fatigue recovery, and 7 action points. It occupies two tiles, one for the head and one for the tail. They share health, armor, fatigue, and morale. The head acts, then the tail acts right after. Gorge costs 4 action points and 15 fatigue for 80-140 damage at 2 tiles. Tail Slam variants cost 4 action points and 20-25 fatigue, hit multiple targets, and can daze or stun. Acid Blood sprays the next tile when melee deals 10 or more health damage, corroding armor 10 percent per turn for three turns. The tail ignores zone of control.

Schrat has 600 health, 70-75 melee skill, -5 melee defense, -5 ranged defense, 200 resolve, 60 initiative, 400 fatigue, 25 fatigue recovery, and 7 action points. Shielded reduces damage to 30 percent while the shield is up, and it takes only 25 percent from bolts and arrows. Grow Shield costs 5 action points and 50 fatigue. Uproot costs 5 action points and 25 fatigue, hits 3 tiles in a straight line for 70-100 damage, and staggers. Taking 60 damage or more spawns a Sapling on a free tile. Fire pots do triple damage.

Kraken has 3800 health and high armor. It is fixed in place. Devour instantly kills if you end your turn next to the mouth. Tentacles use Ensnare to root and drag, and Move Tentacle to reposition. The fight has phases based on health and tentacle deaths. Tentacles respawn and ignore zone of control.

Ifrit comes in sizes from 110 to 440 health. They take reduced damage from many ranged and piercing attacks. They can Merge Living Sands to combine three smaller ones into a larger one with full health, or Throw Living Sand to attack and split. Head hits redirect to the body.

Nachzehrer has 80 to 380 health depending on size. Gruesome Feast eats a corpse on the same tile to grow, heal, and improve morale. Swallow Whole removes a brother until the Nachzehrer dies, dealing limited non-lethal damage.

In Bohemia the same numbers and costs stay. The look changes to large machines, reassembling parts, and chip-bodies that match the analog horror stiffness. Melee stays the next tile. Ranged reaches further. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Lindwurm, Schrat, Beasts, Skills, Kraken, Ifrit, and Nachzehrer.
