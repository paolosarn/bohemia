## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers plays footsteps that change with the ground the party walks on. The wiki lists different movement speeds for plains, forest, mountains, and roads. The game also plays different sounds for those grounds. It plays the sound of a camp being made, the sound of a contract being accepted, the sound of a fight starting, and the sound of a brother dying. The wiki does not list every sound file or the exact moment each one plays. The data does not say the full list of every sound Battle Brothers plays and when.

Games that use sound well add layers the player does not notice until they are gone. A coin or battery click when a trade finishes. A page turn when the notes screen opens. A short UI tick when a button is pressed and a different tick when the button is disabled. Hover sounds that are quieter than the click. Ambience that changes by place and by hour. A market has distant voices and a cart wheel. A night road has insects and wind. A settlement at night has fewer voices and a generator. Footsteps that match the ground the character is standing on and that do not repeat the same sample twice in a row. A crowd that gets louder when more people are on screen and quieter when the player is alone. A door that sounds different if it is wood or metal. A gun that sounds different if it is fired indoors or outdoors.

Bohemia already has the rule that a sound is a recording of the real object or built from its real physics. The missing layers are the small UI ticks, the page and coin sounds, the full ambience stack per place and hour, and the footsteps that change with every ground type without repeating. These can be added without new art. They make the phone feel like a place instead of a menu.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Global Map terrain table; player descriptions of Battle Brothers sound design; common practice in games that use layered ambience and material-matched footsteps.
