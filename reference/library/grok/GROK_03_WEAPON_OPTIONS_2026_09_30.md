# ASK 3. WEAPON OPTIONS

Battle Brothers floor. His locks already in: pistol is a dagger (9/29). Car is the handgonne (this chat), reload stays, the shot spends resources because the shot is gas. I am not pricing anything. Everything costs one until he plays it.

Handgonne, from the wiki and the dev blog: 35-75 damage, 25% ignores armor, 90-100% vs armor, range 2 tiles, fire 3 AP, reload 9 AP. It cannot fire, reload, and move in one turn. It hits a cone and can strike tiles behind the target. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Weapons and https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Dev_Blog_132:_Gunpowder_Weapons

Firelance: one shot per fight, a line of fire, burns, refills after the battle.
Mortar: city-state tube, a blast on a tile, not a gun you carry in one hand.

## Options, by job

A. Dagger job. Close, fast, armor-piercing, one tile.
A1. Pistol. His 9/29 line. Already the default.
A2. A knife. Too plain. Only if the pistol feels like a gun and not a dagger.
A3. A stun gun or a nailer. Same range, worse story.

B. Handgonne job. Loud, short, cone, reload eats the turn, spends a pile.
B1. A car. His words. Ram or a shotgun-blast from the lane. Spends resources (gas).
B2. A junk cannon on a trailer. Same job, no driver fantasy. Weaker story than the car.
B3. A dump truck. Same as B1, heavier, slower reload.

C. Crossbow job. One hard shot, then a reload, accurate, punches armor. Range 6 in Battle Brothers. Light 30-50, normal 40-60, heavy 50-70.
C1. A rifle. Bolt-action. One shot, rack the next.
C2. A hunting bow is wrong. We are not in a forest.
C3. A scoped air rifle. Quiet. Analog horror. Weaker than C1.

D. Bow job. Many shots, weaker each, no reload.
D1. A semi-auto pistol. This fights A1. Do not use both.
D2. A nail gun on full. Fast, cheap, poor armor punch.
D3. Skip the bow. A phone fight does not need two rapid guns.

E. Spearwall job. Hold a tile, stop a charge.
E1. A spike strip and a parking bumper.
E2. A dog on a lead. The barbarian beastmaster already has this job.
E3. A drone that hovers the lane. Only if goblins are not already the drones.

F. Throw job. One hand, short, you run out.
F1. Bottles, bricks, flares.
F2. Grenades. Too loud for act 1. Save for city-state.
F3. Skip throw. The car and the rifle cover it.

G. Firelance job. One line of fire, then it is dead until the next fight.
G1. A flare gun or a road flare line.
G2. A propane jet from a food truck. One tank, then empty.
G3. Skip it. Horror does not need a flamethrower in the demo.

H. Mortar job. A blast on a tile you are not standing on.
H1. A trailer tube. City-state only.
H2. A drone drop. Only if goblins are drones.
H3. Skip until the demo has more than one fight.

I. Hammer and axe job. Break armor, break doors.
I1. A sledge and a halligan. Fire-crew tools. Tools pile repairs them.
I2. A car again. Do not give the car three jobs.
I3. A robot arm. Only if orcs are machines.

J. Shield job. A held wall.
J1. A car door.
J2. A riot shield from a dead casino cop.
J3. Skip shields. Phone fights are short. A door is enough.
