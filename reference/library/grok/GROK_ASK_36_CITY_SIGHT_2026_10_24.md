# ASK 36 HOW FAR YOU CAN SEE IN A CITY THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Research how far people can realistically see, because it's a city; you should only see things straight down a street you're near.

## MY PROPOSAL
Real city sight is short and blocked. Urban street design guidelines recommend clearing parking within 20 to 25 feet of intersections so drivers and pedestrians can see each other. Buildings, trees, parked cars, and signs cut lines of sight down a street. Night reduces visibility further because headlights and street lights only light limited distances. Games with city fog of war often clear only along the street the player is on and stop at corners or building blocks. Battle Brothers shows an encounter card before a fight that includes faces of units, numbers, terrain type, and a chance to fall back. The wiki does not say the exact pixel layout of that card. Followers are non-combat retinue members unlocked by renown that grant world-map benefits such as better contracts, more experience, or extra tools after fights. There are five slots.

In Bohemia the same rules stay. Sight in a city follows the street the company is on and stops at corners. The encounter card shows faces, numbers, terrain, and fall-back chance. Followers stay non-combat. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
