# ASK 38 MAP SIZE THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
The map should feel the right size the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers generates a hex world map at the start of each campaign. Vanilla size is listed in mod posts as 140 by 140 hexes. Beasts and Exploration increased it. Community data from about 10,000 seeds shows diameter ranging from the 140s to the 200s. Total size ranges from just above 10,000 to just above 18,500. Plains are the most common biome. A typical map has about 17 settlements split among three noble houses. Mountain ridges and other terrain act as barriers. Mods that raise SizeX and SizeY past 140 by 140 hit rendering limits when zoomed in. The wiki does not give the exact default SizeX and SizeY numbers.

In Bohemia the same generation stays. Map diameter falls in the same range. Settlements and roads follow the same placement rules. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Global Map page, Reddit seed data, and Nexus mod posts on map size. The wiki does not say the exact default hex count.
