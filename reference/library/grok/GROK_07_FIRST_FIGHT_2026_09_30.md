# VAMILY. THE FIRST FIGHT, HOW BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

Mechanics stay. Skin only.

Easy civilian contract: defend a settlement. You face brigands or zombies, whichever camp is closer. 20% chance someone is taken and you chase. 20% chance the local militia wants to take your orders. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Contracts

A fight starts with two lines, about 5 hexes apart. 9 action points. Flat step is 2. One-hand swing is 4. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_Mechanics

Brigand bands are the early human fight. A Russian wiki page says they walk in groups of about 6, and a thug has about 55 health and no perks. Treat the 6 and the 55 as recall-adjacent until the English page is pulled. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/ru/wiki/%D0%91%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%B4%D1%8B_%D1%80%D0%B0%D0%B7%D0%B1%D0%BE%D0%B9%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%B2

Zombies are the other early fight. Slow. Bite 15-35. 66% to stand once at half health unless the head is gone. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Wiederganger

## Skin for the demo fight

Humans stay people. Six road crew on a side street. Pipes, a car door, one pistol. Your side is a small hire crew. Same 9, same step, same swing.

If the closer camp is the dead: chip-bodies in gowns and vests. They do not shoot. Head shot keeps them down. A living person in the back can stand one up if the head is there.

Car is the handgonne. One shot, reload eats the turn, spends gas. Pistol is the dagger, 1 tile, spends ammunition.
