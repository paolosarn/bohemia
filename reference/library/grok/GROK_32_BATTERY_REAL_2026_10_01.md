# IS 10 CROWNS TO 1 BATTERY REAL

Paolo asked. The skin is real. The grain is not.

A dead grid makes charge into money. Vegas already runs on it. Phones, cars, clinics, doors. People trade cells when cash dies. That part holds.

A battery in this rate is not a car battery. 10 crowns is a small coin. A per-head bounty is 10 crowns, so 1 battery. A level adds 2 crowns, so a fifth of a battery. If a battery is a truck cell, the small spends vanish. If a battery is a phone cell, or a unit of charge, the rate holds.

Shop prices still get the town markup first, then divide by 10.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: The 10 to 1 is his. The grain problem is the math of his rate against daily pay.

WHAT IS NOT: He has not said the battery is a cell or a unit of charge.
