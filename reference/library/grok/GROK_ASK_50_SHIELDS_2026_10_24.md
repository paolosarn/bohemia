## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers gives shields durability, melee defense, and ranged defense. Blocking a normal attack costs the shield 1 durability. Split Shield skills deal their listed damage and always hit. Broken shields can appear as loot at zero and be repaired. Living Tree Shield regenerates 50 percent each turn. The wiki does not list every edge case for every shield type.
