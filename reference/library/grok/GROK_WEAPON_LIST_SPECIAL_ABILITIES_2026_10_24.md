# WEAPON LIST AND SPECIAL ABILITIES THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
I need you to do true research on all battle, Brothers weapons, and the special abilities, each weapon type pass and then we can start understanding what awesome translations can come from that. A combat tile is house-sized. Melee is likely the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows reach 2, 3, or up to about 12-13 tiles.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the weapons. Action points, fatigue costs, and ranges stay the way Battle Brothers does them. Melee is the next tile. Polearms reach two tiles. Bows reach about 6 to 7 tiles. Crossbows reach 6 tiles. Handgonnes reach 2 tiles and hit a cone.

One-handed swords use Slash for 4 action points and 10 fatigue with a 10 percent hit bonus. Riposte costs 4 action points and 25 fatigue and counters until the next turn. Two-handed swords use Overhead Strike for 6 action points and 15 fatigue, Split for two targets in a line, and Swing for up to three targets. Axes use Chop for 4 action points and 13 fatigue with extra head damage, and Split Shield for direct shield damage. Two-handed axes use Split Man for 6 action points and 15 fatigue that hits head and body. Cleavers use Cleave for 4 action points and 12 fatigue that causes bleeding, and Decapitate that scales with lost health and can remove the head. Whips reach 3 tiles and can Disarm. Spears use Thrust for 4 action points and 10 fatigue with a 20 percent hit bonus, and Spearwall that can stop charges. Maces use Bash that adds fatigue and Knock Out that can stun. Flails ignore shield defense.

Polearms use Strike or Impale for 6 action points and 15 fatigue at two tiles. Billhooks use Hook to pull a target closer and stagger. Pikes use Repel to push a target away. Reap hits up to three targets. Polearm Mastery reduces the cost to 5 action points.

Bows use Quick Shot for 4 action points and 15 fatigue and Aimed Shot for 7 action points and 20 fatigue. Range is 6 or 7 tiles. Crossbows use Shoot Bolt for 3 action points and 5 fatigue and Reload for 4 action points and 20 fatigue. Range is 6 tiles. Handgonnes use Fire Handgonne for 3 action points and 5 fatigue that can hit multiple targets in a cone, and Reload for 9 action points and 20 fatigue. Range is 2 tiles. Throwing weapons reach about 4 tiles.

In Bohemia the same numbers, costs, and ranges stay. The look changes to modern tools, blades, poles, bows that become guns or drones, and cars or handgonnes that still reload and use ammo. A car can be the handgonne equivalent. Ammo can be pulled with fuel. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
