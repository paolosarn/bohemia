# ASK 32. WHAT WE DO NOT KNOW

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Know what you don't know. What features need lots and lots of rounds of research.

## MY PROPOSAL
The master file and the vote record show several systems that are still weak or missing. The map still looks like one sand color and does not match Battle Brothers density at the far zoom. The settlement screen does not yet show the lead walking up to a person while the talk opens. Portraits are still ugly and do not match the body in skin tone, hair, or build. Sounds still contain crushed-sand and glass-jar reverb on footsteps and doors. Combat still has no confirmed solution for showing both teams at the same time on a phone in portrait and on a laptop in widescreen without black bars or a swipe. The 3D-to-pixel style that was liked on the robot and the thug has not been applied to the rest of the roster, the beasts, or the buildings. The economy translation is locked but the actual daily costs, the scavenging button, and the settlement growth visuals are not in the playable build. The three-generation flip is locked but the visual and audio filter that must hit every UI element when the player switches acts is not designed. The haunting side content and the Backrooms are named but have no contracts or maps.

These systems need more than one page of research. The map color and density need a full pass against real Las Vegas satellite photos and against Battle Brothers screenshots at the same zoom. The settlement walk-up needs a timing test so the talk window opens in the same number of frames the lead takes to walk. The portrait and body match needs a style guide that is enforced by a gate before any new face is shown. The sound layers need a material list and a no-repeat rule that is tested on every ground type. The combat camera needs a prototype that shows four tiles north of the top fighter and four tiles south of the bottom fighter on both phone orientations with no black. The 3D-to-pixel pipeline needs one full character, one beast, and one building finished and compared side by side before it is called the method. Each of these will take multiple rounds. None of them are solved by a single research page.
