# ASK 90 THE DAY LOOP AND CAMPING THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
The day loop and camping stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers runs a real-time day on the map. Wages pay at noon. Food is eaten at noon. Night slows travel and cuts sight. Camping speeds repair and healing but raises the chance of an ambush. Vendors close at night. The wiki does not list every hour tick or every camp modifier.

In Bohemia the same day loop and camping system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
