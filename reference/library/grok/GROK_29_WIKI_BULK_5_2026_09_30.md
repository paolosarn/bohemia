# WIKI BULK 5. TRAITS, DOGS, NAMED GEAR

Traits. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Traits
Loyal is 50% less likely to desert. Disloyal is 100% more. Greedy wants more pay. Glutton eats 3. Spartan eats 1. Iron Lungs recovers 3 extra fatigue. Asthmatic recovers 3 less. Survivor has a 90% chance to live at 0 health and takes a permanent injury. Player character takes no wage and the run ends if he dies. Drunkard +10% hit. Dumb -15% hit. Bright +10% hit. Huge +10% damage. Tiny +5 defense and -15% hit. Fear of a faction is -10 morale in that fight. Hate is +10. Impatient acts first in round one.

Dog. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Wardog
Bite is 20-35. You let it go and it runs. It wants runners, shooters, and anyone not in a fight. You do not steer it. Armor is 55 or 85.

Named gear. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Named_and_Legendary_Items
A named weapon always rolls durability, 0.9 to 1.4, plus two other stats. Damage 1.1 to 1.3. Armor damage +10% to 30%. Head chance +10% to 20%. Named armor durability 1.1 to 1.25 and a lighter fatigue penalty. Legendary pieces are fixed. Emperor's armor is 400 and reflects 25%.
