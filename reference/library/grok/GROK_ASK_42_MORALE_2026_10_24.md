# ASK 42 MORALE THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Morale stays the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers uses six morale states: Unbreakable (undead only), Confident, Steady, Wavering, Breaking, and Fleeing. Steady is the default for living units. Good mood can start a brother at Confident. Bad mood lowers the maximum. Each state multiplies melee skill, ranged skill, melee defense, and ranged defense (1.1 for Confident, 0.9 for Wavering, 0.8 for Breaking, 0.7 for Fleeing). Fleeing units lose control, run from enemies, and act last. Morale checks raise or lower one state. Positive checks happen on enemy death or Rally. Negative checks happen on 15 or more hitpoint damage, ally death, or being surrounded. Resolve is the base chance. Maximum success is 95 percent. The wiki does not list every exact modifier for every check.

In Bohemia the same states and checks stay. Undead-style units stay Unbreakable. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
