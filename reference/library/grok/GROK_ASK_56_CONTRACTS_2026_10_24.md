# ASK 56 CONTRACTS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Contracts stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers offers contracts in settlements. The player takes one at a time. Completing a contract pays crowns, raises renown, and improves relations. Failing or canceling lowers both. Pay scales with type, skull difficulty, renown, and a small random factor. Some contracts are civilian and some are noble. Contracts can branch on random chance, player choices, or past actions. The wiki does not list every edge case for every contract.

In Bohemia the same contract system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Contracts and Game Guide pages, and developer notes. The wiki does not say every edge case.
