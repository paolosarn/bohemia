## MY PROPOSAL

Tiles. Junk workplace. Rusted metal sheets. Cracked floors. Stuck green clay cartridges. Scrap piles. Dented pipes. Barbed wire. Conveyor scraps. Same limited palette. Soft & Dirty or Dry Sea. Visible steps. Grain baked in. Square house tiles. No glow. The machine is a workplace of junk.

Word Shake. Chef Pierre is a live-action French chef in a nickelodeon machine. He mixes words. The kitchen explodes with flour. He runs and laughs. Black and white. Mixed media. The explosion is the analog. Parts fly. The workplace falls apart.

Dither and grain. Checkerboard or Bayer for texture on rust and clay. Not on small faces. Bake the grain. Static across frames so it does not swim. Random noise only for dirt. Limited palette 16 to 32. Squint test. Value first. Nothing glows.

### COOK

One junk tile set with stuck cartridges and rust. One Word Shake style flour explosion. One dithered rust surface and one grain baked clay face. Lock these against the earlier sheets. The art coordinator can cook them. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows.
