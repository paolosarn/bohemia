# ASK 49 WEAPON DURABILITY THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Weapon durability stays the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers lowers melee weapon durability by 3 when it hits armor that has at least 50 durability left. Hits on flesh do not damage the weapon. Area attacks lose durability per hit. Bows and crossbows lose 2 durability per shot. Shields lose durability on blocks and split attacks. At zero durability the weapon breaks and is lost unless Blacksmith is present. Broken items can appear as loot at zero and be repaired. Enemy weapons do not lose durability in battle. The wiki does not list every edge case for every weapon.

In Bohemia the same durability loss stays. Break at zero stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
