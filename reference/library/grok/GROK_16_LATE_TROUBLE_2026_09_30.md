# VAMILY. THE LATE TROUBLE

You can pick the first crisis, or leave it random. A warning lands around day 50 to 70. It starts around day 80 to 100. The same one does not happen twice in a row. If permanent destruction is on, greenskins and undead can wipe a settlement. If it is off, the town gets a bad situation instead. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Late_Game_Crises

Noble war: houses fight. They take towns. Towns do not die. The wiki calls this the safer first crisis. Jobs are a big battle, a raid, a siege, a rescue.
Undead scourge: old dead wake. More dead camps. More raids.
Greenskin invasion: orcs and goblins. Can wipe towns if destruction is on.
Holy war: north against south. Three holy sites. Towns are not the prize.

Skin, not a lock: compound war is the noble war. Chip-bodies rising is the scourge. Yard machines and drones flooding a district is the greenskin invasion. A north-strip fight over three sites is the holy war. His pace stands. First big trouble around day 100.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: The days and the four crises are from the wiki. Day 100 is his.

WHAT IS NOT: The skins. I did not lock a first crisis for the demo.
