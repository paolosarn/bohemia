## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for traits and the game guide. New recruits may have 0 to 2 randomly selected traits. Traits can be positive, negative, or mixed. Some backgrounds disallow certain traits. Some traits disallow others. Traits can trigger special events or new event options. Some traits can be gained or lost through events.

Examples of positive traits include Iron Lungs (recovers an extra 3 fatigue every turn), Determined (starts combat at confident morale), Strong (+10 fatigue), Impatient (acts first in the first round), Survivor (90 percent chance to survive at 0 health, gains permanent injury), Deathwish (no morale check on losing hitpoints), Iron Jaw (+25 percent injury threshold), Bright (+10 percent experience gain), and Athletic (builds 2 less fatigue per tile travelled).

Examples of negative traits include Asthmatic (-3 fatigue recovery per turn), Craven (-10 resolve), Fragile (-10 hitpoints), Clubfooted (+2 fatigue per tile travelled), Clumsy (-5 melee skill), Dastard (starts at wavering morale), and Disloyal (more likely to desert).

The negative pain trait stays the same. Do not invent a different hurt name per body part.

In Bohemia the same trait list, effects, and event rules stay. Traits show on the hire row before payment. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Traits, Iron Lungs, and the Game Guide.
