# SETTLEMENT MENU

He said lean into the settlement menu. The hire sheet was the wrong cut. This is the menu you open when you walk into a block.

Battle Brothers buildings, wiki. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Settlement_buildings

1. Marketplace. Buy and sell tools, medicine, food, weapons, armor. Broken gear sells for less. Stock refreshes about every 3 days, and after some jobs.
2. Recruits. The hire list. Try out, then buy. Indebted are bought here when a manhunter lot is attached.
3. Tavern. Buy a round. Mood up. Drunk can get them killed. Price scales with crew size.
4. Temple. Pay to cut injury days. A 2-4 day wound becomes 1-2. A 1-3 becomes 1.
5. Arena. Three men. No retreat. Smaller map. South, mostly.
6. Kennel. Buy a dog. Not a pet. A fight dog.
7. Contract board. The elder, the house, the block. One job at a time.

Attached lots change the menu. A furnace means better armor in the market. A watchtower means militia. A tavern is guaranteed in a real town. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Settlements_and_attached_locations

Skin, same menu. Market is the stall. Recruits is the post wall, and the bought men are on it. Tavern is the bar. Temple is the clinic. Arena is the pit. Kennel is the dog lot. The board is the job.

The slice opens this menu after the fight. Not a single hire card.
