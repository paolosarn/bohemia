# ASK 47 DAMAGE THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Damage stays the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers rolls two independent damage numbers from the weapon range. One is for armor and one is for hit points. Armor damage is multiplied by the weapon's armor effectiveness. Hit point damage that ignores armor is reduced by 10 percent of remaining armor. If armor is destroyed, leftover damage flows through. Head hits deal 150 percent hit point damage. Multipliers from traits and perks stack. The wiki does not list every edge case for every skill.

In Bohemia the same damage rolls stay. Separate armor and hit point damage stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
