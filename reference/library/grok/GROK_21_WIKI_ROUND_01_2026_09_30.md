# WIKI ROUND 1. SETTLEMENT BUILDINGS

Hire is an icon on the settlement menu. Click it and you get a list of people. Not a separate screen I invented. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Settlement_buildings

The hiring page URL 404'd. The building is Recruits.

Alchemist. Exotic gear for sale.
Arena. Smaller map. Three men. You see who you fight. Wait a day for a new match. Every fifth match is harder and can drop gladiator gear you cannot buy.
Armorer. High armor and shields, full durability, about 25% over market. Alt-click repairs without tools, expensive.
Barber. Hair, beard, scars.
Fletcher. Bows, crossbows, javelins, throwing axes, ammo. About 25% over market.
Harbor. Sail to another port. Price is distance times crew size.
Kennel. Fight dogs only.
Marketplace. Tools, medicine, food, weapons, armor. Broken gear sells for less. Stock refreshes about every 3 days, and after some jobs.
Recruits. The list of people. This is the icon.
Tavern. Drinks for mood. Drunk can get them killed, then a hangover. Price scales with crew size. Rumors, pay for drinks up to 4 times a visit.
Taxidermist. Craft from trophies.
Temple. Pay to cut injury days. 2-4 becomes 1-2. 1-3 becomes 1.
Training Hall. Pay for an XP boost. Sparring +50% next fight. Veteran's Lessons +35% next 3. Rigorous Schooling +20% next 5. A level 1 lesson was 253 crowns. A level 10 was 1106.
Weaponsmith. Good weapons, sometimes named. About 25% over market. Alt-click repairs without tools.
