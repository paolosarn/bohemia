# ASK 33. WHAT BATTLE BROTHERS LEAVES ON THE GROUND

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Every visual effect of a hit and a death in its fights, exactly: the hit flash, blood spray by damage, blood decals on tiles and how long they stay, the pool under the fallen, corpses and what they keep, dropped gear, severed heads, what the undead and beasts leave instead.

## MY PROPOSAL
The Battle Brothers wiki page on Fatality describes three main types of killing blows. Decapitated comes from slashing damage to the head. Smashed comes from blunt damage to the head. Disemboweled comes from slashing damage to the body. Destroying or cutting off the head prevents a corpse from rising as a Wiederganger. The wiki page on Permanent Injuries states that a character whose hit points reach zero has a chance of surviving with a permanent injury unless the last hit was a fatality. The combat log shows "struck down" instead of "killed" when a brother survives. The wiki page on Bloodthirsty states that finishing hits to the head with a slashing weapon cause decapitation, to the head with a crushing weapon cause a smashed head, and to the body with a slashing weapon cause disembowelment. The developer blog on fatalities notes that decapitation or a smashed head prevents necromancers from raising the body in most cases.

The wiki does not say the color or duration of the hit flash. It does not say how the blood spray scales with damage. It does not say how long blood decals stay on tiles or how large the pool under a fallen body is. It does not say what a corpse keeps visually or how long it remains before the fight ends. It does not say the exact appearance of a severed head or what undead and beasts leave on the ground instead of blood. A Nexus mod called More Blood increases blood spray and splatter, adds more blood decals on the ground and bodies, and increases blood particle effects from attacks and decapitations. The mod description states that the base game is restrained and that the mod doubles the amount of blood spouting and quadruples blood puddles. Players and the combat tutorial video show that gear remains on the body for looting after the fight and that destroyed armor cannot be taken. Corpses stay until the fight ends so that the player can collect dropped items.

In Bohemia the same rules apply. A hit produces a short flash and a spray whose size matches the damage. Blood marks the tile and fades over the fight. A fatal hit leaves the body and any gear it still holds. Destroying the head stops a chip-body from rising. Beasts leave their own marks instead of blood. The player sees the result on the ground before the next turn.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages Fatality, Permanent Injuries, Bloodthirsty, and Combat Mechanics; developer blog on fatalities; Nexus mod More Blood; player descriptions and the official combat tutorial video.
