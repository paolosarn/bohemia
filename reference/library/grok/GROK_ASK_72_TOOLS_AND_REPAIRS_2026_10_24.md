# ASK 72 TOOLS AND REPAIRS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Tools and repairs stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers uses tools to repair item durability. One point of tools repairs 15 points of durability. Equipped items and bag items repair automatically. Stash items must be marked. Repair happens over time at a set rate per hour. Camping and certain contracts double the rate. The Blacksmith increases the rate. Running out of tools stops repairs. Weapons that hit zero durability break and are lost unless the Blacksmith prevents it. The wiki does not list every edge case for every item or follower.

In Bohemia the same tools and repair system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Tools and Supplies page. The wiki does not say every edge case.
