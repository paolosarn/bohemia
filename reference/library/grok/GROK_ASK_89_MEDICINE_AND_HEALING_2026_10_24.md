# ASK 89 MEDICINE AND HEALING THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Medicine and healing stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers uses medical supplies for temporary injuries. One point is spent each day per injury. Hit points heal on their own. A temple halves the healing time for a fee. Bandages stop bleeding during the fight they were taken. Permanent injuries need the Water Skin. The wiki does not list every healing rate or every injury duration.

In Bohemia the same medicine and healing system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Medical Supplies and Temporary Injuries pages. The wiki does not say every rate.
