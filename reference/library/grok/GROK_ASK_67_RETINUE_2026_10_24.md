## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers lets the company hire up to five non-combat followers. Slots unlock with renown. Each follower costs crowns and gives a lasting bonus outside combat. Some require special unlocks. Followers can be swapped for a cost. The system also includes cart upgrades for inventory space. The wiki does not list every edge case for every follower.
