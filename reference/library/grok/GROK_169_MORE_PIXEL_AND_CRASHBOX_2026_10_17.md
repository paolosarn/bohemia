## MY PROPOSAL

More practical rules. Linked cels in Aseprite share one image across frames. Use them for held poses and parts that do not move. Edit one and all update. Tags name each animation. Export by tag so the code gets clean sheets. Frame duration holds the key poses longer. 80 to 120 ms for most frames. 200 ms for holds. Preview at 12 fps. No tween.

Dithering and grain. Checkerboard or light random dither between close values adds texture without new colors. Use it for rust, dirt, clay ridges, and soft grain. Do not dither everything. Block flat first. Add pattern only where the surface needs it. Random noise works for junk and rust if it is intentional. Grain is baked in. Do not add a filter later. Value still does the work. Shadows cooler. Highlights warmer. Nothing glows.

More Crashbox look. The Revolting Slob is a fat puppet with pale skin and a dirty outfit that shows the stomach. He does disgusting things. He explodes at the end in a cloud of smoke. Season 1 he is grumpy in a living room. Season 2 he is exuberant in a kitchen. Riddlesnake is a pinkish-purple coiled wooden snake with a turban and a flute. He does not speak. The raj does. The body is stiff and only the head and tail move. Both are wrong and handmade. The stiffness and the parts that do not fit stay the analog.
