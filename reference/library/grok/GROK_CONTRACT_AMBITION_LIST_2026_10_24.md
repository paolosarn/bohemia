## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for contracts, ambitions, renown, and the game guide. Early civilian contracts include Return Item (base value 400), Deliver Item, Discover Location, Defend Settlement, and Roaming Beasts. Completing one gives +25 renown. Failing gives -75. Canceling gives -100. Betrayal gives -200. Winning a fight gives +5. Destroying a location gives +10. An ambition completed gives +100. Daily renown decay is -3. Renown levels include Professional at 1050, which unlocks noble contracts after the related ambition.

Contract pay scales with type, a random 90 to 110 percent modifier, skull difficulty, and renown. Renown does not raise difficulty. Difficulty scales with roster strength (10 + 2 times (level minus 1) for the 12 strongest), skulls, and contract type. Negotiations let you shift pay to advance, completion, or per head. One active contract at a time.

Early ambitions include Allied Civilians (friendly relations), roster of at least 12 men, at least 2000 crowns for a battle standard, discover and destroy a ruin or camp, one man with Rally the Troops, win a battle against 12 or more enemies, and Reach Professional renown at 1050. Completing one raises mood and renown.

In Bohemia the same contract types, renown changes, and early ambitions stay. Renown is Clout. Crowns convert at 10 to 1 battery. The settlement menu stays the lean. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Contracts, Ambitions, Renown, Factions and Relations, and the Game Guide.
