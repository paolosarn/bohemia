# CHARACTER BACKGROUND LIST THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for character backgrounds and the game guide. Backgrounds define a character's story, starting attributes, equipment, possible traits, and special events. Attributes are randomized inside ranges. Apprentice is the baseline with no modifiers. Daily wages start from a base (roughly 3 to 35 crowns) multiplied by a random 90 to 110 percent, then scale with level (1.1 to the power of level minus 1 for the first 11 levels). Hire cost includes background, starting gear, and level.

Examples include Sellsword (high melee and defense, wage 35, level 2-4), Hunter (high ranged, wage around 20), Indebted (purchased people working off a debt, low stats, wage low), Farmhand (high hitpoints and fatigue), and Hedge Knight (very expensive, high combat stats).

Attached locations and situations change which backgrounds appear for hire. The hire row shows stats, stars, traits, and background before payment.

In Bohemia the same background list, attribute ranges, wage scaling, and hire display stay. Indebted stay purchased people working off a debt. Crowns convert at 10 to 1 battery. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Character Backgrounds, Character Backgrounds/Attribute Ranges, and the Game Guide.
