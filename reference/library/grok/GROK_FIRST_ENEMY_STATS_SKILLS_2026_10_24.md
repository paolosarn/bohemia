## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the units. Action points and fatigue costs stay the way Battle Brothers does them.

Brigand Thug has 55 health, 55 melee skill, 45 ranged skill, 0 melee defense, 0 ranged defense, 40 resolve, 95 initiative, 95 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. The wiki page lists Hand-to-Hand Attack as 4 action points and 5 fatigue for 5-10 damage. Equipment is light and random. They are early-game and easy to hit.

Orc Young has 125 health, 60 melee skill, 50-55 ranged skill, -5 melee defense, -5 ranged defense, 65 resolve, 120 initiative, 150 fatigue, 25 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Charge costs 4 action points and 25 fatigue, moves the orc next to a brother within 3 tiles, and can stun. The chance starts at 100 percent and drops by melee defense from shields and Shieldwall. Orc Hand-to-Hand Attack is 4 action points and 5 fatigue for 15-30 damage. They charge first and can be blocked by Spearwall.

Orc Warrior has 200 health, 70-75 melee skill, 40 ranged skill, -10 melee defense, -10 ranged defense, 75 resolve, 120 initiative, 200 fatigue, 30 fatigue recovery, and 8 action points. Line Breaker costs 4 action points and 30 fatigue, knocks a target 1 tile back, never misses, and removes Shieldwall, Spearwall, and Riposte. They wear heavy armor and push through lines.

Goblin Skirmisher has 40 health, 75 melee skill, 65-70 ranged skill, 20-25 melee defense, 10-15 ranged defense, 55 resolve, 130 initiative, 100 fatigue, 20 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. They use throwing weapons and nets, Footwork often, and stay fragile but evasive. All goblins have Footwork with fatigue cost halved.

Wiederganger has 100 health, 45 melee skill, -5 melee defense, -5 ranged defense, 45 initiative, and 6 action points. Bite costs 4 action points and 0 fatigue for 15-35 damage. They rise again once with 50 percent health unless the head is destroyed. They walk slowly and come in numbers. Undead do not accumulate fatigue and are not affected by morale.

In Bohemia the same numbers and costs stay. The look changes to scavenger crews, heavy robots that charge or push, small drones that throw and net, and chip-bodies that keep moving after the person is down. Melee stays the next tile. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Brigand Thug, Brigands, Orc Young, Orc Warrior, Orcs, Goblin Skirmisher, Goblins, Wiederganger, Zombies, Skills, and Undead.
