# ASK 69 COMPANY ROSTER AND FIELDING THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Company roster and fielding stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers starts most companies with a small number of men. The roster can grow to 20. Only 12 can be fielded in a battle. Peasant Militia and Manhunters raise the roster to 25 and the field limit to 16. Contract difficulty scales with the strength of the 12 strongest brothers. Extra men on the roster serve as reserves. The wiki does not list every edge case for every origin.

In Bohemia the same roster and fielding system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Game Mechanics and origin pages. The wiki does not say every edge case.
