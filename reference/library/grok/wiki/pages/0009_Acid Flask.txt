== Description ==
''A flask filled with the concentrate of a Lindwurm's acidic blood. It is highly corrosive to many materials and burns through armor quickly. Can be thrown at short ranges.''

== Where Can They Be Found? ==
* Can be bought from an [[Alchemist]].
* Can be crafted at a [[Taxidermist]] from [[File:Inventory_lindwurm_blood.png|25px|bottom|Lindwurm Blood (Lindwurm)]] [[Lindwurm Blood]] for 50 [[File:Crowns.png|bottom|25px]].

== Notes ==
* '''Effect''':
** Reduces the affected target's current body and head armor value by 20%.
** Armor damage is dealt after affected unit's turn.
** Effect lasts for a maximum of 3 turns.
* '''Protection''':
** [[File:Upgrade_06.png|25px|bottom]] [[Bone Platings]] armor attachment protects the body armor for 1 turn.
** [[File:Upgrade_04.png|25px|bottom]] [[Lindwurm Scale Cloak]] armor attachment negates effect for the body armor.
** [[File:Inventory_body_armor_82.png|20px|bottom]] [[Lindwurm Armor]] and [[File:Inventory_helmet_152.png|25px|bottom]] [[Lindwurm Helmet]] are completely unaffected by the acid.
** [[File:Perk_03.png|25px|bottom]] [[Battle Forged]] perk doesn't lower armor damage.
** [[File:Perk 04.png|25px|bottom]] [[Resilient]] perk lowers effect duration to 1 turn.
* Carried in offhand in shield slot. Shield slot is free again after usage.
* Can be used while engaged in melee, although risking possible collateral armor damage.
* [[File:Perk_50.png|25px|bottom]] [[Throwing Mastery]] perk lowers [[File:Fatigue.png|25px|Fatigue]][[Attributes#Fatigue|Fatigue]] build-up to 15.
