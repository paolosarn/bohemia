==Where can it be found?==
* Can be bought at a [[Settlement_buildings#Marketplace|Marketplace]] in settlements with [[Settlements_and_attached_locations#Watchtowers|Wooden Watchtower]], [[Settlements_and_attached_locations#Watchtowers|Stone Watchtower]], [[Settlements_and_attached_locations#Barracks|Militia Barracks]], [[Settlements_and_attached_locations#Barracks|Barracks]].
* Can be looted from [[Brigand Thug]], [[Mercenary|Mercenary (ranged)]], [[Bounty Hunter|Bounty Hunter (ranged)]], [[Militia]], [[Militia|Militia (ranged)]], [[Militia Veteran]], [[Caravan Hand (Enemy)|Caravan Hand]], [[Caravan Guard]], [[Billman]], [[Arbalester]], [[Wiederganger]], [[Armored Wiederganger]].
* Possible starting gear of new mercenaries: [[Companion (Background)|Companion (1h)]], [[Deserter (Background)|Deserter]], [[Disowned Noble (Background)|Disowned Noble]], [[Militia (Background)|Militia]], [[Retired Soldier (Background)|Retired Soldier]], [[Squire (Background)|Squire]].
* Can be found in the [[Dogs Dig up Loot]] event.
* It is one of the suitable items required to craft the [[Cultist Leather Hood]].
