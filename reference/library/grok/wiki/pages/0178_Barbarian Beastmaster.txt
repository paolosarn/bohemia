==Behavior==
The main job of Beastmasters is controlling [[Armored Unhold|Armored Unholds]]. One use of Crack the Whip whips all Armored Unholds at once without range limit.

Beastmasters may approach under protection of other barbarians and Unholds, and use their [[Thorned Whip|whips]] against you directly.

==Tips==

*Difficult to hit with [[Ranged Weapons|ranged weapons]].
*Can not [[Crack the Whip (Barbarian Beastmaster)|Crack the Whip]] in melee.
*They can use [[Barbarian Fury (5 fatigue)|Barbarian Fury]] to escape melee.
*They can't destroy armor or hurt through it. However they try to use [[Disarm (25 fatigue)|Disarm]] or attack someone whose armor got destroyed in a range of 3 tiles.
*If not whipped, an [[Armored Unhold]] has a 33% chance to turn wild at the start of its turn.
*Uncontrolled Armored Unholds are hostile to barbarians and controlled Armored Unholds, but not to each other.
*Whipped Armored Unholds still retain their wild status until their next turn (verify?).
*Can be [[Daggers|punctured]] for their armor.
