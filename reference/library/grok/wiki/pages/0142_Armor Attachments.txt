
Armor Attachments can be used to augment body armor, boosting the stats of the equipment in some way. Attachments are very powerful: they can add durability, reduce stamina penalty, scare enemies, or even prevent incoming damage.

Attachments can be applied to the body armor only, which can benefit from only one attachment at a time. Replacing a current attachment will irrecoverably destroy it in favor of a new one being applied.

To apply an armor attachment, the player must select a character that's wearing a body armor and then either drag the attachment onto the body armor or simply right-click the attachment.
