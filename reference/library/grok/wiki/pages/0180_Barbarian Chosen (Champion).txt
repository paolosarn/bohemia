==Tips==

* Tougher, more brave and accurate and deals more damage than a regular Chosen, especially with a [[Named Weapons|named weapon]], otherwise similar.
* Usually you see him among many other Chosen. These are numerous but easier to kill, so deal with them first.
* A named weapon makes him more dangerous. Control him by [[Maces|stunning]] or [[Battle Whip|disarming]]. He counters it by acting ahead of you and waiting, and uses [[Adrenaline (20 fatigue)|Adrenaline]] to get ahead. If he does it, you can only stun to prevent Adrenaline.
* [[Battle Forged]] brothers must have [[Steel Brow]] to avoid death in one hit.
* [[Named Rusty Warblade]] is deadly to [[Nimble]] brothers and even to Battle Forged, if generates with very good stats.
