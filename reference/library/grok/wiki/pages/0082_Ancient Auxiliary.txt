== Behavior ==
These enemies often [[Shieldwall (20 fatigue)|shieldwall]] to protect themselves.

== Tips ==

* Early game enemies.
* Fragile and unarmored, they rely on shieldwall which they can maintain indefinitely. Low level brothers with poor melee skill have trouble hitting them when they are shieldwalling.
* [[Flails]] are the best weapons against them because they bypass their shields. [[Swords]] have +10 to hit. [[Spears]] have +20 to hit but deal piercing damage which they resist. [[Ranged Weapons|Ranged weapons]] are almost useless, except [[Bundle of Throwing Axes|throwing axes]].
* [[Woodcutter's Axe]] does 15 damage to their shield, leaving it with 1 durability, and subsequent hit with any weapon destroys it. [[Hatchet]] needs 4 hits. [[Crude Axe]] and [[Handaxe]] need 3.
* [[Fast Adaptation]] can help hitting them.
* They have [[Fearsome]], and in the early game you have nothing to counter it.
* Wait and move two tiles back on your first turn. Wait on the second turn, enemies will approach with no shieldwall. You have two turns in a row to attack them.
