==Behavior==
Unholds usually approach with their [[Unstoppable Charge (30 fatigue)|Unstoppable Charge]], that can't be repelled by a spearwall. They prefer fighting with a regular attack, but can use [[Fling Back (20 fatigue)|Fling Back]] to get close to a vulnerable brother, take the high ground, cancel a shieldwall, or kill someone with 10 HP or less.

It's impossible to swap places with Unholds with [[Barbarian Fury (5 fatigue)|Barbarian Fury]].

If not [[Crack the Whip (Barbarian Beastmaster)|whipped]], an Armored Unhold has a 33% chance to turn wild at the start of its turn. Uncontrolled Armored Unholds are hostile to barbarians and controlled Armored Unholds, but not to each other, and they are allied with other [[Beasts]] in a three-way fight. Whipped Armored Unholds still retain their wild status until their next turn (verify?).

Controlled and wild Armored Unholds can be distinguished by their tokens' bases. Barbarians' base means it's controlled, and Beasts' base indicates it's wild.

==Tips==

*Normal Armored Unholds are similar to unarmored Unholds, but Armored Frost Unholds have very heavy armor that requires anti-armor weapons to destroy.
*Low priority targets because of their toughness.
