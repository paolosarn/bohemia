== Description ==
A grisly aspect of Davkul, an ancient power not from this world, and the last remnants of <span style="color:olive">brother name</span> from whose body it has been fashioned. It shall never break, but instead keep regrowing its scarred skin on the spot.

==Where can it be found?==
* Via the [[Events#cultist_finale|Cultist Finale]] or [[Events#cultist_origin_finale|Cultist Origin Finale]] event with [[Cultist (Background)|Cultist]] brothers.

Requirements for the event:

*it has to be night time
*201 days or older campaign
*roster of at least 12 men
*at least 1 open inventory slot
*have 6 or more cultists (converted or true)
*1 level 11+ cultist among them
*1 level 11+ non-cultists to sacrifice.

Requirements for the event if you play the [[Davkul Cultists]] origin:

*it has to be night time
*151 days or older campaign
*roster of at least 12 men
*at least 1 open inventory slot
*have 6 or more cultists (converted or true)
*2 level 11+ cultists (the sacrifice is a random cultist among those excluding the best one)
