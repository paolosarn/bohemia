== Behavior ==
You must fight him one on one. The game lets you choose one of four most experienced brothers who are not currently in reserve. Unlike most enemies, he prioritizes attacking a [[Wardog|dog]] over a brother. He is excluded from [[morale]] checks.

His armor does not drop. You always receive [[Broken Ritual Armor]] no matter what.

His token's base is the one used for [[beasts]], and battle with him counts as battle with beasts.

==Tips==

* This fight is especially random and can kill a high-level brother with bad enough luck.
* Popular weapon choices against him are [[Two-Handed Hammer|two-handed hammers]], [[Throwing Weapons|throwing weapons]] and hard-hitting [[Named and Legendary Items|named]] two-handed weapons.
* [[Throwing Net|Nets]] and an armored [[Wardog|dog]] distract him for a while, winning time.
* Being unaffected by [[morale]], he does not gain confidence after killing a dog.
* The [[Named Rusty Warblade|named cleaver]] he wields is more dangerous to [[Nimble]] than to [[Battle Forged]] brothers.
