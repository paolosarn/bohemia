}}Ancient Honor Guard is a heavily armored enemy from the [[Ancient Dead]] faction.

Can become a [[Ancient Honor Guard (Champion)|champion]] with a 1% chance.

== Equipment ==
* An [[Ancient Sword]], [[Khopesh]], [[Rhomphaia]], [[Crypt Cleaver]] or [[Warscythe]].
* An [[Ancient Plated Scale Hauberk]] (210), [[Ancient Plate Harness]] (200), [[Ancient Scale Coat]] (190) or [[Ancient Plated Mail Hauberk]] (180).
* An [[Ancient Honor Guard Helmet]] (180).
* If the weapon is one-handed, an [[Ancient Tower Shield]].

== Tips ==
* Armor piercing or anti-armor weapons are required to penetrate their armor, just make sure they do blunt or slashing damage and not actual piercing.
* Prioritize those who have two-handed weapons.
* Their weaponry doesn't work well against heavy [[armor]], but will cause early [[morale]] checks on [[Nimble]] brothers because of [[Fearsome]].
* [[Bundle of Throwing Axes|Throwing Axes]] are required for your ranged brothers to be effective.
* Can be [[Daggers|punctured]] for their armor if the risk is low enough or you can reliably [[Maces|stun]] or [[Battle Whip|disarm]] them.
