==Tips==

* Isn't as fragile as a regular Honor Guard and deals more damage with a [[Named Weapons|named weapon]] and damage modifier. Otherwise similar to a non-champion version, wears the same armor and is easy to hit.
* [[Named Crypt Cleaver]] can be very deadly to [[Nimble]] brothers and even to [[Battle Forged]], if generates with very good stats.
* If has a [[Khopesh]] and shield, isn't much of a threat.
* Concentrate damage to kill him before he attacks.
* Usually you don't want to [[Maces|stun]] or [[Battle Whip|disarm]] [[Ancient Dead]] because killing them is easier, but against this enemy it sometimes may be worthwhile.
