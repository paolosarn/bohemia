Can become a [[Barbarian Chosen (Champion)|champion]] with a 1% chance.

*A [[Rusty Warblade]], [[Heavy Rusty Axe]], [[Two-Handed Skull Hammer]] or [[Two-Handed Spiked Mace]].

== Behavior==
Like all barbarians, they move forward and attack the enemy. They will use [[Adrenaline (20 fatigue)|Adrenaline]] to get ahead of the enemy if not already faster than them. Usually they wait after acting, and activate it at the very end. They can [[Barbarian Fury (5 fatigue)|rotate]] with each other to save a wounded ally, attack someone vulnerable, or without a clear purpose.

==Tips == 

*Late game enemy. You are supposed to fight them with a strong squad or allies.
* Armor piercing or anti-armor weapons are required to penetrate their armor.
*They can come in large numbers.
*Control them by [[Maces|stunning]], [[Battle Whip|disarming]] or [[Spears|spearwall]].
*Their two-handed weapons can instantly kill without high HP or defensive [[perks]] even through a helmet.
*Take [[Nine Lives]] to survive a heavy hit.
*[[Bone Plating]] absorbs one hit to the body.
*When they are mixed with [[Barbarian Reaver|Reavers]], kill Reavers with two-handed weapons first as they pose similar threat but are easier to kill.
*Can be [[Daggers|punctured]] for their armor it you can reliably stun or disarm them.
*They like to break shields, don't bring fragile shields you don't want to lose. [[Shield Expert]] and extra shields can win more time.
*If they waited, don't [[Taunt (Skill)|Taunt]] or Disarm them as the effect will instantly go away. Stun goes away but prevents [[Adrenaline (20 fatigue)|Adrenaline]].
*While a [[Throwing Net|net]] doesn't do much, breaking free makes them unable to attack with a 6 AP weapon.
