'''Special'''

*Your brothers may never be of confident morale, which makes certain traits less undesirable
*Slaying non human enemies for the first time will yield unique [[Anatomists potions|potions]]
*Each potion will provide a permanent buff but will make the brother who drank it sick, the sickness will last longer the more potions they have drank
*If a potion is lost or the brother who drank it killed another can be acquired by killing the relevant enemy again
*Begins the game with an apotheosis potion and four ledgers to keep track of what potions you have acquired
*Has access to a plethora of events; [[Anatomist vs Ailing]], [[Anatomist vs Asthmatic]], [[Anatomist vs Clubfooted]], [[Anatomist vs Dog]], [[Anatomist vs Historian]], [[Anatomist vs Iron Lungs]], [[Anatomist Wants Skull]], [[Anatomist Bummed at Mutations]], [[Anatomist Okay With Mutations]], [[Anatomist Old Patient]], [[Anatomist Creeps Out Locals]], [[Anatomist Dead Knight]], [[Anatomist Exhumes Hero]], [[Anatomist Joins]], [[Anatomist Helps Blighted Guy 1]], [[Anatomist Helps Blighted Guy 2]], [[Anatomist Confronts Healer]], [[Black Market]], [[Killer on the Run Reminisces]], [[Missing Kids]], [[Mutated Gladiator Annoys Others]], [[Something in Barn]] & [[Strange Scribe]]
