== Behavior ==
Drummers will always simply stand there and beat their drums. If engaged in melee, they, however, draw weapons and try to fight. Sometimes they continue to beat drums even with melee attackers next to them.

Their drums don't drop.

==Tips==

* This enemy only reduces fatigue of other barbarians. Barbarians already have a high fatigue limit and almost never reach it, and especially it isn't happening instantly when Drummers are killed, so it's better to simply ignore them.
* Don't let them escape because they are worth XP.
