== Description ==
''An antidote for various types of poisons. Tastes quite bitter.''

== Where Can They Be Found? ==
* Can be bought at a [[Settlement_buildings#Marketplace|Marketplace]] in settlements with [[Settlements_and_attached_locations#Supplies|Herbalist's Grove]].
* Can be bought from an [[Alchemist]] in more quantity.
* Can be crafted at a [[Taxidermist]] from 2 [[File:Inventory_ghoul_teeth_01.png|25px|bottom|Jagged Fangs (Nachzehrer)]] [[Jagged Fangs]] for 50 [[File:crowns.png|25px|bottom]].
* Can be created by a [[Witchhunter (Background)|Witchhunter]] [[File:Background_23.png|30px|bottom]] from  [[File:Inventory_ghoul_teeth_01.png|25px|bottom|Jagged Fangs (Nachzehrer)]] [[Jagged Fangs]] in the [[Witchhunter Ghoul Teeth]] event.
* Can be found in the [[Greenskins Investigation]] and [[Dried Oasis]] events.
