== Description ==
''Assorted ammunition: arrows, bolts and throwing weapons. A new bundle of ammo contains 50 units worth of ammunition.'' 

== Where Can They Be Found? ==
* Can be bought at a [[Settlement_buildings#Market|Marketplace]] and from a [[Settlement_buildings#Fletcher|Fletcher]].
** [[Settlements_and_attached_locations#Supplies|Arrow Maker's Shed]] increases ammo availability at a lower price.
* Can be looted after battle.
* [[The Scavenger (Follower)|The Scavenger]] will recover some [[Ammunition]] used by the player during battle.

== Notes ==
* Arrow and bolt quivers, powder bags and bundles of throwing weapons are automatically refilled after battle. 
* Replacing one arrow or bolt will take up one point of ammunition.
* Replacing one shot of a Handgonne will take up two points.
* Replacing one throwing weapon (axe, javelin, bola) or charge of a Fire Lance will take up three.
* Running out of ammunition will leave quivers and bundles empty.
* Maximum carry capacity is determined by Economic Difficulty of the game: 300 - for Veteran, Expert; 500 - for Beginner.
* [[The Quartermaster (Follower)|The Quartermaster]] will increase carrying ammo capacity by 100.
* Some amount can be lost or used in the [[Deserter Origin Volunteer]], [[Shooting Contest]], [[Thief Caught]] events.
