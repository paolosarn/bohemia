==Tips==

*Always has a [[Named Weapons|named weapon]], and is extremely deadly with it. Can kill a [[Battle Forged]] brother in one hit even if he has [[Steel Brow]] if the named weapon is above average. [[Named Rusty Warblade|Rusty Warblade]] needs two hits.
*If he kills someone, he will gain [[Berserk]], [[Killing Frenzy|Frenzy]] and [[Morale|confidence]].
*So your tactics should be all about not even letting him attack.
*Control him by [[Maces|stunning]] or [[Battle Whip|disarming]]. He counters it by acting ahead of you and waiting, and uses [[Adrenaline (20 fatigue)|Adrenaline]] to get ahead. If he does it, you can only stun to prevent Adrenaline.
*Use [[Indomitable]] if he is about to attack.
*[[Bone Platings|Bone Plating]] and [[Nine Lives]] can be useful.
