==Behavior==
Like all barbarians, they move forward and switch to [[Throwing Weapons|throwing weapons]] if they have them. They will use [[Adrenaline (20 fatigue)|Adrenaline]] to get ahead of the enemy if not already faster than them. They can [[Barbarian Fury (5 fatigue)|rotate]] with each other to save a wounded ally, attack someone vulnerable, or without a clear purpose.

==Tips==

*They can sometimes hit the head of your backliner with a [[Bundle of Crude Javelins|crude javelin]]. Protect brothers accordingly.
*Barbarians with [[Throwing Weapons|throwing weapons]] won't charge into Spearwall, and can be problematic if they take a high ground.
*Prioritize those who have [[Militia Spear|Militia Spears]] because they hit more often.
