<onlyinclude>
==[[Axes]]==
</onlyinclude>
Axes are versatile [[Melee Weapons|weapons]]. From a utility perspective, they can deal direct damage to shields with the Split Shield attack, which can be effective against shield-carrying opponents or [[Schrat]]s.
<onlyinclude>
==='''One-Handed Axes'''===
</onlyinclude>
Offensively, 1-handed axes do 50% additional damage to HP on a hit to the head, which compounds with the normal head shot bonus (of +50% HP damage).

[[Perks#Tier_4|Axe Mastery]] perk gives these bonuses:
* [[File:Split Shield.png|25px|bottom]] [[Split Shield (15 fatigue)|Split Shield]] damage is increased by <span style="color:green">50%</span>.
* Axe skills require <span class="" style="color:green">25%</span> less [[File:Fatigue.png|25px|bottom|Fatigue]].

Orc Weapons require 5 additional fatigue per skill use for humans.

<onlyinclude>
==='''Two-Handed Axes'''===
</onlyinclude>
[[Split Man (15 fatigue)|Split Man]] skill damage mechanics are explaned [https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_Mechanics#Split_Man here].

[[Longaxe]] has an attack range of 1-2 tiles, making it possible to attack from behind the frontline. Note that the Longaxe is not considered a [[Polearms|polearm]], so it will not receive bonuses from Polearm Mastery. Longaxe has <span style="color:red">-15%</span> accuracy penalty for hitting adjacent targets.

[[Perks#Tier_4|Axe Mastery]] perk grants:
* [[File:Split Shield.png|25px|bottom]] Split Shield damage is increased by <span style="color:green">50%</span>.
* [[Round Swing (35 fatigue)|Round Swing]] gains <span style="color:green">+5%</span> chance to hit.
* [[Longaxe]] no longer has penalty for hitting targets directly adjacent.
* Axes skills require <span class="" style="color:green">25%</span> less [[File:Fatigue.png|30px|bottom|Fatigue]].

Orc Weapons require 5 additional fatigue per skill use for humans.
