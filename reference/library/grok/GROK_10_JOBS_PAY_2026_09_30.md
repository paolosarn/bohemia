# VAMILY. HOW A JOB PAYS

Mechanics stay. Skin only.

A contract is a mission in a settlement. You finish it and get crowns, renown, and better relations. You fail it and relations drop, and renown drops. One job at a time. Pay goes up as renown goes up. Some noble jobs do not show until your renown is high enough. Skulls are the danger. More skulls, more pay. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Contracts

A real pay line from that page: nothing up front, 610 after, 10 per head, 15 heads, 760 total. Another line pays 150 up front.

Skin: a job on the phone. Batteries are the crowns. Clout is the renown. The block that posted it likes you more if you finish. One job at a time. A one-skull job is a side street. A three-skull job is a bigger camp and more batteries.

Easy jobs from the same page: defend the block against road crews or chip-bodies, whichever camp is closer. Drive a crew off a lot. Hunt a beast.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: One job, crowns, renown, relations, and skulls are from the contracts page. Batteries and clout are his.

WHAT IS NOT: I did not price a Bohemia job. Everything still costs one until he plays it.
