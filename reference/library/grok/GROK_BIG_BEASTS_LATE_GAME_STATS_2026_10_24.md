# BIG BEASTS AND LATE GAME UNITS STATS AND SKILLS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
A combat tile is house-sized. Melee is likely the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows reach 2, 3, or up to about 12-13 tiles. A boss is never an animal.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the units. Action points and fatigue costs stay the way Battle Brothers does them.

Lindwurm has 1100 health, 75-85 melee skill, 10 melee defense, -10 ranged defense, 180 resolve, 80 initiative, 400 fatigue, 30 fatigue recovery, and 7 action points. It occupies two tiles, head and tail, that share health, armor, fatigue, and morale. Gorge costs 4 action points and 15 fatigue for 80-140 damage at 2 tiles. Tail Slam variants cost 4 action points and 20 or 25 fatigue and can hit multiple targets or stun. Acid Blood sprays the next tile when at least 10 health is taken in melee and corrodes armor for three turns. The tail ignores zone of control.

Kraken has 3800 health. Devour costs 6 action points and 25 fatigue and instantly kills a brother who ends their turn next to the mouth. Ensnare roots and drags. Tentacles can move up to 8 tiles. The fight has two phases. A boss is never an animal, so this stays a machine or a large structure.

Schrat has 600 health, 70-75 melee skill, -5 melee defense, -5 ranged defense, 200 resolve, 60 initiative, 400 fatigue, 25 fatigue recovery, and 7 action points. Shielded reduces damage taken to 30 percent. Uproot costs 5 action points and 25 fatigue and hits three tiles in a straight line. Grow Shield costs 5 action points and 50 fatigue. A hit of 60 or more damage can spawn a Sapling.

Hexe has 80 health, 5 melee defense, 5 ranged defense, 160 resolve, 100 initiative, 80 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Charm costs 5 action points and 15 fatigue and can mind-control a brother for 2 turns within 8 tiles. Hex costs 3 action points and 5 fatigue and makes the brother take the same damage as the Hexe. They have no zone of control.

Ancient Legionary has 55 health, 65 melee skill, 0 melee defense, 0 ranged defense, 100 resolve, 65 initiative, and 9 action points. They resist most ranged and piercing damage. They shieldwall and hold formation. Orc Warlord has 300 health, 80-85 melee skill, 40 ranged skill, -10 melee defense, -10 ranged defense, 90 resolve, 125 initiative, 250 fatigue, 30 fatigue recovery, and 8 action points. Warcry costs 3 action points and 15 fatigue and affects nearby morale. Line Breaker costs 4 action points and 30 fatigue. Barbarian Chosen has 130 health, 75 melee skill, 15 melee defense, 10 ranged defense, 90 resolve, 115 initiative, 140 fatigue, 20 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Barbarian Fury costs 3 action points and 5 fatigue and swaps places with an adjacent ally.

In Bohemia the same numbers and costs stay. The look changes to multi-segment industrial machines, large structures, living-tree machines that grow small parts, chip-bodies that control others, and heavy late-game robots. Melee stays the next tile. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
