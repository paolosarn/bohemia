# HOW THE MAP PICKS A FIGHT

The map is new every run. Three noble houses own the towns. A normal map has about 17 settlements. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Global_Map

Enemy factions hold ground and fly a banner, same as a house. Orcs, goblins, bandits, and the dead each have their own ground. They patrol and raid near that ground. They defend it. https://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-83-enemy-factions/

Barbarians are the north. Nomads and the city states are the south. Beasts follow the ground. Forest, swamp, snow, and desert do not share a pool.

A party you meet comes from the ground you are on. The size of that party still looks at days, crew size, and level. There is no scripted first six.
