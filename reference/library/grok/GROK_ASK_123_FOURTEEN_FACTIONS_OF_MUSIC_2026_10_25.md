# ASK 123: The 14 Factions of Music

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Read the whole master file. 14 factions of music.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The master states at least 12 factions and roughly 75 enemy types. Colour is territory. Each faction owns a colour. The soundscape rule says every screen and every building is heard from real material. Sounds are currently on hold until the look holds. The master does not lock a list of 14 named factions or a music theme for each.

Battle Brothers uses three noble houses as the main human factions on a typical map. The Factions and Relations wiki page says you track relations with settlements and noble houses. Relations run from Hostile 0-9 to Allied 90-100. Fortified settlements share the house relation score. Civilian settlements have their own bar. Dev Blog 57 says noble houses are procedurally generated with distinct traits that change the contracts they offer. The wiki does not say a unique music track per house or per settlement type. The wiki does not say whether the soundtrack changes when you enter a hostile versus friendly settlement.

Bohemia has no locked rule that says each of the 14 factions gets a unique 120 BPM loop, a shared base track with faction-specific percussion, or a single valley-wide track that only changes at the generational handoff. The master does not say whether enemy parties play their faction's music when they enter vision range.

A LITTLE THING FROM the colour-is-territory law that already paints the map by faction.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: At least 12 factions. Colour is territory. Sounds are on hold. Battle Brothers has three noble houses.
WHAT IS NOT: Named list of 14. Music per faction. Whether music changes on the world map or only in settlement screens.
WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: Whether the first three factions should skin the three noble houses before the other eleven are named.
