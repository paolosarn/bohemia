# SILHOUETTE TEN OUTLINE

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants more analog horror and assets so the sprites and animations look way better. Claude makes a really bad graphic artist.

## MY PROPOSAL

Silhouette. Fill the sprite with one flat color. If you cannot tell what it is, the design fails. Break symmetry. Leave gaps so limbs do not fuse. Test at 1x. One signature detail that survives. Squint test. Value first. Nothing glows.

Ten 2nds. Off-screen announcer. Rebus puzzles on a screen. Digital timer counts from 10. Words move and position themselves. The screen wipes. Mixed media. Stiff. The machine is a workplace of junk.

Outlines. Optional. Use a dark shade of the local color, not pure black, if you use them. Outer only. Soften or drop on the lit side. Skip on faces if the value contrast holds. No outline on small clay faces if the grain does the work. Consistent or none. Selective. Preview at real size.

### COOK

One silhouette-tested clay face. One Ten 2nds style rebus screen with a timer. One selective outline test on a rusty robot. Lock these against the earlier sheets. The art coordinator can cook them. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light the stillness and the parts that do not fit, decided at the source.
