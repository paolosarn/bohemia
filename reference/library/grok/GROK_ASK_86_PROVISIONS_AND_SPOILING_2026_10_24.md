# ASK 86 PROVISIONS AND SPOILING THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Provisions and spoiling stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers gives each man two provisions a day. Food sits in the inventory and spoils after a set number of days. Men eat the stack that is closest to spoiling first. Missing food lowers mood and can lead to desertion. Variety can raise mood through an event. The Cook follower adds three days to every stack. The wiki does not list every spoil time or every variety event.

In Bohemia the same provisions and spoiling system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
