# ASK 52 TRAITS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Traits stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers gives recruits 0 to 2 random traits. Traits can raise or lower stats, change events, or lock other traits. Bright adds 10 percent experience. Dumb lowers experience by 15 percent. Iron Lungs adds fatigue recovery. Survivor gives a high chance to live at zero hit points with a permanent injury. Some traits are origin-only. Events can add or remove traits. The wiki does not list every edge case for every trait.

In Bohemia the same trait count and effects stay. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Traits page and community trait lists. The wiki does not say every edge case.
