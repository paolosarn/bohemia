## MY PROPOSAL:

This page covers how Battle Brothers handles weapon durability and breaking so Bohemia can keep the same rules with tool skins and the ammo pile. The wiki pages Tools and Supplies, Weapons, and the Combat Mechanics source give the numbers. The wiki does not say the exact durability lost on every single skill beyond the general rules.

Melee weapons lose 3 durability when they hit armor that still has at least 50 durability left. They take no damage when they hit flesh. Area attacks check each target separately, so one swing can cost more. Bows and crossbows lose 2 durability each time they shoot. When any weapon hits zero durability it breaks and is lost forever unless you have the Blacksmith follower. With the Blacksmith the broken weapon returns to your inventory at 0 so you can repair it. The same tools rules apply as armor: one tool point fixes 15 durability, 3 per hour, doubled when camped, Blacksmith speeds it and saves gear at zero. Equipped weapons repair themselves. You mark stash ones with Alt plus right-click. The wiki lists durability numbers for many weapons but does not say every edge case for named items beyond the general break rule.

Ammo is separate from durability. Throwing weapons use their own bundles. Bows use arrows from a quiver. Crossbows and handgonnes need reloads. The handgonne is the closest to a car shot in Bohemia.

For Bohemia the rules stay the same so fights feel like Battle Brothers. Melee weapons become scrap pipes, rusty crowbars, or zip-tied hammers that lose durability only when they hit solid scrap armor. Ranged weapons become duct-taped guns or nail-throwers that lose 2 per shot. A car is the handgonne skin: it takes a reload action, uses ammo from the gas can plus loose bullets and nails pile, and breaks the same way if durability hits zero. Tools skins stay duct tape, zip ties, and a hammer for repairs. A broken weapon is scrap you lose or tape back from zero with the hammer equivalent of the Blacksmith. Main character downs instead of dying. Nothing glows. House tiles stay. The five weapon skin options for a vote are: rusty crowbar, duct-taped pipe, zip-tied hammer, scrap car door club, and nail-throwing gun. One becomes the skin. The rest are not in the game.

The wiki pages used are Tools and Supplies, Weapons, Combat Mechanics, and The Blacksmith. Numbers not listed there are marked the wiki does not say.
