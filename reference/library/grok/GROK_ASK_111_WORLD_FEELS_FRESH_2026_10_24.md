# ASK 111: A World That Feels Made Fresh

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

I am not getting procedurally generated world vibes. Every time I start new I spawn next to the church settlement.

## MY PROPOSAL:

Battle Brothers generates a new world map at the start of every campaign. The Global Map page states that a typical map has 17 settlements aligned to three noble factions, with distribution that can be balanced or lopsided. Settlements include cities, towns, villages, strongholds, castles, and fortified manors. Roads connect them. Attached locations such as farms, mines, or watchtowers spawn randomly and change what the settlement sells and who can be hired. Factions and their leaders are also generated with unique coats of arms. The start place is near a settlement but the exact first settlement is not fixed to one church. The wiki does not say the precise algorithm for placement or the seed formula.

### What Makes a World Feel New at First Look

Players feel a world is new when the first view shows variation in layout, landmarks, and immediate options rather than the same starting cluster. Procedural systems that mix template structures with random placement, as in Minecraft villages near unexpected terrain, create the sense of discovery. Consistent but varied faction colors, road shapes, and attached location icons prevent the same-every-time feeling. A LITTLE THING FROM Minecraft.
