# ENEMY MATH

Party strength. The 12 strongest men. Peasant militia and manhunters use 14. Each man is 10 plus 2 for every level past 1. A level 1 is 10. A level 5 is 18. Gear does not count. Benched men still count. Extra men past the field cap do not, the weakest drop off. https://steamcommunity.com/app/365360/discussions/0/6169410450150404382

A job uses that score, the skulls, and the job type. Clout does not change the fight. Some harder twists are locked behind clout, then the party still scales off the score.

A camp already on the map does not use the crew. It uses days. It caps at day 267, and by then it has tripled. Legendary camps stay fixed.

A roaming party uses days and the crew score. Day scaling for those stops at day 100. Some roaming parties do not scale.

Combat difficulty, set at the start, raises both the count and the tier. Higher tier means more leaders and fewer thugs. That is the equipment step. There is no separate gear-level formula on the wiki.

Enemies are unit types, not a level like a hire. Late days add small buffs, like 5 melee skill or 10 percent damage. Nomad outlaws get Dodge on day 40.

Hover count. A few is 2 to 3. Some is 4 to 6. Many is 7 to 10. Lots is 11 to 13. A plethora is 14 or more. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Mechanics
