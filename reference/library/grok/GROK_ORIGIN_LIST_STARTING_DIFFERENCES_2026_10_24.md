## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for origins. Lone Wolf starts with one level 4 hedge knight, great equipment, low funds (about 750 to 1250 crowns depending on economic setting), a roster of 12 at most, 90 inventory slots, and the campaign ends if the hedge knight dies. Peasant Militia starts with a roster of 12 poorly equipped peasants, can take 16 into battle, has up to 25 in the roster, and can never hire anyone that is not a lowborn peasant. Scaling uses the 14 strongest for Peasant Militia instead of the 12 strongest.

A New Company starts with three companions. Rebuilding a Company starts with three after a defeat. Trading Caravan starts with two caravan hands, 10 percent better prices, no starting renown, and 66 percent renown gain. Other origins include Southern Mercenaries, Gladiators, Manhunters (up to 16 in battle), Deserters, and Beast Slayers. Starting renown ranges from -100 to 200 depending on origin.

In Bohemia the same origins and starting differences stay. Lone Wolf remains one strong start who is downed never dead. Peasant Militia remains the 16-man field. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Origins, Lone Wolf, Peasant Militia, and related developer blogs.
