# ASK 87 TOOLS AND REPAIRS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Tools and repairs stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers uses tools to repair gear. One tool point restores fifteen durability. Equipped and bag items repair automatically. Stash items need an alt-right-click mark. Repair happens at three durability per hour and doubles while camped. The Blacksmith follower raises the speed. Gear that hits zero durability breaks and is lost unless the Blacksmith is present. The wiki does not list every repair rate or every break exception.
