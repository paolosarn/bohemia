# ASK 84 THE ROSTER AND THE RESERVE THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
The roster and the reserve stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers lets the company hire up to twenty men. Only twelve can fight in a battle. The rest sit in the reserve. The player drags men between the fighting rows and the reserve. Reserve men do not fight and do not gain experience from the fight. Peasant Militia can field sixteen. The wiki does not list every reserve rule or every experience exception.

In Bohemia the same roster and reserve system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
