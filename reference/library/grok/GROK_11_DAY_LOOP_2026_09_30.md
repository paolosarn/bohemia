# VAMILY. THE DAY LOOP

Mechanics stay. Skin only.

Each hired man eats 2 food a day. Hard ground eats more. Desert is 1.5. Mountains are 2. Food spoils. They eat the pile that dies first. Miss food and mood falls, then men leave. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Provisions

One medicine per injury per day or the wound stays. Lost hitpoints come back alone. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Medical_Supplies

Wages are crowns out every day. A level adds 2. Greedy adds 2. Miss pay and mood falls, then desertion. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Game_mechanics

Tools repair gear while time passes on the map. Ammo refills the quivers after a fight from the company pile.

His locks on the skin: food, gas, and water are one resources pile. A car shot spends that pile. Batteries are the pay. A hire can walk only a couple of days after batteries hit zero. I am using 2 days. Traits stretch that clock. Loyal is 50% less likely to desert. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Loyal

A day in town does not eat the clock. A fight does not eat the clock. The road does.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: 2 food, 1 medicine, daily wages, and Loyal are from the wiki. The piles and the 2-day grace are his.

WHAT IS NOT: I did not price a day. Everything still costs one until he plays it.

WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: Nothing mechanic.
