## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers lets the player retire the company at any time for closure. There are five endings based on renown, crises finished, and ambitions completed. The endings range from early failure to a lasting legacy. A score is calculated from items, renown, crises, and days. The game does not force an end after a crisis. The wiki does not list every edge case for every ending.
