# SLICE SHEET. FIRST FIGHT A BUILDER CAN CODE

Not a lesson. The slice.

Map. 8 tiles wide, 6 deep. Houses on the edges. Street down the middle. Player starts on the south row. Six road crew start on the north row, 5 tiles away.

You. One person. 9 action points. 60 health (recall, a weak early brother). Pistol, 4 action points, 1 tile, spends 1 ammunition, ignores armor. Pipe, 4 action points, 1 tile, stun, spends nothing. Car on the south edge. Shot is 3 action points, cone at 2 tiles, spends 1 resources. Reload is 9. If he dies, the run ends.

Them. Six people. About 55 health (recall, early thug). Four pipes. One car door as a shield. One pistol. They walk at you. No perks.

Win. All six down, or they flee. Pay is batteries and a post to hire one person.

Lose. Your one person dies.

Do not build. Chip-bodies, crisis, day 100, beasts. Those wait until this fight plays.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: 9 action points, step 2, swing 4, pistol-is-dagger, car-spends-resources, house tile, one-man start. His.

WHAT IS NOT: 60 and 55 health. Marked recall. Prices are 1 until he plays it.
