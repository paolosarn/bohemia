# BRIGAND AND NOBLE UNITS STATS AND SKILLS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
A combat tile is house-sized. Melee is likely the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows reach 2, 3, or up to about 12-13 tiles.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the units. Action points and fatigue costs stay the way Battle Brothers does them.

Brigand Thug has 55 health, 55 melee skill, 45 ranged skill, 0 melee defense, 0 ranged defense, 40 resolve, 95 initiative, 95 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Hand-to-Hand Attack costs 4 action points and 5 fatigue for 5-10 damage. They are early game enemies that advance in groups.

Brigand Raider has 75 health, 65-70 melee skill, 55-60 ranged skill, 10 melee defense, 10 ranged defense, 55 resolve, 115 initiative, 125 fatigue, 20 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. They use the same basic attack and can carry better weapons and shields. Later groups include marksmen who stay back and shoot.

Footman has 70 health, 70 melee skill, 50 ranged skill, 10 melee defense, 5 ranged defense, 60 resolve, 110 initiative, 120 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. They shieldwall and use Rotation to protect billmen and arbalesters. Knight has 135 health, 90 melee skill, 60 ranged skill, 20 melee defense, 10 ranged defense, 90 resolve, 115 initiative, 140 fatigue, 20 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Captain increases nearby allies' resolve by 15 percent within 5 tiles. Knights fight aggressively and can spend all action points to close distance.

In Bohemia the same numbers and costs stay. The look changes to scavenger crews and organized robot or chip-body squads that hold formation. Melee stays the next tile. Ranged reaches further. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Brigand Thug, Brigand Raider, Brigands, Footman, Knight, Noble Houses, and Skills.
