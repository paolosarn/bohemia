# LIGHT POOP LINKED

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants more analog horror and assets so the sprites and animations look way better. Claude makes a really bad graphic artist.

## MY PROPOSAL

Light. Flat or one hard source. Upper left default. Hard edges. No soft glow. No bloom. Cast shadow is short and dark. Value first. Squint test. Limited palette. Grain baked in. Nothing glows. The Crashbox light is flat and harsh.

Poop or Scoop. A carnival host with only arms on screen. He spins a wheel of real animal clips. True or false. Scoop or poop. He scores at the end. Mixed media. Stiff.

Think Tank. Captain Bob is a Jamaican submarine captain stuck in a fish tank. He shows three objects. Find the common thing before the tank drains. Carved foam. 36 separate mouths. The wrong mouths stay the analog.

Linked cels. Static parts share one cel. Body, background, held pose. Edit one and it updates all. Unlink only when the part moves. Tags for each animation. Durations set per frame. Preview at real speed.

### COOK

One flat light clay face with one hard shadow. One Poop or Scoop style arm and wheel. One Think Tank style submarine in a tank with wrong mouths. One linked-cel idle. Lock these against the earlier sheets. The art coordinator can cook them. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light the stillness and the parts that do not fit, decided at the source.
