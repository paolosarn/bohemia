## MY PROPOSAL

Idle. The minimum is 2 frames. Base pose and a 1 pixel rise or drop. Hold each 400 to 500 ms. Optional 4 frames with a peak hold. Blink on a separate longer clock. 1 or 2 frames for the closed eye. Feet stay planted. No skating. Stiffness is allowed. The Crashbox hold is the point. Preview at real speed. Linked cels for the body. Tags for idle.

Professor Rocket. He is a mix of puppet pieces and robotic attachments. His head is a robotic piece with a calculator screen for a mouth. Eyes are a plus sign and a 6. Hair is bent metal. He wears a short sleeved green tailcoat. Ruler arms. He is fully mobile. His head explodes. He is eccentric and loud. The body is assembled from junk. That is the translation for chip bodies. Parts that do not fit. Mouths that are screens. The stiffness and the wrong mouth stay the analog.

Hue shift. Shadows shift cooler toward blue or purple. Highlights shift warmer toward yellow or orange. Saturation peaks in the mid tones. 10 to 20 degrees per step is enough.
