# IDLE ROCKET HUE

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants more analog horror and assets so the sprites and animations look way better. Claude makes a really bad graphic artist.

## MY PROPOSAL

Idle. The minimum is 2 frames. Base pose and a 1 pixel rise or drop. Hold each 400 to 500 ms. Optional 4 frames with a peak hold. Blink on a separate longer clock. 1 or 2 frames for the closed eye. Feet stay planted. No skating. Stiffness is allowed. The Crashbox hold is the point. Preview at real speed. Linked cels for the body. Tags for idle.

Professor Rocket. He is a mix of puppet pieces and robotic attachments. His head is a robotic piece with a calculator screen for a mouth. Eyes are a plus sign and a 6. Hair is bent metal. He wears a short sleeved green tailcoat. Ruler arms. He is fully mobile. His head explodes. He is eccentric and loud. The body is assembled from junk. That is the translation for chip bodies. Parts that do not fit. Mouths that are screens. The stiffness and the wrong mouth stay the analog.

Hue shift. Shadows shift cooler toward blue or purple. Highlights shift warmer toward yellow or orange. Saturation peaks in the mid tones. 10 to 20 degrees per step is enough. Value does the work first. Squint test. Limited palette 16 to 32. Grain baked in. Nothing glows. Light is flat or one hard source.

### COOK

One 2-frame idle with a 1 pixel rise and long holds. One Professor Rocket style calculator mouth and green tailcoat. One hue-shifted rust ramp and one sick clay ramp. Lock these against the earlier sheets. The art coordinator can cook them. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light the stillness and the parts that do not fit, decided at the source.
