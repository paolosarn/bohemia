# ASK 109: How Far You Can See in a City

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Research how far people can realistically see, because it is a city. You should only see things straight down a street you are near.

## MY PROPOSAL:

Real pedestrian and driver sight distances in a city street are limited by buildings, parked cars, and light. Daytime clear line of sight down an open street can reach hundreds of meters for a large object, but around a corner or past parked cars it drops to 20 to 50 meters. At night with street lighting a person in dark clothes is often visible only 15 to 50 meters away, while a reflective vest can be seen beyond 200 meters according to eye-tracking studies. From a roof the view opens further but still cuts at the next block. Games with city fog of war usually draw line of sight tile by tile and block it with buildings or height, as in Ultimate General Gettysburg where ridges and houses hide units until you advance. ### Battle Brothers Encounter Card

The wiki does not list an exact encounter card page. From Combat Mechanics and developer blogs the engage screen shows the terrain type, whether the location has fortifications, the approximate number and kind of enemies, and a chance to retreat before the fight starts. Faces of individual enemies are not shown before the fight. Followers or retinue appear on the company screen and can join certain contracts but are not listed on the pre-fight card.

The sources for real distances are the NHTSA headlight workshop notes and the 2021 ScienceDirect pedestrian conspicuity study. The wiki Combat Mechanics page covers terrain and height but does not give street sight numbers. The wiki does not say the exact pixels or icons on the pre-fight card.
