# DOWNED HAUNTED PALETTE

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants more analog horror and assets so the sprites and animations look way better. Claude makes a really bad graphic artist.

## MY PROPOSAL

Downed. The main character is downed, never dead. 3 to 5 frames. Anticipation stagger. Impact hold. Collapse into parts or a low pose. Hold the downed pose long. 200 ms or more. Optional white flash on the hit frames. X eyes or blank. Parts can separate and rebuild. No final death. Variable holds. No tween. Preview at 12 fps. Stiffness is allowed. The Crashbox hold is the point. The body is parts.

Haunted House Party. An unseen Horrid One hosts. Silhouettes of historical figures talk to a butler or guests. The guest is revealed as a cardboard cutout after lightning. Mouths move wrong in later seasons. Mixed media. Stiff. The machine is a workplace of junk.

Lens McCracken. A near-sighted detective. Photos are zoomed too close. The Solutionator reveals them. He makes up a wrong story. Mixed media. The parts do not fit.

Palette. Soft & Dirty or Dry Sea or a custom set. 16 to 32 colors. Dirty greens, dull rust, sick clay, ash black, rubble brown. Hue shift shadows cooler. Highlights warmer. Value first. Squint test. Grain baked in. Nothing glows. Light is flat or one hard source.

### COOK

One downed collapse that holds and can rebuild. One Haunted House Party style silhouette and cardboard guest. One Lens McCracken style zoomed photo and Solutionator. One locked Soft & Dirty or Dry Sea ramp. Lock these against the earlier sheets. The art coordinator can cook them. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light the stillness and the parts that do not fit, decided at the source.
