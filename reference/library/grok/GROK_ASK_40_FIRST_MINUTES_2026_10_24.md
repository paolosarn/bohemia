# ASK 40 FIRST MINUTES OF A FIGHT THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
There is no fixed first fight. Fights pull from a pool based on level, crew size, and days.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers starts a typical fight with both parties in lines and at least five hexes between them. Most units have nine action points. Moving one flat hex costs two action points. Human forces cannot close to melee in one round. Ranged units can fire on the first turn. Early fights often face thugs or similar low-tier humans. Players often wait, take high ground, or use choke points. The first contract is often a simple track or drive-off brigands. The wiki does not script a single fixed first fight. Enemy count and composition scale with days, company strength, and map position.

In Bohemia the same start stays. Lines form, distance stays, and the pool scales the same way. No scripted opening group. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Combat Mechanics page, community guides on early fights, and contract pages. The wiki does not say a fixed first fight.
