# ASK 53 BACKGROUNDS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Backgrounds stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers gives each recruit a background that sets starting attribute ranges, starting level, daily wage, and possible traits. Backgrounds can open or block events. Indebted have zero wage and lower starting stats. Apprentice gains 10 percent experience. Some backgrounds exclude certain talents. Starting gear and hire cost depend on the background. The wiki does not list every edge case for every background.

In Bohemia the same background system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Character Backgrounds and Indebted pages, and community notes. The wiki does not say every edge case.
