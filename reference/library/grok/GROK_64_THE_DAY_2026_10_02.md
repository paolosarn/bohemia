# THE DAY

No new names. No health numbers. This is the spend a builder can code.

Noon. Pay batteries from the wage table. A level adds 1 battery every 5 levels. The bought man pays 0.

Food and water are one pile. A man eats 2 a day. Water skins are boiled pool water, and still water. Line: may have brain eating amoeba.

An injury is one pain line. 1 medicine a day until it closes. Medicine skins are old bandages, expired ibuprofen, and super glue. Health comes back on its own.

Tools fix gear. 1 point repairs 15 durability. Skins are duct tape, zip ties, and a hammer.

Ammo pile holds gas, and loose bullets and nails as one skin. A pistol shot takes bullets or nails. A car shot takes gas from this pile.

Zero batteries, then 2 days, then a hire can walk. Traits can stretch that clock.
