# BATTLE BROTHERS ENEMY LIST AND BOHEMIA TRANSLATION OPTIONS

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Goblins-to-drones and orcs-to-robots were examples, not a lock. He wants a full enemy and beast translation, researched, not atom-by-atom rulings. Undead translation is a body with a chip still in it, analog horror, not an empty robot and not magic. Robots, drones, Roombas and other real tech can stand in for medieval fantasy enemies.

## MY PROPOSAL
The Battle Brothers wiki page on Enemies lists the main groups. Humans include Brigands (Thug, Raider, Poacher, Marksman, Leader and champions), Nomads, Barbarians, Noble Houses, and civilians. Undead include Zombies (Wiederganger and variants), Ancient Dead, and related units such as Geist and Necromancer. Greenskins include Orcs (Young, Warrior, Berserker, Warlord) and Goblins (Skirmisher, Ambusher, Wolfrider, Shaman, Overseer). Beasts include Webknecht, Direwolf, Hyena, Nachzehrer, Serpent, Alp, Unhold, Hexe, Ifrit, Schrat, Lindwurm, and others. Animals include Wardog, Warhound, Wolf, and Donkey.

These are options, not locked skins. The rest are not in the game until a vote picks one winner per type.

Brigands become scavenger crews that ambush roads for batteries and resources. One option is people in mismatched scavenged gear. Another is small groups of armed looters who live off the same resource piles the company uses.

Orcs become large industrial machines. One option is heavy salvage robots that charge and break lines. Another is tracked construction machines that have been re-purposed and still carry crude armor plates.

Goblins become small agile drones. One option is flying recon drones that throw nets or poison darts and stay at range. Another is wheeled Roomba-class units that dart in and out.

Undead become bodies with a chip still in them. One option is a person who died but whose implant kept the limbs moving. Another is a corpse whose remaining collar or chip forces it to stand and walk. Not an empty robot and not magic.

Direwolves become packs of fast autonomous drones or scavenged animal-shaped machines that flank and overwhelm. Unholds become large slow machines that throw or shove. Lindwurms become long multi-segment industrial machines that spit acid or damage armor on hit.

The wiki does not say a single visual style for any unit beyond the medieval fantasy art. In Bohemia each group keeps the same combat role and scaling. The look is the economic-apocalypse translation: robots, drones, chip-bodies, and scavenged tech. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
