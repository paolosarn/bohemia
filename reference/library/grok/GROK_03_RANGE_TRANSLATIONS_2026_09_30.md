# WEAPON TRANSLATIONS AT HOUSE SCALE

His scale, this chat: one tile is a house. Melee is the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows can reach 2, 3, or out toward 12-13. Melee with no gun can be a way to play, and a perk can pay you for it. Options, not a lock.

Battle Brothers ranges, wiki: polearms hit 1-2 tiles and are worse on the near tile. Bows list 6 or 7 tiles. Quick Shot is 4 AP. Aimed Shot is 7 AP. Handgonne is range 2. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Polearms https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Bows

## Range bands I am using

- Same tile: nobody. Too close for a house.
- 1 tile: melee. The next house, the yard, the gap.
- 2 tiles: polearm, spearwall, handgonne, throw. Across the street.
- 3 to 6: rifle and crossbow job.
- 7 to 13: bow job, a real rifle from a roof. His maybe.

## Options per skill

1. Puncture, ignores armor. A1 pistol at 1 tile, his dagger. A2 a halligan through a gap in a vest. A3 a cattle prod. A1 is the default.
2. Riposte, swing back at a miss. B1 a pistol in the off hand that fires if they miss you. B2 a dog that bites the one who swung. B3 skip. Riposte is a perk, not a weapon.
3. Knock Out, stun. C1 a bat or a pipe at 1 tile. C2 a flashbang at 2. C3 a taser. C1 is the mace.
4. Spearwall, stop the step. D1 spike strip on the tile edge. D2 a parked car nose across the gap. D3 a dog on a lead. D1 is the clean one.
5. Split Shield. E1 a sledge on a car door. E2 a rifle shot into the shield, not the man. E3 skip shields.
6. Decapitate, worse as they bleed. F1 a cleaver or a machete at 1 tile. F2 a shotgun at 1 tile that gets meaner when they are low. F1 is the reward path.
7. Hammer, break armor. G1 sledge. G2 a car ram, but the car is already the handgonne. Do not give it this job too.
8. Polearm, hit at 2. H1 a rifle with a bayonet, shoot at 3, stab at 1. H2 a hooked pole from a pool fence. H3 skip. H1 lets one man do both.
9. Flail, ignore the shield, hunt the head. I1 a chain or a tow strap. I2 a bouncing shot. I2 is the gun version. I1 is the perk version.
10. Whip, disarm at 3. J1 a thrown chain. J2 a drone that snatches the gun. J3 skip.
11. Bow, many shots, no reload, 6-7 tiles in the wiki, maybe 12-13 here. K1 a semi-auto rifle. K2 a nail gun, short. K3 skip, and let the rifle be the only long gun.
12. Crossbow, one hard shot, then reload. L1 a bolt-action rifle, 3 to 6 tiles. L2 a crossbow, real, quiet, analog horror. L3 an air rifle.
13. Handgonne, cone, range 2, reload eats the turn. M1 the car, his lock, spends resources. M2 a shotgun at 2 tiles, spends ammunition. M3 both: shotgun is the man-carried cone, car is the big one.
14. Throw, 2 tiles min in the wiki. N1 bottles and bricks. N2 flares. N3 grenades, later.
15. Net. O1 a literal net. O2 a spike strip that roots. O3 a drone tangle.
16. Firelance, one line, then empty. P1 a flare. P2 a propane jet. P3 skip for the demo.
17. Mortar, a tile you are not on. Q1 a trailer tube. Q2 skip until district two.

## Melee as a way to play

R1. Melee is legal at 1 tile only. Guns still work there, but a melee hit does not spend ammunition. That is the reward.
R2. A perk, Close, pays you: more damage at 1 tile, and Puncture stays free of ammo.
R3. Melee is a dare. You can do it, the game does not help you. Weak for a demo.
