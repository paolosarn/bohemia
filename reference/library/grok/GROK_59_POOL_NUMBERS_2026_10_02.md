# POOL NUMBERS

From the game mechanics page. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Game_mechanics

Roster strength is the 12 strongest men. Each man is 10 plus 2 for every level past 1. Gear does not count.

A job scales with that roster strength, the skulls, and the job type. Clout does not change the fight. Clout changes the pay.

A camp that was already on the map does not scale to the crew. It scales with how far it is from towns, and with time.

A roaming party scales with distance and time, and a little with the crew. Less than a job does.

Hover counts. A few is 2 to 3. Some is 4 to 6. Many is 7 to 10. Lots is 11 to 13. A plethora is 14 or more.

The ground still picks the faction. These numbers pick the size.
