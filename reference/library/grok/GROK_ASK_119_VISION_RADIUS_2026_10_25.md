# ASK 119: World Map Vision Radius

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

You can only see roaming parties when they are in visible radius to you, how Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The Global Map wiki page lists visibility radius multipliers by terrain. Plains and most open ground use 1. Hills use 1.25. Mountains use 2. Forests and other dense terrain reduce effective sight. Night reduces sight radius on the world map and in combat. The Lookout follower increases sight radius by 25 percent and reveals more footprint information. The Scout increases travel speed by 15 percent and prevents terrain sickness. Fog of war covers unexplored areas. Settlements and roads start revealed. Parties and locations appear when they enter your vision circle. Dev Blog 7 states that forest makes detection harder and can enable ambushes. Player code reads list a base vision value of 500 that is then multiplied by terrain and time factors. The wiki does not say the exact night reduction percentage, whether enemy parties see you at the same distance, or the precise pixel size of the party marker. The wiki does not say whether settlements are drawn at a fixed world size.

A LITTLE THING FROM the Lookout's 25 percent sight increase on the Retinue page.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Terrain multiplies sight. Night narrows it. Lookout widens it 25 percent. Fog hides unexplored ground.
WHAT IS NOT: Exact night percentage. Mutual visibility distance. Party marker pixel size.
WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: Whether Bohemia should show a faint shadow for parties just outside full vision.
