# ASK 113: The Haunting as Side Content

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Slightly purple-aura ghosts from the Amalgamation, not the main quest, no possession. The Backrooms twice an act, always paranoid.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The best analog horror works and ghost encounters create paranoia through incomplete evidence, degraded media, and things that are almost normal but wrong, without a jumpscare. Home Safety Hotline uses a 1990s computer interface where callers describe household problems that turn uncanny. Maple County and Assessment Examination use official training or exam formats that slowly reveal the faces are not quite human. The Complex shows empty fluorescent halls with only footsteps and brief glimpses. I'm On Observation Duty forces the player to spot small changes on cameras. A LITTLE THING FROM Home Safety Hotline. A LITTLE THING FROM Maple County.

### Side-Quest Shapes That Fit a Battle Brothers Style Company

A contract to clear a building that shows a purple-aura figure only on the edge of the map or in a single frame of a recording. A road event where a chip-body rearranges its limbs when no one is looking, then stands still again. A Backrooms-style floor that appears twice per act as a short contract: endless yellow halls with no combat, only the sound of footsteps and one wrong door. A settlement rumor that a portrait in the tavern changes when the company leaves and returns. Each stays optional, pays in batteries or clout, and never becomes the main path.
