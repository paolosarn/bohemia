## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for level and experience and talents. Level 2 needs 200 total experience. Level 3 needs 500. Level 4 needs 1000. Level 5 needs 2000. Level 6 needs 3500. Level 7 needs 5000. Level 8 needs 7000. Level 9 needs 9000. Level 10 needs 12000. Level 11 needs 15000. Each level gives one perk point and three attribute increases. Veteran levels past 11 need 4000 plus 1000 times (current level minus 11) experience. They give no perk points and the maximum roll is +1. The maximum level is 33.

Primary attributes are Melee Skill and Melee Defense. With no stars they roll 1-3. One star rolls 2-3. Two stars roll 3. Three stars roll 3-4. Secondary attributes are Ranged Skill, Ranged Defense, Hitpoints, Fatigue, and Resolve. With no stars they roll 2-4. One star rolls 3-4. Two stars roll 4. Three stars roll 4-5. Initiative rolls 3-5 with no stars, up to 5-6 with three stars. Stars are assigned randomly to three attributes. One star is 60 percent, two stars are 30 percent, three stars are 10 percent.

A character who deals the killing blow gets 20 percent of the enemy experience. The other 80 percent is split among the party. There is an unconditional 15 percent reduction. Student adds 20 percent until level 11. The killer bonus and split stay the same.

In Bohemia the same thresholds, star rolls, and experience split stay. Stats, stars, traits, and background show before the hire button spends batteries. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Level and Experience, Talents, Game Mechanics, and related experience tables.
