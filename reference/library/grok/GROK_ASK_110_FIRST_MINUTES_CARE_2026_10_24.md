# ASK 110: The First Minutes That Make a Player Care

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

We have a short amount of time to make people really give a fuck. Understand what is going on, each play unique. Whether we get a cutscene or a text box or straight into the journey depending on our origin. Search up online what people want.

## MY PROPOSAL:

Studies and playtesting show that the first five minutes decide retention because the brain forms a competence memory. The first eight to ten seconds must establish trust that the game respects the player's time. Primary mechanics such as movement and a clear threat come before secondary systems. Flow theory says a challenge that is neither too hard nor too easy raises engagement. Emotional feedback of numbers, sound, and visible results creates attachment. Sources include the Emhance psychology piece on first sessions, the PocketGamer trust article, and the COGconnected first-30-minutes analysis.

### How Battle Brothers Starts Each Origin

The Origins page and Dev Blog 113 state that each origin has a flavor introduction, different starting characters, equipment, resources, and special rules. The default rebuilding origin shows a short text about the company being shattered by Hoggart. A New Company drops the player straight into the world with three experienced men and no forced fight. Other origins such as Lone Wolf, Peasant Militia, or Cultists open with their own short flavor text then place the company on the map. The wiki does not say a full cutscene plays for any origin. Most openings are text plus immediate map control.

### Best Run-Based and Sandbox Openings

Games that hook fast either drop the player into immediate action with competence, as in Ghost of Tsushima's first five minutes of combat, or drop them straight into the world with no cutscene, as in Kenshi, Minecraft, or Subnautica after a brief sequence. Straight-into-the-journey openings rank highest for sandbox replay because the player owns the first choice. Text-box flavor ranks next for tone without delay. Long cutscenes rank lowest when they block the first action. A LITTLE THING FROM Kenshi. A LITTLE THING FROM Ghost of Tsushima.
