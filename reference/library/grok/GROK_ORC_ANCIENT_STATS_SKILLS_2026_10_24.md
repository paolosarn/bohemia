# ORC AND ANCIENT DEAD UNITS STATS AND SKILLS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
A combat tile is house-sized. Melee is likely the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows reach 2, 3, or up to about 12-13 tiles.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the units. Action points and fatigue costs stay the way Battle Brothers does them.

Orc Young has 125 health, 60 melee skill, 50-55 ranged skill, -5 melee defense, -5 ranged defense, 65 resolve, 120 initiative, 150 fatigue, 25 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Charge costs 4 action points and 25 fatigue, moves them next to a target within 3 tiles, and can stun. Orc Hand-to-Hand Attack costs 4 action points and 5 fatigue for 15-30 damage. They rush and can stun early.

Orc Warrior has 200 health, 70-75 melee skill, 40 ranged skill, -10 melee defense, -10 ranged defense, 75 resolve, 120 initiative, 200 fatigue, 30 fatigue recovery, and 8 action points. Line Breaker costs 4 action points and 30 fatigue, knocks a target back one tile, and never misses. Shield Bash adds damage on knockback. They disrupt formations to reach backliners.

Orc Warlord has 300 health, 80-85 melee skill, 40 ranged skill, -10 melee defense, -10 ranged defense, 90 resolve, 125 initiative, 250 fatigue, 30 fatigue recovery, and 8 action points. Captain increases nearby allies' resolve by 15 percent within 5 tiles. Warcry costs 3 action points and 15 fatigue and bolsters allies while weakening nearby brothers. They lead and are heavily armored.

Ancient Legionary has 55 health, 65 melee skill, 0 melee defense, 0 ranged defense, 100 resolve, 65 initiative, and 9 action points. Resistant to Ranged Attacks reduces damage from bows to 10 percent, javelins to 25 percent, and many piercing attacks to 50 percent. They shieldwall and use Rotation to protect pikemen. Ancient Legionary with Polearm uses the same resistances and can rotate out of melee.

In Bohemia the same numbers and costs stay. The look changes to heavy robots that shove and charge, and chip-bodies in formation that keep the stiffness and part rearranging. Melee stays the next tile. Ranged reaches further. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Orc Young, Orc Warrior, Orc Warlord, Orcs, Ancient Legionary, Ancient Legionary with Polearm, Ancient Dead, and Skills.
