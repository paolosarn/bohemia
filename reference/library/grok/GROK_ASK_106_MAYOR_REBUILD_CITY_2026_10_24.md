# ASK 106: Becoming the Pseudo-Mayor and Rebuilding the City

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Slowly over time become the pseudo-mayor of Bohemia and build things. Research how we are going to do this. It has to be lore friendly. The costs have to sound right. It is a big cost to revamp a whole city over three acts. Every building needs to come with a purpose. It has to look right.

## MY PROPOSAL:

Rebuilding a real dead city costs tens to hundreds of billions of dollars and many years of labor. New Orleans received more than 140 billion dollars after Katrina and still has not fully recovered according to a 2025 New York Times opinion. Detroit's long decline used smaller federal programs like Empowerment Zones of 100 million dollars with limited effect. Games make building feel earned when every structure has a clear purpose that changes logistics or survival, as in Manor Lords where villagers must walk the paths and food rots if the granary is too far, or Cities Skylines where traffic and services fail without proper placement. ### Twenty Building Kinds for Bohemia

Each costs batteries and the six resources. Looks change by act from ruin salvage in act one to cleaned and powered in act three.

1. Salvage Yard. Purpose: pull scrap from ruins for tools and ammo. Cost: 50 batteries, 20 tools, 10 resources. Act 1: rusty claw machines and piles. Act 2: covered bays. Act 3: sorted bins and conveyor.

2. Battery Bank. Purpose: store and trade power. Cost: 100 batteries, 30 tools. Act 1: jury-rigged car batteries. Act 2: racked cells. Act 3: clean wall of cells with meters.

3. Water Still. Purpose: boil pool water into the resource pile. Cost: 40 batteries, 15 resources, 10 tools. Act 1: barrels and fire. Act 2: sealed tanks. Act 3: glass columns.

4. Food Cache. Purpose: hold non-spoiling provisions. Cost: 30 batteries, 25 resources. Act 1: plastic bins. Act 2: locked cages. Act 3: climate shelves.

5. Clinic Tent. Purpose: use medicine points on injuries. Cost: 60 batteries, 20 medicine, 15 tools. Act 1: stained sheets. Act 2: partitioned rooms. Act 3: stainless tables.

6. Ammo Bench. Purpose: pack nails and gas into shared ammo. Cost: 45 batteries, 15 ammunition, 10 tools. Act 1: oil drums. Act 2: work tables. Act 3: presses.

7. Clout Board. Purpose: track and spend renown with factions. Cost: 20 batteries, 5 clout. Act 1: graffiti wall. Act 2: chalkboard. Act 3: lit screens.

8. Tool Shed. Purpose: repair gear with duct tape skins. Cost: 35 batteries, 20 tools. Act 1: leaning wood. Act 2: metal lockers. Act 3: organized racks.

9. Lookout Nest. Purpose: extend sight down streets. Cost: 25 batteries, 10 tools. Act 1: scaffolding. Act 2: elevated platform. Act 3: enclosed tower.

10. Trade Gate. Purpose: sell and buy with caravans. Cost: 70 batteries, 15 resources, 10 tools. Act 1: chain link. Act 2: booths. Act 3: lit stalls.

11. Power Substation. Purpose: distribute batteries to buildings. Cost: 120 batteries, 40 tools. Act 1: sparking transformers. Act 2: fenced yard. Act 3: clean switchgear.

12. Dorm Block. Purpose: house extra roster without desertion. Cost: 55 batteries, 30 resources, 10 tools. Act 1: cots in ruins. Act 2: bunks. Act 3: private rooms.

13. Workshop Floor. Purpose: craft named gear from scrap. Cost: 80 batteries, 25 tools, 15 ammunition. Act 1: dirt floor. Act 2: benches. Act 3: precision tools.

14. Signal Tower. Purpose: call roaming parties or contracts. Cost: 40 batteries, 15 tools. Act 1: antenna on roof. Act 2: reinforced mast. Act 3: powered dish.

15. Grave Cache. Purpose: store chip-bodies for later. Cost: 30 batteries, 10 medicine. Act 1: body bags. Act 2: sealed coolers. Act 3: labeled drawers.

16. Fuel Depot. Purpose: hold gas for the ammo pile. Cost: 50 batteries, 20 ammunition, 10 resources. Act 1: leaking cans. Act 2: barrels. Act 3: tanks.

17. Street Barricade. Purpose: block one approach in fights. Cost: 20 batteries, 15 tools. Act 1: cars and debris. Act 2: welded plates. Act 3: powered gates.

18. Market Stall. Purpose: daily trade of provisions. Cost: 25 batteries, 10 resources. Act 1: tarp. Act 2: counter. Act 3: lit display.

19. Research Desk. Purpose: unlock new skins or perks. Cost: 90 batteries, 20 tools, 10 medicine. Act 1: scavenged books. Act 2: notes. Act 3: screens.

20. Mayor Office. Purpose: spend clout to change settlement rules. Cost: 150 batteries, 30 clout, 20 tools. Act 1: commandeered room. Act 2: desk and maps. Act 3: clean desk with power.
