# MORALE SYSTEM AND HOW CHECKS TRIGGER THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for morale. Morale has states: Confident, Steady, Wavering, Breaking, and Fleeing. Confident multiplies hit and defense by 1.1. Wavering multiplies by 0.9. Breaking multiplies by 0.8. Fleeing multiplies by 0.7, cancels stances, moves the unit to the end of the turn order, and makes them run from enemies. The player loses control of fleeing brothers. Undead are Unbreakable and cannot change.

A morale check is a roll against Resolve. The maximum success chance is 95 percent. Positive checks raise morale by one level. Negative checks lower it by one level. A check for 15 health damage uses a modifier of -40 times the missing health percent. An enemy death uses a positive modifier of -30 plus 0.04 times the enemy experience value minus the distance cubed. The killer gets an extra 15. Allies adjacent add 3 to negative checks. Enemies adjacent subtract 3. Fearsome subtracts 0.15 times the user's Resolve, capped at 20, and triggers on 1 health damage instead of 15.

Rally the Troops costs 5 action points and 25 fatigue. It triggers a positive check on allies at Wavering or worse within 4 tiles. The bonus is 0.4 times the user's Resolve, with a penalty of 10 per tile of distance. Fleeing allies ignore the distance penalty. It cannot raise above Steady. A banner gives nearby allies 10 percent of the carrier's Resolve.

Mood affects starting morale. Good mood can start Confident. Bad mood caps the highest state. Mood also ties to desertion after unpaid days, as in the battery lock.

In Bohemia the same states, checks, and Rally stay. Chip-bodies and machines can still break and rally. The main character is downed, never dead. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Morale, Mood, Rally the Troops, and the bravery and morale developer blog.
