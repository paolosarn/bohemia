I did not open the nut files. The morale page states the states. Confident multiplies by 1.1. Steady is the base. Wavering is 0.9. Breaking is 0.8. Fleeing is 0.7. A check succeeds on resolve, capped at 95 percent, and it can fail at 0. An ally next to you adds 3 on a negative check. An enemy next to you subtracts 3. Fearsome subtracts 15 percent of the attacker's resolve, capped at 20. A check is skipped if the chance is over 500 percent before the cap. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Morale

A player sheet gives the check math. I am not treating it as the script. A hit check is current resolve minus 40 times the share of health lost. A kill nearby is current resolve minus 30 plus 0.04 times the enemy experience, minus distance cubed, and the killer gets 15 more. https://www.reddit.com/r/BattleBrothers/comments/iyy9wu/deleted_by_user/
