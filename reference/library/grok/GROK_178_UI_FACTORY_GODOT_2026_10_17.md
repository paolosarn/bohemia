# UI FACTORY GODOT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants more analog horror and assets so the sprites and animations look way better. Claude makes a really bad graphic artist.

## MY PROPOSAL

UI. Cartridge plate or dented green clay rectangle. Button is a rusty plate or stuck cartridge. 9-slice safe borders. Same limited palette. Soft & Dirty or Dry Sea. No glow. No rounded candy shapes. Grain baked in. Visible steps. The machine is a workplace of junk.

Factory. Rusty tin robots make green clay cartridges. Rube Goldberg junk. Flashlight robot fixes wires with a hammer and a button. Cafeteria robots eat donuts. Maintenance robots repair. Power goes out. The robots are stiff. Parts do not fit. The workplace is organic chaos.

Godot. AnimatedSprite2D with SpriteFrames. Import Aseprite with the wizard or drop the sheet and JSON. Default texture filter Nearest. Integer scale. FPS from the durations. Tags become animation names. Loop only on idle and walk. Point sampling. No mip maps. Nothing glows.

### COOK

One dented green clay cartridge plate. One rusty button. One flashlight robot with a hammer. One cafeteria donut tile. One Godot scene with nearest filter and integer scale. Lock these against the earlier sheets. The art coordinator can cook them. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light the stillness and the parts that do not fit, decided at the source.
