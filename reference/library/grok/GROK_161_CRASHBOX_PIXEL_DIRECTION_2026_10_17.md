# CRASHBOX PIXEL AND SPRITE DIRECTION

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said Crashbox. He said he needs more research. He said help with our direction and like pixels and how to make shit look like fire, sprites, and shit for the whole enchilada. He said he has been saddened by the art direction that the chat has been doing on Claude. He said Analog horror.

## MY PROPOSAL

Crashbox is the reference. Cuppa Coffee Studios built it. They used clay, puppets, physical models, and mixed media. The framing is a low-tech computer made of junk. Green cartridges are sculpted clay. Rusty tin robots load them. Movement is stop-motion. Faces are simple and a little wrong. Nothing is clean. That is the look to push into sprites.

### HOW TO MAKE IT LOOK LIKE FIRE IN PIXELS

Bake the handmade into the pixels. Do not smooth it. Do not anti-alias the edges hard. Leave visible steps in the curves. Clay should look like clay even at 16 or 32 pixels. Use limited palettes per object. A robot is rust brown, dull metal gray, and one sick green for the light or the clay cartridge. Do not add clean highlights. Dirt and fingerprints are the highlights.

Sprites should move like stop-motion. Fewer frames. Hold each pose. The walk is not fluid. It is a series of deliberate poses. A body turns in three or four steps, not eight. That stiffness is the point. It matches the chip-in-the-body undead and the broken machines.

Bodies are assembled. A person sprite can have a head that sits slightly off, an arm that is a different tone, a torso that looks patched. The Revolting Slob was a puppet who looked wrong on purpose. Captain Bones was a skeleton whose bones rearranged. Do the same. A hire can have a face that is simple clay shapes. A robot can have mismatched parts. A drone can look like it was taped together.

Tiles are junk. A street tile is cracked asphalt with a piece of metal or a faded line. A building is not clean brick. It is boards, rust, and one clay-green cartridge stuck in a wall. The tactical map stays house-sized tiles. The art on those tiles looks built by hand inside a machine that is already falling apart.

UI and menus borrow the same. A hire row is a green clay cartridge shape. A button is a rusty robot part. Text is not clean. It sits a little off. Grain is baked into the sprites, not a filter on top. The light is flat or from one hard source. Nothing glows.

### WHAT TO COOK FIRST

One person sprite. Simple clay face, assembled body, stop-motion walk. One robot. Rusty, mismatched, loads a green clay cartridge. One tile set. Street, wall, and one object that looks handmade. One UI plate. Green cartridge shape with rusty edges. The art coordinator can use those as the lock. Everything else grows from them.

This is the direction. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light and the stillness, decided at the source. The Claude chat has been soft. This is the harder handmade version.
