# ASK 117: Battle Brothers' Company Screen, From Screenshots

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Find screenshots of how Battle Brothers' company menu works. They really simplify how easy it can look. You're over complicating it. It's really hard to navigate your equipment, the levelling up.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The company screen opens with C or I. On the left is the paper doll with slots for helmet, body armor, main hand, off hand, ammo, accessory, and four backpack slots. On the right are the stats with icons for hit points, fatigue, resolve, initiative, melee skill, ranged skill, melee defense, ranged defense, and others. Below the stats sit the active skills and traits. At the bottom is the roster: two rows of nine for front and back lines, one row of nine for reserve. Drag a portrait into a formation slot to set starting position. Drag into reserve to keep them out of the fight. The stash tab swaps the roster for the item list, sorted by type. Right-click equips an item to the selected man. Alt-right-click marks an item for repair. Level-up opens a window that shows three attribute rolls with stars, then a perk tree of three branches. You pick three stats and one perk. The dismiss button removes a man for a cost. Dev Blog 10 and the beta UI post describe the paper doll and the three-row formation. The Keyboard Shortcuts page lists C/I to open and Tab to switch men. Screenshots appear in Dev Blog 10, the IndieDB progress report, and tutorial videos. The screenshot does not show the exact pixel layout of every icon or the precise dismiss cost.
