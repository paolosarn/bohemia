# ASK 35 A WORLD THAT FEELS MADE FRESH THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
I'm not getting procedurally generated world vibes; every time I start new I spawn next to the church settlement.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers generates a new world at the start of every campaign from a seed. Settlements are placed near fertile land and rivers. Roads connect them and often avoid dark forests. There are typically about 17 settlements split among three noble houses, but the split can be uneven. Mountain ridges and other terrain act as barriers. Villages and cities look different by surrounding terrain and purpose. Factions have agendas and send parties. The start location varies by seed. The wiki does not say the exact algorithm for choosing the first settlement. Articles on procedural worlds say freshness comes from rules that create coherent regions, rare interesting events, and visible landmarks rather than pure randomness. Players notice the same patterns if the rules are too simple.

In Bohemia the same generation stays. Settlements sit near resources. Roads connect them. Different parts of the map have different factions. The first look shows a fresh layout. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Global Map and Settlements pages, Dev Blogs on worldmap and village screens, and articles on procedural generation. The wiki does not say the exact start settlement rule.
