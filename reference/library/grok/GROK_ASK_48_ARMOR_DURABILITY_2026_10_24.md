## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers tracks armor durability separately for head and body. Hits reduce durability. Zero durability means no protection. One tools point repairs 15 durability. Equipped items repair automatically. Marked stash items repair too. Base rate is 3 durability per hour. Camping doubles it. Blacksmith adds 33 percent. Destroyed armor can be salvaged by Scavenger. Zero durability armor stays but is useless until repaired. Weapons break and are lost at zero unless Blacksmith is present. The wiki does not list every edge case for every attachment.

In Bohemia the same durability and repair rates stay. Tools still repair 15 points. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
