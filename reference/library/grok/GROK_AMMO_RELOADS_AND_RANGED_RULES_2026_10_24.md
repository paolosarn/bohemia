## MY PROPOSAL:

This page covers how Battle Brothers handles ammunition and reloads for ranged weapons so Bohemia can keep the same rules with the gas can and nails ammo skin. The wiki pages Ammunition, Weapons, Bows, Crossbows, and Firearms give the numbers. The wiki does not say the exact amount the Scavenger recovers or every edge case for mid-battle swaps.

There is one global ammunition stockpile. A new bundle holds 50 units. Maximum carry is 300 on Veteran or Expert, 500 on Beginner. Quartermaster adds 100. After each battle the quivers, powder bags, and throwing bundles refill automatically from the stockpile if you have enough. One arrow or bolt costs 1 unit. One handgonne shot costs 2 units. One throwing weapon costs 3 units. Running out leaves the quivers empty. The Scavenger recovers some of what you used. You can buy more at a Marketplace or Fletcher. Arrow Maker's Shed makes it cheaper and more available. The wiki does not say the exact Scavenger recovery amount.

Bows use an arrow quiver that holds 10 (14 for crafted). You equip one in a special slot and can carry more in bags. Changing an empty quiver costs 4 action points. Crossbows use a bolt quiver the same way. They shoot for 3 action points and reload for 4. They start the fight loaded if you have one equipped. They cannot reload while engaged in melee. Handgonnes fire for 3 action points and reload for 9. Mastery drops the reload to 6. Range is 2 tiles. They shoot in a cone and ignore some line of sight. Throwing weapons use their own bundles that refill the same way. Slings use stones and need no ammo.

For Bohemia the rules stay the same. The ammo pile is one shared skin: a gas can plus loose bullets and nails. Cars are the handgonne skin. They take a reload action, use 2 units from the pile per shot, and start loaded if you have ammo. Bows and crossbows become nail-throwers or duct-taped guns that use 1 unit per shot from the same pile. Quivers become scrap pouches that hold the same number. Running out empties the pouch. Main character downs instead of dying. Nothing glows. House tiles stay. The five ammo skin options for a vote are: gas can with loose nails, rusty bullet box, duct-taped powder bag, plastic jug of fuel and screws, and expired medical vial full of nails. One becomes the skin. The rest are not in the game.

The wiki pages used are Ammunition, Weapons, Bows, Crossbows, and Firearms. Numbers not listed there are marked the wiki does not say.
