## MY PROPOSAL

These rules are from the perks page. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Perks I have not opened every DLC perk page, so a perk that is not in this list is not a claim that it does not exist.

Tier 1. Fast Adaptation adds 10 percent to hit for each miss, and the bonus resets on a hit. Crippling Strikes lowers the injury threshold by 33 percent. Colossus raises hitpoints by 25 percent. Nine Lives once a battle survives a killing blow with 11 to 15 hitpoints. Bags and Belts adds two bag slots. Pathfinder cuts movement action points by 1, to a minimum of 2, and halves fatigue. Adrenaline costs 1 action point and 20 fatigue and puts you first next round. Recover costs 9 action points and cuts current fatigue by half. Student adds 20 percent battle experience and an extra perk point at level 11.

Tier 2. Executioner adds 20 percent damage to an injured target. Bullseye cuts the blocked-shot penalty from 75 percent to 50 percent. Dodge adds 15 percent of current initiative to both defenses. Fortified Mind raises resolve by 25 percent. Steel Brow stops a head hit from being a critical. Quick Hands makes one swap a turn free, except shields.

Tier 3. Backstabber doubles the surround bonus to 10 percent per ally. Rotation costs 3 action points and 25 fatigue and swaps two characters, ignoring the zone of control. Rally the Troops costs 5 action points and 25 fatigue. Taunt costs 4 action points and 15 fatigue, range 3.

Tier 5. Footwork costs 3 action points and 20 fatigue and lets you leave a threatened tile without a free strike. Lone Wolf, with no ally within 3 tiles, adds 15 percent to attack, defense, and resolve.

Tier 6. Berserk once a turn gives 4 action points back on a kill, capped at the maximum. Nimble reduces hitpoint damage by up to 60 percent based on armor weight.

Tier 7. Duelist, with the offhand free, makes 25 percent of damage ignore armor. Killing Frenzy adds 25 percent damage for 2 turns after a kill. Indomitable costs 5 action points and 25 fatigue.

Weapon masteries are tier 4. Each cuts skill fatigue by 25 percent. The firearms mastery reloads a handgonne for 6 fatigue and lets it fire every turn. The page states that. I am not translating these into Vegas skins on this page.
