# ASK 127: The Mayor Arc

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Read the whole master file. The mayor arc is the jewel. Territory to popular mandate to governance. The more the city backs you, the easier building becomes.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The master locks the mayor arc as three rungs across the three acts. Territory is building where you are loved. Popular mandate is when the city backs you. Governance is when you are the mayor and building is easier across the map. The courthouse is the physical seat. MAYOR_SHARE is null and pending. The master does not lock the number that flips a rung, or whether a destroyed settlement can be rebuilt under the mayor.

Battle Brothers has no mayor system. The Restore Location contract lets you secure a ruined attached location and escort workers to rebuild it. Settlement situations can include a rebuilding effort that raises building-material prices for a few days. The wiki does not say a player can become mayor of a settlement, or that relations above a threshold unlock city-wide building. The wiki does not say whether a fully destroyed settlement can ever be restored.

Bohemia has no locked rule that says the first rung is unlocked by relations above 70 with one settlement, by completing a set of contracts, or by surviving the first crisis. The master does not say whether the mayor seat is a single city or a valley-wide title that appears only in Act 2.

WHAT IS NOT: Number that flips a rung. Rebuild power over destroyed settlements. Single city or valley title.
