# CHIP DRUMS LOOP LINES

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

Paolo said more. He wants better 120 BPM 8-bit songs that play on HTML without the crushed-sand sound so Claude can make stronger tracks for the light game engine.

## MY PROPOSAL

Drums. Noise for snare and hats. Sine or triangle pitch drop for kick. Low short burst for kick. Mid filtered noise for snare. Tiny high click for hats. Keep every hit under 100 ms. Fast exponential decay. High-pass the noise so it does not smear. This keeps the low end clean and stops the sand.

Loops. Write 8 or 16 bars. End on the same chord or note as the start. Land on a downbeat. Leave a little space before the repeat. Use the Web Audio clock, not setTimeout, so the seam stays tight. Test the loop for at least 30 seconds. No clicks. No cut-off tails.

Independent lines. Three voices move as separate melodies, not stacked chords. One square does the lead. One does a counter line or arpeggio. Triangle holds the bass. Think counterpoint. Each line is clear. The mix stays dry and readable. Limited channels force the clarity.

### COOK

One noise snare and pitch-drop kick. One 16-bar 120 BPM loop that seams clean. One counterpoint pair of square lines over triangle bass. Lock these against the earlier sheets.
