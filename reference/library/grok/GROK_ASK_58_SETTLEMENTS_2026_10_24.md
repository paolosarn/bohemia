# ASK 58 SETTLEMENTS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Settlements stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers places civilian towns and military fortifications on the map. Size changes prices and the quality of goods and recruits. Attached locations add specialized goods, services, and background chances. Situations change prices, recruit pools, and contract odds. Settlements belong to noble houses. The player visits them to trade, hire, rest, and take contracts. The wiki does not list every edge case for every settlement type.
