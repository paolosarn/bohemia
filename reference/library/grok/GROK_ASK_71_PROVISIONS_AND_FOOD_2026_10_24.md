# ASK 71 PROVISIONS AND FOOD THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Provisions and food stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers requires each brother to consume two units of provisions per day. Traits can change the amount. Food comes in stacks that spoil after a set number of days. The company always eats the food closest to spoiling first. Running out of food lowers mood and can lead to desertion. Food variety can raise or lower mood through events. Terrain can increase consumption. The wiki does not list every edge case for every food type or terrain.

In Bohemia the same provisions system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
