# ASK 54 ORIGINS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Origins stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers lets the player pick a company origin at the start. Origins set the starting roster, gear, funds, and special rules. Lone Wolf starts with one hedge knight and ends if he dies. Peasant Militia starts with 12 lowborn and fields 16. Manhunters start with indebted and can field 16. Some origins lock hiring or change renown. The wiki does not list every edge case for every origin.

In Bohemia the same origin system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Origins, Lone Wolf, and Peasant Militia pages, and developer notes. The wiki does not say every edge case.
