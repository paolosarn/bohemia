# SETTLEMENT SCREEN THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Lean into the Battle Brothers settlement menu. Not a single hire card. Your lead walks up to whoever you tap while the talk opens at the same speed.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers presents the settlement as a panorama made of about fifteen layers. The background changes with the terrain around it. A settlement near forest or snow looks different from one on the steppe or in the mountains. A town hall or fortification of different size sits on a hill. Buildings that the player can interact with are placed along the incline. Almost every settlement has a marketplace. Larger ones add specialized traders for weapons and armor. A tavern lets the company rest until morning. Coastal settlements have docks for sea travel. The people who appear on the screen are only there when there is a reason. Recruits show only when there are people to hire. Contract givers show only when contracts are available. Night closes the shops. Only the tavern stays open. The ambient sound changes with the place and the buildings. A coastal village has seagulls. A settlement with a smithy has hammering. Every settlement has the sound of people.

Attached locations sit outside the settlement on the world map and change what the settlement offers. There are about thirty of them. Wheat fields make food cheaper and bring farmhands. Iron mines increase metal goods and bring miners. A watchtower can spawn militia and add militia to the hire list. Destroying an attached location removes its benefits. Settlement size and the number of attached locations affect prices, the quality of goods, and the number of recruits. The wiki lists the buildings that can appear. Marketplace, weaponsmith, armorer, temple, training hall, kennel, harbor, and arena are among them. Market stock refreshes every three days unless a contract or caravan changes it. Temple speeds injury healing for a price. Training hall gives an experience boost for a price that scales with the brother's level.

In Bohemia the settlement screen is the same panorama. The buildings the player can tap are the ones that have a service. When the player taps a person the lead walks to them and the talk opens in the same time the walk takes. Background people walk short loops between buildings and do not block the path. Night closes the shops except the place that stays open. Ambient sound changes with the hour and the buildings that are present. Attached locations on the map change what the settlement sells and who is available to hire. Nothing waits. The player sees the place and the people before any menu opens.

Sources: Battle Brothers developer blogs 37, 53, 56, and 60 on village screens and attached locations; wiki pages on Settlement buildings and Settlements and attached locations; player and modder descriptions of market refresh and recruit generation.
