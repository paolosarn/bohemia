# WIEDERGANGER AND BARBARIAN UNITS STATS AND SKILLS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
A combat tile is house-sized. Melee is likely the next tile, not the same tile. Guns and bows reach 2, 3, or up to about 12-13 tiles.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for the units. Action points and fatigue costs stay the way Battle Brothers does them.

Wiederganger has 100 health, 45 melee skill, -5 melee defense, -5 ranged defense, 45 resolve, and 6 action points. Bite costs 4 action points and 0 fatigue for 15-35 damage. They come in large numbers and walk slowly into spearwalls. They have a 66 percent chance to rise again once with 50 percent health unless the head is destroyed. They can pick up weapons but cannot use ranged attacks. A human they kill can rise as a Wiederganger Brother with the same rules.

Barbarian Thrall has 70 health, 55 melee skill, 50 ranged skill, 0 melee defense, 0 ranged defense, 70 resolve, 115 initiative, 120 fatigue, 15 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. Barbarian Fury costs 3 action points and 5 fatigue and lets two adjacent units swap places. They advance, use throwing weapons if they have them, and use Adrenaline to act first.

Barbarian Chosen has 130 health, 75 melee skill, 60 ranged skill, 15 melee defense, 10 ranged defense, 90 resolve, 115 initiative, 140 fatigue, 20 fatigue recovery, and 9 action points. They use the same Barbarian Fury and advance aggressively. They are heavily armored and appear in late game groups.

In Bohemia the same numbers and costs stay. The look changes to chip-bodies that keep moving after a hit and scavenger crews that swap places and throw. Melee stays the next tile. Ranged reaches further. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Wiederganger, Wiederganger Brother, Zombies, Barbarian Thrall, Barbarian Chosen, Barbarians, and Skills.
