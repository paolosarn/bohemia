## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers generates a new world map at the start of each campaign. The map has terrain types that change movement speed, visibility, and food use. Settlements, roads, camps, and fog of war cover the map. North is colder and south is hotter. Parties move, leave tracks, and act on their own. The wiki does not list every edge case for map generation.

In Bohemia the same world map system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Global Map page and developer notes on worldmap generation. The wiki does not say every edge case.
