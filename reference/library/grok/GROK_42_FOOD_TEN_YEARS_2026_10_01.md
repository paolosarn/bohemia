# FOOD THAT LASTS TEN YEARS

One winner per stack. The rest are not in the game.
Vegas heat is the test. A hot room cuts shelf life about in half.

Lives. White rice in a sealed bucket, 10 to 30 years cool, about 10 at 70F. Honey, forever if dry. Hard liquor, forever. Sound commercial cans, USDA says safe with no rust, dent, or swell. Quality peaks at 2 to 5 years, then gets worse, not poisonous. Freeze-dried food in a sealed can, 25 to 30 years cool, about 10 if the room is hot. Hardtack and pemmican, years if bone dry.

Dies. Bread, tortillas, string cheese, queso bags, deli ham, cryovac ham, fruit leather, tallboys, home brew, cup noodles, vending pastry, thawed anything. Spam pantry life is listed at 2 to 5 years. An MRE is about 3 to 5 years, less in heat.
