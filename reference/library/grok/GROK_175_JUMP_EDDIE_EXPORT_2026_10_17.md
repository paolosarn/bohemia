## MY PROPOSAL

Jump. Anticipation crouch 1 or 2 frames. Hold 80 to 120 ms. Launch stretch 1 pixel taller. Apex hold. Fall. Landing squash 1 or 2 pixels wider and shorter. Hold the land 80 to 200 ms. Volume stays roughly the same. Arcs not straight lines. No tween. Variable holds. Preview at 12 fps. Stiffness is allowed. The Crashbox hold is the point.

Eddie Bull. He is a southern caricature of a rodent. Overalls, bowtie, backwards red cap. His mouth is a real human mouth edited onto the clay model. He is swallowed whole and regurgitated. The wrong mouth is the analog. Synchro-vox. The body is stiff. The mouth moves wrong. That is the translation for chip bodies. Mouths that do not belong. Parts that do not fit.

Export. Aseprite sheet with 1 pixel shape padding. Border padding optional. Tags become animation names. Export durations. JSON with the sheet. Trim only if the engine supports source size. Prefer grid for tiles. Point sampling. Integer scale. Power of 2 sheet if the engine wants it. No compression. No mip maps. Nothing glows.
