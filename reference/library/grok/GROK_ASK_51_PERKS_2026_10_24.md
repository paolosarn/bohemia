## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers has 7 perk rows. Unlocking the next row needs the number of prior rows spent. Each level grants one perk point and three attribute rolls. Colossus raises hit points by 25 percent. Gifted gives one extra level-up with maximum rolls. Fast Adaptation adds stacking hit chance on misses. Executioner adds 20 percent damage to injured targets. Student adds 20 percent experience until level 11. The wiki does not list every edge case for every perk.
