## MY PROPOSAL:

This page covers how Battle Brothers handles the day loop, camping, and repairs so Bohemia can keep the same rules with the resource pile and tool skins. The wiki pages Provisions, Game Mechanics, and Tools and Supplies give the numbers. The wiki does not say the exact real-time length of every phase beyond one full day being about 1 minute 45 seconds.

A full day moves through Dawn, Morning, Midday, Afternoon, Evening, Dusk, and Night. Vendors and recruit screens close at night. Taverns stay open. Time pauses in town, during events, and in combat. On the world map, while unpaused, the company consumes provisions, repairs marked gear if you have tools, and heals wounds if you have medicine. Each brother needs 2 provisions per day by default. Gluttonous and Spartan traits change that. Hard terrain costs more: plains and forest cost 1 times, steppe and snow cost 1.1, badlands 1.25, desert 1.5, mountains 2. They eat the food that spoils soonest. Running out lowers mood and can make people desert before they starve. Food variety can raise or lower mood. The Cook follower makes provisions last 3 extra days. The wiki does not say the exact desertion timer beyond a couple of days after zero.

Camping doubles the repair rate for the same time. It also lowers vision range and makes you more visible to parties, especially at night. You can camp in a settlement too. Repairs still use the same tools rules: 1 tool point for 15 durability, 3 per hour base. Batteries (crowns) pay daily wages. A missed payday can make a hire leave a couple of days after batteries hit zero, stretched or shrunk by traits.

For Bohemia the rules stay the same. Resources are the shared pile of food, gas, and water. Water skins are boiled pool water and still water that may have brain-eating amoeba. Food skins stay the ones already voted: communion wafers, cup noodles, hardtack, tuna can, and the rest. Tools skins stay duct tape, zip ties, and a hammer. Camping still doubles repair speed with those tools. Running out of resources lowers mood and can make people leave a couple of days after zero batteries. Main character downs instead of dying. Nothing glows. House tiles stay. The five resource skin options already locked stay. No new vote needed here.

The wiki pages used are Provisions, Game Mechanics, and Tools and Supplies. Numbers not listed there are marked the wiki does not say.
