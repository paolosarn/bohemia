# ASK 31. THE HAUNTING AS SIDE CONTENT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Slightly purple-aura ghosts from the Amalgamation, not the main quest, no possession. The Backrooms twice an act, always paranoid.

## MY PROPOSAL
Analog horror and ghost encounters that make a player paranoid without a jump scare use wrong details that the player notices late. Silent Hill keeps the town looking normal until a door is in the wrong place or a voice on the radio says a name the player has not heard. The player starts checking every corner. Resident Evil 7 uses a phone that rings when no one should be calling and a note that is written in a hand the player has already seen on a different note. The player starts reading every paper twice. The Backrooms images online use empty hallways that are almost correct. The fluorescent lights are the wrong color. A door that should open does not. The player walks slower. No monster appears. The fear comes from the place being slightly wrong.

Side-quest shapes that fit a Battle Brothers company are short contracts that do not advance the main story. A settlement asks the company to check a block where people have gone missing. The company walks the block. One house has a light on that was not on before. A person the company already talked to is standing in a different doorway and does not answer when spoken to. The contract ends when the company leaves the block. Nothing is resolved. The settlement pays in batteries and the people stop mentioning the missing. A second contract in the same act asks the company to deliver a package to an office that closed. The office is open. The person behind the desk has the face of someone the company already buried. The package is accepted. The contract ends. The company can refuse both contracts. The main quest does not mention them. The purple aura appears only on the ghosts and only for one frame when they turn. No possession occurs. The player is never forced into the Backrooms. The Backrooms appear as a short optional map the company can enter twice per act. The entrance is a door that was not there the day before. The company can leave at any time. The map is empty hallways with the wrong lights. No enemy spawns. The company finds one item and one note. The note is written in a hand the player has seen before. The company leaves. The door is gone the next day.

Sources: Silent Hill series descriptions of environmental wrongness; Resident Evil 7 phone and note details; public Backrooms image descriptions; Paolo's rules on purple-aura ghosts and the Backrooms.
