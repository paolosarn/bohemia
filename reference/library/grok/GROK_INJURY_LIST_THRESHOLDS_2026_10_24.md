## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for injuries. A hit must deal at least 10 health damage. Then the relative amount is checked against a threshold of 25 percent or 50 percent of maximum health, depending on the injury. Head hits raise the threshold by 25 percent. Crippling Strikes lowers the threshold by 33 percent. Iron Jaw raises it by 25 percent. The type depends on the weapon: blunt, cutting, piercing, or burning. One random matching injury is applied. Some enemies are immune to injuries.

Temporary injuries heal over days with medicine or at a temple. Examples include Broken Arm at 50 percent threshold that cuts melee and ranged skill by 50 percent, Broken Leg at 50 percent that adds 2 action points per tile and cuts initiative by 40 percent, Bruised Leg at 25 percent that adds 1 action point per tile, Cut Arm at 25 percent that cuts skills by 15 percent, and Cut Artery that cuts health by 35 percent and bleeds. There are about 45 temporary injuries.

Permanent injuries happen when health reaches zero and the character survives the blow. The chance is 33 percent, or 90 percent with Survivor. It is not possible on a fatality, while retreating, or from self-damage like bleeding. The log says struck down. Examples include Brain Damage that raises resolve by 15 percent and cuts experience and initiative by 25 percent, Broken Elbow Joint that cuts melee and ranged skill by 20 percent and melee defense by 30 percent, Broken Knee that cuts defense and initiative by 40 percent, and Weakened Heart that cuts health by 30 percent. There are about 14 permanent injuries. The main character is downed, never dead.

In Bohemia the same thresholds and effects stay. The negative pain line stays one name. Chip-bodies and machines can still take the same injuries. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Injuries, Temporary Injuries, Permanent Injuries, and the injury mechanics developer blog.
