# ASK 33. THE MAP'S SIZE IN NUMBERS

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
How many hex tiles across and down the Battle Brothers world map is at each map size setting. How many in-game days a company at walking speed takes to cross it corner to corner and edge to edge. How many settlements and how many locations a map holds at each size. The camera's near and far zoom stops. The party marker's size in pixels against the map.

## MY PROPOSAL
The Battle Brothers wiki page on the Global Map states that a typical setup involves a total of 17 settlements. It lists 2 City Halls, 3 Town Halls, 6 Village Halls, 2 Strongholds, 2 Castles, and 2 Fortified Manors. The wiki does not say the exact number of hex tiles across or down the world map at each map size setting. The wiki does not say how many in-game days a company at walking speed takes to cross the map corner to corner or edge to edge. The wiki does not say the camera's near and far zoom stops or how many tiles fit on a 1080p screen at each stop. The wiki does not say the party marker's size in pixels against the map. The wiki does not say how many camps, ruins, or legendary places a map holds at each size.

Players and modders have measured the generated maps. An older Reddit comment states that before the Beasts and Exploration DLC the map was about 140 by 140 hexes and after it became about 140 by 170. A 2025 Reddit post that sampled about 10,000 seeds reports that a size value ranges from just above 10,000 to just above 18,500 and that diameter ranges from the 140s to the 200s. Nexus mod posts that edit the world generation script show SizeX and SizeY values. Vanilla is described as returning to 140 by 140 when those values are set low. Mods that push the map larger use values such as 210 by 180 or 185 by 185. One comment notes a rendering limit around the original 140 by 140 bounds. The wiki does not confirm any of these numbers. The developer blog describes the world map as covering the scope of a single continent with geography and climate similar to a middle-European country. It is procedurally generated at the start of every new game. Terrain types have different movement speeds listed on the wiki. Plains, farmland, and steppe move at 1. Mountains move at 0.25. Roads are faster. Players report multi-day trips for distant contracts. The sources do not give a fixed number of days to cross the full map corner to corner or edge to edge.

For Bohemia the map must match or exceed these scales so that travel takes real time and settlements are not on top of each other. The Las Vegas valley becomes the dense middle of a larger region. The party marker stays readable at every zoom. Settlements, roads, and locations keep their true world size at every zoom stop. The camera never shows black. The far stop shows the whole valley the way Battle Brothers shows its continent.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Global Map page; Reddit r/BattleBrothers posts on map sizes, DLC expansion, and seed measurements; Nexus mod posts that edit world.nut SizeX and SizeY; Battle Brothers developer blog posts on worldmap generation and rework.
