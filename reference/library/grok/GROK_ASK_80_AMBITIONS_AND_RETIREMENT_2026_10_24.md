# ASK 80 AMBITIONS AND RETIREMENT THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Ambitions and retirement stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers has ambitions that are mid-term goals the player chooses. Completing one gives renown and raises company mood. Some give unique rewards. The player can cancel one at a morale cost. Retirement ends the campaign and gives one of five endings based on progress. The score uses renown, items, crises ended, and days. The wiki does not list every ambition reward or exact score formula.

In Bohemia the same ambitions and retirement system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
