### Real Las Vegas Valley Colours

From above the valley shows red rock of the Spring Mountains and Red Rock Canyon from iron oxide in the sandstone, basalt dark patches, irrigated green lawns and golf courses that appear as bright red in false-color Landsat, blue pools, the dark water of Lake Mead with its white bathtub ring, grey concrete grids of streets, rust tones on older structures, and neon remnants at night. Street level adds the same red rock walls, faded concrete, and sparse green against the desert. NASA Landsat SVS images and aerial stock photos of Red Rock and Southern Highlands confirm these. Sources are the NASA SVS Las Vegas Landsat series and Getty and Dreamstime aerial descriptions. The map should use these instead of a single beige flood so factions and territories read by colour.
