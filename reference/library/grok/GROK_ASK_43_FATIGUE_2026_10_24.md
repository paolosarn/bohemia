# ASK 43 FATIGUE THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Fatigue stays the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers uses fatigue as a separate limit from action points. Moving, striking, and many skills cost fatigue. Getting hit adds 5 fatigue. Dodging or blocking adds 2. A character recovers 15 fatigue at the start of each turn (20 with Iron Lungs). Armor and heavy weapons reduce maximum fatigue. When current fatigue reaches the maximum, the character cannot act until recovery. Some enemies do not accumulate fatigue. The Recover skill spends a turn to cut accumulated fatigue by half. The wiki does not list every exact fatigue cost for every skill and terrain.

In Bohemia the same fatigue system stays. Recovery stays at 15. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Combat Mechanics page, developer notes on stamina, and community guides. The wiki does not say every exact fatigue cost.
