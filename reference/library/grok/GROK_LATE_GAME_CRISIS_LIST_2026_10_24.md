# LATE-GAME CRISIS LIST THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for late game crises and the developer blog. There are four types. The player can choose the first at start or leave it random. It is foretold around day 50 to 70 and starts around day 80 to 100 if the roster is strong enough. There is no renown requirement. A crisis lasts 30 to 100 days. After one ends, another can start in the next 100 days with at least 50 days of peace. The same crisis never happens twice in a row.

War of the Noble Houses has houses capture rival settlements without destroying them. Greenskin Invasion has orcs and goblins combine, form mixed units, raid attached locations, and can burn settlements. Undead Scourge has ancient dead combine with necromancers and wiedergangers, and dead can return as wiedergangers. Holy War is a north-south religious conflict over three holy sites and does not destroy settlements.

In Bohemia the same four crises and the day-80-to-100 start stay. The 50-to-100-day ramp stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Late Game Crises, Greenskin Invasion, Undead Scourge, and the late game crises developer blog.
