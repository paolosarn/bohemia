# TRADE GOODS AND CARAVAN RULES THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Medicine = medicine. Crowns = batteries/electricity. Provisions = Resources (food, gas, and water). Renown = Clout. Ammunition = Ammunition. Tools = Tools. Water and food share the resource pile. Gas is in the ammo pile. 10 crowns equal 1 battery. 1 battery is the lowest price anything can trade for. Crowns are not in the game. They are the wiki source only. Lean into the Battle Brothers settlement menu.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for trade goods, provisions, contracts, and settlement situations. Trade goods include peat bricks (base 100), cloth rolls (140), quality wood (180), copper ingots (220), amber shards (260), furs (300), spices (320), salt (340), incense (380), dyes (400), silk (460), and uncut gems (520). They are produced by attached locations. Buy where produced and cheap. Sell far away where not produced and expensive. Prices scale with settlement size, number of attached locations, relations, and situations.

Well Supplied gives -10 percent buying and selling prices and +15 percent items available. Completing an Escort Caravan contract makes the destination well supplied. Escort Caravan locks movement to the caravan. Losing all donkeys fails it. Losing some poorly completes it (50 percent less crowns and relation, full renown). The Trading Caravan origin gives 10 percent better prices and 66 percent renown gain.

In Bohemia the same trade goods, price rules, and caravan contracts stay. Provisions stay the resource pile. Crowns convert at 10 to 1 battery. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
