# ASK 46 HIT CHANCE THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Hit chance stays the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers calculates hit chance as skill minus defense. Skill and defense can be multiplied by status effects. Defense above 50 is halved for the calculation. Surrounding reduces effective defense. Height difference adds or subtracts 10 percent. Shields add defense that some skills ignore. The final chance is clamped between 5 percent and 95 percent. A d100 roll decides the hit. Beginner difficulty shifts the numbers by 5 percent. Utility skills always hit. The wiki does not list every modifier for every skill.

In Bohemia the same formula stays. The 5 to 95 clamp stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Hit Chance and Combat Mechanics pages, and community code notes. The wiki does not say every modifier.
