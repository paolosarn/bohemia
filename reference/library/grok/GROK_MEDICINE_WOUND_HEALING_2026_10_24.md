## MY PROPOSAL:

This page covers how Battle Brothers handles medicine and wound healing so Bohemia can keep the same rules with the old bandages and expired ibuprofen skins. The wiki pages Medical Supplies, Temporary Injuries, and Permanent Injuries give the numbers. The wiki does not say the exact healing speed beyond one medical supply point per day per injury.

One point of medical supplies is needed each day for every temporary injury to improve and heal. Lost hitpoints heal on their own without medicine. Running out of medical supplies stops temporary injuries from healing, except through events. Maximum carry is 100 on Veteran or Expert, 150 on Beginner. Quartermaster adds 50. You can buy them at a Marketplace. Herbalist's Grove makes them more available. Bandages can stop bleeding injuries only during the fight they happen. Untreated injuries can fester into an Infected Wound that cuts hitpoints and fatigue. Temples can treat an injury for a price that scales with remaining days and character level. The base temple price starts at 20 crowns times remaining days times a level multiplier. The wiki does not say every infection chance beyond the listed injuries that can trigger the event.

Permanent injuries happen when a character is struck down and survives. Survival chance is 33 percent, or 90 percent with the Survivor trait. They cannot survive a fatality, a retreat hit, or self-damage. The Water Skin legendary item is the only way to remove them. They lower mood. Examples include Broken Knee that cuts defense and initiative, or Missing Eye that cuts ranged skill. The combat log shows struck down instead of killed.

For Bohemia the rules stay the same. Medicine skins are old bandages, expired ibuprofen, and super glue. One point of those heals one temporary injury per day the same way. Pain traits stay the same negative names. A struck-down main character gets a permanent chip-body injury instead of dying, matching the downed-never-dead lock. Super glue can stand in for the Water Skin that removes a permanent injury. Nothing glows. House tiles stay. The five medicine skin options for a vote are: old bandages, expired ibuprofen bottle, super glue tube, rusty medical kit, and duct-taped first aid pouch. One becomes the skin. The rest are not in the game.

The wiki pages used are Medical Supplies, Temporary Injuries, and Permanent Injuries. Numbers not listed there are marked the wiki does not say.
