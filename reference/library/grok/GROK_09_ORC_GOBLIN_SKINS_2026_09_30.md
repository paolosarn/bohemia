# VAMILY. ORC AND GOBLIN JOBS

Mechanics stay. Skin only. His chat called robots and drones an example, not a lock.

Orcs, wiki: Young 125 health, 9 action points, jumps and stuns. Warrior 200 health, heavy armor, pushes your line to reach the back. Berserker 250 health, light armor, high damage. Warlord 300 health, 500 armor. Catapult is a machine, 250 health. Stronger orcs do not stun and do not get pushed. Easy to hit. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Orcs

Skin: yard machines with no driver. Young is a forklift that hops the gap. Warrior is the armored loader that shoves. Berserker is the same machine with the limiter off. Warlord is the yard boss. Catapult is a trailer tube. A person in a truck is already the noble. Do not double it.

Goblins, from the class pages and a translation of the Chinese wiki: skirmishers hold the line with spears, nets, and bolas so ambushers can shoot. Ambushers use a spiked impaler that knocks back. Overseer lashes morale and is hard to shoot. Small, many, annoying.

Skin: the small-bot layer. Skirmisher is a roomba with a sting and a net. Ambusher is a roof drone. Wolfrider is a drone on a dog. Shaman is a jammer. Overseer is the handheld controller. A person can still be holding that controller.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Orc health and the jump, push, and stun jobs are from the English orc page.

WHAT IS NOT: Goblin lines are thin on the English fetch this pass. The skins are a proposal.
