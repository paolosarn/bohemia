# ASK 45 SURROUNDING THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Surrounding stays the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers gives a surrounding bonus when two or more units are adjacent to a target. For each unit after the first, allies attacking the target gain +5 percent melee hit chance. The same rule applies to both sides. Ranged weapons and stunned units do not contribute. Backstabber doubles the bonus to +10 percent. Underdog reduces or removes it. The bonus is a direct addition to hit chance, not a defense reduction in the final display. Facing does not matter. The wiki does not list every edge case for every weapon.

In Bohemia the same surrounding bonus stays. +5 percent per extra adjacent unit stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Combat Mechanics and Hit Chance pages, and community guides on surrounding. The wiki does not say every edge case.
