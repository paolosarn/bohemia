# ASK 118: The Map's Size in Numbers

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Our map has to be as big as Battle Brothers. A larger grid or something.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The Global Map wiki page states a typical setup of 17 settlements: 2 City Halls, 3 Town Halls, 6 Village Halls, 2 Strongholds, 2 Castles, and 2 Fortified Manors. Settlement numbers can vary by noble house split. Enemy camps and other locations spawn separately and can be revealed by contracts or exploration. The wiki does not say the exact hex count across or down the world map, the number of in-game days to cross corner to corner, or the camera zoom stops that fit on a 1080p screen. Player data from a seed survey of about 10,000 maps lists a diameter range from the 140s to the 200s and a size value from just above 10,000 to just above 18,500. A Campaign Map Config mod notes that the base map is locked near 140 by 140 and that larger sizes require changes outside the scripts. Dev Blog 7 describes the map as one continent similar to a middle-European country, procedurally generated each game. The data does not say the party marker size in pixels.

A LITTLE THING FROM the attached locations that change what a settlement sells, from Dev Blog 56.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Typical 17 settlements. Map regenerates each campaign. Terrain slows movement and changes sight.
WHAT IS NOT: Exact hex dimensions. Days to cross. Zoom tile counts.
WHAT WE SHOULD ASK NEXT: Whether Bohemia should lock one map size or offer three like the wiki settings.
