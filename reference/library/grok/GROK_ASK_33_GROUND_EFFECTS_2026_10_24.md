# ASK 33 WHAT BATTLE BROTHERS LEAVES ON THE GROUND THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
What happens in Battle Brothers during combat, the live effects that get left on the ground. A pile of blood underneath him.

## MY PROPOSAL
The wiki does not say the exact hit flash, blood spray amount by damage, blood decal size or how long decals stay, the size of the pool under the fallen, or precise corpse visuals. Base game blood is restrained. A community mod doubles blood spray and quadruples blood puddles and adds more decals on the ground and bodies. Fatalities can produce decapitation, smashed heads, or disembowelment depending on weapon and hit location. Decapitated or smashed heads prevent some undead from rising. Corpses remain on the tile or an adjacent free tile. Dropped gear follows durability and chance rules and stays on the ground until looted after the fight. Undead and beasts leave their own remains. The page does not say exact particle counts or pool duration.

In Bohemia the same effects stay. Blood and parts stay on the house-sized tiles. Nothing glows. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
