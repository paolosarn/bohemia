# NAMED AND LEGENDARY ITEM RULES THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Nothing glows.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for named and legendary items and the game guide. Named items (famed items) are rare items with unique looks, randomized stats, and names. Legendary items are unique items with powerful special abilities. Named items always drop unless destroyed. Legendary items always drop. Champions always carry at least one named item. Three-skull contracts raise the chance of champions. One- and two-skull contracts that target a camp overwrite any named items the camp holds. Three-skull contracts do not.

The chance for a named item in a non-legendary location is (resources + 4 times distance) divided by 5, minus 37. Resources come from the location type. Distance is to the nearest settlement. Farther from civilization raises the chance. Tavern rumors can point to named items. Shops in large settlements can sell them, especially under well-supplied or safe-roads situations.

Legendary locations offer unique rewards that exist only once per campaign. Examples include the Black Monolith and the Goblin City.

In Bohemia the same named and legendary rules stay. Nothing glows. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Named and Legendary Items, Named Item, Legendary Locations, and the Game Guide.
