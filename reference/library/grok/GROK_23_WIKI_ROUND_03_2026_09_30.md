# WIKI ROUND 3. PERKS 4 TO 7, AND THE FIGHT

https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Perks
https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_Mechanics

Perk 4. Mace mastery, stun sticks. Flail ignores a shield. Hammer +33% armor damage. Axe +50% shield damage. Cleaver bleed doubles. Sword riposte has no hit penalty. Dagger drops to 3 action points. Polearm drops to 5. Spearwall stays up. Crossbow and handgonne: handgonne reloads for 6 fatigue and can fire every turn. Bow +1 range. Throw +30% at 2 tiles, +20% at 3.

Perk 5. Reach Advantage +5 defense a hit, max 5. Overwhelm -10% skill on anyone who acts after you. Lone Wolf +15% if no friend within 3 tiles. Underdog ignores surround. Footwork is 3 action points.

Perk 6. Berserk gives 4 action points on a kill, once a turn. Head Hunter chains a head hit. Nimble cuts hitpoint damage up to 60%. Battle Forged cuts armor damage by 5% of current armor.

Perk 7. Fearsome makes a 1-point hit a morale check. Duelist, empty off hand, +25% ignores armor. Killing Frenzy +25% damage for 2 turns. Indomitable is 5 action points, 50% dodge, no stun.

Fight. 9 action points. Flat step 2. Rough 3. Swamp 4. Height +1. One-hand swing 4. Two-hand 6. Fatigue back 15 at the start of the turn. Leave a man's tile and he swings free. Lines start at least 5 hexes apart. Bows 7, 8 with mastery. Crossbows 6. Head is a second roll. Higher ground is +10% hit.

ROUND 4 IS: injuries, mood, and the map day.
