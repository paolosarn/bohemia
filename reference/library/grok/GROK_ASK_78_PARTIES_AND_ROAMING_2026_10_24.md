## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers has roaming parties that move on the world map. Their strength scales with days passed, distance from settlements, and the player's party strength. Camps spawn parties to raid or defend. Parties leave tracks that show their type. The player's party strength is the sum of the twelve strongest brothers where each brother scores ten plus two per level past one. The wiki does not list every spawn rule or exact scaling formula.

In Bohemia the same parties and roaming system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
