## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers tracks relations per settlement and noble house on a 0 to 100 scale. Completing contracts raises relations. Attacking, failing contracts, or betraying lowers them. Relations drift toward 50 each day. Better relations improve buy and sell prices. Fortified settlements use the noble house relation. The wiki does not list every edge case for every action.

In Bohemia the same relations system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
