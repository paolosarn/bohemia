# ASK 28. THE SETTLEMENT THAT FEELS ALIVE, AND THE WALK-UP

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Your lead walks up to whoever you tap while the talk opens at the same speed.

## MY PROPOSAL
Games that make a town screen feel alive without slowing the player use walking to talk, background people with jobs, day and night cycles, sound beds, and small reactions. Rune Factory 3 has townspeople jog when they are late, greet the player when they pass, and talk to each other with small symbols above their heads. The player can walk up to them and start a conversation without a loading screen or a wait. The talk opens as the player arrives. Harvest Moon and Story of Seasons titles keep every named character on a schedule. They walk from house to shop to field and back. The player can watch them do it. The town changes with the hour. Lights come on at night. People go home. Sound beds shift from market noise in the day to insects at night. Majora's Mask tracks every character through a full day. The player can stand still and watch them walk home. Guild Wars 2 fills city streets with hundreds of unnamed people who walk, talk, and have no quest attached. They exist only to make the place look occupied.

In Bohemia the settlement screen shows the buildings the player can tap. When the player taps a person the lead character walks to them at the same speed the talk window opens. Background people walk between buildings on short loops. They do not block the path. Day and night change the lights and the sound bed. A person near the player turns their head for one frame when the player passes. The talk window opens at the same moment the lead stops walking. Nothing waits. The player can leave the conversation and the lead is already facing the next building. The settlement never feels empty and the player never waits for a walk that the game could have done for them.
