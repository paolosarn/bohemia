## MY PROPOSAL:

This page covers how Battle Brothers handles armor attachments and gear repairs so Bohemia can keep the same rules with scrap and duct tape skins. The wiki pages Armor Attachments and Tools and Supplies give the numbers. The wiki does not say the exact cost formula for settlement repairs or how much durability a single hit removes.

Armor attachments go on body armor only. One attachment per piece. Replacing an attachment destroys the old one forever. You drag it on or right-click while the person wears the armor. Craftable ones come from a Taxidermist and use trophies plus crowns. Purchasable ones come from an Armorer. Some armor comes pre-attached, and you lose that attachment if you swap it. Examples from the wiki: Additional Fur Padding receives only 66 percent of damage that ignores armor and costs 2 more fatigue, needs Thick White Fur and Unusually Large Wolf Pelt, 400 crowns. Bone Platings absorbs the first hit that does not fully ignore armor each battle, 450 crowns. Direwolf Pelt Mantle reduces nearby enemy Resolve by 5. Horn Plate cuts melee body damage by 10 percent. Unhold Fur Cloak cuts ranged body damage by 20 percent. Metal Plates and Rivets add 30 durability for 3 more fatigue. The wiki lists more but does not say every interaction with every skill.

Tools repair gear over time. One tool point fixes 15 durability. Repair speed is 3 durability per hour. Camping or escorting a caravan doubles that rate. The Blacksmith follower speeds repairs by 33 percent and stops gear from being lost when durability hits zero. The Scavenger pulls some tools from every fully destroyed armor you break. Equipped gear repairs itself if you have tools. You mark stash items with Alt plus right-click. Weapons that hit zero durability break and are gone forever. Armor at zero is useless until repaired. You can pay a Weaponsmith or Armorer in town for faster repairs, but it costs more based on the item's worth. Max tools carried is 150 on Veteran or Expert, 200 on Beginner. Quartermaster adds 50. The wiki does not say the exact settlement repair price formula.

For Bohemia the rules stay the same so the economy and fight feel match. Attachments become scrap add-ons you zip-tie or duct-tape on: rusty car door panel, cooler lid with zip ties, tin robot shoulder plate, plastic bucket patch, medical vest with super glue. Tools skins stay duct tape, zip ties, and a hammer. One point of those fixes 15 durability the same way. A fully broken weapon is scrap metal you lose. Broken armor is a useless husk you can still tape back together later. Main character downs with a chip-body injury instead of dying. Nothing glows. House tiles stay. The five attachment skin options for a vote are: duct-taped car door panel, zip-tied cooler lid, rusty tin robot plate, plastic bucket with super glue seams, and expired medical vest patch. One becomes the skin. The rest are not in the game.

The wiki pages used are Armor Attachments, Tools and Supplies, Body Armor, and The Blacksmith. Numbers not listed there are marked the wiki does not say.
