# ONE PASS: HOW A BATTLE BROTHERS FIGHT RUNS

Wiki, combat mechanics, pulled 2026-09-30. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Combat_Mechanics

The board is hexes. Height matters. A zone of control stops a free walk past a man.

Turn order is initiative. Highest goes first. Fatigue and heavy gear drop your initiative, so a tired man slides to the back of the round. Action points refill at the start of every round. Most men have 9. A wiederganger has fewer. A direwolf and a goblin wolfrider have more.

A move spends AP. A hit spends AP and fatigue. At 9 AP a man can step and swing, or swing twice if the weapon is cheap. A handgonne cannot fire, reload, and move in one turn. Reload is 9 AP.

Morale is a ladder. Drop to Fleeing and the man drops his shieldwall, spearwall, and riposte, and moves to the end of the round. Next round he acts last. He runs. You lose the stick. He can rally if he is not next to an enemy, or if someone shouts him back.

Injuries are separate from lost hitpoints. Hitpoints come back on their own. An injury needs one medicine a day or it stays. A head hit does 50% more hitpoint damage, not more armor damage.

The dead, already sourced: 66% to stand once at half health unless the head is gone. Standing on the body shoves you. The living controller can raise twice a turn and juice one body.

## Options, same rules, house tiles

1. Keep 9 AP. One tile is a house, so a step is expensive. A pistol shot is 4. A car shot is 3 and the reload is the whole turn. This is the wiki.
2. Shrink the round. 4 AP. Step or shoot, not both, unless a perk says so. Faster demo. Less Battle Brothers.
3. Initiative stays. Tired men go later. A chip-body does not get tired. That is the horror. They keep their place in line.
4. Fleeing stays. A hire who breaks runs for the street. You do not drive him. He comes back if he gets a house away and nobody is on him.
5. Injuries stay. One medicine a day. A chip-body does not bleed and does not take a medicine. Head shot is the off switch.
6. Zone of control stays. You do not walk through a man on the next tile. A car can, and that is the ram. Spends gas.

I would ship 1, 3, 4, 5, 6. Do not shrink the round until he has played 9 AP on a house tile.
