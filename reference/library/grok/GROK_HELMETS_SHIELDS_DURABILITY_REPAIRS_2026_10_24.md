## MY PROPOSAL:

This page covers how Battle Brothers handles helmet and shield durability and repairs so Bohemia can keep the same rules with scrap and duct tape skins. The wiki pages Shields, Headgear, Tools and Supplies, and Shield Expert give the numbers. Loading screen tips and player reports fill some gaps. The wiki does not say the exact durability lost per hit on a helmet or the full Split Shield damage formula.

Helmets work like body armor. They have durability and a fatigue penalty. A head hit damages the helmet first. Once the helmet hits zero it stops protecting, but it can still be repaired later with tools. Helmets drop from enemies if they meet the durability threshold. The wiki lists many helmet durability numbers from 10 to over 300 but does not say the exact damage formula for a head hit beyond the general armor rules.

Shields have their own durability. They take 1 durability when they block a normal hit. Certain skills like Split Shield and some two-handed attacks deal more damage to the shield. When a shield hits zero it breaks and is lost forever unless you have the Blacksmith follower. With the Blacksmith the broken shield returns to your inventory at 0 durability so you can repair it. Shieldwall doubles the shield's defense bonus for one turn and adds more if nearby allies also use it. Shield Expert raises the defense bonus by 25 percent and cuts shield damage taken by 50 percent to a minimum of 1. The Living Tree Shield regenerates half its max durability each turn. The wiki lists shield durability from 12 for small skirmisher shields up to 72 for heavy metal ones. The wiki does not say every skill that damages shields beyond the loading tip that axes and some two-handed weapons can destroy them.

Repairs for both use the same tools rules as body armor. One tool point fixes 15 durability. Speed is 3 per hour, doubled when camped. The Blacksmith speeds it by 33 percent and saves gear that hits zero. You mark stash items with Alt plus right-click. Equipped gear repairs itself.

For Bohemia the rules stay the same. Helmets become scrap bucket lids, rusty car hubcaps, or tin robot head plates that lose durability the same way and get duct-taped back. Shields become car door panels, cooler lids with zip ties, or plastic riot shields that take 1 damage on a block and break unless you have the hammer-tool Blacksmith equivalent. A broken shield is scrap you lose or tape back from zero. Main character downs instead of dying. Nothing glows. House tiles stay. The five helmet skin options for a vote are: rusty car hubcap, plastic bucket with super glue, tin robot head plate, cooler lid with zip ties, and expired medical face shield. The five shield skin options are: car door panel, zip-tied cooler lid, plastic riot shield, rusty tin robot plate, and duct-taped wooden pallet. One of each becomes the skin. The rest are not in the game.
