# ASK 96 RELATIONS AND REPUTATION THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Relations and reputation stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers tracks relations per settlement from zero to one hundred. Relations trend toward fifty each dawn. Good relations lower buy prices and raise sell prices. Reputation starts at fifty and mainly blocks raze contracts above eighty. The wiki does not list every price formula or every reputation effect.

In Bohemia the same relations and reputation system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Relations and Reputation pages. The wiki does not say every formula.
