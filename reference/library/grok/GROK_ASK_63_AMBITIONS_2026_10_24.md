# ASK 63 AMBITIONS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Ambitions stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers offers ambitions as mid-term goals for the company. The player picks one from a short list. Completing an ambition raises renown by 100 and lifts company mood. Some ambitions unlock noble contracts or give unique rewards. Cancelling an ambition hits morale. The available choices change with the company state and past ambitions. The wiki does not list every edge case for every ambition.

In Bohemia the same ambition system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
