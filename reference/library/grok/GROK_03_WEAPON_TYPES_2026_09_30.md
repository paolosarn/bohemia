# ASK 3. EVERY WEAPON TYPE AND ITS SKILLS

Research pass. No translation yet. Source is the public wiki, pulled 2026-09-30. Class pages: https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Melee_Weapons and https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Ranged_Weapons

Global rules from the melee page: a skill hits hitpoints for the listed damage, and armor for that damage times armor effectiveness. A head hit does 50% more hitpoint damage, not more armor damage. Orc weapons cost humans 5 extra fatigue per skill.

Ranged rules: throwing bundles hold 5 shots (bolas 6). Bows use a quiver. Crossbows and handgonnes start the fight loaded, then need bolts or a powder bag. Ammo refills after the fight from the company stock. Slings use stones and do not spend ammo.

## Melee types

1. Daggers. One-hand: Knife, Dagger, Notched Blade, Rondel Dagger, Qatal Dagger. Two-hand: Estoc. No fatigue to carry. Skills: Stab. Puncture, 4 AP, 20 fatigue, -15% to hit, ignores armor, no crit, no double-grip bonus. Deathblow, 4 AP, 10 fatigue, extra damage if the target is dazed, stunned, or netted. Qatal uses Deathblow. Estoc uses a repeat stab and a high-pen stab. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Daggers

2. Swords. One-hand: Shortsword, Falchion, Arming Sword, Noble Sword, and named variants. Two-hand: Greatsword, and the fencing sword line. Skills: Slash, accurate, weak vs armor. Gash, 4 AP, 20 fatigue, +10% to hit, injuries land easier. Riposte, 4 AP, 25 fatigue, -10% to hit, swings back at every miss until next turn. Two-hand: Overhead Strike, Split (2 in a line, 6 AP, 30 fatigue), Swing (up to 3 around you, 6 AP, 30 fatigue). https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Swords

3. Maces. One-hand and two-hand, plus orc clubs and barbarian maces. Skills: Bash. Knock Out, 4 AP, 25 fatigue, 75% stun, half damage, 10 extra fatigue on the target. Two-hand Strike Down, 6 AP, 30 fatigue, 75% stun for 2 turns. Split Shield, 6 AP, 20 fatigue, damage straight to the shield. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Maces

4. Spears. One-hand spear and two-hand pike, plus goblin and ancient spears. Skills: Thrust, accurate. Spearwall, hold the tile, stab anyone who steps in. Knock Back. The spear is the anti-charge tool. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Spears

5. Axes. One-hand and two-hand, plus orc and barbarian axes. Skills: Chop. Split Shield. Round Swing on the two-hander, hits around you. Head hits hurt. Axes are the shield-breakers. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Axes

6. Flails. One-hand and two-hand, plus threshers. Skills: a basic swing that can ignore the shield's defense, and a head-seeking strike. Lash-style attacks roll the head more often. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Flails

7. Cleavers. One-hand and two-hand, plus orc cleavers and whips. Skills: a cut that bleeds. Decapitate, 6 AP, 20 fatigue, bonus damage as the target loses health, and a kill can take the head. Whips use a disarm and a bleed at range 3. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Cleavers

8. Hammers. One-hand and two-hand, plus orc and barbarian hammers. Skills: a smash with high armor damage. Shatter on the two-hander, breaks armor in a small arc. Destroy Armor is the single-target version. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Hammers

9. Polearms. Bill, pike, polehammer, warbrand, swordlance. Skills: a reach hit at 2 tiles, often with hook or repel. Swordlance swings an arc. This is the back-line melee. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Polearms

## Ranged types

10. Bows. Many shots, no reload, uses arrows. Aimed Shot costs more fatigue for a better hit. Quick Shot is the fast one. Damage falls with range. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Bows

11. Crossbows. Light, normal, heavy. Shoot Bolt, 3 AP, 5 fatigue, +15% to hit, -3% per tile, 50% armor ignore on the skill line. Reload is 4 AP and 20 fatigue. Starts loaded. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Crossbows

12. Firearms. Handgonne only on this page. Fire, 3 AP, 5 fatigue, +10% to hit, -10% per tile, ignores cover, can hit 5 tiles behind the target, 25% armor ignore. Reload, 9 AP, 20 fatigue. Range 2. Damage 35-75. Worth 3000. Mastery cuts reload to 6 AP. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Firearms

13. Throwing. Bolas, javelins, axes, spear, sling. 4 AP, 15 fatigue. +20% to hit, -10% per tile past 2. Bundles hold 5 (bolas 6). Sling does not spend ammo. Minimum range 2, except slings. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Throwing_Weapons

14. Throwable items. Off-hand, one use, no refill. Net roots and cuts defense. Acid flask. Blessed water hurts undead. Fire pot burns the tile, friend or foe. Smoke pot. Flash pot dazes up to 7. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Throwable_Items

Firelance and mortar are city-state tools, not on the firearms table above. Firelance is one line of fire, then dead until the next fight. Mortar is a blast on a tile. Dev blog: https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Dev_Blog_132:_Gunpowder_Weapons
