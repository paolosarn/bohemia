# ASK 81 RELATIONS AND REPUTATION THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Relations and reputation stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers tracks relations with each settlement and noble house. Good relations improve buy and sell prices. Relations move toward fifty each day. Completing contracts raises them. Attacking or failing contracts lowers them. Reputation is a separate score that mostly affects which contracts appear at the extremes. The wiki does not list every price modifier or every reputation effect.

In Bohemia the same relations and reputation system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Relations and Reputation pages. The wiki does not say every price modifier.
