# ASK 73 AMMUNITION AND RANGED RELOADS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Ammunition and ranged reloads stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers keeps a global ammunition stockpile. Quivers, powder bags, and throwing weapon bundles refill automatically after battle from that stockpile. Arrows and bolts cost one point each. A handgonne shot costs two points. A throwing weapon costs three points. Slings use stones and do not need ammunition. Enemies have infinite arrows and bolts. The wiki does not list every edge case for every weapon or event.

In Bohemia the same ammunition system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.
