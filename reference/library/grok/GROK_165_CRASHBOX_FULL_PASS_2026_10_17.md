## MY PROPOSAL

This is a longer pass. Crashbox intro toggles between a falling computer with mechanical sounds and a distorted lullaby before it becomes the groove. The computer crashes every episode. A robot fixes it and complains. Game loaders are physical motherboards. Scrap Metal Game Loader is rusted scrap held by a handle. It falls apart when it loads and puts itself back together. Wireframe Game Loader is yellow red orange sheet metal tangled with wires. It slides. Arcade Game Loader is brown and silver and closes the last game. Dora Smarmy in Distraction News is a cardboard cutout. Dirty Pictures is a maid dusting a portrait while a wooden Old Fossil holds cue cards. Lens McCracken is noir comic with zoomed photos and a darkroom machine called the Solutionator. The whole show is mixed media that never fully cleans up.

### MORE SEGMENTS FOR THE LOOK

Distraction News is a cardboard cutout anchor with random images popping in. The cutout is flat and wrong. Dirty Pictures is dust and a wooden guard. The portrait reveals in stages. Lens McCracken is monochrome comic with a broken lens. The Solutionator looks like it has a face. Eddie Bull has a real human mouth on a clay body. Captain Bones falls into bones. The Revolting Slob is a puppet that explodes. Haunted House Party is cardboard cutouts and lightning. Sketch Pad is hand-drawn and the pictures do not make sense until the missing page. The framing robots are rusty tin. Some are voiced by Jerry Stiller. They pressure each other. The machine is always almost broken.

### ANALOG HORROR IN PIXELS

Limited palette. Soft & Dirty or Dry Sea or a custom rust dull metal sick green clay set. Value does the work. Shadows cooler. Highlights are scratches or dust. Grain is baked into the pixels not a filter. Stop-motion frames. 3 or 4 for a walk. Hold the poses. No tween. Bodies are assembled. Heads sit off. Mouths can be wrong. Parts can fall and rebuild. A green clay cartridge can be stuck in a wall. A robot can have a scrap loader arm that falls off. Tiles can have wire and scrap. UI can be cardboard cutout shapes or cartridge shapes with rusty edges. Light is flat or one hard source. Nothing glows. The stiffness and the parts that do not fit are the horror.

### COOK THIS SET

One scrap metal loader that falls apart and rebuilds. One cardboard cutout face. One clay body with a wrong mouth. One body that falls into parts and rebuilds. One green clay cartridge stuck in a wall tile. One monochrome comic style zoomed photo reveal. One dusty portrait reveal. One wireframe or scrap tile. Lock these. The art coordinator can cook against the earlier sheets. The main character is still downed, never dead. Square house tiles stay. Nothing glows. Analog horror is the light the stillness and the machine that is always almost broken, decided at the source.
