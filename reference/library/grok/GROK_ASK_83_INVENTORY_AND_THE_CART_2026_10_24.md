# ASK 83 INVENTORY AND THE CART THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Inventory and the cart stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers starts the company with a donkey and about ninety-nine inventory slots. Cart, wagon, and big wagon upgrades each add twenty-seven slots. Brothers have paper-doll slots and a small bag. Tools, ammo, and medicine sit in global piles. Food spoils in the inventory. The wiki does not list every starting slot count or every bag rule.

In Bohemia the same inventory and cart system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Retinue and Getting Started pages. The wiki does not say every starting count.
