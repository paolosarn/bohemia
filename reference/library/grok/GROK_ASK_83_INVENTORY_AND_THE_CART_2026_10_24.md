# ASK 83 INVENTORY AND THE CART THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Inventory and the cart stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers starts the company with about ninety-nine inventory slots. Cart upgrades bought from the retinue screen add more slots in steps. Brothers also have bag slots that hold spare gear. Tools, ammunition, and medicine use separate stockpiles with their own limits. The wiki does not list every slot count by origin or every upgrade cost.

In Bohemia the same inventory and cart system stays. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Retinue and Getting Started pages. The wiki does not say every slot count.
