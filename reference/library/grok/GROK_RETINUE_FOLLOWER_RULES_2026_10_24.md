# RETINUE AND FOLLOWER RULES THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Lean into the Battle Brothers settlement menu. Not a single hire card. Renown = Clout. 10 crowns equal 1 battery.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for retinue and renown. The retinue is non-combat followers that grant advantages outside combat. There are five slots. Slots unlock by renown: 1 at 250, 2 at 750, 3 at 1400, 4 at 2250, and 5 at 3350. Each follower has a hire cost and unlock conditions. You can replace one at any time for a cost.

Examples include the Blacksmith (repairs even broken or lost gear, 20 percent faster repairs, 20 percent less tool use, cost 3000), the Bounty Hunter (+3 percent champion chance, pays 300 to 750 per champion slain, cost 4000), the Scout (15 percent faster travel, prevents terrain sickness, cost 2500), the Scavenger (recovers tools, supplies, and some ammo), the Drill Sergeant (more experience, no mood loss in reserve), the Agent (reveals contracts and situations), and the Surgeon (better healing).

The cart can be upgraded for more inventory space.

In Bohemia the same retinue slots, unlocks, and follower effects stay. Renown is Clout. Crowns convert at 10 to 1 battery. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Retinue, Renown, and the retinue developer blogs.
