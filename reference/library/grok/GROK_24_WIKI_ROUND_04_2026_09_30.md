# WIKI ROUND 4. INJURIES, MOOD, THE DAY

Injuries last 1 to 9 days. Grazed neck 1-2. Bruised leg 2-3. Broken nose 3-5. Stabbed guts 3-5. Broken arm 5-7. Fractured skull 6-9. The injuries page did not spell the medicine spend. Game mechanics says wounds heal if medical supplies are on hand, while the map is not paused.

Mood runs 0 to 100. Content is 43-57. Angry is 0-15, and that is when a man can desert. Not paid is -1. Not eaten is -1. A win is +0.35. A tavern drink is +1. Left alone, mood walks back to 50.

The day. Each man eats 2 food. Wage is crowns at noon. A level adds 2. Greedy adds 2. A full day is about 1 minute 45 seconds. Night slows travel and cuts sight. Food, repairs, and healing tick while the map is not paused.
