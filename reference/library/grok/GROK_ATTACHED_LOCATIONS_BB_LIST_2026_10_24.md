# ATTACHED LOCATIONS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Lean into the Battle Brothers settlement menu. Not a single hire card.

## MY PROPOSAL
Attached locations sit outside a settlement on the world map. They change what the settlement sells, what it charges, and who shows up to be hired. Destroying one removes its benefits. The developer blog says there are no less than thirty of them. The wiki groups them and lists what each one does.

Military ones bring fighters. A wooden watchtower or a stone watchtower increases militia, deserters, and retired soldiers. Militia barracks and barracks also bring sellswords and hedge knights.

Supply ones stock the marketplace. A workshop produces tools and supplies. An arrow maker's shed produces ammunition. An herbalist's grove produces medical supplies.

Provision ones stock food. A beekeeper produces mead and attracts farmhands. A brewery produces beer. Fishing huts produce dried fish, make throwing nets more available, and attract fishermen. A gatherer's hut produces roots and berries. Goat pens produce goat cheese or dried lamb in the south. A hunter's cabin produces cured venison. A mushroom grove produces mushrooms, can make strange mushrooms available, and attracts cultists. An orchard produces dried fruits. A pig farm produces smoked ham and attracts farmhands. A plantation produces dates or spices. Wheat fields produce ground grains and bread and attract farmhands. A winery produces wine.

Trade ones stock trade goods. An amber collector produces amber shards. A dye maker produces dyes. A gem mine produces uncut gems. An incense dryer produces incense. A lumber camp produces quality wood and attracts lumberjacks. A peat pit produces peat bricks. A salt mine produces salt. A silk farm produces silk. A surface copper vein produces copper ingots. A trapper produces furs. A wool spinner produces cloth rolls. A surface iron vein, blast furnace, leather tanner, and ore smelters support armor and weapon production.

The settlement's market prices rise with the number of attached locations. A settlement that produces a good charges less for it. The wiki does not list a fixed maximum number of attached locations per settlement type. It states that they are randomly generated and that some may not appear on a given map. Contracts exist to raze them. Once destroyed, the benefits are gone until they are restored.

In Bohemia the same system decides what a settlement sells and who is available to hire. Looking at the attached locations on the map tells the player what to expect before they walk in.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki page Settlements and attached locations; developer blog 56 on attached locations; wiki pages on Character Backgrounds and Market prices for various items.
