# DEMO BUILD. HAND THIS TO A BUILDER

He already knows Battle Brothers. This page is the skin on the rules he locked. Not a lesson.

Start. One person. Lone Wolf job. If that person dies, the run ends. Roster cap 12 when he hires. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Lone_Wolf

Board. One tile is a house. Same tile is nobody. Melee is the next tile. A step costs 2 action points. A one-hand swing costs 4. Most people have 9. Lines start about 5 tiles apart.

Guns. Pistol is the dagger. One tile. Spends ammunition. Ignores armor. Car is the handgonne. Cone. Range 2. Shot spends the resources pile, which is food, gas, and water. Reload eats the turn.

Melee that spends nothing, only at 1 tile: pipe stuns, machete gets meaner when they are low, sledge breaks a door. That is the reward for stepping in.

Dead. Bodies with a chip. Slow. Bite. No gun. 66% to stand once at half health unless the head is gone. Standing on the body shoves you. A living person in the back can stand one up if the head is there.

Buy. Indebted are bought. Batteries up front. Almost no daily pay.

Day. 2 resources a man. 1 medicine per injury. Batteries every day. Zero batteries, then a couple of days, then a hire can walk. Town does not eat the day. A fight does not. The road does.

First job. Defend a block. Road crew or chip-bodies, whichever camp is closer.

Pace. Hard early. Mean by day 50 to 100. First big trouble around day 80 to 100. He can pick it or let it roll.

Build order for the alpha. One man on a street of houses. Six road crew. 9 action points. Pistol, pipe, car. Win, get batteries, hire one person. That is the slice. Do not build the crisis until that slice plays.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Pistol, car, chip-body, buy-the-indebted, house tile, and the day-100 pace are his.

WHAT IS NOT: Prices. Everything still costs one until he plays it.
