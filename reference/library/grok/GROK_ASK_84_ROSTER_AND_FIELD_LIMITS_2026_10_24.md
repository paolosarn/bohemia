# ASK 84 ROSTER AND FIELD LIMITS THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
The roster and field limits stay the way Battle Brothers does it.

## MY PROPOSAL
Battle Brothers lets the company hire up to twenty brothers. Only twelve can enter a battle at once. The rest stay in reserve. Some origins raise the field limit to sixteen and the roster limit higher. The twelve strongest count for contract scaling. The wiki does not list every origin limit or every scaling exception.

In Bohemia the same roster and field limits stay. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki Game Mechanics page. The wiki does not say every origin limit.
