# SETTLEMENT SITUATIONS LIST THE WAY BATTLE BROTHERS DOES IT

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT
Lean into the Battle Brothers settlement menu. Not a single hire card. Renown = Clout. 10 crowns equal 1 battery.

## MY PROPOSAL
These numbers come from the wiki pages for settlement situations and the game guide. Settlements can have multiple situations at once. Situations change prices, item and recruit availability, and which contracts appear. Percentages apply multiplicatively on the base price modifiers.

Examples include Ambushed Trade Routes (+20 percent buying, +10 percent selling, -25 percent items), Well Supplied (-10 percent buying and selling, +15 percent items), Abducted Children (+25 percent buying, -25 percent selling, -50 percent recruits), High Spirits (better trade), Safe Roads (+10 percent buying and selling, +10 percent items), Besieged (+25 percent buying, +100 percent food prices, -25 percent items, -50 percent recruits), and Raided (varies by duration).

Many situations last a set number of days or until a related contract is completed. Completing an Escort Caravan sets Well Supplied. Completing a Drive Away Bandits contract can clear Ambushed Trade Routes.

In Bohemia the same settlement situations and their effects stay. Renown is Clout. Crowns convert at 10 to 1 battery. A translation vote is only when Paolo asked for that item.

Sources: Battle Brothers wiki pages for Settlement Situations, Ambushed Trade Routes, Well Supplied, and the Game Guide.
