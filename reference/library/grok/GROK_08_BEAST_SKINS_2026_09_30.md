# VAMILY. BEAST SKINS

Mechanics stay. His 9/29 lock: beasts are lab animals, Ice Age and after, power went out. Numbers are the wiki. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Beasts

1. Direwolf. 130 health, 12 action points, can swing 3 times if it does not move. Skin: dire wolf from a lab. Pack, flank, fast.
2. Hyena. 120 health, 14 action points. Skin: a real hyena. Same job, laughs, hangs back until you are hurt.
3. Nachzehrer. 80 to 380 health. Grows when it eats. Skin: a big scavenger, short-faced bear or a giant hyena. Eats the dead and gets bigger.
4. Serpent. 130 health, bite. Skin: a big desert snake. Not a dragon.
5. Unhold. 500 health, 9 action points, throws. Skin: a short-faced bear or a ground sloth. The frost one is the same animal in a cold room. The armored one is a bear in a vest some crew put on it. Wiki says armored unholds are barbarian, not beast.
6. Lindwurm. The big snake with acid. Skin: a lab croc or a komodo. Acid stays as a spit, not magic.
7. Webknecht. 60 health, web. Skin: a big spider from a lab. Eggs are the sack. Horror fits.
8. Schrat. 600 health, a tree that walks, saplings. Skin: not an animal. A dead greenhouse rig still moving. Weak. Do not put it in the first district.
9. Alp. Night thing, fear. Skin: not an animal. A speaker and a light that ruins sleep. Same job as the geist.
10. Hexe. A person. Charm, range 8, resist is your resolve minus 35. Hex makes you take her damage. Skin: a living woman with a collar remote. Not a beast. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Hexe
11. Ifrit. Fire thing. Skin: a gas leak and a flame, or skip. Not an animal.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: The health and action points above are from the beast table. Hexe charm is from her page. Lab animals are his lock.

WHAT IS NOT: Schrat, alp, ifrit, and hexe are not animals. I skinned them. He can kill those skins.
