# ASK 124: The Soundscape Rule

## PAOLO SAID IN THIS CHAT:

Read the whole master file. The soundscape rule says every screen and every building is heard from real material. Sounds are on hold until the look holds. Every pixel and every sound is analog horror.

## MY PROPOSAL:

The master locks analog horror for every pixel and every sound until the game is done. The soundscape rule requires every screen and every building to be heard from real material. Sounds are currently on hold. The master does not lock a list of which screens exist or which real materials each one uses.

Battle Brothers ships an original soundtrack of roughly 39 tracks by Breakdown Epiphanies. The Steam page lists signature themes for places and factions. Tracks exist for brigands, undead, orcs, goblins, beasts, noble houses, villages, towns, and the world map. Dev Blog 119 says the composers treated each faction as its own RPG approach: undead uses dark strings and bone-like percussion, orcs use brass and metal, brigands use instruments you can carry. The wiki does not say the exact trigger that starts or stops a track, whether the world-map theme changes by terrain, or whether settlement music changes by size or relation. The wiki does not say the file format or the BPM of any track.

Bohemia has no locked rule that says a settlement screen plays a loop built from recorded duct tape, zip-tie clicks, and hammer hits, or that the world-map theme is a single analog-horror bed that only changes when a party enters vision. The master does not say whether combat music is a 120 BPM loop that stays locked to the turn beat or a free-form track that ignores the beat.

A LITTLE THING FROM the Battle Brothers track "Bone March (Undead)" that already treats the undead as a distinct sonic faction.

WHAT IS CERTAIN: Analog horror for every sound. Soundscape from real material. Sounds on hold. Battle Brothers has faction and place themes.
WHAT IS NOT: Screen list. Material list. Trigger rules. BPM lock.
