# ROAD TIERS

Wiki types. Not Vegas names. The math swaps these in as the score and the days rise. https://battlebrothers.fandom.com/wiki/Brigand_Thug

Thug. Health 55. Head armor 0 to 45. Body 5 to 70. Melee 55. Most of the time a short sword or hatchet. Sometimes an axe, goedendag, or pitchfork.

Poacher. Health 55. Body armor 20. Ranged 50. XP 175.

Marksman, low. Health 60. Body 20. Ranged defense 10 before day 40, 15 after. Bullseye after day 20.

Raider, low. Health 75. Head 0 to 110. Body 55 to 110. Melee 60. Resolve 55. Perks: brawny, bullseye, executioner, recover, rotation.

Leader is the top of this faction. Higher tier means more of these and fewer thugs. Combat difficulty, set at the start, pushes that mix.
