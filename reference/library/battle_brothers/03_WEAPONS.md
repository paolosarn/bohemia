# 03 WEAPONS: CLASSES, SKILLS, AND THEIR SHAPE ON THE TILES (recall unless cited)
# Paolo 9/24: "some weapons hit multiple tiles at the same time, six or three; a machine gun is comparable
# to a greatsword or a battle axe." This file is the shape table he asked about. Numbers are recall.

ONE-HANDED (4 AP an attack, two attacks a turn):
- SWORDS (Shortsword 25-30, Arming Sword 30-40, Noble Sword 35-45; low fatigue 10-12; ignore ~25% armour,
  70% armour damage): SLASH (one tile), RIPOSTE (free counter-attack on every miss against you this turn).
  Shape: ONE TILE. The precise cheap weapon.
- AXES (Hatchet 25-35, Hand Axe 30-40, Fighting Axe 35-45; fatigue 15; ignore ~30% armour, 110-120% armour
  damage): CHOP (one tile, +30% head chance recall), SPLIT SHIELD (destroys shield durability fast). Shape:
  ONE TILE, kills shields.
- MACES (Club, Mace 30-40, Winged Mace 35-45; ignore ~25%, 100% armour damage): BASH (one tile, dazes: the
  target loses AP next turn), KNOCK OUT (stun one turn). Shape: ONE TILE, controls a man.
- HAMMERS (Wooden Hammer, Military Pick / Hammer 35-45; ignore 20-30%, 150% armour damage; some hammers
  ignore 100% of a shield's block): STRIKE (one tile), SHATTER / DEMOLISH ARMOUR. Shape: ONE TILE, eats
  armour.
- CLEAVERS (Butcher's Cleaver, Military Cleaver 40-50, Orc Cleaver; ignore ~20%, 80% armour damage):
  CLEAVE (one tile, BLEED 5 a turn), DECAPITATE (bonus vs low hitpoints; a decapitation cannot rise as
  undead). WHIP (Lash 2 tiles away, Disarm). Shape: ONE TILE, bleeds.
- FLAILS (Flail 35-45, Three-headed Flail): FLAIL (one tile, IGNORES SHIELDS: the shield's defence does
  not count), LASH. Shape: ONE TILE over the shield.
- SPEARS (Spear 25-35, Boar Spear; +20% to hit): THRUST (one tile), SPEARWALL (any enemy entering an
  adjacent tile is hit first and stopped). Shape: ONE TILE + a zone.
- DAGGERS (Knife, Dagger, Rondel 15-25): STAB, PUNCTURE (ignores ALL armour, low damage: the finisher
  for stealing armour intact). Shape: ONE TILE through armour.
- THROWING (Javelins 2-4 range, Throwing Axes, Throwing Spears): one tile at range 2 to 3.

TWO-HANDED (6 AP an attack, one attack a turn; high fatigue 20-25; the greatsword class Paolo means):
- GREATSWORD (Greatsword 55-75, Warbrand 50-70): SWING (hits THREE tiles: the target and the two tiles
  beside it in front, one roll each), SPLIT (hits TWO tiles in a line: the target and the one behind).
  *** SHAPE: A 3-WIDE ARC or a 2-DEEP LINE. THIS IS THE MACHINE GUN. ***
- TWO-HANDED AXE (Battle Axe 60-80; Greataxe 70-90): ROUND SWING (hits ALL SIX adjacent tiles, friend or
  foe, one roll each), SPLIT MAN (one tile, +head chance, huge). *** SHAPE: SIX AROUND. ***
- TWO-HANDED MACE (Two-handed Flanged Mace 60-80): CUDGEL (one tile, dazes), STRIKE DOWN (one tile,
  stuns). Shape: ONE TILE, hard.
- TWO-HANDED HAMMER (Two-handed Hammer 55-75): SHATTER (one tile, 200%+ armour damage), DEMOLISH ARMOUR.
- TWO-HANDED FLAIL (Two-handed Flail 50-70): THRESH (hits THREE tiles in front, ignores shields), LASH.
  *** SHAPE: 3-WIDE, over shields. ***
- TWO-HANDED CLEAVER (Two-handed Cleaver / Orc Cleaver): CLEAVE (one tile, bleed, big), DECAPITATE.
- POLEARMS (range 2, attack from the second row): PIKE: IMPALE (two tiles away, one target), THRUST;
  BILLHOOK: HOOK (pulls the target one tile toward you), IMPALE; LONGAXE: CHOP at range 2, SPLIT
  SHIELD at range 2; POLEHAMMER; WARSCYTHE: REAP (hits the target and adjacent), GOBLIN PIKE. Shape:
  ONE TILE AT DISTANCE 2 (our rifle), REAP is an arc at 2.
- RANGED: BOWS (Short Bow, Hunting Bow, War Bow 30-60 range 7; QUICK SHOT 4 AP, AIMED SHOT 6 AP +hit):
  one tile at range, no reload; CROSSBOWS (Light, Heavy 55-75, range 6; SHOOT 5 AP, RELOAD 4 AP): one
  tile, hard, a reload beat; HANDGONNE (Blazing Deserts: fires a CONE of pellets hitting everything in
  a 3-tile-deep wedge, one shot then a long reload): *** THE SHOTGUN SHAPE. ***
- SHIELDS as weapons: Shield Bash / Knock Back (pushes a man one tile).
NAMED AND LEGENDARY ITEMS: rolled variants with +damage, +durability, -fatigue, unique names and
descriptions; found in legendary locations and on champions.
THE MAP TO OURS (rule 33i default, COMBAT decides the table): pistol = one tile (arming sword), rifle =
two tiles away (pike), scope = three away (bow), machine gun = a 3-wide sweep in one beat (greatsword
SWING), shotgun = a cone (handgonne), a bat or a pipe = mace (daze), a knife = dagger PUNCTURE. Every
shot still costs one; the dial is the important shot.
