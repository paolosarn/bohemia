# 06 BACKGROUNDS (recall; wages in crowns per day; hire cost 50 to 2,000+)
A recruit has a BACKGROUND (his past), a level, stats rolled in the background's ranges with 3 hidden
TALENT STARS on stats, a wage, a hire cost, and HIDDEN TRAITS revealed after hiring (some backgrounds
lean to some traits). Backgrounds also gate EVENTS (a Thief steals, a Monk preaches, a Cultist recruits,
a Noble insults a Beggar) and dialog choices.
CHEAP (wage 5-9): BEGGAR (low everything, may have Lucky), DAYTALER (average, cheap, often decent), FARMHAND
(decent hitpoints, fatigue), VAGABOND, THIEF (high initiative, ranged defence, steals from the company),
HISTORIAN?, JUGGLER (ranged skill, initiative), MINER (fatigue, hitpoints), FISHERMAN, SHEPHERD, GRAVEDIGGER
(resolve vs undead), CRIPPLE (cheap, injured), MESSENGER (initiative), CULTIST (resolve, cultist events),
BUTCHER (cleavers), TAILOR, APPRENTICE (+xp), BRAWLER (melee skill), BEAST SLAYER? (mid).
MID (10-16): MILITIA (balanced soldier-ish), CARAVAN HAND, POACHER (ranged skill, cheap archer),
WILDMAN (hitpoints, fatigue, low resolve/initiative), MONK (resolve, low melee), HOUNDMASTER, WITCHHUNTER
(resolve, ranged), RATCATCHER, BOWYER, LUMBERJACK (hitpoints, two-handed leaning), MINSTREL, DESERTER
(good stats, coward trait risk), GAMBLER, GRAVEROBBER, THIEF.
ELITE (18-30+): HEDGE KNIGHT (best melee, expensive, arrogant), SWORDMASTER (huge melee skill and
defence, low hitpoints, old), SELLSWORD (solid all-round, expensive), RAIDER (former brigand, good melee,
cheap-ish, hated by nobles), ADVENTUROUS NOBLE (resolve, melee, -ranged defence, wage 25, start level
1-3), ASSASSIN (initiative, melee, wage 20, start level 4), RETIRED SOLDIER (veteran, old), BASTARD,
SQUIRE, HUNTER (best ranged), NOMAD, ANATOMIST, MANHUNTER, BARBARIAN (huge hitpoints/fatigue, low
resolve), INDEBTED (a slave with a debt, cheap, miserable), MASTER ARCHER? (rare), KILLER ON THE RUN.
STATS: Hitpoints, Fatigue, Resolve, Initiative, Melee Skill, Ranged Skill, Melee Defence, Ranged Defence.
Level-ups: 3 stats gain a roll each; a star adds to the roll. Level 11 is the practical cap for perks.
TRAITS (two-edged, hidden until hired unless a Barber/event reveals): Strong, Tough, Iron Lungs, Quick,
Brave, Fearless, Lucky, Sure Footing, Eagle Eyes, Deathwish, Bloodthirsty, Determined | Weak, Fragile,
Asthmatic, Clumsy, Coward, Dumb, Fat, Greedy (+wage), Drunkard, Hesitant, Night Blind, Superstitious,
Dastard, Bleeder, Paranoid, Insecure, Impatient, Old (declines), Young (grows). Traits gained by events
and injuries: Survivor, Battle-hardened, Fearsome, Brute, Huge, Tiny, Hate for X, Lost eye, etc.
WHAT A BACKGROUND DOES FOR THE STORY: the roster reads as a cast at a glance (a Beggar, a Hedge Knight,
a Cultist); events pick men by background; a man's death is a story because his past was shown.
(OURS, rule 33 [bb company]: a person's background is what they did before the money died: lineman,
dealer, nurse, cop, casino floor, delivery, ex-con; the deed ledger is our traits.)
