# 07 THE ECONOMY (recall)
CROWNS: one currency. A starting company has ~500-700 crowns and 3 men after the origin fight.
DAILY COST: wages (5 to 30 per man per day; +2 per level; Greedy +2), food (a man eats ~1 unit a day;
bread 3-4 crowns, dried fruit, cheese, salted meat, fish, mead; spoilage per type), tools (to repair;
~1 crown per 3 armour points repaired recall), medicine (per wound day), ammo (arrows/bolts per shot).
A 12-man company burns 150-300 crowns a day; the game is a treadmill that forces contracts.
INCOME: contracts (100 to 3,000+ crowns by difficulty and renown; a negotiation with a mood face;
pushing too hard costs relations), loot sold (weapons and armour sell for a fraction; damaged sells for
less), trade goods (buy where produced: salt, furs, dyes, silk, incense, wine, amber, gems; sell where
absent; margin 20-100%; prices rise as you sell, fall as you buy), legendary loot.
PRICES: per settlement, driven by its type (a fishing village has fish; a city has everything at a
premium), its SITUATIONS (raided: food scarce and dear; well-supplied: cheap), and your relation (allied
house discount). Buying raises the local price, selling lowers it; prices recover over days.
RENOWN: gates contract tiers and pay; shown as a bar. RELATIONS: per house and per settlement.
AMBITIONS: chosen goals with renown rewards, a light spine ("hire 12 men", "amass 5,000 crowns", "win a
contract with three skulls", "defeat a champion", "reach 1,000 renown", "gain a legendary item").
THE LOOP: contract -> pay -> resupply -> a better man -> a harder contract; the crash is running out of
crowns with wages due, which strips men away. (OURS: batteries; a day costs one; the loan of one a night
is the wage; prices per place by supply; ECONOMY [bb money] writes the table.)
