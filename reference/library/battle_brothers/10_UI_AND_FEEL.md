# 10 WHAT IS ON SCREEN, AND THE FEEL (recall; UI [bb interface] verifies)
THE MAP HUD: top bar with crowns, food days, tools, medicine, ammo, day count and time-of-day sun; the
company's banner and name; a bottom bar with the roster's faces (small portraits with hitpoint and
morale rings), buttons for camp, roster, inventory, ambitions, the relations screen; a pause/play/2x;
a map with the marker and named settlements; a legend for terrain. Hovering a party shows its kind and
size; a settlement shows its buildings and situations. Right-click cancels.
THE SETTLEMENT SCREEN: the town in one painted view with its buildings as clickable icons; inside, the
market (grid of items with prices), the recruits (a row of faces with background and cost), the tavern
(rumours as one line each; a drink for morale), the temple (heal), the hall (contracts).
THE FIGHT HUD: the hex board; the active man's skills as a row of icons with AP and fatigue cost; his
bars (hitpoints, fatigue, morale); a turn order strip at the top with every face in order; a combat log;
end turn / wait / retreat. Hovering an enemy shows hit chance and his stats. The camera is fixed
isometric with zoom.
THE ROSTER: each man's page: portrait (painted, aged by injuries), background story text, stats with
stars, perks tree, equipment paper doll, traits list with icons, level and xp. A man's page is where the
story lives.
THE FEEL: a hand-painted map that reads like a board game; a fight that is slow, heavy and legible
(every number visible on hover); a company that outlives its men; the loss of a man with a name; the
tension of crowns vs wages; the day/night light on the map; music per region and per crisis; ambient
sound of the terrain; the drum on the fight's first turn.
SOUND (SOUNDS [bb ambience] verifies): map ambience by terrain and time (wind, birds, crickets at
night, rain); footsteps of the marker; a horn when a party is spotted; the fight has a drum, weapon
impacts by material, grunts, death cries, the shield thud; the settlement has crowd murmur and a
blacksmith; music tracks per biome and a battle theme per enemy kind.
(WHAT WE DO NOT COPY, rule 33g: the stills. Ours move: the map breathes, a place is a living street, an
event is a person speaking, under the analog horror bible with Newgrounds' hand-made energy.)
