# 09 ENEMIES (recall)
BRIGANDS (human): Thug (rags, clubs, weak), Poacher (bow), Marksman (crossbow, dangerous), Raider (mail,
proper weapons), Leader (the tough one, named), Reaver? (heavy). They break and flee; they loot; the
first hours of every campaign are brigands. Teaching: morale, shields, a leader worth killing first.
NOBLE HOUSE TROOPS: Footman, Arbalester, Billman (polearm), Sergeant, Knight (heavy armour, two-hander).
Teaching: armour walls and second-row polearms.
GOBLINS: Ambusher (bow, poison), Skirmisher (nets, spears), Wolfrider (mounted, fast), Shaman (roots and
buffs), Overseer (whips and buffs). Teaching: reach, nets, status effects; goblins are the "annoying" enemy.
ORCS: Young (fast, weak armour, many), Berserker (rage, two-handers), Warrior (heavy armour, big), Warlord
(huge). Teaching: knockback, armour, a wall that pushes you around.
UNDEAD: Wiederganger (rises again unless decapitated or burned; slow), Fallen Hero (heavy armour), Ancient
Dead (legionaries: shieldwall, spear line, honour guard), Necromancer (raises the dead mid-fight), Ghoul /
Nachzehrer (eats corpses to grow), Vampire (teleports, drains). Teaching: kill order and cleavers.
BEASTS: Direwolf (fast, pack), Webknecht (giant spider, webs), Unhold (troll: regenerates, throws men),
Lindwurm (acid blood, tail), Schrat (tree: spawns saplings, shields), Hexe (charms your men), Alp
(nightmares, night), Kraken (legendary, tentacles), Serpent (drags men), Hyena (desert pack), Sand Golem,
Ifrit (desert). Teaching: each beast is one mechanic made flesh.
BARBARIANS (north): Thrall, Marauder, Chosen (huge), Beastmaster with Unholds, Drummer. NOMADS (desert):
Outlaws, Slingers, Assassins; GILDER? SOUTHERN CITY-STATES troops: Conscript, Footman, Officer, Assassin,
Gunner (handgonne), Mortar.
CHAMPIONS: named variants with more stats and a named item.
THE SHAPE: every enemy is a rule you must learn; the roster of a fight is telegraphed by the party's size
and kind on the map; fights are chosen, not stumbled into. (OURS: the factions' crews on the map, sized
and named at a distance; FACTIONS [bb houses], COMBAT [bb fight].)
