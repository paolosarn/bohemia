# 02 COMBAT RULES (recall unless cited)
BOARD: hex tiles with height levels; a fight is generated from the map terrain where it happens (forest
tiles block sight, hills give height, swamps cost more AP to enter, snow slows). Deployment in your own
zone; the enemy deploys opposite; ambushes place you surrounded.
TURN ORDER: by INITIATIVE, highest first, recomputed each round; fatigue lowers effective initiative.
Waiting (delay your turn) is allowed once per round.
ACTION POINTS: every man has 9 AP a turn. Moving one tile on flat ground costs 2 AP (more into hills,
swamp, snow: 3 or 4). Attacks cost by weapon: most one-handers 4 AP (two attacks a turn), two-handers 6
AP (one attack), some skills 3 or 5. Switching weapons costs AP (4, or 0 with Quick Hands).
FATIGUE: every action costs fatigue (attacks 10 to 25, moving per tile 2 to 4, being hit adds some);
the cap is the man's max fatigue minus the weight of what he wears; each turn 15 recovers; at the cap
he cannot act (only wait and recover). Heavy armour is the fatigue tax.
HIT CHANCE: attacker's MELEE SKILL minus defender's MELEE DEFENCE (ranged: RANGED SKILL minus RANGED
DEFENCE), plus modifiers: height (+/-10% for high ground vs low), SURROUNDED (+5% per extra attacker
adjacent to the target, Backstabber makes it +10), shield (defender's shield adds defence: buckler 5,
heater 15 melee / 20 ranged, kite 15 / 25 recall), Shieldwall (+ more), spear +20% to hit, night (ranged
-), fatigue does not lower hit chance but morale does (wavering -10 skill, breaking -20 approx).
DAMAGE: a weapon has a damage range, an ARMOUR PENETRATION % (how much of a hit passes through armour to
hitpoints; "ignores X% of armour") and an ARMOUR DAMAGE % (how much damage the armour itself takes).
Head hits (25% base, more with some skills) do 150% damage to hitpoints. Armour must be chewed through:
a 30 damage sword against 200 body armour barely scratches; an axe or hammer strips armour fast.
INJURIES: hits past a threshold of hitpoints inflict INJURIES (cut artery, cracked skull, broken arm,
missing nose, gaping wound...) with stat penalties that heal over days with medicine; some are PERMANENT
(lost eye: ranged skill down, missing finger, brain damage) and become part of the man.
MORALE: five states per man: Fleeing, Breaking, Wavering, Steady, Confident. Checks on: an ally dying
nearby (negative), an enemy dying (positive), being hit hard, the company's resolve (leader's Resolve
matters, Rally the Troops). Fleeing men run for the edge; Fearsome and Lone Wolf change it. Enemies have
morale too; brigands break and run when the fight turns.
ZONE OF CONTROL: leaving a tile adjacent to an enemy costs a free attack on you unless you have Footwork
(or the enemy is stunned or you use Rotation). Shields can be destroyed by axes (Split Shield). Stun:
maces knock a man out of his next turn. Daze (Mace: Bash) lowers AP. Knockback moves a man a tile.
TERRAIN: high ground +10% to hit and ranged range; low ground -10%; forest blocks line of sight and
ranged fire; swamp costs AP and fatigue; night lowers ranged accuracy and sight. (THE MOUND: Paolo 9/24
keeps ONLY high ground = accuracy for us; none of the rest.)
RANGED: bows and crossbows shoot over allies with a penalty at distance and a chance to hit a friend in
the line (friendly fire is real); crossbows need a reload action; javelins and throwing axes 2 to 3 range.
ROUNDS: a fight lasts until one side is dead, fled or you RETREAT (men off the map edge; those left
behind may be lost). Loot after: enemy gear, damaged; named items rare.
