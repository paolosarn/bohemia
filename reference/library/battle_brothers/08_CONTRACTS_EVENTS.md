# 08 CONTRACTS AND EVENTS (recall)
CONTRACTS come from a settlement's hall (a speaker with a portrait: the village elder, the town
magistrate, a noble's steward, a merchant). Difficulty in skulls (1-3), pay offered, a NEGOTIATION: ask
for more (the client's face and words shift: pleased, annoyed, angry; pushing too hard he withdraws and
relations dip), ask for an advance, ask for supplies. Types: escort a caravan; deliver a package (it may be
stolen goods or a body); clear a camp (brigands, orcs, goblins, undead, beasts); hunt a monster (a
Lindwurm, an Unhold, a Nachzehrer pack); patrol a road (kill N enemies); protect a village from a raid
(defend at night); retrieve a stolen item; investigate a disappearance; a noble house's war contracts
(raze a village, defend a stronghold, assault a fortified camp, intercept a supply train); a paid
tournament; a "help the peasants" that turns out to be a rebellion. THE TWIST in most: the caravan carries
contraband and the guards turn on you; the camp is bigger than said; the delivery's recipient is dead;
a third party (another mercenary company) shows up wanting the same pay; the client refuses to pay and you
choose to take it by force; the kidnapped daughter left willingly.
EVENTS: while travelling or camping, a screen with an illustration (painted) and 2 to 4 choices. Kinds:
company events (two men fight over a woman, a man deserts with your crowns, food spoils, a man finds a
puppy, the priest wants to bless the banner), background events (the Thief steals, the Cultist recruits
a brother into the cult, the Hedge Knight demands respect from the Beggar, the Monk heals), location
events (a wounded man on the road who may be bait; a fork with tracks; a merchant offering a deal; a
witch's hut), relationship events (two men become friends or rivals; a mentor-student pair), injury and
trait events (a man loses an eye, gains Fearsome, becomes a Drunkard). Outcomes: morale, hitpoints,
items, crowns, traits, a man leaves, a new man joins, relations change. Cooldowns keep them rare; the
company's composition drives which fire.
AMBITIONS (see 07). ORIGINS (starting stories: the Peasant Militia with cheap men and a 16-man roster,
the Lone Wolf, the Beast Slayers, the Cultists, the Trading Caravan, the Northern Raiders, the Deserters,
the Manhunters, the Gladiators, the Anatomists): each changes rules, not just the start.
(OURS: an ask is a person in a place; one twist each; an event on the map is a mouth with a portrait, two
or three choices, never in the first minute; rule 33 s2, QUESTS [bb events], WORDS [bb event writing].)
