# 04 ARMOUR, HELMETS, SHIELDS (recall)
BODY ARMOUR: durability (armour points) and a fatigue penalty (weight). Tiers: rags/linen 15-25 (-1 to
-2 fatigue), padded tunic/leather 45-65 (-4 to -6), gambeson 75, mail shirt 95 (-10), leather lamellar 105,
scale/reinforced mail 130-160 (-16 to -20), heavy lamellar/coat of plates 200-230 (-26 to -32), named
armours higher. Armour absorbs a hit until it is broken through; repaired with tools between fights.
HELMETS: same shape, 20 (leather cap) to 300 (great helm); full helms lower vision (some -ranged skill or
-initiative). A head hit against no helmet is where men die.
SHIELDS: a shield adds melee and ranged defence (buckler +5/+5 low fatigue; heater/kite +15/+25 recall;
tower shields more, heavy), has DURABILITY that axes destroy, and enables SHIELDWALL (+defence this
turn, costs AP and fatigue) and KNOCK BACK. Two-handed weapons cannot use a shield: the trade is the
whole tactical spine (offence and fatigue vs survival).
ATTACHMENTS (later DLC): a cloak, a padding, a spiked impaler, a leather aketon under mail etc., each a
trade of armour vs fatigue vs a special.
FATIGUE TAX: total fatigue penalty of armour + helmet + weapon + shield is subtracted from max fatigue;
a 230 armour man in a great helm with a two-hander has little left to swing with. Nimble (perk) rewards
light armour; Battle Forged rewards heavy.
