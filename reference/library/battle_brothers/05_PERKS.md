# 05 PERKS (recall; 7 tiers, one point per level, a tier unlocks every level; ~44 perks)
TIER 1: COLOSSUS (+25% hitpoints), NINE LIVES (survive one killing blow per fight at a few hitpoints),
BAGS AND BELTS (+2 bag slots, no fatigue penalty on bag items), PATHFINDER (all terrain costs 2 AP, less
fatigue), ADRENALINE (act first next turn, costs fatigue), RECOVER (drop fatigue by half, ends turn),
STUDENT (+20% xp until level 11, then a free perk), CRIPPLING STRIKES (more injuries), FAST ADAPTATION
(+hit chance per miss, resets on hit), GIFTED (+3 to every stat... recall: a free level-up's worth).
TIER 2: EXECUTIONER (+20% damage to injured), DODGE (+15% of initiative as defence), FORTIFIED MIND (+25%
resolve), RESILIENT (injuries and debuffs shorter), STEEL BRAW (hits to the head do not injure/kill on
first pass... recall: SHIELD EXPERT +25% shield defence, shields take less damage), BRAWNY (armour fatigue
penalty -30%), RELENTLESS (fatigue does not lower initiative), HOLD OUT (+?), STEADFAST (morale check
bonus).
TIER 3: BACKSTABBER (surrounded bonus +10 per attacker instead of +5), ANTICIPATION (+ranged defence per
distance), SHIELD EXPERT, ROTATION (swap places with an ally, no ZOC penalty), QUICK HANDS (swap weapons
for 0 AP once a turn), TAUNT (enemies attack you), BATTLE FLOW? (recall uncertain), FOOTWORK (leave ZOC
free once), UNDERDOG (surrounded penalty against you halved), LONE WOLF (+15% stats when alone).
TIER 4: WEAPON MASTERIES (Sword, Axe, Mace, Hammer, Cleaver, Flail, Spear, Dagger, Polearm, Bow,
Crossbow, Throwing: each cuts fatigue cost and adds a weapon-specific bonus like +chance to hit or
bleed), REACH ADVANTAGE (two-handers gain melee defence per hit this turn), OVERWHELM (each attack lowers
the target's defence), RALLY THE TROOPS (raise nearby allies' morale, leader perk), INDOMITABLE (halve
damage for a turn).
TIER 5: BERSERK (a kill refunds 4 AP), HEAD HUNTER (misses raise head hit chance), LOOKOUT? (recall), BRAWNY.
TIER 6: FEARSOME (hits cause morale checks), DUELIST (one-hander with no shield ignores more armour),
KILLING FRENZY (+25% damage for two turns after a kill), NIMBLE (light armour: take a fraction of
hitpoint damage), BATTLE FORGED (heavy armour: armour takes less damage per point worn), DEVASTATING
STRIKES? (recall).
TIER 7: THE LAST STAND? (recall).
THE SHAPE: a man is built as a ROLE (tank, two-hander, polearm second row, archer, dagger looter) by the
tier-7 pick (Nimble vs Battle Forged) and the mastery; perks are one line each and read at a glance; the
tree is the same for every man, so builds are the player's story about him.
