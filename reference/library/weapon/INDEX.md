- TEACHES: a pistol is TWO readable masses at a glance, the slide (a long flat bar) and the grip (a block leaning back under it), joined by the trigger guard; at a dozen pixels the L of slide over grip IS the pistol, and it is the slide's value against what is behind it that makes the L read.
