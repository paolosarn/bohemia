# TRACK REFERENCES (what the ground says after somebody has gone)

FOR: COOK's [footprints] row (Paolo, first votes: "make pixel art of
footsteps that lead you to the conclusion"). A single print is a texture. A
LINE of prints is a sentence, and real trackers and forensic examiners read
that sentence off measurable things: how far apart the prints are, how far
apart the two lines of feet are, which way the foot points, and which part
of the sole carries the weight. Those four are the whole drawing brief -
everything here is structure, never style, and nothing here enters the
design vocabulary (8/28).

### TRK-01  The trackway's four measurements (forensic podiatry / gait)
- WHERE: https://www.policechiefmagazine.org/gait-footprints-and-footwear-how-forensic-podiatry-can-identify-criminals/ + https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/nursing-and-health-professions/stride-width
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a trackway is read from STEP LENGTH (heel to heel of opposite feet), WALKING BASE or step width (the side-to-side gap between the two lines of feet, 8-12 cm in an adult), and the FOOT PROGRESSION ANGLE (the angle between the print's own midline and the direction of travel, positive when the toe points outside the line) - so a print that does not point where the trail is going is drawn wrong no matter how good the sole is.

### TRK-02  Walking against running, in the impression itself
- WHERE: https://anatomypubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ar.b.20025 (Naples, Making tracks: the forensic analysis of footprints and footwear impressions)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: as a person accelerates the gap between prints grows and the TOE end drives deeper while the heel lightens or never registers, so a running print is SHORTER THAN A WALKING ONE even though the person is moving faster - a run is drawn by taking the heel away and pressing the ball in, never by stretching the same sole.

### TRK-03  Trail width and the shape of the pattern
- WHERE: https://crowspath.org/natural-history/wildlife/gaits/ (tracking: gaits and trail patterns)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: trail width (straddle) is the whole group of tracks measured across the direction of travel, and it NARROWS as speed rises until a runner's prints land on one line - so the two lines of feet converging is the readable tell that the pace changed, before any single print is examined.

### TRK-04  The marks that are not prints
- WHERE: https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/09710973251322020 (Forensic importance of footwear impression evidence: a review)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the ground also keeps CONTINUOUS marks - a drag leaves two parallel furrows with the heels at their head, a pivot leaves a crescent where the sole rotated in place, and a hard push-off throws material BACKWARD behind the print - and those continuous marks carry the event, where the prints only carry the person.
