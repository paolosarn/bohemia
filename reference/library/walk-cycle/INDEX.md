# WALK CYCLE REFERENCES (a body moving on the beat)

### WALK-01  Walk Cycle — Pedro Medeiros (saint11)
- WHERE: https://lospec.com/pixel-art-tutorials/walk-cycle-by-pedro-medeiros
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: a sprite walk is CONTACT-DOWN-PASS-UP; the head bobs one pixel on pass, and losing the bob kills the weight before anything else does.

### WALK-02  Walk Cycles — Sandy Gordon
- WHERE: https://lospec.com/pixel-art-tutorials/walk-cycles-by-sandy-gordon
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: at small sizes arms read by their SWING SILHOUETTE, not their pixels; opposite arm to leg or the walk reads as a march.

### WALK-03  The Animator's Survival Kit (Richard Williams)
- WHERE: book (ISBN 978-0571238347)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the walk is a controlled fall; the body is always leaving balance and catching it, so a cycle where the torso never leans is a treadmill, not a walk.

### WALK-04  The low crawl (US Army FM 21-75, Combat Skills of the Soldier, ch. 3; the ROTC tactics text that copies it)
- WHERE: US Army FM 21-75 (1984) chapter 3, Movement; University of Akron Army ROTC MS1 lesson 9 (uakron.edu/armyrotc/MS1/9.pdf); army.mil 'Introduction to the low crawl' (2015). Read from the search engine's excerpts (this box's network refused the pages), ANIMATION 11/7
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: the slowest way a man moves: the body pressed flat, the arms pushed out ahead, the body PULLED up to the hands; the hand that pulls stays where it was put while the body comes to it, so a crawl is a planted hand and a sliding body, never a hand that skates.

### WALK-05  Care under fire (TCCC, Tactical Combat Casualty Care, module 3; the UN field medical assistants' skill cards)
- WHERE: TCCC 'Care Under Fire' instructor guide (mhs.osd.mil, 2011, web archive) and module 3 (tccc.org.ua); UN Field Medical Assistants Course skill cards (unsco.unmissions.org, 2026). Search excerpts, ANIMATION 11/7
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a wounded man who can is told to move himself to cover; one who cannot move lies flat and still. The wounded crawl AWAY from the fire toward cover and their own side, and the crawl ends in stillness, not in a pose.

### WALK-06  The belly ('commando' or 'army') crawl, as an infant first learns it
- WHERE: parentingscience.com 'When do babies crawl' (an illustrated guide); flintrehab.com 'Commando crawling and cerebral palsy'; pathways.org 'Types of baby crawls'. Search excerpts, ANIMATION 11/7
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: on the stomach the arms do the work and the legs drag behind; the body swings side to side (fishtails) as each arm pulls. The first way a body crawls is the last way a broken one does: the drag is that crawl with dead legs and one good arm.
