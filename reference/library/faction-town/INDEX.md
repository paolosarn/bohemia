# FACTION TOWN REFERENCES (fortress / town / camp, the three tiers at our tile size)

FOR: the faction-towns ruling (9/4: every faction has a seat; FORTRESS /
TOWN / CAMP derived from its power) and COOK's [fortress buildings] work.
Three annotated sheets, one per tier, so the seats are cooked to a card
and not a guess. Our scale: a ground cell's art is 44 px, one house per
combat tile, a district tile is 128 x 128 cells. Nothing here enters the
design vocabulary (8/28): rulers only.

## SHEET ONE — THE FORTRESS (a walled compound that reads as power)

### FTC-01  The compound (the enclosure as a building type)
- WHERE: https://www.itzaarchaeology.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Keeley-et-al-2007-Baffles-and-Bastions-The-Universal-Features-of-Fortifications.pdf
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: every real fortification on earth converges on the same three parts - a continuous perimeter, a BAFFLED gate (you enter by turning, never straight in), and a tower that sees along the wall; a fortress without a bent entrance reads as a fence.

### FTC-02  Guomari's three tiers (the fortress interior)
- WHERE: https://www.mdpi.com/2075-5309/15/13/2218
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: inside the wall the fortress KEEPS defending - narrow L- and T-shaped alleys, no straight sightline through, roofs that connect; a fortress interior is a maze with one plaza, never a suburb with a wall around it.

### FTC-03  The Vegas fortress (what OUR fortresses are made of)
- WHERE: laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_FACTION_TOWNS_9_4_26.md + the walked casino/resort blocks
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: this valley's fortresses are not built, they are CLAIMED - a casino podium, a substation yard, a storage compound already have the wall, the one gate and the tower; a faction fortress cook starts from the building the faction seized, and the faction's colour is on the gate, not the whole wall (COLOUR IS TERRITORY).

## SHEET TWO — THE TOWN (a main street that reads as commerce)

### FTC-04  Main Street anatomy (the American small town)
- WHERE: https://www.udg.org.uk/publications/udlibrary/learning-las-vegas
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a town is ONE street with two faces of continuous storefronts, party wall to party wall, doors every few paces - and it ends visibly (a church, a bank, a grain elevator, for us a sign pole or a tower); a town whose shops are scattered is a camp with buildings.

### FTC-05  The market street at our tile size
- WHERE: reference/library/city-builder/INDEX.md entries CB-02, CB-03, CB-07
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: every stall and shop lot FACES the one street; the whole loop is one errand long; the sign pole is the cheapest tallest thing the faction can claim - the town tier is the block-and-lot rules already in the library, compressed onto a single spine.

### FTC-06  The campaign reference's settlements (his named reference, measured)
- WHERE: Battle Brothers town screens (the campaign-layer reference Paolo named 8/28; measurement only)
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: a settlement's tier reads at map range from THREE marks - perimeter (none / partial / full), the tallest silhouette, and how many attached situations hang off it; the same three marks are how our FORTRESS / TOWN / CAMP must tell apart at CITY zoom.

## SHEET THREE — THE CAMP (a tent field that reads as people holding on)

### FTC-07  The planned camp grid (UNHCR standards)
- WHERE: https://sswm.info/sites/default/files/reference_attachments/UNHCR%202015c%20Camp%20Planning%20Standards%20(Planned%20Settlements).pdf
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a planned camp is family shelters in rows with fixed gaps, water and latrines at measured walking distances, one market edge - the grid is legible from the air the day it goes up, and that legibility IS the "new camp" read.

### FTC-08  Za'atari's drift (what a camp becomes)
- WHERE: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40410-020-00120-z
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: within months families DRAG their shelters into U-shaped compounds around shared courtyards and the grid dissolves into lanes - a camp that has existed a while shows both layers at once: the official rows at the edge, the grown courtyards at the heart. Age of camp = how far the grid has melted.

### FTC-09  The desert camp's materials (ours)
- WHERE: the walked world's own trailer, swapmeet and boneyard districts (engine kits)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: this valley's camp tier is not canvas - it is trailers, tarps over frames, dead cars as walls, a fire barrel plaza; the approved trailer/swapmeet art already carries the material palette, so a camp cook is mostly ARRANGEMENT (rows melting into courtyards) on art that exists.
