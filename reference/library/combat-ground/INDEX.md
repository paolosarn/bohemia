# COMBAT GROUND REFERENCES (the fight's floor, a tile is a house — 9/4)

### CGRD-01  Into the Breach interface (Game UI Database)
- WHERE: https://www.gameuidatabase.com/gameData.php?id=483
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: every tile state must read from silhouette and value alone at a glance; if a player has to hover to know whether ground is dangerous, the floor has failed.

### CGRD-02  "Sacrifice cool ideas for the sake of clarity every time" (the ItB dev interview)
- WHERE: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/-i-into-the-breach-i-dev-on-ui-design-sacrifice-cool-ideas-for-the-sake-of-clarity-every-time-
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: on a tactical floor, clarity outranks beauty on every collision; a gorgeous tile that hides its walkability is a worse tile than a plain one that shows it.

### CGRD-03  A Vegas block from the air (satellite, any residential mile)
- WHERE: https://www.google.com/maps/@36.1699,-115.1398,500m (satellite)
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: at house-per-tile scale the ground alphabet is HOUSE / YARD / STREET / LOT and almost nothing else; a backyard is as big as its house (the 9/4 1x2 ruling is what the air actually shows).
