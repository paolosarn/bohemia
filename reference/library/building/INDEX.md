# BUILDING REFERENCES (one structure, its bones)

### BLDG-01  UNLV "Dreaming the Skyline" architecture archive
- WHERE: https://www.library.unlv.edu/speccol/collecting_strengths/architecture
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: real Vegas buildings from drawings to construction; a building's massing comes from its PROGRAM (what happens inside), so draw the use first and the walls around it.

### BLDG-02  Antoine Predock's Las Vegas Library (already canon: the library district is built on it)
- WHERE: https://www.e-architect.com/lasvegas/architecture-behind-the-las-vegas-wonder
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: desert civic architecture is MASS + SHADOW, sandstone and concrete; colour comes from the desert, and glazing is small and deep because of the sun.

### BLDG-03  SLYNYRD Pixelblog catalogue (buildings series)
- WHERE: https://www.slynyrd.com/pixelblog-catalogue
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: a pixel building is drawn in three planes (front face, side face, roof) with ONE light direction; windows sit IN the wall plane on a shared grid line, never floating between floors.

### BLDG-04  The Strip's three races (pool, sign, porte-cochere — Stefan Al)
- WHERE: https://www.stefanal.com/the-strip
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: a casino is a SMALL CITY with a giant forecourt; the front is theatre and the back of house (laundry, kitchens, docks) is where the building actually works — draw both or the building is a facade.

### BLDG-05  Structural sanity list (the 9/4 law's own words)
- WHERE: laws/BOHEMIA_LAW_COMPARE_EVERY_PIECE_OF_ART_TO_THE_WORLD_9_4_26.md
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: windows sit in a wall plane; a wall meets a roof; a door is on the ground; a street meets a curb — the four checks every building cook runs before calling itself done.
