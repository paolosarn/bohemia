# OVERWORLD MAP REFERENCES (the valley crossed the campaign way)

FOR: rule 33 (Paolo 9/24, "an executive decision"): THE OVERWORLD IS BATTLE BROTHERS. The
valley is crossed on a map with a party marker, places as destinations and roads faster than
dirt. This folder is the map's own art: what a marker has to do at map range, how a place is
built up, and what may and may not be drawn on top of terrain. Rule 33(g) is the line we do
NOT take from the reference: their map is still pictures and ours moves. Nothing here enters
the design vocabulary (8/28).

### BBM-01  The overworld party marker (the campaign reference Paolo named)
- WHERE: Battle Brothers world map, the company marker (the campaign-layer reference, 8/28; measurement only) + https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_Brothers
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: the thing that is YOU on a campaign map is a SMALL FIGURE, tiny against the terrain, and it is found by silhouette plus ONE loud mark rather than by size or colour - which is why a company carries a banner; and it TRANSLATES rather than animating, so movement alone never tells you anything about it.

### BBM-02  A place is a kit, not an icon
- WHERE: http://battlebrothersgame.com/dev-blog-19-on-worldmap-locations/ (the developers' own worldmap-locations post and its asset sheet)
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: a location on that map is ASSEMBLED out of small building pieces rather than drawn as one badge, so the marker itself says what the place HAS; the construction rule to take is base + body + one tall piece, which makes the next place an arrangement instead of a new drawing.

### BBM-03  Roads are the only lines on the map
- WHERE: Battle Brothers world map (measurement only) + reference/library/district/INDEX.md DIST-03
- KIND: pixel
- TEACHES: on a travel map the only long thin bright elements are the ROADS, because a route is the one thing a player traces with their eye - so anything else long and thin reads as a route and lies; a marker's tall pieces must be vertical and short, never a horizontal streak.

### BBM-04  What a still map costs (his ruling, the part we do not take)
- WHERE: records/BOHEMIA_PAOLO_BB_IS_PICTURES_WE_PUT_LIFE_IN_IT_9_24_26.md (Paolo 9/24) + laws/BOHEMIA_LAW_THE_OVERWORLD_IS_BATTLE_BROTHERS_9_24_26.md
- KIND: real
- TEACHES: his words are "Battle Brothers is just a bunch of pictures, we can do more and put more life into it with this analog horror pixel direction" - so the rule for our map is ONE MOVING PART ON A STILL BODY per marker, on the 120 beat, and the feet never move; a marker that wobbles all over is not alive, it is loose.
