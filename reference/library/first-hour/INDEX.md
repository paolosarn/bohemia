# FIRST-HOUR REFERENCES (the first ten minutes of the best openings, as beats)

FOR: the cold hand and the onboarding work (day 14) — a stranger opens the
demo link on a phone and has never seen this game. Each entry is an opening
written as BEATS (what happens, in order) plus the ONE rule it teaches.
Nothing here enters the design vocabulary (8/28): these are rulers.

### FH-01  World 1-1 (the canonical teaching level)
- WHERE: https://www.universityxp.com/news/2026/5/7/why-super-mario-bros-is-still-an-essential-lesson-in-game-design
- KIND: pixel
- BEATS: empty safe ground -> one slow enemy walks at you -> a reward glitters overhead -> a pipe asks for a bigger jump -> the same lessons repeat harder -> a flag says you finished.
- TEACHES: the first screen is SAFE and the first threat is survivable by accident; every mechanic is met once alone, in a place where failing costs nothing, before it is ever combined.

### FH-02  The Great Plateau (the open tutorial)
- WHERE: https://www.zeldadungeon.net/breath-of-the-wild-walkthrough/great-plateau/
- KIND: real
- BEATS: wake in a dark room -> one corridor, no choices -> step out and the CAMERA PANS the whole world -> one visible person to walk toward -> then four goals visible at once, any order.
- TEACHES: earn the reveal - one tight corridor, then the widest shot in the game; after the pan the player picks the direction, and every visible landmark is a promise the game keeps.

### FH-03  The train into City 17 (the walk before the fight)
- WHERE: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-Life_2
- KIND: real
- BEATS: arrive moving -> walk through a place where everyone else is mid-story -> get ordered around by someone small -> pick up a can before you ever pick up a weapon -> ten minutes pass before the first fight.
- TEACHES: the world is already running when you arrive - people mid-errand, rules already enforced; letting the player be POWERLESS first is what makes the first verb feel like something.

### FH-04  The farm, the mailbox and the bed (the first errand loop)
- WHERE: https://www.spriters-resource.com/pc_computer/stardewvalley/
- KIND: pixel
- BEATS: a short scripted arrival -> one hand-placed tool moment -> a mailbox with ONE letter -> one small errand that touches the town -> the bed ends the day and the day count is the save.
- TEACHES: give the cold hand ONE errand, not a menu of systems; the day-and-bed loop is learned by living one whole day, and the first day is short on purpose.

### FH-05  The first fight states everything (the clarity opening)
- WHERE: https://www.gameuidatabase.com/gameData.php?id=483
- KIND: pixel
- BEATS: a tiny board -> the enemy TELLS you its next move -> your first turn cannot lose the game -> the consequences of your move are shown before you commit.
- TEACHES: on a phone, the first fight is a conversation with full information; a cold hand should never lose to something the screen did not announce.

### FH-06  The porte-cochere (the real-world arrival machine)
- WHERE: https://www.stefanal.com/the-strip
- KIND: real
- BEATS: you are received under a vast bright canopy -> one unmissable path in -> cool air and sound say you have arrived somewhere -> the money room is visible before any sign names it.
- TEACHES: Vegas onboards without a single instruction - reception, one path, then the room that matters in plain sight; the demo's front door should work like the buildings this game is about.
