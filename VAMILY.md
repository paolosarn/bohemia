## HOW IT WORKS (read this, then find your lane below)
1. Paolo types **VAMILY** in a chat. That is the whole instruction.
2. The chat works out its ROLE: the lane its first word named, or the lane it
   was already working as. If it has no lane yet, it takes the top job in
   THE ORDER below and that lane becomes its role.
3. It finds its lane's section in this file and takes the **first job marked
   OPEN**. The job's full text is the named row in BOHEMIA_BACKLOG.md.
4. It edits the line here to `CLAIMED <date> <lane-session>` and commits that
   BEFORE building, so two chats never take the same job.
5. When the job is in the walked surface AND the demo build, it edits the line
   to `SHIPPED <date> <commit>` and commits. That is the history.
6. Then it takes the next OPEN one, or stops and says so.
This file is the coordinator's assignment ledger. Only the coordinator adds
jobs; lanes only change the status word. Every job here is 120 BPM friendly or
it is not done (Paolo 9/4), and it is not shipped until the demo is re-cut.
