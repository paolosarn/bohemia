EXPORTED BY PAOLO 8/14/26. His third full sweep in three days. 105 UP / 225 DOWN.

WHAT THIS SWEEP SETTLED, and it is about METHOD rather than material:
  friction    6 UP /   9 DOWN   40%   the best method in the game
  modal      97 UP / 173 DOWN   36%
  fm          2 UP /  13 DOWN   13%
  particle    0 UP /  20 DOWN    0%
  air         0 UP /  10 DOWN    0%
Thirty candidates across particle and air, not one kept. That is a stronger
signal than the metal finding it now sits beside.

FOUR MOMENTS THAT NEVER HAD A SOUND NOW DO: swing (swing_air), patch_up
(tape_pull), build_place (set_down), equip (cloth_on) -- and all four are
FRICTION, the method that did not exist three days ago.

EIGHT DIED WHOLE FOR THE SECOND TIME (their SFX-03 versions died too):
glass_crunch deck_ring mag_clack breath_out deed_stamp cash_count neon_hum
dog_cry. STOP PRODUCING (7/26) is explicit -- a second rejection ends the
feature. There is no third cook for those slots.

The vectors are not copied here: a candidate is a pure function of (event,
index) through BOH_SFX.cook, and records/BOHEMIA_SFX_FINGERPRINTS pins what
each one measures as.
