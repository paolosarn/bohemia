================================================================================
THE SWEEP SAID "WIRE IT OR DELETE IT" AND NEITHER WAS RIGHT UNTIL IT WAS READ
================================================================================
sayWhy is becauseOf plus the quest's own @LOG sentence, and its header says why
it exists: a standing the player cannot read is a standing they cannot play
around.

The city's standing card did exactly that, itself. ctWhyTheyThinkThat walked
becauseOf and looked the label up on its own. So there were TWO ANSWERS TO ONE
QUESTION and the engine's -- the published one, the one another surface would
reach for -- was called by nothing at all.

That is how the orphan happened. It is not that nobody needed it; it is that the
surface that needed it grew its own copy.

================================================================================
TWO DEFECTS IN THE DEAD FUNCTION, BOTH FOUND BY ASKING WHAT IT WOULD DO
================================================================================
1. `LABELS[kind] || kind` HANDS THE PLAYER A MACHINE ID, which is the exact thing
   the function's own header says must never happen. The fallback reads like a
   safety net and is a hole.

   MEASURED BEFORE CHANGING IT, AND THE HONEST ANSWER IS THAT IT HAS NEVER FIRED:
     deeds with a weight in his corpus     83
     of those with no authored sentence     0
   loadCorpus fills DEED_WEIGHT and LABELS in ONE loop off the same row, so for a
   quest deed the two always arrive together. This was not a live bug. It was a
   hole the corpus happened to be covering.

   IT IS REACHABLE FROM THE OTHER SIDE AND THE REPO ALREADY NAMES IT.
   bohemia_lend.js declares DEED_WEIGHT['loan:short'] as a placeholder waiting on
   Paolo's number: a kind that gets a WEIGHT without ever passing through the
   quest corpus, so it has no @LOG line and never will. Fill that value and the
   old body says "loan:short" out loud. The city card survives it because it
   wrote its own sentence for that kind; every other surface gets the id.

   NULL IS THE HONEST EMPTY. A deed nobody wrote a sentence for has no sentence,
   and the caller drops the row or supplies its own. The module keeps its own law
   now instead of trusting every caller to keep it.

2. IT DROPPED `kind`, so a caller with its own wording for a kind it authored had
   to walk becauseOf a SECOND time to get it back. That is precisely how the copy
   on the card grew. kind and turn ride along now, so one walk serves both.

THE WATCHED/HEARD SPLIT STAYED ON THE SURFACE ON PURPOSE. `heard` is handed back
untouched. Whether an eyewitness and a retelling get different sentences is a
WORDS decision and the sentences are Paolo's; the module returns what is true and
the card says it in his voice.
