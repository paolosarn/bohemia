BOHEMIA -- IT WAS NEVER JUST THE HAIR: A SLOT BUG WEARING A HAIR BUG'S CLOTHES
CHARACTER lane, 8/21/26.  Continuing from "Continue fixing east and west hair pls".

WHAT YESTERDAY'S FIX ACTUALLY WAS
----------------------------------
His painted hair/curtain-bob was still drawing under a worn hairstyle. Front-on the
generated hair covered it; in profile it did not, because a generator spans the PART
GRID and his paint reaches two cells past that grid at the crown. His bob's ramp holds
a near-white, so what leaked was a bright blob over the forehead. Fixed by not drawing
the PD hair layer while a hair garment is worn.

I SHIPPED THAT AS A HAIR FIX. IT WAS NOT A HAIR FIX.
-----------------------------------------------------
Hair is one of nine PD layers and six have generated counterparts. Nothing about the
mechanism was specific to hair -- any painted layer whose slot gets a generated garment
would do the same thing. So I audited all of them on the real worn path, all 8 facings
(tools/bohemia_pd_leak_audit.js):

    pants/leather-legwarmer    68 px leaking through BLUE JEANS
    shoes/balenciaga           61 px leaking through WHITE SNEAKERS
    jacket/japanese-fuzz       18 px leaking through WASTELAND DUSTER
    shirt/cowl-hoodie           0
    hair/curtain-bob            0    (fixed the day before)

THREE MORE, LIVE, IN THE BUILD HE IS PLAYING. Fixing hair and stopping would have left
them there indefinitely, because nobody would have gone looking: the visible symptom was
on the head and the head was fixed.

THE FIX IS THE GENERAL ONE
---------------------------
The hair-only line is gone. If a generated garment of a slot is worn, the painted layer
of that same slot is not drawn. One rule, seven slots, no special case:
    hair->hair  hat->head  shirt->base  jacket->outer  pants->legs  shoes->feet
    glasses->face
body and facial are ABSENT FROM THAT MAP ON PURPOSE. A garment never replaces his face
or his body, and the gate fails if either is ever added.

TWO THINGS THIS COULD HAVE BROKEN, BOTH MEASURED RATHER THAN ASSUMED
---------------------------------------------------------------------
HIS DEFAULT LOOK. window.G_WORN is null until something dresses him, so out of the box
he wears his painted outfit exactly as painted. Verified by rendering 32 frames (8
facings x 2 clips x 2 phases) off origin/main and off this build and comparing hashes:
BYTE-IDENTICAL. The suppression fires only when a generated garment of that slot is
deliberately worn, and then the generated garment IS the garment.

HIS FACE. Dress him head to toe and his painted face must still be on the frame. It is,
and the gate asserts it two independent ways -- the source map must not contain 'facial',
and the pixels must still be there after dressing him. Mutation: add facial to the map
and BOTH fire, the pixel one reporting 0 painted face pixels on screen.

RIG LAW IS UNTOUCHED. Nothing of his is edited. One layer is not DRAWN while its slot is
filled, and it returns the moment the slot is empty.

A MISTAKE IN MY OWN TOOL, WORTH RECORDING
------------------------------------------
The first run of the audit reported "no generated counterpart" for all six layers and
looked like a clean bill of health. IT WAS A TYPO IN A LOOKUP TABLE: I assumed the PD
slots were named base/legs/feet/outer like the wardrobe's layers. They are shirt/pants/
shoes/jacket. Every pairing missed, and a missed pairing printed as reassurance.
A LOOKUP THAT MISSES SILENTLY IS THE SAME BUG AS A GATE THAT PASSES VACUOUSLY, and the
only reason I caught it is that a clean result was the answer I wanted -- which is
exactly when to look twice. The slot names are read from PD.meta.order now.
