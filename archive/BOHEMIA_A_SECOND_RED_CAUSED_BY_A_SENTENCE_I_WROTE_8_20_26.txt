DRESS has a SECOND check -- "every canon garment in the alpha is in the bank BY NAME"

THE FIX IS THE RULER, AND IT IS THE SAME LAW AS YESTERDAY
---------------------------------------------------------
A CHECKER THAT CANNOT TELL A MENTION FROM A USE IS THE BROKEN ONE (8/1). Block

exactly 236 and touches nothing else in the file.
This gate's own next comment already said A COUNT IS A SMOKE ALARM, NOT A DIAGNOSIS
(8/4). The alarm was working; nobody had done the diagnosis.

I COULD HAVE REWORDED MY COMMENT. It would have gone green in ten seconds and left
the next person who writes about the wardrobe holding the same landmine. Fixing the

--------------------------------------------------------
This is the THIRD mention-counted-as-a-use defect found in two days, in three

means any gate that greps the whole file will eventually be fooled by its own
documentation. IF YOUR GATE GREPS A SOURCE FILE, STRIP THE PROSE FIRST.
