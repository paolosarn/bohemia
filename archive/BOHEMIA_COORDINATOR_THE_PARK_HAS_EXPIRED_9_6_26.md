# THE REASON QUESTS WAS PARKED HAS EXPIRED (coordinator, this round, my own call + one question for him)

## WHY IT WAS PARKED, IN HIS OWN REASON
Paolo parked QUESTS on 9/4 and the reason was good: "I need to be able to have
aesthetic supervision on just like experiencing it." A quest he cannot walk is a
quest he cannot judge, so no quests chat until he says. Nine jobs have sat in that
section since, untouched, on purpose.

## WHAT HAS CHANGED SINCE, MEASURED OFF THE BOARD
Every condition that made a quest unjudgeable has now shipped:
- the walked street works, with a title, auto-walk and a first minute that teaches
- there are hostile bodies who come at you, and a fight starts where you stand
- you can walk through a door and fight in the room, with its real walls as cover
- factions hold ground, borders follow something visible, every block has a name
- people have former jobs, standing, homes, schedules, and outfits at spawn
- money is real and it bites: buildings cost, nights cost, the valley runs out
- the generation fold exists and is turnable
- the city talks on a phone feed; the room has its own song; 65 sounds accounted for
He can walk into a place, meet somebody who wants something, and be threatened
while doing it. That is a quest, minus the quest.

## THE RESEARCH: WHAT A QUEST SHOULD BE IN A GAME SHAPED LIKE OURS
The distinction that matters for us: you design SYSTEMS, not scripts. Emergence
cannot be authored, only the conditions for it. The strongest finding for a game
with our parts is that the interactions BETWEEN systems do not have to be
complicated to produce interesting behaviour -- simple rules across several real
systems is where the complexity comes from. We have more live systems than most
games ever build (standing, gossip, debt, territory, hunger, power, the fold), and
a hand-scripted quest chain would use almost none of them.
Sources: [Unity: systems that create ecosystems, emergent game design](https://unity.com/blog/games/systems-that-create-ecosystems-emergent-game-design),
[The Artifice: systemic games, a design philosophy](https://the-artifice.com/systemic-games-philosophy/),
[Game Studies: theorizing emergence for videogame studies](https://gamestudies.org/1902/articles/soleradillon),
[Emergent gameplay (overview)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergent_gameplay).

## SO THE QUESTION IS NOT "SHALL WE BUILD QUESTS", IT IS "WHICH KIND"
- **A. THE SPINE ONLY.** The 60-boss ladder is already the story spine and 53 of
  the 60 are wired. Build the main line: each boss is a place, a person and a verb
  you take from them. Quests as such stay parked. Least new machinery, and the
  thing that carries the three generations.
- **B. THE SYSTEMS ASK.** No quest is written; the systems generate the asking. A
  person you know, in a place with an owner, wants something they cannot get,
  because of a system that is already running (a debt, a bill, a border, an empty
  shelf). The verbs are the ones we have. This is what the research points at and
  what our parts are unusually ready for.
- **C. STAY PARKED.** Keep building the world until he says otherwise.
MY PICK IS B, WITH THE SPINE FROM A KEPT AS THE MAIN LINE. But which shape the
game's main content takes is not a technical question and not mine: it is the
biggest creative fork left, and it is his.
