# BOHEMIA — 45 CURRENCY ICON OPTIONS (RESOURCES / ENERGY / CLOUT), 7/28/26

Paolo's order: *"do online big brain research and for each i need 15 icon
options the best to represent wtf they are."*

Research, then options. **No art was cooked.** These are candidates for him to
pick from; the winner goes to the art lane as a tile form.

---

## WHAT IS ALREADY LOCKED (so no option contradicts it)

`laws/BOHEMIA_ADDENDUM_THREE_CURRENCIES_CENTURY_7_26_26.md`:
- **RESOURCES** — physical goods. **apple = food** and **duct tape = materials**
  are already his; a **third sub-icon is [PENDING]**.
- **ELECTRICITY** — batteries / tech.
- **CLOUT** — producible by buildings, spendable many ways.
- Anti-reference, his words: *"spreadsheet simulators and I'm not a fan."*

So RESOURCES may need TWO answers: a single symbol that means the whole
currency, and/or the pending third sub-icon. Options below are marked
**[3rd]** where they'd serve as that third sub-icon instead of the umbrella.

## THE RULES EVERY OPTION IS JUDGED BY (from the research)

1. **Silhouette first.** It must read at 32px from shape alone — status icons
   should be meaningful from silhouette, and detail that disappears at small
   size is worse than no detail.
2. **Distinct from each other.** The three must never be confusable at a glance.
   This is the hardest constraint and it kills several otherwise-good options.
3. **Few parts.** More shapes and thinner strokes = busier and less readable.
4. **Fits our constitution.** Act 1, dead world, no purple (Amalgamation
   reservation), no glow (act 1 windows are dead dark glass), one light
   direction, no black keyline.
5. **Vegas-true.** Every icon should be a thing that actually exists in this
   valley, not a generic fantasy token.

# RESOURCES — 15 OPTIONS

The job: "physical stuff that keeps people alive and lets you build."

| # | icon | why it reads | our-world grounding | risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | **Duct tape roll** (side-on ring) | already his; unmistakable ring-with-core silhouette | the universal fix-it of every American garage; the post-collapse repair material | it currently means *materials*, so as the umbrella it demotes food |
| R2 | **Apple** | already his; the most legible food shape there is | — | a fresh apple in a dead desert is the one thing here that isn't Vegas-true |
| R3 | **Crate / wooden box** | flat-topped trapezoid, reads instantly, the universal "goods" | swap-meet and warehouse stock | generic; says *storage* more than *supply* |
| R4 | **Jerrycan** | strong asymmetric handle+spout silhouette, very distinct | fuel and water haul, everywhere in the valley | reads as FUEL specifically, which we don't have as a currency |
| R5 | **Tin can** (ribbed cylinder, peeled lid) | the single most iconic survival object in games | canned food is the only food that survives a decade | overused in the genre; near-cliché |
| R6 | **Water jug** (5-gal, handle) | huge distinct silhouette | **the most Vegas-true object on this list** — water is THE founding miracle of Bohemia (the reclaim plant) | says water only, not all resources |
| R7 | **Bundle / tied sack** | soft organic shape, contrasts with hard-edged energy icons | what a scavenger actually carries home | mushy silhouette at 32px |
| R8 | **Pallet stack** | horizontal banding, industrial | warehouse district, the swap meet | too similar to the crate; banding vanishes small |
| R9 | **Wrench + bolt** | the Metro series' actual answer for materials | garages, the boneyard | two objects = busy; and it says *tools*, not *supply* |
| R10 | **Scrap metal sheet** (torn corrugated) | jagged asymmetric edge is memorable | the valley is built of corrugated metal buildings | jaggedness is noisy at 32px |
| R11 | **Rebar bundle** [3rd] | tight parallel lines + tie wire | every tilt-up and every collapsed slab in the city | reads as lines, not an object, when small |
| R12 | **Cardboard box, taped** | the tape *is* the duct tape callback | delivery-economy corpse; swap meet | close to crate/pallet |
| R13 | **Shopping cart** [3rd] | unmistakable silhouette, nothing else looks like it | **THE CART IS ALREADY CANON** (the mobile base) — the icon would be the player's own vehicle | it means the CART, so using it for resources may confuse a real system |
| R14 | **Seed packet / sprout** [3rd] | soft vertical, distinct from everything | the farm district, the 100-year rebuild | a green sprout fights "everything is dead"; would need to be a *dry* seed |
| R15 | **Ration brick / MRE pouch** | flat rounded rectangle with a notch | what a faction actually hands out | flat rectangle is the weakest silhouette on the list |

**MY TOP 3, and why:** **R6 water jug** (the most honest to Bohemia's own
founding story, and no other game's resource icon is a water jug), **R1 duct
tape** (already his, already legible), **R5 tin can** (safest, most instantly
understood, at the cost of being the genre default).

# ENERGY — 15 OPTIONS

The job: "electricity, batteries, the thing 12% of this city has."

Research note: the lightning bolt became the standardized electrical symbol in
the early 20th century and carries mythological weight (the weapon of the sky
god) that makes it read *before* you think. That is why it is both the obvious
answer and the boring one.

| # | icon | why it reads | our-world grounding | risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | **Lightning bolt** | the universal electrical symbol; zero learning curve | — | maximum cliché; says *power* generically, not *stored* power |
| E2 | **AA battery** (side, with terminal nub) | the nub is a killer silhouette detail | scavenged cells are the real currency of a dead grid | small; can read as a can (collides with R5) |
| E3 | **Car battery** (box + two terminals) | squat box with two posts = unmistakable | **the boneyard is full of them**; the most realistic power store in a dead city | two posts vanish at 32px |
| E4 | **Power line pylon** | tall lattice silhouette, very distinctive | the transmission lines crossing the valley | too tall/thin for a square icon slot |
| E5 | **Wall plug / two prongs** | the American two-blade plug is instantly readable | every dead outlet in every looted house | says *connection*, not *stored energy* |
| E6 | **Light bulb** | universal, warm | LIGHT=TERRITORY — light is the whole social metaphor of this game | says *idea* as often as *power*; and a lit bulb fights the dead-glass law |
| E7 | **Solar panel** (tilted grid) | the tilted-plane read is distinctive | **the valley is ringed by real solar farms** and it's already a district | a grid of cells is exactly the fine detail that dies at 32px |
| E8 | **Fuse / breaker switch** | tiny lever, strong asymmetry | every substation and control house | too obscure — most players don't read a breaker instantly |
| E9 | **Transformer can** (cylinder + fins) | the fins are memorable | substation district, on every pole in Vegas | obscure to non-electricians |
| E10 | **Jumper cable clamp** | aggressive toothed jaw, unique | the boneyard again; scavenger culture | complex shape, busy small |
| E11 | **Generator** (box + pull handle) | boxy with a handle | what a lit block actually runs on | reads as a toolbox |
| E12 | **Power button symbol** (circle + stem) | the most universally understood tech glyph on earth | — | too *modern-UI*, not an object in the world; breaks the diegetic feel |
| E13 | **Battery bank / cell stack** | repeated verticals, reads as "stored" | **the battery district is already generated in our world** | repetition is noise at small size |
| E14 | **Extension cord coil** | circular coil with a plug end | how a lit block actually shares power, neighbor to neighbor | coil reads as rope |
| E15 | **Lightning bolt INSIDE a battery outline** | combines the universal glyph with the object, and says *stored* not *raw* | both halves are true to the world | two-part icon = the busiest option here, and the research says fewest parts wins |

**MY TOP 3:** **E3 car battery** (the most Vegas-true, and it's what a scavenger
would actually carry), **E15 bolt-in-battery** (says *stored power* precisely,
if it can survive the size test), **E1 lightning bolt** (the safe default that
needs zero teaching).

# CLOUT — 15 OPTIONS

The hardest of the three, because clout is abstract. Research finding worth
weighting: games represent status with **stars, gems, coins and guild marks**,
and the strongest social symbols are ones that mean *belonging*, not just score.
Our own constraint is sharper than any of those: **the visible CLOUT badge was
KILLED on 7/21** — no QUIET/RISKY/RECKLESS label on posts. The data drives
engagement invisibly. So this icon is for the WALLET readout, not a tag on
posts.

| # | icon | why it reads | our-world grounding | risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Eye** | "being seen" is exactly what clout is | the feed watches everything (TOTAL RECALL) | eyes read as *stealth/detection* in most games |
| C2 | **Star** | the universal status glyph | — | says *achievement*, and it's the most generic option here |
| C3 | **Signal / wifi arcs** | broadcast, reach, three-arc silhouette | the phone network is a patched-together relic | says *connectivity*, easily confused with energy |
| C4 | **Megaphone** | loud, unmistakable outline | the swap meet, faction criers | says *shouting*, i.e. propaganda more than fame |
| C5 | **Speech bubble** | the single clearest "people are talking" shape | the feed's comments ARE the mechanic (volume scales with followers) | plain; but plain is what survives 32px |
| C6 | **Crowd silhouette** (3 heads) | says *followers* literally | followers is the actual stat on the ME tab | three heads is fine detail; blobby small |
| C7 | **Raised fist** | strongest emotional read on the list | Babypunk; the factions | reads as one specific politics — a real risk in a game about many factions |
| C8 | **Camera / lens** | posts carry "a picture of the deed" | the feed's own mechanic | collides with a real CAMERA app if we build one |
| C9 | **Heart / like** | instantly understood as social approval | the feed | too soft, too modern-app, and it's borrowed from a platform that died with the economy |
| C10 | **Spray-tag / graffiti mark** | a hand-made mark = reputation you *earned* | **most Babypunk option on the list**; a tag is literally how street reputation is claimed | risks looking like a logo rather than an icon |
| C11 | **Neon sign glyph** (a lit outline) | Vegas is the neon city; fame as a lit sign | **the most Vegas-true clout idea there is** | act-1 law says no glow and dead dark glass — a *dead* neon tube could still work and might be better |
| C12 | **Crown** | status, plainly | the dynasty across 100 years | too fantasy/royal for a Vegas street game |
| C13 | **Microphone** | being heard | broadcast, the radio district | close to the megaphone; says *music* in a game that has a music tab |
| C14 | **Antenna / broadcast tower** | vertical with radiating arcs | the radio district is already generated | tall silhouette, same problem as E4 |
| C15 | **Playing card / chip** | Vegas's own symbol of stake and status | **the most on-the-nose Vegas object in the game** | gambling reads as *money*, and we deliberately have no money currency |

**MY TOP 3:** **C11 dead neon glyph** (Vegas fame, and the deadness is the
story — a sign that used to be lit), **C5 speech bubble** (the mechanic is
literally comments, and it survives 32px better than anything else), **C10 spray
tag** (the most Babypunk, the most "you earned this on the street").

# THE ONE THING THAT MATTERS MORE THAN ANY SINGLE PICK

The research's hardest rule is that the three must be **instantly distinct from
each other**. Several individually-great options collide:

- tin can (R5) vs AA battery (E2) — same cylinder
- crate (R3) vs generator (E11) — same box
- signal arcs (C3) vs lightning bolt (E1) — both read as "power/transmission"
- camera (C8) vs a real camera app

**The safest distinct trio by pure silhouette:** a **soft/organic** resource
(water jug or sack), a **hard rectangular** energy (car battery), and a
**round/radiating** clout (speech bubble or neon glyph). Three different base
shapes, readable at any size, in any order.
