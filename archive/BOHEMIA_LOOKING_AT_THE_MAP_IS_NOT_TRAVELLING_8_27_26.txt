LOOKING AT THE MAP IS NOT TRAVELLING
8/27/26 -- RUN lane. His report, and I made it loud the day before.


WHAT HE SAID
============

    "how come in the run like it wants to keep spawning me like outside of like
     my starter Neighbourhood it's so confusing like I'll just throw me some
     randomly on the map it's it's really weird bro. You gotta fix this shit."


WHAT I CHECKED FIRST, AND IT WAS ALL CLEAN
==========================================

He said SPAWNING, so I measured spawning, four ways, before touching anything:

    two fresh boots            cell [48,48], suburb, home 0 cells away
    four mornings each         cell [48,48], every single one
    walk, save, reload         0 tiles moved across the reload
    WATCH the opening          identical to SKIP, [48,48], 0 away

*** EVERY PROBE I HAD EVER WRITTEN SKIPS THE OPENING, so I ran that one too --
he watches it. Also clean. If I had stopped at "spawning is fine" I would have
told him he was wrong, and he was not.


WHAT IT ACTUALLY IS, REPRODUCED WITH REAL TOUCH
===============================================

    standing                 tile 6205,6271   cell [48,48]   marker [48,48]
    pinch out to the map      tile 6205,6271   cell [48,48]   marker [48,48]
    three taps on the pad     tile 6205,6271   cell [48,48]   marker [49,48]
    pinch back in             tile 6336,6208   cell [49,48]   194 TILES AWAY

*** HE LOOKED AT THE MAP AND CAME BACK 194 TILES FROM WHERE HE WAS STANDING. ***
"Randomly on the map" is exactly right, and it is not spawn code at all.


TWO HALVES, AND THE FIRST IS WHY IT FEELS RANDOM
================================================

1. THE PAD DOES NOT MEAN THE SAME THING AT BOTH ZOOMS. Zoomed out, the movement
   pad is still in the same corner under the same thumb -- it does not move, dim
   or hide -- and a press there moves the MARKER a whole overmap cell (96 metres)
   and silently spends TEN MINUTES of his day. Three taps he never meant to be
   travel. Nothing on screen distinguishes that from walking.

2. AND COMING BACK NEVER RETURNED HIM TO HIS BODY. swapMode's city->human branch
   opens by throwing away where he was standing:

       hx=city.x*FN+(FN>>1); hy=city.y*FN+(FN>>1);

   the centre of the marker cell, then a spiral of up to FN rings hunting a
   road-touching tile. That is CORRECT for arriving somewhere you chose to go,
   and completely wrong for a glance at the map.


*** AND I MADE IT LOUD YESTERDAY ***
====================================

Before 8/26 you had to deliberately press DROP IN to cross that seam, so this
only cost you something when you asked for it. THE ACTION BUTTON DOES ACTIONS
made zoom the primary way in and out -- his ruling, and correct -- which routed
EVERY GLANCE AT THE MAP through a landing built for arrival.

A CHANGE CAN BE RIGHT AND STILL HAND A LATENT BUG A MUCH BIGGER AUDIENCE. The
bug was always there; making the gesture cheap is what made it constant. That is
worth remembering the next time a control is made easier to reach.


THE RULE, AND THE GAME ALREADY SAID IT SOMEWHERE ELSE
=====================================================

From the phone's GO handler, written long before any of this:

    "GO on the phone posts a cell; the run moves the CITY MARKER to it...
     IT NEVER MOVES HIS BODY."

THE MARKER IS A CAMERA. That was already the stated rule in one place and the
zoom seam was the one place that disagreed with it.


THE FIX: TWO GESTURES, TWO MEANINGS
===================================

    A PINCH IS A LOOK.  He comes back to the exact tile he left, wherever the
                        marker wandered while he was up there. 194 -> 0.
    THE CHIP IS A GO.   Pressing DROP IN still lands him where the marker is,
                        because that is deliberate and it is the travel the game
                        currently has.

BOTH HALVES OR IT IS A DIFFERENT BUG. Without the first, a glance teleports him.
Without the second the marker is scenery and he can never cross the valley --
NO DISTRICT IS A PRISON (Paolo 8/1).

THE PATTERN IS NOT INVENTED HERE: the VISTA has done exactly this since 8/23
with returnTo {hx,hy,mode,cx,cy} -- go and look at something, come back to your
feet. The overlook had it. The map never did.

NOT CHANGED: walking the marker still travels, still costs ten minutes a cell,
still lands you by a road. Removing that is not this patch's business --
FT-JOURNEY is the locked ruling about what travel becomes and it is a build of
its own.


AND A SECOND BUG I HAD SHIPPED THE DAY BEFORE, FOUND ON THE WAY
===============================================================

The DROP IN chip I created yesterday was UNREACHABLE. I gave it a hardcoded
left:12/bottom:46 in .wrap -- underneath #blstack, the column that owns that
corner at z-index 39. A real click on it timed out.

*** THAT IS WORD FOR WORD THE BUG THAT COLUMN EXISTS TO PREVENT, COMMITTED BY
THE PERSON WHO FIXED IT, ONE DAY LATER. *** blStack's own comment says it:
"THE DAY LOOP ADDED THREE MORE CHIPS TO THIS CORNER AND NEVER JOINED THE COLUMN
-- each an absolute hardcoded offset, which is word for word the bug the note
above this function describes fixing."

AND MY OWN GATE SAID IT WAS FINE. The ACTION BUTTON claim read the chip's TEXT
and never asked whether a thumb could reach it. A CONTROL IS NOT OFFERED BECAUSE
IT EXISTS; IT IS OFFERED BECAUSE IT CAN BE PRESSED. Both fixed: the chip joins
the column like every other chip, and that claim now measures reachability the
way the panel sweep does.


THE GATE
========

gates/looking_at_the_map_is_not_travelling_gate.js -- LOOK NOT TRAVEL, 11 claims.

It taps the pad while he is zoomed out on purpose, because that is not a
contrived input -- it is where his hand already is.

MUTATION TESTED BOTH WAYS, each hitting only its own half:
    everything is a GO again    8 passed, 3 failed -- and the log reproduces his
                                report exactly: "moved him 194 tiles"
    everything is a LOOK        9 passed, 2 failed -- travel deleted, the marker
                                becomes scenery

ACTION BUTTON is 18 claims now (was 17) with the reachability claim added.
