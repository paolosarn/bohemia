## FIVE ROUNDS OF MECHANISM, AND WHERE THEY STOPPED

1. people live at their front doors, on ground that has houses

   meeting somebody became 9 of 32**

Rounds 4 and 5 made the world honest and legible and **did not move the meeting rate
at all**. That is the fact that made this round a measurement instead of a sixth
mechanism.

## THE QUESTION: CAN ANY PLACEMENT RULE GET FURTHER?

It is one division.

```
a 400-step walk sweeps                    3,600 cells   (400 steps x a 9-cell screen)
the nine neighbourhoods around him are    2,359,296 cells
so one walk sees                          0.153% OF THE GROUND
for ONE meeting per walk you need         ~655 people in them
there are                                 61
```

**PLACEMENT MOVES WHERE PEOPLE ARE INSIDE THAT FRACTION. IT CANNOT CHANGE THE
FRACTION.** The shortfall is a factor of about **eleven**, and no rule about
doorsteps, gathering, earshot or commuting closes it. Five rounds got from 2 of 32 to
9 of 32 by concentrating people onto the corridors he happens to walk; that is the
whole of what concentration can buy.

## WHY THIS IS A GATE AND NOT JUST A RECORD

`gates/enough_people_gate.js` does not check a mechanism. It checks the **reading**,
so the arithmetic cannot rot and so the day somebody changes the population **the
gate says what it bought**.

Proved live rather than hard-coded by running the whole thing at four times the
population:

```
                        shipped        at 4x
people around him          61            241
shortfall                10.7x          2.7x
the GDD gap              11.6x          2.9x
```

**The two numbers track each other**, which is precisely the claim, and A1 goes red
the moment the dial stops being the module's own GDD landmark — which is the gate
noticing somebody moved it rather than the gate breaking.
