BUILT THIS TURN: the CHAPEL icon. A cruciform church reads by its plan and its
silhouette, so it got both -- a long nave crossed by transepts under real PITCHED
roofs, a rounded apse cone, the narthex porch, and a BELL TOWER under a STEEPLE with
a cross finial. Two new shared primitives came out of it, _gable and _spire, both
built as proper quads with a truncated ridge and a truncated tip: a true apex needs
triangles, and a triangle in this renderer is a quad with two coincident vertices --
the exact degenerate sliver that made every round roof look like a tarp.
