WHAT SHIPPED
  Tapping RUN opens the city - the one isometric view, same world, same save as
  the CITY tab. The quest slice is dead as a tab.

ONE CITY, NEVER TWO, which was the whole design decision:
  - two instances would be two worlds with two saves that silently drift apart;
    he builds something in one tab and it is missing in the other
  - MOVING the iframe into the run panel would reparent it, and reparenting
    reloads an iframe in every browser that matters, so he would lose where he is
    standing on every tab change
  So the RUN tab ROUTES to the city panel. One frame, one world, one save.

WHAT WAS KILLED, EXACTLY
  The RUN tab showing the quest slice. Registered in gates/bohemia_graveyard.txt.

WHAT WAS NOT KILLED, AND WHY THAT IS NOT A LOOPHOLE
  The run slice stays WIRED in the shell - cast delivery, saves, combat handoff -
  and gates/run_gate.js still drives it. That section is the fleet's ONLY
  end-to-end proof that the character, the valley, the districts and the loop work
  together; those assertions are about systems that are very much alive and were
  merely OBSERVED through the run slice. Killing a tab is not a licence to delete
  thirty integration assertions at 5am. The harness now opens that panel directly
  and says so in its own comment: every click is a real click on a real rendered
  panel, and the one synthetic step is opening a surface the UI no longer exposes.
  If the shell ever stops wiring runFrame, that section gets deleted outright
  rather than propped up.

  converted "a person can play this" into "the function runs", which is exactly
  what a gate exists to prevent. The change was reverted and the question asked
