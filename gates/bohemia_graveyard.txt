THE POST-MORTEM, and it is not a close call:
  1. HE DATED IT AND HE WAS BEING GENEROUS. That topology IS sfxr, which shipped
     in 2007. It was also behind Risset's bell of 1969, which already gave every
     one of eleven partials its OWN duration.
