THE POST-MORTEM, and it is not a close call:
  1. HE DATED IT AND HE WAS BEING GENEROUS. That topology IS sfxr, which shipped

  3. HARMONIC PARTIALS. Bells, glass and metal are inharmonic; that irregular
     spectrum is what the ear reads as "metal". v1 detuned copies of one wave.
  4. NO BEATING. Real bells warble because their modes are slightly asymmetric.
     v1 sat dead still.
  5. NO TRANSIENT/BODY/TAIL SPLIT, NO SPACE, AND MONO. FFX ran recorded resonant
     objects through the PS2's hardware reverb, and FFX was the FIRST Final
     Fantasy to move its sound effects off mono. All 60 v1 candidates were pan 0.
     On the exact axis FFX treated as its upgrade, v1 shipped what FFX left.

NO REMAKE OF THESE RECIPES. GRAVEYARD IS FINAL: the sfxr core is not coming back
under a new name, and "one oscillator through one filter" is now a build failure
by name -- gates/sfx_gate.js requires inharmonic modal banks whose per-partial
decay shortens as the partial rises, and it was proved able to fail by flattening
a bank back to one shared decay and watching it go red.

WHAT SURVIVED, because it is measurement and structure rather than taste:
  - the 12 game moments and their one-line "why", judged as right
  - the judge surface in the MUSIC tab (thumbs, per-item note, SUN, .txt export)
  - the deliberate loudness ladder, re-measured for v2
  - both gates, extended rather than replaced
  - the three bugs v1's gates caught, all of which stayed fixed in v2
