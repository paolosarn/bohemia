POSTMORTEM -- THE EDGE NAMES AND THEIR POINTS (the waypoint arrows on the map)
Killed 11/22/26 by Paolo, VAMILY rule 473 (NO WAYPOINT ARROWS ON THE MAP). Removed by JOBS TWO, BUILD 11/22zi.

WHAT IT WAS
slices/bohemia_job_kinds.js markPlace(): when a held job's next place, the phone's lit town (markPhone) or a side quest's place
(BohemiaRoadEvents.places()) was off the glass, its name ('ESCORT TO PRIMM', 'JOB AT REMNANTS', 'FIND · <PLACE>') was drawn at the
glass's edge on the line from the middle of the glass toward it, with a small amber wedge pointing (wedgesDraw, drawn over the fog),
and both took a touch that walked there. Rule 164 item 3 (10/24, 'show me where the job is') asked for the place to be named; WORLD
[eyes: the place in the dark] moved the names out from under the phone; this file kept them since.

WHY IT DIED, HIS WORDS (11/22)
'with the side quest on the map... I don't want direction arrows if my map is zoomed in... if a bunch of side quests are populating and
I'm not completing them, then my whole map gets cluttered with waypoint arrows towards these little side quests... just have the part
highlighted when you're close, like when you see it normally on the map, but I don't want waypoints... I have like 14 white points on my
map for these offshoot side location missions.'

THE LESSON
A pointer per thing scales with the things. One job's arrow was help; fourteen side places' arrows were a frame of clutter round the
whole glass, every one of them stacked on the same edge. Battle Brothers marks a contract's place where it stands and never points at
the edge; you find it by looking, and the contract's own screen says where. Ours: the place is ringed where it stands when it is on the
glass, and the phone's job line says which way and walks there (rule 448).

WHAT STAYS
The ring and the name on the glass (markPlace's on-the-glass half); the phone's line and its which-way mark; a tap on the ring walks there.

A DEAD SHAPE DOES NOT COME BACK UNDER A NEW NAME: no name, point, chevron or count at the glass's edge for a place off the glass, for a
job or a side quest, in any colour. Held by gates/no_waypoints_gate.js (W1 the files, W2 the phone).
