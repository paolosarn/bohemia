  Since 10/19 (rule 121(e), __THE_MAP_IS_ALL_RIG__) the demo's map draws every person from the 3-D rig sheets. These 2-D
  frames only ever stood in for a 3-D sheet still on its way: his old 2-D body for 2.9 s after GO, measured frame by frame
  (gates/only_the_final_look_gate.js against BUILD 10/25o). Baking them saved boot time for a picture that should never be on
  his glass at all. The demo now neither fetches nor bakes them; the cut writes neither file and removes any copy left in
  slices/; the door waits for the 3-D people instead (DEVELOPING THE PICTURE).

  The fix for a slow final look is to wait for it honestly on the loading screen, never to show the old one meanwhile.
