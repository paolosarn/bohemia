POST-MORTEM: HOME BASE, THE CAMERAS ON PATROL AND THE 20-SECOND CUTS (killed 11/28/26 by Paolo, ask row 788)

WHAT IT WAS
- Rule 354(h) (11/3): his place shown by three fixed security cameras (CAM 1 the street light, CAM 2 the power pole, CAM 3 the
  billboard) that cut on their own every 20 seconds; he never picks one.
- HOME BASE 4 (11/26, rule 482, __THE_CAMERAS_ON_PATROL__): between cuts each camera pushed in slowly (1.5x to 2.3x) on what was going on
  (his man at work, the dead at the grinder, the people who live there, else a building), held, and pulled back before the cut.
- A thing's sheet (its station) pushed the camera in 1.6x to 3x on that thing.

WHY IT DIED (his words, 11/28)
'We can just have it to one camera angle from that angle from now on so we can focus more on the assets that we need to create from just
one intimate security camera perspective. I clicked, but I couldn't build anything... I could just like move things and then the plots
aren't even on the correct part of the land.'

WHAT WENT WRONG (measured on the demo, a fresh place, a phone upright)
- The plot under his finger moved: the picture changed camera every 20 seconds and zoomed between cuts, so a tap aimed at one place
  landed on another, or on nothing.
- Three cameras tripled every picture the pipeline had to make (each piece x state x lot x camera x shape) for a view he could not
  hold still long enough to use.
- Nothing on the ground said where a plot was, so a moving picture of an empty block gave him nothing to aim at.

WHAT REPLACED IT
ONE CAMERA (CAM 1, the street light), still. The plots are painted on their own ground (the manifest's ground_px, the plot through the
camera's lens). A sheet open shows the part of the same picture the sheet leaves, at the same size, round what he tapped; never a push in.
slices/BOHEMIA_SETTLEMENT_SCREEN.html, ONE CAMERA, AND THE PLOTS ON THE LAND; gate gates/one_camera_it_builds_gate.js; record
records/BOHEMIA_HOME_BASE_ONE_CAMERA_AND_IT_BUILDS_11_28_26.md.

THE LESSON
A camera that moves on its own is a camera the player cannot point at. Motion is for what lives in the picture (his people), never for
the picture itself while he is trying to touch it.
