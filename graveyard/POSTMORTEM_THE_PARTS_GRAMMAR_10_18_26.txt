WHY IT DIED
  It was solving the wrong problem well. Both rounds measured honestly against his tiles (contour, colour count, the
  top-eight share) and closed the numbers they set out to close: round two took the pistol from 9 colours to 752 with
  every colour provably his. But the number was never what he was judging. In the same sitting he saw DIRECTION's truck
  and robot come off a model and said it was the first thing that had impressed him visually in a long time. A flat
  shape filled with his grain is still a flat shape: no form, no light falling on anything, no thickness, a trigger
  guard that had to be a square U because the grammar could not draw a curve. The model has all of that for free.
  THE LESSON: when he has just fallen in love with a method in the next card over, a better version of the old method is
  not a candidate, it is a fourth version. Change road the same round.
