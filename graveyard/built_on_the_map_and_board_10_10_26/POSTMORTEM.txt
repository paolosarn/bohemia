WHY THEY FAILED (the lesson, for every lane)
  1. Rule 40b listed three places a build shows (the map, the fight board, the derived future) and I built all three
     because a law said so, without asking whether a player READS them. Battle Brothers' map shows a town, not its
     sheds; its fights are cut from terrain, not from your purchases. Copying a law's list is not the same as
     recreating the reference game's feel (rule 46c: how did Battle Brothers do it, first).
  2. Both pictures were tiny things on busy screens (a 16 px wall beside a base; a tank on one tile of a 9x11 board).
     A feature you need a caption to find is a micro feature. The test is "would a stranger notice it unprompted".
  3. Green gates proved both worked. Neither proved anybody wanted them (STOP PRODUCING: green gates are never an
     argument).
