POST-MORTEM: ui-the-phone-in-your-pocket-9-23   (UI lane 11)
KILLED BY PAOLO, 9/23, in the VOTE tab, thumb DOWN.

HIS WORDS, VERBATIM:
  "YOU ONLY SEE THE PHONE WHEN ITS UR ZOOMED OUT TO THE WHOLE CITY VIEW not when its the
   human close shit bro. And the phone should look like a cracked iphone bro"

WHAT IT WAS
  The drawn phone folded into the top right of the WALKED street, 46 x 62, cracked glass
  with the hour lit behind it, the gold rim when something was unread, a 44 x 44 reach pad.
  One tap opened the phone on the street exactly as it does in the city.

WHY IT WAS BUILT
  RUN's bounce-back of 9/23 ([phone door]) measured that #cityfeed only drew in CITY mode,
  so from waking up until he zoomed out there was no phone on the screen at all -- and
  rule 19a had just moved the morning and the night onto that phone. The words were there
  and the door was not. A phone on the street answered it.

WHY IT DIED, AND THE LESSON THAT IS MINE
  The defect RUN measured was real. THE ANSWER WAS NOT MINE TO PICK. "The morning is only
  reachable in the city view" is a problem with two ends: put the phone on the street, or
  put the morning somewhere a street has -- a mouth, which rule 19 already says every
  sentence needs. I took the end that put a new object on the screen he had just told us
  was too busy (rule 18g, LESS ON SCREEN, his own words "I'm overwhelmed"), on the one
  surface the playable cut is about, and I did it without a ruling that the phone belongs
  there. EVERYTHING IS A THUMB says decide and build; it does not say decide against a
  standing direction and let him find it.
  He was also right on the merits and it takes one sentence to see it: the phone is the
  CITY view's object (his 9/4 law puts the feed there), and a phone in the corner of a
  close third-person walk is a HUD, which is exactly what he keeps killing.

WHAT SURVIVES, AND IT IS NOT A CONSOLATION PRIZE
  Two things built on that row were never about the street and stay in:
   - THE PHONE'S CLOCK TELLS THE TIME. It read variables (HH, MM) that do not exist
     anywhere in that file, so it had shown an empty clock since it was written, in the
     city too. It reads the game's own clockStr() now.
   - THE PHONE NEVER OPENS BLACK (row [phone black], shipped 9/24): the open phone wears
     its own face while its 2.1 MB page arrives.
  The crack the pocket phone wore is now the crack on that face, cloned from the same
  shape, so the one piece of drawing that came out of this row is still on his screen.

WHAT REPLACES IT
  Row [phone city only]: the phone stays a CITY-VIEW object, and it becomes A CRACKED
  IPHONE -- a real slab, black glass, a spider crack, the chipping he liked on 9/21. Any
  redo comes back as a NEW id quoting this verdict, never this one again.

THE REVERT LANDED THE SAME ROUND HE WAS READ: no phone is drawn on the walked street, and
gates/the_bar_fits_his_glass_gate.js now HOLDS that (its street legs used to hold the
opposite and were turned round with the ruling rather than left to defend a dead thing).
