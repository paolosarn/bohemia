WHY THESE LINES EXIST
Day 1's refusal is a smell mode that paints a coloured line to the objective.
That is the most copied and most criticised mechanic in the whole animal-game
aisle, and it turns a sense into a quest marker. The replacement comes from the
real aisle: a tracking dog needs three to five consecutive footprints and about
three to five seconds to work out WHICH WAY somebody went, by comparing how
strong the smell is between adjacent prints. Scent decays, so the older print is
weaker.
  A TRACK HAS A DIRECTION BECAUSE IT HAS AN AGE.
So the nose is a READOUT, not an overlay. It obeys day 17's standing rule --
WORDS FOR THE WORLD, ONE NUMBER -- and the one number is HOW OLD.

---- WHAT THE READING NEVER SAYS --------------------------------------- (the refusals)
No objective marker. No coloured trail on the ground. No arrow.
No distance in metres. No "he is 40m north east".
No hunger number, no thirst number, no bar of any kind on the body.
No damage number anywhere. NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE DIAL.
No dialogue menu, ever. An animal that picks a reply off a list is a person
in fur, and it throws away the only mechanic on this page that is ours.

WHAT IS HIS AND IS NOT DECIDED HERE
Whether generation one is a coyote at all. How long it runs. Whose animal it
is, and whose people those are. What happens at the fold. Every name.
