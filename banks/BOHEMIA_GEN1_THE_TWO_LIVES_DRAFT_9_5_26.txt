WHY THIS SHAPE
The literature splits every coyote into a RESIDENT (holds defended ground with
one mate and a family, measured mean range about 9 km2) and a TRANSIENT (holds
nothing, measured mean range about 59 km2, living in the undefended strips
between other families' territory and waiting for a vacancy). Young leave at
nine to eleven months. A territory is never inherited, it is waited for, and
most die waiting. The pair bond ends only at death: 236 animals, six years,
genetic testing, no coyote ever left a living mate.
Against our valley (about 151 km2, about 9.2 km across, day 19): a family holds
about 6% of it and a homeless one walks about 39% of it, so the valley has room
for roughly sixteen territories.

---- THE FIVE STAGES, AS A SHAPE ONLY ---------------------------------- draft:true
Structure is ours. WHO, WHERE and HOW LONG are his and are left blank.

  AT HOME       you are on ground your parents hold. it is small and it is
                safe. you did not earn any of it.
  SENT AWAY     nine to eleven months. not a choice and not a punishment.
                the ground stops being yours on a day nobody marks.
  THE SEAM      you live in the strips between other families. the ground you
                can cross is six times bigger and none of it is yours.
                this is the long stage. this is the game.
  A VACANCY     somebody stops holding a piece of ground. you were near it,
                or you were not.
  HELD          ground, and one other. it ends when one of you dies.
