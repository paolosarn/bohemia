WHY THIS SHAPE
A hundred hours is between 200 and 1,000 separate decisions to come back. That is
the most repeated moment in the whole game and nothing greets it. The recap is
already written: 27 quests carry 164 journal rows, first person, past tense, one
per stage, and the only code that reads them is the tool they were typed into.
SO THIS BANK INVENTS ALMOST NOTHING. It is the shape of a return, and the words
it would say are mostly words we already have.

---- THE THREE THINGS A RETURN HAS TO ANSWER ------------------------------ draft:true
(in this order, because the last one is what actually brings somebody back)
  1  WHAT DID I DO           the journal line you already wrote
  2  WHAT CHANGED WHILE I WAS GONE   the world moved, and it moved without you
  3  WHAT WAS I ABOUT TO DO   the intent. the save protects the state and
                              nothing anywhere protects this.
