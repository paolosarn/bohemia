=== BOHEMIA SFX BANK (batch SFX-02 "THE RELIQUARY", 7/29/26) ===
Batch SFX-01 is in the graveyard (Paolo: "sounds like 2006 software"). These are
the remade v2 sounds: struck materials in a dead room, FFX-style.
THE APPROVED SOUND FOR EACH GAME EVENT. One line per event, and the line only
exists once Paolo has thumbed a candidate up.

MECHANISM-MINE / CONTENTS-PAOLO'S. The synth, the 60 candidates and the judge
surface are mechanism and they shipped. WHICH sound the game makes when you take
a step, land a hit or close a door is HIS, and this table is EMPTY until he says
so. BOH_SFX.BANK is empty in the build to match, and BOH_SFX.play() on an
unbanked event is silent on purpose — the game makes no sound he did not choose.

HOW A LINE GETS HERE
  1. he thumbs candidates in the MUSIC tab (SOUND EFFECTS, top of the panel)
  2. EXPORT SFX -> bohemia_sfx.txt -> lands in records/ as the verdict file
  3. the winning candidate's VECTOR line is copied here, with the verdict file
     named beside it, and BOH_SFX.setBank() is fed the same table
  4. gates/sfx_gate.js fails if a line here cites no verdict file

A LINE IS THE 24-NUMBER VECTOR, NOT AN AUDIO FILE. That is the whole design: a
sound is parameters, so the bank weighs nothing and the sound he approved is
reproducible sample for sample (gates/sfx_render_gate.py fingerprints it).
