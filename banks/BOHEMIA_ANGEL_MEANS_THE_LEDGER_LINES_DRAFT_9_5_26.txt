WHY THIS SHAPE
The nine built verbs are all things you do TO THE WORLD. Every intercessor verb
below is something you do ABOUT WHAT SOMEBODY ELSE DID. That is a different
layer, not a tenth combat move, so round 10 (the rules must not change in the
last act) and round 16 (SAME in the Angel column nine times) both stand.
The measured hole: our game can RECORD and it cannot RESOLVE. A deed is written,
it travels, it fades, and it is never settled.

---- THE SIX, AND WHAT EACH ONE ACTS ON ------------------------------- draft:true
Five of the six act on data we already write and never touch again.

  CARRY          take a record somewhere it can be heard
  WITNESS        record on purpose what the first two generations recorded
                 only by being in the room
  WEIGH          put the two ledgers side by side for one person
  SETTLE         close a line
  SPARE          decline to close it, and let it stand
  STAND BETWEEN  put yourself between two parties

---- THE ONE SENTENCE --------------------------------------------------- draft:true
  THE FIRST TWO GENERATIONS WRITE THE LEDGER.
  THE THIRD IS THE ONLY ONE THAT CAN CLOSE A LINE IN IT.

---- WHAT A CLOSED LINE READS LIKE ------------------------------------- draft:true
(the deed is still there. it is finished, not deleted. round 11's rule: a scar is
a repair, never an erasure.)
  THAT IS PAID.
  NOBODY IS OWED FOR THAT ANY MORE.
  IT HAPPENED. IT IS DONE.
  THE LAST PERSON WHO WAS OWED FOR THAT IS GONE.
  IT STOPS HERE.

---- WHAT A SPARED LINE READS LIKE ------------------------------------- draft:true
(the refusal is a verb. saying no is not the absence of a choice.)
  LET IT STAND.
  SOMEBODY SHOULD STILL BE ANGRY ABOUT THIS.
  NOT YET.
  IT IS NOT MINE TO CLOSE.

---- WHAT WEIGHING TWO LEDGERS READS LIKE ------------------------------ draft:true
(we have exactly two -- what was recorded, and what nobody saw -- and nothing in
the game compares them for a person.)
  EVERYONE KNOWS ABOUT THE FIRST ONE.
  NOBODY EVER KNEW ABOUT THE SECOND.
  THE ONE THEY REMEMBER IS NOT THE WORST ONE.
  HE WAS NEVER CAUGHT FOR THE ONE THAT MATTERED.

---- WHAT STANDING BETWEEN COSTS --------------------------------------- draft:true
(MY ARGUMENT, NOT A CITATION. the record says so twice.)
  TO STAND BETWEEN TWO THINGS YOU HAVE TO STOP BEING ONE OF THEM.
  YOU ARE NOT IN THIS.
  THEY ARE NOT ASKING YOU. THEY ARE ASKING THROUGH YOU.
  YOU CARRY IT UP. YOU DO NOT ANSWER IT.

---- WHAT THIS DELIBERATELY DOES NOT HAVE ---------------------------- (the refusals)
No tenth combat verb. The nine stay exactly as built.
No undo. SETTLE closes a line; nothing here erases a deed, because a deed that
never happened would break the witness web.
No right answer. The traditions themselves disagree about whether a guardian
intercedes at all, and that disagreement is the mechanic, not a bug to fix.
No new rule to learn at hour ninety.
No name for anything, no event, no scene, no number.
