WHY THIS SHAPE
Two rounds already ruled out the obvious answers. The VERBS cannot change,
because skill is domain-specific and a new rule set at the fold makes a beginner
of a master. The HANDS cannot carry, because skill does not transfer. So the
difference cannot live in what the heir can DO.
The real research puts it somewhere else entirely. The best-studied second
generation is the children of immigrants, where the field expected DECLINE and
found broad upward mobility instead, and the mechanism they name is not a trait,
it is a POSITION: the second generation CAN SELECT FROM BOTH WORLDS. They know
what the parent knew and are not bound by it. That is called an ADVANTAGE, and it
is entirely about what they can draw on and how they are read.
The games fix is randomised traits per heir, and a critic names its cost exactly:
the traits are independent of the parent, so the children read "as if they are
all adopted" and the player gets a pre-determined identity instead of developing
one. A CLEAN HEIR IS A RESKIN AND A RANDOM HEIR IS UNRELATED. Both look for the
difference inside the character; one finds none and the other invents some.
THE HEIR IS NOT A NEW CHARACTER. THE HEIR IS THE SAME HANDS IN A ROOM THAT HAS
ALREADY DECIDED SOMETHING ABOUT THEM.
And our machine is already running the divergence. A story carries WHOSE deed it
originally was all the way through retelling, and an inherited deed keeps 45% of
its force per generation crossed while the heir's own deeds enter at full weight.
The heir overtakes the father inside one life, by arithmetic, and nothing shows
it happening.
