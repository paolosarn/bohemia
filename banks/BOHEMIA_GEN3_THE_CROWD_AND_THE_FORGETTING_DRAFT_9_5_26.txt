WHY THIS SHAPE, AND WHAT IT REFUSES
The famous curse (shirtsleeves to shirtsleeves, 30% then 12% then 3%) rests on a
contested number, so nothing here scripts a decline. What the evidence actually
says is that the third generation is CROWDED and has FORGOTTEN: ownership splits
into a cousin consortium with many owners and few of them working, the hardest
problem is resentment between branches, and the best single predictor of a
child's resilience in the study was how much of the family's story they knew,
including the terrible parts.
Our own machine has the opposite problem. Buildings ratchet and never fall,
districts only improve, the hidden advantage only grows, there is no subtraction
anywhere in the fold, there are no cousins at all, and a wound is written and
never read.
