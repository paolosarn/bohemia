WHY THIS SHAPE
Losses are felt harder than equal gains. So a fold that takes a life and hands
back the same amount does not feel level, it feels like a loss, and the heir has
to arrive holding VISIBLY MORE than was taken.
And the machine already knows the exact number. ctFold() returns
{gen, carried, died}: how many of your father's deeds survived because somebody
RETOLD them, and how many died with the last witness. Measured: those two words
appear in exactly two places in the walked city, where they are computed and
where they are returned. NOTHING SHOWS THEM. The game does the most important
arithmetic in the whole dynasty and throws the answer away.
THE ORDER IS THE DESIGN. `died` is the loss, quantified, arriving at the exact
moment a loss is felt hardest. `carried` is the only thing that answers the
question the job is asking. WHAT YOU KEPT COMES FIRST.
And the continuity is already total: the heir keeps the player id, so every card,
every rung and every outfit view still works. The player loses nothing and would
still feel they lost everything, because nothing tells them what they kept.
