WHY THIS SHAPE
The old complaint is that death is a loading screen: a game calls it tragic while
its systems undo it. The fixes that work are all one idea, either something is
taken and does not come back, or THE WORLD KEEPS THE CONSEQUENCE. We cannot use
the first one, because THERE ARE NO RUNS and our fold cannot subtract anything.
We already built the second one. bohemia_succession.js is real and is called: a
role holds requirements rather than a person, killing the holder opens a VACANCY,
the vacancy is CONTESTED with a winner rather than silently reassigned, it
resolves later on a fuse, and if nobody can fill it the thread closes with a
consequence ripple instead of an error.
Two things are missing. The fuse resolves in DAYS (two if uncontested, seventeen
with five claimants) while the locked law says the consequences bloom in DECADE
TWO AND THREE. And a person cannot carry a mark: the only scars in this game are
a rockfall scar on a mountain and the words "combat scars" describing a district.
THE MOUNTAIN SCARS. THE DISTRICT SCARS. THE PERSON DOES NOT.
The biology says a scar is not damage, it is a repair that never finishes: it
never gets back to full strength, it never regrows hair, oil or sweat glands, and
the collagen goes down in parallel bundles instead of the basket weave real skin
has. Stiffer, shinier, visibly different, permanently. THAT IS A STATE, NOT A
SUBTRACTION, which is how a permanent injury lives inside NO DAMAGE BEFORE THE
DIAL.
