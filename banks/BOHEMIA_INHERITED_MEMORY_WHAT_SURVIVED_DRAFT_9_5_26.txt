WHY THIS SHAPE
Spoken memory has a measured lifespan: 80 to 110 years, three to four
generations, and then the FLOATING GAP, after which only what was written,
ritualised or built survives. BOHEMIA IS A HUNDRED YEARS AND THREE GENERATIONS,
so the game is exactly as long as a family's spoken memory and ends precisely
where people stop being able to tell you about it.
The forgetting is faster than anybody expects: more than half of people cannot
name all four grandparents, one in five cannot name a single great-grandparent,
and yet 84% say knowing where they came from matters. THE GAP BETWEEN CARING A
LOT AND KNOWING ALMOST NOTHING IS THE FEELING THE HEIR SHOULD HAVE.
We already model the fading half properly: a deed has to be seen from nine
tiles, can be retold at most twice, weakens 55% each retelling, loses 45%
crossing a generation, and only a deed somebody RETOLD survives the death of the
eyewitness at all. The machine even counts what carried and what died.
What we do not model is the other half. The only thing that crosses the hundred
years is one function that turns two numbers into one of three words.

---- WHAT THIS DELIBERATELY DOES NOT HAVE ---------------------------- (the refusals)
No summary screen of your father's life. That is the exposition failure, and it
contradicts the measured amnesia: real second generations get fragments and a
lot of missing, never a briefing.
No complete family history. One in five people cannot name a single
great-grandparent. An heir who knows everything is a fantasy about memory.
No rewrite of the standing web. It is the best modelled thing in this repo and
this is not a criticism of it.
No fourth memory system. There are already two folds; the answer is fewer.
No damage, health or armour numbers.
