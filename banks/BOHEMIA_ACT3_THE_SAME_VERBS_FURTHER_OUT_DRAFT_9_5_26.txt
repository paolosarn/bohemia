WHY THIS SHAPE
The obvious read of "transcendent last act" is new powers. The learning research
says that is the one move that would break it: far transfer between loosely
related domains is very hard to find at all, expertise is domain-specific, and
the longer somebody trains a skill the MORE specific their proficiency gets, so
being an expert can actually LIMIT transfer. A NEW RULE SET IN THE LAST ACT MAKES
THE PLAYER A BEGINNER AT THE EXACT MOMENT THE STORY SAYS THEY BECAME A GOD.
His own zoom law already holds the answer, written about a camera: one camera,
one scalar, the same diamond at every band, no dead ends. THE RULES DO NOT
CHANGE, THE SCALE DOES. Same verbs, further out. That is near transfer, which
works.
And the cost is already drawn on the screen. Experiments on power found that
high-power people draw an E on their forehead facing THEMSELVES, fail to allow
for others not knowing what they know, and read emotions less accurately. POWER
DOES NOT MAKE YOU CRUEL, IT MAKES YOU UNABLE TO SEE FROM WHERE ANYBODY ELSE IS
STANDING. At planetary zoom a person is not visible. That is the measured cost of
power, rendered as a camera we already built.
