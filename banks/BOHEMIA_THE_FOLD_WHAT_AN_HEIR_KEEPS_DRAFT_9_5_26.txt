WHY THIS SHAPE
Our fold folds eight kinds of thing and decays exactly one of them: a faction
standing loses 25% a generation, while territory, builds, economy capacity,
investment, karma and virtues carry at 100% forever. The measured world is the
other way round. Social status persists at about 0.79 a generation and takes ten
to fifteen generations to fade; wealth and occupation persist at about 0.3 to
0.4. THE NAME OUTLASTS THE MONEY BY ROUGHLY TWO TO ONE.
So the 0.75 our engine already uses is a good number on the wrong field.
And a relative in our tree is {id, rel, alive}: three fields, no name. A wound is
a bare target: no who, no when.

---- WHAT THIS DELIBERATELY DOES NOT HAVE ---------------------------- (the refusals)
No skill carryover. Both directions are the genre's known failure: withhold
everything and the handoff reads as deleting your character, carry everything and
the family is what goes missing.
No inheritance screen with numbers on it. What the heir gets should be met, not
read.
No stat bonus for being well born. Growth is horizontal (day 9) and what you
inherit is a door, not a number (day 10).
No rewrite of the 0.75 to match a paper. It is already within 0.04 and false
precision on a game is worse than the rough number.
No second inheritance system beside the fold. There is one fold.
