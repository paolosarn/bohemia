WHY THIS SHAPE
Two earlier findings look like they contradict each other here. The animal's verb
set is eight things and not one needs a hand, so the animal cannot play the human
game. And the rules must NOT change in the last act, because skill is
domain-specific and a new rule set makes a beginner of a master.
Both are right. THE TWO FOLDS ARE NOT THE SAME KIND OF FOLD. At the first fold
the player has about five hours invested and losing it is cheap, and it is the
strongest teaching moment the game will ever have. At the second the player has
about ninety hours invested and losing it is unacceptable.
THE FIRST FOLD CHANGES THE VERBS. THE SECOND ONE MUST NOT.
