WHY THIS SHAPE
Ageing measured is two curves crossing, not one slope. Reaction time peaks in the
early-to-mid twenties and is about 20 to 30 percent slower by sixty; muscle goes
3 to 5 percent a decade from thirty; grip peaks in the early thirties. But
anticipation and pattern recognition RESIST age and keep growing with experience,
and expert older performers beat younger novices at reading their own domain. The
one cost with no compensation at all is HEALING: a cut that closed in two weeks
takes three or four.
AN OLD FIGHTER DOES NOT GET WEAKER. HE GETS FEWER CHANCES AND HE STOPS NEEDING AS
MANY.
And we already own the equation: bohemia_memory.js runs
clarity = 0.5^(age/halflife) with halflife growing as log2 of familiarity,
commented "familiarity slows the fog". That is the ageing curve. It is pointed at
what the city remembers instead of at what a body can do.

---- WHAT THIS DELIBERATELY DOES NOT HAVE ---------------------------- (the refusals)
No new portrait plus a stat penalty. That is exactly the thing the job says not
to do, and it is a subtraction the player cannot answer.
No age in years. No birth years. No calendar. The PEOPLE lane already refused a
cohort-by-birth-year generator because it would be inventing canon to do
arithmetic on, and that refusal was right.
No wisdom stat. Knowing what happens next is a READ, not a roll: the old
character sees the tell sooner, never rolls higher.
No damage, health or armour number of any kind.
No single ageing curve across all three generations. Gen 1 is short on purpose
and a coyote's danger is a vacancy and a car, not a slow decline. One curve for
three lives is the generation-blind mistake round 1 already found in the fold.

WHAT IS HIS AND IS NOT DECIDED HERE
Where the curves cross and how sharply. Whether anybody ages inside a generation
at all or only between them. How long a wound takes. What the old one is called,
and who is still alive to remember him.
