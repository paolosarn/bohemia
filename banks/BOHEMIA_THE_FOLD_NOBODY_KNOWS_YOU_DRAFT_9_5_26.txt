WHY THIS SHAPE
Games do a time skip as an art job: decay the rooms, dirty the textures, close
the routes. Real life does it as a CAST CHANGE. If half a neighbourhood's renters
move every two years, about three in a hundred are still there after ten. The
buildings stand. The people are gone. And when researchers ask long-term
residents what changed, they name institutions, safety, and who lives here now,
never the frontages.
Our valley has been a ruin for ten years and will be a ruin for a hundred, so a
repaint means nothing here. What means something is walking into a place you own,
that looks identical, where almost nobody knows your face.
AND HIS OWN LAW ALREADY RULES THE CUT ITSELF: TEN YEARS COLD says the crash is
never played, only its aftermath. Same for the gap. DO NOT PLAY THE TEN YEARS.
