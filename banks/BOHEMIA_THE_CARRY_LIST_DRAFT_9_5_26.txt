WHY THIS SHAPE
The brief names "the debt" first among what a child of a debtor inherits, and it
is the one item that legally does NOT carry: a child is not personally liable for
a parent's unsecured debts, creditors claim against the ESTATE, and an insolvent
estate means the creditor loses. YOU DO NOT INHERIT A BILL. YOU INHERIT LESS, AND
YOU INHERIT THE PEOPLE HE OWED, STILL STANDING THERE.
And that is where it becomes ours: debt stops inheriting only because there is a
court to stop it. BOHEMIA HAS NO COURTS. Our standing web's "you are born owing
what your father owed" is not a fantasy mechanic and not a softening. It is what
the law exists to prevent, running in a valley where the law is gone.
The trade carries as a PULL, not a destiny. A son of a military father is five
times more likely to enlist and only one in four does. Only 3% of farmers' sons
stay, and that 3% is seven and a half times the base rate. Huge multipliers,
small absolute numbers. THE STRONGEST PULL IN A CHILD'S LIFE USUALLY LOSES.
