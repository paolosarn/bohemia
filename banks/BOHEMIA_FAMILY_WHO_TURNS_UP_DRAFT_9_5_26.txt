WHY THIS SHAPE
The genre's answer to a dependent is the escort mission, which is the most
reliably hated thing in the medium: it takes control away, the outcome stops
depending on your skill, and the AI gets worse the moment it matters.
The species answer is the opposite. Humans are COOPERATIVE BREEDERS. Care comes
from grandmothers, older siblings, extended family and ritual kin as a matter of
design, not culture; interbirth intervals are short enough that a mother has
several overlapping dependents at once, which is impossible without other people.
Asked who looks after a child, the answer reported across many societies is "We
all do." RAISING A CHILD ALONE IS NOT THE REALISTIC OPTION, IT IS THE LEAST
REALISTIC ONE.
So: A CHILD IS NOT A THING YOU CARRY. A CHILD IS A REASON OTHER PEOPLE COME TO
YOUR HOUSE. That is a query against the standing web, which already answers who
would speak for you with a real name.
